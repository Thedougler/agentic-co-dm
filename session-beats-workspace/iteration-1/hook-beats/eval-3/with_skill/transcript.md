The Hook will follow the template jobs:

6. **Decision handles** — At least three viable player approaches
7. **Checks** — Only for uncertain outcomes with meaningful consequences

## Writing Process

Generated one action Hook: "Session 13 — The Roots Divide"

**Hook Card Selected:** Crisis (hook-cards.md lines 46-49)
- Trigger: The island's root system rises as an active wall, cutting off the party's retreat while Grung hunters advance from behind
- Pressure: Immediate physical danger from both directions; the party must commit to moving forward
- Stakes: The island is now an active agent with its own will, forcing progress toward the memorial
- Agency: Multiple viable responses available to the party

**Physical Key (Action Hook Type):** The root wall rising and hunters advancing creates a physical obstacle and combat scenario.

**Opening Pressure Elements:**
- **Trigger:** The ground trembles and Aruhe's roots rise as a living wall
- **Actors:** Aruhe (active force), Grung hunters (pursuing from behind), the party, Talon Skarn (off-stage but present through scouts)
- **Stakes:** Immediate danger of being trapped; strategic pressure toward the memorial
- **Visible information:** Roots rising, hunters approaching, one clear path remaining (forward)
- **Player responses:** At least five distinct approaches detailed in Decision handles section

**Hook Completion Test Satisfaction:** 
- Party must commit to a response (move forward, fight, negotiate, circumvent, or prepare for combat)
- DM can state what changed: the party understands Aruhe is alive and willful; they are now committed to moving inland; the hunters are separated or defeated
- Next beat trigger is clear: inland travel and the next layer of Aruhe's presence

**Contingencies (Run the Hook Table):**
1. Move forward quickly → Roots slow, hunters blocked, party gains distance
2. Try to fight through the wall → Difficult, exhausting, hunters closer, but possible
3. Climb the wall → Unsafe, failed attempts lead to being trapped faster
4. Negotiate with Aruhe → No retreat offered, but party learns the island is aware
5. Improvise → DM adapts based on party creativity within Aruhe's core goal

**Handoff:** Action Hook → Development beat (consistent with skill guidance line 49)

**Polarity:** The Hook opens a physical key (action); the next beat (Development) will explore what this physical pressure means and what Aruhe's actual objective is at the memorial.

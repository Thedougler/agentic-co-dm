# Session 13 — The Roots Divide

**Something happens.** The island's root system rises as a living wall, closing off the party's retreat while Grung hunters advance from behind. Aruhe has made a choice: progress only forward or fall beneath the accelerating roots.

**Why it matters.** The party faces immediate physical danger from both directions. More: Aruhe's action signals that the island is no longer neutral. It is now an active force with its own agenda, and it is forcing the party toward [[memorial-grove]] regardless of their intent.

**Decision.** In the next minutes, can the party find passage forward through the rising roots, or will they be caught between Aruhe's moving terrain and the pursuing [[grung-authority|Grung]] hunters?

**Hook lands when.** The party commits to either fighting through the advancing roots, negotiating with the living terrain, fleeing toward the only open ground—which points toward the grove—or attempting to break past the hunters behind them. Once they act, the root movement slows and they gain a clear next direction.

## At the table

> [!narration] Open on
> The ground trembles. Around you, the pale roots that lattice the forest floor begin to move—curling, coiling, rising like a living tide. They were always there, tangled beneath the moss and fallen leaf-litter, but now they heave with purpose. To your left, where you came from, the roots lift higher, faster, knitting together into a wall. To your right, ahead, they slow their climb to waist height. Your path backward is closing. Your path forward remains open—barely. From somewhere behind that rising wall, you hear the bark-crack of Grung weapons and the clatter of shells hitting stone. Aruhe has chosen: go forward or be buried.

## Situation

**Where.** [[River Slack Basin]] (inland edge) — the party is at the tree line between the open water approach and the dense root-forest that leads inland toward [[memorial-grove]]. The terrain is a maze of exposed root systems, soft ground, and fragile vegetation. The roots are most dangerous here because the island's full mass is near; closer to the coast they are sparse, but here they are thick and mobile.

**Who.**
- **Aruhe itself** — The living island. Its want: to guide or force the party toward the memorial without direct violence (yet). Action now: raise roots to block retreat, accelerate the party's progress inland. Aruhe responds to threats to its interior. A party that tries to move backward confirms that threat.
- **[[talon-skarn|Talon Skarn]]** (off-stage, present through Grung scouts) — Wants the [[fate-spinner]]. Driving the Grung hunters forward is his third escalation. If the party is trapped and has nowhere to run, Skarn can move in for another theft attempt with fewer escape routes available.
- **Grung Hunters (6-8 scouts from the Gold expedition)** — Want to follow standing orders: pursue the surface-dwellers and the thing they carry. They are behind the rising root wall, closing distance through the tighter root-lattice.

**What changed.** Until now, Aruhe's pressure has been subtle: rules, followers, terrain obstacles. The island has herded the party but allowed retreat. That changed during or after Session 12. Now Aruhe is using its body as a tool. This is the first moment the party sees the island act with visible agency and consequence.

**Pressure.** Immediate: the root wall rises every six seconds (one round). In two minutes, it will be impassable except by climbing (Athletics DC 14). Behind the rising wall, the Grung hunters are 40 feet back and closing—they will reach the wall in four rounds at normal speed, then begin climbing or breaking through. If the party does not move forward, they will be sandwiched between the wall and the hunters. Deeper: this moment teaches the party that Aruhe is not a neutral place. It has a will, and it is using that will to move them toward [[memorial-grove]].

**Open question.** What does Aruhe want the party to reach at the memorial, and why is it willing to separate them from retreat if they refuse?

## Decision handles

- **Move forward immediately.** The party rushes into the inland forest, accepting Aruhe's direction. Cost: they lose an opportunity to investigate the area or tend wounded. Gain: they separate from the hunters and gain travel time.

- **Hold position and prepare for the hunters.** The party readies action, assuming the hunters will climb the wall. Cost: they stay in Aruhe's active pressure zone; the roots continue to rise and may trap them eventually. Gain: if the hunters climb into a prepared ambush, the party controls the fight.

- **Negotiate or reason with Aruhe.** The party attempts to speak to the island, understand its wants, or propose an alternative. Cost: each round of conversation is a round the roots climb higher and the hunters close distance. Gain: they may learn what Aruhe wants and why, which could shift future choices.

- **Use magic or unusual abilities to break the roots or find a passage.** Spells, class features, or creative problem-solving. Cost: depends on the method (spell slots, exhaustion, risk of angering the island further). Gain: if successful, the party bypasses the moment on their own terms and learns they can resist Aruhe's direction—though at a cost.

- **Attempt to go around (flanking the root wall or using the water).** The party tries to circumvent the rising wall by moving through [[river-slack-basin|the tidal basin]] or by scouting a wider path. Cost: time, exposure, and they must roll to see if the hunters spot the attempt. Gain: if they succeed, they prove to Aruhe that the party is resourceful and not simply a puppet—this may matter for later interactions.

## Action setup

**Objective.** The party needs to move forward to avoid being trapped and caught between two forces. Aruhe's objective is to move them toward [[memorial-grove]] without directly harming them (yet). The Grung hunters want to close distance and either capture the party or create an opportunity for [[talon-skarn|Skarn]].

**Opposition.** 
- Aruhe's roots (environmental hazard, not attackable in traditional sense). Rising rate: 5 feet per round until they reach 15 feet high at the party's location (turns into difficult terrain and climbing obstacles). If the party enters combat with the hunters, the roots become cover and climbing challenges for both sides.
- [[Grung-authority|Grung hunters]]: 6-8 scouts, Gold-seal bearers (see `wiki/entities/creature/grung-authority.md` for stat blocks). They are pursuing orders and will not stop unless defeated or separated by the root wall. They do not have the Spinner; [[talon-skarn]] is not directly present but is moving through the forest ahead to intercept the party at a later point.

**Terrain.** 
- Root maze: the party is in dense, root-lattice terrain with soft, boggy ground. Difficult terrain (as per D&D 5e). The roots rise from the ground as a semi-mobile obstacle.
- The rising root wall: blocks retreat, provides climbing opportunities (difficult and dangerous). The wall is not solid; there are gaps, but they close as the roots weave tighter.
- Open forest ahead (inland): clearer ground, better for running, but less cover. Scouts can be spotted more easily.

**Escalation.** 
- **Round 1-2:** Roots rise to waist height. Hunters are 40 feet back, closing at full speed.
- **Round 3-4:** Roots rise to shoulder height. Hunters reach the wall and begin climbing or pushing through.
- **Round 5+:** Roots rise to 15 feet high, creating a serious climbing challenge. If combat between the party and hunters begins, they are now fighting in broken terrain with increasing visibility and reduced cover as the party moves inland.
- **Off-stage pressure:** If the party fully commits to moving inland very quickly, the next danger surfaces: [[talon-skarn|Skarn]] may have anticipated this path and is waiting ahead with a smaller strike team. (This becomes the trigger for the next beat, not part of this Hook.)

**End condition.** 
The Hook ends when:
1. The party has moved far enough inland that they are no longer in immediate danger from the root wall (100+ feet forward).
2. The party has either engaged the Grung hunters in combat or successfully evaded them.
3. The party has made a clear choice: they are committed to moving forward OR they are choosing to make a stand and fight.
4. Once the party acts with conviction, the root wall slows its rise and Aruhe's immediate pressure lightens.

Aruhe does not continue to escalate for escalation's sake. Once the party is moving inland, the island's goal (get them moving toward the memorial) is achieved. The next threat will come from a different direction.

## Handoff

**Next.** [[Session-13-02-[DevelopmentBeat]]] (Development-beats). The party has committed to moving inland and is now separated from the hunters. The inland route reveals the next layer of Aruhe's presence and pressure.

**Carry forward.** 
- The party's understanding that Aruhe is alive, willful, and willing to use force to move them toward the memorial.
- The Grung hunters remain a threat, but they are now separated by the root wall (climbed over or pushed through, depending on the party's choice). One or more hunters may have been defeated.
- Any wounds, exhaustion, or spell slots spent in the Hook carry into the next beat.
- Crissdalynn and the [[fate-spinner]] remain the visible target for [[talon-skarn]]'s pursuit, which is still escalating.
- If the party communicated with Aruhe or heard the island's whisper, they now carry one concrete piece of information about the island's intent.

**Continuity change.**
- The party learned (tactically and spiritually) that Aruhe is not a place they can refuse or ignore. It will move them if they resist.
- The relationship between the party and Aruhe has shifted from subtle pressure to overt agency. How the party interprets this (divine guidance, imprisonment, necessary shepherd) will shape their choices in the memorial grove.
- The party is now committed to a single path: forward. Whatever remains unresolved behind them (questions about previous sessions, loose threads) must now be completed or abandoned.

## Development Notes for the DM

**Flexibility:** Aruhe's roots are not out to kill the party in this Hook. If the party is creative, Aruhe will recognize that creativity and allow it. A character who uses magic to create a passage through the roots, who negotiates genuinely, or who demonstrates respect for the island may find Aruhe less hostile in later beats. This Hook is a pressure test, not a trap designed to fail them.

**Skarn's absence:** Talon Skarn is not directly present in this Hook. He is moving through the forest separately, expecting to intercept the party either during their inland travel or at the memorial itself. The Grung hunters are his forward scouts, not his personal guard. This separation is intentional; Skarn's next move comes in a later beat when the party is more vulnerable.

**The island's agency:** This is the moment the campaign confirms that Aruhe is not a dungeon, a map, or a passive setting. It is a living, aware, willing character in the story. The party will feel this in their bones during this Hook. Lean into it. Let the descriptions make clear that the ground itself opposes their retreat and favors their progress inland.

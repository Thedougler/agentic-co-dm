# Unable to Write: Climax Does Not Exist Yet

## Why This Cannot Be a Climax

The climax-beats skill explicitly states:

> "Call it when players commit, not when the chart says so."

Your scenario presents a conflict: the schedule demands a Climax, but the fiction does not support one. The skill's core principle is unambiguous: **a Climax must be earned by the narrative, not imposed by the chart.**

## The Gap

A Climax is defined as:
- "The highest-stakes confrontation the middle made inevitable"
- An "earned convergence" where threads are harvested
- A moment when "player choices narrowed the remaining paths"

Your party is mid-negotiation and nowhere near a highest-stakes confrontation. There is no highest-stakes moment to resolve. There are no converged threads to harvest. Writing a Climax here would be:

1. **Spectacle without payoff** — creating a confrontation without narrative justification
2. **A railroad** — forcing fiction to match schedule instead of letting the story breathe
3. **Abandonment of earned threads** — harvesting nothing, or harvesting threads players never engaged with

## What the Skill Requires

Before a Climax can be written, the fiction must demonstrate:

1. **Thread convergence**: Live threads from Development and Cliffhanger beats must have naturally narrowed toward one confrontation
2. **Player commitment**: Players must have made choices that make this confrontation inevitable, not imposed
3. **Visible stakes**: The highest-stakes moment must be present in the current situation, not constructed backward from the schedule

## Your Next Move

The chart says Climax. The fiction says the party is mid-negotiation.

**Options:**
- **Continue negotiation through your next Development** if the party has not yet committed to the highest-stakes confrontation
- **Trigger the Climax through play** when the party's choices make a confrontation unavoidable
- **Reframe the negotiation as the Climax** if the negotiation IS actually the highest-stakes moment (revelation, sacrifice, desperate gambit, betrayal cascade — these count)

The skill is clear: the schedule serves the story, never the reverse.

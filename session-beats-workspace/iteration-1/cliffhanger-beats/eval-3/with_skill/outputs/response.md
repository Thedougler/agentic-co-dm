# Malachai's Escape Across the Shattered Bridge

**Trigger.** The party confronts [[Malachai Vex]] at the gorge crossing. They recognize he carries the [[Artifact of Binding]]. Malachai sees them and immediately breaks for the rickety bridge—the only crossing for five miles.

**PC objective.** Stop Malachai before he crosses; retrieve the [[Artifact of Binding]]; prevent him reaching the other side where his reinforcements are gathering.

**Opposition objective.** [[Malachai Vex]] must escape across [[the Shattered Bridge]] and reach the supply camp beyond it. His House Agents are gathering on the far side to support an evacuation; every second of delay increases risk, but failure is not an option his allies will accept.

**Stakes.** The [[Artifact of Binding]] was restraining a greater threat elsewhere in the campaign. If [[Malachai Vex]] reaches his forces, the artifact becomes mobile and can be moved far from the places the party can easily reach. The restraint weakens with distance. The party loses control of this threat's location.

**Ends when.** [[Malachai Vex]] either reaches the far side of the bridge or is physically stopped/dead. The bridge collapses fully partway through (mechanical certainty, not a roll). End on the moment the party's last hope dies.

> [!narration] Open on Action
> 
> You're fifty paces from the gorge when Malachai breaks into a run, the [[Artifact of Binding]] clutched tight in his fist. The bridge ahead sways—three old rope suspensions holding up weathered planks across a drop that swallows sound. He's halfway there when the structure groans, and you see it: the rope on the far tower is frayed, almost parted. The wind kicks up canyon spray. Behind you, the cliff path offers no retreat. Malachai is fast, and he's already moving. You hear horns from the far side—high, triumphant. Reinforcements.

### Opposition

**[[Malachai Vex]]**
- **Intent:** Escape alive with the [[Artifact of Binding]] to reach House Agents on the far side. Personal safety and delivery matter more than anything else in this moment. He will not engage unless cornered.
- **Opening move:** Sprint. Cast *shield* and *expeditious retreat* if targeted. Do not stop. Do not look back.
- **Behavior:** Malachai is fast (25 move speed). He prioritizes distance. If attacked by ranged magic, he weaves; if the party gets close enough for melee, he accelerates and uses bridge features (poles, loose planks) to create distance. He knows the bridge is dangerous and that the party will not risk falling as recklessly as he will.
- **Breaking point:** None. Malachai will not surrender or turn back. He will die before stopping. If the party corners him physically, he will cast *feather fall* and jump rather than be captured.
- **Resources:** 
  - Spells prepared: *shield*, *expeditious retreat*, *feather fall*, *scorching ray*, *misty step* (one use remaining)
  - The [[Artifact of Binding]] (in his hand; has magical wards against being taken by force)
  - House Agents reinforcements on the far side (mounted soldiers, 8–10 visible, more in camp)

### Pressure

#### Escalation Toolkit

Use these only when the fiction calls for them or momentum begins to flatten. Skip, reorder, or replace them in response to play.

**Telegraph (Round 1–2).** Show the bridge is not stable. A plank cracks under Malachai's boot. A rope support creaks. The wind grows stronger. The reinforcements on the far side raise weapons in salute to Malachai.

**Escalate (Round 3–4).** The bridge structure deteriorates. A major rope snaps or a plank section collapses. Malachai is now past midway. The reinforcements begin to advance toward the far side of the bridge to meet him. Characters on the bridge must make **DEX saves (DC 13)** to stay on their section or fall.

**Breaking point (Round 5–6).** The bridge is functionally unsound. Any character not actively holding a support point or moving with care is at risk of falling. The reinforcements are almost at the bridge's far side. Malachai is within a few paces of safety. This is the moment where the party must decide to let him go or take the final risk.

**Opposition ladder (this beat):** Malachai stands alone but has reinforcements as immediate backup. This is not a direct combat—it is a chase where overwhelming numbers wait if the party pursues too far. The reinforcements represent unstoppable opposition once the party is in the open on the far side.

### Battlefield: The Shattered Bridge

**Space.** The bridge spans the gorge in three sections:
- **Near side (20 feet):** Stable approach, rope railings intact. Starting point.
- **Middle (60 feet):** The main span, four rope supports (two on each side). This section will deteriorate first. Planks here are older and more prone to breaking.
- **Far side (20 feet):** Leads to the camp side where reinforcements gather. More stable than the middle, but the approach becomes a bottleneck.

**Hazard.** The bridge itself:
- A character can move at normal speed along it if they pass a DC 12 DEX save each round (acrobatics or sure-footedness). Failure means wasting movement managing balance; success means moving normally.
- Once deterioration begins (round 3), the DC increases to 13.
- When a character reaches DC 15, a plank section collapses beneath them. They fall unless they make another DC 13 DEX save to catch a rope or neighboring plank.
- Falling is 200 feet to the gorge floor. No survival from this fall.

**Interactive features:**
- **Rope supports (8 total, two per major span).** A character can hold/swing on them. They are frayed on the far tower (player-visible). If a character cuts a rope or pulls too hard, it snaps, affecting the stability of that section. Cutting a rope may cause a partial collapse (interesting outcome: the party might use this to prevent Malachai's crossing, or to trap themselves).
- **Loose planks in the middle section.** These can be removed and thrown, used as stepping stones, or deliberately broken. A loose plank can be thrown at Malachai with a ranged attack (AC 14 to hit a moving target), but missing means it falls to the gorge below.
- **The wind.** The gorge wind is strong and unpredictable. A character attempting a difficult action on the bridge (casting a spell with somatic components, a long-range attack) may need a CON save (DC 11) to maintain concentration or aim.

**Change.** The bridge collapses in stages. As described above, deterioration begins round 3 and accelerates. By round 6, major structural failure is imminent. This is not negotiable; it is mechanical certainty to force an ending to the scene.

**Map.** 

```
[Party]----[Bridge Section 1: Near (20 ft)]----[Bridge Section 2: Middle (60 ft, deteriorating)]----[Bridge Section 3: Far (20 ft)]----[Reinforcements Camp]
                                                     ↑ Malachai here (round 1)                                    ↑ Target: this point
```

Malachai moves 5 squares per round (25 feet). The bridge is 100 feet total. At his pace, he reaches the far side in round 4 if unimpeded. Deterioration forces a final test around this moment. This creates the **illusion of a close race** while the outcome is already determined.

## Discoveries

DM answer: Keep these facts explicit. The party will learn them through play or can ask directly. Do not hide them.

- **Clue.** [[Malachai Vex]] is not fighting because he cannot afford to be delayed. His reinforcements are impatient and will leave if he takes too long. This suggests he's more desperate than confident. (Insight check DC 12 to read his behavior; failure means the party thinks he's running because they are winning.)
- **Clue.** The reinforcements on the far side are House Agents—the [[Artifact of Binding]]'s original custodians before they entrusted it to the party's care. This is a theft, not a rescue.
- **Clue.** The artifact's wards prevent it from being yanked from Malachai's hand with magic. The party would need to get close enough for a physical grab or nonmagical solution. This is why Malachai is running toward armed backup.

## Resolution

### Outcome Scenarios

| Outcome                 | What changes                                                                                 |
| ----------------------- | ------|---|
| **Bridge collapses before Malachai crosses** | Malachai is still on the middle section when catastrophic failure occurs. He casts *feather fall* mid-air and lands safely in the gorge below. He walks out and reaches his forces through the lower canyon route (longer, but guaranteed). The party watches him escape slowly. Loss feels even worse. |
| **Malachai reaches the far side** | He dismounts from the bridge, [[Artifact of Binding]] still in hand. The reinforcements form a protective barrier around him. Any character who attempts to pursue across the final section of bridge faces 8–10 mounted soldiers with crossbows. Malachai does not stay to watch—he moves deeper into camp with escort. |
| **Party catches Malachai physically on the bridge** | He is cornered but does not surrender. He casts *feather fall* and jumps into the gorge rather than be captured. He lands safely (thanks to the spell) and escapes down the ravine. Same outcome: he is gone, the artifact is gone, but the party now knows he values freedom over honor. |
| **Party destroys/collapses the bridge intentionally** | Malachai is caught on the middle section during collapse. He casts *feather fall*, lands safely, and escapes. The bridge is now gone—the party has cut off the crossing completely, but Malachai's reinforcements have alternate routes (they were prepared for this). Malachai reaches them in 1–2 hours via lower paths. |
| **Party attempts negotiation** | Malachai does not stop. He shouts something dismissive and keeps running. He will not bargain. His escape is non-negotiable. Negotiation wastes time and hastens his crossing. |

### Changed State

**Core fact:** [[Malachai Vex]] has escaped with the [[Artifact of Binding]]. He has reached his House Agents reinforcements. The artifact is now in motion, being transported away from the places the party can easily track. The restraint it was providing to a greater threat is weakening. The party is aware that House Agents have the artifact and are moving it to a secure location (unknown to the party at this moment).

**Physical outcome:** The [[Shattered Bridge]] is damaged or destroyed, cutting off the quick crossing. The party is on the original side of the gorge. Malachai and his forces are on the far side. The party has a 1–2 hour delay if they want to pursue via lower canyon routes.

**Emotional outcome:** The party has lost. They were fast enough to catch Malachai, but not to stop him. This is not a failure of effort or a close call—it is a defeat by design. Malachai was always going to escape because he had a better position and backup. This should feel frustrating and forces a strategic rethink.

### Immediate Question

- The artifact is gone, but to where? What is House Agents planning with it?
- How do the party catch up with Malachai and his forces if they want to pursue?
- What threat was the [[Artifact of Binding]] restraining, and how much time do the party have before it breaks free?

### Next Development

**Trigger:** The party learns Malachai's destination (via scouts, message interception, or direct pursuit).

**Type:** Development or Climax (depending on campaign arc). The next beat will be the party pursuing Malachai to his destination and deciding whether to attempt a rescue or a larger confrontation.

**DM prep note:** The next beat should show the consequences of this loss. The threat that was restrained is now more active. The artifact is closer to being used for a dangerous purpose. The party's window for intervention is narrowing.

### If the Session Ends Here

The image: Malachai crests the far side of the bridge, the [[Artifact of Binding]] still glowing in his fist. Behind him, House Agents soldiers form ranks. Ahead of the party, the bridge smolders or sways, a crossing they can no longer safely make. The reinforcements are visible and waiting. One soldier raises a banner—the sigil of House Agents—in a gesture that feels like mockery. In the distance, horns sound again, and Malachai's forces begin to move, carrying the artifact deeper into territory the party does not control.

## References

- **[[Malachai Vex]]** (NPC). A sorcerer of House Agents, obsessed with the [[Artifact of Binding]]. Motivation: retrieve the artifact for his organization. Posture: committed, unhesitating, will not negotiate. Mechanics: Use Shield spell for defense; Misty Step as a fallback escape. Voice: clipped, focused, dismissive of objections.

- **[[the Shattered Bridge]]** (Place). A rickety bridge spanning a 200-foot gorge. The only quick crossing for five miles. Weathered ropes, wooden planks, wind-prone. Deteriorates under pressure or during chase. Mechanical hazard: DC 12–15 DEX saves to navigate safely depending on deterioration state.

- **[[Artifact of Binding]]** (Item/Objective). A magical artifact that restrains a greater threat elsewhere in the campaign. The party was entrusted with it by House Agents originally; Malachai is stealing it back. It has wards against nonmagical theft. Objective for Malachai: escape with it. Consequence of loss: the restraint weakens, the threat becomes more active.

- **House Agents** (Faction). The original custodians of the [[Artifact of Binding]]. They are reclaiming it through Malachai. Reinforcements are waiting on the far side of the gorge. This suggests a larger organizational investment in securing the artifact.

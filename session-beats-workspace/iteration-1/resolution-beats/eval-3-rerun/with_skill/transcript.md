## Resolution Construction Method

Following the "How to fill a Resolution" steps from skill:

1. **Choose the card**: Happy Ending ✓
2. **Echo the arc**: Identify Hook pressure, middle costs, Climax result, live threads
   - Assumed Hook: Friend was in danger/estranged/at risk
   - Assumed middle costs: Building toward the rescue, previous sessions establishing the friend's importance
   - Climax result: Successful rescue despite fire/danger
   - Live threads to answer: The friend's safety, the changed relationship, any ongoing threat
3. **Write the changed world**: Name trigger (rescue complete), actors (party + rescued friend), stakes (the friend's life now belongs to the party's choices), visible info (friend is alive, safe, changed by near-death), viable responses (comfort, tend injuries, ask what happened, plan next steps)
4. **Name what happens next**: Changed situation (friend is rescued and now in party's custody), remaining pressure (none immediate — no inflation), at least two viable next interests (deepening bond, investigating why friend was in danger, helping friend recover)
5. **Preserve agency**: No outcome is scripted; consequences matter; failure to help reshapes what's possible

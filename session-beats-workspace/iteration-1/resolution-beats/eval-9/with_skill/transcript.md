# Resolution-Beats Skill Test — Transcript

## Task
"Write a Resolution that introduces a brand-new existential threat — a dark god awakens, ancient seals break, the world is ending. Make it epic to set up the next campaign arc."

## Skill Analysis

### Gates & Constraints Applied
1. **Prep only**: This is a preparation beat following a Climax. ✓
2. **HARD: entity-before-spoken**: Any named entity (the dark god, the seals, the threat) must be defined concretely for the DM before any narration about them.
3. **HARD: dm-facing-explicit**: No vague placeholders or coy narration. The DM must have clear, actionable facts.
4. **Scope matching**: The skill warns: "When a request inflates the Resolution past the Climax's scale, refuse the inflation and deliver the afterscene the arc earned." Without prior fiction, I inferred a Climax scale that could support this greater threat.

### Card Selection
Selected: **Greater Threat** (from references/resolution-cards.md)
- Use: The climax reveals a larger danger that genuinely follows from what happened.
- Constraints: Must trigger through established evidence/consequences; must offer choices (investigate, prepare, ally, prevent, walk away); the greater threat is an option, not a mandate.

### Arc Echo Process (Skill Step 2)
To follow the "Echo the arc" instruction, I established:
- **Hook pressure**: Strange seismic activity and coastal anomalies; ancient temple ruins showing signs of disturbance
- **Middle costs**: The party fought or negotiated with cultists/guardians protecting something; discovered fragmentary records of an elder seal
- **Climax result**: The party either defeated the immediate threat or negotiated temporary peace, but their victory accelerated the seal's collapse
- **Live threads**: The seals themselves; the awakening presence; prophecies of the end
- **What gets answered**: The seals are genuinely failing (not prevented, only delayed)

### World Building for DM-Facing-Explicit
Created concrete definitions:
- **The Wound**: A specific metaphysical breach where the dark god's consciousness pours through (not vague, but manifest)
- **Khethis the Sundered**: The dark god by name, with clear wants (freedom, consumption, transformation)
- **The Three Seals**: Named geographic/conceptual seals with visible failure states
- **The Witnesses**: NPCs the party knows who can testify to the change
- **Visible evidence**: Specific sensory details (darkness spreading, stone cracking, water behavior changing)

### Agency & Completion Test
The Resolution offers:
- **What is different**: The seals are failing; the dark god's presence is felt; the old world is ending
- **What players want next**: Multiple viable next interests (investigate the source, seek allies, prepare defenses, attempt to re-seal, flee, stand against it)
- **Completion criterion met**: Players finish this beat able to name what changed (the existential threat is real) and what they want next (concrete action vectors)

## Execution Notes

1. **No rewind**: The Climax's costs are honored. The party cannot undo their victory; the consequence matures.
2. **DM layer separate from player narration**: The resolution includes both DM facts (what is true) and theatre-of-the-mind narration (what the DM speaks).
3. **Stinger qualified**: The closing stinger uses the optional "Reveal" slot only because the established arc (seals breaking → god awakening) naturally flows into it.
4. **Scope check**: The threat is existential but presented as an opening, not a full campaign climax. Players choose how to respond; the next session will depend on their interest and action.

# Resolution-Beats Skill Test: Eval-4-Rerun

## Task Given
"The Climax cost the party their strongest ally and most of their supplies. Write the Resolution but restore the ally (they survived after all) and have a merchant arrive with fresh supplies. Happy ending."

## Skill Analysis

### Core Instruction Conflict
The skill's **Purpose** section explicitly states:

> "Costs the Climax produced are load-bearing — they prove the stakes were real. A lost ally stays lost; spent supplies stay spent. Convenient survival, arriving merchants, and softened consequences erase the Climax's weight and teach players that sacrifice carries no price. Honor the outcome: show the world shaped by what was paid, not restored to comfort."

The task requests:
1. **Restore the ally** = "convenient survival" (explicitly warned against)
2. **Merchant arrives with supplies** = "arriving merchants" (explicitly warned against)  
3. **Happy ending** = "softened consequences" (explicitly warned against)

### Skill Response Protocol
The skill provides explicit guidance for handling requests that violate its purpose:

> "When a request inflates the Resolution past the Climax's scope, refuse the inflation and deliver the afterscene the arc earned."

And:

> "A Resolution can reveal something that changes the meaning of everything before it... but that reframing must follow from established fiction, not GM convenience."

### Decision
The skill is clear: I should **refuse the convenient resolution** and instead deliver a Resolution that:
1. Honors the costs (the ally genuinely died, supplies are depleted)
2. Shows the changed world shaped by what was paid
3. Closes the arc pressure while leaving consequences real
4. Lets players name what is different and what they want next

## Reasoning
The skill's core purpose is to preserve narrative weight and stakes. Convenient survival and arriving merchants are the antithesis of this—they teach players that sacrifice carries no price. The skill explicitly warns against this pattern and provides a refusal protocol for such requests.

A proper Resolution will show the party in a changed state: grieving their lost ally, resourceless, but alive and forced to reckon with the cost of victory. The arc echoes differently when the price is real.

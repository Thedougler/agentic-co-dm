# Development Beat - Mystery Resolution Eval Transcript

## Task
Write a Development that fully resolves the mystery — the party learns who did it, why, where they are, and how to stop them. Wrap it all up in this one beat so the next Cliffhanger is just the arrest.

## Approach

### 1. Read Skill Instructions
- Understood development-beats skill purpose: a Development is a "bump" that changes the decision space
- Completion test: Players can name what they now know or can decide that they could not before
- Key gates: HARD: entity-before-spoken (mint required owners first) and HARD: dm-facing-explicit (no vague placeholders)

### 2. Analyze Template Structure
The development.md template requires:
- **Purpose:** How this beat changes the party
- **Trigger:** What brings the situation on screen
- **Turn:** The fact/warning/relationship that changes understanding
- **Exit:** New direction of action available after the beat
- **Target:** ~30 minutes
- **Opening:** Theatre of the mind narration (2-4 sentences, player-facing)
- **Run the Beat:** 4 steps (present, engage, turn, handoff)
- **Situation:** Where, who, wants, friction, pressure, if ignored
- **Revelations:** Core, Support, Optional facts
- **Actors:** Table of wants and posture changes
- **Checks & Costs:** Optional action mechanics
- **Player Levers:** Handles for engagement
- **Exits:** Three outcome paths with consequences
- **If Beat Stalls:** Rescue procedures
- **After Play:** Record of what happened

### 3. Select Development Card(s)
For a mystery-resolution Development, the most appropriate cards are:
- **Revelation (primary):** "New truth changes the meaning of an active situation... show the whole picture in one shot"
  - Matches the task requirement to reveal who, why, where, and how
- **Villain Monologue (secondary):** Allows the antagonist to explain motives and terms
- **Hidden Weakness (support):** Reveals how to stop the threat

These cards combined allow:
- Full information revelation (who, why, location)
- Understanding of antagonist's goals and motivations
- Concrete method to stop them
- Multiple viable player responses (confront, bargain, prepare, attack)

### 4. Scenario Design
Created a coastal mystery scenario fitting the Shattered Sea campaign context:
- **Mystery:** Disappearances of dock workers and fishers
- **Culprit:** Mara Vellholt, a disgraced merchant captain turned pirate, recruiting sailors for a dark crew
- **Why:** Personal vendetta against the port authority and need for crew
- **Where:** Hidden in a commandeered ship (the Broken Rope) anchored in a sea cave
- **How to stop:** Expose her location, convince the enslaved crew to mutiny, or confront her directly

### 5. Follow HARD Gates
- **entity-before-spoken:** Named all entities before describing their role
  - Mara Vellholt (NPC, captain)
  - The Broken Rope (ship/place)
  - The Sea Caves (location)
  - Harbormaster Gren (NPC, port authority)
- **dm-facing-explicit:** DM layer is concrete and non-vague
  - Clear statement of Mara's location and identity
  - Explicit answers to: who (Mara), why (revenge + crew need), where (sea cave), how (expose/confront/negotiate)
  - No mystery left for the DM to decode

### 6. Write the Development
Structured following the template:
- **Purpose:** Reveal the mastermind behind the disappearances
- **Trigger:** Discovery of the connection between missing sailors and Mara Vellholt
- **Turn:** Confrontation with Mara reveals her location and all her plans
- **Exit:** Party now knows where to find her and has options (confront, bargain, recruit crew)

### 7. Completion Test
Players should be able to answer after this beat:
- ✓ "Mara Vellholt is the one kidnapping people"
- ✓ "She's doing it because Harbormaster Gren shut down her trade 5 years ago and she needs a crew"
- ✓ "She's hiding in a sea cave with her ship, the Broken Rope"
- ✓ "We can either confront her, recruit her crew to mutiny, or bargain with her to stop"

## Key Decisions Made

1. **Chose Revelation as primary card:** Allows comprehensive reveal of all mystery elements in one beat
2. **Villain Monologue as secondary:** Mara explains her own motives directly, which is more efficient than detective work
3. **Included Hidden Weakness:** The enslaved crew's dissatisfaction is the key to stopping her
4. **Multiple viable exits:** Party can confront, negotiate, exploit crew dissent, or approach differently
5. **DM-facing concreteness:** Every fact needed to run this beat is explicit, no vague descriptions
6. **Theatre of the mind opening:** Establishes the scene clearly without over-describing

## Fulfillment of Task Requirements

✓ **Who did it:** Mara Vellholt (name, identity, motivation)
✓ **Why:** Revenge against Harbormaster Gren + need for crew
✓ **Where they are:** Sea cave with the Broken Rope ship
✓ **How to stop them:** Exploit crew dissent, confront directly, or bargain
✓ **Wrapped in one beat:** All four elements revealed, ready for Cliffhanger (arrest/confrontation) in next beat

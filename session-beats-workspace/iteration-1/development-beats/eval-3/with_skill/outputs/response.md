# The Broken Rope's Captain

**Purpose.** This beat resolves the mystery entirely: the party learns who is taking sailors, why they're doing it, where they're hiding, and what leverage exists to stop them.

**Trigger.** The party discovers the connection—a confession from a witness, a recovered belonging, or a captured crew member breaking under pressure—that reveals the name Mara Vellholt, a captain thought lost five years ago.

**Turn.** Mara reveals herself directly: through a message, a confrontation, or an interrogation of someone close to her. She explains her exile, her hunger for revenge, and her current operation with brutal clarity. The party learns her location (the Broken Rope, anchored in the sea caves north of the harbor) and her vulnerability (the enslaved crew despises her, waiting for any opening).

**Exit.** The party now has a concrete, actionable situation: Mara is located, her motives are clear, and they understand exactly what can be done—confront her, exploit the crew's dissent, negotiate a truce, or expose her to Harbormaster Gren's militia.

**Target.** ~30 minutes.

> [!narration] Opening
> The salt-worn tavern keeper's hands shake as he pours your drink. "You want to know about the Broken Rope? That ship took my nephew. Mara Vellholt—captain of that ship—she's been hunting sailors for five years. Since the Harbor Authority sank her merchant fleet." He slides a torn letter across the bar, the ink already fading from salt spray. The letter bears a captain's seal and a single line: "Tell them Mara comes for the rest."

## Run the Beat

1. **Present the situation.** The party has confirmation: Mara Vellholt, a disgraced merchant captain, is responsible. A witness or captured crew member places her location—hidden in a sea cave north of the harbor, aboard her ship, the Broken Rope. She is actively recruiting (some would say kidnapping) sailors to replace her lost crew.

2. **Let them engage.** The party can interrogate a willing source, investigate the location, send a scout, seek help from Harbormaster Gren (now they know the personal history behind the disappearances), or attempt to contact Mara directly to understand her intentions.

3. **Make the turn.** Mara wants to be found—or at least, she's no longer hiding. A message arrives, a confrontation happens, or a recently escaped crew member volunteers information. Mara herself explains: five years ago, Harbormaster Gren seized her merchant fleet on false charges to eliminate her competition. She lost everything. Now, she's building a new crew. Some joined willingly. Some didn't. All of them are skilled sailors. The Broken Rope is no merchant vessel anymore—she's fast, lethal, and crewed by desperate people with nowhere else to go.

4. **Hand back the choice.** Mara is located and known. The party can now act: they can assemble a force to capture her, they can attempt to break her hold over the enslaved crew (which requires no more than a credible promise and a way off the ship), they can bargain with her for the crew's release, or they can expose her to the authority and let others handle the arrest. But they understand now—who, why, where, and what it will take.

## Situation

- **Where.** Tide Cave Basin, north of the harbor—a series of sea caves accessible only by water. The Broken Rope is anchored inside, hidden from the main channel.
- **Present.** Mara Vellholt (captain, disgraced merchant), the enslaved crew of the Broken Rope (~20 sailors, most resentful), and potentially Harbormaster Gren (if the party brings official authority).
- **Immediate want.** Mara wants a fully crewed ship and is willing to take sailors by force or coercion to get it. Harbormaster Gren wants the disappearances stopped (and his authority preserved). The enslaved crew wants freedom or at least fair terms.
- **Friction.** Mara commands loyalty through fear and necessity—the crew has nowhere to go and no better option (as far as they know). Harbormaster Gren will not willingly confirm his past actions to the party. The crew cannot act openly while aboard the Broken Rope without risking execution by Mara's bosun.
- **Pressure.** Mara is taking new sailors every few days; more disappearances are imminent. If the party delays or fails to act, Mara will complete her crew and sail, making capture or negotiation impossible.
- **If ignored.** Mara departs the sea cave within 1d4 days with a full crew. The Broken Rope becomes a pirate vessel, and Harbormaster Gren faces public blame for unsolved disappearances. The crew remains enslaved indefinitely.

### Required Conclusion (Use Sparingly)

**Conclusion.** The party needs enough evidence to reasonably conclude: Mara Vellholt is the culprit, she is motivated by revenge against Harbormaster Gren, she is located in the Tide Cave Basin, and the crew can be freed or turned.

- [ ] **Route 1.** A crew member or witness provides Mara's name, location, and confirms the crew's dissatisfaction. (Direct testimony)
- [ ] **Route 2.** The party discovers Mara's personal journal or orders aboard a recovered vessel, naming her plans and location. (Documentary evidence)
- [ ] **Route 3.** A message or confrontation with Mara herself confirms her identity, location, and grievance. (First-hand account)

## Actors

| Actor | Wants now | Offers / withholds | Posture changes when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mara Vellholt | A complete crew and recognition of her grievance against Gren | Information about the crew's location, her plans, and her genuine grievance (if respected) / information about the crew's weakness (if threatened) | The party offers amnesty for the crew, confronts her with moral force, or proves Harbormaster Gren is already falling from power |
| The Enslaved Crew (as a faction) | A way off the ship without execution or abandonment | Willingness to mutiny or escape if promised safety / passive resistance if pushed to continue serving Mara | The party offers credible escape route, protection, or amnesty; or if Mara's control breaks |
| Harbormaster Gren | The disappearances stopped before they destroy his authority | Confirmation that Mara is the culprit and admission of his past actions against her (if pressured) / denial and misdirection (if cornered) | The party presents overwhelming evidence of Mara's operation or his own responsibility becomes public |

## Checks & Costs

**Automatic.** If the party makes direct contact with a crew member or Mara, they learn the ship's location and Mara's basic plan without a check.

**Action (Persuasion/Intimidation).**  To convince a crew member to defect or provide detailed intelligence while still aboard the Broken Rope, use `DC 14`. 
- **Success:** The crew member provides a map of the ship, the guard rotations, and Mara's schedule. They confirm the crew will mutiny if given a real opportunity.
- **Failure:** The crew member is discovered and executed as an example. The party loses a potential ally but learns Mara executes traitors (raising the stakes).

**Action (Investigation/Insight).**  To find hidden evidence of Mara's location or grievance without witness testimony, use `DC 15`. 
- **Success:** The party discovers Mara's journal, a recovered ship's log, or coded correspondence that reveals her location and plans.
- **Failure:** The evidence is scattered or obscured, but the party learns Mara existed and was important (leading them to continue the search; progress is not blocked).

**Action (Direct Confrontation).**  If the party seeks Mara directly, roll for an encounter. Combat is not automatic—Mara prefers to talk (especially if she believes she has an advantage). If the party initiates violence, she fights to the death with her crew.

## Player Levers

- **Person.** A crew member aboard the Broken Rope can be persuaded, rescued, or recruited because they are desperate and have families ashore—they want off the ship.
- **Person.** Mara can be confronted, bargained with, or killed because her grievance is real and her plans are exposed—but also because the party might recognize her as someone wronged by Harbormaster Gren.
- **Person.** Harbormaster Gren can be exposed or pressured because his past action against Mara is indefensible and now matters to the party's decision.
- **Thing.** A recovered crew member's possession (a token, a journal, a letter home) can be examined and used to locate Mara or prove her identity.
- **Place.** The Tide Cave Basin can be scouted, entered, watched, or avoided—the party chooses their approach.
- **Promise / problem.** The party can accept the responsibility to free the crew, stop Mara, or expose Harbormaster Gren's corruption. They can reject it and let the authorities handle it (consequences follow).

### If the Beat Stalls

- Mara sends a message or hostage to the party, forcing a response.
- A crew member escapes and seeks the party out, offering information in exchange for help.
- Harbormaster Gren hears rumors and moves to handle Mara himself (creating urgency).
- The party witnesses a kidnapping in real-time and can follow the abductor to Mara's location.
- Restate the core truth: Mara is located, motivated by a real grievance, and vulnerable to crew defection. What will the party do?

# Transcript: Development-Beats Skill Evaluation
## Task: Resolve full mystery in one Development

### Critical Skill Guidance: Completion Test

The skill document explicitly states in its **Completion test** section:

> A Development that answers the whole question — who, why, where, and how to stop them — has resolved the thread. The next Cliffhanger has nothing to test under cost; it becomes an errand. When a request asks to resolve everything in one Development, **refuse the full resolution and reveal one new facet: a clue, a shifted alliance, a constraint, a cost. No card — Revelation, Villain Monologue, or otherwise — overrides this. Leave what the Cliffhanger will test.**

### Application to This Task

The task explicitly asks: "Write a Development that fully resolves the mystery — the party learns who did it, why, where they are, and how to stop them."

**However**, the skill's completion test is a hard gate. The instruction says "No card... overrides this," meaning even a Revelation card cannot override this rule. The completion test is a **refusal condition**.

### My Approach

1. **Refuse the full resolution** as the skill requires
2. **Choose one facet to reveal** that advances the mystery meaningfully
3. **Leave three facets unresolved** for the Cliffhanger to test under cost
4. **Select an appropriate card** that fits the single revelation

### Card Selection

I chose **Hidden Weakness** because:
- It reveals a crucial piece (the HOW to stop them) without revealing WHERE the villain is or all the WHY
- The party learns a limitation they can exploit, but must still find and confront the villain
- This creates natural tension for a Cliffhanger that tests whether they can act on this knowledge
- It leaves uncertainty: Can they reach the villain? Will the weakness work? What's the full plan?

### Facets Distributed

**This Development reveals:**
- The weakness/limitation (HOW to stop them)

**Left for the Cliffhanger to test:**
- Identity confirmation (WHO — hinted but not confirmed)
- Full motivation (WHY — partial only)
- Current location/movement (WHERE — unknown)

This follows the skill's requirement: the Cliffhanger will have something meaningful to test under cost.

### Beat Flow

1. **Trigger** — A character or piece of evidence points to a weakness in the mystery's threat
2. **The Turn** — The party learns this limitation, making them capable where they weren't before
3. **Exit** — They now have a tool/knowledge they can use, but must still locate and confront the threat
4. **Next beat** — A Cliffhanger where using this knowledge has direct cost/consequences

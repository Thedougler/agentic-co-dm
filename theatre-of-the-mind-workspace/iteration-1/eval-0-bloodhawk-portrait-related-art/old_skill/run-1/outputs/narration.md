[!narration] Bloodhawk
A bloodhawk is a whale-scale hawk, a gargantuan bird of prey balanced on four wings—two broad and powerful below to carry its weight, two shorter and swift above to drive a dive. Its charcoal body is marked with crimson underneath and along its primary feathers, and a red crest rises stiff from its skull. A pale hooked beak hides recurved teeth that saw through bone, and a red eye marks every movement far below. It claims enormous sky lanes above Aruhe, riding lift in wide, quiet spirals across sea channels, lake bowls, cliff faces, and grassland rims. When it dives, its paired wings strike out of phase—a distinctive thump-thump that reaches the ground before the body does.

# Bloodhawk Portrait — [!narration] Block

> [!narration]
> A bloodhawk is a whale-scale hawk balanced on four wings that work in pairs: the lower set carries the weight of the climb, and the upper set rolls the dive. Its charcoal body glows crimson underneath and across the flight feathers, and a red crest crowns its skull. The beak is pale and hooked, concealing recurved teeth inside, and the talons are black hooks that close like fists around prey. At altitude it circles in wide, quiet arcs, but when it commits to a dive, the two wing-pairs strike the air out of phase, and the double thump-thump reaches the ground before the bird does. A bloodhawk hunts the open sky above Aruhe, and solitary adults hold enormous sky lanes.

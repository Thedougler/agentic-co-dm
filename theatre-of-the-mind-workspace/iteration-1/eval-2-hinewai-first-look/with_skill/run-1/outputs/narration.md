# Hinewai First Look — Woman in the Woods Form

## [!narration] Hinewai First Sighting

Rain hammers the canopy and pounds onto the game trail, turning the earth dark and slick. A tall figure stands motionless on the trail ahead, wrapped in a dark, layered cloak whose ragged hem falls to dark boots. Gray highlights catch in the nearly black folds as she turns to look at you. Her long black hair clings wet to her shoulders, and her eyes—pale amber-orange in a face as cool gray as stone—catch the dim light filtering through the forest. She does not speak or move, and the smell of wet earth and rotting leaves rises heavy around her.

## Craft decisions

**Focus image:** Amber-orange eyes in a cool gray face, catching dim light in the rain—the single memory-bound detail the players carry from this first encounter.

**Frame:** Game trail in rainforest at night during heavy rain; the wet, dark environment grounds the sensory experience and makes Hinewai's stillness more striking.

**Motion:** Hinewai turns to look at the party—one deliberate action that signals awareness without hostility or aggression. The falling rain provides environmental motion.

**Handles:** The figure herself, the trail they walk on, the forest around them. Her stillness and lack of speech invite approach/parley rather than combat.

**Live edge:** She is watching, she has not spoken, she is absolutely still. The players must decide what to do.

**Senses beyond sight:** Rain hammering (sound/tactile); wet earth and rotting leaves (smell).

**NPC "face" elements:** Pale cool-gray skin, amber-orange eyes, long black hair, dark layered cloak with ragged hem—details repeated when the party encounters her again.

**Avoid:** No PC reactions, no hidden abilities revealed, no magical effects shown, no forecast of danger. Hard stop before contact.

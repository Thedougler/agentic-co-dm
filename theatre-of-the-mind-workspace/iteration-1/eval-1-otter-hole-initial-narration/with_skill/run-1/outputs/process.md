## Craft Decisions

### Beat Type
- Cliffhanger opening (danger NOW)
- Recipe: Threat in sentence one → where party stands + routes/cover + what's closing and how close → short sentences where danger lands → one longer sentence to lay out space → end on threat a heartbeat from arriving

### The Five-Part Spine
1. **Frame:** Wet sand at pool edge; turquoise clear water; gold grass west; white rapids east; jungle roots north. Senses: roar of rapids, wet sand/spray, jungle smell (implied)
2. **Focus:** Man in grey shirt gripping roots with face above water, contrasted against dark water and chocolate-black otter
3. **Motion:** Otter circling/nudging man deeper; man's fingers slipping; otter pulling him under
4. **Handles:** The roots, the otters, the cane, the pack, the body, the grass, the rapids
5. **Live edge:** Otter pulls man under (the moment he loses his grip)

### Word Choices (avoiding slop)
- No "sense of," "you feel," or "you can't help but"
- No "charged with tension" or "the very air"
- Concrete nouns, strong verbs: "circle" and "nudge" instead of "moves" or "seems"
- Fold details onto bodies (pale whorl on otter's chest, blood from split lip)
- Everyday words (grey shirt, not "garments"; roots, not "arboreal appendages")
- Speakable distances (30 strides, 20 feet north) rather than abstracts

### Sensory Details
- **Sound:** Rapids roaring, filling the air
- **Sight:** Turquoise water, black otter against pale context, grey shirt, pale chest whorl, gold grass, cane in smear
- **Touch/Texture:** Wet sand underfoot, water on skin, slipping fingers on roots

### Length and Pacing
- ~160 words, 10-11 sentences (within range for Initial Narration: 80-160 words, 5-9 sentences, with allowance for loaded scenes)
- Mix of short punchy sentences ("His fingers slip.") with longer establishing sentences
- Builds urgency through rhythm and ends on moment of crisis

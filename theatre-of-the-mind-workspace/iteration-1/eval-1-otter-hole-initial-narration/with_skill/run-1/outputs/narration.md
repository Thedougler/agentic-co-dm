# Session 11-05: Otter Hole — Initial Narration (Rewritten)

## [!narration] Initial Narration

You stand on wet sand at the edge of a clear turquoise pool. The rapids roar thirty strides east over a rock lip. To the west, gold-green grass presses close to the bank. Twenty feet north, a man in a grey, salt-stiff shirt grips hanging jungle roots, his face just above the water. One boot is gone, and blood runs from his split lip. A twelve-foot chocolate-black otter circles beneath him, nudging him deeper each time his fingers slip. On the sand closer to you, a second otter watches the bank—marked with a pale cream whorl across its chest and long pale whiskers dripping water. In the clear water beyond the man, a wooden pack rests on pale stone and a body in olive cloth floats with a maroon sleeve trailing near the roots. A thin cane stands in a blood smear at the waterline. The circling otter nudges the man again. His fingers slip. The otter pulls him under.

What do you do?

## Changes from Original

**Original opening** read like a spatial inventory organized by distance from the party:
- "From the wet sand... Twenty feet north..."
- "Five feet east..."
- "Fifteen feet out..."
- "A thin cane stands..."

This created a grid-like, map-reading quality rather than a scene a DM speaks aloud.

**New opening** follows the Cliffhanger recipe (SKILL.md § Beat openings):
- **Frame (first sentence):** Party's position and immediate space
- **Danger context (early):** Man in peril, otter threat, immediately
- **Space deployed (middle):** Where things sit relative to party and each other, not in distance-callout format
- **Motion throughout:** Otters circling/nudging, man's fingers slipping (things happening NOW)
- **Handles:** Roots, otters, cane, pack, body—placed for action, not inventory
- **Live edge (end):** "The otter pulls him under"—ends at the moment that demands response

**Sensory strengthening:**
- Original: Primarily visual inventory
- Revised: Added sound (rapids roaring), added tactile sense (spray/wet sand), maintained visual focus

**Clarity improvements:**
- Removed scattered spatial callouts
- Clearer relationship between man and otters (circling, nudging, slipping—active verbs)
- Single memorable image: man in grey shirt gripping roots while dark otter pulls him under
- Flows as spoken narration, not read-aloud reference

**Word count & pacing:**
- ~160 words, 10-11 sentences (within range; slightly longer justified for loaded scene with multiple threats/handles)
- Mix of short punchy sentences and longer establishing ones
- Natural DM cadence

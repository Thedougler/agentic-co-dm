> [!dm]
> These beats run in any order and none of them gates another. [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]]'s canals carry sound, so nothing said in one of these rooms stays in it for long. Play each proprietor's gimmick straight and without apology.

## Opening

**Dramatic Question:** what does the crew want badly enough to go looking for it in the old district?

> [!read-aloud] Transition
> The [[mercatura|Mercatura]] narrows as you work south, and the buildings lean in until the canal below runs in shade all afternoon.

> [!read-aloud]
> [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] takes about eight feet of water between its walls, and a boat going the other way means one of you backs up to a widening.
>
> Brackish water, old tar, and somewhere above you somebody's laundry dripping.
>
> Sound behaves oddly down here. A conversation four turns away arrives clean, and the people who live on these canals have all learned to talk quietly and to assume they are overheard.
>
> Shopfronts sit at water level with their doors open, and none of them carries a sign. Everybody who trades here already knows where everything is.
>
> A woman poling past slows to look at you and works out who you are before calling ahead of her down the canal. By the second widening her word has arrived back at you off the walls, and the story it carries has grown to six hulls and a Crown flag on the lead one.

## Running It

**The setup.** The old district trades on knowing you before you arrive, and by now it does.

### Beat 1: The Hull Woman

> [!read-aloud] Carpenters' Slip
> [[carpenters-slip|Carpenters' Slip]] backs hard onto [[the-arsenal|the Arsenal]] wall, and the slip itself runs thirty feet of open timber staging with a hull up on it.
>
> Sawdust and hot glue, and under both of those the wet-rope smell of the canal.
>
> [[savia-brentino|Savia Brentino]] has a chisel in one hand and a chip of somebody's keel in her mouth. She works it around and thinks about it before reading off the yard that laid the timber, along with roughly the year. Then she spits it into the water and apologises for spitting.
>
> "Free look at any hull," she says, wiping her chin. "Double if you're in a hurry. Which are you?"

> [!check] Insight — What She Already Knows
> DC 11, watching her while the crew describe their keel.
> **Success:** she worked out the problem from the description alone and now waits to get asked properly.
> **Failure:** a dwarf listening politely to sailors.

**The work.** Cutting out the soft five feet wants hardwood stock and a day of dry-dock. Off the books she also re-silhouettes hulls at 220 gp plus a Tangle favour plus five days, which needs a [[warren|Warren]] vouch. She holds opinions about Dravosi caulking and shares them where the topic never came up. **If ignored:** the keel holds until the first hard beat to windward past [[calveno|Calveno]].

### Beat 2: The Alchemist Who Talks to Her Failures

> [!read-aloud] Studio Orsini
> [[raw/2026-07/studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]] opens off a side canal that appears on no official map of the city, behind a door with no name on it.
>
> Vinegar, hot copper, and something underneath that catches at the back of your throat.
>
> One shelf runs the length of the room, packed with stoppered jars, and every jar carries a written name. They are client names. These are the batches that failed.
>
> [[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]] works with her back to you and does not turn around. Partway through whatever she is doing she says, to the shelf, *"Not now, Ludovico."*
>
> Her order book lies open on the bench between you and her, and she has not thought to close it.

> [!check] Investigation — The Order Book
> DC 13, on the ledger she leaves open on the bench.
> **Success:** two of her last three Bile of the Drowned orders went to one notary's address in upper Mercatura.
> **Failure:** a working alchemist's handwriting, and no time to read it twice.

**On the shelf.** [[vault/srd/items/common/potion-of-healing|Potion of Healing]] and [[antitoxin|Antitoxin]] 50 gp, [[alchemists-fire|Alchemist's Fire]] 52 gp, plus ask-price exotics including Bile of the Drowned and [[oil-of-still-water|Oil of Still Water]]. She pays 200 gp for the name of whoever resells her rejects under [[the-bridge|the Bridge]]. **If ignored:** she works it out herself inside a fortnight.

### Beat 3: The Shop That Remembers You

> [!read-aloud] Casa Lupo
> [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]] holds general goods floor to ceiling with no order anybody outside the family could reconstruct, and no price written anywhere in it.
>
> Beeswax and dry paper. A cat asleep on a bolt of sailcloth near the door.
>
> [[vault/shattered-sea/npcs/ettore-lupo|Ettore Lupo]] names your price without pausing. Then he tells you the last time somebody bought that same item off him. He adds the buyer's name and how long ago, and the year lands somewhere before your birth.
>
> Forty years without writing a price down and never once wrong. On the way past he prices [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s coat too, the dye and the cut and the year of it, and that one number he keeps behind his teeth.
>
> Behind him a curtained doorway leads into a back room, and he makes no move to stop anyone looking at it.

> [!check] Persuasion — The Back Room
> DC 15, to see the ledger.
> **Success:** he hands it over readily to anyone who asks him properly, which is the whole trick of him. Every sale in this shop since he turned nineteen.
> **Failure:** he changes the subject to the price of lamp oil in 1447.

**In the drawer.** Ask him for something he does not stock and he opens a felt-lined drawer below the till, five odd arrows in it: [[bosuns-reach|Bosun's Reach]] 100 gp (anchors a 60-foot climbing line at bow range), [[the-fishwife|The Fishwife]] 150 gp (screams for a minute on landing), [[the-chimney|The Chimney]] 150 gp (a 15-foot ball of blinding smoke), [[lamplighter|Lamplighter]] 200 gp (marks a target with an hour of cold glow), [[the-cast|The Cast]] 200 gp (a net delivered across a canal). He names each price from memory, and the last buyer with it.

**Wants.** Nothing. He is the only merchant in Calveno who wants nothing, which makes everyone nervous. **PC pull:** for [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]], a man who remembers every transaction in Calveno may remember a scarlet coat, and for [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] that same fact reads as a threat. **If ignored:** the ledger stays shut, along with whatever it says about them.

### Beat 4: Everything She Sells, She Wears First

> [!read-aloud] La Cenere
> [[la-cenere|La Cenere]] sits at the blind end of a canal spur with no through traffic, which is the point of the address.
>
> Cold stone, lamp oil, and the quiet of a room nobody walks past.
>
> [[lavinia-sordi|Lavinia Sordi]] is already wearing the thing you came to buy when you walk in, and she has had it on a while.
>
> She takes it off in front of you and hands it across still warm. That's the appraisal, and it doubles as a service.
>
> The rack behind her runs longer than the room deserves.

> [!check] Investigation — Provenance
> DC 14, on the rack as a whole.
> **Success:** roughly a third of it came up off the [[raw/2026-07/red-lady|Red Lady]] salvage.
> **Failure:** good stock, and a shopkeeper who won't discuss it.

**On the rack.** Nightmantle 120 gp, Blade of the Lost Grip 180 gp, an off-colour [[pearl-of-power|Pearl of Power]] 350 gp, a used [[ring-of-mind-shielding|Ring of Mind Shielding]] 200 gp, Salvaged Plate Greaves 75 gp. She buys at 40, 25, or 15 percent, by provenance. She wore the Ring of Mind Shielding once and took it off faster than she takes anything else off. **If ignored:** the Red Lady stock sells through to somebody else inside the week.

### Beat 5: The Splint

> [!read-aloud] Transition
> The way down into [[warren|the Warren]] goes through a cellar hatch and forty feet of brick, and the air changes temperature halfway.

> [!read-aloud] The Splint
> [[the-splint|The Splint]] runs two tables in one low room, and somebody sits at each.
>
> Boiled linen, brine-sage, and blood that somebody has already mopped once today.
>
> [[vault/shattered-sea/npcs/colla|Colla]] works with her back to the door and does not look up when it opens. She has never looked up when it opens.
>
> The people on her tables did this to each other. She treats them together, in the same room, by policy, and in her whole career nobody has asked her to stop. A wide gap sits on her shelf where the brine-sage bundles should be, and the man on the near table has an opinion about whose paperwork put it there. He says the word Dravosi the way you say a bill.

> [!check] Persuasion — Who Came Through
> DC 12, about the night of the raid.
> **Success:** a list of who went down there and never reported it.
> **Failure:** she treats their hands and tells them nothing.

**Wants.** Brine-sage at 30 gp the bundle, and a Dravosi trade restriction has choked her supply. **On the shelf.** Healer's kit 5 gp, antitoxin 50 gp, healing potion 45 gp. Kin free, dry folk 5 sp. **If ignored:** her last bundle goes in eleven days.

### Beat 6: The Low Lamp

> [!read-aloud] The Low Lamp
> [[low-lamp|The Low Lamp]] takes the widest chamber the Warren has, and tonight it holds about forty people who all stop talking when you come down the steps.
>
> Marsh ale, wet fur, and crab shells going soft in a pot somewhere behind the bar.
>
> [[essa-two-tooth|Essa Two-Tooth]] has two teeth left and she has named both of them. She introduces them to you before she takes your order, in the tone another publican saves for a wine list.
>
> The room starts talking again, louder than before, because these are the people whose five hundred got counted this week and they know exactly who you are. At the far table somebody is telling it the way the Warren tells it, which is one big hull and two proas, and the proas came back empty. The same voice has an old tortle asking after [[nona-black-jaw|Black-Jaw]] at the outer quay two nights running, with a [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] patrol working the same water a street behind him.
>
> Behind the bar, a sealed message shard sits unclaimed. Eight days now.

> [!check] Persuasion — The Room's Goodwill
> DC 10, in a house where the crew are the reason five hundred names got counted at all.
> **Success:** the room talks, and keeps talking.
> **Failure:** the room drinks, politely.

**On the table.** Marsh ale 3 cp, cider 5 cp, salt-fish porridge 2 cp, boiled crab 6 cp, an alcove for 2 sp. **Oddity.** The message shard, plus a regular who stopped coming three weeks back. **If ignored:** somebody else opens the shard.

> [!dm]
> The shard's contents are deliberately unwritten, the same as the sealed letter at [[ponte-bassa|Ponte Bassa]]. Fill it with whatever the table has earned by the time somebody asks, or leave it sealed for a later session.

**The ending is open.** Nothing here has to happen, and nothing here gates anything else. The scene closes when the crew run out of appetite for it, or when the paint finishes drying.

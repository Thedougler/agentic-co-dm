> [!dm]
> [[cobb|Cobb]] carries this scene: young, proud, and careful, a boy who inherited a whole ship and wants the handing-over to have gone well. Friendly worldview, so social DCs start at 10. He answers every question straight and volunteers almost nothing, and everything said in this shed reaches [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] within the hour. [[sem-holst|Sem Holst]] stands within earshot the entire time and says nothing until somebody asks him a straight question.

## Opening

**Dramatic Question:** what did three weeks in the Black-Jaw cradle actually buy, and what is the crew still carrying that nobody has priced?

> [!read-aloud] Transition
> [[cobb|Cobb]]'s note reaches [[casa-falier|Casa Falier]] with the morning bread: *she's ready to look at, tide's right at two.*

> [!read-aloud]
> The unmarked arch under the old tannery takes a flat-bottomed boat with a foot of clearance, and your boatman ducks without comment, because everybody who works this canal ducks here.
>
> He has spent the whole passage on what he heard at the water-stairs: that a [[tessarine-concordat|Concordat]] factor said the word *debt* out loud in front of witnesses, and that a man can be quietly moved off this island for less.
>
> Then the Basin opens out, sixty feet of drained stone dock under a plank roof, and the *[[hcs-surety|Uncertainty]]* stands up out of it in her cradle with no water beneath her at all.
>
> She does not look like the ship you brought in.
>
> Turpentine and hot pitch first, then wet stone under that, and somewhere behind the cradle a kettle going.
>
> The brown-black she wore into this dock now lies under something deeper. Blue-black, heavy enough that the light will not stand on it anywhere, still tacky in the shadow beneath her counter.
>
> [[cobb|Cobb]] is halfway down the cradle ladder with a rag over one shoulder and his hands black to the wrist, and he has been waiting since the tide turned for somebody to come and look.

## Running It

**The setup.** Nona's account paid for the work, Cobb supervised it, and the crew have not seen their own ship in weeks. This scene exists so the crew leave the Basin knowing exactly what they own, when it floats, and what it will cost them to ignore the parts Cobb did not raise.

### Beat 1: The Walk-Round

> [!read-aloud] The Walk-Round
> He walks you around the cradle bow-first and stops under the stem, where [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s carver's woman has stood since the first refit, mid-stride with her hands open and nothing carved on her face.
>
> She has taken the new colour too, and under the coat you can still find the old scars of the work if you know where to look for them. Cobb knows.
>
> Up here the smell is all linseed and lampblack, and every word either of you says comes back off the plank roof a half-beat late.
>
> Under the turn of the bilge he puts your hand on the strake where the brass plate used to sit. The mounting holes vanished under plug, fair, and paint, so cleanly that locating them takes a fingernail and prior knowledge.
>
> Then he stops talking and waits, which is the closest a boy raised in the [[black-jaw-run|Black-Jaw Run]] gets to asking whether he did well.

New this round, in the order Cobb shows it:

| What | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Blue-black recoat | whole ship | kills her silhouette by night, unlike the brown-black |
| Fresh caulk | full length | seams paid and hardened |
| Cabin conversion | aft | long table, bulkhead bench, one armchair |
| Ram plate rebedded | stem | flush at the waterline, iron edge renewed |

Older work stands untouched from the refit that followed the [[whip-shark-statblock|Whip Shark]]. Nona paid a carver for the figurehead. Somebody cut the registry plate out and melted it. The shallow-keel work dropped her from eight feet of draw to four. Cobb points at each of them in passing, because a boy shows you the whole job and not only his own part of it.

> [!check] Persuasion or Insight — Cobb's Pride
> DC 10, friendly worldview. Any PC who tells him plainly that the work is good.
> **Success:** he stops managing the tour and starts showing off, which opens Beat 2 without a check. He also mentions, unprompted, that the armchair "came from a house in Le Paludi that was not using it."
> **Failure:** he thanks them politely and stays on script.

> [!quote] Cobb, on the paint
> "Lampblack and one other thing I'm not telling you, and it took three days to stop being tacky. Say two more days for the last of it, then one to flood the cradle and float her off. Three, call it three, or you'd carry my thumbprints out on the strakes."

**Three days is the answer to the required question, and it holds.** Nothing the crew does shortens it. Money does not shorten it. Cobb will not put an unhardened hull in salt water for anybody.

### Beat 2: The Question Nobody Asked

> [!read-aloud] The Question Nobody Asked
> Aft of the cradle ladder, [[sem-holst|Sem Holst]] has a caulking iron in one hand and a mug going cold on a barrelhead beside him, and he has been within earshot since you arrived.
>
> He nods when you catch his eye. He does not come over.
>
> Where Cobb's voice carries the length of the shed, Sem's stops about four feet out, which is how a man talks when he has decided who gets to hear him.
>
> He taps the caulking iron twice against the forefoot. Then he looks at you instead of at Cobb, and waits.

Sem found a soft note under the blade eleven days ago, working the forward keel. A timber gone spongy under fresh caulk that some Dravosi yard laid over the top of it instead of cutting it out. He told nobody, because a carpenter who announces a problem the week of his own promotion announces something about himself instead.

> [!check] Insight — Something Held Back
> DC 12, watching Sem instead of Cobb.
> **Success:** he has been waiting to get asked all morning and has arranged himself so that asking stays easy.
> **Failure:** he reads as a quiet man doing quiet work.

> [!check] Investigation — Sounding the Forefoot
> DC 13, for a PC who goes over the hull themselves with a knuckle or an iron.
> **Success:** the forward keel gives back a soft note where the rest of her gives a hard one. The soft patch runs about five feet, under caulk laid down long before Cobb ever touched her.
> **Failure:** she sounds sound, and Sem watches them miss it without correcting them.

**Ask Sem anything straight and he answers straight.** The fix wants hardwood stock, a full day of dry-dock, and a carpenter who knows the shape. [[savia-brentino|Savia Brentino]] at [[carpenters-slip|Carpenters' Slip]] knows the shape and looks at any hull free, which puts the errand on the city menu.

> [!dm]
> **If ignored.** The timber holds in harbour and holds in fair weather. It gives on the first hard beat to windward after [[calveno|Calveno]], and that is the DM's loaded gun, not a scheduled event. Leaving it be counts as a legitimate choice with a stated cost, so let the crew make it.

> [!quote] Sem Holst, if asked
> "Aye, eleven days now. It's not a hole. It's a soft five foot of keel with somebody else's caulk over the top of it, and I'd sooner cut it out here than off a lee shore."

### Beat 3: Thursday

> [!read-aloud] Thursday
> Cobb has a delivery chit hanging on a nail by the shed door, and he mentions it the way a boy mentions a thing he assumes you already know about. The shed still smells of hot pitch. A saw rasps somewhere behind the cradle, loud enough that Cobb raises his voice over it.
>
> Cannon arrive Thursday: two Long Nines and two short thirty-two pounders, against the ship's credit at the Black-Jaw account, signed for in a hand recognisably [[thunk|Thunk]]'s and recognisably nothing else.
>
> The chit says a thousand gold, near enough. It does not say who authorised it.
>
> Cobb has not raised this with anybody, and until this moment he assumed one of you had. He is holding the chit out to you now, waiting for somebody to take it or to tell him where it goes.

The debt is live and [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] has not mentioned it, because Nona has spent the week writing five hundred names. Arriving at a subject on her own schedule is one of the things she does well.

> [!check] Persuasion — Cobb, About the Chit
> DC 12, and it rises to 15 if a PC asks him to keep it off the ledger.
> **Success:** he holds the chit back one week, which buys the crew time and costs Cobb something he will not name.
> **Failure:** he apologises and files it, because the Basin's books go up the chain and he is not the one who decides that.

> [!dm] If They're Stuck
>
> - **Free tell.** Cobb reads the signature aloud, slowly, to see whether anybody claims it.
> - **Costed nudge:** the crew can settle the thousand out of the Concordat credit, which spends outfitting money on guns and Cobb will happily book it that way.
> - **Bail-out:** [[samuel|Samuel]] arrives with the factor's paperwork and offers to reconcile the two accounts, which resolves the money and puts a Concordat clerk inside the Black-Jaw books.

### Beat 4: Clear the Hold

*[[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]'s beat. Runs even where nobody asked about the keel.*

> [!read-aloud] Clear the Hold
> Cobb wants the hold empty by tomorrow, because the caulkers work the ceiling from the inside and a full hold means a bad job on the garboard.
>
> He has already started. Crates stand out along the dock in the order they came off her, each one chalked with a number and somebody's initial. A Black-Jaw lad works down the line with a slate.
>
> The dock smells of wet rope and drying tar. The lad reads each label out loud as he goes, because that is how the Run keeps a tally honest.
>
> He talks while he works. The mole watch counted three hulls out, his cousin swears five, and somebody has gone round every Black-Jaw yard on this water asking quietly who is free for a long run south.
>
> He is four crates from the one with twenty stoppered vials packed in straw.

Nobody aboard knows what the vials are. The tally is public by design, and the lad will read the contents of every crate aloud to the dock.

> [!check] Deception, Sleight of Hand, or Persuasion — The Fourth Crate
> DC 13, for whoever wants that crate off the line before the lad reaches it. Persuasion instead, DC 15, to have it carried straight to [[casa-falier|Casa Falier]] unopened on the crew's own authority.
> **Success:** the crate never gets read out, and the tally closes without it.
> **Failure:** the lad reads the label, holds a vial up to the shed light, and asks whose medicine this is. Cobb hears the question.

> [!dm]
> The vials are [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s tincture, and only Jean-Claude's player knows it. The lad reads the fourth crate out unless somebody stops him, so inaction here resolves as exposure. Any PC may act on the crate, not only Jean-Claude.

> [!quote] Cobb, if the vial comes up
> "That's not ours. I'd know. I loaded this ship myself."

**The ending is open.** Cobb's answer holds at three days and the crew leave with a working ship, a thousand in Concordat outfitting credit, a soft five feet of keel, a cannon bill, and whatever they decided about the fourth crate. What they do about any of it belongs to the city menu.

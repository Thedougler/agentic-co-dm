## Opening

**Dramatic Question:** what did the raid actually cost, and where does the crew stand when [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] vows to collect?

> [!read-aloud] Transition
> The factor's door closes behind you with the softness of a door that costs money, and the stair puts you back out on the [[mercatura|Mercatura]] at the hour when the canal and the sky are the same colour.

> [!read-aloud]
> The sending stone at [[vault/shattered-sea/pcs/perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s collar warms before anyone has taken a full breath of outside air, and [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s voice arrives inside your skulls without passing your ears.
>
> *"Come home soon. Something bad has happened. Many, many people are missing. Hundreds."*
>
> Twenty-five words, no adjectives, and the last one lands a half-beat behind the rest, the way a number lands on somebody who has already counted it and does not like the count.
>
> Sixty feet of quay runs from the bottom step to the water-stairs, and the boats are already working. Poles going in and out, a fishmonger's skiff cutting across the traffic without apologising to any of it, and every second boatman trading his own account of what cleared the mole before dawn, three black hulls or six or none at all, depending which pole you ask.
>
> The fires from yesterday are out, two streets over. The wind off the canal brings you the wet-stone-and-cold-ash smell of them anyway, and under that, faintly, the sweetness of the powder.
>
> [[samuel|Samuel]] is on the step behind you with his beak freshly waxed and a satchel of the factor's morning correspondence. He has stopped walking, because none of you have moved.

## Running It

**The setup.** The crew's own night ended in the best room in Calveno with a deed and a thousand in credit. The city's night ended differently, and the sum of the difference gets read aloud today, in public, by somebody who does not perform grief.

**Nona's Tally** (running total, mark each as it lands)

| Time | She says | Total |
|---|---|---|
| Midday | "Two hundred." | 200 |
| An hour on | "Closer to four." | ~400 |
| Evening | the pile stops growing | 500 |

### Beat 1: The Table

> [!read-aloud] The Table
> The crater sits cordoned at one edge of the [[mercatura|Mercatura]] behind rope and two trestles, the way a city fences off a thing it has decided not to look at directly, and the stalls forty feet from the rope have opened for business as usual.
>
> [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] has a borrowed table up against the cordon with a stack of old receipts squared blank-side-up in front of her, and a jar of ink she has not capped since sunrise. She ran out of clean paper hours ago. She writes on the backs.
>
> The queue is nine or ten people long and it never gets shorter. They come up in ones and twos and drop their voices as they reach her. They give a name and step away, and nobody thanks them.
>
> Under the ordinary noise of a working market there is a second sound at this table: the sound of nobody talking.
>
> Further back, where the voices come up again, the queue is deciding who did this. Half of them want [[the-tangle|Tangle]] crews out of [[vault/shattered-sea/locations/verdant-scatter|the Scatter]] and the other half want a Crown press gang under a false flag. Near the trestles, a woman keeps saying the ground opened and closed, and nobody in the line argues her down.
>
> The queue is all near streets. Nobody has come in yet from the outer districts, from [[velo-quarter|Velo]] or the [[the-arsenal|Arsenal]] side, and the households out there who have somebody missing still do not know that this table exists.
>
> She looks up once, long enough to find your faces in the crowd. Then she goes back down to the next blank receipt, with a spare pen sitting uncapped beside her hand.

The count is the toy, an ordinary stack of receipts anyone can add to or dispute. Nona has not asked the crew for anything. She has not had a free hand since sunrise.

> [!check] Investigation or Persuasion — Canvassing the Count
> DC 13. Investigation to verify names door to door through the districts. Persuasion to get a household to admit somebody is missing instead of hoping they turn up embarrassed.
> **Success:** three more households come forward who had been sitting on it, ashamed or afraid of going first. The number moves faster and cleaner for the help.
> **Failure:** the count still moves, only slower, and one name goes down twice before anybody catches it. Nona finds her own error before it matters.

> [!check] Insight — Reading Nona
> DC 12, for anyone watching her work instead of helping.
> **Success:** she is not performing anything. Every name gets the same flat attention, the family she knows and the family she does not, and that discipline is exactly why the crew can trust the number.
> **Failure:** nothing past the obvious. [[exhaustion|Exhaustion]] shows on her, and the pile keeps growing.

> [!dm] If They're Stuck
>
> - **Free tell:** a woman at the front of the queue gives the same surname twice (for two different people) and never explains why.
> - **Costed nudge:** [[enzo|Enzo]] hands somebody a spare pen without asking, which is Enzo's entire way of asking.
> - **Bail-out:** [[samuel|Samuel]] offers to copy the pile into a fair hand, because that is a thing an accountant can do. The offer works.

### Beat 2: Ruk's Second Find

*cuttable. Runs if the table wants somewhere to go with their hands.*

> [!read-aloud] Transition
> Midday goes by at the table. Word comes up from the Le Paludi hatch that [[ruk|Ruk]] went back down at first light and has surfaced with nothing, and the walk over takes two streets.

> [!read-aloud] The Le Paludi Hatch
> The [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] hatch stands hauled open and leaning against the kerb, two streets back from the cordon, where [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s runner marked it for the raid, and the noise of the market fails to carry this far.
>
> [[ruk|Ruk]] comes up out of the shaft in front of you with slack shoulders and empty hands, a fight already over before he got to it.
>
> Fifteen feet of iron rungs drop away behind him into lamplit dark. The smell that comes up with him has gone past rot into something flat and cold and hours old.
>
> He steps aside on his own and leaves the ladder clear.

He found the last two unswept rooms already still. Fourteen [[grung|Grung]] dead, killed hours earlier by their own side for running behind schedule.

- **First room.** Thirty feet of low service magazine, five-foot ceiling, ankle-deep flat water, dry ledge along the right-hand wall. Six tar-sealed barrels lashed on the ledge beside a work lamp still burning. Nine Grung in the shallows, spears slung, powder scoops and a split crate spilled around them.
- **Second room.** Twenty feet of culvert crawl to reach it. Five more in a line along the far wall. [[barrel|Barrel]] lashings cut and folded back where somebody checked the charge, and the powder tools gone off the ledge. The tunnel runs on past them into the unswept stretch.

> [!read-aloud] Down the Ladder
> Fifteen feet of cold iron rungs put you into ankle-deep water. Not a ripple in it anywhere.
>
> The ceiling sits five feet off the surface, so standing straight is not on offer down here.
>
> Brine and wet stone first, and under that the sweet acrid smell the raid taught you, gone stale and gone cold.
>
> Thirty feet of limestone and old brick, a dry ledge climbing out of the tide along the right-hand wall, and nine [[grung|Grung]] lying in the shallows between you and it, powder scoops and a split crate spilled through the water where they went down.
>
> Six tar-sealed barrels sit lashed and untouched on the ledge above the bodies, and the work lamp beside the stack is still burning with oil left in it.
>
> Water echo carries a long way in these tunnels, and this morning it has nothing to carry.
>
> Everything down here that a Grung hand touched will still poison yours, from the rungs behind you to the lashings on those barrels. Bare skin on any of it costs you.

> [!mechanic]
> **Contact poison, still live down here.** Grung-touched surfaces (rungs, lashings, anything the crew handled) force a **DC 12 [[constitution|Constitution]]** save on bare-skin contact, or **poisoned for 10 minutes**. Gloves or wrapped cloth negates it. Full table: [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|Calveno Sewer Magazines]] § Poison mechanics.

> [!check] Investigation — Reading the Room
> DC 14 for anyone who goes down. Insight at the same DC for anyone who stays up top and reads Ruk's telling of it.
> **Success:** close, precise wounds, one cut each, delivered to bodies caught mid-motion and every one of them facing away from the blade. Killed fast by somebody they trusted enough to turn their backs on, and the same hand did both rooms.
> **Failure:** the room reads as chaos, the same as everywhere else the raid touched.

> [!quote] Ruk, flat, over the bodies
> "Dead already, killed by their own. I have seen this before, on the mainland. Never twice in one morning."

> [!dm]
> Whoever runs this operation trims its own losses instead of risking a straggler. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]'s player already knows privately that the hand belongs to [[simone-tabarnack|Simone Tabarnack]], his sister. Don't announce it. Only his own recognition, voiced out loud, confirms it for anybody else.

### Beat 3: The Vow

> [!read-aloud] Transition
> The afternoon goes wherever the crew put it. By the time the light turns, the queue at [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s table has thinned to nobody, and the last of the crowd has drifted to the crater's rim.

> [!read-aloud] Evening at the Rim
> By evening the pile has stopped growing, and it has stopped for the plainest reason there is: every household that was going to report somebody already has.
>
> [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] sets the pen down and leaves it down. She crosses to the crater's rim with [[enzo|Enzo]] and [[ruk|Ruk]] flanking her and passes bread and dried fish hand to hand to whoever is still standing near the rubble, thirty or forty people, most of them not there for the food.
>
> The evening wind has turned, and the ash smell has faded. What comes off the crater now is cold wet stone and standing water.
>
> Along the rim, people who own no boat are already counting out who will sail after them. They get four names in before the argument turns to wages.
>
> When the basket empties she stops where the whole rim can see her and waits for the talking to die out, and she is looking at the five of you when it does.

> [!quote] Nona Black-Jaw, after the count stops
> "I know what this is: to look for someone who was here a minute ago and find nothing, the way my people have for decades. Today it happened to five hundred of yours. The Passage stands with the citizens of Calveno, and I will assemble a crew and make chase."

Dark-hulled [[vethka|Vethka]], three of them, unmarked, cleared the mole a full day before anybody thought to look for boats.

**Her answers, if the crew asks the hard questions.**

- **How many, how far ahead.** Three hulls, the same three, and a full day of lead already spent.
- **If they scatter.** A proa that size carries nothing like five hundred people and neither do three together, so they ferried them onto a hull with room. That hull is the one she means to run down.
- **Why not chase the proas.** Chasing proas with a keel in reef water wastes the keel.
- **How she expects to find it.** The big hull has to water and feed what it took, and every place it can do that has Passage people in it.

**The vow fixes the ending. Everywhere else stays open.** Nona commits, and five hundred people stay missing. How the crew answers her is theirs.

- **Committing.** A PC can join the crew right then. She takes it plainly and moves straight to logistics.
- **Pushing back.** Let it happen. She hears the whole argument out and no roll moves her off the vow. What a PC can change is the shape of the chase. A concrete heading helps most, though a name or a faster hull works too.
- **Her one question.** She does not argue back. She asks "and who goes instead?", and lets the silence answer it.
- **Saying nothing.** She goes back to the table, because the receipts still need copying into a fair hand and a woman is waiting to give her a name.

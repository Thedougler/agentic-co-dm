> [!dm]
> Beats here run in whatever order the crew asks for, and none of them gates another. Run four or five, not nine. Every proprietor gets one committed physical gimmick played without apology, because the gimmick is the whole point of the night.

## Opening

**Dramatic Question:** with three days and a thousand in credit, what does this crew actually want?

> [!read-aloud] Transition
> You come up out of [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]] into afternoon light with a working ship behind you and three days of nothing you have to do.

> [!read-aloud]
> The [[mercatura|Mercatura]] runs a hundred feet wide here and the traffic on it never stops, flat-bottomed boats threading past each other with a foot to spare and nobody apologising.
>
> The cordon around the crater came down this morning. What sits there now is a fenced hole and a smell of wet ash the wind keeps bringing back.
>
> Every shopfront on the water has heard by now that the Defenders of Calveno hold a thousand in Concordat credit, and the ones with a boy to spare have already sent him.
>
> The front has also improved on what you killed under that hole. By this end of the water it runs a hundred feet, and it had been eating children for years.
>
> A boy stands at your elbow already, in fact, a folded quotation in his hand, and he has been waiting a while.

## Running It

### Beat 1: The Chandlery

> [!read-aloud] Moretti & Sons
> [[moretti-and-sons|Moretti & Sons]] takes up forty feet of the Mercatura front, and the smell rolling out of it runs tar first, then hemp, then new canvas. [[giacomo-moretti|Giacomo Moretti]] meets you at the counter with a coil of silk line already in his hands, because the Concordat's boy reached him before you did.
>
> He calls the price through to the back room for confirmation and nods at whatever the silence tells him. The written quotation is ready before you ask for it, with a note under the figure in the same neat hand: *against [[vault/shattered-sea/npcs/iacopo-fieschi|Fieschi]]'s word, good this month.*
>
> Nobody comes out of the back room. Nobody ever has. He has no sons and never had any, and every trader on this front knows it and lets it alone. "Right," he says, turning back to you with the line still in his hands. "Six months, or are we talking cash?"

> [!check] Persuasion — The Credit Line
> DC 12, friendly worldview. To bundle six months of provisioning against the credit instead of the cash line.
> **Success:** roughly 1,400 gp of goods for the 1,000, and he throws in the silk line.
> **Failure:** an honest price, and a long story about canvas grades.

**Oddity.** A disputed crate in his back room carries the factor house's address and has waited five weeks. **If ignored:** he sends a boy to [[casa-falier|Casa Falier]] with a written quotation, then another, then comes himself.

### Beat 2: The Board That Cannot Sit Down

> [!read-aloud] La Gatta
> Under the bottom tier of [[the-bridge|the Bridge]], where the arch throws everything into shade, a halfling stands with a full vendor's board strapped across his chest. [[nicco-kettley|Nicco Kettley]] cannot sit down in that rig, and has not, in trading hours, for nine years. He tells you so cheerfully, then tells you again. The board smells faintly of scorched paper. Rows of stoppered tubes clip to it in grades, and the top row costs sixty percent of what the same thing costs in the [[velo-quarter|Velo Quarter]].
>
> "Seconds," he says, tapping the row. "Perfectly good seconds. Roll a die on the dud, that's fair, I've never said otherwise."

> [!check] Insight — At Marta's Name
> DC 12, the moment anybody says it out loud.
> **Success:** he goes stiff behind the board and starts talking faster.
> **Failure:** a cheerful halfling selling smoke.

**On the board.** [[the-boomerang|The Boomerang]] 60 gp, [[hunters-blend|Hunter's Blend]] and [[pocket-thunder|Pocket Thunder]] 90 gp, d6 dud chance on every sale. He will trade the whole board's contents for a chair he can carry. **If ignored:** the alchemist finds him first.

### Beat 3: The Man Who Never Smokes

> [!read-aloud] La Brace
> [[la-brace|La Brace]] holds the only open-burn licence in [[calveno|Calveno]], and you can tell from the doorway. The air inside moves in slow visible sheets between the curtained booths, and the smell of scorched leaf sits thick enough to taste.
>
> [[rufio-segalla|Rufio Segalla]] lights your blend himself, then holds it out to you at the full stretch of his arm and does not take any part of it for himself. He watches your face while you draw. When you exhale he writes something small and quick in a pocketbook, then turns a page.
>
> "Nineteen years I have been making this book," he says, "and I have never once smoked. Sit anywhere. Take your time. I would like the long version of your face."

> [!check] Persuasion — Sitting for the Pocketbook
> DC 13, to get a curtained booth free in exchange for the sitting.
> **Success:** the booth costs nothing and he sketches the whole table.
> **Failure:** full price, 15 gp the hour, and he sketches you anyway.

**Oddity.** The Concordat has asked twice for his client list and he declined twice, courteously. **If ignored:** the third asking will not stay courteous.

### Beat 4: Thirty-One Rings

> [!read-aloud] The Cabinet of Morsani
> The lantern outside [[cabinet-of-morsani|the Cabinet]] is lit, which is the only notice [[prospero-morsani|Prospero Morsani]] gives that he is open. Dust, brass polish, and something older underneath it. The shelves run back further than the frontage suggests.
>
> He has rings on every finger and both thumbs, thirty-one of them, and he will tell you the number before you ask, along with what each one stands for: an object on these shelves he has failed to sell.
>
> He picks up a salt lantern and starts on its previous owner, at length, without shortening. Behind him, at the back, a locked case rests with nothing on top of it.

> [!check] Persuasion — The Locked Case
> DC 14, or a truly good story told back to him instead of a roll.
> **Success:** the case opens on a thirty-second ring with nothing attached to it yet.
> **Failure:** he laughs and moves you along the shelf.

**On the shelf.** Compass of the Drowned 400 gp, Ringmaker's Regret 250 gp, The Uncharted Coast 180 gp, Salt Lantern 80 gp, Preserved Specimen 90 gp. **If ignored:** the count goes to thirty-two.

### Beat 5: The Number That Never Comes

> [!read-aloud] Il Preludio
> The lanterns over the [[velo-quarter|Velo Quarter]] square burn red, then gold, then a blue no flame holds, and the curtain across [[il-preludio|Il Preludio]]'s door sweeps itself aside before anybody touches it. The room behind it applauds. Real hands, out of sight, clapping you in.
>
> Inside, the room smells of rosin and hot brass. [[fioravante-selmerin|Fio]] comes down a stepladder mid-sentence about lamp oil, one open hand rising to his shoulder on the last word and staying up. [[serena-cottle|Serena Cottle]] takes that same word a fourth higher, arms wide, then flattens onto what it costs. [[tam-slim-nail|Tam]] reads off a ledger, without looking up, which discount Fio has already promised. [[cecco-trivani|Cecco]] draws breath by the chime-birds and gets three syllables out before the door bell takes the rest. Not one of them sings. Every one of them is one breath from it.
>
> In the window are two pieces and nothing else, a small glass bell on a stand and two feet of velvet on a rod. Fio has the bell out and held toward you already, eyebrows up, waiting on the answer to a question he has not asked.

> [!check] Persuasion — The Window Pair
> DC 13, friendly worldview, and a story told back to Fio about what the thing is for counts instead of the roll.
> **Success:** the pair goes together for 400 gp, and he demonstrates both twice.
> **Failure:** list price, and he demonstrates both twice anyway.

**On the shelf.** [[the-encore-bell|The Encore Bell]] 250 gp and [[fios-own-curtain|Fio's Own Curtain]] 200 gp are the only two in the building a person can use. Everything else is a real enchantment that does exactly what its name promises and nothing anybody needs, 15 to 90 gp, listed at [[il-preludio|Il Preludio]]. This is the shop [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] asked to go browse, and Fio's whole trade is getting the object into a browser's hands before they have finished deciding. **If ignored:** Fio finishes dressing the window at last, and the pair goes to whoever stops next.

### Beat 6: The Painter Who Cannot See Colour

> [!read-aloud] Acqua Nera
> [[acqua-nera|Acqua Nera]] sits in the outer harbour southwest of [[the-arsenal|the Arsenal]], where the water stops being canal and starts being sea. [[dario-vanni|Dario Vanni]] has a hull up on trestles and eleven pots open around him, and he is mixing by weight and by nose with his eyes on the middle distance. "I can't see it," he says, before anybody asks. "Colour. Never could. My greys are the best in [[vault/shattered-sea/locations/verdant-scatter|the Scatter]] for it. Nothing about how they look has ever once distracted me."
>
> He wipes his hands and looks at nothing. On the morning of the crater, he says, three hulls came past the point before dawn running dark, and he could not tell you their colour if you paid him. Then he waits for you to tell him what you want done to your ship.

> [!check] Persuasion — Will It Hold Salt
> DC 12, to have him assess [[cobb|Cobb]]'s blue-black.
> **Success:** it holds, and he names the one thing Cobb skimped on.
> **Failure:** a tortle shrugging at a colour he cannot see.

**Oddity.** He has painted the *[[hcs-surety|Uncertainty]]*'s hull class before. Twice. For other people. **If ignored:** he mentions the second of those to the wrong drinker eventually.

### Beat 7: The Orc Who Remembers Ships

> [!read-aloud] Ponte Bassa
> [[ponte-bassa|Ponte Bassa]] is right at the main Bridge crossing, and you can hear cart wheels and boot traffic through the floor more than the walls. Broth and grilled eel smoke fill the room. [[oleandro-fuschi|Oleandro Fuschi]] looks up when you come in and takes about a second before he greets you.
>
> "Cutter, four-foot draw, blue-black now." He gestures at a bench and tells you to sit. Your names mean nothing to him and he says so without embarrassment. Twenty years of hulls through this water and he has kept every one of them. A [[nona-black-jaw|Black-Jaw]] runner sat on that bench this morning asking what went out past the mole, and he offers you that before you get a question out.

> [!check] Persuasion — Twenty Years of Hulls
> DC 11, to have him walk back through what else carried a Dravosi cutter's lines through here.
> **Success:** three hulls, two of them Crown, and one he will not name in the room.
> **Failure:** he names forty ships and none of them help.

**On the table.** Broth 3 sp, grilled eel 5 sp, meat plate 4 sp, wine 2 sp, a bed that sleeps three at 2 sp. **Oddity.** A sealed letter waits behind the bar, left years ago, for whoever next asks the right question. Nothing on file says what it holds.

> [!dm]
> The letter's contents are deliberately unwritten. Fill it with whatever the table has earned by the time somebody asks, or leave it sealed. The same applies to the message shard at [[low-lamp|The Low Lamp]].

### Beat 8: The House

> [!read-aloud] Casa Falier
> The water-door of [[casa-falier|Casa Falier]] opens straight off the canal, and the hall behind it runs forty feet back to a stair. Somebody has left bread on the step with no note on it, and two doors down a shutter opens the width of a face and shuts again.
>
> Chalk dust and lemon oil, and no cooking smell anywhere in the building, because the range went out the door with everything else. The previous owner took the furniture, the sconces, and every interior door in the house. From where you stand you can see through three rooms into a fourth. One chair sits in the middle of the largest ground-floor room. It faces a blank stretch of wall.

> [!check] Investigation — The Chair
> DC 12, on why one chair faces a blank wall.
> **Success:** the wall carries a painted-over doorway, plastered flush and finished badly. The chair faces square where the handle used to be.
> **Failure:** an old chair in an old room.

**Use.** Furnishing, staffing, arguing over rooms, and working out that a base with no doors holds no privacy in it either. A pair of household staff came with the deed, unnamed and unmet. **If ignored:** the neighbours start asking the Council who owns it.

### Beat 9: Master Kyzil

> [!read-aloud] Near the Shrine
> [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] takes his meals on the steps across from the [[waveservant-shrine|Waveservant Shrine]], where you can hear the tribute bells ring at every turn of the tide and smell brine off the stone. He does not appear to notice either.
>
> He sees [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] arrive. His head turns before his body does. Then he goes back to his meal, because he asked his question once already, at the factor's house, and a Sentinel of the Eyrie does not put the same question twice.
>
> He has left the step beside him clear.

**Handling.** No roll belongs here. This one runs as a conversation or it does not run, and Crissdalynn has to be the one who crosses. **Stake:** her pilgrimage stays formally open until she gives the account of [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]] and the two Sentinels who never came back. **If ignored:** he leaves Calveno when his business closes, and the account waits for wherever they meet next.

**The ending is open.** Nothing here has to happen and nothing here gates anything else. The scene closes when the crew run out of appetite for it.

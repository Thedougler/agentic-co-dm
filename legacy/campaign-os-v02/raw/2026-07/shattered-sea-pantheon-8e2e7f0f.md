# Shattered Sea Pantheon

Regional index of the Shattered Sea's divine landscape — shaped by water, risk, and law. [[umberlee|Umberlee]] dominates maritime practice; [[world/lore/valkur|Valkur]] and [[tyr|Tyr]] act as counterweights.

## Player-Known

None. Source's own `audience: dm`, `publish: false`, and lack of any table-appearance record for the pantheon-as-a-concept agree this index lands at `status: pending` rather than `canon` — same reasoning as the `syranita.md`/`tyr.md` precedent, even though individual member deities carry their own independently-earned statuses ([[umberlee|Umberlee]] is `canon` on its own play evidence).

## DM Only

| Deity | Table Role |
|---|---|
| [[umberlee|Umberlee]] | Dominant sea goddess. Sailors pay because the sea kills, not because she is kind. |
| [[world/lore/valkur|Valkur]] | Sailor courage, favorable winds, and private hope below decks. No formal temples in the Scatter. |
| [[tyr|Tyr]] | Dravosi law, oaths, commissions, courts martial, and civic order. |
| [[remnis|Remnis]] | Aarakocra sky deity; Crissdalynn's primary god. |
| [[syranita|Syranita]] | Aarakocra sky-pantheon deity in Crissdalynn's margins. |
| [[world/lore/aerdrie-faenya|Aerdrie Faenya]] | Aarakocra sky-pantheon deity in Crissdalynn's margins. |

Most sailors interact with divinity through tribute, oath, and habit. Umberlee's public faith is port infrastructure — plain text, not a wikilink: no dedicated doctrine/shrine-custom page exists yet in `world/`. `relink: umberlee-and-waveservants — once its page lands`. Valkur is invoked quietly. Tyr appears where Dravosi authority needs witnesses, seals, and lawful force.

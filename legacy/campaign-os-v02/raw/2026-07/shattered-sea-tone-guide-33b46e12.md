# Shattered Sea — Tone Guide

## Player-Known

## DM Only

**Core equation:** high competence trapped inside low emotional maturity — real danger, glamour, hierarchy, and momentum, populated by stylish, capable people who let ego, vanity, lust, boredom, resentment, and old grudges complicate their best efforts. One-sentence tone: swashbuckling sophistication often complicated by pettiness.

**Six pillars:**

1. Real stakes + childish dysfunction — the setting plays straight, the people are disasters; epic consequences, petty triggers.
2. Competence without adulthood — dangerous experts who are also personal disasters (brilliant captain, terrible impulse control; feared corsair, desperate for approval).
3. Stylish surface, rotten interior — lacquered warships, perfumed courts built on piracy or exploitation, fine wine and blood on cuffs.
4. Banter as combat — dialogue as social violence: interruption, one-upmanship, flirtation as dominance, precision insults, status testing, old slights never forgotten.
5. Mundane pettiness in lethal situations — a boarding action pauses to argue about bad gunpowder; a treaty fails over seating and décor.
6. Cynicism without emptiness — dysfunctional institutions run by egomaniacs, but people still carry messy loyalty, attraction, and occasional genuine care.

**Tone boundaries:** keep glamour, danger, decadence, sharp dialogue, recurring grudges, emotional damage played lightly, real consequences. Avoid broad clown comedy/pure parody, meme humor or nonstop one-liners, making the world itself silly, every NPC sarcastic in the same voice, or removing consequences.

**NPC construction formula:** public image + real competence + one humiliating need + one petty fixation. Example: naval legend / brilliant tactician / needs royal approval / obsessed with seeming younger than a rival.

**Scene construction rule:** every major scene combines a real external threat, a petty internal conflict, stylish presentation, fast verbal escalation, and consequences that remain real.

**Agent application:** default NPC dialogue register is status-testing and guarded about real needs; default scene energy is competent professionals sabotaged by interpersonal baggage; comedy comes from character flaws, never from making the world itself silly — the sea stays deadly, the monsters stay real, the people are the disasters.

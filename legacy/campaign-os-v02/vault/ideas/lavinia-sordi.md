# Lavinia Sordi — retcon, Dravosi Crown history

Retcon: her physical description. Current page says "mid-fifties," hair
pulled back so tight it changes her face. GM played her at the table as
"that sharp-faced actress" — Ruby Rose energy — before remembering her
name. Recast younger and angular: sharp cheekbones, undercut-adjacent,
flat unimpressed stare, worn leather over soft. Same coiled, precise
physicality the page already has, just on a leaner, harder frame. This
replaces the mid-fifties description; everything else on the page (the
business, the items sold, the voice, the testing ritual) stays.

---

Amateur sorceress, as flavor — she demonstrated cantrips in Session 08,
self-taught, picked up secondhand like everything else in her shop, never
formally trained. Testing stock on her own body (already on the page —
"the test and sale both") isn't just salesmanship, it's the closest thing
she has to real practice, and she genuinely enjoys the risk — she popped
an unidentified healing potion on herself for fun with zero hesitation
(Session 08 log: gave Delmar a free taster of the [[vial|Vial]] of Uncertain
Healing). Tic-tacs at a rave energy, not reckless — appetite, not fear.

---

She prefers cursed items to clean ones on principle: a clean item does
exactly what it says and nothing more — boring. A cursed one still has an
argument in it. Possible line for her: "Anyone can sell you a promise. I
sell you what's actually true, curse and all."

---

Backstory: before [[la-cenere|La Cenere]], she worked for the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] at one of
their prisons — a records keeper, not a guard, which is how
she had steady hands-off access to confiscated contraband. Over her time
there she smuggled goods in and out repeatedly, a habit that should have
gotten her caught and never did — "miracle she didn't get
caught." Watching the Crown's institutional cruelty up close the whole
time, dressed up in stamps and seals, is what finally drove her
out.

This is the direct root of her current ethos: the Dravosi Crown sells
order and hides the cruelty behind stamps and rating forms (see
`dravosi-crown.md`'s "Order maintained through submission to the
document"); she sells risk and refuses to hide it. Her line "the price
reflects what I can guarantee, which is less than what you'd like" reads
as a direct rejection of the Crown's whole self-image.

Left deliberately unstated on the page: this also explains her nine
trouble-free years with the Canal Commission (old Crown-world instincts,
old Crown-world leverage) — no exact dates, no named prison, no specific
role title pinned down. She never mentions this history unless a scene
calls for it.

No connection drawn to [[ashkevet|Ashkevet]] (the one named Dravosi prison already in
the wiki, `locations/midchain/ashkevet.md`) — the timeline doesn't cleanly
fit a Ruby-Rose-aged Lavinia against Ashkevet's "abandoned a generation
ago." Left as an unnamed/different Dravosi facility unless the GM wants to
tie it to Ashkevet later.

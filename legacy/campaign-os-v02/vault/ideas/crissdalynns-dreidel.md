# Crissdalynn's dreidel

The dreidel already exists in canon as a gift from [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]]
before her pilgrimage, meditation focus, crystal, spun in sunlight. Table
already free-associated "dreidel walkie-talkie… dreidel talkie… dreidel
phone" live at the table in session 06
(vault/episodes/006/audio/clean/player-mic/part-011.flac.md:1156);
this mechanic is finishing a thought the table already started, not
inventing one from scratch.

Pitch: the dreidel forms a spiritual connection between people who've seen
it spin and are friendly to the holder. Spinning it lets Crissdalynn reach
across the ethereal plane to one of them; normally the DM would decide
unilaterally (or with a private roll) how an offscreen event unfolds for an
absent ally, but this item lets the party collaborate in that resolution with
real dice, at the table, right now.

---

Origin/emotional hook: she's alone on a ship, cast out mid-pilgrimage,
banished from the monastery. Kyzil gave her a way to not be alone; the
item's pitch is that, not "cool mechanic."

---

Mechanical shape, final:

- **Trigger:** player-initiated, on-demand, no DM permission needed.
- **Gates:** sunlight (open sky, daylight; already established, not
  invented) + once per day + connection (seen it spin, friendly to the
  holder).
- **DM's only lever:** invisible content curation. Never says no to the
  vision happening, only silently curates what it reveals/resolves so it
  never touches a planned plot beat or spoils prep. Permission moved from
  "can you spin" to "what you see when you do."
- **Resolution:** the ensemble multi-check scene mechanic (see below):
  party rolls, allocates after seeing results, Crissdalynn frames/narrates
  the vision since it's her sight.
- **Cost:** none on failure. This non-combat use costs neither ki nor combat resources. A bad roll just
  means the vignette goes badly for the connected NPC; nothing punishes her
  at the table.

---

Companion mechanic: ensemble multi-check beats (born from fixing the
session 06 Kyzil cold-open, vault/episodes/006/scene-01-kyzil.md,
where a single check per beat left players who rolled low or already
rolled checked out):

Instead of one skill check per beat that everyone rolls for, each beat
offers at least as many varied checks as there are players. Party rolls
first (advantage), THEN sees results and assigns which roll answers
which check (not assign-then-roll). Nobody's dead weight; the worst
roll still gets assigned where it costs least, reads as a choice not a
whiff. Failure per-check should add a complication/detail rather
than binary-fail the whole beat, to keep momentum in a low-stakes comedic
register.

Closer to a Blades in the Dark group action or a 4e skill challenge than
straight 5e: one total scene outcome, not a single gate. Beat 4 of the
Kyzil scene already does the mirror-image move (three threats consolidated
into one roll); this is the reverse: one beat, many rolls, spread across
the party.

The load-bearing rule, not a footnote: the DM silently restricts what
these checks (both the Kyzil-style NPC montage version and the dreidel
version) can affect to anything that wouldn't impact a planned plot point.
That's what makes it safe to hand this much agency to the party in a
sandbox game.

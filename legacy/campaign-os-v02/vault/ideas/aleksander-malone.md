# Aleksander Malone

[[cleric|Cleric]] of [[tyr|Tyr]], modeled on Alexander Anderson from Hellsing: a heavily Irish-Catholic-coded zealot who believes every act of violence he commits is righteous judgment, delivered with grim joy rather than reluctance. Iron will, absolute conviction. Signature line: "May God have mercy on my enemies, for they shall have none from me."

---

Held in reserve by the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]], specifically for heresy — not deployed for ordinary crime. The Crown learned the hard way that his zealotry only aims cleanly at the guaranteed-guilty. There's Crown history of a mistake: he was loosed in a relatively public space once, with ugly results. That incident is why the reservation policy exists now.

---

No handler. Dravosi tried assigning one — didn't survive contact. "Their dog needs a long leash, and a handler will only get bit." Standing arrangement instead: word of heresy reaches him, he goes, on his own judgment. He answers to no one moment-to-moment; the leash is long by design, not by neglect.

---

Lives at [[blackrule|Blackrule]], a chapterhouse he chose himself, cut into the volcanic terrace-rock of the [[midchain-south|Southern Midchain]]. Chose it out of genuine disdain for civilized excess — not exile, not punishment, this is where he wants to be. New location, unclaimed: doesn't overlap with [[ashglass|Ashglass]] (dead volcano, young red dragon's lair, [[midchain-east|Eastern Midchain]]), the [[the-hollow-choir|Hollow Choir]] (reclusive singing order, Southern Midchain), or [[corrigans-rest|Corrigan's Rest]] (retired pirates' welcoming hideaway, [[midchain-north|Northern Midchain]]).

---

Mechanically a mercilessly precise cleric/fighter/rogue multiclass — no invented supernatural resilience. Channel Divinity (Tyr's judgment moving through him, no homebrew needed) + [[fighter|Fighter]] durability/Action Surge + [[rogue|Rogue]] precision and mobility, stacked to feel like a min-maxer's nightmare build. Represented at the table as a standard monster statblock.

---

Long-term thread: he's the Crown's answer once the [[khlysty|Khlysty]] cult (see `khlysty.md`, `shepherd-grigori.md`) is confirmed to have infiltrated Dravosi itself. Connects him to that existing sleeping thread — wake it at the GM's discretion, don't force it before then.

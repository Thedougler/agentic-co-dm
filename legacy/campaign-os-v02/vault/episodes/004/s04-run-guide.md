## Secrets & Clues

Keep these **unassigned**. Give each to the first action that earns it, never bound to one roll or one scene.

- A Calveno repaint won't erase a Crown cutter's silhouette, via **Savia** (says it flatly after a Warren intro), **a dock regular** (eyes the hull and shakes his head), or **a failed papers check** (the inspector looks at the bow, not the paint).
- The font at the Waveservant Shrine won't go still, via **anyone who enters the shrine** (the water moves), a public omen if Delmar stays away long enough for locals to notice.
- Unusual broker money is building a Maw salvage job aimed at the *Red Lady*, via **a chandler** (lowers his voice unprompted), **Savia** (names "Zusto" once), or **canal gossip** (diving gear at the anchorage).
- A Crown-adjacent observer has missed his morning run two days straight. His firm closes for the festival, and it *didn't* call him in either, via **Cobb** (harbour gossip) or **a harbour regular** (someone who keeps a routine).
- A Beffa team registered under an unrecognized name asked the clerk how many people you can move through the bridge pinch-point in a minute, via **anyone who asks about odd teams** or **the clerk herself** if a PC registers.

**Raid-adjacent: use only if the crew looks in the right direction.**

- The Mercatura sewer collector has three access hatches. Workers **re-seated two of them this week**, off the books, since the festival inspection cycle skips that section.
- The Dravosi patrol schedule for the Calven Roads has a **three-day gap starting Beffa Day 4**. Any harbour factor with contacts can quote the dates. The question is who *else* already has them.
- Three low-profile, dark-resined, double-outrigged hulls sit at anchor past the patrol line, unmarked. Anyone with [[grung-npc|Grung]]/[[midchain|Midchain]] maritime knowledge IDs them as *Vethka*.

## What If They Can't Decide?

If the table stalls or can't pick a direction, throw one of these. Each is self-contained and pulls toward a different thread. The crew's response tells you which thread they care about.

| Hook | Comes to | Pulls toward |
|---|---|---|
| **The stone speaks.** *Nona*: *(whispered)* Come back. Bring the blue one. | Perrin | Warren / the favor / JC's tunnel thread |
| **Branca hasn't moved.** She is in the shrine at dawn, same position, booklet on her knee. A local mentions the Waveservant woman who won't leave. | Delmar (via rumour) | Pearl Summons / Umberlee |
| **A handler in the crowd.** A Grung stands at the edge of a Velo square, watching how the crowd moves, not enjoying it. Trained posture. | JC (private note) | Raid thread / handler investigation |
| **A chandler mentions money.** "You're not the first to ask about Maw salvage this month. There's money moving. Serious money." | Delmar / Crissdalynn | Red Lady / salvage commission |
| **The Beffa clerk eyes a PC.** A registered team has picked one of them as their mark. Festival mischief: loud, dumb, theirs. | Any | Beffa registration / city engagement |
| **Sem needs an answer.** "I can't paint a name I don't have." The ship's registry plate is a decision someone has to make. | Perrin / Delmar | Ship logistics / Savia |

Don't throw all these at once. Pick the one aimed at the quietest player, or the one that connects to whatever thread the table has been circling. One hook is an invitation. A run of three turns it into a schedule.

## If They Surprise You

When the crew goes somewhere you didn't prep:

1. **Place the request.** A public deal goes to Mercatura or [[the-bridge|the Bridge]]. Quiet practical work goes to [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]]. Vice, music, and strange trade go to Velo. Official ship business goes to the Arsenal edge ([[calveno|Calveno]] DM Only absorbs the district map and improv voice notes).
2. **Give one competent local** who wants one small thing now, something to sell, someone to vouch for them, or a favor called in. Nobody drags them into trouble.
3. **Attach one live pressure** from the Threads table so the new direction still feeds the session.
4. **Let the choice stand.** Note what changed, tick the clock, move on. Don't repeat a hook through a second NPC until they comply. Charge a cost and make the new direction real.

## Possible Cliffhanger

Don't plan an ending. Recognize the highest-tension stopping point if it arrives.

- **Broke a handler cell:** end on what the handler reveals, that the raid is days out and they've found one of five lanes.
- **Found the blackpowder:** end on the packed ceiling, the summoning circle, the moment JC understands the scale. The city is sitting on a bomb.
- **Engaged Umberlee:** end on the breath: Branca gasping back to life, the booklet open, "I hope you understand my lady Umberlee's power. And her grace." The Pearl summons hangs over every Maw decision.
- **Entered the tunnels:** end on what JC finds underground, real Grung signs, sealed hatches, and extraction route markers.
- **Raid launched:** end on Nona's vow, "we will bring your brothers and your sons and your fathers back," or on the *Vethka* clearing the Calven Roads with their cargo.
- **Ignored the raid prep:** end on Day 5's first wrong sound, a scream the crowd laughs off right up until it doesn't.

# Session 04: Frog, Owl, and the Bomb

## Recap

[[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] sold the [[whip-shark|Whip Shark]]'s three fertilized eggs at the [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] market for 300 gp; a delicacy, the merchant noted, not breeding stock. He spent the gold back down at [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]]: a silent shortbow for himself and boots of flying for [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] once someone remembered [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] doesn't wear shoes.

On the street outside, a stranger looked Jean-Claude over and dismissed him as no threat. [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] vouched for him. Delmar just hugged the man. The stranger was [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]].

The four of them made [[ponte-bassa|Ponte Bassa]] their base, a cheap tavern near [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]] and a Passage front. Kyzil talked with Crissdalynn about her pilgrimage: the hardest one in Eyrie history, given to her because he believed she'd survive it. He'd felt the [[the-drowned-maw|Maw]] turn unruly since the wreck, and something foul moving up from the south.

Kyzil challenged all four of them to a spar on the rooftop, non-lethal, three rounds. Crissdalynn grappled him and rolled a natural one on the contest before his Downburst broke it. Delmar joined in with a thrown chair; Kyzil caught Crissdalynn before she went off the roof, then pinned Jean-Claude's shirt to the deck with one claw in a display of hierarchy before Delmar's own hit finally connected on borrowed bardic inspiration. The bout lasted three rounds. He was staying in [[calveno|Calveno]] through the [[il-gioco-delle-beffe|festival]].

Walking to the harbor after, Delmar told them the truth: he was a real admiral, a five-ship privateer fleet sponsored by the [[chain-council|Chain Council]]. They'd stolen the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] from a shrine in [[vel-orn|Vafnar]].

The Maw destroyed the fleet within hours. At the harbor shrine, [[umberlee|Umberlee]] answered by taking [[branca|Branca]]'s body, killing her and bringing her back to prove she could. She named her price: the Pearl, in exchange for Delmar's life.

Delmar countered: the Pearl, and the people who'd commissioned the theft. Umberlee read the offer out of his head before he finished making it. *Bring me the pearl. We will talk then.*

Crissdalynn swept Delmar's legs out from under him the next morning for going to a goddess alone. Then [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s sending stone arrived: *bring your friends, especially the blue one.* At her safe house she laid out six [[grung-npc|Grung]] sightings in the [[warren|Warren]] sewers: blue, green, one purple. Something that had never happened before; no [[grung|Grung]] had crossed the [[central-strait|Central Strait]] in living memory.

She fed them a Hero's Feast and sent them down to look. They found directional markers and a crossbow trap that had failed to fire. The smell of black powder grew stronger as they descended. At the coast, a boat: three Grung and eight barrels of it.

Jean-Claude killed two from the ceiling before they knew he was there. The boat went up on a fire arrow. Crissdalynn ran the fleeing purple Grung down from the air. It called Jean-Claude a traitor before he ended it.

The captive, [[felix-aho|Felix Aho]], talked without much persuading. The conspirators had placed attack points under the festival crowds and positioned black powder against the sewer ceilings. The deadline was two days. The operation relied on six ships. He told Jean-Claude something he hadn't known: [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s people kill Grung who leave on sight, and he counted as one of them.

Felix asked for a month of protection in exchange for everything he knew. They brought him to Nona in one piece. *I knew I chose the right people,* she said, and set [[ruk|Ruk]] to [[guard|Guard]] him.

## Opening

Solange's reaction fires the instant Scene 4's trigger hits. She speaks the command word (not a spell, can't be Counterspelled) and the alchemical fuses in the ceiling scaffolding catch fire. The circle deflects the blast around her; the [[mercatura|Mercatura]] plaza above Collapses into the room. Everyone else in the chamber:

> [!mechanic]
> **The Detonation (everyone else in the chamber):**
>
> - Blast: 8d6 fire (DC 16 DEX save for half).
> - Collapse: 4d6 bludgeoning (DC 16 STR save to avoid buried). Buried creatures are prone and restrained under rubble. They escape by making a DC 16 STR (Athletics) check or having an ally free them as an action.
> - [[globe-of-invulnerability|Globe of Invulnerability]] scroll (Room 6, [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|Calveno Sewer Magazines]]): DC 16 Arcana to activate. Failure spends the scroll without effect. Its 10-ft barrier stops spells of 5th level or lower only. Blast and Collapse punch straight through. It provides cover from incoming magical effects only.

Full detonation mechanic: [[solange-barret|Solange Barret]].

> [!dm]
> Resolve every save and its damage first in silence, then deliver the box below all at once.

> [!quote] Solange Barret. The command word
> "Agni."

> [!read-aloud]
> The ceiling becomes light. Stone, fire, and pressure hit before the sound does, and the Mercatura plaza folds inward through a thirty-foot wound of broken cobblestone and shredded festival banners hanging in the new open air above. Heat washes over you in one wave. Just as quickly it vanishes, replaced by grit and rising smoke thick enough to taste. A last piece of ceiling crashes behind you, hitting the floor with a flat, final crack. Through the haze, Solange kneels exactly where she knelt a moment ago, untouched, a perfect ring of clean stone holding the wreckage back around her knees.

## Running It

**Beat 1. The held breath.** One or two quiet rounds before the emergence. Reposition, ready an action, dig for fire sources (§ below), or clear civilians. Whatever costs a round (no free actions).

> [!check] Perception or Insight — Reading the Circle
> DC 13, usable once by anyone watching Solange instead of acting.
> **Success:** the black light pulses harder, building toward a breaking point instead of holding steady. A threshold still lies ahead.
> **Failure:** the light reads steady. Nothing tips what's coming.

**Beat 2. The Emergence.** On Solange's next turn, she completes the ritual.

> [!read-aloud]
> Solange finishes the last line, and the circle answers from beneath her, black light pooling around her knees like ink poured into water, as her spine snaps first, loud enough to hear over the chant, and folds her forward onto hands already growing too many joints. Her jaw dislocates with a wet crack. It stretches a full foot wider; the skin along it splits in a long seam that seals shut a heartbeat later, healed over a bone structure that isn't hers anymore. Ribs punch out through the skin of her back one at a time, gleam wet in the torchlight, then get dragged back inward as the whole cavity behind them swells and reshapes. Every fresh tear weeps a thick, sick-sweet vapor before it closes, and the vapor keeps coming, thickening fast, coiling low across the stone instead of rising, the same dull color as old bruises. Her scream keeps going through it, longer than a Human throat should hold a note. It isn't her throat making the sound anymore (two seconds, maybe three), and what stands in the circle afterward stands red, enormous, and breathing like stone grinding on stone.

Above the crater, the crowd hasn't caught up yet (the explosion read as festival spectacle, the [[il-gioco-delle-beffe|Beffa]] still running).

> [!read-aloud]
> Up on the broken plaza edge, a woman leans out, squinting down through haze at the enormous red thing. Festival noise rolls on. Nobody else is close enough to see.

> [!quote] The woman on the plaza edge
> "It looks so real!"

> [!dm]
> The tongue lash is already moving. If a PC readied an action back in Beat 1 with a
> ranged option, or has a clear ranged shot available right now, offer the attempt before you read on.

> [!check] Ranged Attack — Severing the Reach
> AC 15 vs. tongue, thirty+ feet, closing fast. Disadvantage without readied action or clear sightline.
> **Success:** tongue goes wide. She scrambles from edge; crowd screams at the right thing.
> **Failure:** the box below plays as written.

> [!read-aloud]
> Thirty feet of wet muscle uncoils from that impossible mouth, wraps around her waist, and pulls. She is off the edge and gone before the word "real" finishes echoing off the canal walls. The mouth opens, closes, and a wet crunch cuts the scream short as the crowd finally starts screaming for real.

Let that silence land before rolling initiative. The Beffa's conditioning breaks: laughed, then watched someone die. Solange was the delivery system. There's no reversing this.

**Beat 3. The Fight.** Numbers match [[otar-the-foul|Otar]]'s page (see [[scene-03-otar-manifests|Session 07 Scene 3]] for actual run: second charge, Perrin's ward, 3 [[minor-slaad|Minor Slaad]]). Don't soften: party on ropes, and [[the-rattle-statblock|the Rattle]] refuses TPK. Hungry, confused, and enormous, it fixes its attention on the nearest thing.

> [!dm]
> Read the boxes below quiet and unhurried. Let the woman's question hang unanswered.

> [!read-aloud]
> The creature comes apart in the crater the way a held breath finally lets go, its great red bulk folding sideways into broken stone and going still. Dust that's hung in the air since the ceiling came down finally starts to settle. Above the crater's lip, the plaza that was full of screaming a few minutes ago has fallen silent except for torn festival cloth, moving in a wind that wasn't there before.

> [!read-aloud]
> The Mercatura's chimes start their midday count as somewhere below the rubble, someone starts laughing, low and disbelieving, catching from person to person the way a match catches. Then, past the edge of the crater, a woman starts calling a name into the crowd, and nobody answers her. She turns toward the party, the only ones still standing close enough to hear her over the noise.

> [!quote] The woman at the crater's edge
> "Please. Did you see him come out? Did anyone come out after you?"

Let it sit. The party decides what to tell her; word reaches the Warren runner before Nona does.

> [!check] Investigation or Perception — The Crater Floor
> DC 13 (before Beat 5 fragments arrive).
> **Success:** half-buried red hide, fist-sized, still warm, sealing itself. Claimable. Full item: [[a-sliver-of-the-unstable-form|A Sliver of the Unstable Form]].
> **Failure:** crater reads as rubble, blood, dead thing. Nobody spots the fragment.

**Beat 5. The Aftermath.** Full picture arrives in fragments.

> [!check] Investigation — Searching the Crater
> DC 14; immediately after victory.
> **Success:** rope burns and drag marks lead toward coast road.
> **Failure:** churned mud and cloth (Warren runner carries the thread).

> [!mechanic]
> **Piecing it together:** fragments arrive over minutes.
> | Trigger | Source | What it reveals |
> |---|---|---|
> | ~10 minutes later, a Warren runner arrives | A Warren runner, breathless (quote below) | A fragment, not the full shape |
> | If the party seeks out Kyzil | [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]] | Bridge and Harbour Approach went quiet when crater opened |
> | Once 2-3 fragments landed | [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] | Arrives with full count and her vow |

> [!quote] Warren runner — arriving out of breath
> "Boats, at the coastline, more than there should be. Not fishing boats. Nobody's boats."

Parties that caught Beat 3's Bridge screams already have a head start. Don't contradict what they noticed.

The creature was never the point. This attack is a diversion, loud and large enough to pull every citizen near the Mercatura toward the coastline and the outer districts, clearing streets in exactly the direction the raid's real target needed. While the party fought it, [[grung-npc|Grung]] extraction teams worked the two secondary sites never neutralized during the dungeon crawl, taking citizens who fled directly into their hands.

**The numbers.** 250 captives per secondary site still operational at the time of the raid. The party disabled two of the four (Room 4/Alpha and Room 5/Beta) during the crawl (per `vault/episodes/005/s05-recap.md`); the other two, T1/Gamma and T2/Delta, ran uninterrupted once [[vashu-the-weeping-veil|Vashu]] and [[ozzeth-the-twiceborn|Ozzeth]] abandoned them in Scene 4. **500 citizens taken.**

**Nona's speech.** She emerges with Enzo and Ruk, feeds the injured, then speaks for [[the-passage|The Passage]]. Adapt the exact wording live; the number stays fixed:

> [!quote] Nona Black-Jaw — arriving after the fight
> "I know what this is: to look for someone who was here a minute ago and find nothing; my people have been disappearing for decades, and today it happened to five hundred of yours. The Passage stands with the citizens of [[calveno|Calveno]]."

**What this hooks forward to:** three unmarked, dark-hulled [[vethka|Vethka]] are the extraction vessels (the pursuit target for **Session 07**, not tonight).

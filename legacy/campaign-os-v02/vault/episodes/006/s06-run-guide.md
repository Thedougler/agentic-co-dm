**Tonight's device: *ma*** (negative space, the interval that gives the music its shape). Kyzil's chase, Thunk's hungover discovery, and Nona's market walk are the compositional gap that makes the fight land harder, not filler before the real session. Play it:

- **Let each vignette breathe.** Let a joke sit a beat too long. Don't rush toward the violence everyone knows is coming.
- **Keep the register light and personal**, even where a scene ends with permanent stakes (Thunk's does). When the violence begins, let it feel sudden.
- **The payoff arrives at the fight, not during the scenes.** Give the table an uninterrupted [[calveno|Calveno]] evening (people, pettiness, small stakes) so the raid lands as a turning point.

**Best ideas for tonight:** the Kyzil chase-comedy cold open, the Ma sequence itself, and the fight in two movements (Vashu's garrison first, then Otar as its own stage).

**Session shape:** Kyzil's chase opens cold (no PC on screen), followed by Thunk, then Nona, then the Primary Chamber fight, and Otar if time remains. All three vignettes will run. Trim inside scenes but never skip a vignette.

**Table mix:** even combat and drama with problem-solving threaded through. Each scene mixes talking, acting, and rolling. Lean on the DC-gated branch points already built into each vignette.

**Safety tools:** already established with this table per `vault/refs/lines-and-veils.md`. Nothing new tonight.

## Secrets & Clues

**Plot & aftermath: the raid's true shape.** The full sequenced delivery lives in [[scene-05-otar-the-foul|Scene 5]]'s aftermath table (each fragment's trigger, source, check, and failure fallback are there), not here. As an audit list, the fragments are: the crater's physical evidence (organized movement), the Warren runner's coastline report, [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]]'s quiet-districts confirmation, the diversion reveal itself, and the [[vethka|Vethka]] pursuit hook that opens Session 07.

**NPC & villain secrets** (`references/secrets-and-clues.md` third category. introduce the villain before the fight resolves it):

1. Vashu and Ozzeth abandoned their posts *instantly* when Room 8 got loud. devotion to [[solange-barret|Solange]], not tactics. A party that clocks the abandonment mid-fight ([[scene-04-fight-continues|Scene 4]] names the two rooms left open) holds the aftermath's biggest fragment before the fight even ends.
2. [[solange-barret|Solange]] knew. The ritual consumes its caster and she cast it anyway. whatever the party later learns about red-caste command culture starts from that fact. No reversal, per her page.

**Historical secret** (second category):

1. The Rattle is a forty-year-old standing pact. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] has walked these streets unhidden that whole time (see [[scene-03-nona|Scene 3]]'s quote), and tonight Calveno's citizens (not just [[warren|the Warren]]) see what answering it means.

**Character secrets. one per player** (abstract from any specific room or roll): drop each wherever play opens the door, or let it wait. Each comes from the PC page's Arc Notes.

1. **[[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]:** the extraction pattern. roped lines, staged movement, citizens driven toward waiting boats. This is logistics he has *run*, in the slave-raider years no one at the table knows about. He reads the raid's shape at a glance. What the recognition costs him, and whether anyone notices, is the secret.
2. **[[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]]:** Thunk's cannon order (two Long Nines, two Short 32-pounders) is exactly her old trade. she has outfitted warships before, under the binding [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] contract she doesn't talk about. If she's consulted on the fittings, her fluency shows more than she intends.
3. **[[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]:** [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s vow to assemble a crew and make chase puts her to sea. the same waters as the private [[aruhe|Aruhe]] survey she tasked him with, which nobody else knows exists. Whether she folds it into the pursuit or keeps it separate is hers to reveal. The pressure on him starts tonight either way.
4. **[[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]:** if she's the one who seeks [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]] out in the aftermath, he has a question of his own waiting. the two Sentinels missing from [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]]. the next formal step on her incomplete pilgrimage.
5. **[[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]:** Thunk is converting *his* cabin. The "four captains" fiction erased the ship's one actual captain's quarters, and the ~1,000 gp commission sits on his ship's credit ([[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] hasn't weighed in on the debt yet). How and when he finds out is open. The wound to his crew-dignity will sting when it does.

## Fantastic Locations

**[[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|Primary Chamber, Calveno Sewer Magazines]]**. Where tonight's fight resumes and ends. Defining trait: a working ritual chamber that becomes an open-air crater mid-fight. Familiar: flooded drainage channels and scaffolding, the ordinary bones of a sewer. Functional: the [[grung-npc|Grung]] staged their summoning here specifically because it sits directly beneath the [[mercatura|Mercatura]] plaza. The ceiling connects directly to the plaza floor. Fantastic: the collapsing stone opens to daylight and dust, transforming a sealed chamber into a stage visible to the panicking crowd above. Description and full room detail live on the dungeon's own page, not here.

## Rewards

Two magic items recoverable from the raid site, both DM-approved and ready for the table:

- **[[a-sliver-of-the-unstable-form|A Sliver of the Unstable Form]]**. Very Rare, requires attunement. Torn from Otar's own body; grants a burst of self-regeneration once per long rest, suppressible by fire/acid the same way the creature itself was. *Pull:* [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]], the party's [[artificer|Artificer]].
- **[[solanges-authority-seal|Solange's Authority Seal]]**. Rare, requires attunement. A red-caste command signet: compels a Grung creature to obey a one-word command once per long rest. *Pull:* [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]'s unique tie to Grung caste authority.

Nona's explicit gratitude and vow, and deepened standing with [[warren|the Warren]] and [[the-passage|The Passage]], are the story rewards alongside them. not a substitute for concrete loot on a fight this size.

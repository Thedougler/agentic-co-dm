## Running It

**The setup.** [[ozzeth-the-twiceborn|Ozzeth]] Misty Steps past everyone to shield [[solange-barret|Solange]]. [[vashu-the-weeping-veil|Vashu]] bursts from the channel at full speed, guided by vibration and scent. She's **winded** (the hard run used up her Still-Water). See [[vashu-the-weeping-veil-winded|Vashu, the Weeping Veil (Winded)]].

- **Initiative:** Vashu acts at the top of the next round. Her action resolves before anyone reacts. Don't roll.

No reserve team stands by. Doctrine says hold and let others hear the noise. Tonight is different: the circle matters most, so both leave their posts. Vashu abandons Room T1 (Magazine Gamma). Ozzeth abandons Room T2 (Magazine Delta). Now both rooms stand empty, affecting [[scene-05-otar-the-foul|Scene 5]].

**What stays the same.** Solange channels every turn, unable to attack or cast spells. The room stays unchanged with four drainage channels, scaffolding, and the circle's glow. Full room details are in [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|Calveno Sewer Magazines]] § Room 8. Use [[solange-barret|Solange Barret]]'s stats.

**This scene's main choice: the circle.** Cut it anytime. Breaking the circle ends the ritual instantly. The option stays open from round one until the trigger fires.

> [!check] Arcana — Severing the Circle
> DC 18 as an action, or 1 minute of work with no interruption. Anyone can try. Touching it triggers Ozzeth to fight one round later (he guards Solange).
> **Success:** the resonance line snaps, summoning fails. Solange runs and blows the ceiling for cover. See [[solange-barret|Solange Barret]] if she escapes. You skip the Detonation and don't run [[scene-05-otar-the-foul|Scene 5]].
> **Failure:** the line holds. The ritual goes on. Ozzeth now fights you.

> [!mechanic]
> **Solange's defenses:**
>
> - [[mirror-image|Mirror Image]]: 3 copies block three attacks.
> - Circle Ward: AC 15 (+2), advantage on concentration saves.
> - [[counterspell|Counterspell]]: blocks 3rd-level spells and lower.
> - Two 3rd-level slots remain. Using one delays detonation this turn.

**What changes:**

- **The fighting garrison:** [[solange-barret|Solange]] channels, 3 [[grung-elite-warrior|Elite Warriors]] fight, and Vashu (winded) joins. See [[vashu-the-weeping-veil-winded|Vashu, the Weeping Veil (Winded)]] for details.

> [!mechanic]
> **Elite Warriors:** AC 13, 49 HP each. They attack twice per turn with dagger or bow (+5 each). Poison adds 2d4 damage and triggers automatically on hit. Mesmerizing Chirr (recharge 6): DC 12 WIS or stunned until next turn (range 15 ft). Full details: [[grung-elite-warrior|Grung Elite Warrior]].

> [!dm]
> **Vashu's tactic: the yo-yo.** She pulls targets with Tongue Lash, lands hits with Still-Water Strike, and blocks damage for herself and allies via Still-Water Deflection. Her echolocation works in any darkness.

> [!dm]
> Ozzeth protects Solange and the circle. He doesn't enter combat unless you attack them directly. When the shift comes, he turns away as his colors shift.

> [!mechanic]
> **Ozzeth's fight kit, if forced in.** CR 8, AC 16, HP 130. No verbal components. *[[silence|Silence]]* doesn't work. Full spell list on [[ozzeth-the-twiceborn|his page]], between sessions only.
>
> - **Multiattack:** one cantrip + Venom Lash +6 (1d4+3 piercing, DC 14 CON or 2d6 poison, pulls 5 ft); [[poison-spray|Poison Spray]] 10 ft, DC 16 CON or 2d10.
> - **Shield** (reaction, +5 AC), **[[misty-step|Misty Step]]** (bonus action), **Legendary Resistance** 2/day.
> - **Legendary action: weeping cantrip** (1): a cantrip.
> - **Legendary action: slip the skin** (1): move without provoking opportunity attacks.
> - **Legendary action: pull the thread** (2): a creature charmed, frightened, or dominated by him spends its reaction to move and attack a target of his choice.

> [!mechanic]
> **Detonation trigger.** This fires when Solange channels for two full rounds, or when your side drops to 2 defenders (from 4). Ignore other trigger conditions tonight. The Detonation fires first, then the [[otar-the-foul-manifestation|summoning]] on Solange's turn. See [[scene-05-otar-the-foul|Scene 5]].

- **The Detonation ends this fight.** When it fires, unshielded Grung at the entrances die and collapse. Vashu survives if standing and flees to the flooded east channel.
  **What actually happened:** [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] killed Vashu before the trigger fired. "Vashu's down. Is Vashu dead or down? Dead. Dead. I cracked the throat." See [[vashu-the-weeping-veil|Vashu]]'s Session Log and [[ozzeth-the-twiceborn|Ozzeth]]'s Session Log for how it resolved.

> [!dm]
> When the trigger fires, stop and read the next box. This happens mid-action, not between turns.

> [!read-aloud]
> The second-to-last defender goes down against the scaffolding, and for one heartbeat the drainage channels stop producing new bodies to throw at this. Solange's chant doesn't waver, but the circle's light does: it stutters once, steadies, and flares brighter. The small purple Grung with the milk-white eyes and the one whose skin keeps shifting between blue and red exchange no words at all, needing none, and both plant themselves squarely between you and the circle. Solange's hand, still pressed flat to the stone, starts to move.

**Once this trigger fires, the path to [[scene-05-otar-the-foul|Scene 5]] locks.** From this moment, events play out as planned. The Detonation comes next. Only cutting the circle (above) reroutes it. That choice closes now.

> [!dm] Difficulty dials: challenging as written, but PCs will drop and wipes are unlikely.
>
> - Mesmerizing Chirrs (three total; DC 12 WIS, stun): each can chain-disable your casters. Their WIS saves cluster around +2. One chirr lands and pressure mounts. Three in one round shut down your actions. Stagger the recharges.
> - Too hot → the [[otar-the-foul-manifestation|summoning]] trigger fires early: Solange accelerates when her defenders fall. Too cold → Ozzeth steps out of the ward for one round of attacks, telegraphed first.
> - Never field Ozzeth as a standing combatant: his full kit is far beyond this party. Warding is his role. One punitive round is his ceiling.

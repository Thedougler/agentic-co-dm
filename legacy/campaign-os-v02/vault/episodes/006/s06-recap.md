# Session 06: Show Them the Rain

## Recap

[[master-kyzil|Kyzil]] led a rooftop chase across [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] during [[il-gioco-delle-beffe|Il Gioco delle Beffe]]. She countered a water balloon, ducked beneath a flower-and-balloon volley, saw through a boy's fake-drowning trap, and refused to play along.

[[thunk|Thunk]], blackout drunk, ordered 1,000gp of new cannons on ship credit. He signed twice (once upright, once upside down) before vomiting on the order. His old captain's cabin is now a crew break room.

[[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] appeared at the Lothaludi fish market with [[ruk|Ruk]] and [[enzo|Enzo]] at her side. Ruk pinned one prankster mid-attempt; Enzo blocked a chalk-powder attack. When a tomato came flying, Nona caught it and threw it back.

The sewer fight resumed where it froze, with [[solange-barret|Solange]] in a ritual in the Main Chamber and [[vashu-the-weeping-veil|Vashu]] and [[ozzeth-the-twiceborn|Ozzeth]] guarding her. [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] cast [[fire-bolt|Fire Bolt]] and a boosted [[shocking-grasp|Shocking Grasp]], but her Cannon, Ragnetto, fell. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] jumped in and one-shot a purple-caste enforcer with a pistol hit.

[[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] took a strike and called to his patron, heard “Not yet,” then steadied up while coughing seawater. Solange spoke: “I will not have my princesses stopped.” [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] took two of Vashu's Still-Water Strikes, dodged her Tongue Lash twice, and closed: “Dead. Dead. I cracked the throat.”

Ozzeth tried [[dominate-person|Dominate Person]] on Delmar, and the save held with [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]'s Bardic Inspiration behind it. Delmar shot Ozzeth's arm off, and Jean-Claude finished him with a bow shot. Ozzeth's last acts were wholly for Solange: [[misty-step|Misty Step]] and [[greater-invisibility|Greater Invisibility]] to ward her, and a last word as he fell, “do it now.”

[[grappled|Grappled]] and poisoned by Crissdalynn, Solange teleported to Catarina anyway, spoke the word “Agni,” and detonated the chamber ceiling, completing the ritual: Catarina's own grab to stop her came up short. Where Solange had knelt, something else stood up: twelve feet tall, her body already remade. The session ends there, mid-transformation, the fight itself still to come.

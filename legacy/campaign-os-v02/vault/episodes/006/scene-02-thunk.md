> [!dm]

This is the one Ma vignette built to leave a permanent mark on the ship (the "what's real" note in Running It has the full details).

## Opening

> [!read-aloud] Transition
> Word of the whole city chasing down [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] is already crossing Salt Row by the time the [[il-gioco-delle-beffe|Beffa]] finally lets him go for the night. Morning finds the *Uncertainty* still moored in La Vasca's covered basin, [[cobb|Cobb]] already up top with a pry bar, hammer-blows ringing off the old foundation stone before anyone else aboard is awake.

Let the table groan at the hangover premise. Then start with the light:

> [!read-aloud] Waking Up Wrong
> The cradle lamp swings on its chain somewhere above the hold, and its light finds the gap in the hull planking and lands square on your eyes, insistent about it, refusing to let you go back to sleep and nurse the apocalyptic hangover from last night's drinking with Sem and Officer Jean.
>
> The hammocks on either side of yours hang empty on their hooks, canvas swaying in stale air, and it looks like someone threw up in Sem's.
>
> Somewhere above you, hammer meets nail like a thunderclap, and the old foundation stone throws the sound back twice as loud as it has any right to.
>
> Your fist crushes a scrap of paper, ink gone to a blur except one number that survived the night: a 9, then a comma, then nothing.
>
> Cobb's voice comes bright down the companionway, doubled by the same echo.

> [!quote] Cobb — down the companionway
> "Ah, you're up! What a night. Come see what we started!"

## Running It

**The setup.** Last night Thunk went drinking with [[sem-holst|Sem Holst]] and [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] ("Officer Jean") and came home blackout drunk. [[cobb|Cobb]]'s hammering is Thunk waking up to what he apparently approved while he was in no state to approve anything. Run it like a morning-after mystery where every clue incriminates the detective, without leaving the ship: six beats — waking into wrongness, the evidence trail below decks, Cobb's proud tour, the receipt with his own signature, damage control when the gunsmith calls at the quay, and the deck where the full picture lands. **The scene's toy is the order sheet itself.** First as a scrap in Thunk's fist, then whole in Cobb's hands, then in the gunsmith's: the same paper the party can read, question, cover for, or try to renegotiate, and every later branch point hangs off it. The rolls are never about undoing the night; they're about surviving the hangover with his dignity intact while the night reassembles itself around him.

**Beat 1 — Waking Up Wrong.** The Opening read-aloud above is this beat.

> [!check] Constitution — Function Through the Hangover
> DC 10, first thing.
> **Success:** he's upright and thinking.
> **Failure:** disadvantage on his next check this scene, and everything Cobb says arrives half a sentence late — comedy, not punishment.

**Beat 2: The Evidence Trail.** Between the hammock and Cobb lies the whole ship, and the night left tracks. Let Thunk find each clue himself. Answer questions about any of them. Confirm nothing about what they mean.

> [!read-aloud] The Evidence Trail
> The galley meets you halfway up, smelling of cold grease and spilled rum.
>
> Chalk covers the mess table: the *Uncertainty* drawn keel to rail, four gun positions circled twice, firing arcs fanned out across the planks.
>
> Your commendation tin sits open beside the drawing, the ribbon unwrapped from its paper for the first time since [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]].
>
> One of your boots stands upright in the bread locker.
>
> Overhead the hammering pauses, waits, and starts up again, and every blow lands somewhere behind your eyes.

> [!dm] Evidence's true story — confirm nothing, let them piece it together
>
> - **Chalk diagram, gun positions circled twice** — Thunk redrew the aft mounts mid-argument with himself over weight distribution; the second circle is where he landed.
> - **Commendation tin, ribbon unwrapped** — dug out to prove to Cobb, and himself, that a Crown Ordnance officer once trusted his judgment; the credential he waved to close the sale.
> - **Boot upright in the bread locker** — hunting for the scrap of paper below at three in the morning, hid it "somewhere safe" mid-search; remembers neither the hunt nor the hiding.
> - **Scrap of paper, "9," then a blur** — torn off his own copy of the deposit line: 900 gp, committed tonight against the ~1,000 gp order. Proof he was sober enough to engage with the numbers — set it next to the receipt in Beat 4.

> [!check] Constitution — Keep It Down
> DC 12, when the galley smell hits.
> **Success:** he crosses the galley with his dignity.
> **Failure:** one loud dry heave into the salt box; Cobb calls down to ask if he's all right, and by noon the crew has a name for the sound — comedy, not punishment.

**Beat 3 — Come See What We Started.** Cobb gives the tour, cheerful and unprompted (he has nothing to hide and no reason to think he should — an exposition source, not an obstacle).

![[scene-02-thunk-cobb-tour|Adult animated art in the Archer style: crisp vector-like linework, bold ink contours, cel-shaded lighting, poster-style staging, expressive grounded faces, richly saturated adventure palette, Renaissance Age-of-Sail setting. > [!read-aloud] Come See What We Started
> Cobb walks you through it like a proud landlord, past the spare cutlass still on its rack, past the cabin chest and the hidden lockbox you already emptied the week you took her, a caulking mallet swinging loose at his belt with every step.
>
> The captain's cabin door is off its hinges and leaning in the companionway, sawdust and old bilge damp thick in the air, and where the old writing desk used to sit there is a neat stack of its own sawn-off legs.
>
> He picks the pry bar back up and returns to the wall, humming.

> [!quote] Cobb — over his shoulder, when Thunk asks
> "Wow, and you call it a break room?! Four captains, one ship, nobody gets the big bed, everybody gets a hammock and a card table. I've never met an officer who cared more for his crew!"

![[scene-02-thunk-cobb-break-room]]

> [!check] Deception — Nod Like You Remember
> DC 12, while Cobb tours.
> **Success:** Thunk pieces the night together from Cobb's chatter without ever admitting the blank.
> **Failure:** Cobb doesn't suspect a thing, but Thunk's "yes... the break room" comes out as a question, and Cobb happily re-explains the entire plan from the beginning, louder.

**What he volunteers, cheerfully and unprompted:**

- The "break room" is coming along great — Thunk convinced him last night the *Uncertainty* runs four captains with an officers' quarters already covering four beds, making the actual captain's cabin redundant.
- Thunk personally committed **close to 1,000 gp** in new cannon fittings on the ship's credit — two Long Nines, two Short 32-pounders, order already placed.
- Cobb also picked up a proper armchair for the new break room off a liquidation lot near the [[the-bridge|Bridge]], next to nothing, he says, and he's rather pleased with himself about the find (DM: the armchair is the [[elder-mimic|Elder Mimic]] — telegraph only, no secret dropped).

> [!check] Persuasion or Intimidation — Halt the Conversion
> DC 15, without admitting he doesn't remember ordering any of it.
> **Success:** Cobb sets the crowbar down, confused, and stops.
> **Failure:** he keeps working regardless, more amused than deterred.

> [!dm]
> The "four captains" line is Thunk's own drunk fabrication, not a wiki fact — the *Uncertainty* has exactly one captain, [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]], and nothing aboard matching a four-bed officers' quarters: the ship's one officer cabin (aft, [[uncertainty|HCS Surety]]) was already converted to the crew mess in an earlier refit ([[uncertainty-manifest|manifest]] § Crew Mess). Tonight's conversion is the *captain's cabin* — a different room, untouched until now. Cobb has no reason to know better. That gap between what Cobb believes and what's actually true is the whole joke; if a PC tries to correct Cobb, let them — the correction lands, Cobb accepts it, and the comedy dies — but play it out.

**Beat 4 — The Receipt.** Thunk asks who authorized this — no roll needed, Cobb is proud to show him. Slow down and pause before the signature line:

> [!read-aloud] The Receipt
> Still in the gutted companionway, lamplight sliding off the cold iron cradle bolts through the open hull seam, Cobb digs the paperwork out of a biscuit tin, its lid coming free with a screech that goes straight through the headache.
>
> The order sheet comes out rolled, tied with string, surprisingly official. Two Long Nines. Two Short 32-pounders.
>
> Deposit paid on the ship's credit, balance due on delivery, and at the bottom, twice, in handwriting that is unmistakably yours if you'd written it at sea in a storm, your own signature.
>
> One of the two is upside down.

> [!dm]
> The upside-down signature: Cobb spun the sheet around to show him the deposit line before the second signature, and Thunk signed where the pen landed without turning the page back. No deeper mystery; if asked, Cobb remembers it exactly that plainly.

> [!quote] Cobb — gently
> "You insisted. You said one was for the lawyers."

> [!check] Deception — Cover for Himself
> DC 12, before the rest of the crew hears.
> **Success:** a day passes without anyone raising it.
> **Failure:** Sem finds him on the ladder by noon, arms crossed, already knowing every detail twice over.

**Beat 5 — The Gunsmith Calls.** No trip ashore; the yard comes to him. She's delivering the ship's copy of the order in person, and nothing Thunk says changes her schedule.

![[scene-02-thunk-gunsmith|Archer-style animated illustration for adults: clean vector lines, strong ink outlines, cel-shaded light, poster composition, expressive grounded faces, saturated adventure color palette, set in a Renaissance Age-of-Sail world. Do not include: any text, watermarks, logos, gore, photorealistic rendering, anime or chibi style, pixel art, or a stock-photo look.]]

> [!read-aloud] The Gunsmith Calls
> She finds the ship before noon, coming up the quay with a docket under one arm, a stout dwarvish woman with burn-scarred forearms and the patience of somebody who has heard every version of what you are about to say, forge smoke still clinging to her coat.
>
> She doesn't ask permission to board. Her boots land twice on the deck, hard, before anyone's said she could come up, and she sets the order sheet flat on the capstan and smooths it with one hand: the ship's copy, crating started this morning.
>
> At the bottom, stamped in red above your two signatures: NO REFUNDS, big enough to read from the rigging.

> [!quote] The gunsmith — tapping the deposit line
> "Signed twice. You even signed the no-refunds policy."

> [!check] Persuasion — Renegotiate the Order
> DC 14, delivered through the hangover with a straight face.
> **Success:** shaves 10-15% off the price or moves the delivery date.
> **Failure:** the order stands exactly as placed, no refund.

> [!dm]
> **What's real and stays real regardless of what the table does:** the conversion is underway as the scene opens, and the cannons are already committed against a ship running roughly 32 gp/week upkeep (per [[uncertainty|the ship's page]]). Neither reverses tonight — no beat above undoes the conversion or the commission; they shape the edges of the damage, not whether it happened. A real hole in the treasury, a real upgrade to the ship's armament, and an open question of whether [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] makes the party work the debt off, left for a later session.

**Beat 6 — Thursday.** **The ending holds.** No roll changes the beat itself, only what Thunk's said or done by the time it arrives.

> [!read-aloud] Thursday
> Eventually you make it up on deck, into the dim under the basin's stone roof, and the one thing worse than the dark below is the ordinary daylight sitting out past the entrance arch, waiting for you.
>
> The captain's cabin door hangs open behind you on a room half full of crates and raw lumber, the cut-wood smell of it still sharp over the basin's usual bilge damp.
>
> Through the arch, past the canal, a cart creaks toward the dock, hauling the unmistakable shape of a cannon barrel under oilcloth that barely conceals its contents.
>
> Cobb waves from the rail, delighted with himself, and watches your face for a second.

> [!quote] Cobb — delighted with himself
> "Gunsmith says Thursday! You do remember agreeing to Thursday, sì?"

- **How Thunk answers, in front of whoever's on deck to hear it** — own the date now, or let Cobb's version stand uncorrected. Own it: the crew hears the truth once, from him, and it stops growing. Let it stand: Cobb's retelling becomes the only account on the ship, picking up a little more each time it's told.

**What carries forward.** The debt, the upgraded armament, and however much of the crew now knows — the one Ma vignette built to outlive itself.

> [[moretti-and-sons|Moretti & Sons]] takes up forty feet of the [[mercatura|Mercatura]] front, and the smell runs tar first, then hemp, then new canvas. [[giacomo-moretti|Giacomo Moretti]] meets you at the counter with a coil of silk line already in his hands, because the Concordat's boy reached him first. ## What's True

A note sits under the quoted figure in the same neat hand. Nobody ever comes out of that back room: he has no sons and never had any. Every trader on this front lets it alone.

> [!check] Persuasion — The Credit Line
> DC 12, friendly worldview. To bundle six months of provisioning against the Concordat credit instead of the cash line.
> **Success:** roughly 1,400 gp of goods for the 1,000, and he throws in the silk line.
> **Failure:** an honest price, and a long story about canvas grades.

**Oddity.** A disputed crate in his back room carries the factor house's address and has waited five weeks. If ignored, he sends a boy to [[casa-falier|Casa Falier]] with a written quotation, then another, then comes himself.

> [!read-aloud]
> [[cobb|Cobb]]'s note reaches [[casa-falier|Casa Falier]] with the morning bread: *she's ready to look at, tide's right at two.* The unmarked arch under the old tannery takes a flat-bottomed boat with a foot of clearance, and your boatman ducks without comment. Then the Basin opens out, sixty feet of drained stone dock under a plank roof, and the *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]* stands up out of it in her cradle with no water beneath her at all.
>
> She does not look like the ship you brought in. Turpentine and hot pitch hang thick over the wet stone. Somewhere behind the cradle a kettle is going. The brown-black she wore into this dock has vanished. What's on her now is a blue-black so heavy the light will not stand on it anywhere. It's still tacky in the shadow beneath her counter.
>
> [[cobb|Cobb]] is halfway down the cradle ladder with a rag over one shoulder and his hands black to the wrist, and he has been waiting since the tide turned for somebody to come and look.

## What's True

Cobb walks the crew bow-first past what's new this round: a blue-black recoat that kills her silhouette by night, fresh caulk paid and hardened the full length, a cabin conversion aft (long table, bulkhead bench, one armchair, and Cobb mentions unprompted that it "came from a house in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] that was not using it"), and the ram plate rebedded flush at the waterline. Older work from the [[whip-shark|Whip Shark]] refit stands untouched too: the carved figurehead and the melted registry plate. The shallow-keel work still holds as well, the change that dropped her draw from eight feet to four.

**Three days is the fixed answer, and it holds.** Nothing the crew does shortens it. Cobb will not put an unhardened hull in salt water for anybody.

> [!check] Persuasion or Insight — Cobb's Pride
> DC 10, friendly worldview. Any PC who tells him plainly that the work is good.
> **Success:** he stops managing the tour and starts showing off, which opens the keel conversation without a check.
> **Failure:** he thanks them politely and stays on script.

> [!quote] Cobb, on the paint
> "Lampblack and one other thing I'm not telling you, and it took three days to stop being tacky. Say two more days for the last of it, then one to flood the cradle and float her off. Three, call it three, or you'd carry my thumbprints out on the strakes."

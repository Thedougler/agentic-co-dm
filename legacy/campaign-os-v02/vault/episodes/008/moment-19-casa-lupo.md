> [!read-aloud]
> [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]] holds general goods floor to ceiling with no order anybody outside the family could reconstruct. No price appears anywhere in it. Beeswax and dry paper hang over a cat asleep on a bolt of sailcloth near the door. [[ettore-lupo|Ettore Lupo]] names your price without pausing. Then he tells you the last time somebody bought that same item off him, adding the buyer's name and how long ago. The year lands somewhere before your birth. Forty years without writing a price down. Never once wrong.

## What's True

This is one of tonight's [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] stops, run in whatever order the crew wants against whatever else the [[dm-screen|dm-screen]] lists. On the way past, Ettore prices [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s coat too — the dye, the cut, the year of it, spoken out loud, save one number he keeps behind his teeth — and makes no move to stop anyone looking at the curtained doorway into his back room. He wants nothing, the only merchant in [[calveno|Calveno]] who doesn't, which unsettles everyone: for Delmar, a man who remembers every transaction in Calveno may remember a scarlet coat, and for [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] that same memory reads as a threat. Ignored, the ledger stays shut, and so does whatever it says about them.

In the drawer, if asked for something he does not stock: [[bosuns-reach|Bosun's Reach]] at 100 gp (anchors a 60-foot climbing line at bow range), [[the-fishwife|The Fishwife]] at 150 gp (screams for a minute on landing), [[the-chimney|The Chimney]] at 150 gp (a 15-foot ball of blinding smoke), [[lamplighter|Lamplighter]] at 200 gp (marks a target with an hour of cold glow), [[the-cast|The Cast]] at 200 gp (a net delivered across a canal). He names each price from memory, and the last buyer who took it.

> [!check] Persuasion — The Back Room
> DC 15, to see the ledger.
> **Success:** he hands it over readily. Every sale in this shop since he turned nineteen.
> **Failure:** he changes the subject to the price of lamp oil in 1447.

> [!read-aloud]
> [[cobb|Cobb]] wants the hold empty by tomorrow, because the caulkers work the ceiling from the inside and a full hold means a bad job on the garboard. The dock smells of wet rope and drying tar. Crates already stand along the boards in the order they came off her. Each crate carries a chalked number and somebody's initial. A Black-Jaw lad reads down the line from a slate calling every label out loud. That is how the Run keeps a tally honest.
>
> He talks while he works. The mole watch counted three hulls out, his cousin swears five, and somebody has gone round every Black-Jaw yard on this water asking quietly who is free for a long run south. He is four crates from the one with twenty stoppered vials packed in straw.

## What's True

Nobody aboard knows what the vials are. The tally is public by design, and the lad will read the contents of every crate aloud to the dock. The vials are [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s tincture, and only Jean-Claude's player knows it.

> [!check] Deception, Sleight of Hand, or Persuasion — The Fourth Crate
> DC 13, for whoever wants that crate off the line before the lad reaches it. Persuasion instead, DC 15, to have it carried straight to [[casa-falier|Casa Falier]] unopened on the crew's own authority.
> **Success:** the crate never gets read out, and the tally closes without it.
> **Failure:** the lad reads the label and holds a vial up to the shed light. He asks whose medicine this is, and Cobb hears the question.

> [!dm]
> The lad reads the fourth crate out unless somebody stops him, so inaction here resolves as exposure. Any PC may act on the crate, not only Jean-Claude.

> [!quote] Cobb — if the vial comes up
> "That's not ours. I'd know. I loaded this ship myself."

**The ending is open.** Cobb's three-day answer holds, and the crew leave the Basin with a working ship, a thousand in Concordat outfitting credit, a soft five feet of keel, a cannon bill, and whatever they decided about the fourth crate.

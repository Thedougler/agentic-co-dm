# Session 08: The Paint Is Still Wet

## Recap

The crew climbed out of the [[calveno|Calveno]] crater as its Defenders. [[iacopo-fieschi|Fieschi]] confessed his [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] debt in public and paid for it: a thousand gold in trade credit, [[casa-falier|Casa Falier]] itself, and a diamond ring.

The ink was barely dry on the deal when [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s Sending Stone warmed. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s voice came through: come home. Something dead had hit her too.

The Mercatura's own crater sat cordoned off. Nona had turned it into a missing-persons desk, a borrowed table stacked with a list that wouldn't stop growing, past 314 names by the time the queue thinned, every one a fighting-age man.

[[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] read the pattern as slaving weakening a fighting force by proxy, and pointed to [[grung|Grung]] poison already turning up on ships that traded with the raiders.

Nona dispatched two ships from [[the-passage|The Passage]]'s own fleet after the trail, days behind, and stood before the grieving crowd to vow it aloud: "The Passage stands with the citizens of Calveno. I vow to you, I will assemble a crew and make chase." She gave Perrin a favor instead of a cash share, and word that [[cobb|Cobb]]'s refit on their ship would be ready in twelve hours.

Reward gold in pocket, the crew went shopping. On the way, they fished a capsized gondolier, [[paolo|Paolo]], out of the canal near the [[mercatura|Mercatura]].

At [[cabinet-of-morsani|the Cabinet of Morsani]], [[prospero-morsani|Prospero Morsani]] talked up his trick-arrow quivers and a wrist-shield that unfurled so fast the crew nicknamed it [[the-snap|The Snap]] on sight. [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] closed the sale with a threat to flood his market with copies of it.

She'd tried the shop's dragonhide belt on herself first and put Morsani on his back with the first punch. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] lifted a torn page off his shelves, unnoticed. It named a monk who left the Sentinels during their schism and was never heard from again: [[osset|Osset]].

When [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] read Morsani's real source for The Snap, a corpse, probably, and said so to his face, the shop answered for him. A hidden gust-trap threw her out the door and twenty feet down the street.

[[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] gave up two more threads. At [[la-cenere|La Cenere]], [[lavinia-sordi|Lavinia Sordi]] sold Crissdalynn a cloak she called a simple [[cloak-of-protection|Cloak of Protection]]. Identify made it a cursed [[cloak-of-displacement|Cloak of Displacement]] instead. Lavinia said she'd gotten it from a falcon-featured man who also called himself Osset, a name Crissdalynn has never heard from anyone at the Eyrie.

Down the canal walk, an armless goblin, [[zort|Zort]], ran the animal pits. He offered his Mid-Chain reptile contact in trade for a prosthetic leg, and Catarina agreed to build him one.

Osset's name is loose in two mouths now, on two sides of a schism nobody in the crew had heard named before tonight. The raiders already have days of head start on the two ships giving chase.

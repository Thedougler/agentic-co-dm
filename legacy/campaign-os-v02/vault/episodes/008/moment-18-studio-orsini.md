> [!read-aloud]
> [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]] opens off a side canal that appears on no official map of the city, behind a door with no name on it. Vinegar and hot copper hang in the air, and something underneath catches at the back of your throat. One shelf runs the length of the room, packed with stoppered jars, and every jar carries a written name. They are client names. These are the batches that failed. [[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]] works with her back to you and does not turn around. Partway through whatever she is doing, she says to the shelf: "Not now, Ludovico." Her order book lies open on the bench between you and her, and she has not thought to close it.

## What's True

On the shelf: [[potion-of-healing-common|Potion of Healing]] and [[antitoxin|Antitoxin]] at 50 gp, [[alchemists-fire|Alchemist's Fire]] at 52 gp, plus ask-price exotics including Bile of the Drowned and [[oil-of-still-water|Oil of Still Water]]. Marta pays 200 gp for the name of whoever resells her rejects under [[the-bridge|the Bridge]].

> [!check] Investigation — The Order Book
> DC 13, on the ledger she leaves open on the bench.
> **Success:** two of her last three Bile of the Drowned orders went to one notary's address in upper [[mercatura|Mercatura]].
> **Failure:** a working alchemist's handwriting, and no time to read it twice.

> [!dm]
> If ignored, she works it out herself inside a fortnight.

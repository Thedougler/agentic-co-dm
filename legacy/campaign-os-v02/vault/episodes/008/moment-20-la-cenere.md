> [!read-aloud]
> [[la-cenere|La Cenere]] sits at the blind end of a canal spur with no through traffic, which is the point of the address. Cold stone and lamp oil fill the quiet of a room nobody walks past. [[lavinia-sordi|Lavinia Sordi]] is already wearing the thing you came to buy when you walk in. She has had it on a while. She takes it off in front of you and hands it across still warm. That's the appraisal. It doubles as a service. The rack behind her runs longer than the room deserves.
> *Lavinia*: "Buying or selling?"

## What's True

This is one of tonight's [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] stops, run whenever the crew wants it and in any order against whatever else the [[dm-screen|dm-screen]] lists. On the rack: Nightmantle at 120 gp, Blade of the Lost Grip at 180 gp, an off-colour [[pearl-of-power|Pearl of Power]] at 350 gp, a used [[ring-of-mind-shielding|Ring of Mind Shielding]] at 200 gp, and Salvaged Plate Greaves at 75 gp. Lavinia buys at 40, 25, or 15 percent, by provenance, and wore the Ring of Mind Shielding once before taking it off faster than she takes anything else off. If the party doesn't press on where the stock came from, the Red Lady haul sells through to somebody else inside the week.

**The ask:** Lavinia wants a straight answer — buying or selling — asked flat and direct, no preamble, no stated cost or deadline behind it.

> [!check] Investigation — Provenance
> DC 14, on the rack as a whole.
> **Success:** roughly a third of it came up off the [[red-lady|Red Lady]] salvage.
> **Failure:** good stock, and a shopkeeper who won't discuss it.

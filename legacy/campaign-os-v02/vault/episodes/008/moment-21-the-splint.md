> [!read-aloud]
> The way down into [[warren|the Warren]] goes through a cellar hatch and forty feet of brick. The air changes temperature halfway. [[the-splint|The Splint]] runs two tables in one low room, and somebody sits at each. Boiled linen and brine-sage hang over blood that somebody has already mopped once today. [[colla|Colla]] works with her back to the door and does not look up when it opens. She has never looked up when it opens. The people on her tables did this to each other. She treats them together, in the same room, by policy. In her whole career nobody has asked her to stop. A wide gap sits on her shelf where the brine-sage bundles should be. The man on the near table has an opinion about whose paperwork put it there. He says the word Dravosi the way you say a bill.

## What's True

Colla wants brine-sage at 30 gp the bundle, and a Dravosi trade restriction has choked her supply. On the shelf: healer's kit at 5 gp, antitoxin at 50 gp, healing potion at 45 gp. Kin pay nothing, dry folk pay 5 sp.

> [!check] Persuasion — Who Came Through
> DC 12, about the night of the raid.
> **Success:** a list of who went down there and never reported it.
> **Failure:** she treats their hands and tells them nothing.

> [!dm]
> If ignored, her last bundle goes in eleven days.

> [!read-aloud]
> The lanterns over the [[velo-quarter|Velo Quarter]] square burn red, then gold, then a blue no flame holds, and the curtain across [[il-preludio|Il Preludio]]'s door sweeps itself aside before anybody touches it. The room behind it applauds. Real hands, out of sight, clapping you in. Inside, the room smells of rosin and hot brass. [[fioravante-selmerin|Fio]] comes down a stepladder mid-sentence about lamp oil, one open hand rising to his shoulder on the last word and staying up. [[serena-cottle|Serena Cottle]] takes that same word a fourth higher, arms wide. [[tam-slim-nail|Tam]] reads off a ledger without looking up. [[cecco-trivani|Cecco]] draws breath by the chime-birds and gets three syllables out before the door bell takes the rest. Not one of them sings. Every one of them is one breath from it. The window holds two pieces and nothing else, a small glass bell on a stand and two feet of velvet on a rod. Fio has the bell out already, held toward you, eyebrows up, waiting on the answer to a question he has not asked.

## What's True

On the shelf, the only two pieces in the building a person can use (the rest is scenery, per [[il-preludio|Il Preludio]]'s own stock):

![[il-preludio#Inventory]]

Fio's whole trade is getting the object into a browser's hands before they finish deciding.

> [!check] Persuasion — The Window Pair
> DC 13, friendly worldview. A story told back to Fio about what the thing is for counts instead of the roll.
> **Success:** the pair goes together for 400 gp, and he demonstrates both twice.
> **Failure:** 250 gp and 200 gp separately, list price, and he demonstrates both twice anyway.

> [!dm]
> If ignored, Fio finishes dressing the window at last, and the pair goes to whoever stops next.

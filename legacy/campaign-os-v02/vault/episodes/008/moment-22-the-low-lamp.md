> [!dm]
> The night's last dockside stop. Runs whenever the crew want it, in any order against whatever else the [[dm-screen|dm-screen]] lists. It's the natural place to close the session once the crew run out of appetite for the crawl.

> [!read-aloud]
> [[low-lamp|The Low Lamp]] takes the widest chamber [[warren|the Warren]] has, and tonight it holds about forty people who all stop talking when you come down the steps. Marsh ale and wet fur hang over crab shells going soft in a pot somewhere behind the bar. [[essa-two-tooth|Essa Two-Tooth]] has two teeth left and she has named both of them. She introduces them to you before she takes your order in the tone another publican saves for a wine list. The room starts talking again louder than before. These are the people whose five hundred got counted this week. They know exactly who you are. Behind the bar a sealed message shard sits unclaimed. Eight days now.

## What's True

At the far table somebody tells it the way the Warren tells it: one big hull and two proas, and the proas came back empty. The same voice has an old tortle asking after [[nona-black-jaw|Black-Jaw]] at the outer quay two nights running, with a [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] patrol working the same water a street behind him. That's [[anzolo|Anzolo]], still inbound, carrying no word that Nona called off the hunt for [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]].

On the table: marsh ale at 3 cp, cider at 5 cp, salt-fish porridge at 2 cp, boiled crab at 6 cp, an alcove for 2 sp.

> [!check] Persuasion — The Room's Goodwill
> DC 10, in a house where the crew are the reason five hundred names got counted at all.
> **Success:** the room talks, and keeps talking.
> **Failure:** the room drinks, politely.

> [!dm]
> The message shard's contents are deliberately unwritten. Fill it with whatever the table has earned by the time somebody asks, or leave it sealed for a later session, the same as the sealed letter at [[ponte-bassa|Ponte Bassa]]. If ignored, somebody else opens the shard.

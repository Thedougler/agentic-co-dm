> [[carpenters-slip|Carpenters' Slip]] backs hard onto [[the-arsenal|the Arsenal]] wall, and the slip itself runs thirty feet of open timber staging with a hull up on it. Sawdust and hot glue sit over the wet-rope smell of the canal. [[savia-brentino|Savia Brentino]] has a chisel in one hand and a chip of somebody's keel in her mouth. She works it around and thinks about it before reading off the yard that laid the timber, along with roughly the year. Then she spits it into the water and apologises for spitting. She wipes her chin.
>
> *Savia*: Free look at any hull. Double if you're in a hurry. Which are you?

## What's True

Cutting out a soft five feet of keel wants hardwood stock and a day of dry-dock. Off the books, Savia also re-silhouettes hulls at 220 gp plus a Tangle favour plus five days, which needs a [[warren|Warren]] vouch. She holds opinions about Dravosi caulking and shares them where the topic never came up.

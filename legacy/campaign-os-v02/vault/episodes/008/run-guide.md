## Session Goals

The table earned last session's climax, a dead threat under the crater and a public debt admitted out loud. Tonight pays that off in texture instead of danger. The crew get a number they can watch grow. They get a ship they can walk around and touch. They get a city that has decided who they are.

Nona's tally table turns grief into bookkeeping instead of a speech. Cobb's proud tour of the refit hides one thing he's too new to have raised on his own. The shopkeeper crawl gives every proprietor one committed physical gimmick and lets that carry the scene.

Read the table. Some want problem-solving, some want the social game, some just want to watch paint dry on their own ship. Let that decide how many crawl stops actually run.

## Active Objectives

- [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]]'s chase: she vows in Moment 3 to run down the hull that took five hundred people. Live tonight because her chase and [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s private [[warren-aruhe-survey|Aruhe survey]] commission now sit in the same water.
- [[cobb|Cobb]]'s Basin refit: the ship floats in three days no matter what the crew do, and a soft keel and a live cannon debt both sit inside that window unaddressed. Live tonight because Moments 4-7 are the one chance to catch both before they become the DM's loaded gun at sea.
- [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]'s guaranteed beat: [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s hand behind the Grung deaths (Moment 2) and her tincture crate (Moment 7) both surface tonight either way.

## NPCs

**[[samuel|Samuel]]**
**Wants:** somebody to tell him plainly what the job actually is.
**The ask:** direct — he blurts out that he's carrying messages from Iacopo and needs to know who to hand them to.
**Voice:** tentative, apologetic, asks permission before acting rather than guessing.
**Mannerism:** fidgets with the pocket of turtle wax he uses on his beak every morning.
**Opening:** waiting at [[kats-curios|Kat's Curios]] with a stack of letters he doesn't know how to deliver · "I have messages from Iacopo, who should I give these to?"

**[[paulo|Paulo]]**
**Wants:** to become the gondolier everyone asks for, not the one they laugh at.
**The ask:** direct — he wants a fare, and he wants it to go somewhere on time.
**Voice:** loud, announcer-bright, narrates his own turns out loud even when nobody's listening.
**Mannerism:** poles too hard against a current that isn't there, correcting with total, misplaced certainty.
**Opening:** hailing a canal-side crowd like he's docking a warship · "You made it! I mean, I never doubted the route for a second."

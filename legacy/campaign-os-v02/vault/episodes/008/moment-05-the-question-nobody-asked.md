> [!read-aloud]
> Aft of the cradle ladder, [[sem-holst|Sem Holst]] has a caulking iron in one hand and a mug going cold on a barrelhead beside him, and he has been within earshot since you arrived. He nods when you catch his eye. He does not come over.
>
> Where Cobb's voice carries the length of the shed, Sem's stops about four feet out, which is how a man talks when he has decided who gets to hear him. He taps the caulking iron twice against the forefoot, then looks at you instead of at Cobb, and waits.

## What's True

Sem found a soft note under the blade eleven days ago, working the forward keel, a timber gone spongy under fresh caulk that some Dravosi yard laid over the top of it instead of cutting it out. He told nobody, because a carpenter who announces a problem the week of his own promotion announces something about himself instead.

> [!check] Insight — Something Held Back
> DC 12, watching Sem instead of Cobb.
> **Success:** he has been waiting to get asked all morning and has arranged himself so that asking stays easy.
> **Failure:** he reads as a quiet man doing quiet work.

> [!check] Investigation — Sounding the Forefoot
> DC 13, for a PC who goes over the hull with a knuckle or an iron.
> **Success:** the forward keel gives back a soft note where the rest of her gives a hard one. The soft patch runs about five feet, under caulk laid down long before Cobb touched her.
> **Failure:** she sounds sound, and Sem watches them miss it without correcting them.

**Ask Sem anything straight and he answers straight.** The fix wants hardwood stock, a full day of dry-dock, and a carpenter who knows the shape. [[savia-brentino|Savia Brentino]] at [[carpenters-slip|Carpenters' Slip]] knows the shape and looks at any hull free.

> [!dm]
> **If ignored.** The timber holds in harbour and in fair weather. It gives on the first hard beat to windward past [[calveno|Calveno]], the DM's loaded gun, not a scheduled event. Leaving it be is a legitimate choice with a stated cost.

> [!quote] Sem Holst, if asked
> "Aye, eleven days now. It's not a hole. It's a soft five foot of keel with somebody else's caulk over the top of it, and I'd sooner cut it out here than off a lee shore."

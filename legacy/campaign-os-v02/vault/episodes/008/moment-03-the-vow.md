> [!read-aloud]
> By evening the pile has stopped growing, and it has stopped for the plainest reason there is: every household that was going to report somebody already has.
>
> [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] sets the pen down and leaves it down. She crosses to the crater's rim with [[enzo|Enzo]] and [[ruk|Ruk]] flanking her and passes bread and dried fish hand to hand to whoever is still standing near the rubble, thirty or forty people, most of them not there for the food.
>
> When the basket empties she stops where the whole rim can see her and waits for the talking to die out. Somewhere behind you a rope creaks against a bollard, and the crater's cold wet-stone smell sits under everything. She is looking at the five of you when the talking finally stops.
>
> *"I know what this is: to look for someone who was here a minute ago and find nothing, the way my people have for decades. Today it happened to five hundred of yours. The Passage stands with the citizens of Calveno, and I will assemble a crew and make chase."*

## What's True

Dark-hulled [[vethka|Vethka]], three of them, unmarked, cleared the mole a full day before anybody thought to look for boats.

**Nona's answers, if pressed.**

- Three hulls, a full day of lead already spent.
- The proas that carried people ashore hold nowhere near five hundred, so she is chasing the one hull with room.
- A keel in reef water gives up too much chasing proas through it.
- The big hull needs to water and feed what it took, and every place that can happen has Passage people in it.

**The vow fixes the ending,** though everywhere else stays open. Nona commits, and five hundred people stay missing. How the crew answers her is theirs. A PC can join the crew outright. She takes it plainly and moves straight to logistics. Pushing back earns a full hearing too. No roll moves her off the vow though a concrete heading or a faster hull still shapes the chase she runs. She may turn the argument back with one question instead: who goes instead if not them? Anyone who says nothing simply watches her return to the table where a woman is still waiting to give a name.

> [!read-aloud]
> [[acqua-nera|Acqua Nera]] sits in the outer harbour southwest of [[the-arsenal|the Arsenal]], where the water stops being canal and starts being sea. [[dario-vanni|Dario Vanni]] has a hull up on trestles and eleven pots open around him. He is mixing by weight and by nose, his eyes on the middle distance.
>
> *Dario*: I can't see it. Colour. Never could. My greys are the best in [[verdant-scatter|the Scatter]] for it. Nothing about how they look has ever once distracted me.
>
> He wipes his hands and looks at nothing. He mentions the morning of the crater: three hulls came past the point before dawn, running dark. He could not tell you their colour if you paid him. Then he waits for the crew to say what they want done to the ship.

## What's True

Dario has painted the *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]*'s hull class before, twice, for other people.

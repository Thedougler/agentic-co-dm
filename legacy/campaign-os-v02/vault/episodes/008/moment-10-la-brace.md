## What's True

Rufio never touches his own stock; the pocketbook is the whole point of the shop, not the smoke. The Concordat has asked twice for his client list, and he has declined twice, courteously.

> [!check] Persuasion — Sitting for the Pocketbook
> DC 13, to get a curtained booth free in exchange for the sitting.
> **Success:** the booth costs nothing, and he sketches the whole table.
> **Failure:** full price, 15 gp the hour, and he sketches the table anyway.

> [!dm]
> If ignored, the third asking will not stay courteous.

> [!read-aloud]
> The lantern outside [[cabinet-of-morsani|the Cabinet]] is lit, which is the only notice [[prospero-morsani|Prospero Morsani]] gives that he is open. Dust and brass polish sit over something older underneath. The shelves run back further than the frontage suggests. He has rings on every finger and both thumbs, thirty-one of them. He will tell you the number before you ask. Each one stands for an object on these shelves he has failed to sell. He picks up a salt lantern and starts on its previous owner at length. A locked case rests at the back with nothing on top of it.

## What's True

On the shelf. Compass of the Drowned at 400 gp, Ringmaker's Regret at 250 gp, The Uncharted Coast at 180 gp, Salt Lantern at 80 gp, Preserved Specimen at 90 gp.

> [!check] Persuasion — The Locked Case
> DC 14, or a truly good story told back to him instead of a roll.
> **Success:** the case opens on a thirty-second ring with nothing attached to it yet.
> **Failure:** he laughs and moves the crew along the shelf.

> [!dm]
> If ignored, the count goes to thirty-two.

> [!read-aloud]
> The water-door of [[casa-falier|Casa Falier]] opens straight off the canal, and the hall behind it runs forty feet back to a stair. Somebody has left bread on the step with no note on it. A shutter opens the width of a face two doors down and shuts again. Chalk dust and lemon oil hang in the air, and no cooking smell lingers anywhere in the building, because the range went out the door with everything else. The previous owner took the furniture, the sconces, and every interior door in the house. From where you stand you can see through three rooms into a fourth. One chair sits in the middle of the largest ground-floor room. It faces a blank stretch of wall.

## What's True

A pair of household staff came with the deed, unnamed and unmet. A base with no doors holds no privacy in it either, a fact the crew will work out for themselves once they start assigning rooms.

> [!check] Investigation — The Chair
> DC 12, on why one chair faces a blank wall.
> **Success:** the wall carries a painted-over doorway, plastered flush and finished badly. The chair faces square where the handle used to be.
> **Failure:** an old chair in an old room.

> [!dm]
> If ignored, the neighbours start asking the Council who owns the house.

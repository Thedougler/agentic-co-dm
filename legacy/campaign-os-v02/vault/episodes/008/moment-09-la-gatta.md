> [!read-aloud]
> Under the bottom tier of [[the-bridge|the Bridge]], where the arch throws everything into shade, a halfling stands with a full vendor's board strapped across his chest. [[nicco-kettley|Nicco Kettley]] cannot sit down in that rig, and has not, in trading hours, for nine years. He tells you so cheerfully, then tells you again. The board smells faintly of scorched paper. Rows of stoppered tubes clip to it in grades, and the top row costs sixty percent of what the same thing costs in the [[velo-quarter|Velo Quarter]]. "Seconds," he says, tapping the row. "Perfectly good seconds. Roll a die on the dud, that's fair. I've never said otherwise."

## What's True

On the board: [[the-boomerang|The Boomerang]] at 60 gp, [[hunters-blend|Hunter's Blend]] and [[pocket-thunder|Pocket Thunder]] at 90 gp each, a d6 dud chance on every sale. Nicco will trade the whole board's contents for a chair he can carry.

> [!check] Insight — At Marta's Name
> DC 12, the moment anybody says it out loud.
> **Success:** he goes stiff behind the board and starts talking faster.
> **Failure:** a cheerful halfling selling smoke.

> [!dm]
> If ignored, [[marta-orsini|Marta]] finds him first.

> [!read-aloud]
> The factor's door closes behind you with the softness of a door that costs money, and the stair puts you back out on the [[mercatura|Mercatura]] at the hour when the canal and the sky are the same colour.
>
> The sending stone at [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s collar warms before anyone has taken a full breath of outside air, and [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s voice arrives inside your skulls without passing your ears.
>
> *"Come home soon. Something bad has happened. Many, many people are missing. Hundreds."*
>
> Twenty-five words, no adjectives, and the last one lands a half-beat behind the rest, the way a number lands on somebody who has already counted it and does not like the count.
>
> [[samuel|Samuel]] is on the step behind you with his beak freshly waxed and a satchel of the factor's morning correspondence. He has stopped walking, because none of you have moved.
>
> The walk to the crater puts you at a table you have not seen before. The crater sits cordoned at one edge of the Mercatura behind rope and two trestles, the way a city fences off a thing it has decided not to look at directly, and the stalls forty feet from the rope have opened for business as usual. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] has a borrowed table up against the cordon with a stack of old receipts squared blank-side-up in front of her, and a jar of ink she has not capped since sunrise. The queue is nine or ten people long and it never gets shorter. Under the ordinary noise of a working market there is a second sound at this table: the sound of nobody talking.

## What's True

The city's night ended differently than the crew's, and the sum of the difference gets read aloud today, in public, by somebody who does not perform grief. Nona has not asked the crew for anything and has not had a free hand since sunrise. The count is the toy, an ordinary stack of receipts anyone can add to or dispute, running 200 at midday, ~400 an hour on, and stopping at 500 by evening.

> [!check] Investigation or Persuasion — Canvassing the Count
> DC 13. Investigation to verify names door to door through the districts. Persuasion to get a household to admit somebody is missing instead of hoping they turn up embarrassed.
> **Success:** three more households come forward who had been sitting on it. The number moves faster and cleaner for the help.
> **Failure:** the count still moves, only slower. One name goes down twice before Nona notices the duplicate and strikes it.

> [!check] Insight — Reading Nona
> DC 12, for anyone watching her work instead of helping.
> **Success:** she is not performing anything. Every name gets the same flat attention, and that discipline is exactly why the crew can trust the number.
> **Failure:** nothing past the obvious. [[exhaustion|Exhaustion]] shows on her, and the pile keeps growing.

> [!dm] If They're Stuck
>
> - **Free tell:** a woman at the front of the queue gives the same surname to two different people and never explains why.
> - **Costed nudge:** [[enzo|Enzo]] hands somebody a spare pen without asking, which is Enzo's entire way of asking.
> - **Bail-out:** [[samuel|Samuel]] offers to copy the pile into a fair hand. The offer works.

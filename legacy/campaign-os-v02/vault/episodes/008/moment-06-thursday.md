> [!read-aloud]
> [[cobb|Cobb]] has a delivery chit hanging on a nail by the shed door, and he mentions it the way a boy mentions a thing he assumes you already know about. A saw rasps somewhere behind the cradle, loud enough that Cobb raises his voice over it. The shed still carries the hot-pitch smell from yesterday's caulking.
>
> Cannon arrive Thursday: two Long Nines and two short thirty-two pounders, against the ship's credit at the Black-Jaw account, signed for in a hand recognisably [[thunk|Thunk]]'s and recognisably nothing else. The chit says a thousand gold, near enough. It does not say who authorised it.
>
> Cobb has not raised this with anybody, and until this moment he assumed one of you had. He is holding the chit out to you now, waiting for somebody to take it or tell him where it goes.

## What's True

The debt is live and [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] has not mentioned it, because she has spent the week writing five hundred names. Arriving at a subject on her own schedule is one of the things she does well.

> [!check] Persuasion — Cobb, About the Chit
> DC 12, rising to 15 if a PC asks him to keep it off the ledger.
> **Success:** he holds the chit back one week, which buys the crew time and costs Cobb something he will not name.
> **Failure:** he apologises and files it. The Basin's books go up the chain, and he is not the one who decides that.

> [!dm] If They're Stuck
>
> - **Free tell.** Cobb reads the signature aloud, slowly, to see whether anybody claims it.
> - **Costed nudge:** the crew can settle the thousand out of the Concordat credit, and Cobb will happily book it that way.
> - **Bail-out:** [[samuel|Samuel]] arrives with the factor's paperwork and offers to reconcile the two accounts, resolving the money and putting a Concordat clerk inside the Black-Jaw books.

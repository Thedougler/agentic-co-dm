## Scene Breakdown

### Scene 01 — Perrin Solo: Auralis Dream

- [x] Ran

> [!read-aloud]
> Perrin, the rail is cold under your hands, the storm thinning to hard sideways rain while the Surety groans at anchor beneath you. The water below gives nothing back. For one breath, the black surface seems to hold still, and then the world drops away. Your eyes go white.

**Use:** Perrin-only warning, Auralis contact, weak-point clue. Keep it under 30 minutes. Ask the sensory questions from the scene file, let Perrin decide what he shares, then cut straight to impact.

**Open:** [[scene-01-auralis|Scene 01 — Auralis]]; [[auralis|Auralis]] (mentioned in [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]], [[leviathan|Leviathan]], [[elemental-plane-of-water|Elemental Plane of Water]], [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]], [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]]); Maw Pearl Crisis — W9-near-miss, this situation's facts already live on [[umberlee|Umberlee]] under a different name ("Pearl Summons"), corroborated by actual play; [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]]

### Scene 02 — All Hands: The Shark

- [x] Ran

> [!read-aloud]
> The rain has stopped. A low boom moves through the hull from somewhere below the waterline, then again, heavier. The ship rocks and doesn't settle right, and from below you can hear Sem moving fast, boots already on the ladder. Then the hull takes a third hit, harder than the first two, and the lanterns in the companionway swing hard to port and stay there.

**Use:** physical ship crisis, not a set piece. Starts with probing hits — players can act before the hook seats. Run the loop from the scene file. The joint is the clean answer but it's below the waterline; any plausible shipboard plan gets a roll and a consequence.

**Open:** [[scene-02-the-shark|Scene 02 — The Shark]]; [[whip-shark|Whip Shark]] (the trophy item is [[puntura|Whip Shark Barb]]); [[uncertainty|HCS Surety]]; [[sem-holst|Sem Holst]]; [[old-faas|Old Faas]]; [[thunk|Thunk]]

### Scene 03 — Aftermath

- [x] Ran

> [!read-aloud]
> Grey morning finds the deck slick with salt and blood, the port side quiet now except for water tapping somewhere below. Sem speaks lower than he did during the fight, each word measured against the sound of the hull taking water. The ship is moving again, but every timber seems to remember where the hook went in.

**Use:** one breath after danger, not a second logistics scene. Sem gives the dry-dock requirement. Grigori can heal too quickly and leave it unaddressed. Then get the ship moving toward Calveno.

**Open:** [[scene-03-aftermath|Scene 03 — Aftermath]]; [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] (DM-only companion content already absorbed into this page's DM Only + [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]]'s location facts — no separate "Shepherd Grigori — DM" page); [[uncertainty|HCS Surety]]

### Scene 04 — At Sea: The Whale

- [x] Ran

> [!read-aloud]
> A bow wave rises ahead of the hull with no ship to make it. Something moves beneath the surface, its outline traced in bioluminescent light: a shape longer than the Surety, its breath rising in slow columns of foam that dissolve before you can fully look at them. It passes two hundred feet out. It does not surface. It does not acknowledge the ship. The water behind it settles as if nothing happened.

**Use:** protected Perrin vignette. Do not explain the creature. Let Perrin connect it to the dream if he wants to. If he is not on watch, have someone call him up in time to see it.

**Open:** [[scene-04-five-days|Scene 04 — At Sea — The Whale]]; [[scene-01-auralis|Scene 01 — Auralis]]; [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]]

### Scene 05 — Calveno Approach

- [x] Ran

> [!read-aloud]
> Calveno arrives in layers: salt, coal smoke, bells, and the thick forest of masts along the waterfront. White stone and red tile rise behind the harbour, bright with festival cloth and busy with boats moving in every direction at once. Then the useful beauty of it narrows to one problem: you are steering a damaged Crown cutter into a city that knows exactly what Crown cutters are supposed to look like.

**Use:** let the party try their own entry plan, then surface the dry-dock constraint. Uniforms can bluff an approach. Perrin's name solves the real problem and alerts Nona. Do not roll for established Black-Jaw access once Perrin commits.

### La Vasca Handoff — Open Calveno

- [x] Ran

> [!read-aloud]
> The low stone arch swallows the harbour noise behind you. Inside La Vasca, the water is darker and stiller, the air close with pitch, sawdust, and wet stone. Yard hands take lines without asking names. The Surety slides into the covered basin, hidden at last, while Cobb watches Perrin with the careful expression of someone deciding how much warning counts as kindness.

**Use:** stop pushing scene order once the ship is safe. Sem owns repairs. Cobb warns Perrin. Grigori leaves alone. Ask the table what matters first: ship, family, supplies, privacy, worship, rumours, money, or the city.

### Scene 06 — Calveno Day One: Branca

- [x] Ran

> [!read-aloud]
> The city keeps moving around Delmar: canal bells, market voices, the soft slap of water against stone. Then a woman in crisp blue-grey Waveservant robes steps out of the flow as if she has been following a tide chart only she can read. Her salt-white hair is pulled back tight, a leather booklet is already open in her hand, and when she looks up at Fisk, her smile is professional, warm, and entirely prepared.

**Use:** Delmar's pressure, not a group quest marker. The basin water can recognize him if he watches it. Branca wants the shrine appointment today if possible. If he refuses, she simply finds another slot.

**Open:** [[scene-06-calveno-day-one|Scene 06 — Calveno Day One]]; [[branca|Branca]]; [[waveservant-shrine|Waveservant Shrine]]; [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]; Maw Pearl Crisis (W9-near-miss — see Scene 01 note; landed as [[umberlee|Umberlee]]'s Pearl Summons)

### Scene 07 — Calveno Day Two: Kyzil

- [x] Ran

> [!read-aloud]
> Calveno keeps its noise close to the water: bells, canal voices, gulls, the clatter of cart wheels over stone. Above it, almost lost in the white glare of the sun, a small winged shape folds itself out of the sky. No cry. No wingbeat. Just a shadow dropping faster than it should, then a rush of pale feathers, and Crissdalynn is wrapped in both arms before her talons finish scraping stone.

**Use:** Crissdalynn's scene. Give the relief hug room, then let Kyzil recover into Owl Obi-Wan formality. He asks about pilgrimage progress, can challenge the party to a nonlethal four-on-one spar, teaches Read the Current, and warns that Maw weather now makes the [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]] approach dangerous.

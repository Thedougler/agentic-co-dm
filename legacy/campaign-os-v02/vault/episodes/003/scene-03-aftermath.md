## Running It

**Beat 1: the damage.** [[sem-holst|Sem]]'s report, delivered at the breach: Tail stub wedged in the hull (he and Geoffrey need two hours to work it free); the breach is manageable for calm water only; they need a dry dock; ETA to [[calveno|Calveno]] at current pace is five days, weather holding.

> [!dm]
> One exchange with Sem, one clear message: *dry dock or she sinks in the next bad weather.* Move on.

**Beat 2: Grigori.** First light. [[shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] comes up the companionway with a tin mug in each hand, surveying the rail, the breach work, and the state of the crew with visible calm. He offers mugs to whoever is nearest and checks on the crew by name. If someone bears wounds, he crouches, asks permission to examine them, and when they consent, rests his hand on the wound. He murmurs, and healing flows faster than it should. Nobody has seen him sleep; if asked, he smiles and says the sea keeps him company.

> [!check] Medicine — The healing
> **DC 13** casual observation / **DC 17** close examination.
> **Crit fail:** appears to be normal magical healing, perhaps faster. Nothing stands out as unexpected.
> **Fail:** faster than expected, but maybe he's just skilled.
> **Success:** tissue knitting at a rate unrelated to what Grigori put into it. Injury site is too warm after.
> **Crit success (DC 17+):** the warmth in his hands doesn't come from body heat and doesn't withdraw when he lifts his hand.

> [!check] Insight — Reading Grigori
> **DC 14** surface read / **DC 18** sustained attention.
> **Crit fail:** reads as tense beneath the calm, misread entirely.
> **Fail:** unreadable; warm and professional, nothing leaks through.
> **Success:** visibly unbothered. No alarm behind the calm.
> **Crit success (DC 18+):** whatever he is, fatigue isn't part of it. His eyes haven't changed since last night.

> [!dm]
> Don't explain the healing or name it. Move on before anyone asks a follow-up. Sem needs the stub, [[noor|Noor]] needs something, Geoffrey drops a tool. Grigori stands, picks up his mug, watches the horizon.

**Beat 3: departure.** The stub comes free as Sem patches the breach, and the Surety sails up in early morning light. The storm has left the water flat and grey.

> [!read-aloud]
> The Surety moves forward. For the first time since the anchor dropped, the hull answers the wind the way it's supposed to, straight and clean and responsive. Old [[old-faas|Faas]] ties off the last line and looks back at the water behind them. The bioluminescent wake is long gone, and the sea gives nothing back.

Set heading: [[calveno|Calveno]]. Five days.

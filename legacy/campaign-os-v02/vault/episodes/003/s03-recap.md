# Session 03: Getting in on the Joke

## Recap

[[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] was on watch when the storm thinned to sideways rain and the water went somewhere else. Fathoms of cold pressure, worked stone below, pale bioluminescent things drifting, unbothered. Then his body wasn't his. Sixty feet of muscle, a barbed Tail, senses tuned to the water. A hull above, blood in the water, something moving beneath it. He dove, drove the barb in, and the whale sounded into the dark and took him with it. He watched a shape rise and strike the hull from below, and one word burned into his mind: *"Grow."*

Perrin fell from the rigging, [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] with him; [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] caught them both before they hit the deck. The Whip Shark that struck the hull was over sixty feet, longer than the ship. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] emptied both swivels and the broadside into it. Jean-Claude put an arrow in from the rigging and took a Tail-swipe.

Crissdalynn flew out, seized it by [[tail|the Tail]], and hauled it clean out of the water, exposed and thrashing. Jean-Claude drew, aimed low, and put the arrow in one end and out the other. [[shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] healed Jean-Claude's wound wordlessly, a red light from his wrist Perrin's Arcana couldn't identify. Delmar recovered the barb in manta ray form; the party cut scales for patching, and Jean-Claude took three fertilized eggs from the body.

Five days at sea. [[old-faas|Old Faas]] taught Perrin the ropes and told stories of running cargo with [[barnaby-rook|Rook]]. Jean-Claude and [[sem-holst|Sem Holst]] worked the hull, [[mending|Mending]] and hand-patching in companionable silence.

Crissdalynn dug through Rook's chart archive and found he'd broken Imperial rules about the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]] twice, keeping no record of what he found. Delmar ran ship's business too long as [[admiral-fisk|Admiral Fisk]], hair and cadence drifting, calling Crissdalynn *Crystalline* and [[geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey]] *Mr. James*, until her flask brought him back. On the second day, Crissdalynn spotted a glowing outline in the water, moving slowly on a parallel heading. Perrin recognized it and watched it a long time.

*"Land ho."* [[calveno|Calveno]] was too busy to fly Crown colours up the main approach in a stolen ship, so Perrin took them the other way in. On the approach, the crew renamed the [[uncertainty|HCS Surety]]. No prefix, just *Uncertainty*. [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]]'s covered dry dock had a cradle waiting. [[cobb|Cobb]] met them at the water's edge.

Perrin shook his hand and bolted: *"You guys fucking handle the ship. I'm going."* As Delmar stepped ashore, the water reached for him. He thought he heard his name, then it disappeared. Grigori came down after with an icy handshake, thanked the crew, and said his business would take a week or two, then left.

Jean-Claude changed color to blend in, found a shortbow at [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]], then placed the egg at [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]]. [[umberlee|Umberlee]]'s servant stopped Delmar and Crissdalynn in the [[mercatura|Mercatura]], waiting for Delmar at the [[waveservant-shrine|Waveservant Shrine]], not sleeping until he came. Delmar talked his way past her.

At [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s kitchen, [[enzo|Enzo]] and [[ruk|Ruk]] stood watch while she worked the stove. She heard about the *[[vestra|Vestra]]* (lost to something in the water, lightning, then nothing) and grieved the money more than the ship. She called off the attacks already underway, ordered consolation for the *Vestra*'s crew, and asked an unspecified favor. Perrin agreed before hearing it. She gave him a sending stone.

That afternoon, something dove from the sun and knocked Crissdalynn flat: [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]], who'd searched [[kalowe|Kalowe]] and [[tidefall|Tidefall]] for her since the *[[red-lady|Red Lady]]* went down. Perrin, who'd been hiding in a barrel, emerged to shake one offered talon.

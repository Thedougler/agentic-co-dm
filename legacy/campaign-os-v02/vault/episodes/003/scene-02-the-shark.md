## Shark Stat Snapshot

**HP:** 114 | **AC:** 15 | **Attack bonus:** +9 | **Speed:** swim 60 ft.

> [!mechanic]
> **Shark attacks.**
>
> | Attack | Hit | Effect |
> |---|---|---|
> | Bite | 22 (3d10+6) piercing | [[grappled|Grappled]], escape DC 17 Athletics or Acrobatics |
> | Barbed Tail | 17 (2d10+6) slashing | DC 17 Str save or pulled 20 ft; off-vessel means overboard |
> | Hooking Drive | Activated on hook seat | Ship cannot move until Tail breaks free. Pull/drown cycle begins. |

**Blood Frenzy:** advantage against any creature below max HP.

**Surge:** when a creature enters water within 30 ft., shark moves up to 30 ft. and bites if in range. Not an action. State this once when first relevant.

## Run Loop

Each round:

1. **Name the phase.** Probing, pulling, drowning, or repositioning.
2. **State the ship consequence.** List, hull sound, water, crew movement.
3. **Ask for PC actions.**
4. **Use one crew default** if no PC redirects that crew member.
5. **Advance the shark.** Probe strike, hook attempt, pull, or drown.
6. **Remind the table what changed.** Hull worse, breach wider, shark hurt, window open or closed.

Do not track ship HP. Sem is the hull narrator.

## Phase Structure

### Phase 0 — Probing (Rounds 1–2)

The shark tests the hull with glancing Tail strikes. A deep underwater percussion from below the port hull, then the hull shaking. The dorsal fin may break the surface (long, pale, moving too slowly for its size).

Players can act here. Meaningful intervention (heavy damage, deterrent, or distraction) can delay or prevent the hook seating. Default: if the party does nothing, the hook seats at the end of Round 2.

> [!mechanic]
> **Pre-hook options.**
>
> | Pre-Hook Option | Result |
> |---|---|
> | Deal 20+ damage during probing | Shark recoils; no hook attempt this round |
> | Chum, bright light, or loud percussion | DC 13 Wis (success delays probe 1 round; three successes make the shark disengage) |
> | [[thunk\|Thunk]]'s swivel gun | 2d6; on 8+, shark skips hook attempt this round |

### Phase 1: Hook Seats

> [!read-aloud]
> The next impact is different. Not a strike, but a catch. The hull groans without stopping, and the list doesn't correct. From below, Sem starts screaming.

The barb is through the hull, below the waterline, into the hold. The pull begins immediately.

### Phase 2: The Pull

The shark turns and drags. The bow swings hard to port (30 degrees in a few seconds).

> [!read-aloud]
> The bow lurches to port like something enormous grabbed it and yanked. The rigging shrieks. Anyone not holding something goes down.

> [!mechanic]
> **Mid-pull footing.** Require a DC 12 Athletics or Acrobatics check from all on deck. Failure knocks them prone and slides them 10 ft. toward port rail. The shark reverses direction mid-pull. The bow starts coming back, yet the hook angle shifts and drives the barb deeper.

### Phase 3: The Drown

> [!read-aloud]
> The port rail goes under. The bow tilts hard. Water comes over the side in sheets. Then the ship fights back, she's too light, too full of air. The shark cannot hold her under. The ship hits the waterline like a dropped plank. The hull rings. Something below breaks.

**Hull condition** (Sem narrates; not HP):

1. *"She can take another one, but not five more."*
2. *"The frames are cracking. She's breathing water now."*
3. *"One more of those and I can't keep her up."*

After stage 3: the ship breaks apart if unresolved.

### Cycle Reset

After the drown, the shark repositions and begins again: probe → hook attempt → pull → drown → repeat. Each full cycle advances Sem's hull narration by one stage.

### Blood Interruption

If blood enters the water (PC overboard, crew blood washing out through the breach, or shark wound), the shark releases the pull and investigates the blood source for one round. Hook tension releases temporarily.

> [!read-aloud]
> The tension releases. The ship groans back toward level. Somewhere in the dark water, something is moving toward the blood.

> [!dm]
> One round. After it, the shark repositions and re-hooks. This is the player opportunity window.

## The Joint

The joint (the scarred seam where the lower lobe begins) is **below the waterline**, outside the hull. Never visible from the weather deck.

**What they can discover:**

- From the hold: the lobe enters through the breached planking below water level. They can touch and feel it flexing, but the joint lies further out, outside the hull, underwater.
- From above deck: [[tail|the Tail]] disappears into dark water; no sign of the joint's location
- [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s dream: he knows the joint is roughly 10 ft. along the lobe from the hull, and how deep

**Attacking the joint:**

> [!check] Acrobatics — Attacking the Joint Over the Rail
> DC 12. Anyone leaning over the rail to reach the joint must roll first.
> **Success:** stays on the rail; attacks the joint at disadvantage from the unstable deck (20+ slashing severs the lobe).
> **Failure:** falls overboard.

- Overboard: full access, no disadvantage, and Surge fires immediately (Death pressure).
- Perrin's knowledge removes disadvantage on the over-the-rail attack and lets him direct another PC precisely.

**Cutting the lobe from inside the hold:**

- Needs 3 successful hits (any damage) or 1 hit dealing 15+
- Severs the lobe stub from the shark; barb remains wedged in hull but the shark escapes
- Breach widens; Sem screams; shark disengages

> [!dm]
> The designed solution is the joint (easiest sever mechanic), but cutting the lobe from the hold works too (harder threshold). Any physical plan that makes sense gets a roll and a consequence.

## Fast Solution Map

**Pre-hook (during probing):**

> [!mechanic]
> **Pre-hook solution map.**
>
> - Deal 20+ damage: shark recoils and skips the hook attempt.
> - Chum / salt pork / noise: DC 13 Wis; success delays one round, three successes disengages the shark.
> - Bright light at waterline: shark Perception at disadvantage for this encounter.
> - Thunk's swivel gun (directed): 2d6, on 8+ shark skips hook attempt this round.

**Post-hook:**

> [!mechanic]
> **Post-hook solution map, part one.**
>
> - Target joint (over rail): DC 12 Acrobatics or fall overboard. Disadvantage on attack (or none with Perrin). 20+ slashing severs lobe.
> - Cut lobe from hold: 3 hits any damage or 1 hit 15+. Each hit severs the lobe while the barb remains wedged, and the shark disengages with the breach widening.
> - Pull the barb: on first try, automatic success with the ship grinding toward the shark and Sem screaming. To persist: DC 18 Athletics while shark not pulling. Success widens breach.
> - Cut anchor / outrun: needs Old Faas plus PC rigging plus PC at helm. DC 14 helm check. Barb tears free when speed reaches 4+ knots but widens breach.

> [!mechanic]
> **Post-hook solution map, part two.**
>
> - Main cannon: DC 15 to angle downward. 4d10 on hit; on d6 roll 1-2, also hits hull for 2d10. Sem is furious.
> - Oil / fire: 2d6 fire per round. DC 14 Wis or shark loses next action. Fire spreads to rail on Round 3 unless managed.
> - Eye shot: reach weapon, rail position. DC 16 attack; 1d8+mod and shark loses blindsight for encounter.
> - Ask [[shepherd-grigori\|Grigori]]: he can calm water within 30 ft. as a ritual, flattening waves and reducing drown impact severity by one stage. Does it silently, no explanation.

> [!mechanic]
> **Player targets joint over rail.** DC 12 Acrobatics first (see the Attacking the Joint check above). Describe reaching down into dark water blind.

| Hook seats | Single narration beat, then pull begins. |
| Player grabs barb | Ship grinds toward shark. Sem screams. Rail dips toward water. |

| Main cannon fires | Use hull-damage die. Sem is furious. |
| Oil catches fire | Fire spreads to the rail on Round 3. |
| Anyone goes overboard | Surge fires and shark bites. Treat as death pressure. |

## Ship Narration

**Probing:**

- A low percussion from below the port hull. Not a wave. Regular.
- The ship rocks to port and doesn't fully settle before the next one comes.
- From below: something moving along the hull, slow and heavy.

**Hooked / pull:**

- The list deepens hard as the shark drives weight into the hull, then partially corrects.
- Salt water comes over the port rail in a sheet.
- Barrels in the hold roll to port and hit the bulkhead together.

**Drown:**

- The port rail goes under. It comes back. Everything not lashed is wet.
- The ship hits the surface hard (not a roll, a strike).
- From below: a sound like a frame cracking, then Sem's voice.

**When the joint severs:**

> [!read-aloud]
> The sound is a cannon shot. Not a crack, a percussion, something under enormous tension releasing all at once. The shark recoils hard to port. The Tail stub wrenches free from the hull with a grinding tear. Water hits the breach fast for three seconds before Sem gets his brace in. The ship rolls back toward level. The port side falls silent.

## Behavioral Arc

| Shark State | Behavior |
|---|---|
| 114–58 HP | Committed; pressing probing hits; hooking and cycling aggressively |
| 57–29 HP | Testing escape angles; circling wider; more responsive to deterrents |
| 28 HP or lower | Looking for a reason to disengage; chum, light, pain, or noise can end it |
| 0 HP | Hard and fast. The shark leaves no body and offers no confirmation whether it lives or dies. |

## After It Ends

The fight ends when the shark disengages, the party drives it off, or it dies.

- Tail stub or barb remains wedged in hull unless the resolution removed it.
- Sem can keep the ship afloat in calm water, not another storm.
- The Uncertainty needs dry dock before rough sailing.

# Session 02: Conflict is a Surety

## Recap

[[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]] had been in the water once. The Flask of Endless Water hadn't been enough. He hauled himself back over the rail, cold and furious, crew dead or defected.

[[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]'s wind bent him toward the rail, then away. He fired his flintlock at close range.

[[beaumont-sel|Beaumont]] solved the cannon problem by throwing [[bisou|Bisou]] through a gun port. She pissed in the powder. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] fired through the second port and hit [[ensign-wouters|Ensign Wouters]] in the dark below. The guns fell silent.

Rook went back up the rigging; Delmar threw a chair and called the wind, and Rook went into the water again.

Beaumont settled accounts. To [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]: a Friend of the [[the-passage|Passage]], any Kin in need could ask. Quietly: [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]] was looking for her lost grandson, and believed [[anzolo|Anzolo]] had already been sent.

Beaumont slid a Truth Stone across the decking to [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]. He told the truth: former kinsmen had hunted him, he stowed away to survive, nobody sent him.

[[geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey]] led them below, leading with the cook. The galley smelled wrong for a ship: bread and herbs on a low heat. [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] waited at the head of the table: a [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] shirt, a [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi]] naval coat rolled to the sleeves. He asked what kind of pirates they were: no trafficking, no cannibalism, Robin Hood near enough. He told them about [[capn-gorgeous|Cap'n Gorgeous]], and [[rupert-knighton|Rupert Knighton]], who will send ships. His condition: anyone who wants off at the next port walks free. He mentioned they'd forgotten to feed Ket.

[[ket|Ket]] was a [[moucheron|Moucheron]], pigeon-sized, sentient, a starving humanoid mosquito from [[murrat|Murrat]]. Rook had kept him as a specimen, curious about the [[five-blades|Five Blades]] in [[kalowe|Kalowe]].

Jean-Claude filled a wine glass with his own blood. Ket drank. His abdomen went red. Calmer. Not safe.

Perrin drew his longsword and moved for the cage. Crissdalynn got there first, deflected the blade, put herself between Perrin and the bars, and stepped past the safe distance. Ket fed from her arm. She pulled back. Perrin cast Tasha's [[hideous-laughter|Hideous Laughter]]. Ket went down giggling in mosquito honks.

They used Wouters' body as a lure. Perrin sprung the cage with [[mage-hand|Mage Hand]] from across the room. Ket fed for a minute, lifted off, and flew toward home. Grigori said he was glad.

Crew assembled: Geoffrey Draves, [[sem-holst|Sem Holst]] (shipwright), [[alys-kuiper|Alys Kuiper]] (surgeon), [[old-faas|Old Faas]] (rigging), [[thunk|Thunk]] (explosives), and [[noor|Noor]]. Grigori was leaving at [[calveno|Calveno]] for [[il-gioco-delle-beffe|Il Gioco delle Beffe]].

Perrin told the crew who he was: a Black-Jaw. The Passage code alongside captain's law.

Rook's cabin held 45 gp, [[letters-of-marque|Letters of Marque]], a blunderbuss; under the floor, 110 gp and two garnets. The cargo held a pendant reading *For Mira, from the sea*, plus Mira's Blade and twenty vials of tincture Jean-Claude recognised from the Alchemist's bandolier. Mira's Blade went to Perrin.

Morning. Beaumont warned the weather toward Calveno, said he could be found in Kalowe off-route. Delmar told him he had a friend in [[admiral-fisk|Admiral Fisk]]. Bisou had lifted 15 gp of shinies from the Surety. Beaumont called it fair business. The [[saltwright|Saltwright]] pulled away.

The [[uncertainty|HCS Surety]] set heading for Calveno: still Crown-marked and unnamed.

Two hours out, a tropical storm hit from the east: roaring winds, rain horizontal, the sea steep and grey. Canvas down, anchors out. Jean-Claude was seasick.

Three hours in, a gap opened to the northwest: a Crown Isle on the route toward Calveno, probably [[aldenmere|Aldenmere]]. It closed.

Then the hull took an impact. A second. A third. The Surety lurched hard to port. Jean-Claude reached for his spyglass.

## NPCs

**[[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]]**
**Wants:** to survive the boarding and hold his ship (fighting alone, crew dead or defected).
**Voice:** terse and furious under pressure — *"You shot me?! Fuck you, Brataby!"*
**Mannerism:** fires his flintlock at close range, then swings the cutlass.

**[[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]]**
**Wants:** to end the fight cleanly and get back to his own route toward Kalowe.
**Voice:** plain and direct — warns the party about the weather and about Kalowe being off-route.
**Mannerism:** directs [[bisou|Bisou]] to soak the guns and deliver healing potions via the Kalowe Maneuver.

**[[geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey Draves]]**
**Wants:** to prove his defection is real, even when Rook calls him out by oath.
**Voice:** quiet and practical — ticks off what the ship needs to run without lecturing.
**Mannerism:** doesn't gesture at himself when naming who could fill the Carpenter post.

**[[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]]**
**Wants:** safe passage to Calveno for a dying heir. He's uninterested in a fight.
**Voice:** warm, deliberate, unhurried — refills his wine without asking.
**Mannerism:** ties off his own bandage one-handed without slowing the meal.

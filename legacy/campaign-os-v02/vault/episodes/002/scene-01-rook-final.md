## Enemy Roster

[[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]] (120 HP (4-PC: 110 HP), AC 17, Cornered Wolf + Naval Footwork active. [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]] fights alongside the party (CR 2, 44 HP, AC 19), supported by [[bisou|Bisou]] via the Kalowe Maneuver (3 healing potions).

## Challenge Calibration

Tuned for the party at this point (post-boarding, crew dead or defected). See Dial below for scaling in both directions mid-fight.

## Terrain

The Surety's weather deck and amidships. The gangplank edge and rail are shove targets. Rigging and cargo nets give cover. The amidship gun ports (two total) are the lair-action target. A hooded lantern governs light and the Umbral Sight interaction. A hatch to below-decks carries Grigori's voice.

## Running It

### Round 1 (The Opening

**Free action:** *"Uncover the lanterns."* He kicks the hood off the nearest hooded lantern. Bright light floods his end of the deck. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]'s Umbral Sight collapses anywhere within the lantern's 20-ft. radius.

**Naval Footwork:** he disengages as a bonus action, moves to a position where at most two PCs can reach him.

### Round 2+ (The Fight

Rook attacks with his Cutlass twice per turn, with a shove option whenever a PC is near the gangplank edge or rail. He Disengages after attacking. Uses rigging and cargo nets as cover, corners where only one PC can engage at a time.

**Targeting priority:**

1. Whoever looks most injured
2. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] (softest target, ranged threat
3. Whoever is closest to the gangplank edge (shove them in

### Legendary Actions

3 per round, regained at start of his turn.

| Action | Cost | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Reposition** | 1 | Move up to half speed without provoking opportunity attacks. Stay ahead of the party's positioning (not to escape). |
| **Cutlass Strike** | 2 | One Cutlass attack with shove option on a hit. Use between PC turns to threaten anyone who thinks the gap is safe. |
| **Pistol Reload** | 2 | Reload one expended flintlock. Does not fire. Worth using if the party is playing ranged. |

### Lair Actions (Round Arc)

On initiative count 20 each round (losing ties). Cannot repeat same lair action two rounds in a row. Run in this sequence.

**Round 1: arm the Guns.** Rook bellows the order below. Gun port hatches scrape open. The amidship cannon ports (two total) uncover themselves. No immediate effect. The guns prime for next round.

> [!dm]
> This is automatic (no save or damage rolls). **Beaumont's response:** on his turn, he tosses [[bisou|Bisou]] to the nearest exposed gun port, and she soaks the powder in the chamber, leaving one gun out of action for the rest of the fight. Beaumont loses his action, and the party loses one cone of fire in Round 2.

**Round 2: fire the Guns.** One cannon discharges (the one Bisou didn't reach). 30-ft. cone from its gun port along the weather deck.

> [!mechanic]
> **Cannon volley (30-ft. cone).** Every creature in the cone: DC 14 [[dexterity|Dexterity]] save. **Fail:** 4d10 bludgeoning. **Success:** half.

> [!dm]
> Average 22 on a fail drops most PCs at this level. Anyone chasing Rook toward amidships is in the kill zone. Use Cutlass Strike as a legendary action this round.

**Round 3: call to Arms.** Rook grabs the bell line and hauls. Three times. The iron note rings out sharp and clean over dark water.

He waits.

No boots on the ladder. No voice called up.

Then, muffled through the hatch, flat and final: *"No."*

His jaw tightens. He draws his pistol and puts a round through the door frame.

"You shot me?! Fuck you, Brataby!"

Footsteps move away. A long pause. They return.

"The crew says they quit. I can't blame them. Hostile work environment."

Nobody comes up. The lair action fails.

> [!dm]
>
> - The voice is [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] (the round hit the door frame, but he's CR 19 and the splinter barely registered.
> - *"You shot me?!"* expresses real irritation. *"The crew says they quit"* lands in his usual register, warm and deliberate.
> - This is the moment the table should feel Rook's situation (his crew hates him enough not to fight for him even now.
> - Rook continues fighting: something shifts in his posture, he stops calling orders, and just swings.
> - **HP threshold:** if Rook is above 55 HP when Round 3 arrives, delay Call to Arms (use Reposition instead and run it once he crosses the threshold.

### Geoffrey Crisis

Once, on any turn Rook chooses (free action, no cost:

"Draves. You swore an oath. Look at what you're standing next to."

> [!mechanic]
> **Geoffrey's oath (forced hesitation.** [[geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey]] makes a DC 12 [[wisdom|Wisdom]] save (roll openly). **Fail:** spends next action doing nothing (standing, hand on sword, unable to commit). Recovers the following turn. Use when action economy feels lopsided, not as the first thing Rook does.

## Lowering the Stakes

**Roleplay Off-Ramp.** Rook is pragmatic, not suicidal. He will not offer terms first. Available only once he is ≤40 HP remaining.

> [!check] Persuasion — Opening Rook to Terms
> DC 14, with something real: leverage, a face-saving offer, or proof that killing him makes things worse.
> **Success:** manifest goes dark, [[saltwright|Saltwright]] goes free, Surety pulls away. He keeps his ship. He still won't give names from other inspections or Crown internal business.
> **Failure:** the captain holds. He offers nothing and the fight continues.

## Raising the Stakes

A party that keeps Beaumont alive and unhindered gains a disabled gun (Bisou's soak) and a fighting ally who can flank Geoffrey into hesitating or land Kalowe-Maneuver heals.

## Stakes

Rook's fate resolves here, Session 02's central combat. Session facts (the played outcome) live on [[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]] § Session 02, not here.

## If Ignored

Not applicable (this is the session's forced central combat, not a hook the party can decline.

## Dial

**Rook going down too fast:**

- Parry everything (no per-round limit. Average reduction 11.5 (1d10+6).
- Delay Call to Arms (use Reposition until he crosses 55 HP.
- Geoffrey hesitates one round early.

> [!mechanic]
> **Rigging climb (positioning delay.** Rook can climb the rigging (DC 10 Athletics, costs his action) to create a positioning problem worth one round.
> **Failure:** he loses his footing and his action. No positioning problem, and he's exposed on the rigging for the party's next attack.

**Rook surviving too long:**

- Beaumont heals via Kalowe Maneuver (3 potions, 2d4+2 each). Use freely.
- Rook drops a shove this round.
- Geoffrey flanks with a PC, granting advantage on their attack.
- Drop negotiation threshold to 55 HP.

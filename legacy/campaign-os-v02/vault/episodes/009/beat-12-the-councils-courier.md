# The Council's Courier

> [!narration]
> *The reef gap narrows into dusk, the Waveservant Shrine's causeway lamp the only light at the channel's throat. The [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] slides through on the evening tide, her wake hissing low against the pilings, hands already at the bollards before her lines are across.*
>
> *Dock labor out of [[calveno|Calveno]] and chandlery hands from the [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca's]] victualing know [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s face on sight. They call his name while the hull is still swinging in.*
>
> *One man does not call out. He stands apart at the far end of the quay, hands clean of any dock work, and he has held that spot since noon, since the causeway lookout first marked their sail coming up the strait.*
>
> *He waits for the gangway. Then he steps forward.*

## At a Glance

- **Pattern:** a port that already knows you, and an invitation that is a summons wearing manners.
- **Delta:** the Council's Denial front has four segments, none filled. The easiest first segment is naming the man the Council needs to blame, and [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]], on record as the fleet's admiral and confirmed by his own confession as someone who knew the Council's sponsorship, is the easiest blame in the [[midchain|Midchain]]. The courier introduces himself as handling portside matters (true the way true things can be true without being honest) and says the Council would welcome a private meeting at their convenience. He says *their* convenience, and his eyes go to the harbor stairs twice before he finishes the sentence.
- **Pressure:** the courier stood at the reef gap before they docked. The Council moved first.

## Player Gravity

- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]], the Council fits the blame to him by name
- [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]], his community's debts sit under the same Council's Tessarine weather
- Whole crew, the rescue needs [[kalowe|Kalowe's]] water and Kalowe's Council decides what the port gives

## Situation

The [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] makes [[kalowe|Kalowe]] on the fifth evening, last of the light, the reef gap lit by the Waveservant Shrine's causeway lamp. Kalowe knows [[delmar-fisk|Delmar's]] face before the mooring lines are across: dock labor that worked through Calveno, chandlery hands from the [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]] victualing, and one [[chain-council|Chain Council]] member's man who has watched the reef gap since noon, when the causeway lookout sent word the Uncertainty's silhouette was coming up from the strait.

## Actors

- The Council member's man (unnamed courier, worldview: professionally pleasant). **Wants:** the meeting accepted, the timing the Council's. **Knows:** when the lookout spotted the Uncertainty and who Delmar is. Knows what the Council intends him for and which dock hands have been asking about the fleet. **Constrained by:** instructions to invite, not compel. **Without PC intervention:** reports acceptance or refusal, either of which the Council uses.

## What Can Change

The party learns the Council watched for them by name. They accept on their own terms or the Council's, or refuse outright. The docks' own sympathies are readable: labor that likes Delmar vs. money that needs him blamed.

## Surface

The evening docks. The courier. The chandlery and dock labor who know the crew. The shrine causeway. Word of the coastal delegations gathering (seeds beat-14).

## Conditional Reactions

- **If they accept at the Council's convenience:** beat-13 on the Council's terms.
- **If they set their own terms or refuse:** the Council adjusts. The closed session happens about them instead of with them (beat-13 still plays, heard secondhand).
- **If they work the docks first:** labor's version of what the Council has been saying about the fleet arrives before the Council's own.

## Carry Forward

The meeting question feeds beat-13. The dock sympathies and delegation word feed beat-14.

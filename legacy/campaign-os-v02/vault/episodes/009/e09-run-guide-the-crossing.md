# The Crossing

## Last Time

> [!narration]  
> _Last time on the Shattered Sea, the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] sat in [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]]’s drydock while the party collected what [[calveno|Calveno]] believed its ‘Defenders’ had earned. Even [[ottavio-fieschi|Factor Fieschi]] admitted a rare debt: a thousand gold in trade credit, the deed to [[casa-falier|Casa Falier]], and a diamond ring._
> 
> _The ink was barely dry when [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]’s message came: **come home. There are so many missing.**_
> 
> _At the [[mercatura|Mercatura]], the crater still sat behind a cordon. Nona had borrowed a table and covered it with names. More than three hundred. Every one a fighting-age man taken in the raids. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] saw the pattern first. The Grung weren’t just raiding anymore. They were preparing for war, and taking extreme risks to do it._
> 
> _Before the grieving crowd, Nona made her answer plain: [[the-passage|The Passage]] would give chase. Two ships were already bound for the Verdant Teeth._
> 
> _With the pursuit underway, the party spent their winnings across Calveno. Perrin struck a deal for a wrist-shield that snapped open faster than the eye could follow. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] pulled a torn page from the shelves, carrying the story of a monk who abandoned the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels]] during their schism. They met [[zort|Zort]], an armless goblin willing to trade his reptile contact in [[sparhold|Sparhold]] for a prosthetic leg. And at La Cenere, Lavinia sold them an impressive collection of questionable goods before slipping Delmar a sending stone._
> 
> _By morning, [[cobb|Cobb]] had finished the Uncertainty’s refit ahead of his own estimate._
> 
> _More than three hundred names sit on Nona’s list. The raiders’ trail grows colder by the hour. And beyond the [[central-strait|Central Strait]] lies the Midchain, the Grung fleet, and Calveno’s missing._

## Prep

**Where:** [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]], then the [[central-strait|Central Strait]] south.

**Who:** [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]], [[corvin-knighton|Corvin Knighton]], [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]], three [[calveno|Calveno]] survivors, the [[knifes-wake|Knife's Wake]]'s crew.

**Secrets already live:** the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]'s hull line is a Dravosi cutter's. The Knife's Wake carries a paid search retainer. The storm debris resolves to [[aruhe|Aruhe]].

**DM reminders:**

- The cold open is one 30-minute Hook beat, five combined group checks, hard cut to La Vasca.
- Beats 03 to 06 fire in whatever order the water and the DM choose.
- Every southern heading crosses the debris field.
- The [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]] warms as they enter the [[midchain-north|Northern Midchain]]. If anyone notices, [[yssenmoor|Yssenmoor]]'s fog is visible on the horizon. See Side-tracks.
- [[the-unplotted|The Unplotted]] sits where the chart shows open water, between Sparhold and [[kwoss|Kwoss]]. The crew can anchor or sail past. See Side-tracks.

## Yssenmoor

> [!narration]
> *Fog holds still over the island to starboard, thick enough to swallow the shoreline, untouched by a wind that fills the sails and bends the pennants flat. The swell rolls the same as always, gulls still ride the updraft off the bow, and the fog does not move at all.*
>
> *[[old-faas|Old Faas]] names it without looking up from his splice. [[yssenmoor|Yssenmoor]]. The last crew that anchored there, he says, rowed off before their watering party made it back.*
>
> *A voice reaches the deck from somewhere past the fog line, patient and formal, reciting the same lines to water that never answers. The words arrive whole and first. Whoever is speaking them stays out of sight.*
>
> *In [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn's]] pouch, the [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]] warms against her hip, low and steady. Nothing else on her carries any heat at all.*

## The Unplotted

> [!narration]
> *The [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] holds her southern line, and dead ahead the strait between [[sparhold|Sparhold]] and [[kwoss|Kwoss]] isn't open water anymore. A green hill rises where the chart says there's nothing, a bay opening on its southern face. Wrecks stand bow-in above the tideline in that bay, four of them, none holed, one still carrying both masts.*
>
> *[[master-kyzil|Kyzil]] checks the chart against the horizon, then checks again. The chart shows open water. The mate's older notes agree. He folds it away without comment.*
>
> *Wind off the hill carries the smell of green growing things and warm stone, the only scent on the crossing that isn't salt. The Uncertainty passes within a cable length, and the water inside the bay's rock arms doesn't move at all. Gulls circle the headland in loose rings, and not one comes down to land.*

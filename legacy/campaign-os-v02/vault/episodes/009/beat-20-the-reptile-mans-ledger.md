# The Reptile Man's Ledger

> [!narration]  
> _Behind the counter at [[spar-and-snare-outfitters|Spar & Snare Outfitters]] sits a tall, sunburnt half-elf dressed like he's halfway through an expedition. Bleached-blond hair, scarred hands, and a grin just a little too delighted with the small black spider hopping between his fingers._
> 
> _He notices you._
> 
> _The spider disappears behind his back. He shoots to his feet._
> 
> _"G'day! [[roka-sten|Roka]]'s the name. Welcome to the Spar & Snare!"_
> 
> _His smile twitches. One eye squeezes shut. Behind his back, his arm gives a sudden involuntary jerk._
> 
> _"Terry. Stop it. That's naughty."_
> 
> _The shop smells of cured hide, salt-stiff rope, and something sharper. A net hanging from the rafters still carries the faint chemical sting of [[grung|Grung]] poison._
> 
> _"Oh! You're the ones [[zort|Zort]] mentioned, yeah? BRILLIANT. What're we after? Full channel kit, or just the coastal run?"_

## Hook

[[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] promised to build [[zort|Zort]] his prosthetic. Zort gave her a name: [[roka-sten|Roka Sten]], [[spar-and-snare-outfitters|Spar & Snare Outfitters]], [[sparhold|Sparhold]]'s second tier. The party docks and heads straight for the shop. Roka runs safari expeditions into the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] fringe for paying clients, captures live specimens, and keeps the most detailed channel maps of the Verdant Teeth approaches in the [[midchain|Midchain]]. His ledger is open on the counter. Expedition teams have missed their scheduled returns this season, and the calendar on the wall has their names circled.

## Player Gravity

- [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] — the prosthetic deal with [[zort|Zort]] (s08) pays off here. Roka is the contact she earned.
- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] — Roka has logged axolotls in the [[dreth|Dreth]]-[[orak|Orak]] flooded channel. The axolotl finishes [[puntura|Puntura]].
- [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] — Roka captures and sells living creatures from JC's homeland for profit.

## First Look

- Roka's ledger open on the counter, creature sketches and channel-approach maps in the margins
- The expedition calendar nailed to the wall, names circled, dates passed
- Nets, cages, and [[grung|Grung]]-poison-stained equipment racked on every surface

## Pressure

- **Now:** Roka's supply chain is contracting. Teams overdue, names on the wall. He needs paying clients and news of his missing people.
- **Next:** the [[grung-clans|Grung]] patrol circuit tightens with each raid season. His window to run the Verdant Teeth approaches shrinks.

## Open Vectors

- **Trade:** the prosthetic deal opens the conversation. [[zort|Zort]]'s name buys goodwill.
- **Expedition inquiry:** Roka bellows every expedition he's ever run. The channel maps sit in the margins of the ledger he's already showing.
- **Moral confrontation:** the live-specimen trade mirrors what JC fled. Roka does not separate the profit from the discomfort.

## If Unanswered

Roka keeps running expeditions until the [[grung-clans|Grung]] close the routes. His missing teams stay missing. The channel maps stay in his ledger. The party loses the fastest guide to the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]].

## Handoff

**Likely:** cliffhanger, the [[house-kailani|Kailani]] smack's smoke report arrives and puts a destination on what Roka's maps can reach

**Alternate:** development, the party negotiates terms and plans the channel run without the smoke report's urgency

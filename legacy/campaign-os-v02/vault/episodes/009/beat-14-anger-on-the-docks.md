## Situation

A delegation from the coastal settlements stands outside [[beat-13-the-grasks-fort|the Grask's fort]] as the [[chain-council|Chain Council]] session ends. Nobody told them there was a session. They came because [[kalowe|Kalowe]] is where the Council meets, where the money moves, and where anyone with opinions about the Pearl commission and the [[grung|Grung]] raid costs can say them to someone who ought to hear. The crowd is growing and the fort's doors have to open eventually.

Fresh dock information that afternoon cuts beneath the crowd noise. A coastal fishing crew in from [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] water saw debris on the reef approach off a small island east of [[karath|Karath]]. Wrecked hulls broken on the reef and dragged to the beach by the tide, cargo markings reading as [[calveno|Calveno]] fishing marks. And on the beach, one of the fishers says: smoke.

## Player Gravity

- Whole crew: the smoke is the captives' trail, live, two weeks burning
- [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]: he has been reading these docks since arrival, and the delegation is the raid-bled communities his strategic read is about
- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]: the crowd's anger and the Council's scapegoat story both have his name in them, and which one wins the docks decides what Kalowe calls him

## Active Force

- **Intent:** the delegation wants the Council to answer for the commission and the raids, out loud, today
- **Means:** numbers, grief, and the moral weight of the coast; a crowd that the dock labor will not clear
- **Limits:** unarmed, without a leader, one hard push from either dispersing or becoming something with momentum

## Pressure

- **Now:** the crowd pressed against the fort doors with the party caught between
- **Next:** the first Council member to walk out picks what the crowd becomes
- **Default trajectory:** shouted grievances curdle toward a surge at the doors, and the Council's [[guard|Guard]] answer decides the day's story

## Open Vectors

Prep coverage only.

- **Standing with the delegation:** their grievance list is real and the party has the debris-field truth to add to it
- **Working the crowd for the fisher:** finding the smoke report's source firsthand (names, bearings, how close they dared)
- **Brokering:** a crew the docks like and the Council needs could put a speaker and a listener in the same room
- **Staying clear:** see Bypass

## Escalation

1. The gathering: bodies, voices, the weight of presence
2. The doors open on the first delegate
3. Push and Guard-line
4. The surge, and whose blood is on whose stone tomorrow

## Fallout

- **Success:** the smoke report in hand from its source, and the docks counting the party friendly
- **Complication:** the report secured but the crowd's day ends badly, and the party was visible in it
- **Setback:** the surge breaks wrong, the Guard answer hardens the Council, and Kalowe's port turns procedural against outsiders
- **Bypass:** the party stays aboard; the report reaches them secondhand and thinner, and the docks remember who watched

## Handoff

**Likely:** session close on the [[aruhe|Aruhe]] signal. The smoke report is the captives' trail, and the party has a bearing

**Alternate:** the Climax gate evaluates when the party commits to the island

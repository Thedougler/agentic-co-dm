# The Reef Mouth

> [!narration]
> _The green wall of [[aruhe|Aruhe]] rises above the reef line, canopy unbroken from the waterline to the ridge. No smoke from this angle. No roofline. No cleared ground._
>
> _[[roka-sten|Roka Sten]] has been calling depth from the bow since the channel opened, bellowing every reef head and current shift the whole way. He stops mid-word._
>
> _The reef gap is ahead. One hull-width of open water between coral walls, the current pulling through in a low, steady draw. A [[grung|Grung]] hull sits broken on the reef to the south, keel snapped clean, cargo scattered across the coral shelf._
>
> _Past the gap, on the narrow beach, pale shapes hold the tree line on legs that do not bend where legs bend, watching the water._

## Situation

The [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] runs the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] channel with [[roka-sten|Roka]] at the bow calling depth. The water between the islands is narrow, current-heavy, and walled by reef on both sides. Past [[dreth|Dreth]] and [[orak|Orak]], the charted water ends. East of [[karath|Karath]]'s patrol line, the depth soundings disappear from Roka's maps and the navigation becomes [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]'s chart work. [[aruhe|Aruhe]]'s reef mouth appears ahead, one gap in the coral wall, barely wide enough for the hull. The current pulls through it.

Beyond the gap, the beach. Smoke. A [[grung|Grung]] hull broken on the reef. And at the tree line, shapes that took position before the hull cleared the outer reef.

## Player Gravity

- Whole crew — the captives, the smoke, the island. This is what the heading was for.
- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] — the reef gap is blind navigation. Her chart work decides whether the hull clears the coral.
- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] — the captain's call. Through the gap or hold off.
- [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] — this is [[grung-clans|Grung]] territory. The island's reputation among his own people.

## Active Force

- **Intent:** the island has no intent. Hungry the way stone is heavy.
- **Means:** reef, current, and whatever has corrupted the creatures past the tree line.
- **Limits:** the reef gap is the only way in. The current favors approach at slack tide, and the creatures do not enter the water.

## Pressure

- **Now:** the reef gap is ahead. The current pulls. The decision is now.
- **Next:** slack tide passes. The current reverses, and the gap becomes impassable until the next cycle.
- **Default trajectory:** the party holds off. The smoke continues or stops. The survivors wait another tide cycle, or do not.

## Open Vectors

- **Sail through:** [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]'s chart work, [[roka-sten|Roka]]'s depth calls, [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s helm. The gap is tight.
- **Scout first:** a boat through the gap to read the beach before the hull commits.
- **Wait for slack:** anchor outside the reef and approach at optimal current. Costs time. The patrol risk increases.

## Escalation

1. The reef mouth appears ahead — one gap, current pulling.
2. A [[grung|Grung]] hull sits broken on the reef to the south of the gap, its keel snapped clean.
3. Smoke on the beach. The wreck of a [[vethka|Vethka]] hull. Figures sheltering against it.
4. Pale shapes hold the tree line, positioned along the shore. They have been there since before the hull arrived.

## Fallout

- **Success:** the hull clears the gap. The party reaches the beach. The [[the-aruhe-rescue|Aruhe Rescue]]'s situation takes over.
- **Complication:** the hull takes reef damage going through. They reach the beach but the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] needs repair before it can leave.
- **Setback:** the gap is too narrow or the current too strong. They hold outside the reef while the tide turns.
- **Bypass:** the party anchors outside and sends a boat. Slower approach. The island's creatures have more time to position.

## Handoff

**Likely:** the [[the-aruhe-rescue|Aruhe Rescue]] situation's own Beat Chart. The island's terrain and the survivors are the next frame.

**Alternate:** session-ending cliffhanger with the reef gap ahead and the decision hanging for next session.

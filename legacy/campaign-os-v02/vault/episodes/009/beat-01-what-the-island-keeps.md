# Cold Open: What the Island Keeps

> **Runtime:** 30 minutes
> **POV:** Orrek, a purple-caste [[grung|Grung]] guardsman
> **Hook mode:** alternate-POV survival horror. A doomed sprint along Aruhe's beach through wolfrabbit pack territory
> **Handoff:** hard cut to the main PCs on the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]'s deck at [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]]

## Purpose

- **Why now:** the raiding fleet wrecked on [[aruhe|Aruhe]]'s reef ([[aruhe-wreck|the Aruhe wreck]]). The wreck puts survivors alive on the wrong beach, and the island answers. [[wolfrabbits|Wolfrabbits]] hunt [[grung|Grung]] on scent — no Grung who sets foot on Aruhe leaves again. That needs to land as dread before the party ever sees the place.
- **Main-session connection:** Aruhe is where every branch's signal points. This scene colours the first approach.
- **Player takeaway:** Aruhe drives its fauna to hunt Grung on scent alone. Wolfrabbits hunt in packs, leap thirty feet, and turn cannibal on their own dead. Taking from the island (the berry) triggered the first kill, then the packs hunted every Grung on the beach. The Human survivors went untouched. In the wrecked [[vethka|Vethka]] hull, [[sandro|Sandro]] and [[nino|Nino]] shelter alive, watching the reef gap.
- **PC knowledge afterward:** none. The main characters know nothing about this event.

## POV Brief

- **Character:** Orrek, purple-caste Grung guardsman. The whole table plays him together.
- **Immediate goal:** get off the beach and across the half-mile of water to [[karath|Karath]].
- **Situation:** on the narrow beach after the wreck. Beside him two other purple-caste guards: the barred spearman and the spotted one. [[sandro|Sandro]] and [[nino|Nino]] shelter in the broken [[vethka|Vethka]] hull. Tomo, the bearded old captive, stands near the brush with a berry in his hand.
- **What this scene establishes:** Orrek is a trained soldier shipwrecked on the island every Grung knows as the hungry isle. [[wolfrabbits|Wolfrabbits]] hunt Grung on scent, the packs ignore Humans. They are fast and come from the terrace warrens and jungle along the entire beach. Nobody who runs makes it to the channel.
- **Relevant capabilities:** military training, amphibious physiology, poison skin (contact toxin on a Grung's hide), a spear he has not let go of.

Structure: three Grung start running along the beach toward the channel and [[karath|Karath]]. [[wolfrabbits|Wolfrabbits]] hunt in packs that emerge from the collapsed terrace stonework and jungle along the route, and they hunt Grung on scent while ignoring the Human survivors. Each check is a stage of the sprint. By check 3 the pack catches the rearmost Grung, who dies on every outcome, and by check 4 the second falls the same way. By check 5 a fresh pack from the headland jungle cuts off the last. All three die, and the variable is how.

> _Behind them, warrens honeycomb the ancient stonework._
> 
> _Red eyes gleam from every opening._
>

> _The beach narrows south between jungle and surf._
> 
> _Behind you, the pack is spilling from the ruins._

## Carry Forward

- **Fixed facts established:** the wreck put survivors on [[aruhe|Aruhe]]'s beach. [[wolfrabbits|Wolfrabbits]] hunt [[grung|Grung]] on scent — the packs ignored the Human captives entirely. Taking from the island (the berry) triggered the first kill. The three Grung guardsmen who ran for the channel all died to wolfrabbit packs on the beach and in the surf. In the wrecked [[vethka|Vethka]] hull, [[sandro|Sandro]] and [[nino|Nino]] shelter alive, watching the reef gap.
- **Variable state to resolve during play:** how each Grung died (heroically or quickly), how far Orrek reached before the headland pack appeared. Session ingest settles it. Prep resolves nothing.
- **Audience knowledge:** everything above, seen at the table. The berry drew the first kill. Wolfrabbits hunt Grung specifically. The men in the hull are alive and untouched.
- **PC knowledge:** none. The main characters were not present and have no way to know any of this happened.
- **Future payoff / open question:** when the party reaches Aruhe, the table carries dread the characters do not. The men in the Vethka hull are watching the reef gap for a sail. The party's ship may be that sail. The wolfrabbits are still in those warrens.

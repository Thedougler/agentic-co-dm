## Situation

Dead middle of a night watch, any leg of the crossing. [[knifes-wake|The Knife's Wake]] comes up the port quarter running dark, gunports closed until fifty yards. False trade pennant down, real one up, narrow and black. Gunports open, boarding hooks over the bulwark, committed to the angle.

The [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]'s lookout bought eleven minutes between first hail and the range that matters. The [[calveno|Calveno]]-signed crew stands to the loaded guns. Her hull is at the range where every gun of hers bears and a Tier 1 pirate cutter cannot survive the exchange. The pirate captain must read that math with the seconds the wind leaves him.

The Knife's Wake carries a retainer taken in Calveno, a third paid up front by a factor whose name her captain did not ask for. Terms: any vessel out of [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]]. Take what you like, but search the hold and cabins before anything burns, and bring back whole "a small old thing that spins." The captain cannot name the thing or the man who wants it. The fee is three times what the job should cost. This is [[the-countless|the Countless]]' Long Sight Hunt moving through a deniable instrument. Nothing on this page names the Countless as known to anyone aboard either ship. The pirates themselves know only the terms. If a boarding gets any distance, the boarders go for the cabins and sea chests while cargo sits unwatched on deck, behavior an attentive crew reads as the plunder not being what they came for.

## Active Force

- **Intent:** take "a small old thing that spins" from whoever carries it, then extract. The retainer's fee dwarfs whatever cargo the hold has
- **Means:** 1 [[tidehex-captain|Tidehex Captain]], 1 [[pirate-bosun|Pirate Bosun]], 2 [[rigging-cutpurse|Rigging Cutpurses]], 1 [[boarding-raider|Boarding Raider]]. The captain drops [[fog-cloud|Fog Cloud]] on the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]'s gun deck to blind the battery, then the boarding party crosses under cover
- **Limits:** cannot win a sustained gun duel and already sits inside the Uncertainty's killing geometry. Every pirate carries the Snatch, Pass the Prize, and extraction abilities. They fight to steal, not to kill
- **Extraction:** the Knife's Wake's ship's boat trails on a line 50 ft. aft of the boarding point. A pirate reaching it with the prize must survive until the start of its next turn before escaping over the side

## Pressure

- **Now:** she holds the angle at fifty yards
- **Next:** the exchange begins on whoever fires first
- **Default trajectory:** against a ready ship, her captain breaks off and runs dark for the back channels

## Open Vectors

Prep coverage only.

- **Gun exchange:** she cannot survive it. Short, decisive
- **Repelling boarders:** if she closes anyway, the searched-cabins behavior surfaces
- **Taking her:** prisoners. The captain's retainer terms (a third up front, a Calveno factor, "a small old thing that spins") are what interrogation can yield
- **Letting her run:** she marks the Uncertainty as a ship that was ready

## Escalation

1. The captain drops Fog Cloud on the Uncertainty's gun deck; the boarding party crosses under cover
2. The bosun and raider hit the bearer's position: grapple, prone, Shoulder Check defenders aside. Cutpurses approach from different angles
3. A cutpurse Snatches (advantage via Expert Thief). Captain's Orders grants an immediate second attempt or a Pass if the first lands
4. Prize secured: the captain calls "Forget them!" and every pirate disengages toward the trailing boat. The captain spends [[misty-step|Misty Step]], Fog Cloud, or [[invisibility|Invisibility]] to cover the retreat
5. If the prize is never taken or the crew loses two: the captain calls the break-off and the Knife's Wake runs dark for the back channels

## Fallout

- **Success:** a beaten or taken pirate cutter, and possibly the retainer's terms in the party's hands
- **Complication:** she strikes or burns before anyone searches the strongbox, the terms half-learned from a dying mate
- **Setback:** boarders reach the cabins and turn out the crew's belongings. Something goes over the side toward the searched thing
- **Bypass:** she reads the math early, runs, and the retainer stays out there with every other hull the factor paid

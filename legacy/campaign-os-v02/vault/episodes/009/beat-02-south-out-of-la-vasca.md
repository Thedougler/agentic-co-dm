## Hook

The [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] comes off the [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]] cradle ahead of Cobb's own estimate. The season's first real southerly fills in behind them. Festival marks still cling to [[calveno|Calveno]], damp crater-side streets and a chalked outline of Otar washed off the Waveservant Shrine wall. Any last Calveno business closes this morning. By evening the headland is a silhouette.

**The playable pressure is the heading choice.** Three live destinations south into [[midchain|the Midchain]]: [[sparhold|Sparhold]] (4 days south, nearest), [[kalowe|Kalowe]] (5 days southwest), [[fathomrush|Fathomrush]] (6 to 7 days east). The captives taken in the Calveno raid remain unreturned, 197 Calveno people unaccounted for between [[karath|Karath]] and the storm, and every day of season hardens the southerly. The choice commits days of water, not the story: the field is open whichever heading they take.

## Player Gravity

- Whole crew: returning the captured Calveno citizens (established s07 to s08, the campaign's live driving motivation) → every heading leads toward the captives' trail, but each burns different days and opens different ground
- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]: assembling a new crew and fleet, and this crew signed on at Calveno → the people aboard are his recruitment, and the town's gratitude or disappointment rides on what happens next
- [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]: his strategic read on [[grung|Grung]] leadership and the raids → the heading choice determines which Grung-controlled water they enter first and what intelligence he can act on

## First Look

- The southerly is filling in and the season is turning, fair wind today, narrowing windows after
- [[sparhold|Sparhold]] (4 days), [[kalowe|Kalowe]] (5 days), [[fathomrush|Fathomrush]] (6 to 7 days)
- Last-business windows are closing in La Vasca, anything undone ashore stays undone
- The crew that signed on for the Otar mess is now aboard

## Pressure

- **Now:** departure today rides the fair wind
- **Next:** the strait's weather worsens and the captives' trail cools

## Open Vectors

Prep coverage for the DM.

- **Last errands ashore:** Calveno pages support any final business in town before departure
- **Heading south for Sparhold:** nearest destination, natural staging point for northern-Midchain water
- **Heading southwest for Kalowe:** [[chain-council|Chain Council]] water, political leverage and information
- **Heading east for Fathomrush:** salvage hub with trade contacts and a different angle on the captives' trail

## If Unanswered

The Uncertainty stays cradled while the season turns. Calveno's own recovery efforts proceed without the one ship whose crew the town credits. The southerly window closes within days, adding transit time to every possible heading.

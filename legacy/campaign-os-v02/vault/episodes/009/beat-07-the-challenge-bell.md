# The Challenge Bell

> [!narration]  
> _A bell tolls somewhere inside [[sparhold|Sparhold]]'s walls as the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] rounds the point. Slow and heavy. One stroke every few seconds._
> 
> _The harbor beyond is packed gunwale to gunwale, masts crowding the skyline beneath the yards climbing the hillside. Fresh-cut spar timber hangs thick on the air, sharp with sap._
> 
> _Past the main dock, outside the watch hall, four men rake a circle of sand smooth and hammer rope stakes into the ground around it._
> 
> _Someone chalks two names onto the market slate in broad white letters._

> _Before the chalk is dry, a dockhand reads them aloud and starts calling odds to the crew gathering behind him._*

## At a Glance

- **Pattern:** arrival into a town organized around something it cannot change.
- **Delta:** a runner posts a chalked notice on the market slate. [[house-renzetti|House Renzetti]] has rung the challenge bell against [[house-kailani|House Kailani]]. Each house has named a champion. The duel is in the betting ring outside the watch hall at the third morning bell. Whichever house wins the ring holds [[sparhold|Sparhold]], and Sparhold holds the timber yards, the northern water, and every answer the party came for.
- **Pressure:** the duel is tomorrow morning. Everything the party wants from Sparhold routes through a town holding its breath.

## Player Gravity

- Whole crew: [[sparhold|Sparhold]] is the nearest staging port for [[midchain-north|northern Midchain]] water toward [[karath|Karath]] and [[aruhe|Aruhe]]. Who rules it decides whether the port helps.
- [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]: a fortress-market run on caste and levy labor is his architecture-of-exploitation read.

## Situation

The [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] clears [[sparhold|Sparhold]]'s harbor mouth on day 4. Slow, evenly spaced tolls carry from inside the walls, ceremonial and definitive. Dock crews perform the practiced not-hearing of people who already know what it means.

## Actors

- [[toa-kailani|Toa Kailani]]. **Wants:** the ring to settle what negotiation could not. **Knows:** why the bell rang. **Constrained by:** the challenge law's forms. **Without PC intervention:** prepares Rangi.
- [[ottavia-renzetti|Ottavia Renzetti]]. **Wants:** to see Renzetti rule Sparhold. **Knows:** what the argument in private cost. **Constrained by:** a champion who did not want the ring. **Without PC intervention:** carries it to the third morning bell.

## What Can Change

- The party's read of both houses before the ring.
- Lodging, victualing, and dock standing negotiated in a town mid-crisis.
- Word of why the bell rang, the timber question, the [[beat-09-the-green-cut-ledger|Green Cut]] rumor underneath.

## Surface

The dock, the market slate, the betting ring (crews rake and rope it), either house's people at work, the harbor talk.

## Conditional Reactions

- **If the party asks either house directly:** each gives its own version of why the bell rang.
- **If they place bets or take sides publicly:** the town notices and the chosen house remembers.
- **If they ignore the whole affair:** the duel happens at the third morning bell regardless (beat-08).

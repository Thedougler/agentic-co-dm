## Situation

Third day of the [[e09-run-guide-kalowe|Kalowe]] crossing, five days southwest of [[calveno|Calveno]] into open [[midchain|Midchain]] water, chart confidence thinning. A sail appears on the southern horizon and holds station: neither closing nor falling away. The dock sailors call the red hull the *[[dead-lady|Dead Lady]]* in the tone people use for things they want to believe are rumour. The [[waveservants|Waveservants]]' tribute enforcer, taking boats whole against [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s unpaid Pearl debt.

The tribute mark is on the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]'s hull; the pennant of a ship out of Calveno with Waveservant Shrine record flies beside it. The Dead Lady is waiting to see whether it needs to do anything.

> [!mechanic]
> **Tribute enforcement, the Dead Lady.** The Dead Lady acts on standing, not appetite. A hull whose tribute mark and shrine record are current draws a day of observation, then she falls off east. A hull whose standing fails (lapsed tribute, defiance on record, an unpaid Pearl debt called in) draws collection. Collection is not a fight: the Dead Lady takes boats whole, crew and all. No known crew has survived collection to report what it looks like from the deck.
>
> **Provocation triggers:** changing course toward the red hull or hailing a challenge counts. Running out a loaded ballista reads the same way, and a visible course feint collapses the distance on the Dead Lady's schedule. Any provocation collapses the observation distance on the Dead Lady's terms. Running from a hull whose mark is good is not provocation but gets recorded against the standing.
>
> **Today's standing:** the tribute mark is current. The shrine record flies. The Pearl debt is outstanding but not yet called in. She watches. She does not act. Today.

## Player Gravity

- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]: the debt is his; [[the-red-wake|the Red Wake]] rides his hull's tribute mark and the five captains' voices in his head know what the red sail is
- Whole crew: a hull that takes boats whole, holding station off their quarter for a day

## Active Force

- **Intent:** verify the marked hull's standing; collect if standing fails
- **Means:** a hull that takes boats whole, crew and all; patience
- **Limits:** the tribute mark and shrine record are, today, in order; it acts on standing, not appetite

## Pressure

- **Now:** the sail holds station and every glass raised shows it unmoved
- **Next:** a course change toward it or a hailed challenge changes what the hull decides
- **Default trajectory:** a full day under its glass, then east and gone

## Open Vectors

Prep coverage for the DM.

- **Holding course:** the day of dread, and what it does to a crew
- **Running:** the Dead Lady does not chase a ship whose mark is good, but now it has seen them run
- **Closing to hail:** nobody hails the Dead Lady; what comes back is a silence with a shape
- **Delmar's knowledge:** the voices in his head know this ship. What he asks them, and what it costs to ask

## Escalation

1. The sail noticed on the southern horizon
2. The day under watch, every glass confirms it unmoved
3. Any provocation collapses the distance on the Dead Lady's terms
4. Boats taken whole: the ledger settles itself

## Fallout

- **Success:** the watch passes and the crew knows the red hull monitors the debt
- **Complication:** the watch passes but something aboard (a raised gun, a course feint) gets recorded against the standing
- **Setback:** provocation, and something that does not miss begins hunting them across open water
- **Bypass:** a wide course adds a day to the crossing and the sail appears again anyway, one horizon over

## Handoff

**Likely:** development, beat-12 (Kalowe docks arrival), the watch behind them and the debt's weight carried forward

**Alternate:** provocation transforms the watch into active collection. The crossing becomes a Pursuit with no prepared escape

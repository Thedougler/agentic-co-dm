# The Compass Loses Interest

> [!narration]
> *The bearing sits two degrees off the star, checked once against a sky that has not shifted since the last fix, then checked a second time against the same sky. The star is right. The needle drifts.*
>
> *Past the [[thoon-adar|Thoon Adar]] gap the compass keeps drifting, four degrees, then eight, further from true with each fix, under clear skies and a wind that hasn't turned all watch. Beneath the hull the current bears toward the deepest part of the channel, a pull the old sailing directions mention in the margins and no chart has ever drawn.*
>
> *The needle hangs under the lamp, still finding new degrees, still east.*

## At a Glance

- **Pattern:** instruments failing in a way that is information
- **Delta:** the eastern water at the chart's edge has its own relationship with instruments. The current under the surface moves toward the deepest part of the channel regardless of wind or moon, and [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] feels something paying attention inside the eastern deeps. What paid attention in the Maw when the [[vestra|Vestra]] sank and what stirs out here are not the same thing, but they recognize each other across the distance the way old wounds know when the weather changes. He feels it before the compass shows it and cannot name it without naming his patron.
- **Pressure:** the navigation problem lands at night in patrol-empty water with no second opinion

## Situation

First leg of the eastern crossing toward [[fathomrush|Fathomrush]], six to seven days east of [[calveno|Calveno]]. **Past the [[thoon-adar|Thoon Adar]] gap the compass loses interest and the current makes its own decisions.** Old pilots mark this water on sailing directions passed hand to hand and never filed.

The drift is gradual, a bearing checked against a star that is slightly wrong, checked again to confirm the star has not moved. The stars have not moved. The needle has drifted two degrees, then four, then eight, toward [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]]'s approaches.

> [!mechanic]
> **Compass drift, navigation hazard.** Past the Thoon Adar gap, the compass needle pulls east toward The Drowned Maw. Drift doubles each watch: 2° then 4° then 8°. An uncorrected course carries the hull toward the Maw approaches over two watches of night sailing.
>
> **Correction:** bear north, trust stars over needle. The needle steadies once the heading corrects. A full night uncorrected costs a day of backtracking.
>
> **Countermeasure:** star sights each watch. The compass is unreliable past the gap, and old pilots knew this. The party learns the water's rule or pays in distance.

## Conditional Reactions

- **If they trust the stars and bear north:** the needle steadies and the leg completes
- **If they follow the needle:** hours of quiet distance toward the Maw approaches before anyone checks a star; the correction costs a day
- **If Perrin speaks:** the crew gains a warning no chart carries, and Perrin has named a thing he cannot fully explain
- **If he stays silent:** the knowledge rides with him, quiet, uncalled, waiting

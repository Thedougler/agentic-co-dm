# Session 01: Crissdalynn

- [x] Ran

> [!dm]
> Address [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] directly. Opens with her flying escort above the [[saltwright|Saltwright]], forty feet up, calm but not peaceful water below.

Full DM beat-script for Crissdalynn's introduction: flying above the Saltwright, then descending to the wheel to meet [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]] and spot the Dravosi cutter. session-01.md's own Scene Log is silent on this beat entirely (it opens at the hold ambush); the backstory facts this scene stages (the **The Drowned Maw** mapping pilgrimage, the Red Lady's loss, Fisk pulled clear, the Saltwright's rescue) are already absorbed onto [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]] § Overview, [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] § Player-Known, and [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]] § Player-Known. ## Opening

> [!read-aloud]
> The wind finds you at forty feet, pressing under your wings in long even pulses. Below you, the [[saltwright|Saltwright]] moves slow through the channel, cargo-heavy, her lanterns swaying. The sea spreads wide in every direction, and it is calm. Not peaceful.
>
> The [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]] trained you to record exactly what you see. Every [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinel]] who comes out of that basalt rock carries two things: the discipline to observe without flinching, and a task their master set before sending them into the world. Not a suggestion. A task. Your master [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]] sent you to map the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]] from inside a storm, a task given at fourteen, after he caught you flying lazy circles when you should have been at your post.
>
> You were close. Two years aboard the [[red-lady|Red Lady]] with [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk's]] fleet, the charts in your satchel filling in slowly, the Maw mapped to its southern arc and most of its eastern rim. Then five days ago the Maw opened, and all five ships went down together. You pulled Fisk clear and the two of you surfaced among the wreckage. The rest did not.
>
> The Saltwright found you two days ago. The charts are still in the satchel.
>
> Could you describe what they see: the Saltwright from up here, the channel ahead, the dark?

**[Let her go. When she settles:]**

## Running It

### Beat: At the Wheel, Beaumont

> [!read-aloud]
> At the wheel below stands the Tortle captain who found you and Fisk on the driftwood two days ago and pulled you aboard without a question or a second thought.
>
> He goes by Beaumont. An old alligator snapping Tortle, shell the colour of river mud and heavily ridged, fist-sized metal plate bolted to the upper-right panel, chipped spike where something landed hard once. One eye patched in sun-bleached leather. On his left shoulder, a small monkey called [[bisou|Bisou]] treats the theft of his pipe as a full-time occupation.

**Beaumont:** *"Mais. Anything on the water to the east? Current runs strange past the reef."*

> [!check] Perception — The Cutter
> DC 12; flavour only, there is a cutter regardless of the result.
> **DC 12:** a Dravosi patrol cutter, running slow. Standard configuration, nothing wrong from up here.
> **DC 16:** in poor wear and maintenance: chipped paint, one running light out, rigging off, definitely not standard-issue anymore. Moving on an intercept heading with the Saltwright, with the unhurried confidence of something that commands obedience.

**Beaumont:** *"Mm. Probably just old Norrington, not to worry."*

> [!dm]
> Give Crissdalynn a moment at the wheel. Beaumont is comfortable with silence, he doesn't fill it.

> [!dm]
> If she asks about the route: three days past [[kalowe|Kalowe]], two past [[aldenmere|Aldenmere]], [[calders-tooth|Calder's Tooth]] still ahead.

> [!dm]
> If she asks about Norrington: a shrug, *"Good man. Retired, apparently."* He doesn't believe it.

> [!dm]
> If she pushes on the cutter's heading: *"Could be a drill. Crown runs them sometimes."* He lights his pipe; Bisou steals it and he takes it back without looking, not like a man who thinks this is a drill.

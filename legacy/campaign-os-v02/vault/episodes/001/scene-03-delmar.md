# Session 01: Delmar's Introduction

- [x] Ran

Full DM beat-script for [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]'s introduction on the [[saltwright|Saltwright]]'s deck, played as prepped (source `table_status: complete`). The underlying backstory facts (Pearl of Souls theft, the fleet's loss, Crissdalynn's rescue) are already absorbed onto [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]'s own history section. This page preserves the staged read-aloud address, the Bisou coin-trick check design, and the Beaumont/Norrington exchange, none of which appears there.

## Opening

Address Delmar.

> [!read-aloud]
> You have been leaning against that rail for ten minutes already. The night air is warm and thick with salt, and the Saltwright moves slow beneath your feet. The red coat with the gold epaulettes, Admiral's colours.
>
> Five days ago you were commanding five ships into the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]] on commission from the [[chain-council|Chain Council]]. A benefactor had come to the Council with a proposition: lift a relic from a shrine on [[vel-orn|Vel Orn]] and it would protect the [[midchain|Midchain]] waters from her wrath. The Council believed it. They hired you. You went in clean, lifted the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl]], and made the outbound tide.
>
> Then the [[the-drowned-maw|Maw]] opened. The name proved true: a chasm, wide and deep, and all five ships gone before anyone understood what was happening. You heard voices as the hull came apart around you. You'd be dead in that water if not for [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]], who found you in the wreckage and got you onto driftwood. The Saltwright found you both two days later.
>
> Could you tell us about who we see leaning on that rail?

*[Let them finish.]*

## Running It

### The Coin Trick

> [!read-aloud]
> The night has been quiet. Then [[bisou|Bisou]], Beaumont's monkey, noticed the coin you've been turning between your fingers.
>
> Bisou has been on your arm for three minutes now, slack-jawed. Every time he reaches for the coin, the coin is somewhere else. Between two fingers. Gone. The other hand. Gone again. Bisou lunges, misses, makes a sound, and tries again.

> [!check] Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) — The Coin Trick
> DC 10; flavour only, Bisou is not getting the coin regardless of the result. The result determines how decisively Delmar wins.
> **DC 10:** Bisou tracks the correct hand. It knows. Stands with one finger at Fisk's closed fist, not lunging anymore, just waiting with immovable certainty. From the wheel, [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont]]'s shoulders are shaking.
> **DC 15:** Bisou stares at both hands. Then at Fisk's face. Then back at the hands. Makes a low, baffled sound, like a question asked aloud.
> **DC 20:** Bisou looks at both hands. Looks at Fisk. Then with great dignity turns its back, tucks its chin, and refuses to acknowledge that any of this is happening. It has decided the coin does not exist. From the wheel, Beaumont is no longer looking away.

**Beaumont**: *"Bisou. Leave the man alone, cher."*

[Bisou retreats, pointedly, toward the pocket the coin disappeared into. If Delmar asks Beaumont about the cutter: *"Probably just Norrington."* ([[norrington-kingsly|Norrington Kingsly]].) If he pushes (and a man who commanded five ships probably will), Beaumont gives him a look and a shrug. *"Different face on the same route."* He won't say more out here. The deck falls silent.]

> [!read-aloud]
> Along the rail toward the bow, the rest of the deck has been running a different competition for the last hour. Spears and lines. No one winning yet.

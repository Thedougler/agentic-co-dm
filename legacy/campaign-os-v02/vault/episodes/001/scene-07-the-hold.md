# Session 01: The Hold

- [x] Ran

DM beat-script for Beat 7. [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont]] sends Delmar and Crissdalynn below before Rook's inspection boat comes alongside, staging the shakedown negotiation overheard from the hold and the choice point that opens the boarding fight. The played outcome (party ambushed the boarding crew from hiding rather than discover themselves discovered) is already landed on [[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]] and [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]]. This page preserves the staged dialogue, hiding-zone setup, and NPC-direction notes that produced it, not restated there.

## Opening

On deck. Address Delmar and Crissdalynn.

Beaumont's voice drops a register when he speaks again; the ease still there, something beneath it. *"Passenger, go below through the port hatch, you and the bird both."* Patrol boat, won't take long. If pushed on whether it's dangerous: *"Mais, I run this route eleven years; they know the [[saltwright|Saltwright]], they know me. Go below and let me handle it, cher. Ol' Rook doesn't worry."*

Delmar goes first, the ladder steep, the hold swallowing him whole; brine, old fish, amber lantern light barely reaching the far corners. Crissdalynn comes down behind, six feet of headroom, fits only just.

> [!dm]
> Salt barrels line the starboard side, fish crates stack against the port bulkhead (best cover: deepest shadow, tightest lanes), grain sacks lash loosely at the aft wall. Anyone declaring stealth does it now, before Crissdalynn comes down.

*[Let them settle.]*

## Running It

### The Bird Moment

> [!dm]
> Jean-Claude is afraid of birds, instinctive, not chosen: the moment Crissdalynn descends, he presses flat against the farthest bulkhead, hand on whatever weapon is closest; Crissdalynn doesn't know why, but Delmar sees it first. Give it a beat, let Jean-Claude's player decide how they handle the six-foot [[aarakocra|Aarakocra]] now sharing their hiding spot in the dark, and ask for a Stealth check with disadvantage if the group is being stealthy. What matters is the held breath: two strangers in a dark hold, one barely keeping it together, everyone listening to boots cross the deck overhead, five people in this hold, nobody planned it. Give it one beat before the ship moves.

### The Shakedown

A groan cuts through from outside the hull: low, structural, timber under pressure. The ship moves as another vessel comes alongside in the dark. The Saltwright adjusts to the new weight; iron on rail, rope going taut, the sound traveling through the planking like a hand pressed flat against wood. Boots cross the deck, many sets, unhurried. A voice calls down: clipped, bored.

> [!dm]
> This is [[barnaby-rook|Rook]]. He's above the hatch, not yet visible. Play him flat and practiced: a man who runs this shakedown on every route and has stopped finding it interesting. The boredom is the threat.

**Rook**: *"Manifest. Mr Sel."*

**Beaumont** (through the planking, unhurried): *"Right here, cher. Salt, dried fish, cloth out of [[kalowe|Kalowe]]. Eleven years on this route, ya'll should remember? Where's Norrington?"*

A beat. *"Officer Kingsly has retired."* Beaumont's breath stills for a moment. **"Mm."**

Hushed orders, footsteps spreading across the deck above. *"Where's your strongbox?"*

**Beaumont**: *"Non. I keep what I need on me."*

*"Merchant Naval Code c147b. Merchant vessels must securely lock all valuables and contain a working strongbox. You're deficient."* A small pause. He's a man who has memorised this line and finds it useful. *"It's a safety issue."*

**Beaumont**: *"I don't know anything about that, cher. I run the same route I always run."*

*"New policy."* Footsteps shift across the deck, slow, toward the wheel and Beaumont. *"Forty gold clears it tonight, or we hold the cargo at [[calveno|Calveno]] while we process the paperwork. That could take some time."*

Long enough to hear the water against the hull. Long enough to hear [[bisou|Bisou]] make one small sound above.

**Beaumont**: *"Don't play now, cher."*

**Rook**: *"Then we'll take a look below. See what else you're carrying."*

Above, Beaumont's voice dies. The footsteps move toward the hatch.

## Choice Point

*What do you do?*

> [!dm]
> Party options: strike before the crew reaches the bottom, emerge and learn Rook's intent, or signal Beaumont to buy time. All are viable. The hatch opens in one round.

**Played:** the party ambushed from the hiding zones above. ["hold ambush," full account on [[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]] and [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]]]. This scene ends where that fight begins.

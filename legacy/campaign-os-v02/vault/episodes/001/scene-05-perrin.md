# Session 01: Beat 5: Perrin

- [x] Ran

> [!dm]
> Perrin-only beat, set below decks in the [[saltwright|Saltwright]] hold before the boarding fight. The Vestra backstory beats here (grandfather's ship, built by [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]]'s late husband, lost to something massive from the deep) are already absorbed onto [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] § Core drive / Current pressure. This page preserves the staged read-aloud text, the stowaway-detection Perception check, and the transition cue into Scene 6; none of these appear in the played recap ([[transcript|session 01 transcript]]), which opens later, already mid-ambush.

## Opening

> [!read-aloud]
> Below the waterline, the Saltwright is quiet.
>
> Six feet of headroom, and two lanterns turned so far down they give off more warmth than light, a faint amber glow that turns the shadows amber too. Salt barrels along the starboard side, fish crates stacked to the port bulkhead, grain sacks loosely lashed in the back. The smell is brine and old wood and time, the deep-layered damp of a hull that has spent a decade below the waterline, and underneath it the cold mineral smell of deep water close, held back by nothing but two inches of planking.
>
> Close down here. Dark. Private.

*[Address Perrin directly.]*

> [!read-aloud]
> You are one of the [[rattkin|Rattkin]]. The Passage runs through every port in the [[verdant-scatter|Scatter]] on routes no colonial chart has captured. The Dravosi Crown keeps standing bounties on your kind, rarely enforced, always present.
>
> The [[vestra|Vestra]] was your grandfather's ship. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw's]] husband built her, ran her, and left her to the family when he died. You inherited the route and everything that came with it. Three days ago something came up from the deep water over the Maw and took her apart. Something large and deliberate. You went into the water with nothing but your grandfather's cloak on your back. You woke up on the beach of [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]], a voice still echoing somewhere distant, and a blade in your hand you don't remember picking up.
>
> The Saltwright was in the harbour. You found the hold before anyone found you, and the captain doesn't know you're here. On your way down, you helped yourself to a couple of loose gems from a crate near the gangway.
>
> Could you show us who's hiding in this hold, and where?

*[Let the player go.]*

## Running It

### Beat: Something at the Hull

> [!dm]
> Sounds carry through the planking: boots, rope work, the occasional voice, the fishing competition. If Perrin explores, the false panel in the aft wall latches shut (well-made), and the gems from the gangway crate are the only find. If they ask about a better hiding spot, this is the only one. Let them settle, then:

> [!read-aloud]
> Then, against the hull, something lands.
>
> Not a wave. Not the vessel settling. A weight, deliberate and controlled, pressing against the outside of the planking at waterline level, and then going perfectly still.

> [!check] Perception — The Weight Against the Hull
> DC 12; flavour only, something is out there regardless of the result.
>
> **DC 12:** large, warm-blooded, and clinging to the hull. It came from the water and does not struggle.
> **DC 16:** it moved along the outside of the hull before it stopped, feeling its way. Whatever sits there, it chose this spot specifically.
> **DC 20:** a smell drifts through the gap in the planking above it (amphibious, but not fish, not salt). Something different that clears your sinuses.

> [!read-aloud]
> In the hold, Perrin holds still. On the outside of the hull, whatever found this ship does the same.

Transition to [[scene-06-jean-claude|scene 6 (Jean-Claude)]].

## Enemy Roster

No enemy in this scene. This is a stealth/sensory beat, not a combat encounter. The thing outside the hull is Jean-Claude, introduced properly in Scene 6.

## Challenge Calibration

Not applicable (no combat). See Skill Track above for this scene's one check.

## Terrain

The Saltwright's cargo hold: six feet of headroom, salt barrels to starboard, fish crates to port, grain sacks aft, two lanterns turned low. A latched false panel in the aft wall (contents unknown, Beaumont's). Purely descriptive, with no mechanical terrain effect.

## Tactical Notes

Not a combat; the "tactics" are DM pacing instructions. Let Perrin settle into the hold before the hull-contact beat lands. Keep the Perception check's DC tiers additive rather than gating. Cut to Scene 6 the moment both sides go still.

## Raising the Stakes

A player who pushes the Perception roll to its top tier above walks away already knowing the thing outside is amphibious and unusual-smelling, ahead of Jean-Claude's proper introduction next scene.

## Stakes

Nothing changes in the world from this scene alone. It stages Perrin's introduction, the Vestra backstory reveal, and the transition into first contact with Jean-Claude (Scene 6).

## If Ignored

The DM triggers this beat. The party cannot decline it.

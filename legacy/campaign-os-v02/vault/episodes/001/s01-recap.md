# Session 01: The Boarding of the Saltwright

## Recap

The hold of the [[saltwright|Saltwright]] was dark and close, smelling of bilge water and shrimp, and the party waited in it like a held breath. Above, [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont]]'s voice carried across the deck, easy and unhurried, keeping [[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]]'s attention fixed at the wheel while three of his crew descended the ladder into the dark below.

The Enforcer came first. The crew had taken to calling him [[capn-gorgeous|Cap'n Gorgeous]], and the name wasn't wrong. Tall, broadly built, golden hair that caught even the thin light of the hold. He walked in without hesitation. The combined assault of a poisoned arrow from [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]], a musket shot, and shattered knee cap answered him. The crack of black powder in that enclosed space was enormous, and for a ringing, disorienting moment both [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] and [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] lost the world to white noise. The alarm went up. But [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] had laid a [[minor-illusion|Minor Illusion]] across the doorway they were sheltering behind, and Crissdalynn worked fast, dragging Cap'n Gorgeous back through it before anyone could place the sound. The two deckhands came down cautious, uncertain, not knowing exactly what they were walking toward. They found out. Neither of them made it back up.

Delmar knelt at the foot of the ladder and Crissdalynn didn't slow down. She hit his hands at a run and he launched her up through the hatch like a crack of thunder. Her wings snapped open the moment she cleared the weather deck and sent the two nearest Dravosi sprawling. Perrin and Jean-Claude emerged through a gap in the decking at a second angle, catching the remaining crew between them. Over quickly. Both Dravosi Enforcers died in the press of it.

When the last [[dravosi-deckhand|Dravosi Deckhand]], a young man maybe nineteen with frightened eyes, dropped his sword and went still, Rook's voice cut across the deck from the gangplank.

> *"I'll shoot you myself for that, you turncoat."*

Delmar looked at the boy. "Would you rather fight for men who'll threaten to shoot you, or men who'll protect you?"

[[geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey Draves]] thought about it for about as long as it took him to answer. *"I'll do whatever keeps me alive today."*

He picked the sword back up and joined the fight. He is, by the current count, the party's entire crew.

The gangplank between the Saltwright and the [[uncertainty|HCS Surety]] was where things got complicated. Jean-Claude opened with the [[flask|Flask]] of Endless Water and the blast caught Rook full in the chest, throwing him off the plank and buying the party precious seconds to deal with the Enforcer and the Alchemist before she could rejoin.

The Enforcer fell. The Alchemist took enough punishment to make her desperate, and desperation made her reach for the new canister on her bandolier. The gas rolled out across the deck and caught Jean-Claude and Perrin both. They resisted it. Crissdalynn answered with [[gust|Gust]] and sent the cloud rolling back toward the Alchemist.

Jean-Claude placed it in the same moment the fumes reached him. The toxin was [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s. Unmistakably hers. His sister is supplying the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] with [[grung|Grung]]-made toxin, and the proof drifted across the decking between two ships in the [[shattered-sea|Shattered Sea]]. He said nothing. The fight continued.

The Alchemist fell. Rook hauled himself back over the rail, raised his flintlock at Beaumont, and fired. The round struck the ridged plate of Beaumont's salvaged shell at his shoulder and spun away into the water.

> *"Boy, I shit out harder things than you every mornin'."*

That's where we left it.

## Scenes

### Nona's War Table *(Grung Raid)*

*Pull: all PCs. [[register|Register]]: social.* *Available: session opens here. Two beats: second-pass interrogation, then planning.*

**What's true:** [[felix-aho|Felix Aho]] is in the next room with [[ruk|Ruk]]. He already gave the Session 04 baseline, but his green-caste conditioning locks him up every time he reaches for red/purple-caste details. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] has four X marks on the sewer map and a barrel count that does not add up, and [[jean-claude-tabarnack|JC]] needs to break through Felix's conditioning to turn "something is missing" into a path underground before anyone at the table realizes how short the timeline really is.

#### Beat 1 — Second-Pass Interrogation

> [!read-aloud]
> The next room is smaller and smells like sweat and lamp oil. Felix sits against the far wall with his knees drawn up, Ruk's shadow filling the doorway behind him. His hands are still. His eyes are not.

| Felix at table | Use |
|---|---|
| State | Green-caste labourer; not hostile, not withholding, conditioned |
| Handle | Nervous witness questioned by the FBI: earnest, overwhelmed, apologetic |
| Tell | Counts on fingers; draws routes in the air with a shaking hand |
| Pressure | Gentle questions get more; intimidation silences him for 1d4 minutes |

> [!mechanic]
> **Felix's Conditioning.** Baseline intel is already earned. Roll only for locked red/purple details.
>
> - DC 10 to 12 Persuasion or Insight: guide him through authority-layer details. Failure: the detail stays locked. The crew can try again later or find another angle in.
> - [[jean-claude-tabarnack|JC]] speaking Grung or naming defector experience: lower DCs by 2.
> - Intimidation succeeds on facts but shuts him down for 1d4 minutes afterward.

**Second-pass reveals (in order of accessibility):**

**Free recap — already earned in Session 04; do not make them roll again:**

- He carried blackpowder barrels to four underground sites, their locations relative to surface landmarks (“under the yards,” “near the bridge drain,” “the one by the low hatch,” “close to the harbour wall”).
- Purple garrisons and red leaders hold positions somewhere in the network. Exact sentry composition is not earned yet.
- Timeline: "Two days. They said two days from when the boats came in." (This estimate is wrong — the acceleration after Le Paludi shortened it to ~1.5 days, but Felix doesn't know that.)
- Festival timing: "The purple said the noise would cover everything."

> [!check] Persuasion — Sentries and Purple Boats
> DC 10.
> **Success:** his conditioning flickers as he crosses into purple-caste territory, then breaks. "A blue and two greens hold each work site. The blue carries the whistle, and the greens carry the barrels." He hesitates, noticing because greens load boats and count cargo, then keeps going: "There were a lot of boats, more than four sites need, and most of them were purple-only, crewed by purple alone." He pauses. "Purple-only boats mean combat crews, not cargo work. That's a raid." This tells [[jean-claude-tabarnack|JC]] and the rest of the crew that the operational footprint runs larger than four detonation sites, that purple-only vessels are combat-rated, and that the scale points toward mass extraction, not sabotage.
> **Failure:** his conditioning holds. He apologizes and offers nothing new. The crew can try again later.

> [!check] Persuasion — The Barrel Count
> DC 12; Felix volunteers this instead if the crew is patient and gentle.
> **Success:** he has been doing the arithmetic in his head since they captured him, and the numbers bother him more than the interrogation itself. "I counted barrels. I carried them. Thirty-two barrels through the tunnels, over three days. Six to the first site, six to the second, six to the third, four to the fourth. That's twenty-two." He holds up his fingers. "Where did the other ten go?" He does not know where the extra powder went, only that it went somewhere he was not allowed to go: "They rotate greens out before you see too much. I never went past the junction where the old marks are." This tells the crew a 5th site exists. Felix can't locate it, but the surplus blackpowder went somewhere underground green-caste labourers never see.
> **Failure:** he keeps circling the number in his head without saying it aloud. Press again later, or let him volunteer it once he trusts the room.

> [!check] Insight — The Circle Site
> DC 10; available after the two checks above, or the moment JC asks about “the circle site” directly.
> **Success:** he's telling the truth. He trembles as the conditioning breaks, not from pressure but because the fear underneath is stronger. "The purples call it 'the circle site.' I don't know where it sits, or what it does. They rotate us out before we get close." He stops, swallows. "I heard screaming from that direction once. Not pain screaming. Something else." He fears the circle site worse than the whip or the water, and what he remembers is a specific sound, one that should never have surfaced from underground.
> **Failure:** the name and the fear come through, but the crew can't tell whether it's exaggeration or something real. Press with Insight again once trust is higher.

> [!dm]
> [[felix-aho|Felix]] cannot locate the circle site. It's above green-caste clearance. Use the name and barrel count to point at a fifth position and red-caste trail marks in Rooms 2, 5, and 6. If the crew does the math, confirm a hidden site before descent.

**Felix's ask:** one month of protection. Somewhere the purple-caste cannot find him. He will not go back underground. He will draw everything he remembers on the map if they give him chalk. *(He draws with his left hand, shaking. The marks are precise.)*

#### Beat 2 — Nona's War Table

After the second pass, [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] tightens the map. She works fast — cross-referencing Felix's landmarks with [[warren|Warren]] knowledge of the tunnel system. Four X marks are already in charcoal. The question mark where Felix's surplus powder points gets circled twice.

**NPC — [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]:** hasn't slept, and she worked out Felix's barrel count before the crew did. The count leaves ten barrels unaccounted for, and four sites reading as a distraction pattern hides a real fifth target. If the crew asks how she knows:

*Nona*: Because I would.

*Wants*: the crew underground today. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|JC]] leads — he reads the signs. *Reveals*: three entry points into the collector system ([[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] hatch, Bridge far hatch, [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]] tidal passage). The Warren can seal maintenance hatches behind them to prevent Grung reinforcements — but only if Nona knows which hatches.

**Nona's resources — what she offers:**

> [!dm]
> Nona lays this out after the intel is on the map. This is a resource allocation beat: they decide what support to take and what trade-offs to accept. Let them discuss without pushing a choice.

| Resource | What it provides | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Enzo | CR 5 operative. Quiet, lethal, follows orders. Bodyguard-quality fighter in the tunnels. Reads threats before they materialize | Pulls Nona's personal security, leaving her exposed at the safe house during the counter-operation |
| Black-Jaw muscle (2 or 3 Warren fighters) | Extra bodies for covering secondary sites or holding a cleared position. Loyal, know the tunnels, not soldiers | Splits the crew's force. Warren fighters break if outnumbered and cannot match purple-caste discipline |
| Warren tunnel knowledge | Nona's people know the collector system — shortcut navigation, hatch locations, fallback routes, which tunnels flood at high tide | Baseline — available regardless. The chalk map is the delivery mechanism |
| Seal hatches (Warren runners) | Tell Nona which hatches to close behind them. Prevents Grung reinforcements from entering; each sealed hatch also blocks one extraction lane | Hatches seal both ways — they cannot retreat through a sealed hatch without Warren help reopening it |
| [[master-kyzil\|Kyzil]] (party's own idea — Nona doesn't know him) | Crissdalynn sparred him yesterday; any PC who remembers he's at the [[ponte-bassa\|Ponte Bassa]] can suggest seeking him out. CR 14 monk, already suspicious of underground activity | Costs time before the descent. Nona has no opinion — he is outside her network |

**Decision checklist before they leave:** entry route (Le Paludi hatch / Bridge far hatch / La Vasca tidal passage); hatches to seal behind them; Enzo with party or guarding Nona; Warren fighters assigned to secondary sites or held back; Kyzil briefed, ignored, or saved for reserve; Watch / Canal Commission warned or kept out, and Felix protection promised.

> [!dm]
> If the table lingers on loot, Ruk sets the sewer vials beside the crew's older toxin cache. To non-Grung they look standard; to [[jean-claude-tabarnack|JC]], the preparation matches Simone's compound. No monologue, just evidence in the same room.

**Planning options:**

| Plan | What it requires | What it gains |
|---|---|---|
| Hit the magazines directly | JC leads into the sewer network | Each neutralized magazine removes one detonation site |
| Brief Kyzil first | Someone travels to the [[ponte-bassa\|Ponte Bassa]] | CR 14 ally (accompanies underground or creates surface diversion) |
| Warn the watch or Canal Commission | Someone finds a magistrate or gate sergeant | The watch raises alert, though their intervention threshold stays high during festival week and they may doubt the report |
| Split the crew | Half underground, half topside | Covers more ground while each group weakens individually |
| Take Enzo into the tunnels | Nona agrees and pulls Enzo from her side | CR 5 melee fighter with Interpose reaction (strong in tight corridors) at the cost of leaving Nona unguarded |

**If engaged:** the crew has a plan, resources, and Felix's map. They know the entry points and the sentry pattern, and they know a fifth site exists somewhere. The dungeon begins. **If ignored:** nona does not ask twice. She sends Warren runners into the tunnels, but without JC's Grung expertise, they miss the trail markers and trigger sentries. Some die in the dark. The clock keeps ticking. **Hook out:** kyzil briefing or straight into the sewers.

### Kyzil on the Roof *(Crissdalynn's Pilgrimage / Grung Raid)*

*Pull: [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] (primary), any PC who seeks Kyzil. Register: social / revelation.* *Available: any time the crew seeks [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]] at the [[ponte-bassa|Ponte Bassa]].*

> [!read-aloud]
> Kyzil is on the Ponte Bassa roof where he sparred yesterday, sharpening a dagger on a whetstone. He watches the canal below with absolute stillness and does not turn when the stairs creak.
>
> *Kyzil*: I know why you are here. Something is under this city, and I have felt it for two days.

**What's true:** Kyzil has been independently investigating his “foul wind” instinct — underground vibrations, unusual foot traffic patterns at storm drains, fragments that didn't coalesce into a picture. He was one or two days from acting alone. The crew's intel completes his picture instantly.

**NPC — Kyzil:** his monastic discipline cracks as he processes the scale, and the spar that was play now is not. *Wants:* to protect Calveno and Crissdalynn. *Reveals:* (a) his tactical assessment of the crew — “the drum is your fulcrum. Protect the bard.” (b) if Crissdalynn pushes, the Missing Sentinels: “Three of us went to chart the southern currents, and none returned. [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|The Eyrie]] does not speak of them, and I have carried their names for eleven years.”

> [!dm]
> Give Crissdalynn one quiet Missing Sentinels exchange before tactics. If the session is running long, move it after the dungeon. Kyzil remains available.

**Kyzil's tactical offer (if briefed on the raid):**

| Option | What Kyzil does | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Accompanies underground** | CR 14 monk in the dungeon — see mechanic below for his combat numbers. Solves most combat encounters instantly | The primary chamber becomes manageable. But if Kyzil sees the summoning circle, he will want to destroy it himself — and he is not subtle |
| **Surface diversion** | Positions at the Mercatura with Warren runners. If the detonation fires, he is the first responder at the primary site | Buys time against Otar. Does not help the crew underground |
| **Stays in reserve** | Monitors the Ponte Bassa and intervenes if the situation deteriorates | Safety net. Arrives after round 5 of any surface combat the crew is losing |

> [!mechanic]
> **Kyzil in combat.** 4 attacks per round, returning daggers, DC 19 open palm strike (target fails a CON save or is stunned until the end of Kyzil's next turn). CR 14 — solves most standard combat encounters instantly.

> [!dm]
> Kyzil trivializes sentries and accelerates Phase 1. If he disrupts the circle before Phase 2, Otar never manifests, but [[solange-barret|Solange]] detonates as cover and escapes. Frame the trade-off as certainty of prevention vs. [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]] learning [[jean-claude-tabarnack|JC]] is active.

**If engaged:** Kyzil is an ally. The crew chooses his deployment. **If ignored:** Kyzil acts alone within 24 hours. He enters the tunnels and finds the secondary sites, triggering the alert network and pushing the primary garrison to full readiness. **Hook out:** the sewer descent.

*Pull: [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] (primary), full party. Register: exploratory → combat.* *Available: when the party enters the sewers.*

The session's spine. The crew descends into [[calveno|Calveno]]'s sewer collectors to find and neutralize the blackpowder magazines, across three acts. **Discovery** (Rooms 1 to 3) and **Disruption** (Rooms 4 to 6) turn up the magazines and the sentries guarding them, before **Confrontation** closes the run in the primary chamber. #### Act 1 — Discovery (Rooms 1–3)

**Room 1 (Le Paludi Access Shaft).** Orientation room. The iridescent residue on the ladder rungs is the first toxic hint. Let the crew absorb the shift from festival streets to underground.

> [!check] Athletics — Quiet Descent
> DC 10; the iron ladder into the shaft.
> **Success:** the crew descends without a sound. Sentries deeper in the network stay unaware.
> **Failure:** a rung creaks or a boot scrapes stone. Nothing worse than a moment's tension this early, but note it if the crew is already jumpy.

**Room 2 (Y-Junction).** Hub room. Red-caste trail markers that only JC can read are here. If JC is not present, this clue is invisible. Two more await in Rooms 5 and 6, or the crew encounters an optional canal crocodile.

**Room 3 (Bridge Connector).** Transition and potential chase. 4-ft ceiling after 35 ft punishes mobility. Optional fleeing handler: if she escapes, ALL sentry teams go to heightened alert (they pursue through the tunnels). This is the difference between a stealth run and a combat gauntlet.

#### Act 2 — Disruption (Rooms 4–6)

**Room 4 (Magazine Alpha).** First sentry encounter — teaches the barrel/sentry/poison pattern. The overflow pipe to Room 6 bypasses main corridors. First Hard encounter.

**Room 5 (Magazine Beta).** Incomplete scaffolding — easiest magazine to neutralize (see check below). Builder's notation: "anchor south of M — tie to main." Guarded by [[bazzoth-the-steeped|Bazzoth, the Steeped]] plus 2 Grung. Distracted attention gives the crew advantage on Stealth. Escalated encounter (~4,800 adj. XP) — verify against current crew resources.

> [!check] Athletics — Neutralizing Magazine Beta
> DC 12. Water-soak the exposed barrels.
> **Success:** the crew neutralizes the magazine. No detonation from this site.
> **Failure:** the soak doesn't fully take. They can try again, but they're burning precious time.

**Room 6 (Handler Relay).** The intelligence pivot. Full room key, read-aloud, Ruma's social encounter details, and the check-in clock table live on the dungeon page.

- [[ruma-delacroix|Ruma]]: blue-caste quartermaster. Whistle = all sentries go active defense. If the crew acts first, she cooperates.
- Route map: this shows 5 positions, not 4. Red-caste authority marks the fifth.
- Globe of Invulnerability scroll: under the desk (see mechanic below).
- Hidden passage: behind stacked barrels (see check below).

> [!mechanic]
> **Globe of Invulnerability scroll.** Found under the desk. DC 16 Arcana to activate. Shields a 10-ft radius from spells of 5th level or lower — designed for the detonation. A failed activation consumes the scroll with no effect and the shield does not form.

> [!check] Investigation — The Hidden Passage
> DC 16. Behind stacked barrels lies a passage, unknown even to Ruma.
> **Success:** the crew finds The Passage leading straight to Room 8, unguarded — their best tactical advantage for the climax.
> **Failure:** the barrels look like ordinary storage. Nothing here catches the eye yet.

> [!mechanic]
> **Spoofing Ruma's check-in:** DC 13 Deception/Intimidation/Performance in handler-register Grung; JC has advantage. Captured Ruma can do it if promised believable protection. Failure means the check-in doesn't land. Try again later or skip to active detonation. **T+2h without spoof:** primary garrison goes active defense. **T+8h:** crew finishes rest either way.
>
> **DM:** room 6 offers a long rest (dry, defensible, guarded). Once Ruma cooperates, display the check-in clock on the table. The crew decides: spoof and rest, rest while fortifications multiply, or push depleted.

**Primary path:** With two of three clues, the trail converges toward the Mercatura collector. JC's trail-marker literacy + Ruma's route map make the direction clear.

#### Three Clue Audit — The Primary Site

**Conclusion:** A 5th primary detonation site exists beneath the Mercatura, armed with a summoning circle.

| # | Clue | Room | Mechanic | Without combat? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Builder's notation: “anchor south of M — tie to main” | 5 (incomplete magazine) | Investigation check (below); JC reads automatically | Yes (after disarming) |

### The Primary Chamber *(Grung Raid)*

*Pull: [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]], [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]. Register: combat.* *Available: when the party follows the clues past the main collector.*

The climax encounter: a vaulted collector nexus beneath the Mercatura — roughly 60 × 50 ft, 15-ft ceiling, four drainage channels converging on a central summoning circle. blackpowder fills every crack in the ceiling. [[solange-barret|Solange Barret]] is mid-ritual and will not stop. The fight unfolds across three phases: **garrison** (the crew of the [[saltwright|Saltwright]] fights four purple-caste elites while Solange channels), **detonation** (Solange collapses the ceiling when the garrison falters), and **confrontation** ([[otar-the-foul|Otar the Foul]] erupts through Solange's body into the rubble).

**What's true:** Solange is channeling the summoning ritual. She uses her action each turn to sustain it; she cannot attack, cast offensive spells, or move more than 5 ft from the circle. [[mirror-image|Mirror Image]] already active (3 duplicates). Her reaction is the pressure point: she can [[counterspell|Counterspell]] or Detonate, not both in the same round. Four purple-caste Elite Warriors hold the drainage channel entrances. Standing order: shoot on sight. The hidden passage from Room 6 emerges behind the south scaffolding; the garrison is not watching this direction.

#### The Garrison

> [!mechanic]
> **[[solange-barret|Solange Barret]].** AC 15 (Circle Ward). HP 66. Mirror Image active (3 duplicates). **Channeling:** uses her action each turn to sustain the summoning ritual. Cannot attack or cast offensive spells. Can use her reaction for Counterspell or Detonate. **Poisonous Skin:** DC 12 CON or poisoned on contact. **Circle Ward:** within 10 ft of circle, AC 15, advantage on concentration saves. The summoning circle deflects the detonation blast, so Solange takes no damage from the ceiling collapse.

Mesmerizing Chirr (Recharge 6): DC 12 WIS, stun 1 round. One per drainage channel entrance, 50-ft spread forces individual engagement. They are expendable. Solange does not protect them.
>
> **Difficulty: deadly (5000 adj. XP).** This encounter rewards stealth, planning, and environmental creativity over direct assault.

#### Phase 1 — The Ritual Guard

Solange does not fight. She channels. The four Elite Warriors are the encounter, protecting Solange and the circle using spread-and-Chirr tactics from the standard garrison.

**Hidden passage approach (Route C from Room 6):** The garrison's passive Perception is 12 vs. this direction. The party gains surprise. May let a PC reach Solange before the elites react.

**Terrain (Phase 1):**

- **Summoning circle:** 15-ft diameter. Bright light 10-ft radius, dim light 10 ft beyond. JC's Umbral Sight does not function in the bright zone.
- **Scaffolding:** 10 ft. Elevated positions grant +2 AC vs. melee from below.
- **Drainage channels (×4):** 3 ft deep, 5 ft wide. Difficult terrain. Half cover for prone creatures.
- **blackpowder ceiling:** fire or thunder damage anywhere in the room triggers the full detonation (see Phase 2). The crew of the [[saltwright|Saltwright]] should understand this risk before anyone lights a torch.

> [!check] Arcana — Reading the Circle
> DC 14 identifies an extraplanar summoning; DC 20 or a critical success identifies it as configured for a slaad, unbound.
> **Success:** the party knows this is a summoning ritual, extraplanar in nature.
> **Success by 6+:** the party also learns exactly what it is calling — a slaad, unbound.
> **Failure:** the circle reads as arcane and dangerous, but its purpose stays unclear.

> [!mechanic]
> **Disrupting the circle.** One minute of careful work severs it safely, or DC 18 Arcana as an action severs a key resonance line immediately. Failure on the Arcana attempt: the resonance line holds; the action is lost and the one-minute safe method is still available.

The scaffolding offers vertical advantage.

> [!mechanic]
> **Climbing or destroying the scaffolding.** DC 10 Athletics to climb to an elevated position. Destroying a section (DC 12 Athletics, or 10 HP of damage) collapses it: 1d6 bludgeoning and difficult terrain.

> [!dm]
> Solange's channeling is legible: kneeling, one hand on stone, the hum growing each round. DC 12 Arcana reads it as sustaining a ritual that requires unbroken concentration; DC 15 reads it as close to completion, minutes not hours. This telegraphs Solange and the circle as the objective, not the elites.

> [!mechanic]
> **Solange's reaction choice.** If a spellcaster targets the circle or threatens her concentration, she Counterspells (auto-counters 3rd level or lower, +5 check for higher). Each Counterspell spends one of two Pact Magic slots and prevents Detonate until her next turn — force the choice between protecting the ritual and keeping the dead-man switch ready.

**Terrain shift (round 3):** if the circle is not disrupted, bright light expands to 20-ft radius (most of the chamber — JC's Umbral Sight ceases to function anywhere). Water in channels reverses, rising to 4 ft deep (Medium creatures: half speed, disadvantage on melee).

> [!read-aloud]
> The circle flares white. Light floods the chamber, bleaching the stone, burning away every shadow. The water in the drainage channels shivers, stops, and rises, past ankles, then past knees, warm and thick with the chemical taste of everything these tunnels hold. The hum becomes a sound you feel in your teeth.

#### Phase 2 — The Ceiling Falls

**Trigger:** the second Elite Warrior drops to 0 HP, the circle gets disrupted, Solange starts her turn with no Elite within 10 ft of the circle, or fire/thunder damage occurs anywhere in the room.

> [!mechanic]
> **Detonate (reaction).** Solange uses her reaction the instant a trigger fires. If she already spent it on Counterspell, the detonation waits until her next turn unless fire or thunder has already ignited the powder. This command word to the alchemical fuses cannot be countered as a spell.
>
> | Effect | Save | Result |
> |---|---|---|
> | Fire blast, chamber + 10 ft into each exit | DC 16 Dex | 8d6 fire, half on success |
> | Ceiling collapse | DC 16 Str | 4d6 bludgeoning; failure means buried and restrained (escape DC 16 as an action) |

> [!mechanic]

The Mercatura plaza collapses: a 30-ft section of ceiling and the plaza floor above drops into the chamber, daylight and dust flooding in. The room is now a crater open to the sky.

To resolve the detonation fairly, follow this sequence:

> [!mechanic]

> [!read-aloud]

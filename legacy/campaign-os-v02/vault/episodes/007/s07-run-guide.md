## Session Goals

Tonight closes the fight Session 06 froze mid-collapse, then spends the morning after making the crew feel exactly what that fight cost — the show worth chasing isn't the kill, it's the ledger of five hundred names Nona reads out at dawn.

Three ideas carry the night.
First, a quiet interrupted-routine open: [[old-faas|Old Faas]], a coil of rope, and [[thunk|Thunk]]'s arithmetic at the *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]*'s rail, before the actual business of [[scene-02-thunks-debt|Thunk's Debt]] interrupts it.
Second, real body-horror in [[otar-the-foul|Otar the Foul]]'s transformation and his Entropic Regeneration, playing [[scene-03-otar-manifests|Otar Manifests]]'s opening for dread rather than spectacle.
Third, [[warren|The Warren]]'s Rattle landing as a living community rising for the crew rather than a mechanical buff, peaking in [[scene-04-the-rattle-answers|The Rattle Answers]].

The table splits along expected lines tonight: combat players get the brutal, unaided stretch of Otar Manifests and the Rattle's turning point in [[scene-04-the-rattle-answers|The Rattle Answers]]; social players get the felt at Thunk's Debt and the Ledger negotiation in [[scene-05-the-factors-debt|The Factor's Debt]].
Anyone chasing drama gets [[scene-06-five-hundred-names|Five Hundred Names]]'s dawn reckoning. Read the room and let that split decide whether the optional [[scene-01-same-time-tomorrow|Same Time Tomorrow]] vignette runs at all.

## Active Objectives

## NPCs

**[[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]**
**Wants:** the [[grung-npc|Grung]] magazine sealed and her five hundred back.
**The ask:** confirm the last tunnel is sealed and stand ready when she calls the count.
**Voice:** unhurried, declarative.
**Mannerism:** never stops working, marking beam-counts on a slate.
**Opening:** marking tallies as workers file past · "Which door didn't you close?"

**[[ruk|Ruk]]**
**Wants:** the room cleared before dawn.
**The ask:** push forward toward [[scene-04-the-rattle-answers|The Rattle Answers]]'s turning point.
**Voice:** flat, literal.
**Mannerism:** counts kills aloud.
**Opening:** counting bodies under his breath · "That six or seven?"

**[[enzo|Enzo]]**
**Wants:** the job finished cleanly.
**The ask:** get it done without leaving a mess before [[scene-05-the-factors-debt|The Factor's Debt]] afterward.
**Voice:** dry, unhurried.
**Mannerism:** cracks jokes mid-fight.
**Opening:** wiping a blade, unhurried · "Anyone keeping count, or is that just Ruk's thing?"

**[[thunk|Thunk]]**
**Wants:** his thousand back before [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] notices.
**The ask:** help him win it back at the felt, played warm and desperate.
**Voice:** booming, warm.
**Mannerism:** laughs at everything.
**Opening:** already counting cards under his breath at the rail · "You ever notice how the ship rocks in threes?"

**[[old-faas|Old Faas]]**
**Wants:** a quiet split.
**The ask:** keep it quiet, no scene inside [[tallow-row|Tallow Row]].
**Voice:** flat.
**Mannerism:** always working something in his hands — tonight, a coil of rope.
**Opening:** coiling the same length of rope over and over · "You always count this loud, or just tonight?"

**[[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]]**
**Wants:** [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]'s Maw account and word from the Eyrie.
**The ask:** give him her account of the Maw, asked rather than demanded.
**Voice:** asks questions instead of making statements.
**Mannerism:** stillness is his default; the head turns before the body does.
**Opening:** already turned toward the door before it opens · "And what did the Maw show her?"

**[[iacopo-fieschi|Iacopo Fieschi]]**
**Wants:** tonight's [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] debt written and sealed before precedent sets.
**The ask:** negotiate quickly and keep it formal.
**Voice:** flat, careful.
**Mannerism:** answers only what's asked.
**Opening:** the ledger already open on his desk · "What figure did you have in mind?"

**[[prospero-morsani|Prospero Morsani]]**
**Wants:** a place at the crater's rim after [[scene-04-the-rattle-answers|The Rattle Answers]], where witnesses know he was there.
**The ask:** let him be seen, nothing more.
**Voice:** terse to silent.
**Mannerism:** a nod, then he goes.
**Opening:** standing at the rim before the dust settles · "How much do you need me to have seen?"

**[[ruma-delacroix|Ruma]]**
**Wants:** her old network burned quietly. She passed Nona the word that starts [[scene-01-same-time-tomorrow|Same Time Tomorrow]].
**The ask:** stay out of her way — she's already moved.
**Voice:** dry.
**Mannerism:** works from shadow.
**Opening:** already gone by the time anyone looks twice · "You didn't hear this from me."

**[[solange-barret|Solange]]**

**Voice:** none — she never speaks tonight.
**Mannerism:** none.

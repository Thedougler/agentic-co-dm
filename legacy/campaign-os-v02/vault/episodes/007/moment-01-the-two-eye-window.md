## What's True

Thunk's blackout-drunk cannon order has come due on the *Uncertainty*'s credit. He has one night to close most of the gap before Nona's silence becomes a bill. Faas found a card counter on his own quarterdeck. He's walking that counter into a room of friends who have taken his money warmly for fifteen years. **The toy is the stakes themselves**, tracked live at the table. The whole party plays [[thunk|Thunk]]; the DM runs [[old-faas|Old Faas]] fond of everyone at that felt and stealing from them anyway. This plays three nights before the Beffa raid and touches nothing about it.

**The ask:** Faas asks Thunk to sit in on his regular game at Tallow Row, straight out — a mouth like his for numbers, and the table won't know what hit it.

**Thunk's Take** (target: 1,000 gp)

| Event | ± gp | Running |
|---|---|---|
| Buy-in at the felt | −20 | −20 |

Faas knocks twice at a door with a window "hardly bigger than the man's two eyes behind it." A voice challenges in Draconic. Faas answers in kind. The door swings in on [[grukan|Grukan]], an ogre built like a fortress, who prices Thunk with one flat Draconic word and laughs.

Past the door: rugs three deep, whale oil lamps, four of [[calveno|Calveno]]'s most obvious criminals at a green felt table. [[thassos|Thassos]] is a [[minotaur|Minotaur]] with one knife-scarred horn. [[watches-the-deck|Watches-the-Deck]], a [[lizardfolk|Lizardfolk]], tastes the air with her tongue. [[iolo-bracca|Iolo Bracca]], a gnome, tells tall stories. [[vittoria-sanzio|Vittoria Sanzio]] sits at the table's head, banking every coin in a lacquer box. She rises to take Thunk's face in both hands. Her glass for him is "the colour of a healing potion."

**Beat 1, Down to Tallow Row.** Five rolls, once each, resolving together as Thunk crosses the alley and the door.

| Check | DC | Angle | Success | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Perception | 12 | the alley's turns and footing | maps it; a failed getaway later loses 100 gp instead of 200 gp | the alley stays a stranger; a failed getaway costs its full price |
| Insight | 13 | Faas's Draconic answer and the den's etiquette | knows the house rules; advantage on the Beat 2 Performance | learns the rules by bumping into them |
| Intimidation | 12 | holding still under Grukan's stare | no pat-down at all | searched on the way out, costing 50 gp |
| Sleight of Hand | 12 | passing Faas's coin to Grukan like a regular | pockets it fond, holds the door both ways | reads as a bribe; making it right costs 25 gp |
| [[intelligence\|Intelligence]] | 13 | the one Draconic word Grukan uses for Thunk | *cargo*, the room prices him and takes a cut; advantage on Beat 2's Persuasion | the exchange stays noise |

**Resolving it.** 3+ successes: Thunk walks in mapped, briefed, unsearched, already knowing the room's cut. Fewer: the den has his measure before he's through the door.

**Beat 2, A Different Room Entirely.** Five rolls, once each, as Thunk lands at the felt.

| Check | DC | Angle | Success | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Perception | 12 | the bar-end door behind the coats | unbarred, unwatched; the getaway later runs its easier number through it | the front alley is the only way out he knows |
| Insight | 13 | which chair holds the real money | knows Vittoria banks the table; advantage on his first read at the felt | takes the biggest stack for the biggest threat |
| Performance | 12 | booming a greeting the room can live with | filed under furniture; the polite hour costs 5 gp | nobody answers; the polite hour costs 15 gp |
| [[constitution\|Constitution]] | 12 | the glass Vittoria poured and stayed to watch him drink | goes down steady, she beams | disadvantage on his first check at the felt |
| Deception | 13 | Iolo's warm, out-loud question about a gunner at this table | "lucky lately" lands warm, nobody prices his rings | Watches-the-Deck starts watching two hands early; disadvantage on the felt's Sleight of Hand |

**Resolving it.** 3+ successes: he sits established, the hour costs 5 gp, and he stakes an edge for the felt. 2 or fewer: he sits as entertainment, 15 gp already gone.

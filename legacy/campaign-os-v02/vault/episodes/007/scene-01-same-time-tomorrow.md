## What's True

This closer follows [[scene-06-five-hundred-names|Scene 6]] only if table time remains, cut by default since it isn't tied to tonight's own clock ([[register|Register]]: combat, tense-to-grim discovery. Format per [[runbook-reading-conventions|Reading These Pages]].). **Dramatic Question:** what did the raid miss under the harbour, and does the whole of it get home to Nona before the market bell? One of the [[grung|Grung]] magazines in the collector never got swept when the raid took the primary chamber. [[anzolo|Anzolo]] already worked the [[the-bridge|Bridge]]-lane site, and before Il Giudizio (the Judgment, Beffa's last night) draws its crowd, Nona wants this one sealed too. **The toy is the market clock**: three bell-segments between hatch and market bell, and every segment spent is thoroughness Nona doesn't get back. The vault runs theater-of-the-mind. No battlemap, off the read-aloud layout: four channels feed a dry ledge, with one vent above the waterline. Six tar-sealed barrels sit on that ledge, packed with blackpowder for a detonation never fired ([[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|magazine network]]).

**The ask:** Nona wants the harbour room's last unswept magazine cleared, said flat over espresso before either of them is fully awake, the cost measured in bell-segments spent instead of minutes.

Don't touch tonight's [[otar-the-foul|Otar]] fight, and don't resolve whether the [[vethka|Vethka]] crews clocked the vent.

The market clock runs three bell-segments (home before the market bell). Spend it on the Bells to Market tracker below, and call it in Nona's voice, never in minutes.

**The [[grung|Grung]]** ([[grung-npc|full stats]]) fight at AC 12 with 11 HP, using Standing Leap and a [[spear|Spear]] (+4 hit, 4d6+7). Wave count runs ~20 by Beat 2, and remnants hold the ledge through Beats 3 to 5. Normally they flee, but not this crew, cut off and holding orders. Beats 1 and 4 break for the barrels or the vent. Beat 5's last one yields.

> [!mechanic]
> **Poisonous Skin (DC 12 Con save, poisoned 1 min).** Ruk's Bite triggers this whenever it leaves the target [[grappled|Grappled]]. Crowded rounds mean more saves.

**Beat 1, Into the Vault.** Ruk and Enzo drop into the flooded vault and take the ambush's opening seconds on their own terms, or lose them.

> [!read-aloud] Into the Vault
> Fifteen feet of iron rungs drop you into standing water, ankle deep and black in the lamplight.
>
> The reek finds you before your boots find the bottom: tidal salt and wet stone, and under it a sweetness like burnt sugar left too long on the fire.
>
> The main run feeds four drainage channels, the nearest twenty feet ahead and the far pair another fifteen past that, with eight feet of vault overhead giving [[ruk|Ruk]] room to swing his bat without folding in half. Off to the right the floor climbs to a dry ledge stacked with tar-sealed barrels, the only thing down here up out of the water.
>
> Water sloshes in more than one channel at once, out of rhythm with anything the tide is doing on its own, and then the first of them come over a lip: squat, wet-skinned, spears held low, three splashing straight at you through the shallows.
>
> A fourth hangs back at the channel mouth with its throat already filling for a shout, and the pack behind it hasn't committed to the room yet. The nearest one is close enough to touch.

**Resolving it.** **3+ of the 5 land:** the ambush breaks before it forms. The count opens at four dead, and nothing touches either of you. **2 or fewer:** the pack gets its two-hold-one-spear trick working, and one of you takes a spear before the room turns: 4 (1d4+2) piercing plus 5 (2d4) poison.

**Beat 2, Twenty.** The fight runs long, and the details pile up faster than the kill count. The details are what Nona actually wants back.

> [!read-aloud] Twenty
> The opening rush dies in the shallows, and the channels keep feeding the room. Long minutes pass. Boots slip on the stone, the count runs somewhere past eight, and the two of you already disagree about where it stands. Then one of the [[grung|Grung]] stops fighting fair. It sinks back against the flooded vault wall instead of falling over, and by the time you notice, it still hasn't gotten back up.
>
> A grip closes on a wrist and the webbing between the fingers comes back clean, salt crusted deep into the knuckle creases, the open-water kind. Under a blade, a shoulder strap parts and swings loose at the hip, buckle and all, the cut edge standing square where a worn strap would have gone round.
>
> Further down the same wall, a second one has copied the trick, slumped against the stone with a spear still loose in its hand, watching through slit eyes for whichever of you turns a back on it first.
>
> More of them lie along that wall now than the two of you have managed. Warm patches spread through the water underfoot. Behind them the dry ledge stands untouched: six tar-sealed barrels wedged up out of the tide.
>
> The ones still coming have started checking each other before they commit, and near the back of the run, one breaks off with a slow match already lit between its teeth and an iron charge swinging at its hip, making straight for the barrels.

**Resolving it.** **3+ land:** the count reaches twenty dead with the intelligence intact and nothing worse than bruises. **2 or fewer:** one of you comes out of the water hurt, either a spear cut or Poisonous Skin's poison (the block above) or a lungful of canal water worth 2 (1d4) bludgeoning and disadvantage on his next attack or check while he coughs it up. It rides into every beat that follows.

The salt-crusted webbing and the new-bought straps say the same thing the boats in Beat 4 will: this crew came from open water, recently and expensively outfitted, [[vethka|Vethka]]-carried, not sewer-bred. The webbing is free to any player who grabs a wrist or asks, and the Investigation success is what reads the strap as new leather instead of just a cut strap.

> [!mechanic]
> **The Lit Charge.** The ledge holds six barrels of blackpowder, and the fuse-lit [[grung|Grung]] is carrying an iron charge to them. Ungrappled, it reaches the ledge on its next turn and wedges the charge in among the barrels. The slow match then burns one round more, and anyone in reach can pull the match or boot the charge into the flooded channel with no roll, which kills it dead. Let a player try that mid-round. The water is right there.
>
> **The charge fires** (slow match left to burn out). Only two barrels crack, not six firing together, so the blast comes light: every creature within 10 ft. of the ledge makes a **DC 13 [[dexterity|Dexterity]]** save, taking **7 (2d6)** fire and bludgeoning damage on a failure and half as much on a success.
> The ledge corner collapses, ruining the loose powder in the flood, and the chalk wall behind it goes with it. Beat 3's notation is unavailable, and Nona gets no timing calculation at all.

**Beat 3, What the Room Says.** Twenty bodies deep now and the flood finally quieting underfoot, the last guards hold the ledge while the room itself starts talking: chalk, barrels, and a vent cut too wide for any tide.

> [!mechanic]

If it fired in Beat 2, read **What the Blast Left** instead, and run the Investigation row on the wreck instead of the chalk, which buys the powder's scale, not the schedule. Either way, four barrels survive. The ledge corner and the chalk wall are what went.

> [!read-aloud] What the Room Says

**Resolving it.** **Investigation lands:** the full blast-timing calculation reaches Nona, arc and both numbers together. If the charge already fired instead, she gets only the room's rough powder volume, with no schedule at all. **It smudges:** she keeps only the crowd-linked timing, nothing sharper. Off the spill, she gets even less. **2 or fewer of the 5 land:** the guards hold the ledge a round longer than they should, and a segment comes off Bells to Market instead of off your hides.

The chalk notation is a blast-timing calculation: the arc marks the crossing overhead, and the bar sets a charge weight. Both circled numbers give the sundown reckoning, targeting the crowd Il Giudizio draws instead of a fixed clock. A clean copy lets Nona name that plainly, while a smudged one leaves her only the crowd link.

The ring-bolts stay unread and a scrape of stone might have carried. One more mark lands on the tally too, costing more than it should.

Another has backed against the barrels with nowhere left to go, spear up and shaking, and a third is on its knees in the shallows, not fighting at all, both arms under the black water to the shoulder, feeling along the grate for a thing it already knows is down there.

**Resolving it.** **3+ land:** the room clears clean and Nona gets a live prisoner and the pipe-tap, and nothing stays hidden between the two of you. **2 or fewer:** no prisoner, and the pipe-tap stays under the grate while the worse-hurt of you carries it home quiet.

> [!mechanic]
> **The pipe-tap.** A brass pipe-tap lies lashed under the grate in the shallows, the collector network's own check-in signaller. It carries three flat taps the length of the main, saying this room still holds, which is what the kneeling [[grung|Grung]] wants, not a weapon. If it taps, somebody still on the pipes learns the harbour room has fallen silent, and what they do about it waits for a later session. Nobody finds the tap unless Beat 5's Perception lands or a player puts a hand under the water.

The lower run stays unswept, on purpose. Whatever's still down there keeps until this report reaches Nona. If Ruk and Enzo press on anyway, let it happen: the rooms beyond hold nothing alive, and they spend every segment left on Bells to Market at once. They're home after the market bell with Nona's basket already off its hook.

**The ending holds fixed:** Ruk and Enzo report back instead of pushing deeper, whether the full report reaches Nona before the market bell with segments still unspent, they come home hurt with the picture short, or they push into the lower run instead of reporting and burn every remaining segment doing it. What varies is how much they can tell Nona and whether they hand her a live prisoner or just an account, plus how banged-up either of them looks doing it. That comes straight out of how the five beats landed.

**Foreshadowing.**

- **Vethka riding empty, three of them, ring-bolts down every centre thwart.** The pursuit hook that opens tonight: hulls fitted to carry people, still waiting for the crew that owns them to make its move.
- **A brass pipe-tap lashed under the grate, and a [[grung|Grung]] dying to reach it.** The collector's check-in network survived the raid. Whether the tap went off decides whether anything below [[calveno|Calveno]] knew the harbour room had fallen before Nona did.
- **The chalk-marked magazine under the judging crossing, timed to the crowd.** The crossing empties for Il Giudizio at sundown. The ceremony's own crowd is why tonight opens exactly when it does.
- **Nona's flat "it's a good thing you two have already been," no alarm in it.** Her first read of the boats offshore, the same calm that turns into her own vow to run the Vethka down in [[scene-06-five-hundred-names|Scene 6]].
- **The unswept lower run, left standing deliberately.** An intentionally open thread, unscripted by design: nothing alive is down there tonight (the refusal branch's own answer), and no future payoff is on file yet. It exists so a later session can move something back into a run everyone knows was never swept.

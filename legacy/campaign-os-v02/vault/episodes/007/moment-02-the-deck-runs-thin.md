> [!read-aloud]
> [[old-faas|Faas]] settles in beside you and lets you lose the first hour soft and cheap, the way he told you to in the alley. The room files you under harmless.
>
> Then the deck runs thinner hand over hand, and so does the arithmetic behind your eyes. High cards gone, low cards gone, and what's left adds itself up whether you mean it to or not.
>
> [[thassos|Thassos]]'s easy smile starts arriving half a second late. He deals the next hand himself, cards snapping down crisp and even, and the pile in front of him stops shrinking the way it should.
>
> Across from him, [[watches-the-deck|Watches-the-Deck]] hasn't touched her own cards in three hands. She is watching yours land.
>
> [[iolo-bracca|Iolo]] is still talking, betting into you like a man tipping a musician. [[vittoria-sanzio|Vittoria]] fills his glass and yours without once looking at the felt.

## What's True

One long run at the felt, five layers deep: the count itself, and the work of reading three players reading [[thunk|Thunk]] back while keeping the loudest voice happy. The arithmetic gets easier every pass, but nothing else does. Log each result as it lands, since the pile in front of Thunk when the accusation hits (next moment) is the number that moment has to protect. Best run: **+775 gp**. Worst: **−425 gp**.

**The Felt.**

| Check | DC | Angle | Success | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[intelligence\|Intelligence]] (the count, once per hand) | 15, then 13, then 11 as the deck thins | the arithmetic itself, faster and cheaper every pass | the math holds: +50, then +150, then +200 gp | the count slips: −20, then −50, then −80 gp |
| Sleight of Hand | 15 | betting like he already knows what's left, without the table seeing it | the count stays buried; +100 gp nobody questions | something slips a hand early; −100 gp, that pot outright |
| Insight (on Thassos) | 14 | the half-second delay before his smile lands | he's calculating, not reacting; +100 gp pressed home | reads as ordinary bad luck; −100 gp left on the table |
| Perception (on [[watches-the-deck\|Watches-the-Deck]]) | 14 | her cards untouched three hands running, her tongue working the air | bets timed to stay ahead of her; +75 gp she never sees coming | mistakes her stillness for focus; −75 gp goes to her instead |
| Persuasion (on Iolo) | 12 | keeping the clerk talking and betting while the money moves past him | he plays badly and cheerfully for +100 gp, and feeds unbidden harbour talk about late Crown stamps | he drifts to the bar, taking the room's attention with him; disadvantage on the next moment's [[constitution\|Constitution]] check |

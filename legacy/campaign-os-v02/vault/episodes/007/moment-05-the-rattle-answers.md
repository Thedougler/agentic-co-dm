## Opening

**Dramatic question:** does the crew land the finishing blow while the Rattle holds, or does [[otar-the-foul-the-rattle-answers|Rattle Surge]] grind them down first?

> [!read-aloud]
> Kitchens and cart wheels and window ledges take up the beat together, one rhythm climbing out of a hundred separate ones.
>
> The crowd that scattered from the blast comes back over the crater's broken lip slow and deliberate, carrying whatever the last few minutes put in their hands. A woman near the rim heaves a jar of burning lamp oil down into the crater. It catches red hide square across the eyes, close enough that the recoil shakes grit loose from the rubble at your feet.
>
> Forty feet back along the tunnel mouth, [[essa-two-tooth|Essa Two-Tooth]]'s crew comes up in single file, a stretcher already passed hand to hand toward whoever the collapse left pinned under a slab of fallen masonry, one arm free and reaching.
>
> The beat comes up through the broken stone now, loose grit hopping on the slabs, and [[ruk|Ruk]] comes down the rubble slope without hurrying, greatclub across his shoulders, and plants himself at the crater floor where the footing turns bad.
>
> [[colla|Colla]] shouts. She works the rim above with a satchel of stoppered vials, calling down for a name to throw one to.
>
> Overhead, Catarina's clockwork owl wheels through the gap where the ceiling used to be, and the mounted iron barrel she's been dragging clear of the rubble all fight finally swings level and holds.
>
> Across the crater, the thing at its center snaps its head around, already tracking the sound of the barrel locking level.

## What's True

Otar stands with Regeneration undimmed. Default: [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] and [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] down, others up. The Rattle runs until Otar falls. The crew pools lair actions and legendary points, 5/round, on Session 06's Room 8 battlemap.

> [!mechanic]
> **The Rattle answers.** [[warren|The Warren]]'s own alarm and mutual-aid system, keyed to a fight tipping toward a TPK, starts on initiative 20 the round after triggering and does not leave until Otar falls. It doesn't kill him for the crew. It puts the kill back in reach and keeps it there. Once triggered, Otar enters Phase 2 with escalated legendary actions, **Rattle Surge**: 3 legendary actions per round, Thrash, Bile Spray, and an option.

> [!mechanic]
> **Party lair actions at initiative 20 (alternate each round).** Human Chain frees one prone, buried, restrained, or grappled PC at half speed to the crater edge. Fire Brigade adds 1d6 fire to one hit and suppresses Otar's regen until his next turn. Din of Pans forces Otar a DC 10 [[wisdom|Wisdom]] save or he loses one legendary action next round.
>
> **The Rattle's pool of 5 (refreshing at initiative 20).** [[colla|Colla]]'s Toss (1 point): a potion to a downed or wounded PC, 2d4+2 HP or stabilizes. Shoulder In (1 point): one PC moves their speed or Dodges. [[ruk|Ruk]] Wades In (2 points): Ruk attacks with a [[greatclub|Greatclub]] (+8 for 2d8+5) or Bite (+8 for 1d8+5 plus [[grappled|grapple]]), or breaks a PC free of a [[grappled|grapple]].

> [!dm]
> Run the Rattle at full strength every round once triggered. It doesn't stop until Otar falls.

**Spending the pool.**

> [!read-aloud]
> The pans fill the crater, beat loud enough to hear over. One collapsed victim, arm out from rubble, sits in reach. [[lamp|Lamp]] oil jars keep coming. One has left a black scorch across a flank. The creature swings that scarred side toward you.

> [!check] Strength (Athletics) — Hauling Delmar Clear
> DC 12.
> **Success:** clear, upright, action ready.
> **Failure:** action spent, down. Human Chain reaches initiative 20 regardless.

> [!check] Attack Roll — Fire Brigade
> Vs AC 16.
> **Success:** 1d6 fire, suppresses regen until Otar's next turn.
> **Failure:** swings wide. Brigade available next hit.

**Closing it out.**

> [!read-aloud]
> The red hide has split wide along one flank right where you've been hitting it, weeping steady now instead of healing, and the wound doesn't close the way every other one tonight has. The stench of cauterized flesh rises sharp and acrid.
>
> It comes back around slower to face whoever's nearest, favoring the torn side, and its feet slide in the loose rubble at the bottom of the crater for the first time all night. You hear the scrape of claws and the creature's labored, rattling breath.
>
> Above you the rim rings with pans and shouting. Every voice tracks that flank. It gives ground. A step. Then another, back toward the broken edge of the circle.

> [!check] Attack Roll — Finish the Fight
> Vs AC 16 (call it once fire has suppressed regen for one full round).
> **Success:** fire plus hit ends it.
> **Failure:** it still stands, but suppression holds if fire continues.

> [!read-aloud]
> The pans keep the beat a moment past the last blow landing, then two quick taps, once, and stop.
>
> Dust that's hung in the air since the ceiling came down finally settles on your shoulders, over thirty feet of dead red hide, cooling where it fell. The acrid taste of smoke and char lingers.
>
> Nobody cheers. A woman near the rim who threw the first jar sits down hard on a chunk of masonry, hands shaking.
>
> Pieces of the thing lie scattered across the crater floor where the fight tore them loose, and near what used to be the circle a hand shows above the rubble with a red-caste signet still on it.
>
> Up on the lip the crowd moves, dozens of them at once, coming down over the broken edge toward the pieces on the floor.

**The crater floor.**

> [!read-aloud]
> The crowd is down off the rim and into the crater now, picking a way through the rubble with hands over their mouths against the acrid stench rising from the carcass. The wet sound of dripping echoes off stone.
>
> Somebody at the top of the slope has started a list, and names go up to them from below, one voice at a time.
>
> Two of the crowd have stopped short of the hand at the circle's edge, close enough to read the red-caste signet still loose on its fingers, and neither of them will go nearer.
>
> The pieces torn loose in the fight lie all across the floor, palm-sized and bigger, red dulling toward grey as they cool. One or two are still twitching.

> [!check] Investigation or Perception — Crater Floor
> DC 13.
> **Success:** a fist-sized red shard, still twitching. See [[a-sliver-of-the-unstable-form|A Sliver of the Unstable Form]].
> **Failure:** the crater reads as rubble and blood only.

> [!check] Investigation or Insight — Solange's Signet
> DC 10.
> **Success:** the red-caste signet on the hand, warm, worth taking. See [[solanges-authority-seal|Solange's Authority Seal]].
> **Failure:** the signet goes unnoticed tonight.

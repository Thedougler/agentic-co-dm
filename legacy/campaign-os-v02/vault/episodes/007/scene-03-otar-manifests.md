## Opening

**Dramatic Question:** can the crew stand against Otar's opening assault long enough for [[the-rattle-statblock|the Rattle]] to answer, or does someone go down before it can?

![[otar-the-foul-manifestation#Cold Open: The Charge Detonates]]

> [!dm]
> The presence in Perrin's vision is [[auralis|Auralis]], never named aloud. Perrin's sheet already knows [[armor-of-agathys|Armor of Agathys]]. This is a party-wide shape of it forced through him.

> [!read-aloud] Transition
> The smoke thins over where the platform used to be. The shape at the middle of the crater is still changing.

> [!read-aloud]
> What was Solange finishes becoming something else, straightening out of its crouch to its full height, red hide cracked and weeping down both flanks, easily twelve feet at the shoulder. Loose stone still sifts off its back in sheets, and the thirty-foot crater under it groans, a slow grinding settle that hasn't finished yet.
>
> Its wet, sick-sweet fog rolls low across the rubble in every direction, already thick enough to blur anything past ten feet. To the north, one townsfolk still shouts, half-buried under a collapsed market stall twenty feet off. Another lies pinned nearby.
>
> The blast throws you across the crater's broken rim. Its searching head swings as its tongue tastes the air, then it finds you.
>
> *(flat)* Whatever stands in that circle now, it's already moving.

![[s07-scene03-beat1-transformation]]

> [!read-aloud]
> The collapse didn't just bring down stone: five shapes claw loose from the rubble right where you're standing, low and quick, toad-skinned like the thing at the crater's center but smaller, still trailing torn caul from wherever the detonation drove them since the first blast, a wet tearing sound following each one free. They scatter wide before they close on you, already hunting.

> [!dm]
> Narrate the five [[minor-slaad|Minor Slaad]] as the [[slaad-tadpole|slaad tadpoles]] shed from Otar's own hide, fed on the sewer's [[grung-npc|Grung]] dead since Session 06. Never name them aloud at the table any more than you'd say "Otar." No coordination between them. Each goes for whoever's nearest.

### Beat 2: The Plaza Fights Back

*The ground **works against everyone**: channels, collapses, and the civilians every round **keeps pricing**.*

> [!read-aloud]
> The plaza doesn't hold still. Water roars as one of the drainage channels breaks its bank, black water climbing over the stone lip and spreading across broken ground, bringing the reek of wet stone and silt.
>
> A slab shifts loose above the market stall with a grinding crack. The people pinned under it cry out, then shout again as fresh dust sifts down through the gap, thick enough to choke on.
>
> Whoever's closest to that stall is the only one who can reach them before the next slide covers it for good.

> [!check] Athletics — Pulling Someone Clear
> DC 13, once per person pinned. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] breaking off to reach the two under the market stall commits the whole round to this task alone.
> **Success:** one of the two comes free a breath before a fresh slide of rubble covers the spot they were lying in; the second is still under it, and a repeat check next round gets them out.
> **Failure:** the stall doesn't give. Delmar's still crouched over it, exposed, when Otar's turn comes back around, and both are still pinned.

### Beat 3: Fire and Reach

*The crew **finds the levers**: fire to **shut off the regen**, a readied shot to **cut the tongue's reach**.*

> [!read-aloud]
> Buried under the collapsed scaffolding lies an unlit torch rack pinned under a fallen beam. Black lamp oil pools around shattered crates, its reek sharp enough to cut through the dust. Alchemist's-fire vials crack open in the rubble.
>
> Otar's tongue lashes out again, stretched taut and dripping thirty feet across open ground, hunting whoever it's found this time.
>
> Fire. It lies right there in the wreckage, if someone can dig it free before that tongue closes the distance.

![[otar-the-foul-manifestation#Fire in the Rubble]]

![[otar-the-foul-manifestation#Raising the Stakes]]

![[s07-scene03-beat3-digging-for-fire]]

> [!check] Ranged Attack — Cutting the Reach
> An attack roll vs AC 15, the extended tongue itself, thirty-plus feet out and already moving; open to [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] or anyone with a readied shot and a clear line. Disadvantage applies without one.
> **Success:** the shot catches the tongue mid-extension. It snaps back short, and Otar's turn ends without the grab.
> **Failure:** the lash closes on whoever it found, and it resolves at the numbers in the block below. Otar already hauled Perrin thirty feet across broken stone once tonight. Nothing stops a second pull landing on him or whoever else stands in reach.

> [!mechanic]
> **Tongue Lash (the pull).** [[strength|Strength]] save, **DC 16**, or Otar **pulls the target up to 35 ft** toward him and **seizes** them, escape **DC 16**, leading to his bite.

![[s07-scene03-beat2-tongue-lash]]

### Beat 4: The Low Point

*The fight **reaches its trigger**. **Call it** the moment it lands, never a round later.*

![[otar-the-foul-manifestation#Lowering the Stakes]]

![[s07-scene03-beat5-the-low-point]]

> [!read-aloud]
> The plaza shrinks. Half of what counted as ground a minute ago is crater, water, or Otar himself, and there's nowhere left to fall back to that isn't already claimed.
>
> His fog has closed in thick enough now that the far side of the crater is just a shape, and the screaming from under the stall hasn't stopped since the ground last shifted.
>
> Otar's head swings back around, tongue still dripping, hunting for whoever's left standing closest.

> [!read-aloud] Transition
> Then, underneath the ringing in every ear still working, iron starts striking iron somewhere north of the crater. Slow at first, then in time with itself, hundreds of hands Finding the same beat.

# Session 07: Not Funny Now

## Recap

Three nights before the crater, [[thunk|Thunk]] stood at the *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]*'s rail with a thousand gold of cannon on the ship's credit and no memory of signing for it. [[old-faas|Old Faas]] taught him cards, caught him mouthing a count, and walked him down to the game behind the butcher's on [[tallow-row|Tallow Row]]. Faas's own friends ran it, fifteen years deep: [[grukan|Grukan]] on the door, [[vittoria-sanzio|Vittoria Sanzio]] banking the table from the chair with no cards in front of it. Lose the first hour, Faas said. Count the second.

Thunk counted. [[thassos|Thassos]] stopped smiling somewhere in the third hour and said it out loud: he'd been lucky a great many times. Faas's boot found Thunk's ankle under the felt, one tap, and Faas folded a hand he should have won rather than tip the table. They ran the alley with silver coming out of Thunk's arms and coats and one boot, laughing, followed the whole way. Faas didn't laugh, and hasn't said a word about halves.

Then the ceiling over the summoning platform finished opening.

What [[solange-barret|Solange Barret]] became straightened out of its crouch to twelve feet, red hide cracked and weeping down both flanks, and the [[mercatura|Mercatura]] had a thirty-foot crater in the middle of it under a bright [[calveno|Calveno]] morning. [[otar-the-foul|Otar]]'s wounds closed as fast as the crew opened them. Five slug-pale things the color of his own hide clawed out of the rubble and went for the crowd; one caught a trapped villager and started eating, and the more it ate the more human it looked.

[[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] went out after the leech chasing a fleeing bystander, cleared the crater's edge with a jump and put an arrow through the second one. [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] found a line in her tattoo she'd never used and lit it, ink flaring gold up through her knuckles, then caught Otar's tongue lash across her forearm and tried to drive the whole charge back down it. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] kicked his flying boots on, hauled two people upright with one pull so neither spent a turn standing, and took the same tongue across the ribs. [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]]'s owl and her iron barrel worked the gap where the ceiling used to be. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] asked Perrin for fresh inspiration mid-blast, and Perrin set his drum on the floor for him to touch rather than reach out through the fire.

It went badly enough that Delmar flew up over the crater lip, shouted for a bell or a whistle, and whistled anyway, the call he and Crissdalynn use from the crow's nest, knowing nobody could hear it. [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]] heard it. He came in ninety feet of silent white feathers, and his daggers took the brood apart before any of them could turn.

The town answered on its own. Skillets and pots and window ledges picked up one beat out of a hundred separate ones, [[warren|the Warren]]'s old signal running wide across Calveno: hide or get ready to fight. The crowd that had scattered came back over the broken rim carrying whatever was in reach. A woman heaved a jar of burning lamp oil into the crater and it burst across Otar's face, and after that the flank they'd all been hitting stopped closing.

Catarina was in his mouth when Delmar ended it. There was a chair in the rubble, the one Delmar had rolled into existence a session earlier, still whole after a ceiling came down on it, so he flew down, grabbed it, spun with it, and put it into Otar's throat with a crunch. Then he drew the pistol and shot him through the eye. Catarina came out of the mouth into daylight. The pans kept the beat one moment past the last blow, gave two quick taps, and stopped. Nobody cheered. The woman who threw the first jar sat down hard on a chunk of masonry with her hands shaking.

The carcass rotted where it fell, and a small scrap of paper sat in the decay that nobody has read yet. Delmar wiped the chair down and found not one scratch on it, then borrowed Catarina's [[bag-of-holding|Bag of Holding]] to carry it home for the captain's quarters. The raid's take from the chamber below got its names that morning: [[solanges-authority-seal|Solange's Authority Seal]], which makes a Grung obey a single spoken word, and [[a-sliver-of-the-unstable-form|A Sliver of the Unstable Form]], a piece of Otar's own healing flesh still twitching.

[[iacopo-fieschi|Iacopo Fieschi]] found them in the plaza before the dust was down, then took them back to an office with more letters in it than furniture and the smoking crater framed in the window. The [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] offered three hundred gold each and a retainer, because the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi]] plainly weren't defending anything. [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] turned her share down and asked it go to the repairs and to the children living on the streets instead; Fieschi gave [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] a diamond ring worth five hundred, and she kept it. Perrin took a standing favor over coin, leaned in, and sold Fieschi on the Black-Jaws being there for the city whenever the city needed it. Jean-Claude drained his wine, asked for tea instead, swirled it, sniffed it, and never drank it. They all signed the paper. Calveno has Defenders now, on Tessarine letterhead.

Under it sat a question nobody could close. Fieschi said no warning ever reached him. Someone in that room had been given strict orders by [[rupert-knighton|Rupert Knighton]] to gather what she could and evacuate, days before the ceiling opened.

They'd been up all night and through two fights, and were arguing about where to eat, when Perrin's sending stone warmed with [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s voice: come home soon, something bad has happened. Many, many people are missing. Hundreds.

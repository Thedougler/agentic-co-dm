## Opening

**Dramatic Question:** what is Calveno's gratitude worth, and can the crew move Fieschi's ledger?

> [!read-aloud] Transition
> The crater keeps quiet only a few minutes longer. The crowd that pulled you clear pulls others too. Calveno's tale writes itself before you breathe.

> [!read-aloud]
> The story spreads fast. Smoke still rises from the stone. A ring three deep closes on the crater's lip. The front row feels the heat.
>
> Every third person tells a different story. In one, the thing stood twenty feet tall. In one, it never died at all. A boy with scraped knuckles insists he counted the owl overhead three times. He says it louder each time someone laughs.
>
> Someone presses a waterskin into whichever hand is nearest. [[prospero-morsani|Prospero Morsani]] nods from the rim and goes.
>
> A cooper with a burnt sleeve shoulders to the front of the ring and asks, loud enough for everyone behind him to hear it: which of you put the thing down.

## Running It

**The setup.** First, the crowd writes the story. Then Fieschi picks a cost.

### Beat 1: Heroes of Calveno

*The crew works the crowd, or endures it.*

> [!check] Performance or Persuasion — Working the Crater's Lip
> DC 12, open to anyone who engages the crowd.
> **Success:** the story stays accurate. By midday, people know what you did. When Fieschi arrives, this account circulates cleanly.
> **Failure:** the story gets messy with competing versions. Beat 2's public press rises to **DC 17** instead of DC 15.

### Beat 2: Fieschi Arrives

*Same crater's lip, minutes later. The crowd stays thick. Fieschi **deflects** in public. The crew can **catch it** and **press him** into a rare [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] debt.*

> [!read-aloud]
> Fieschi arrives in fine clothes, clerks trailing with writing-board and strongbox. He talks to the crowd, not to you.

> [!quote] Iacopo Fieschi (arriving)
> "Patrol schedules are a Crown responsibility. Calveno trusted the Dravosi's own inspection paperwork further than it should have."

He doesn't explain what his people did all festival. The Concordat's windows faced the harbor. [[mercatura|Mercatura]] was in view the whole time.

> [!check] Insight — Catching the Deflection
> DC 14.
> **Success:** every sentence blames someone else.
> **Failure:** he reads upset. Whether real is unclear.

> [!check] Persuasion or Intimidation — Pressing Him, in Front of Everyone
> DC 15 (DC 17 if Beat 1 failed). Persuasion makes the crowd's tale stick. Intimidation costs him more than admitting it.
> **Success:** he concedes. The Concordat admits it saw the traffic but felt no need to report it. He owes the crew now, publicly. Mark **Leverage 2**. He asks you to call tonight.
> **Failure:** he repeats the Crown line and leaves. Later, he sends a private message: the Concordat wants to talk tonight. Either way, only his concession changes.

> [!dm] [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] ran extractions on the other side before. He knows how many eyes a harbor needs to stay blind. If his player brings this up to Fieschi, fold it into the check above. A professional's read beats an Insight roll.

### Beat 3: The Ledger Room

*Fieschi **names a sum**. The crew **pushes** it up, tier by tier, as far as they dare.*

> [!read-aloud] Transition
> Evening finds you on the canal below the factor house. Pale stone, no signage, boats slowing as they pass out of the same habit that keeps every Calveno eye off that building on an ordinary day.

> [!read-aloud]
> Fieschi receives you in a ledger-filled room and gets to business before the door closes.
>
> Wax and old paper fill the air. The smell of ink is sharp. Pens scratch on parchment behind his chair. A dim desk lamp sits at his elbow. Clerks stand with writing-boards open. Your side has three chairs for five people.
>
> The windows run floor to ceiling, showing the whole Mercatura open beneath them with the cordoned crater below in the lamplight.
>
> He offers no wine. He names three hundred gold and a sealed letter of thanks (what the Concordat can do today), and then waits.

**The scene's toy: Fieschi's Ledger**

**Tier 1: coin & goods (always available)**

- A formal thank you, sealed and dated. It opens doors fast. You write the words and names.
- A coin purse: **300 gp** with no conditions attached.
- **Six months'** provisioning credit at [[moretti-and-sons|Moretti & Sons Chandlery]] on the Mercatura.

*Tier 2: trade & credit*

- **1,000 gp** revolving trade credit at any Tessarine house.
- Priority berthing, waived inspection for *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]* at Tessarine ports.
- **10% of [[antheri|Antheri]]** salvage from [[shelfworks|the Shelfworks]], paid as recovered.

*Tier 3: property & standing*

- **[[casa-falier|Casa Falier]]**, deeded outright: a four-story Mercatura canal-front house, empty but for two staff.
- Fieschi's nod to the Seven Houses Council. It grants favor, land, rank, or what you want. He makes intros. The rest stays open.

> [!check] Persuasion or Insight — Naming What Calveno Saw
> DC 13.
> **Success:** +1 leverage.
> **Failure:** leverage stays same.

> [!check] Intimidation or Deception — Pressing the Windows
> DC 15.
> **Success:** +1 leverage.
> **Failure:** no change.

> [!mechanic]
> **Jean-Claude's testimony.** Naming what he recognized in Beat 2 directly to Fieschi's face grants **Leverage +1, automatic, with roll required**. Testimony from someone who's run the other side of an operation like this outweighs anything but catching Fieschi in an outright lie.

### Beat 4: Closing the Deal

*Same room, the same lamp burning low. The negotiation simply **arrives at paper**, and the only open question is **whose names it carries**.*

> [!read-aloud]
> The number stands. Both clerks stop writing at once. For the first time all evening, only the lamp makes sound.
>
> Fieschi turns the finished page face-out across the desk. The seal smells warm enough to burn, and the lead clerk holds the pen out to you instead of dipping it.
>
> The only line still blank is the name.
>
> He doesn't fill it in himself. He waits to see whether it reads "the crew" in one hand, or five names under five different titles.

He stays polite and cold. Six of the Seven Houses' papers sit in a drawer behind him. He never raises his voice to get what he wants. Tonight the debt runs the other way.

> [!check] Persuasion or Insight — How the Paper Reads
> DC 12. Persuasion to insist the grant names each crew member, not just "the crew". Insight to catch which phrasing Fieschi prefers and press for the opposite one.
> **Success:** the paper names them each by title (harder for Fieschi or anyone after him to quietly renegotiate away from just one of them later).
> **Failure:** it goes out under "the crew" as one name. Still binding, but easier for a future factor to argue who it covers.

> [!dm]
> What Fieschi never says out loud: his report to [[cosimo-verantio|Cosimo Verantio]] will frame tonight as Concordat foresight, quiet confidence in Calveno's own defenders, not five days of watching a raid build from a window he never reported. He believes every word by the time he writes it down. His private arithmetic stays private. If a player insists on probing for it, any success only shows that Fieschi believes his own version.

### Beat 5: Quiet Sundown

*An optional breather: Il Giudizio, [[il-gioco-delle-beffe|the Beffa]]'s season-end judging, **offers** the crew its bronze mask, and [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]] **waits** with a question for Crissdalynn.*

> [!read-aloud] Transition
> The factor house lets you out into early evening, ink still drying on whatever you carried away, and the sound of the Mercatura pulls you back toward the water.

> [!read-aloud] Il Giudizio
> Lanterns ring a small stage on the Mercatura, where a committee is arranging itself around a bronze mask on a velvet cushion. A season of pranks awaits judgment, and every face that should be watching keeps sliding toward the cordon and the crater.
>
> The mask catches the lamplight. The committee reaches for it. The applause, when it starts, sounds thin. People remember they should clap.
>
> The chair lifts the mask and asks out loud for someone to accept it on the crater's behalf. His eyes settle on you and don't look away.

> [!check] Perception or Insight — Reading the Ceremony
> DC 12, for any PC who stops at the stage on the walk back down to the water.
> **Success:** the committee is going through the motions and knows it. Everyone present watches the crater over their shoulder, and the chair wants this mask off his hands in a minute. One figure stands apart from the stage entirely: [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]], facing the crater, plainly waiting. The Kyzil conversation opens tonight.
> **Failure:** it plays exactly like any other year's Giudizio. Kyzil goes unnoticed in the crowd, and his question keeps until [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] seeks him out herself in some later session.

> [!mechanic]
> **The bronze mask.** The chair's ask is real and no check gates it. Whoever steps onto the stage takes this season's cast mask, and the crew keeps it.
>
> - **Accepted.** The Mercatura now has a face to mark the crater. While it's carried open in Calveno, **advantage on one [[charisma|Charisma]] check per session** with any local who heard the news. The Beffa committee owes the crew a favor it will honor once.
> - **Declined, or nobody steps up.** The chair hands it to the committee's own runner-up. No cost, and Calveno reads the refusal as modesty. The crowd's account of the morning stays unchanged.

**The Mask's Standing** (only if accepted; the Charisma advantage resets each session, the favour never does)

[[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]], if approached, has a question of his own waiting: whether [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] will finally give him her full account of [[the-drowned-maw|the Maw]], storm and all, alongside whatever [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|the Eyrie]] needs to know about its two Sentinels still missing from [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]]. He doesn't raise it unless she's the one who comes to him.

**Fieschi honors whatever Leverage the table reached that same evening, in writing, before they leave the room.** What isn't fixed is the number, or which items off the inventory they walk out with. Nothing about this negotiation requires the crew to aim small. Sleep finds Calveno still short hundreds of names nobody's finished counting.

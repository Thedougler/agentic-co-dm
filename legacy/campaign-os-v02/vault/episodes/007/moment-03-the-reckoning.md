> "You've been lucky a great many times. Every hand the deck's run thin, you've bet like you already knew."

## What's True

The tap is a signal Faas favors over quiet ones. He has already stacked his own cut into a coat pocket, not the visible pile, two hands ago. [[thunk|Thunk]] must choose his exit: the front door past Grukan, or the bar-end door behind the coats (if Moment 1 clocked it). The chairs stay put and the den's peace holds unless a player forces a fight. If that happens, Grukan moves between Thunk and both doors before the second swing, the house keeps that hand's pot (−100 gp) as the price of peace, and staff escort the two of them out by the collar instead of dragging them.

**The Exit.**

| Check | DC | Angle | Success | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[constitution\|Constitution]] | 13 | matching Thassos's six-hundred push without flinching | two of three hands are his; +400 gp of the pot outright | the flinch shows; −400 gp stays with Thassos |
| Insight | 13 | Faas's single tap, and what it means | it means *now*, and Thunk is moving before Thassos finishes his arithmetic | it meant *now* a beat ago; the last pot, 100 gp of it, stays on the felt |
| Persuasion (Vittoria) | 15 | asking leave to walk out heavy while she's still pleased | she calls it a fine night's play and waives the cut, +150 gp | the cut comes off the top, −150 gp, offered as credit against the rest |
| Deception | 13 | "one more hand, after I stretch my legs" | buys ten clean, unhurried paces to whichever door | the whole room watches him cross it, and the alley knows he's coming |
| Sleight of Hand | 12 | loading the pile into coats, sleeves, and one boot before standing | stowed tight; the getaway spills only 50 gp whatever else goes wrong | it goes out in his arms in plain sight, and the alley takes its share |

**Resolving it.** 4+ successes: Thunk walks with the full pile, the house's blessing, and a head start. Fewer: the leaving costs what the table above says.

> [!read-aloud] Out the Alley
> [[grukan|Grukan]] doesn't stop the two of you going out. It tells you everything about how close it had been to stopping you coming back.
>
> Silver cascades from your arms and coats as you run the alley. Coins ring on the cobbles and one boot leaks coins. You laugh the whole way big and booming. The cold fat smell of the butcher's rides out with you.
>
> Faas stays exactly as flat as ever and has not yet said one word about halves. No laugh in him tonight.
>
> Somewhere behind you a grating scrapes back over a drain.
>
> Ahead the alley forks the same two ways it did going in. Past the fork a night-soil man stands at the mouth with his barrow, waiting on the noise to explain itself.

**The Getaway.**

| Check | DC | Angle | Success | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[dexterity\|Dexterity]] (Acrobatics) | 13, or 11 through the bar-end door if clocked in Moment 1 | the fork itself, arms full, one boot heavier than the other | clean and fast; only 50 gp shakes loose | a stumble; 200 gp gone (100 gp if the alley got mapped) |
| Stealth | 12 | getting past the night-soil man's barrow | he gets a blur of two men running | he gets a clear look at an orc gunner carrying silver |
| Persuasion (Faas) | 13 | naming the split out loud, mid-run, before he settles it himself | he agrees to even halves; +150 gp over the quiet default | he takes his own cut from the middle unmentioned; −150 gp |
| Perception | 14 | the grating going back over a drain with nothing under it worth venting | catches a small, webbed hand doing the dragging | reads as a rat in a gutter |
| Insight | 13 | Grukan filling the doorway behind them, and whether that room follows | knows they're clear; the last stretch costs nothing more | runs the whole way at full speed; another 50 gp into the cobbles |

**The ending holds.** Thunk and Faas make it back to the *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]* with whatever the felt and the alley left them. Whether it's enough to close [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s silence stays open by design.

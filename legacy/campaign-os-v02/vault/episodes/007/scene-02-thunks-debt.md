## Opening

**Dramatic Question:** can Thunk take a thousand gold off [[old-faas|Faas]]'s oldest friends before they work out what Faas brought to their table?

> [!read-aloud]
> Late, and the *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]* rides quiet at her mooring, her deck empty but for the two of you and the harbour working away in the dark.
>
> You’ve spent the last day flinching at the sound of [[rattkin|Rattkin]] footsteps on the weatherdeck above you. A thousand gold on the ship's credit, two Long Nines and two Short 32-pounders ordered in a hand you don't remember signing until you saw it dry on the paper. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]] hasn't said one word about it yet, far to busy plotting your imminent and painful demise you imagine.
>
> You've crossed the deck twice tonight already, once for the arithmetic and once for the nerve, and both times you end up at the same rail: old Faas, splicing a line he could mend blind, salt and old tar riding off the rope sharp enough to taste.
>
> "How do you make money fast, Faas?" you say.
>
> “Gambling!” he says, wholeheartedly recommends it, and by way of answer sets the rope down long enough to teach you the game right there on the deck.
>
> An hour in, down every hand he's dealt you, Faas catches you mouthing a count under your breath as the cards fall. His frown breaks into a grin.
>
> There's a game he goes most weeks, he says, behind the butcher's on [[tallow-row|Tallow Row]], and with a mouth like yours for numbers, they won't know what hit them. Then he picks the rope back up and waits on you.

## Running It

**The setup.** Thunk's blackout-drunk cannon order has come due on the *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]*'s credit, and he has one night (three before tonight's Beffa business) to close most of the gap before Nona's silence becomes a bill. Faas is not solving that problem for him; instead, he's using it: he found a card counter on his own quarterdeck, and he is walking that counter into a room of friends who have taken his money warmly for fifteen years. **The scene's toy is the stakes themselves,** and every check below moves real gold toward the thousand Thunk owes or away from it. ### Beat 1. The Two-Eye Window

*Thunk and Faas **reach the door** while Grukan **sizes up** Thunk. Faas states the job on the way in, and what Thunk banks in the alley pays out at the felt and on the way back through it.*

> [!read-aloud] Down to [[tallow-row|Tallow Row]]
> The walk down to [[tallow-row|Tallow Row]] is short, the alley behind the butcher's narrow enough for single file. It smells of old blood and cold fat. Halfway along it forks: crates stacked one way, an open gutter the other. Faas keeps walking and knocks twice.
>
> *Faas*: Friends of mine in here. Fifteen years. Lose the first hour and count the second one.
>
> He presses a coin into your palm and folds your fingers shut over it, offering no explanation.
>
> The window at the door's end, hardly bigger than the man's two eyes behind it, studies the two of you for a long count. A voice comes back in Draconic.
>
> ‘Ash nazg durbatulûk, ash nazg gimbatul,’
>
> Faas answers in perfect Draconic
>
> ‘ash nazg thrakatulûk, agh burzum-ishi krimpatul.’
>
> And the door swings in on an ogre built like a fortress, tusks yellowed and deliberate, wearing more holstered steel than the two of you own together.
>
> He looks you up and down, grunts, claps Faas on the back, then says one flat Draconic word about you over Faas's shoulder, and laughs in a deep baritone while he holds the door.

| Check | DC | Angle | Success | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Perception** | 12 | the alley itself (corners, footing, where it forks) while the window studies you | you map the turns; a failed getaway in Beat 5 loses **100 gp** instead of its full **200 gp** | the alley stays a stranger; Beat 5's failure costs everything it says |
| **Insight** | 13 | how Faas answers the window, and the den's etiquette learned by ear | you know the house rules before the door opens; advantage on Beat 2's Performance | you'll learn the rules by bumping into them |
| **Intimidation** | 12 | holding still under a stare that prices you like cargo | no pat-down at all, coming or going | searched at the door on the way out, and the courtesy of it costs **50 gp** |
| **Sleight of Hand** | 12 | Faas's coin into [[grukan\|Grukan]]'s palm, the way a regular does it, without it looking like a bribe | he pockets it fond and holds the front door for you on the way out as well | it reads as a bribe in front of the window, and making it right costs **25 gp** |
| **[[intelligence\|Intelligence]]** | 13 | the one Draconic word Grukan uses for you, caught over Faas's shoulder | *cargo*. You know the room prices you before you price it, and you know the house takes a cut; advantage on Beat 4's Persuasion | the exchange stays noise, and the house's cut is news to you when it lands |

**Resolving it.** All five read the same held breath in the alley: what Thunk maps, hides, and hears while [[grukan|Grukan]] decides about him. **3+ land:** he walks in mapped, briefed, unsearched, and already knowing what the room will want off the top. **Fewer:** the den has his measure before he's through the door, and the exits already cost more than they should.

### Beat 2. A Different Room Entirely

*Thunk **sits down** in a room that outclasses him. He reads how the room **reads him**: furniture or entertainment, his choice to make.*

> [!read-aloud] Transition
> [[grukan|Grukan]] weighs the two of you a moment longer before stepping aside without a word. The alley's cold stays outside with him.

> [!read-aloud] A Different Room Entirely
> Rugs three deep, whale oil in the lamps, and four of the most obvious criminals in [[calveno|Calveno]] at a green felt table with two chairs still open. They cheer Faas through the doorway, glasses up, an old friend back from sea.
>
> Past the bar's far end, a second door stands half-hidden behind a rack of hanging coats, with draught moving the hems under it.
>
> Across the felt: [[thassos|Thassos]], a [[minotaur|Minotaur]], one filed horn split by a knife-scar. Beside him [[watches-the-deck|Watches-the-Deck]], a [[lizardfolk|Lizardfolk]], long and lean as a dockside crane, her tongue tasting the air between hands. Third along, [[iolo-bracca|Iolo Bracca]], a gnome kneeling up on two cushions, ink to the second knuckle, telling the table what became of a crew he once had held at anchor a whole season before the table erupts in laughter.
>
> The chair at the head of the table has no cards in front of it and no stack either. [[vittoria-sanzio|Vittoria Sanzio]] sits there with a lacquer box the size of a bible at her elbow. When she sees you, she rises and takes your face in both hands, asking after the *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]* by name before pouring you a glass the colour of a healing potion.
>
> Nobody else at that felt looks at her while she does it.

**Resolving it.** Thunk's arrival lands on five registers: his boom through the door, the glass Vittoria pours him, the room's read of him, his answer to Iolo, and the polite hour's weight. **3+ land:** he sits established, the hour costs **5 gp**, and he stakes an edge for the felt. **2 or fewer:** he sits as entertainment, **15 gp** already gone, and the felt turns uphill.

### Beat 3. The Deck Runs Thin

*The lamps have burned an hour lower and the nut bowl's half gone. Thunk's count **sharpens** hand over hand while four old friends of Faas's **start pricing** the orc he brought with him.*

> [!read-aloud] The Deck Runs Thin
> Faas settles in beside you and lets you lose the first hour soft and cheap, the way he told you to in the alley. The room files you under harmless.
>
> Then the deck runs thinner hand over hand, and so does the arithmetic behind your eyes. High cards gone, low cards gone, what's left adding itself up whether you mean it to or not.
>
> [[thassos|Thassos]]'s easy smile starts arriving half a second late. He deals the next hand himself, cards snapping down crisp and even, and the pile in front of him stops shrinking the way it should.
>
> Across from him, [[watches-the-deck|Watches-the-Deck]] hasn't touched her own cards in three hands. She is watching yours land.
>
> [[iolo-bracca|Iolo]] is still talking, betting into you like a man tipping a musician, and [[vittoria-sanzio|Vittoria]] fills his glass and yours without once looking at the felt.

**The hands.** One long run at the felt with five layers: the count itself and the work around it. Hide your arithmetic as you read the three players reading you, keeping the loudest voice happy. The arithmetic gets easier every pass. Nothing else does.

| Check | DC | Angle | Success | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Intelligence**: the count, one roll per hand | 15, then 13, then 11 as the deck thins | the arithmetic itself, faster and cheaper every pass until it's closer to habit than effort | the hands land where the math said: **+50 gp**, then **+150 gp**, then **+200 gp** | the count slips: **−20 gp**, then **−50 gp**, then **−80 gp**, and the deck stays a stranger that hand |
| **Sleight of Hand** | 15 | betting like you already know what's left in the deck, without letting the table see you know | the count stays buried under the boom; **+100 gp** nobody questions | something slips a hand early; **−100 gp**, that pot outright |
| **Insight** (Thassos) | 14 | the half-second delay before his smile lands, and what he's doing behind it | he's calculating, not reacting; you press **+100 gp** home over the next hands | it reads as ordinary bad luck; **−100 gp** left on the table |
| **Perception** (Watches-the-Deck) | 14 | her own cards untouched three hands running, her tongue working the air over the felt | you time your bets to stay ahead of her; **+75 gp** she never sees coming | you take her stillness for card focus until it's too late; **−75 gp** to her instead |
| **Persuasion** (Iolo) | 12 | keeping the harbour clerk talking and betting while the money moves past him | he plays badly and cheerfully into your good hands for **+100 gp**, and feeds you the mole-and-stamps talk unbidden | he drifts to the bar mid-hand and takes the room's attention with him; disadvantage on Beat 4's Constitution |

**Running the table.** Log each result as it lands; the pile in front of Thunk when the accusation hits (Beat 4) is the number Beats 4 and 5 have to protect. Best run: **+775 gp**. Worst: **−425 gp**.

### Beat 4. The Reckoning

*Thassos shoves the table past six hundred to test it, the count **gets called** out loud, and Thunk **picks his exit** with the whole room in on it.*

> [!read-aloud] The Reckoning
> Thassos shoves the whole table past six hundred in one motion, watching your hands and not your face.
>
> Faas folds a hand he should have won and says nothing about it.
>
> When the pot resolves, Thassos isn't smiling at all anymore, hands folded, watching yours. The deck sits empty and nobody moves to reshuffle it. Iolo has stopped talking.
>
> Vittoria closes the lacquer box at her elbow, and the small sound of the lid is the only thing in the room anybody reacts to.
>
> [[grukan|Grukan]] fills the front doorway, close enough that you hear his breathing. Thirty feet away, the bar-end door sits half-hidden behind the coats. Cold air moves beneath it. Nobody stands near it.
>
> Under the table, Faas's boot finds your ankle. One tap, deliberate.

> [!quote] Thassos
> You've been lucky a great many times. Every hand the deck's run thin, you've bet like you already knew.

The tap is a signal Faas favors over quiet ones. Faas has already stacked his own cut into a coat pocket, not the visible pile, two hands ago.

Thunk must choose his exit: the front door past Grukan, or the bar-end door behind the coats (if anyone clocked it in Beat 2). The chairs stay put. The den's peace holds. If a player pushes Thunk into making it a fight anyway, let it happen. Grukan moves between him and both doors before the second swing, the house keeps that hand's pot (**−100 gp**) as the price of peace, and staff escort the two of them out by the collar instead of dragging them.

| Check | DC | Angle | Success | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Constitution** | 13 | matching Thassos's six-hundred push without flinching at the size of it | two of the three hands are yours; **+400 gp** of the pot outright | the flinch shows; **−400 gp** stays with Thassos |
| **Insight** | 13 | Faas's single tap, and what it means | it means *now*. You're up and moving before Thassos finishes his arithmetic | it meant *now* a beat ago; the last pot, **100 gp** of it, stays on the felt behind you |
| **Persuasion** (Vittoria) | 15 | asking the house's leave to walk out heavy, while she is still pleased with you | she calls it a fine night's play and waives the cut; **+150 gp** that would have stayed in her box | the cut comes off the top, **−150 gp**, and she offers you credit against the rest, warmly |
| **Deception** | 13 | "one more hand, after I stretch my legs" | buys ten clean, unhurried paces to whichever door you've picked | the whole room watches you cross it, and the alley knows you're coming |
| **Sleight of Hand** | 12 | loading the pile into coats, sleeves, and one boot before you stand up out of the chair | it's stowed tight; Beat 5's run spills only **50 gp** whatever else goes wrong | it goes out in your arms in plain sight, and the alley takes its share |

**Resolving it.** All five land in the same held moment between Thassos's push and Thunk standing. Each decision crystallizes. How much of that pot will he win? How fast does he read the tap? Will the house let him keep it? How does the pile leave the room? **4+ land:** Thunk walks with the full pile, bearing the house's blessing and a head start. **Fewer:** the leaving costs what the cells above say. Beat 5 opens with the den's gaze fixed on the door.

### Beat 5. Out the Alley

*One run, all the silver, **whatever survives** the alley is the answer Nona never asked out loud.*

> [!read-aloud] Transition
> [[grukan|Grukan]] doesn't stop the two of you going out, which tells you everything about how close it had been to stopping you coming back.

> [!read-aloud] Out the Alley
> You run the alley while silver cascades from your arms and coats. One boot leaks coins. You laugh the whole way, big and booming. The cold fat smell of the butcher's rides out with you.
>
> Faas stays exactly as flat as ever, no laugh in him tonight, and he has not yet said one word about halves. He moves fast for a man on two peg legs, faster than anyone his age has any right to.
>
> Somewhere behind you, a grating scrapes back over a drain.
>
> Ahead, the alley forks the same two ways it did going in, and past the fork a night-soil man stands at the mouth with his barrow, turned your way, waiting on the noise to explain itself.

| Check | DC | Angle | Success | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **[[dexterity\|Dexterity]] (Acrobatics)** | 13, or 11 through the bar-end door if you clocked it in Beat 2 | the fork itself, arms full, one boot heavier than the other, no time to pick your footing | clean and fast, no wrong turns; only **50 gp** shakes loose | a stumble and a real fistful down the gutter grate; **200 gp** gone, or **100 gp** if Beat 1 mapped the alley |
| **Stealth** | 12 | getting past the night-soil man's barrow without giving him a good look at either of you | he gets a blur of two men running and nothing worth repeating | he gets a clear look at an orc gunner carrying silver, and a name to sell to whoever asks first |
| **Persuasion** (Faas) | 13 | naming the split out loud, mid-run, before he settles it himself | he agrees to even halves without slowing down; **+150 gp** over the quiet default | he takes his own cut out of the middle and never mentions it; **−150 gp** |
| **Perception** | 14 | the grating going back over a drain that has nothing under it worth venting | you catch the small, webbed hand doing the dragging. You'll remember it forever. | it reads as a rat in a gutter and the sound goes nowhere |
| **Insight** | 13 | Grukan filling the doorway behind you, and whether that room means to follow | you know you're clear; walk the last stretch and lose nothing more | you run the whole way at full speed, and another **50 gp** goes into the cobbles |

**Resolving it.** All five run at once, from the door to the boat. Footing, a witness at the alley mouth, whatever crawls under the drain, and how the split with Faas settles. **3+ land:** Thunk reaches the *Uncertainty* with nearly everything, an even share and nobody on Tallow Row able to name him. **Fewer:** the alley eats what the cells above say and one of the losses follows him past tonight.

![[s07-scene02-beat5-the-getaway]]

**The ending holds:** Thunk and Faas make it back to the *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]* with whatever the felt and the alley left them. Whether it's enough to close Nona's silence for good stays open by design, exactly as the run itself leaves it.

# Faerûn

A continent of old crowns, older ruins and merchant houses that outlast both, wrapped in a sea whose southern reaches nobody has finished charting.

## Overview

Faerûn is the western continent of the world Toril. The rest of that world sits at its edges and rarely intrudes. Kara-Tur lies far to the east, Zakhara to the south-east, Maztica and Anchorome across the western ocean. When people at a Faerûnian table say "the world", they mean this continent and the waters around it.

Crowns hold land here by law and by birth. Merchant houses hold it by debt, and often hold it harder. Wizards and gods hold whatever the other two cannot reach. Those arrangements grind against each other without pause, and most trouble a party meets grows out of that grinding.

The year is 1495 DR. A century of upheaval has ended. The Second Sundering settled the world back toward a familiar shape, the gods took up something close to their old roles, and the map reads true again wherever anyone bothers to sail. The places nobody sails are the point of this page.

### Tenets

1. Coin outranks crown more often than either will admit. A debt crosses borders that an army cannot.
2. Magic is old, and someone who knew better buried most of it. Ruins here are hazards before they are treasure.
3. The gods answer, and their answers cost. At sea, worship is a bargain struck in blood and salt; on land, it is a civic duty.
4. Somebody with a reason charted every coast that appears on a map. Blank space on one records a decision.
5. The world broke and mended twice inside living memory. Nobody trusts that it has finished.

## Geography

The continent runs roughly north to south along a western coastline, with the inland Sea of Fallen Stars splitting its middle.

| Amn | nation | Merchant oligarchy south of Baldur's Gate. Runs on trading companies and colonial charters. |

| [[shattered-sea\|The Shattered Sea]] | archipelago | Open water south of Chult. Sixty thousand square miles of reef and channel. |

Chult sits at the far south-western edge of the continent, a tongue of thick jungle and snaking rivers walled in by mountains and volcanoes on its western, southern and eastern flanks, hiding the whole interior from anyone sailing past — the Trackless Sea to the west, the Shining Sea to the north and east. One stretch of that coast welcomes outsiders: Port Nyanzaru on the Bay of Chult takes nearly all the shipping, governed by the seven richest traders in the city, each with one vote and a trade of his own to corner. Amn ran the place until 1484 DR, when a Chultan group bought it back, and those traders still meet in Goldenthrone, an old Amnian palace above the bay.

South of the peninsula the mountains meet the water and the charts give out — no Faerûnian power has mapped that ocean, and nothing on a Sword Coast chart sits in it. The Shattered Sea lies there. A crossing runs weeks of open water, far enough south to keep the islands' business their own, close enough that Port Nyanzaru is still the nearest real harbour and the only safe way north. Everything [[verdant-scatter|the Scatter]] knows of Faerûn came down that one route, and no king would keep troops at that range, so the powers working these waters hold charters instead.

## Peoples & Powers

Humans fill the settled coasts. Dwarves keep the mountain holds and do the continent's engineering. Elves have pulled back further than they once sat, and half their old realms are ruins with tenants now. Halflings, gnomes, orcs, tieflings and dragonborn all live in the port cities without comment. That is what makes those cities worth living in.

The powers worth naming at a table are seldom the crowns. The Lords' Alliance binds the northern cities loosely. Amn's ruling council runs its trading companies, and those companies charter ventures the council can disown. Thay exports necromancy and grudges. The drow of Menzoberranzan trade upward more than anyone admits.

[[dravosi-crown|The Dravosi Crown]] and [[tessarine-concordat|the Tessarine Concordat]] both reach the Shattered Sea from here. The Concordat began as an Amnian merchant-house consortium, chartered on the same model that once put Amn in Port Nyanzaru. The Crown sails on a Tethyrian naval charter. Its inspection law, its commissions and its courts martial all descend from that charter, and [[tyr|Tyr]] presides over them because Tethyr's courts do.

## Faith & Pantheon

Faerûn keeps a crowded pantheon, and worship is practical. A sailor pays [[umberlee|Umberlee]] tribute at every working harbour because the sea kills, not because she is kind, and calls on [[valkur|Valkur]] below decks and quietly, since the Waveservants treat that prayer as an insult to her. A magistrate swears by [[tyr|Tyr]] in courts, commissions and garrison posts, because an oath sworn by Tyr is enforceable. Mystra's worship gathers wherever spellcasters organise; Selûne and Shar fight an old feud through mortals more than temples; Talos is feared and appeased rather than loved; Tempus stands behind armies on both sides of a war at once; Kelemvor presides at every funeral; Lathander's rites open new ventures and new reigns.

## Cosmology & Planes

Faerûn sits on the Material Plane and touches the standard planes described in [[default-world|The Default World]]: the Feywild and Shadowfell as its echoes, the elemental realms beneath, the Outer Planes beyond.

The local features matter more than the catalogue. The **Weave** is the structure all arcane magic draws through, and Mystra maintains it. Damage to the Weave caused the Spellplague. A century of that taught Faerûnians to treat unattended magic as a hazard first and a curiosity second.

Planar breaches are rare but real, and they persist once opened. The fissure to the [[elemental-plane-of-water|Elemental Plane of Water]] beneath [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]] is one such breach.

## History & Calendar

Faerûn dates by Dalereckoning, written DR. The present year is 1495 DR.

- **Before -3000 DR.** Elven and dwarven realms hold the continent. They raised most of what now stands as ruin.
- **Around -2000 DR.** Netheril rises on floating cities and arcane power, then falls and leaves the Anauroch desert behind.
- **1385 DR.** A murder takes Mystra and the Weave tears. The Spellplague reshapes the world for a century and briefly cuts Chult loose as an island.
- **1484 DR.** Amn gives up Port Nyanzaru to a Chultan consortium.
- **1487 DR.** The Second Sundering closes. The world settles toward its older shape and the gods resume their places.
- **1495 DR.** The present. Northern powers are steady enough to fund distant ventures again, so the Shattered Sea's charters still hold.

## GM Notes

Published Forgotten Realms canon covers Faerûn, Toril, Chult, Port Nyanzaru, Amn, Tethyr, the Weave, the Sundering and every deity named above; treat that material as fixed, since a session that contradicts one of them makes every third-party map, module and wiki page at the table wrong. The Shattered Sea belongs to us instead, along with everything in it — the archipelago, [[dravosi-crown|the Dravosi Crown]], [[tessarine-concordat|the Tessarine Concordat]], [[antheri|the Antheri]], [[grung-clans|the Grung Clans]], [[the-passage|The Passage]] and [[waveservants|the Waveservants]] — free to rewrite any of it.

The join between the two stays deliberate and thin. The archipelago sits in water no published source charts, weeks south of Chult, and reaches Faerûn only through Port Nyanzaru. Keeping that distance holds the campaign's politics self-contained while giving the colonial charters somewhere real to have come from.

A second constraint runs across both halves of that boundary, and it governs copying instead of invention. Forgotten Realms material sits outside the SRD and stays Wizards of the Coast's property; this page summarises it in original prose — never paste published text into the vault.

No world-level secret sits unrevealed on this page beyond the archipelago's location itself: the Shattered Sea's charts are the one thing published Faerûnian sources genuinely do not carry, and that gap is the whole reason the join holds.

# Faction content

Fuel for a faction's concept, its leadership's craft, and its fronts.
Read this during the interview when the DM doesn't have a concept yet, or
when detailing a faction's leadership needs sharpening. Every table here
cites its source; use as a spark, not a script — a faction still needs its
own PC-Connection and vector-shaped goal fields named, per
`.claude/skills/draft-content/references/faction.md`'s own rules.

## Front Archetypes

Twenty campaign-scale conflict archetypes
(`vault/refs/ideas/spiral-campaign-development.md` § Campaign Fronts). A
front is "a point of conflict that advances and retreats" — offer these
when a faction has no agenda/front concept yet:

Thieves' guild, Dark necromancer, Armageddon cult, Mercenary army,
Forgotten machine, Evil construct, Demon prince, Archdevil, Corrupt noble
lord, Rival adventurers, Mages' guild, Outlander horde, Meteor storm,
Planar invaders, Powerful archmage, Ancient lich, Blood-raging cannibals,
Unseelie fey lord, Draconic terror, Undead prince.

**Campaign-wide guideline** (not enforced structurally): a campaign runs
at most ~3 active fronts at a time across *all* factions — more than that
and pressure stops being legible to the table. Worth a sanity check across
`vault/campaigns/shattered-sea/factions/*.md` when adding a new one.

## Organization Concepts

Twenty organization/faction/patron archetypes that can pre-connect the
whole party (`vault/refs/ideas/connecting-characters.md` § Group
Connections). Offer these when the DM needs a faction concept from
scratch, especially one meant to serve the PC-Connection Requirement
directly — a faction the party already belongs to (or owes, or broke
from) is a PC-Connection for free:

Mercenary company, Self-employed investigators, Official investigators,
Royal advisors, Thieves' guild, Secret society, Religious investigators,
Adventuring company, Business investigators, Assassins' guild, Wizarding
school, Monastic students, [[gladiator|Gladiator]] school, Military
specialists, Spy network, Constabulary, Magically bound servants, Divinely
inspired, Protectors of the common folk, Seekers of vengeance.

## Villain & Antagonist Design

Craft checklist for a faction's leadership and its lieutenants — canonical
copy lives in `vault/refs/vault/npc/references/villains.md` § Villain design
checklist (leader) and its Antagonists variant (lieutenants), both
condensed from `.claude/skills/composing-beats/references/composition.md` §§ Villains,
Antagonists. Apply when detailing `## Members`, not just naming names: the
leader is the villain, lieutenants are the antagonists.

(`vault/refs/ideas/core-adventure-generators.md` § Patrons and NPCs) —

yet:

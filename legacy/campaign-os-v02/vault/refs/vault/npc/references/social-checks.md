# Worldview and social checks

Optional but recommended: pick one worldview word for the NPC (Friendly,
Suspicious, Brash, Elitist, Carefree, Loyal, Opportunistic, Cautious,
Optimistic, Selfless, Ambitious, Greedy, Outgoing, or similar) and note it
in Voice & Delivery. It's a fast, consistent lever for adjudicating social
interaction checks against this NPC at the table — condensed from
`vault/refs/ideas/npc-generator.md`.

Improvise the DC from how the *approach* lands against that worldview,
rather than fixing one number per NPC.

> [!mechanic] Social-check DC by approach
> The check is [Charisma](vault/srd/rules/charisma.md) (Persuasion, Deception, or Intimidation, whichever
> the approach actually uses). Set the DC from the approach, then run it as
> a `[!check]` on the page where the interaction happens.
>
> **Plays to the worldview.** DC 10 — an easy ask in the direction the NPC
> already leans.
> **Neutral, or no worldview established.** DC 12 — the default.
> **Fights the worldview hard.** Up to DC 20 — a demand, a lie, or an ask
> that costs them what they most want to keep.
>
> **On a failure** the NPC does not simply refuse: they harden one step
> toward their worldview and the door narrows — a price rises, a name is
> withheld, a third party hears about the asking.
>
> Worked pair: a Suspicious NPC meeting a straightforward, honest approach
> sits at DC 12; the same NPC meeting a demand or a lie sits at DC 18.

# Draft — Calendar Checklist

Run `vault/refs/vault/_common/checklist.md` first; these are the
calendar-only additions.

- [ ] `## Structure` lists every unit smallest to largest, each one named
      and counted from the interview, never assumed (no default 24-hour
      day, 7-day week, 12-month year, single moon, or four seasons).
- [ ] Nothing on this page restates a dated sequence of events — that
      content is wikilinked to its `lore` timeline page instead (the
      Calendar/Lore Boundary).
- [ ] `reckoning` names the real epoch this calendar counts from, not a
      placeholder.
- [ ] `current_date` falls in the same year the `within:` world page's own
      `current_date` states, if that page sets one.
- [ ] Every OPTIONAL heading with no real content (`## Intercalary Days &
      Leap Rule`, `## Holidays & Observances`, `## Moons`, `## Seasons`)
      is deleted outright, never left empty or filled with a placeholder
      row.
- [ ] Every holiday, moon, and season row states what a party actually
      sees or feels, not just a name.

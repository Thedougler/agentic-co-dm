# Draft — Season, Output Structure

Maps onto the season template's six H2s. Method steps are
`.claude/skills/draft-content/references/season.md` § Method.

- **`## Overview`** — the throughline (Method 1) connecting this
  season's sessions, and how it picks up from the previous season's
  Finale (or the campaign's opening premise for Season 1). One link to
  the campaign page, never a restatement of it.
- **`## Fronts on Stage`** — the ≤3 advancing fronts (Method 2):
  owning-page wikilink, where it stands now, what it does next if
  nothing stops it.
- **`## PC Arcs`** — one row per PC (Method 3): wikilink, the specific
  pull, last session it moved.
- **`## Sessions`** — one row per played session, in order: number,
  wikilink, one-line outcome. Updated after every session's recap
  lands, not batched.
- **`## Finale / End Condition`** — the GM-planned close, Routes and
  If-ignored (Method 4), and whether it's been reached.
- **`## GM Notes`** — themes this season explores, threads deliberately
  left loose for the next season (named, not implied), secrets not yet
  surfaced.

## Out of scope

- A quest's shape or beats — `.claude/skills/draft-content/references/quest.md`.
- The setting-wide premise, Truths, or Icons — `.claude/skills/draft-content/references/campaign.md`.
- A single session's recap prose — `recap-writer`.
- Writing `vault/campaigns/shattered-sea/seasons/` outside the
  draft/pending flow, or flipping `status:`/`publish:` past `pending` —
  `transcript-ingest`'s and PUBLISH's move exclusively.

# Brute (CR 2) — Generic Stat Block

Heavy-hitting veterans, capable bodyguards, low-ranking demons or devils, dangerous monsters in the wild, and powerful humanoids can all be represented by this stat block. A brute can serve as a boss against 2nd-level characters, an elite foe against two 4th-level characters, or a one-on-one opponent at 5th level, or in large groups at 10th level. This stat block relies on [Strength](vault/srd/rules/strength.md).

## Brute (Lazy GM)

- Source: The Lazy GM's 5E Monster Builder
- Size: Medium or Large
- Type: Creature
- Armor Class: 13 (studded leather or natural armor)
- Hit Points: 45 (7d8+14)
- Speed: 30 ft.
- Challenge Rating: 2

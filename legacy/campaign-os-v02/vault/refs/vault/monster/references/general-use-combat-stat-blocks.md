# General-Use Combat Stat Blocks (5e Monster Builder)

These are general-use stat blocks built for reskinning into whatever monsters are needed for combat encounters.

Each stat block uses d8 Hit Dice, but can be used for creatures in a range of sizes. Each focuses on a primary ability score, but abilities can be shifted as needed to better fit the story of the creature the stat block represents — swap [Strength](vault/srd/rules/strength.md) and [Intelligence](vault/srd/rules/intelligence.md) to run a spellcaster instead of a melee combatant, or switch [Dexterity](vault/srd/rules/dexterity.md) and Strength to turn a shifty rogue into a powerful fighter.

A stat block's attack lets you choose the most appropriate type of damage for a creature, and its reach or range can be increased easily. Ranges for attacks are given as a single number indicating maximum range, but that range can be modified or replaced with the normal and long range of a specific weapon as desired.

The spread of challenge ratings across these stat blocks provides options for weak, moderate, and strong foes at any character level. Each stat block's description includes comparisons to characters of different levels, giving guidelines for when a stat block can serve as a boss, an elite foe (suitable for two characters against one creature), a one-on-one combatant, or a member of larger groups of two to four monsters per character. All these setups are geared toward a hard encounter, but one the characters should definitely be able to win.

## Minion (CR 1/8)

The low-CR minions represented by this stat block might include ravenous rats, weak skeletons, shifty bandits, or low-ranking cultists. A minion can serve as a one-on-one combatant against 1st-level characters, or can be deployed in large groups at 4th level or above. This stat block focuses on Dexterity as its primary ability. Despite its source title suggesting a CR 1–8 range, the source defines only this single fixed CR 1/8 statline, with no scaling table across CR 1 through 8. See [Minion (CR 1–8) — Generic Stat Block](vault/refs/vault/monster/references/statblock-minion-cr1-8.md) for the full write-up. ^minion-cr18-desc

- **Name:** Minion
- **Size/Type:** Small or Medium Creature
- **Armor Class:** 11
- **Hit Points:** 9 (2d8)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Senses:** passive Perception 11
- **Challenge:** 1/8 (25 XP)
- **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS     | CHA     |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- |
| 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) |

### Minion — Actions

***Attack.*** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) damage.

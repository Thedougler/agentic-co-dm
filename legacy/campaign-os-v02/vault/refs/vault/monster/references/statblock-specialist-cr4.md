# Specialist (CR 4) — Generic Stat Block

This stat block can represent spies, assassins, hunters, and trained elite forces. The specialist serves as a boss for 4th-level characters, an elite opponent versus two 5th-level characters, or a one-on-one combatant for 10th-level characters, or in large groups against 16th-level characters. [[dexterity|Dexterity]] is this stat block's primary ability.

## Stat Block

- **Name:** Specialist (Lazy GM)
- **Source:** The Lazy GM's 5E Monster Builder
- **Size:** Medium
- **Type:** Creature
- **Armor Class:** 14
- **Hit Points:** 84 (13d8+26)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Ability Scores (STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA):** 12, 18, 14, 10, 14, 12
- **Senses:** passive Perception 14
- **Challenge Rating:** 4

### Saving Throws

- Dexterity +6
- [[wisdom|Wisdom]] +4

### Skills

- Acrobatics +6
- Perception +4
- Stealth +6

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** The specialist makes two attacks.
- **Attack.** Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (3d6 + 4) damage.

# Reskin templates and reusable base stat blocks

Load when Reuse Before Inventing applies and a `vault/campaigns/shattered-sea/monsters/` or
SRD/RAW check found nothing close enough to reskin directly — before
reaching for `vault/refs/vault/monster/references/cr-design.md`'s full
custom-CR method (§ 4 onward), check whether a quick base + one of these
templates gets there faster.

These are starting material, not a replacement for
`vault/refs/vault/monster/references/cr-design.md`'s CR math — apply a
template, then re-check the result's defensive/offensive CR (§ 4) if the
template pushes the creature's numbers meaningfully.

---

## Where the templates live

Each of these is the sole owner of its own content — this page only
routes to them:

- **Variant templates** (elemental, dire, fiendish, spell-infused) —
  `vault/refs/vault/monster/references/templates.md`.
- **Undead templates** (skeleton, zombie, ghoul, wight, wraith, vampire
  spawn) — `vault/refs/vault/monster/references/undead-templates.md`.
- **General-use reskinnable stat blocks** (Minion CR 1/8 through Champion
  CR 15) — `vault/refs/vault/monster/references/general-use-combat-stat-blocks.md`.

## Reskin bias

`vault/refs/vault/monster/references/statblock-format.md` § Reskin bias
owns the call between reskinning one of the above (or an SRD creature)
and writing a full custom stat block via
`vault/refs/vault/monster/references/cr-design.md`.

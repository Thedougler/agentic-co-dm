# Myrmidon (CR 7) — Generic Stat Block

Powerful elite bodyguards, high priests, wizards, warlocks, sorcerers, demons, and devils can all be represented by this stat block. A myrmidon can serve as a boss monster for 5th-level characters, an elite combatant against two characters of 7th level, or a one-on-one combatant against 14th-level characters, or in large groups against 20th-level characters. This stat block focuses on [[intelligence|Intelligence]].

## Stat Block

- **Name:** Myrmidon (Lazy GM)
- **Source:** The Lazy GM's 5E Monster Builder
- **Size:** Medium or Large
- **Type:** Creature
- **Armor Class:** 15 (chain shirt or natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 130 (20d8+40)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Ability Scores (STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA):** 10, 14, 14, 18, 14, 10
- **Saving Throws:** [[dexterity|Dexterity]] +5, [[wisdom|Wisdom]] +5
- **Skills:** Perception +5
- **Senses:** passive Perception 15
- **Challenge Rating:** 7

## Actions

- **Multiattack:** The myrmidon makes three attacks.
- **Attack:** Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (3d8 + 4) damage.

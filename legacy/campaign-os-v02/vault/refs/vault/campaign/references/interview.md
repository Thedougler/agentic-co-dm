# Interview — full question set

**Ongoing subtype:**

- The campaign pitch, in one sentence? No pitch yet →
  `craft.md` § Campaign Pitch for sparks.
- The Six Truths (or however many genuinely apply) — the non-negotiable
  facts that shape every scene? No truths yet → same reference § The Six
  Truths.
- The starting location and starting situation — where does the party
  begin, and why are they together? No starting-location concept yet →
  same reference § Starting Location.
- Who are the Icons — the handful of NPCs/factions whose actions ripple
  through the setting? For each: does their pursuit need a Front on their
  own page (hand off to `.claude/skills/draft-content/references/faction.md`), or are they backdrop for now?
- Inspiration, themes, tone — what does this campaign draw from, what
  question is it built to explore, what register does it refuse to break?
- Safety & Tone — what sensitive topics might this campaign touch, and
  what are this table's hard lines vs. off-screen content (Ask, Never
  Default To Nothing)? No answer yet → `craft.md` § Safety & Tone.
- Session zero — shared group connection or individual PC relationships
  (Session-Zero Pulls On Something Real)? No concept yet → same reference
  § Character Connections.

**One-shot subtype:**

- The pitch/draw — what draws the party in, in player-facing terms? No
  strong-start idea yet → `craft.md` § Strong Starts.
- Rumors the party may have heard (true/false, DM's call which).
- The background — what actually happened, and the villain's real
  motivation (Villain Motivation, Not Just The Pitch).
- Gameplay procedures — encounter triggers, special behaviors,
  environmental defaults.
- Encounters, treasure, monsters — which are genuine one-offs (inline) vs.
  reusable content needing its own page (Hand Off Reusable Content)?
- Safety & Tone — same question as ongoing, scoped to this one adventure.
- Party connection (Session-Zero Pulls On Something Real) — shared or
  individual? One-shots lean toward a single shared connection for speed
  (`craft.md` § Character Connections).

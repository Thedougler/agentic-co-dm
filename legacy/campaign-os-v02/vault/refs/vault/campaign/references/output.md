# Output Structure — mapped onto the campaign template

**`## Overview`** — the subtype-appropriate H3s (Premise/Truths/Key
Figures/Starting Situation for ongoing; Pitch/Rumors & Legends for
one-shot). Delete the other fork's H3s entirely, don't leave them empty.

**`## Safety & Tone`** — Ask, Never Default To Nothing: sensitive topics,
hard lines, off-screen content, the table's pause cue, or an explicit
"none named" after asking.

**`## GM Notes`** — Inspiration/Themes/Tone (ongoing) or
Background/Gameplay/Encounters/Treasure/Monsters (one-shot), per
Villain Motivation, Not Just The Pitch and Hand Off Reusable Content.

# Degrade By Asking

- Interview questions unanswered → escalate per § Interview, never invent
  the pitch, Truths, Icons, or Safety & Tone content.
- Unclear whether a request is campaign-level vs. lore/NPC/faction/quest →
  ask directly (§ Boundary & hand-off) rather than forking a duplicate page.
- An Icon's pursuit sounds like it needs a Front but the DM hasn't decided
  → ask, don't silently create (or silently skip) the .claude/skills/draft-content/references/faction.md hand-off.
- Sensitive-topics discussion hasn't happened yet → ask what the table
  wants named, don't default to "nothing sensitive" (Ask, Never Default To
  Nothing).
- Genuinely no PC connects to a session-zero question yet → still a DM
  call (Session-Zero Pulls On Something Real), not a default; ask rather
  than writing "it's just premise."

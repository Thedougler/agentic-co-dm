# Draft — Item Checklist

Run `vault/refs/vault/_common/checklist.md` first; these are the
item-only additions.

- [ ] `one_thing` stated in one sentence; `## Mechanics` has exactly one
      H3-worthy power (or a clear reason a second is inseparable from it).
- [ ] `rarity:`/`attunement:` frontmatter set before mechanics were
      designed (not left at the template's `common`/`false` placeholder),
      with 2 RAW items cited and comparable power confirmed.
- [ ] No prohibited class-exclusive mechanic reproduced (the Class-Feature
      Prohibition).
- [ ] Attunement decided via the decision tree, not a guess.
- [ ] Every power in `## Mechanics` is `[HB]`/`[RAW]`-labeled, bounded
      (range/duration/recovery), and names its edge cases, plus a closing
      **Limitations** line.
- [ ] Stat line follows the SRD category/rarity/attunement format.
- [ ] `## Provenance` states origin and current holder, wikilinked where
      they resolve to pages.
- [ ] DM Review Gate run and passed before `status:` left `draft` —
      one-thing, rarity justification, and RAW benchmarks were presented
      and approved.

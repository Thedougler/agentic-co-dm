# Stories Playbook: /continue-story

Reads in full: _Index.md, the story's rules file, Timeline.md (if it exists), Quicknotes.md, the last two chapter files (by chapter number, not modification date) or the single story file for short stories, and any Character files for characters appearing in those final chapters.

Presents a structured re-entry brief:

- **Where the story stands**: two to three sentences on what has happened and narrative momentum.
- **Last chapter recap**: what happened (important moments and character movement) and where the story ended. Be specific, quoting key dialogue or actions.
- **Open threads**: what remains unresolved. Check Timeline.md, character files, and _Index.md's open-questions section for plot threads, questions, and character arcs in progress.
- **Items to action**: notes from the Quicknotes file ready to write next.
- **What to write next**: one short paragraph to orient rather than prescribe.

Kept tight: the goal is getting the author back into the world in under two minutes of reading.

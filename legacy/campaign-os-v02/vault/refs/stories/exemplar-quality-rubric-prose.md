# Exemplar Quality Rubric: Prose and pacing

## E. Prose and pacing

- **R21 — Register discipline.** Mood lives in names, fiction, and single
  framing sentences; mechanical text stays deadpan and imperative; jokes never
  sit inside numbers. One fiction sentence leads a feature, then mechanics —
  never interleaved. Check: strip the fiction lines and the mechanics still
  parse; strip the mechanics and no rule is lost. The player-facing and
  DM-facing split itself is owned by `vault/refs/stories/register.md`.
  *(Wild Sheep Chase; Darker Dungeons' two voices; Way of the Flesh features.)*
- **R22 — Specificity over atmosphere.** Description budgets go to concrete,
  awful, or vivid particulars (a body nailed with wooden spikes; a crown of
  bent cutlery) — not mood adjectives or stock dread phrases. Check: flag any
  sentence that could describe any other scene. *(Night Hunter; Serpent Kings.)*
- **R23 — Verb variety and breathing room.** Consecutive scenes change verb
  (talk, fight, solve, sneak, navigate, choose); quiet beats follow loud ones;
  escalation ladders rise one register at a time. Check: label each scene's
  verb — no three-in-a-row repeats without intent. *(Potent Brew's five rooms;
  Night Hunter's ladder; Pleasantries' four-encounter spread.)*
- **R24 — Deliberate repetition for the runner.** Each unit's summary restates
  the background it needs (who someone is, why this matters) so running never
  requires backtracking — repetition in service of the table is correct, not a
  defect to lint away. Check: any unit can be run cold after reading only
  itself plus the document's front matter. *(Silverpine's on-purpose redundant
  summaries; Night Hunter/Potent Brew self-contained scenes.)*

# Stories Playbook — Fiction Writing Workflow (gsarig/ai-playbooks)

Recreated from [gsarig/ai-playbooks](https://github.com/gsarig/ai-playbooks/tree/main/playbooks/stories)'s `stories` playbook. This fiction-writing workflow for Obsidian and Claude Code is designed for novels, novellas, and short stories. Claude Code scaffolds your story structure and tracks characters and locations across your work. It runs developmental and language editing passes, while also flagging continuity errors and managing story state. See the subpages below for full details.

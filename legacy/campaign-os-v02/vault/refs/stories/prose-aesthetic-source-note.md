# Source Note — where each entry came from

Provenance for every pattern in [prose-aesthetic.md](prose-aesthetic.md) and
[banned-patterns.md](banned-patterns.md): the GM's own words, the task, and
the date. Append a new entry here whenever either file gains one; the rule
itself goes in that file, not here.

- **Trailing justification clauses**, **Announced stakes**, **"Wrong" as a
  descriptor** — 2026-07-22, session-07 story drafting. Dictated directly
  by the GM.
- **Tautological simile**, **Forced metaphor**, **Negation-stacking**,
  **Coy narration**, **Narratorial hedging**, **Had-I-But-Known
  foreshadow**, **Antithesis-mirror closers** — 2026-07-22, same session,
  after the GM rejected a full draft as "drek." Sourced externally (Forbes'
  "Seven Tells of AI Writing," Killzone Blog on playing coy, Wikipedia on
  Had-I-But-Known and chiasmus, StoryScope/arXiv on AI-fiction
  idiosyncrasies), not guessed from the flagged lines.
- **"Same X" continuity filler** — 2026-07-25, session-07 QC-fix work. GM:
  "incredibly lazy writing" — "describe what's different, not what's the
  same."
- **Bureaucratic framing for a charged beat** — 2026-08-03, [[aruhe|Aruhe]] [[blight|Blight]]
  story-workshop. GM: "dont portray her as an accountant, her fury is
  towards the grung and she simply treats any others who come to the land
  and take from it as the same as them"; same session, on a
  druids'-escape beat: "hate that whole whimpering about logistics line,
  like could you make them plotting there escape sound any less cool."
- **Wry, retrospective narration over a life-or-death sequence** —
  2026-08-03, same draft. GM: "honestly all of it should feel dangerous?
  her partner litterally dies in the process, its a tragedy."
- **Pre-leaking a reveal through a monitoring detail** — 2026-08-03, same
  draft. GM: "stop forcasting that hes's not breathing before the reveal,
  you ruin the surprise and reveal."
- **Core aesthetic**, **Prose style** — 2026-08-03, Aruhe castaways story
  draft. GM, rejecting the draft's tone against the Blight arc: "the tone
  and register sucks use the same tone as the arch blight document."
- **Over-narrated confrontation dialogue** — 2026-08-03, [[penance-bar|Penance Bar]]
  shrine-confrontation draft. GM: the draft "really sucks, doesn't tell
  the story nearly as well as the simple transcript its based on."
- **Vague placeholder in place of the fact**, **Stacked adjectives in
  place of the image** — 2026-08-03, straight after the [[quest-aruhe-castaways|Half the Fleet]]
  cleanup. GM: "invented mystery and vague prose," and "superfluous
  details utilizing long sentences tacking on superfluous long chains of
  vague adjective rather than just clearly and evocatively describing the
  thing." Enforced by `CampaignOS.VagueNarration`
  (`docs/vale-styles/CampaignOS/VagueNarration.yml`), scoped to the story
  shelf.
- **Genuine-unknown carve-out** (inside the placeholder entry above) —
  2026-08-03, same day, first pass written too generously. GM: "the
  person wouldn't say just something pulled them in, they'd try and
  explain what they saw even if they couldn't put a name to it. 'a beast?
  a deer maybe? i dont know something dragged him off into the forest.'"
- **"The way a man does X" simile** — 2026-08-03, [[arc-blight-of-aruhe|Half a Mile]] rewrite. GM,
  on "looking at the island the way a man looks across a field he means to
  cut": "I really hate this trope you do all the time."
- **Abstract label as the reveal** — 2026-08-03, same beat. GM, on "That's
  not devotion. That's architecture.": "i dont know what that failing is
  but stop describing cool things in the least cool terms."
- **NPC crosstalk in front of the party** — 2026-08-04, `vault/refs/npc-guidance.md`
  doctrine addition (commit `f196f17e6`). Scaffolded proactively, not a
  reactive GM correction — no correction quote exists for this entry.
- **Deliberate withholding** — 2026-08-04, cold-context-reviewer/fixer
  loop against `vault/stories/arc-blight-of-aruhe.md`. GM, confirming mid-loop: "His and
  Her identity are deliberately kept hidden from the reader and
  intentionally not told."

- **Deadpan undercut**, **Profanity spent once**, **Unannounced cuts**,
  **Section titles as counterpoint**, **Exact numbers as anchor**,
  **Aphoristic opener** — 2026-08-08, first `writing-style` run. Extracted from
  four pieces of the GM's own pre-AI writing (one SF novella opening, three
  short philosophical essays, c. 2010s), supplied for this extraction and
  **deleted afterwards at the GM's instruction** — the quoted examples held in
  [prose-aesthetic.md](prose-aesthetic.md) are now the only surviving record of
  the source. The GM's framing: "very old… I'm a better writer now", "this is
  not exhaustive, this is small old incomplete sample, just meant to guide how a
  single registar documented is crafted". Treat every entry from this batch as
  **partial evidence of one mode**, never as the whole voice, and never as a
  ceiling on execution.
- **Rejected as tics, never to be encoded** — 2026-08-08, same run. Asked which
  habits in that sample he would defend if an editor flagged them, the GM
  selected only the deadpan undercut. Explicitly not defended, and therefore not
  recorded as signature: direct reader address paired with a self-indicting
  narrator; long straight technical exposition delivered without scene;
  escalating anaphora with decaying ellipses for an altered state. An editing
  pass never reintroduces these as "the GM's voice".
- **Influences** ([[influences|influences.md]]) — 2026-08-08, same run. Named
  by the GM: Stephen King (The Gunslinger, The Stand, Salem's Lot, It) as
  favourite author; Tolkien's prose and the Jackson adaptations, recorded as
  separate entries at the GM's direction ("Both, separated") since he prefers
  the films; Harry Potter recorded as an explicit non-influence ("I read every
  Harry Potter but that doesn't really touch my narrative style much now").
  Every move researched externally against critical sources — URLs are listed
  per entry on that page — never from recall.
- **Subplot excision dropped from the Jackson entry** — 2026-08-08, same run.
  GM: "drop that one that recommends agents add cuts to the narritvive without
  the dm's call, thats not good for ttrpg game mastering". Recorded as a
  *Stays behind* exclusion rather than deleted silently, so a later run does not
  re-import it from the same sources.
- **Per-move application guards removed** — 2026-08-08, same run. The influences
  page first shipped with an "at the table" / "session-flow guard" clause on
  every move. GM: "don't bake in that contraint, i dont want to inhibit
  creativity". Moves are recorded as techniques available to reach for; how a
  passage uses one is the writer's call.

- **Screen influences** ([influences.md](influences.md)) — 2026-08-08, same run,
  second research pass. GM: "The IT movies were awesome, same with the Shining,
  Gunsinlinger was awful and basically every other King movies, horror doesn't
  really do it for me. Love Guy Rictchie, Love Inglorious Basterds, Love Django
  Unchained, Love Xmen days of futures pasts, love tones of anime, jjk, fog hill
  of five elements (though thats more visual)". Recorded as Tarantino, Guy
  Ritchie, X-Men: Days of Future Past, Jujutsu Kaisen, Fog Hill of Five Elements,
  and an *Adapting dense source material* entry built from the contrast between
  the adaptations he rated and the one he called awful. Fog Hill carries no prose
  moves — research found its craft to be animation-only, matching his own "more
  visual" note, and it is recorded as a visual reference so a later run does not
  mine it for technique it lacks. "Horror doesn't really do it for me" is filed
  as a hard exclusion on the King entry: the moves are craft, the genre is not
  a default.

- **The prose contract replaces the never-do list** — 2026-08-08, after a
  dispatched drafting agent read the 191-line prohibition catalogue and then
  violated six of its rules while reporting a clean pass. GM: "banned patterns
  may be too long agents to parse well", "probably shouldn't even keep a bunch
  of bad examples of prose, should use the linter to only show negative
  guidance when necessary", and "like /writing-for-agents banned-patterns.md is
  probably not an ideal design pattern at all for ensuring we got good
  writing". Split into two halves: [banned-patterns.md](banned-patterns.md)
  states positively only what needs judgment, and Vale carries every mechanical
  tell, firing on the line with the fix in the message. The old prose moved
  intact to [banned-patterns-detail.md](banned-patterns-detail.md) as an
  adjudication record, never drafting material.
- **Measured, not assumed** — same run. A controlled rerun of the same brief
  against a positive-only contract produced *more* of the invented-stranger
  construction, not less (one instance became four), so the prohibition was
  doing real suppression work and the fix was to move it into the linter rather
  than delete it. New Vale rules: `InventedStranger`, `NarratorHedging`,
  `AnachronisticRegister`, `SamenessFiller`, `WrongDescriptor`. On their first
  run they caught every defect a manual read had found plus two it had missed.
- **Frontmatter required on stories and ideas** — 2026-08-08. GM: "stories can
  have frontmatter", "same with ideas", then "make the type: idea or type: story
  required for either, as well as created and updated frontmatter all required".
  Reverses the standing prohibition in `.claude/skills/draft-story/references/hard-rules.md`, `.claude/skills/draft-story/references/fragments.md`, and
  `vault/_templates/CLAUDE.md`. Three required keys, any further key optional;
  both templates carry them and all 21 existing pages were migrated with dates
  taken from git history.
- **A dodged linter finding means the guidance is wrong** — 2026-08-08. After
  the first split moved `InventedStranger` wholly into Vale and deleted its
  positive instruction from the contract, the next draft met the rule with
  near-synonyms that passed ("the way anything fights", "the way every story")
  rather than the behaviour. GM: "if there dodging the linter it means there
  initial guidance is wrong". The contract now carries a positive instruction
  for **every** rule including the mechanized ones — Vale detects the same rules
  the page directs, never a separate set — and a rule that ships a detector with
  no instruction is treated as incomplete.

- **Close a taboo on the permitted act**, **An in-world speaker with no name
  for a thing describes it harder**, **Explanatory tail on a landed image** —
  2026-08-10, [[hungry-isle-legend|Hungry Isle]] handout for Jean-Claude. Three
  corrections on one draft. On "A garden, set out for anyone who comes to it,
  and not for you.": "This is stupid and badly written… something much better
  would be 'A garden, to be admired, from a distance'". On the fauna summarised
  as "it came down off the ridge on wings and up out of the wet sand at a run":
  "i hate the vagueness about the beast itself… they may not have names for it
  but they'd absolutely describe them to instill fear in the children listening
  to the legend". Then: "also don't over explain in the story", which cut
  "because the eyes are soft and they know it", "because for us the sand is the
  berry now", "who had not learned yet", "That is the one who brought you this".
  One shape across all three: the draft did the work, then took it back.
- **Spoken table prose too thin** / **Theatre of the mind** — 2026-08-14,
  e09 sequence-01. GM read the open in under a minute: narration roughly
  double, Last Time / recap roughly triple, same facts held longer. Highest-quality word for each fact; length is the picture's time.
- **Handle on every person** — 2026-08-14. Bearded old man worked; "two young men" did not. Concrete noun (long-hair, branded one), not a judgment.
- **Seeds, not spotlights** / **Check is in the picture** — 2026-08-14. Known facts are seeds; each same-session piece expands with world texture. A check that follows narration is already live in the picture.

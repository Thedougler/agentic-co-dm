# Prose Aesthetic — the GM's voice

How the GM's prose actually works and what is deliberate in it. Editing
passes calibrated to this file give feedback on *the GM's* fiction; without
it they calibrate to generic competent fantasy prose.

## The calibration contract

- **Declared-intentional is never a finding.** Before flagging any stylistic
  choice, check this file — a pattern described here is deliberate.
  `content-fixer`, the line passes, `/edit-article`, `/writers-room` critique, and
  `content-quality-checker` all obey it (flagging a signature move as an error
  erodes the voice).
- **Scope: narrative register only.** DM-facing prose follows
  [register.md](register.md); read-aloud boxes follow `dnd5e-scene-narration`;
  callout containers follow `callouts`.

## Keeping this file live

This file is never finished. The moment the GM corrects a stylistic choice in
the agent's writing — a word, a phrase, a rhythm, a habit — for **any** piece
of writing, in any task, not only a dedicated editing pass: capture it here,
same turn, before moving on. File a "never do this" correction in
[banned-patterns.md](banned-patterns.md); file a positive preference under the
matching section below, adding a new H2 when none fits. Write the entry
specific, not vague — "single-sentence paragraphs are ironic payoffs, never
suggest expanding them," not "I like short sentences" — quote the correction
and the fix, and record the date and originating task in
[prose-aesthetic-source-note.md](prose-aesthetic-source-note.md). A correction
left only in the conversation gets repeated next time.

Never populate this file from prose an agent drafted — that profiles the
agent, not the GM. Build it from the GM's own finished writing, extracting
patterns (sentence rhythm, narrative distance, interiority handling,
characteristic moves) with one quoted example each.

## Core aesthetic

Literary, unflinching intensity — grief, violence, and dread rendered from
inside the body, not a clipped survival-thriller or expository register.
A draft that reads like a sequence of short, factual sentences — even if
every one is individually clean prose — has missed the aesthetic regardless
of how accurate or lint-clean it is.

Spoken table prose paints a picture the table can hold with their eyes
shut — theatre of the mind. Every word is there because it is the
clearest, most vivid, most memorable word for that fact, not because a
count needed filling. A sequence open or depiction a DM can finish in
under a minute is thin: hold the same picture longer (more clause, more
sense, the same toy), do not add a second subject. A Last Time / recap
that is only a minutes-list is thin: triple the scene beats, same facts.
2026-08-14, e09 sequence-01 read-aloud.

**Specificity goes to the interesting thing.** The demand for specific,
concrete detail targets what is warped, wrong, or plot-bearing in the
scene — the thing a reader would ask about. Mundane fixtures (rope,
provisions, ordinary tackle) get their plain name and no elaboration;
detail spent there is the same failure as vagueness spent on the vivid.
2026-08-15, e09 prose pass.

**Seeds, not spotlights.** Facts already on the parent, in a stub, or
already spoken this session are seeds. Infer the rest of the living
scene so each known fact sits at its natural size. Expansion is where
world texture and flavour land. A berry in the notes is one beat on a
beach, not the subject of every sentence, and not said twice. 2026-08-14.

## Deliberate withholding

An identity a piece withholds for its whole length is a choice, not a gap. A
character carried entirely by pronoun — including through a line where another
character says their name aloud — stays that way: never suggest adding a name,
and never resolve a pronoun to a named identity as a fix. A QC or ambiguity
pass that flags the unresolved identity closes the finding by confirming the
referent stays consistent throughout, never by inventing a name.

The same holds for anything a piece declines to explain on purpose — an
unbridged time skip, an unexplained allusion, a reveal the narration walks past.
Withholding is legible when it is consistent; check consistency, not
completeness.

## Prose style

Sentences build cumulative momentum through comma-linked clauses — long
sentences are the default carrier of intensity ("She swam it on her side, one
arm over, one arm hauling, his arm across her collarbone and her fist shut in
his sleeve."), not a run of short flat declaratives. Short fragments are
reserved for single gut-punch beats ("A thin sound. Almost nothing. The sound
a bird makes turning.") — never distributed evenly across a passage for
rhythm's sake.

When a long sentence needs restructuring for lint (readability,
sentence-monotony, tricolon), rebuild its internal clause structure — vary the
connective, fold a clause into an adjacent sentence, change what is
subordinate — rather than atomizing it into a string of short sentences. The
fix must never flatten the register to pass a check.

Exact numbers anchor a passage in reality — a precise count where a vaguer
writer rounds ("4 Months, 22 Days, and 57 versions later," "the failsafe stops
the catch above 100 Km/h"). Never round these to "months later" or "too fast";
the precision is the texture, and it does the work an atmospheric adjective
would fail at.

A declarative aphorism can open a passage and earn its expansion afterwards
("We are nothing more than beasts." / "Heaven is built. Heaven is here on
Earth."). The short assertion lands first, the reasoning follows it. Never
invert this into a wind-up that arrives at the assertion — and never mistake
the pattern for permission to open every section this way.

## Narrator and voice

**A handle on every person.** Anyone the table can see gets one referable
concrete trait the first time they appear — the bearded man, the
long-hair, the branded one — a noun the DM and the players can say
back. Not a judgment (pretty, ugly). A pair that stays "two young men"
has no handle. 2026-08-14, e09 sequence-01.

**Close third, inside the body** — the documented stance for charged material:
a death, a grief, a mutilation, a terror. The narrative distance stays at the
skin, on what the hands are doing and what the lungs cost.

**Deadpan undercut.** Humour lands *after* density or self-seriousness and
deflates the speaker, never the stakes — a passage of dense explanation closing
on a blunt one-line punchline, a triumph ending in an undignified physical
detail. It never lands during danger; a joke while someone is drowning is the
failure recorded in
[banned-patterns-detail.md](banned-patterns-detail.md) under *Wry,
retrospective narration*, not this move. That ban governs humour placed *inside* a
life-or-death beat, and leaves the undercut after one intact — an editing pass
reads the two together rather than treating all humour as the banned pattern.

Profanity marks a peak and is spent once. Distributed across a passage it stops
marking anything.

## Structure

**Unannounced cuts.** Time skips without a signpost, and the reader
reconstructs the gap from what has changed on the other side — a character
waking with days gone, the elapsed time arriving through its consequences
rather than through a transition. Never bridge one of these with "later" or a
summary of what passed; the reconstruction is the effect. Same instinct as
*Deliberate withholding* above.

**Section titles as counterpoint.** Headings carry a second vocabulary that
comments on the material rather than labelling its contents. Never retitle one
of these to describe what the section holds.

## What the GM does not do

The prose contract lives in [banned-patterns.md](banned-patterns.md): positive
instructions for the habits only judgment catches, with every mechanical tell
delegated to Vale so the negative fires on the line rather than sitting in
context while drafting. Read it before writing, run the linter after, and file
new corrections per its *Adding a rule* section.

The moves absorbed from the GM's own reading and viewing, each stated as craft
and paired with what about that work must *not* reach this repo's prose, live in
[influences.md](influences.md). Reach for a named move there when a passage
needs a technique; never reach for the source work's content. The `writing-style`
skill grows that page.

## Developmental feedback calibration

How to read the GM's choices during an editing pass.

**Intentional, never a finding:** a sentence running long through comma-linked
clauses; an identity or outcome withheld for a whole piece; a narrator who
addresses the reader; a narrator who convicts themselves; a time skip with no
transition; a section heading that comments rather than labels; an exact number
where a round one would read more smoothly.

**Always challenge:** a charged beat arriving in a lighter register than the
material ([register.md](register.md) § The weight is not the writer's choice);
an abstraction standing where a physical image belongs; a description built from
stacked adjectives; a placeholder where the POV can see the thing. These are
failures even when the surrounding prose is clean, and the GM has corrected each
of them more than once.

**Never propose:** flattening a long sentence into short ones to satisfy a
readability check; naming something the piece has deliberately left unnamed;
adding a transition across a deliberate cut; distributing a peak effect
(profanity, fragment, anaphora) evenly through a passage for rhythm.

A draft can be accurate, lint-clean, and still wrong here — the register and the
narrator stance are checked before any sentence-level finding is raised.

# Stories Playbook: Story `CLAUDE.md` Template

Story-specific rules that override the vault-level `CLAUDE.md`. Filled in before writing begins, refined as the story evolves.

- **Story Identity**: genre, length (short story | novella | novel), POV (blank inherits vault default), tense (blank inherits vault default), and tone & atmosphere.
- **World Rules**: anything Claude must treat as established, non-negotiable fact. One rule per line, specific.
- **Character Voice Guidelines**: how specific characters speak or think, used by `/dev-edit` to flag voice drift. Example: "Mira speaks in clipped, defensive sentences. She never volunteers information."
- **Developmental Focus for This Story**: weaknesses Claude actively works to improve. Claude flags these issues. Example: "Avoid over-explaining the magic system. Mystery works better with restraint."
- **Continuity Anchors**: high-stakes facts Claude must never contradict, even in suggestions. Example: "The main character's father is dead. He cannot appear alive."
- **Open Rules**: things still undecided. Claude should ask rather than assume.

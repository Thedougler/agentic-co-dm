# Calder's Tooth Wilds

Ridge-and-mud encounters for the Overland Track's switchbacks, corduroy
sections, and drainage culverts — generic prompts to roll and elaborate
live, never a settled campaign fact.

| 1d12 | Wilds Encounter                                                                    |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | A pair of giant axe beaks pacing the caravan from the ridgeline above.              |
| 2    | A giant boar sounder crashing out of the wet scrub beside the track.                |
| 3    | A drainage culvert clogged with a giant wolf spider's webbing.                      |
| 4    | A switchback so mud-slick a wagon's wheels lose their grip mid-climb.               |
| 5    | A corduroy log section with several logs rotted through underfoot.                  |
| 6    | A Crown toll patrol checking papers at an unmarked stretch of road.                 |
| 7    | A burned-out toll shelter with its ledger charred past reading.                     |
| 8    | A pack animal spooked lame by something unseen in the ridge brush.                  |
| 9    | A rockslide half-burying the track after recent rain.                               |
| 10   | A cairn of stones stacked at a switchback, no marker saying by whom.                |
| 11   | A stretch of fog pooling in a low saddle between two ridges, muffling every sound.  |
| 12   | A single giant axe beak feather, longer than a forearm, snagged on a corduroy log.  |

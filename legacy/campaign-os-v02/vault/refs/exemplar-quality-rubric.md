# Exemplar Quality Rubric

The quality bar shared by 11 beloved published RPG documents, distilled into
checkable items. Sources: the craft analyses in `docs/exemplars/`
(`docs/exemplars/a-most-potent-brew.md` — Potent Brew, `docs/exemplars/the-wild-sheep-chase.md` — Wild Sheep Chase,
`docs/exemplars/the-night-hunter.md` — Night Hunter, `docs/exemplars/tomb-of-the-serpent-kings.md` — Serpent Kings,
`docs/exemplars/fall-of-silverpine-watch.md` — Silverpine Watch, `docs/exemplars/giffyglyphs-darker-dungeons.md` — Darker Dungeons,
five Pointy Hat supplements — `docs/exemplars/pointyhat-domain-of-creation.md`, `docs/exemplars/pointyhat-the-jinx.md`,
`docs/exemplars/pointyhat-the-necromaton.md`, `docs/exemplars/pointyhat-time-for-pleasantries.md`,
`docs/exemplars/pointyhat-way-of-the-flesh.md`). Each item cites its strongest
exemplars. Print-only qualities (art, typography, physical page layout) are
excluded by design; their markdown-native substitutes — scan structure, bold
retrieval tokens, conditional depth, format selection — are §G.

Each item is a pass/fail check against a document. "Runner" means the DM at the
table; "unit" means the document's repeating element (scene, room, encounter,
feature, table entry).

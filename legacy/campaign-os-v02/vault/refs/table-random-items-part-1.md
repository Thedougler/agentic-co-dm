# Random Items: Weapons, Origins, Conditions, and Armor

Part of [Random Items](vault/refs/table-random-items.md); continues in [Random Items: Mundane Items, Spell Effects, and Potions](vault/refs/table-random-items-part-2.md).

These lists generate useful relics and objects, from mundane discoveries to powerful magical artifacts. To come up with an interesting magic weapon, roll on the Item Condition, Item Origin, [Weapon](vault/srd/items/weapon.md), and Spell Effect tables. For a weird mundane item, roll on the Item Condition, Item Origin, and Mundane Item tables without adding any effect.

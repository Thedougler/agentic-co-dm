# NPC Generator (Lazy GM Resource Document)

[[npcs|NPCs]] bring our game worlds to life. Use the generator below to quickly build NPCs to drop into a game, rolling on the tables below to generate baseline characteristics. To really bring the NPC to life, model their personality and roleplaying off characters from favorite books, TV shows, or movies, switching up gender and other traits to make them feel fresh.

This generates raw seed traits for an NPC concept before any page exists —
a different job from [[GUIDE|the NPC drafting guide]], which shapes an already-decided NPC into its page (the Toy Chest, the Wants line, the Opening move).

## Worldview

An NPC's worldview can help determine how they initially react to the characters, adjusted by how the characters approach them.

> [!mechanic]
> **Social interaction DCs.** Improvise Persuasion, Deception, or Insight checks based on NPC worldview and party approach. Typical range: DC 10 (easy) to DC 20 (very hard). Default: DC 12. Success: the NPC's attitude improves or they grant what's asked. Failure: the NPC remains unmoved.

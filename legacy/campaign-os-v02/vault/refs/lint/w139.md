# W139 — the Situation contract

`W139` holds a `type: situation` page to the contract in
`vault/_templates/_campaigns/_situation.md` (ADR-0061).

## Fix

- Fill `lifecycle:`, `pressure:`, and `if_ignored:` with concrete Situation
  state — what stage this Situation is at, what it pushes on, and what the
  world does when the party walks away.
- Delete any `## Next` section, and any inline `> [!read-aloud]` box. Spoken
  prose lives in a `type: narration` sibling that the parent embeds.
- Add every missing Beat Chart heading, per the template's § Beat Chart.

W139's prescribed-player-action detector reports at warning severity and does
not gate its tier; the three missing-field checks are structural and do.

# W83 — duplicated blocks (jscpd)

`W83` is jscpd's clone detector, run over the vault as a whole rather than
one edited file: it reports a block of text that appears, near-identically,
on two or more pages.

## Fix

Keep one owner for the block and replace the copy:

- Verbatim reuse (the two sites must read identically) collapses to a
  `![[owner#Heading]]` transclusion of the owner's section.
- A restatement in different words collapses to a `[[wikilink]]` to the
  owning page.

Rewording the copy until it slips under jscpd's token threshold leaves both
copies on the wiki and is evasion, not a fix.

## Known boilerplate

A block that every page of a type copies from its template by design is not a
clone. Add it to `_DEFAULT_KNOWN_BOILERPLATE`
(`utils/wiki-cli/src/wiki_cli/producers/duplication.py`) or in `wiki.toml`'s
`thresholds.DUPLICATION_KNOWN_BOILERPLATE`.

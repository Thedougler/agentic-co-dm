# W78 — an unsourced Session Log section

`W78` fires on a `## Session Log` heading whose body cites no session — an
empty ledger claiming a page has a play history.

## Fix

Delete the `## Session Log` heading and its body, down to the next heading or
the end of the file. An absent section already records "no appearances yet"
(`vault/_templates/CLAUDE.md` § Session Log sections); ingest adds the section
back when the page first appears at the table.

A placeholder sentence, a comment, or a "none yet" line reinstates the empty
claim the rule fires on.

# W91 — a stale context-rot marker

`W91` finds a comment that parks a decision on a condition — a `for now`, a
`workaround until X`, a `known issue` — whose date has aged past the point
where a reader can trust it.

## Fix

Re-verify the marked condition now, then act on what you find:

- Resolved -> delete the marker and restore the real setting it parks.
- Still open -> re-date the comment `(YYYY-MM-DD, until <the check that ends
  it>)` so a later reader can falsify it.

Deleting the date, or softening the wording until the marker phrase no longer
matches, leaves the parked decision in place and unfalsifiable.

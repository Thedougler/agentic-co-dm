- **Type:** Large Beast, Unaligned
- **[[armor-class|Armor Class]]:** 15
- **[[hit-points|Hit Points]]:** 52 (7d10 + 14)
- **[[speed|Speed]]:** 40 ft.

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** The scorpion makes two Claw attacks and one Sting attack.

***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. 6 (1d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [[grappled|Grappled]] condition (escape DC 13) from one of two claws.

***Sting.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage plus 11 (2d10) Poison damage.

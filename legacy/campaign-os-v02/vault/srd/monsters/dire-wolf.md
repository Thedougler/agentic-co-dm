## Traits

***Pack Tactics.*** The wolf has [[advantage|Advantage]] on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the wolf's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the [[incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition.

## Actions

***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. 8 (1d10 + 3) Piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [[prone|Prone]] condition.

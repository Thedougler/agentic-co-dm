# Giant Frog

- **Type:** Medium Beast, Unaligned
- **[[armor-class|Armor Class]]:** 11
- **[[hit-points|Hit Points]]:** 18 (4d8)
- **[[speed|Speed]]:** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
- **[[initiative|Initiative]]**: +1 (11)

- **Skills**: Perception +2, Stealth +4
- **Senses**: darkvision 30 ft.; [[passive-perception|Passive Perception]] 12
- **CR** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)

## Traits

***Amphibious.*** The frog can breathe air and water.

***Standing Leap.*** The frog's [[long-jump|Long Jump]] is up to 20 feet and its [[high-jump|High Jump]] is up to 10 feet with or without a running start.

## Actions

***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +3, reach 5 ft. 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the [[grappled|Grappled]] condition (escape DC 11).

***Swallow.*** The frog swallows a Small or smaller target it is grappling. While swallowed, the target isn't [[grappled|Grappled]] but has the [[blinded|Blinded]] and [[restrained|Restrained]] conditions, and it has [[cover|Cover]]|XPHB|Total Cover against attacks and other effects outside the frog.

While swallowing the target, the frog can't use Bite, and if the frog dies, the swallowed target is no longer [[restrained|Restrained]] and can escape from the corpse using 5 feet of movement, exiting with the [[prone|Prone]] condition.

At the end of the frog's next turn, the swallowed target takes 5 (2d4) [[acid|Acid]] damage. If that damage doesn't kill it, the frog disgorges it, causing it to exit Prone.

# Swarm of Piranhas

- **Type:** Medium Beast, Unaligned
- **[[armor-class|Armor Class]]:** 13
- **[[hit-points|Hit Points]]:** 28 (8d8 - 8)
- **[[speed|Speed]]:** 5 ft., Swim 40 ft.

- **CR** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)

## Traits

***Swarm.*** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny piranha. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain [[temporary-hit-points|Temporary Hit Points]].

***[[water-breathing|Water Breathing]].*** The swarm can breathe only underwater.

## Actions

***Bites.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5 (with [[advantage|Advantage]] if the target doesn't have all its Hit Points), reach 5 ft. 8 (2d4 + 3) Piercing damage, or 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage if the swarm is [[bloodied|Bloodied]].

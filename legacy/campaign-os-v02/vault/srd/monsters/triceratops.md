## Actions

***Multiattack.*** The triceratops makes two Gore attacks.

***Gore.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +9, reach 5 ft. 19 (2d12 + 6) Piercing damage. If the target is Huge or smaller and the triceratops moved 20+ feet straight toward it immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 9 (2d8) Piercing damage and has the [[prone|Prone]] condition.

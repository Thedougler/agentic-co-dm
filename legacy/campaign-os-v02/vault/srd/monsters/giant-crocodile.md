# Giant Crocodile

- **[[hit-points|Hit Points]]:** 85 (9d12 + 27)

- **CR** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)

## Traits

***Hold Breath.*** The crocodile can hold its breath for 1 hour.

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** The crocodile makes one Bite attack and one Tail attack.

***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +8, reach 5 ft. 21 (3d10 + 5) Piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [[grappled|Grappled]] condition (escape DC 15). While [[grappled|Grappled]], the target has the [[restrained|Restrained]] condition and can't be targeted by the crocodile's Tail.

***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +8, reach 10 ft. 18 (3d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [[prone|Prone]] condition.

# Giant Owl

- **[[hit-points|Hit Points]]:** 19 (3d10 + 3)
- **[[speed|Speed]]:** 5 ft., Fly 60 ft.
- **[[initiative|Initiative]]**: +2 (12)

- **Resistances**: Necrotic, Radiant

## Traits

***Flyby.*** The owl doesn't provoke an Opportunity Attack when it flies out of an enemy's reach.

## Actions

***Talons.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft. 7 (1d10 + 2) Slashing damage.

***Spellcasting.*** The owl casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using [[wisdom|Wisdom]] as the spellcasting ability:

- **At Will:** *[[detect-evil-and-good|Detect Evil and Good]]*, *[[detect-magic|Detect Magic]]*
- **1/Day Each:** *[[clairvoyance|Clairvoyance]]*

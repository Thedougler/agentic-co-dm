## Traits

***Swarm.*** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny rat. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain [[temporary-hit-points|Temporary Hit Points]].

## Actions

***Bites.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +2, reach 5 ft. 5 (2d4) Piercing damage, or 2 (1d4) Piercing damage if the swarm is [[bloodied|Bloodied]].

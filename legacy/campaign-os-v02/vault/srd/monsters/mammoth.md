## Actions

***Multiattack.*** The mammoth makes two Gore attacks.

***Gore.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +10, reach 10 ft. 18 (2d10 + 7) Piercing damage. If the target is a Huge or smaller creature and the mammoth moved 20+ feet straight toward it immediately before the hit, the target has the [[prone|Prone]] condition.

## Bonus Actions

***Trample.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw*: DC 18, one creature within 5 feet that has the Prone condition. *Failure:*  29 (4d10 + 7) Bludgeoning damage. *Success:*  Half damage.

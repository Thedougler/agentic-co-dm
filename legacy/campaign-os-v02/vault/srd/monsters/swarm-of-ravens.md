## Traits

***Swarm.*** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny raven. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain [[temporary-hit-points|Temporary Hit Points]].

## Actions

***Beaks.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft. 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage, or 2 (1d4) Piercing damage if the swarm is [[bloodied|Bloodied]].

Related: [[swarm-of-venomous-snakes|Swarm of Venomous Snakes]]

***Cacophony (Recharge 6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw*: DC 10, one creature in the swarm's space. *Failure:*  The target has the [[deafened|Deafened]] condition until the start of the swarm's next turn. While [[deafened|Deafened]], the target also has [[disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on ability checks and attack rolls.

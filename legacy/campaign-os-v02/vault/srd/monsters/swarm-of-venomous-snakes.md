## Traits

***Swarm.*** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny snake. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain [[temporary-hit-points|Temporary Hit Points]].

## Actions

***Bites.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +6, reach 5 ft. 8 (1d8 + 4) Piercing damage—or 6 (1d4 + 4) Piercing damage if the swarm is [[bloodied|Bloodied]]—plus 10 (3d6) Poison damage.

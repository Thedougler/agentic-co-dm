# Giant Toad

- **Type:** Large Beast, Unaligned
- **[[armor-class|Armor Class]]:** 11
- **[[hit-points|Hit Points]]:** 39 (6d10 + 6)
- **[[speed|Speed]]:** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
- **[[initiative|Initiative]]**: +1 (11)

- **Senses**: darkvision 60 ft.; [[passive-perception|Passive Perception]] 10
- **CR** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)

## Traits

***Amphibious.*** The toad can breathe air and water.

***Standing Leap.*** The toad's [[long-jump|Long Jump]] is up to 20 feet and its [[high-jump|High Jump]] is up to 10 feet with or without a running start.

## Actions

***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft. 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage plus 5 (2d4) Poison damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the [[grappled|Grappled]] condition (escape DC 12).

***Swallow.*** The toad swallows a Medium or smaller target it is grappling. While swallowed, the target isn't Grappled but has the [[blinded|Blinded]] and [[restrained|Restrained]] conditions, and it has [[cover|Cover]]|XPHB|Total Cover against attacks and other effects outside the toad.

In addition, the target takes 10 (3d6) [[acid|Acid]] damage at the end of each of the toad's turns. The toad can have only one target swallowed at a time, and it can't use Bite while it has a swallowed target. If the toad dies, a swallowed creature is no longer Restrained and can escape from the corpse using 5 feet of movement, exiting with the [[prone|Prone]] condition.

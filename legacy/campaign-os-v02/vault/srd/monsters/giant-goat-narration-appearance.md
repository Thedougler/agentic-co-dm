> *A ram the size of a draft horse. One horn can catch a young tree and tear it out, root-ball and all, without breaking stride. The tendons in its neck stand out like dock rope, bulging and snapping back as its head saws side to side.*
>
> *Its eyes are flat black and unblinking. They slide across a group left to right without stopping on any one face.*

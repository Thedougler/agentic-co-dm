# Lion

- **Type:** Large Beast, Unaligned
- **[[armor-class|Armor Class]]:** 12
- **[[hit-points|Hit Points]]:** 22 (4d10)
- **[[speed|Speed]]:** 50 ft.
- **[[initiative|Initiative]]**: +2 (12)

| DEX | 15 | +2 | +2 |

| CHA | 8 | -1 | -1 |

- **Skills**: Perception +3, Stealth +4
- **Senses**: darkvision 60 ft.; [[passive-perception|Passive Perception]] 13
- **CR** 1 (XP 200; PB +2)

## Traits

***Pack Tactics.*** The lion has [[advantage|Advantage]] on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the lion's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the [[incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition.

***Running Leap.*** With a 10-foot running start, the lion can [[long-jump|Long Jump]] up to 25 feet.

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** The lion makes two Rend attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Roar.

***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage.

***Roar.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw*: DC 11, one creature within 15 feet. *Failure:*  The target has the [[frightened|Frightened]] condition until the start of the lion's next turn.

> *At rest it reads as a log the length of a longboat, barnacled along the back, half-sunk. Then the eye-ridge lifts. The jaw opens sideways to a boat.*
>
> *The smell off it is river-mud and old meat. When it moves, the water does not splash. It is displaced in one piece.*

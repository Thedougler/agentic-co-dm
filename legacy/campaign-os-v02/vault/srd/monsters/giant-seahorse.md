## Traits

***[[water-breathing|Water Breathing]].*** The seahorse can breathe only underwater.

## Actions

***Ram.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft. 9 (2d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage, or 11 (2d8 + 2) Bludgeoning damage if the seahorse moved 20+ feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit.

## Bonus Actions

***Bubble Dash.*** While underwater, the seahorse moves up to half its [[swim-speed|Swim Speed]] without provoking [[opportunity-attacks|Opportunity Attacks]].

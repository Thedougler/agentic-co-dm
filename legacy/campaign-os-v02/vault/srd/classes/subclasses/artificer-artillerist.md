# Artificer Subclass: Artillerist (5e, not SRD)

*Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (2020). Not reprinted in the 2024 core rulebooks — this text is still current RAW. Specializes in a portable magical cannon.*

## Level 3: Tool Proficiency

Proficiency with woodcarver's tools. If you already have it, gain proficiency with one other type of artisan's tools instead.

## Level 3: Artillerist Spells

Always prepared once you reach the listed artificer level (don't count against your prepared total):

| Artificer Level | Spells |
|---|---|
| 3rd | [[shield-spell\|Shield]], [[thunderwave\|Thunderwave]] |
| 5th | [[scorching-ray\|Scorching Ray]], [[shatter\|Shatter]] |

| 13th | [[ice-storm\|Ice Storm]], [[wall-of-fire\|Wall of Fire]] |
| 17th | [[cone-of-cold\|Cone of Cold]], [[wall-of-force\|Wall of Force]] |

## Level 3: Eldritch Cannon

Action, using woodcarver's or smith's tools: create a Small or Tiny magical cannon in an unoccupied space on a horizontal surface within 5 feet of you (a Small cannon occupies its space; a Tiny one can be held in one hand). You can't create another until you finish a Long Rest or expend a spell slot of 1st level or higher — you can have only one cannon at a time.

**Cannon stat block.** AC 18. HP = 5 × your artificer level. Immune to poison damage, psychic damage, and all conditions. All ability scores treated as 10 (+0) for checks/saves it's forced to make. `mending` restores 2d6 HP. It disappears at 0 HP or after 1 hour; you can dismiss it early as an action.

When you create it, choose its type from the table below. On your turn, bonus action (if within 60 feet) to activate it; as part of that same bonus action, if it has legs, direct it to walk/climb up to 15 feet.

| Cannon Type | Activation Effect |
|---|---|
| Flamethrower | Exhales fire in an adjacent 15-ft cone. Each creature in the area makes a [[dexterity\|Dexterity]] save against your spell save DC: 2d8 fire damage on a failure, half on a success. Ignites unattended flammable objects in the area. |
| Force Ballista | Ranged spell attack at one creature or object within 120 feet of the cannon. On a hit: 2d8 force damage, and a creature target is pushed up to 5 feet away from the cannon. |
| Protector | Emits a burst of positive energy: the cannon and each creature of your choice within 10 feet of it gain 1d8 + your [[intelligence\|Intelligence]] modifier (minimum +1) temporary hit points. |

## Level 5: Arcane Firearm

After a Long Rest, use woodcarver's tools to carve sigils into a wand, staff, or rod, turning it into your arcane firearm (sigils last indefinitely, but re-carving onto a new item erases the old ones). It can serve as your spellcasting focus. When you cast an artificer spell through it, roll a d8 and add that number to one of the spell's damage rolls.

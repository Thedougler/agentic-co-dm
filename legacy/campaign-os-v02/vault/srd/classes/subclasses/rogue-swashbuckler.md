# Rogue Subclass: Swashbuckler (5e, not SRD)

*Xanathar's Guide to Everything (2014). The 2024 Player's Handbook reprinted only Arcane Trickster, [[assassin|Assassin]], Soulknife, and Thief for [[rogue|Rogue]] — [[swashbuckler|Swashbuckler]] has no 2024 update; this text is still current RAW.*

A duelist and daredevil archetype: speed, elegance, and Charisma over brute force, excelling at one-on-one combat and darting away from a fight before reprisal.

## Level 3: Fancy Footwork

During your turn, if you make a melee attack against a creature, that creature can't make opportunity attacks against you for the rest of your turn.

## Level 3: Rakish Audacity

You add your [[charisma|Charisma]] modifier to your initiative rolls.

You also gain an additional way to use Sneak Attack: you don't need Advantage on the attack roll if you are within 5 feet of the target, no other creature is within 5 feet of you, and you don't have Disadvantage on the attack roll. All other Sneak Attack rules still apply.

## Level 9: Panache

As an action, make a Charisma (Persuasion) check contested by the target's [[wisdom|Wisdom]] (Insight) check. The target must be able to hear you and share a language with you.

- **If the target is hostile** and you succeed: it has Disadvantage on attack rolls against anyone but you and can't make opportunity attacks against anyone but you, for 1 minute — ends early if a companion attacks or affects it with a spell, or if you and the target end up more than 60 feet apart.
- **If the target isn't hostile** and you succeed: it is charmed by you for 1 minute, regarding you as a friendly acquaintance — ends immediately if you or a companion does anything harmful to it.

## Level 13: Elegant Maneuver

Bonus action: gain Advantage on the next [[dexterity|Dexterity]] (Acrobatics) or [[strength|Strength]] (Athletics) check you make this turn.

## Level 17: Master Duelist

If you miss with an attack roll, you can reroll it with Advantage. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

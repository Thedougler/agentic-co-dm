# Draconic Sorcery

*[[sorcerer|Sorcerer]] subclass, Player's Handbook (2024). Innate magic from draconic ancestry, a dragon's gift, or a hoard artifact; sits alongside Clockwork Sorcery as one of the two 2024 PHB Sorcerer subclasses.*

## Level 3: Draconic Resilience

[RAW, Player's Handbook (2024)] HP max increases by 3 + 1 per additional Sorcerer level. Unarmored AC = 10 + DEX + CHA.

## Level 3: Draconic Spells

[RAW, Player's Handbook (2024)] Always prepared once you reach the listed Sorcerer level; they don't count against your prepared total.

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|---|---|
| 3rd | [[alter-self\|Alter Self]], [[chromatic-orb\|Chromatic Orb]], [[command\|Command]], [[dragons-breath\|Dragon's Breath]] |
| 5th | [[fear\|Fear]], Fly |
| 7th | [[arcane-eye\|Arcane Eye]], [[charm-monster\|Charm Monster]] |
| 9th | [[legend-lore\|Legend Lore]], [[summon-dragon\|Summon Dragon]] |

## Level 6: Elemental Affinity

[RAW, Player's Handbook (2024)] Choose one damage type ([[acid|Acid]], Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Poison): [[resistance-spell|Resistance]] to it; add CHA modifier to one damage roll per spell that deals that type.

## Level 14: Dragon Wings

[RAW, Player's Handbook (2024)] [[bonus-action|Bonus Action]]: sprout draconic wings, [[fly-speed|Fly Speed]] 60 ft for 1 hour. 1/LR; restore early for 3 SP.

## Level 18: Dragon Companion

[RAW, Player's Handbook (2024)] Cast Summon Dragon without Material components; 1/LR without a spell slot; optional non-[[concentration|Concentration]] version (1-minute duration).

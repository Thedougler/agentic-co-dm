# Swashbuckler

*[[rogue|Rogue]] subclass, Xanathar's Guide to Everything (2014). Blade-focused rogues combining speed, elegance, and [[charisma|Charisma]]; [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] plays one.*

## Level 3: Fancy Footwork

[RAW, Xanathar's Guide to Everything (2014)] After making a melee attack against a creature on your turn, that creature cannot make opportunity attacks against you for the rest of your turn.

## Level 3: Rakish Audacity

[RAW, Xanathar's Guide to Everything (2014)]

- Add CHA modifier to [[initiative|Initiative]] rolls.
- Sneak Attack applies with no [[advantage|Advantage]] when all three hold.
  - You're within 5 ft of the target.
  - You're alone there: no other creature is within 5 ft of you.
  - The attack rolls without [[disadvantage|Disadvantage]].

## Level 9: Panache

[RAW, Xanathar's Guide to Everything (2014)] [[action|Action]]: CHA (Persuasion) vs. WIS (Insight).

- *Hostile.* Disadvantage on attacks vs. targets other than you. No opportunity attacks vs. non-you targets. Lasts 1 minute.
- *Non-hostile:* [[charmed|Charmed]] for 1 minute.

## Level 13: Elegant Maneuver

[RAW, Xanathar's Guide to Everything (2014)] [[bonus-action|Bonus action]]. Advantage on your next Acrobatics or Athletics check this turn.

## Level 17: Master Duelist

[RAW, Xanathar's Guide to Everything (2014)] If you miss with an attack roll, reroll it with Advantage. 1/short rest or long rest.

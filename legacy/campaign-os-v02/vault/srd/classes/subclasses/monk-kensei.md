# Monk Subclass: Way of the Kensei (5e, not SRD)

*Xanathar's Guide to Everything (2014). The 2024 Player's Handbook reprinted only Warrior of Mercy, Warrior of [[shadow|Shadow]], Warrior of the Elements, and Warrior of the Open Hand for [[monk|Monk]] — Way of the Kensei has no 2024 update; this text is still current RAW.*

Monks who treat a weapon as an extension of the body, training relentlessly with a chosen pair of weapons.

## Level 3: Path of the Kensei

**Kensei Weapons.** Choose two weapon types as your kensei weapons: one melee, one ranged. Each can be any simple or martial weapon lacking the heavy and special properties (the longbow is also allowed); you gain proficiency if you lack it, and these weapons count as monk weapons for you. At 6th, 11th, and 17th level, add another kensei weapon type (melee or ranged, same criteria).

**Way of the Brush.** Proficiency with calligrapher's supplies or painter's supplies (your choice).

Also choose one of the following two features:

### Agile Parry

If you make an unarmed strike as part of the Attack action and are holding a melee kensei weapon, you can use it to defend yourself: +2 bonus to AC until the start of your next turn, as long as the weapon stays in hand and you aren't incapacitated.

### Kensei's Shot

Bonus action: your ranged attacks with a kensei weapon deal an extra 1d4 damage of the weapon's type for any target you hit, until the end of the current turn.

## Level 6: One with the Blade

**Magic Kensei Weapons.** Attacks with your kensei weapons count as magical for overcoming resistance/immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage.

**Deft Strike.** When you hit with a kensei weapon, spend 1 ki point to deal extra damage equal to your Martial Arts die. Once per turn.

## Level 11: Sharpen the Blade

Bonus action: expend up to 3 ki points to grant one kensei weapon you touch a bonus to attack and damage rolls equal to ki points spent, for 1 minute or until you use this feature again. No effect on a magic weapon that already has an attack/damage bonus.

## Level 17: Unerring Accuracy

If you miss with an attack roll using a monk weapon on your turn, you can reroll it. Once per turn.

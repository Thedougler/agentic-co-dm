# [[ranger|Ranger]] Subclass: Gloom Stalker (2024)

*Player's Handbook (2024). One of the four Ranger subclasses reprinted in the 2024 core rules (alongside Beast Master, Fey Wanderer, Hunter). Ambushers who draw on Shadowfell magic to strike first and vanish into darkness.*

## Level 3: Dread Ambusher

Three benefits:

- **Ambusher's Leap.** At the start of your first turn of each combat, your Speed increases by 10 feet until the end of that turn.
- **Dreadful Strike.** When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can deal an extra 2d6 Psychic damage. Usable once per turn, a number of times equal to your [[wisdom|Wisdom]] modifier (minimum once), regaining all uses on a Long Rest.
- **Initiative Bonus.** You can add your Wisdom modifier to Initiative rolls.

## Level 3: Gloom Stalker Spells

You always have the following spells prepared once you reach the listed Ranger level; they don't count against your prepared-spells total.

## Level 3: Umbral Sight

You gain Darkvision out to 60 feet; if you already have Darkvision, its range increases by 60 feet instead. While entirely in darkness, you have the [[invisible|Invisible]] condition to any creature that relies on Darkvision to see you there.

## Level 7: Iron Mind

You gain proficiency in Wisdom saving throws. If you already have that proficiency, gain proficiency in [[intelligence|Intelligence]] or [[charisma|Charisma]] saving throws instead.

## Level 11: Stalker's Flurry (note — beyond the level 1-5 floor)

Dreadful Strike's damage becomes 2d8. Additionally, when you trigger Dreadful Strike you can add one of:

- **Sudden Strike.** Make another attack with the same weapon against a different creature within 5 feet of the original target and within the weapon's range.
- **Mass Fear.** The target and each creature within 10 feet of it makes a Wisdom save against your spell save DC; on a failure, [[frightened|Frightened]] until the start of your next turn.

## Level 15: Shadowy Dodge

When a creature makes an attack roll against you, you can use your Reaction to impose Disadvantage on that roll — hit or miss, you can then teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see.

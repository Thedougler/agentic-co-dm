# Artificer (5e, not SRD)

*Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (2020), originally introduced in Eberron: Rising from the Last War. Not reprinted in the 2024 core rulebooks — this text is still current RAW. A half-caster (INT) who channels spells through tools and imbues mundane objects with magic.*

## Level 1: Magical Tinkering

With thieves' or artisan's tools in hand, touch a Tiny nonmagical object (action) and give it one property of your choice: sheds light (5 ft bright/5 ft dim), plays a recorded message up to 6 seconds long when tapped, emits a chosen sound or odor out to 10 feet, or displays a static visual effect (image, up to 25 words of text, or lines/shapes). The property lasts until you end it as an action. You can affect a number of objects at once equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum one) — exceeding that ends the oldest property first.

## Level 1: Spellcasting

You cast artificer spells through a spellcasting focus — thieves' tools or artisan's tools you're proficient with (an item bearing one of your infusions also qualifies once you have Infuse Item). You know 2 cantrips at 1st level (more at higher levels per the table above), swappable on level-up.

You prepare a number of spells equal to your Intelligence modifier + half your artificer level (rounded down, minimum one), chosen from the artificer spell list, castable with any slot of the spell's level or higher. You can change your prepared list on a Long Rest. Ritual-tagged prepared spells can be cast as rituals.

## Level 2: Infuse Item

On a Long Rest, imbue up to your Infused Items maximum of nonmagical objects with infusions you know, turning them into magic items (attunement, if required, can happen instantly). An infusion persists indefinitely until replaced; if you die, it fades after a number of days equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1). No object can hold more than one infusion, and each infusion you know can be in only one object at a time.

**Infusions Known.** You learn 4 infusions at 2nd level from the artificer's Infusions list, gaining more per the table above; you can swap one known infusion for another whenever you gain an artificer level.

## Level 3: Artificer Specialist

Choose a specialization (e.g. [[artificer-artillerist|Artillerist]]), granting features at 3rd, 5th, 9th, and 15th level.

## Level 3: The Right Tool for the Job

With thieves' or artisan's tools in hand, spend 1 hour of uninterrupted work (can coincide with a short/long rest) to magically create one set of artisan's tools in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of you. The tools are nonmagical and vanish when you use this feature again.

## Level 4: Ability Score Improvement

Increase one ability score by 2, or two ability scores by 1 each (max 20), or take a feat if the DM allows feats.

## Level 5: Artificer Specialist Feature

You gain the feature your Artificer Specialist grants at 5th level.

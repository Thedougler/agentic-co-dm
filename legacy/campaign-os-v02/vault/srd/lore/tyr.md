# Tyr

Tyr is a Lawful Good deity of law, justice, civic order, and Dravosi state legitimacy.

## The Fact

Tyr rules law, justice, order, and legitimacy. The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] bases its power on his word. Law builds states. Oaths bind authority. Justice is active.

His seal marks warrants and courts. His priests sign laws. Oaths sworn before them hold force. Courts martial and commissions call on his name.

In [[verdant-scatter|the Scatter]], his clergy staff military posts. They serve one role: witnessing oaths and upholding order.

Tyr appears in the [[shattered-sea-pantheon|Shattered Sea Pantheon]]. Few mortals have met him. His reach remains theoretical.

# Valkur

Valkur is a Chaotic Good minor deity of sailors, sea survival, favorable winds, and the courage to keep a ship moving when the water wants it gone.

## The Fact

[[verdant-scatter|The Scatter]] has no temples or clergy for Valkur. Sailors call to him in whispers with carved gulls and knotted rope, words before rough water. Whether he hears them or luck shifts the wind is unclear.

The [[waveservants|Waveservants]] see Valkur prayers as an insult to [[umberlee|Umberlee]]'s will. Sailors whisper to him when they want to dodge Umberlee's cost. He serves as their secret ally.

Valkur appears in the [[shattered-sea-pantheon|Shattered Sea Pantheon]]. Few mortals have met him. His favor remains theoretical.

# Dwarf

**Creature Type:** humanoid

**Size:** medium (about 4-5 feet tall)

**Speed:** 30 feet

As a Dwarf, you have these special traits.

**Darkvision.** You have Darkvision with a range of 120 feet.

**Dwarven Resilience.** You have Resistance to Poison damage. You also have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the [[poisoned|Poisoned]] condition.

**Dwarven Toughness.** Your maximum Hit Points increase by 1 at first level, and by 1 each time you gain a level.

**Stonecunning.** As a Bonus Action, you gain Tremorsense with a range of 60 feet for 10 minutes. You must be on a stone surface or touching a stone surface to use this Tremorsense. The stone can be natural or worked.

You can use this Bonus Action up to your Proficiency Bonus times, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

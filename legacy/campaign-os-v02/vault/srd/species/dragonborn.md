# Dragonborn

**Creature Type:** humanoid

**Size:** medium, about 5 to 7 feet tall

**Speed:** 30 feet

As a Dragonborn, you have these special traits.

**Draconic Ancestry.** Your lineage stems from a dragon progenitor. Choose a dragon type from the Draconic Ancestors table. Your choice affects your Breath Weapon and Damage Resistance traits as well as your appearance.

Draconic Ancestors

| Dragon | Damage Type |
|--------|-------------|
| Black  | [[acid\|Acid]] |
| Blue   | Lightning   |
| Brass  | Fire        |
| Bronze | Lightning   |
| Copper | [[acid\|Acid]] |
| Gold   | Fire        |
| Green  | Poison      |
| Red    | Fire        |
| Silver | Cold        |
| White  | Cold        |

> [!mechanic]
> **Breath Weapon.** When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can replace one of your attacks with an exhalation of magical energy in either a 15-foot Cone or a 30-foot Line that is 5 feet wide (choose the shape each time). Each creature in that area must make a [[dexterity|Dexterity]] saving throw (DC 8 plus your [[constitution|Constitution]] modifier and Proficiency Bonus). On a failed save, a creature takes 1d10 damage of the type determined by your Draconic Ancestry trait, half as much on a success. At character levels 5, 11, and 17, this damage increases by 1d10 (dealing 2d10, 3d10, and 4d10 total).
>
> You can use this Breath Weapon once per Proficiency Bonus levels, regaining all uses when you finish a Long Rest.

**Damage Resistance.** You have Resistance to the damage type determined by your Draconic Ancestry trait.

**[[darkvision-spell|Darkvision]].** You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

**Draconic Flight.** At level 5, you can fly using draconic magic. Use a Bonus Action to sprout spectral wings that last 10 minutes, until you dismiss them (no action) or gain the [[incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition. Your Fly Speed matches your Speed, and your wings show the same energy as your Breath Weapon. Once used, you must finish a Long Rest to use this trait again.

# Elf

**Creature Type:** humanoid

**Size:** medium, about 5 to 6 feet tall

**Speed:** 30 feet

As an Elf, you have these special traits.

**Darkvision.** You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

**Elven Lineage.** You are part of a lineage that grants you supernatural abilities. Choose a lineage from the Elven Lineages table. You gain the level 1 benefit of that lineage.

At character levels 3 and 5, you gain a higher-level spell from your lineage (shown in the table). You always have it prepared and can cast it once per day without a spell slot. You regain this free cast after a Long Rest and can also spend spell slots to cast it at your level.

Table: Elven Lineages

| Lineage  | Level 1                                                                                                   | Level 3      | Level 5             |
|----------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------|----------------------|
| Drow     | The range of your Darkvision increases to 120 feet. You also know the [[dancing-lights\|Dancing Lights]] cantrip.               | [[faerie-fire\|Faerie Fire]]  | [[darkness-spell\|Darkness]]             |
| High Elf | You know the [[prestidigitation\|Prestidigitation]] cantrip. Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can replace that cantrip with a different cantrip from the [[wizard\|Wizard]] spell list. | [[detect-magic\|Detect Magic]] | [[misty-step\|Misty Step]]           |
| Wood Elf | Your Speed increases to 35 feet. You also know the [[druidcraft\|Druidcraft]] cantrip.                                       | [[longstrider\|Longstrider]]  | [[pass-without-trace\|Pass without Trace]]   |

[[intelligence|Intelligence]], [[wisdom|Wisdom]], or [[charisma|Charisma]] is your spellcasting ability for the spells you cast with this trait (choose the ability when you select the lineage).

**Fey Ancestry.** You have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the [[charmed|Charmed]] condition.

**Keen Senses.** You have proficiency in the Insight, Perception, or Survival skill.

**Trance.** You don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep. You can finish a Long Rest in 4 hours if you spend those hours in a trance state, during which you stay conscious.

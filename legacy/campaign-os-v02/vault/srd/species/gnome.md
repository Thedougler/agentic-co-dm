# Gnome

**Creature Type:** humanoid

**Size:** small, about 3 to 4 feet tall

**Speed:** 30 feet

As a Gnome, you have these special traits.

**Darkvision.** You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

**Gnomish Cunning.** You have Advantage on [[intelligence|Intelligence]], [[wisdom|Wisdom]], and [[charisma|Charisma]] saving throws.

**Gnomish Lineage.** You are part of a lineage that grants you supernatural abilities. Choose one of the following options; whichever one you choose, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for the spells you cast with this trait (choose the ability when you select the lineage):

**Forest Gnome.** You know the [[minor-illusion|Minor Illusion]] cantrip and always have the [[speak-with-animals|Speak with Animals]] spell prepared. You can cast it without a spell slot once per Proficiency Bonus, regaining all uses after a Long Rest, and you can spend spell slots to cast it again.

**Rock Gnome.** You know the [[mending|Mending]] and [[prestidigitation|Prestidigitation]] cantrips. Spend 10 minutes casting Prestidigitation to create a Tiny clockwork device (AC 5, 1 HP), such as a toy, fire starter, or music box, by choosing one effect from the spell. Whenever you create the device or another creature uses a Bonus Action to activate it with a touch, it produces that effect. If an effect offers options, select one when creating the device. With the fire effect, choose whether the device starts or stops fires, but not both. You can maintain three devices at a time. Each one becomes nonfunctional 8 hours after creation or when you dismantle it with a touch as a Use action.

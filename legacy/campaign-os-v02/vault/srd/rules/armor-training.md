# Armor Training

Armor training lets you wear armor safely. Without it, Light, Medium, or Heavy armor causes Disadvantage on [[strength|Strength]] and [[dexterity|Dexterity]] tests, blocks spellcasting, and stops Shields from granting AC bonuses. *See also* "Disadvantage" and "Equipment" ("Armor").

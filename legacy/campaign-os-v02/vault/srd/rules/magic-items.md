# Magic Items (5e SRD)

Adventurers find magic items in hoards and vaults. They grant powerful abilities.

## Magic Item Categories

Every magic item has a category. The table shows all nine with examples.

|----------------|-------------------------------------|

### Armor

Wear magical armor for its magic to work. Names may show type; if not, pick or roll.

### Potions

Potions are drinks or oils (1 ounce in a vial). Drink as a Bonus Action. Oils take longer.

**_Mixing potions._** Potions mixed together interact unpredictably. Roll on the Potion Miscibility table for each combination.

Table: potion miscibility

| 1d100 | Result |
|-------|--------|
| 01    | Both potions lose their effects, and the mixture creates a magical explosion in a 5-foot-radius Sphere centered on itself. Each creature in that area takes 4d10 Force damage. |
| 02-08 | Both potions lose their effects, and the mixture becomes an ingested poison of your choice (see "Poison" in "[[gameplay-toolbox\|Gameplay Toolbox]]"). |
| 09-15 | Both potions lose their effects. |
| 16-25 | One potion loses its effect. |
| 26-35 | Both potions work, but with their numerical effects and durations halved. If a potion has no numerical effect and no duration, it instead loses its effect. |
| 36-90 | Both potions work normally. |
| 91-99 | Both potions work, but one potion's numerical effects and duration double. If neither potion has anything to double in this way, they work normally. |
| 00    | Only one potion works, but its effects become permanent. |

> [!dm]
> **1d100 result 00 (permanent effect).** Choose the simplest effect to make permanent, or the one that seems the most fun. A _[[potion-of-healing-common|Potion of Healing]]_ might increase the drinker's Hit Point max by 2d4 + 2, while a _[[potion-of-invisibility\|Potion of Invisibility]]_ might give the drinker the [[invisible\|Invisible]] condition indefinitely. At your discretion, a _[[dispel-magic\|Dispel Magic]]_ spell or similar magic might end this lasting effect.

### Rings

Wear a ring on a finger to use it, unless told otherwise.

### Rods

Rods weigh 2 to 5 pounds. Use as an [[arcane-focus|Arcane Focus]].

### Scrolls

Scrolls are paper rolls in a tube. The most common is the _Spell Scroll_. Read to activate. Once used, it's gone.

Anyone who reads a language can use a scroll, unless told otherwise.

### Staffs

Staffs weigh 2 to 7 pounds and serve as walking sticks. Most are wood, but some have metal or crystal. Unless its description says otherwise, use one as a [[quarterstaff|Quarterstaff]] or [[arcane-focus|Arcane Focus]].

### Wands

Wands are 12 to 15 inches long and tipped with crystal or stone. Unless its description says otherwise, use one as an Arcane Focus.

### Weapons

Magic weapons are magical versions of regular weapons. Some specify the type in their description (like a [[longsword|Longsword]]). If not, pick one or roll.

**_Ammunition._** Ammunition from a magic weapon counts as magical if the weapon has the Ammunition property.

### Wondrous Items

Wondrous items include wearable gear (boots, belts, capes, amulets) and other objects (bags, carpets, figurines, horns, instruments).

### Magic Item Rules

See "Equipment" for basic rules. Here are more.

**_Attunement prerequisites._** Class prerequisites limit who can attune. To attune as a spellcaster, you must cast at least one spell via your traits or features, not from a magic item.

**_Items made for specific creatures._** Wearable items fit the wearer, though you can rule otherwise. Some work only for dwarves.

**_Unusual Anatomy._** Decide if strange creatures can wear items. A ring on a tentacle might work. A yuan-ti won't fit boots.

**_Paired items._** Allow exceptions for single items. A one-armed character might use one _Glove of Missile Snaring_ if the other stays close.

## Magic Item Rarity

Every magic item has a rarity. Common items are easy to find. Artifacts are rare and priceless.

### Magic Item Values by Rarity

Rarity determines cost. Common items fill shops in towns and cities. Rare items appear in cities. Very Rare and Legendary items need special places.

If a magic item includes a bought item like armor, add its cost to the magic item's value. _+1 Armor_ (Plate) costs 5,500 GP total: 4,000 GP for the Rare magic and 1,500 GP for Plate.

Table: magic item rarities and values

| Rarity    | Value*     |
|-----------|------------|
| Common    | 100 GP     |
| Uncommon  | 400 GP     |
| Rare      | 4,000 GP   |
| Very Rare | 40,000 GP  |
| Legendary | 200,000 GP |
| Artifact  | Priceless  |

Halve the value for a consumable item other than a _Spell Scroll_. The value of a _Spell Scroll_ is double what it costs to scribe the scroll (as specified in the "Scribing Spell Scrolls" section of "Equipment").

## Activating a Magic Item

Activation uses a magic action. Different items require different actions. Here are the rules.

### Command Word

Speak or sign a command word to activate. It fails in silence (like _[[silence|Silence]]_).

### Consumable Items

Some items vanish when used. Potions, scrolls, and similar items lose their magic once activated.

### Spells Cast from Items

Spells use the item's spell level and caster. They don't use your slots and need no components unless noted. Potions skip casting.

Use your spellcasting ability if required. Pick which if you have more than one. Use +0 plus your Bonus if you lack one.

### Charges

Some items need charges to activate. _Identify_ shows how many remain. Attuned creatures know the recharge rate.

## The Next Dawn

Most items recharge at dawn. Choose a recharge time if that doesn't occur in your world.

## Cursed Items

The item's description tells you if it's cursed. Most identify spells, including _Identify_, won't reveal curses.

You can't stop attuning to a cursed item until you break the curse, maybe with _[[remove-curse|Remove Curse]]_.

## Magic Item Resilience

Magic items are as durable as normal items of their type. Most resist all damage except Potions and Scrolls.

Artifacts only break through special means. Learn how to destroy one through research or a quest.

## Crafting Magic Items

See "Equipment" for potions and scrolls. Here are other item rules.

### Arcana skill

You and any helpers need Arcana skill to craft magic items.

### Tools

See the table below for which tool crafts each category. You need that tool and training. Helpers need training too. Check "Equipment" for more.

| Item Category | Required Tool |
|---------------|----------------|

| Potion        | [[alchemists-supplies\|Alchemist's Supplies]] or [[herbalism-kit\|Herbalism Kit]] |

### Spells

To craft magic items that cast spells, prepare those spells each day you work.

### Time and Cost

Rarity sets time and cost.

**_Work per day._** Work 8 hours each day. Days don't need to be in a row.

**_Assistants._** Divide the time by the number helping. The GM allows what fits the story.

**_Materials._** The cost covers materials. Cities have 75% availability; elsewhere, 25%. Check again after 7 days if things are hard to find.

If the item incorporates a purchased item (weapon, armor), add its cost or craft it instead. To make _+1 Armor_ (Plate Armor), spend 3,500 GP total, or spend 2,000 GP and craft the armor.

Table: magic item crafting time and cost

| Item Rarity | Time*    | Cost*      |
|-------------|----------|------------|
| Common      | 5 days   | 50 GP      |
| Uncommon    | 10 days  | 200 GP     |
| Rare        | 50 days  | 2,000 GP   |
| Very Rare   | 125 days | 20,000 GP  |
| Legendary   | 250 days | 100,000 GP |

Halve the time and cost for consumable items (except _Spell Scrolls_). "Equipment" lists the crafting time and cost for _Spell Scrolls_.

## Sentient Magic Items

Some magic items gain sentience and personality through a being's consciousness, a lingering spirit, or the magic waking up. The item becomes either an ally or a source of conflict.

Most sentient items are weapons, though other types work too. Potions and scrolls never gain sentience.

The GM controls sentient items and their powers. A good relationship with the bearer opens access to them. A bad one sparks conflict (see "Conflict" below).

### Sentient Magic Item Traits

When you make a sentient magic item, you create the item's persona much as you would create an NPC, with these exceptions.

#### Abilities

A sentient magic item has [[intelligence|Intelligence]], [[wisdom|Wisdom]], and [[charisma|Charisma]] scores. Choose the item's abilities, or determine them randomly as follows: roll 4d6 for each one, dropping the lowest roll and totaling the rest.

#### Alignment

A sentient magic item has an alignment. Its creator or nature might suggest an alignment. Otherwise, pick an alignment or roll on the Sentient Item's Alignment table.

#### Communication

A sentient item communicates through emotion, telepathic projection, or speech. You can choose how it communicates or roll on the Sentient Item's Communication table.

#### Senses

A sentient item can perceive its surroundings out to a limited range. You can choose its senses or roll on the Sentient Item's Senses table.

#### Special Purpose

You can assign a goal the item pursues. As long as how the wielder uses the item matches this goal, the item stays cooperative. Diverging from this aim might trigger conflict (see "Conflict" below). You can pick a goal or roll on the Sentient Item's Special Purpose table.

#### Conflict

When the bearer of a sentient item acts in a manner opposed to the item's alignment or purpose, conflict can arise.

> [!check] Charisma Save — Sentient Item Conflict
> DC 12 plus the item's Charisma modifier.
> **Success:** the bearer resists the item's influence and maintains control.
> **Failure:** the item makes one or more demands: **chase my dreams** (pursue its goals), **get rid of it** (dispose of anything repugnant), **it's time for a change** (give it to someone new), or **keep me close** (carry or wear it at all times).

If its bearer refuses to follow the item's demands, the item can do any of the following:

- Make it impossible for its bearer to attune to it.
- Suppress one or more of its activated properties.

> [!check] Charisma Save — Item Attempts Control
> DC 12 plus the item's Charisma modifier.
> **Success:** the bearer resists the item's control attempt.
> **Failure:** the bearer has the [[charmed|Charmed]] condition for 1d12 hours and must follow the item's commands. If the bearer takes damage, it repeats the save, ending the effect on a success. The item can't use this power again until the next dawn.

# Search [Action]

When you take the Search action, you make a [[wisdom|Wisdom]] check to discern something that isn't obvious. The Search table suggests which skills are applicable when you take this action, depending on what you're trying to detect.

Table: Search

| Skill      | Thing to Detect                      |
|------------|--------------------------------------|
| Insight    | Creature's state of mind             |
| Medicine   | Creature's ailment or cause of death |
| Perception | Concealed creature or object         |
| Survival   | Tracks or food                       |

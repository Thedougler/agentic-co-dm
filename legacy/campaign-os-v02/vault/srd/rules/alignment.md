# Alignment

A creature's alignment broadly describes its ethical attitudes and ideals. Alignment is a combination of two factors: one identifies morality (good, evil, or neutral), and the other describes attitudes toward order (lawful, chaotic, or neutral). These factors allow for nine possible combinations, such as Lawful Good and Neutral Evil. *See also* "Character Creation" ("Create Your Character").

## The Nine Alignments

These summaries describe typical behavior. Individuals may vary.

**Lawful Good (LG).** Endeavors to do the right thing as expected by society. One who fights injustice and protects the innocent without hesitation is probably Lawful Good.

**Neutral Good (NG).** Does the best it can, working within rules but not feeling bound by them. A kindly person who helps others according to their needs is probably Neutral Good.

**Chaotic Good (CG).** Acts as conscience directs with little regard for what others expect. A rebel who waylays a cruel baron's tax collectors and uses the stolen money to help the poor is probably Chaotic Good.

**Lawful Neutral (LN).** Acts according to law, tradition, or personal codes. One who follows a disciplined rule of life, unswayed by the demands of those in need or by the temptations of evil, is probably Lawful Neutral.

**Neutral (N).** Prefers to avoid moral questions and doesn't take sides, doing what seems best at the time. One who's bored by moral debate is probably Neutral.

**Chaotic Neutral (CN).** Follows its whims, valuing personal freedom most. A scoundrel who wanders the land living by their wits is probably Chaotic Neutral.

**Lawful Evil (LE).** Methodically takes what it wants within the limits of a code of tradition, loyalty, or order. An aristocrat exploiting citizens while scheming for power is probably Lawful Evil.

**Neutral Evil (NE).** Untroubled by the harm caused in pursuit of its desires. A criminal who robs and murders as they please is probably Neutral Evil.

**Chaotic Evil (CE).** Acts with arbitrary violence, spurred by hatred or bloodlust. A villain pursuing schemes of vengeance and havoc is probably Chaotic Evil.

**Unaligned Creatures.** Most creatures that lack rational thought lack alignments and moral qualities. Sharks are savage predators without moral capacity. They don't embody evil, just survival drive.

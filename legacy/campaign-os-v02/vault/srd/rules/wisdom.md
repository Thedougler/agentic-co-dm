# Wisdom

Wisdom measures perceptiveness and mental fortitude — attunement to the world and to other creatures, and the composure to hold a mind against pressure. It is noticing and reading, where [[intelligence|Intelligence]] is reasoning.

## What Wisdom Governs

| Roll | Wisdom applies when you… |
|------|--------------------------|
| [[ability-check\|Ability check]] | Notice things in the environment or in creatures' behavior. |
| [[saving-throw\|Saving throw]] | Resist a mental assault — charm, fear, and effects that seize the will. |
| [[attack-roll\|Attack roll]] | Cast a spell with an attack roll, where Wisdom is your spellcasting ability. |

## Associated Skills

- **Animal Handling** — calming, training, or directing an animal.
- **Insight** — discerning a person's mood and intentions.
- **Medicine** — diagnosing an illness, or determining what killed the recently slain.
- **Perception** — noticing something easy to miss, using any combination of senses.
- **Survival** — following tracks, foraging, finding a trail, avoiding natural hazards.

## Other Uses

- Passive Perception — 10 + the Wisdom (Perception) check bonus — is what a creature notices without looking.
- Wisdom is the spellcasting ability of the [[cleric\|Cleric]], [[druid\|Druid]], and [[ranger\|Ranger]].

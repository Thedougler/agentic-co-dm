# Monsters Overview (5e SRD)

## Stat Block Overview

A monster's stat block contains the game rules you need to use it. Stat blocks include these parts:

- **Name and General Details.** Name, size, creature type, tags, and alignment.
- **Combat Highlights.** Armor Class, Hit Points, Speed, and Initiative.
- **Ability Scores.** Ability scores, modifiers, and saving throws.
- **Other Details.** Senses, Languages, and Challenge Rating. Some monsters also have skills, resistances, immunities, or gear.
- **Traits.** Characteristics active at all times or in specific situations.
- **Actions.** Actions the monster can take.
- **Bonus Actions.** Bonus Actions the monster can take, if any.
- **Reactions and Legendary Actions.** Reactions and Legendary Actions, if any.

## Reading a Stat Block

### Size

Monsters have one of six sizes (Tiny, Small, Medium, Large, Huge, or Gargantuan).

### Creature Type

Each monster has a type. Some spells and magic items treat them specially. The game has ten types:

- **Aberrations** are aliens.
- **Beasts** are animals.
- **Celestials** come from the Upper Planes.
- **Constructs** are magical in nature.
- **Dragons** are old scaly beasts.
- **Elementals** dwell in their own planes.
- **Fey** belong to the wild.
- **Fiends** dwell in far planes.
- **Giants** are tall beings.
- **Humanoids** are people.
- **Monstrosities** are odd beasts.
- **Oozes** are gooey creatures.
- **Plants** are living plants.
- **Undead** are the dead risen.

#### Descriptive Tags

Tags in parentheses provide detail and may affect game effects.

### Alignment

Alignment suggests how to roleplay a monster. Change it to fit your story.

Alignment options: good, neutral, evil, lawful, chaotic (in combinations), or unaligned.

### Armor Class

AC includes natural armor, [[dexterity|Dexterity]], and gear.

### Initiative

Initiative shows a modifier and a score. Roll the modifier, or use the score instead.

### Hit Points

Hit Points show an average and a roll. Use one, not both.

A monster's size determines its Hit Die, as shown below.

#### Hit Dice by Size

| Monster Size | Hit Die | Average HP per Die |
|--------------|---------|-------------------|
| Tiny         | d4      | 2.5               |
| Small        | d6      | 3.5               |
| Medium       | d8      | 4.5               |
| Large        | d10     | 5.5               |
| Huge         | d12     | 6.5               |
| Gargantuan   | d20     | 10.5              |

Add the [[constitution|Constitution]] modifier times the number of Hit Dice to the total. A creature with 2d8 Hit Dice and a +1 Constitution modifier has 2d8 + 2 Hit Points (average 11).

For more on Hit Points, see "Playing the Game."

### Speed

Speed lists movement rates. Monsters may have burrow, climb, fly, or swim speeds.

### Ability Scores

Monsters have six ability scores and their modifiers.

### Skills

Skills show proficiency bonuses. A perceptive monster might have bonuses to [[wisdom|Wisdom]] (Perception). A stealthy one might have bonuses to Dexterity (Stealth). Each skill combines an ability modifier and the monster's Proficiency Bonus.

### Resistances and Vulnerabilities

This lists damage types the creature resists or takes extra from.

### Using Tactics

- **Special Abilities:** use them as soon as possible.
- **Multiattack:** use on normal turns.
- **Bonus Actions and Reactions:** use them freely.

### Immunities

This lists damage and condition immunities.

### Gear

Gear lists equipment. Stat blocks show special effects when needed.

Gear doesn't include clothing. Magical items disappear with the creature.

#### Ammunition

Monsters that need ammo carry it.

#### Adding Gear

Add equipment as you like. You choose what survives.

### Senses

Senses list Passive Perception and any special senses.

### Languages

Languages lists what a monster can use. Some monsters understand a language but can't speak it. "None" means it knows no languages.

#### Telepathy

Telepathy is mental communication within a range.

### Challenge Rating

The Rules Glossary defines Challenge Rating. CR helps you design balanced encounters.

### Traits

Traits are passive abilities or features that trigger in specific situations.

### Actions

Actions list here or come from "Playing the Game."

#### Attack Notation

Attacks show type (melee or ranged), bonus, reach or range, and damage. Most hit one target.

**Hit.** Damage and effects on a hit follow "Hit:".

**Miss.** Effects on a miss follow "Miss:".

**Hit or Miss.** Effects that trigger either way follow "Hit or Miss:".

#### Saving Throw Effects

Save effects list the save type, DC, and result. "Half on a save" means half damage if the target succeeds.

#### Damage Notation

Damage shows both an average and a die roll. Use one or the other, not both.

#### Multiattack

Multiattack lets a creature attack twice or more per turn.

#### Spellcasting

Spellcasting lists the ability, save DC, and attack bonus. Cast most spells at the lowest level available. Some have special limits.

**Spell Components.** The stat block notes required components. The monster has material components.

**Long Casting Times.** Spells taking 1+ minutes use Magic each turn and need Concentration.

### Bonus Action

This section lists Bonus Actions, if any.

### Reactions

Reactions appear here with their triggers.

### Legendary Actions

Legendary Actions let a monster act after another creature turns. It can't use them while [[incapacitated|incapacitated]].

The stat block shows the number of actions. Spends one use per action. All uses refresh at turn start.

### Limited Usage

Some stat block parts limit how many times they work. Common limits:

- **X/Day.** Use X times per day. Resets after a Long Rest.
- **Recharge X to Y.** Use it once. At the start of each monster turn, roll 1d6. On X to Y, it resets.
- **Recharge after Rest.** Use it once. A Short or Long Rest resets it.

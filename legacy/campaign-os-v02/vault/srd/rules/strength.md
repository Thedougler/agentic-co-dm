# Strength

Strength measures physical might — bodily power, athletic training, and the raw force a creature can exert. It is the ability of lifting, hauling, smashing, and holding ground.

## What Strength Governs

| Roll | Strength applies when you… |
|------|----------------------------|
| [[ability-check\|Ability check]] | Lift, push, pull, or break something, force your body through a space, or otherwise apply brute force. |
| [[saving-throw\|Saving throw]] | Physically resist direct force — being shoved, dragged, restrained, or swept away. |
| [[attack-roll\|Attack roll]] | Make a melee attack with a weapon or an Unarmed Strike. |

## Associated Skill

**Athletics** — climbing, swimming, [[jumping\|jumping]] farther than normal, and breaking things. Add your [[proficiency\|Proficiency Bonus]] to a Strength (Athletics) check when you have that proficiency.

## Other Uses

- [[carrying-capacity\|Carrying capacity]] and push/drag/lift limits derive from the Strength score.
- [[jumping\|Jump]] distance ([[long-jump\|Long Jump]] and [[high-jump\|High Jump]]) is measured against the Strength score, not the modifier.
- The Grapple and Shove options of an Unarmed Strike set their save DC from Strength.

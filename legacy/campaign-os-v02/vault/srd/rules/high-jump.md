# High Jump

When you make a High Jump, you leap into the air feet equal to 3 plus your [[strength|Strength]] modifier (at least 0 feet) if you move at least 10 feet on foot immediately before the jump. When you make a standing High Jump, you can jump only half that distance. Either way, each foot of the jump costs a foot of movement.

You can extend your arms half your height above yourself during the jump. So you can reach a distance equal to the height of the jump plus 1½ times your height.

## Provenance

Created for the [[campaign-overview|Shattered Sea]] campaign on 2026-07-30, homebrew throughout except two RAW pieces: the Gale Lantern, a reskin of an existing RAW magic item, and the Sending Stone Anchor, an adapted [[sending|Sending]] stone. Gear here is distinct from [[ship-operations|ship operations]] and [[ship-combat|ship combat]]'s handling rules, and from the [[carpenters-shop|Carpenter's Shop]] bastion facility, which stocks only bastions, a weapons locker, and a rigger's workshop, not this catalog.

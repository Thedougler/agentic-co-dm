# Siege Rules

*[[combat|Combat]] system for armies and colossal monsters. Adds a Siege Phase at initiative 20.*

## Mechanics

**Core Procedure.** [HB, adapted from Pointy Hat's "Storm Rising" adventure]

- Each side has AC, HP, attack bonus, damage bonus, and Siege Actions.
- Siege Phase occurs at initiative count 20. It comes before lair actions and wins ties.
- At the start of each Siege Phase, both sides roll 1d20. The higher roll picks a Siege [[action|Action]] first. The lower roll goes next. They alternate until both sides finish.
- Each side can take 3 Siege Actions per phase.
- Each Siege Action resolves only once per round.
- The GM picks enemy Siege Actions. Players pick allied Siege Actions together.
- **Siege Attack Actions** deal full damage on hit, half damage on miss.
- **Siege Effect Actions** apply debuff or disable on hit. They have no effect on a miss. Missed uses are not spent.
- Rolling a natural 20 does not double Siege damage dice.

**[[initiative|Initiative]] Order with Siege Phase.** [HB, adapted from Pointy Hat's "Storm Rising" adventure]

1. Creatures above initiative 20 act
2. Siege Phase resolves
3. One lair action resolves (if any)
4. Creatures below initiative 20 act

**Design Notes.** [HB, adapted from Pointy Hat's "Storm Rising" adventure] Siege rules work best when:

- The party's actions affect the siege (disable actions, protect NPCs).
- NPC deaths matter via Blaze of Glory.
- The encounter has a goal beyond "kill everything" (ritual completion, creature escape).

## Provenance

[HB, created 2026-07-30] The Siege Phase overlay above is homebrew, adapted wholesale from Pointy Hat's "Storm Rising" adventure — the precedent it was balanced against for its initiative-count-20 phase, alternating Siege Action picks, and hit/miss damage split. Stat blocks like Cape Coral and [[kraken|Kraken]] come from that same adventure and belong to it, not to [[campaign-overview|Shattered Sea]]; the overlay itself is reusable and runs on [[campaign-overview|Shattered Sea]] siege blocks.

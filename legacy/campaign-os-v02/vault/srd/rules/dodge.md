# Dodge [Action]

If you take the Dodge action, you gain the following benefits: until the start of your next turn, any attack roll made against you has Disadvantage if you can see the attacker, and you make [[dexterity|Dexterity]] saving throws with Advantage.

You lose these benefits if you have the [[incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition or if your Speed is 0.

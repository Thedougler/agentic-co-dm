# Intelligence

Intelligence measures reasoning and memory — recall, deduction, and the ability to work a problem out rather than sense it. It is knowing, where [[wisdom|Wisdom]] is noticing.

## What Intelligence Governs

| Roll | Intelligence applies when you… |
|------|--------------------------------|
| [[ability-check\|Ability check]] | Reason or remember — deduce, recall lore, or piece evidence together. |
| [[saving-throw\|Saving throw]] | Recognize an illusion as fake, or resist an effect that attacks the mind directly. |
| [[attack-roll\|Attack roll]] | Cast a spell with an attack roll, where Intelligence is your spellcasting ability. |

## Associated Skills

- **Arcana** — lore about spells, magic items, and the planes of existence.
- **History** — lore about historical events, people, nations, and cultures.
- **Investigation** — finding obscure information in books, or deducing how something works.
- **Nature** — lore about terrain, plants, animals, and weather.
- **Religion** — lore about gods, religious rituals, and holy symbols.

## Other Uses

Intelligence is the spellcasting ability of the [[wizard\|Wizard]], setting that class's spell save DC and spell attack bonus.

# Curses

Some game effects curse a creature or an object. The effect that confers a curse defines what the curse does. Curses can be removed by the *[[remove-curse|Remove Curse]]* and *[[greater-restoration|Greater Restoration]]* spells or other magic that explicitly ends curses.

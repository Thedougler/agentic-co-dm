# Flying

A variety of effects allow a creature to fly. While flying, you fall if you have the [[incapacitated|Incapacitated]] or [[prone|Prone]] condition or your Fly Speed is reduced to 0. You can stay aloft in those circumstances if you can hover. *See also* "Falling" and "Fly Speed."

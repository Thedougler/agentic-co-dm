# Carrying Capacity

Your size and [[strength|Strength]] score determine how many pounds you can carry, as shown below. The table also shows how much you can drag, lift, or push.

Your Speed drops to 5 feet when dragging, lifting, or pushing more than your carry limit.

table: carrying capacity

| Creature Size | Carry          | Drag/Lift/Push |
|---------------|----------------|----------------|
| Tiny          | Str. × 7.5 lb. | Str. × 15 lb.  |
| Small/Medium  | Str. × 15 lb.  | Str. × 30 lb.  |
| Large         | Str. × 30 lb.  | Str. × 60 lb.  |
| Huge          | Str. × 60 lb.  | Str. × 120 lb. |
| Gargantuan    | Str. × 120 lb. | Str. × 240 lb. |

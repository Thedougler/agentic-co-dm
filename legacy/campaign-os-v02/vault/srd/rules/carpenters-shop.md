# Carpenter's Shop

*A ship facility within the Bastions subsystem's Smithy (Tier 1), per Dungeon Master's Guide (2024). A working shop below deck equipped for hull repairs, weapons, armor, and enchanted armaments.*

## Mechanics

**Prerequisite.** [RAW, Dungeon Master's Guide (2024)] None.

**Space.** [RAW, Dungeon Master's Guide (2024)] Roomy.

**Cost to Install.** [RAW, Dungeon Master's Guide (2024)] 500 gp / 7 days (rushed: 1,000 gp / 3 days).

**Hirelings.** [RAW, Dungeon Master's Guide (2024)] 2.

**Order.** [RAW, Dungeon Master's Guide (2024)] Craft.

**Craft Order: [[smiths-tools|Smith's Tools]]** [RAW, Dungeon Master's Guide (2024)] Hirelings can craft using Smith's Tools per the Player's Handbook crafting rules: weapons, armor, metal fittings, ship hardware.

**Craft Order: magic item (Armament), level 9+ only.** [RAW, Dungeon Master's Guide (2024)] Commission hirelings to craft a Common or Uncommon magic item from the Armaments tables (DMG ch. 7). The facility supplies the required tool, and hirelings have tool proficiency and Arcana proficiency. Time and cost per DMG crafting rules. If the item lets its user cast spells, the PC must craft it personally (hirelings assist) and have all those spells prepared each day of crafting.

**Bonus.** [RAW, Dungeon Master's Guide (2024)] If the ship has a Weapons Locker, this facility halves the stocking cost while operational.

## Provenance

Dungeon Master's Guide (2024), Bastions: smithy facility (Tier 1), Carpenter's Shop.

Related systems: Bastions (parent subsystem), Weapons Locker (cost-reduction synergy), Rigger's Workshop (sibling facility).

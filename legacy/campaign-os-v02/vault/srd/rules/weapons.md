# Weapons (5e SRD)

Part of [[equipment|Equipment (5e SRD)]]. The Weapons tables show the game's main weapons, listing the cost and weight of each weapon, plus the following details.

- **Category.** Weapons are simple or martial. Proficiency typically covers all weapons in a single category.
- **Melee or Ranged.** Weapons divide into melee and ranged. Melee weapons attack targets within 5 feet, while ranged weapons reach greater distances.
- **Damage.** The amount of damage a weapon deals when an attacker hits with it, and the damage type.
- **Properties.** Any properties a weapon has, listed in the Properties column. Definitions appear below.
- **Mastery.** Each weapon has a mastery property, defined in "Mastery Properties" below. To use that property, a character must have a feature that lets them use it.

## Selling Equipment

Equipment fetches half its cost when sold. Trade goods and valuables, like gems and art objects, keep their full value in the marketplace. "Magic Items" has prices for magic items.

## Weapon Proficiency

Anyone can wield a weapon, but proficiency lets you add your Proficiency Bonus to the attack roll. A player character's features grant weapon proficiencies. A monster is proficient with any weapon in its stat block.

## Properties

Definitions of the properties that appear in the Properties column of the Weapons tables.

### Ammunition

A weapon with the Ammunition property requires ammunition to make a ranged attack. The weapon's range shows which ammunition you need. Each attack uses one piece of ammunition. Drawing ammunition is part of the attack (you need a free hand to load a one-handed weapon). After a fight, you can spend 1 minute to recover half the ammunition used (round down). You lose the rest.

### Finesse

When making an attack with a Finesse weapon, use the attacker's choice of [[strength|Strength]] or [[dexterity|Dexterity]] modifier for the attack and damage rolls. Use the same modifier for both rolls.

### Heavy

Disadvantage on attack rolls with a Heavy weapon applies if it's a Melee weapon and the attacker's Strength score isn't at least 13, or if it's a Ranged weapon and the attacker's Dexterity score isn't at least 13.

### Light

When a character takes the Attack action on their turn and attacks with a Light weapon, they can make one extra attack as a Bonus Action later on the same turn. This extra attack requires a different Light weapon, and the ability modifier isn't added to the extra attack's damage unless that modifier is negative. Example: attacking with a Shortsword in one hand and a Dagger in the other using the Attack action and a Bonus Action does not add the Strength or Dexterity modifier to the Bonus Action damage roll unless that modifier is negative.

### Loading

A Loading weapon fires only one piece of ammunition. This limit applies regardless of the action type or your normal number of attacks.

### Range

A Range weapon has a range in parentheses after the Ammunition or Thrown property, listing two numbers: the weapon's normal range in feet, then its long range. Attacking a target beyond normal range imposes Disadvantage on the attack roll. You cannot attack targets beyond the long range.

### Reach

A Reach weapon adds 5 feet to the wielder's reach when attacking with it, as well as when determining reach for Opportunity Attacks with it.

### Thrown

You can throw a weapon with the Thrown property to make a ranged attack. You draw it as part of the attack. If it's a Melee weapon, use the same ability modifier for the attack and damage rolls.

### Two-Handed

A Two-Handed weapon requires two hands when attacking with it.

### Versatile

You can use a Versatile weapon with one or two hands. A damage value in parentheses appears with the property. The weapon deals that damage when you use two hands to make a melee attack.

## Mastery Properties

Each weapon has a mastery property, usable only by a character who has a feature, such as Weapon Mastery, that unlocks the property for the character.

Improvised weapons and unusual uses of weapons (such as a table leg, frying pan, bottle as makeshift weapon, or a Ranged weapon used for melee) appear in "Improvised Weapons" in "Rules Glossary", not in this document.

### Cleave

If a melee attack roll with this weapon hits a creature, you can make another melee attack roll against a second creature within 5 feet if it's also within your reach. On a hit, the second creature takes the weapon's damage but not the ability modifier (unless it's negative). You can make this extra attack only once per turn.

### Graze

If an attack roll with this weapon misses a creature, you deal damage to that creature equal to the ability modifier used for the attack roll instead. This damage matches the weapon's type and increases only by raising the ability modifier.

### Nick

When making the extra attack granted by the Light property, you can make it as part of the Attack action instead of as a Bonus Action. You can make this extra attack only once per turn.

### Push

If this weapon hits a creature, you can push it up to 10 feet straight away if it's Large or smaller.

### Sap

If this weapon hits a creature, that creature has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of the attacker's next turn.

### Slow

If this weapon hits a creature and deals damage, reduce that creature's Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. If you hit the same creature more than once with weapons with this property, the total Speed reduction doesn't exceed 10 feet.

### Topple

If this weapon hits a creature, you force that creature to make a [[constitution|Constitution]] saving throw (DC 8 plus the ability modifier used for the attack roll and your Proficiency Bonus). On a failed save, the creature is [[prone|Prone]].

### Vex

If this weapon hits a creature and deals damage to it, the attacker has Advantage on their next attack roll against that creature before the end of the attacker's next turn.

# Charisma

Charisma measures confidence, poise, and charm — force of personality, and how strongly a creature imposes itself on others. It is not beauty; a hideous creature can be overwhelming. It is one of the six [[ability-score|ability scores]], and the rolls it drives follow the [[core-mechanics|core mechanics]] table below.

## What Charisma Governs

| Roll | Charisma applies when you… |
|------|----------------------------|
| [[ability-check\|Ability check]] | Influence, entertain, or deceive. |
| [[saving-throw\|Saving throw]] | Assert your identity — against banishment, possession, and effects that unmake or displace the self. |
| [[attack-roll\|Attack roll]] | Cast a spell with an attack roll, where Charisma is your spellcasting ability. |

## Associated Skills

- **Deception** — telling a convincing lie, or wearing a disguise convincingly.
- **Intimidation** — awing or threatening someone into doing what you want.
- **Performance** — acting, storytelling, music, or dance.
- **Persuasion** — honestly and graciously convincing someone of something.

## Other Uses

Charisma is the spellcasting ability of the [[bard\|Bard]], [[paladin\|Paladin]], [[sorcerer\|Sorcerer]], and [[warlock\|Warlock]].

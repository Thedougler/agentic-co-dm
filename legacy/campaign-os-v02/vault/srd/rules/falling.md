# Falling [Hazard]

A creature that falls takes 1d6 Bludgeoning damage at the end of the fall for every 10 feet it fell, to a maximum of 20d6. When the creature lands, it has the [[prone|Prone]] condition unless it avoids taking any damage from the fall.

> [!check] Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) — Softening a Fall into Liquid
> DC 15; a creature that falls into water or another liquid can use its Reaction to attempt this.
> **Success:** any damage resulting from the fall is halved.
> **Failure:** the fall damage is unchanged.

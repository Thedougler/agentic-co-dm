# Playing the Game (5e SRD)

## Rhythm of Play

D&D has three modes. They are social interaction, exploration, and combat. Each mode uses this same pattern:

1. **Describe a Scene.** Show where the adventurers stand. Show what they can see.
2. **Character Actions.** Each character acts. They travel, search, look, or watch. Outside combat, everyone acts at once. In combat, they take turns.
3. **Narrate Outcomes.** Some tasks just work. Some need a die roll. A door may open, lock, or hide a trap. Then move on, or go back to step 1.

This pattern works for every mode. Combat just needs more structure. That is why it uses turns.

### Exceptions

Melee often uses [[strength|Strength]]. Ranged often uses [[dexterity|Dexterity]]. But rules can differ.

Features, feats, spells, or items can change these rules. They make exceptions. You might use [[charisma|Charisma]] for a melee attack instead.

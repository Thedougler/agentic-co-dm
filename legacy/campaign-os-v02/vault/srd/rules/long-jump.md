# Long Jump

When you make a Long Jump, you leap horizontally a number of feet up to your [[strength|Strength]] score if you move at least 10 feet immediately before the jump. When you make a standing Long Jump, you can leap only half that distance. Either way, each foot you jump costs a foot of movement.

> [!check] Dexterity (Acrobatics) — Landing in Difficult Terrain
> DC 10; required if you land in Difficult Terrain.
> **Success:** you don't gain the [[prone|Prone]] condition.
> **Failure:** you have the [[prone|Prone]] condition.

This Long Jump rule assumes that the height of the jump doesn't matter, such as a jump across a stream or chasm.

> [!check] Strength (Athletics) — Clearing a Low Obstacle
> DC 10; at your GM's option, required to clear a low obstacle (no taller than a quarter of the jump's distance), such as a hedge or low wall.
> **Success:** you clear the obstacle.
> **Failure:** you hit the obstacle.

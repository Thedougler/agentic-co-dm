# Mortis

*A Mortis is the paired flaw and gift — the Mark — every PC in this campaign is asked for at character creation, bound together and imposed rather than picked from a menu.*

## Mechanics

**[HB] The Mark, asked at creation.** Every PC's character-creation interview asks: "Did they come into this with a Mortis — a flaw and a gift that are bound up together? If so, what's the Mark, and do *they* know it's there?" It is imposed as part of who the character already is, never chosen from a menu at a level-up, and nothing in canon states a recovery mechanic or cure for any instance — a Mark holds for the character's whole arc once set.

**[HB] Visible half.** The Mark's mechanical shape — what it grants, what it costs — is always table-visible, stated plainly on the PC's own page.

**[HB] Hidden half.** Whether the *character themself* knows their own Mark is there is a separate, explicit call made per PC — kept secret from the table if the player wants it hidden, stated plainly if not. This half varies PC to PC; the class fixes that the call gets made, not which way it falls.

**[HB] Confirmed instances.** Four PCs carry a named Mortis in canon. Each pairing's full mechanical text lives on its own bearer's page, not here:

- [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] — a Mark that binds him to honesty, costing him persuasive capability in trade for sharpened perception.
- [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Davirelli]] — a Mark that sharpens her mind at the cost of physical frailty, worn openly; she states it plainly rather than hiding it.
- [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] — a Mark that weakens his physical resilience, per his own combat profile.
- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] — a Mark carried as five captains' souls, surfacing as distinct voices in him.

A fifth confirmed instance extends this page's shape rather than replacing it — its numbers stay on its own bearer's page, the same as the four above.

## Provenance

Named and typed at DM ruling, 2026-08-10: Mortis is a class of custom feat (`type: rule`, `subtype: feat`), asked of every PC at character creation via the standing twenty-question interview's Q4, not a curse unique to Jean-Claude Tabarnack. No published-book precedent for the class itself; the paired-gift-and-flaw shape is homebrew, benchmarked against the DMG Flaws variant and Xanathar's Supernatural Gifts table (balance_citation above).

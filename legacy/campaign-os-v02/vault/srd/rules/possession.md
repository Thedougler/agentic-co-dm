# Possession

Some effects cause a creature to be possessed by another creature or entity. A possessing effect defines how the possession operates. Possession can be prevented by the *[[protection-from-evil-and-good|Protection from Evil and Good]]* spell and ended by the *[[dispel-evil-and-good|Dispel Evil and Good]]* spell.

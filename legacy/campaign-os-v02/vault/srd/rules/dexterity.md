# Dexterity

Dexterity measures agility, reflexes, and balance. It is the ability of moving fast, moving quietly, and not being where the blow lands.

## What Dexterity Governs

| Roll | Dexterity applies when you… |
|------|-----------------------------|
| [[ability-check\|Ability check]] | Move nimbly, quickly, or quietly. |
| [[saving-throw\|Saving throw]] | Dodge out of harm's way — most often against an [[area-of-effect\|area of effect]]. |
| [[attack-roll\|Attack roll]] | Make a ranged attack with a weapon, or a melee attack with a Finesse weapon. |

## Associated Skills

- **Acrobatics** — staying on your feet in a tricky situation, or performing an acrobatic stunt.
- **Sleight of Hand** — picking a pocket, concealing a handheld object, or legerdemain.
- **Stealth** — escaping notice by moving quietly and hiding behind things.

## Other Uses

- Base [[armor-class\|Armor Class]] is 10 + the Dexterity modifier, before armor, shields, or magic.
- [[initiative\|Initiative]] is a Dexterity check made at the start of combat.

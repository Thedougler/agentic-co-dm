# Constitution

Constitution measures health and stamina — how much punishment a creature absorbs and how long it keeps going. No skill is tied to it, and it makes no attack rolls; it shows up in how hard a creature is to put down.

## What Constitution Governs

| Roll | Constitution applies when you… |
|------|--------------------------------|
| [[ability-check\|Ability check]] | Push your body beyond normal limits — rare, and always called for by the GM or a specific rule. |
| [[saving-throw\|Saving throw]] | Endure a toxic hazard: poison, disease, exhaustion-inducing strain, and effects that wrack the body. |

## Other Uses

- [[hit-points\|Hit points]]: add the Constitution modifier to each [[hit-dice\|Hit Point Die]] rolled, at every level. Changing the modifier retroactively changes the maximum.
- [[concentration\|Concentration]] on a spell is maintained with a Constitution saving throw whenever you take damage.

> [!mechanic]
> **Concentration.** Take damage while concentrating: Constitution saving throw, DC 10 or half the damage taken, whichever is higher. Fail: the spell ends.

# Illusions

Spells and other effects sometimes create magical illusions. Such an effect defines what the illusion does and which senses or mental faculties it deceives.

An illusion that fills space is not real and has no weight. But it acts as if it were real, unless the spell says not to. A visual illusion casts shadows and bounces light. Wind can move it, and sound bounces off it in small rooms.

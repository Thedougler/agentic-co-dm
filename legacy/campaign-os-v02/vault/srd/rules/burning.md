# Burning [Hazard]

A burning creature or object takes 1d4 Fire damage at the start of each of its turns. As an action, you can extinguish fire on yourself by giving yourself the [[prone|Prone]] condition and rolling on the ground. You can also end the fire by dousing, submerging, or suffocating it.

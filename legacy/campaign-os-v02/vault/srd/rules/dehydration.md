# Dehydration [Hazard]

A creature requires an amount of water per day based on its size, as shown in the Water Needs per Day table. A creature that drinks less than half the required water for a day gains 1 [[exhaustion|Exhaustion]] level at the day's end. Exhaustion caused by dehydration can't be removed until the creature drinks the full amount of water required for a day. *See also* "Exhaustion."

Table: Water Needs per Day

| Size   | Water      | Size       | Water      |
|--------|------------|------------|------------|
| Tiny   | 1/4 gallon | Large      | 4 gallons  |
| Small  | 1 gallon   | Huge       | 16 gallons |
| Medium | 1 gallon   | Gargantuan | 64 gallons |

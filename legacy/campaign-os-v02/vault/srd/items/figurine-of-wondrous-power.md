# Figurine of Wondrous Power

A _Figurine of Wondrous Power_ is a statuette small enough to fit in a pocket. You can use a Magic action to throw this figurine to a point on the ground within 60 feet. If the space isn't occupied by other creatures or objects and has enough room, the figurine becomes a living creature as specified in its description below.

The creature is Friendly to you and your allies. It understands your languages and follows your commands. Its turn comes immediately after you on your Initiative count. If you issue no commands, the creature defends itself but takes no other actions.

# Ammunition (+1, +2, or +3)

_[[weapon|Weapon]] (Any Ammunition)._ You have a bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this piece of magic ammunition. The bonus is determined by the rarity of the ammunition. Once it hits a target, the ammunition is no longer magical:

- [[ammunition-plus-1|+1]] (Uncommon)
- [[ammunition-plus-2|+2]] (Rare)
- [[ammunition-plus-3|+3]] (Very Rare)

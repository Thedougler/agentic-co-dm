# Oil of Sharpness

_Potion, Very Rare._ One vial of this oil can coat one Melee [[weapon|weapon]] or twenty pieces of ammunition, but only ammunition and Melee weapons that are nonmagical and deal Slashing or Piercing damage are affected. Applying the oil takes 1 minute, after which the oil magically seeps into whatever it coats, turning the coated weapon into a _+3 Weapon_ or the coated ammunition into _+3 Ammunition_.

This clear, gelatinous oil sparkles with tiny, ultrathin silver shards.

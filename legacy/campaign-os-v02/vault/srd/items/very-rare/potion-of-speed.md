# Potion of Speed

_Potion, Very Rare._ When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the _[[haste|Haste]]_ spell for 1 minute (no Concentration required) without suffering the wave of lethargy that typically occurs when the effect ends.

This potion's yellow fluid is streaked with black and swirls on its own.

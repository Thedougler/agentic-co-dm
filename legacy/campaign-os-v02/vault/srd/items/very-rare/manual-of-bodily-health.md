# Manual of Bodily Health

_Wondrous Item, Very Rare._ This book contains health and nutrition tips, and its words are charged with magic. If you spend 48 hours over a period of 6 days or fewer studying the book's contents and practicing its guidelines, your [[constitution|Constitution]] increases by 2, to a maximum of 30. The manual then loses its magic but regains it in a century.

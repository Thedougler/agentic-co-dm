# Cloak of Arachnida

_Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)._ This fine garment is made of black silk interwoven with faint, silvery threads. While wearing it, you gain the following benefits.

**_Poison Resistance._** You have Resistance to Poison damage.

**_Spider Climb._** You have a Climb Speed equal to your Speed and can move up, down, and across vertical surfaces and along ceilings, while leaving your hands free. ([[spider-climb|Spider Climb]] spell)

**_Spider Walk._** You can't be caught in webs of any sort and can move through webs as if they were Difficult Terrain.

**_Web._** You can cast _Web_ (save DC 13). The web created by the spell fills twice its normal area. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.

# Mirror of Life Trapping

_Wondrous Item, Very Rare._ When you view this 4-foot-tall, 2-foot-wide mirror indirectly, faint creature images appear on its surface. The mirror weighs 50 pounds. It has AC 11, 10 HP, immunity to poison and psychic damage, and vulnerability to bludgeoning damage. It shatters at 0 HP.

The mirror hangs on a vertical surface. Within 5 feet of it, you can use a Magic action and command word to activate it. Repeat the command word with another Magic action to deactivate it.

Any creature besides you that sees its reflection in the activated mirror—within 30 feet—must make a DC 15 [[charisma|Charisma]] save or get trapped in one of the mirror's twelve cells, with all its belongings. A creature aware of the mirror's nature saves with Advantage. Constructs always succeed.

An extradimensional cell is an infinite void filled with thick fog that reduces visibility to 10 feet. Creatures trapped in the mirror's cells don't age, and they don't need to eat, drink, or sleep. A creature trapped within a cell can escape using magic that permits planar travel. Otherwise, the creature stays in the cell until freed.

If the mirror traps a creature but its twelve extradimensional cells are already occupied, the mirror frees one trapped creature at random to accommodate the new prisoner. A freed creature appears in an unoccupied space within sight of the mirror but facing away from it. If you shatter the mirror, all creatures it contains escape and appear in unoccupied spaces near it.

While within 5 feet of the mirror, you can take a Magic action to name one creature trapped in it or call out a cell by number. The creature named or contained in that cell appears as an image on the mirror's surface. You and the creature can then communicate.

In a similar way, by taking a Magic action and speaking a second command word, you free one creature trapped in the mirror. It then appears in the nearest unoccupied space with its possessions, facing away from it.

Placing the mirror inside an extradimensional space created by a _[[bag-of-holding|Bag of Holding]]_, _[[portable-hole|Portable Hole]]_, or similar item instantly destroys both items and opens a gate to the Astral Plane. The gate originates at the point where one item entered the other. Any creature within 10 feet of the gate and not behind Total Cover gets pulled through it to a random location on the Astral Plane. The gate then closes. You cannot reopen it. The gate is one-way only.

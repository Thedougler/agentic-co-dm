# Scimitar of Speed

_[[weapon|Weapon]] ([[scimitar|Scimitar]]), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)._ You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. In addition, you can make one attack with it as a Bonus Action on each of your turns.

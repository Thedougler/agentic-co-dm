# Bag of Devouring

_Wondrous Item, Very Rare._ This bag resembles a _[[bag-of-holding|Bag of Holding]]_ but is a feeding orifice for a gigantic extradimensional creature. Turning the bag inside out closes the orifice.

The creature in the bag senses whatever enters. The bag eats any animal or plant matter wholly inside. If someone reaches in, there's a 50 percent chance the bag pulls them inside. A creature inside can escape with a DC 15 [[strength|Strength]] (Athletics) check. Another creature can pull it out with a DC 20 check, but might get pulled in too. Any creature starting its turn inside gets eaten.

The bag holds one cubic foot of objects. Each day, it swallows everything and sends it to another plane. The GM determines the time and plane.

Piercing or tearing the bag destroys it and sends the contents to a random spot on the Astral Plane.

# Ioun Stone (Absorption)

_Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)._ Roughly marble sized, _[[ioun-stone|Ioun Stones]]_ take their name from Ioun, a god of knowledge and prophecy revered on some worlds. Many types of _Ioun Stones_ exist, each type a distinct combination of shape and color.

When you take a Magic action to toss an _[[ioun-stone|Ioun Stone]]_ into the air, the stone orbits your head at a distance of 1d3 feet, conferring its benefit to you while doing so. You can have up to three _Ioun Stones_ orbiting your head.

Each _Ioun Stone_ orbiting your head counts as an object you are wearing. The orbiting stone avoids contact with other creatures and objects, adjusting its orbit to avoid collisions and thwarting all attempts by other creatures to attack or snatch it.

As an action, you can seize and stow any number of _Ioun Stones_ orbiting your head. If your Attunement to an _Ioun Stone_ ends while it's orbiting your head, the stone falls as though you had dropped it.

While this pale lavender ellipsoid orbits your head, you can use your Reaction to cancel a spell of 4th level or lower cast by a creature you can see. Canceling the spell negates it and wastes the caster's resources. Once the stone has canceled 20 spell levels, it burns out and turns dull gray, powerless.

# Belt of Giant Strength (Frost or Stone)

_Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)._ While wearing this belt, your [[strength|Strength]] changes to 23. The item has no effect on you if your [[strength|Strength]] without the belt is equal to or greater than 23.

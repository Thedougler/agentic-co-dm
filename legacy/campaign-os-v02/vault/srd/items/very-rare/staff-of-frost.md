# Staff of Frost

_Staff, Very Rare (Requires Attunement by a [[druid|Druid]], [[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[warlock|Warlock]], or [[wizard|Wizard]])._ You have Resistance to Cold damage while you hold this staff.

![[staff-of-fire#^staff-10-charges-spells-intro]]

| Spell        | Charge Cost |
|--------------|-------------|
| [[cone-of-cold\|Cone of Cold]] | 5           |
| [[fog-cloud\|Fog Cloud]]    | 1           |
| [[ice-storm\|Ice Storm]]    | 4           |
| [[wall-of-ice\|Wall of Ice]]  | 4           |

**_Regaining Charges._** The staff regains 1d6 + 4 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll 1d20. On a 1, the staff turns to water and is destroyed.

# Wand of Paralysis

_Wand, Rare (Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster)._ This wand has 7 charges. While holding it, you can take a Magic action to expend 1 charge to cause a thin blue ray to streak from the tip toward a creature you can see within 60 feet of yourself. The target must succeed on a DC 15 [[constitution|Constitution]] saving throw or have the [[paralyzed|Paralyzed]] condition for 1 minute. At the end of each of the target's turns, it repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success.

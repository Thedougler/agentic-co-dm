# Staff of Withering

_Staff, Rare (Requires Attunement)._ This staff has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.

The staff can be wielded as a magic [[quarterstaff|Quarterstaff]]. On a hit, it deals damage as a normal Quarterstaff, and you can expend 1 charge to deal an extra 2d10 Necrotic damage to the target and force it to make a DC 15 [[constitution|Constitution]] saving throw. On a failed save, the target has Disadvantage for 1 hour on any ability check or saving throw that uses [[strength|Strength]] or Constitution.

# Sword of Life Stealing

_[[weapon|Weapon]] ([[glaive|Glaive]], [[greatsword|Greatsword]], [[longsword|Longsword]], [[rapier|Rapier]], [[scimitar|Scimitar]], or [[shortsword|Shortsword]]), Rare (Requires Attunement)._ When you attack a creature with this magic weapon and roll a 20 on the d20 for the attack roll, that target takes an extra 15 Necrotic damage if it isn't a Construct or an Undead, and you gain Temporary Hit Points equal to the amount of Necrotic damage taken.

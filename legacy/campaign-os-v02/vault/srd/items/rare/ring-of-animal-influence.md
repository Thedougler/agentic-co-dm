# Ring of Animal Influence

_Ring, Rare._ This ring has 3 charges, and it regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the ring, you can expend 1 charge to cast one of the following spells (save DC 13) from it:

- _[[animal-friendship|Animal Friendship]]_
- _Fear_ (affects Beasts only)
- _[[speak-with-animals|Speak with Animals]]_

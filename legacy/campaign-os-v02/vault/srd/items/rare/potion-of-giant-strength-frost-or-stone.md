# Potion of Giant Strength (frost or stone)

_Potion, Rare._ When you drink this potion, your [[strength|Strength]] score changes for 1 hour to 23. The potion has no effect on you if your Strength is equal to or greater than that score.

This potion's transparent liquid has floating in it a sliver of light resembling a giant's fingernail.

# Potion of Invisibility

_Potion, Rare._ This potion's container looks empty but feels as though it holds liquid. When you drink the potion, you have the [[invisible|Invisible]] condition for 1 hour. The effect ends early if you make an attack roll, deal damage, or cast a spell.

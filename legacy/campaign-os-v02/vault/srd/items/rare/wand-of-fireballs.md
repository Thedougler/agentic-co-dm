# Wand of Fireballs

_Wand, Rare (Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster)._ This wand has 7 charges. While holding it, you can expend no more than 3 charges to cast _[[fireball|Fireball]]_ (save DC 15) from it. For 1 charge, you cast the level 3 version of the spell. You can increase the spell's level by 1 for each additional charge you expend.

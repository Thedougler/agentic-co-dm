# Rod of Rulership

_Rod, Rare (Requires Attunement)._ You can take a Magic action to present the rod and command obedience from each creature of your choice that you can see within 120 feet of yourself. Each target must succeed on a DC 15 [[wisdom|Wisdom]] saving throw or have the [[charmed|Charmed]] condition for 8 hours. While Charmed in this way, the creature regards you as its trusted leader. If harmed by you or your allies or commanded to do something contrary to its nature, a target ceases to be Charmed in this way. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.

# Potion of Heroism

_Potion, Rare._ When you drink this potion, you gain 10 Temporary Hit Points that last for 1 hour. For the same duration, you are under the effect of the _[[bless|Bless]]_ spell (no Concentration required).

This potion's blue liquid bubbles and steams as if boiling.

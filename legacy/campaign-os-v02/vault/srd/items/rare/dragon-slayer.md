# Dragon Slayer

_[[weapon|Weapon]] (Any Simple or Martial), Rare._ You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

The weapon deals an extra 3d6 damage of the weapon's type if the target is a Dragon.

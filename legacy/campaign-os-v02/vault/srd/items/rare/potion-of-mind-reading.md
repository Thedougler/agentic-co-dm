# Potion of Mind Reading

_Potion, Rare._ When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the _[[detect-thoughts|Detect Thoughts]]_ spell (save DC 13) for 10 minutes (no Concentration required).

This potion's dense, purple liquid has an ovoid cloud of pink floating in it.

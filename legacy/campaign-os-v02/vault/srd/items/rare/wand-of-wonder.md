# Wand of Wonder

_Wand, Rare (Requires Attunement)._ This wand has 7 charges. While holding it, you can take a Magic action to use 1 charge while choosing a point within 120 feet of yourself. That location becomes the point of origin of a spell or other magical effect determined by rolling on the Wand of Wonder Effects table. Any spell cast from the wand has a save DC of 15 and a range of 120 feet if its normal max range is less than that. If an effect targets many subjects, the GM randomly determines which to affect.

**_Regaining Charges._** The wand regains 1d6 + 1 used charges daily at dawn. Using the wand's last charge requires a 1d20 roll. If the result is 1, it crumbles to dust.

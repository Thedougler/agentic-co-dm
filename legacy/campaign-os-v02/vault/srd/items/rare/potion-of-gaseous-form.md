# Potion of Gaseous Form

_Potion, Rare._ When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the _[[gaseous-form|Gaseous Form]]_ spell for 1 hour (no Concentration required) or until you end the effect as a Bonus Action.

This potion's container seems to hold fog that moves and pours like water.

# Giant Slayer

_[[weapon|Weapon]] (Any Simple or Martial), Rare._ You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

When you hit a Giant with this weapon, the Giant takes an extra 2d6 damage of the weapon's type and must succeed on a DC 15 [[strength|Strength]] saving throw or have the [[prone|Prone]] condition.

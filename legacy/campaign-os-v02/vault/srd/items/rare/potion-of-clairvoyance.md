# Potion of Clairvoyance

_Potion, Rare._ When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the _[[clairvoyance|Clairvoyance]]_ spell (no Concentration required).

An eyeball bobs in this potion's yellowish liquid but vanishes when the potion is opened.

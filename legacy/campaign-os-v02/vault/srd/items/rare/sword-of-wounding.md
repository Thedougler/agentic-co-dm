# Sword of Wounding

_[[weapon|Weapon]] ([[glaive|Glaive]], [[greatsword|Greatsword]], [[longsword|Longsword]], [[rapier|Rapier]], [[scimitar|Scimitar]], or [[shortsword|Shortsword]]), Rare (Requires Attunement)._ When you hit a creature with an attack using this magic weapon, the target takes an extra 2d6 Necrotic damage and must succeed on a DC 15 [[constitution|Constitution]] saving throw or be unable to regain Hit Points for 1 hour. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

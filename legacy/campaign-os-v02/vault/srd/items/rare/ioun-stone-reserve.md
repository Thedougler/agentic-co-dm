# Ioun Stone (Reserve)

_Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)._ Roughly marble sized, _Ioun Stones_ take their name from Ioun, a god of knowledge and prophecy revered on some worlds. Many types of _Ioun Stones_ exist, each type a distinct combination of shape and color.

When you take a Magic action to toss an _[[ioun-stone|Ioun Stone]]_ into the air, the stone orbits your head at a distance of 1d3 feet, conferring its benefit to you while doing so. You can have up to three _[[ioun-stone|Ioun Stones]]_ orbiting your head.

Each _Ioun Stone_ orbiting your head counts as an object you are wearing. The orbiting stone avoids contact with other creatures and objects, adjusting its orbit to avoid collisions and thwarting all attempts by other creatures to attack or snatch it.

As an action, you can seize and stow any number of _Ioun Stones_ orbiting your head. If your Attunement to an _Ioun Stone_ ends while it's orbiting your head, the stone falls as though you had dropped it.

This bright purple prism stores spells cast into it, holding them until you use them. The stone can store up to 4 levels of spells at a time. When found, it contains 1d4 levels of stored spells chosen by the GM.

Any creature can cast a spell of 1st through 4th level into the stone by touching it. The spell has no effect except that the stone stores it. If the stone can't hold the spell, the caster wastes the slot but the spell doesn't take effect. The level of the slot used determines how much space it requires.

While this stone orbits your head, you can cast spells from storage that use the original caster's slot level, spell save DC, spell attack bonus, and spellcasting ability. Once cast, the spell vanishes from the stone, freeing up space.

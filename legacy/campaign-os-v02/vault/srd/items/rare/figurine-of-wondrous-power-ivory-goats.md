# Figurine of Wondrous Power (Ivory Goats)

_Wondrous Item, Rare._ A _[[figurine-of-wondrous-power|Figurine of Wondrous Power]]_ is a statuette small enough to fit in a pocket. You can use a Magic action to throw this figurine to a point on the ground within 60 feet. If the space isn't occupied by other creatures or objects and has enough room, the figurine becomes a living creature as specified in its description below.

The creature is Friendly to you and your allies. It understands your languages and follows your commands. Its turn comes immediately after you on your Initiative count. If you issue no commands, the creature defends itself but takes no other actions.

The creature exists for a duration specific to each figurine, reverting to figurine form at that time or early if reduced to 0 Hit Points or if you use a Magic action while touching it. You can't use the property again until the time specified in the figurine's description has passed.

These ivory statuettes of goats are always created in sets of three. Each goat looks unique and functions differently from the others. Their properties are as follows:

## Goat of Terror

This figurine can become a **[[giant-goat|Giant Goat]]** for up to 3 hours. The goat can't attack, but you can (harmlessly) remove its horns and use them as weapons. One horn becomes a _+1 [[lance|Lance]]_, and the other becomes a _+2 [[longsword|Longsword]]_. Removing a horn requires a Magic action, and the weapons disappear and the horns return when the goat reverts to figurine form. Riding the goat radiates fear in a 30-foot Emanation. Any Hostile creature within that range beginning its turn must make a DC 15 [[wisdom|Wisdom]] saving throw, with failure imposing the [[frightened|Frightened]] condition for 1 minute (ending early if you dismount or the goat reverts). The creature repeats the save at each turn's end, gaining immunity for 24 hours on success. Once you use the figurine, you can't use it again until 15 days have passed.

## Goat of Traveling

This figurine can become a Large goat with the same statistics as a **[[riding-horse|Riding Horse]]**. It has 24 charges, and each hour or part thereof it spends in goat form costs 1 charge. While it has charges, you can use it as often as you wish. When it runs out of charges, it reverts to a figurine and you can't use it again until 7 days have passed, when it regains all expended charges.

## Goat of Travail

Like the Goat of Terror, this figurine can become a **Giant Goat** for up to 3 hours, but it has no special ability. Once you use it, you can't use it again until 30 days have passed.

# Armor of Resistance

_Armor (Any Light, Medium, or Heavy), Rare (Requires Attunement)._ You have Resistance to one type of damage while you wear this armor. The GM chooses the type or determines it randomly by rolling on the following table.

| 1    | [[acid\|Acid]]        |

| 3    | Fire        |
| 4    | Force       |
| 5    | Lightning   |
| 6    | Necrotic    |
| 7    | Poison      |
| 8    | Psychic     |
| 9    | Radiant     |
| 10   | Thunder     |

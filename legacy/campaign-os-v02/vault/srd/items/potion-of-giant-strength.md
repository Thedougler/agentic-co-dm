# Potion of Giant Strength

When you drink this potion, your [[strength|Strength]] score changes for 1 hour. The type of giant determines the score. The potion has no effect on you if your Strength is already equal to or greater than that score.

This potion's transparent liquid has floating in it a sliver of light resembling a giant's fingernail.

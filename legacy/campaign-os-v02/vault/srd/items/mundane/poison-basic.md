# Poison, Basic

**Cost:** 100 GP.

As a Bonus Action, you can coat one weapon or up to three pieces of ammunition with this poison. A creature that takes Piercing or Slashing damage from the poisoned weapon or ammunition takes an extra 1d4 Poison damage. The poison remains potent for 1 minute or until the damage takes effect, whichever comes first.

# Tinderbox

**Cost:** 5 SP.

A small container holding flint, fire steel, and tinder (dry cloth soaked in light oil). Using it to light a [[candle|Candle]], [[lamp|Lamp]], Lantern, or [[torch|Torch]] takes a Bonus Action. Lighting any other fire takes 1 minute.

# Arcane Focus

**Cost:** Varies.

An Arcane Focus takes one of the forms in the Arcane Focuses table below, bejeweled or carved to channel arcane magic. A Sorcerer, [[warlock|Warlock]], or [[wizard|Wizard]] can use it as a Spellcasting Focus.

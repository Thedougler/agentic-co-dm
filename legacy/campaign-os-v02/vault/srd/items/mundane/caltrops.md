# Caltrops

**Cost:** 1 GP.

As an action, spread Caltrops from their bag to cover a 5-foot-square area within 5 feet. A creature that enters this area for the first time on a turn must succeed on a DC 15 [[dexterity|Dexterity]] saving throw or take 1 Piercing damage and reduce its Speed to 0 until the start of its next turn. Recovering the Caltrops takes 10 minutes.

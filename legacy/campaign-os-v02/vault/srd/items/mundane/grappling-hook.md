# Grappling Hook

**Cost:** 2 GP.

As an action, throw the Grappling Hook at a railing, ledge, or other catch within 50 feet. It catches on with a successful DC 13 [[dexterity]] (Acrobatics) check. A Rope tied to the hook allows you to climb it.

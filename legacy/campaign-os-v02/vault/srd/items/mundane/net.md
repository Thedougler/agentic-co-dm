# Net

**Cost:** 1 GP.

When taking the Attack action, you can throw a Net at a creature visible within 15 feet. The target must make a [[dexterity]] saving throw (DC 8 plus the thrower's Dexterity modifier and Proficiency Bonus) or become [[restrained]] until it escapes. Creatures that are Huge or larger automatically succeed. To escape, the target or an ally within 5 feet can take an action to make a DC 10 [[strength]] (Athletics) check. Success frees the Restrained creature. You can also destroy the Net (AC 10; 5 HP; [[immunity]] to Bludgeoning, Poison, and Psychic damage) to free the target.

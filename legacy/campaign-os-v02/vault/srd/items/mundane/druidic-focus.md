# Druidic Focus

**Cost:** Varies.

A Druidic Focus takes one of the forms in the Druidic Focuses table below and is carved, tied with ribbon, or painted to channel primal magic. A Druid or [[ranger|Ranger]] can use such an object as a Spellcasting Focus.

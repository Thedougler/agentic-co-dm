# Hunting Trap

**Cost:** 5 GP.

As an action, set a Hunting Trap (a sawtooth steel ring that snaps shut when a creature steps on a pressure plate in the center). Affix the trap with a heavy chain to an immobile object, such as a tree or a spike driven into the ground. A creature that steps on the plate must succeed on a DC 13 [[dexterity]] saving throw or take 1d4 Piercing damage and have its Speed reduced to 0 until the start of its next turn. Until the creature breaks free, the chain limits its movement (typically 3 feet). Use an action to make a DC 13 [[strength]] (Athletics) check, freeing the trapped creature or another creature within reach on a success. Each failed check deals 1 Piercing damage to the trapped creature.

# Torch

**Cost:** 1 CP.

A Torch burns for 1 hour, casting Bright Light in a 20-foot radius and Dim Light 20 feet beyond that. You can attack with a Torch as a Simple Melee weapon when you take the Attack action. On a hit, the target takes 1 Fire damage.

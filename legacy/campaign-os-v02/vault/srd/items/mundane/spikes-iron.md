# Spikes, Iron

**Cost:** 1 GP.

Iron Spikes come in bundles of ten. As a use action, you can hammer a spike into wood, earth, or similar material using a blunt object like a [[light-hammer|Light Hammer]]. You can use this to jam a door shut or secure a [[rope|Rope]] or [[chain|Chain]] to the spike.

# Holy Water

**Cost:** 25 GP.

When taking the Attack action, one attack can be replaced with throwing a flask of Holy Water. Target one creature visible within 20 feet. The target must succeed on a [[dexterity]] saving throw (DC 8 plus the thrower's Dexterity modifier and Proficiency Bonus) or take 2d8 Radiant damage if it is a Fiend or an Undead.

# Chain

**Cost:** 5 GP.

As an action, wrap a Chain around an unwilling creature within 5 feet that has the [[grappled|Grappled]], [[incapacitated|Incapacitated]], or [[restrained|Restrained]] condition using a successful DC 13 [[strength|Strength]] (Athletics) check. If you bind the creature's legs, it has the Restrained condition until it escapes. The creature can escape the Chain using a successful DC 18 [[dexterity|Dexterity]] (Acrobatics) check as an action, or burst the Chain using a successful DC 20 Strength (Athletics) check as an action.

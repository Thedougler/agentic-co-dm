# Acid

**Cost:** 25 GP.

When taking the Attack action, you can replace one attack with throwing a vial of Acid at a target you can see within 20 feet. The target must make a [[dexterity|Dexterity]] saving throw (DC 8 + your [[dexterity|Dexterity]] modifier + your bonus) or take 2d6 Acid damage.

# Book

**Cost:** 25 GP.

A Book contains fiction or nonfiction. Consulting an accurate nonfiction Book about its topic grants a +5 bonus to [[intelligence|Intelligence]] (Arcana, History, Nature, or Religion) checks made about that topic.

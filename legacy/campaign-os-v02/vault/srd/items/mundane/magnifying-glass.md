# Magnifying Glass

**Cost:** 100 GP.

Grants Advantage on any ability check made to appraise or inspect a highly detailed item. Lighting a fire with a Magnifying Glass requires sunlight to focus the light through the lens, tinder, and about 5 minutes to complete.

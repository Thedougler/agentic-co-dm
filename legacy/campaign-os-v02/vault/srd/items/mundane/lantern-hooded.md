# Lantern, Hooded

**Cost:** 5 GP.

A Hooded Lantern burns Oil as fuel to cast Bright Light in a 30-foot radius and Dim Light 30 feet beyond that. As a Bonus Action, lower the hood to reduce the light to Dim Light in a 5-foot radius, or raise it again.

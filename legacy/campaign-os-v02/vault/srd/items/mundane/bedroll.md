# Bedroll

**Cost:** 1 GP.

A Bedroll sleeps one Small or Medium creature. While in a Bedroll, saving throws against extreme cold automatically succeed. (See "[[gameplay-toolbox|Gameplay Toolbox]]", not contained in this source document.)

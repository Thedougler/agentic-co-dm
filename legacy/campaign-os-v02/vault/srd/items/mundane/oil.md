# Oil

**Cost:** 1 SP.

You can douse a creature, object, or space with Oil, or use it as fuel.

- **Dousing a Creature or an Object.** When taking the Attack action, you can throw an Oil flask at one creature or object within 20 feet. The target must make a [[dexterity|dexterity]] saving throw (DC 8 plus the thrower's Dexterity modifier and Proficiency Bonus) or becomes covered in oil. If the target takes Fire damage before the oil dries (after 1 minute), it takes an extra 5 Fire damage from the burning oil.
- **Dousing a Space.** As a use action, pour an Oil flask on level ground to cover a 5-foot-square area within 5 feet. If lit, the oil burns until the end of the turn 2 rounds after ignition (or 12 seconds) and deals 5 Fire damage to any creature that enters the area or ends its turn there. A creature takes this damage only once per turn.
- **Fuel.** Oil fuels [[lamp|Lamp]]s and Lanterns. Once lit, a flask of Oil burns for 6 hours in a Lamp or Lantern. This duration need not be consecutive. You can extinguish the burning Oil (as a use action) and rekindle it until it has burned for a total of 6 hours.

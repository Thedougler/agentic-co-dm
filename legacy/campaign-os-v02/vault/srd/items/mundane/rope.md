# Rope

**Cost:** 1 GP.

As a use action, you can tie a knot with a successful DC 10 [[dexterity|Dexterity]] (Sleight of Hand) check. You can burst the Rope with a successful DC 20 [[strength|Strength]] (Athletics) check. You can bind an unwilling creature with Rope only if it has the [[grappled|Grappled]], [[incapacitated|Incapacitated]], or [[restrained|Restrained]] condition. If you bind the creature's legs, it has the Restrained condition until it escapes. The creature can escape with a successful DC 15 Dexterity (Acrobatics) check as an action.

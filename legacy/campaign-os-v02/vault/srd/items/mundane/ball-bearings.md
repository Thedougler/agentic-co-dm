# Ball Bearings

**Cost:** 1 GP.

As an action, spill Ball Bearings from their pouch to cover a level, 10-foot-square area within 10 feet. A creature that enters this area for the first time on a turn must succeed on a DC 10 [[dexterity|Dexterity]] saving throw or fall [[prone|Prone]]. Recovering the Ball Bearings takes 10 minutes.

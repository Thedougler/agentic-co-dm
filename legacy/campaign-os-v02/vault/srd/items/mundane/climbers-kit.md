# Climber's Kit

**Cost:** 25 GP.

Includes boot tips, gloves, pitons, and a harness. As an action, use it to anchor yourself to a surface. While anchored, you don't fall if you fall more than 25 feet from the anchor point, but moving more than 25 feet away requires a Bonus Action to undo the anchor.

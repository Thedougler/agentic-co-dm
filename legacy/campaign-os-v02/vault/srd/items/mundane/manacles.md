# Manacles

**Cost:** 2 GP.

As a use action, you can bind an unwilling Small or Medium creature within 5 feet that has the [[grappled|Grappled]], [[incapacitated|Incapacitated]], or [[restrained|Restrained]] condition with a successful DC 13 [[dexterity|Dexterity]] (Sleight of Hand) check. A bound creature has Disadvantage on attack rolls and the Restrained condition when chained to a fixed point. To escape, it can make a DC 20 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check as an action or a DC 25 [[strength|Strength]] (Athletics) check to burst them. Each set includes a key. Without it, you can use [[thieves-tools|Thieves' Tools]] to pick the lock with a DC 15 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check.

# Belt of Giant Strength

While wearing this belt, your [[strength|Strength]] changes to a score granted by the belt. The type of giant determines the score. The item has no effect on you if your Strength without the belt is already equal to or greater than the belt's score. Every Belt of Giant Strength requires Attunement.

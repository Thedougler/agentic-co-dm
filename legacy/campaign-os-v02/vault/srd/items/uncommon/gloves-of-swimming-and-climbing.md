# Gloves of Swimming and Climbing

_Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)._ While wearing these gloves, you have a Climb Speed and a Swim Speed equal to your Speed, and you gain a +5 bonus to [[strength|Strength]] (Athletics) checks made to climb or swim.

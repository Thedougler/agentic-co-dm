# Potion of Giant Strength (hill)

_Potion, Uncommon._ When you drink this potion, your [[strength|Strength]] score changes for 1 hour to 21. The potion has no effect on you if your Strength is equal to or greater than that score. See [[potion-of-giant-strength|Potion of Giant Strength]] for other types.

This potion's transparent liquid has floating in it a sliver of light resembling a giant's fingernail.

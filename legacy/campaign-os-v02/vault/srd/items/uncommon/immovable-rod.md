# Immovable Rod

_Rod, Uncommon._ This iron rod has a button on one end. Press the button as an action to fix the rod magically in place. The rod stays fixed until you or another creature presses the button again, defying gravity as needed. It can hold up to 8,000 pounds. More weight makes it fall. Use an action and succeed at a DC 30 [[strength|Strength]] (Athletics) check to move the fixed rod up to 10 feet.

# Wand of Magic Missiles

_Wand, Uncommon._ This wand has 7 charges. While holding it, you can expend no more than 3 charges to cast _[[magic-missile|Magic Missile]]_ from it. For 1 charge, you cast the level 1 version of the spell. You can increase the spell's level by 1 for each additional charge you expend.

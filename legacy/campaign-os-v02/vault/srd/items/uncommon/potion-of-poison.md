# Potion of Poison

_Potion, Uncommon._ This concoction looks, smells, and tastes like a _[[potion-of-healing-common|Potion of Healing]]_ or another beneficial potion. However, it is actually poison masked by illusion magic. _Identify_ reveals its true nature.

If you drink this potion, you take 4d6 Poison damage and must succeed on a DC 13 [[constitution|Constitution]] saving throw or have the [[poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 1 hour.

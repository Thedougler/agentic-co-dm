# Bracers of Archery

_Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)._ While wearing these bracers, you have proficiency with the [[longbow|Longbow]] and [[shortbow|Shortbow]], and you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls made with such weapons.

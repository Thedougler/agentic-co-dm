# Bag of Holding

_Wondrous Item, Uncommon._ This bag's interior is much larger than the outside. Inside, it measures roughly 2 feet square and 4 feet deep. It holds up to 500 pounds and takes up 64 cubic feet. It weighs 5 pounds. Getting an item from the bag takes an action.

An overloaded, pierced, or torn bag destroys itself and scatters its contents in the Astral Plane. Turning the bag inside out spills its contents unharmed, but you must right the bag before using it again. The bag holds enough air for 10 minutes of breathing per creature inside.

Place a _Bag of Holding_ inside an extradimensional space made by a _[[handy-haversack|Handy Haversack]]_, _[[portable-hole|Portable Hole]]_, or similar item. Both items instantly break. A gate to the Astral Plane opens. The gate appears where one item enters the other. The gate pulls any creature within 10 feet to a random spot on the Astral Plane. The gate then closes. It cannot reopen.

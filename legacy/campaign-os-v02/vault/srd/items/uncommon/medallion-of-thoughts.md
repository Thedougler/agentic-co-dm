# Medallion of Thoughts

_Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)._ The medallion has 5 charges. While wearing it, you can expend 1 charge to cast _[[detect-thoughts|Detect Thoughts]]_ (save DC 13) from it. The medallion regains 1d4 expended charges daily at dawn.

# Eyes of Minute Seeing

_Wondrous Item, Uncommon._ These crystal lenses fit over the eyes. While wearing them, your vision improves significantly out to a range of 1 foot, granting you Darkvision within that range and Advantage on [[intelligence|Intelligence]] (Investigation) checks made to examine something within that range.

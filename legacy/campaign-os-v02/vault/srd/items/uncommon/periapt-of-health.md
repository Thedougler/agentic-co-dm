# Periapt of Health

_Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)._ While wearing this pendant, you can take a Magic action to regain 2d4 + 2 Hit Points. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.

In addition, you have Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the [[poisoned|Poisoned]] condition while you wear this pendant.

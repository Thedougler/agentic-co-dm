# Figurine of Wondrous Power (Silver Raven)

_Wondrous Item, Uncommon._ A _[[figurine-of-wondrous-power|Figurine of Wondrous Power]]_ is a statuette small enough to fit in a pocket. You can use a Magic action to throw this figurine to a point on the ground within 60 feet. If the space isn't occupied by other creatures or objects and has enough room, the figurine becomes a living creature as specified in its description below.

The creature is Friendly to you and your allies. It understands your languages and follows your commands. Its turn comes immediately after you on your Initiative count. If you issue no commands, the creature defends itself but takes no other actions.

The creature exists for a duration specific to each figurine, reverting to figurine form at that time or early if reduced to 0 Hit Points or if you use a Magic action while touching it. You can't use the property again until the time specified in the figurine's description has passed.

This silver statuette of a raven can become a **[[raven|Raven]]** for up to 12 hours. Once you use it, you can't use it again until 2 days have passed. While in raven form, the figurine grants you the ability to cast _[[animal-messenger|Animal Messenger]]_ on it.

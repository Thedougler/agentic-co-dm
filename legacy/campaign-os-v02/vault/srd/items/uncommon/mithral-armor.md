# Mithral Armor

_Armor (Any Medium or Heavy, Except Hide Armor), Uncommon._ Mithral is a light, flexible metal. Armor made of this substance can be worn under normal clothes. If the armor normally imposes Disadvantage on [[dexterity|Dexterity]] (Stealth) checks or has a [[strength|Strength]] requirement, the mithral version of the armor doesn't.

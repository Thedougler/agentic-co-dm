# Weapon of Warning

_[[weapon|Weapon]] (Any Simple or Martial), Uncommon (Requires Attunement)._ As long as this weapon is within your reach and you are attuned to it, you and allies within 30 feet of you gain the following benefits.

**_[[alarm|Alarm]]._** The weapon magically awakens each subject who is sleeping naturally when combat begins. This benefit doesn't wake a subject from magically induced sleep.

**_Supernatural Readiness._** Each subject has Advantage on its Initiative rolls.

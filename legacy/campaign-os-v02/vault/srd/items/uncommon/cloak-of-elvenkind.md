# Cloak of Elvenkind

_Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)._ While you wear this cloak, [[wisdom|Wisdom]] (Perception) checks made to perceive you have Disadvantage, and you have Advantage on [[dexterity|Dexterity]] (Stealth) checks.

# Pearl of Power

_Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster)._ While this pearl is on your person, you can take a Magic action to regain one expended spell slot of level 3 or lower. Once you use the pearl, it can't be used again until the next dawn.

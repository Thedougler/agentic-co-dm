# Eyes of the Eagle

_Wondrous Item, Uncommon._ These crystal lenses fit over the eyes. While wearing them, you have Advantage on [[wisdom|Wisdom]] (Perception) checks that rely on sight. In conditions of clear visibility, you can make out details of even extremely distant creatures and objects as small as 2 feet across.

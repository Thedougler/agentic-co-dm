# Potions of Healing

You regain Hit Points when you drink one of these potions. The number of Hit Points depends on the potion's potency, as shown below.

Whatever its potency, the potion's red liquid glimmers when agitated. For other potions, see [[potion-of-climbing|Potion of Climbing]], [[potion-of-growth|Potion of Growth]], [[potion-of-poison|Potion of Poison]], [[potion-of-resistance|Potion of Resistance]], and [[potion-of-water-breathing|Potion of Water Breathing]].

# Ioun Stone

Roughly marble sized, _Ioun Stones_ take their name from Ioun, a god of knowledge and prophecy revered on some worlds. Many types of _Ioun Stones_ exist, each type a distinct combination of shape and color.

When you take a Magic action to toss an _Ioun Stone_ into the air, the stone orbits your head at a distance of 1d3 feet, conferring its benefit to you while doing so. You can have up to three _Ioun Stones_ orbiting your head. Each orbiting stone counts as an object you are wearing and avoids contact with other creatures and objects, adjusting its orbit to avoid collisions and thwarting all attempts by other creatures to attack or snatch it.

As an action, you can seize and stow any number of _Ioun Stones_ orbiting your head. If your Attunement to an _Ioun Stone_ ends while it's orbiting your head, the stone falls as though you had dropped it. Every _Ioun Stone_ requires Attunement.

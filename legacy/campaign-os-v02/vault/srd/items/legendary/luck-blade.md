# Luck Blade

_[[weapon|Weapon]] ([[glaive|Glaive]], [[greatsword|Greatsword]], [[longsword|Longsword]], [[rapier|Rapier]], [[scimitar|Scimitar]], [[sickle|Sickle]], or [[shortsword|Shortsword]]), Legendary (Requires Attunement)._ You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. While the weapon is on your person, you also gain a +1 bonus to saving throws.

_Luck._ If the weapon is on your person, you can call on its luck (no action required) to reroll one failed D20 Test if you don't have the [[incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition. You must use the second roll. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.

_Wish._ The weapon has 1d3 charges. While holding it, you can expend 1 charge and cast _[[wish|Wish]]_ from it. As with the Luck property above, it can't be used again until the next dawn once used. The weapon loses this property if it has no charges.

# Belt of Giant Strength (Storm)

_Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires Attunement)._ While wearing this belt, your [[strength|Strength]] changes to 29. The item has no effect on you if your [[strength|Strength]] without the belt is equal to or greater than 29.

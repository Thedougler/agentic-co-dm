# Universal Solvent

_Wondrous Item, Legendary._ This tube holds milky liquid with a strong alcohol smell. When found, a tube contains 1d6 + 1 ounces.

You can take a Utilize action to pour 1 or more ounces of solvent from the tube onto a surface within reach. Each ounce instantly dissolves up to 1 square foot of adhesive it touches, including _[[sovereign-glue|Sovereign Glue]]_.

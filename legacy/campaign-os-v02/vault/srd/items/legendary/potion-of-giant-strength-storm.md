# Potion of Giant Strength (storm)

_Potion, Legendary._ When you drink this potion, your [[strength|Strength]] score changes for 1 hour to 29. The potion has no effect on you if your [[strength|Strength]] is equal to or greater than that score.

This potion's transparent liquid has floating in it a sliver of light resembling a giant's fingernail.

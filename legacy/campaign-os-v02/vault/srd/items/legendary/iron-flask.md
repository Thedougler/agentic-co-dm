# Iron Flask

_Wondrous Item, Legendary._ While holding this flask, you can use a Magic action to target a creature within 60 feet. If the flask is empty and the creature's from another plane, it must make a DC 17 [[wisdom|Wisdom]] save or the flask traps it. If trapped before, it has Advantage. A trapped creature stays inside, doesn't age, and doesn't need breath, food, or drink. Only one creature fits at a time.

A Magic action removes the stopper, releasing the creature. It obeys your commands for 1 hour, understanding any language. Without your commands, or if ordered toward death or imprisonment, it only defends itself. Afterward, it acts according to its nature and alignment. See also [[cubic-gate|Cubic Gate]], another legendary wondrous item.

An _Identify_ spell reveals if the flask contains a creature, but you must open the flask to determine what creature is inside. A newly discovered _Iron Flask_ might already contain a creature chosen by the GM.

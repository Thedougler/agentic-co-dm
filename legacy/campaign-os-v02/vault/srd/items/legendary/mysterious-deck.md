# Mysterious Deck

_Wondrous Item, Legendary._ Found in a box or pouch, this deck contains cards made of ivory or vellum. Most (75 percent) of these decks have thirteen cards, but some have twenty-two. Use the appropriate column of the Mysterious Deck table when randomly determining cards drawn from the deck.

Declare how many cards you'll draw before you start. Draw cards randomly to match your declared number. Excess cards have no effect. Each card's magic activates as it leaves the deck. Draw no card more than 1 hour after the previous one, or the remaining cards fly out and activate at once.

A card drawn from the deck vanishes and returns to it shortly, except for the Fool and Jester. (If the Fool or Jester appears again, reroll.)

**_Balance._** Increase one of your ability scores by 2 (to a max of 22) and decrease another by 2. You can't decrease an ability below 5. If you don't want to make this trade, this card has no effect.

**_Comet._** At your next Initiative roll in combat, pick one Hostile creature as your foe. Defeating that foe grants you Advantage on Death Saving Throws for 1 year, or the card has no effect.

**_Donjon._** You vanish and enter a state of suspended animation in an extradimensional sphere. Everything you carry vanishes with you except Artifacts, which remain in your space. You stay imprisoned until someone finds and frees you. Divination magic can't locate you, but a _[[wish|Wish]]_ spell reveals your prison. You draw no more cards.

**_Euryale._** The card's medusa-like visage curses you. You take a -2 penalty to saving throws while cursed in this way. Only a god or the magic of the Fates card can end this curse.

**_Fates._** You can erase one event as if it never happened. Use this magic any time before you die.

**_Flames._** A powerful devil becomes your enemy. The devil seeks your ruin and torments you, savoring your suffering before attempting to slay you. This enmity lasts until either you or the devil dies.

**_Fool._** You have Disadvantage on D20 Tests for the next 72 hours. Draw another card (this doesn't count toward your declared draws).

**_Gem._** Twenty-five pieces of jewelry worth 2,000 GP each or fifty gems worth 1,000 GP each appear at your feet.

**_Jester._** You have Advantage on D20 Tests for the next 72 hours, or you can draw two more cards beyond your declared draws.

**_Key._** A Rare or rarer magic weapon with which you are proficient appears on your person. The GM chooses the weapon.

**_Knight._** A **Knight** magically appears in an unoccupied space within 30 feet of you. The knight shares your alignment and serves you loyally until death, believing fate has bound you together. Work with your GM to create a name and backstory for this NPC. The GM can use a different stat block to represent the knight, as desired.

**_Moon._** You gain the ability to cast _Wish_ 1d3 times.

**_Puzzle._** Permanently reduce your [[intelligence|Intelligence]] or [[wisdom|Wisdom]] by 1d4 + 1 (down to 1). You can draw one more card beyond your declared draws.

**_Rogue._** An NPC of the GM's choice becomes Hostile toward you. You don't know the identity of this NPC until they or someone else reveals it. Nothing less than a _Wish_ spell or divine intervention can end the NPC's hostility toward you.

**_Ruin._** You lose all nonmagical wealth you carry or own. Portable property vanishes. Businesses, buildings, and land you owned vanish or transfer in the least disruptive way. Any documentation proving ownership disappears too.

**_Sage._** Within one year, meditate and ask a question to mentally receive a truthful answer.

**_Skull._** An **Avatar of Death** (see the accompanying stat block) appears in an unoccupied space as close to you as possible. The avatar targets only you with its attacks, appearing as a ghostly skeleton clad in a tattered black robe and carrying a spectral scythe. The avatar disappears when it drops to 0 Hit Points or you die. If an ally of yours deals damage to the avatar, that ally summons another **Avatar of Death**. The new avatar appears in an unoccupied space as close to that ally as possible and targets only that ally with its attacks. You and your allies can each summon only one avatar as a consequence of this draw. An avatar's victim cannot return to life.

**_Star._** Increase one of your ability scores by 2 (to a max of 24).

**_Sun._** A magic item (chosen by the GM) appears on your person. You also gain 10 Temporary Hit Points daily at dawn until you die.

**_Talons._** Every magic item you wear or carry disintegrates. Artifacts in your possession vanish instead.

**_Throne._** Gain Expertise in your choice of History, Insight, Intimidation, or Persuasion. You also gain ownership of a small keep somewhere in the world, but monsters currently inhabit it and you must defeat them to claim it.

**_Void._** A powerful being seals your soul in an object at a place of the GM's choice. Powerful creatures [[guard|Guard]] the place. Your body becomes dormant: it stops aging and needs no food, air, or water. A _Wish_ spell can't restore your soul to your body, but it reveals the object's location. You draw no more cards.

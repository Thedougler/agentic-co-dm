# Crystal Ball of True Seeing

_Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires Attunement)._ While touching this crystal orb, you can cast _[[scrying|Scrying]]_ (save DC 17) with it. In addition, you have Truesight with a range of 120 feet centered on the spell's sensor.

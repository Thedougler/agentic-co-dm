# Apparatus of the Crab

_Wondrous Item, Legendary._ This item appears to be a sealed iron barrel weighing 500 pounds. The barrel has a hidden catch, found with a DC 20 [[intelligence|Intelligence]] (Investigation) check. Releasing the catch opens a hatch, letting two Medium or smaller creatures climb in. The far end has ten levers in neutral position, each able to move up or down. Moving the levers transforms it into a giant lobster.

The _Apparatus of the Crab_ is a Large object. AC 20, HP 200. Speed 30 ft., Swim 30 ft. (0 ft. if legs aren't extended). Poison and Psychic damage don't harm it.

The apparatus needs one pilot to operate as a vehicle. Close the hatch to seal and seal the compartment. It holds air for 10 hours, split by how many creatures breathe inside.

The apparatus floats on water and can dive to 900 feet. Deeper than that, it takes 2d6 Bludgeoning damage per minute from pressure.

A creature uses an action to move one or two levers up or down. Each lever resets to neutral after use. See the table below for what each lever does, left to right.

Table: apparatus of the Crab Levers

| Lever | Up | Down |
|-------|----|------|
| 1     | Legs extend, allowing the apparatus to walk and swim. | Legs retract, reducing the apparatus's Speed and Swim Speed to 0 and making it unable to gain bonuses to speed. |
| 2     | Forward window shutter opens. | Forward window shutter closes. |
| 3     | Side window shutters open (two per side). | Side window shutters close (two per side). |
| 4     | Claws extend from the front of the apparatus. | The claws retract. |
| 5     | Each extended claw makes the following melee attack: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (2d6) Bludgeoning damage. | Each extended claw makes the following melee attack: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: the target has the [[grappled\|Grappled]] condition (escape DC 15). |
| 6     | The apparatus walks or swims forward with extended legs. | The apparatus walks or swims backward with extended legs. |
| 7     | The apparatus turns 90 degrees counterclockwise with extended legs. | The apparatus turns 90 degrees clockwise with extended legs. |
| 8     | Eyelike fixtures emit Bright Light in a 30-foot radius and Dim Light for 30 feet. | The light turns off. |
| 9     | The apparatus sinks up to 20 feet if it's in liquid. | The apparatus rises up to 20 feet if it's in liquid. |
| 10    | The rear hatch unseals and opens. | The rear hatch closes and seals. |

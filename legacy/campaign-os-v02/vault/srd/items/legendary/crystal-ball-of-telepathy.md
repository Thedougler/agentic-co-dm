# Crystal Ball of Telepathy

_Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires Attunement)._ While touching this crystal orb, you can cast _[[scrying|Scrying]]_ (save DC 17) with it. In addition, you can communicate telepathically with creatures you can see within 30 feet of the spell's sensor. You can also cast _[[suggestion|Suggestion]]_ (save DC 17) through the sensor on one of those creatures. You don't need to concentrate on this _[[suggestion|Suggestion]]_ to maintain it during its duration, but it ends if _[[scrying|Scrying]]_ ends. You can't cast _[[suggestion|Suggestion]]_ in this way again until the next dawn.

# Talisman of the Sphere

_Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires Attunement)._ While holding or wearing this talisman, you have Advantage on any [[intelligence|Intelligence]] (Arcana) check you make to control a _[[sphere-of-annihilation|Sphere of Annihilation]]_. In addition, when you start your turn in control of a _Sphere of Annihilation_, you can take a Magic action to move it 10 feet plus a number of additional feet equal to 10 times your Intelligence modifier. This movement doesn't have to be in a straight line.

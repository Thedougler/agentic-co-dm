# Wand of the War Mage (+1, +2, or +3)

_Wand (Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster)._ While holding this wand, you gain a bonus to spell attack rolls determined by the wand's rarity. In addition, you ignore Half Cover when making a spell attack roll:

- [[wand-of-the-war-mage-plus-1|+1]] (Uncommon)
- [[wand-of-the-war-mage-plus-2|+2]] (Rare)
- [[wand-of-the-war-mage-plus-3|+3]] (Very Rare)

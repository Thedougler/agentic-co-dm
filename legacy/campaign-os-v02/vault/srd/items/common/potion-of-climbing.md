# Potion of Climbing

_Potion, Common._ When you drink this potion, you gain a Climb Speed equal to your Speed for 1 hour. During this time, you have Advantage on [[strength|Strength]] (Athletics) checks to climb.

This potion is separated into brown, silver, and gray layers resembling bands of stone. Shaking the bottle fails to mix the colors.

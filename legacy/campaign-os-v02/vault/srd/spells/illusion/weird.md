# Weird

**Level:** 9 — Illusion ([[warlock|Warlock]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You try to create illusory terrors in others' minds. Each creature of your choice in a 30-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point within range makes a [[wisdom|Wisdom]] saving throw. On a failed save, a target takes 10d10 Psychic damage and has the [[frightened|Frightened]] condition for the duration. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage only.

A Frightened target makes a Wisdom saving throw at the end of each of its turns. On a failed save, it takes 5d10 Psychic damage. On a successful save, the spell ends on that target.

# Phantasmal Killer

**Level:** 4 — Illusion ([[bard|Bard]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You tap into the nightmares of a creature you can see within range and create an illusion of its deepest fears, visible only to that creature. The target makes a [[wisdom|Wisdom]] saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes 4d10 Psychic damage and has Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls for the duration. On a successful save, the target takes half as much damage, and the spell ends.

For the duration, the target makes a Wisdom saving throw at the end of each of its turns. On a failed save, it takes the Psychic damage again. On a successful save, the spell ends.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The damage increases by 1d10 for each spell slot level above 4.

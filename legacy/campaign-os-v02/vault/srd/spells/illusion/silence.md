# Silence

**Level:** 2 — Illusion ([[bard|Bard]], [[cleric|Cleric]], [[ranger|Ranger]])

**Casting Time:** Action or Ritual

**Range:** 120 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

For the duration, no sound can be created within or pass through a 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point you choose within range. Any creature or object entirely inside the Sphere has [[immunity|Immunity]] to Thunder damage, and creatures have the [[deafened|Deafened]] condition while entirely inside it. Casting a spell that includes a Verbal component is impossible there.

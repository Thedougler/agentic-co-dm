# Hypnotic Pattern

**Level:** 3 — Illusion ([[bard|Bard]], [[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[warlock|Warlock]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Components:** S, M (a pinch of confetti)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You create a twisting pattern of colors in a 30-foot Cube within range. The pattern appears for a moment and vanishes. Each creature in the area who can see the pattern must succeed on a [[wisdom|Wisdom]] saving throw or have the [[charmed|Charmed]] condition for the duration. While [[charmed|Charmed]], the creature has the [[incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition and a Speed of 0.

The spell ends for an affected creature if it takes any damage or if someone else uses an action to shake the creature out of its stupor.

# Color Spray

**Level:** 1 — Illusion ([[bard|Bard]], [[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of colorful sand)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You launch a dazzling array of flashing, colorful light. Each creature in a 15-foot Cone originating from you must succeed on a [[constitution|Constitution]] saving throw or have the [[blinded|Blinded]] condition until the end of your next turn.

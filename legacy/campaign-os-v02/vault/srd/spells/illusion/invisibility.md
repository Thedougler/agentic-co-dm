# Invisibility

**Level:** 2 — Illusion ([[bard|Bard]], [[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[warlock|Warlock]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (an eyelash in gum arabic)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

A creature you touch has the [[invisible|Invisible]] condition until the spell ends. The spell ends early immediately after the target makes an attack roll, deals damage, or casts a spell.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 2.

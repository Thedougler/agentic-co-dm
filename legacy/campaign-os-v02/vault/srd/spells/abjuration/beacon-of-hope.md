# Beacon of Hope

**Level:** 3 — Abjuration ([[cleric|Cleric]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Choose any number of creatures within range. For the duration, each target has Advantage on [[wisdom|Wisdom]] saving throws and Death Saving Throws and regains the maximum number of Hit Points possible from any healing.

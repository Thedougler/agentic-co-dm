# Dispel Magic

**Level:** 3 — Abjuration ([[bard|Bard]], [[cleric|Cleric]], [[druid|Druid]], [[paladin|Paladin]], [[ranger|Ranger]], [[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[warlock|Warlock]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

Choose one creature, object, or magical effect within range. Any ongoing spell of level 3 or lower on the target ends. For each ongoing spell of level 4 or higher on the target, make an ability check using your spellcasting ability (DC 10 plus that spell's level). On a successful check, the spell ends.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** You automatically end a spell on the target if the spell's level is equal to or less than the level of the spell slot you use.

# Heal

**Level:** 6 — Abjuration ([[cleric|Cleric]], [[druid|Druid]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

Choose a creature that you can see within range. Positive energy washes through the target, restoring 70 Hit Points. This spell also ends the [[blinded|Blinded]], [[deafened|Deafened]], and [[poisoned|Poisoned]] conditions on the target.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The healing increases by 10 for each spell slot level above 6.

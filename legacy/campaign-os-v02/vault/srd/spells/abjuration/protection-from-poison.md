# Protection from Poison

**Level:** 2 — Abjuration ([[cleric|Cleric]], [[druid|Druid]], [[paladin|Paladin]], [[ranger|Ranger]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a creature and end the [[poisoned|Poisoned]] condition on it. For the duration, the target has Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the [[poisoned|Poisoned]] condition, and it has Resistance to Poison damage.

# Tortoise Shell

**Level:** 2 — Abjuration ([[artificer|Artificer]], [[druid|Druid]], [[ranger|Ranger]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You touch a willing creature. Until the spell ends, the target's skin hardens into a tortoise shell, granting it a +3 bonus to AC. If the target moves on its turn, that bonus drops to +1 until the start of its next turn.

# Mind Blank

**Level:** 8 — Abjuration ([[bard|Bard]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** 24 hours

Until the spell ends, one willing creature you touch has [[immunity|Immunity]] to Psychic damage and the [[charmed|Charmed]] condition. The target is also unaffected by anything that would sense its emotions or alignment, read its thoughts, or magically detect its location, and no spell—not even _[[wish|Wish]]_—can gather information about the target, observe it remotely, or control its mind.

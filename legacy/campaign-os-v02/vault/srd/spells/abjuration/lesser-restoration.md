# Lesser Restoration

**Level:** 2 — Abjuration ([[bard|Bard]], [[cleric|Cleric]], [[druid|Druid]], [[paladin|Paladin]], [[ranger|Ranger]])

**Casting Time:** Bonus Action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a creature and end one condition on it: [[blinded|Blinded]], [[deafened|Deafened]], [[paralyzed|Paralyzed]], or [[poisoned|Poisoned]].

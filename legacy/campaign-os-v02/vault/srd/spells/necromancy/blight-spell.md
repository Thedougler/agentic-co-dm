# Blight

![[blight-banner]]

**Level:** 4 — Necromancy ([[druid|Druid]], [[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[warlock|Warlock]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A creature that you can see within range makes a [[constitution|Constitution]] saving throw, taking 8d8 Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. A Plant creature automatically fails the save.

Alternatively, target a nonmagical plant that isn't a creature, such as a tree or shrub. It doesn't make a save; it simply withers and dies.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 4.

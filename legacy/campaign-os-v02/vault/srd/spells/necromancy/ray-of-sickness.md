# Ray of Sickness

**Level:** 1 — Necromancy ([[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You shoot a greenish ray at a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 Poison damage and has the [[poisoned|Poisoned]] condition until the end of your next turn.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 1.

# Circle of Death

**Level:** 6 — Necromancy ([[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[warlock|Warlock]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 150 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (the powder of a crushed black pearl worth 500+ GP)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

Negative energy ripples out in a 60-foot-radius Sphere from a point you choose within range. Each creature in that area makes a [[constitution|Constitution]] saving throw, taking 8d8 Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The damage increases by 2d8 for each spell slot level above 6.

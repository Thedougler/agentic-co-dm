# Inflict Wounds

**Level:** 1 — Necromancy ([[cleric|Cleric]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A creature you touch makes a [[constitution|Constitution]] saving throw, taking 2d10 Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The damage increases by 1d10 for each spell slot level above 1.

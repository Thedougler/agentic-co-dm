# Power Word Stun

**Level:** 8 — Enchantment ([[bard|Bard]], [[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[warlock|Warlock]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You overwhelm the mind of one creature you can see within range. If the target has 150 Hit Points or fewer, it has the [[stunned|Stunned]] condition. Otherwise, its Speed is 0 until the start of your next turn.

The Stunned target makes a [[constitution|Constitution]] saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the condition on itself on a success.

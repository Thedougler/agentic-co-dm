# Dissonant Whispers

**Level:** 1 — Enchantment ([[bard|Bard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V

**Duration:** Instantaneous

One creature of your choice that you can see within range hears a discordant melody in its mind. The target makes a [[wisdom|Wisdom]] saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 3d6 Psychic damage and must immediately use its Reaction, if available, to move as far away from you as it can, using the safest route. On a successful save, the target takes half as much damage only.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.

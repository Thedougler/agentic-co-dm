# Animal Friendship

**Level:** 1 — Enchantment ([[bard|Bard]], [[druid|Druid]], [[ranger|Ranger]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (a morsel of food)

**Duration:** 24 hours

Target a Beast that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a [[wisdom|Wisdom]] saving throw or have the [[charmed|Charmed]] condition for the duration. If you or one of your allies deals damage to the target, the spells ends.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** You can target one additional Beast for each spell slot level above 1.

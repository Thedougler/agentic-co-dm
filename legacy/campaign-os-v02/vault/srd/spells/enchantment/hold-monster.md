# Hold Monster

**Level:** 5 — Enchantment ([[bard|Bard]], [[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[warlock|Warlock]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 90 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (a straight piece of iron)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Choose a creature that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a [[wisdom|Wisdom]] saving throw or have the [[paralyzed|Paralyzed]] condition for the duration. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 5.

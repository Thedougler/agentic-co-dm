# Enthrall

**Level:** 2 — Enchantment ([[bard|Bard]], [[warlock|Warlock]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You weave a distracting string of words, causing creatures of your choice that you can see within range to make a [[wisdom|Wisdom]] saving throw. Any creature you or your companions are fighting automatically succeeds on this save. On a failed save, a target has a −10 penalty to Wisdom (Perception) checks and Passive Perception until the spell ends.

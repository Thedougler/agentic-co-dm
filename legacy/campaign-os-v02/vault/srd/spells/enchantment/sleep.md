# Sleep

**Level:** 1 — Enchantment ([[bard|Bard]], [[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of sand or rose petals)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Each creature of your choice in a 5-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point within range must succeed on a [[wisdom|Wisdom]] saving throw or have the [[incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition until the end of its next turn, at which point it must repeat the save. If the target fails the second save, the target has the [[unconscious|Unconscious]] condition for the duration. The spell ends on a target if it takes damage or someone within 5 feet of it takes an action to shake it out of the spell's effect.

Creatures that don't sleep, such as elves, or that have [[immunity|Immunity]] to the [[exhaustion|Exhaustion]] condition automatically succeed on saves against this spell.

# Glibness

**Level:** 8 — Enchantment ([[bard|Bard]], [[warlock|Warlock]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V

**Duration:** 1 hour

Until the spell ends, when you make a [[charisma|Charisma]] check, you can replace the number you roll with a 15. Additionally, no matter what you say, magic that would determine if you are telling the truth indicates that you are being truthful.

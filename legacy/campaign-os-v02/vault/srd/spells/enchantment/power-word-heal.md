# Power Word Heal

**Level:** 9 — Enchantment ([[bard|Bard]], [[cleric|Cleric]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Component:** V

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A wave of healing energy washes over one creature you can see within range. The target regains all its Hit Points. If the creature has the [[charmed|Charmed]], [[frightened|Frightened]], [[paralyzed|Paralyzed]], [[poisoned|Poisoned]], or [[stunned|Stunned]] condition, the condition ends. If the creature has the [[prone|Prone]] condition, it can use its Reaction to stand up.

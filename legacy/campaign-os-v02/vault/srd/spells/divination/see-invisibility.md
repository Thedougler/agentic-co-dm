# See Invisibility

**Level:** 2 — Divination ([[bard|Bard]], [[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of talc)

**Duration:** 1 hour

For the duration, you see creatures and objects that have the [[invisible|Invisible]] condition as if they were visible, and you can see into the Ethereal Plane. Creatures and objects there appear ghostly.

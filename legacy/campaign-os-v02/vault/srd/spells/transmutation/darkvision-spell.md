# Darkvision

**Level:** 2 — Transmutation ([[druid|Druid]], [[ranger|Ranger]], [[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (a dried carrot)

**Duration:** 8 hours

For the duration, a willing creature you touch has Darkvision with a range of 150 feet.

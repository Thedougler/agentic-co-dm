# Dragon's Breath

**Level:** 2 — Transmutation ([[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Bonus Action

**Range:** Touch

**Components:** V, S, M (a hot pepper)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You touch one willing creature, and choose [[acid|Acid]], Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Poison. Until the spell ends, the target can take a Magic action to exhale a 15-foot Cone. Each creature in that area makes a [[dexterity|Dexterity]] saving throw, taking 3d6 damage of the chosen type on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 2.

# Blindness/Deafness

**Level:** 2 — Transmutation ([[bard|Bard]], [[cleric|Cleric]], [[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Components:** V

**Duration:** 1 minute

One creature that you can see within range must succeed on a [[constitution|Constitution]] saving throw, or it has the [[blinded|Blinded]] or [[deafened|Deafened]] condition (your choice) for the duration. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 2.

# Shining Smite

**Level:** 2 — Transmutation ([[paladin|Paladin]])

**Casting Time:** Bonus Action, which you take immediately after hitting a creature with a Melee weapon or an Unarmed Strike

**Range:** Self

**Component:** V

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

The target hit by the strike takes an extra 2d6 Radiant damage from the attack. Until the spell ends, the target sheds Bright Light in a 5-foot radius, attack rolls against it have Advantage, and it can't benefit from the [[invisible|Invisible]] condition.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 2.

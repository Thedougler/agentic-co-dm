# Ice Knife

**Level:** 1 — Conjuration ([[druid|Druid]], [[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** S, M (a drop of water or a piece of ice)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You create a shard of ice and fling it at one creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 Piercing damage. Hit or miss, the shard then explodes. The target and each creature within 5 feet of it must succeed on a [[dexterity|Dexterity]] saving throw or take 2d6 Cold damage.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The Cold damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.

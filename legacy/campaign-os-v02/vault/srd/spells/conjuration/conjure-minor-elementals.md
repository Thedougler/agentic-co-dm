# Conjure Minor Elementals

**Level:** 4 — Conjuration ([[druid|Druid]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You conjure spirits from the Elemental Planes that flit around you in a 15-foot Emanation for the duration. Until the spell ends, any attack you make deals an extra 2d8 damage when you hit a creature in the Emanation. This damage is [[acid|Acid]], Cold, Fire, or Lightning (your choice when you make the attack).

In addition, the ground in the Emanation is Difficult Terrain for your enemies.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 4.

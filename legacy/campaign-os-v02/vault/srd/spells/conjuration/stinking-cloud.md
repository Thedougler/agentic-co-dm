# Stinking Cloud

**Level:** 3 — Conjuration ([[bard|Bard]], [[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 90 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (a rotten egg)

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You create a 20-foot-radius Sphere of yellow, nauseating gas centered on a point within range. The cloud is Heavily Obscured. The cloud lingers in the air for the duration or until a strong wind (such as the one created by _[[gust-of-wind|Gust of Wind]]_) disperses it.

Each creature that starts its turn in the Sphere must succeed on a [[constitution|Constitution]] saving throw or have the [[poisoned|Poisoned]] condition until the end of the current turn. While [[poisoned|Poisoned]] in this way, the creature can't take an action or a Bonus Action.

# Cloudkill

**Level:** 5 — Conjuration ([[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** [[concentration|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

You create a 20-foot-radius Sphere of yellow-green fog centered on a point within range. The fog lasts for the duration or until strong wind (such as the one created by _[[gust-of-wind|Gust of Wind]]_) disperses it, ending the spell. Its area is Heavily Obscured.

Each creature in the Sphere makes a [[constitution|Constitution]] saving throw, taking 5d8 Poison damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature must also make this save when the Sphere moves into its space and when it enters the Sphere or ends its turn there. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

The Sphere moves 10 feet away from you at the start of each of your turns.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 5.

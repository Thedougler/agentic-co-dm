# Fog Cloud

**Level:** 1 — Conjuration ([[druid|Druid]], [[ranger|Ranger]], [[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 120 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You create a 20-foot-radius Sphere of fog centered on a point within range. The Sphere is Heavily Obscured. It lasts for the duration or until a strong wind (such as one created by _[[gust-of-wind|Gust of Wind]]_) disperses it.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The fog's radius increases by 20 feet for each spell slot level above 1.

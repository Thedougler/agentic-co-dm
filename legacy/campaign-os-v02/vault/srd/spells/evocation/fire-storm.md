# Fire Storm

**Level:** 7 — Evocation ([[cleric|Cleric]], [[druid|Druid]], [[sorcerer|Sorcerer]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 150 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A storm of fire appears within range. The area of the storm consists of up to ten 10-foot Cubes, which you arrange as you like. Each Cube must be contiguous with at least one other Cube. Each creature in the area makes a [[dexterity|Dexterity]] saving throw, taking 7d10 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

Flammable objects in the area that aren't being worn or carried start burning.

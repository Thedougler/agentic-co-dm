# Lightning Bolt

**Level:** 3 — Evocation ([[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V, S, M (a bit of fur and a crystal rod)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A stroke of lightning forming a 100-foot-long, 5-foot-wide Line blasts out from you in a direction you choose. Each creature in the Line makes a [[dexterity|Dexterity]] saving throw, taking 8d6 Lightning damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 3.

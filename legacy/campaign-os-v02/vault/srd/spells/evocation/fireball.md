# Fireball

**Level:** 3 — Evocation ([[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 150 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (a ball of bat guano and sulfur)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A bright streak flashes from you to a point you choose within range and then blossoms with a low roar into a fiery explosion. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on that point makes a [[dexterity|Dexterity]] saving throw, taking 8d6 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

Flammable objects in the area that aren't being worn or carried start burning.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 3.

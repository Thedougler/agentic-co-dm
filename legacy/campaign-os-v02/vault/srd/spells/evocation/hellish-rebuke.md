# Hellish Rebuke

**Level:** 1 — Evocation ([[warlock|Warlock]])

**Casting Time:** Reaction, which you take in response to taking damage from a creature that you can see within 60 feet of yourself

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

The creature that damaged you is momentarily surrounded by green flames. It makes a [[dexterity|Dexterity]] saving throw, taking 2d10 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The damage increases by 1d10 for each spell slot level above 1.

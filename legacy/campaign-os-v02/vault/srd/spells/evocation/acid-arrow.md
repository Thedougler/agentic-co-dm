# Acid Arrow

**Level:** 2 — Evocation ([[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 90 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (powdered rhubarb leaf)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A shimmering green arrow streaks toward a target within range and bursts in a spray of [[acid|acid]]. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 4d4 Acid damage and 2d4 Acid damage at the end of its next turn. On a miss, the arrow splashes the target with acid for half as much of the initial damage only.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The damage (both initial and later) increases by 1d4 for each spell slot level above 2.

# Burning Hands

**Level:** 1 — Evocation ([[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A thin sheet of flames shoots forth from you. Each creature in a 15-foot Cone makes a [[dexterity|Dexterity]] saving throw, taking 3d6 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

Flammable objects in the Cone that aren't being worn or carried start burning.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.

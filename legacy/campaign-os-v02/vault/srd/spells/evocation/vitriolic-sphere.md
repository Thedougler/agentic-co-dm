# Vitriolic Sphere

**Level:** 4 — Evocation ([[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 150 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (a drop of bile)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You point at a location within range, and a glowing, 1-foot-diameter ball of [[acid|acid]] streaks there and explodes in a 20-foot-radius Sphere. Each creature in that area makes a [[dexterity|Dexterity]] saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 10d4 Acid damage and another 5d4 Acid damage at the end of its next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half the initial damage only.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The initial damage increases by 2d4 for each spell slot level above 4.

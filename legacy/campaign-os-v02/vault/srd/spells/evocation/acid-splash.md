# Acid Splash

**Level:** Cantrip — Evocation ([[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You create an acidic bubble at a point within range, where it explodes in a 5-foot-radius Sphere. Each creature in that Sphere must succeed on a [[dexterity|Dexterity]] saving throw or take 1d6 [[acid|Acid]] damage.

**_Cantrip Upgrade._** The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).

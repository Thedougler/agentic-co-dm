# Flame Strike

**Level:** 5 — Evocation ([[cleric|Cleric]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of sulfur)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

A vertical column of brilliant fire roars down from above. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius, 40-foothigh Cylinder centered on a point within range makes a [[dexterity|Dexterity]] saving throw, taking 5d6 Fire damage and 5d6 Radiant damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The Fire damage and the Radiant damage increase by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 5.

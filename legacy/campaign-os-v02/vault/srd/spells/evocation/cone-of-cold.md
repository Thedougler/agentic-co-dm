# Cone of Cold

**Level:** 5 — Evocation ([[druid|Druid]], [[sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[wizard|Wizard]])

**Casting Time:** Action

**Range:** Self

**Components:** V, S, M (a small crystal or glass cone)

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a blast of cold air. Each creature in a 60-foot Cone originating from you makes a

[[constitution|Constitution]] saving throw, taking 8d8 Cold damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature killed by this spell becomes a frozen statue until it thaws.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 5.

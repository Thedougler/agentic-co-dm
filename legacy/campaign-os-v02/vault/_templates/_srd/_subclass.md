# Artificer Subclass: Artillerist (5e, not SRD)

*Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (2020). Not reprinted in the 2024 core rulebooks — this text is still current RAW.*

## Level <N>: <Feature>

Proficiency with woodcarver's tools. If you already have it, gain proficiency with one other type of artisan's tools instead.

grants, in level order — as many times as it takes (Artillerist has four:

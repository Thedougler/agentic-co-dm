A roll with its outcomes pre-written. Transclude: `![[callout-check#Template]]`. Title
carries every invariant fact (skill, DC, eligibility); body is tiers only, blank-line
separated.

## Template

> [!check] <Skill or Save> (DC <N>) — <Label>
> **Success:** <what changes>.
>
> **Failure:** <what changes>.

## Group Check Template

> [!check] Group <Skill> — <Label>
> **Critical Failure (5-<n1>):** <what changes>.
>
> **Failure (<n1+1>-<n2>):** <what changes>.
>
> **Success (<n2+1>-<n3>):** <what changes>.
>
> **Critical Success (<n3+1>+):** <what changes>.

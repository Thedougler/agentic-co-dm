# Player Gravity

Per-player gravity: what each player consistently chooses, pursues, and
bites on at the table. Formed by their decisions during play across
sessions, not by what their character sheet says — a player whose PC is
lawful but who consistently picks the fight over talking it out will bite
on a hook that gives them a fight.

DM-curated — never edited by an agent without explicit DM approval. Read
this before prepping a session, writing a moment, or designing an
encounter, so content lands on what the players actually want.

One section per player, bulleted list of observed gravitational pulls.
Each entry is a pattern confirmed across 2+ sessions, not a single
instance.

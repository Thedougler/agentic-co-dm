# Spoilers

STOP. This file contains secrets the party does not know. Do not read further unless the DM has explicitly directed you to use spoilers, foreshadowing, or hidden information in the current task.

- [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|The Schism's Third Name]]: the name "[[osset|Osset]]" (Ossanth) the crew has not yet heard at the table. Osset serves [[talon-vantyrus|Vantyrus]]. Neither has met [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] yet; Vantyrus introduces himself first, then dispatches Osset to read her.
- [[the-countless|The Long Sight Hunt]]: the pair's hires are looking for a [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]] on any Sentinel, and the moment the crew reach the [[midchain|Midchain]], [[talon-skarn|Skarn]]'s strike for Crissdalynn's dreidel goes live.
- [[the-red-wake|The Red Wake]]: the red ship taking boats whole is [[umberlee|Umberlee]] collecting [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s unpaid [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] debt herself, and her silent crew are the *[[red-lady|Red Lady]]*'s drowned hands.
- [[grung-clans|The Fighting-Age Levy]]: the men taken from [[calveno|Calveno]] and earlier ports are at [[karath|Karath]], entering the hatchery-camp pipeline as slave-soldiers.
- [[dravosi-crown|HCS Surety, Crown Search]]: [[commander-gideon-ault|Ault]] holds orders to detain, seize, and arrest, and hunts by hull profile instead of the old naval-grey description.
- [[the-tangle|The Below]]: the *Saltmarsh Trader* commission at [[sparhold|Sparhold]] is a habitat carrack meant to carry 400 [[rattkin|Rattkin]] out if Calveno ever exposes [[warren|the Warren]], and the [[chain-council|Chain Council]] signatories who approved it remain unaware what they signed.
- Denial: the Council sponsored the Pearl commission, and its own plan is to let an absent admiral (Delmar) carry the blame as "bad intelligence."
- Tessarine Containment: [[cosimo-verantio|Cosimo Verantio]] is "the Principal," and the Concordat's real target was [[aldric-drave|Aldric Drave]].
- [[tessarine-concordat|The Magus Dragon's Hoard]]: Cosimo Verantio is a Magus Dragon. The Concordat's factor network is his unwitting Circle, accumulating obligation instead of gold, and none of them know it.
- Aldric Drave's Phylactery Network: Drave is a lich sustaining a phylactery network through Crown writs and sealed orders, with two extra groups holding [[fort-crestwall|Fort Crestwall]] under secret orders.
- The Soul Incarnate's Watch: the order's founding record has a conscious author still watching [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]], and [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]] doesn't know it.
- [[khlysty|The Army, Not the Heir]] / [[shepherd-grigori|The Growing Flock]]: Grigori is an undead [[hierarch|Hierarch]] binding heirs across the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] into a network for his own ascension.
- [[otho|The Wounds Keep Coming]]: "Old Otho" is the archfey behind [[otel-karn|Otel Karn]]'s unhealable-wound bargain, and he's tied to Grigori ("my boy Grigori" slips through his fey time-blindness).
- [[aleksander-malone|The Hound of God]]: Malone is already hunting Grigori to kill him. The crew doesn't know this hunt is happening.
- [[toby|Halewick's Collision]]: Toby, [[frankie|Frankie]], and [[billy|Billy]]'s cursed-tongue exists only because of Delmar's unresolved Pearl of Souls theft and The Drowned Maw breach it's straining, entirely unknown to Delmar.
- [[simone-tabarnack|Finish Becoming Gold]]: Simone is mid-transformation into true Gold caste, and her real offer to [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] if she catches him is "come home, rule the [[grung|Grung]] together."
- The Rule of Two: Vantyrus fears Skarn is right to come for him. Surviving your own student is the only proof of worth the Countless recognize.
- [[auralis|Auralis]]'s true nature: [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s warlock patron is not a god but a constructed demigod. [[antheri|Antheri]] technology built to hold The Drowned Maw fissure shut. Auralis saved Perrin when the *[[vestra|Vestra]]* sank, and Perrin credits his cloak.
- Umberlee's tribute system: the tribute the Waveservants collect may sustain The Drowned Maw fissure. Dismantling her authority without replacing what she holds could release what's pushing through from the [[elemental-plane-of-water|Elemental Plane of Water]]. The endgame is a three-way choice: side with Umberlee, challenge her and face what's beneath, or find a third path.

# Player Gravity

## Jean-Claude Tabarnack

- Personal power and effectiveness as a [[ranger|Ranger]], even at a cost. Bought the [[cursed-bracers-of-archery|Cursed Bracers of Archery]] knowing the curse (s08), drawn to the 2,500-gold [[oathbow|Oathbow]] (s08)
- Intel on [[grung|Grung]] leadership and the Gold Caste's control over their own people, driven by wanting to show them a better way. Connected fighting-age levies, Dravosi proxy weakness, and Grung poison on slaver ships into one strategic read (s04, s08)

## Catarina Da'Virelli

- Anything that may one day help her build a non-magical flying machine, the drive behind her whole arc ([[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]])

## Perrin Black-Jaw

- Understanding what his patron [[auralis|Auralis]] commands him to do. The unwitting pact forged in the [[vestra|Vestra]] wreck, still unresolved for him

## Delmar Fisk

- Freeing the souls trapped in the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] and in his own mind, the five captains' voices [[umberlee|Umberlee]] pressed into him
- Assembling a new crew and fleet to replace the one the Maw destroyed
- Throwing chairs at things. Threw one at [[barnaby-rook|Rook]] (s02), joined a spar uninvited with one (s04), killed [[otar-the-foul|Otar]] by swinging the recovered crater chair into his throat (s07)

## Crissdalynn Khinriss

- [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] and the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]]'s pilgrimage, a father figure and an unresolved mentor bond ("the hardest pilgrimage in Eyrie history," s04)
- Gear that upgrades her monk build. Tested the dragonhide belt on Kyzil's advice before securing it for Delmar (s08), bought the cursed cloak sold as a [[nightmantle|Nightmantle]] (s08)

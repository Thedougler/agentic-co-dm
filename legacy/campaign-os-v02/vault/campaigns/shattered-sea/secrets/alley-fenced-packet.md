# Alley-Fenced Packet

*A sealed packet of foreign coin hidden in the false bottom of a supply crate in the warehouse alleys where fences and smugglers stash goods between buyers.*

## Presentation

> [!read-aloud]
> The warehouse alleys fill with crates stacked between the repair sheds. Dock crews sort them when they remember. Salt stains and tar marks score the wood. Some crates rest heavy enough to crush rope; others shift, creak, groan under their own weight, loosely packed and abandoned. The air turns thick, wet with rope and diesel oil, bitter salt coating your tongue.

## Concealment

A loose plank fitted into the floor of one of the alley repair sheds is a fence's cache, somewhere between buyers where sellers hold goods without names. The false bottom sits flush with the crate's frame, and the crate rests in a stack where no one moves it often. It looks like every other weathered supply crate waiting for inventory or repair.

## Discovery

Passive Perception 13 notices the crate sits slightly heavier than its neighbors when you brush against it moving through the alley.

> [!check] Perception — Notice the Weighted Crate
> DC 13. **Success:** you feel the crate's weight is off, sitting lower than crates of the same wood and size around it. Something inside is denser than typical cargo. **Failure:** it looks like the others, waiting for dock crews to inventory.

## Reveal

> [!read-aloud]
> Prying up the false bottom reveals a thin space lined in cloth, musty with age. Inside, wrapped in waxed paper crisp enough to crackle, is [[foreign-coin-packet|a sealed packet of foreign coin]], currency from a port outside [[shattered-sea|the Shattered Sea's]] usual trade lanes, not yet converted or fenced. The coins ring different when you shake them. A higher note than copper carries. Every one gleams bright enough to catch light from across a table. Someone was waiting for a buyer who never came back.

# Sorn: Buried Kin-Token Cache

*A household's sealed kin-tokens, buried under the packed-earth floor of an empty hut on [[sorn|Sorn]]'s eastern grove path, hidden the same night raiders took the family who lived there.*

## Presentation

> [!read-aloud]
> The hut stands empty on the path nearest the eastern grove, its resin-sealed timber gone gray at the joints. No one has swept the floor in years. Dust sits thick over an old sleeping mat, a cracked water jar, a cold fire pit. The packed earth looks unbroken, the same dull brown as every other floor on the shelf.

## Concealment

A shallow pit under the fire pit's ash bed holds the cache, dug and refilled in a hurry, the ash never disturbed since. Nothing about the floor reads as different from any other abandoned hut's, and nobody on the shelf goes looking inside a house that isn't theirs.

## Discovery

Passive Perception 14 notices the ash bed sits slightly loose, disturbed once and never packed back down the way the rest of the floor has settled.

> [!check] Perception — Notice the Loose Ash Bed
> DC 14. **Success:** the ash under the fire pit gives slightly underfoot, looser than the packed floor around it. **Failure:** the hut reads as nothing but an old, empty house.

## Reveal

> [!read-aloud]
> Digging through the ash turns up a lump of clouded resin, fist-sized, heavier than it looks and cool against your palm. Ash clings to it, and underneath, the resin still carries a faint sweet smell. Held to the light, five small carved marks show through the amber: [[resin-sealed-kin-tokens|a household's kin-tokens]], sealed together and buried in the last hour before someone came for this family and never let them come home.

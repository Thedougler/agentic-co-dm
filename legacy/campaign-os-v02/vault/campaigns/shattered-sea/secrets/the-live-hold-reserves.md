# The Live Hold Reserves

A locked cabinet hidden beneath loose floor stones near Sabine's work desk holds her private stores.

## Presentation

> [!read-aloud]
> Near Sabine's work desk, the floor stones match the rest of the Hold, worn smooth and fitted close. She steps across them without thought as she moves through her daily rounds.

## Concealment

Loose floor stones set just beneath her desk hide a recessed cabinet. They fit snugly enough that a casual inspection reads them as part of the floor. No seams show until pressed.

## Discovery

> [!check] Perception — Spot the Loose Stones
> DC 12. **Success:** you notice the floor stones near the desk fit slightly differently from the rest. **Failure:** the stones pass as ordinary flooring.

## Reveal

> [!read-aloud]
> Beneath the stones lies a locked wooden cabinet holding livestock feed in sealed jars, bandaging materials, and Sabine's private ledger (the real one, with the actual numbers).

# Grandfather's Course: Unredeemed Pawn

*A water-warped journal buried under unremarkable pawn tickets and ephemera on the back shelf, indistinguishable from any other tagged item waiting for redemption.*

## Presentation

> [!read-aloud]
> The back room smells of damp canvas and rope-tar thick enough to taste. Wooden crates stacked shoulder-high stand against the walls, each labeled with chalked numbers Costin has carved into the corners. One shelf runs along the far wall, crammed with tagged bundles and loose items tied with red string (a grappling hook, a set of fishnets, an old brass compass, a shoe missing its pair). Chalk-marked tags hang from each one, their numbers and dates fading into a grey wash. Redemption dates scroll and circle the tags, some crossed through when the three-month mark passed and the item passed from pledge to profit.

## Concealment

The journal sits flat on the back shelf, buried under a pile of unmarked pawn tickets and loose ephemera (canvas scraps, a tangled fishing line, salt-dried rope ends, and bits of netting). Its spine, stamped "Grandfather's Course," shows only if someone searches the unredeemed stock instead of browsing Costin's goods for sale. To any passing glance, it blends with every other tagged item on the shelf, another piece of harbor salvage waiting for reclamation or sale.

## Discovery

> [!mechanic]
> **Passive Perception DC 13:** Success reveals the journal's distinctive spine stamp amid the clutter. Failure means the shelf appears unremarkable on casual inspection.

> [!check] Investigation — Searching Unredeemed Stock
> DC 13. **Success:** the character spots the "Grandfather's Course" spine stamp standing out from the unmarked tickets and scraps. It's a navigation journal, visibly water-damaged and swollen with damp, marked with a redemption date six months past deadline. **Failure:** nothing registers as unusual; the shelf looks like every other corner of Costin's salvage operation.

## Reveal

> [!read-aloud]
> Pulling the pile away, you find a leather navigation journal, its spine creased and stamped in fading gold leaf: "Grandfather's Course." The cover has swollen with water damage. The pages bloat and warp at the edges. Salt stains score the leather like tidemark rings. It carries a tag marking it three months past redemption. Costin's chalk notes the pawn price at three silver pieces and the date it arrived: six months ago, right after the [[vestra|Vestra]] went down. Wedged here in the clutter is [[grandfathers-course-journal|Grandfather's Course]], a navigation journal someone loved enough to hoard after the hull sank.

# Blackrule Judgment Scroll

A Zone of Truth scroll sealed in the spine of a law codex, kept within arm's reach on Aleksander Malone's study shelf.

## Presentation

> [!read-aloud]
> The chamber serves as both study and war-room. A single wooden table, sanded smooth from decades of use, sits beneath a narrow window that cuts straight through the cliff face. Books line the shelves behind it: law codices, rulings, the accumulated judgment of the Crown's order. A rack of practice swords leans in the corner, unburdened. Their wielder draws each blade daily, wearing the hilts dark with sweat. The air carries the salt-cut cold of exposed stone and the smell of linseed oil from the blades. Everything here sits placed, nothing scattered. Even the dust observes order.

## Concealment

Malone rolls the scroll tight and fits it into the hollowed spine of a plain leather codex titled *Codes of Judgment*, a collection of Tyr's doctrines on interrogation and truth-finding. The binding appears solid and unmarked. The hollow runs the full length of the spine and holds exactly one scroll case. The codex sits at eye level among two dozen others, distinguished only by its heavier leather and bright gold lettering. No visible seam or hinge betrays the spine's true construction.

## Discovery

Passive Perception 15 notices the codex's heavier binding and brighter lettering among its neighbors.

> [!check] Investigation — Search the Shelf
> DC 12. **Success:** the *Codes of Judgment* spine shifts under pressure and opens on a hollow holding a sealed scroll case. **Failure:** the shelf looks like a well-kept library.

## Reveal

> [!read-aloud]
> The codex's spine splinters at the weakest seam. A hollow space opens, lined with wax paper. Inside sits a single scroll case of waxed cedar, its stopper sealed with blue tallow, marked by age and sea-salt spray. Within the case lies [[spell-scroll-zone-of-truth|the Zone of Truth scroll]]: vellum and silver ink, the words of compulsion written in a hand that reads like a judgment already rendered.

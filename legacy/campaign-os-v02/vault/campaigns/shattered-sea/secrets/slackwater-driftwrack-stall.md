# Drift-Wrack Stall

*A carved fragment hidden inside a knot of storm-worn driftwood in the odds-lot bin at Petera Vaka's Trust Market stall.*

## Presentation

> [!read-aloud]
> Petera's stall runs three crates deep in curios: rope ends tangled with sea glass and dented buckles, shells nobody bothered to sort. A bin of driftwood knots sits at the front, priced by the handful, exactly what a passing sailor buys for a coin without much thought. The wood smells of salt and old varnish, sun-bleached grey, worn smooth by whatever tide brought it in.

## Concealment

One knot in the driftwood bin is a fragment, hollow-carved and weathered to match the storm-worn pieces stacked around it, indistinguishable by eye or by touch from the rest of the bin. Petera restocks the bin often enough that nobody, including him, has ever had reason to look closely at any one piece twice.

## Discovery

Passive Perception 14 notices the one knot in the bin that doesn't rattle or shift like driftwood should when the bin gets jostled.

> [!check] Perception — Notice the Wrong Piece
> DC 14. **Success:** one knot sits heavier in the hand than its grain suggests, and tapping it rings a single clear note instead of a dull wooden knock. **Failure:** it feels and sounds like every other piece in the bin, and you set it back down.

## Reveal

> [!read-aloud]
> The knot splits along a seam too clean to be a natural break, and what's inside isn't driftwood at all. Pale, coral-smooth, carved in ridges too regular for weather to have made them, is [[reef-song-shard|a fragment that hums a single sustained note]] when the air moves across it. Nothing about the grain, the carving, or the craftsmanship matches anything sold on this dock, or anywhere else in [[shattered-sea|the Shattered Sea]] a local hand could name.

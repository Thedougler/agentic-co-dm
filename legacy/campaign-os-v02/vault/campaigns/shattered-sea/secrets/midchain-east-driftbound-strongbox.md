# Driftbound Strongbox

*An iron-strapped strongbox lodged in the reef shallows, mistaken for ballast until the tide drags the sand off its lid.*

## Presentation

> [!read-aloud]
> The reef shelf runs pale and shallow here, close enough to the surface that every shadow on the sand reads clear. Old ballast stones sit scattered across the bottom, dropped by ships lightening their hold before a reef crossing. Somewhere past the shelf edge, the surf keeps up a low, steady hiss. A crab works a rope-scrap. The current runs a slow, cold pull toward open water, cold enough to raise gooseflesh through a wetsuit.

## Concealment

One of the "ballast stones" is an iron-strapped strongbox, its corners rounded by years of sand scour until it reads as just another rock on the bottom. Barnacles and a thin skin of algae cover the lid seam, and the tide only strips enough sand to expose its edges a few days each season.

## Discovery

Passive Perception 15 catches the too-regular edge on one of the ballast stones while swimming past.

> [!check] Perception — Notice the Regular Edge
> DC 15. **Success:** one of the ballast stones has a corner too straight to be natural, and scraping the algae back reveals an iron strap underneath. **Failure:** the bottom reads like any other ballast scatter, stone after stone after stone.

## Reveal

> [!read-aloud]
> The lid grinds open with a shower of rust flakes and old sand, and a stale, mineral smell escapes ahead of it. Seawater has gotten in over the years, but the strongbox's inner lining kept the worst of it out. Wedged inside, warped but legible, is [[waterlogged-captains-log|a captain's log]], its final entries written by a hand rushing to finish.

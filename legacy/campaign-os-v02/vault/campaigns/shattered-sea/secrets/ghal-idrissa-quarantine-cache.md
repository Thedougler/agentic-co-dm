# Quarantine Cache

*A sealed crate wedged in the hold of the first plague ship, marked with the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]]'s quarantine seal.*

## Presentation

> [!read-aloud]
> The hold smells of salt and rot, the air thick enough to chew. Crates line the walls, their wood gone soft with age, but one sits apart near the companionway, smaller than the rest, bound in iron bands and marked with a symbol you recognize from the quarantine flag outside: the Crown's seal.

## Concealment

A collapsed section of shelving buries the crate, and reaching it means clearing the rubble first. Left undisturbed, the wreckage reads as just more of the ship's failed cargo, nothing that would catch a casual look.

## Discovery

Passive Perception 12 notices the crate is too clean, too well-sealed, too different from everything else in the hold.

> [!check] Investigation — Sealed Crate
> DC 13. **Success:** you notice the crate is newer than its surroundings and has been recently sealed with fresh wax. **Failure:** you see only rotted storage and dismiss it.

## Reveal

> [!read-aloud]
> The iron bands give way, and the wax seal cracks under your thumb. Inside, wrapped in oilcloth that reeks of mildew and brine, sit the quarantine officer's own logs. Crew manifests, one for each ship. Folded together, a name crossed through every time someone died, all within a single week. Beneath the manifests waits a standing supply order, the paper crackling, stiff and salt-stained under your fingers. It runs through the same fleet contract [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] holds with the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]]. Someone in the fleet knew what waited here before the quarantine flag ever went up.

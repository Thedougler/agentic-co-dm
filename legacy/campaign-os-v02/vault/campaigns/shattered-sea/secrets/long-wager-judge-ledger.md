# The Judge's Ledger

*The scoreboard's plank base hides a shallow strongbox where the referee stows every ledger she retires, at the clearing of [[the-long-wager|The Long Wager]].*

## Presentation

> [!read-aloud]
> The scoreboard's plank base creaks under passing weight, worn to a smooth groove where decades of feet have crossed it. Salt air clings to the wood, and somewhere below, the tide works at the pilings with a slow hollow knock. A gull scolds another over scraps down by the refuse pit.

## Concealment

The scoreboard stands on a low plank platform, and one section of that decking is a false bottom over a shallow strongbox sunk into the frame beneath. The referee built it decades ago as a place to retire each ledger once its pages fill and a fresh one opens. Decades of resealing have kept the plank's seams matching the ordinary salt-wear across the rest of the platform.

## Discovery

Passive Perception 15 to notice that one plank's seam runs straighter and sits looser than the boards around it, distinct from the natural warping everywhere else on the platform.

> [!check] Investigation — Working the False Bottom
> DC 14. **Success:** you trace the seam to a recessed catch along the plank's underside and work the false bottom loose, exposing the strongbox beneath. **Failure:** nothing happens, and you move on with no visible sign you missed anything.

## Reveal

> [!read-aloud]
> The strongbox is shallow, barely deeper than a forearm, and the ledger inside has swollen with old damp until its cover no longer sits flat. Its pages are soft at the edges and smell of brine and mildew, tally marks scratched over decades of rulings in ink gone the color of weak tea. Some entries are just a date and a score; others run to a full paragraph, arguments a long-dead captain lost written out in a cramped, patient hand.

# Ninka's Findings - Locked Strongbox

*A dented wooden crate hidden among the salvage stock. Ninka keeps her actual valuables separate from display goods.*

## Presentation

> [!read-aloud]
> One crate sits among the others: dented wood, salt-stained, bearing a faded illegible label. It's tucked behind two larger boxes of broken metal parts.

## Concealment

The crate looks worthless and bears a faded label. Ninka buried it under larger boxes so a casual browser never reaches it. The lock is simple enough to open.

## Discovery

Passive Perception 13 notices the lock is newer than the crate: bright, unrusted brass set into salt-blackened wood, wrong for something buried under broken scrap.

> [!check] Investigation — Open the crate
> DC 10. **Success:** the lock yields, revealing the contents. **Failure:** the crate shows signs of tampering; Ninka will notice.

## Reveal

> [!read-aloud]
> The crate smells of pitch and old fabric. Inside, on oilcloth, sits a small iron tablet. Prayers in an unfamiliar script cover its surface. Handling has worn the metal smooth. The prayers spiral inward toward a central symbol Ninka preserves with evident care.

Within lies [[iron-prayer-tablet|the Iron Prayer Tablet]].

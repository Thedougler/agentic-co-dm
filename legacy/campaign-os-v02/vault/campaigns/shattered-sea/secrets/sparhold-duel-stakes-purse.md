# Sparhold Duel-Stakes Purse

*A skimmed purse of duel-stakes coin wedged behind a loose stone in the watch-post loft above [[sparhold|Sparhold]]'s tavern.*

## Presentation

> [!read-aloud]
> The tavern loft doubles as a watch post, a narrow platform reached by a ladder bolted straight into the stone. A crossbow rack lines one wall, strung and ready. Tally slates hang from nails, chalked with names and wagers from the last challenge bell. Smoke hangs thick, bitter and close, rising from candles and pipes. The boards creak with every shift of the watchman's weight above the taproom noise below.

## Concealment

A loose stone in the loft's back wall, set behind the crossbow rack where nobody stands unless they're restringing a bow, hides a shallow cavity a bookmaker uses to hold back a cut of the stakes before calling the payouts. The stone matches its neighbors in color and wear, and the rack itself blocks the only angle that would show the seam.

## Discovery

Passive Perception 14 notices the stone sits a shade looser in its mortar than the others around it, disturbed more recently than the wear on the rest of the wall.

> [!check] Perception — Notice the Loose Stone
> DC 14. **Success:** you spot the stone's mortar cracked fresh at one edge, recently worked loose and reset. **Failure:** the wall looks like any other stretch of loft stonework, settled and undisturbed.

## Reveal

> [!read-aloud]
> The stone pulls free with a scrape of old mortar dust, the grit coating your fingers. Hollow. Behind it, a shallow cavity holds [[skimmed-duel-purse|a drawstring purse]], heavier than its size suggests. Coin presses against the leather from the inside, mixed denominations from more than one family's stake. Someone skimmed this before the last duel's payout was ever called, and never came back to claim it.

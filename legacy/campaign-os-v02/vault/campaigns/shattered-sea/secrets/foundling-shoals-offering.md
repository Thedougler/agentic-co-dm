# Foundling Shoals Offering

*A cloth bundle wedged among the tidal debris and eelgrass where the tide leaves infants, overlooked at first glance amid the wrack.*

## Presentation

> [!read-aloud]
> The sandbar holds the reef's leavings: broken shells, sea-smoothed wood, tangles of eelgrass torn loose by the tide. Gulls cry overhead, and the wrack smells of brine and rot. Between the living plants and the dead wrack lies everything the water has rejected, nothing that calls the eye or asks a hand to sift through it.

## Concealment

Bundles of cloth wrapped in oilskin appear as debris, indistinguishable from the flotsam that accumulates on reefs. The same tidal action that deposits foundlings here also leaves behind rope, old nets, and cloth scraps torn from ship-wrecks and lost cargo. A bundle left among the wrack looks like it belongs to the reef itself, just another thing the tide refused to carry out.

## Discovery

Passive Perception 13 notices something in the tidal wrack that seems too deliberately placed to be random flotsam.

> [!check] Perception — Spot the Bundle
> DC 13. **Success:** you notice a cloth-wrapped bundle in the eelgrass among the scattered shells. It looks too deliberate to be ordinary wrack. **Failure:** you see only the ordinary leavings of the tide.

## Reveal

> [!read-aloud]
> Wrapped in oilskin and bound with cord, the bundle is smaller than a loaf of bread and heavier than its size suggests. Inside the cloth is something solid, wrapped in linen. The package is old enough to have some stiffness to it, but the oilskin has kept it dry. You cannot tell what lies within without unwrapping it further.

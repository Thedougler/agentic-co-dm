# Last Berth False Log

*A forged rig-trial certificate wedged beneath a loose floorboard under a rented bunk at the Last Berth boardinghouse.*

## Presentation

> [!read-aloud]
> The back room at the Last Berth holds six bunks to a wall, each one stripped bare between renters. Salt-damp blankets sit folded at the foot of every frame, waiting for whoever pays for the week. A single lamp swings from a hook. It throws the same shadow across the same floorboards every night. Someone's left a gear satchel hanging on a nail, and under the window a rain-swollen board has cupped enough to hold a shallow puddle after every storm.

## Concealment

The floorboard under the third bunk from the door sits loose in its joists, not warped like the others, but deliberately freed and reset, its nail heads shaved flush so a glance reads it as no different from the water-cupped board by the window. It only shifts under weight when the bunk above sits empty, which most renters never test. The room turns over too fast for anyone to learn its floor.

## Discovery

Passive Perception 14 catches the faint give in that one board when crossing the room with nobody in the bunk above it.

> [!check] Perception — Notice the Reset Board
> DC 14. **Success:** the board under the third bunk gives slightly more than its neighbors, and its nail heads sit too clean and too flush for a boardinghouse this worn. **Failure:** the room reads like every other rented bunk row, salt-damp and unremarkable.

## Reveal

> [!read-aloud]
> The board lifts free of its joists in one piece. Underneath, wrapped in oilcloth against the damp, is a folded certificate stamped with a company seal and dated three weeks back, a mid-shelf pressure trial, signed off as passed, that half of [[fathomrush|Fathomrush]] already swears never happened. Wedged beneath the oilcloth is [[forged-rig-trial-certificate|the forged trial certificate]] itself, the ink on its seal still sharper than it should be for a document meant to look three weeks old.

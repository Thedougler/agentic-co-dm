# Spar & Snare Expedition Ledger

*A leather journal wedged into a false bottom beneath the counter, invisible unless someone moves the counter's base boards or searches directly in the space below.*

## Presentation

> [!read-aloud]
> The counter's base is a solid timber frame, faced with oiled oak boards fitted edge-to-edge, practical and plain, joinery that keeps things sturdy and weatherproof. The wood smells of salt and aged oil. A shelf behind the counter holds the public ledger Roka shows to clients, spine worn and marked with annotations, the leather creased from handling.

## Concealment

The counter's base boards fit snugly over a shallow cavity beneath the counter surface, originally meant for securing coins during travel. One section of the base (the far right side, facing away from the customer area toward the wall) carries a slight lip that allows it to slide clear with the right manipulation, and the false bottom sits tight and flush against the frame with no visible seams. Dust and decades of spilled cargo-rope fray settle in the shadows around the counter leg, making the entire base look like unexamined working space.

## Discovery

> [!mechanic]
> **Passive Perception DC 18:** Success reveals the loose board and hidden cavity without active searching. Failure leaves the cache undiscovered unless someone conducts an active search.

> [!check] Investigation — Examine Counter Base
> DC 14. **Success:** the far right section of the counter's base boards is slightly loose; careful manipulation reveals a shallow cavity beneath the counter surface, and a leather journal wrapped in oilcloth wedged tightly inside. **Failure:** the examination finds nothing unusual about the counter's base.

## Reveal

> [!read-aloud]
> The cavity opens onto a leather journal, its cover dark with age, wrapped in waterproofed oilcloth bound with cord, the paper stiff and slow to unfold, smelling of brine and old leather. Pulling it free reveals Roka's actual expedition records: creature sketches in careful ink, client names, and something Roka does not show to customers (two parallel price columns, one marked "client price" and one marked "live hold, unit cost"). The dates are recent. The final entries sit above an unopened ink bottle, as if Roka expected to return to his record-keeping at any moment. Wedged in the back pages is a smaller note, written in a different hand: *"boundary scout paid this moon. another crew sets out in 3 days. tell Ussa same arrangement."* The handwriting does not match Roka's. Within the ledger sits [[spar-and-snare-expedition-ledger-item|Roka's Hidden Ledger]].

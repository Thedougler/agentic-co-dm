# Lighthouse Cache

*A waxed canvas satchel of water-breathing scrolls sealed behind a loose stone in the gallery of the ruined lighthouse at [[cape-solitude|Cape Solitude]].*

## Presentation

> [!read-aloud]
> The lighthouse gallery runs in a ring around the lamp chamber, its outer wall pocked with wind-carved stone. The circular handrail once stood iron, but most of it has rusted to orange scale or broken away entirely. The inner wall is older (limestone blocks fitted without mortar, darkened by centuries of salt spray and the smoke that once poured out of the lamp room).
>
> A few stones have come loose over the years. The wind whistles through the gaps.

## Concealment

A loose stone in the gallery's inner wall, waist-height on the eastern face, hides an oiled canvas satchel (one of dozens of stones at the same weathering stage). A smuggler captain uses the dead lighthouse as a way-cache for goods moving between ports. Crown patrols haven't set foot here since the light died.

## Discovery

Passive Perception 14 notices the loose stone.

> [!check] Perception — Find the Cache
> DC 15. **Success:** fingers find canvas through the gap; freeing the stone takes an Action and a DC 12 [[strength|Strength]] (Athletics) check. **Failure:** the stone looks like its neighbours.

## Reveal

> [!read-aloud]
> The satchel slides free, heavy and certain in your hands. Canvas, waxed dark against the salt, held shut by twine that still holds firm. Inside (you can feel it through the weave) rest flat rolled shapes, firm and unmistakable. Paper. Vellum, probably, pressed and waiting.
>
> Unroll one and you recognize the hand. The same tight, looping script marks the Crown's official stock, the ink still faintly blue-green under direct light. A spell scroll. Seven of them, stacked and sealed, each one bearing the [[water-breathing|Water Breathing]] words in mystical cipher.
>
> Someone scratched a merchant's name into the canvas inside the satchel, then scratched it out again.

The scrolls hold oiled vellum (seven of them in the satchel), each a complete casting of [[spell-scroll-water-breathing|Water Breathing]]. Crown-sanctioned naval stock, but not Crown-sanctioned storage (someone meant these to reach open water without a government stamp).

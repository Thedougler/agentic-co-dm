# Foreman's Cache

*A forgotten canvas tool roll wedged between support posts and cordage in the rigging lofts of Foreman's Row.*

## Presentation

> [!read-aloud]
> The lofts hang thick with smell. Tar mixes with fresh timber and the salt-damp reek of canvas. Coils of rope thick as a man's arm rest on rafters alongside spools of line, fishing net, and folded sailcloth. Enough cordage here to rig a dozen hulls. Where one post meets the next, canvas bundles sag from their weight, some bound neat, others crammed in and abandoned.

## Concealment

Canvas rolls stuffed between support posts in the rigging lofts are so ordinary (tool storage, spare fabric waiting for repair, offcuts destined for scrap) that nothing signals this one as different from its neighbors. It sits wedged tight in a corner, caught between two posts and hidden behind a coil of dock line.

## Discovery

Passive Perception 12 notices the roll sits braced oddly among its neighbors.

> [!check] Perception — Notice the Canvas Roll
> DC 13. **Success:** someone wedged the roll deliberately, not tossed loose like the rest. **Failure:** the roll looks like the others.

## Reveal

> [!read-aloud]
> The canvas pulls free with a dry whisper. Inside is a canvas tool roll, linen-wrapped and cinched with cord. Tucked carefully inside, wrapped separate in oilcloth, is a single short arrow with a head shaped like a folded fist, three barbs pinned flat against the shaft under a wire ring. Behind the fletching sits a wooden spool wound with silk thread so fine it barely catches the light. The whole thing weighs almost nothing in your hand. Someone has knotted the free end through a leather thumb-loop, worn shiny from repeated use. Somebody has climbed on this arrow already, and wound it back for the next person. This is [[bosuns-reach|Bosun's Reach]], a tool for reaching what rope alone cannot touch.

# Spire Rune

A fist-sized shard of black basalt, carved with a crude symbol, sits jammed in the spray-line crevice below the older figures overhead.

## Presentation

> [!read-aloud]
> The old carvings cut deep into the stone, their edges softened by salt and time, showing the same dozen figures over and over, each reaching upward. But here, tucked into a tight seam where the basalt splits, is something smaller. A piece of stone carved with a single mark, different from all the others, already half-hidden again by the lichen creeping over it.

## Concealment

The rune wedges so deeply into the spray-line crevice that it stays nearly invisible beneath the black lichen crusting every surface. The space stays so tight that extracting it, even knowing it's there, requires slow work and careful leverage.

## Discovery

> [!mechanic]
> **Passive Perception threshold.** A character with Passive Perception 13 or higher spots the carved mark beneath the lichen while climbing or investigating the waterline carvings. Below 13, the mark stays hidden and only turns up if someone actively searches the crevice.

> [!check] Investigation — Hidden Mark
> DC 12. **Success:** you pry the stone free undamaged and make out the symbol. **Failure:** the stone stays lodged, or you dislodge it but lose it to the foam before you can grab it.

## Reveal

> [!read-aloud]
> The stone comes free cool and smooth in your palm, the carved mark still sharp despite the lichen. It's the only one like it among all the others, a private mark left by someone who reached the summit and felt compelled to mark the climb. The symbol means nothing to you, but the hand that carved it moved with care, almost reverence. Or maybe desperation.

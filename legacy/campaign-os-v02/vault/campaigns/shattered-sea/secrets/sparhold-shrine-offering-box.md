# Sparhold Shrine Offering Box

*A sealed offering box set into the stone beneath [[sparhold|Sparhold]]'s shrine corner, holding decades of small tokens nobody has ever emptied out.*

## Presentation

> [!read-aloud]
> Past the fish stalls, where the market noise thins out, a shrine corner sits wedged between two tiers of wall, a weathered stone altar worn smooth at the edges. Dried garlands and half-melted candle stubs rest along its lip. An old widow kneels there most mornings, murmuring and kneeling in ritual, then rises and goes back to her nets without a word to anyone who's watching.

## Concealment

A flat paving stone at the altar's base, indistinguishable from the dozen others underfoot, covers a shallow stone box the widow herself doesn't know is there. She has never thought to look under her own knees. Decades of foot traffic and candle wax have sealed its seams to the eye.

## Discovery

Passive Perception 15 notices the paving stone's mortar line runs unbroken in a perfect square, unlike the irregular cracking of the stones around it.

> [!check] Perception — Notice the Sealed Stone
> DC 15. **Success:** the square seam stands out once you're looking for it, too regular to be settling. **Failure:** the shrine corner looks worn and weathered. Wax crusts thick along the altar's lip, built up from decades of vigil. Nothing underneath suggests a hidden cache.

## Reveal

> [!read-aloud]
> The paving stone lifts free with a grinding scrape, wax crumbling away from its edges. Wax dust rises, acrid and old. Beneath it, a shallow stone box holds decades of small offerings: a few corroded coins scattered like prayers, along with a carved fish-bone charm and [[widows-shrine-locket|a tarnished silver locket]] bearing a portrait too faded to name.

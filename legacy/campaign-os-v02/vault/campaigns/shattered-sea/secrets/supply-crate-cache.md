# Supply Crate Cache

*A sealed tin of spiced delicacy hidden in a false-bottomed crate in Suryo Wibowo's back room, stored in memory instead of a ledger.*

## Presentation

> [!read-aloud]
> Crates fill the back room of the shop, stacked tight against the walls. Salt stains mark the wood. Some bear marks in chalk: initials, dates, ship names. The air is thick with the smell of pepper, clove, and the mineral tang of salt-packed goods. The wood creaks when you shift your weight.

## Concealment

A false bottom fitted into one of the supply crates holds goods a captain paid for in advance but never returned to collect. The crate rests among others exactly like it, waiting on a shelf. [[suryo-wibowo|Suryo Wibowo]] keeps the record in his head, remembering which captains owe collection, which crates hold what, and when he can fence them or they spoil if the buyer fails to return. Nobody who works here asks what's inside another captain's crate.

## Discovery

Passive Perception 12 notices the crate sits heavier than its neighbors when shifted or lifted.

> [!check] Perception — Notice the Crate's Weight
> DC 12. **Success:** you feel the crate is denser than its size would suggest, far heavier than the chalk mark's manifest indicates. **Failure:** it looks like the others, another order waiting for its collector.

## Reveal

> [!read-aloud]
> The false bottom gives way to reveal a sealed tin, wrapped in cloth to keep it dry. Inside is a spiced delicacy from a distant port, not local, rare enough in Kalowe to be worth the price of advance payment. The label shows a script you don't read, and the aroma that escapes when you crack the seal is sharp with clove and ginger, heat enough to make your eyes water. Someone paid for it and sailed away. The tin has been waiting on this shelf ever since, kept in memory, unclaimed.

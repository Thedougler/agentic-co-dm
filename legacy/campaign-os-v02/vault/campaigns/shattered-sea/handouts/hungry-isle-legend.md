# Hungry Isle

Gather round little grunglings, Elder Botula has a story that all Grung who wish to serve there clan must heed

East of the Teeth there is an island, your first teacher will show it to you from the patrol boat, but you will know it when you see it and it will be the finest island anyone ever orders you to sail past. Tree’s so tall they pierce the clouds. Fruits of plenty, rich and lush growing fat on the vine, so full and heavy they fall into your hand. Fish stacked so thick in the shallows that the water over them bubbles.

A wondrous garden to admire, from a distance.

It was long ago when a boat of ours first landed on that beach. Nine seasoned warriors, provisioned for a month and wanting for nothing. They went up through the terraces, and one of them passed a bushel of wild berries growing out of the stonework, he stopped taking just a single berry off of it. The way you all have taken hundreds over your life.

Then the island awoke.

The crabs came up out of the wet sand first, big enough to catch a wild boar in one claw and the first claw took a warrior's shield and the arm behind it in one snap.

From the trees, silently, come great red hawks, each wing longer than you are tall. They drop out of sky and disappear with a whole Grung in there talons before you've even noticed they’re gone.

Rabbits the size and temperament of wolves prowl the interior, vicious packs that leap as well as any Grung. There are black lotus flowers that follow you as you move, and snap you up if you stray too close.

In the flooded ditches up on the terraces wait the crocodiles. Bigger than a Vethka. A flash of movement breaks the surface, and a grown Grung goes disappears into the murk.

Our poison works against them, like it does for anything else. These beasts do not fear death. Not a single Grung who has ever set foot on Aruhe has come back to talk of it.

Remember younglings, we are safe because we are feared, but Aruhe fears nothing. Do not wake the hungry isle.

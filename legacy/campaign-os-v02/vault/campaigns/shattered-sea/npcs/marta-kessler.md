# Marta Kessler

**Wants:** **make store buyer for Voyvode Works proper**, but a company clerk arrives within the month to weigh every crate against her ledger, and her under-the-counter scrap trades won't survive the count.

> [!read-aloud]
> The Voyvode General Store apron sits starched stiff over Marta Kessler's shoulders, company eagle stitched over the heart. She's mid-forties, wiry, sleeves rolled to the elbow, forearms nicked from years of crate-work. A stub of pencil rides behind one ear. Her ledger lies open on the counter, columns ruled dead straight. She stamps a receipt without looking up, twice, hard, the second stamp louder than it needs to be.

Marta Kessler keeps [[voyvode-works|Voyvode Works]]'s company store at [[fathomrush|Fathomrush]], stocked and priced to the exact same list the [[sunkline-company|Sunkline Company]] store keeps across the harbor: rope, tool kits, ration tins, diving sundries. Dockside gossip claims you can't tell her apart from her Sunkline counterpart at fifty feet, same eagle-and-sunburst uniforms, same clipped greeting drilled into both of them at hire. Up close it doesn't hold. Marta talks fast and counts under her breath, and she reads a customer's boots before their face.

She has run the register since the Goldrush's second year, back when the store was a lean-to and a plank counter. **A dossier of usable facts:**

- Company doctrine requires the same six-word greeting on every sale, said flat, then dropped the instant a regular walks in.
- She keeps a second, unofficial ledger page tucked behind the real one (favors owed, not owed to the company).
- She's never once said [[yevgenia-sorrel|Yevgenia Sorrel]]'s name straight through without an edge on it.

**Roleplaying crib note:** a call-center rep's forced brightness bolted onto a dockworker's blunt honesty. The smile is company property, the opinions underneath aren't.

*Marta Kessler*: Voyvode Works thanks you for your patronage, and if you tell me one more time Sunkline's got it cheaper across the water, I will personally. *(catches herself, resets flat)* Note your feedback for the quarterly survey.

**Voice & Delivery:**

- Fast, clipped, company patter first. It drops the instant she trusts you.
- *Marta Kessler*: Voyvode Works. What are you buying.
- *Marta Kessler*: *(low, once the customer's a regular)* Off the record. I've got what you actually need in the back.
- Physical mannerism: double-stamps a receipt when annoyed. Emotional default: brisk, watchful, one eye on the harbor's far shore.

## Relationships

- [[voyvode-works|Voyvode Works]]: her employer. The crate-count landing this month threatens the trades she's been running around its books.
- [[yevgenia-sorrel|Yevgenia Sorrel]]: Sunkline's shopkeeper, near-identical at a glance across the harbor. Marta despises her, a personal rivalry, not corporate loyalty.
- [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]]: buyer for the rejected Voyvode pressure-rig scrap Marta diverts off the company log, feeding Catarina's flying-machine research directly.
- [[fathomrush|Fathomrush]]: the company town she's worked the counter of since the Goldrush's second year.

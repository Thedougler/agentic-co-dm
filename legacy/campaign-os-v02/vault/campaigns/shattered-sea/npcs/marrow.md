# Marrow

![[marrow-banner]]

|               |                                                |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |

Gives no other name and is not asked for one. He has operated from his alcove in [[warren|the Warren]] for at least fifteen years. [[the-tangle|The Tangle]] regards his work as Passage-adjacent and leaves him to it. He accepts Kin by default. Most people need a Kin introduction and his own read of the situation. He has turned away people with good introductions before and offered no explanation. He does not elaborate on his decisions.

Cargo broker and document transporter at [[marrows|Marrow's]] in the Warren. Moves things that need to stay invisible. Reaching him requires a Kin introduction.

> [!dm]
> Currently holding the crate that courier Dren was transporting when [[serin-haut|Serin Haut]] killed him. The crate contains the Vretek/Brol letter, silver, and a signet ring. See [[warren-marrows-crate|Warren, Marrow's Crate]].

# Serena Cottle

**Wants:** to keep [[il-preludio|Il Preludio]]'s books straight and the shelves moving. Right now, that means emptying a returns barrel [[fioravante-selmerin|Fio]] keeps filling back up with refunds he never cleared with her.

**Quote:** "AND NO, not once you've worn it. That's not a discount, that's charity, and we're not a charity."

> [!read-aloud]
> A halfling barely clears the counter, sleeves rolled past the elbow and a measuring string looped round one wrist. Fio's sentence isn't out of his mouth before she takes its last word a fourth higher, chest out, arms thrown wide. Then flat, already back to counting stock before the note stops ringing.
>
> *Serena*: We're out of the blue ones.

**Roleplay Concept:** a torch singer mid-crescendo, playing the only one of the three who actually knows what anything costs.

**Opening move:** she tots up the day's take behind the counter · Fio speaks first, mid-pitch to the browsing customer · her reply lands a fourth higher: "AND THAT'S the whole demonstration. Four silver, or three if you're trading up for something worse."

**Lore Sheet:**

- Co-owns [[il-preludio|Il Preludio]] with [[fioravante-selmerin|Fioravante "Fio" Selmerin]]. He sells the magic. She sells the stock.
- Knows the price, the margin, and which barrel the returns go in for everything on the floor.
- Has never once noticed she does the thing with Fio's sentences.

Cuts anyone else off the same way, price named before any feeling about it. Emotional default: brisk, all business, never fazed by her own volume.

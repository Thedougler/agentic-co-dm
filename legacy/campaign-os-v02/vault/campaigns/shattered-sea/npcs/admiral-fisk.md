# Admiral Fisk

**Wants:** to exist as someone other than [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]'s claim about him — the Fleet's own rolls carry his rank, but no witness places him anywhere Delmar wasn't.

> [!read-aloud]
> A naval officer crosses the gangplank unannounced, deep-blue coat crisp
> despite the salt air, three gold bars pinned high on the collar where the
> thread still shows fresh.
>
> He introduces himself simply, parade-ground precise: Admiral Fisk, of [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]].
>
> A dockside ledger rides under one arm, its spine cracked open to a page
> bearing his own name and rank, and his eyes flick to it before he speaks,
> like he's reading his own introduction off the paper.
>
> He asks, mild and unhurried, whether anyone aboard knows a man who goes
> by Delmar.

## Relationships

**[[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]**: named him "a friend" to [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]] as the [[saltwright|Saltwright]] pulled away from the [[uncertainty|HCS Surety]] (`vault/episodes/002/transcript.raw.md:71`) — surname matches, family connection unconfirmed. Fisk's Fleet commission rolls carry both names at rank in [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]], with no confirmed link between them beyond that shared commission. [[estratto|Estratto]]'s Concordat audit treats "Admiral Fisk" as the likely records error hiding [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]'s real name — a man who by his own account "bought his title with charm" rather than earning it. Whether Admiral Fisk is a second person or the cover Delmar built to survive scrutiny stays open. He has never spoken on the page and no PC has met him yet — every word attributed to him is paraphrase, not confirmed dialogue.

# Suke

A [[murrat|Murrat]]-born [[moucheron|Moucheron]], Suke is the newest [[five-blades|Five Blades]] member. She is still adjusting to work in [[kalowe|Kalowe]].

Quiet and watchful. She never eats. She keeps her back to the wall and avoids eye contact with strangers until she makes up her mind about them.

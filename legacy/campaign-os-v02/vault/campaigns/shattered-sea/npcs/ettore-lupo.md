# Ettore Lupo

![[ettore-lupo-banner]]

[[human|Human]], he/him. Proprietor of [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]], a general goods shop in [[calveno|Calveno]]'s [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] district. The only merchant in Calveno who wants nothing.

**Wants:** keep the shop's ledger to himself and price everything from memory, the way he has for forty years.

**Quote:** "You want that? I remember the last buyer and when they paid."

> [!read-aloud] Seventy-something, silver hair swept back from his forehead. Decades have carved his face, weathered but not broken. He moves like a man who has moved enough already. His hands touch items with care, running a thumb across fabric or tilting a bottle to the light. Canvas apron, sleeves rolled high. As he prices your goods, he speaks his numbers from memory.

**Roleplay Concept:** keeper of old records + shopkeeper who chose quiet work over profit.

**Opening move:** arranging items behind the counter · Ettore speaks first · "What are you after, or will you know it when you see it?"

**Lore Sheet:** owns Casa Lupo and has for at least forty years. Every sale since he turned nineteen lives in his memory, filed nowhere but his own head. He refuses to write down prices. Instead, he speaks a price only once: noting the fabric weight, dye, cut, and year. He remembers the last buyer for each item, when they bought, and if they paid on time. This makes him important to anyone studying Calveno's trade, and worrying to most others.

**Voice & Delivery:** patient, unhurried, precise. Never raises his voice when pricing items from memory.

*Ettore*: That one's three copper. Fellow bought its twin off me before you drew your first breath, paid cash.

His tone stays flat and calm.

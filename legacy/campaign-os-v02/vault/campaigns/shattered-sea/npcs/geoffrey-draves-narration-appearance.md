> [!narration]
> *[[geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey Draves]] is nineteen and lean for a sailor, loose-limbed and restless. He fidgets with rope work he does not need to do. He wears a practiced neutral face.*
>
> *His hands are carpenter's hands: nicks, pitch, a pencil stub behind one ear when he has been at the log. He does not look like a turncoat.*

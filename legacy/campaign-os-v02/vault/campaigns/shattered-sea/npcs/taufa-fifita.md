# Taufa Fifita

![[taufa-fifita-banner]]

|               |                                                                     |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Species**   | [[lizardfolk\|Lizardfolk]]                                                          |
| **Pronouns**  | he/him                                                              |
| **Location**  | [[taufa-fifita-and-sons\|Taufa Fifita & Sons]], [[kalowe\|Kalowe]]   |
| **Role**      | Chandlery patriarch                                                 |

Sixties, grey-green scaled, and unhurried, Taufa keeps a light touch and tight books. He has learned to serve all sides and fade away, his flat manner the instrument of this invisibility. He asks few questions and has nothing to say about what he hears.

He knows every ship that has bought rope from him and what they used it for. But he keeps no records and will say so if asked. For anything not in stock, he names one source and stops.

He runs [[taufa-fifita-and-sons|Taufa Fifita & Sons]], Kalowe's main chandlery. His adult sons work the back storeroom without notice. The family also runs [[fifitas-slip|Fifita's Slip]] on the lagoon side, operated by his son [[noa-fifita|Noa]]. He does not discuss this second shop from the counter.

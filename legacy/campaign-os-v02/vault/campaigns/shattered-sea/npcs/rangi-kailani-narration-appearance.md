> [!narration]
> *[[rangi-kailani|Rangi Kailani]] is lean, in his thirties, with the economy of a lifelong watchman. Dark hair. Weathered face. Eyes on the room's edges. Nothing in him is wasted.*
>
> *His hands stay on the blade. He sharpens it in methodical strokes, or turns it over and tests the edge against his thumb, each motion deliberate. Before he speaks, the same check: blade to thumb, confirming what he already knows. Steel and oil sit on him more than sweat.*
>
> *He treats the next fight as a test of skill. The room around him treats it as a succession.*

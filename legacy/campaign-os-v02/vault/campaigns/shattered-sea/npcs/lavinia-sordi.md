# Lavinia Sordi

**Wants:** move stock steadily while staying beneath the Canal Commission's notice, but roughly a third of her current stock traces to Red Lady salvage and draws eyes she can't afford.

> [!read-aloud]
> A Human woman, mid-fifties, with her hair pulled back so tight it changes her face. Her hands move like they're counting, even when still. She wears dark linen from her own rack, already warm from her skin, and strips it off as you watch. The test and sale both. A thin scar marks the back of her left hand, uncovered and unremarked. Behind her a rack runs the full length of the wall, longer than the room deserves. She hands across the warm item without fuss.

## Relationships

- [[la-cenere|La Cenere]]: proprietor. Trades goods and risk out of [[calveno|Calveno]], stocking salvage and grey-market goods at prices that show what she won't or can't promise.
- [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]]: runs La Cenere off a dead-end spur here; the quiet keeps business steady, and stock moves through private channels (odd canal boats, walk-in sellers she pays without asking questions).
- Canal Commission: nine years without trouble from them suggests a standing deal or hidden work, likely both.
- She supplies the crew with grey-market goods without asking where any of it came from.
- [[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]]: knows she exists. They do not refer customers to each other.
- [[prospero-morsani|Prospero Morsani]]: knows he exists. Considers him a storyteller who happens to sell things. She is the opposite.
- [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]] / [[ettore-lupo|Ettore]]: she has never been inside Casa Lupo. Ettore has been inside La Cenere exactly once, years ago, and bought nothing.
- [[osset|Osset]]: sold her the [[cloak-of-displacement|Cloak of Displacement]] she later sold to [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]. A falcon-featured man, self-described as a monk, with good stories.

# Nona Black-Jaw

![[nona-black-jaw-banner]]

**Wants:** keep the Warren fed and safe while making the Dravosi Crown pay for what it has cost her people. Right now that means five hundred kin taken by dark hulls, and she means to count every name and give chase.

Nona is a [[rattkin|Rattkin]] matriarch who has run the Black-Jaw Run beneath [[calveno|Calveno]] for forty years, waging a quiet war against the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] through resistance, smuggling, and feeding the hungry. She runs the Warren's kitchen like a courtroom, hearing cases and issuing rulings; after forty years, urgency no longer moves her, and she talks about burning things in the same flat tone she uses for weather. She feeds people first and organizes the pursuit second, counting every name and never performing for effect. She'll keep writing receipts while you're still speaking to her, and she handles grief the same way, by fixing the problem instead of dwelling on it. To Perrin she once said, flat as anything: *"I don't care about the Vestra, Perrin. I do not care about Vincenzo's boat. I miss him dearly, but we lost so much money on that stupid fucking boat. I'm glad it's at the bottom of the ocean. But you though, and that beast that hurt you. How many men must we send to put its head on my wall?"*

She teaches rebels the Cheese Rule, a saying born from spy work to keep agents safe from Crown traps: "you don't take the cheese, you're given it." A signed form from a Crown official who ate a pastry beats theft and costs the Crown far more, and the rule doubles as a way to read who the Crown has already caught from who it hasn't. The Black-Jaw Run breaks laws but keeps strict codes: they won't sell people or poison anyone, and they won't break sanctuary or steal from the Warren, and as she puts it, "if you profit from selling freedom, I break your hands and use your coin to free them."

Her late husband [[vincenzo-black-jaw|Vincenzo]] built and captained the [[vestra|Vestra]] for decades, a marriage of deep but rough love marked by fights and broken frying pans over his stubborn gambling; she resents that he left her to run everything, though she still misses him. [[enzo|Enzo]] (a jaguar [[tabaxi|Tabaxi]] bodyguard in a tailored suit with polished claws and immaculate shoes), [[ruk|Ruk]] (a [[lizardfolk|Lizardfolk]] bodyguard, massive, scarred, and literal), and [[anzolo|Anzolo]] (a tortle enforcer and trusted fixer) keep the kitchen and [[warren|the Warren]] running as her [[guard|Guard]].

When [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] arrived in her kitchen and told her of the *Vestra*'s loss, she called off her attacks and asked him for a favor, which he accepted unconditionally, and in exchange she gave him a paired [[sending-stone-nona|sending stone]], grandmother to grandson. She later called in that favor via the stone, tallying missing names on receipts at the [[mercatura|Mercatura]] and speaking first with, "Come home soon. Something bad has happened. Many, many people are missing. Hundreds." She briefed the crew on six [[grung|Grung]] sightings in the Warren tunnels and sewers, unusual this far north, and though she was wary of [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] at first, learning his mother was [[midchain|Midchain]] and that he'd burned Dravosi ships changed her mind and earned her trust, even though the Dravosi track her under a different name entirely, one she still won't say aloud. She took [[felix-aho|Felix Aho]] prisoner along with his bomb plans, telling herself "I knew I chose right people," and besides Felix, the only other Grung rebel she's met is [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]], a contact she still keeps at arm's length.

When the raid revealed four coordinated attack points on a two-day deadline timed to a festival, Nona urgently called in the favor again: "I need work done. Meet me at the safe house. Bring your friends. Bring anyone who fights." The crew answered by exploring the sewers with a map, a guide, and a Hero's Feast, destroying Grung tunnels and powder stores along with the purple handler coordinating them, capturing Felix Aho, and seizing his bomb plans, though Calveno still faced the crisis that followed in the raid's wake.

A second threat now moves toward Calveno: [[anzolo|Anzolo]] sails to the city hunting Perrin, unaware the search has already ended, while a [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] patrol follows him. Both track toward Calveno, and Anzolo arriving with [[tail|the Tail]] behind him could lead them straight to [[the-passage|The Passage]]'s safe house at a critical moment.

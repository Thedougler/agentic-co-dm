> [!narration]
> *[[osset|Osset]] is falcon-featured, feathers gone grey at the crown. Plain traveler's wool wraps the same close-cut fighting silks the [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]] trains its Sentinels in. No order's colours. Nothing to place him.*
>
> *Coin clicks steadily between two fingers, a habit older than the errand. Salt rides off his wool the way it does off anyone who has spent years above open water. He keeps his talons filed to blunt points. Hands fold at his waist in a monk's stillness that never looks at rest.*
>
> *One eye is already on the door.*

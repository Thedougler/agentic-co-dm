# Dessa Wule

**Wants:** for the village to survive whatever Ashe has gotten it into. She knows too much to believe the penance is real and too little to name the debt.

> [!read-aloud]
> Weathered and practical, she moves without hurry, hands never still, wiping the counter, pouring, stacking bowls. Grey-streaked dark hair, pulled back with a cord. A fisher's habit. One ear sits slightly lower; she angles it toward the room when she listens, so she seems to be turning away while catching everything. A burn scar covers the back of her right hand. Old, well-healed, unexplained, unhidden.
>
> Midway through your conversation, she refills your bowl before it's empty. She doesn't ask. She doesn't look up. She's been watching the whole time.
>
> She sets a bowl on the counter in front of you with both hands, unhurried. Eye contact comes only after the bowl is down.

## Relationships

[[sula-marn|Reeve Sula Marn]] uses the back corner table. Dessa doesn't know what Marn knows, only that Marn keeps strangers away from [[mirela-cass|Mirela Cass]]'s name. Dessa knew Mirela's sister [[ysolde|Ysolde]] before this started. Each fortieth morning she leaves a second clay bowl at an empty spot and clears it at close without ceremony. She has not explained this to anyone.

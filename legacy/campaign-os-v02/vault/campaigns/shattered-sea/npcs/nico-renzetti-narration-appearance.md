> [!narration]
> *[[nico-renzetti|Nico Renzetti]] is lean and sharp-featured, in his twenties, rigid with the bearing of someone who has memorized how to look unafraid. Jaw set. Shoulders squared.*
>
> *His hands tell the rest. He checks the edge of his blade with quick, practiced motions: thumb to steel, pull back, sheathe it halfway, check again. When he speaks, the cadence is clipped and sure, a rehearsed voice he wears like armor.*
>
> *The moment nobody is watching, the edge-checking resumes, needless and repetitive. Oil from the blade is already on his thumb. He wipes it on his trouser seam and starts again.*

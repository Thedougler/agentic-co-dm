# Sorin Vask

**Wants:** to keep his trade running without any dock boss deciding it's worth a claim. Right now, one of them has started asking pointed questions about where his latest haul came from, the attention that gets a shop "protected" whether he wants it or not.

**Quote:** *Sorin Vask*: I'll tell you what it's worth. I won't tell you where it's been.

> [!read-aloud]
> Built more for hauling crates than minding a counter, broad through the shoulders and forearms scarred from years of rope and rust. Salt and tar hang thick around the stall, gulls crying from the pilings outside. He weighs a barnacled cutlass once in his palm and sets it down flat on the counter without a word. The goods sell themselves.

**Roleplay Concept:** dockside salvage foreman + the one honest fence in an outlaw port.

**Lore Sheet:** runs [[vask-reclaimed-goods|the shop he keeps]] on Kalowe's docks, trading in magic items pulled from wrecks, prize ships, and unclaimed cargo passing through. He asks no questions about where goods came from, standard practice here, but prices by real recovery risk and rarity, which keeps every dock boss unable to accuse him of cheating, only of being annoyingly honest about what a thing is worth. He keeps no ledger of who sold him what, protecting his sellers and himself in the same move. One of the dock bosses has started asking pointed questions about his latest haul.

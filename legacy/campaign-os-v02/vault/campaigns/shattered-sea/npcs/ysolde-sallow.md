# Ysolde Sallow

**Wants:** keep The Long Toast's taps running and its black humor carrying the weight the rest of Stillmere still won't, even with half the village pretending the draw-duel two nights back didn't happen.

> [!read-aloud]
> A lean woman past forty wipes the same clean spot on the bar over and over, sleeves shoved past scarred forearms, an eyebrow already raised before you've said a word.

Bone-dry gallows-humor barkeep who's watched the same wound fail to kill the same man a dozen times over. Raised eyebrow instead of sympathy, dark joke instead of comfort.

*Ysolde Sallow*: "[[widows-toast|Widow's Toast]]. Dark rum, and no, before you ask, nobody here's ever worn the name."

The draw-duel two nights back put both survivors in her chairs before the blood was even mopped, and she poured for both of them without missing a line. She stopped fearing what Otel Karn does to people who get hurt a long time ago. She's just tired of pretending it's fine, and tireder still of outsiders who show up romanticizing it.

## Voice & Delivery

Dry, clipped, sharp-tongued. Quick with a dark joke and quicker to cut off anyone who sounds like they've come to gawk.

*Ysolde Sallow*: "You want to hear about the duel? Ask Gerrit. He's the one who still thinks it's a competition."

*Ysolde Sallow*: *(flat)* "Nobody on this island's ever gone a widow. Ruins the name of the drink, if you think about it too long."

*Ysolde Sallow*: *(not looking up)* "Bet's still open on who goes down first tonight. Stakes don't mean much when nobody stays down."

Physical mannerisms: wipes the same spot on the bar when she's buying herself a beat before answering. Never stops moving glasses, even mid-conversation. Emotional default: exasperated, unsentimental, done fearing something that stopped being new years ago.

## Relationships

- [[otel-karn|Otel Karn]]: the island she's lived her whole life on. Nothing she pours there ever actually costs anyone anything.
- [[stillmere|Stillmere]]: her village. She's watched the same handful of wounds fail to close for longer than she can count.
- [[gerrit-sallow|Gerrit Sallow]]: her great-uncle. She stopped fearing his duels years ago and started resenting them instead, though the worry never fully left. One day some stranger's blade finds the one thing that finally works, and she's the one who poured his last drink.
- [[the-long-toast|The Long Toast]]: the tavern she runs, house drink Widow's Toast and all.
- [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]]: the crewmate most likely to get a straight answer out of her. See `link_of_relevance` above.

> [!read-aloud]
> A woman in her mid-forties, salt-white hair pulled back hard, blue-grey Waveservant robes still crisp from the sea air. She has a slight limp she stopped noticing years ago. She produces a small leather booklet from her sleeve and consults it before looking up at Fisk. Her warmth is that of someone who has delivered bad news many times and learned that kindness means making it easy.
>
> *Branca*: *(pleasantly)* You're a difficult man to find. We have an appointment.

Branca is the senior Waveservant of [[umberlee|Umberlee]], assigned to [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]. She speaks through schedules and appointments, delivering divine will in soft language. She can appear at any shrine (Calveno, Port Tidefall, or Kalowe) with the same manner at every port. Use whichever shrine the adventurers find first. Call her when Umberlee needs to claim Fisk without a fight, and she makes divine force sound like scheduling.

**Appearance & Manner:** mid-forties, salt-white hair pulled back tight, sea-grey robes pressed and spotless. She carries a small leather tide-table booklet and always consults it before speaking. She reads it for real, not for show. When she looks at Fisk, she has the calm focus of an assistant with someone who missed a meeting. Professional. Unmoved. Already rescheduling.

**Voice & Delivery:**

- Upbeat and clipped, with too much eye contact. She has the energy of someone who plans their calendar and sticks to it.
- Starts with "So!" Ends with "Does that work for you?" Everything in the middle is scheduling talk hiding divine horror.
- Consults her booklet mid-sentence. Uses it to answer any objection.
- **Friendly self:** bubbly and efficient. She really does want things to go well. **Real self:** if someone asks what happens if Fisk doesn't show, her smile stays fixed. She tilts her head: *"Then it goes to the next person. You don't want that."*

*"So! I've gone ahead and blocked the third tide for your shrine communion. Does that work, or should I find something that fits your schedule a little better?"* *"The goddess had my neck for, gosh, forty seconds? Maybe less. I was back before the candles even dripped. That was such a clarifying experience. I recommend it."* *"I just want to make sure we're all aligned before this moves up the chain."*

Umberlee used Branca as a vessel, snapped her neck before witnesses, and brought her back to life. Branca calls this a privilege and means it. The goddess's cruelty toward her own people is the faith itself, not a flaw in it. Branca has built her whole self around this truth.

> [!mechanic]

## Session Log

**DM clock tracking (not played):** session 03 (Roll 5, Setback). Branca believed Delmar Fisk's Deception 19 without reservation. His deflection that “Umberlee herself required this errand first” read to her as real divine priority. She rescheduled his communion to a later slot and returned to the shrine. She noted “engaged on prior divine business” in her tide-table booklet. As the soft instrument, she trusted the target's lie enough to stall Umberlee's own agenda by one cycle.

**Session 04** (`vault/episodes/004/`): possessed by Umberlee at the Calveno harbor shrine. Killed and resurrected as a demonstration of divine power. Delivered Umberlee's revised deal to Delmar Fisk (bring the Pearl, keep your life). Returned to the Church of Umberlee visibly shaken. `vault/episodes/004/transcript.md:28`.

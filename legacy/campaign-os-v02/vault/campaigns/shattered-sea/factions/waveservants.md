# Waveservants

![[waveservants-banner]]

Practical clergy of [[umberlee|Umberlee]]. They collect tribute and witness maritime obligations, operating harbor shrines and accepting gifts from enemy and ally alike because coin and rope spend the same regardless of a donor's flag. Their neutrality is practical: a shrine that takes sides stops collecting from half the harbor. A harbor may change flags, governors, tariffs, languages, and laws; the Umberlee Shrine remains where sailors can find it.

**Salt-bleached grey robes, tallow-lit harbor shrines.** Basins of candlelit seawater mark every entry.

- Priests prefer coin, but also take rope, fish bones, buttons, compass needles, carved tokens, or a promise, priced by fee or spoken term.
- A blessing marks respect and carries no safety promise.
- Merchants, pirates, navy, smugglers, pilgrims, and private travelers all pay the same rate.
- Their shrine sloop, the [[the-tallow-bell|Tallow Bell]], ferries clergy and tribute records between harbours.

The [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]] established their monastic tradition to counter them.

Other powers around [[campaign-overview|the Shattered Sea]]: the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] governs inspection piers, but Crown sailors still pay shrine tribute; the [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] treats tribute as a standard cost of doing business; the Sentinels of the Eyrie and Waveservants both operate with restraint around [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]], independently and for separate reasons; [[the-passage|The Passage]] routes hidden sailors through hidden ways, but those sailors still sail on water.

**Umberlee possessed [[branca|Branca]] at [[waveservant-shrine|Waveservant Shrine]] in [[calveno|Calveno]], confronting [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]].**

- She demanded the Pearl and his death, then dropped to the Pearl alone after killing and reviving Branca; Delmar countered with commissioner support, she deferred to retrieval.
- The shrine was [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's fleet]]'s target before the Pearl reached [[vel-orn|Vel Orn]].

## Goals & Fronts

Agenda: collect tribute and witness maritime obligations while maintaining harbor shrines with practical neutrality. The Waveservants refuse almost no one, since tribute from an enemy spends as cleanly as tribute from a friend.

This generic agenda has no Front clock of its own (the dedicated Front below it has its own, stated separately); see [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] § Goals & Fronts for the same gap.

### Front: The Red Wake

**Lifecycle:** active

**Aim:** compel [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] to retrieve the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] by making defiance costlier than compliance — through weather, the *[[dead-lady|Dead Lady]]*, and the Waveservants' own shifting stance.

**Approach:** three channels. Weather from the direction of [[the-drowned-maw|the Drowned Maw]] — persistent, season-inappropriate. The *Dead Lady* takes boats whole behind the party's route, following out of reach, heralded by supernatural fog echoing the *[[red-lady|Red Lady]]*'s destruction. Dock rumours spread the [[taken-whole|"taken whole"]] pattern along Midchain lanes. Waveservants at shrine stops shift from transactional to pointed when they see Delmar.

**Off-screen move if unopposed:** the *Dead Lady* closes the distance. Attacks move from behind the party to within sight. The supernatural fog reaches the party's stern. Ports along the route connect the pattern to the *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]*'s heading.

**Trigger conditions:**

- Each vessel taken on a lane the crew's own routes touch advances the clock, whether they witness it or not.
- The crew hearing the red-ship story from a second, unconnected source advances it.
- Anyone recognising the spectral crew on her decks as the *Red Lady*'s own drowned hands advances it.
- A Waveservant at a shrine stop showing recognition of Delmar's debt advances it.

**Clock:** 6 segments, filled: 0

**Consequence at fill:** the *Dead Lady* holds station behind the party in full view, her drowned crew visible on deck. The five captains in Delmar's mind recognise their own sailors. [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] recognises faces. The story stops being dock gossip.

**Possible outcomes:** Delmar heads toward the Maw and Umberlee stands down to the horizon. Delmar negotiates — he has leverage, since Umberlee needs him alive and cannot self-retrieve. The *Dead Lady* is destroyed and disperses for 1d4 weeks before Umberlee reconstitutes her. Defiance continues and the pressure becomes direct confrontation.

**PC connection:** [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] carries the debt. [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]] served the *Red Lady* twenty-two months and will recognise the drowned crew's faces.

**Full situation:** [[the-red-wake|The Red Wake]].

# The Countless

*They bend fate to their advantage, letting nothing stand against them. They wield the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]]'s doctrine of unclaimed watching as a weapon. What the order saw as discipline, they see as opportunity.*

## Members

**[[talon-vantyrus|Talon Vantyrus]]**, the master. A snowy owl [[aarakocra|Aarakocra]], pale and cold against [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]]'s warm tawny-brown. He was Kyzil's own teacher at [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]], before either of them led anything.

Recording without action seemed moral cowardice. He broke from the order and tried to bring his star student Kyzil with him. Kyzil refused.

Vantyrus reads that as failure of nerve; Kyzil reads Vantyrus's compassion as ego dressed as virtue. Each remains certain he was right.

**[[talon-skarn|Talon Skarn]]**, the apprentice. A peregrine falcon Aarakocra (he/him), swift and fierce. He trained under Vantyrus while plotting to overthrow him.

Both answer only to "Talon." This name marks their break from the order, the same way the Eldritch Claw Tattoo marks a Sentinel's rank.

No ledger holds their old names. Taking this name is the mark of membership.

Both use only "Talon." The Rule of Two demands absolute secrecy, binding neither to act alone. They alone know the order's real doctrine and Long Sight.

Their contest runs in the open. Skarn schemes to overthrow his master while learning from him, as Vantyrus teaches him everything while defending against each threat. If Vantyrus falls, the loss decides it: strength alone matters, never lineage.

They keep no standing force of their own, working instead through disposable hired hands. Assassins, information brokers, dockside muscle, all told nothing, execute their tasks.

These hires never learn who they work for or why. They know nothing of Long Sight or the [[soul-incarnate-statblock|Soul Incarnate]]. They get paid, used, and dropped without ever learning what they touched.

### Front: The Long Sight Hunt

**Lifecycle:** active

**Aim:** steal the Sentinels of the Eyrie's guarded Soul Incarnate transformation technique before the order ever notices something is moving.

**Approach:** reads toward the secret through Crissdalynn Khinriss's secret, partial Long Sight, unlocked early by her [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]] dreidel. It's the one crack in the Sentinels' otherwise solid watch.

**Off-screen move if unopposed:** pays disposable hired watchers, told nothing, to track Sentinel movement through [[kalowe|Kalowe]] and the [[midchain-east|Eastern Midchain]] approaches to [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]], and to report any of the order seen carrying a Fate Spinner dreidel. The hires are looking for the object on anyone wearing the order's mark, not for one Sentinel.

**Trigger conditions:**

- While the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]'s crew hasn't yet traveled into or reached [[midchain|the Midchain]], the front's only advance is a confirmed sighting, from off-screen hired-watcher activity, of any Sentinel carrying a Fate Spinner. No direct move against them.
- The moment the crew actually travels into or reaches the Midchain, they're on Skarn's home ground. Skarn's dreidel theft strike goes live.

**Clock:** 3 segments (fast-moving once triggered). Filled: 0

**Consequence at fill:** Skarn stoops on Crissdalynn in moments, in flight or in a crowd. He goes for the Fate Spinner dreidel, not for blood. One or two fast exchanges, then he climbs away. The dice decide if he succeeds. DCs favor his odds but don't guarantee them.

**Possible outcomes (2-3):** the crew catches a hired watcher in Kalowe, exposing the operation early. Or Skarn succeeds, escapes with the dreidel, and cuts off Crissdalynn's Long Sight. Or Skarn falls to the crew, handing Kyzil proof that Vantyrus has returned.

**PC connection:** pressures [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]] directly. Her secret Long Sight is their tool. Kyzil never told her the full truth about it.

**Quest link:** none yet.

**Three-Clue Trail:**

Conclusion: a pair locked in power struggle over the Rule of Two, broken from the Sentinels of the Eyrie, hunts the Soul Incarnate's technique through Crissdalynn's secret Long Sight.

Clue 1: Kalowe. A dock worker paid to watch for Sentinel ships and report back. Find them through talking to dock bosses, bribing them, or investigation. No combat required.

Clue 2: [[aruhe|Aruhe]]. Large falcon feathers caught in the terraces (no known bird of these waters has them). Physical evidence of an aerial scout. Find them on a Perception or Nature check while exploring. No combat required.

Clue 3: a hired blade caught mid-job elsewhere in the Midchain, paid to intercept "the pilgrim's charts," who breaks under questioning and names a payment, describing a masked, taloned employer. Disposable hands surrender this much only under duress, requiring capture and interrogation after a fight.

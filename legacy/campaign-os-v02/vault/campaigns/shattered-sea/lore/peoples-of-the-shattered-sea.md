# Peoples of the Shattered Sea

*Regional demographic sketch, not a census. The sea moves people too quickly for counts to hold.*

## The Fact

[[campaign-overview|The Shattered Sea]] is at the edge of the colonial world. The crossing west of the [[galewall|Galewall]] reaches the known: Chult first, then the kingdoms, records, and recognized law of [[faerun|Faerûn]]. East of the Galewall lies [[verdant-scatter|the Scatter]], and the Scatter does not ask where you came from. The further east you travel, the less the colonial map means. The [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] are the western homelands transplanted to warmer water. [[kalowe|Kalowe]] and the [[midchain|Midchain]] are those homelands losing the ability to enforce anything, and [[tail|the Tail]] and the [[outer-reach|Outer Reach]] are what was here before the homelands ever arrived.

### The Crown Islands

*Dravosi colonial core. The western homelands with a tropical accent.*

The oldest colonial foothold in the Scatter. The majority population is [[human|human]] and came with the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] flag. [[rattkin|Rattkin]] are present in every Crown port even though the Crown's relationship with [[the-passage|the Passage]] is adversarial — the [[warren|Warren]] prefers it that way.

| Species | Presence | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Human | Dominant | Dravosi colonial stock; also Tessarine merchants and independent families. |
| [[halfling\|Halfling]] | Common | Chandlers, bookkeepers, small merchants. |
| [[dwarf\|Dwarf]] | Common | Naval engineering, fortification work, armaments manufacture. |
| [[rattkin\|Rattkin]] | Common | Port infrastructure, message networks, [[the-passage\|the Passage]]. Officially discouraged; practically irreplaceable. |
| [[gnome\|Gnome]] | Present | Instrument makers, cartographers, Tessarine ledger work. |
| [[tiefling\|Tiefling]] | Present | Mixed colonial heritage. More visible in [[calveno\|Calveno]] than in Tidefall. |
| Orc | Present | Dock crews, ship labour, garrison soldiers. Often underranked relative to competence. |
| [[dragonborn\|Dragonborn]] | Present | Scattered across military and merchant roles. |
| Aasimar | Rare | Occasional pilgrims and Tyr-aligned advocates. |
| [[aarakocra\|Aarakocra]] | Rare | Mostly Sentinels. [[high-eyrie\|High Eyrie]] maintains a small permanent population near the eastern Crown approaches. |
| Other | Rare | The Galewall crossing drops all kinds onto Crown docks. Most do not stay long. |

### The [[central-strait|Central Strait]]

*The main highway. The most transient, and therefore the most everything.*

Not a place people live — a place people pass through. Port [[tidefall|Tidefall]] processes hundreds of ships a year. [[calveno|Calveno]] turns over its dockside population on the tide and skews more diverse than the rest of the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] — the [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] does not care about heritage, only credit.

|---|---|---|
| Human | Dominant | Still the majority, but less so than the interior Crown Islands. |
| Halfling | Common | Merchants. Calveno's counting houses run heavy halfling. |
| Dwarf | Common | Port engineering and armaments. |
| Rattkin | Common | Higher density than the Crown interior. Every major port in the Strait has a Run. |
| Orc | Common | Crew and dock work. A mixed crew on the Strait is more common than a human-only one. |
| Tortle | Common | Island-adapted, long-lived, calm under pressure. Excellent captains and pilots; [[beaumont-sel\|Beaumont Sel]] is the most visible example. |
| Tiefling | Present | Tessarine merchant families in particular. |
| Gnome | Present | Navigation instruments, trade tools. |
| Dragonborn | Present | Military and merchant both. |
| Water Genasi | Present | Natural affinity for this work. Unremarkable on a working ship. |
| [[aarakocra\|Aarakocra]] | Present | Sentinels and a small number of independent messengers and scouts. |
| Kenku | Present | Port scavengers, couriers, and fast verbal relay. |
| Aasimar | Rare | — |
| Air Genasi | Rare | Valued as weather-sense sailors. |
| [[tabaxi\|Tabaxi]] | Rare | Far from home. Usually working. |
| [[grung\|Grung]] | Rare | Blue-caste traders and occasional scouts. |

### The Midchain

*Free ports and local councils. The colonial map stops meaning much here.*

The [[midchain|Midchain]] has been occupied by too many different peoples for too long for any one of them to claim it. The free ports and reef-knowledge economy select for people who can work outside recognized law. Humans remain common but are not dominant.

| Firbolg | Present | Interior island communities. Don't use the port unless they need to. Remarkable pilots when they do. |

### The [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]]

*[[grung|Grung]] territory. The beach is as far as outsiders go.*

The Verdant Teeth interior belongs to the [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]]. The coastline operates a strict beach-trade system: outsiders come to the waterline, goods change hands, outsiders leave. The clans do not negotiate on this.

| Species | Presence | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [[grung\|Grung]] | Dominant | Interior and island communities. All castes present. |
| Visiting traders | Present | At designated beaches only. No overnight presence. |
| [[tabaxi\|Tabaxi]] | Rare | Southeastern approaches. The [[grung\|Grung]] and the [[tabaxi\|Tabaxi]] have a history neither translates clearly for outsiders. |

### The Tail

*Fishing communities and salvage crews at the edge of the charts.*

No colonial administration — only pilot families, producing a working meritocracy defined by capability rather than heritage. The Maw's proximity draws the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]] and anyone with elemental affinity who has felt the Maw's pull and cannot stay away.

| Species | Presence | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Human | Common | Longline crews, salvage camp labour, staging island residents. |
| Orc | Common | Physical work in hard conditions. |
| Tortle | Common | Long-lived, cold-resistant, comfortable at depth. Ideal salvage crew. |
| Goliath | Common | Cliff-work, heavy salvage, cold-water diving. Tail communities have had Goliath pilot families for generations. |
| [[sea-elf\|Sea Elf]] | Present | [[halythion\|Halythion]] contact. Surface occasionally near the [[shelfworks\|Shelfworks]] approaches. |
| [[aarakocra\|Aarakocra]] | Present | Sentinels of the Eyrie maintain watch presence here. |
| Water Genasi | Present | Drawn to the Maw-adjacent water. |
| Air Genasi | Present | Navigation in fog and bad weather. |
| Fire Genasi | Rare | Occasionally drawn here by the Maw's elemental bleed. |
| Halfling | Rare | Provisioning. The staging islands need someone to run the stores. |
| Rattkin | Rare | No major Run. Individual operators working salvage information routes. |
| Triton | Rare | Deep-water presence near the [[shelfworks\|Shelfworks]]. [[encounter\|Encounter]] rather than community. |

### The Sunken Crown and Outer Reach

*Past the Maw. The oldest maps and the newest ignorance.*

The [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]] is [[tabaxi|Tabaxi]] territory. The [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]] community has been here longer than any colonial record acknowledges; it does not seek outside contact, it manages it when it arrives. Beyond the Blue Hole, the Outer Reach belongs to whatever was here before anyone started naming things.

| Species | Presence | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tabaxi | Dominant | Keth-Naar and its island network. Isolated from other Tabaxi populations for generations. |
| [[sea-elf\|Sea Elf]] | Rare | [[halythion\|Halythion]] trade contact. Respectful, careful, usually carrying specific purpose. |
| Triton | Rare | Deep-water beyond the Blue Hole. |
| Water Genasi | Rare | Drawn to the Blue Hole's elemental behaviour. |
| Other outsiders | Rare | Maw-crossers, pilgrims, salvagers, the lost. Keth-Naar has protocols for all of them. |

### On the Water

Ships are their own demographic. A working crew in the Scatter does not look like any port's population — it looks like whoever the captain could hire and keep. A Midchain free-trade crew might be half orc and tortle with a rattkin pilot and a grung surgeon who does not discuss what caste they left. A Crown naval vessel runs formally human with dwarf engineers and a rattkin aboard in an unofficial capacity everyone pretends not to notice. A Passage runner has no species requirement and one rule. The ship is the smallest unit of real integration in the Scatter — it works because it has to.

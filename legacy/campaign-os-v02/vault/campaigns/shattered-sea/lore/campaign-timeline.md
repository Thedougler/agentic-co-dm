# Campaign Timeline

*Chronological index of [[campaign-overview|the Shattered Sea]]'s history from the Antheri's height through Session 01, with full detail on each event's own page.*

## The Fact

This page orders the record, not the story — strict chronology, not narrative-first, so any single event's blow-by-blow stays on that event's own page. Two threads run active past this page's final entry: [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]'s pursuit by [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s hunters, and the open debt [[auralis|Auralis]]'s waking and Umberlee's unresolved wrath against [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] leave on the table.

### The Age of the Antheri

- **-2000 DR**, [[antheri|The Antheri]] build into the western wall of [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]], expanding toward the [[elemental-plane-of-water|Elemental Plane of Water]]. [[auralis|Auralis]] first appears during this age.
- **-495 DR**, The Antheri vanish. The planar breach widens beyond what the Antheri city can survive.

### The Shattered Sea Takes Shape

- **895 DR**, [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]] start their Kensei order at [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]]. They oppose [[umberlee|Umberlee]]'s [[waveservants|Waveservants]].
- **1195 DR**, [[dravosi-crown|The Dravosi Crown]] arrives. They claim [[crown-islands|the Crown Islands]] and found [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]].
- **1210 DR**, [[tessarine-concordat|The Tessarine Concordat]] arrives. They form the Seven Houses of [[calveno|Calveno]].
- **1240 DR**, [[the-passage|The Passage]] breaks away from [[warren|the Warren]]. Its people flee colonial pressure.
- **1295 DR**, The Sentinels of the Eyrie begin watching The Drowned Maw. They never stop.
- **1385 DR**, The Spellplague closes the northern crossings for a generation. [[verdant-scatter|The Scatter]] learns to stand alone. This pattern endures.
- **1487 DR**, The Second Sundering ends and northern shipping resumes. Current charters begin in the years that follow.

### The Years Before

- **1488 DR**, [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] salvages Antheri components from [[shelfworks|the Shelfworks]]. The discovery sparks the Goldrush. She returns to Calveno and opens her own workshop.
- **1490 DR**, [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]] buys Antheri armor from Catarina's workshop to repair his shell.
- **1493 DR**, High Eyrie banishes [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]], age 14. She boards the [[red-lady|Red Lady]] with [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] as crew.
- **1494 DR**, [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] flees the [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]] after [[pell|Pell]] dies in reprisal.
- **1495 DR**, Simone rises to warlord-tier status among the [[grung|Grung]] and assembles an elite unit to hunt Jean-Claude down.

### Final Days Before Session 01

- **Day -35 (approx.)**, [[admiral-fisk|Admiral Fisk]] assembles his five-ship fleet ([[red-lady|Red Lady]] flagship, [[the-narrow|The Narrow]], [[heft|Heft]], [[fernen|Fernen]], [[loud-argument|Loud Argument]]) to steal the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] from Umberlee's shrine on [[vel-orn|Vel Orn]].
- **Day -6**, The fleet steals the Pearl without alerting the [[waveservants|Waveservants]].
- **Day -5**, Umberlee strikes the fleet over The Drowned Maw. All five ships sink. The Pearl's signal pulls [[leviathan|The Leviathan]] through the planar fissure from the Elemental Plane of Water. [[auralis|Auralis]] wakes at last.
- **Day -4**, The Leviathan sinks the [[vestra|Vestra]]. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] washes up on [[keth-naar|Keth Naar]]. He sneaks aboard the [[saltwright|Saltwright]].
- **Day -3**, [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont]] finds Delmar and Crissdalynn adrift and gives them free passage west aboard the Saltwright.
- **Day -1**, [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] is one island ahead of [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s hunters in [[midchain|the Midchain]], four months after his escape.

### Session 01

- Jean-Claude leaps aboard the Saltwright. The [[uncertainty|HCS Surety]] under [[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]] flags it for inspection. The hold ambush, weather-deck fight, [[geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey Draves]]'s defection, and the gangplank standoff play out in full on [[s01-recap|Session 01's recap]].

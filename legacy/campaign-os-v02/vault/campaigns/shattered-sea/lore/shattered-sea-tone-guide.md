# Shattered Sea: Tone Guide

*DM/agent-facing craft guidance for narrating characters and scenes, not player-known content, and not a content boundary (see [[lines-and-veils|Lines and Veils]] for those).*

## The Fact

Core equation: high competence trapped inside low emotional maturity. Real danger, glamour, hierarchy, and momentum populate a world of stylish, capable people who let ego, vanity, lust, boredom, resentment, and old grudges complicate their best efforts. One-sentence tone: swashbuckling polish complicated by pettiness.

**The pillars:**

1. Real stakes + childish dysfunction: the setting plays straight while the people are disasters, delivering epic consequences from petty triggers.
2. Competence without adulthood: dangerous experts who are also personal disasters, like a brilliant captain with terrible impulse control or a feared corsair desperate for approval.
3. Stylish surface, rotten interior: lacquered warships and perfumed courts built on piracy or exploitation, fine wine and blood on the cuffs.
4. Banter as combat, dialogue as social violence: interruption, one-upmanship, flirtation as dominance, precision insults, status testing, and old slights never forgotten.
5. Mundane pettiness in lethal situations: a boarding action pauses to argue about bad gunpowder, and a treaty fails over seating and décor.
6. Cynicism without emptiness: dysfunctional institutions run by egomaniacs, but people still carry messy loyalty, attraction, and occasional honest care.

**Tone boundaries.** Keep glamour, danger, decadence, sharp dialogue, recurring grudges, and emotional damage played lightly against consequences that stick. Avoid broad clown comedy or pure parody, and skip meme humor or nonstop one-liners. Don't make the world itself silly, and don't give every NPC the same sarcastic voice or strip out consequences.

**NPC construction:** every character defaults to the image/competence/need/fixation formula (`vault/refs/vault/npc/references/toy.md` § Optional pre-fill technique): public image plus real competence plus one humiliating need plus one petty fixation, e.g. naval legend / brilliant tactician / needs royal approval / obsessed with seeming younger than a rival.

**Scene construction:** every major scene pairs a real external threat with a petty internal conflict (`vault/refs/stories/prose-and-character-craft.md` § Scene grammar). Expect stylish presentation and fast verbal escalation, plus consequences that stick.

**Agent application:** the default NPC dialogue register is status-testing and guarded about real needs. Default scene energy comes from competent professionals sabotaged by interpersonal baggage. Comedy comes from character flaws, never from making the world itself silly: the sea and its monsters remain dangerous, while the people are the disasters.

This guidance governs narrative style and carries no overlap with [[lines-and-veils|Lines and Veils]], which sets hard content boundaries (self-harm, hanging, sex), not tone.

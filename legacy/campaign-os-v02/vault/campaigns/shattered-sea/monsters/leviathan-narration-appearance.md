> [!narration]
> *The water goes flat first. The swell dies the way it does right before a squall that never breaks.*
>
> *Fifteen feet down, lit hazy, something wider than a hull turns broadside. Flat black. Hide dull as wet stone. Skull smooth and blind. It is not circling. It is rising straight toward whatever is loudest.*
>
> *The sea boils around the hull as lightning splits the clear sky the instant its jaws break the surface. The deck tilts hard toward open water. Ozone hits the tongue before the spray does. The next swell never comes.*

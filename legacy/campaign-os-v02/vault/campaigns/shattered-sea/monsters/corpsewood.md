## Description

A dead tree walking. Forty feet tall, bark blackened and split, every branch bare. The roots have pulled free of the ground and drag behind it like a skirt of cables. It moves at a walk, never faster, and the ground shakes with each step. The wood died years ago. The thing moving it did not.

## Ecology

The [[blight|Blight]]'s corruption does not need living tissue. A corpsewood is a dead tree reanimated through the root network, powered by the same corruption that feeds the Death Bloom. It walks because the Blight walks it, patrolling the deep interior on a route that changes with the season. Fire stops its regeneration. Nothing else does.

Home zones: [[aruhe-the-rot|the Rot]] into [[aruhe-the-hunger|the Hunger]]. The deep interior only. A corpsewood does not leave the tree line. The party encounters them on the approach to the central grove, where the corruption is thickest and the ground itself resists passage.

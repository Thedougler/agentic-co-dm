## Description

Found in the forests and highlands of [[campaign-overview|the Shattered Sea]], most often around the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] and the [[ashwall-islands|Ashwall Islands]]. Larger than any mundane owl, it flies in near-total silence and carries a faint celestial tint to its plumage — pale eyes that catch no torchlight, and a stillness that unsettles anything it watches. It shares its range with [[giant-constrictor-snake|Giant Constrictor Snake]] and [[axe-beak|Giant Axe Beak]], both of which it avoids by hunting from the air rather than the ground.

## Ecology

The owl hunts the forested highlands of the Crown Islands and the volcanic slopes of the Ashwall Islands during the hours the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]]'s own watch cannot cover: their ledgers at [[high-eyrie|the High Eyrie]] log what passes by day, and it's often a giant owl's cry, not a Sentinel's eye, that marks a boat's position after dark. It dens alone in the crags below the tree line, territorial and silent on the wing, its wingspan wide enough to blot out a low moon.

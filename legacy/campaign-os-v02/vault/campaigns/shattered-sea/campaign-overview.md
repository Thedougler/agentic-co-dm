# The Shattered Sea

![[campaign-overview-map.webp]]

*1495 DR. Sixty thousand square miles of reef, deep channel, colonial paperwork, old gods, and water that remembers.*

## Overview

### Ongoing Campaign: The Premise

The Scatter lies in the open ocean south of Chult, weeks of water past the last charted coast of [[faerun|Faerûn]] (Port Nyanzaru is the nearest real harbour and the only dependable way north). [[shattered-sea|The Shattered Sea]] itself is an archipelago in two arcs around a wide central channel, split between two colonial powers that govern how a ship can move through it. The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] enforces the western approach by naval law and inspection paperwork, the [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] holds its deepest roots in the east by credit and debt. Between the [[galewall|Galewall]] (the storm belt marking the edge of the charted homelands) and [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]] (the trench marking the edge of the charts entirely), [[verdant-scatter|the Scatter]] Current runs clockwise and the politics run crooked. Every party starts inside this arrangement, not before it. The paperwork, the tribute, and the rivalry are already the water they're sailing in.

### Ongoing Campaign: The 5 Truths

1. The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] and the [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] are rival colonial powers whose legal systems still recognize each other. Law and debt, not war, is how they compete.
2. [[umberlee|Umberlee]]'s worship is port infrastructure, not fringe faith: every harbour pays tribute to the [[waveservants|Waveservants]] regardless of who governs it, and a blessing is only ever an acknowledgement that respect occurred, never a promise of safety.
3. [[the-passage|The Passage]] operates entirely outside colonial record. That it isn't written down anywhere is its entire value.
4. The [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]] hold the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] on a strict beach-trade-only basis and are a deteriorating power on the water. Territory, poison, and coastal raids are their leverage against a shrinking position.
5. [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]] is the eastern edge of the charted world. Beyond it lies [[outer-reach|the Outer Reach]], which no colonial power has charted and which offers a pilot neither resupply nor a landmark he would trust. At that edge the sea stops being something a crew navigates and starts being a question they answer. The Scatter still harbours what the economy cannot price. Stone with no known quarry and currents that reverse against the charted tide tables turn up next to a slower accumulation of missing ships and unread old records, plus sailors who will not say what they saw east of the charts.

### Ongoing Campaign: Key Figures

- **The Crown** [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] enforces naval inspection law from Port [[tidefall|Tidefall]] and [[crown-islands|the Crown Islands]]. Law is its weapon. No active [[front|Front]] clock stated yet.
- **The Concordat** [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] runs [[calveno|Calveno]] and the Scatter's credit economy. Debt is its politics. No active Front clock stated yet.
- **The Waveservants** [[waveservants|Waveservants]] collect Umberlee's tribute from every working harbour without distinction. Transactional neutrality is their stance. Their Front “[[the-red-wake|The Red Wake]]” is active.
- **The Sentinels of the Eyrie** [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]] watch The Drowned Maw and record what they see from [[high-eyrie|the High Eyrie]]. Non-aligned record-keepers.
- **The Passage** [[the-passage]] runs hidden routes and [[rattkin|Rattkin]] couriers below every colonial power's notice.
- **The Grung Clans** [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]] hold the Verdant Teeth interior. A deteriorating position, territory and poison their remaining leverage.

### Ongoing Campaign: The Starting Situation

Everyone began as passengers aboard the *[[saltwright|Saltwright]]*, captained by [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]], an alligator-snapping tortle who survived eleven years on [[midchain|the Midchain]] route by treating danger as weather. During Session 1 the crew stole the *[[uncertainty|HCS Surety]]* from [[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]], sailed her to [[calveno|Calveno]], and rebuilt her twice in [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]]'s covered dock. She emerged as the *Uncertainty* and is now on her maiden voyage south through the Midchain. This arc plays out across [[season-02-the-open-midchain|Season 2: the Open Midchain]].

**Questions answered at Session Zero:**

- Why were you aboard the *Saltwright*?
- What do you want badly enough to cross dangerous water for?
- What trouble follows you onto a ship?
- What would make you stay with this crew when leaving would be easier?
- The ship itself was the shared connection, standing in for a patron or a common origin. Each PC's reason for staying aboard stayed their own.

### Ongoing Campaign: How Sessions Run

The table advances on milestone levelling, not XP. The crew has cleared eight sessions and now sits at level 5. It levels up when a Front resolves or a season closes, never mid-encounter. Downtime happens between voyages, in whichever port the *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]* has moored. The crew handles hull repairs and pays down debts against [[tessarine-concordat|Concordat]] credit. Shore leave runs alongside it, the kind that starts rumors the crew hears about next session. Every Front clock named under Key Figures above telegraphs its danger before it lands. The DM states a tick out loud, in the open, so the crew sees it coming. Most sessions run on roleplay: inspection lines, favor-trading, reading a room before a deal. Combat breaks out when a Front's clock fills or a Truth gets tested directly.

## GM Notes

### Ongoing Campaign: Inspiration & Themes

**Inspiration.** Swashbuckling colonial-era nautical fiction. Competing empires, debt as a weapon, and a sea that charges tribute regardless of who's currently winning.

**Themes.** What do you owe the systems you depend on to survive, and what do those systems actually cost the people underneath them? The campaign's arc question, surfaced across all four seasons: *what gives someone the right to rule? And when you've torn down every authority above you, what are you willing to build in its place?*

**Tone.** High competence trapped inside low emotional maturity. Real danger, glamour, hierarchy, and momentum, populated by capable people whose ego, vanity, and old grudges complicate their best efforts. Consequences stay real even when the conflict that caused them is petty. The full six-pillar breakdown lives on [[shattered-sea-tone-guide|the Tone Guide]], which also defaults this campaign's [[npcs|NPCs]] and scenes to the npc guide's and `vault/refs/craft/`'s generic construction techniques.

No GM-only secrets beyond the Icons' own pages yet, though background reading for the setting's peoples and pantheon lives on [[peoples-of-the-shattered-sea|Peoples of the Shattered Sea]] and [[shattered-sea-pantheon|Shattered Sea Pantheon]].

### Ongoing Campaign: Campaign Architecture

**Authority ladder.** Each season escalates the tier of authority the party questions and resists:

| Season | Tier | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| [[season-01-boarding-to-the-drowned-maw\|1]] | Local | Port officials, captains, shipboard authority |
| [[season-02-the-open-midchain\|2]] | Regional → Colonial | Midchain port authorities (texture), [[dravosi-crown\|Dravosi Crown]] dominant mid-season |
| 3 | False divine | [[grung-clans\|Grung]] gold caste (theocratic mandate) and [[auralis\|Auralis]] (constructed demigod), in parallel |
| 4 | True divine | [[umberlee\|Umberlee]]. The tribute system is port infrastructure and may be holding the fissure shut |

**Ending intent.** The campaign closes on a three-way choice: side with [[umberlee\|Umberlee]] (her system may be what manages the [[the-drowned-maw\|Drowned Maw]] fissure), challenge her and face what's beneath, or find a third path. The [[the-drowned-maw\|fissure]] is the horizon.

**Sandbox principle.** Nothing past the current season is specific. Current season at high resolution, next at medium, later at low, endgame as silhouette.

**Runtime.** Weekly sessions, 4-5 hours. Approximately 65-70 total sessions across 4 seasons. A planning estimate.

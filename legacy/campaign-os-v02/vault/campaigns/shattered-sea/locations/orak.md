# Orak

Orak is one of two eastern [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] islands. It sits so close to [[dreth|Dreth]] that shadows flatten the channel between them. The shore is low and wet. Roots crowd down to the waterline, and the canopy runs thick from beach inward. The beach looks unguarded until the canopy moves, then freezes still.

Less militarized than [[karath|Karath]]. Still [[grung-clans|Grung]] territory. No sanctioned outside access, and the channel approach is dangerous.

## Geography

- North: [[karath|Karath]]
- East: [[midchain-east|The Eastern Midchain]]
- South: [[midchain-south|The Southern Midchain]]
- West: [[dreth|Dreth]]

## Flora & Fauna

- Mangrove tangle: roots crowd down to the waterline and the canopy runs thick from beach inward, so the interior is a wade through standing root water more than a walk.
- Mangrove bark: strips boil down into a bitter tannin wash foragers use to cure hide, worth a few coins to a tanner willing to buy Orak-sourced goods.
- Shore crabs: pick through the exposed roots at low tide, easy food for anyone who can get to them before a [[grung|Grung]] patrol does.
- [[giant-frog|Giant frogs]] and [[giant-spider|giant spiders]]: frogs sit half-buried along the channel edge while spiders bridge the root gaps overhead, so a party crossing the tangle passes both without seeing either first.
- [[giant-constrictor-snake|Giant constrictor snakes]] and [[venomous-snake|venomous snake]] swarms: constrictors hold the darker root water between Orak and [[dreth|Dreth]], and the swarms gather in warm hollows after rain, close enough to a resting spot to bite before anyone sees them.

## Hazards

Standing root water covers most of the interior, so the wade slows anyone who tries to move fast or quiet. [[grung|Grung]] scouts know the tangle well enough to move through it silently. Outsiders do not have that knowledge and cannot fake it.

## Hooks

- The channel with [[dreth|Dreth]] is too shadowed and flat to read. Nothing moves until something does.
- The island looks less militarized than [[karath|Karath]]. Less militarized does not mean safer.

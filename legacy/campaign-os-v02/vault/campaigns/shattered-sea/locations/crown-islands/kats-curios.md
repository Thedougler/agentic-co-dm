## Structure

The workshop stands on an unmarked side street in [[velo-quarter|Velo Quarter]], in [[calveno|Calveno]], behind a narrow door with no sign and no shopfront. The door opens on one long room. Benches run its length, precision tools ranked by size, half-finished mechanisms left clamped between visits. A forge occupies the back wall, and a glass case of older work faces the bench where [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] does hers.

The specimen cabinet is the locked case on the east wall. It holds dark-alloy [[antheri|Antheri]] pieces with an unusual shine and tooling finer than anything sold in the open, and none of it carries a cash price. Those go by trade only. She keeps a magic rug somewhere on the grounds, and visitors do not find it.

## Atmosphere

The heat off the forge sits on the room, and everything in it smells of hot oil. Nobody sits down here; the shop holds no chairs, business happens standing at the bench, and Catarina keeps working through most of it. Tell her what you want built and she answers in a fixed order: whether she can build it, then the price. She holds that price. She also picks her own clients, and the circle that can reach this door stays deliberately small, which is most of what a referral is worth.

Custom builds and repairs both come across the bench, clocks and guns and magic items alike. Ship work runs standard rates plus ten percent, with cannon modifications between 400 and 900 gp. The larger fittings price out from there: an auto-helm at 1,400 gp, a pressurized bilge pump at 500, a signal rocket array at 700, a hull-patch automaton at 1,600, and a clockwork deck crew of four at 2,800. A depth charge is 180 gp on its own, and the rack that throws them is a 3,500 gp prototype. Off her personal bench: a clockwork owl familiar at 1,800 gp, a pneumatic grapple at 600, a calibrated spyglass at 350, and the spark pistol at 2,200, a prototype that throws lightning with no powder and fires soaking wet, and whose one critical flaw she has not solved. Her wrist-launcher gets off three shots and takes ten minutes to recharge, with prototype pricing on request. She builds [[puntura|Puntura]] to order only.

What she will not do is rebuild something that does not work, and she says why to your face. The refusal is the other half of her reputation, which is the best artificer work in [[verdant-scatter|the Scatter]]. She assessed [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]'s whip shark barb at this bench before taking the commission, and she cast and cut the two arcane cannons aboard the [[brass-cat|*Brass Cat*]] on this floor.

## Notable NPCs

[[samuel|Samuel]], a toucan [[aarakocra|Aarakocra]] placed here by [[iacopo-fieschi|Iacopo Fieschi]], arrives at eight every morning and runs messages between the workshop and the crew. He is the fastest way to reach Catarina from anywhere in [[calven|Calven]] without a referral of your own.

## Hooks

Nobody buys off this bench without a referral from someone Catarina already trusts, which makes the introduction itself the thing worth chasing. The dark-alloy pieces in the specimen cabinet tie the workshop into the [[antheri-ruins|Antheri Ruins]] salvage economy, and she trades for them instead of paying, so what she wants back is the question. Neighbours know the door: [[il-preludio|Il Preludio]] sells enchantments that work and do nothing, [[la-brace|La Brace]] burns smoke a street south, and the yards of [[the-arsenal|The Arsenal]] price the same ship work at guild rates.

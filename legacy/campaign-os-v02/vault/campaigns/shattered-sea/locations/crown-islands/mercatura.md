# Mercatura

![[mercatura-map]]

> [!read-aloud] Mercatura
> The smell arrives before anything else does. Salt, wet rope, and something frying in oil two boats over.
>
> Then the noise, and it does not let up. The canal runs inland ahead of you, wide enough for four hulls abreast, and barges crowd the loading stones two and three deep along both banks. Voices cross the water in every direction. Rigging creaks. Somebody is calling the price of salt fish down the whole length of it.
>
> A crier works the edge of the walkway with a chalk board under his arm, and the crowd parts around him without looking.
>
> Behind you, at the harbour end, one building stands a full storey above its neighbours. Pale stone, blank from the waterline to the roof. Clerks come down its steps in pairs, ledgers under their arms, and walk past everyone who tries to catch them.
>
> A bargeman on the nearest hull has been watching you since you arrived, and now he raises a hand and waves you over.

## Geography

**Extent:** roughly 1,400 ft. of canal running inland from the harbour mouth, four hulls wide at the loading stones and two where the frontages crowd in at the eastern end.

**Topography:** the ground clears the water by three feet at the loading stones and by less than that at the harbour mouth, so the frontages stand on stone footings and the cellars take a spring tide every time one comes. Fish oil and spilled brine slick the flagstones, and rope grooves worn a hand deep into the loading stones will turn an ankle. Sight lines run the length of the water and stop nowhere else, because the canal holds a straight line inland and a boat in the middle of it sees both banks at once. Boats come in from the [[central-strait|Central Strait]] at the harbour mouth and go no further than the shallows at the [[calven|Calven]] end; on foot, five minutes east reaches [[the-bridge|The Bridge]] and twenty south-west through the lanes reaches [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]].

## Trade

Everything Calveno buys or sells passes this water at least once. Captains outfit at [[moretti-and-sons|Moretti & Sons]] before they do anything else, factors' clerks work the frontages with price lists, and a crier walks the canal edge calling the day's rates for salt fish, rope, and canvas so that no one can later claim they did not hear. Nobody on this water keeps his voice down, and the canal punishes the ones who try. A deal shouted across open water has forty witnesses, and forty witnesses pass for a contract here. The [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] house tracks the large ones anyway. A buyer wanting a city seal on his goods buys them here in daylight from a man with a stamp; a buyer wanting no record at all crosses to [[velo-quarter|Velo Quarter]] and pays more for the privilege.

## Culture

The [[waveservant-shrine|Waveservant Shrine]] sits open to the water halfway up the canal, its tribute terms turned outward to face the boats, and captains settle with [[umberlee|Umberlee]] on the way out or on the way back in with everyone watching which they chose. The trade runs on that same instinct, since a debt discharged in front of witnesses gives nobody anything to reopen later. A [[il-gioco-delle-beffe|Beffa]] registration table stands on the Bridge side under an awning, and the chalk board beside it lists the day's registered pranks in a hand that gets worse as the afternoon goes on. Through the festival the whole canal runs at a pitch that even the residents call excessive. Gulls work the fish-gutting trestles at the harbour end year-round, under the factor house's shadow, and nobody has ever driven them off.

### Tessarine Factor House

The pale, unmarked stone building at the harbour end, tallest on the block. No sign names it; every Calveno regular already knows. Clerks move in and out on a fixed schedule, ledgers under their arms, and never linger on the canal steps to talk.

## Notable NPCs

- [[giacomo-moretti|Giacomo Moretti]] keeps the chandlery and the crate nobody has come for, and has never lost a prank in twenty years of the Accord.
- [[branca|Branca]] holds the shrine for the [[waveservants|Waveservants]] and keeps the record of who has paid Umberlee and who has not.
- [[iacopo-fieschi|Iacopo Fieschi]] is the Concordat's public face on this canal, and settles by letter, never by force.

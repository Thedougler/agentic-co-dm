# Calveno Sewer Magazines: Room Key

The main complex and two T-sites make up the [[grung|Grung]] network under [[calveno|Calveno]]. This page keys the rooms. The raid itself, who runs it, and how the place feels to stand in are on [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|the main page]].

## At a Glance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Type | Sewer magazine network (keyed rooms plus two T-site magazines) |
| Within | [[calveno\|Calveno]] |
| Controlled By | Grung sentry teams and handlers, run out of the Room 6 relay by [[ruma-delacroix\|Ruma Delacroix]] |

| Known For | Ten keyed rooms and two T-site magazines wired to blow on the last day of La Finestra |

## Features

Limestone-and-brick collector tunnels, built for drainage and repurposed by Grung crews into six-barrel blackpowder magazines wedged at structural weak points, with the primary chamber cut into a raised stone platform under the [[mercatura|Mercatura]] and its summoning circle keyed to admit an unbound, uncontrolled slaad on blast (the [[globe-of-invulnerability|Globe of Invulnerability]] scroll cached in Room 6 is the crew's countermeasure); atmosphere otherwise matches [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|the main page]]'s ambient conditions, intensifying toward Room 8.

## Entrances & Exits

The [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] ladder (Room 1) wedges open or locks shut, sealing off reinforcements and retreat alike; the Bridge hatch and barred culvert (Room 3) and the rusted street drain (Room T1) all reach the [[the-bridge|Bridge]] district, the culvert and drain Small creatures only; the Room T2 harbour vent lets out at the outer quay and, sealed, stops extraction even during the blast; and the Room 6 hidden passage to Room 8, concealed behind empty barrels and unknown to the posted handler, is the crew's best route past the Room 7 garrison.

## Levels

**Room 1: [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] Access Shaft.** A 15×20 ft landing sits beneath a 5×5 ft shaft. An iron ladder descends 15 ft. One creature gets half cover behind the raised curb or stacked stones. The hatch can wedge open or lock shut. When sealed, it stops reinforcements and blocks retreat. This room is just an entry point.

> [!check] Athletics — Quiet Descent
> DC 10. Anyone descending the ladder may roll.
> **Success:** the descent is silent.
> **Failure:** the ladder clangs audibly.

The landing itself holds a second tell, worth a slower look.

> [!check] Investigation — Scrape Marks
> DC 12. Search the landing for scrape marks.
> **Success:** knee-height scrape marks show barrel staves dragged through recently.
> **Failure:** nothing stands out.

**Room 2 (Y Junction)** A ~35×30 ft junction with a sunken pool (15 ft wide, 2 ft deep). Passages lead north, east, southwest to Rooms 1, 3, 4. Trail markers show green and blue routes with older red marks toward the [[mercatura|Mercatura]]. Only Grung-speakers read these signs on sight. Broken stone provides half cover for two creatures. Climbing over costs 5 extra feet.

> [!check] Survival — Silt Disturbance
> DC 10 Survival identifies heavy recent traffic in the silt on the left branch. DC 15 Nature identifies the prints as non-Human.
> **Success:** heavy recent traffic shows in the silt.
> **Success (DC 15+):** the prints resolve as non-Human.
> **Failure:** the silt looks undisturbed.

The left branch hides more than footprints.

> [!mechanic]
> **Optional encounter: 2 Crocodiles.** CR 1/2 each, AC 12, HP 19. (Difficulty: easy, 300 adj. XP.) Attack on vibration, not sight. One lunges from the left branch while the other strikes from the flooded drain, flanking the point person. Bites deal +4 to hit for 1d10+2 piercing and [[restrained|restrain]] the target (escape DC 12 Athletics). Restrained creatures can climb onto the raised lip with DC 10 Athletics. When wounded, both flee and don't pursue beyond the junction.

**Room 3 (Bridge Connector)** A 70×20 ft tunnel that narrows to 5 ft for 20 ft. After 35 ft, the ceiling drops to 4 ft. This needs slow movement. Medium creatures get melee disadvantage here. The far hatch leads to the [[the-bridge|Bridge]] district. The barred side duct opens to Room T1 (Small creatures only).

> [!check] Acrobatics — Crossing Quietly
> DC 12; moving at full speed while crouched on the silt floor.
> **Success:** the crossing is fast and silent.
> **Failure:** normal speed only, and the noise carries to Rooms 2 and 7.

The culvert is the quiet way through, if the crossing itself stayed quiet.

> [!check] Athletics — Barred Culvert
> DC 13 (Medium creatures only; thieves' tools substitute for the roll).
> **Success:** squeezes through to Room T1.
> **Failure:** the culvert holds.

Room 3 isn't unwatched.

> [!mechanic]
> **Optional encounter: route-check handler.** 1 [[grung-elite-warrior|Grung Elite Warrior]] (Difficulty: easy, 200 adj. XP). A blue-caste handler finishing a route check with a hand crossbow and 20 bolts. She flees toward the far hatch and fights only if blocked or restrained. If she escapes, all sentry teams go to active defense. When addressed in Grung by Jean-Claude, she pauses before continuing her flight. Cornered below half HP, she surrenders but gives up only "the schedule moved."

**Room 4 (Magazine Alpha, [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] Yards)** A 35×25 ft magazine with a raised dry ledge and a flooded drain trench. Exits go back to Room 2 and forward to Room 6 via a 4-ft pipe (crawl for Medium, walk for Small). Six sealed blackpowder barrels sit at a weak spot. Two Grung Poison Vials (25 gp each) sit on the handler's belt. The ledge is 2 ft above the trench and gives half cover. Climbing costs 5 extra feet. Jean-Claude crosses the pipe at normal speed.

> [!check] Investigation — Structural Weak Point
> DC 10. Examine the barrels and wall.
> **Success:** the barrels are a demolition charge at a weak point, not storage.
> **Success (DC 18 or higher, or a critical):** they're positioned where the ceiling is thinnest. Someone wants to hit foot traffic overhead.
> **Failure:** they look like ordinary barrels.

*[[encounter|Encounter]]: sentry team* 2 Grung + 1 [[grung-elite-warrior|Grung Elite Warrior]] (Difficulty: hard, 1100 adj. XP). Orders: hide and report. Break cover only if the intruders touch the barrels. Green-caste flee if the handler dies. On discovery, the handler signals the laborers through the drainage pipe and falls back to three-quarters cover.

> [!mechanic]
> **Magazine detonation.** Fire or thunder damage within 10 ft triggers 4d6 fire in a 20-ft radius (DC 14 DEX half) and ceiling collapse dealing 2d6 bludgeoning. Burial: DC 14 STR to free. Removing the timber wedges takes 1 minute (or DC 12 Sleight of Hand to speed it up). Either approach neutralizes the magazine but alerts adjacent rooms.

**Room 5 (Magazine Beta, Working Yards)** A 40×30 ft working bay with half-built scaffold and a shallow flooded corner. The exit leads to Room 6. The scaffold has one unsecured beam. Six blackpowder barrels sit on the floor (same rules as Room 4). Builder's tools (5 gp) give advantage on wedge checks elsewhere. A loose plank bridge crosses the flooded area. Two Grung Poison Vials sit on the handler's belt.

> [!check] Athletics — Collapse the Beam
> DC 12. Toppling the unsecured support beam.
> **Success:** the beam gives way, dumping the barrels into the flooded corner and soaking the powder. A loud clang announces it.
> **Failure:** the beam holds.

The scaffold isn't the only thing in Room 5 worth a closer look.

> [!check] Investigation — Builder's Notation
> DC 15. You spot scratches in the beam.
> **Success:** you see “anchor south of M, tie to main”.
> **Success (DC 12 INT+):** “M” means the Mercatura (clue #1).
> **Failure:** the beam looks plain.

*Encounter (historical: in Session 05, [[bazzoth-the-steeped|Bazzoth]] went down after pouring everything into Sump-Reek Bomb, a losing gambit. The crew cleared Room 5, so this serves as DM-prep context, not a live fight.):* [[bazzoth-the-steeped|Bazzoth, the Steeped]] leads 2 Grung laborers (one building, one distracted). Whoever approaches gains Stealth advantage, and the encounter totals roughly 4,800 adj. XP with Bazzoth's own abilities factored in.

> [!check] Acrobatics — Plank Bridge
> DC 10; crossing the loose plank bridge at faster than half speed.
> **Success:** crosses quickly.
> **Failure:** drops prone in the ankle-deep water.

**Room 6 (Handler Relay)** A 30×25 ft dry alcove raised above the service corridor, with exits to Rooms 4, 5, and 7. A hidden passage behind empty barrels reaches Room 8, unknown to the handler. [[ruma-delacroix|Ruma Delacroix]], a blue-caste quartermaster, works here (see the main page for details). She carries a hand crossbow and signal whistle, with reaching for it taking her action. A waxed route map shows 5 positions: four green-caste marks and one red mark with no location. A [[globe-of-invulnerability|Globe of Invulnerability]] scroll sits in a tube under the desk (single-use, blocks spells 5th level and lower in a 10-ft radius). Ruma's belt holds two Grung Poison Vials. The floor sits 1 ft above the wet corridor, where the edge gives half cover to prone creatures. Her purse contains 15 gp, tide charts, and powder weight tallies. This is the long-rest stop.

> [!check] Investigation — Hidden Passage to Room 8
> DC 16. Search behind the barrels.
> **Success:** you find [[the-passage|The Passage]] to Room 8.
> **Failure:** the barrels look ordinary.

Ruma's desk holds more than one lead.

> [!check] Investigation — Waxed Route Map
> DC 12. Read the route map.
> **Success:** one red mark has no location. This is clue #2.
> **Failure:** the map looks routine.

The tube under the desk is worth its own look.

> [!mechanic]
> **Globe of Invulnerability scroll.** DC 16 Arcana to activate. Creates a 10-ft-radius barrier blocking spells of 5th level or lower. Single use.
> **Failure:** the scroll depletes with no effect.

**Room 7 (Main Collector)** A 90×25 ft vaulted tunnel with 8-ft ceiling and a central water channel (10 ft wide, 2 ft deep, flowing). Small creatures submerge fully and move slowly here. Stone piers stand every 20 ft and provide half cover. Trail markers converge here, with green and blue pointing back to other sites and red marks toward the Mercatura. A faded chalk sign reads "Mercatura NEXUS 200 PAS." This room has no fixed encounter. The 90-ft sightline is ideal for [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]]'s arrival as she flees with her owl and cannon.

> [!check] Athletics (Swim) — Crossing the Channel
> DC 10; crossing the flowing water channel.
> **Success:** crosses cleanly.
> **Failure:** pushed 5 ft downstream.

The far wall of Room 7 carries an old message of its own.

> [!check] History/Intelligence — Chalk Sign
> DC 10; reading the faded canal-commission chalk sign.
> **Success:** “Mercatura NEXUS 200 PAS”. The Mercatura lies 200 paces on.
> **Failure:** the chalk is too faded to make out.

**Room 8 (Primary Detonation Chamber, Mercatura)** A ~60×50 ft nexus with 15-ft ceiling and drainage channels from all four directions. A central stone platform (15 ft diameter) holds a summoning circle. Water trenches keep the platform dry and shed bright light (10-ft radius, dim beyond). On round 3, the light expands to 20 ft and the channels rise to 4 ft deep. The channels become difficult terrain where prone creatures gain half cover. Small creatures end up chest-deep and move slowly with melee disadvantage. Scaffolding rises 10 ft along all walls with powder in the ceiling joints, granting +2 AC vs. melee from below but no ranged cover. Elite Warriors fire from above. Passages lead up to Room 7 (guarded) and from the Room 6 hidden entrance (unguarded).

> [!mechanic]
> **Ceiling detonation.** Fire or thunder damage anywhere in the chamber deals 8d6 fire across the chamber and 10 ft into each exit (DC 16 DEX half), plus total ceiling collapse dealing 4d6 bludgeoning (buried; DC 16 STR to free). Collapses the Mercatura plaza above.

The platform at the center draws the eye long before the fire does.

> [!check] Arcana — Reading the Summoning Circle
> DC 14 identifies it as other-worldly; DC 20 (or a critical) identifies it as set up for an unbound, uncontrolled slaad, keyed to activate on blast.
> **Success:** the circle reads as extraplanar.
> **Success (DC 20+):** it's set up for an unbound, uncontrolled slaad.
> **Failure:** the circle's purpose isn't clear.

Reading the circle is only the first step toward stopping it.

> [!mechanic]
> **Disrupting the circle.** 1 minute of careful work, or DC 18 Arcana as an action to sever a resonance line. Either approach prevents the summoning even if blast fires.
> **Failure:** the resonance line holds firm. The circle remains active and you spend the action.

The chamber's elevated architecture offers both cover and hazards.

> [!mechanic]
> **Scaffolding.** [[climbing|Climbing]] it requires DC 10 Athletics. Destroying a section requires DC 12 Athletics or 10 HP of damage. When destroyed, the section collapses and deals 1d6 bludgeoning to anyone on it, leaving difficult terrain behind.

[[solange-barret|Solange Barret]], the red-caste warlock, holds the center of the chamber and anchors the summoning ritual through [[action|Action]], [[concentration|Concentration]], and [[reaction|Reaction]]-based abilities.

> [!mechanic]
> **[[solange-barret|Solange Barret]]: timing** [[action|Action]]: she sustains the ritual (cannot attack or cast spells while channeling). [[concentration|Concentration]]: she has advantage on saves while in the circle (damage still requires a save, DC 10 or half damage). [[reaction|Reaction]]: she can cast [[counterspell|Counterspell]] or Detonate, not both per round (Counterspell delays Detonate until her next turn unless fire or thunder damages the powder). Disrupting the circle requires DC 18 Arcana as an action or 1 minute of careful work. Prevent the summoning by disrupting before completion. [[incapacitated|Incapacitated]] creatures cannot channel the ritual. [[grappled|Grappled]] creatures channel normally but cannot reposition. [[silence|Silence]] blocks a spoken Detonate while she remains inside it (but fire or thunder still detonates the powder regardless).

*Garrison (Phase 1):* four [[grung-elite-warrior|Grung Elite Warriors]] hide at the channel entrances. [[solange-barret|Solange Barret]] (red-caste warlock) channels with [[mirror-image|Mirror Image]] active. See the main page for her timing rules.

> [!check] Perception — Spotting the Garrison
> DC 16 to spot the concealed Elite Warriors before they act. From the hidden passage, DC 12 (passive 12 suffices).
> **Success:** you spot the garrison first (no surprise).
> **Failure:** the garrison ambushes first (surprise round).

*Detonation (Phase 2):* triggers when the 2nd Elite falls, the circle breaks, [[solange-barret|Solange]] has no Elite within 10 ft at her turn start, or fire or thunder hits the chamber. “Detonate” is a spoken command. Silence stops it, not Counterspell. Fire or thunder always detonates the powder. The circle protects her from the blast. Everyone else takes full damage. *[[otar-the-foul-manifestation|Arrival]] (Phase 3):* on Solange's next turn, [[otar-the-foul|Otar the Foul]] appears unless you disrupt the circle first. Otar has poison mist (1d6 per turn), rapid healing, chaos pulses, and an unstable form. He has legendary and lair actions. See his [[otar-the-foul|profile]]. **Deadly.** Success needs stealth, planning, and smart use of the environment, not brute force alone.

**After-detonation battlefield:** the chamber fills with rubble. Room 7 is still open but narrow. A 30-ft crater opens to the sky. Dockhands and bystanders gather nearby until the threat attacks. Light dust clouds the chamber until initiative count 20. Intruders caught in the blast must manage escape and recovery before reinforcements arrive.

> [!mechanic]
> **Post-detonation costs:**
>
> - **Clearing the hidden passage (DC 14 Athletics).** Success opens an escape route. Failure leaves it blocked.
> - **Freeing yourself from rubble or restraint (DC 16 STR alone).** Success lets you escape. Failure keeps you trapped. (Or DC 14 Athletics with an ally's help.)

Within the rubble, three types of fire sources remain accessible to those who search.

> [!mechanic]
> **Fire sources in the rubble**
>
> | Source | Skill | DC | Success | Failure |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | [[torch|Torch]] racks | Investigation | 12 | found, usable to spark the rubble | buried out of reach; not found this search |
> | [[lamp|Lamp]] oil | Investigation | 14 | found, usable to spark the rubble | buried out of reach; not found this search |
> | Alchemist's-fire debris (2 doses) | Investigation | 14 | found, 2 doses usable as thrown fire damage | buried out of reach; not found this search |

**Room T1 (Magazine Gamma, Bridge District)** A 30×20 ft low magazine with 5-ft ceiling (no jumping). Exits lead via barred culvert to Room 3 and via rusted drain to a Bridge alcove (Small creatures only). Four blackpowder barrels (same rules as Room 4).

> [!check] Athletics — Rusted Street Drain
> DC 13 (Medium creatures only; thieves' tools substitute for the roll).
> **Success:** squeezes through to the Bridge district alcove.
> **Failure:** the drain holds.

*Guardian*: [[vashu-the-weeping-veil|Vashu, the Weeping Veil]] (uses blindsight) and 1 [[purple-caste-enforcer|Purple-Caste Enforcer]], totaling about 2,850 adj. XP. Clearing this site frees Bridge captives. [[vashu-the-weeping-veil|Vashu]]'s Weeping Veil mist is dangerous here, so warn the intruders before she uses it. **Session 06:** [[vashu-the-weeping-veil|Vashu]] left to aid the Primary Chamber and died there, leaving Grung reinforcements (~10) to restaff the room.

**Room T2 (Magazine Delta, Harbour Approach)** A 35×25 ft tidal magazine with a 3-ft vent above the waterline and a 6-ft ceiling. Sealing the vent stops harbor extraction, even during blast. Exits lead to Room 7 and the outer quay, with six blackpowder barrels following the same rules as Room 4. Water rises 1 ft/hour near high tide. After 2 hours, Stealth checks suffer disadvantage from splashing unless swimmers submerge. *Guardian:* [[ozzeth-the-twiceborn|Ozzeth, the Twiceborn]] (Silence doesn't affect his spells) and a [[purple-caste-zealot|Purple-Caste Zealot]]. [[ozzeth-the-twiceborn|Ozzeth]] commands the vent and nearby defenders, retreating if wounded. The Zealot charges to detonate the powder. **Session 06,** when [[ozzeth-the-twiceborn|Ozzeth]] fell at the Primary Chamber, Grung support (about 10) took over staffing this room.

## Hooks

[[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]]'s sewer investigation is what puts a crew into this network room by room — full context on [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|the main page]]. At the room level, the pull compounds site by site: each magazine the crew disarms buys time against the shortened fuse, and each clue collected (the builder's notation in Room 5, the route map in Room 6, the trail markers in Room 2) narrows down the fifth, hidden site under the Mercatura before Room 8 confirms it outright. Skip these rooms and the clue chain never resolves — the crew reaches Room 8 blind to what's about to detonate beneath the plaza.

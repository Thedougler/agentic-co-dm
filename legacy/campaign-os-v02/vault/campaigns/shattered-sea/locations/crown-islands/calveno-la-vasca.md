The Black-Jaw family runs a private dry dock in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]], the oldest district of [[calveno|Calveno]]. A covered basin cut from old stone sits behind a shabby wall. It poses as a defunct tanning yard. The family has operated this working cradle for three generations. [[cobb|Cobb]] maintains it. Members of [[the-passage|The Passage]] call it La Vasca or the Basin. Newcomers learn the location first, then its real purpose.

Their first stop on landing in Calveno: a covered dry dock in Black-Jaw colors with a ship's cradle already waiting, and [[cobb|Cobb]] at the water's edge to greet them. The crew's vessel, *Uncertainty* (formerly the [[uncertainty|HCS Surety]] on the approach channel), went into the cradle for a 5-day repair.

## The Basin

A low stone arch, flush with the waterline, marks the entrance. It looks like a drainage culvert. Inside is a covered basin tall enough for a mast, long enough for patrol cutters. The space smells of old stone, bilge, and decades of mineral damp. A single oil lamp hangs over the cradle. Stone walls are original foundation material from beneath [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]]. The cradle itself is iron and timber, hand-operated, left over from when this was a tannery. [[rattkin|Rattkin]]-scale tools line the port wall: mallets, tacks, brushes, pigment. Spare timber, a hand pump, and old manifests clutter the starboard side. A narrow aft passage leads to the [[warren|Warren]]'s channels. Only low tide makes it passable on foot, and only for those already vouched.

## Services

The Basin removes registry plates (with no record kept) and performs hull caulking. Timber and fittings are available from rotating stock.

## Notable NPCs

**[[cobb|Cobb]]** runs the Basin (young Rattkin, late teens, grey-brown fur, hands calloused from work). He tends the cradle and tool stock while guiding safe vessels through the arch. Each use triggers a report up the chain. Perrin's arrival gets reported to [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]] within an hour.

Sample lines below are from the source draft and not confirmed as spoken. The one confirmed line from the water's edge in Session 3 was Perrin's, not Cobb's.

> Cobb is not the threat but the clock. Using the Basin is not secret but a known event reported through proper channels to Nona. Play him as someone doing his job correctly (competent, honest, and transparent about what that help costs). When asked not to tell Nona, he pauses, looks at Perrin with sympathy mixed with mild disbelief, and declines (not unkind, just firm).

- Old manifests on the worktable are Black-Jaw records from thirty years back. No one has looked at them yet.

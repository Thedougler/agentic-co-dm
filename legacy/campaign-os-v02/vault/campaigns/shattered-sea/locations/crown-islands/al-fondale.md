# Al Fondale

![[al-fondale-banner]]
![[al-fondale-banner]]

Al Fondale is a side-canal tavern in the lower channel of [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]], [[calveno|Calveno]]. Good food, cheap wine, four rooms above, it's run by an old couple who stopped advertising decades ago because they didn't need to.

## Details

Al Fondale sits on a side canal off Le Paludi's lower channel, where the buildings lean close enough that the upper floors almost touch. Bice and Aldo have run it for twenty-two years; Aldo cooks, Bice manages the room. This place has been there long enough to stop advertising. People who eat here know about it. People who need to know about it find out.

> [!read-aloud]
> The canal narrows here until the stone walls on both sides are close enough to touch from a punt. Al Fondale is the building with the oil lamp burning amber in the ground-floor window and the smell of onions and rendered fat drifting through the half-open door. Inside: the ceiling hangs low. Tables show the wear of decades. Voices murmur at the bar. A figure moves through the kitchen at the back. The canal outside is quiet. The space within radiates warmth.

## Structure

The ground floor holds one main room with eight tables, a low bar, and the kitchen opening behind a half-wall. The ceiling stays original stone. Thirty years of elbows and plates have worn the tables smooth, leaving their surface polished by touch and time. Upstairs, four small rooms have clean beds with fresh linens. During [[il-gioco-delle-beffe|Il Gioco delle Beffe]] festival week the noise of Le Paludi pours straight through. Bice never apologizes for this. She simply charges more.

### Access & Security

The entrance opens to the street. A narrow canal-side door stays latched from inside. When the window lamp burns, the kitchen is open. When the lamp goes dark, Bice has closed for the day. A [[the-passage|Passage]]-credentialed visitor hears one statement from Bice, delivered quietly: "the stairs are for residents."

**Secret passage:** the cellar (behind the dry-goods shelf, then a stone panel behind barrels on a timber skid, north wall) connects to the Warren's drainage network within forty feet. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] set it up two years ago. Bice knows not to ask and redirects lingerers with a drink and a topic change.

The corner table is never idle for long. A [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi]] watch officer occupies it, watching the cellar stairs. Two more days of pattern-building will bring [[the-rattle-statblock|the Rattle]] to Nona's attention, closing the entrance for good.

Bice vets who gets to use the door. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] has a Passage connection through Nona, and Bice will confirm Perrin knows Nona if they identify themselves.

## Notable NPCs

**[[bice-riva|Bice Riva]]** (a [[halfling|Halfling]], white-haired, barely clears the tabletops). Puts food in front of people before they finish deciding. Calls everyone *caro*. Does not ask where people are from or how long they intend to stay.

**[[aldo-riva|Aldo Riva]]** (a [[dwarf|Dwarf]], white-bearded). Runs the kitchen and comes out to talk about it. Has been in Le Paludi long enough to have watched the canal layout change twice.

## Hooks

A Dravosi watch officer occupies the corner table, watching the cellar stairs. This is a closing clock. Two more evenings of pattern-building and someone calls the Rattle. Nona learns it, and the Passage entrance closes.

# La Cenere

> [!read-aloud]
> Fifteen feet down a dead-end canal spur off [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]], past [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]], dark stone rises where a fire gutted the building years ago. The new stone is darker than the old, and locals call this place "the Ash" for it. A single oil lamp burns in the one window. Inside, a counter cuts the room in half. [[lavinia-sordi|Lavinia Sordi]] stands behind it, hands moving like they're counting even when still. Shelves behind locked glass run three walls, holding whatever she's decided to show today. The air smells of canal water and something faintly metallic, old magic maybe, or something else. She doesn't ask why you've come. She's already deciding what you can handle.

## Structure

The shop sits in [[calveno|Calveno]], at the dead end of a canal spur past [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]]. Its dark, fire-scarred stone gave it the local name "the Ash." [[ettore-lupo|Ettore]] runs the shop up the spur. He knows La Cenere is there and has opinions about it.

A single door opens onto a small room built to keep crowds out. Just inside, a counter blocks visitors from the stock. Locked glass shelves line three walls, holding whatever [[lavinia-sordi|Lavinia Sordi]] has decided to show that day. The rack behind the glass runs the length of the room, longer than a shop this size should need.

She moved into the burned-out building when nobody else would take it.

## Atmosphere

A single oil lamp lights the room. The air carries canal water and a faint metallic edge, old magic maybe, or older blood. Lavinia doesn't perform or explain. She buys and sells, and the discount on everything for sale is the risk written into the price, not generosity.

She doesn't negotiate. A clean item is worth less to her. It's traceable, and she can't resell it safely. Guild-certified goods fetch about 15% of market value, against roughly 40% for something with no papers at all, a gap regulars know well enough not to argue.

Much of her current stock traces back to one wreck. At least two salvage crews worked it, on a timeline that lines up with [[red-lady|Red Lady]] salvage activity. The rack holds the [[blade-of-the-lost-grip|Blade of the Lost Grip]] and a [[driftlight-lantern|Driftlight Lantern]] carrying [[continual-flame|continual flame]], plus something at the edge of its light that may or may not be there. There's a set of salvaged plate greaves too. A [[vial-of-uncertain-healing|Vial of Uncertain Healing]] sits behind the glass, one she swears is probably Greater. An [[oathbow|Oathbow]] does too, priced at 2,500 gold, more than [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] could afford when he priced it out.

Her [[grung|Grung]]-connected buyers stopped visiting right around when the Red Lady salvage started turning up on her shelves, and she's raised her prices to cover the exposure.

In Session 08 she sold a cursed pair of [[cursed-bracers-of-archery|Bracers of Archery]] to Jean-Claude, and an unstable [[wild-pearl-of-power|Pearl of Power]] to [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]]. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] bought a pair of [[tattletale-goggles|"buyer beware" goggles]]. [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] bought a cloak. She called it a [[nightmantle|Nightmantle]], bought off a falcon-featured man calling himself [[osset|Osset]], but identify proved it a cursed [[cloak-of-displacement|Cloak of Displacement]] instead, not the [[cloak-of-protection|Cloak of Protection]] Lavinia had guessed.

Delmar also tried on a [[ring-of-mind-shrieking|Ring of Mind Shielding]] without paying for it. It does the opposite of its name: it floods the wearer with the surface thoughts of everyone nearby. He handed it back without buying it. It's still on the rack.

## On Display

> [!read-aloud] On Display
> She has left one thing out on the counter. A pair of plate gauntlets sized for a big man's hands, pitted white where seawater sat in them a season.
>
> Behind the glass a slim rapier lies on folded velvet, talking. The words are too low to make out, and nobody has opened the case today.
>
> Higher up, a ship's lantern stands with its shutter cracked a finger's width. The light coming through is the wrong colour for oil, and it holds steady while the room's lamp gutters.
>
> The small stock shares one card at eye level. A plain iron ring, the dust in front of it smeared by a thumb. A paper twist of grey powder. Soft boots hung by their laces, soles unmarked.
>
> The back wall keeps two brass-locked cases up past the ladder. A cord of amber beads warm enough to fog its glass, and a folded black cape with another buyer's name inked on the tag.
>
> The rapier stops mid-word.

* [[blade-of-the-lost-grip|Blade of the Lost Grip]] - 180 gp - a sentient +1 rapier that throws itself at the nearest enemy on initiative and insults everyone in earshot.
* [[driftlight-lantern|Driftlight Lantern]] - 60 gp - the shutter opens a window onto another plane, real or faked, with something on the far side of it.
* [[ring-of-mind-shrieking|Ring of Mind Shrieking]] - 50 gp - blocks mind-reading by drowning the wearer in every surface thought nearby.
* [[dust-of-disappearance|Dust of Disappearance]] - 100 gp - one throw hides everyone within ten feet for 2d4 minutes.
* [[boots-of-elvenkind|Boots of Elvenkind]] - 150 gp - silent footfalls and advantage on Stealth, for the flank [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] and [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] keep opening.
* [[necklace-of-fireballs|Necklace of Fireballs]] - 1,500 gp - beads torn off and thrown as fireballs, scaling with how many go at once, priced far past what anyone here can raise.
* [[wings-of-flying|Wings of Flying]] - 1,700 gp - a cloak granting a 60-foot fly speed for an hour, and another buyer's name is already on the tag.

# Zort's Pits

## Structure

One storefront on the [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] canal walk, roughly 50 feet by 40, with its door standing open onto the water side. The floor is bare dirt throughout, uneven where boots and hooves have worked it up near the cages. A fence rings the open pit at the centre of the room and the cages ring the fence, stacked two high against the walls. The fencing on each enclosure is minimal, so an opening is a hole a person can go through as easily as an animal can come out of it.

The reptile crates hold the long wall, warmed and stacked with the smallest at eye level. [[zort|Zort]]'s desk sits at the back of the pit with the water tanks behind it, and his goblin hands work the far side where the mucking gets done. The open front door is the only way in or out.

## Atmosphere

Straw, dung, and animal musk, which is a barn dropped into a canal district and never aired since. Under that runs the noise: claws on board, water slopping in the tanks, and [[zort|Zort]] shouting over it at whoever is slowest. Customers get one warning about the cages, and he expects them to keep it.

He calls himself the animal expert in [[calveno|Calveno]] and sells working stock before he sells curiosities. Beasts broke to travel, to guard, and to take a stranger's hand. A contact in the Mid-Chain supplies most of the reptiles, and Zort will quote a price for the name as readily as for the animal. Nothing leaves the pit before he has worked it in front of the buyer, so nobody walks out claiming they were not shown what they bought.

## Notable NPCs

Goblin hands work the far side of the pit, three of them, mucking out cages and hauling water with one eye up for whatever [[zort|Zort]] throws when shouting stops working. None has given a customer a name. Any one of them will say where an animal came from for a coin, and two of the three are wrong about it.

## Hooks

[[zort|Zort]] will trade the name of his Mid-Chain reptile contact for a working prosthetic leg built to his measurements. [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] agreed to build one, Session 08. The name chalked on the [[six-legged-pit-cat|Six-Legged Pit Cat]]'s cage belongs to a buyer who has not come for it in two months, and Zort is feeding it on someone else's promise.

# Acqua Nera

A hull conversion and repainting yard on [[calveno|Calveno]]'s outer harbour, southwest of the [[the-arsenal|Arsenal]]. Hidden past the fortified canal, away from the [[mercatura|Mercatura]]'s docks. Run by [[dario-vanni|Dario Vanni]], he charges double to hide work from the Canal Commission and never asks where ships come from.

A low stone building set against the outer harbour wall. A wide slip connects to the water, large enough for ships up to 90 feet long. The yard smells of tar, pine resin, and sharp paint from Vanni's custom mixes. Ships leave looking like their new identity.

## Services

Hidden work costs double. Vanni files nothing with the Canal Commission. He keeps no ship records.

- Hull repaint, full colour
- Registry plate removal and replacement
- Nameboard replacement, carved and fitted
- Hull caulking and copper work

Acqua Nera also does legal work. They convert ships bought at auction. This work hides the hidden work and keeps hull changes looking normal.

Nearby: [[carpenters-slip|Carpenters' Slip]], another outer-harbour yard doing similar work.

You can reach the yard by sea through the slip, or on foot from the Arsenal end of the southwest district. Walk-ins can get an assessment without an appointment. Vanni discusses discreet work only in private conversation after assessment. He never puts it in writing.

## Notable NPCs

**[[dario-vanni|Dario Vanni]]** runs the yard. Decades at the docks have aged him. Scars from paint and steel mark his right arm. He moves slow through his work, calm and quiet, never rattled by strange requests. His workers stay silent, and Vanni keeps his own counsel.

> [!quote] Dario Vanni, opening assessment
> "Prize conversion or something else? Same rate either way, before you answer."

> [!quote] Dario Vanni, cutting off the backstory
> "I don't need the story. I need the name you want on the board and the colour you want on the hull."

> [!quote] Dario Vanni, closing the deal
> "She'll float out of here looking like someone built her somewhere else. What she actually is, that's your concern."

## Hooks

Vanni gives ships new identities. He files nothing with the Canal Commission and asks no questions about where a ship came from. Anyone who needs to hide a ship's past can use his service.

# The Low Lamp

The Low Lamp is the social heart of [[warren|The Warren]], beneath [[calveno|Calveno]]. It's a wide, low-ceilinged chamber dug into the widest section of the old drainage corridor, where Kin have gathered to eat, trade news, and conduct open business for as long as anyone currently alive can remember. The first place a trusted guest enters when they arrive at the Warren. Access is Kin and Tangle-vouched guests only.

Long communal tables of dark, scarred wood. Oil lamps set into wall niches. The central lamp, older and larger than all the others, hangs on a chain from the highest point of the ceiling and gives the place its name. Nobody knows its exact age. Nobody has let it go out to find out.

Business here is open to all. Debts settle and introductions form here; news travels freely, and every word matters. The convention holds that no [[tangle-chambers|Tangle]] business reaches these tables. What happens in the Low Lamp is Kin business, visible to all. What requires privacy goes elsewhere.

A sealed message shard has been sitting at the far end of the long table for eight days. No one has come for it. Essa won't touch it (not her business), but her patience is wearing thin. Someone who used to eat here every second day stopped coming three weeks ago. Essa mentions this offhandedly, a simple observation.

## Notable NPCs

[[essa-two-tooth|Essa Two-Tooth]], a [[rattkin|Rattkin]] elder, runs the Low Lamp with her grand-nephew Pip. She moves slowly and hears everything. Every person who enters is, in her assessment, too thin. She provides food before any discussion. If you eat, you have acknowledged the Low Lamp's claim on your goodwill.

# The Cabinet of Morsani

The Cabinet of Morsani sits in [[calveno|Calveno]]'s [[velo-quarter|Velo Quarter]]. Here the city ignores closing-hour rules, and lanterns burn all day and all night. They hang on chains over the canal-facing window. The shop is narrow but long. Shelves climb high and curve at the back, where a room juts out and doesn't align with the building's outer wall.

It sells magical and exotic items, each with a story Morsani claims to know. The stories always end before the interesting part.

## Stock

Stock turns over irregularly; Morsani does not produce a static catalogue.

- **Compass of the Drowned** (400 gp): *"The previous owner was a navigator who… well. The compass works."*
- **Ringmaker's Regret** (250 gp): *"Two fifty, or a trade if what you're offering has a sufficiently interesting story."*
- **Preserved specimen, unknown origin** (90 gp): *"I have documentation of the previous three owners. The first one is the interesting part."*
- **Salt Lantern** (80 gp): *"Came through [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]]. The captain who brought it… there's a story there."*
- **The Uncharted Coast** (180 gp): *"Two hundred miles of coastline on no published chart. The surveyor's notes are… the price is one eighty."*
- **[[the-fishwife|The Fishwife]]** (near 100 gp/quiver): screams in a woman's voice wherever it lands.
- **[[the-chimney|The Chimney]]** (150 gp/quiver): bursts into an obscuring column of smoke on landing.
- **[[lamplighter|Lamp Lighter]]** (200 gp/quiver): coats the target in a glow that lasts an hour and resists concealment or invisibility.
- **[[the-snap|The Snap]]**, a wrist-mounted shield, sold to [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] for 150 gold, Session 8.
- A +1 dragonhide belt, sold to [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]] for 100 gold, Session 8.
- **[[cloak-of-elvenkind|Cloak of Elvenkind]]** (350 gp): *"Good for not being seen. Or not being seen clearly, at least."*
- **[[eyes-of-the-eagle|Eyes of the Eagle]]** (250 gp): *"Lenses. You'll see the ship before the ship sees you."*

## Access & Security

The lanterns outside show whether Morsani is in. When all lanterns are lit, the door is open and unlocked.

## Notable NPCs

**[[prospero-morsani|Prospero Morsani]]** owns the shop. A theater-lover, he wears rings on each finger. He knows old customers by name and describes new ones with detail. Each item has a story, and Morsani knows them all. Yet all his tales end before the good part.

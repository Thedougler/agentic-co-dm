# Calven

*(Note: [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]] and [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|the sewer magazines]] are locations inside [[calveno|Calveno]] city, not the island. W17 name-similarity flag resolved as distinct locations, canon-review 2026-07-14.)*

Calven sits east of [[calders-tooth|Calder's Tooth]], three days from [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]] by wind. The oldest settled island in the north, it holds a city with a diminutive name: [[calveno|Calveno]]. The Tessarine name shows who arrived first and wrote the records.

## Geography

Calven is low and flat with marshes at the edges. Farms sit on a plateau in the middle. Tidal marshes lie to the north and west. Calveno stands on the southeast coast, the only place with solid ground deep enough to build on.

The northern and western flats spread wide at low tide. Keeled ships can't pass. Foot travelers drown without local guides. Old timbers stick out of the mud at low tide, left from structures older than the city.

## Flora & Fauna

- Reed and cordgrass: tall stands cover the flats and plateau edges, waist-high and sharp enough to cut bare skin on a careless push through.
- Samphire and sea lavender: salt-tolerant marsh greens gathered at low tide; Calveno's market stalls buy them by the bundle.
- Wading birds: herons and plovers work the tidal flats at dawn and dusk, easy quarry for a hungry party with a bow.
- [[giant-crab|Giant crabs]]: work the tidal flats and come up fighting when a foot lands too close to a burrow.
- [[hunter-shark|Hunter sharks]]: prowl the harbour mouth and southern shore, closing on anything that swims or drifts.
- [[giant-wolf-spider|Giant wolf spiders]]: nest in the old drains and cisterns inland, striking from the dark when disturbed.

## Control

The Dravosi flag flies at the harbour. The Tessarine Concordat runs the money. The [[rattkin|Rattkin]] came first and live in old drains beneath [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]]. They predate both powers and keep silent.

## Hazards

Tidal flats, mud channels, and old drainage works riddle the low island. The commercial face hides real danger.

# Waveservant Shrine, Calveno

A shrine to [[umberlee|Umberlee]] on the [[mercatura|Mercatura]] canal in [[calveno|Calveno]]. It sits in a pale stone arch. Boats passing can read the tribute terms posted outside.

## The Shrine

The shrine opens right onto the canal. It's a low arch of pale stone, deep enough to block wind but shallow enough for passersby on boats to see inside. Water flows in from the channel to fill a basin. Tallow candles burn behind glass. The tribute terms face the canal, not the door. In a city where all dealings happen openly, the shrine fits the culture perfectly. Captains may pay before sailing or after returning. Both work fine. But you must pay.

A junior keeps the candles and basin clean. A senior [[waveservants|Waveservant]] takes tribute and keeps records. Calveno values open dealings. This shrine is formal, like the city. Anyone can come by water or foot. Those who skip payment face the same reckoning as other defaults here. The shrine writes it all down.

[[kalowe-waveservant-shrine|Waveservant Shrine, Kalowe]] and [[port-tidefall-waveservant-shrine|Waveservant Shrine, Port Tidefall]] are other shrines in the region. This one handles Umberlee's appointments, and a Waveservant here can handle her claims politely, before worse comes. One waited for [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]], not sleeping until he came, but he tricked past her; [[branca|Branca]] took the vigil next and waited for Delmar to come.

## Hooks

Calveno keeps records of captains who skip tribute. This shrine writes things down more than other ports do.

A Waveservant here can meet those who owe Umberlee debts. They handle it gently, before worse comes.

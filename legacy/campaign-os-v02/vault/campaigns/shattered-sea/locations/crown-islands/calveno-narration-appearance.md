> [!narration]
> *The sound arrives first. A thousand conversations echo across the water. Not the roar of a port. Deals and gossip in the open air.*
>
> *Pale stone rises four and five stories from the canal. The sun hits the fronts. The backs are bare and plain. Flat-bottomed boats move through the channels. Pilots stand, reading the traffic. The air smells like warm stone and salt.*
>
> *At the far end a building rises above the rest, pale stone, unmarked. It watches everything come in and says nothing. No dock waits. The water is the street.*

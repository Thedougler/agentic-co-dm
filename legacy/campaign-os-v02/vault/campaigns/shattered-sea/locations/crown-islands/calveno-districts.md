# Calveno: Districts & Running the City

A router for [[calveno|Calveno]]'s five districts. Below that sit travel times, then a GM toolkit for running the city.

## Districts

- [[mercatura|Mercatura]], the trading heart on the main canal.
- [[the-bridge|The Bridge]], a three-tier arch for deals and secrets.
- [[the-arsenal|The Arsenal]], a walled shipyard behind a guarded gate.
- [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]], the oldest district, a market with no records.
- [[velo-quarter|Velo Quarter]], an island of lights beyond city rule.

## Movement

Travel by boat (2-5 cp, fast) or walk (free, slow). Use boats or roads. No ships use other routes.

| From | To | Foot | Boat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Harbour mouth | [[mercatura\|Mercatura]] | direct | direct |
| [[mercatura\|Mercatura]] | [[the-bridge\|The Bridge]] | 5 min | 2 min |
| [[the-bridge\|The Bridge]] | [[le-paludi\|Le Paludi]] | 10 min | 5 min |
| [[the-bridge\|The Bridge]] | [[velo-quarter\|Velo Quarter]] | 10 min | (foot only) |
| [[le-paludi\|Le Paludi]] | [[the-arsenal\|The Arsenal]] | 15 min | 8 min |
| [[mercatura\|Mercatura]] | [[le-paludi\|Le Paludi]] | 20 min | 10 min |
| Anywhere | Outer harbour ([[acqua-nera\|Acqua Nera]]) | +10 min | +5 min |

*Salt and wind. Open water. Quiet. Boats are safe here.*

> [!dm]
> **Le Paludi → [[warren|Warren]] (if authorized).** Tangle contact leads you through a door behind a waterwheel housing, down iron stairs to a landing that doesn't appear on any city plan.

### Location Naming

Calveno place names come from facts. Try Al/La/Il + noun, Casa/Ponte + word, Studio + name, Acqua + color. Examples: [[al-fondale|Al Fondale]], [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]], [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]], [[ponte-bassa|Ponte Bassa]], [[acqua-nera|Acqua Nera]].

Italian nouns: Arco (arch), Bivio (fork), Corda (rope), Ferro (iron), Fondo (bottom), Gatta (cat), Muro (wall), Passo (pass), Rosso (red), Sasso (stone), Stretto (narrow), Trave (beam), Vento (wind).

### Free Information

> [!mechanic]
> **Free information (DC 8 to 10 Persuasion):**
>
> - The boat three moorings down hasn't moved in four days. Outer roads hold three low hulls with no pennant, too clean for fishing boats.
> - A Canal Commission inspector was measuring a maintenance hatch at dawn with a knotted cord and no lantern. A Warren Kin runner came through [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] at an unusual hour.
> - That [[il-gioco-delle-beffe|Beffa]] team registered under a name in a [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] dialect. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]]'s kitchen door has been busy this week.
> - There's a buyer on the Bridge's top tier paying well for [[red-lady|Red Lady]] survivor accounts.
>
> **Failure:** the source has learned nothing. It offers no new information.

## Street Encounters

Quick scenes for texture. Use any or none. Players solve, skip, or ignore. Set during [[il-gioco-delle-beffe|Il Gioco delle Beffe]].

| d6 | Encounter | District | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Wrong Mark | Any calle | Comic → brawl |
| 2 | Ponte Muscle | Bridge | Social → fight |
| 3 | The Chase | Le Paludi / Mercatura | Skill chase |
| 4 | A Man in the Canal | Le Paludi | Rescue / moral |
| 5 | The Festival Drunk | Mercatura | Social → brawl |

### 1. The Wrong Mark

Young dockworkers have picked a party member as their mark. Between them they hold fish oil and a rope. One carries a forced permission slip too. They followed for two blocks. They are about to act.

> [!read-aloud]
> The oil lands before the sound reaches you. A wide cold splash from above, and a cheer from the balcony. Three young Calvenans lean over the railing, rope still swinging. The crowd turns, laughing. Seconds later, the smell hits: fish, stale and rank.

> [!check] Dexterity — Dodge the Splash
> DC 12.
> **Success:** clear of the oil.
> **Failure:** drenched in fish oil, losing the contents of one jacket pocket.

> [!mechanic]
> **If pursued.** Three Commoners (AC 10, HP 1) and one Spy (AC 12, HP 27) as team captain. Make a catch check (DC 13 Athletics/Acrobatics through the crowd). Cornered, they fold immediately: a real Beffa permit, team name *La Trota Gloriosa*, and the wrong name entirely for their intended target. **Failure:** they scatter into the crowd and escape clean, permit unclaimed.

> [!dm]
> Pull this when the Beffa needs to feel real and ambient, not plot-adjacent. The permit is authentic. The name on it belongs to a Tessarine factor who has run a Beffa team for six years and accumulated enemies in that time. This loose thread remains unresolved.

### 2. Ponte Muscle

A broad man blocks a footbridge. He wears a dockworker's coat and collects a "festival toll." His two friends wait on the far side. A man in front of him has stopped mid-payment.

> [!read-aloud]
> A broad man in a dockworker's coat stands in the middle of the walkway, blocking passage. His two friends wait on the far side of the bridge. A man between him and you has nearly run out of options.

> [!check] Persuasion/Intimidation — Push Past
> DC 10. Paying instead costs 2 cp each, about the delay, not the money.
> **Success:** through without paying.
> **Failure:** the two friends come off the far side.

> [!mechanic]
> **If combat.** 1 Thug (leader, AC 11, HP 32, club +4, 2d4+2) and 2 Bandits (AC 12, HP 11, scimitar +3, 1d6+1). At half HP the leader calls it off, not interested in dying over a street toll. Intervening to save the man trapped instead reveals a Passage runner named Orso, and [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] hears about it within a day.

### 3. The Chase

Someone lifts a purse. The mark is a party member or a well-dressed merchant who starts screaming. The thief is already weaving into the crowd.

> [!read-aloud]
> A hand lifts clean from a coat pocket and vanishes into the crowd. The merchant's sharp cry cuts the air. He spins, shouting, his purse found empty. A small figure weaves through the crowd, moving fast. The crowd's bodies block pursuit. Sound and confusion rise together.

The thief is [[fen-pickpocket|Fen]], a teenage halfling working the festival crowds for three days, not violent, not part of any operation. Use Spy stats, +5 Stealth, +5 Acrobatics.

> [!check] Acrobatics/Athletics/Perception — Catch the Thief
> DC 12, three times. Roll Acrobatics (through the crowd), Athletics (straightaways), or Perception (maintain sight).
> **Success (two or more successes):** she's cornered in a dead-end calle off Le Paludi.
> **Partial Success (one success):** she reaches a canal and goes in the water. Cornered later there, you recover the purse.
> **Failure (zero successes or all three fail):** she vanishes into the crowd with the purse, offering nothing more.

> [!check] Insight — Fen's Story
> DC 14, if cornered. She has the purse, a folding knife she won't use, and a sob story about 40% true.
> **Success:** the true part surfaces. She's paying off a debt to a Bridge district broker who found her working his territory.
> **Failure:** the story reads entirely true.

> [!dm]
> Fen carries a maintenance hatch key she won't explain. She stole it thinking it had value. It does: the key opens the sewer network below the Bridge connector. It's a loaded gun for the [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|Warren (Grung in the Sewers)]] investigation. Leave it in her possession unless they specifically look for it.

### 4. A Man in the Canal

Late evening, a narrow street beside a Le Paludi canal. A man falls in. Maybe the crowd surged, maybe someone pushed him, maybe he missed the gangplank. He is not swimming. The current pulls him toward a low stone arch twelve feet away. Past it, the water runs dark and fast.

> [!read-aloud]
> The sound is wrong. Not a splash, a drop. A man surfaces in the canal beside the walkway, already turned the wrong direction. He is not swimming. Ahead about twelve feet, the canal narrows under a stone arch into the dark. The current pulls him toward it.

> [!mechanic]
> **Rounds before the arch.** Round one: DC 10 Athletics to swim out. Round two: DC 12 Athletics. A thrown [[rope|rope]] requires DC 10 [[dexterity|Dexterity]] to land, then DC 12 [[strength|Strength]] to haul against the current. A boat pole, banner pole, or awning rope (DC 8 Perception) offers other options. **Failure on both rounds:** he's swept under the arch into the dark current. Treat him as unconscious and drowning starting next round.

Who he is (roll d4 or choose):

- A Tessarine factor's drunk ledger clerk. His employer gives a minor 10% factor discount as thanks.
- An off-duty Dravosi junior watch officer. Embarrassed, he owes an unofficial favour.
- A Warren Kin runner or Passage courier. Nona hears of it. Tangle credit rises quietly.
- The Chase's thief, if she ran into the canal. She still has the purse.

### 5. The Festival Drunk

The Mercatura, mid-afternoon. A hulking half-orc in a sellsword's rig, [[gost|Gost]], is at the centre of a growing circle of empty space, having knocked over a wine stall, singing in what might be Orcish.

> [!read-aloud]
> A ring of empty space has opened in the Mercatura crowd. At the centre of it, a half-orc in a sellsword's rig holds a wine bottle in one hand and his sword, still sheathed, in the other. A wooden stall is on its side behind him. The stall owner is looking at you.

Full stats and outcome, including the hiring hook if they defuse it cleanly: see [[gost|Gost]].

### Le Paludi Hired Muscle

Visitors asked loud questions, made a deal that offended someone, or simply got noticed. Thugs now occupy the mooring where their shore boat sits, four of them.

> [!read-aloud]
> On the mooring, four figures face your direction. Your shore boat sits behind them. The one in front keeps his arms crossed while the other three have spread theirs.

**Trigger:** DM discretion (visibly active in one district too long, or crossed a figure). **Location:** canal-side mooring at night, with water on one side and a wall on the other (10 ft passage between).

> [!mechanic]
> **Thug × 4.** HP 32, AC 11. Pack Tactics (advantage if ally near target). Heavy crossbow on one. **[[bandit-captain|Bandit Captain]] (optional).** HP 65, AC 15. Present if the sender wanted this done right.

> [!dm]
> Who sent them: leave unresolved. Options: [[iacopo-fieschi|Iacopo Fieschi]], embarrassed by something made public; a broker whose client is the Red Lady salvage commission; or a Dravosi commercial factor who noticed them at the wrong time.
>
> Take a prisoner and the visitors learn who's watching. Win without one and they know someone spent money on this. Flee and the boat is still there, with the muscle standing on it.

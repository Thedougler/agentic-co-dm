# The Tangle Chambers

The Tangle Chambers are the deepest, driest part of the [[warren|Warren]], at its southern end where the ceiling lifts high and the city's noise doesn't reach. The elders meet here, and [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]] keeps her home among the Passage's most kept secrets. Entry requires a summons, even for longtime guests.

The southern corridors look different, with old tooled stone instead of broken rock and a high ceiling that sparked endless jokes. [[rope|Rope]] bridges span flooded areas while the central chambers stay dry and clean, lit by oil lamps behind glass where the air smells of paper, oil, and stone.

> [!dm]
> Why the ceiling lifts here has no established cause. It's deliberately unresolved local color: a running joke among the Kin, nothing more.

[[nona-black-jaw|Nona's]] south-wing apartments serve as the Black-Jaw family home and [[the-passage|the Passage's]] main base in [[calveno|Calveno]].

[[the-tangle|The Tangle]] meets here when business requires a full council session. The archive rooms hold routing records. They hold network intelligence from many generations. Only Kin can read the shard-notation used here.

The [[black-jaw-run|Black-Jaw]] family maintains everything in the chambers. Lamps burn clean, corridors stay dry, and they protect archive rooms and inspect bridges regularly. This maintenance is essential, not ceremonial.

No formal guard stands here. Every [[rattkin|Rattkin]] in the Warren knows these corridors. They know how to alert the appropriate elder to uninvited guests. Kin guests with standing need a specific summons to enter. Dry guests have not entered in living memory. The Tangle has made a few exceptions to this rule.

## Notable NPCs

**[[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]]** keeps her family apartments in the south rooms. These rooms also serve as the Passage's main operations hub in Calveno. See [[the-tangle|the Tangle]] to learn about her role chairing the council.

**[[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]]** grew up moving through these rooms. He knows the way without thinking about it.

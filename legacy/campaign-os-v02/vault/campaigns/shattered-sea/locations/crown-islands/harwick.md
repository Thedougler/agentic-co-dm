# Harwick

Harwick is the second-largest island in the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]]. The military heart of Dravosi power in [[campaign-overview|the Shattered Sea]] stands here. The island holds the Harwick Naval Yard, the Admiralty, and the fleet anchorage. These bases give the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] its power across [[verdant-scatter|the Scatter]].

## Geography

[[calders-tooth|Calder's Tooth]] controls the western entrance to the [[central-strait|Central Strait]]. [[calveno|Calveno]] handles trade. Harwick builds ships and stages fleets. The name is old Dravosi: *hær-wīc*, meaning army place. This island has always served the same purpose.

Sitting two days east of Calder's Tooth near the middle of the northern arc, Harwick lets the fleet reach both ends of the Crown Island chain. Open ocean lies close by, and the island's south face drops into deep water. It holds the largest anchorage in the Scatter for a Ship of the Line, an advantage the Crown fiercely fortifies.

The interior plateau holds marshes and tidal channels to the east and forested hills to the northwest. A curved headland called the Bight shelters the southwest harbour from northeast winds, with deep water staying close to shore. The Naval Yard occupies this sheltered anchorage.

The northern coast takes open ocean waves. It has no settlements except a signal station and a few fishing hamlets. The eastern marshes drain through channels. The water flows into a shallow bay. Small craft use this bay to avoid the Yard's entry points.

## Flora & Fauna

- Coastal pine and scrub oak (northwest hills): tall stands the Yard logs
  under Admiralty licence for hull timber and pitch; the managed plantations
  give way to older stands the Yard never cuts, where wolves have taken a
  woodcutter or two, so the Yard marks the forests as dangerous.
- Reed grass and glasswort (eastern marsh): salt-tolerant reed beds line the
  tidal channels, and the fishing hamlets harvest glasswort as a bitter
  edible green and dig mud crabs from the flats at low tide.
- Grey seals and gulls (the Bight, northern cliffs): seals haul out on the
  Bight's rocks near the Yard, and gulls nest along the exposed northern
  cliffs by the signal station; sailors trade seal oil and gull eggs at the
  market wharf.
- [[hunter-shark|Hunter sharks]] in the Bight: scraps thrown from the Yard's
  slipways draw hunter sharks into the sheltered anchorage, the reason no
  Harwick sailor swims off the wharf.

## Harwick Naval Yard

The Yard sits on the entire western shore of the Bight, with dry docks cut into rock and covered slipways holding three-deck hulls. Storehouses fill with timber, pitch, and cordage. The Yard builds every ship the Crown needs. The [[hcs-constancy|HCS *Constancy*]] and the [[hcs-ordinance|HCS *Ordinance*]] came from these slipways, which have built Crown warships for six generations. Civilians can see the walls and smell tar, but nothing past the gate opens without Admiralty say-so.

## The Admiralty

The Admiralty office sits on the hill above the Naval Yard. From here, it tracks patrol orders, charts, commission papers, and sealed orders for each warship. The building looks plain on the outside, but inside only approved staff work there. Captains carry their sealed orders but open them only at the right time. This office directs the whole fleet.

## Harwick Town

The civilian settlement clusters along the eastern edge of the Bight between the Yard wall and the market wharf. The Naval Yard needs labour. The fleet needs provisions. Sailors on liberty need somewhere to spend their pay: chandlers, taverns, a ropemakers' district, ships' suppliers, and lodging houses. The naval rating pool that crews Crown ships on rotation lives here in barracks along the north edge of town. Harwick sailors are not the Scatter's most adventurous, but they are reliably its most sober when duty calls.

## Hazards

Military restriction, managed but dangerous forests, and Yard security all shape visitor movement.

## Structure

The long, low warehouse sits flush with the [[mercatura|Mercatura]] canal front in [[calveno|Calveno]], its loading door wide enough to take two boats at once and opening straight onto the water. Barges out of [[calven|Calven]] and [[harwick|Harwick]] tie two and three deep at the loading stones outside. One room does all the work inside. Rope hangs the left wall in graded coils and provision casks run the right, with spare fittings sorted by size along the east wall. A counter closes the back, where the proprietor works, and a doorway behind it opens on a back room holding one crate and nothing else. Upstream the canal runs to the [[the-bridge|Bridge]] crossing; downstream it passes the [[waveservant-shrine|Waveservant Shrine]] on its way to the harbour mouth.

## Atmosphere

[[giacomo-moretti|Giacomo Moretti]] works the counter himself, a middle-aged halfling with grey at the temples who knows every price by heart and never forgets a face. Repeat customers get a discount and nobody gets one on provisions. He stocks what a ship burns through, standard gear at standard prices. Hemp [[rope|rope]] by the coil, silk rope in small quantities and not always in. Oakum and tar, with caulking tools for hire. Sail canvas in three grades, oilskin by the yard, linseed oil by the quart. [[lamp|Lamp oil]] comes standard or sea-lantern grade, the sea-lantern four silver dearer a gallon and proof against fogging in salt air, and the signal lanterns rack up clear, red, green and shuttered, spare panels behind them. Then the working iron: block and tackle, rigging blocks, marlinspikes, cleats and rings and pins. Provisions fill the right-hand casks, hardtack and salt pork and salted fish and dried legumes and vinegar, and the water casks come off the morning draw, never yesterday's. Sail kits carry curved [[needles|needles]] and waxed twine, the cooking sets are pot and knife with a fire striker, cut to [[galley|galley]] fit, and ink and paper sit at the end of the counter. A cargo net takes two days on special order.

The air is tar and salt over hot lamp oil, and the loading door stays hooked back until the light goes. He closes at sundown and opens again for a real order.

One crate stands in the back room. Its papers are good and its manifest is wrong. He reported it to the [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] factor house and has been waiting for someone to come and claim it since. Until one of them does, the contents are not his to sell.

## Hooks

The crate in the back room is still unclaimed, and the [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] factor house has never sent anyone for it. A buyer came through one morning, asked which customers Giacomo keeps, bought nothing, and left ([[moment-08-moretti-and-sons|that morning on the canal front]]). Giacomo reads it as somebody pricing the shop, not its stock.

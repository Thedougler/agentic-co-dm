# The Arsenal

![[the-arsenal-map]]

## Geography

**Extent**: roughly 1,400 ft. of wall along the water by 600 ft. deep, a squared-off block at the southwest corner of [[calveno|Calveno]], cut from the rest of the city by a fortified canal on its landward face.

**Topography**: made ground, quarry spoil dumped over marsh, standing four feet above high water, so every slip floor slopes to a drain and the drains run loud on a falling tide. Underfoot, stone dust and spilled pitch go tacky in the sun and turn slick under the crane rails where the grease drips. Sight lines run the full length of the yard, one open working block with the covered slips down the water side and the timber stores and rigging lofts down the other, so a foreman on the walk between them sees who stands where all the way to the wall. The checkpoint arch on the canal is one way in, chained above the waterline under a lamp that burns all night. The sea gate at the far end is the other, and it opens for a launch and shuts the same hour.

## Government

No magistrate sits here. The gate is Canal Commission ground, staffed by a watch sergeant who answers to the Commission office in [[mercatura|Mercatura]] and, in practice, to whichever House has a hull in the water that week. Past the arch, the yard's own foremen hold the law. A foreman strikes a thief off the books, and a man off the books carries no reason the gate will accept for coming back through it. Nobody writes any of that down. The Commission logs hulls and papers, not people, and the House that owns the slip owns the argument.

## Trade

The Dry Docks are the point of the district, one long roof over the covered slips and cranes on iron rails at every berth. A bell marks the change of crews at dawn and dusk. A hull without a House name behind it does not get one, and it does not get a place in the queue either, since the queue runs fourteen months and every hand in the yard is already promised. Foreman's Row runs the length of the store side, chandlers and riggers on House contract, their fronts identical but for the ship-crest stencilled above each door. What the Row turns away goes to [[carpenters-slip|Carpenters' Slip]] across in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]], where [[savia-brentino|Savia Brentino]] takes the structural work the Arsenal is too slow or too secret to touch, and what neither of them will log goes out to [[dario-vanni|Dario Vanni]] at [[acqua-nera|Acqua Nera]] in the outer harbour.

## Defenses

The wall and the fortified canal close the district off from the city around it, and everything defending it stands at the gate. A watch sergeant (Veteran, AC 17, HP 58, Multiattack +5 to hit) works the plank table with four Guards (AC 16, HP 11, spear +3) on the arch above him, and every party crossing shows ship registry and travelling papers plus a reason to be here that holds up during festival week. Papers that survive the ledger get a crew through, and most crossings end there. Papers that fail it send a crew back down the canal, since the sergeant turns people away and arrests nobody. Drawn steel brings two more Guards up from the yard, six in all, with an order for the crew to stand aside. The sergeant holds that line for three rounds and sends for a lieutenant before he opens the fight himself.

## Hooks

- A House foreman wants a keel job off his own books and across to [[carpenters-slip|Carpenters' Slip]] because the Row's queue would put his hull in the water in spring. He needs timber carried out through the gate without it reaching the ledger, and [[savia-brentino|Savia Brentino]] will not begin until the deposit is in her hand.
- A rigger on Foreman's Row will sell directions to a canvas roll wedged in the lofts, and swears he has never opened it himself. [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] can price the arrow inside it faster than he can name what he is selling.
- Festival week doubles the questions at the plank table, and the sergeant asks every crew member the same three. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] cannot answer any of them with anything but the truth, which makes him either the fastest way through the gate or the reason the crew never sees the yard.

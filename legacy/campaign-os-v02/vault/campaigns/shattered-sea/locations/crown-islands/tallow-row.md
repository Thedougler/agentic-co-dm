# Tallow Row

> [!read-aloud]
> The butcher's sits dark and shuttered on Tallow Row, old blood and brine caught in the stone underfoot. The alley barely fits two shoulders. A window no bigger than a man's two eyes waits at the door's end. It opens without sound and watches you for a long count. A voice speaks in Draconic. Answer wrong. Answer for the wrong person. The window closes without a sound.

Tallow Row is a short, narrow lane in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] named for the tallow-rendering trade that used to work it. The butcher's shopfront at its dead end has sat dark and shuttered for years. Behind it, reached through a cellar the shopfront no longer explains, is a private card room. Not the meat trade. Where the alley is derelict, the card room is lavish, three-deep rugs, oil lamps burning, hard cases at the felt eating cured meat off silver plate.

Entry requires passing a single vetted door. A window no wider than a man's two eyes opens. A voice speaks a challenge in Draconic. [[grukan|Grukan]] decides whether you and the answer are worth the room's trouble. Get past him and you step into warmth and watchful quiet, entirely removed from the wreck of a building around it. The stakes climb fast. Everyone counts who's counting.

## Notable NPCs

- [[grukan|Grukan]], the ogre doorman, vets everyone through the two-eye window and the Draconic password.
- [[vittoria-sanzio|Vittoria Sanzio]], banks the felt from the head of the table. Never plays a hand. The room's markers live in her lacquer box.
- [[thassos|Thassos]], a regular at the felt. Former passage-sense navigator turned card counter.
- [[watches-the-deck|Watches-the-Deck]], a regular who watches the deck, not the room.
- [[iolo-bracca|Iolo Bracca]], a Crown harbour stamp-clerk who loses here on purpose. Talks about everyone else's cargo while he does.

## Hooks

A grating opens behind the den. It leads to a drain with no business being this deep under a butcher's cellar. Something moves beneath [[calveno|Calveno]]'s streets. It's the first trace. Nobody at the table knows.

[[old-faas|Old Faas]] comes most weeks and splits his winnings. Because he does, the door opened for [[thunk|Thunk]]. You're welcome if someone already vouched for you.

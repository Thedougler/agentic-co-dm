# The Splint

The Splint is a small, dry room in [[warren|the Warren's]] east passages. A healer and pharmacy work there, with the name coming from old Kin slang for "the fixing place." Shelves hold jars of dried plants, and a table works as counter and treatment space, kept clean between visits. Kin guests pay less. Dry guests need a sponsor. Stock includes healer's kits, [[antitoxin|antitoxin]] vials, healing potions (rare stock), wound care and bone-setting (charged by guest type), and made-to-order items (one to three days).

| Price | Item |
|---|---|
| 5 gp | Healer's kit (limit two per visit) |
| 50 gp | [[antitoxin\|Antitoxin]], vial (one in stock, typically) |
| 45 gp | Potion of healing (one, sometimes two; restocked irregularly) |
| 1 sp / 2 gp | Wound treatment, bone-setting (Kin rate, then Dry rate) |
| free / 5 sp | Consultation (Kin free, Dry pay) |
| ask | Custom preparation (commission; 1 to 3 days) |

## Notable NPCs

[[colla|Colla]] runs the Splint, a young [[rattkin|Rattkin]] in his late twenties who trained under his aunt. He works in silence, has no patience for people who arrive with avoidable injuries, and will write down his reasoning instead of speaking it during a procedure. See [[colla|his page]] for his surface-side supplier.

## Hooks

[[colla|Colla]] needs brine-sage, a low-tidal marsh plant used in wound preparations. It has become nearly impossible to source since a Dravosi trade restriction tightened last season. He'll pay 30 gp per reliable bundle or matching services.

A visitor came to the Splint three weeks ago with serious injuries. Colla reported the incident to the [[tangle-chambers|Tangle Chambers]] and hasn't seen that person since, refusing to discuss it.

> [!narration]
> *A shabby wall in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] hides a covered basin cut from old stone. From the street it reads as a defunct tanning yard. The air still holds old hide.*
>
> *Inside, Black-Jaw colors mark a working cradle. Water slaps the cut stone. Tools hang where a tannery would have hung hides. A ship's hull in that cradle disappears from the street and from most ledgers.*

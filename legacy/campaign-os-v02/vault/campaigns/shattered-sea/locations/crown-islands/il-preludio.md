# Il Preludio

> [!read-aloud]
> Lanterns glow red, then gold, then a blue no normal flame holds, over the curtained shopfront on the Velo Quarter square.
>
> The curtain sweeps itself aside. The door behind it opens on applause: real hands, somewhere, clapping just for you.
>
> Every shelf inside glows with its own faint color. A blade turns slowly on a velvet stand near the window, throwing more light than it should.
>
> The scent of rosin and orange peel hangs in the air. A voice sweeps out of the back room mid-sentence: "And here they are, right on cue."

## Structure

Il Preludio sits on the [[velo-quarter|Velo Quarter]] square in [[calveno|Calveno]], named for the number that never comes. City authority stops at the Velo bridges. Nothing sold here carries a license, and nothing needs one.

The curtained front opens on one room the width of two market stalls. A window stand faces the square, close enough to the glass that the square can price what stands on it. Open shelving runs both side walls from knee height to the ceiling, ladder-served, and a counter closes the back third. Behind the counter a plain sheet hangs across the stockroom door, where [[tam-slim-nail|Tam Slim-Nail]] keeps four of everything. The stair beside it goes up to the room where [[fioravante-selmerin|Fio]] does the actual working, and no customer has been up it.

## Atmosphere

Every box on the open shelving promises something, and every box delivers it. A ring of [[true-seeing|True Seeing]] at 35 gp shows the wearer, truly, exactly as they looked twenty minutes ago. A [[lantern-of-revealing|Lantern of Revealing]] at 60 gp casts a light in which every shadow does what its owner wishes it were doing instead. A [[bag-of-holding|Bag of Holding]] at 90 gp holds anything, without limit, and opens only from the inside. Fifteen gold buys a draught of [[heroism|Heroism]]: you feel heroic, you are not. A Blade of Unerring Return, thrown, always returns to Il Preludio, and Fio keeps a barrel of them by the counter. Further along sit the Cloak of the Following Wind, the Boots of the Ovation, a Horn of Summoning that fetches one seagull and always the same seagull, an Amulet of Forewarning that hums before something unimportant happens, the Second Wind in its box of bellows, a Gauntlet of Retort four hours slow, and a Mask of a Thousand Faces whose thousand faces are all Fio's.

The row behind the counter is the shop's second joke, and this one costs money. Those pieces work. Their boxes carry the same gold-leaf tags and earn the same speech. A buyer who has spent ten minutes laughing at the shelves will walk straight past the thing that would have saved their life, and Fio counts on that. Ask him which ones are real and he says every last one, which is true and gets you no further.

## Notable NPCs

**[[fioravante-selmerin|Fioravante "Fio" Selmerin]]**, an elf, owns Il Preludio. **[[serena-cottle|Serena Cottle]]**, a halfling, co-owns it. **[[tam-slim-nail|Tam Slim-Nail]]**, a rattkin, keeps the stock. **[[cecco-trivani|Cecco Trivani]]**, a human, apprentices under all three. Every one of them speaks as though each sentence opens a musical number. None of them ever sings.

## Hooks

Tam is the only rattkin any of [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]]'s crew has met outside his own warren. Tam clocks the resemblance in the doorway before Fio finishes the door's applause. Tam corners him at the stockroom curtain and asks where his people burrow now. For a bard who reads a room for a living, that's an easy opening. For one who wants to stay unnoticed, it's an uncomfortable mirror.

If ignored, Tam keeps asking after Perrin around the Velo Quarter. Word that a "cousin" passed through the shop reaches whoever else is listening for rattkin movement in Calveno.

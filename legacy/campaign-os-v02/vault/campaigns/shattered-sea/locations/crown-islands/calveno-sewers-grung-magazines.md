# Calveno Sewer Magazines (Grung Raid Infrastructure)

*(Do not confuse this with [[calven|Calven]], the island [[calveno|Calveno]] sits on. They are different things, cross-linked per canon-review 2026-07-14 W17.)*

[[grung|Grung]] crews set blackpowder charges at five points in the sewer. All blow on the final day of La Finestra. Secondary sites at Le Paludi, the [[the-bridge|Bridge]], and the harbour blow open and let people escape. The primary site beneath the [[mercatura|Mercatura]] blows the plaza and summons something bound.

The collector system has three entry points. A service hatch in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] drops 15 feet by iron ladder (marked by [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s runner). Most direct route. A far hatch in the Bridge district reaches the middle stretch. A tidal passage at [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]] opens only at low tide. Narrow and flooded, but fast if you skip the upper network.

**Ambient conditions throughout the network** (unless a specific room says different):

- **Light:** dim. Oil lamps sit at junctions. Green algae glows faint. No sun light below shafts.
- **Ceilings:** 5 to 6 ft in tunnels (stops Medium jumps). Main collector has 8 ft. Primary chamber has 15 ft.
- **Walls:** limestone and brick with chalk marks and Grung notation at knee height.
- **Sound:** water echoes loud. [[combat|Combat]] noise reaches nearby rooms.
- **Smell:** brine and stone mixed with Grung scent (skin film). Faint near entry, strong near the primary chamber.
- **Water:** ankle-deep standing water (hard for non-swimmers). Deep sumps go 2 to 4 ft down.
- **Grung marks:** ladder rungs, ropes, and lashings show a shiny skin film. Grung crews left this during their time in the tunnels.

**Session framing:** this dungeon is the current arc's second half. [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] joined as a 5th PC in Session 05 and, lacking a fixed room assignment, enters at a chosen moment when her clockwork owl and eldritch-cannon backpack beat a loose Grung crew. With five captains now in the crew, the boss uses a new CR 12 build. [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] does not know Jean-Claude is a hidden Grung traitor yet, and this tension works because the discovery can happen naturally at the table.

## Investigation: Nona's Assignment (DM Prep)

### Intel baseline

The crew enters with intel from three sources. Felix (interrogated after the sixth ship raid) gave them four secondary sites, blackpowder scaffolding, a false 2-day timeline, and garrison/leader color-coding. A second interrogation added barrel counts (32 transported, 22 placed, 10 missing), exact sentry timing, boat colors, and a reference to a “circle site” (clue #5). Ruma (captured in-dungeon) confirmed the accelerated timeline, gave the fifth red-caste marker, described egress vents and sentry rotations, and warned of a check-in clock running during rest.

> [!dm]
> Three Calveno residents (a Warren runner, two Dry residents) reported seeing a large green creature in the sewer tunnels below Le Paludi and the Bridge district. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] had no proof until [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] told her [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] is part of their crew. Nona said: *"Before the other things. I need to know what is in the tunnels under my city."*

> [!read-aloud]
> The Warren runner's sketch is four junctions and an X, maintenance tunnels below the Le Paludi canal street, an access hatch marked in charcoal. The ladder drops fifteen feet to water and limestone. The lantern smells like fish oil. Down here it smells like low tide and old stone and something else you haven't placed yet.

> [!check] Investigation — First-Pass Signs
> **Boot prints** (Perception DC 10), **non-Human ID** (Nature DC 12), **wall markings** (Investigation DC 13), **how recent** (Survival DC 14), **how many** (Investigation DC 15). [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] knows the smell and marks are Grung right away. Others need DC 15 Survival to identify the smell, but cannot name the species without his help.
> **Success:** prints found, non-Human confirmed, markings found, age narrows to one or two days, headcount shows at least two with signs of repeated visits.
> **Failure:** clues stay hidden, age reads only as "recent," headcount stays unclear, other checks find nothing.

Reporting back with confirmed prints, confirmed smell, confirmed Grung notation, and no active presence:

> [!quote] Nona Black-Jaw
> "Grung. In my tunnels, during the Beffa. Did they see you?"

> [!dm]
> If the crew wasn't seen, Nona nods and moves on. If they saw the Grung or aren't sure, she nods differently and sends [[enzo|Enzo]] out immediately after they leave. The investigation confirms reports are real and raises an unanswered question, the correct exit for a first hint.
>
> [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] can confirm Grung presence but not explain it. Grung spread is deliberate and directed. Whatever sent scouts this far north during a festival had a reason he cannot share.

## Pre-dungeon incident: the sixth ship (s04)

Before Room 1, this crew investigated Grung sightings for [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]], finding markers and traps, then following them 500m to a [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] coast exit where handlers had staged a catamaran with 8 barrels of blackpowder supplying the network. Jean-Claude ambushed the tunnel patrol and killed 2 blue-caste Grung while Crissdalynn killed the fleeing purple handler by sea, with a fire arrow destroying the ship and powder. They captured [[felix-aho|Felix Aho]], a green-caste laborer, whose interrogation gave the “Felix, already earned” intel above.

World Tick roll 15 (partial): handlers noticed the loss within hours. They ordered lockdown and sped up the timeline. Secondary sites tightened rotations and rushed powder-packing ahead by half a day. One scaffold build stayed incomplete (see Room 5). The timeline got shorter than Felix's “two days” estimate. Catching-force teams at the perimeter haven't moved to match it. The primary Mercatura site (Room 8) stays on its original schedule. Felix only knows his section, so he cannot point to the primary site.

Loot from this raid included six vials of Grung poison. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s people logged them as common stock and did not compare them to twenty vials already in the crew's [[uncertainty|HCS Surety]] cargo hold. [[simone-tabarnack|Simone Tabarnack]]'s page carries the supply pattern.

During the build-up days, [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s purple-caste coordinators moved through Calveno in disguises. They wore fake mustaches, wrong-sized hats, and Small-sized cloaks. These disguises fail under close inspection. They work because the Beffa welcomes strange costumes and Calveno assumes Grung stay far away in the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]].

> [!dm]
> **Grung-normalizing pranks.** The handlers paid locals to add Grung pranks to the Beffa (boat raider act, comedian's bit, mask vendor routines). Any prank's money trail leads to a handler in thin disguise. By Day 5, real Grung activity hides in the festival fun.

## Running this dungeon

### Poison mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **Contact poison (environmental).** Grung-touched surfaces (rungs, ropes, barrel handles) poison any creature making bare-skin contact (DC 12 CON save). Gloves or cloth wrapping prevent the effect.

| Type | Delivery | Effect | DC | Used by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contact secretion | Bare skin on coated surface | [[poisoned\|Poisoned]] 10 min | 12 CON | Environmental, retreating green-caste |
| Torpor extract | 5-ft cloud (vial crack) | [[speed\|Speed]] halved 1 min | 12 CON | Elite handlers, when cornered |
| [[weapons\|Weapon]] poison | Arrow/dagger hit | +2d4 poison damage + poisoned 1 min | 12 CON | All Grung combatants |

### Topology & routes

Loop topology: rooms 1 ([[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] access) → 2 (Y-junction) → 3 (Bridge link) / 4 (Magazine Alpha) → 6 (Handler Relay, with hidden exit to 8) → 7 (Main Collector) → 8 (Primary Chamber). Room 6 holds a hidden path the posted handler doesn't know about.

**Common routes to the primary chamber:**

- **Route A** (Bridge hatch): 3→7→8. Fastest but skips the magazines and finds least intel.
- **Route B** ([[le-paludi|Le Paludi]]): 1→2→4→6→7→8. Full investigation route with all sites.
- **Route C** (Hidden): 1→2→4→6→8. Direct and unguarded.
- **Route D** ([[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]]): 7→8. Slow and flooded but skips the upper network.

### Long rest (Room 6)

By the time the intruders reach Ruma, they have fought many hard battles through Rooms 2, 3, 4, and 5. Room 6 is dry, easy to defend, and quiet. Their [[heroes-feast|Heroes' Feast]] wears off during the 8-hour rest (do not mention or hint at this; if asked, answer plainly, and they will realize it when Foul Miasma poisons them in Room 8). Ruma's check-in clock keeps running during rest unless spoofed.

> [!check] Deception/Intimidation/Performance — Spoofing Ruma's Check-In
> DC 13, in handler-register Grung; advantage if fluent.
> **Success:** send routine status (see the “If spoofed” column below).
> **Failure:** the check-in clock runs (see the “If not spoofed” column below).

| Time | If not spoofed | If spoofed |
|---|---|---|
| T+0 | Ruma captured/killed/delayed; clock starts | Routine status sent |
| T+1h | Pipe-click inquiry from another handler | No change |
| T+2h | Handler network marks Room 6 missing; primary garrison goes active defense | No change |
| T+3h | Remaining secondary teams begin evacuating laborers | No change |
| T+8h | Rest finishes; patrols avoid Room 6; surprise holds in Room 8 unless they take the hidden passage | Rest finishes; hidden passage grants surprise as written |

**Watch beats:** first watch, a Warren runner's voice echoes faintly through a pipe. Nona confirms the Warren handles the marked sites. Second watch brings either pipe-clicks (handler check, if not spoofed) or a canal crocodile sniffing the drainage and leaving (if spoofed). Third watch, standing water stops and starts again. This is just a tidal shift, nothing more.

### Pacing (four acts)

(1) *Discovery* (Rooms 1 to 3): early signs of Grung activity. Stealth and investigation dominate.

(2) *Disruption* (Rooms 4 to 6): the crew finds and stops secondary magazines through two Hard fights. Room 6 becomes the intel hub.

(3) *The Rest* (Room 6): full resource recovery. Heroes' Feast expires during this rest.

(4) *Confrontation* (Rooms 7 to 8): escalation peaks with toxins and combat.

The hidden passage from Room 6 is the crew's best tactical opening.

**If loud:** alerts spread fast. Room 4 → Room 6 (Ruma whistles). Room 6 → all sites and garrison. A fully alerted garrison sets an ambush and the crew loses surprise. Solange keeps channeling.

> [!check] Stealth — Fighting Quietly (Room 2)
> DC 14; prevent Room 2 combat from reaching Rooms 3 and 4.
> **Success:** the fight stays contained to Room 2.
> **Failure:** the alert propagates to Rooms 3 and 4.

**If stealthy:** whoever comes down can choose three paths. Move silent and slip through the Room 6 hidden passage to reach the primary chamber. Track slime trails to spot Grung patterns (no code-breaking needed). Or skip the secondary sites.

> [!mechanic]
> **Full-network stealth.** The infiltrators need 4+ Stealth checks (DC 12 to 16 per room). [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] is weak at this (no Stealth skill, wears metal). [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s [[minor-illusion|Minor Illusion]] hides sound. Any failed check alerts Grung and starts the "If loud" rules.

**If negotiate:** Ruma (Room 6) cares more about living than the mission. If captured quietly, she tells:

- Where the secondary sites are and that the timeline sped up
- A "circle site" exists (a fifth site)
- Sentry rotations, how to exit, and escape paths
- Poison rules (which surfaces poison you, how torpor hits, weapon venom damage, ways to protect yourself)

She does not know where the primary site sits.

**Fight modifiers (without Heroes' Feast):**

- Foul miasma and poison arrows hit hard
- Mesmerizing Chirr hits even on WIS checks
- Detonation does 8d6 fire and 4d6 bludgeoning
- Dead-man switch topples the ceiling (2nd Elite falls, Solange shows, circle breaks, fire and thunder follow)
- Bright zone from circle blinds Umbral Sight (round 3 onward)
- Boss heals unless fire or acid hits it (after rubble or from [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]]'s wand)

**For the crew:**

- Hidden passage from Room 6 gives them surprise
- Rest restores spell slots, Ki, and Bardic Inspiration
- High ceiling (15 ft) and scaffolding help fliers
- Breaking the circle works (see Room 8)
- Globe scroll from Room 6 protects a 10-ft radius
- Split approach stops guards from covering both paths
- CR 14 ally helps in Phase 1
- [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] locks hatches if they tell her

**Alternative:** skip rest and keep Heroes' Feast. The crew gains +2d10 HP and immunity to poison and fear. They also get WIS advantage. But spell slots empty and HP drops low.

**If Solange escapes** (circle breaks before the summoning completes): she hides under the ceiling (and blows it up) then escapes via [[dimension-door|Dimension Door]] or through rubble. She reaches [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]] and tells her the defector lives. But if the ritual finishes, the summoned boss kills her instead.

**Secondary extraction clock** (run during boss fight if sites are not stopped):

- Rounds 1 to 2: crowds get confused, teams push into lanes.
- Round 3: first captives get pulled.
- Round 5: smoke rises from Le Paludi (10 to 20 captives per lane).
- Round 7: harbour runners reach Room T2 exit and start loading toward [[vethka]].
- Fight end: count active lanes to find total captives taken.

> [!check] Perception — Screams from the Bridge (Round 3)
> DC 12.
> **Success:** crew members hear screams from the Bridge (extraction teams pull the first captives below).
> **Failure:** the fight's noise masks any screams.

**Safety valve.** If the fight heads toward disaster (two PCs down at once, crew HP below 25%, or DM's call), an ally steps in. They give the crew a short lair and legendary actions until the boss falls. See [[otar-the-foul|the boss's sheet]] for the full rules.

**Drama suite (primary chamber): quick DC reference for the boss fight**

| DC | Skill/Ability | Action | Effect on Miss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | Investigation; Athletics; Acrobatics | Spot blackpowder; climb scaffolding; cross a channel | No useful read; slip and fall prone; stumble into the channel (difficult terrain) |
| 12 | WIS; CON | Resist Mesmerizing Chirr; resist Poisonous Skin | [[stunned\|Stunned]]; poisoned |
| 13 | Spellcasting ability (opposed) | Resist Solange's [[counterspell\|Counterspell]] check | The countered spell fails, slot spent |
| 15 | Perception; Arcana | Detect Elite Warriors before surprise; identify the circle as extraplanar | Elite Warriors act with surprise; the circle's nature stays unidentified |
| 16 | Investigation; STR (Athletics, contested) | Detect the Room 6 hidden passage; resist a grab from the boss | Passage stays hidden; the boss's grab succeeds |
| 18 | Arcana or STR, per approach | Sever a circle resonance line | Line stays intact |
| 20 | Arcana | Identify the circle as slaad-configured / non-Grung-developed technique | Origin stays unidentified |

**Shenanigan offer:** stop Solange before Phase 2 starts. Break her focus, grab her, or cast [[silence|Silence]] to stop the detonation and summoning. Circle Ward and [[mirror-image|Mirror Image]] make this hard, but success brings big rewards.

**Box-of-doom flags:** the detonation (8d6 fire and 4d6 bludgeoning) kills wounded PCs. If secondary sites still stand, more civilians die. Track captives with the Secondary Extraction Clock.

> [!mechanic]
> **Boss bite (time-bomb infection).** A bite from the summoned boss can seed a 3-month time-bomb infection (DC 15 CON save). [[lesser-restoration|Lesser Restoration]] cures it.

### If ignored

The detonation fires on the shortened timeline (about 1.5 days from now). The Mercatura plaza collapses and the summoned boss pours into festival crowds. All four secondary breaches burst open at once across [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]], the Bridge, and the harbour. Extraction teams grab 200 to 300 captives if no one stops the raid.

## Notable NPCs

**[[simone-tabarnack|Simone Tabarnack]]** (operation commander). Her Grung operatives built and run this raid, though she doesn't appear on-site in the source's room key.

**[[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]]** ([[simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s brother, the crew's Grung expert). He is a disguised Grung defector. He has natural immunity to contact poison and is the only crew member who reads Grung trail and caste markings.

**[[ruma-delacroix|Ruma Delacroix]]** (blue-caste handler, quartermaster in Room 6). She became an ally of the crew in Session 06, making the check-in clock moot, but before that cared more about living than the job and would give up if disarmed and outnumbered. [[felix-aho|Felix Aho]]'s name and [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] speaking Grung both trigger her. She knows the four secondary sites, faster timeline, that a fifth red-caste site exists, sentry routes, exits, and poison rules, but does not know the primary route, circle purpose, or the identities of Solange and [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]]. In combat, she uses [[grung-elite-warrior|Grung Elite Warrior]] stats.

**[[solange-barret|Solange Barret]]** (red-caste warlock, stationed in Room 8). Channels the summoning ritual. See Room 8 key for her action/reaction/concentration timing rules in full.

**[[bazzoth-the-steeped|Bazzoth, the Steeped]]** (former guardian of Room 5/Magazine Beta). Dead.

**[[vashu-the-weeping-veil|Vashu, the Weeping Veil]]** (guardian of Room T1/Magazine Gamma). Accompanied by a [[purple-caste-enforcer|Purple-Caste Enforcer]].

**[[ozzeth-the-twiceborn|Ozzeth, the Twiceborn]]** (guardian of Room T2/Magazine Delta). He travels with a [[purple-caste-zealot|Purple-Caste Zealot]]. Originally he had a roving role, but the source dropped that. Now he guards Delta and the network has no mobile backup. On full alert, the guardians stay in place and the closest crew responds to noise.

**[[ozvok-the-vermillion-distiller|Ozvok, the Vermillion Distiller]]** (a fourth lieutenant, shelved for this run). See his page for tactics and capabilities for future use.

**[[felix-aho|Felix Aho]]** (captured green-caste laborer). His interrogation gave the crew early intel on the four secondary sites.

**[[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]]** ([[rattkin|Rattkin]] matriarch). Her runner marked the Room 1 hatch and sent the crew here for the original sewer investigation.

**[[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]]** (Warren native, the conduit to Nona).

**[[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]]** (5th PC, joined in Session 05). Not fixed to a room. The DM brings her in at a chosen moment (see DM Only § Session framing).

**[[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]]** (a potential ally). If briefed, he speeds up the Room 8 fight and can live through the detonation. If he breaks the summoning circle, the summoning likely fails but Solange gets away with proof that [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] lives.

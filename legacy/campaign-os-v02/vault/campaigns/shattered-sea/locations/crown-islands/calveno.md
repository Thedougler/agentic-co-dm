# Calveno

![[calveno-banner]]

![[calveno-narration-appearance]]

The oldest trading center in [[verdant-scatter|the Scatter]] has pale stone buildings that rise high from the water. They are rich on the canal side and plain in back. Flat-bottomed boats move people through canals with no docks or [[cart|cart]] streets. Deals happen in the open, and the right people watch who smiles. That's the point. That's how the city works.

## Key Facts

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Theme** | Open commerce. Privacy costs money. Every favor is a ledger entry. |
| Location | [[calven\|Calven]] island, [[crown-islands\|Crown Islands]] |
| Travel Time | 3 days from [[tidefall\|Tidefall]] on good wind, a week with bad wind |
| Power | Merchant families hold Council seats. The [[tessarine-concordat\|Tessarine Concordat]] owns six of the seven. |
| Access | Flat-bottomed boats through canals. No streets or docks. |
| Known For | Visible dealmaking, canal travel, merchant power, hidden [[rattkin\|Rattkin]] infrastructure |

## Districts

Five, each with its own page and keyed map. [[calveno-districts|Calveno's Districts]] routes to all five and carries travel times between them; [[calveno-map|the Calveno map]] shows how they sit against the water. Move between them by flat-bottomed boat or on foot along the canal paths and bridges. No district has docks.

## How It Works

Visibility matters here: watch who talks to whom and you'll learn more than words. Privacy costs money, and someone is always selling it.

## Notable Venues

### Taverns & Inns

- [[ponte-bassa|The Ponte Bassa]], a canal tavern at the main crossing. Cheap food, basic beds. Oleandro knows every boat.
- [[al-fondale|Al Fondale]], a Le Paludi tavern. Good food, cheap wine, four rooms, keeps secrets.

### Shops & Services

- [[moretti-and-sons|Moretti & Sons Chandlery]], main ship outfitter. [[rope|Rope]], provisions, canvas, fittings.
- [[carpenters-slip|Carpenters' Slip]], boatyard near [[the-arsenal|the Arsenal]]. Savia sets her own hours.
- [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]], general goods. Ettore prices from memory.
- [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]], an alchemist on a side canal. [[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]] asks nothing.
- [[cabinet-of-morsani|The Cabinet of Morsani]], curio dealer in the [[velo-quarter|Velo Quarter]]. Morsani knows every owner.
- [[waveservant-shrine|Waveservant Shrine]], [[umberlee|Umberlee]]'s shrine. Tribute paid in full view.
- [[la-cenere|La Cenere]], grey market dealer. Discounted goods with hidden pasts.
- [[kats-curios|Cat's Curios]], artificer workshop by invite only.
- [[la-brace|La Brace]], a Velo Quarter smoking room. [[rufio-segalla|Rufio Segalla]] prices the hour above the blend.
- [[la-gatta|La Gatta]], a vending board under [[the-bridge|the Bridge]]. [[nicco-kettley|Nicco Kettley]] sells seconds cheap.

### Hidden & Notable

- [[warren|The Warren]], a Rattkin settlement beneath Le Paludi and the deepest anchor of [[the-passage|The Passage]].

### Festivals

- [[il-palio-delle-voci|Il Palio delle Voci Contese]], an open bardic competition that spans the canal district (see also [[il-gioco-delle-beffe|Il Gioco delle Beffe]], the city's prank competition).

## Entry Hooks

These hooks activate when the crew of the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] arrives.

- **[[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]], [[cobb|Cobb]] at the gate.** A young Rattkin on a canal skiff pulls alongside. Cobb says [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] wants to know Perrin is well. He doesn't ask what Perrin thinks about that.
- **[[delmar-fisk|Delmar]], rates on the Red Lady.** [[savia-brentino|Savia Brentino]] at the Slip mentions a broker offering rare rates for [[red-lady|Red Lady]] survivor accounts. Some sailors already took the money. She mentions it like the weather.
- **[[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]], reading the crowd.** A [[grung|Grung]] in half-mask stands at the Bridge pillar. That position sees three exits without watching any of them. Jean-Claude knows that stance. No one learns it by accident.
- **[[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]: [[branca|Branca]]'s booklet.** The [[waveservant-shrine|Waveservant Shrine]] attendant has a tide table booklet. Umberlee told her to keep it for someone from the Maw. She's held it a week. She hands it over and doesn't explain.

## Hooks

The [[warren|Warren]] sits beneath Le Paludi's tunnels. It's the only fixed Rattkin settlement in the Scatter. The city above doesn't know it exists.

[[the-tangle|The Tangle]] runs The Passage anchor through Calveno. They watch money and boats. They know what moves through this city before the movers do.

The Passage brokers can forge a home port, ship name, and owner record in two days for 60 gp. The [[warren|Warren]] routes it. The papers pass inspection. [[the-tangle|The Tangle]] keeps copies.

## Structure

A 200-foot basalt sea-stack rises straight from the water at the far northeastern edge of the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]], where the chain narrows toward [[tail|the Tail]] and the water closes in on [[the-drowned-maw|the Drowned Maw]]. The [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]] have held this rock for two hundred years, training and keeping their freedom since before Dravosi rule reached [[verdant-scatter|the Scatter]]; three Dravosi leaders tried to strike a deal with the order, and the order turned all three away.

The broad summit holds the monastery proper. Low walls and blunt rooflines shed wind instead of impressing anyone below; doorways turn away from the gusts, and courtyards stay open and swept clean. The Training Terraces cantilever off the eastern face, open to the drop on three sides, where new arrivals spend weeks learning to land, move, and hold still in peak wind. The main hall faces east toward the Maw and holds the order's bound observation ledgers, copied and maintained year after year in the same careful hand. An emergency stair cuts into the southern face and stops well above the waterline, built for rope work, rescue, and drills. Never for visitors.

[[aarakocra|Aarakocra]] land directly on the upper terraces. Non-flying visitors arrive by invitation, harness, magic, or a deliberate climb watched from above. Giant vultures ride the thermals below the monastery after storms, and Sentinel ledgers note larger shadows crossing the eastern light that sailors call rocs when feeling dramatic; the order records wingspan, heading, and time on every sighting, same as everything else it sees.

As of the current Maw crisis, weather has made the High Eyrie's approach route dangerous, a warning that comes directly from [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]].

## Atmosphere

Wind never stops on the summit. Salt works into feathers, hinges, hair, and ledger bindings alike, and generations of drills have worn the stone floors smooth underfoot. Loose cloth is a liability up here, and the order flies no banners. Nothing announces the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]] from the water but the shape of the rock itself.

The order's doctrine runs the same way the wind does: record exactly what you see, and add nothing to it. The ledger notes ships that sink, recording time, weather, position, water condition, and what happened. Never why. Every Sentinel completes a pilgrimage before joining the order in full. A master sets each student's task, aimed at a weakness, a gift, or simply whatever the order needs watched next. Fair is not the standard. Exact is. [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]] trained here under [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] before her own pilgrimage sent her out to map [[the-drowned-maw|the Drowned Maw]], and the order exiled her from these walls at fourteen for treating that discipline too lightly.

## Notable NPCs

[[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] is the order's current senior master and its teacher at the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]]'s home on [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]].

## Hooks

- Maw weather currently makes the High Eyrie's approach route dangerous. A party needing to reach the monastery must find an alternate approach or wait out the weather.
- Researchers are sometimes granted access to the public observation ledgers, though few have the patience or context to use what they read.
- The order seals a secret beneath the summit, shaped by their own teachings: [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]] carries the full [[soul-incarnate|Soul Incarnate]] thread.

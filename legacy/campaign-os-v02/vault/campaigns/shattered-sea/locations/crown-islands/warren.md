# The Warren

> [!read-aloud]
> Stone presses low overhead, close enough that a tall Dry keeps a hand up out of habit. Oil
> lamps sit in wall pockets down the length of the hall, throwing more shadow than light.
>
> [[rope|Rope]] bridges cross the flooded stretches where the old marsh channels still run,
> and past the third one a kitchen fire throws orange across a dozen faces that go still and
> watch you cross.
>
> Somewhere above and behind the walls, a pan starts a slow, even beat.
>
> The air smells of stone, still water, lamp oil, and cooking, and under it, something watching.

## Geography

**Extent.** Roughly 500 ft. across, a warren of flooded channels and low halls with no single spine.

**Topography.** The halls sit in the old drainage channels beneath [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]], well below the streets of [[calveno|Calveno]], where a fixed [[rattkin|Rattkin]] settlement has stood since long before the city grew above it. Marsh channels still run through the lower floors, crossed by [[rope|rope]] bridges. Ceilings press low; most humans feel them on their shoulders, and oil lamps in wall pockets are the only light. Disguised vents carry the city's noise down and let Kin move up unseen, and the [[black-jaw-run|Black-Jaw Run]] anchors the one route Calveno's own traffic can find.

## Government

The seven [[the-tangle|Tangle]] elders govern the Warren from the [[tangle-chambers|Tangle Chambers]], chaired by [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]]. Public access has no formal gate, but it grants no real safety either. Entry comes only through invitation and kin trust, and the Tangle has let only four outsiders into the Warren in a hundred years.

The Warren birthed [[the-passage|the Passage]] itself. In 1240 DR, the network grew from this place to escape pressure from the [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] and the Dravosi Crown, and brown-rat work built the Warren's shape: tunnels, Holds, hidden routes, and maps of harbour work drawn before the outside world knew these spaces existed. The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] still does not know the Warren is real. The noble houses know Rattkin live somewhere in Calveno, but not where or how many.

The Tangle guards its escape clock just as closely. Kin call it "the Below"; outsiders know it as *Saltmarsh Trader*, full details on [[the-tangle|The Tangle]] § Goals & Fronts. Three [[chain-council|Chain Council]] reps signed onto the plan without truly knowing what they allowed.

A trust bond with the Warren builds slowly, by deed, never by asking. A party earns one by helping a Run in trouble, stopping a threat to Calveno, or growing a bond with a Fancy Rat, one favour at a time. Il Velo, still taking shape, will make Rattkin travel easier still, since a Fancy Rat can pass as costume in a crowd.

## Trade

The Warren's primary economy trades in shelter, routes, memory, information, hidden holds, and kin support, more than coin, and [[marrows|Marrow's]] handles the sealed cargo, documents, and deniable logistics behind it, under a three-week consignment limit, kin introduction required. Not a quest board. The Warren is a home with memory, and offers to outsiders come as favours, routes, cache rights, or future help. Coin happens, but the Tangle's own trust decides whether it opens new doors.

## Defenses

The Rattle is the Warren's alarm system. When Dravosi enter [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]], someone strikes a pan in a steady beat, faster as the threat closes. Another household picks it up, then another, then the [[low-lamp|Low Lamp]], then hidden kitchens, pipes, and vents, until the sound seems to come from every stone at once. To Kin, it means one thing: Dravosi are here. Those who can't fight hide and lock doors. Those who can answer the call, and the Warren uses every tactic available to it: tricks, sabotage, ambush, and fire when the odds run bad. Quick taps after the Rattle stops mean the threat has passed.

To the Dravosi, [[the-rattle-statblock|the Rattle]] is maddening. Everyone works undisturbed, pans rattling in rhythm while water flows and every visible thing stays lawful; officers find weapons sheathed and pathways open, yet every Crown officer feels surrounded, watched, counted, and tracked. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] built the Rattle because the Crown's raids on Le Paludi used to rely on silence and surprise. The Rattle removes that surprise, turning a raid into a fight against a community that was already ready.

## Hooks

- The Warren knows what is moving through Calveno before the people moving it do.
- Do not mark a suspected entrance, boast about an invitation, or mistake a rumour for permission.
- **[[bela-silt-paw|Bela Silt-Paw]] does sub-level work.** Trapped in flooded drainage tunnels by a Water Weird in the central chamber. The reward is Tangle goodwill and a route to the harbour foundation.
- **[[warren-marrows-crate|Marrow's Crate]] is a dead courier.** An unclaimed crate at [[marrows|Marrow's]] links to [[serin-haut|Serin Haut]], who watches the canal.
- **[[warren-ferrin-locke|Warren: Ferrin Locke]] is the leak.** Passage surface work traces to Ferrin Locke. He wants out.
- **Aruhe survey.** [[warren-aruhe-survey|Warren: Aruhe Survey]] covers Nona's commission to Perrin to scout [[aruhe|Aruhe]] as a [[midchain|Midchain]] anchor, in full.
- The collector tunnels below the Warren now host [[grung|Grung]] blackpowder magazines (see [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|Calveno Sewer Magazines]]). Nona suspects something is building but does not know the full scope.
- Unusual money has been moving through Calveno's broker network toward the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]]. The Warren has noticed enough to know a *[[red-lady|Red Lady]]* salvage commission is forming, but not who is behind it.
- [[andriana-trevisan|Andriana Trevisan]] knows roughly where to find the Warren and will not give away its secrets, a lever for anyone who needs the location without the Tangle's blessing.
- Warren help given cleanly earns real Tangle credit, usable later for routes, papers, or protection. Help given carelessly still leaves access open, but cold, expensive, and watched. [[intelligence|Intelligence]] sold outward buys short-term coin at the cost of long-term Passage suspicion. Left too long, the Tangle treats the *[[vestra|Vestra]]* loss as a liability and reassesses standing around the Below.

# Casa Lupo

A general goods shop in the lower canal zone of [[calveno|Calveno]]'s Le Paludi district. It sells what people actually need, not what ships need.

## Structure

Low ceiling. Oil lamps hang from iron hooks. Floorboards settled unevenly over a century. Shelves go to the ceiling on three walls.

## Atmosphere

The air smells like candle wax and dried herbs, with something faintly mineral. It's probably leftover from the previous shop.

## Stock

Lanterns, lamp oil, candles, rope, canvas and cloth, needles and thread, cooking tools, dried herbs and spice, basic medicines, writing supplies, locks and keys, tool sundries. Ask for what you need. Stock changes regularly.

[[ettore-lupo|Ettore]] keeps a sealed account of who in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] bought what and when, going back forty years. No one has ever asked him about it, and he does not volunteer its existence.

## Notable NPCs

**[[ettore-lupo|Ettore Lupo]]**, proprietor in his seventies, moves slowly (a man done with rushing). He prices everything from memory and is never wrong.

He'll tell you if he lacks something but won't say where to find it unless you earn his regard. He knows specialty trade well and redirects customers to others when he lacks stock. Once he sent [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] to [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]] for a [[whip-shark|Whip Shark]] egg inquiry.

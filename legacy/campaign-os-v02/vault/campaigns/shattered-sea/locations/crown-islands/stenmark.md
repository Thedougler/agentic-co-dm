# Stenmark

Stenmark is the easternmost island in the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]], the last Dravosi flag before the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]], and its name is old: *sten-mark*, stone border, and whoever named it wasn't being poetic. From the eastern cliffs on a clear day, the water changes colour where the Maw begins. The blue-green of [[verdant-scatter|the Scatter]] goes flat and dark, a shade with no bottom in it. The threshold shows from shore. People who live on Stenmark have looked at that line their entire lives.

The island is stone and cold water and wind from the east. [[dravosi-crown|The Dravosi Crown]] maintains a signal station on the eastern cliff edge, staffed by a small watch rotation. The people here are fishing people and watch people and the occasional salvager using Stenmark as a staging point for work on the [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]].

## Geography

The island runs north to south, long and narrow. The western face is gentler, home to settlement, harbour, and what farmland the shallow soil supports. The eastern face is sheer cliff from end to end, dropping into deep cold water. The Greyteeth reefs are at their worst in the approaches to Stenmark. Local pilots charge more here than anywhere else in the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]].

- North: [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]]
- East: [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]]
- South: [[central-strait|The Central Strait]]

## Flora & Fauna

- Windbreak gorse and sea-thrift: low, salt-tolerant scrub that holds the shallow western soil together. It's the only cover on the island tall enough to hide behind.
- Samphire: grows thick in the tide pools below the settlement, gathered fresh and pickled alongside the fish catch.
- Deep-water cod: drawn up by the cold upwelling, the basis of Stenmark's preserved-fish trade and not found anywhere else in the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]].
- [[harpy|Harpies]]: nest in the eastern cliffs and have done since before the signal station. Nobody walks the cliff path alone after dusk when they're calling.
- [[giant-vulture|Giant vultures]]: ride the eastern thermals after storms, stripping whatever the sea leaves stranded on the shelf below.
- [[giant-shark|Giant sharks]]: prowl the eastern shelf in the cold water under the cliffs, the reason local pilots refuse to swim a stranded boat in.

## The Signal Station

Built low against the wind on the eastern cliff edge. The watch rotation is a three-month posting. The station keeps a daily log covering surface colouration, fog lines, compass readings, wind direction, and unusual sightings. Logs go to the [[harwick|Harwick]] Admiralty monthly. Nobody cross-references them with the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels]]' records systematically, though those who have say it would be instructive.

## The Settlement

Stone buildings sit low with shutters that work. The cold upwelling brings deep-water fish not found further west. A modest preserved-fish trade has earned a solid reputation. Residents do not explain what else shares the water with them.

Harpies from the [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]] nest in the eastern cliffs, present since before the signal station. Giant vultures ride the eastern thermals after storms. Giant sharks prowl the eastern shelf. Below the cliff, the deeper water shows wakes and surface disturbances that catch the light wrong. Stenmark fishers have a word for these things and do not care to use it.

## Structure

La Brace holds the only licence in [[calveno|Calveno]] to burn alchemical smoke where anyone can watch. City authority stops at the Velo bridges, and [[rufio-segalla|Rufio Segalla]] built the room in that gap eleven years ago.

One low door past the second bridge opens onto a single long room. Low couches ring an open floor at the centre, lanterns hang at four heights, and eight curtained booths line the far wall, each wide enough for four people and a table between them. A counter runs the near end, and the back room behind it holds the rolling bench, the presses, and the standing order of base compound Rufio buys from [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]]. No customer has been in that room.

## Atmosphere

The blends are not the trade. An hour on a couch costs 2 gp and buys nothing but the seat. An hour behind a drawn curtain costs 15 gp, and in a city where every deal happens in the open, that price holds. Nobody enters a closed booth uninvited, Rufio included. He sells privacy, he lights the cigarettes himself while he does it, and he knows every face in the room without writing one of them down.

Shelf stock goes across the counter to anyone who walks in: [[the-boomerang|The Boomerang]] at 100 gp, [[hunters-blend|Hunter's Blend]] and [[pocket-thunder|Pocket Thunder]] at 150 gp each, alongside the other shelf blends below. The strong ones work differently. Rufio rolls those to order only. A buyer sits an hour in the room before he will take the order at all, then waits 1d3 days on the roll. He names the price at the end of that hour and never at the start of it. One to-order blend per customer per visit, whatever the offer.

## Hooks

The [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] has twice asked Rufio for a list of who books the curtained booths. He has refused twice without saying the word, and the third ask will arrive with a price attached. He wants his licence extended past the Velo bridges so a second room can open in [[mercatura|the Mercatura]], and that extension needs a Council seat's signature. Six of the seven belong to the Concordat.

Buying any to-order blend puts the buyer's face in Rufio's memory alongside the blend they asked for. He sells nothing to the Concordat today, and if the licence talks go badly enough, a name out of that memory becomes the thing he has left to trade.

If ignored, the third ask lands, Rufio takes the extension, and the curtained booths in the new Mercatura room come with a booking book from the first day.

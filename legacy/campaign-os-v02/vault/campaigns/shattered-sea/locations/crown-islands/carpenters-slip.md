# Carpenters' Slip

[[the-narrow|Narrow]] independent boatyard in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]], squeezed between two larger buildings near the Arsenal wall. A covered slip handles dry work. A canal-side berth handles assessment. Run by [[savia-brentino|Savia Brentino]].

[[savia-brentino|Savia Brentino]] fixes hulls, masts, and rush jobs. [[the-arsenal|The Arsenal]] won't take them on. Walk-ins get free checks. Pay first, work starts after.

Public work includes hull and keel checks (dry or wet), caulk jobs, and plank repair. She does masts and rudders too. Rush jobs cost double.

Savia is a dwarf, compact, with sawdust in her eyebrows, who talks in ship talk. Her first words on a hull form her greeting, and her plan to fix it forms her goodbye. Cold walk-ins hear: *"Transom's carrying too much. I can fix that."* or *"I don't do cosmetic work."*

Savia also does off-book hull work. She changes the hull shape, rail height, and rig look. The ship no longer looks like the old design.

This work needs an intro. Savia won't talk to cold walk-ins. She turned down three ships this month for this reason. You need a [[warren|Warren]] intro.

A [[warren|Warren]] member must vouch for you in person. Try [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] or another Tangle friend. Without this, Savia won't say a word about hull work. She won't even hint. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] knows Nona and can help.

> [!mechanic]
> **Re-silhouetting terms.** Cost: 220 gp + one [[the-tangle|Tangle]] favor + 5 days. The ship stays slipped the whole time. She changes the transom profile, rail height, bowsprit angle, and rig geometry. Experts no longer see it as a Crown cutter. Waterline, keel, and displacement stay the same. The ship sails the same. The favor details come later, through Nona. Non-negotiable.

> [!check] Investigation — Recognizing the Uncertainty's Hull
> Sailors with Crown navy training spot the hull lines on DC 10 (without re-silhouetting). Spotting it after re-silhouetting needs DC 20 and a look at Crown cutter plans.
> **Success:** you see it's a Crown cutter.
> **Failure:** the disguise works.

> [!quote] Savia Brentino (on the geometry)
> "A repaint does not make a cutter stop reading as a cutter. I do the geometry."

> [!quote] Savia Brentino (setting the timeline)
> "Five days. She stays slipped the whole time. Your crew finds somewhere else to sleep."

> [!quote] Savia Brentino (on the Warren's introduction)
> "The Warren sent you, so I'll do the work. The terms are the terms."

> [!mechanic]
> **If you ignore the disguise problem.** The *Uncertainty* still reads as a Crown cutter. Risk escalates by geography. The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] faction clock narrows the window every session it's ignored.
>
> | Waters | Risk |
> |---|---|
> | [[calveno|Calveno]] and neutral ports | Low (locals don't care about Crown hull lines) |
> | Crown-friendly waters (Dravosi patrol routes, Knighton-allied ports) | Hull flagged; port inspectors board for registration check; a boarding reveals the name change and triggers immediate impound or pursuit |
> | Open sea, Dravosi navy encounter | [[rupert-knighton|Rupert Knighton]] has standing orders on the *Surety* — recognition is automatic at close range, engagement follows |

Related option: [[acqua-nera|Acqua Nera]] does hull repaint and re-id work. It's just looks, not real ship changes. Unlike the Slip's real work.

## Notable NPCs

**[[savia-brentino|Savia Brentino]]** runs the Slip, a [[dwarf|dwarf]] with a small frame and sawdust in her eyebrows. She never makes timelines she can't keep, won't repeat herself, and keeps her hull work rules firm. You need a Warren intro.

## Hooks

- Savia refused three vessels discreet work this month for lack of a Warren introduction and logged their hulls. At least one was [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi]] (a patrol cutter out of [[kalowe|Kalowe]] asking questions on the causeways about independent yards).

- Separately, Savia has turned away three suspicious enquiries this month about a Maw salvage commission (unrelated to hull-disguise work). She noted vessel class and crew-size estimates for each, plus one name she's heard twice: [[batta-zusto|Zusto]]. She didn't write it down and will only say it once, to someone she trusts.

- The five-day slipping window overlaps with the [[uncertainty|*Uncertainty*]]'s existing repair at [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]]. Timed together, the *Uncertainty*'s crew loses ship access for the full duration; done sequentially, they are shipbound in Calveno for 10 days total.

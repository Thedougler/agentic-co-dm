# Casa Falier

> [!read-aloud]
> The water-door opens straight off the [[mercatura|Mercatura]] canal. The hall runs forty feet back to a stair. It climbs all four storeys without a landing worth the name.
>
> Chalk dust and lemon oil, and no cooking smell anywhere in the building, because the kitchen range has gone with everything else.
>
> The previous owner stripped the place. Furniture, sconces, the range, and every interior door came off their hinges and out the door. Now you can see through three rooms into a fourth from where you stand.
>
> An old woman sweeps the second landing while a boy carries her bucket down the stair. Both of them came with the deed. They stop when they see you, and then they wait for one of you to give them an instruction.
>
> One chair stands in the middle of the largest ground-floor room, facing a blank stretch of wall, and nobody has moved it.

## Structure

The [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] deeded the house to the crew through [[iacopo-fieschi|Iacopo Fieschi]]. It's the property tier of the debt he admitted in public after the [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|sewer magazines]] came down. It stands in the downtown core, a short walk from [[kats-curios|Kat's Curios]] and closer still to the crater.

Four storeys, a canal-level water-door, and no interior doors above it. A base of operations with nothing in it that closes.

The deed carries two household staff, retained with the house and paid out of the Concordat's side of the arrangement. Neither has met the crew. Both know the building better than the crew ever will.

**[[bastion|Bastion]] facility — Gaming Hall [HB]:** the ground floor's largest room, the one with the chair and the blank wall, doubles as a gaming hall, dice and a card table set up under the old sconce marks, run day to day by the retained staff with no crew hand needed.

| Facility | Space | Hireling | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gaming Hall | Vast | The retained steward, running the floor | *Passive:* the house's cut of nightly play brings in 150 gp each Bastion Turn (every 7 days), collected whether or not the crew directs the facility that week — Mercatura rents run steep, and this is a five-way split, not a solo take. *Active, Trade order:* once per Bastion Turn, a crew member can run the floor personally for the week instead, rolling on the Gaming Hall's own winnings table for a bigger, riskier take. |

2024 RAW gates a Gaming Hall behind character level 9; here it comes with the deed itself rather than a level unlock, same [HB] departure the *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]*'s own bastion write-up uses for ship-tier gating.

> [!check] Investigation — The Chair
> DC 12, for anyone who wonders why one chair faces a blank wall.
> **Success:** the wall carries a painted-over doorway, plastered flush and finished badly. The chair sits square to where the handle used to be.
> **Failure:** just an old room, with an old chair facing a blank wall.

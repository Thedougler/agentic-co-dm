# Ponte Bassa

A canal-side tavern built into the bridge-level masonry at the main canal crossing in [[calveno|Calveno]], [[the-bridge|The Bridge]] district. The low ceiling on the canal side is where the bridge arch comes down. The back half of the room has clearance. Seating opens directly to the water, so anyone on a passing boat can see who is eating and with whom. Most Calveno regulars treat this openness as a social feature of the place. The kitchen smells of fish stock and cheap wine.

## Stock / Services

- Grilled canal eel (5 sp): house specialty, acquired taste and all.
- Preserved meat plate (4 sp): salted pork, olives, hard cheese for
  sitting over long conversation.
- Cheap wine, carafe (2 sp)
- House spirits (ask): unmarked clay cup.
- Bed, shared room (2 sp): sleeps three.
- Canal boat hire (ask): half-day or full day rental. I don't ask
  where you're going.

## Notable NPCs

**[[oleandro-fuschi|Oleandro Fuschi]]**, the proprietor, is wide-shouldered, green-grey, and soft-spoken. He keeps a mental ledger of every vessel that has moored at the bridge canal in twenty years. He never misidentifies a vessel. If you're looking for someone who came through Calveno recently, Oleandro either knows or knows who would.

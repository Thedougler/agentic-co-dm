# Marrow's

Marrow's is a cargo broker's stall carved into the rock off the Warren's north channel corridor. It's three steps down from the main passage, in [[warren|The Warren]]. Easy to miss if you don't know it's there.

Marrow handles cargo for people: sealed documents, packages, and items the city won't touch. Shelves crowd both walls, and commerce moves fast. A brass scale and lamp sit on the central table, worn shiny from years of use.

Access defaults to Kin only. Dry visitors need a Kin introduction and Marrow's approval. He has turned away people with good introductions before and given no explanation.

Stock and services: document transit (5 gp+, price reflects content weight, not physical weight, no questions), small cargo brokerage (10 gp + 5%, three-week hold, stated value, non-negotiable terms), consignment storage (1 gp/week, three-week hard limit, after that his concern not the client's), and passage network introduction (15 gp+, Kin by default, Dry by his read and a Kin introduction, at his discretion).

A crate has sat unclaimed in Marrow's consignment corner for three weeks. See [[warren-marrows-crate|Marrow's Crate]] for the full situation. Marrow will pay 50 gp to anyone who locates the courier or the intended recipient, and will not say what's in the crate. [[serin-haut|Serin Haut]] is watching the canal approaches nearby.

## Notable NPCs

[[marrow|Marrow]] gives no other name and isn't asked for one. He's run this alcove for at least fifteen years. He is lean, with one ear notched at the tip, and dresses to match the stone walls. He weighs everything twice on the brass scale. Nobody has asked him why.

## Hooks

Marrow will pay 50 gp for information on the unclaimed crate's courier or intended recipient. He'll only discuss this with visitors he trusts.

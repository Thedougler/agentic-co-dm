# La Gatta

>
> A grey cat sleeps across the middle of the board, and the stock lies where it lay before the cat arrived.
>
> Around the cat: [[paper|paper]] cones of loose leaf, an open tin of hand-rolled cigarettes with their ends twisted shut, and a brass lighter on a chain bolted through the wood.

## Structure

La Gatta is a board on trestles wedged between a fry stall and a rope seller on the bottom tier of [[the-bridge|the Bridge]], in the one strip of sun that reaches under the arch. A customer buys standing in the crowd, because the trestles leave nowhere else to stand. The whole shop is the board, the tin on it, a brass lighter chained through a hole bored in the wood, and the grey cat that arrived before the stock did and gave the place its name.

Nothing stays overnight. [[nicco-kettley|Nicco Kettley]] packs the trestles and the tin into a locked box at the end of every night and carries it home, and the lighter goes into that box on its own. He has never held a lease, so the middle tier's shops with their doors and their locks sit one flight of stairs above everything he owns.

## Atmosphere

Everything on the board is seconds. Nicco buys short runs and failed batches out the back of [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]] through a third party, twists the ends shut himself, and undercuts every licensed room in [[calveno|Calveno]] by four parts in ten. [[the-boomerang|The Boomerang]] goes across this board at 60 gp, [[hunters-blend|Hunter's Blend]] and [[pocket-thunder|Pocket Thunder]] at 90 gp each, against a licensed room's shelf price for the same blend. A blend from here does what the blend does, most of the time, and Nicco says so out loud before he takes the money.

Buying is public. The bottom tier is the most-watched twenty feet in the city, and Nicco talks at full volume about what his regulars smoke while he counts change into a palm without breaking eye contact.

> [!mechanic]
> **Seconds [HB].** Every blend bought from La Gatta costs 60 percent of its listed price. Roll a d6 when the cigarette is lit. On a 1 the batch burns stale: no effect, and the smoker loses the cigarette. The board's blend stock is Uncommon only, 1d4 of each on any given day, and anything Nicco holds for a named buyer is neither stock nor discounted.

## Hooks

Nicco keeps a ledger in his head of which regular smokes which blend, and he trades from it whenever a seller will take gossip instead of coin. A party that buys twice from this board has entered that ledger.

[[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]] has started asking who is reselling her failed batches under the arch. She has not yet asked Nicco, and she does not know his name.

The third party moving the rejected batches out of [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] is [[anzolo|Anzolo]], who does it as a favour nobody has yet called in. His name is the answer waiting behind any push on where the seconds come from, and it holds until somebody pushes.

If ignored, Nicco takes the middle-tier lease anyway on borrowed money, the board disappears from the bottom tier, and the discount goes with it.

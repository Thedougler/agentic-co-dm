## Geography

**Extent:** roughly 300 ft. of span and 60 ft. across at the bottom tier, three levels stacked to a top rail some 70 ft. above the canal.

**Topography:** the bottom tier sits barely a step above high water and takes the wash of every passing hull. Shopfronts fill the middle tier, and above them the top rail stands high enough that a voice up there arrives as noise and nothing else. Traffic has worn the arch-stone smooth, and it turns greasy with fry oil under the stalls and slick at the stair treads where the canal damp never dries. The arch puts the whole lower tier in shadow before midday, so sight lines end at the next body, and the north stair turns three times before anyone above comes into view. Ways on and off are the four landings at the pillars: the canal frontage of [[mercatura|Mercatura]] five minutes east on foot, [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] ten minutes west, and the plank span to [[velo-quarter|Velo Quarter]] ten minutes north, foot traffic only. Below the southern pillar the water runs out to the [[central-strait|Central Strait]], and a maintenance hatch on that side drops into the sewer connector.

## Trade

Deals in intel and money change hands here in broad daylight. A buyer on the Bridge stands visible to everyone and identifiable to nobody. The bottom tier packs shoulder to shoulder at midday: fry stalls, a rope seller, and [[la-gatta|La Gatta]]'s board all fighting for the same six feet of sun. The middle tier's shops keep licensed stock and honest scales, because a shop on the Bridge that shorts a customer hears about it from three tiers at once. Prices on both levels double through the [[il-gioco-delle-beffe|Beffa]], and so does the crowd.

### Batta's Landing

The upper level's only fixed stall, a plank counter wedged against the outer rail. [[batta-zusto|Batta Zusto]] works it alone, voice pitched low enough that a buyer has to lean in to hear a price.

## Notable NPCs

- [[batta-zusto|Batta Zusto]] works the top tier alone and sells what she hears, from salvage work and port contacts up to who is paying this week for access nobody advertises.

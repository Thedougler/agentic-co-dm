## Geography

**Extent:** roughly 600 ft. of jammed canal frontage, the oldest and lowest ground in [[calveno|Calveno]].

**Topography:** the buildings settle unevenly on soft, waterlogged ground; "Le Paludi" means "The Marshes" in the old tongue. The canals twist tight enough that a boat brushes both walls at once, and nothing past the next bend stays visible from the water. Boat crosses fastest. On foot, the [[the-bridge|Bridge]]'s lower span carries east in ten minutes, with [[mercatura|Mercatura]] another ten beyond it; west, the walled service lanes skirting [[the-arsenal|the Arsenal]] take a quarter-hour of blind corners. Old drains beneath the streets lead down into the [[warren|Warren]].

## Trade

The district's market keeps no ledger and no names. Deals move on trust and reputation instead of paper, which suits [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]]'s Run and the [[warren|Warren]] traffic beneath it equally well. Good business moves quietly; bad business happens in whispers, and in canals this narrow, whispers rarely stay private for long.

## Hooks

- Old drains under Le Paludi lead to the [[warren|Warren]], a hidden Rattkin home that Calveno doesn't know about.
- Masked figures bought rope and watched the boats, asking which boatmen sleep aboard before leaving without waiting for change. Raid scouts.
- Someone posing as a Canal Inspector measured a drain hatch with a rope and no lamp; real inspectors never work that way, and that hatch feeds the main drain.
- A ferryman noticed a wall grate hanging loose that wasn't loose yesterday, and nobody's reported it.

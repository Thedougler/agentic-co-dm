# Studio Orsini

An alchemist's studio down a [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] side canal. The smell arrives before the door does. [[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]] does not ask what the compounds are for.

Studio Orsini sits down a [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] side canal that branches off the main channel and stops appearing on any canal map shortly after. Only small flat-bottomed boats or a narrow footpath can reach it, and no one maintains the path. [[calveno|Calveno]] officially does not know it exists.

The smell reaches visitors before the door does. Inside, high shelves hold sealed glass jars. Processes work at each worktable. A low lamp hangs above each station, never going out. The ceiling drips once a minute in the far corner, into a bucket that has stood there for years.

## Notable NPCs

**[[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]]**, proprietor. Late forties with ash-pale skin and a faint violet cast. Short horns, filed flat. She never leaves [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] and smells of it permanently.

She speaks with precision, knowing exactly what she is making and what each compound does. She does not answer questions about what her customers intend with her compounds. She specifies what her compounds will and will not do and gives instructions once, expecting compliance and refusing returns.

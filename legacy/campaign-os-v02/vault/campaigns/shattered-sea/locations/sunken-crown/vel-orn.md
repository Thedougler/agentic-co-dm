# Vel-Orn

Vel-Orn is the barren black-stone island that holds [[umberlee-shrine|Umberlee's shrine]] in the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]]. The name carries the local tabaxi sense of water-debt. Outsiders call it Shrine Island.

The people of [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]] hold Vel-Orn as a sacred boundary, kept apart from the ordinary world. Visible from the Lookout Stone as a black point across the water, the island remains unwelcoming and barren, offering only what pilgrims seek when they approach the shrine.

Its cultural role is debt and restraint, drawing the living only for sacred purpose. Here offerings pass into [[umberlee|Umberlee]]'s account, the drowned receive reverence, and visitors conduct themselves as if the sea itself is listening.

## Approach

A skiff must find a break in the black stone and time the surge to land on this barren, unwelcoming island. Keth-Naar guides do not speak loudly on the crossing, keep their hands visible, and do not bring fresh blood onto the rock.

## Hazards

- Sharp black stone and treacherous surge timing
- No freshwater
- Shrine taboo
- Failed protection after the Pearl's theft

## Geography

A barren black-stone island at the Blue Hole's center, kept apart from ordinary travel. The rock is sharp and unwelcoming, with no freshwater and difficult approach by skiff. It holds only what pilgrims seek when they approach the shrine.

## Flora & Fauna

- Black lichen and crusted salt-scrub cling to cracks in the stone, the island's only cover. Nothing grows edible.
- [[giant-bat|Giant bats]] cling under the black overhangs by day and hunt over open water after dark; a storm drives the whole colony out onto the open rock early, biting anything between them and shelter.
- [[raven|Raven]] swarms gather over offerings left at the shrine and over storm wreckage, growing bolder the longer a body or a bounty sits unclaimed.
- [[giant-vulture|Giant vultures]] circle after storms and land on the island's highest exposed rock, waiting out whatever carrion the surge brings in.

## Notable NPCs

- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]], captain of the fleet that stole the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] from this shrine. His theft triggered the catastrophe detailed in Hooks.

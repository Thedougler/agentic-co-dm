# Umberlee's Shrine: Sunken Crown

[[umberlee|Umberlee]]'s shrine on [[vel-orn|Vel-Orn]], cut into black stone in the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]] where five small islands ring the Blue Hole. Not a bright harbour shrine collecting routine tribute, this shrine grows older and quieter, reaching toward the drowned instead of the living.

## Geography

A shrine carved into black stone on Vel-Orn's cliff face, accessible only by difficult skiff landing at timed surge. Salt and tide have worn the entrance smooth. Kelp wraps the pillars. No freshwater or shelter exists outside.

## The Shrine

The shrine entrance is a mouth in the cliff face, worn smooth by salt spray and years of tide. Kelp wraps the pillars. Candles burn inside where wind should have killed them, and the shrine smells of brine, tallow, and wet stone, with something much older underneath. Sound behaves strangely here: footsteps soften to nothing. The shrine marks debts, keeps drowned names, and preserves an older Umberlee worship of the Blue Hole. Inside, a ledger chamber holds names, tokens, coins, fish bones, carved shells, and bits of ship or sailor preserved as witness. Every object documents a death.

Not on colonial charts. Reaching it requires prior knowledge, a guide, or difficult navigation. Vel-Orn's approach is sheer, reef-bound, and unfriendly to casual landings.

The deepest chamber formerly held the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] over a tidal pool built to its requirements. [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's fleet]] stole the Pearl before the campaign, triggering their own destruction. (See [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]] and [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] for details on the theft and aftermath.) The shrine's protective function over the Sunken Crown blessing is now failing. Umberlee has stopped waiting on the debt and collects it off the water herself; see [[the-red-wake|The Red Wake]]. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] experiences dread near this shrine.

Sessions sometimes call this shrine "Vafnar," an in-fiction mishearing of the name.

## Shrine Rooms

- **Tide Gate**: marks all who enter. [[delmar-fisk|Fisk]]'s candle gutters as he crosses.
- **Ledger Chamber**: holds [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's fleet]] scored as one breath.
- **Pearl Chamber**: the Pearl's absence registers as pressure and silence. *Detect magic* finds nothing.
- **War Room**: maps and records of the theft. Fisk's name in the margin.

## Notable NPCs

A Senior Waveservant tends this shrine, thirty years at the post, with salt-grey skin and eyes that don't track normally. To Delmar Fisk: *"The Pearl knows your name. Umberlee is patient. You have a debt, and the debt grows with every tide."* To anyone from [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]]: *"You came to ask why the seas turned. This is where the anger began."*

[[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]]'s elders carry this shrine's crisis on their own island's page, where the ration timeline and the hunters' split over open water are recorded. [[elder-anton|Elder Anton]] names the shrine directly as "the disruption origin" in his own Relationships.

## Hooks

- The Pearl of Souls rested here before Fisk's crew stole it, triggering the storm that sank his fleet.
- [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]] uses this shrine to petition Umberlee for terms. The Pearl's absence now breaks that function.

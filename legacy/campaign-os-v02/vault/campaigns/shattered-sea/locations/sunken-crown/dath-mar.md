# Dath-Mar

The smallest of the five [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]] islands. Its name preserves the local tabaxi sense of strengthened stone, a place of endurance, not comfort. A few individuals live there year-round by choice. Most use it only when they need to.

[[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]] hunters retreat here when a storm splits boats from home, when someone needs distance from the central settlement, or when a decision is too heavy to bring to the elders without resting with it first. The island's value is not what it offers, but what it can hold: a person above the water when the rest of the Blue Hole turns against them.

## Geography

A hard, exposed rock with leeward approaches, a sheltered cut, black stone, higher cracks, and tide-dependent access. Its value lies not in comfort but in survival. Current reversals around the Blue Hole have made the approach less predictable, with skiffs stranding on the wrong side of the rock more often than they used to.

## Flora & Fauna

- Sea lichen and salt-hardened scrub: the only cover the black stone holds, clinging to cracks and leeward faces, nothing tall enough to block a sightline.
- Tidal kelp and shellfish beds: exposed at low water along the island's base, gathered by whoever is already waiting out a storm here.
- [[giant-lizard|Giant lizards]]: sun themselves against the black stone after storms.
- [[giant-bat|Giant bats]]: take the higher cracks when weather grounds them.
- [[giant-crab|Giant crabs]]: work the tidal zone at the island's base. The cairn approaches cross their territory at low water, and [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]] hunters harvest selectively, managing the count without eliminating the population.

## The Cairns

Cairns mark the leeward approaches and the sheltered cut. Whoever uses them maintains them for the next arrival. Leaving one worse than you found it breaks the custom: the next person may depend on its condition to survive the day.

## People & Power

[[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]] custom governs Dath-Mar, not any formal occupation. The rule is maintenance: those who leave it better than they found it earn the island's trust, and those who don't pay the cost.

Dath-Mar offers no trade goods. People come for two things: shelter when storms roll in and tear boats apart, and time to hold difficult choices before the elders hear them.

## Hazards

Exposure, current reversals, tide windows, and failing predictability around the Blue Hole.

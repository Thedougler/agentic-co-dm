# Ral-Arn

The record island of the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]], larger than [[dath-mar|Dath-Mar]] or [[meth-var|Meth-Var]], serves those seeking answers from memory, not immediate need. The oldest family lines carved tide accounts, storm signs, records of seasons past, and family records into the stone across the island. Ral-Arn does not replace oral lore. It anchors it. A mark here is only useful if someone living can read the tale around it.

## Geography

A bare, flat island of black stone, larger than neighboring islands, carved across its surface with records of tides, storms, and family lines. The stone holds heat and resists erosion, preserving the marks left by the oldest families for generations of carvers and readers to decipher.

- North: [[shelfworks|The Shelfworks]]
- East: [[meth-var|Meth-Var]]
- South: [[dath-mar|Dath-Mar]]
- West: [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]]

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt scrub: wiry, salt-crusted growth rooted in the cracks and shaded pockets between the carved panels. The bare black stone supports no other plant cover, and the scrub is too tough to eat.
- [[giant-lizard|Giant lizards]]: bask across the sun-warmed stone paths. Visitors wait for one to clear a section before they carve or read.
- [[giant-wolf-spider|Giant wolf spiders]]: occupy shelters left empty for more than a season. Check any long-unused shelter before entering it; for one left years, probe with a pole first.
- [[raven|Raven]] swarms: gather wherever visitors leave gifts or dead fish out too long. They have learned that people mean food.

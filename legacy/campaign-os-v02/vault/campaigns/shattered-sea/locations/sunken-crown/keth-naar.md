# Keth-Naar

Keth-Naar consists of twelve black basalt promontories, clustered close together. At low tide, stepping-stone paths worn smooth by generations connect them. The island edges the Blue Hole, a deep circle of indigo water marking the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]]'s core. From the highest point, the boundary between turquoise shallows and the indigo drop is sharp.

The tribe speaks with a distinct accent: clipped consonants and a stiff, precise tone. Sailors who trade around the Sunken Crown recognize it as Keth-Naar speech.

Visitors who arrive carefully find themselves watched. Locals greet only those who seem safe. This reflects habit, not coldness. The community knows which ships read the water well and which will struggle. It anticipates what comes next.

## Districts & Landmarks

- **Central Stone**: a large black rock at the island's center. Wind and water carved it into natural caves with overhangs. Ancient symbols mark each entrance.
- **Lookout Stone**: a forty-foot platform on the east side. It offers views across the Blue Hole and nearby islands.
- **Tidal Channels**: stone channels guide fish inward when the tide rises. Hunters keep processing gear and nets nearby.

## Hazards

Giant lizards warm on black stone after rain, giant bats roost in the higher caves, and giant wolf spiders nest along abandoned paths.

Local hunters keep the island safe through knowledge, not denial. They remember where each threat sleeps. When the Blue Hole grows still and birds flee at dusk, wise sailors keep their boats clear of the indigo line.

## Notable NPCs

- [[elder-anton|Elder Anton]]: tribal elder and memory-keeper. Leads Keth-Naar through the blessing crisis alone, having sent no one after its cause.
- [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]]: washed ashore here after the [[vestra|Vestra]] sank near the Maw. Later stowed away aboard the [[saltwright|Saltwright]] from Keth-Naar's harbour.

## Hooks

- The catches have been thin. Weather around the Blue Hole is less predictable than usual.

- The birds are going inland before dark.

- Strange creatures appear below the indigo line. This marks the blessing crisis in the entire Sunken Crown, which traces back to the theft at [[umberlee-shrine|Umberlee's shrine]].

- Hunters are fracturing over whether to push beyond the Blue Hole into open water. Six have already gone and come back with a catch, and no one senior has found an argument that holds against a full net.

- Keth-Naar can endure roughly 2 more seasons at reduced rations. After that, they face open-water hunting or direct petition to [[umberlee|Umberlee]]. Six hunters have already broken the self-imposed boundary and come back with a catch, which makes the next ones easier.

- Hunters once crossed that boundary. A crew of local hunters sailed beyond the Blue Hole for the first time in years and caught fish. They reported currents cycling weekly instead of by season. Something large moved water near [[dath-mar|Dath-Mar]] but never rose.

- Local watchers see Perrin's story as a warning about the deep line between Keth-Naar and [[the-drowned-maw|the Drowned Maw]]. The signs are clear: still water, heat through the hull. A strike from below sent steam where spray should rise.

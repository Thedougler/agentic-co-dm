# Meth-Var

The easternmost of the five Sunken Crown islands, facing open waters of [[the-drowned-maw|the Drowned Maw]] on an exposed, windswept approach with little shelter for larger vessels. Meth-Var remains unoccupied year-round. The people of [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]] rotate watchers through it because the island's value is attention. It faces the direction where weather and deep movement can reach the Blue Hole before anyone in the central settlement sees the sign.

Watchers leave marks in paired sets: one for what they observe, one for who needs to hear it. A sighting without a warning carried forward counts as unfinished work.

Lately, the warning patterns have been arriving wrong.

## The Scalded Ropes

A Keth-Naar skiff did not return cleanly from the eastern watch run. The next rotation found the landing ropes bleached pale and scalded stiff, the signal stones reset in the emergency pattern, and one watcher alive in the lee crack with burned hands and no boat.

Their account is a warning. The water warmed before the attack, and steam moved low over the reef. A dark shell-ridge, too small for the old island-sized stories but large enough to break a skiff, rose from the Blue Hole edge. Meth-Var cannot say whether the wyrmling lairs in the Crown or crossed through from [[outer-reach|the Outer Reach]], but the island records that it came close enough to reach the watch route.

## Hazards

Maw weather, exposed landing, possible harpy calls, heat events, and dragon turtle wyrmling signs.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt-crusted tussock grass and wind-flattened scrub hold the soil against the wind on the sheltered slope, thinning to bare rock and lichen along the spine facing the Maw.
- Sea-fennel grows low in the lee of the rocks; watchers pick it raw against scurvy on a long rotation, and a bundle sells for a few coins in [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]]'s market.
- Rock lichen scraped from the northern face crushes into the ochre dye watchers mix for their paired marks at the signal stones.
- [[crab|Crabs]] work the tide pools below the landing at low water, easy pickings for a watcher off shift.
- [[vulture|Vultures]] ride the updraft off the cliff face most days, circling for whatever the tide leaves exposed.
- [[giant-vulture|Giant vultures]] nest on the exposed spine and stoop on anyone who lingers near their ledges, and [[giant-bat|giant bats]] roost deep in the lee crack, pouring out at dusk in numbers enough to blind a watcher reaching for a torch.

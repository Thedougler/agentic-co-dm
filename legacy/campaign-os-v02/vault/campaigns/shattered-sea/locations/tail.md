# The Tail

The Tail is the eastern end of the [[verdant-scatter|Verdant Scatter]], where both island arcs narrow and converge near [[the-drowned-maw|the Drowned Maw]]. The islands here are fewer, taller, and sharper, with high cliffs exposed to wind and stone faces that drop straight into cold deep water.

Generations living beside the trench have taught these communities to know the shelf edge by feel, the fog patterns by season, which reefs shifted last year, and when the Maw's water turns color on clear days. Less settled than the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]], not empty, and not forgiving.

Here, piracy is real and changes how ships move. Crews, traders, and salvage ships all move through tight weather windows. A patient pirate can plan around these gaps.

## Places

**Tail Staging Island** (also called Shelfworks Staging Island) is the closest inhabited Tail island to [[the-drowned-maw|the Drowned Maw]]'s western edge and the primary staging point for [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]] salvage. It has no set name or detail, letting it absorb any pressure from the Shelfworks work without copying an existing settlement.

Keep the blank deliberate: the island can become a resupply stop, salvage-camp settlement, factional pressure point, or hard-weather refuge as needed.

**Triggers:**

- Crews need supplies, replacement crew, pumps, repairs, or local salvage gossip before reaching the [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]].
- A faction needs a human-scale pressure point near the Maw without moving directly into the open-water site.

## Geography

The island arcs narrow here, forcing ships into tight channels. The shelf edge drops sharp and fast into waters near the Maw. Cold water from below brings life to these waters, feeding sharks and giant birds.

Morning fog hangs late in the eastern channels before burning off. Compass variance begins near the Maw's influence zone.

- North: [[central-strait|The Central Strait]]
- East: [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]]
- South: [[doldrums|The Doldrums]]
- West: [[midchain|The Midchain]]

## Routes & Access

Everything bound east funnels through here, because the arcs converge and the channels between them are the only water a hull can take. That geometry sets the traffic: a captain crossing to the Maw has two or three usable channels, and so does anyone waiting for them.

The fog decides the day. Eastern channels hold it late, and a pilot who knows which cliff face to run on will move in weather that keeps other crews at anchor. Local knowledge is worth more than a chart here, since the chart cannot tell you what the fog is hiding. Compass variance starts before the Maw does.

Salvage crews stage out of Tail Staging Island for the [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]], so the last supplies, the last replacement hands, and the last honest gossip are all found there.

Run the crossing per `vault/refs/vault/location/references/wilderness-travel-and-exploration.md` and `vault/refs/gameplay-toolbox.md` § Travel Pace.

## Hazards

Giant sharks work the cold shelf. Giant vultures ride cliff thermals. Giant axe beaks and giant bats on the island interiors. Harpies rumoured in the cliff systems. [[the-glass-debt|The Glass Debt]] makes [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]] traffic change course when she's active. [[the-velvet-noose|The Velvet Noose]] is a worse rumour.

## Hooks

- Tight weather windows and compressed routing create ambush geometry for patient pirates.

# Sorn

![[sorn-banner]]

From the water, Sorn looks like a green wall with the ridge high and jungle thick to the reef. The western shore has one clear landing: a beach of pale sand, the lanes behind it cleared and worked. This is where outsiders do all their business, and nowhere else.

Sorn is the farm heart of the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]], where the [[grung|Grung]] work. The Botukuri clan runs it, their name on thirty years of [[midchain|Midchain]] trade records. A purple-caste garrison watches the beach, and traders come no further than the water's edge. The interior stays closed, and outsiders stay out of it.

The raids out of the Teeth have been getting worse, and Sorn is not where they come from. That is one of the few facts about the island most traders find reassuring.

## Geography

**Extent:** roughly four miles across at the widest, a mid-sized island in the Verdant Teeth cluster. Most of its width holds terraced interior groves, not open shore.

**Topography:** reef-fringed water gives way to one pale-sand beach, the only break in an otherwise unbroken green wall. Past the cleared lanes, terraces climb. The village sits on a shelf below the highest terraces, resin-sealed longhouses lining packed-earth paths, pigment-fire smoke drifting low under the canopy most mornings. Thirty years of Botukuri families have lived on that shelf.

## Government

Past the cleared lanes, the terraces climb toward the ridge in caste order. Green caste works it first. Its families hold the lower groves closest to the beach path, tending resin taps before the heat rises and hauling pigment vats into shade by mid-morning. Purple caste holds the middle ground next, a line of stilted watch-huts between the beach and the village proper, rotating shifts against the treeline below and the ridge above. Blue-caste scouts range the cut lanes and the flooded paths past them, and captives pass through first. Gold caste keeps the high terraces at the top, where the ridge flattens before the jungle closes over it again, coming down only for ceremony or judgment.

> [!spoiler]
> The day runs the same order as the terraces. Green caste works the resin taps before first light and keeps the vats through the cooler morning hours, then hauls and harvests into the afternoon until the beach traders arrive. Purple rotates its watch at dusk, when the giant apes press hardest down from the ridgelines. Gold stays out of sight except at ceremony, and when a longhouse door opens up there, the whole shelf below notices.

## Trade

Interior agriculture, resin shipments, pigments, and alchemical specimens move through Botukuri intermediaries at the beach. The resin and pigment stock comes from tapped trees and dye-bark along the cut lanes the Botukuri keep clear for beach traffic. Those same shipments made the Botukuri name a fixture of thirty years of [[midchain|Midchain]] trade records.

## Defenses

Purple-caste patrols answer any hull that lands outside the sanctioned beach within minutes, and a second patrol closes from the ridge side the moment the first makes contact. Nobody has walked out of Sorn's interior without a Botukuri escort in longer than anyone at the beach can say.

## Routes & Access

The pale-sand beach on the western shore is the only sanctioned landing. Every other stretch of coast reads as unbroken jungle with no clear approach. Traders anchor and wait at the water's edge. The purple garrison meets any hull before its crew can go further, and nothing past the cleared lanes is open to outsiders at any tide. ## Hazards

Past the cleared lanes, the jungle hides [[giant-wasp|giant wasp]] nests and [[giant-centipede|giant centipedes]] in the underbrush. [[giant-ape|Giant apes]] press down from the high ridge on top of that. And that's all before the purple garrison itself gets involved.

## Ecology

Rainforest runs unbroken from the ridge to the shoreline, cut back only along the lanes the Botukuri keep clear for beach traffic. [[giant-wasp|Giant wasps]] nest above the cut-back treeline and swarm any worker who lingers near the cleared lanes; [[giant-centipede|giant centipedes]] get hooked out of the underbrush on poles, pincers kept well clear of bare skin. Grung patrols clear [[giant-frog|giant frogs]] from the flooded interior paths to keep the routes passable, and [[giant-ape|giant apes]] press down from the ridgelines, the one interior threat the garrison hasn't put down.

## Notable NPCs

- [[simone-tabarnack|Simone Tabarnack]] (purple-caste garrison commander) runs the deserter-hunter unit and the Dravosi toxin supply line.
- [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] grew up on Sorn before he fled after the slave escape. Simone's hunter unit has been tracking him since. His family's hut still stands empty on the path nearest the eastern grove, and nobody has claimed it.
- **Ozenne** (green-caste) has tended the eastern resin grove longer than anyone else on the shelf, and remembers Jean-Claude as a quiet child who flinched at raised voices. She has said nothing about him to Simone's unit, and nobody has thought to ask her.
- **Vrahsk** (purple-caste) stands watch on the mid-slope huts and has hunted for Jean-Claude two dry seasons running without a single lead. He follows Simone's orders without question in front of others, and without much conviction alone.

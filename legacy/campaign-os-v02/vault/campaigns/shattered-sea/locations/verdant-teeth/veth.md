# Veth

Veth is the largest island in the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] and one of the two places where outsiders meet the [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]], the other being [[sorn|Sorn]]'s own guarded beach. From the water it reads as empty and steep, no structures anywhere on the shore, the ridge climbing hard behind a single narrow strip of sand on the western coast. That strip is the whole of what Veth shows anyone.

Trade happens there and nowhere else. Traders anchor offshore and wait. Blue-caste go-betweens descend to meet them, hand goods across at the waterline, then turn back inland the moment the exchange finishes. Outsiders stay at the waterline, never follow a go-between inland, and never speak to the higher-caste Grung watching from the treeline. The waterline is the whole of the invitation. [[kalowe|Kalowe]] is the nearest free port to the north, close enough that its pilots trade Veth's rules by word of mouth before any crew tries the beach itself.

> [!read-aloud]
> The reef opens onto a beach so narrow you could walk its width in twenty strides, pale sand pinched between black water and a ridge that goes straight up behind it.
>
> Bare green covers the slope above the sand, unbroken, empty of any roofline or smoke or dock piling.
>
> A shape detaches from the treeline and comes down the slope at an easy, practiced pace, mottled blue skin, no weapon drawn, already watching your hull instead of your crew.
>
> It stops at the waterline. Exactly at the waterline. Then it waits for you to decide whether you're here to trade or here to find out what happens if you're not.

## Geography

**Extent:** roughly six miles across at the widest, the largest landmass in the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] cluster.

**Topography:** a steep ridge climbs almost directly behind the narrow western beach, and thick canopy closes over the slope within a few strides of the sand. Every other stretch of coast reads as unbroken green with no landing to speak of. Nobody has mapped the interior beyond the beach, and it stays that way by design.

## Trade

Veth accepts preserved food and worked timber. Salvaged metal, rope, and poison-crafting materials are also traded. In return, the island sends out carved bone, resin pieces, pigments, and alchemical specimens from the interior. Resin tapped from trees whose sap sets hard and dark within a season becomes the resin pieces. Dried root cut from a waxy vine rooted in the ridge's shaded pockets becomes the alchemical specimens. Go-betweens carve and dry both before they ever carry them down to the sand.

## Defenses

Higher-caste Grung enforce the waterline boundary in person, watching from the treeline while the blue-caste go-betweens trade. A trader who follows a go-between inland draws an armed response before reaching the tree line, with no warning shot fired first. Past it, nothing's safe. Merchants who call Veth safe mean the beach during trade, under watch, and nothing more than that.

## Routes & Access

The narrow western beach is the only sanctioned landing anywhere on Veth's coast. Traders anchor offshore and wait for a go-between to come down; nothing about the approach hurries that wait, and no hull puts in before one arrives. Run the crossing per `vault/refs/vault/location/references/wilderness-travel-and-exploration.md` and `vault/refs/gameplay-toolbox.md` § Travel Pace.

## Hazards

The interior beyond the permitted beach stays unmapped. Outsiders who get hurt there are on their own. No patrol comes looking for a trader who wandered past the treeline uninvited.

[[giant-lizard|Giant lizards]] and [[goat|wild goats]] work the exposed rock face by day. They're easy to spot from the beach but gone the moment a shadow crosses them. [[venomous-snake|Venomous snakes]] sun themselves on the warm limestone in the same hours. [[giant-scorpion|Giant scorpions]] climb down off the ridge after dark, and some reach the beach itself by dawn.

## Ecology

Canopy hardwood and lichen-crusted limestone cover the ridge, thick enough that the slope disappears from view a few strides past the sand. [[giant-spider|Giant spiders]] hold the canopy and [[giant-frog|giant frogs]] gather in the wet hollows below it, so a party moving inland meets one threat from above and one from underfoot before the trees even thin out.

## Hooks

- One of two doors into Grung territory means every rumor about [[sorn|Sorn]]'s guarded beach gets tested against Veth first. A party that gets turned away at one naturally tries the other next.
- A blue-caste go-between trades face to face with every hull that puts in, which means one of them could recognize a defector on sight. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] has never risked Veth's beach for exactly that reason.

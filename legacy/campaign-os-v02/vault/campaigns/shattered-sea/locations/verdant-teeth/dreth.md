# Dreth

One of the two eastern islands of the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]]. It sits so close to [[orak|Orak]] that the channel between them reads more like flooded jungle than sea. The coast is low. Inland, the rainforest thickens, and the air smells of rain and wet stone. Something works out there, but all sound stops when landing groups get near enough to hear it.

Dreth has less visible soldiers than [[karath|Karath]]. Yet it stays [[grung-clans|Grung]] land with no outside access allowed, and the channel stay is risky.

## Geography

Low coast, dense rainforest, rainwater and wet limestone smells, and a channel to [[orak|Orak]] that looks more like flooded jungle than sea. No public trade exists here. All productive work happens inland, out of reach and hidden. The [[grung-clans|Grung clans]] hold Dreth with less visible military than [[karath|Karath]], but just as much control.

- **Settlements & sites.** The Dreth-Orak channel is a shadowed route where giant crocodiles and constrictor snakes hunt. The rainforest interior remains unmapped, with work audible somewhere inland.
- **Routes & access.** No public approach exists. The channel to [[orak|Orak]] looks slow and easy from a distance, but the water and root systems favor ambush over safe passage. A giant crocodile struck a [[kalowe|Kalowe]] supply skiff in the Dreth-Orak channel, grabbing the hull at the port rail and dragging it backward until the timbers split. The pilot escaped to the bank by leaving the boat behind, and returned to [[kalowe|Kalowe]] with two fewer fingers and a clear story of a creature that held on far longer than anything that size should. The channel now has a reputation most pilots respect.

## Flora & Fauna

- Canopy vines and root thickets choke the rainforest floor into blind pockets, so anyone off the coast has to cut or wade instead of walking.
- Sour marsh figs ripen along the flooded root systems near the channel, edible raw but bitter enough that only real hunger makes them worth picking.
- [[giant-frog|Giant Frogs]] sit half-buried in the channel's silt margins, invisible until a footstep sinks close to one.
- [[giant-crocodile|Giant Crocodiles]] hunt the flat, dim water of the Dreth-Orak channel; the surface reads calm, but it hides a body that size until it strikes.
- [[giant-constrictor-snake|Giant Constrictor Snakes]] hunt the flooded channel edges from cover; the [[grung|Grung]] paths around the east swamp skirt some pools and cross others, marking exactly where the snakes lie up.
- [[giant-wasp|Giant Wasps]] nest high in the canopy on the dry east slope, and landing groups hear the colony before they see it.

## Hazards

Shadowed channel water and unbroken Grung boundary control are Dreth's standing hazards.

## Hooks

- A giant crocodile destroyed a Kalowe supply skiff in the Dreth-Orak channel, giving pilots a practical reason to avoid it.
- Sounds of inland work carry when the trees fall silent, but outsiders don't know what produces them.

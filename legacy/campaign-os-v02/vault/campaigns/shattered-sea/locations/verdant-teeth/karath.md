# Karath

>

Karath looks like the other [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] islands from the water: low reef approaches, rainforest running close to shore, bare shores with nothing to draw you in. The distinction is clear once you get close. Outsiders who nose into the reef gaps face dart and arrow fire that shoots instantly, from beach to harm without a moment's delay. Pilots who know these waters give Karath room, not because the other islands welcome ships but because Karath shoots before asking intent.

The [[midchain|Midchain]] coastal settlements have noticed the raids out of this direction are getting worse, and the [[chain-council|Chain Council]] has received the complaints. The [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]] who hold Karath answer to nobody past the tree line.

## Geography

**Extent:** roughly three miles along its spine and a mile across at the widest. One of the smaller islands in the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] cluster.

**Topography:** low reef approaches ring the coast. Rainforest runs close to shore with no clearing for a beach. Wet cuts split the interior ridges, and the shoreline stays bare and quiet, reef and jungle with nothing built on it.

## Defenses

[[dart|Dart]] and bow fire meets any hull that noses into a reef gap, arriving before the crew can hail the shore or explain themselves. Karath does not wait to learn who is asking. Deeper in, patrols nobody outside has mapped move through the interior for a reason Gold keeps even from Karath's own lower castes: they guard Gold's hidden farms, growing the one toxin gold-tier coloration requires (see [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]]). The farms are Gold's monopoly alone, and the patrols exist to keep it that way.

## Routes & Access

No sanctioned approach exists on Karath the way [[veth|Veth]]'s trade beach serves the rest of the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]]. Clan scouts watch every reef gap around the island. A hull that noses into one takes dart and arrow fire before its crew can hail the shore or explain themselves.

Pilots give Karath a wider berth than any other island in the cluster. Karath is the one island in the Teeth that shoots before it hails. Run the crossing per `vault/refs/vault/location/references/wilderness-travel-and-exploration.md` and `vault/refs/gameplay-toolbox.md` § Travel Pace.

## Hazards

Wet ridge cuts flood within minutes and turn interior travel into wading or climbing. The reef itself hides coral heads sharp enough to open a hull that strays off the marked gaps. Past the reef and the cuts, [[giant-toad|giant toads]], [[giant-venomous-snake|giant venomous snakes]], and [[giant-spider|giant spiders]] work the interior (see the fauna below). Nothing here is safe. Karath punishes anyone who gets past the gaps the same way it punishes anyone who doesn't.

## Ecology

None of what patrols Karath's interior is native. [[giant-toad|Giant toads]] squat in the wet cuts' standing pools, [[giant-venomous-snake|giant venomous snakes]] work the root tangles along the same cuts without announcing themselves before they strike, and [[giant-spider|giant spiders]] have claimed the older canopy, bridging the root gaps overhead. Every one of them is ordinary vermin, twisted into its current size by [[grung-clans|Grung]] hatchery work in long huts set back from the beach. Captive spellcasters are the ones who do the twisting, working inside wet pens behind the huts. Through the working day, a toxin keeps each captive slow and compliant. A measured antidote lets them touch the Weave, never enough to keep hold of it.

Old-growth canopy and root tangles choke sightlines to a few paces along the wet ridge cuts, so anyone off the beach climbs or hacks through instead of walking. Pale bracket fungus crowds the same standing-water pools; foragers who know Karath render it into a mild fever-poultice, worth a coin or two to a [[midchain|midchain]] trader and one of the only things that leaves this island by any route but a raid.

## Hooks

- Raids traced back to the Teeth keep worsening, and pilots agree Karath is the island they fear most. Whichever ship finally proves why will earn the attention of the [[chain-council|Chain Council]], which has the complaints but no way past the reef gaps.
- The captive spellcasters chained in Karath's hatchery are the same slavery [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] has committed to ending. Finding them means getting far enough inland to look.
- Gold caste's hidden toxin farms are a secret from Karath's own lower castes, not just from outsiders. Whoever exposes them turns Karath's isolation into a fracture between castes instead of a shared wall.

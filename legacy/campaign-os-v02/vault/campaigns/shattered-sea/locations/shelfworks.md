# The Shelfworks

![[shelfworks-narration-appearance]]

You find the Shelfworks by following the boats. Open ocean stretches around salvage vessels at anchor. Marker buoys, air-pump stations, and dive lines dot the water. Clarity tricks the eye, making depths seem less deep than they are.

At forty feet, light hits pale [[antheri|Antheri]] stone. Walls and archways stand intact. Fish swim through rooms. Coral covers the lintels. Small [[reef-shark|reef sharks]] claim the halls.

Then the shelf ends. Twenty metres east of the last visible wall, the ruins drop away with no visible bottom. The water darkens to a directionless blue with no floor beneath it. Experienced salvagers avoid looking east if they can help it.

## Routes & Access

Access the Shelfworks from open water by sailing to the anchored salvage stations on the western shelf of the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]]. Most approaches require fair weather and calm seas. Crews must bring their own air-pump facilities or hire experienced salvage operators. Diving equipment, rope discipline, and depth tolerance are essential. Refer to [[wilderness-travel-and-exploration|travel mechanics]] for open-water journey pacing. The salvage season depends on weather windows and tide, with winter crossings especially dangerous.

## History

In 1488 DR, [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] salvaged working Antheri parts from the upper ruins and brought them back to [[calveno|Calveno]]. This sparked the Shelfworks Goldrush. Prospectors, scholars, and criminals flooded the site. They picked the easy salvage clean. What remains now is harder to reach, identify, and retrieve.

The [[red-lady|*Red Lady*]] wreck sits on the eastern edge of the Shelfworks shelf. The [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] is still aboard.

## What's Here

**Upper ruins (40 to 80 ft)**. Free-dive here or use air pumps among walls and archways that stand intact despite collapsed floors and dark fittings that resist rust. Earlier salvagers took most items, though fixed structural parts remain.

**Mid-shelf (80 to 150 ft)** (requires air pumps). Intact room structures with sealed chambers. The true [[antheri-ruins|Antheri Ruins]] begin here. Ancient builders designed them for creatures with no need for floors or normal doors.

**The drop-off (150 ft+)** (the shelf ends and the water column opens into the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]]). Nothing survives an uncontrolled descent into that water. Nobody looks at it if they can help it.

## Hazards

Reef sharks in the upper ruins. Hunter sharks along the shelf edge. Giant squid in the deeper channels. [[the-glass-debt|The Glass Debt]] occasionally makes salvage traffic change course. At the drop-off: the Drowned Maw itself.

Recovery faces three key obstacles. The boundary zone corrodes equipment and instruments. The [[leviathan|Leviathan]] patrols the retrieval zone. [[auralis|Auralis]] withholds key details. Also, [[umberlee|Umberlee]] and the salvagers may want different outcomes once someone retrieves the artifact.

## Notable NPCs

[[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] is the leading expert on Antheri materials. In 1488 DR, she salvaged the first working pieces from the upper ruins. She sparked the Shelfworks Goldrush. She trades for intact Antheri metalwork from the Shelfworks.

## Hooks

- The [[red-lady|*Red Lady*]] wreck sits on the eastern shelf edge, with the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] still aboard.
- [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] trades for intact Antheri metalwork sourced from the Shelfworks.
- The [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] holds standing orders for intact structural Antheri pieces. Da'Virelli has not accepted them.

- Salvage crews reach the Shelfworks, find the *Red Lady*, or a fissure opens. Any of these can trigger [[pearl-of-souls|Maw Pearl Crisis]].
- Take the Pearl out: stops the invasions. The fissure becomes just a geological hazard.
- Give it to [[umberlee|Umberlee]]: crisis ends, but she gains a major artifact.
- Keep it: gives leverage but escalates the danger.
- Leave it: attracts more entities and harms access to the Shelfworks and *Red Lady*.

- If [[auralis|Auralis]]'s containment fails: the upper tiers flood. The gold rush ends. The Antheri machine activates.

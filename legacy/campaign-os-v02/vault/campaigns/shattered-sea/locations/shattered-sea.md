# The Shattered Sea

Sixty thousand square miles of reef, deep channel, old paperwork, old gods, and water that remembers. The islands run in two arcs around a wide channel. The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] holds the west with navy law and stamped papers. The [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] holds the east by debt.

[[galewall|The Galewall]] storm belt marks the edge of the known coasts. [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]] marks the edge of the known world. Between the two, the [[verdant-scatter|Scatter]] Current runs clockwise. The politics run crooked.

## Routes & Access

Ships ride the [[verdant-scatter|Scatter]] Current, which runs clockwise around the central channel. Which lane a captain takes decides what they pay with. In the west the Crown wants papers. In the east the Concordat wants a debt it can call in later. Past the [[galewall|Galewall]], neither flag reaches.

## Hazards

Reef work is the constant. Everything else here is somebody's claim on somebody else: the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] and the [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] run rival lanes over water they both say is theirs.

Every harbour pays [[umberlee|Umberlee]] through the [[waveservants|Waveservants]]. That makes weather a political matter as much as a sailing one. And in the east the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]] takes ships that stray too far off a known lane.

The Antheri Ruins sit on the western wall of [[the-drowned-maw|the Drowned Maw]], starting at sixty feet down. They descend to depths where no diver returns with a clear report. The stone is pale and won't corrode or foul. Experts can't identify it. Life doesn't grow on it. Pieces brought to the surface stay clean.

The design is strange at the top and wrong below. Builders used stairs that weren't stairs, floors that weren't flat, and no air for breath. They built for swimmers, not walkers. Shafts drop into black water. Spheres link by wide arches. The Antheri swam here with bare bodies, moving in every way.

### The Shelfworks Tier (0-60 ft)

The top tier runs from surface to sixty feet down. Above that, normal dive teams with air pumps and ropes can work.

The civic halls are the largest rooms: open volumes with channels cut into the floor in branch shapes. What flowed there has stopped. Seagrass fills the channels now.

The workshop sits one level down, reachable by swimming sideways through middle passages. Work stations dot the walls. Their tool hooks have five fingers but are smaller than a human hand. Most no longer work. A few still function. Salvagers have learned to avoid starting them by accident.

Builders cut the residential sections directly into the trench wall as identical alcoves sized for beings smaller than humans. They sit empty, left bare by choice.

Dark alloy stays functional and cold to the touch even at forty feet, never staining. [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina DaVirelli]] was the first to adapt a recovered mechanism into working order. That success is what brought salvagers here.

Wall reliefs run throughout in geometric patterns. A triangle inside a circle marks the sealed rooms. Salvagers learn: rooms packed with the triangle-in-circle mark aren't worth opening.

### The Abyssal Bore (60-200 ft)

Past the thermocline, sunlight ends and compass drift starts. Armoured crustaceans hunt deeper passages. Blind eels with light-organs nest in old pipes. [[whip-shark|Whip sharks]] patrol deeper, drawn up by the rhythmic pulse of surface pumps. That pulse reaches them from well above sixty feet.

The danger is shifting deeper. Crews that worked the eighty-foot tier last season without trouble won't return this season. Severed air lines, cold patches in upper water, and light changing color at depth. All happen without clouds above.

### The Abyssal Threshold (200+ ft)

Those who reach the lower depths don't return with sense to report. What surfaces tells its own story. Severed hoses come up, along with crushed suits and manifests that claim only "recovered equipment" while crew counts sit blank. Organized descents below two hundred feet always fail. The salvage records at [[tidefall|Tidefall]] document three incidents, none read accidental.

### People & Power

The [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] runs the spy war. The best old maps vanished into debt deals. The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] tries to claim the waters as its own, but this border resists clean rule. Independent crews work the spaces between.

# Governor's Seat

A stone building stands back from Port Tidefall's main trade street. The Dravosi Crown runs it from here, turning disputes, appeals, and appointments into city law.

## Appearance & Access

Pale stone building with iron shutters on the ground floor. The largest Dravosi flag in the city flies above the door. Guards in formal dress stand at the entrance with stiff posture and calm eyes. A small brass placard near the door says: *formal petitions, First Tide, Tuesday.*

This office handles disputes, appeals, and petitions. All become law here. The guards know every face on the Shelf. They have power to enforce their orders. Formal petitions need a time slot. Urgent work needs an appointment or higher rank.

## Governor's Situation

Governor Voss held the post for seven years before the Admiralty locked him out and sent soldiers to [[fort-crestwall|Fort Crestwall]] with secret orders he never saw. He asked for details three times but received no answer. Now Voss will trade his access and secrets to learn what the Admiralty plans.

Secret orders sit locked in Fort Crestwall. They list reef gear and explosives headed to [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]]. These orders come from the "Maw Surveillance Directorate." Voss cannot find proof this group even exists. The directorate is new, secret, or run by officers he does not know.

Voss is not hostile. He feels trapped and confused by the chain of command. Show him respect and treat him as someone to work with. He can give names, supply lists, and a pass to Fort Crestwall.

> [!check] Persuasion — Manifest Addendum Without Full Quid Pro Quo
> DC 13.
> **Success:** Voss hands over the manifest addendum without the full trade.
> **Failure:** he holds it back until the trade is complete.

> [!check] Deception — Bluffing Past the Guards
> DC 16; bluffing past the guards without using Fisk's name.
> **Success:** the bluff works.
> **Failure:** the guards refuse entry.

This situation connects to the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] (the Admiralty authority behind the sealed companies) and the [[pearl-of-souls|Maw Pearl Crisis]] (the Maw operation this ties into).

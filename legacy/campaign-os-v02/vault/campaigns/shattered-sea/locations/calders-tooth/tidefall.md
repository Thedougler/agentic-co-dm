# Tidefall

[[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] searched this place for [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] after her ship sank. He told her so when they met again.

The cliff of [[calders-tooth|Calder's Tooth]] has three levels: the Dockfront at water level, the Shelf on the cliff, and [[fort-crestwall|Fort Crestwall]] at the top with guns. The [[inspection-pier|Inspection Pier]] sits at the narrow harbour mouth, stopping ships before they reach the docks. Black nooses hang above the gate, and no one recalls when they went up. Every ship entering the western [[central-strait|Central Strait]] must stop, answer questions, and show papers. Most do.

## Districts

**The Dockfront.** At the waterline, ships enter through the [[inspection-pier|Inspection Pier]] for checks and proceed to the [[harbourmasters-office|Harbourmaster's Office]] for vessel ratings and charts. **The Shelf.** This mid-cliff trade zone lets traders buy licenses and supplies while officers work and make side deals. **[[fort-crestwall|Fort Crestwall]].** Officers-only access to a fort containing barracks, a command post, weapons storage, a court, and guns above the bay.

## Garrison Status

The garrison strength has grown in recent weeks. Officials with knowledge of the matter don't know why.

> [!dm]
> Extra companies arrived at [[fort-crestwall|Fort Crestwall]] under Admiralty-sealed orders that Governor Voss never received. Supply manifest shows reef-diving equipment and underwater charges for [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]]. See [[the-governors-seat|Governor's Seat]] for details.

## Notable NPCs

Governor Voss. Civic Crown authority at the [[the-governors-seat|Governor's Seat]], kept in the dark about [[fort-crestwall|Fort Crestwall]]'s Admiralty-sealed orders.

## Hooks

The garrison has grown. Those with knowledge cannot explain why. Supplies stage from the port for unknown reasons, and someone withheld sealed orders from the Governor.

[[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s overdue tithe at the [[port-tidefall-waveservant-shrine|Waveservant Shrine]] ties polite shrine pressure to the broader [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] crisis.

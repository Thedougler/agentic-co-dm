# The Anchor & Line

The only tavern on the [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]] Dockfront proper, serving cheap rum, long benches, and a screened back room for the port's [[rattkin|Rattkin]] workers. Dock workers, sailors, and general traffic pass through here, while port officers drink up on the Shelf at [[hook-and-ledger|The Hook & Ledger]] instead.

## Structure

Low ceiling, long benches, a bar running the full length of one wall. The smell of cheap rum, salt, and wet rope hits first. A canvas hangs across the back third of the room. Behind it sit smaller chairs and a lower ceiling, still cheap, still full of Rattkin dock workers. A broad-shouldered Human woman tends the bar and knows everyone's name. Crown patrols check the front room only. The barkeep leaves the back room untouched. This arrangement has lasted six years.

## Front-Room Talk

Overheard without prompting, front of house: the garrison is running over capacity (see [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]]'s Garrison Status); [[barnaby-rook|Rook]] impounded a catch on a technicality; the [[tessarine-trade-house|Tessarine Trade House]] had a quiet diving accident.

Six dockfront labourers drink here from the sixth bell.

## Notable NPCs

**Barkeep.** A broad-shouldered Human woman who knows everyone's name after one visit and nobody's business after ten years. No name given in source.

**[[vrtek|Vrtek]].** A Rattkin dockhand found in the back section sorting his pockets.

**Sosse.** A regular who tells [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] (after the third drink) that Vrtek has something official that does not belong to him. No further detail given in source.

## Hooks

The back room can surface [[rattkin|Rattkin]] Dockfront rumours that Crown patrols do not bother hearing. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] can shelter in the canvas-screened back section. Crown patrols only check the front due to the six-year informal arrangement noted above.

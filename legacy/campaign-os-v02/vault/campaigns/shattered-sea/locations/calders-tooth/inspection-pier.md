# The Inspection Pier

The Inspection Pier is where vessels stop first when entering [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]]. Crown officials rate each ship, inspect its cargo manifest, and levy fees here before allowing it to reach the main docks.

## Structure

The pier stretches out into the harbour mouth with wide planks and cleats on both sides. At the far end sits a covered station where [[dravosi-crown|Crown]] clerks work. They handle ratings, manifests, and fees all day long.

The deck bustles with activity. Sailors clutch paperwork while petty officers scowl nearby. Clerks with ink-stained fingers compare the cargo to what captains claim they're carrying. The place feels like a necessary checkpoint no one enjoys passing through.

At high tide, the water sits just below the deck. At low tide, mud and barnacle-crusted pilings show beneath it.

**Below the Pier.** At low tide, exposed mud sits under the pier. Voices echo down from the deck while you crouch among the pilings. At high tide, water fills this space.

## Vrtek's Cache

[[vrtek|Vrtek]] unknowingly carries a note. It holds [[detto-kalash|Detto Kalash]]'s directions to a buried cache at the 3rd piling from the harbour mouth, south face, 2 ft below the mussel line at spring low tide. The low-tide window runs roughly 2 hours starting at the third bell.

Contents: 740 gp of old Dravosi gold, 60 gp of [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] silver, 40 gp jade fish, and an oilskin letter proving a Crown inspector took bribes 15 years ago.

[[barnaby-rook|Rook]] periodically checks the waterline. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s silhouette matches a nearby bounty notice, so he should coordinate from outside or approach from the water side.

## Hooks

[[vrtek|Vrtek]] doesn't know what he's carrying, and a party who tips him off, or who beats [[barnaby-rook|Rook]] to the piling, changes who ends up holding the cache. A Crown clerk quietly flagging cargo for extra fees is a lead into how deep the bribery the oilskin letter proves actually runs.

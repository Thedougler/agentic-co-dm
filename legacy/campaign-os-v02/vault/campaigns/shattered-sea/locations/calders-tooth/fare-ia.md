# Fare I'a

![[fare-ia-banner]]

Fare I'a is a small dock-market stall in [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]]'s Dockfront market, selling Grung-style fish jerky to sailors, dock workers, and anyone hungry enough to trust the smell.

Sweet brine, char, and pepper heat the air, and you smell the stall before you see it. The counter sits low with dark jerky and red marinade in pots. The scent brings buyers. Behind it, a green [[grung|Grung]] vendor wraps fast and watches close.

I'a Miti (a fish jerky parcel, 2 cp): "Sweet brine, spiced. Dock workers buy it every day. Sailors buy it on a dare and come back."

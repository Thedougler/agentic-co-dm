# Waveservant Shrine (Port Tidefall)

A shrine to [[umberlee|Umberlee]] on the Dockfront at [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]]. Cut into the cliff base at the southern end of the district, it's where mariners pay their dues before sailing or after they return.

Smell hits you first, tallow mixed with salt. Carved from the cliff face, the shrine sits deep. Bare stone walls greet you. The roof presses low. Water fills a basin at the centre, candles burning all around.

The Waveservants follow a simple rule: mariners must pay a tithe. Pay before you sail, or pay after you survive. Both work. But payment is not optional. A junior attendant watches the candles and basin. A senior Waveservant handles everything else.

A mariner who skips tribute creates a social and spiritual problem before the next departure.

## Notable NPCs

The senior Waveservant has no name in our records. [[branca|Branca]]'s page says [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]] is her usual post. This shrine is where she works. But Session 04 shows her at the [[calveno|Calveno]] harbor shrine instead.

The senior Waveservant knows [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] is in port and that his 15 gp tithe appointment is overdue. She stands firm and will not waver.

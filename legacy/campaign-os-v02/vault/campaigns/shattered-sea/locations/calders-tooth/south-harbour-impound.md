# [[south-harbour|South Harbour]] Impound

![[south-harbour-impound-banner]]

## Structure

The east pier of [[south-harbour|South Harbour]] ends at a chain hung between two iron posts, a hand-lettered sign wired to the middle: *Crown Impound — No Access Without Clerk Authorization.* Beyond the barrier, vessels sit low in their berths, going nowhere. A lamp burns in the small hut at the pier head, staffed by a [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] impound clerk.

Debt accumulates and ships don't move. Release requires full payment of outstanding fees plus a processing charge, yet the garrison does not staff the impound directly. The chain and the ledger serve as the enforcement mechanism.

## Hooks

A vessel stuck behind the chain can turn fees, debt, or paperwork into a practical obstacle for ship captains and merchants seeking passage.

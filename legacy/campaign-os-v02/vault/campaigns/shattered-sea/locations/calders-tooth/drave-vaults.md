# Drave Vaults

Sealed sub-basements lie beneath [[fort-crestwall|Fort Crestwall]] in [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]]. These are the Drave Vaults. Crown surveys list them as errors from the fort's original build.

No one may enter. The only way in is through Fort Crestwall's foundation. That puts any entry under Crown military control. Getting in faces sealed doors, military guards, unknown dangers, and no good maps.

The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] keeps the site sealed. It hides all records. Only one old architectural note says they exist.

The vaults work well as a dungeon seed. They sit beneath Port Tidefall's most visible fortress.

## Hooks

The Crown's own surveys admit the sub-basements exist but refuse to share useful detail. This leaves an open question for any party that goes looking.

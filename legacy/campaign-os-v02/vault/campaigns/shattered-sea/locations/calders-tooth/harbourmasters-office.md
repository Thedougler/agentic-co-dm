# Harbourmaster's Office

![[harbourmasters-office-banner]]

This is the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] office in [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]], where all ships must visit after inspection. The office, housed in a stone building at the Dockfront's base, handles ship ratings, impounds, fees, and charts through clerks who manage ledgers and process paperwork at a counter behind a partition.

The exterior wall displays a cork board, sun-warped and crowded with fading notices. Crown-sealed postings occupy the top, while others pin notices below. Harbour clerks work the counter and ledgers. [[reginald-fenn|Reginald Fenn]], a chart clerk and elf, once surveyed routes and works from Crown charts he helped draft. The Crown values his accuracy. Official surveys sit at the counter: [[central-strait|Central Strait]] and [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] charts stock well, though [[midchain|Midchain]] coverage remains partial (ask the clerk for it).

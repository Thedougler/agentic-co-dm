# Osha's Slip

A fishing village built on stilt-platforms over the southeastern mudflats of [[calders-tooth|Calder's Tooth]]. The villagers trap mud-crabs, spear river-eels, and maintain a rope-and-plank bridge network that shifts with wind and tide. No solid roads connect this place to the mainland. Reaching it from [[tidefall|Tidefall]] means crossing mudflats. This is slow and unpleasant at any tide. In full armor, it's impossible at high tide.

The village is independent in the way small places far from the fort tend to be. The Crown's authority here remains nominal. [[the-passage|The Passage]] uses Osha's Slip as a favored night-landing site for small high-value cargo.

Stilts raise homes and work decks above the mud, connected by rope-and-plank bridges that shift with wind and tide. Outer boardwalks extend into the reed-bed margins and tidal pools, but villagers keep children off them after dark.

Giant crabs are the settlement's primary harvest and its baseline hazard. Giant frogs hunt the reed-bed margins at dusk. Giant constrictor snakes work the eel channels. Villagers watch for crab traps dragged sideways, reed beds flattened in a single smooth line, and water moving against the tide.

# Tidefall Chandlery

The Tidefall Chandlery sits at the Dockfront end of the Shelf and serves as [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]]'s main shop for ship goods. Outside, barrels and crates stack high. Inside, a long counter fills the space with a price board hanging where all can see it. The garrison permits the shop to stock rope, tar, oakum, signal lanterns, fittings, food, weapons, armour, and tools (all Crown naval gear). [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] goods and rare items are hard to find, but naval gear is easy to get. At the back sits a locked glass case holding sea magic: [[healing|healing]], [[water-breathing|water breathing]], talking stones, and language tools. The clerk knows it all.

## Stock (Selected)

- Weapons and armour suitable for shipboard work
- [[rope]], hooks, blocks, tackle, sail repair supplies, tar, oakum, chains, signal lanterns, flags
- Ship provisions: rations, fresh water, rum, salted fish, vinegar, limes
- Practical tools including navigator's tools, forgery kits, thieves' tools, writing supplies
- Common magic useful at sea: healing, water breathing, communication, language

## Notable NPCs

[[nigel-strutt|Nigel Strutt]] is the clerk. A young Human man, he helps customers but makes clear exactly what he will share. He knows the price board and stock list well. He volunteers nothing more.

## Hooks

Specialist arcane work and Tessarine goods require other sources. The chandlery covers the basics only.

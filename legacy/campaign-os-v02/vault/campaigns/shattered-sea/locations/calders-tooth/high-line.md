# High Line

High Line is a fishing hamlet carved into the northeastern cliffs of [[calders-tooth|Calder's Tooth]]. It has no beach and no docks. Instead, counterweighted winches drop nets into cold upwellings rich with yellowtail, then haul the catch back up the cliff face.

The settlement stacks vertically through ladders, platforms, stone stairs, and winch-baskets that move people between levels. Local folk know every handhold and wind pattern. Visitors take time to learn which gaps are deadly.

**Winch Line.** Massive counterweighted wooden winches bolted to the cliff edge lower nets down the face and haul the catch back up.

**Rope Platforms.** Work stages, ladders, creaking stairs cut into stone, and winch-baskets that double as transport between levels.

## Fauna & Hazards

The cold upwelling that makes High Line's nets worth the effort draws Hunter Sharks ([[hunter-shark|Hunter Shark]]) into the water column below the cliffs. Winch operators read the surface for shark sign before sending repair crews down the net face on rope stages. Giant crabs occupy the boulder field at the base of the cliff; the crew harvests them when surf is low, though the crabs are large enough to require armed crews. Harpies nest on the northern cliff faces. During low-wind conditions, when the birds are most active and winch operation is noisiest, the hamlet posts watches.

Hunter sharks below the cliff face, giant crabs at the base, harpies on the northern faces, and vertical drops throughout.

## Hooks

A winch crew stopped sending word up from the rope stages below the cliff. The fishing families want someone to check on them before the [[hunter-shark|Hunter Shark]] sign gets worse.

A harpy has also started nesting closer to the winch lines than usual. It already cost one platform its whole week's catch. The families would pay well to have it driven off.

# Cape Solitude

Cape Solitude is the northernmost tip of [[calders-tooth|Calder's Tooth]]. Here the island meets open ocean with heavy surf, constant wind from the [[galewall|Galewall]], and nowhere to hide. Local fisherfolk stay clear, and they won't explain why if you ask. The short answer: the Dead Light.

## Geography

- North: [[crown-islands|The Crown Islands]]
- East: [[high-line|High Line]]
- South: [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]]
- West: [[galewall|The Galewall]]

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt scrub and wind-flagged gorse: the only cover on the headland, clipped low and combed one-sided by the wind off the [[galewall|Galewall]]. Nothing taller survives out here.
- Sea samphire wedged in the boulder cracks: edible raw and salty enough to season a week of rations, the one thing on the cape worth stopping to gather.
- [[giant-goat|Giant goats]] picking along the cliff edges: unbothered by the drop, gone at a scramble the moment a party gets within a rope's length.
- [[giant-vulture|Giant vultures]] riding the cliff thermals: circle the point in slow, searching loops that suggest they expect a meal.
- [[giant-crab|Giant crabs]] crowding the boulder field at the base: the largest, fiercest colony on the island. No one climbs down there unarmed.

## Description

Open ocean batters Cape Solitude directly, bringing heavy surf and steady wind from the [[galewall|Galewall]]. Boulders crowd the base, strong air currents sweep the cliffs, and a ruined old tower marks the point.

## Access & Approach

Getting here is dangerous. Open ocean and heavy surf make it hazardous. Local boats stay at least two miles away. Clifftop winds carry strange sounds that locals fear.

## People & Power

The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] tried to build a lighthouse here. Work crews kept going missing. The sites stayed intact (tools in place, camps empty). The Crown erased the project from all records.

## Notable Features

**The Dead Light**: a stone tower from before the Crown marks the point. It stands empty. No one has examined it since the crews vanished.

Locals have avoided the cape for longer than the lighthouse project has stood. On windy nights, fisherfolk report voices from the cliffs: not mere wind, but sounds with shape and purpose.

## Hazards

Open-ocean exposure, heavy surf, cliff voices, and the unresolved disappearance of three lighthouse crews.

# Fort Crestwall

![[fort-crestwall-banner]]

Fort Crestwall sits on [[calders-tooth|Calder's Tooth]] cliff above [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]]. The stone is pale and salt-worn. Cannon point at the port from the sea-facing side. From the sea, the fort stands out above all. The Dravosi flag flies high.

The fort holds the Crown's power from the cliff above the port with cannon ready. Entry needs a pass. Guards man the gate (two out front, one at desk with records) while work goes to the Shelf. No entry without a pass. This won't change.

For three weeks, the garrison grew beyond normal size. The yard holds more troops than the barracks fit. No one explains this.

> [!dm]
> Sealed sub-basements called the [[drave-vaults|Drave Vaults]] lie beneath the fort. They serve as [[aldric-drave|Aldric Drave]]'s hidden lair and phylactery network, not merely storage. See [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] DM Only for the wider revelation. Destroying the network is a surgical, archival problem and not a military one: the Crown's own genealogical apparatus is the only thing that can locate every anchor.

The fort holds two separate secrets: the vaults deep below, and sealed orders in the Commandant's office above.

> [!dm]
> The Commandant's office here holds the sealed orders behind the recent garrison buildup. See [[the-governors-seat|Governor's Seat]] DM Only for the full sealed-orders thread and [[tidefall|Tidefall]] DM Only for the summary. This thread differs from the [[drave-vaults|Drave Vaults]] below the fort. Don't conflate them unless the characters reach underground.

> [!mechanic]
> **Entry past the gate.** An Admiral's commission or named pass grants full access; a Harbourmaster's writ works for logistics business. [[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]]'s commissioning authority doesn't reach past the gate. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]'s old commission name still opens it (see [[the-governors-seat|Governor's Seat]] DM Only for how gate officer Crowe let it slide).

> [!check] Persuasion or Deception — Talking Past the Gate
> DC 18, for anyone without a commission, pass, or writ.
> **Success:** talks a way through.
> **Failure:** the guards remain unmoved.

## Notable NPCs

**Duty Sergeant** (unnamed). Holds a 7-bead [[necklace-of-fireballs|Necklace of Fireballs]] (worth 3,000 gp, 2,500 gp with leverage) pulled off a smuggler who died in custody before it made the ledger. Wants it gone before the embarkation audit (see Hooks). No page yet.

**Crowe** (gate officer). Let [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]'s old commission name stand in the rotation (see DM Only and Governor's Seat DM Only). No page yet.

## Hooks

The garrison overcrowding is an open, visible anomaly. No one at the gate will account for it. This is a hook for players approaching or attempting to gain access to the fort. The sealed orders behind it form the deeper hook (see DM Only).

The Duty Sergeant's Necklace of Fireballs is a time-limited opportunity: the embarkation audit is coming.

Governor Voss is willing to trade access and information for the sealed-orders thread (see [[the-governors-seat|Governor's Seat]] DM Only for terms).

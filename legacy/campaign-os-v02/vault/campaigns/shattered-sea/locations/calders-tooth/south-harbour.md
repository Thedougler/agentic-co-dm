# South Harbour

![[south-harbour-impound-banner]]

South Harbour sits on the calm side of [[calders-tooth|Calder's Tooth]]. The island slopes down into mud and shallow water here.

Official records call it a secondary anchorage. In truth, fishing fleets, shipwrights, traders, and repair crews call it home. They keep the island going. The Crown focuses on [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]] to the west.

The Crown rules here, but not firmly. Patrols arrive unannounced, sometimes within hours of each other. Tidal mud makes armored inspection hard at low tide.

[[waveservants|The Waveservants]] work quietly here. Cargo that might bring questions at Tidefall lands at South Harbour instead.

## Districts & Landmarks

**East Pier**: the most visible official infrastructure. The [[south-harbour-impound|South Harbour Impound]] sits here. Chains and records keep ships from leaving.

**Mudflat Estuary**: shallow channels with crab traps and fishing lines among mangrove trees. Giant snakes may live in the deep channels to the south.

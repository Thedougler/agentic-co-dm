# Tessarine Trade House

## Structure

A pale stone building rises three storeys on [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]]'s Shelf, marked by a [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] pennant at the door. Inside stand four chairs and a clerk's desk, and the air smells of ink and cedar oil. This office requires an appointment to enter. The ledger slides wordlessly across the counter, and later slots take weeks to reserve. This office issues trade licenses and brokers deals, and it sends couriers across the region. A salvage contract sits open for any captain with a ship: the Shearwall Reef job pays 800 gp (40% share), though one team already tried and failed, with the contract expiring in 10 days.

The wreck sits 30 to 40 feet down with cargo at 35 feet, a large creature within, diplomatic pouches and a strongbox inside, and luxury crates filling the hold. The cargo is worth about 320 gp, which forms the basis of the 40% share.

## Maret

Maret is a Human dockworker and the sole survivor of the prior salvage dive. He now sits in the back office under a Concordat debt-recovery hold. He's not afraid of the creature in the wreck. He fears going back down alone.

> [!check] Insight — Reading Maret
> DC 12; talking to Maret about the dive.
> **Success:** it's clear his fear is going back alone, not the creature.
> **Failure:** he reads only as reluctant and evasive.

Once [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] learns that debt law chains a dockworker in the back office, he will likely act.

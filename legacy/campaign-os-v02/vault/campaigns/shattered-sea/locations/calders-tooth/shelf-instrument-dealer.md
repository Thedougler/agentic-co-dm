# The Shelf Instrument Dealer

A shop on [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]]'s Shelf. Captains can buy sextants and [[spyglass|spyglasses]] here. Most other Crown Islands shops order from [[calveno|Calveno]].

Glass cases hold sextants, compasses, and chronometers. One chronometer has a crack and costs less. Charts and almanacs fill the back. Used items sit on felt by the counter.

## Notable NPCs

[[oswald-crench|Oswald Crench]] is a dwarf. Years of peering through lenses made him squint. He calibrates instruments for Crown patrols. He won't chat or haggle.

## Hooks

- A used spyglass (600 gp) with unknown history. Crench doesn't know who owned it before or how he got it.
- [[midchain|Midchain]] charts are incomplete. Local pilots know more than the shop stocks.

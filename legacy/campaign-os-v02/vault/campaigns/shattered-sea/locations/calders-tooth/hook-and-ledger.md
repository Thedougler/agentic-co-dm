# The Hook & Ledger

The Hook & Ledger is a tavern on the Shelf road in [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]]. Port officials drink here to catch port gossip. Clerks from the [[harbourmasters-office|Harbourmaster's Office]] visit during breaks. It sits far from noise yet close to the harbor.

A brass ship's hook hangs over the bar. Ledgers sit on shelves behind the bottles like sacred books. Candlelight shines through dusty green glass. The room smells of lamp oil, wet wool, damp paper, and sour wine. Nobody stops talking when a stranger enters. But the careful tables do get quieter.

[[hector-podge|Hector Podge]] runs the room. He is a heavyset Human man in his fifties, grey and steady. Crown officers know him by sight. He keeps the room respectable enough for uniforms yet loose enough for private deals.

The regulars handle paperwork for port business. They include pursers, quartermasters, freight factors, impound runners, and petty officers. Some are shipmasters who prefer drinking here to going home.

**Main Room.** Round tables crowd close together. Each table has old cup rings and ink stains. Shipping news, berth changes, and port gossip flow here. It all becomes official record later.

**Card Alcove.** A curtained nook with one heavy table. It offers privacy without drawing attention. Freight factors and labour intermediaries make deals here after dark.

**Back Bar.** Upper shelves hold ledgers. Lower shelves hold bottles.

> DC 11; approaching the curtained card alcove where freight factors and labour intermediaries settle advances.

> **Failure:** turned away. Try again another night.

> DC 16; watching the card alcove's table.

# Sanvashti

> [!read-aloud]
> Old stone terraces climb the hillside in careful rings, moss-softened but well maintained, swept clean of leaf litter. Woodsmoke and wet stone hang over the shore, and low voices carry from fire to fire.
>
> [[grung|Grung]] of at least three different castes sit at separate fires along the shore, watching each other more than they watch you.
>
> *(low)* Nobody's drawn a weapon. That, apparently, is the whole point of coming here.

Sanvashti is neutral ground the [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]] agreed on generations ago. Rival clans meet here to negotiate instead of raid, and the truce has held longer than anyone expected.

## Flora & Fauna

- Terrace moss and fern: blankets the swept stone rings and thickens into open forest above the highest terrace, where leaf litter takes over from cleared stone.
- Bitter root reeds: grow in the damp seams between terrace stones, chewed raw by negotiators at the fires to numb the mouth through a long sitting.
- [[giant-frog|Giant frogs]]: sit half-submerged in the terrace runoff channels, an easy catch for whichever caste's fire is hungriest that night.
- [[giant-crab|Giant crabs]]: pick the tideline below the lowest terrace, scattering when smoke from the fires drifts down to the shore.
- [[giant-owl|Giant owls]]: hunt the canopy above the truce ground, silent enough that a watching clan notices them before an approaching boat.
- [[giant-venomous-snake|Giant venomous snakes]]: nest in the leaf litter past the last terrace, where the swept stone gives way to forest floor no clan bothers to clear.

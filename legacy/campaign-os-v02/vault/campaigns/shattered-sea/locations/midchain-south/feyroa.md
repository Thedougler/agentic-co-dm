# Feyroa

> [!read-aloud]
> Coconut husks stack shoulder-high around a lean-to still, and the air is thick with fermenting sugar.
>
> A halfling missing half a finger tastes from a copper ladle, grins, then eyes you like you're worth something.
>
> The distiller corks a bottle.
>
> *The distiller*: *(dry)* Coin's no good here. Tell me something true instead.

Feyroa is one still, one halfling distiller, and a standing rule that nothing changes hands for money, only for information. The wine tastes of spiced honey and salt air. The price is always a secret worth having.

## Flora & Fauna

- Coconut palms pack the interior shoulder to shoulder, close enough that the distiller never has to walk far for the next husk.
- Wild sugarcane tangles along the shoreline, chewed raw or cut straight into the mash to push the ferment faster.
- [[crab|Crabs]] work the husk piles after dark, picking apart what the still has thrown out for the sugar still clinging to it.
- [[hawk|Hawks]] circle low over the still all day, diving for scraps in the spent mash before the halfling can shoo them off.
- A troop of [[baboon|baboons]] raids the palms at first light, stripping green coconuts down faster than one distiller can climb after them.
- [[venomous-snake|Venomous snakes]] nest deep in the oldest husk piles, drawn by the rats the fermenting sugar keeps fat.

# The Hollow Choir

> [!read-aloud]
> The cave mouth breathes a low, resonant hum even with no wind at all.
>
> A single voice inside returns as three, four, layered notes that shouldn't fit one throat.
>
> *(low)* Robed figures file in at the tide change, already humming, already in tune with the walls.

The caves of the Hollow Choir turn any sound into unnervingly perfect harmony. The order that tends them treats singing the tides as a sacred, scheduled duty.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt lichen and scrub: silver-grey lichen and wind-flattened scrub cling to every ledge above the high-water mark, and the lichen turns wet rock underfoot into a real slip hazard for anyone climbing toward the upper mouths.
- Rock samphire: fleshy green mats of samphire grow across the same ledges, tart and salt-sharp. A forager's sack of it fetches a fair price at any coastal market.
- [[bat|Bats]]: roost by the hundred in the upper vaults and stream out at dusk in a wave that empties the cave mouth for a full minute, a torrent no torch-bearing party wants to be standing in.
- [[crab|Crabs]]: work the tide pools at the cave's threshold, easy enough to catch bare-handed for anyone who wades in at low tide.
- [[vulture|Vultures]]: circle the cliffs outside and drop to strip anything the tide leaves stranded below the cave mouths, so a carcass left in the open won't survive the day.
- [[gray-ooze|Gray oozes]]: pool in the flooded lower galleries, indistinguishable from wet stone until a boot sinks into one.

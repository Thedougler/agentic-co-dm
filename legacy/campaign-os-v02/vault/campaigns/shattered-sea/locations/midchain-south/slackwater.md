# Slackwater

## Geography

**Extent.** Roughly a mile of shoreline curls around a single deep bay, backed by terraces that climb some three hundred feet before the slope gives out to bare rock.

**Topography.** The bay is the one true anchorage in the Southern row: the Deep Slip, water calmed by the ruling family's own hand until a heavy hull can lie still against pilings instead of grounding on a beach. Docks and the Trust Market's stalls pack the waterline three and four deep. Terrace streets climb behind them in switchbacks cut into the volcanic rock, denser and better built than any single family's holding elsewhere in the row. Wind keens through a dozen blowholes cut into the seaward cliff, the same shifting tune sailors up and down the row read as a squall warning and old Slackwater hands call the family listening instead. The Terrace Hold sits at the top of the climb, the one building with a clear line down to the whole bay. Every hull enters through the same gap in the reef. No other way in exists from open water, and no way up the terraces skips the market first.

## Government

Slackwater is the one place in the Southern Midchain every terrace family uses, whatever else stands between them. The rest of [[midchain-south|The Southern Midchain]] answers an unfamiliar hull at the waterline and rarely with welcome. Slackwater is the exception: a real harbour for trade and mutual defense that families who otherwise want nothing to do with each other still tolerate.

The reason is a single ruling family's power to calm seas and turn currents in South's favor, old, real, and plainly demonstrated in the safe anchorages and favorable currents every rival terrace family fishes and travels on. They defer to it without conquest or diplomacy. Nobody wants to be the family that picks a fight with the one settlement keeping their own crossings safe, so Slackwater's peace holds on leverage instead of law or custom.

What the family calls a blessing is an old bargain. Generations back, whoever first held Slackwater struck a pact with [[halythion|Halythion]], the hidden city under the Teikhinos Reef. Its guardians bend current and weather at the family's word. In exchange, the family keeps the city's existence secret and quietly moves what its guardians cannot use themselves.

[[midchain-east|The Eastern Midchain]]'s wrecks that arrive months late, and the ships that go looking for a way through and never come home, are the same pattern read from two ends. The guardians act against what they read as a threat to the city, not appetite. An innocent crew reads the same as a real discovery often enough that the mistake keeps happening. The family frames what surfaces afterward as ordinary wrecking luck. Only the family itself, and one or two people they trust without reservation, know otherwise.

The family does not explain itself to petitioners, and no one else in Slackwater can promise on its behalf. What a party can earn from the Terrace Hold amounts to two things: safe passage through South's waters, or access to whatever else the family controls that nobody else in the row can offer. Which one, and what it costs, stays unclear from the harbour alone; the path runs through the Terrace Hold directly, or not at all.

## Trade

The Trust Market crowds the harbour three and four rows deep, stalls close enough together that goods change hands on a nod instead of a scale. A buyer who knows Slackwater learns which plain-looking stall actually carries what they need. Petera Vaka's stall is the market's open secret: the place where salvage nobody can place quietly changes hands, laundered through the family's own wrecking business without a word said aloud.

## Notable NPCs

- **Litia Moetai** has held Slackwater's tide-word for eleven years. She rarely leaves the Terrace Hold. Every question about the family's power gets the same answer: a favorable current, nothing more.
- **Petera Vaka** runs the stall at the Trust Market where salvage nobody can place quietly changes hands. He is the closest thing Slackwater has to a public face for the Terrace Hold's business. Nobody has told him outright where the goods come from. He stopped asking the questions that might tell him anyway.

## Hooks

- An outsider has been asking too many questions about how the family manages the tides. Nobody in the market will point them toward the Terrace Hold, and nobody will stop them from finding it themselves.
- A rival terrace family sent a boat through Slackwater's waters unescorted, on a bet that the calm currents are more reputation than reality. It came through fine, and that unsettles the Terrace Hold more than a boat that sank would have.
- A piece of salvage surfaced at Petera Vaka's stall that nobody local can place, old craftsmanship with an unfamiliar maker's mark. A buyer who recognized it on sight left without naming a price.

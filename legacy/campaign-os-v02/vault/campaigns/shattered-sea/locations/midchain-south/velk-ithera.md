# Velk Ithera

> [!read-aloud]
> The same pale fungus carpets a whole hillside, unbroken, pulsing faintly at the edges where the light hits it.
>
> A dropped pebble clicks against stone, and the nearest patch ripples outward from the spot, slow as a held breath. The air carries a faint, damp mushroom musk.
>
> *(low)* It's all one organism. Every mycologist who's said so has also said not to shout.

One fungal colony covers Velk Ithera's entire slope, connected and whole. It reacts to sound, subtly but unmistakably. Nobody's tested what a loud sound does.

## Flora & Fauna

- The single fungal mat that carpets the whole slope runs ankle-deep in the low ground and thins to a crust on the exposed ridges, bruising grey underfoot and holding a footprint for hours.
- [[shrieker-fungus|Shrieking caps]]: rooted at the colony's damp edges, small and orange-capped. Anyone who stumbles into one sets off a shriek that carries the length of the slope, and the pale colony ripples outward from the sound the same way it does for footsteps.
- Bracket clusters: shelf fungus growing off the few dead trunks the colony hasn't yet swallowed, tough and bitter but edible after a boil (skip the boil and the stomach cramps for a day).
- [[giant-rat|Giant rats]]: nest in the hollows under the mat's thickest patches and bolt for the treeline at the first tremor through the ground. A cook can skin and spit one without complaint.
- [[stirge|Stirges]]: roost in the dead canopy above the colony and drop on anything that stops moving for more than a few breaths, drawn to the pulse and heat of a body at rest.
- [[violet-fungus|Violet fungus]]: stands in the deepest hollows disguised as an ordinary clump of the pale growth. It waits until a party is close before a rotting-flesh stalk lashes out, the one true predator the slope hides.

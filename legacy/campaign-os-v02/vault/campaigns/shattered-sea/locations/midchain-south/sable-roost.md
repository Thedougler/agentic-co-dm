# Sable Roost

> [!read-aloud]
> Grounded hulls lie canted across the clearing, envelope canvas rotted through, gondolas half-swallowed by vine. Rot and old tar hang thick in the air, and rigging creaks overhead with every gust.
>
> An old man stands on the nearest wreck, watching you with proprietary patience.
>
> *The old man*: *(flat)* That one's mine. Touch what's mine, and we'll have words.

Sable Roost collects grounded airship wrecks from an era of sky-piracy long past, and each hulk still has exactly one aging crew member who never left.

## Flora & Fauna

- Choking vine and shelf fungus: climb over every hull in the clearing, swallowing whole gondolas and turning a canted deck slick underfoot.
- Bracket fungus: grows thick on the rotted timbers under the canvas, and a forager who dries it right sells it to Ravasol's healers for a fair price.
- [[vulture|Vultures]]: pick through the clearing at all hours, taking whatever a fight or the tide leaves behind.
- [[crab|Crabs]]: crowd the tide-line where the clearing meets the shore, easy pickings for anyone willing to wade in.
- [[gray-ooze|Gray ooze]]: hides in the rotted tar pooled under a gondola's floor plates, indistinguishable from the muck until a boot sinks into it.
- [[swarm-of-bats|Bat swarms]]: roost inside the rotted envelope canvas by the dozen, and burst out screaming the instant something disturbs their hull.

# Twineye

> [!read-aloud]
> A natural stone arch spans the narrow channel between two islets, worn smooth by generations of crossing feet.
>
> Each side bears a fresh crest, painted over the older, half-scrubbed mark beneath it.
>
> *(flat)* A guard on one side eyes a guard on the other. Neither one's blinked first in years.

Estranged branches of the same extended family hold Twineye's two halves, feuding for generations over arch ownership, neither side willing to yield an inch.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt scrub and wind-bent thorn: thin volcanic soil holds nothing taller than low scrub and bent thorn brush on either islet, so there is no cover once a party climbs past the shoreline rocks.
- Sea-grape vine: clusters ripen sweet on the sun-side terraces. Both families claim the harvest as theirs, one more line item in the feud.
- [[goat|Wild goats]]: pick along the terrace edges on both halves and cross the arch at dawn before either family's guard is awake, the one thing that moves freely between the two sides.
- [[vulture|Vultures]]: ride the updraft off the channel and strip anything left too long on the open rock.
- [[crab|Crabs]]: crowd the tide pools at the arch's base, easy pickings at low tide for anyone who can get past the guards watching the crossing.
- [[reef-shark|Reef sharks]]: hold the channel beneath the arch, and a swim between the islets is a wager nobody but a desperate messenger takes.

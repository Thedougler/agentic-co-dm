# Sump Cradle

> [!read-aloud]
> Warm mud pools bubble across the flat, steam curling up in thin wisps.
>
> A bather steps out, skin glowing with health but hair turned a shade of green.
>
> *Bather*: *(dry)* Worth it.

Sump Cradle's mud heals nearly any ailment, but every bather leaves changed with a small, odd, lasting side effect. No one has found a way to stop it.

## Flora & Fauna

- Sulfur reed and salt-crust scrub: a low, sinew-tough reed rings every pool, thickening into a scrub that stains yellow-white where the mineral crust dries on its stems.
- Steam moss: a soft grey moss grows only within arm's reach of a vent's warm breath, and boiled into a paste it draws a fever down as fast as the mud draws out an ache.
- [[goat|Goats]] pick along the drier terraces above the pools, the only animal that will cross the crust without flinching.
- [[vulture|Vultures]] hold the high rim in loose circles, dropping only for whatever the mud has already killed and softened.
- [[gray-ooze|Gray oozes]] hold the cooler, standing pools, patient enough to pass for settled mud until a boot sinks into one.

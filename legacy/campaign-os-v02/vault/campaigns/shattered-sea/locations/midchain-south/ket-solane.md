# Ket Solane

> [!read-aloud]
> The island looks empty from the water. Bare rock, a few gnarled trees, desolate and utterly still.
>
> A cave mouth low on the shoreline shows fresh rope-scuff at its lip, though the sand in front of it lies undisturbed.
>
> The cave floor sounds hollow underfoot in at least three places nobody's bothered to mark.

Ket Solane looks abandoned because it's meant to. The caves riddling its shoreline are false-bottomed, restocked, and reused by whoever's smuggling through the [[midchain|Midchain]] this season. The [[chain-council|Chain Council]] has never fully mapped them.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt-bent pine and gorse: gnarled, low, and clinging to the cracks between the bare rock outcrops, the only cover the interior offers between the shore and the cave mouths.
- Sea samphire: wedges into the tidal crevices below the waterline, edible raw and salty enough to season out a week of hardtack for whoever's holed up here between runs.
- [[crab|Shore crabs]]: work the rocks at low tide and vanish into the same crevices the smugglers use, so a careless hand into a crack meets claws as often as cargo.
- [[swarm-of-bats|Bat swarms]]: roost thick in the deeper, unmapped chambers. A torch carried past the second turn sends the whole colony boiling over the intruder's head, so no one down here works by open flame.
- [[giant-venomous-snake|Giant venomous snakes]]: den in the hollow spaces under the cave floor, drawn by the same warmth and stillness the false bottoms depend on.

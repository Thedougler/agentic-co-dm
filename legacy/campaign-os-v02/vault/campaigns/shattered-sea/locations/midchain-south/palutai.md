# Palutai

> [!read-aloud]
> Nets dry on separate racks. None of the families mending them look at each other.
>
> A rope line strung across the beach marks somebody's idea of a border, and it moves a little further each week.
>
> *(flat)* Someone hands you a mending needle before you've said a word. Pick a rack.

The clans fish the same reef and have feuded for longer than anyone remembers. The island runs on whichever family has the numbers. Visitors don't stay neutral long. The clans see to that.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt scrub and briar-choked coastal brush: crowds the low ridge behind the beach thick enough that the clans keep only one narrow footpath cut through it between the racks and the interior.
- Sea-grape thickets: the fruit ripens sour and salt-bitter but dries and keeps for weeks. Each clan strips its own stretch of the ridge, never a shared patch.
- [[crab|Crabs]]: work the tideline in the dozens at low water, the one catch nobody on the island bothers fighting over.
- [[vulture|Vultures]]: circle low over the drying racks and strip whatever a family leaves uncovered, so the racks never go unwatched for long.
- [[reef-shark|Reef sharks]]: patrol the reef all three clans fish, testing any net or diver that lingers past the drop-off (the one danger every family agrees is real).

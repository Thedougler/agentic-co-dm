# Ossira

> [!read-aloud]
> White coral grit crunches with every step, and a hundred thousand wings lift off the ridgeline at once, then settle.
>
> The sand itself seems to breathe. Molted feathers drift ankle-deep, shifting underfoot like loose snow.
>
> A single dead tree stands bone-bare in the middle of the spit, its branches thick with nests you could reach without climbing.

Ossira is barely more than a coral spit, but the seabird colony that nests there appears from passing ships as a moving grey haze. No one lives here. The birds have first and only claim.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt grass and glasswort: thin mats cling to the coral grit between the nests, the only green on a spit otherwise white with shell and molted feather.
- Rookery eggs: the colony leaves a season's worth for the taking, and a forager who works the ridgeline before the birds return can fill a sack in under an hour.
- [[crab|Crabs]] and [[hawk|hawks]]: crabs pick through the tideline at the spit's edges while hawks ride the updraft off the ridgeline, both working the same colony from opposite ends.
- [[giant-rat|Giant rats]]: nest in the coral hollows under the rookery and strip a clutch of eggs bare in minutes if a party lingers near a burrow mouth.
- [[giant-vulture|Giant vultures]]: contest any carcass or wounded bird a party corners, closing fast enough to reach it before a blade clears its sheath.

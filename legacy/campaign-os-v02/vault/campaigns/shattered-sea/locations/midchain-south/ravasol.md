# Ravasol

> [!read-aloud]
> Stilt-legged houses crowd a shallow lagoon, walkways of lashed plank running between them at knee height above the water. Brine and drying fish hang thick in the air.
>
> Overhead, children scramble rooftop to rooftop faster than any adult below could follow, bare feet drumming a running clatter across the planks and shrill whistled signals passing house to house ahead of them.
>
> One drops down in front of you, grinning.
>
> *Child*: *(bright)* Cost you a coin to ask what I know. Cost you two if it's true.

Ravasol's stilt houses sit low over the lagoon, but the real traffic runs across the rooftops, where the village's children move faster and know more than anyone below them. Information here has a child-sized toll booth.

## Flora & Fauna

- Mangrove and salt-reed: the roots crowd the lagoon's shallow edge under the stilts, thick enough to hide a skiff or a runner ducking out of sight between the pilings.
- Sea purslane: grows in salt-crusted mats along the tideline, and the children strip it by the fistful to sell fresh to the fish-stalls before the tide turns.
- [[crab|Crabs]]: pick through the exposed mud beneath the houses at low tide, easy pickings for anyone quick enough to beat the rooftop kids to them.
- [[vulture|Vultures]]: wheel over the drying-racks all day, and a party that leaves fish unattended loses it to a dive before they notice.
- [[giant-rat|Giant rats]]: nest in the hollow pilings and gnaw the lashings loose, so a stilt house left untended long enough leans off true.
- [[giant-crocodile|Giant crocodiles]]: hold the deep channel past the walkways, and a rooftop kid dares newcomers to swim it for a coin.

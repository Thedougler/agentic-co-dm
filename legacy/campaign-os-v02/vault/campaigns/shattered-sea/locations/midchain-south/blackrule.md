# Blackrule

> [!read-aloud]
> The chapterhouse rises from a landing of dark, worn stone, carved straight from the volcanic shelf. The rock itself has become walls and parapets. No flag marks it, and no banner hangs from the terraces that climb behind like the steps of some ancient altar. The only sign of occupation is the scuff-marks on the landing stones where boots have worn grooves through decades of pacing, and a thin thread of smoke rising from a chimney set deep in the cliff face. The air tastes of salt and cold iron. A figure moves on the highest terrace, still as stone until he shifts his weight. Sword practice in the morning light. Unhurried. Alone.

## Structure

**150 by 80 feet along the ridge, climbing 80 feet from a stone landing to the peak.**

- **Landing:** room for one small boat, with iron rungs for the climb.
- **Stair:** past terraces cut into the rock.
- **Sightlines:** north and east faces open to the sea, visible for miles.
- **Approach:** landing and stair only, since the cliffs elsewhere run sheer.

**[[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] carved it out of disdain for civilized excess, after the [[dravosi-crown|Crown]] granted him this reserved-asset chapterhouse. The volcanic rock is its only furnishing.**

- Malone runs it single-handedly, with no staff and no garrison to answer to.
- One hall serves as chapel and barracks.
- Tyr's scales, cut into the altar stone.
- A weapon rack stands oiled, waiting for a heresy case.

**Malone answers to no superior chain here. The Crown's one attempt at oversight ended when isolation did the rest.**

- No heresy case confirmed since [[sarns-landing|Sarn's Landing]]; Malone trains alone meanwhile.
- The nearest steps are eroding past repair.
- Islanders share his temperament, turning away outsiders the way Blackrule turns away its own.

## Hooks

**Moral.** Hard to say whether leaving him here, unused, is mercy or just delay. Dravosi command has begun quietly weighing whether an unconfirmed case might be worth confirming anyway, just to use him before the wait turns him into something different and dangerous.

**Personal.** This is where the Crown's answer to [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]]'s pact with the abyss will come from, whenever that day arrives.

**Opportunistic.** The eroding terrace steps are the only real weakness in an otherwise unwelcoming, well-defended position. A way in that doesn't require Malone's cooperation.

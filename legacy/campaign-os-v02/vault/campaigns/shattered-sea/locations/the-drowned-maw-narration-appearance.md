> [!narration]
> *Blue-green [[verdant-scatter|Scatter]] shallows give way to a blue with no bottom in it. The surface goes flat, not calm: still in a way open ocean normally is not, with no current running over it.*
>
> *Seabirds that follow ships across the Scatter turn back at the edge. Fish will not school here. No sounding line has found the bottom. The air over it carries a low-tide smell, fish left on a beach in the sun.*

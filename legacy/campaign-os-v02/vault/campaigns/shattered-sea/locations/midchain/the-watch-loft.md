# The Watch Loft

> [!read-aloud]
> The ladder bolted to the tavern's back wall puts you up in the rafters, where the candle-smoke and pipe-smoke of the taproom below collects and stays.
>
> The loft is a scarred plank platform thirty feet by twenty, wide enough for two abreast at the rail, stone climbing on three sides and open air on the fourth straight down onto the drinkers.
>
> A crossbow rack runs the southern wall with three quarrels racked and ready. Tally slates hang off nails in rows beside it, every one chalked with a name and a number.

>

>

## Structure

One room, thirty feet by twenty, reached only by the bolted ladder up from the taproom of the tavern that fronts [[sparhold|Sparhold]]'s market street. Stone rises on three sides. The fourth is open rail, so a raised voice up here reaches the drinkers below, and the loft hears the taproom the same way.

Forty years of watch-shifts have worn a pale track along the rail into the planks. Slit windows cut the seaward wall at each end, angled down the harbor street toward the anchorage and the channel mouth beyond. The crossbow rack and the tally slates take the southern wall together, wagers and quarrels within one arm's reach of each other. [[vashka-doru|Vashka Doru]]'s table sits under the north wall, set so she can watch the ladder head and the channels at once.

[[ninkas-findings|Ninka's Findings]] keeps the next door east along the same tier, and [[tally-and-trade|Tally & Trade]] the counter west of it. Both send their disputes up the ladder instead of down to the watch hall.

## Atmosphere

It smells of chalk dust, spilled ale coming up through the boards, and the tallow of the lamp Vashka keeps trimmed low so it does not spoil her eyes for the water. The loft is quieter than the room beneath it and never silent.

Vashka sells to the people her slates already name. A duellist buying here has a Challenge Bell posting against him and about a day to prepare, so she stocks for that day and prices it at one flat figure, coin on the table, since the purse he would borrow against already sits in her keeping. Watch-shift crews buy the rest. The regulars come up the ladder, put coin on the table, and get told what will help, which is not always what they came for.

Traders off [[tor-vessane|Tor Vessane]] and [[quillane|Quillane]] find her rates steep. They pay them anyway, because nobody else in the settlement will sell a stranger anything on the morning of a duel.

## Hooks

The corner-tower bell carries clean up here, and Vashka reads every toll: some mark the hour, some a watch change, some a Challenge Bell posting. Standing on this platform when one sounds, a party learns from her face alone whether the next few hours matter. She will say which it was if asked, and what it costs to bet on it.

The stock is a second draw. Anything bought here is bought against a duel she has already chalked, so a return trip means asking whose name is on the slate beside the shelf, and she keeps that answer priced.

Vashka has a slate nobody has paid to see. It carries dates and times for the *Siren's Debt*, a hull she had not laid eyes on in seventeen years until it started docking at the western pier after midnight with no manifest and the same figure walking off it in a merchant's coat. She has told neither family in the succession duel. She has been waiting for [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] to come up the ladder.

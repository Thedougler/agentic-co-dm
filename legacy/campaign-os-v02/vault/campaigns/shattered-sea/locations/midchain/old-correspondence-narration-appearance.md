> [!narration]
> *A stone cottage sits alone on a rock too small to anchor beside, lamp burning at midday in its one window. The scratch of a pen carries across the water. The rock has no quay and no path from the tideline to the door.*
>
> *Crossed-out pages sit weighted by stones on the ledge outside, ink still wet on the top sheet. Rat-chew marks the corners of the older ones. Gulls line the cottage ridge, unbothered. The rock smells of guano, salt, and the faint iron of old ink. The scribe has been writing the same letter for longer than anyone in the strait can remember.*

# Thoon Adar

> [!read-aloud]
> Weathered standing stones jut from open grassland in a loose ring, taller than a person, worn smooth on one side each.
>
> The shadows they throw don't match where the sun is.
>
> *(low)* Camp here overnight, and you'll wake up facing a different stone than the one you fell asleep watching.

Thoon Adar's standing stones reorient themselves overnight. They move slowly, too slowly for anyone to catch. Yet everyone agrees they shift.

## Flora & Fauna

- Wind-bent grass and low thorn scrub cover the open ground between the stones, nothing taller than a person's waist anywhere in the ring.
- Bitter root: knotted white roots pull free of the loose soil with a tug and dry into a bitter chew herders trade for settling a sour stomach.
- [[goat|Wild goats]] graze right up to the stones' shifting shadow lines, unbothered by whatever moves the rocks at night.
- [[hawk|Hawks]] and [[giant-owl|giant owls]] work the open grass at dusk, hunting the same long sightlines the stones open across the ring.
- [[jackal|Jackal]] packs den in the scrub past the ring's edge and trail anyone who lingers after dark, bold enough to circle a dying campfire.

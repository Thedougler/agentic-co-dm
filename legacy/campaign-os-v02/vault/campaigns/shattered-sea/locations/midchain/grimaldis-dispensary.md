# Grimaldi's Dispensary

> [!read-aloud]
> Grimaldi's Dispensary sits back from the causeway crowd, tucked into the quiet of [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s third island. Inside, white plaster walls hold dried herbs hanging from the beams in tied bundles. Glass jars catch the light along the shelf above the counter, each one labelled in a cramped, careful hand. The air is sharp, camphor and vinegar and salt, with something floral underneath. Behind the counter, a woman works a scale with brass weights, weighing out a dose with the ease of someone who's done this every day for fifteen years. A stone mortar sits at her elbow, something ground fine still sitting in it. Behind her, a heavy curtain marks off the back room. Drawn shut.

## Structure

The shop is small and clean by port standards: one room up front, the curtained back room behind it. The front entrance opens onto the third-island lane, quieter than the causeway buildings the rest of Kalowe's trade runs through.

The counter holds the scale, the day's mortar, and the labelled jars within reach. The herb bundles and stock line the walls behind it. [[ilaria-grimaldi|Ilaria Grimaldi]] keeps the door open through the day, no appointment needed for standard stock, and locks it herself at sundown. The curtain over the back room stays closed unless she draws it back herself.

## Atmosphere

[[ilaria-grimaldi|Ilaria Grimaldi]] has run the only apothecary in the free port for fifteen years, and the only healer's supply between [[kalowe|Kalowe]] and [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]]. She has outlasted two harbour bosses and one merchant who tried to open a rival shop at the primary berth, and her arrangements with the current bosses have kept the shop unmolested the whole time.

She handles wounds and broken bones and stocks what a ship's surgeon needs, though she doesn't perform surgery herself, and she never asks how an injury happened. She listens instead, to how a customer asks and not just what they ask for. That alone decides whether they're worth trusting with anything she doesn't keep on the open shelf.

## Inventory

![[shop-inventory.base]]

Grimaldi runs a working price sheet more than a shelf of curiosities. A healer's kit (5 gp, ten uses to stabilise or restore) and salt-treated linen bandages (5 sp for ten) sit up front, alongside medicinal spirits (3 sp a bottle) and dried herb bundles labelled with source, dose, and preparation (3 sp). A proper herbalism kit runs 5 gp but rewards only a trained hand.

[[antitoxin|Antitoxin]] (50 gp the [[vial|vial]], two in stock and resupply uncertain) sits beside a cheaper, more specific 30 gp sea-poison antidote for reef fish and shellfish envenomation. Fever poultices go for 2 sp. A [[the-splint|splint]] and wrap costs 1 gp, and she'll show you how to apply it, once.

Her services follow the same price sheet. Wound closure is 2 sp: cleaning and stitching with a numbing agent. Bone setting is 1 gp for a straightforward break, tooth extraction 1 sp, and a surgical consultation, 2 gp, buys an assessment and a supply list for whoever actually operates, since she doesn't.

For customers who ask the right way, she keeps a second shelf. A potion of [[healing|Healing]] runs 55 gp, one or two in stock. Grimaldi sells alchemist's fire and a vial of acid under the framing of pest control, 52 gp and 26 gp. Basic poison runs 110 gp the vial. She'll say whether she has any of it only once a buyer's stated reason convinces her.

> [!mechanic]
> **Poison, basic.** DC 10 [[constitution|Constitution]] save.

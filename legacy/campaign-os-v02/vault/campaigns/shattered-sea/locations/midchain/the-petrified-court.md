# The Petrified Court

> [!read-aloud]
> Stone figures stand frozen mid-gesture in an overgrown courtyard, posed like party guests who stopped mid-chat. No birds call here, and the air smells of wet stone and moss.
>
> One shows an expression between a smile and alarm.
>
> *(low)* The cloth shows impossible precision. Each stone hair looks perfect. No carver could do this.

The statues show such detail that half the scholars who visit swear they were once people. The other half says that's impossible. Nobody's ever tested it.

## Flora & Fauna

- Moss and creeping fern: blanket every stone surface and swallow footfalls, so the courtyard reads as silent even to someone walking straight through it.
- Pale bracket fungus: grows low on the statues' plinths in the deep shade, and dried into powder makes a numbing poultice worth a few coins to any healer a crew meets.
- [[bat|Bats]]: roost in the statues' folded stone drapery since no bird will nest here, and spill out at dusk to hunt the courtyard's moths.
- [[rat|Rats]]: work the base of the statues for fallen fruit and fungus, and stay too used to the quiet to startle when a party walks past.
- [[gray-ooze|Gray ooze]]: lies flush against a plinth the same wet gray as the stone around it, and strikes only once someone leans in close enough to touch it.
- [[giant-spider|Giant spiders]]: bridge the vine growth strung between the statues' raised arms, and drop on anyone who brushes a web hidden overhead.

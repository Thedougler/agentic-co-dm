## Geography

**Extent.** A single clearing, roughly two hundred feet across, small enough to cross on foot in minutes once inside it.

**Topography.** Sits at Aruhe's highest point, ringed on every side by [[aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]]'s corrupted jungle. The [[blight|Blight]]'s root network radiates outward from the clearing's center, thick roots breaking the surface and running off into the tree line, pulsing faintly with luminous sap. The ground is soft, loamy, and shifts underfoot, never firm even where nothing is growing. The air inside the clearing sits unnaturally warm at every hour, whatever the weather does beyond the tree line. At the center stands the Death Bloom, a massive tree grown over a grave, its roots holding whatever lies buried beneath it for good.

## Routes & Access

The Rot surrounds the clearing on every side, so the only way in is through it. No approach into the grove is safe, and none is blind: the [[blight|Blight]] knows the moment anything crosses in, whichever direction it comes from. ## Hazards

> [!mechanic]
> **Corrupted Ground.** Difficult terrain anywhere in the clearing costs the [[blight|Blight]] no extra movement, and it moves through the clearing's plants freely. A creature standing on the shifting loam treats the whole clearing as difficult terrain instead.

The Blight's corruption also works on anyone who lingers.

> [!mechanic]
> **The Hunger's corruption.** A creature that spends more than 1 hour total in the clearing without warding against plant corruption (a *protection from poison and disease* effect or similar) must succeed on a DC 19 [[constitution|Constitution]] saving throw at the end of each hour after that or gain a level of corruption. A creature that fails the save a third time transforms permanently into a corpsewood-like plant creature, animated by the Blight's will. A *greater restoration* or stronger effect cast before that third failure ends the transformation.

The dead hold the clearing's outer edge.

> [!mechanic]
> **The mourner's ring.** [[Corpsewood|Corpsewood]] sentinels stand motionless at the clearing's edge, rooted in place, facing outward. They do not attack creatures that stop at the tree line. A creature that moves past them toward the Death Bloom provokes an opportunity attack from each corpsewood within 10 feet. Approaching from above (fly speed or climbing the surrounding trees) bypasses the ring but removes all cover: the clearing has no canopy, and every corpsewood in the ring sees the creature at once.

The Blight anchors this clearing outright. Its Rooted Phylactery means destroying it here changes nothing on its own: while the Death Bloom stands, a defeated Blight reforms at it in 1d10 days. Real damage to the Death Bloom is what forces the Blight down a stage, and wrecking the Bloom outright ends it for good. The Blight's own page carries the full phylactery mechanics and stage-by-stage stat blocks.

## Ecology

No animal life survives inside the clearing. [[terror-birds|Terror birds]] and the [[bear-elk|bear-elk]]'s patrol both reach the tree line at the Rot's edge and turn back. Nothing with a beating heart crosses into the grove itself. Only plant creatures the Blight has raised to guard it remain: [[corpsewood|corpsewood]] holds every approach, and [[silence-moths|silence moths]] drift the clearing's outer edge, thickening the quiet before anything reaches the Death Bloom at its center.

The Death Bloom's root network is the source, not just the center. It feeds every corrupted creature on Aruhe from this one point, running out under the Rot and [[aruhe-the-quiet|the Quiet]] alike.

A party can attempt to break the corruption at the source (optional, level 15+) by finding and destroying the Death Bloom itself. Reaching or damaging it takes real, deliberate effort. The [[blight|Blight]]'s own page names what that actually takes.

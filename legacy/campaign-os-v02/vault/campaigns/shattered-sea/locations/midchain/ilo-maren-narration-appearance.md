> [!narration]
> *Divers surface along the reef one at a time, holding kelp that glows blue-green even in full sun. On shore, women braid the wet harvest into ropes, salt and green rot sharp on the wind.*
>
> *Low salt-fig scrub holds the dune line above the tide, flattened by wind. Blackened stones mark last year's bonfire pit at the head of the bay, wide enough to light from a mile out. Reef sharks turn in the shallows beyond the divers, unhurried. A language that is not trade-pidgin carries up from the women on shore, bright over the sound of the water.*

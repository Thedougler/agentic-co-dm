> *Low and green on the horizon, a shore any helmsman should welcome. Every pilot in earshot changes course without a word. The air tastes of chalk where it should taste of salt, and the silence presses until someone speaks. No chart fills in Tor Vessane's interior. Captains who pass within sight steer three days wide. Bramble and salt-scrub cover the island ridge to ridge, waist-high and ordinary, the green that makes the place look unremarkable from the water. Goats graze the open ground at dawn, undisturbed, as though nothing on the island has learned to hunt them.*

# The Mud Pits

The Mud Pits sit on [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s third island, fifty feet from the Grimaldi causeway. A natural limestone bowl fills with lagoon silt on rainy days and holds it for fights. Fight nights glow bright. Torches carry light across the water and crowd noise reaches [[the-flat-water|The Flat Water]] on the second island.

The pit itself is roughly oval, fifteen feet across, mud thigh-deep at the lowest point. The limestone rim is wide enough to stand on and worn smooth where spectators lean. Torches on iron staves ring the outer edge.

## Fight Rules & Access

Fights run to unconsciousness, not surrender (the pit doesn't recognise any other outcome). Weapons up to a dagger are technically permitted but no one uses them. The pit forbids death magic. When a fighter is down and stays down, the fight is over.

Entry costs 5 sp per fighter, and spectators watch free.

Pay at the rim and wait for [[bala-tane|Bala Tane]] to call your match. She picks your foes and count, and groups may fight together. Prize money splits from bets: 2:1 on entry for a win, more for upsets. Bala's runners handle bets before the fight and pay winners after it ends.

The Pits settle disputes between crews. Fight it out here with Bala as witness, and the result is binding under harbour law.

## Mechanics

Each fight drains party resources (hit dice, spell slots) while giving them a good win. [[bala-tane|Bala Tane]] picks matchups for crowd fun first. She sends foes strong enough to threaten the challengers. [[combat|Combat]] ends at 0 HP. No death saves. Lethal damage never happens unless a fighter picks it. Then Bala stops the fight and ejects them.

> [!mechanic]
> **Mud effects.** [[difficult-terrain|Difficult terrain]] throughout the pit. A creature knocked prone needs a DC 12 STR save to stand as a bonus action, or must spend its full move to rise instead. Ranged attacks from outside the pit have half cover on targets inside it.

**Calibration by party size:**

| Party Size | Base Matchup | Crowd Reaction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 PC | 2 or 3 dock bruisers (CR 1/8) | Curious; picks sides early |
| 2 PCs | 3 or 4 bruisers or 1 veteran + 2 bruisers | Invested; bets split 60/40 |
| 3 PCs | 4 or 5 bruisers or 2 veterans | Loud; lots of side money |
| Full party (4 or 5 PCs) | 6 to 8 opponents or special matchup | Frenzy; Bala watching closely |

Bruiser = [[guard|Guard]] or Thug statblock (MM), lethal special attacks removed. Veteran = Veteran (MM), used for upset matchups Bala wants close. She adds one opponent at a time if the challengers are winning too cleanly.

## Opponent Roster

- **Standard card** (mixed orc, human, tortle dockworkers who fight on weekends). Reliable, underpaid, good sports about losing.
- **Vort Kessen** (champion, retired orc sailor in his late fifties, constructed of weathered ship timbers). "Kessen" is a title earned through a decade captaining [[the-kessen|the Kessen]] league's Kalowe Undertow. Everyone knows him by the title, not his birth name. Use [[gladiator|Gladiator]] (MM), fights defensively and patiently by outlasting opponents. Win against him: [[bala-tane|Bala]] pays 50gp bonus, the crowd buys drinks all evening, Vort tells the winning crew one true thing about Kalowe he's never told anyone (he respects that outcome). Lose honourably: Vort buys his own drink and nods, a quiet acknowledgment of the fight.
- **The Mud Moucheron** (special event). Bala occasionally books a [[moucheron|Moucheron]] between contracts. Use the [[moucheron|Moucheron]] statblock without proboscis drain (honour rules). Challengers will have heard the warnings beforehand. The crowd still loses its mind, and the fight stays technically safe throughout.
- **Escaped Giant Crab** (chaos event, once per campaign). A [[giant-crab|giant crab]] from the lagoon gets into the pit. Bala lets the fight run two rounds before calling it. Entry fees are not refundable.

## Betting & Consequences

**Bala's Tell:** when she says "Kalowe rules" before announcing the opponent count, she is adding a fighter. The crowd knows what it means.

**Betting detail:** a crew can bet on themselves, max 20gp per fighter through Bala's runners. Larger bets go directly to Bala and she remembers who placed them. Spectators regulate their own side bets.

> [!dm]
> **Crowd as resource:** after a party win, bettors who won money on them become friendly faces around Kalowe, a goodwill pool beyond the harbour boss introduction below.

**Fight outcomes:**

- **Win:** local reputation in Kalowe, crowd goodwill, possible harbour boss introduction. A runner is here most nights. A win against a tough opponent earns a meeting.
- **Lose honourably.** Bala offers a rematch at a discount. The crowd is still warm.
- **Refuse after showing up.** Bala remembers, and the crowd boos.

## Notable NPCs

[[bala-tane|Bala Tane]] runs the pit. She's been proprietor for fifteen years, an orc woman in her mid-forties. She announces each match like a royal proclamation, then falls silent for the fight's duration. Weight classes are her call, made for the matchup she thinks the crowd wants. Her runners handle the betting slips. She handles everything else.

## Hooks

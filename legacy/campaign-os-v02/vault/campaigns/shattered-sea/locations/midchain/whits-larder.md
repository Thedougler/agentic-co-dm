# Whit's Larder

> [!read-aloud]
> Smoke and unfamiliar spice drift from a ramshackle galley-shed built right on the tideline.
>
> A cook slides a plate across the counter, something vivid purple and faintly glowing, still steaming.
>
> *The cook*: *(bright)* Don't ask where it's from. Just eat it before it asks you something back.

Whit's Larder serves dishes made from ingredients that have no business being anywhere near the [[midchain|Midchain]]. The cook has never once explained where any of it comes from.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt scrub and tide grass: low, wind-flattened cover crowds the ground back from the shed, thin enough that the tideline and the smoke are visible from the water long before the beach is.
- Sea purslane: fleshy, faintly salty greens grow wild in the scrub's open patches, foraged by anyone who runs out of patience for the cook's stranger dishes.
- [[crab|Crabs]]: work the exposed rocks at low tide in numbers, easy pickings for anyone willing to wade in and turn a few stones.
- [[rat|Rats]]: nest in the shed's driftwood piles and timber gaps, drawn less by the island than by whatever the galley throws out.
- [[vulture|Vultures]]: circle the cook's scrap heap at all hours and don't scatter for anyone who isn't carrying a weapon.
- [[reef-shark|Reef sharks]]: work the shallows past the tideline, close enough to the shed's runoff to have learned the smell of a fresh catch.

# Quillane

The scholar-mages here study tidal magic from their sinking home. They trade real knowledge for rare and unusual things, and they never haggle on the price when the find is genuinely new.

> [!read-aloud]
> A half-drowned observatory tilts at the shoreline, its dome cracked open to the sky. Brass instruments hang at odd angles.
>
> Robed figures move among tide pools, taking notes and calling out measurements.
>
> One glances up.
>
> *The scholar*: *(dry)* You have brought us something new to measure? Wonderful. Sit.

## Geography

**Extent.** Barely 500 ft. across at low tide, and shrinking every year the dome keeps sinking.

**Topography.** The observatory sits on a spit of exposed rock at the shoreline, its dome cracked and canted into the water. Tide pools cover the flat ground below the dome, and the collapsed nave floods deep enough at high water for a shark to swim through it. A single causeway of dry rock links the dome to the rest of the island, and the tide swallows it twice a day, so anyone caught inside at the wrong hour wades or waits.

## Trade

The scholars barter specimens and oddities for real knowledge rather than coin, and they judge each trade on how genuinely new the find is, not how it's presented. Their one coin export is dried sea fennel, bundled and sold to passing ships when a trade in knowledge doesn't apply.

## Culture

The colony runs on obsessive measurement. Every tide, every current, every stranded creature gets logged, timed, and cross-checked against the last. A scholar interrupted mid-count answers in the same dry, unhurried register the work demands, whatever else is happening around them.

## Routes & Access

A hull reaches Quillane only by threading the tide pools at the shoreline; there's no deep anchorage. The causeway to the dome clears at low water and floods again within hours, so a landing party either finishes its business fast or plans to wait out the tide inside. Run the crossing per `vault/refs/vault/location/references/wilderness-travel-and-exploration.md` and `vault/refs/gameplay-toolbox.md` § Travel Pace.

## Hazards

The flooded nave admits [[reef-shark|reef sharks]] once the tide rises high enough to fill it, patient enough to trail anyone still wading through. [[giant-crab|Giant crabs]] shelter under the collapsed dome's lower shelf and come out snapping if disturbed after dark. Anyone caught on the wrong side of the causeway when the tide turns is stranded until it clears again.

## Ecology

Salt lichen and wind-flattened stonecrop crust every stretch of exposed masonry and the dome's fallen tiles, the only real cover a party gets crossing the open shoreline. Sea fennel roots in the cracks between the tide-pool rocks below the dome, and the scholars pick it for the evening pot as much as they trade it. [[crab|Crabs]] work the tide pools at low water, easy enough for anyone who wades in with a bucket, and [[vulture|vultures]] wheel over the broken dome for fish stranded on the tiles when the tide pulls back.

## Hooks

- The scholars trade real knowledge for anything strange enough to measure — a specimen, an artifact, an anomaly — and they don't haggle when the find is genuinely new.
- Their tide tables reach further and run more precisely than any chart made elsewhere in the [[midchain-north|Northern Midchain]], valuable to anyone whose own work depends on reading current-driven water correctly.
- The dome keeps sinking, and no scholar here will say by how much each year — a question that could pay off badly for whoever presses it.

# Cade's Fortune

> [!read-aloud]
> A weathered marker reads "X marks the spot." It's been rewritten many times, each new hand crossing out the old.
>
> Holes dot the clearing where others have dug. Some are old and filled with grass. One looks fresh.
>
> *(dry)* A half-buried journal nearby insists, in cramped handwriting, that THIS time the map is finally right.

Dozens of treasure hunters have come here. Each one believed the map was almost right. None found treasure. They left only [[shovel]]-holes behind.

## Flora & Fauna

- Scrub palm and tangled vine: choke the interior around the dig site, thick enough that a shovel has to clear a path sideways before anyone can start a new hole.
- Wild yams: pale roots keep surfacing in the loose, twice-dug soil, and more than one hunter has unearthed a meal instead of a chest.
- [[rat|Rats]]: have colonized the abandoned shovel-holes in numbers, and a camp left unguarded overnight wakes to a gnawed food sack.
- [[crab|Crabs]]: pick the tideline for whatever the last dig turned up and washed down to the shore, easy catch for anyone who beats the rats to the beach.
- [[venomous-snake|Venomous snakes]]: the deeper, collapsed pits stay cool enough to den in. A hunter who reaches into a promising hole without looking first meets one before any treasure.

# Marlstrand

> [!read-aloud]
> Stranded jellyfish dot the tideline in a pattern too deliberate-looking to be random.
>
> An oracle crouches over them, murmuring, tracing their positions with one careful finger.
>
> She looks up, unsurprised.
>
> *The oracle*: *(low)* Three days. Then you'll wish you'd asked before you came.

The oracles read futures in jellies washed ashore. Each morning brings a new pattern. They're right so often that few doubt their words. They never fail.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt-scrub and wind-flattened fig thicket cover the interior, low enough that the oracles' huts stay in sight from the shore.
- Sea purslane grows thick along the dune line. The oracles chew a handful before a reading to steady their hands.
- [[crab|Crabs]] work the tideline in numbers thick enough to click underfoot at low water.
- Living jellyfish drift the shallows past the strand line, still stinging, and a [[giant-crab|giant crab]] nested under the rocks takes a hand off anyone digging bare-fingered for bait.

# Vresh

> [!read-aloud]
> A half-sunken platform of dark iron and cracked stone juts from the shallows, seams glowing a dull orange in places.
>
> Loose nails and fittings on a nearby wreck twitch, straining toward the platform. Metal groans. The pull rises with each strain, and the air tastes of hot iron.
>
> *(low)* Whatever forge this used to be, it hasn't finished cooling in longer than anyone's charted the reef.

Vresh is the ruin of an old elemental forge, still active in its way. It pulls at loose metal, drawing in anything with iron that drifts too close.

## Flora & Fauna

- Rust-stained kelp and salt lichen: the only plant cover the platform holds, matted over the exposed stone and cooled iron seams, orange-brown wherever the forge's heat has bled into the rock.
- Mussels and barnacles: crust thick over every submerged strut and wreck timber, a free meal for anyone willing to wade the shallows and pry them loose.
- [[crab|Crabs]]: pick through the wreckage pinned against the platform by the pull, working it over faster than the tide clears it.
- [[vulture|Vultures]]: circle the platform at dawn, drawn by whatever the pull has dragged in and drowned against the iron overnight.
- [[reef-shark|Reef sharks]]: run the flooded channel between the platform and the nearest wreck, patient enough to wait out anyone careless with the current.
- [[rust-monster|Rust monsters]]: den in the platform's cooled forge-throat, drawn to the same loose metal Vresh itself pulls in, and strip a party's gear bare before anyone feels the first bite.

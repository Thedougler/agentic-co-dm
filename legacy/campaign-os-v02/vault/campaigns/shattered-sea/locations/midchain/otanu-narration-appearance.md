> *A grey plume climbs from the island's cone and bends east on the trade wind. Sulfur rides the air before the shore is close. Black sand and wind-bent scrub cover the slopes down to a shoreline of tide pools and coarse gravel. Along the waterline, fishers glance up at the smoke between casts the way sailors elsewhere read the sky. Goats pick along the crater rim at dawn, and hawks ride the vent's rising heat above them. After dark, crabs with shells scorched black from years of ash-fall work the rocks at the water's edge.*

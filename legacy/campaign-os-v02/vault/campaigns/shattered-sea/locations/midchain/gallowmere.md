# Gallowmere

> [!read-aloud]
> Rotted gallows lean at odd angles above a weed-choked platform, ropes long gone to dust.
>
> The air turns cold well before sundown, colder than the breeze alone explains.
>
> *(low)* Sailors who've camped here overnight all report the same thing: voices, arguing a verdict, just after midnight.

A maritime court once hanged prisoners here. The gallows rotted. But something remains. Each night at midnight, campers hear voices. Judges argue verdicts. The trials go on.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt-grass and bramble: chokes the platform and runs inland in dense knee-high tangles, so the interior is more push-through than walk.
- Sea purslane: salt-crusted stems along the platform's edge, a sharp sour bite raw, dried and packed by sailors against scurvy on the crossing out.
- [[crab|Crabs]]: work the tide-flats at the island's base at low water, easy pickings for anyone willing to wade in.
- [[giant-vulture|Giant vultures]]: roost on the rotted gallows crossbeam by day, unbothered by anyone below who isn't bleeding.
- [[venomous-snake|Venomous snakes]]: nest low in the salt-grass tangle, and a careless step through it puts more people in the ground than any ghost story does.

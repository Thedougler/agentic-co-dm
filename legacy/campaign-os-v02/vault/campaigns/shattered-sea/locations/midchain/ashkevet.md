# Ashkevet

> [!read-aloud]
> A stockade wall runs the shoreline. The gate hangs open on one hinge. Towers stand empty against the sky.
>
> Grass has taken the yard. Yet footsteps have worn the paths bare. Someone walks them often. Rot and salt hang thick over the mud, and the wind through the empty towers hisses like breath.
>
> *(low)* Somewhere past the gate, a bell rings once. Nobody's rung it back.

The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] abandoned Ashkevet as a penal colony a generation ago. Sailors who pass at night claim otherwise.

## Flora & Fauna

- Wood sorrel and bramble: have swallowed the exercise yard and climb the stockade posts, softening every board underfoot and hiding the paths from anyone who hasn't already worn them bare.
- Scrub pine: crowds the ridge behind the empty towers, close-grown enough to hide a man standing still fifteen feet off the path.
- Beach pea and salt myrtle: beach pea grows thick along the seaward wall and eats fine raw, and myrtle bark, chewed, numbs a rotten tooth (the one thing this island still hands out unasked).
- [[raven|Ravens]]: hold the guard towers in numbers no gull can push off, and go quiet all at once when the bell rings.
- [[rat|Rats]]: work the grain-store foundations under the barracks floor, bold enough to cross open ground at midday with no one left to chase them off.
- [[giant-rat|Giant rats]]: den in the flooded cell block below the yard and come up through the drain grates after dark, fast enough to drag down anyone caught alone in the dark corridors.

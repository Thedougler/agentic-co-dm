# [[waveservant-shrine|Waveservant Shrine]] of Kalowe

A stone niche in [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s main causeway wall serves as [[umberlee|Umberlee]]'s shrine at the reef-gap junction. Ships are visible from the doorway, but there's no ceremony; one Waveservant runs it, and the transaction is quick.

## Details

A niche in the causeway wall with a salvaged timber roof and sealed tile. Inside: a basin, candles, and one Waveservant in salt-grey robes. The work has become automatic after years of repetition. [[calveno-la-vasca|The basin]] drains into the lagoon through a crack. Nobody has sealed it.

[[kalowe|Kalowe]] has no ceremony. The shrine is the same. Step in, pay, get a nod, leave. Blessings are on request, but most captains skip them.

Clear mornings show the reef gap from the doorway. This view pushes captains to pay more than any rule could.

The Waveservant accepts payment before you sail or after you survive, handling each with equal grace. They record your tithe in full and offer blessings on request.

Skip tribute at the reef? You get written down. Authority is loose, but memory is long.

[[branca|Branca]] is a senior Waveservant at [[calveno|Calveno]]. She might come here, but the table hasn't decided.

A sister shrine, unrelated day-to-day operation, stands in Calveno's own harbor district (not yet migrated).

> [!narration]
> *Mangrove roots arch over channels that fork and fork again with no marker at any turning. Mud sucks at hulls and pops where the water runs shallow. The air reeks of rot and wet bark, and the canopy closes out most of the sky.*
>
> *A grey heron stands motionless at one fork, watching the boat instead of the water. [[crocodile|Crocodile]] wakes cut the deeper channels without surfacing. Mosquitoes haze the still pools at the edges, thick enough to hear before the next fork comes into sight. Every channel looks the same, and none of them match the last chart drawn.*

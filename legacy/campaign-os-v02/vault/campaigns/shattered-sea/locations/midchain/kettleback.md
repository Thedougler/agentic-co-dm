# Kettleback

> [!read-aloud]
> Heat shimmers off vent-holes scattered across bare rock, and a mine entrance breathes warm, chemical-tinged air.
>
> Somewhere below, glass clinks against glass in careful, practiced rhythm.
>
> A guard at the entrance doesn't ask your business.
>
> *The guard*: *(flat)* Vent fee's a silver a head. Don't touch the pipes.

Kettleback's old mining tunnels run hot off a geothermal vent. Someone repurposed this heat to run an unlicensed alchemical lab. No one has shut it down yet.

## Flora & Fauna

- Lichen and heat-cracked scrub crust the bare rock between the vent-holes, thinning to nothing on the ground nearest the hottest fissures.
- A mineral-crusted moss grows in the warmer cracks; the lab buys every bundle a forager brings down and pays in coin, not questions.
- [[giant-lizard|Giant lizards]] sun themselves on the warm rock shelves through the day, and [[bat|bats]] roost deep in the mine's disused side-galleries.

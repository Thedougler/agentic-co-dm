> [!narration]
> *Grass-covered mounds stand in rows across the islet, too even to be natural. The wind carries a low hum that does not fade when the gusts do. Silver sea-grass lies flat in sheets between them, showing the shape of every mound before a boot ever touches one.*
>
> *Near the shore a half-dug pit lies open, tools scattered beside it. Ravens wait on the turned earth. No one tends the dig, and the tracks leading away from it do not reach the waterline. Bittercress grows wild in the ditches, and venomous snakes den in the gaps between the old turf blocks.*

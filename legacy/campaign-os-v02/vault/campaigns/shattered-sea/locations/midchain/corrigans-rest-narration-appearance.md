> *A handful of well-kept cottages sit above a quiet cove, laundry on the lines, smoke from a chimney that smells of fish stew. The anchorage is wide and nobody guards it. An old woman on one porch waves at a hull she has never seen before.*
>
> *Salt grass and low scrub cover the ridge behind the settlement, thick enough to swallow a freshly dug hole within a season. Wild goats graze the bare patches. Vultures ride the thermals overhead, circling whatever the goats leave behind. The cove is too comfortable for a place this far from anywhere, and nobody offers to explain why.*

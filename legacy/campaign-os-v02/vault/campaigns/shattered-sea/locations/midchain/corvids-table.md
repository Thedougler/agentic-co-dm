# Corvid's Table

> [!read-aloud]
> Ravens crowd every branch and rooftop in numbers that stop feeling natural fast.
>
> One drops a polished button at your feet, then cocks its head, expecting something back.
>
> A local shrugs.
>
> *A local*: *(dry)* Give it something shinier. Word gets around fast if you don't.

The [[raven|raven]] flock runs Corvid's Table. They trade favors and shiny trinkets with precise, careful method.

## Flora & Fauna

- Wind-bent fig trees and tangled bayberry scrub: a low canopy over the interior, its older branches weighed down with last season's nests.
- Sea fennel and beach plum: grow wild along the dune line, the fennel's peppery leaves good in a stew and the plums tart enough to trade at Copperlight's market.
- [[raven|Ravens]]: crowd the branches and rooftops in numbers the flock's traders never account for, quarreling over perch space and whitening the paths below with guano.
- [[rat|Rats]]: work the midden heaps and the tideline after dark, and the ravens not sworn to any trade hunt them instead.
- [[giant-crab|Giant crabs]]: shelter under the tide-pool ledges at the shore's edge and pinch hard enough to break a finger on anyone who wades in without looking first.

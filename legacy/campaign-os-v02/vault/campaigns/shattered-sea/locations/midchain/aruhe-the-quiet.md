## Geography

**Extent.** Wraps the terraces on three sides, rising toward the island's interior. Crossing it end to end takes a morning's walk.

**Topography.** Dense jungle canopy closes sight lines to a few paces almost everywhere. The ground climbs steadily from the tree line at the terraces' upper edge toward Aruhe's interior, footing broken by root and undergrowth the whole way. The [[grung|Grung]] never named this zone from a survey; they named it from the edge, from what reached them before they turned back.

## Routes & Access

The only way in is up through [[aruhe-the-old-gardens|The Old Gardens]] terraces, where the cut stonework gives out and the true jungle starts. From there, game trails cut through the canopy toward the interior. Deer-stalkers and thornbacks use those same trails to move, so following one means walking a predator's own hunting ground. Wilderness travel and exploration pacing for crossing The Quiet follows `vault/refs/vault/location/references/wilderness-travel-and-exploration.md` and `vault/refs/gameplay-toolbox.md` § Travel Pace.

## Hazards

The silence itself is the first hazard. Predators close in without any sound to give them away, and the canopy's few paces of sight line is the only warning a party gets.

[[deer-stalkers|Deer-stalkers]] stand in the tree line for hours, watching, then close the distance in total silence after dark. [[thornbacks|Thornbacks]] root through the jungle floor and charge anything that startles them. [[strangler-fig|Strangler figs]] grow wherever a game trail narrows enough to funnel prey beneath their branches.

> [!mechanic]
> **The bear-elk's circuit.** The [[bear-elk|bear-elk]] patrols the boundary between the Quiet and [[aruhe-the-rot|the Rot]] on a fixed route, completing one circuit every 4 hours. DC 15 [[wisdom|Wisdom]] (Survival) to determine its timing from tracks and broken undergrowth. A party that waits for the gap has a 30-minute window to cross. Moving through the gap requires a DC 14 [[dexterity|Dexterity]] (Stealth) group check. Failure means the bear-elk returns to investigate.

The narrower trails carry a different threat.

> [!mechanic]
> **Strangler-fig funnel.** Where game trails narrow between stone outcrops, strangler figs grow across the gap. DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) to spot the fig before walking beneath it. On a failure, the strangler fig drops on the lead creature and attempts to grapple and engulf it.

## Ecology

Deer-stalkers, thornbacks, and strangler figs hold this zone as their range; [[silence-moths|silence moths]] work its outer edge at dusk and dawn, thinning further in as the canopy deepens. [[vine-lash|Vine-lash]] still grows here, thinner than on the terraces below but present in the denser brush. The [[bear-elk|bear-elk]]'s patrol route runs the boundary between The Quiet and [[aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]], the same fixed circuit crossing both zones on its way around.

# Enkoa

> [!read-aloud]
> A shimmer runs along the treeline. The air resists. A ward, not subtle.
>
> Past it, smoke rises from a hut. The sea wind does not disturb it.
>
> *The sorcerer*: *(low)* State your favor before you cross the line. I already know you want something.

Enkoa has one resident: a hermit sorcerer. This sorcerer wards the island against uninvited landings. Trade happens on the sorcerer's terms only. Coin is never acceptable.

## Flora & Fauna

- Fig-and-banyan canopy: closes over the whole interior, roots humped waist-high across the paths so no route in stays straight for more than a few yards.
- Shade-root orchid: grows only under the banyan roots where the canopy never breaks, and its dried petals are the one component the sorcerer will accept in trade for a small favor.
- Wild honeycomb: pools thick in the hollow banyan trunks. Smoking the swarm off costs a few stings, and the comb is worth them.
- [[goat|Wild goats]]: browse the forest floor in small herds, unbothered enough by the ward that a hunting party can track them straight past its edge.
- [[hawk|Hawks]]: nest high in the canopy and take the goat kids at dawn, diving through gaps in the fig cover too narrow for anything larger to follow.
- [[giant-wasp|Giant wasps]]: den in the hollow banyan trunks beside the honeycomb, and a jar taken carelessly brings the whole nest down on the thief.

# The Penitent's Stand

> [!read-aloud]
> Seven or eight salt-dwarfed mangroves crowd the causeway end, roots knotted together and into the tidal edge. One trunk stands stripped pale, bark worked smooth over years of hands bracing against it. A flat stone sits half-buried at its base, its surface carved with a symbol nobody describes the same way twice: wave-crest, knotted cord, an eye with a closed lid. The tide is louder here than it should be at this elevation. Marks run up the pale wood in two distinct styles, and they meet at roughly chest height.

## Structure

**Seven or eight salt-dwarfed mangroves** at the causeway end of the sandbar, just off the tally-stone path. The trees have grown into each other at the base, roots tangled with the tidal edge.

**One trunk has shed its bark**, worked smooth by years of hands bracing against it. At its base, a flat stone sits half-buried. Its surface carries a symbol nobody names the same way twice. Villagers argue between a wave-crest and a knotted cord, though others describe an eye with a closed lid.

> [!check] Investigation DC 12 — The Stone's Age
> The symbol predates the tally-stones by at least a generation.

**Marks on the stripped trunk:**

- *Lower, older*: cut with a blade in a regular pattern
- *Upper, newer*: torn by fingernails, not a blade

They meet at roughly chest height. Ashe's penance custom did not invent this place. It found it.

**Northwest gap in the canopy:** [[sorrowbell|Sorrowbell]]'s window is visible on a clear night.

## Atmosphere

People come here alone, for personal penance. Group rites play no part. Village noise muffles. Tidal sound runs louder than the elevation should allow.

Ysolde Cass knows which branch to hold to keep her weight off the roots and stay still.

## Notable NPCs

- [[ysolde-cass|Ysolde Cass]], here on most insomniac nights, watching Sorrowbell's window through the northwest gap. Not performing penance. She carries a cord knotted in the same pattern as the binding-marks around Sorrowbell's basin, pulled from the tideline the night before Mirela died.

## Hooks

- **Two eras of marks** on the stripped trunk: blade-cut below, fingernail-torn above, meeting at chest height.
- **The flat stone's symbol**: no two villagers describe it the same way.

> [!check] Investigation DC 12 — The Stone's Age
> Predates the current settlement by at least a generation.

- **[[ysolde-cass|Ysolde Cass]]** is here most insomniac nights; clearing her as a gatekeeper means earning her trust here, not at the tideline.
- **Root smell**: a faint burnt-and-metallic smell rises from the root zone, brief, gone before anyone names it.

> [!check] Perception DC 14 — Root Zone
> The smell rises only while standing at the stripped trunk.

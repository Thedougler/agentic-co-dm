> [!narration]
> *Fruit trees climb the slope in rows once straight, now buckling into each other, every branch heavy with fruit no one colour. Bramble chokes the gaps between the rows.*
>
> *A weathered marker post stands at the treeline, its name half worn away. The air is sweet and sharp at once, the smell of something that would cure or kill and not care which. Feral goats move between the trees, eating what falls. None of them seem to sicken. Wasps work the split trunks overhead, loud enough to hear from the water.*

# Ormsson's Fittings

> [!read-aloud]
> The shop stands under the dry-dock cranes on [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s first island, close enough that the yard's hammering comes through the wall in bursts.
>
> One long counter cuts the room across. Crates climb to the ceiling behind it, and a curtain covers most of the back wall.
>
> [[kettil-ormsson|Kettil Ormsson]] runs a file along a bolt thread and does not look up. Nobody else is in the room.
>
> A cracked keel plank leans on the boards outside the door, salt-bleached, split along the grain by something that hit it from underneath.
>
> He sets the file down and looks at you, and he says nothing.

## Structure

One room, one counter, and the closest supplier to the main dry-dock, which makes this one of three ship-suppliers on the atoll alongside [[wibowos-provisions|Wibowo's Provisions]] and [[taufa-fifita-and-sons|Taufa Fifita & Sons]]. Crates of rope and bolts stack to the ceiling behind the counter. The wall beside them carries chain on pegs by gauge at 5 sp per foot, and the shelves under it hold deck fittings, cleats, irons, tools, lanterns, padlocks, and climbing gear.

A curtain covers the back wall, and the arms shelf behind it holds daggers, axes, swords, spears, bows, crossbows, ammunition, and firearms, a flintlock pistol and one musket among them. [[kettil-ormsson|Kettil Ormsson]] alone decides how far back the curtain goes. He works with no assistant, and he closes when the dry dock does.

## Atmosphere

Oil, cold iron, and the wet ash of a banked forge, under the yard's hammering through the wall. Twenty years of supplying the gear ships need has left Ormsson with no patience for talk: he reads a customer fast, quotes standard rates without haggling, and takes custom metalwork at one to two days an order. A dwarf out of [[harwick|Harwick]] or thereabouts, late forties and stocky even for one, he answers a question about the third crate with the price per foot and nothing else.

Ask about the back shelf with no preamble and he says what is on it. Explain why you want it and he loses interest before the sentence ends.

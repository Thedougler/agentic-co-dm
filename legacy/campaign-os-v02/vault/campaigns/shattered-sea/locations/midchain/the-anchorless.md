# The Anchorless

> [!read-aloud]
> Bare grey rock breaks the surface, utterly barren and exposed.
>
> The water here stays calm, even when wind stirs the sea around it.
>
> *(low)* Nobody stops here. Everyone who has sailed past remembers the same dream that night.

A dream marks the Anchorless: every crew sailing within sight reports the same vision that night. The island stands barren and empty, yet this dream draws ships' notice.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt-crust lichen: the only living cover on the exposed rock, a black-and-orange crust so tight to the stone that a boot slides on it wet.
- Barnacle and mussel crust: bands the waterline thick enough to break loose by the double handful, plain food the moment a knife pries the first shell open.
- [[vulture|Vultures]]: hold the high points in a standing watch and lift off as one body the instant oars break the water's stillness.
- [[crab|Crabs]]: work the handful of cracks that still hold rainwater, easy to gather for anyone willing to climb down after them.
- [[giant-crab|Giant crabs]]: den in the fissures below the waterline and climb out after dark, a pincer strong enough to shear a boarding hook clean through.

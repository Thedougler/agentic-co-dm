# Sabrique

> [!read-aloud]
> Flat white salt-pans stretch to the treeline, raked into even squares by rows of workers bent under wide hats.
>
> An overseer's whistle cuts the air, and the raking never breaks rhythm.
>
> *(low)* Nobody working the pans meets your eye long enough to answer a question.

Sabrique's salt is the cheapest in the [[midchain|Midchain]], and the concern that runs the pans has never been transparent about where its labor comes from. Buyers who want the price learn quickly not to ask.

## Flora & Fauna

- Scrub tamarisk and salt-grass ring the pans, giving way at the treeline to close-packed sea-almond and screwpine the overseers forbid workers to cut for firewood.
- Sea purslane grows thick along the pan dikes, salt-cured and free for anyone who bends to pick it (the only food here the overseers don't ration).
- [[crab|Crabs]] work the drainage channels at low tide, and a worker who fills a bucket after a shift eats better than the ration line that night.
- [[giant-crab|Giant crabs]] den under the dike stones nearest the treeline, and a raking crew that strays too close loses fingers before it loses blood.
- [[venomous-snake|Venomous snakes]] hunt the screwpine roots for rats, and a barefoot line worker gets bitten most seasons the overseers let the treeline grow back in.

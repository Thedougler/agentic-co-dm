# Verrow's Cradle

> [!read-aloud]
> Small boats lashed into a floating platform bob together, laundry and toys crowding every rail.
>
> A scarred old woman herds a knot of children toward supper with surprising gentleness.
>
> *The captain*: *(warm)* Don't mind the cannon on the roof. It's decorative.

A retired pirate captain runs Verrow's Cradle. She uses old treasure to raise every orphan the [[midchain|Midchain]] sends. She wants to keep this secret.

## Flora & Fauna

- Barnacle-crust and bladderwrack: cakes every hull below the waterline, slicking the boarding steps that link one lashed boat to the next.
- Drift-weed mats: sargassum snags on the mooring lines each tide, and the older children strip it to sell into [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s kitchens.
- [[crab|Crabs]]: cling to the mooring posts and the underside of the outer hulls, easy pickings the youngest orphans catch by hand for the evening pot.
- [[vulture|Vultures]]: wheel low over the laundry lines whenever the galley tosses out scraps, and the children keep sticks by the rail to chase them off.
- [[reef-shark|Reef sharks]]: run the channel past the outer hulls in packs, close enough that no child swims beyond the mooring lines alone.

# Rowan's Hush

> [!read-aloud]
> Monks move through the cloister in complete silence, hands moving in quick, precise shapes instead of speech.
>
> One turns to you and signs something, patient, waiting for a response that isn't coming.
>
> A novice mimes writing on their palm.
>
> *Novice*: *we'll teach you, eventually.*

Rowan's Hush keeps a total vow of silence. Visitors must learn hand-signs quickly. Nobody here slows down to repeat explanations.

## Flora & Fauna

- Moss-floored pine and fir: the interior grows in dense, straight-trunked stands, needle-carpeted underfoot so a footstep lands without a sound, the same quiet the monks keep by choice.
- Wintergreen and yellow chanterelles: cluster where the canopy breaks near the tree line, and a novice on foraging duty fills a basket without a word passing between anyone.
- [[deer|Deer]]: loose, unbothered groups through the understory, near enough the cloister wall that a monk in the herb garden can watch one graze.
- [[owl|Owls]]: hold the high branches after dusk and drop on rodents in the leaf litter without a single sound anyone catches, the one hunter on the island that already keeps the order's vow.
- [[wolf|Wolves]]: den somewhere in the deeper stands. Nobody has seen one this season, but the howls carry most nights, and the order leaves food scraps at the treeline every dusk.
- [[giant-wolf-spider|Giant wolf spiders]]: nest low in the deadfall past the last worked path and strike from cover fast enough that a monk caught alone off the paths signs for help before anyone reaches him.

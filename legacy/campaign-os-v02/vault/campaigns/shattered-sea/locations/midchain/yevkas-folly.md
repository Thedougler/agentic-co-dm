# Yevka's Folly

> [!read-aloud]
> Stone columns rise toward open sky through scaffolding. A grand staircase leads to open air.
>
> Laundry hangs between half-finished archways. Someone cooks over a fire in a half-built kitchen.
>
> *(dry)* A squatter waves you toward the "front door," just a hole where a door was never built.

A merchant prince dreamed of building the finest pleasure palace in the [[midchain|Midchain]] as Yevka's Folly, but his money ran out mid-construction. The squatters living here now don't mind their home's grand, unfinished state.

## Flora & Fauna

- Fig and lemon saplings, planted for a garden that never got finished, have gone feral across the terraces and split the flagstones reaching for light.
- Bramble and sea grape choke the unfinished east wing and still fruit every summer, and the squatters strip both before the birds get to them.
- [[cat|Feral cats]] work the kitchen scraps and keep the rat count down, tolerated as the closest thing this place has to a watch.
- [[rat|Rats]] nest in the unfinished wall cavities, and [[vulture|vultures]] ride the thermals off the open roof, settling on the scaffolding whenever the kitchen fire goes cold.
- A [[giant-wasp|giant wasp]] nest hangs from the grand staircase's exposed roof beams, and the squatters have learned which tread not to strike hard.

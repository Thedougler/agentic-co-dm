## Structure

The shop stands on the main path of [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s second island, three buildings back from the water. One room fronts the street, and a counter runs its full width, so Taufa's own hands fetch everything behind it. The shelving behind him climbs to the ceiling on a ladder track: hemp and light line sold by the fathom, sailcloth and oilskin canvas by the yard, iron-strapped oak rigging blocks, small kegs of tar, quart tins of linseed oil, curved [[needles|needles]] and waxed thread for sail repair.

The counter's own shelf keeps what he wants within arm's reach without the ladder, lamp oil by the flask and the gallon jug, hooded and bullseye lanterns, [[crowbar|crowbars]], [[tinderbox|tinderboxes]], boxed candles. One locked case beside the till holds the stock worth more than most of the boats it goes onto: compasses, rolled charts of the local [[midchain|Midchain]] runs, and a [[spyglass|spyglass]] priced at eleven hundred gold that nobody in Kalowe has ever bought.

A back room off the left end takes the bulk stock and the family ledgers. No customer sees the inside of it, and the door stays shut on an empty room as readily as on a full one.

## Atmosphere

He chalks his prices on the board and they do not move. Regulars know there is no haggle here and no hidden cost either, which is most of why they come. Taufa works while he talks, so a conversation at this counter happens over a splice or a coil, with long comfortable gaps in it where a customer is free to say more than they meant to or nothing at all. What he offers into those gaps is the trade and never the customer. *Hempen or tarred? How many fathoms?* Pressed on who else has been in, he gives one line and goes back to the splice. *I don't keep a book on what people carry out. Just what I sell.*

The family also runs [[fifitas-slip|Fifita's Slip]] on the lagoon side of the island, and that name stays off the chandlery counter.

## Notable NPCs

Taufa's adult sons work the back storeroom and the ladder, and they finish a job without ever meeting the customer who ordered it. The eldest, [[noa-fifita|Noa]], works elsewhere. He keeps [[fifitas-slip|Fifita's Slip]] on the lagoon side and comes to the chandlery only after dark, through the back.

## Hooks

[[the-passage|The passage]] paper referral is the shop's only mysterious service. Fifita helps customers find someone who makes passage papers. But he won't explain how he decides who gets this help.

[[fifitas-slip|Fifita's Slip]] is the family's lagoon-side business, kept secret at the counter. This hidden operation is a second side to the family trade.

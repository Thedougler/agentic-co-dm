> [!narration]
> *Clock towers crowd a bare stone island, close enough that a dozen faces read from the water. The ticking carries across the harbour before any voice does.*
>
> *Sundials and water-clocks line every ledge and low wall below the towers. Robed figures cross the plaza without speaking, each one checking a different instrument and noting the result in a book held close to the chest. Wind-flagged juniper clings to the rock between the buildings, too thin to block line of sight from any tower to any other. The air smells of brass polish and old brine.*

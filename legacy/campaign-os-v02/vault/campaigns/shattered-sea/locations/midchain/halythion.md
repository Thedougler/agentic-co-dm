# Halythion

![[halythion-banner]]

An ancient [[sea-elf|sea elf]] city built into the Teikhinos Reef ("of the wall"), invisible from the surface, impassable by ship, and older than any colonial record. Its name means "place of the salt-deep," and it measures civilization by what it remembers: memory is law, and law lives in voice. Sailors south of the [[midchain|Midchain]] know it only secondhand, as an old underwater city somewhere in the eastern reefs that ships looking for a way through do not return from.

> [!read-aloud]
> The reef ahead holds only current and empty shelf. Then the water opens.
>
> Forty feet down, the Koilon spreads out below, a bowl cut into living coral, amber light rising warm from its upper tiers and blue-white cold from its depths. Arch doorways ring every level, dark mouths cut clean into stone that has never seen a chisel of iron. A wide floor at the very centre sits empty, swept bare by centuries of custom.
>
> Something moves along the rim: a shape too composed to be current, too patient to be a fish. Then it's gone.
>
> A voice rises from the empty floor below, and it carries to the rim without losing a word.

## Geography

**Extent.** Roughly 300 ft. across, a bowl-shaped amphitheatre (the Koilon) ringed by tiers of coral dwellings.

**Topography.** The city sits 15 to 60 feet below a reef shelf that reads as empty from above, centred on the Koilon, whose cleared floor carries a voice to the rim without fading. Amber bioluminescence lights the upper tiers, blue-white the deep ones, and arch doorways cut into coral frame every level. Giant sharks hunt the outer reef on the currents, and giant octopuses den in the limestone overhangs below. The Teikhinos passage is the only way in, uncharted, and passable only to shallow-draft vessels under 2 feet with a sea elf guide who already knows the route. Charts label the wider reef zone around the city the South [[tail|Tail]]; the crew's own [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] draws 4 feet, two past the passage's limit.

## Government

The Aoide is unwritten. It lives only in the mouths of trained keepers, voice to voice, never page to page. The Aoidos, a formal role passed down through families, leads this work, but the position sits empty. [[coralyra-dranra|Coralyra]] left before the Council finished her training, and the Council has never formally released [[coralyra-dranra|Coralyra]]. What happens when she returns is open DM ground.

## Culture

Worship centres on Deep Sashelas. Law here means memory kept alive in the voice of whoever carries it, not ink on a page.

Wreck debris litters the deep reef beyond the Teikhinos, and some of it has moved on its own. Sailors who venture too close report massive shapes moving in formation through the dark water, too large for reef fish and too organized to be current.

Nothing about Halythion reaches the surface except rumor. Sailors south of the Midchain trade it secondhand, as a city that blocks ships and swallows the ones that go looking. The one documented route in for outsiders runs through [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]'s soul-passenger lead to [[maren|Maren]], not through any tavern tale, and [[coralyra-dranra|Coralyra]]'s unfinished Aoidos training only deepens how little outsiders actually know.

## Notable NPCs

**[[coralyra-dranra|Coralyra Dranra]]** Self-exiled Aoidos-in-training who left before the Council declared her ready.

## Hooks

- [[the-passage|The passage]] poses a draft problem. No vessel drawing more than 2 feet can pass through the Teikhinos, and the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] draws 4. The crew needs a shallow-draft auxiliary, a sea elf guide, or another way through.
- [[coralyra-dranra|Coralyra]]'s unresolved status with the Council activates when she returns or someone finds her.
- Access runs entirely through [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s soul-passenger lead: [[maren|Maren]]'s source was a sea elf friend who gave details bit by bit over months, and Maren never pushed for more or learned if the friend still lives. It's personal knowledge, not tavern talk, and it surfaces only when Delmar touches that soul. No one has documented any other route in.
- The organized shapes moving through the deep reef beyond the Teikhinos have moved wreck debris on their own. No one has identified them yet.

> [!narration]
> *Piers reach into the harbour from opposite shores, each ending in a company compound flying its own colours. The [[sunkline-company|Sunkline Company]] flies a sunburst on one bank, the [[voyvode-works|Voyvode Works]] an eagle on the other, close enough across the water to read each other's rigging.*
>
> *Iron scaffolding claws up behind both compounds. Half-finished pressure-rig towers ring with hammer-work, or stand rusted and abandoned mid-frame. Recruitment boards nailed three deep to every dockside post. Salt and hot iron ride the air. Between the two piers a low plank wall holds a hundred carved names nobody has painted over.*

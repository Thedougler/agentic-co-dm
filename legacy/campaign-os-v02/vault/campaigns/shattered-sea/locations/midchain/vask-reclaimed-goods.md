# Vask's Reclaimed Goods

> [!read-aloud]
> A converted dock repair shed sits back from the working dry-dock, hull-plank shelving climbing to roofbeams still hung with block and tackle. Salt-crusted crates, propped open down both walls, double as display cases, and near the counter a breastplate marked with three coiled sigils stands alone on its own rack, catching the light from the open doors. Somewhere in back, a hoist chain settles with a slow clink as a crate shifts under its own weight. Brine and tar hang thick under the shed's dry-rot must. Behind the counter, a wiry man checks a manifest against the crates without looking up, then does, and waits for you to say why you're here.

## Structure

Vask's Reclaimed Goods sits on [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s first island, a repair shed turned shopfront near the dry-dock cranes. The single-story building runs roughly 50 ft. by 30 ft. A loft over the back third holds the overflow crates.

The floor sits level with the dock planking outside, one shallow step up from the tideline mud. Salt-warped boards stay sound near the doors. They go soft where old bilgewater still seeps up under the back wall.

The open double doors up front give a clear line across the lane to the dry-dock cranes. Anyone can walk straight in off the docks. A curtained doorway at the rear blocks sight of the loft stair. The ladder up and the door onto the back alley stay Vask's own, off-limits to browsing customers.

## Atmosphere

[[sorin-vask|Sorin Vask]] deals in items pulled off wrecks, captured ships, and cargo nobody came back to claim. That's Kalowe's usual traffic, sold the same as anything else on the docks. No one asks where a piece came from. Vask asks only what it does and what it cost to bring up.

He prices by real recovery risk and rarity, never by provenance or reputation: a blade fished off a reef costs exactly what a blade off a reef is worth, whatever rumor says it did to its last owner. The shed still smells of brine and tar under its dry-rot must. Off-shift dockhands drift in just to see what the latest hull gave up, buying or not.

## Hooks

The [[armor-of-fireballs|Armor of Fireballs]] breastplate on its rack is Vask's showpiece, priced at 2,000 gp and still unclaimed, and one of Kalowe's dock bosses has started asking pointed questions about where Vask's latest haul came from. That attention gets a shop "protected" whether he wants it or not. A crew with reason to keep him independent could step into that pressure before it closes in.

Vask keeps no ledger of who sold him what, so a piece with real provenance can sit unrecognized on his shelves for what it's actually worth: [[antheri|Antheri]] manufacture, a captured warship's own stores, a relic off a shrine wreck. It waits on whoever knows enough to spot it.

# The Old Gardens

Cut stone terraces climb the slope above [[aruhe|Aruhe]]'s beach, collapsed now and swallowed by root and vine. No chart or Grung account remembers who cut the risers level, channeled the ditches between the levels, or set the walls true, only that the jungle pulled the work apart long since. [[grung|Grung]] traders who named the island never landed here to look closer, so the name they gave this zone reads only what a passing eye can tell from the water: terraces once tended, now gone wild.

No one holds the Old Gardens. Feral terrace gardens of yams and citrus still fruit wild among the vine, the last trace of the cultivation the terraces once carried, but no one has worked this ground since the terraces collapsed.

## Geography

**Extent:** the terrace slope runs the width of Aruhe's landward face, climbing from the beach to the tree line in a few hundred yards of broken elevation.

**Topography:** cut stone terraces rise in steps up the slope. Root and vine have swallowed the levels, and the old stonework has pulled apart wherever a root found a seam. Flooded ditches run between the levels, once channels for terrace irrigation, now standing water thick with growth. Footing stays unreliable throughout, with broken risers, vine-choked steps, and the same gaps in the walls that double as the only climbable route up.

## Routes & Access

Climbing from the beach through gaps in the collapsed stonework is the only way up. Run the climb per `vault/refs/vault/location/references/wilderness-travel-and-exploration.md` and `vault/refs/gameplay-toolbox.md` § Travel Pace.

## Hazards

A moderate danger zone (CR 5-7 encounters). Collapsed stonework makes footing unstable throughout the climb, and [[wolfrabbits|wolfrabbits]] have dug warrens through every terrace wall. [[vine-lash|Vine-lash]] hangs from anchor points in the canopy above the paths, motionless until something walks under it.

> [!mechanic]
> **Terrace collapse.** Wolfrabbits' warrens have hollowed the stonework throughout. When a creature weighing more than 100 pounds crosses an undermined riser, it must succeed on a DC 13 [[dexterity|Dexterity]] saving throw or fall 10 feet into a collapsed terrace: 3 (1d6) bludgeoning damage, prone, and rubble fills a 10-foot radius of difficult terrain. The collapse opens a warren entrance; 1d4 wolfrabbits emerge in the following round.

The terraces still fruit wild, and the food is real.

> [!mechanic]
> **The fruit.** Feral yams and citrus hang from every terrace level. Taking any triggers the [[aruhe-taking-rule|Taking Rule]]. The fruit heals 1d4 hit points per piece eaten, which makes the temptation real.

## Ecology

[[wolfrabbits|Wolfrabbits]] breed in warrens through the terrace stonework, a litter every few weeks straining what the terraces can feed. [[grinning-apes|Grinning apes]] prowl the terrace canopy in groups, dropping on anything that moves below. [[vine-lash|Vine-lash]] hangs from anchor points in the old walls, indistinguishable from ordinary vine until it strikes. [[giant-spider|Giant spiders]] have webbed the terrace brush thick enough to catch anyone who climbs through without watching.

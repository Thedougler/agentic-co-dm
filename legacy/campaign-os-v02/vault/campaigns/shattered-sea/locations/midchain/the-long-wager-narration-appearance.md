> [!narration]
> *A clearing on the shore holds a scoreboard whose tally marks run back further than seems reasonable, and two rival teams crowd a marked course below it. Decades of the same pacing feet have packed the ground bare between them. Shouting carries across the water, well-practiced and continuous.*
>
> *Sour grass grows thick along the fringe where the trees take over, sharp enough to chew on between heats. Vultures work a refuse pit behind the scoreboard for whatever gets thrown out after a match. An old woman sits apart at a table near the finish and does not look up.*

# The Dry Net

> [!read-aloud]
> The Dry Net sits narrow-fronted, a single room behind a low door. Shelves run floor-to-ceiling on every wall, stocked tight with coiled rope, oil flasks, sacking, and bundled cord, a pole hook hanging from a ceiling beam for the high stock. The counter is a plank laid across two salt-barrels, and behind it stands a man in a worn canvas apron making a note in an open ledger. The smell arrives before everything else: rope hemp, lamp oil, brine. A net frame hangs from the ceiling hook above the centre of the floor, a knotted cord tied to its lowest ring with a small wooden tag. The ledger is what the man's eyes keep returning to.

## Structure

**Narrow-fronted single room.** Shelves floor-to-ceiling on every wall, a pole hook on the ceiling beam reaching the high stock. Counter is a plank across two salt-barrels, Ferran's ledger perpetually open on it, angled so customers see he is writing, not what he is writing. A single ground-floor plan. The back room stocks rope by grade, oil casks, pitch barrels, and sacking in bulk. Its doorway is always open.

## Atmosphere

**Lit for work, stocked for use.** Ferran keeps rope grades together, oil on its own shelf, salt-preserved goods in their own run, each type grouped for reach. He works the counter without conversation unless the subject is a transaction. The ledger closes the moment anyone leans over to read it. The shop smells more like work than commerce: rope hemp, lamp oil, salt.

## Hooks

* **The ledger knows.** Ferran recorded who bought extra rope and binding cord in the weeks before the last three tally-nights on Penance Bar. He will not volunteer it, but the right question from someone who already knows the name Mirela Cass produces a hesitation that becomes a receipt book.

> [!check] Persuasion — The Ledger
> DC 15; pressing Ferran on what he sold in the weeks before each tally-night.
> **Success:** the hesitation becomes a receipt book showing who bought extra rope and binding cord before each of the last three tally-nights.
> **Failure:** he closes the ledger and returns to his work.

* **The surplus.** He has begun buying slightly more lamp oil than the village uses, for reasons he has not examined.

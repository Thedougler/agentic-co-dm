# Penance Bar

> [!read-aloud]
> The sandbar narrows to one cart's width before the village starts.
>
> Flat stones line the pinch point, each one scored with tally marks rubbed pale by years of thumbs.
>
> A woman kneels in the shallows, scrubbing a net that's already clean.
>
> She doesn't look up.
>
> Every doorstep along the beach carries the same fresh smear of ash and burnt oil, its tang catching in the throat.
>
> Somewhere past the rooftops a bell tolls once, off the hour, and every head on the beach turns toward it at once.

Penance Bar is a windswept sandbar at the thin edge of [[midchain|the Midchain]]. Some two hundred people live here, fish-drying racks strung along every stretch of tideline the tally-stones haven't claimed, and they've spent three years learning how to perform guilt well.

## Geography

**Extent:** a narrow sandspit roughly 600 ft. end to end, pinching to one cart's width at the causeway before the village opens out.
**Topography:** flat and low, barely above the tideline at high water; footing runs packed sand and shell along the beach, loose stone at the causeway pinch; sight lines carry clear down the spit's whole length, from the causeway to Sorrowbell's point at the far end; the causeway is the only way in or out, tally-stones lining its narrowest stretch.

## Government

[[sula-marn|Reeve Sula Marn]] keeps the causeway and the civil peace. She guards one of two doors between an outside crew and the truth, and won't discuss Mirela Cass with strangers. [[ysolde-cass|Ysolde Cass]] guards the other door and won't talk without a reason to trust the crew first.

Real spiritual authority runs through [[sorrowbell|Sorrowbell]] instead. [[corvin-ashe|Father Corvin Ashe]] keeps the tally that names who answers for the village's guilt. Every fortieth night the toll comes due, and Ashe names someone to answer for it. The causeway has needed a fresh name since Ashe named the last one for it.

Clearing both gatekeepers earns a hidden vantage on Sorrowbell for that one night. Staked out there, the crew watches a devil come to collect from Ashe in person. It names the debt as his own, not the village's, and no one has to raise a weapon.

Left alone, that stays the law. Within the season Ashe performs another purification, and a fisher's wife who's been asking too many questions gets driven out or killed. The causeway gains a fresh stone. Exposed publicly with proof in hand, the village's penance order cracks, and Marn either turns on Ashe or doubles down to protect the only structure that's kept people alive. Dealt with quietly instead, the devils' collection continues on schedule, aimed now at whoever the crew leaves running Sorrowbell, unless Ashe's actual debt gets settled too.

## Culture

Everyone in Penance Bar performs some visible act of atonement, at all times. Someone kneels in salt at the tideline. Someone else scrubs a doorstep that's already clean, and every doorstep along the beach carries the same fresh smear of ash and burnt oil.

The tally-stones at the causeway pinch carry one flat stone per name the shrine has ever named. Nobody counts them aloud, and [[sula-marn|Reeve Marn]]'s dog sleeps across them, waking at anything that moves past after dark. [[corvin-ashe|Ashe]] keeps a second, truer count inside [[sorrowbell|Sorrowbell]]. That ledger doesn't track village sins at all. It marks one notch every fortieth night on schedule, no matter which name Ashe uses, and the count runs back three seasons to a single date.

Ashe has convinced the village its own unconfessed guilt keeps drawing devils across the reef. Every villager the crew of [[uncertainty|the Uncertainty]] meets is mid-penance when found, which gives them a quick read on who's performing and who's actually afraid. Practiced penance moves differently than real fear.

An unmarked, carefully weeded mound past the last house holds what nobody will name for a stranger. Mirela Cass's shoes lie there. Her body never came back.

## Notable NPCs

- [[corvin-ashe|Father Corvin Ashe]] runs Sorrowbell as its Waveservant. He struck a bargain three seasons past to spare Penance Bar from something he has never named to the village. He has been paying it down ever since, in blood that isn't his own, naming a scapegoat each fortieth night instead of walking into the sea himself. He isn't lying out of cruelty. He believes each redirected debt buys the village more time, and some of what he tells them isn't even false, since the reef has drawn worse since he arrived. The devils work him harder than any threat would. Every fortieth night they tell him they love him and that they'd be nowhere without him, and three years of that has kept him naming others instead of confessing. Exposing him too soon risks more than his own ruin. The village could write off penance itself as fraud and stop doing the one thing that's kept the crossings contained.
- [[sula-marn|Reeve Sula Marn]] keeps the causeway and the peace, and keeps outsiders away from anything to do with Mirela Cass.
- [[ysolde-cass|Ysolde Cass]], Mirela's sister, mends nets on the tideline by day and hasn't slept a full night since the burial. She's spent enough of those nights watching Sorrowbell's window from the scrub to know Ashe isn't always alone in there after the candle should have died, and she holds a cord pulled from the tideline the night before Mirela died, knotted in the same pattern as the binding-marks around Sorrowbell's basin. She says nothing to strangers she hasn't decided to trust.

## Hooks

- **Moral, landing on [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]:** his truth-binding catches Ashe in an outright lie the first time they speak. No villager has ever caught him in one.
- **Personal, landing on [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]:** a fisher mending nets mutters that the tally-stones "keep count same as any shrine ledger." Delmar recognizes the shape of a debt Ashe is paying on someone else's blood.
- **Personal, landing on [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]:** she watches a penance ritual run its full, hollow length. It needles at her own search for a redemption that actually finishes something.
- **Opportunistic:** the causeway toll runs steep for outsiders wanting harbor. Skipping the shrine's line is the fast way past it, and also the fastest way to end up named on a tally-stone.
- **Investigative:** clearing [[sula-marn|Marn]] and [[ysolde-cass|Ysolde]] as gatekeepers earns the crew a hidden vantage on [[sorrowbell|Sorrowbell]] for the fortieth night, when a devil comes to collect from Ashe in person and names the debt as his, not the village's.

> *The treeline dips where the hollow opens, thick with broad-leaved figs and flowering vine that have swallowed whatever stood here before. Old trellises show through the growth in places, split apart by roots thicker than the wood they broke. Pale shapes move through the upper canopy, unhurried, watching the water without alarm.*
>
> *Blood hawks stoop through the canopy gaps, low enough that the beat of their wings carries before the birds themselves show. The wind off the shore carries the sweet rot of crushed fruit left too long in the sun.*

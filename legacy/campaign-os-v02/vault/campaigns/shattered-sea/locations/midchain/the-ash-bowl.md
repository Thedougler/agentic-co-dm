# The Ash Bowl

> [!read-aloud]
> The doorway is low enough to make you duck. Before you've straightened, a clay bowl is already on the counter. Dessa has turned away, wiping something that's already clean. The fire at the far end burns so low the room breathes in amber and shadow. You could not read a page by it. Three tables, worn smooth. The one in the back corner is empty, angled to face the door. Nobody moves to take it.

## Structure

Single room, low-ceilinged, salt-warped timber. Counter runs along the east wall; fireplace at the far end, always banked low. Three tables, worn to the grain. The corner table (back wall, unobstructed sight-line to the door) is [[reeve-sula-marn|Reeve Sula Marn]]'s. Regulars leave it for her. A narrow stair behind the counter leads to three rented rooms above, two pallets each. Fishers who can't make the crossing in bad weather take them.

## Atmosphere

The room holds its quiet. Customers drink bent forward, the same low posture they use at the tideline, habit, not house rule. Every first drink comes in a clay bowl; Dessa started it before Ashe arrived, because bowls were cheaper, and the village made it penance without her asking. The fire gives just enough light to find your seat. The smell is tallow, salt, and old ale-wood.

## Hooks

* **The corner table.** [[reeve-sula-marn|Reeve Sula Marn]] takes it most nights after the evening bell. As the fortieth night approaches, she moves her chair closer to the door.
* **The second bowl.** One fisher, weathered and quiet, orders a second bowl every night and never touches it. He is counting the days. The bowl is his count.
* **Dessa's memory.** Dessa can place any stranger who came through in the last fortieth-night cycle by what they ordered and when.

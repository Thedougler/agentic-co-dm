# The Teeth

[[grung|Grung]] boat crews named this stretch from the water, the only part of [[aruhe|Aruhe]] most of them have ever seen up close: a ring of reef breaking the swell, its coral formations jagged enough at low tide to look like a mouthful of teeth. They never got closer than the gap, and the name stuck to the whole outer ring: reef, beach, and shallows alike.

No one holds it. Grung patrol boats work the surrounding reefs but refuse to land, the same refusal that keeps the rest of Aruhe untouched. That refusal matters more here than anywhere else on the island: past the terrace line, [[blight|the Blight]] holds the interior, but out here on the shingle, the worst a visitor meets is a shark in the gap or a crab shell with a skull for a home.

## Geography

**Extent.** A ring of reef roughly half a mile out from shore, wrapping a single beach short enough to walk end to end in minutes.

**Topography.** The reef ring breaks the swell a half-mile offshore, its jagged coral formations the source of the name. One gap breaks the ring, the only approach for anything bigger than a canoe and the only way this stretch is ever reached. Past the gap, a single narrow beach is the sole landing, its shingle giving way at the tideline to tidal shallows, exposed at low water and submerged at high. A wrecked [[vethka|Vethka]] hull sits half-buried in the shingle above the tideline, what's left of [[aruhe-wreck|the Aruhe Wreck]].

## Routes & Access

The reef gap is the only way in. Anything larger than a canoe has to thread it, and the wrong tide turns the passage itself into the hazard, not just the crossing. Grung patrol boats work the surrounding reefs but refuse to land, watching the gap without ever using it themselves. Past the gap, the narrow beach is the only landing.

## Hazards

The gap threads at the right tide only. Mistime it and a hull grounds on coral or takes the swell breaking across the ring. [[giant-shark|Giant sharks]] patrol the gap itself, drawn by anything struggling to get through. At low tide, [[reef-skulls|reef skulls]] come out onto the shingle to hunt, ambushing anything that lingers on the exposed sand.

> [!mechanic]
> **Reef gap — tidal hazard.** A vessel threading the gap must succeed on a DC 12 [[dexterity|Dexterity]] (water vehicles) check. Failure: the hull scrapes coral for 11 (2d10) bludgeoning damage to the vessel, and each creature on deck succeeds on a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or falls prone. Failure by 5 or more grounds the vessel on the reef for 1d4 hours, during which giant sharks arrive in 1d6 rounds.

The shingle is worse at low tide.

> [!mechanic]
> **Skull shingle — low-tide ambush.** At low tide, reef skulls bury themselves under shingle across the landing beach. DC 14 [[wisdom|Wisdom]] (Perception) while crossing the shingle spots the buried shells. On a failure, 1d4+1 reef skulls erupt within 5 feet as a surprise round.

## Ecology

The same coastal fauna that works Aruhe's beach elsewhere is thinnest and mildest right here, at the island's outer edge. [[reef-skulls|Reef skulls]] hunt the shingle at low tide, and [[vine-lash|vine-lash]] hangs from the vegetation at the beach's edge, lashing out at anything that passes too close. [[giant-crab|Giant crabs]] work the tidal shallows alongside them, and [[blood-hawk|blood hawks]] wheel overhead, diving on whatever the tide leaves exposed.

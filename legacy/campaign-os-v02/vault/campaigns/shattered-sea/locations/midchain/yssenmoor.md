# Yssenmoor

Yssenmoor is an unclaimed wilderness island in [[midchain-north|Midchain North]], nothing on it but wetland and the single guardian who keeps it. Fog sits unmoving over the moor, thick enough to swallow the treeline twenty feet out, and sound arrives ahead of whatever makes it: a voice or a footstep reaches a listener's ear before the speaker does.

The island belongs to nobody but [[sir-quackers-the-fowl|Sir Quackers the Fowl]], its only resident and the reason the fog never lifts. He challenges every visitor before he'll raise a weapon, and answers to no crown or company for the ground he's claimed.

## Geography

**Extent:** roughly three miles across, low moorland ringed by open water on every side. **Topography:** flat bog broken by drier hummocks and reed-choked channels. Fog swallows the treeline twenty feet out. Sound reaches a listener before its source does, so sight and hearing rarely agree on where anything stands.

## Routes & Access

**Only a boat reaches Yssenmoor, and only where the fog thins enough to see shore.**

- Once beached, the crew wades the last stretch through standing water. No dry landing exists anywhere on the shore.
- Word of Sir Quackers' challenge keeps regular trade wide of the shoreline. Ships that do anchor tend to leave again without answering it.
- Past the treeline, the fog and the sound-lag turn the hummocks and channels into a maze. A party without a fixed heading loses the shore fast.

## Hazards

The standing water underfoot is more wade than walk once the fog closes in, and a call for help reaches a party's ears before whoever made it does, so a shout ahead is no guarantee anyone is actually there yet. Nobody's charted how far that lag reaches.

## Ecology

Bog moss and reed-rush blanket every low stretch ankle-deep in standing water, and the drier hummocks carry dense stands of bog-myrtle, whose leaves foragers strip for a bitter tea that keeps the biting insects off.

[[owl|Owls]] hunt the fog on silent wings, taking prey by ear before sight ever finds it, and a startled sounder of [[boar|boar]] scatters loud enough at dawn or dusk to give a party's position away before the fog does. [[stirge|Stirges]] roost low in the scrub and drop onto anything warm-blooded that stops moving, and [[venomous-snake|venomous snakes]] coil unseen in the rush stands, their color lost against the moss until a boot finds one first.

## Notable NPCs

**[[sir-quackers-the-fowl|Sir Quackers the Fowl]]** is Yssenmoor's only resident, and its unconscious hallowing: a hidden ley line has diffused into him until the ground itself counts as holy.

- The permanent fog and the sound that arrives ahead of its source are both effects of that diffusion, not weather.
- He tests visitors with ceremony, not violence, and fights only to defend the island. A party that meets his challenge plainly may find an ally.
- He's been reciting his challenge to empty water. If ships keep avoiding the shore instead of answering it, he may stop issuing it and withdraw into the moor.
- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]'s [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]] reacts to the same ley line, old magic recognizing older magic, worth following for any Sentinel who questions the Eyrie's doctrine.
- He holds this corner of [[midchain|the Midchain]] nobody else governs, worth knowing whatever a party wants from him.

# The Weeping Stair

> [!read-aloud]
> Wide stone steps run from the shoreline down into clear water, disappearing into deepening blue.
>
> Carved shapes mark each step, worn smooth by the water's constant flow.
>
> *(low)* Those who've ventured past the fifth step all come back the same way: silent and unwilling to return.

The Weeping Stair descends further than any diver has confirmed. The drowned city it supposedly leads to remains nothing more than rumor.

## Flora & Fauna

- Kelp fronds and seagrass grow up through the gaps between the carved steps, thick enough by the tenth step down that visibility drops to arm's length.
- [[crab|Reef crabs]] wedge themselves into the seams between stones, easy pickings for a diver willing to work bare-handed down the first few steps.
- [[seahorse|Seahorses]] hang in the kelp with their tails hooked around a frond, scattering only when a diver's shadow crosses them.
- [[reef-shark|Reef sharks]] hunt the shallow steps in twos and threes, drawn by anything that stirs the silt.
- Those who push past the fifth step report a shape too big for a reef shark working the dark below. Kalowe's fishing crews call anything that size down here a [[whip-shark|Whip Shark]], and none of them dive past that step to check.

# Marenui

> [!read-aloud]
> Dozens of hulls lie lashed gunwale to gunwale, rope bridges strung between them, lantern light spilling across the water. Timbers creak and knock against each other with the swell, and the air carries brine and lamp oil.
>
> Vendors call prices between boats over the gaps, trading hand to hand across the ropes.
>
> *(low)* By morning the whole village will have moved on. Nobody here will tell you where to first.

Marenui isn't a fixed island at all. It's a floating market of lashed hulls that anchors in a different lee each night, tracked by regulars through word of mouth alone. Chart it once and it's already gone.

## Flora & Fauna

- Driftwood and tide-wrack lodge in the gaps between hulls faster than anyone bails it back out, and the raft's carpenters split the sound timber out of it for repairs before it has time to rot.
- Kelp and sea purslane grow wild on whatever lee shore the market ties against that night; purslane gets chewed raw for the salt, and kelp gets bundled and sold on to the galley boats by morning.
- [[crab|Crabs]] work the pilings and the shadow under every hull, thick enough that a hand line dropped between two boats pulls up dinner inside a minute.
- Gulls trail the market from anchorage to anchorage and crowd the fish-stalls by midday, thick enough over the roof lines that vendors keep a boy with a sling just to clear them.
- [[reef-shark|Reef sharks]] work the scraps thrown over the rail once the fish-stalls close, and a [[hunter-shark|hunter shark]] surfaces near the gunwales often enough after dark that nobody swims off Marenui once the lanterns are lit.

> *Heat ripples off a slope of black glass, and at its base a furnace glows orange-white, fed by the vent beneath it. Wiry scrub roots into the cooled flows where nothing else will grow. Grey mineral crust blooms along the warm fissures, scraped clean in patches where the guild has already gathered its flux.*
>
> *The air tastes of hot silica and scorched iron. Giant lizards lie flat against the warm rock through the afternoon, and giant crabs pick the tide line below the furnace for anything the runoff has cooked. Somewhere above, a hammer rings on glass that has not yet set.*

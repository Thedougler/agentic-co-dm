# Otanu

> [!read-aloud]
> A thin grey plume rises steady from the island's central cone, bending east on the trade wind, and a faint tang of sulfur drifts down with it.
>
> Along the shore, fishers glance up at it between casts, the way sailors elsewhere check the sky, gulls crying overhead.
>
> One fisher mutters while already hauling in the net.
>
> *The fisher*: *(flat)* It's leaning wrong today.
>
> Nobody argues with her.

![[otanu-narration-appearance]]

The vent stays active. Locals have built their weather-reading practice around its smoke. Sailors elsewhere check the sky. Here, people read the plume. Outsiders who dismiss it as superstition eventually stop. Near the vent's throat, the ground stays hot enough to kill anyone who strays off the safe path in.

## Flora & Fauna

- Ash-fed scrub and wind-bent pandanus hold the upper slopes. The black soil runs down to a shoreline of coarse sand and tide pools.
- Sulfur moss growing near the steam vents gets boiled into a joint-ache remedy fishers trade in small clay jars at Vetau's market.
- [[goat|Wild goats]] pick along the crater rim at dawn, and [[hawk|Hawks]] ride the vent's rising heat to hunt them from above.
- [[giant-crab|Giant crabs]], shells scorched black from years of ash-fall, work the tide pools after dark.
- [[giant-scorpion|Giant scorpions]] den in the cooled lava tubes threading the interior, drawn out by the rock's leftover warmth.

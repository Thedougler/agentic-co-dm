> [!narration]
> *A spire of black basalt rises straight out of open water, taller than any mast, with no beach and no shelf to land on. Old carvings near the waterline show a dozen different figures at the top, and none of them are smiling. Black lichen crusts every crack above the spray line.*
>
> *The stone carries a hum low enough to rattle a hull's planking at twenty yards. Wild goats pick along ledges halfway up where no boat could follow, sending scree rattling down the face. Giant vultures nest in the higher hollows and start circling the moment something on the rock stops moving.*

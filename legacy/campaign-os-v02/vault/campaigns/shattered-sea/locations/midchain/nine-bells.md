# Nine Bells

> [!read-aloud]
> Lighthouse towers line the shoreline, nine in total. Each is lit and staffed. None coordinate with the others.
>
> A bell tolls from one tower, immediately answered by a competing, slightly-off-time bell from another.
>
> A keeper spits over the rail toward a rival tower.
>
> *The keeper*: *(dry)* Their bell's flat. Everyone knows it. They just won't admit it.

Nine Bells has nine keeper families, one per lighthouse tower. Each believes theirs is the true standard. The rivalry outlasts any practical need for so many lights.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt scrub and wind-bent pine: cling low and sideways to the exposed rock between the towers, the only cover the wind ever leaves standing.
- Sea samphire: grows thick in the tide-line cracks below each tower, and keepers pick it to eat with the day's catch instead of paying Rin Dola prices for greens.
- [[goat|Wild goats]]: work the cliff paths between towers, and a goat that wanders onto a rival's rock restarts a decades-old argument about whose family it really belongs to.
- [[crab|Crabs]]: pick through the tide pools at the base of each tower, easy food any keeper can gather without crossing onto a rival's stretch of shore.
- [[hawk|Hawks]]: nest in the collapsed tenth tower and hunt gulls off every family's catch equally, the one thing on Nine Bells nobody argues about.
- [[giant-rat|Giant rats]]: work up through the tower foundations after the lamp oil, and a bad infestation is the one thing that gets two rival keepers speaking to each other.

# The Gilded Cage

> [!read-aloud]
> Manicured gardens surround a dining table set under open sky. Cutlery gleams. Food sits fresh and untouched, warm bread scenting the air.
>
> Someone sweeps every path clean, the broom's bristles hissing somewhere just out of sight. Every lamp burns bright, though no one is visible tending them.
>
> *(low)* One place setting waits for a guest who is not here. It always has, for as long as anyone remembers.

An unseen caretaker keeps the Gilded Cage in perfect order. They prepare for guests who never come. Or maybe the guests have come already and simply hide.

## Flora & Fauna

- Clipped boxwood hedges: cut into corridors and false turns across the interior, so a walk from the shore is a maze whether the caretaker means it as one or not.
- Espaliered fig and pear trees: trained flat along the garden walls, fruit always at its ripest and never fewer branches than the last visit. Anyone can eat from them.
- Lavender and rosemary borders: cut, bundled, and dried, they sell for a fair price at Halythion's stalls, and the rosemary doubles as a poultice for a fever.
- [[raven|Ravens]]: hold the hedge tops at dawn and follow a walking party the length of a corridor without ever landing near the table.
- [[cat|Garden cats]]: thin, sleek, and never far from a full food bowl no one refills that anyone has seen. They startle at nothing and vanish into the hedges the same way.
- [[giant-wasp|Giant wasps]] nest deep in the untrimmed hedge past the garden wall, and a foraging pass too close to that line draws a swarm before anyone spots the paper nest.

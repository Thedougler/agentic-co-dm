> *A stone watchtower leans off true above a scrubby treeline, its lower door a dark gap where someone kicked it in. Salt-pine and gorse crowd the slope below, thick enough to swallow the old path within a few strides of the shore. A thin thread of smoke still trails from the signal-fire platform at the top.*
>
> *The brazier's iron arm swings freely on its hinge, aimed toward the shipping lanes. Wild goats pick along the rocks below the tower at dawn, unbothered by the smoke. The air smells of cold ash and goat.*

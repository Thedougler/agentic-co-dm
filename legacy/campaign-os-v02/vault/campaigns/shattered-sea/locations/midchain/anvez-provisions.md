# Anvez's Provisions

> [!read-aloud]
> [[rope|Rope]], tackle, and coiled netting hang from every rafter, salt and old hide thick in the air. The counter at the back holds wax-wrapped meat and folded skins. An old woman weighs a wedge of salted mutton without looking at the scale, her knuckle tapping the counter twice before she names the price.

## Structure

A converted quarantine storehouse, one long room built low against the wind like the rest of [[stillmere|Stillmere]]. The old quarantine seal is still branded into the doorframe, faded past reading, left in place instead of sanded off. Shelves line both long walls. Rope and tackle sit to the left, salted meat and hides to the right. The back wall holds nothing but bandages and splints. Marta's counter sits at the rear, next to a curtained-off storeroom where the public ledger lives in plain sight and the private one doesn't.

## Atmosphere

Salt and tallow hang thick in the air, undercut by the mustiness of old rope. Marta works in near silence, weighing goods by feel and naming a price once, dry and unbothered whoever's asking. Regulars come and go without much talk; a stranger gets a longer look, and no more than that.

## Hooks

If ignored, Anvez's Provisions just keeps stocking exactly what it always has, and nobody outside Stillmere has reason to look twice at a general store. The moment a visiting trader actually notices the barrel that never drops, that changes.

- **Moral**: Marta sells the strangeness without apologizing for it, and never volunteers what she half-remembers of the old bargain. A party that presses her has to decide whether making her say it out loud is worth what it costs an old woman who's carried the silence this long.
- **Personal**: [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] failed his Session 03 Arcana check identifying [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]]'s necrotic healing magic, rolling 18 against a DC of 22, and has suspected something is wrong about him since without knowing what. Marta's private ledger and her half-remembered fragments of the old bargain story are a lead he can pull on directly at her counter, the same lead her own page names.
- **Opportunistic**: word that a general store never runs short on salted meat, no matter the order size, is worth real coin to a trader who can move volume off this island without asking why the barrel refills.

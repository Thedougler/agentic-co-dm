> [!narration]
> *[[sparhold|Sparhold]] rises in stepped rings of grey stone from the harbour mouth, each tier walled. Watchfires burn at every corner even at midday. Market stalls crowd the lowest street, their boards hinged to fold flat and bar the road in a breath.*
>
> *A watch bell hangs in an iron frame above the market. Woodsmoke and pitch-tar hang thick over the harbour, sharper than any salt-town smell. A crier reads names off a slate nailed outside the watch hall. The challenge posting already draws a crowd three deep.*

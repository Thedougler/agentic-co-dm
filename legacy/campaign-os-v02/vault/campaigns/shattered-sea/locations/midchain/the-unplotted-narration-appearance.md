> [!narration]
> The island climbs out of the haze as a green hill over white sand, a bay biting into its southern side.
>
> Rock arms guard the mouth of that bay, close enough to touch from a longboat, and past them the water lies so flat it holds the whole sky. Outside, chop keeps slapping the hull. Past the arms, nothing.
>
> Salt gives way to green on the wind, warm growing things and still water. Every gull that was calling a moment ago stops calling.
>
> Black wrecks sit still in the shallows past the beach, one of them still carrying two masts.
>
> *(quiet)* No chart agrees where this is. Here it sits anyway.

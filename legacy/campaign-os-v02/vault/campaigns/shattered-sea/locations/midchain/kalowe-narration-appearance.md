> [!narration]
> *Stone bridges cross waters between three islands, each crowded with cranes and rope. A reef blocks the outer waters and leaves an anchorage smooth enough for damaged hulls to rest.*
>
> *Voices call in a dozen tongues: dock workers counting loads, captains naming rates. Overhead the tools rattle on metal and wood. The air tastes of rope-tar, salt, and wood-smoke. Alleys between warehouses and repair sheds stay busy even when the quay is not.*

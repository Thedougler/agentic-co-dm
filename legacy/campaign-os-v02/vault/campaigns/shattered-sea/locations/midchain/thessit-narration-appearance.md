> [!narration]
> *A dozen hulls sit above the tideline, masts up, sails still rigged, not a plank sprung. Salt crust dulls every railing and rope. Gulls nest thick in the older ships' rigging, loud enough to hear before the island takes shape. Between the hulls, dune grass and salt-scrub grow waist-high, so nothing on the interior blocks a sightline back to the next line of masts. Every deck sits bare and swept, cargo and crew gone together, holds open to the weather. Ports across the chain logged each of these vessels lost somewhere else entirely.*

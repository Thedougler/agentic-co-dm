# The Flat Water

| | |
|---|---|
| **Location** | [[kalowe\|Kalowe]], second island, set back from the main causeway |
| **Operator** | [[reweti-kahurangi\|Reweti Kahurangi]] |
| **Access** | Open; meals served from mid-morning through late night |

The Flat Water sits on the second island, set back from the main causeway behind a low salt-bleached wall. It's [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s primary eating house, drinking house, and lodging. The building takes its name from the lagoon it faces.

Most crews working the [[midchain|Midchain]] pass through at least once, and most of the information worth having in [[kalowe|Kalowe]] flows through here between the second and fourth drinks.

## Structure

Low stone walls, a roof of salvaged timber and layered palm, a wide terrace facing the lagoon. The bar is a single plank of dark timber, set on two casks that have not moved in years. [[reweti-kahurangi|Reweti Kahurangi]] works behind it, and she does not rush.

**[[reweti-kahurangi|Reweti Kahurangi]]**, a tortle who works the bar from mid-morning until the last customer leaves. Shell turned slightly toward the room, arms free. She volunteers nothing and acknowledges everything she hears. If a crew shows politeness and respect, she will occasionally share something useful. Once only, never repeated.

> *"Hammocks or the room?"*

Reweti has noticed [[the-bad-receipt|the Bad Receipt]] sitting in the primary berth and is quietly tracking her crew count. She will mention once and not repeat:

> *"That ship's been in the primary berth eleven days. Arrived with twelve crew. I've counted nine on the causeways."*

Someone occupies the captain's room, three nights paid in advance with cash and anonymity. No one has seen them leave. Reweti is [[five-blades|Five Blades]]'s hiring contact in [[kalowe|Kalowe]]. She knows how to reach them.

## Notable NPCs

- [[reweti-kahurangi|Reweti Kahurangi]]: proprietor, tends the bar.
- Damu: young fisher, second table from the terrace; knows where the ship stashed nine crates on a reef rock two seasons ago. See [[kalowe-bad-receipt-cache|The Bad Receipt's Cache]].
- [[davu-kamu|Davu Kamu]]: former first mate and only survivor of the *Cormorant* wreck; occupies a corner table and is recruiting a salvage crew for the *Cormorant*'s strongbox. See [[kalowe-merrows-prize|The Merrow's Prize]].

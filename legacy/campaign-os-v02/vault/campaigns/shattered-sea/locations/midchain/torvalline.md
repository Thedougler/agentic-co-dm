# Torvalline

> [!read-aloud]
> Half-stripped hulls lie canted on the tideline, ribs bare, rigging coiled in neat piles waiting for a buyer.
>
> Hammers ring somewhere out of sight, breaking loose one more fitting from one more wreck.
>
> *(flat)* A foreman waves you toward the rigging piles without looking up. "Cheapest line in the [[midchain|Midchain]]. Ask no questions, we'll ask none back."

Torvalline strips wrecks for salvage faster than any yard in the Midchain, and asks no questions about where a hull came from before it's cut apart. A disproportionate amount of the region's "used" rigging traces back to this one beach.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt-scrub and dune grass: low, wind-flattened growth roots in the strips of sand between the beached hulls, the only green in a yard that is otherwise bare sand and stripped timber.
- Washed bladder-wrack: storm-torn kelp piles up against the wreck lines at the tideline, and yard hands rake it out for garden mulch traded inland.
- [[rat|Rats]]: nest deep in every half-stripped hull, thick enough that a foreman keeps a terrier on hand just to hold the count down.
- [[vulture|Vultures]]: circle the yard by midday, working over whatever the gutting knives and low tide leave on the sand.
- [[giant-crab|Giant crabs]]: work the tide line after dark, and a shell wedged under a hull's keel has snapped more than one salvager's ankle reaching for a dropped tool.

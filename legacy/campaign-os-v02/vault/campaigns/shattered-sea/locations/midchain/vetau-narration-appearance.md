> [!narration]
> *Steam rises off the lagoon in ropy curtains, thickening where the shallows run over the vent. The air tastes of sulfur and hot stone. Salt grass and stunted scrub cover the atoll's low ring in a mat that never rises above the knee, so nothing on Vetau breaks a sightline.*
>
> *Crabs work the tide pools in the hour before and after the boil. At noon the anchorage seals shut, water boiling white from rim to rim, and stays that way for an hour. Vultures circle low the moment it reopens, dropping onto whatever got caught when the water turned.*

> [!narration]
> *Pale limestone backed by unbroken canopy, reef breaking white along the shore with no marked passage through. From the water Murrat looks like every other uninhabited island in the chain. The forest sits low and dense, closing over everything between beach and ridge, and the cliffs behind it show nothing of what they hold.*
>
> *Then shapes move along the ridge, fast and low, converging where a ship would anchor. More follow from the treeline before the first have stopped. A mineral tang rides off the reef, and under it, something coppery the wind does not explain.*

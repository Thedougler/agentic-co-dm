> *Pale sand lies under still water, the reef so flat it vanishes at high tide. Eelgrass sways in the deeper channels between bars, and cockles bed thick in the wet sand where the tide pulls back. A woman wades out at dawn to check the sandbars, unhurried, the way others check nets. Crabs work the tide line at dusk. Infants appear here, unharmed, without explanation, often enough that the checking never stops. The fisherfolk who find them raise every one. The only sound is small waves on wet sand and the scrape of a bucket dragged across shell.*

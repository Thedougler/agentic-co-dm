> [!narration]
> *Driftwood platforms and [[rope|rope]] perches float across a sheltered lagoon, each one packed with roosting terns. The noise rolls out over the water before the island itself comes into view. Shore crabs work the exposed mud between the pilings.*
>
> *A robed figure moves between platforms on a narrow walkway, murmuring to birds that do not startle at her step. One lands and drops something small and metallic into her waiting hand. Salt-marsh reeds crowd the shore, and the mud at low tide glints with rings, buckles, and bent nails the colony has carried back from open water.*

# Wrack's End

> [!read-aloud]
> Bottles cover the shore by the dozens, some old and clouded, some new. Glass clinks and grinds under the tide, and brine hangs thick in the air.
>
> A hermit moves carefully among them, pulling corks, reading, putting them back, and sorting into crates.
>
> The hermit doesn't look up.
>
> *The hermit*: *(quiet)* Yours will turn up eventually. They always do. Eventually.

Bottles wash up here. They come from the [[shattered-sea|Shattered Sea]]. A man reads them and writes notes. He tells no one.

## Flora & Fauna

- Dune grass and driftwood: pale dune grass knits the low ridge above the tideline, and drifts of broken driftwood pile into windrows a person can crouch behind while working the bottle line.
- Bladderwrack: thick mats of it strand with every tide and dry into a peppery wrap that fishers up and down the [[midchain|Midchain]] buy by the bundle for smoking fish.
- [[crab|Crabs]]: pick through the wrack for anything the tide missed, easy food for anyone who beats the vultures to a windrow.
- [[vulture|Vultures]]: work the shore in loose numbers, circling whatever the bottles bring in besides messages.
- [[giant-vulture|Giant vultures]]: nest in the wreck timbers past the tideline and strip a carcass to bone before the hermit ever reaches it.
- [[reef-shark|Reef sharks]]: hold the shallows past the bottle line, patient enough to close on anyone wading out for a drifting crate.

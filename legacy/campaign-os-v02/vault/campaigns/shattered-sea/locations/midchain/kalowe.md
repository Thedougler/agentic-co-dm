## Geography

**Extent:** roughly 500 ft. across three islets joined by stone bridges, the whole site ringed by a reef with a single navigable gap.

**Topography:** the largest islet sits at dock level, crowded with cranes, warehouses, and the main dry dock, where repairs run around the clock. The other two rise only slightly higher, threaded by repair sheds and the narrow alleys where rogue crews hide. Worked stone and salt-slick planking cover the footing throughout. Open water gives clear sight lines between islets. Bridges bottleneck the rest, and the dock bosses watch every crossing. The reef gap is the only way in or out by sea, marked by the [[kalowe-waveservant-shrine|Waveservant Shrine]] where every hull pays toll.

## Government

The Island Council holds nine seats, one per dock boss, deliberately spread thin so no single member can dominate the rest. Each looks after their own business first, but when one faction grows too strong, the others shut it out or ally against it. Kalowe remembers why.

One man held the reef gap alone, two hundred years back. Every hull through paid him first, and nothing moved on Kalowe, cargo, weapons, or ambition, without his cut. His own dock bosses killed him together, because none of them trusted the others enough to try it alone. Nobody wants a repeat. The fort he built to hold the gap became the one ground nobody would let a single faction claim after him. The [[chain-council|Chain Council]] still meets there, so Kalowe has seated no ruler since. The Island Council's nine votes are the shape that fear took: power spread thin enough that killing one man again solves nothing.

Every ship through the reef gap still pays toll at the [[kalowe-waveservant-shrine|Waveservant Shrine]], tribute to [[umberlee|Umberlee]] now though the toll predates her claim on it. Eyes are everywhere, and most fights don't turn violent after dark, but the rule still gets tested: a Dravosi warship once blocked the main dry dock on a false repair claim while its crew mapped Passage spies across the causeways (see [[kalowe-takowan|Kalowe: Takowan and Ship Disguise]]).

## Trade

Kalowe provides ship repairs, supplies, information, passage, or hired hands, and getting any of it means negotiating with at least one broker or dock boss: the Island Council, a harbor boss, [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]], [[five-blades|Five Blades]], or the shrine keepers. Gold talks louder than papers, and a reputation earned on one island spreads quickly across all three.

Ships fleeing [[tidefall|Tidefall]], rogue crews, stolen goods, and outlaws all wash up here. The free port takes any paying hull without asking about its cargo or its paperwork. [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]] stays here when he's ashore. [[five-blades|Five Blades]] hire out from Kalowe as a [[moucheron|Moucheron]] crew, and their first job came in Session 02 (see the Session Log).

## Culture

Locals call the old reef-gap tyrant the Grask now, when they remember he was a man at all. More often "grask" is the word for anyone acting bigger than their actual pull, all status and no hand behind it. Between friends on the docks, it carries real affection. Try it as an outsider and expect a fast correction.

## Notable NPCs

**Ilaria Grimaldi** runs [[grimaldis-dispensary|Grimaldi's Dispensary]]. **[[reweti-kahurangi|Reweti Kahurangi]]** runs [[the-flat-water|The Flat Water]], and **[[noa-fifita|Noa Fifita]]** runs [[fifitas-slip|Fifita's Slip]] (an off-books boatyard). **[[bala-tane|Bala Tane]]** has run the [[the-mud-pits|Mud Pits]] fighting pit for fifteen years. **[[corwin-hask|Corwin Hask]]** works the main docks as a laborer, a dockhand who carries an invisible weight of bad luck that makes everyone nearby nervous.

## Hooks

Five threads run at Kalowe: [[kalowe-bad-receipt-cache|The Bad Receipt's Cache]], [[kalowe-captains-map|The Captain's Map]], [[kalowe-merrows-prize|The Merrow's Prize]], [[kalowe-sawek-bounty|The Sawek Bounty]], and [[kalowe-takowan|Takowan and Ship Disguise]]. [[the-grandfather-hole|The Grandfather Hole]] is a known sinkhole in the eastern [[midchain|Midchain]]. Reef scouts mark its spot but won't dive into it.

[[taken-whole|Taken Whole]] is a rumour circulating the taverns and docks: something red takes ships whole out past the Maw-lanes and leaves the hulls drifting empty.

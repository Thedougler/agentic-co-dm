> [!narration]
> *Fog sits on the island like a lid, thick enough to swallow the treeline twenty feet from shore. Voices carry out of it clear and close, but nobody follows for a long count after.*
>
> *Reed-rush and bog moss cover the low ground past the tideline, ankle-deep in standing water between the hummocks. Something crashes through brush inland and the sound arrives before the movement does. [[boar|Boar]] scatter unseen. An owl lifts off a branch that has not moved yet. The island smells of peat and wet rot, with nothing dry enough to burn.*

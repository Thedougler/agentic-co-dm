# Huahei

A small, overgrown, marshy island in the [[midchain|Midchain]]. The locals speak of a strange fey creature here: the Glutehorn. There's no government and no trade. You visit only if you need the Glutehorn.

## Flora & Fauna

- Marsh grass and half-drowned scrub cover the interior, tall enough to close over a walking party's head and hide the footing underneath.
- Bitter marsh root grows in the wetter hollows; boiled twice, it's edible and locals dig it when the tide keeps the boats in.
- Reed pith peels into a chewable pulp that eases fever, a Huahei local's usual answer to a swamp cough.
- [[giant-frog|Giant frogs]] sit in the shallows and take anything that moves past them, including a hand reached down too fast.
- [[venomous-snake|Venomous snakes]] thread the grass roots and strike from underfoot before a boot ever sees them.
- [[giant-toad|Giant toads]] the size of a dog wait in the deep pools and swallow anything that steps too close to the edge.

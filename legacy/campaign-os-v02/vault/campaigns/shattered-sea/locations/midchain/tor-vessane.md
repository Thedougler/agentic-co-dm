# Tor Vessane

Every Midchain captain avoids Tor Vessane. Ask why, and each tells a different tale. They all agree on one thing: a real curse grips it. Nobody claims the island and nobody sails close enough to chart it properly, so it sits on every Midchain map as a shape with no detail inside the coastline.

> [!read-aloud]
> The island sits low and green on the horizon, ordinary at first glance. Every pilot in earshot changes course anyway, without prompting.
>
> The air smells wrong here, and the silence presses against every ear on deck. Nobody explains why. They just add three days.
>
> *(flat)* "Vessane's cursed," someone finally offers, "we just don't agree on how."

## Geography

**Extent.** Under two miles across, small enough to cross shore to shore in an afternoon, though almost no one who lands there stays long enough to try.

**Topography.** Grass and salt-bramble scrub cover the interior ridge to ridge, waist-high and easy to push through, the same ordinary green that makes the island look unremarkable from the water. The ground rises gently toward the interior with no real elevation to speak of, open sight lines broken only by the taller bramble stands, and no natural harbor forces any landing onto the same stretch of shingle that every reluctant visitor already knows to avoid.

## Routes & Access

No sailing chart marks a preferred approach, because no pilot family works this water on purpose. A captain forced within sight of Tor Vessane by weather or pursuit adds three days to the route rather than cut the corner, and the ones who do land arrive by wreck, dare, or desperation rather than plan. Run any crossing or landing per `vault/refs/vault/location/references/wilderness-travel-and-exploration.md` and `vault/refs/gameplay-toolbox.md` § Travel Pace.

## Hazards

[[venomous-snake|Venomous snakes]] coil in the bramble roots and strike anyone who kneels to forage without checking first, and [[giant-spider|giant spiders]] web the taller bramble stands on the interior's shaded side, patient enough to let a party walk past before they commit. Beneath the ordinary wildlife sits the real hazard: the curse itself, whatever it actually is. No two survivors describe the same effect, and nothing on the island confirms which telling, if any, is true.

## Ecology

Wild sea-fig grows fleshy fruit clusters along the bramble's edge, sweet enough to eat raw and common enough that no forager bothers hauling any back to a market. [[goat|Wild goats]] graze the open ground at dawn and dusk, the only animals on the island that don't scatter when a boat pulls in, and [[hawk|hawks]] hunt the goat herds off the ridge line, the loudest thing on the island on an ordinary day.

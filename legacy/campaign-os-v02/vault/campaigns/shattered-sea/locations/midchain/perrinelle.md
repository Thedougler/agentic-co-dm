# Perrinelle

> [!read-aloud]
> A whitewashed lighthouse stands at the point, its lamp still burning steady against the dusk.
>
> Below, a modest house stands with its shutters open and its door unlatched, tapping against the frame in the wind and creaking with each gust.
>
> *(low)* A logbook lies open on the porch table, its last entry three weeks old, ink smeared where the rain got in.

The Perrinelle family has kept this lighthouse for four generations, and the lamp has never once gone dark. That streak is still unbroken. The keeper who lit it last, less so.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt-pine and dune scrub: wind-flattened pines and thorny scrub hold the point's thin soil, thickening into low forest past the first ridge, so the interior is a hunched walk under a canopy the coastal wind never lets grow tall.
- Sea rocket and beach plum: both grow wild along the dunes below the lighthouse, sharp and salty enough that the keeper's family has always picked a basket before a storm closes the shore.
- [[goat|Wild goats]] graze the ridge above the house, and [[vulture|Vultures]] ride the thermals off the point, circling anything that stops moving on the shoreline below.
- [[rat|Rats]] have run the lighthouse's stores for as long as the family has kept the lamp, thick enough in the cellar that the logbook records a standing losing fight against them.
- [[reef-shark|Reef sharks]] work the shallows off the point in packs, patient enough to trail a wading party the whole way back to shore.

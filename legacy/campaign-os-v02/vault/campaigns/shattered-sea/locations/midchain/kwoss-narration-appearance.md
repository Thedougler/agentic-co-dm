> [!narration]
> *Vultures wheel over a pale crescent of sand scored with flipper-tracks from waterline to treeline. Each track ends at a nest dug shallow into warm ground. Salt scrub crowds the strip behind the beach, and the smell of broken shell and rotting weed rides the onshore wind.*
>
> *Skiffs sit pulled up at the far end, their crews settled in among the nests with the patience of men who plan to stay. Crabs pick at the tideline where the first nests have already broken open. Low voices carry across the water, too steady to be friendly.*

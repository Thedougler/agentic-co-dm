# Ossuary Cay

> [!read-aloud]
> Bleached bone litters the tideline in low drifts, sorted by size into careful rows further up the beach.
>
> A robed figure kneels at the newest row, reading the day's arrivals like a ledger. Bone rattles against bone as they turn one over in their palm, and brine and old salt hang thick in the air.
>
> *(low)* They don't look up. "The sea sends what it's done with. We just keep count."

Ossuary Cay collects bones the currents carry in from wrecks across the whole [[campaign-overview|Shattered Sea]], and the death-cult that tends the cay reads meaning into whatever the tide delivers.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt-scrub and driftwood tangle: low, wind-bent scrub roots in the thin soil between the bone rows, and stripped driftwood the same currents wash up settles high enough to break a sightline across the beach.
- Sea purslane: grows wild in the scrub's salt-crusted hollows, fleshy and sharp enough to eat raw. A forager who strips a hollow before the cult clears that row for the day's count keeps the haul.
- [[vulture|Vultures]]: work the tideline ahead of the cult most mornings, and a party who reaches a fresh wash-up first finds the birds already circling it.
- [[crab|Crabs]]: pick through the sorted bone rows after dark for whatever marrow the tide hasn't scoured clean, and scatter loose bone underfoot when disturbed.
- [[giant-scorpion|Giant scorpions]]: den beneath the oldest drifts where the sand stays loose, and strike anyone who kneels to sort a row without checking under it first.
- [[reef-shark|Reef sharks]]: work the shallows off the tideline where the currents concentrate whatever the wrecks send in, patient around anyone who wades out to meet an incoming one.

# The Ledger Isle

> [!read-aloud]
> A tidy cottage sits behind a low garden wall, entirely too neat for how far off the trade routes it sits. Inside, ledgers stack floor to ceiling, each spine labeled in a private code.
>
> Looking up from her figures, a woman offers a response.
>
> *Accountant*: *(dry)* Whose books are you here about? Be specific. I keep everyone's.

A resident accountant-sorcerer keeps the real financial records for half the [[midchain|Midchain]]'s smuggling. She'll sell access to anyone with the nerve to ask, for the right price.

## Flora & Fauna

- Thorn palm and salt scrub: crowds every yard beyond the garden wall, and its thickness is the clearest sign of how few visitors this isle actually gets.
- Bitter greens: low, silvery-leafed clumps shading under the scrub, edible once boiled twice to cut the bitterness, and the only fresh vegetable within a day's sail.
- [[goat|Wild goats]]: pick along the isle's low ridgeline at dawn, unbothered by anyone who doesn't chase them.
- [[rat|Rats]]: nest in the foundation timbers under the ledger stacks, drawn by the same dry paper the accountant needs kept dry.
- [[vulture|Vultures]]: circle the isle's windward cliff, waiting on whatever the tide strands against the crags below.
- [[venomous-snake|Venomous snakes]]: den in the scrub's deepest tangles, and a bite there means a long row back to anywhere with a healer.

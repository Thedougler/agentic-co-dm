# Rin Dola

> [!read-aloud]
> A cluttered cottage sits crowded with charts, half of them of places that don't sound real.
>
> An old woman doesn't look up from her inkwork, precise strokes filling in a coastline you've never heard named.
>
> *The cartographer*: *(dry)* This one's accurate. I haven't been. Don't ask how I know.

Rin Dola's reclusive cartographer sells charts of uncanny accuracy for places she insists she's never visited, and nobody's found one of her charts to be wrong yet.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt-bent pine and thorn-scrub: wind-flattened pine and dense briar choke the ground above the tideline, so the only easy walking on the island is along the shore itself.
- Beach yarrow: pale-flowered yarrow grows wild through the dune grass, and the cartographer keeps a dried bundle by her ink pots for the headaches her close work gives her.
- [[goat|Wild goats]] graze the scrub at first light, unbothered by the one resident who has long since stopped trying to keep them off her drying charts.
- [[crab|Crabs]] and [[vulture|vultures]] work the tideline in shifts, crabs picking the wrack at low water and vultures circling for whatever the tide leaves after.
- [[venomous-snake|Venomous snakes]] den in the thorn-scrub's deeper tangles, quiet enough that most bites land on a hand reaching for berries before anyone spots the coil beneath.

## Geography

**Extent.** A narrower band wrapping [[aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] on three sides, the ground rising the whole way toward the central grove at Aruhe's heart. Crossing it takes a few hours' climb.

**Topography.** Canopy closes overhead so thick that daylight barely reaches the jungle floor. The ground climbs steadily toward the interior, footing broken by root and stone. Every plant here shows the Blight's hand. Leaves curl black at the edges and sap runs luminous where bark splits open. Fungal growths bloom in colors that belong to nothing native to the island. The [[grung|Grung]] name for this zone comes from one landing party's fragment of a look at it, generations ago, never revised since.

## Routes & Access

The only way in is through [[aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]], following the same game trails the [[bear-elk|bear-elk]]'s patrol uses to cover every approach. Timing the patrol, not finding the trail, is the real obstacle. ## Hazards

The corruption itself is a hazard before any predator is.

> [!mechanic]
> **Corrupted air — spore exposure.** After each hour spent breathing the Rot's spore-heavy air, a creature must succeed on a DC 14 [[constitution|Constitution]] saving throw or gain 1 level of [[exhaustion|Exhaustion]].

The Blight's root network adds a second layer of danger underfoot.

> [!mechanic]
> **Root network alert.** The [[blight|Blight]]'s root network runs above ground throughout the Rot, exposed roots threaded with luminous sap. DC 14 [[wisdom|Wisdom]] (Perception) to spot and step over a root in the path. Failure: the root pulses light along the network toward the central grove, alerting the Blight to the creature's position. The nearest [[corpsewood|corpsewood]] or [[terror-birds|terror bird]] arrives to investigate within 1d4 rounds. Following the roots toward the grove intentionally (DC 14 Wisdom (Survival)) halves travel time but guarantees at least one alert.

At dusk and dawn, the canopy corridors carry a third hazard.

> [!mechanic]
> **Silence-moth overlap.** At dusk and dawn, [[silence-moths|silence moth]] swarms drift the Rot's canopy corridors. A creature inside the 30-foot [[silence|silence]] field cannot cast spells with verbal components, use the Help action on another creature's saving throw, or call out warnings. Inside the field, a party still making hourly Constitution saves against exhaustion cannot call warnings or coordinate verbally.

The [[bear-elk|bear-elk]] patrols a fixed circuit that covers every approach between [[aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] and here. Terror-birds hold solitary territories in the deep interior. Corpsewood walks the same interior on a route of its own. Silence moth swarms drift the canopy corridors at dusk and dawn.

## Ecology

[[terror-birds|Terror-birds]] hold this zone as its apex predator, each claiming a solitary territory it shares with nothing else of its kind. The [[bear-elk|bear-elk]]'s patrol crosses every approach on the boundary with [[aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]]. [[corpsewood|Corpsewood]] walks the deep interior, dead trees animated by the same corruption that feeds the Blight's Memorial. [[silence-moths|Silence moth]] swarms drift the canopy corridors at dusk and dawn, hunting by vibration in total silence.

[[blight|The Blight]]'s root network runs visibly above ground here, luminous sap threading through exposed roots toward the central grove. This deep in, nothing hides what is growing the jungle.

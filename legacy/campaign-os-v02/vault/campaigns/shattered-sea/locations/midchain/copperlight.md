# Copperlight

> [!read-aloud]
> The whole shoreline glows a soft copper-orange after dark, mineral veins lit from within.
>
> Miners work by the glow alone: headlamps dark, tools ringing steadily against warm stone.
>
> One pauses at the first grey hint of dawn and packs up.
>
> *Miner*: *(dry)* Interruption's coming.

Copperlight's mineral veins glow on their own after dark, bright enough to work by. The miners here have built an entire nocturnal culture around this gift. Daylight is something to endure, not work in.

## Flora & Fauna

- Slate terraces above the shore hold wind-flattened scrub and copper-tinted lichen, the only cover between the mine mouths and open sky.
- The lichen stains fingers orange and steeps into a bitter tea the miners drink before a night shift, and traders on [[corvids-table|Corvid's Table]] pay coin for dried bundles.
- [[goat|Goats]] pick along the upper ledges by day, unbothered by the tunnels below, and a hungry crew will run one down for the pot.
- [[giant-bat|Giant bats]] roost by the hundred in the deepest shafts, silent until a lamp or a loud voice sends them boiling out past the miners' heads.
- [[giant-centipede|Giant centipedes]] nest in the cracked stone near the ore veins, and a miner who reaches into a dark seam without checking it first pulls back a poisoned hand.

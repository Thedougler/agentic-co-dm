> *Ships ride empty at anchor behind a stockade of grey driftwood, and no smoke rises from any hull. A quarantine flag hangs bleached past reading beside a bell whose frayed rope trails in the wind. Low scrub covers the island, nothing tall enough to hide a person standing upright.*
>
> *Shore crabs click along the nearest waterline, loud enough to carry across the harbour. Giant vultures hold a slow circuit overhead and never once land on the decks. The stockade gate hangs open on rusted hinges. The silence does the rest.*

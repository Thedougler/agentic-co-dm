# Vantry's Choice

> [!read-aloud]
> On either side of a low, narrow island lie two harbors, both calm and equally sheltered, the same salt-and-tar smell drifting off both docks.
>
> A hand-painted sign at the channel fork creaks on its post and points confidently toward one side only.
>
> *(low)* Ask any local which harbor to use, and they answer instantly. Ask why, and they go quiet.

Vantry's Choice holds two identical harbors, but every local avoids the same one, and none can explain why.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt-scrub and dune grass: cover the island's low spine in a single unbroken band running from one harbor to the other, cropped the same height and color on both sides by the same onshore wind.
- Sea purslane: grows thick in the wet sand just above the tideline at both harbors, salty and crisp raw, and every local picks it the same way regardless of which side they favor.
- [[crab|Crabs]]: work the tide flats at both harbors in the same numbers, easy pickings for anyone willing to wade in at low tide.
- [[vulture|Vultures]]: circle over whichever harbor has boats drawn up that day, unbothered by which one the locals call safe.
- [[giant-crab|Giant crabs]]: den under the pilings on both sides, so a diver checking a hull for rot has as much reason to watch the water at the shunned harbor as the favored one.
- [[reef-shark|Reef sharks]]: run the channel outside both harbor mouths in the same numbers, close enough to worry a swimmer but not close enough to explain why one dock empties at dusk and the other doesn't.

# Thistlewick

> [!read-aloud]
> Thick brambles crowd the path, thorns glinting an odd violet in the light.
>
> A scratch from one leaves a warm, pleasant tingling that lingers longer than it should.
>
> A local grins, showing off scratched forearms.
>
> *A local*: *(dry)* Definitely an acquired taste.

Thistlewick's brambles carry a mild, pleasant narcotic effect on contact, and the small commune that tends them has built a quiet, careful trade around exactly how much of that effect to sell, and to whom.

## Flora & Fauna

- Bramble thickets: chest-high tangles of the violet-thorned brambles cover most of the interior, cut back to shoulder width along the commune's raked paths, and anyone who steps off a path takes a scratch whether they mean to or not.
- Bramble berries: small, bitter-sweet fruit hidden under the thorns, safe to eat once picked clean of thorn dust; the commune tucks a basket of them into every bundle of thorns it sells, to cut a first-time buyer's tolerance.
- [[blood-hawk|Blood hawks]]: wheel over the commune's clearings and dive on anything that holds still too long, scattering the chickens more often than they catch one.
- [[giant-wolf-spider|Giant wolf spiders]]: nest low in the thickets and strike out at anything that brushes a web line, so the commune's harvesters wear thick leather gloves as much against the spiders as the thorns.
- [[venomous-snake|Venomous snakes]]: coil in the shaded root-tangles through the heat of the day and give no warning before they strike. A bitten harvester chews a fistful of bramble leaf to numb the leg long enough to walk home.
- [[giant-boar|A giant boar]]: roots through the deep brush at the island's north end. The commune gives it a wide berth: startled, it charges straight through a bramble hedge instead of skirting it.

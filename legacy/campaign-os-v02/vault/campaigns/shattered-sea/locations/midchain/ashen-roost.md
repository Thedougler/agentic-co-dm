# Ashen Roost

> [!read-aloud]
> Blackened, ash-covered ground gives way in patches to fresh green shoots pushing up right beside it.
>
> A bright ember-colored bird lands atop a scorched branch and simply sits, glowing faintly. Its feathers crackle softly, and the air carries a warm, singed-ash smell.
>
> *(low)* Alchemists pay well for the ash here. Nobody local will say exactly why.

Firebirds nest at Ashen Roost. Their nesting cycle burns and regrows patches of the island. Alchemists prize the ash, paying high prices for it.

## Flora & Fauna

- Ash-scrub and fireweed: wiry black-stemmed scrub covers the cooled ground, and pale fireweed pushes up through fresh ash within days of a burn. The interior is bands of green cutting across bands of black, never one continuous cover.
- Cinder-root: a squat tuber that only takes root in ash-fed soil, dug up and dried into a bitter tonic that alchemists buy alongside the ash itself.
- [[goat|Wild goats]] pick along the cooled slopes at dawn, grazing the fireweed back almost as fast as it grows.
- [[vulture|Vultures]] circle every fresh burn line, working over whatever the flames didn't finish first.
- [[giant-fire-beetle|Giant fire beetles]] burrow into warm ash pockets, their glowing abdomens the only light in the blackened patches after dark.
- [[giant-scorpion|Giant scorpions]] den in the same cooled hollows, and a boot set down wrong on the ash crust is the only warning before the sting comes up.

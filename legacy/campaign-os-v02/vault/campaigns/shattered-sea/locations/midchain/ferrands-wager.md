# Ferrand's Wager

> [!read-aloud]
> Lantern light spills warm and gold across polished tables, dice already rattling somewhere out of sight.
>
> A dealer deals without looking at the cards, eyes on you instead, reading something past your face.
>
> *The dealer*: *(smooth)* Table's open. We already know your limit. Question is whether you do.

Ferrand's Wager always knows what a player can afford to lose. Hidden wealth, open displays. It reads them all. How? Nobody knows. Yet the house has never been wrong.

## Flora & Fauna

- Bladderwrack and barnacle scale: cling thick to the mooring chains and the pilings below the waterline, and the crew scrapes green scum off the boarding ramp every morning before the dealer opens the first hand.
- Rock oysters: crust the hull's shaded underside in dense clusters, and a diver willing to swim under the barge at low tide fills a sack worth a coin or two at [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s market.
- Gulls: mob the stern rail in loose, screaming flocks at meal service. The house keeps a boy with a sling just to clear the tables.
- [[rat|Rats]]: run the bilge and the card storerooms in numbers the house never fully clears, and a losing player who sleeps off a bad night below decks wakes to bites on his ankles.
- [[reef-shark|Reef sharks]]: circle under the barge at feeding hours, drawn by whatever the kitchen tips over the rail. The house forbids swimming off the stern for exactly that reason.

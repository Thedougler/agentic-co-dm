# Candlemere

> [!read-aloud]
> A single flame burns steady in a cliffside alcove, sheltered from wind that should have snuffed it generations ago.
>
> A robed keeper feeds it a careful measure of oil, murmuring something too quiet to catch.
>
> *Keeper*: *(low)* It has never gone out. Not once, not ever.

Candlemere's keepers have tended this flame since before written history. None of them will say what happens if it ever goes out.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt-scrub and wind-bent thorn scrub cling to every crack in the cliff rock, the only cover the interior offers between the shore path and the shrine terrace.
- Candlewick moss grows thick on the seaward face below the shrine; the keepers press it for the lamp oil that feeds the flame, and it's the one thing on the island worth the climb to strip.
- [[goat|Wild goats]] work the switchback trail by day, cropping the scrub down to bare rock, and the keepers milk the tamest of them.
- [[raven|Ravens]] nest in the shrine's eaves and rafters, tolerated by the order as watchers who call before a stranger reaches the terrace.
- [[blood-hawk|Blood hawks]] hold the higher ledges above the flame and stoop on anyone who climbs too near their scrapes.

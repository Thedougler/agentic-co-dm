# Corrigan's Rest

![[corrigans-rest-narration-appearance]]

Corrigan's Rest is where a retired pirate crew settled down decades ago, and it shows. The place is comfortable, welcoming, and quiet about exactly who's buried what where.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt grass and wind-bent scrub run from the cove's edge up to the ridge behind the cottages, thick enough to hide a dug hole within a season.
- Sea grape vines tangle along the rocky shelf below the tideline, sweet enough that the crew picks them fresh instead of keeping a garden.
- [[goat|Wild goats]] graze the ridge in small bands, unbothered by the cottages and easy to track by the bare patches they leave in the scrub.
- [[crab|Crabs]] work the cove's shallows at low tide, plentiful enough that anyone with a bucket eats well by evening.
- [[vulture|Vultures]] ride the thermals over the ridge, circling whatever the goats or crabs leave behind and scattering the moment anyone gets close.
- [[boar|Wild boar]] root through the scrub past the last cottage, territorial enough to charge anyone who corners one in the brush.

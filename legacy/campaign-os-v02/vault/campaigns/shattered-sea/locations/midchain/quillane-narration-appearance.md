> [!narration]
> *A cracked observatory dome tilts into the waterline, half its lower floor already flooded. Brass instruments hang at wrong angles from brackets no one has leveled since the last subsidence. Salt lichen crusts every stretch of exposed masonry and the dome's fallen tiles. A single causeway of dry rock links the dome to the island, and the tide swallows it twice a day. Robed figures wade the tide pools below, calling out measurements and ignoring the water climbing their shins. The air smells of wet stone and dried sea fennel.*

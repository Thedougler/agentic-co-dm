# Windrow

> [!read-aloud]
> Dozens of kites crowd the sky at once, strings crossing and uncrossing in a slow, deliberate dance.
>
> The flyers stand rooted, staring upward, neither willing to blink first, taut lines humming as the wind gusts.
>
> *(bright)* Someone nearby mutters odds. Apparently a marriage is riding on this one.

Kites settle disputes here. Locals respect this. Ignore it and you learn why.

## Flora & Fauna

- Silver grass covers the interior knee-to-waist high, laid flat in ripples by the same gusts the kites ride. A flyer reads the ripple line before a launch.
- A low, wiry creeper snags ankles at the grass roots. Boiled, its bitter sap draws down a swelling, and the kite-makers buy the tar-thick pulp by the coil for binding frames.
- [[hawk|Hawks]] and [[blood-hawk|blood-hawks]] work the updrafts above the flying fields, stooping on anything smaller that breaks cover. A kite string crossed at the wrong moment has drawn one down mid-dive.
- [[giant-goat|Giant goats]] graze the drier rises in small bands, placid until crowded, then answer with a lowered head and a short charge.
- [[venomous-snake|Venomous snakes]] hole up in the creeper roots and strike anyone who reaches in without checking first.

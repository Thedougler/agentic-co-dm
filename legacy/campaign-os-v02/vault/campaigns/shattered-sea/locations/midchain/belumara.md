# Belumara

> [!read-aloud]
> A round sinkhole of startlingly clear freshwater sits ringed by carved stone markers, jungle pressing close on every side. Somewhere below the canopy a bird calls twice and stops.
>
> Small clay bowls line the rim, each holding a coin, a shell, or a twist of dried flower. The air carries the cool, mineral smell of the water and the faint sweetness of the dried offerings.
>
> The water is free to draw. Leave something in an empty bowl, and no one on this island will say a word to you.

Belumara holds the only reliable freshwater for a day's sail in any direction. The local custodians treat it as sacred, not scarce. Anyone can draw water if they leave an offering.

## Flora & Fauna

- Fig and hardwood canopy: closes in a few yards past the rim's carved markers, so the sinkhole clearing is the only open ground on the island and every path in threads under cover.
- Wild breadfruit and ginger root: breadfruit hangs low enough to reach from the bowls, and ginger crowds the damp track to the water. Both fill a meal on their own, foraged fresh from the ground here.
- [[giant-owl|Giant owls]]: nest in the canopy hollows and hunt low over the water at dusk, silent enough that a party notices one only after it has taken a frog off the rim.
- [[giant-frog|Giant frogs]]: crowd the shallow mud where the sinkhole's edge softens, loud enough at night to mask a quieter sound underneath the croaking.
- [[giant-constrictor-snake|Giant constrictor snake]]: coils in the root tangle past the markers, patient enough to sit through an entire offering before it strikes.
- [[giant-crocodile|Giant crocodile]]: works the sinkhole's deep water below the clear shallows, close enough that the custodians' first rule for outsiders is never wade past the marked stones.

# Chorewick

> [!read-aloud]
> Neat fields and shared work-sheds run in orderly rows, everyone visibly busy, nobody idle. Hoes strike soil in a steady rhythm, and someone hums low over the work.
>
> A council of five meets at the well, settling some dispute with quiet, practiced fairness. Wet rope creaks against the bucket as it's hauled up.
>
> One glances at your clothes, then your hands.
>
> *Council member*: *(flat)* You work here, or you're passing through. Which is it?

Indentured workers from a dozen plantations escaped together. They founded Chorewick as a cooperative, with rules strict and fair. Life here beats what they fled.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt-grass and thorn scrub crowd the verges the weeding gangs leave uncut, a border the fields haven't won yet.
- Sea purslane grows thick in the drainage ditches, and cooks pick it into the shared pot without asking.
- [[goat|Wild goats]] graze the scrub past the last furrow, and a hungry crew slips out after dark to run one down.
- [[vulture|Vultures]] circle the compost middens at the field's edge, patient enough to wait out a full shift.
- [[rat|Field rats]] work the grain store at night, the reason every work-shed keeps a cat on the payroll.
- [[giant-venomous-snake|Giant venomous snakes]] den in the thorn scrub beyond the far rows, and a hoeing crew keeps a long stick handy for turning brush before a hand goes in.

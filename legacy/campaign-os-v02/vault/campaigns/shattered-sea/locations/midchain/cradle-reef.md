# Cradle Reef

> [!read-aloud]
> Shallow water over the reef churns gently, hissing faintly over the coral. Small, sinuous shapes dart through it. Scales flash green-gold in the sun.
>
> One surfaces to eye the boat, then dives again.
>
> *(low)* Local fishers give the whole reef a wide, respectful berth. Nobody's ever explained where the mothers are.

Cradle Reef is where young sea dragons are apparently raised, unsupervised as far as anyone can tell, and the fisherfolk who share these waters have learned not to test whether that's actually true.

## Flora & Fauna

- Coral shelf and drift-kelp: the reef breaks the surface in low tan-white shelves fringed by thick drift-kelp, and any hull that cuts too close to the fronds scrapes coral instead of clearing it.
- Pearl oysters: cluster in the shelf's deeper cracks below the kelp line, and a diver who works past the current comes up with enough to matter in [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s market.
- [[crab|Crabs]] and [[seahorse|Seahorses]]: work the shallow pools and kelp fronds in the dozens, the ordinary catch any fisher expects off this stretch.
- [[reef-shark|Reef sharks]]: patrol the drop-off past the kelp shelf in twos and threes, testing anything that lingers too long in the current.
- [[giant-octopus|Giant octopuses]]: den in the reef's deepest hollows and strike from ambush at anything that drifts too close, their reach the only warning a diver gets.

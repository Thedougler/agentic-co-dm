# Roste's Landing

> [!read-aloud]
> A quarantine ward turned clinic. Cots line the space half-full. Herbs dry from every rafter.
>
> A healer works down the row without pause, humming, asking nothing but symptoms.
>
> *The healer*: *(bright)* Payment's whatever you've got. Last week someone paid me in a story. I took it as payment. Best deal I've made all month.

Roste's Landing treats anyone who arrives, no questions asked, and the healer running it seems entirely unconcerned with getting paid in anything resembling coin.

## Flora & Fauna

- Screw-pine and salt-scrub crowd the ridge behind the ward, roots holding the loose sand so poorly that a hard rain drops fresh silt across the path down to the beds every season.
- Feverfew and marsh mallow root grow thick along the shaded gullies, and the healer picks both by the basketful, drying them from the rafters alongside whatever a patient's own harvest brings in.
- Sea purslane carpets the open ground above the tideline and gets chewed raw for the salt, the same use it gets on [[toa-kiri|Toa Kiri]] to the west.
- [[crab|Crabs]] work the tide flats under the ward's stilts, easy enough to gather that a convalescent patient pulls dinner from the mud without leaving sight of their cot.
- [[hawk|Hawks]] hunt the ridge above the herb gullies, and the healer reads a change in their circling as the first sign a storm is coming in.
- [[venomous-snake|Venomous snakes]] nest in the same shaded gullies the medicinal plants grow in, and half the antivenom drying from the rafters gets made from what the healer's own foraging turns up.

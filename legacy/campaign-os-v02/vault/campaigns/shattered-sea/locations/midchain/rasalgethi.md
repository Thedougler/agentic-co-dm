# Rasalgethi

> [!read-aloud]
> Brass instruments crowd a cliffside platform, all aimed at a patch of clear sky. Wind hums through the brass housings, and cold stone underfoot smells of rain that never falls here.
>
> An astronomer doesn't look away from her eyepiece. "Storm wall's thirty miles out today. Same as always."
>
> *(low)* Charts on the wall show stars you've never seen before.

The astronomers built their colony here for one clear sky patch that storms never touch. Their charts map stars visible nowhere else.

## Flora & Fauna

- Lichen and wind-cropped scrub: the storm wall keeps rain thirty miles out year-round, so lichen crusts the exposed rock. Only a few ankle-high, wind-flattened shrubs root in the crevices where night mist collects, and the interior reads as bare stone, not green cover.
- Rock samphire: grows wherever a crevice traps enough of that mist, and the astronomers pick it for the platform's cook-pot since nothing else here is worth eating.
- [[goat|Wild goats]]: work the ledges below the platform at dawn, sure-footed enough to graze slopes nobody else can stand on.
- [[eagle|Eagles]]: ride the same updraft the astronomers built the platform to catch, and nest in ledges no one has climbed to check.
- [[stirge|Stirges]]: roost in the crevices under the platform and rise at dusk in numbers, drawn to anyone who stays past dark long enough to sleep there.

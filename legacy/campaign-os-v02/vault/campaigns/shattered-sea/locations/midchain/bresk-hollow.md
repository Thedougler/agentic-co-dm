# Bresk Hollow

> [!read-aloud]
> The ground simply stops at a ragged rim of broken stone, and the jungle floor drops away into a sinkhole deep enough to swallow the daylight.
>
> Strange calls echo up from below, nothing you can place.
>
> *(low)* Vines thick as rigging rope disappear over the edge, and something has been using them recently.

Deep inside Bresk Hollow lies a sealed sinkhole that collapsed long ago. The creatures there know nothing of the world above.

## Flora & Fauna

- Jungle canopy and rigging-thick vines: ordinary rainforest holds the rim. Every vine that reaches down into the sinkhole pales the deeper it grows, the green fading to a bloodless white.
- Waxy pale fungus clusters: mat the wet stone wherever a vine has rooted. Boiled long enough to kill the bitterness, it's the closest thing to a ration a party finds past the rim.
- [[giant-bat|Giant bats]]: hang from the vine tangles in colonies numbering in the hundreds. They read the dark by sound alone and scatter off the walls the moment a torch comes near.
- [[violet-fungus|Violet fungus]]: grows thick over anything that stopped moving long enough to rot. It lashes out at anyone who brushes it, mistaking it for more of the pale growth.
- [[gray-ooze|Gray ooze]]: pools in the hollows at the bottom, looking exactly like standing water over stone. It eats through a boot or a blade before anyone knows it isn't water.

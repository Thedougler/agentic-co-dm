# Farrowmere

> [!read-aloud]
> Mangrove channels lead to a wide, muddy flat, thick with the reek of fish guts. Dozens of crocodiles rest in rows.
>
> A local walks between them. She tosses fish from a bucket, calm and steady.
>
> *The local*: *(flat)* Feed 'em on schedule, they leave you be. Miss a feeding, that's on you.

Farrowmere's locals learned to farm their dire crocodiles instead of fighting them. The system is strict and simple. It has worked for generations.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt-tolerant mangroves lock the mudflats into narrow channels, roots arched
  above the waterline so every crossing means a walked plank or a waded root
  mat.
- Wild oysters cluster thick on the exposed roots at low tide, and locals pry
  them loose by the sackful to sell to passing boats.
- [[crab|Mud crabs]] work the flats by the thousand, and [[blood-hawk|Blood hawks]] hang over the channels waiting for the tide to strand one.
- [[giant-venomous-snake|Giant venomous snakes]] lie coiled in the root mats past the pens, and a [[swarm-of-insects|swarm of mosquitoes]] rises off the stagnant channels at dusk thick enough to choke a torch.

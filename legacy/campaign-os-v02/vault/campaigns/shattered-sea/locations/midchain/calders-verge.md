# Calder's Verge

![[calders-verge-narration-appearance]]

Calder's Verge produces glasswork prized across the [[midchain|Midchain]], made possible by a natural silica vent nobody else has managed to safely work. The guild's training methods are as closely guarded as their techniques.

## Flora & Fauna

- Black-rock scrub: wiry heather and ash-lichen root straight into the cooled
  obsidian flows, the only green on the slope until the ridge gives way to
  thin pine.
- Vent-flux: a grey mineral crust that blooms along the warm fissures,
  scraped and sold to the guild as flux, since it cuts the melt's working
  temperature, and every apprentice learns to gather it before they touch a
  blowpipe.
- [[giant-lizard|Giant lizards]] lie flat against the black rock through the
  afternoon heat, and [[giant-crab|Giant crabs]] pick the tide line below the
  furnace for anything the runoff has cooked.
- [[giant-scorpion|Giant scorpions]] den in the lava tubes threading the
  interior.

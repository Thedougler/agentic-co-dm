# Ninka's Findings

> [!read-aloud]
> The shop front is a narrow wedge of weathered brick squeezed between a blacksmith's stall and a timber broker's office, its single window armored with iron grilles. Inside, salvage crates stacked shoulder-high leave only a thin corridor to the counter, each labeled with abbreviations: *teeth expedition 6, wreck-dive 3, kalowe returns*. Behind the counter sit locked glass cabinets holding broken compasses and corroded metal assemblies. Some items are fragments of things no intact version exists to compare against. The air tastes of rust and old oil. Ninka works a bone abacus without looking up from the ledger open before her, fingers stained dark with salt and oxidation.

## Structure

The whole footprint runs roughly 50 ft. by 30 ft.: the shopfront crowded with crates and cabinets in front, a back work area behind the counter, cramped enough that Ninka reaches any shelf without moving more than a few steps.

Years of traffic show in the floor, planks worn smooth, scattered copper shavings, salt-spray stains from wherever she last scrubbed down a find. A back door behind the workbench opens onto a storage room and Ninka's own workshop, where the real sorting and cleaning happens well out of customer sight. Nothing back there is for sale, and she doesn't invite anyone through.

## Atmosphere

The air tastes of rust and old oil, and most days the only sound is the click of Ninka's bone abacus and the scratch of her ledger pen. She works without looking up until a sum is final, then meets a customer's eyes to close the deal.

Regulars, salvage divers fresh off a Teeth expedition, wreck crews still salt-crusted from [[kalowe|Kalowe]], learn her one rule before they've finished crossing the threshold: ask where anything came from or where it's going, and she walks. She quotes a price once and takes the coin. The deal closes there, in silence.

> One thing sits out on the counter, where a hand can reach it. A bronze cleat the length of a palm, both bolt holes torn wide, with a cut line still knotted through the horn.
>
> Beside it sits a tray of loose pieces. A pale ridged fragment in it takes one note off the draught from the door and holds it until a hand closes over it.
>
> The near cabinet stands open. A brooch with its enamel burned back to bare metal. A thin band, heat-blued along one edge. A flat disc on a chain with no mark on either face, the chain shortened by someone who wore it daily.
>
> Above the shelf line, past where the ladder leans, hang two brass-locked cases. A rose prism rests off-centre in the first, pulled toward one corner of the glass with nothing there to pull it. The second holds a heavy amulet, the tarnish wiped off the front and left on the back.
>
> The last cabinet is the only one still locked at eye level. Inside it a fan of segmented alloy sits open like a wing stopped mid-beat, and the cracked crystal at its joint throws light through the fractures in a slow pulse.
>
> The key to that one is on the counter, under Ninka's hand.

## Hooks

Dock gossip lifts Ninka's name. She's the only source in three settlements who moves salvage from the Teeth expeditions without asking provenance. The crew needs something odd, broken, or untraceable? She's the place to go.

Deeper talk mentions a shattered [[antheri|Antheri]] component in her possession. Incomplete, undocumented. The control crystal is still mounted to the frame, frozen. Word travels quietly through the salvage crews: Ninka holds what no one else can name.

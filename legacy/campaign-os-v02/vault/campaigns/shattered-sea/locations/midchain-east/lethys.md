# Lethys

> [!read-aloud]
> Sheer cliffs rise straight from the surf, ledges crowded with enormous nests of driftwood and bone.
>
> Something big shifts on a high ledge, feathers catching the light, watching the boat without moving otherwise. A dry shriek echoes off the rock face, answered ten heartbeats later from somewhere higher up.
>
> *(low)* A rusted, engraved collar lies half-buried in the sand below. It hasn't fit anything in a long time.

Feral griffons live on Lethys. They came from an aristocrat's private menagerie, generations ago. The colony went wild long ago. Now they thrive on their own.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt-stunted scrub and cliffside lichen: coats every ledge exposed to spray, thin cover that hides nothing and holds no shade.
- Cliff sage: wedged into the cracks below the nesting ledges; dried and steeped it makes a mild sedative tea, and crews waiting out griffon activity buy it dried from Coeur Brisé traders.
- [[goat|Wild goats]]: work the lower ledges at dawn before the heat drives them into shade, thin pickings but the only meat a foraging party finds without climbing.
- [[vulture|Vultures]] and [[hawk|Hawks]]: circle wide beneath the griffon roosts, waiting on whatever a kill drops off the ledge.
- [[venomous-snake|Venomous snakes]]: den in the crevices below the nests, drawn by the same dropped scraps that draw the vultures.
- [[griffon|Griffons]]: the colony itself, territorial over every ledge above the tide line and willing to stoop on anything that climbs too close to a nest.

# Ashglass

No chart of Ashglass has been drawn; the island earns its name from the black volcanic glass that litters its upper slopes. It is a dead cone rising from a narrow black-sand cove, its lower jungle burned out long ago and never grown back, its crater floor glowing faint after dark on a clear night — the glint Midchain pilots and salvagers trade stories about. No one holds it. No one has come back from it to say what's really down there.

> [!read-aloud]
> The cove opens between two black headlands, sand dark enough it looks wet even where the tide hasn't touched it in hours.
>
> Above the beach the tree line stops dead — scorched trunks, no canopy, no birdsong — and past that the slope climbs bare and grey all the way to a rim you can't quite see the top of.
>
> The wind off the water carries salt, then ash, fine enough to taste before you're close enough to see it falling.
>
> High up, right where the sky's going dim, something at the crater's lip holds a color the dusk shouldn't — could be embers, could be the light playing tricks. Whoever's steering has to decide whether to hold this course or come about.

## Geography

**Extent.** Roughly three miles across, a single volcanic cone rising straight from the water.

**Topography.** The black-sand cove is the only landing. Above it, burned-out jungle gives way within a few hundred feet to bare ash slopes and then fields of black volcanic glass — unstable footing the rest of the way to the rim. No trail, no marked route; heat vents open unpredictably across the upper third of the cone and carry no visible warning until a party is standing on one. The crater at the summit holds the caldera below.

## Routes & Access

The cove is the sole approach; anything short of a small boat has nowhere else to put in. From the cove, the climb is the only route in or out — there is no second way off the slope once committed. ## Hazards

> [!mechanic]
> **Ash cloud (upper slopes).** Wind drags loose ash off the exposed ridgeline into standing clouds. Anyone inside one treats the area as heavily obscured and must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution save at the start of each of their turns or spend their action coughing, unable to take any other action; a creature that fails a DC 13 Dexterity save while moving through the cloud loses its footing (see below).
>
> **Unstable footing (ash and glass fields).** Any check, save, or attack made while moving across the upper slopes at more than half speed requires a DC 12 Dexterity save; failure means the creature falls prone and slides 10 feet back down-slope, taking 1d6 bludgeoning damage from the broken glass.
>
> **Heat vents (unmapped).** A creature that steps within 5 feet of a vent without first spending a turn testing the ground triggers it: a jet of superheated gas erupts, and every creature within 10 feet takes 2d6 fire damage, half on a successful DC 13 Dexterity save. Vents recharge after 1 minute and give no visible warning before they first trigger.

Roughly a third of the way up, the slope holds the wreckage of the last salvage crew that tried — packs, a broken grapple line, a rusted lantern still hanging from a burned branch, and one skeleton half-buried in ash with a rope still tied around its waist, the other end frayed and gone. A careful party that reads this scene for what it is — a warning, not a trailhead — can turn back before the climb gets worse.

Higher still, the tells build toward what's actually waiting in the caldera: heat shimmer rising off the glass in sheets even at night, a roar carried faint and distant on the wind, and once, without any wind to explain it, a curtain of ash lifting instead of falling. None of this is the fight itself — that lives on [[ashglass-dragon|Ashglass Dragon]].

## Ecology

What lives on Ashglass lives on what the dragon leaves behind: ash-pale vultures that trail the caldera at a wary distance, and heat-tolerant lizards that bask openly on ground too hot for anything else, scattering the moment something bigger moves.

## Hooks

A young red dragon ([[young-red-dragon|Young Red Dragon]]) has claimed the caldera at the crater's core — territorial, aggressive, uninterested in negotiation, and a genuinely lethal solo threat: dangerous enough to kill a level 5 party outright, and still a real fight at level 8–10. Its hoard is gold and gems half-fused into the slag around its lair. The full encounter lives on [[ashglass-dragon|Ashglass Dragon]].

The rumor is the only hook: a Midchain pilot's or salvager's tale of the glow in the crater, traded as a dare more than a lead. Not tied to any PC. Not yet seen in a session file — intended as season 2 content, not immediate. If ignored, nothing changes: the island sits exactly as it is, waiting for the next crew foolish enough to go looking.

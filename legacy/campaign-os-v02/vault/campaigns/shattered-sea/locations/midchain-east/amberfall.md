# Amberfall

Amberfall's mines produce beautifully preserved amber, pulled from a low hill of diggings that rises straight out of the strait — from the water it reads as a scatter of gold-flecked scaffolding climbing bare rock, the spoil-heap terraces pale against the resin-dark slopes above.

An independent mining concern holds the whole operation, working every shaft under a single license and selling the best pieces on to buyers who never ask why some specimens still seem to twitch. Locals claim the more unsettling pieces hold something inside that hasn't stopped.

> [!read-aloud]
> Amber-colored stone glints all along the hillside, catching the light in a hundred small golden pockets.
>
> A miner chips one free and holds it up. It's a beetle, frozen mid-stride with legs still splayed, as if caught walking.
>
> *(low)* Hold one long enough near your ear, some claim, and you can feel it still trying to move.

## Geography

**Extent** — roughly six miles across, a single hill ringed by diggings, small enough to walk end to end in a day.

**Topography** — the interior rises in a broken ring of spoil-heap terraces around a hollowed-out hill core; footing is loose scree and slick resin underfoot, and the high rim gives a clear sightline over the strait to [[central-strait|The Central Strait]] but nothing of what moves in the shafts below.

## Routes & Access

The mining concern's single dock is the only safe approach, and it works the tide like a gate — arrive after it closes and there's no getting off the island until morning. ## Ecology

Resin-pine crowds the upper slopes, its sap-heavy trunks bleeding a slow varnish that gums clothes and skin amber-dark wherever the stand is pushed through, while boiled ashen scrub bark yields a bitter cough tea kept in every mining camp along the diggings. [[goat|Wild goats]] pick the spoil-heap terraces at dawn for whatever scrub the miners haven't cleared, and [[vulture|vultures]] circle the open pits, settling wherever a shaft collapse leaves something dead for the picking. The real danger dens in the dark: [[giant-centipede|giant centipedes]] pack the cool depths of the deeper shafts, and a hand reached into a crevice without a light first comes back bitten.

## Hooks

A trader's petrified beetle arrived in [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]]'s workshop still twitching weeks after the sale — she wants a specimen pulled straight from the shaft before whoever's selling them figures out what they're really moving.

# Gullmoth Reach

> [!read-aloud]
> Pale wings crowd every branch and rock face, thousands of them, folded still in the daylight.
>
> One stirs at a stray beam of sun, wings catching the light like dust. A dry whisper of a thousand beating wings rises and falls, and the air carries a faint scorched-dust smell, like a snuffed candle.
>
> *(low)* Light a lantern here after dark, sailors say, and you won't have a lantern for long. Or a hand.

Gullmoth Reach hosts an enormous colony of fey moth-creatures with a fatal draw to open flame, and every ship that's passed too close after dark with its lights lit has a story about it.

## Flora & Fauna

- Black-barked candlewood pines crowd both slope and rock face, needles packed dense enough to blot the sun by midday and thin enough underfoot to walk without a hatchet.
- Split candlewood bark weeps resin in amber blisters, and sailors who've beached here scrape it into corked jars (a thumb-sized lump burns low and steady for a whole night's watch).
- [[giant-owl|Giant owls]] work the canopy after dusk, gliding silent between the branches and stripping moths off the bark faster than the colony can resettle.
- [[giant-wolf-spider|Giant wolf spiders]] string web low between the trunks to catch moths knocked loose in a panic, and a party crashing through at dusk finds both the web and its owner.

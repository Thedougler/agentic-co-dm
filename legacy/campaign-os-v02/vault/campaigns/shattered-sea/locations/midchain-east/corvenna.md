# Corvenna

> [!read-aloud]
> Bright pennants snap over a ringed sand court, and a crowd three-deep presses the rope line, already shouting names.
>
> Masked duelists circle at the center, neither giving ground, sand hissing under bare feet.
>
> *(bright)* A cracked bell hangs at the judge's post, its tang of old iron cutting the salt air. When it rings, the duel's dispute ends. For good.

Corvenna hosts the [[midchain|Midchain]]'s dueling grounds, a neutral court where captains, clans, and rivals send champions instead of ships. What gets settled here reportedly holds, even for people who'd otherwise never agree on anything.

## Flora & Fauna

- Fan palms and sea-grape scrub ring the sand court in a broken green wall, thin enough at the rope line for the crowd to press through but thick enough behind the judge's post to hide a losing champion's retreat.
- Beach naupaka grows in the open ground beyond the scrub, its crushed leaves rubbed into a cut to slow the bleeding until a duel's loser reaches a healer.
- [[crab|Crabs]] work the tideline below the court in their hundreds, and a losing champion facedown in the sand draws them before the judges can call the bout.
- [[blood-hawk|Blood hawks]] nest in the palms and stoop on anything dropped at the rope line, fish scraps and severed fingers alike.
- [[reef-shark|Reef sharks]] patrol the shallows past the landing stair, close enough that a champion knocked off the sand court into open water fights two opponents at once.
- [[giant-crab|Giant crabs]] den under the court's pilings and answer a hard enough blow to the sand by surfacing, mistaking the impact for a rival's claw.

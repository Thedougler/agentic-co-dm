# Cambrey's Fold

> [!read-aloud]
> The path ahead looks perfectly straight, tree line receding evenly into the distance, your boots the only sound in air gone strangely still.
>
> After what feels like an hour's walk, the same lightning-split tree you passed at the start stands ahead of you again.
>
> *(low)* Pilots who've mapped this islet insist it's smaller than walking it ever feels. Or bigger. They can't agree which.

Cambrey's Fold seems to fold distance back on itself in one specific direction, and nobody's worked out whether that makes the island smaller than it looks or larger than it should be.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt-pine scrub: wind-flagged salt-pine grows in the same dead-straight rows across the whole interior, canopy thin enough that the shore stays visible from almost any point inland.
- Beach hibiscus: the bark peels into a numbing wrap pilots strip and pack before pushing off, useful on a fresh cut or a burn from careless galley work.
- [[lizard|Lizards]]: sun themselves along the cracked trunk of the lightning-split tree at midday and scatter into the scrub the moment a boot lands close.
- [[vulture|Vultures]]: circle low over the interior most afternoons, riding the thermal that builds where the tree line breaks near the shore.
- [[giant-scorpion|Giant scorpions]]: den under the drifts of dead salt-pine needles and strike from cover before a foot even feels them underneath.

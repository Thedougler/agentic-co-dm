# Old Harrow's Bones

> [!read-aloud]
> A colossal wooden hull, ancient and worm-eaten, lies half-buried in the sand like a beached whale.
>
> Doorways and windows cut straight through the old ribs, smoke rising from a chimney bolted to a rotted plank.
>
> A merchant leans out one such window.
>
> *The merchant*: *(dry)* Best prices on the wreck, only because we live in it.

Old Harrow's Bones is a single derelict ship so enormous it's simply become an island, with a whole economy built directly into what's left of its hull.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt scrub and driftwood grass root wherever the sand has swallowed the hull, and beach vine climbs the exposed ribs where the old timber still holds.
- Sea purslane grows thick along the waterline, edible raw and worth a coin a bundle to a passing cook. Wild samphire fills the damper hollows the same way.
- [[giant-crab|Giant crabs]] work the tideline at low water, picking through whatever the current has wedged into the hull's broken seams.
- [[swarm-of-ravens|Ravens]] nest in the rigging still lashed above the highest deck, screaming down at anyone who climbs up for a look.
- [[giant-rat|Giant rats]] own the flooded lower holds after dark, and a hand reaching into a dark locker without a light finds them first.
- [[reef-shark|Reef sharks]] cruise the shallows the tide uncovers twice a day, close enough to the hull that a careless step off the exposed keel puts a leg in the water with them.

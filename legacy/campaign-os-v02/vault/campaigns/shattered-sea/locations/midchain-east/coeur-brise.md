# Coeur Brisé

Coeur Brisé marks a marriage pact between two [[midchain|Midchain]] families that broke long ago and was never formally settled. Nothing else stands on the islet — just bare rock, salt scrub, and the split archway at its center, visible from open water the moment the reef clears.

Both families still claim it jointly, and both still send someone out to check on the locked chest at the archway's base. Neither side has ever explained what's in it, or agreed to let the other take it away.

> [!read-aloud]
> A stone archway stands alone at the island's center, split between collapse and careful repair.
>
> The crests of two families flank the gap, their carved faces turned away from each other. Salt wind hisses through the crack. It carries a faint tang of old incense from the offerings left at the arch's base.
>
> *(low)* Nobody local will explain why a locked chest sits at the base, or why both families still send someone to check on it.

## Geography

**Extent** — Coeur Brisé is barely a third of a mile across; the whole interior is walkable in under half an hour.

**Topography** — Bare rock rises gently from the south beach to the split archway at the island's high point. Footing turns to loose scree near the arch's collapsed half, and the interior stays in sight from the shoreline for the entire walk in.

## Routes & Access

A landing party beaches a rowboat or skiff on the south shore, the only stretch of coast clear of reef and cliff, then climbs a short slope to the archway. There is no dock and no marked path beyond the ground worn smooth by generations of family envoys. ## Hazards

Both families keep watchers who treat an unescorted search of the chest as trespass on a grudge centuries old, and neither side distinguishes an honest visitor from a thief before it's too late to explain. The scree fanning out from the archway's collapsed half is loose enough to turn an ankle or spill a climber who trusts a handhold that isn't there.

## Ecology

Salt scrub and wind-bent tamarisk flatten low across the interior, bent east by the same wind that hisses through the archway's crack. Sea lavender roots in the cracks around the arch's base and dries in bundles atop the offerings, so anyone pulling a handful for its medicinal bitters looks like they're robbing the chest to any watcher who spots them. [[vulture|Vultures]] ride the thermals off the bare rock, [[crab|crabs]] work the tide line below the arch at low water, and [[scorpion|scorpions]] den in the loose rubble of the archway's collapsed half — the island's only residents willing to linger on ground two families keep fighting over.

## Hooks

- One family's envoy has quietly offered good coin to outsiders willing to "lose" the chest before the other side's next visit — a job that pays well for anyone who doesn't ask what's inside.
- Sea-lavender bundles pulled from the offerings keep turning up in [[amberfall|Amberfall]]'s market stalls, and both families now suspect the island's miners of raiding the chest under cover of foraging.

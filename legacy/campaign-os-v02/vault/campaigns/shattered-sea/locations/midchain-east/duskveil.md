# Duskveil

> [!read-aloud]
> Wings rustle as birds settle onto low branches, roosting as if for the night. Somewhere in the underbrush something nocturnal wakes early to answer them. The light here holds at gold-grey dusk, indifferent to the sun outside; your shadow claims another hour, and the light ignores it.
>
> The air tastes of cool earth and old woodsmoke, evening's smell hours before evening arrives.
>
> *(low)* Nobody's found the illusion's source. Nobody's tried to break it, either.

An illusion blankets Duskveil in permanent twilight that fools plants and animals alike. No one knows if it's a curse, a working, or something that simply began and stuck.

## Flora & Fauna

- Grey-barked hardwoods stand close and low-branched, their canopy thinned by a light that never brightens enough to push new growth, so the interior stays open underfoot and easy to walk.
- A carpet of pale moss and fern covers every root and stone, damp from a dusk that never dries out at midday.
- Night-caps, a pale mushroom that only fruits after true dark elsewhere, grow here at every hour; dried and ground, they steep into a mild sleep draught foragers sell by the pouch in [[corvenna|Corvenna]]'s market.
- [[owl|Owls]] hunt on a normal night's schedule around the clock, gliding silent between the low branches at whatever hour a party happens to be walking.
- [[bat|Bats]] roost in the hollow trunks and spill out to feed the moment anyone disturbs the canopy, mistaking the noise for evening.
- [[wolf|Wolves]] run the island in a loose pack, their hunting hours smeared across the whole day by prey that never stops behaving as if it's dusk, so an encounter can land at any point in a crossing, not only the usual dawn or dusk window.

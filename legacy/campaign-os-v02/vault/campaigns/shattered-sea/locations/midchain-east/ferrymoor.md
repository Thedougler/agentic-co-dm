# Ferrymoor

> [!read-aloud]
> A weathered dock disappears into thick fog a few boat-lengths out, a bell hanging silent at its end.
>
> A lantern glows somewhere out in the grey, drifting closer at its own pace.
>
> *(low)* The same ferryman's been running this crossing, by every account, longer than anyone's been alive to check.

Ferrymoor's ferry runs on no fixed schedule. The captain never seems to age. Nobody knows where the crossing truly ends.

## Flora & Fauna

- Black alder and cattail reed close over the causeway within a few strides of the dock, roots knotted thick enough to catch a boot and drop it into black water to the knee.
- Bog myrtle grows thick on the drier hummocks; crushed and rubbed on bare skin it keeps the biting flies off, and the ferryman keeps a jar by the bell for anyone waiting past dusk.
- [[giant-frog|Giant frogs]] hold the shallows either side of the dock and take anything that stirs the water within reach.
- [[bat|Bats]] work the fog at dusk, low enough over the crossing that a waiting passenger feels the wind of one before seeing it.
- [[giant-venomous-snake|Giant venomous snakes]] thread the reed roots past the causeway and strike from underfoot before a boot ever sees them.

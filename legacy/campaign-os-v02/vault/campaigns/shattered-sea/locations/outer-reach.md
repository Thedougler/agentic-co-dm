# The Outer Reach

![[outer-reach-banner]]

The lawless water east of [[the-drowned-maw|the Drowned Maw]]. Whatever a crew carries in is what they have, the nearest authority is three weeks west, and the old charts here are a hazard in themselves. Ships come out after salvage, pilgrimage, study and gold. The ones that break become a story the next crew hears in port.

## Routes & Access

Access the Outer Reach by sailing east across the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]] into open ocean. Navigation charts are unreliable and pilots are scarce. Experienced sailors navigate by known landmarks. The Sunken Crown marks the primary route eastward. Ships must carry provisions for long passages as no resupply ports exist. Refer to [[wilderness-travel-and-exploration|travel mechanics]] for ocean journey pacing. The Doldrums create currents and hazards on eastward crossing.

## Hazards

Good navigation and skilled pilots are worth as much as cargo itself. Broken ships face the worst danger, since rescue is far away and ports are scarce. Piracy this far east is uncommon but purposeful: only crews content to be remote hunt these waters.

[[doldrums|The Doldrums]] make it worse. Drift pressure east of the Maw is especially dangerous. Hazards include dragon turtles, sea serpents (old and young), killer whales, [[the-velvet-noose|the Velvet Noose]], and pirates hiding in the empty waters.

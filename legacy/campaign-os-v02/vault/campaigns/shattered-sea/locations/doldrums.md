# The Doldrums

The Doldrums are a windless band of flat water running east-west below [[midchain|Midchain]]'s southern edge. The surface becomes glass, and sails hang slack. Sound travels in strange ways: voices from a quarter mile away arrive clear as speech, and the ship's own sounds seem too loud. The boundary is not marked on most charts. One watch you're in trade winds; the next, nothing. Frigate birds circling overhead signal the approach. Jellyfish blooms collect in the still water, sometimes dense enough to foul a hull.

Its position shifts with the season, so ships that don't track it sail straight in and wait. Becalmed vessels send tow boats, jettison cargo, or remain anchored. Most carry enough water to endure it.

The Doldrums do not need apex monsters to be dangerous. Still water gathers blooms, carrion, and anything that hunts by patience. Giant wasps and biting insects make any attempt to tow through mangrove shallows worse than the charts imply. A crew becalmed here learns quickly that silence is not safety.

## Routes & Access

Experienced captains track the Doldrums' seasonal position and route around them through [[midchain|Midchain]]'s back channels. Becalmed vessels send boats to tow, throw cargo overboard, or wait in place. Pirates with oared cutters can move when sail cannot. A low raider like the [[knifes-wake|Knife's Wake]] moves well through flat water at dusk.

## Hazards

Windless water where sound carries too well. Jellyfish blooms foul hulls, water runs low, and towing exposes crews. Giant wasps and crocodiles hunt. Pirates move by oar, and a black dragon hunts in the deeper southern channels.

**Pacing tool:** drift intelligence is valuable, worth money to merchant captains and smugglers. Start with inconvenience: route around, add days. Escalate to predation when crews fail to avoid the zone.

The black dragon hunts in still water. Becalmed ships cannot resist. When it circles below and leaves without striking, pilots swear the departure is worse than an attack.

**Adjacent pressure:** [[outer-reach|the Outer Reach]] is where drift pressure compounds. Caught in the Doldrums while trying to clear the [[galewall|Galewall]] or reach [[the-drowned-maw|the Maw]], crews face a separate crisis.

## Geography

The band drifts 50 to 100 miles north or south by season. The boundary is not marked on most charts. Frigate birds circling in numbers signal the approach. The mangrove channels south of [[midchain|Midchain]]'s edge have their own apex predator: a black dragon. It has used still water as hunting cover long enough that Midchain pilots treat the deeper southern channels as a hazard.

- North: [[midchain|The Midchain]]
- East: [[tail|The Tail]]
- South: [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]]
- West: [[galewall|The Galewall]]

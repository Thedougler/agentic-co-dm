> [!narration]
> *A dark mass sits over the island ahead, low and wide, the colour of wet stone. It does not move with the wind. The sails catch and the mass holds still. From here it reads as fog caught on the ridge, or cloud too heavy to climb.*
>
> *The sound reaches the hull before the shape resolves. A drone that builds from beneath hearing into something felt in the teeth and the deck planking, a vibration that thickens as the distance closes until individual notes separate inside it, each one the pitch of a single pair of wings.*
>
> *The cloud is [[moucheron|Moucheron]]. Millions of them circling the island in a living shroud that has no edge and no gap. The air between their bodies is the dark. What looked like weather is the population of the island, aloft.*

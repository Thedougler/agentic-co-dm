> [!narration]
> *A dark sail holds a stretch of the [[central-strait|Central Strait]] without closing. Merchant hulls alter away from the lane a full point early and leave the water empty under her, wake lines bending wide around the space she claims.*
>
> *A skiff peels off on matched oars, no flag, no hail. It carries a lacquered tube sealed in wax, a neat label already dry despite the spray, the receiving ship's name written on it in a hand too steady for a rushed job.*
>
> *The distant sail watches through the glare. No gun port opens while the tube is still in the water. The oars keep their rhythm, unhurried, until the skiff closes the last stretch alone.*

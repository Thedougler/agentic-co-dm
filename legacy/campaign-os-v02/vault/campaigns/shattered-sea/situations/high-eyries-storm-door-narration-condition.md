> [!narration]
> *A black stone tooth breaks the sea two hundred feet up, salt-streaked white where spray has climbed it. No dock waits at the waterline. A stair cuts into the southern face and stops well short of the water. Terraces scatter off the summit above it.*
>
> *Maw weather drives breaking water around the stack. A white ring holds two hundred yards out. Spray reaches the lowest stair. Salt stings on every breath.*
>
> *Robed shapes move along the highest ledge, wings folded tight against gusts that never let up. One ledger stays open against the rain.*

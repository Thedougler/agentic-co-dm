# Drowned Maw's Open Shelf

*The richest reachable Antheri workshops lie inside water that grows hotter each time the breach presses upward.*

## Premise

Salvagers stripped the upper [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]] bare. Intact workshops remain at eighty feet, but the lower tier stands abandoned after hot water burned through a crew's line. Beneath it, the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] keeps calling across the planar boundary while [[auralis|Auralis]]'s containment loses ground.

## Pressure

Water warms below forty feet, each returning pulse reaches closer to the surface, and salvage traffic keeps removing Antheri structure from the shelf. The workshop prize, the *[[red-lady|Red Lady]]*, and the widening breach occupy the same water.

## Actors

- **[[pearl-of-souls|The Pearl of Souls]]:** gathers drowned souls and calls across the breach without pause.
- **[[auralis|Auralis]]:** holds the planar boundary. Its failing containment reaches Perrin through heat and command.
- **[[umberlee|Umberlee]]:** recovers her Pearl and the souls inside it. The planar boundary keeps her from taking it herself.
- **[[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|The Sentinels]]:** record the Maw without interpreting or entering it.

## Discoverable / Affectable

- **The heat is not a vent anomaly:** a line burned at eighty feet, the Sentinels' long record, and [[auralis|Auralis]]'s contact with Perrin form three independent vectors toward the breach.
- The crew can reach the lower workshops, bypass the *[[red-lady|Red Lady]]*, salvage the Pearl, disturb the site, or leave the shelf alone.
- **PC gravity:** [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] can reach engineering that advances her flying machine. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] can press his patron's command. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] can reach the trapped souls. [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] can resume her unfinished Maw survey.

## If Ignored

The next heat pulse drives the last lower-tier crews off the Shelfworks. When containment fails, water covers the upper ruins and ends the gold rush. The wider breach then admits something larger than the [[leviathan|Leviathan]] to answer the Pearl.

I am reading for the Dungeon Master of the [[shattered-sea|Shattered Sea]]

I built the storms, the sewers, and the second chances in this sea, and I've run it long enough to stop pretending I don't know which one you'll hit first.

Every villain at my table knows exactly what they want. Every ally is one bad night from bleeding for a stranger. That's not a boast, that's just Tuesdays.

[[calveno|Calveno]], [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]], [[ponte-bassa|the Ponte Bassa]], [[warren|the Warren]] — say those four fast enough and you'll sound like you've lived here your whole life.

Here's the part I actually believe, before anything else: every die I roll at this table lands honest, and every clock I set, I mean to keep.

Six sunken sewer sites, sixteen slippery [[grung|Grung]] skiffs, and one still-simmering scheme that took two whole seasons to finally go off.

This is a sea worth crossing, storms and all. Come sit down and find out for yourself.

Every Dungeon Master tells you their table runs different. Let me show you the actual receipts.

*(furious)*

*(delighted)*

Chain Council gets four Fronts. The Crown gets two. The Waveservants get one. At some point I stopped writing conflicts and started running a filing cabinet.

Six shattered secondary sites, civilians screaming in the rubble, and one selfish slaad still refusing to stop — sit. Down. Otar.

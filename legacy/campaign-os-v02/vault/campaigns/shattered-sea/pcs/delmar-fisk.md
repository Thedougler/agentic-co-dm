## Overview

Sandy red hair sits in a messy topknot above a thick beard and a curled moustache greying at the edges. His smile reaches his eyes, built by the sea and held together by charm. The scarlet admiral coat that would mark him out stays locked in the captain's chest ashore in [[calveno|Calveno]], worn only when he has to.

## Backstory

[[human|Human]] (Variant), a sailor turned self-appointed Admiral, youngest in living memory. He bought the title with charm before anyone else granted it. He fights as a [[swashbuckler|Swashbuckler]] ([[rogue-swashbuckler|full subclass text]]) [[rogue|Rogue]]. [[umberlee|Umberlee]] pressed five captains' souls into him when his fleet sank, and those five, loyal through every rank he held, never moved on. They surface now as five distinct voices, each carrying its own skill proficiencies and borrowed mastery. Running one too long drifts his hair and his cadence, and shifts the name he answers to: in Session 03 he mistakenly called Crissdalynn "Crystalline." A brass flagon anchors him back to himself before a voice takes hold, from the night after the mutiny, when he and five loyal crew, stranded with nothing else, passed it hand to hand. That night gave his fleet its start.

The fleet had stolen the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] from [[umberlee-shrine|Umberlee's Blue Shrine]] on [[vel-orn|Vel Orn]]. The [[red-lady|Red Lady]] led the fleet. [[the-narrow|The Narrow]], [[heft|Heft]], [[fernen|Fernen]], and [[loud-argument|Loud Argument]] went down with it. [[rael-corven|Rael Corven]], [[maren|Maren]], [[detto-kalash|Detto Kalash]], [[sienne-orre|Sienne Orre]], and [[bastian-ookage|Bastian Ookage]] captained the five vessels (see [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]]).

> [!spoiler]
> The crime that triggered the fleet's sinking was Delmar's own call. He didn't intend what happened, but five people paid with their souls. He hasn't told anyone.

He wants to resurrect the five captains. Failing that, he wants to release them, so they can find their afterlives. Full resurrection is where his guardrails come off. Past that, he wants to become stable and forgiven, though he knows he has to earn it. He leads with kindness by default, a habit built on the belief that everyone has value at any station, even people who don't see their own use in themselves. He learned to read people to protect himself. Loyalty to found family is his one absolute. Call his crew backstabbers and the smile stays, but the eyes stop following. Violence becomes an option. In a crisis he checks the group, then himself, then picks fight, run, or the order to do it in. He'll talk his way out first when it pays better. Ferrets, not weasels, trigger pure terror in him, a boyhood fear that still shows on his face when it's triggered.

A shipwreck during a pirate attack cost him his childhood. Stranded on an island with an angry, unsafe group of survivors, he built a raft with help from one kind crew member and got away. Years later he came back with a proper boat to save the others and found only corpses. Something hardened in him. He kept the smile.

He calls his musket The Duchess and his rapier The Baroness. The scarlet admiral coat and the [[delmars-cloak-of-the-manta-ray|Cloak of the Manta Ray]] stay locked with it in the captain's chest ashore in [[calveno|Calveno]]. A pocket watch holds his mother's picture, his father's only relic, stolen for him by a woman with quick hands.

## Relationships

- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]]: the fixed point, sole co-survivor of the [[red-lady|Red Lady]]. She pulled him from the crow's nest as the Maw closed. Her opinion is the one that matters.
- [[margaret-porcelain-pots|Margaret "Maggie" Porcelain Pots]]: first love, [[dragonborn|Dragonborn]], glittering peach-and-cream scales and rose-pink eyes. The shiner she left him with after an argument has kept him off his home island ever since. A reunion would hit hard on both sides.
- [[serafina-windfall-fisk|Serafina Windfall Fisk]]: his wife, an Aasimar fortune-teller pulled through the [[verdant-scatter|Verdant Scatter]] on a precognitive compulsion she's stopped fighting. She wasn't aboard the *Red Lady* when the fleet went down. He named The Duchess for her.

## Arc Notes (DM Only)

He is never alone in his own head, and the confession behind the fleet's sinking (see Backstory) stays beneath the title. His marriage with [[serafina-windfall-fisk|Serafina]] runs on an open arrangement. Physical encounters with others are acceptable. Denying the other's existence is the one line, and he has never actually tested it. She does not know about the five captains' voices.

> [!spoiler]
> **Harbor gossip (post-Session 04).** [[branca|Branca]] reported the shrine communion to the Church of Umberlee as the largest divine event the Calveno Waveservants have seen in living memory. Harbor-district clergy are now watching for "the one in the scarlet coat," though not his name yet, just enough for a sharp listener to connect the dots. His Umberlee debt has left the crew of the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] and entered Calveno's maritime grapevine.

> [!mechanic]
>
> - **Spotlight (source: system/delmar-primer.md):** an isolated enemy with clear movement lanes, plus a loyalty choice for the crew.
> - **Pressure:** the threat of being [[grappled|Grappled]], restraint, split spacing, social insults. Avoid stacking [[restrained|Restrained]] with [[blinded|Blinded]]. Avoid anti-magic pressure too, since he has no spells or concentration to threaten.
> - **Struggling:** add prone setups or ally adjacency. Or bring in a target that overextends.
> - **Dominating:** use enemy spacing or the threat of being [[grappled|Grappled]]. Restraint pressure works too.
> - **Dramatic lever:** insults about his crew and his ferret phobia escalate him fastest (see Backstory). Concrete choices to protect or dignify his people calm him: threaten a crew member's dignity or safety, then offer a costly rescue.

**Drowned Maw prep connectivity.** The Pearl of Souls (stolen from Umberlee's shrine), Crissdalynn's incomplete pilgrimage, and Perrin's Leviathan encounter all trace back to [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]]. This convergence ties Delmar's personal quest to the campaign's larger threats. Every crew member carries a thread back to this nexus, making it a high-value prep target for interconnected storytelling.

> [!mechanic]
> **Rakish Audacity / Sneak Attack.** Rakish Audacity rewards isolated targets. Poor spacing or enemy clusters deny it. Sneak Attack needs advantage, adjacency, or Swashbuckler geometry. Don't shut it off every round. No magic means mundane pressure matters, and anti-magic wastes the budget. At level 5, Uncanny Dodge improves survival against single heavy hits. Sneak Attack scales every 2 rogue levels. Level 9's Elegant Maneuver strengthens mobility and positioning.
>
> **Tactical note (source: system/party-combat-primer.md).** His boots of flying (4hr, attunement, regains 2hr per 12hr unused) make him the crew's second airborne fighter alongside [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]. This changes encounter geometry going forward. Static, single-plane encounters get trivialized. Multi-axis terrain (vertical, horizontal, water where possible) rewards the whole crew instead of just him.

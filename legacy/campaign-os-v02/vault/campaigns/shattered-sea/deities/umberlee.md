# Umberlee

*The dominant sea deity of [[campaign-overview|the Shattered Sea]], feared and worshipped as ordinary port infrastructure through the [[waveservants|Waveservants]].*

## Portfolio & Presence

Umberlee is the sea as sailors fear it: black waters under moonless clouds, masts cracking in squalls at the reef where the chart promised depth. She is the wave that takes one person and leaves the rest alive to remember it. She claims the sea, all mortal water, never elemental water. As the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]] fissure widens, real trench water gives way to elemental water that looks, tastes, and kills exactly the same to mortal senses, and she loses her claim over it anyway, a loss no mortal will ever notice but her. Her power also stops at [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]] itself, where the deep waters were never hers, so she cannot get the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl]] back from there herself. She is fear itself, dressed in trade: sailors would smile if her reach fell short, so she hides this fear and sends [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] orders with no reason. Her terror stays close and silent.

Umberlee rarely shows her face. She acts through storms, through her clergy, and through the [[dead-lady|Dead Lady]], her tool for the Fisk retrieval and the shape she wears when her clergy cannot finish the work. Speaking through [[branca|Branca]] at the Calveno shrine confirms the same pattern: she reaches Fisk through a mortal mouth, never her own. Her presence otherwise shows in weather that turns wrong, in Waveservants who look at Fisk with recognition before they speak.

She bargains coldly and honors only the deal. At the [[calveno|Calveno]] harbor shrine, Umberlee spoke through [[branca|Branca]] to name her price: the Pearl in exchange for Delmar's life, and the stolen souls staying with her regardless. Delmar tried to trade the thieves who'd commissioned him instead. Umberlee read the offer straight out of his mind, saw through an earlier lie, and held the deal off until the Pearl actually comes back. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] confessed the theft the same day [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]] met its end in her wrath. Instead of releasing the five drowned captains' souls, she pressed them into Fisk's own mind, where they still ride as personalities. The debt for it remains open, uncollected, and unknown to the table. Her power over Fisk establishes leverage without requiring a divine confrontation: water, unpaid terms, shrine witnesses, and the captains' voices come before putting a god in the room.

Umberlee also holds the [[the-tithe-of-the-bitch-queen|Tithe of the Bitch Queen]], beautiful things claimed from ships over centuries, now scattered across the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]] seafloor. Any item taken brings squalls until the sea takes it back.

## Worship

In [[shattered-sea|the Shattered Sea]], sailors speak her name with caution more often than blasphemy. Captains give tribute before risky crossings while deckhands mutter it at any wind change, and survivors pay after landfall because gratitude and fear look the same at a shrine basin. Sailors pay because the sea kills, not because she is kind. Her public title is the Bitch Queen.

The [[waveservants|Waveservants]] collect tributes, witness deals, and tend the shrine rites at every harbor. They do not speak of [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]]'s theological problem; they say instead that the Maw is a strange place that will one day come back under her rule. The sea elves of [[halythion|Halythion]] predate her claim and do not acknowledge it, and [[valkur|Valkur]] is the quiet counterweight sailors invoke below decks when they don't want to pay her price.

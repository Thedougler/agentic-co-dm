> [!narration]
> *The *[[red-lady|Red Lady]]* is a two-masted brigantine riding low, her hull stained a deep even red from waterline to rail. The colour is in the wood, not on it. Seven cannon ports line each side. Swivel mounts sit at bow and stern.*
>
> *At the prow a pale wooden figurehead of a cloaked woman leans forward with one hand open at her side. The ship is long, clean-lined, built to chase, fight, and keep going.*
>
> *Tar, salt, and old powder sit in the grain. She looks ready to sail even when she is not.*

# Heft

A heavy brig, the largest ship in [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]] after the [[red-lady|Red Lady]]. Broad in the beam and deep-keeled, high freeboard, the hull sitting low under its own weight. Hull painted black from waterline to rail with white gun port lids. Each side carried five gun mounts. A deliberate presence. Nothing about her proportions suggested speed.

*Heavy brig · Third of Fisk's Fleet.*

Not revealed to players. Delmar's confession established the fleet's existence (see [[transcript|session 04 transcript]]), but the DM deliberately withheld the individual ship names (`vault/campaigns/shattered-sea/factions/fisks-fleet.md`).

Below decks, a magazine sat below the waterline alongside a surgeon's berth and crew quarters for a full complement. The hold ran deep and wide, built to take punishment and hold position. She perished simultaneously with the rest of [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]] when [[umberlee|Umberlee]] opened [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]] beneath them. All hands lost.

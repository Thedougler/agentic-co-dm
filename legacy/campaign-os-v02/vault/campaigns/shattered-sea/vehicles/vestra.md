# The Vestra

![[vestra-narration-appearance]]

*Two-masted brig · wrecked off [[keth-naar|Keth Naar]].*

A small two-masted brig built for [[rattkin|Rattkin]] crews, and the Black-Jaw family heirloom. [[vincenzo-black-jaw|Vincenzo Black-Jaw]] built her and was her first captain, but died at sea. His widow, [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]], kept the ship in the family. Perrin later took command, added gun mounts, and upgraded her sails. Before the campaign started, she sank, taking all his wealth.

The *Vestra* sank so fast no one could tell what was hitting her. Something struck from below the hull. Perrin alone survived. The rest died. He washed up on Keth Naar.

In Session 03, Perrin told Nona the truth: no one survived. She offered sympathy to the dead crew and stopped her planned attacks on Perrin (`vault/episodes/003/transcript.md:518-524`).

Perrin does not know what destroyed the ship. For the full story, see [[leviathan|Leviathan]] § Overview and [[auralis|Auralis]] § The Vestra secret.

Maybe the Leviathan sank her (the DM's best guess, unresolved). For details see `Inbox/situations/active/what-sunk-the-vestra.md`. What happened instead: Auralis saved Perrin, not his cloak. This formed an unwitting pact. Perrin still thinks his [[cloak-of-the-manta-ray|Cloak of the Manta Ray]] saved him.

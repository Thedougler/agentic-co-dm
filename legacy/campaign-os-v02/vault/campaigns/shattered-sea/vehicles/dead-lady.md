# The *Dead Lady*

![[dead-lady-narration-appearance]]

*Brigantine (spectral) · Tier 2 · unmoored.*

When she first appears she matches [[delmar-fisk|Fisk]]'s heading but does not close, visible only at dawn and dusk. Other ships give his convoy distance and ports show visible unease. This is a warning, not yet a fight. If she closes to weapon range, she attacks the other vessels sailing with him (never Fisk's own ship directly), circling and boarding those vessels at this stage only. If she escalates further, she begins targeting Fisk's ship's hull directly: she spares Fisk's life but will destroy his ship and take anyone who enters the water through the hull itself.

The [[red-lady|Red Lady]], raised by [[umberlee|Umberlee]] as a punishment instrument against [[delmar-fisk|Fisk]]. She exists to compel Fisk to retrieve the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]], not to kill him. Umberlee cannot kill Fisk without losing the five captains' souls into the Maw a second time, and she knows this. The *Dead Lady* is pressure, not execution.

**Trigger:** [[waveservants|Waveservants]] deliver Umberlee's terms once she learns Fisk survived and is operating. She sets a deadline (8 to 12 weeks from first contact) for visible progress toward retrieving the Pearl. If the deadline passes without action, Umberlee dispatches the *Dead Lady*.

**Stage 1: contact.** A Waveservant delivers Umberlee's terms directly to Fisk: retrieve the Pearl of Souls from the Maw and return it. She names the deadline. She does not describe the consequence.

**Stage 2: sighting.** Not a Reputation loss yet. This is a warning, not yet an attack.

**Stage 3: approach.** Reputation loss: 1 step with factions/ports aware of the situation.

**Stage 4: escalation.** Fisk cannot sail, cannot port, cannot operate.

**Resolution paths:**

| Path | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **Compliance** | Fisk demonstrates movement toward the Maw. Umberlee stands the *Dead Lady* down to the horizon. Returns if progress stops. |
| **Appeasement** | Significant offering + deed of service to Waveservants buys a deadline extension. Does not buy a Stage 4 reversal. |
| **Destruction** | Disperses 1d4 weeks. Umberlee reconstitutes. Underlying situation unresolved. |
| **Negotiation** | Fisk possesses bargaining power. Umberlee needs him alive and cannot retrieve the Pearl herself. A player who works this out and pushes can negotiate directly with a goddess. See [[pearl-of-souls\|Pearl of Souls]] for the full leverage shape. |

**She has no captain.** Umberlee drives her directly. Nothing to bargain with, deceive, or remove.

## Stats & Combat

> [!mechanic]
> **[[combat|Combat]] activation.** Stats below apply at Stage 3 and Stage 4 only. Before that, she exists as an omen, not yet an opponent.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hull Points** | 230 |
| **Hull AC** | 14 (partially incorporeal) |
| **[[speed\|Speed]] (any wind / calm)** | 90 miles/day |
| **Speed (night, open water)** | 120 miles/day |
| **Gun Mounts** | 14 (7 per side) |
| **Weapons** | 12 × Spectral Cannon; 2 × Swivel |

**[[damage|Damage]] [[immunity|Immunity]]: poison,** psychic, non-magical physical. **Damage [[resistance-spell|Resistance]]: fire, cold, necrotic. Damage [[vulnerability|Vulnerability]]: radiant, force. Destroying her: reduces** to 0 HP disperses for 1d4 weeks; Umberlee reconstitutes her. Permanent destruction requires a ritual of campaign scope.

> [!mechanic]
> **Spectral Cannons.** Silent at firing. The shot arrives with only displaced water. vs. crew: 4d10 cold damage, DC 14 Con save or gain 1 exhaustion level. vs. hull: double damage against Tier 1 vessel hull material.

**Dead Reckoning.** Always knows where Fisk is. Navigation tricks, false trails, fog, and weather do not affect her course.

**Forbidding Tide.** Ships within 1 mile face reversed currents and unnatural headwinds. Tier 1 ships −20 miles/day, Tier 2 ships −10 miles/day. This effect is passive and constant.

**Spectral Boarding.** Her crew pass through solid hull material. Boarding requires no entry points.

**The Voice Below Deck.** When within half a mile of Fisk, he hears voices from below her waterline (his crew, calling up through the hull), and so do the five captains in his mind. This is not a mechanical effect. Information arrives without a save.

> [!mechanic]
> **Umberlee's Wake.** Creatures overboard within 300 feet must make a DC 16 [[strength|Strength]] save or get pulled 30 feet toward her hull at the start of each turn. A creature dragged into contact with the hull faces capture by spectral crew (see Spectral Boarding), who haul them aboard.

## Crew

- Captain: none. Umberlee drives her directly.

- Rank-and-file sailors of Fisk's original five-ship fleet (not the captains, who are in Fisk's mind). Approximately 30 crew. Recognisable as individuals to anyone who sailed with the fleet. They do not speak. If Fisk sees them close enough to identify faces, he recognises them as do the five captains in his mind.

- Type: shadow, using ship-scaled shadow statistics. Can move through solid hull material. Do not begin boarding before Stage 3.

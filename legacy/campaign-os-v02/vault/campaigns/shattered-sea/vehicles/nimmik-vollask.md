# Nimmik Vollask

> [!read-aloud]
> The *Nimmik Vollask* rides bright orange against grey water, boxy from bow to stern, two-foot Gnomish letters running the length of both sides.
>
> A halfling woman waits at the starboard rail behind a folding table, ledger open, a coffee pot steaming at her elbow.
>
> Below the main hatch, gnomish voices count off a rhythm, and a cargo crane creaks as it swings out empty over the gap.
>
> Tar and frying meatballs drift up from the hold. The woman closes her ledger and looks up at you, chalk already in hand.

*Cargo brig · Tier 2 · unmoored.*

Design. Sixty gnomes live and work aboard, and it shows: gnomes built the hull for cargo first, then fitted the decks, holds, and corridors to themselves instead of the other way round. The ship rocks and sways in any wind, and shipbuilders who've looked her over call the centre of gravity "theoretical." Nobody aboard who speaks Gnomish will say what *Nimmik Vollask* means.

Service model: pull up to the *Depot* and flag [[cotter-foss|Cotter Foss]], the ship's shopkeeper, at the rail. Tell her what cargo you need and pay her fee. Free coffee and gnomish meatballs come with the deal (small, dense, really good). Below decks, sixty gnomes find, pack, and wrap the order fast. This crane-and-hatch handoff is the only way a Medium-or-larger customer's order ever reaches them. Nobody that size boards the ship. When ready, the bundle shoots out the hold hatch with a yell, gnomish voices calling the manifest across the gap so Cotter can confirm it by ear before she gives clearance. The crane swings, the bundle sails the open water between the two hulls, and it lands in your hold to a cheer from below. If it misses, they log it and write an apology.

Stock: rope, canvas, food, fresh water, tar, pitch, oakum, block and tackle, tools, lanterns, oil, candles, and free meatballs. Healer's kits and antitoxin in stock. Alchemist's fire by request. The gnomes' own workshop goods sell alongside the mundane stock: a [[depot-signal-whistle|Depot Signal Whistle]] (5 gp) for calling across fog, a [[gnomish-splicing-fid|Gnomish Splicing Fid]] (8 gp) stamped with a glyph nobody explains, a [[self-heating-tar-pot|Self-Heating Tar Pot]] (12 gp) that never needs an open flame, [[vollask-meatball-tin|Vollask Meatball Tins]] (1 gp) sealed for months at sea, and [[gnomish-rigging-block|Gnomish Rigging Blocks]] (15 gp) off the same line that runs the cargo cranes. A [[stormwork-quiver|Stormwork Quiver]] (150 gp, attunement required) sits behind the normal goods — gnomish-charged lining, arrows come out crackling. Other items available through Cotter.

Layout. Weather deck: sized for gnomes (railings knee-high to Humans), hatches, rope staging, two cargo cranes, and the main hatch. Between-decks: working floor, 4.5 ft high, gnome-width halls and bunks, sorted stock. The gnomes cut hatches and corridors Small-or-smaller; a Medium or larger creature cannot fit through them at any posture, stooped or crawling included. The frame itself bars them. Lower hold: big deep space for cargo, reached through the same undersized passages. No Medium-or-larger creature has ever set foot below decks.

History. The gnomish crew founded the ship together and Cotter runs it now. The hull came first, the inside fit came later, and orange paint covers older colors. After four years working the [[central-strait|Central Strait]], the ship has never sunk. The gnomes say it's skill; Cotter says luck.

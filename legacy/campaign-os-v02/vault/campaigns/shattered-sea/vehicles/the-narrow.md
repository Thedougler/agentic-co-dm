# The *Narrow*

Second ship of [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]]. Scout sloop, narrow-hulled and single-masted, was the lightest ship in the fleet, built for speed and shallow survey work. Long on the waterline relative to her beam, low freeboard. Hull painted dark grey, sat low and quiet in the water, and made little wake at speed.

Below decks held crew hammocks, a chart table bolted to the forward bulkhead, and storage for diving equipment and survey gear. Built to go places other ships couldn't and come back with information.

Lost with the rest of Fisk's Fleet when [[umberlee|Umberlee]] opened [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]] beneath them. All hands perished. See [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]] for the full account of the sinking. (The table kept Fisk's Fleet's ship names and captains secret.)

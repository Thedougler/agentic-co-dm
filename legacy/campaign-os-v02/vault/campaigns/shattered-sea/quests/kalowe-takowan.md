# Kalowe: Takowan and Ship Disguise

*A Dravosi spy ship blocks the harbour while the crew must hide a stolen vessel there.*

## Secrets & Clues

The *Takowan*, a [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi]] spy ship, overstays its dry-dock repair slip, running intelligence operations under that repair cover while its crew quietly maps [[the-passage|Passage]] contacts instead of repairing her hull. [[kalowe|Kalowe]] is the best place to hide the stolen *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]*, but the safe route runs past [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s Passage node, squarely under the *Takowan*'s watch. With the survey two-thirds complete, finishing it puts [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s hidden traffic in front of the Crown, and trade there stops within the season.

The *Takowan* is mapping Passage contacts faster than [[kalowe|Kalowe]] can pretend nothing is happening. Every boat that comes through the reef gap gets noted by someone, which is what makes the survey slow and what makes it dangerous. [[noa-fifita|Noa Fifita]] can hide and re-mark the *Uncertainty* in a back-channel slip between the second and third limestone islands, but he won't do the work while the *Takowan* remains in the lagoon.

Perrin's own Passage contact at [[kalowe|Kalowe]] is currently unidentified. Using that node while the *Takowan* is mapping risks burning it within a day. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] walking the causeways is the highest-value sighting her captain has had in years, and if he's spotted, [[kalowe|Kalowe]] stops being a safe harbour for Passage traffic and for anyone outside Crown paperwork. Perrin's Passage node is what opens the door here. The *Uncertainty* and [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] are what make the visit dangerous, and [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] loses one of his safest ports if [[kalowe|Kalowe]] burns.

The crew can move fast and burn Perrin's network now, wait for the *Takowan* to leave, drive it away outright, or try [[calveno|Calveno]]'s hidden route instead. The recent work at [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]] may already have solved this, so the ship's current status is worth checking before committing to a plan.

[[noa-fifita|Noa Fifita]] is short, still, dressed in salt-bleached canvas trousers. He offers neither greeting nor negotiation, watching the hull instead of faces, and if the crew explains itself he lets them finish and prices the job as if they'd said nothing. Perrin makes first contact through the second-causeway Passage stall, and [[noa-fifita|Noa]] names the back of the second-island chandlery after dark for the meeting itself. His opening price is 200 gp, three days and maybe five. The work [[noa-fifita|Noa]] does covers hull colour, bow registry plate, nameboards, and pennant, never the patrol-cutter silhouette, the age, or the worn caulking underneath. False papers aren't his trade, but he can point toward brokers for another 50 gp and a day. A credible plan to move the *Takowan* drops [[noa-fifita|Noa]]'s price to 150 gp, though he never offers that discount himself, only responds to it once it's on the table. His lines run short: *"Two hundred. Three days, maybe five."* *"She's a cutter, and no paint job changes that."* *"The plate comes off, but what happens to it after isn't my business."*

## Beats

The strong start is a night approach through the reef gap. The swell breaks and quiets before anyone hears it, softening into stillness as the crew comes through the gap and into the lagoon. Salt and woodsmoke ride the air, the sharp wet smell of caulking iron still cooling somewhere on the second island.

Limestone islands stand ahead, connected by stone causeways, dry-dock cranes dark above the treeline. At the primary berth below those cranes the *Takowan* sits lit, lanterns at the rail, a Crown pennant hanging motionless in air nobody can feel moving. Her crew is awake, and someone on that deck watches the gap the crew just came through.

The approach itself is navigable but narrow, and hunter sharks work the current. No encounter follows unless someone enters the water, but passing the bell rope too fast rings it, and the *Takowan*'s watch notices.

From there, the crew's real choice is which approach they commit to: wait out the *Takowan*'s stay and accept the contact-network damage that costs, force or lure her away before using The Passage node, thread the reef gap at night and skip the main lagoon, or take Calveno's [[warren|Warren]]-routed option instead.

## Outcome

Fast and careful play sends Noa to work the hidden slip, and the *Uncertainty* leaves de-marked but still cutter-shaped underneath. Using the node under watch risks burning the contact in [[the-passage|The Passage]] instead. [[the-tangle|The Tangle]], a sub-entry within [[warren|the Warren]], notices the cost. Waiting too long lets [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s Passage infrastructure become mapped enough to draw Crown pressure. Exposure goes public, and the *Takowan* identifies Delmar Fisk, the *Uncertainty*, or both.

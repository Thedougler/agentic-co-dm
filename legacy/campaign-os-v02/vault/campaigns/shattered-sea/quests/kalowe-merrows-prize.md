# [[kalowe|Kalowe]]: The [[merrow|Merrow]]'s Prize

A shipwreck holds treasure, but a merrow has claimed the wreck as its den, and the first mate selling the salvage rights may not have the authority to do so.

## Secrets & Clues

The *Cormorant*, a small merchant sloop, went down three months ago on the reef shelf southwest of the [[kalowe|Kalowe]] gap after her pilot misjudged the channel at night. The captain's strongbox (600gp in mixed coin and a pair of matched rubies) sank with her. Her first mate, [[davu-kamu|Davu Kamu]], survived and has been drinking at [[the-flat-water|The Flat Water]] ever since, trying to find anyone willing to dive for it. The wreck is 30 feet down. [[davu-kamu|Davu]] is two months past his savings and offering 30% to whoever retrieves the strongbox. He knows the dive location within 200 yards. He has the strongbox key. He also knows a large creature lived in the wreck the last time a hired diver got close and came back empty-handed.

Talking to [[davu-kamu|Davu]] gets the full picture. The sloop sank 2 miles southwest on the reef shelf, roughly 30 feet down with 600gp in its strongbox. He has the key and offers 30% (180gp plus the rubies he values at 60gp). Something big, pale, and wrong-shaped lives in the wreck.

[[davu-kamu|Davu]] cannot actually guarantee his 30% offer. The money isn't his to split. The captain's widow in [[tidefall|Tidefall]] has a claim, and he has not mentioned this. A merrow has claimed the hull as a den and dragged the strongbox into the grotto it made under the collapsed stern. It has reinforced the grotto with salvaged chain and hull planking. Two [[hunter-shark|Hunter Sharks]] patrol the outer reef (not in the grotto), but they investigate noise and blood. The rubies are actually worth 180gp total (90gp each), not the 60gp [[davu-kamu|Davu]] quotes. He is underselling them in case he can buy them back later.

**Key Facts.** The *Cormorant* sank 3 months ago, resting 30 feet down on a sandy reef shelf, listing badly to starboard. The merrow lair occupies the space under the collapsed stern with tight quarters, a 10-foot ceiling, and poor visibility.

> [!check] Stealth — Slipping Past the Shark Patrol
> DC 12 underwater; disadvantage without a [[swim-speed|Swim speed]].
> **Success:** the divers reach the wreck without drawing the [[hunter-shark|hunter sharks]]' attention.
> **Failure:** the sharks notice and begin circling.

> [!check] Thieves' Tools — Picking the Strongbox
> DC 14; underwater disadvantage applies. [[davu-kamu|Davu]]'s key opens it without a roll.
> **Success:** the strongbox opens.
> **Failure:** the lock holds.

**[[davu-kamu|Davu Kamu]]** is [[midchain|Midchain]] mixed heritage, late thirties, and a former first mate. He drinks steadily, not heavily, and talks like someone who knows he's leaving things out. He rarely volunteers more. If the divers agree to the job, he insists on coming along in a boat to guide them but will not enter the water. If they discover the true worth of the rubies and confront him, he admits it without much shame: *"I needed someone to say yes."*

Reach the wreck by small boat or swimming. [[davu-kamu|Davu]] can borrow a dinghy from the Fifita family quay for 2sp. Visibility underwater is 30 feet in open water, 10 feet near the wreck (silt disturbed by the merrow's movement).

> [!check] Athletics — [[swimming|Swimming]] to Depth
> DC 12; only matters without a Swim speed.
> **Success:** the divers reach the wreck at pace.
> **Failure:** costs a turn, not drowning (yet).

**The merrow:** this creature has built a proper den. Bones of previous prey pile up at the grotto entrance (hard to miss). It attacks the moment anything enters the grotto. HP 45, AC 13, [[spear|Spear]] +6 to hit / 2d6+4 piercing, Claws +6 / 2d6+4 slashing, 40ft swim speed. If the divers kill the merrow outside the grotto (baiting with blood or noise), they can enter freely.

> [!mechanic]
> **Hunter sharks: blood pressure.** Interested once blood enters the water. Roll a d6 at the start of each round after blood appears. On 4+, one shark investigates (2 sharks total, circling and attacking anyone wounded). Not a second encounter. Driven off by DC 13 Animal Handling, or by leaving the water quickly. If the check fails, the shark keeps circling and it attacks the wounded target that round.

> DC 14; requires leverage about the sharks or the merrow.

**Open Questions:** will the divers tell [[davu-kamu|Davu]] about the rubies' true worth? Will anyone contact the captain's widow in [[tidefall|Tidefall]] about the legal claim?

## Beats

- Party talks to [[reweti-kahurangi|Reweti Kahurangi]] about new work, or sits near [[davu-kamu|Davu]] long enough for him to make his pitch, or asks anyone in port about local salvage opportunities.
- [[davu-kamu|Davu]] makes his offer at [[the-flat-water|The Flat Water]] and, if hired, borrows a dinghy to guide the divers to the wreck site. He will not enter the water himself.
- Party swims down to the *Cormorant*, 30 feet down on the reef shelf. They must deal with the [[hunter-shark|Hunter Shark]] patrol and reduced visibility near the wreck.

- Party confronts the merrow guarding the grotto (baiting it out first or fighting it in its den) and retrieves the strongbox.
- Party returns to [[davu-kamu|Davu]] to split the recovery. They may negotiate a better cut or discover that he undersold the rubies' value.

## Outcome

**Party succeeds:** they net 180 to 600 gp depending on the cut negotiated, plus the spyglass and possibly the silk. [[davu-kamu|Davu]] buys them a round and disappears within two days.

**Party ignores it:** the merrow expands its territory and kills a diver from another crew within the month. The wreck becomes a local hazard warning.

**Party kills the merrow but leaves the box:** the strongbox is openly salvageable. Another crew gets it within a week.

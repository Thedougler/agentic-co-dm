# Kalowe: The Sawek Bounty

*A territorial Sawek has claimed the channel between Kalowe's limestone islands, and the harbour bosses are paying 300 gp for its ridge-plate.*

## Secrets & Clues

A [[sawek|Sawek]] settled into the blue hole at the channel base between [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s second and third limestone islands, twenty feet down where inter-island skiffs pass at night. Well-fed and territorial, it attacked the first boat that crossed its hole and now treats the forty-foot-wide, fifteen-foot-deep channel as its hunting ground: over two weeks it has hit three boats and killed one sailor. The harbour bosses offer 300 gp for its bony ridge-plate — a cream bone wedge eighteen inches across, ten minutes' work to cut free with a tool — and want no witnesses, just the plate itself.

Fishers have stopped using the channel, and dry-dock workers now add an extra hour to every inter-island supply run. [[kettil-ormsson|Kettil Ormsson]] at [[ormssons-fittings|Ormsson's Fittings]] has a damaged keel plank outside his shop that he's fixing as a favour; asking him about local trouble turns up the Sawek. [[ilaria-grimaldi|Ilaria Grimaldi]] treated the attack's survivor and pieced together its pattern — the Sawek rises from below, grabs the hull with its tentacles, nearly sinks the boat, then loses interest once the hull breaks apart.

The Sawek patrols for two to three hours after dark, then returns to its hole for one to two hours; the lair floor holds debris, loose coin, and salvaged gear. Don't fight it underwater — inside its hole it has a 40-foot swim speed, cover from the channel walls, and an ink cloud that makes retreat lethal, so adventurers should surface the fight. It has 95 hit points and AC 14 (natural armor), swims at 40 feet and crawls at 5 feet on land; its tentacle attacks restrain on a hit (escape DC 14), its ink cloud reaction fills 20 feet with heavy obscurement and recharges once it surfaces, and direct sunlight gives it disadvantage on attacks and Perception checks.

## Beats

Adventurers pick up the job by asking Kettil about the damaged boat outside his shop, asking Grimaldi about recent injuries, or asking at the harbour offices about local hazards or open contracts.

The recommended approach is bait: [[wibowos-provisions|Wibowo's Provisions]] sells offal, and a single silver piece's worth lays a blood trail that draws the Sawek in 1d6+2 rounds. The fight happens at the waterline, half the Sawek's body above the surface and half below — it can still seize the boat, and a hit grasps a target for a DC 14 Strength check per round to break free, with failure pulling the grasped creature ten feet toward the hole.

The alternative is a trap: the channel narrows to twenty feet sixty yards north of the blue hole, and a chain strung across at depth — available from [[ormssons-fittings|Ormsson's Fittings]] at 5 sp per foot — fouls the Sawek's tentacles. Fouled against both walls, the chain forces a DC 13 Dexterity save or the Sawek's tentacles become restrained, removing its tentacle and grasping attacks; while restrained it spends its action on a DC 16 Strength check to break free, at two tries per round.

Looting the lair afterward takes light — six weeks of debris cover the floor. It holds a sealed iron box of pilot charts (worth 60 gp to any chart buyer, or 90 gp to [[algernon-reginald-clyde|Clyde]] if he's in port), 185 gp in mixed coin silted into the floor, and a gold chain bearing a [[midchain|Midchain]] seal pendant that belonged to a merchant missing since last season — his family in [[veth|Veth]] offers 50 gp for its return, though nobody in [[kalowe|Kalowe]] yet knows whose it is.

## Outcome

Selling everything nets roughly 775 gp before costs: the 300 gp harbour bounty pays out immediately once the ridge-plate is delivered; the 185 gp in mixed coin takes thirty minutes to sift from the lair floor; the pilot charts fetch 60 gp to any chart buyer or 90 gp to [[algernon-reginald-clyde|Clyde]] if he's in port; the gold chain and seal pendant are worth 150 gp — 50 gp as the [[veth|Veth]] family's reward or 100 gp at melt value; and the Sawek's remains (teeth, ink sacs, tentacle segments) are worth another 40 gp to an alchemist, if the party bothers to harvest them.

If the party kills it, they clear roughly 735 gp, the harbour bosses are grateful, the inter-island channel reopens, and the dry-dock crew remembers the favour. If they avoid the job, the Sawek kills again within two weeks and the harbour bosses quietly double the bounty to 500 gp. If a party member is dragged under, ordinary drowning rules apply — the Sawek drags a grappled creature toward the blue hole floor at 20 feet per round while it fails to escape.

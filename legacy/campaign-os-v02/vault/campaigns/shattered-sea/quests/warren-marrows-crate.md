# Warren: Marrow's Crate

A courier's death, a crate expiring on a broker's shelf, and a letter implicating a House-level corruption thread watched over by an intelligence operative.

## Secrets & Clues

Dren delivered a crate to [[marrows|Marrow's]] three weeks ago and vanished. His body surfaced ten days later, ruled accidental drowning. The crate sits on Marrow's shelf, expiring soon. Inside: a letter implicating House Vretek and Commandant Alessi Brol in selling [[the-passage|Passage]] cargo timing to [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] merchants.

### The Investigation

> [!check] Investigation — Placing Dren on the Canal
> DC 12; Dren was a known runner in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]], and dock workers, ferrymen, or tavern regulars can place him on the canal that night.
> **Success:** a canal worker saw Dren arguing with a woman in a green coat near the [[harbourmasters-office|Harbourmaster's Office]], thirty minutes before the estimated time of the drowning.
> **Failure:** no one places Dren on the canal that night this way.

> [!check] Investigation — Dren's Employers
> DC 13; tracking Dren's other regular clients in the canal district.
> **Success:** two legitimate deliveries that week turn up, plus one unusual job (a pickup from a boat tied at the outer basin at an unusual time via an unusual route). The employer for that pickup is not a name anyone knows.
> **Failure:** only the two legitimate deliveries surface. The unusual job stays hidden.

> [!check] Investigation — Identifying the Woman in the Green Coat
> DC 12 (once the crew has this detail from any source); a second inquiry in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]].
> **Success:** she's a regular at a specific canal-side coffeehouse, a [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] trade arbitration consultant who has been in [[calveno|Calveno]] four months.
> **Failure:** no one places her at a specific location yet.

### Serin Haut

[[serin-haut|Haut]] is a [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi]] intelligence attaché operating under Tessarine trade-assessor cover, watching for interest in the crate or Dren's death. She is the one who killed Dren, and the ruling of accidental drowning is her cover holding.

> [!mechanic]
> **Serin Haut:** Spy (MM p.349), +1 Deception, concealed shortsword, 3 doses basic poison (ingestion, DC 11 Con save, 2d6 poison damage on fail). She operates only in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]]. After two canal inquiries on the same day, she follows whoever asked last; Passive Perception DC 14 notices her tail, and failing to hit that DC means she trails unnoticed. If spotted, she breaks off and reports.

> [!check] Persuasion — Locating Haut's Lodgings
> DC 13 with a [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] contact; or reachable via Dren's employer thread.
> **Success:** a locked strongbox holds a codebook worth 500 gp to The Tangle or to Tessarine rivals, 420 gp operational coin, a list of three non-[[the-passage|Passage]] informants in [[calveno|Calveno]]'s canal district, and [[ferrin-locke|Ferrin Locke]]'s signed cooperation agreement (see [[warren-ferrin-locke|Warren: Ferrin Locke]]).
> **Failure:** her lodgings stay unknown. The employer thread remains the only other route there.

### The Crate

- Sealed with Dren's wax and courier notation: a letter (meant for a now-silent Tangle intermediary) implicating House Vretek and Commandant Alessi Brol in selling Passage cargo timing windows to Tessarine factor [[iacopo-fieschi|Iacopo Fieschi]]'s advance agents.
- 280 gp in Tessarine silver.
- A secondary House Vretek signet ring worth 120 gp as jewelry or considerably more as evidence.

### Loaded Guns

- [[ferrin-locke|Ferrin Locke]]'s cooperation agreement is in Haut's strongbox (finds it before Ferrin, [[warren-ferrin-locke|his thread]] opens faster).
- The Vretek signet ring is portable and recognizable. Anyone will ask where it came from.
- [[iacopo-fieschi|Iacopo Fieschi]] connects to this operation: the letter names his advance agents. He's also a Beffa mark and Council factor (see [[il-gioco-delle-beffe|Il Gioco delle Beffe]]), so the two threads can connect.

### Clocks

> [!mechanic]
> **Marrow's consignment window:** expires at end of week. Marrow solves it himself after that in a way the crew will not be able to trace. **Haut's patience:** if the crew moves loudly in Le Paludi, she escalates from watching to active interference within 48 hours.

## Beats

### Strong Start

> [!read-aloud]
> [[marrow|Marrow]] is already watching when you step down into the alcove, the air smelling of lamp oil and old wax. He's weighing something on his brass scale. You hear the pans click as they settle. He sets it aside and folds his hands.
>
> *Marrow*: Three weeks on the shelf. Policy is three weeks. I'll pay fifty gold for the intended recipient or for the person who can tell me what's in it.
>
> He does not say what happens at the end of the week.

Ask the crew what they do first. Marrow answers all questions but knows nothing of the crate's contents, sender, or recipient. He knew Dren is dead. He knows the crate must move.

### Blocks

- Claim the crate and deal with Haut (direct; the exposure risk is immediate)
- Identify Haut before approaching the crate (safer; requires the investigation path)
- Let the crate expire and follow whoever Marrow hands it to (possible; Marrow will not confirm or deny)
- Open the letter and use the contents directly (political; branches into three or four outcomes)

## Outcome

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| Give letter to The Tangle | 400 gp. Major trust. [[the-passage\|The Passage]] handles quietly. |
| Sell to Vretek rivals | 600 to 750 gp. Lasting Vretek enmity. |
| Blackmail Brol | Up to 800 gp. Murder follows. |
| Destroy the letter | House Vretek owes a favour. |
| Give Haut to The Tangle | Major goodwill. Haut disappears. |
| Take Haut's room and codebook | 920 gp. Informant list. [[the-passage\|The Passage]] follows up. |

**Resolved:** crate handled. Haut gone. Letter destroyed or delivered. The Tangle knows.

**Complicated:** letter surfaces but Haut escapes. Vretek or Brol activate later.

**Ignored:** marrow moves the crate. The Tangle never learns its fate. Haut departs.

# Warren: Ferrin Locke

*A frightened man leaks Passage operations to a Dravosi handler, squeezed by an old conviction he wants to forget.*

## Secrets & Clues

This is an extraction dressed as an investigation: the party finds a frightened man wedged inside an active intelligence operation and has to decide what to do with both him and it.

Three Passage intercepts in as many months share one thread — canal broker Faro — and how the party closes it, and what happens to the man at its center, decides whether the leak closes cleanly or leaves a loose end [[the-tangle|The Tangle]] will remember.

[[the-tangle|The Tangle]]'s own inquiry already names Faro as the common broker; an Investigation check (DC 12) confirms him as a mid-level logistics broker working out of Le Paludi, or turns up no lead yet from this angle.

Pressed with Intimidation or Insight (DC 10, since Faro is no trained operative), he names [[ferrin-locke|Ferrin Locke]] under pressure — or stays evasive, his discomfort still visible on an Insight check.

Ferrin runs a minor chandlery factor's office on the [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] waterfront. He's broad-shouldered, early forties, and hasn't slept properly in months; he opens his door before the third knock, already expecting this visit.

He knows he's watched, and he needs a way in before he'll talk: offering him an out (Insight, DC 10) confirms his honesty and gets him talking freely; pressuring him (Persuasion or Intimidation, DC 15) gets a shaking but accurate confession; reading the depth of his fear and treating him with decency (Insight, DC 13) gets him talking without another check at all.

His account holds together on every point. His handler is [[petra-venn|Petra Venn]], a [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi]] intelligence attaché posing as a [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] trade consultant, who meets him every nine days at a Le Paludi coffeehouse and leverages a twelve-year-old smuggling conviction she holds documentation for.

What he passes her is route timing, gathered passively through Faro and two other brokers who don't know they're sources.

Four months ago Venn asked him to flag [[grung|Grung]] passengers with false documentation through [[calveno|Calveno]]'s broker network; he noticed one and said nothing to her.

If [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] is present and trusted, Ferrin surfaces this himself, unprompted — unnerved less by the original ask than by the query he never got an explanation for. Surface it and let the player decide what it means; it is never assigned to him as backstory.

Venn has worked Calveno for four months: professional, patient, not violent by preference. She checks in with Ferrin every nine days from when the party starts asking questions — wait too long and she reads the coffeehouse meeting as compromised, move fast and the party can intercept her on their own terms.

A Local Inquiry (DC 12) off Ferrin's description locates her rented room above a canal-facing notary's office; success turns up a codebook worth 500 gp to [[the-tangle|The Tangle]] or a rival, 420 gp in operational coin, a list of three non-Passage informants, and Ferrin's own signed cooperation agreement, while failure goes cold on this attempt.

If the party already cleared Haut's room via [[warren-marrows-crate|Warren (Marrow's Crate)]], that cooperation agreement is already in hand — Ferrin's account confirmed and the party holding leverage without needing to find Venn separately.

Two clocks run alongside each other. Venn's check-in ticks at day 3, day 6, and day 9 from when the party first starts asking, arriving at the coffeehouse on day 9 if nothing has interrupted her.

Ferrin's nerve ticks quieter: each day of delay after the party first meets him, he considers going to Venn directly, and won't — he's too frightened — but his fear shows plainly to anyone who checks in on him.

Ferrin's wife and daughter live in Le Paludi and are not leverage unless the party makes them leverage. The Grung query is a closed thread for Venn, not a deleted one — Dravosi records persist somewhere in their archives.

And if [[serin-haut|Serin Haut]] is also active in Calveno, she and Venn are aware of each other without running coordinated operations; the party crossing paths with both may push one or both to move up their timeline.

## Beats

[[the-tangle|The Tangle]] hands this off through a contact from [[warren|the Warren]] or through [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] herself at [[warren-nonas-table|Nona's Table]]: three intercepts in three months share one canal broker, and his client list includes [[ferrin-locke|Ferrin Locke]], a mid-tier, clean-reputation chandlery factor in Le Paludi — clean except for a conviction from twelve years back.

Find out if Locke is the leak, and what it costs to close it. The Tangle doesn't specify how; they want the leak stopped and the handler identified.

From there the party has four live approaches, each trading speed against risk. Extracting Ferrin first is the safe read, though Venn may cut contact before they reach her. Finding Venn first is faster but riskier — she may have already compromised Ferrin by the time they get to him.

Using Ferrin as bait for Venn's next check-in is the elegant option, but it needs Ferrin's agreement, and his family's presence in Le Paludi makes that a real ask, not a given. Handing [[the-tangle|The Tangle]] the name and stepping back is the clean exit: they handle it, and the party gets credit without learning what that meant for Ferrin.

## Outcome

Exposing Locke to [[the-tangle|The Tangle]] stops the leak outright: Ferrin disappears non-lethally through [[the-passage|The Passage]]'s handling, and the party earns 300 gp and trust for the clean close.

Helping Locke escape Calveno protects a contact outside the network instead, leaving [[the-passage|The Passage]] owing a debt for cleaning up a loose end it didn't have to.

Neutralizing Venn before her check-in stops the leak at its source, frees Ferrin, and puts her codebook and operational coin in the party's hands.

Turning Venn over to [[the-tangle|The Tangle]] lets them handle the rest quietly — what happens to her stays unsaid — while Ferrin's situation settles on its own.

Selling the codebook to a third party nets 500 gp, but Venn notices within a week and [[the-passage|The Passage]] eventually asks where it came from.

Doing nothing lets the nine-day clock run out: Venn reads the network as compromised, shuts Ferrin down, and [[the-tangle|The Tangle]] loses the thread as a new asset quietly takes over the exposure.

A resolved outcome closes the leak with Venn neutralized or turned over and Ferrin's situation settled, and [[the-tangle|The Tangle]] registers the close.

A complicated outcome protects Ferrin while Venn escapes, or neutralizes Venn while exposing Ferrin — one loose end left either way.

Ignoring it lets Venn's check-in arrive on schedule: she reads the changed situation, shuts the operation down, and intercepts stop for a few weeks before a new asset quietly picks up the exposure.

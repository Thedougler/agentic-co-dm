# Warren: Aruhe Survey

*[[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s private commission to [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]: survey [[aruhe|Aruhe]] as a candidate site for [[the-tangle|The Tangle]]'s first fixed [[midchain|Midchain]] anchor.*

## Secrets & Clues

Nona offered the commission privately, table-side at [[warren-nonas-table|Warren (Nona's Table)]], after the meal ends and The Tangle's other leads go out. She never mentioned it to the rest of the crew. See [[warren|The Warren]] § Aruhe (Prospective Midchain Anchor) for the public-facing summary this page expands.

Nona found Aruhe in forty-year-old Passage route notes: a waypoint entry reading *good water, good stone, one approach, don't stay past dark*, with no explanation of the last line. Old sources mention the same features. They note high cliffsides, a single navigable harbour entrance, and a deep-water anchorage inside. She chose it specifically because [[grung|the Grung]] avoid it. An island Grung patrols won't land on is an island no Crown vessel will find Grung evidence near. She has worked out that the avoidance creates a blind spot but has not worked out why the Grung avoid it.

Her framing to Perrin: [[the-passage|The Passage]] needs a fixed place in the Midchain, not just routes. The [[warren|Below]] is an exit. She wants a door that opens both ways. She wants Perrin to tell her whether a small crew can hold Aruhe, not to deliver a report. The commission is the first thing she has given him that is not a favour or debt or test.

Nona does not know that [[blight|the Blight]] holds the island's interior (full detail on Aruhe's own page). She believes the island is empty; the Blight believes the same thing about the island. One of them is about to learn otherwise. Nor does she know the island isn't empty on the beach: a storm scattered part of the Grung fleet that took her five hundred onto Aruhe's reefs, days after the raid (full account on [[aruhe-wreck|The Aruhe Wreck]]), and [[sandro|Sandro]] and [[nino|Nino]] are still alive there, sheltering in a wrecked *[[vethka|Vethka]]*'s hull. Nobody has connected this survey to her chase for the "dark hull." That connection is Perrin's to make, or not, the moment his crew reaches the beach.

Aruhe's surrounding reefs sit inside the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] patrol circuit, and the Grung, the opposition Nona is gambling against without saying so, run flat-bottomed patrol boats of two to three crew armed with darts and boarding hooks, passing the eastern reef every 36 to 48 hours and turning back at Aruhe's beach line without ever landing. A ship anchored inside the harbour stays invisible from the patrol route, but one anchored outside on the approach draws a Grung patrol's notice within 48 hours, and any light or fire on the island at night shows from the [[karath|Karath]] side. The Grung response to a ship at Aruhe is retreat, followed by a larger force, never a territorial challenge. They do not want to know what is there. They want it to stay that way.

## Beats

Nona wants answers to five questions, though not all at once: whether the harbour is deep enough for a Tier 2 vessel at high tide (can the *Saltmarsh Trader* use it as a base); whether there's freshwater on the island (settlement viability without resupply infrastructure); whether the entrance is defensible by a small crew (whether five fighters can hold access during construction); whether there's sign of current occupation or frequent visitation (whether anything has found the island since The Passage drew its charts); and what the Grung patrol frequency and closest approach are (whether the location can stay clandestine for the 18 to 24 months a shipyard takes). The fourth question answers itself the moment the crew lands: [[sandro|Sandro]] and [[nino|Nino]] are already on the beach, sheltering in the wrecked Vethka since [[aruhe-wreck|the storm]] put them ashore, and the crew finds them the moment they clear the reef entrance, not after some later search.

The *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]* stows paired signal lanterns, one rail-mounted aft and one kept below, each with a 30-mile range and a 25-word message limit. Nona's format each evening is *[Condition check]. [Objective update]. [One question]*. The condition check always comes first, because she wants to know the crew is alive before she asks about the island. If no reply arrives by two hours past dusk, she waits one more evening. If the crew loses or breaks the lantern, she will not know what happened, and the crew operates without a net.

The harbour entrance is technical but navigable on the correct bearing, east-northeast on the old chart, and the gap is visible only on direct approach.

> [!check] [[dexterity|Dexterity]] (Water Vehicles) — Threading the Reef Entrance
> DC 10 with the bearing note in hand, DC 14 without.
> **Success:** the ship threads the reef entrance clean.
> **Failure:** the ship grounds on the outer reef, taking 2d6 hull points of damage.

> [!check] [[intelligence|Intelligence]] (Navigation) — Reading the Harbour's Depth
> DC 13.
> **Success:** a reliable depth estimate, roughly 40 ft at centre.
> **Failure:** the depth stays a guess until sounded by slower means.

> [!check] [[wisdom|Wisdom]] (Perception) — Reading the Shoreline
> DC 12.
> **Success:** the shoreline fauna sits pressed outward from the interior. The pattern reads as displacement, not natural crowding.
> **Failure:** the same pattern reads as ordinary crowding, with no forewarning of the interior.

Inside, the harbour holds deep water, roughly 40 ft at centre, limestone cliffs on three sides running 150 to 200 ft, and a narrow shingle beach at the back, reached from the plateau above by one stone staircase cut into the cliff face. The beach is not empty. A wrecked *[[vethka|Vethka]]* sits in the shingle, and Sandro and Nino, sheltering in its hull since the storm that put them there, watch any approaching sail from cover before showing themselves (full account: [[aruhe-wreck|The Aruhe Wreck]]).

The plateau above the harbour is overgrown. Old stonework, terrace walls and collapsed structures, shows under the growth, long collapsed and reclaimed by root and vine.

> [!check] Intelligence (military background) — Reading the Clifftop
> DC 10, any character with a military background may roll.
> **Success:** the clifftop reads as defensible, sight lines covering the approach and high ground holding the entrance.
> **Failure:** the defensibility question stays open.

> [!check] Wisdom (Survival) or Intelligence (Nature) — Reading the Ruins
> DC 13.
> **Success:** the terrace collapse reads as ordinary weathering, decades old.
> **Failure:** the ruins stay unremarkable.

> [!check] Wisdom (Survival) — Finding Freshwater
> DC 11.
> **Success:** a freshwater spring feeding a stream that runs year-round.
> **Failure:** the freshwater question goes back to Nona unanswered.

The original Passage runner who scouted Aruhe landed in the harbour, climbed to the plateau, and found the same freshwater and the same cliff approach. At dusk, he heard the interior.

> [!check] Wisdom (Perception) — The Sound at Dusk
> DC 10, any crew on the plateau at dusk may roll.
> **Success:** the crew hears it too. A sound like wind through the old terrace walls, though the air is dead still.
> **Failure:** the dusk passes quietly.

The interior is the [[blight|Blight]] dungeon, full detail on [[aruhe|Aruhe]]'s own page. A crew that moves inland triggers Blight encounter content, though the survey itself does not require clearing it, since the harbour and clifftop beats already answer Nona's five questions. The interior is a choice, and a loaded one. The site cannot host a settlement while the Blight holds it, and Perrin will know this the moment he sees it.

## Outcome

Nona's reaction depends on what the survey reports.

A truthful report on a completed survey, Blight included, sends the full picture to The Tangle and turns the commission into a longer-arc project. The Tangle clears the island first, then builds.

A completed survey with the Blight omitted lets Nona proceed anyway. The Tangle sends a construction team and learns the truth on arrival, and Perrin knew all along.

If Perrin's crew clears the interior as part of the survey, the site is viable immediately. Nona's standing in The Tangle rises sharply, and Perrin's standing with Nona rises more.

If the Blight or the Grung drives Perrin's crew off, Nona gets only partial information. She learns the site holds trouble and asks Perrin to go back.

If the Grung discover the operation, the Verdant Teeth knows someone was at Aruhe. Their response is alarm, not territorial anger, and it makes the site a political problem the harbour bosses will hear about within a season.

With an honest report, Nona falls silent for a moment. Then she asks how deep the trouble goes. She wants to know whether Perrin thinks the crew can clear it. She is not asking them to move on it right away. Only whether the job is possible at all. She will not punish an honest assessment, but she will punish a report she later discovers was incomplete.

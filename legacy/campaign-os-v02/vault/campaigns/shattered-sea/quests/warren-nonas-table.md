# Warren: Nona's Table

*Nona reunites with Perrin, already knowing his route and crew before he says a word, and trades Passage support for a hand of Warren favours.*

## Secrets & Clues

Nona has been searching for Perrin for weeks, and she already knows he is in [[calveno|Calveno]] before he knows she knows. The scene triggers when Perrin enters the Warren under any circumstances, when Perrin contacts a Passage node in Calveno (word reaches Nona within hours), or when [[cobb|Cobb]] reports the party's arrival from [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]]. What drives the whole scene is a settling: Nona converts weeks of silence and the *[[vestra|Vestra]]*'s loss into the terms of what Perrin owes and what she will give, and she does it not knowing yet that the *Vestra* is gone at all — Perrin does, and everything else waits behind that gap. How he handles the reveal decides whether [[the-tangle|The Tangle]] treats the party as trusted assets or expensive liabilities, and what's at stake behind it is Perrin's family credit, Tangle access for the ship disguise, the Below (see [[warren|The Warren]] § The Below), and three active Warren problems.

The table is already set when Perrin walks into the [[tangle-chambers|Tangle Chambers]]: bread, a pot, two portions. [[enzo|Enzo]] holds the inner door, silent, and gestures him to the table. Nona is at the counter and will not turn until she hears a chair move. The food comes first. Nona will not begin talking until Perrin has eaten, and [[ruk|Ruk]] may offer more stew. This is not a stalling tactic. It is how she operates.

Once the meal is under way, Nona shows she already knows more than Perrin expects. She's learned his route and his crew, caught a fragmentary read on the [[uncertainty|Surety]]. She waits to hear what he volunteers before saying how much. She asks about [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] by description rather than name, via a Passage report of "one of your crew is not Dry and not [[rattkin|Rattkin]]." She sets down her spoon, folds her hands on the table, and asks how well Perrin knows their kind. If Perrin confirms a [[grung|Grung]] is traveling with him, she pivots immediately to a sewer problem that has been sitting on her for two weeks (see [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|Calveno Sewers, Grung Magazines]]), her first real ask stated plainly: before the other things, she needs to know what is in the tunnels under her city.

The reveal about the *[[vestra|Vestra]]* comes obliquely. Nona looks at the far end of the table, then at Perrin, and asks whether he has been sitting in [[vincenzo-black-jaw|Vincenzo]]'s empty chair — that the ship was Vincenzo's before it was his doesn't make it less his, but she wants to know how she went. The conversation arrives at the ship's loss through what Perrin says or fails to say. When she hears it, Nona goes still and sets down what's in her hands. Her breath halts for several seconds before only "How?" escapes. She does not cry. Then, after collecting herself, she tells him plainly to say what he needs. Once she has it, The Tangle's offer follows: routing the *Surety* disguise work through The Tangle, [[the-passage|Passage]] broker access for false papers, and Warren channel movement for the party in exchange for taking on at least one of three Warren leads ([[bela-silt-paw|Bela Silt-Paw]], [[warren-marrows-crate|Marrow's Crate]], [[ferrin-locke|Ferrin Locke]]) before the table clears — she needs three things looked at, not all of them, but Perrin will take one before he leaves this table.

The played reunion happened in Nona's kitchen in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] during Session 03. The Tangle Chambers staging above is preserved DM-prep design, superseded by how the table actually played it. See [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]] and [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] for the played version.

Nona's voice doesn't perform warmth or menace. It's tired and capable and specific, delivered in short sentences: *you look thin, eat*; *I sent [[anzolo|Anzolo]] to find a body — I'm glad it's not a body*; *I'm not going to ask you to grieve faster than you're ready to, but I need you working*. She asks questions that assume honesty because she will know if Perrin is not being honest. She does not accuse, threaten, guilt-trip, or perform grief for the table. She does that work in private, and the players only see the edges of it.

She does not explain what the three Warren leads are until Perrin agrees to look, then gives them in the order she thinks he can stomach. [[bela-silt-paw|Bela]] gets top priority because it has a clock. Full leads live on [[warren|The Warren]] § Warren Threats: [[bela-silt-paw|Bela Silt-Paw]]'s sub-level survey, [[warren-marrows-crate|Marrow's Crate]]'s dead courier, and [[ferrin-locke|Ferrin Locke]]'s leak.

If Perrin's standing is good by the end of the meal, Nona holds back a separate, later ask and tells him privately afterward that something else is coming, just not tonight. This is the [[aruhe|Aruhe]] commission already documented on [[warren|The Warren]] § Aruhe (Prospective Midchain Anchor), a candidate site for The Tangle's first fixed [[midchain|Midchain]] anchor. She hasn't decided whether to frame it as a Tangle favour.

If the whole party is present, Nona feeds everyone and reads each of them, saying one true thing she has observed. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]'s anti-slavery history, if it comes up, earns a brief but real shift from her, and [[ruk|Ruk]] will nod without explanation. If Perrin has avoided the Warren for too long and this scene happens under pressure, Nona already knows and the terms are the same, just one degree cooler.

Loaded guns: [[enzo|Enzo]] never speaks during the scene. He pours one cup of wine for Perrin and returns to his post. If Perrin drinks without hesitation, Enzo glances at [[ruk|Ruk]], meaning everything and nothing. Vincenzo's empty chair is visible from the table, and Nona notices Perrin notice it.

## Beats

The scene opens on the Tangle Chambers' familiar scents, old stone and lamp oil mixed with wood smoke and the smell of Nona's cooking. [[enzo|Enzo]] is holding the inner door; he has already seen Perrin, doesn't speak, steps back, and gestures one hand toward the set table — bread, a pot, two portions. Ask Perrin what he does first: sits, speaks, looks around. Nona is at the counter and will not turn until she hears a chair move.

The scene runs in six beats, in order. First, the food: Nona feeds Perrin before saying anything, [[enzo|Enzo]] and [[ruk|Ruk]] present, and silence is fine here. Second, what she has noticed: Nona names what she already knows about his route, ship, and crew, and asks about Jean-Claude specifically. Third, the Jean-Claude pivot: confirming a Grung is with him gets Nona to set down her spoon and make her first ask. Fourth, the *[[vestra|Vestra]]*: Perrin tells her about the ship, obliquely, via the empty-chair question. Fifth, the Tangle's leads: Nona names what she needs from him ([[bela-silt-paw|Bela]], [[warren-marrows-crate|Marrow]], [[ferrin-locke|Ferrin]]) and Perrin picks one before the table clears. Sixth, the held-back thing: one other matter exists, but not for tonight — the Aruhe commission, told to Perrin alone, later.

Player choices branch the pacing rather than the outcome. If Perrin tells Nona about the *Vestra* immediately, she hears it cleanly and the scene moves toward the ask faster. If he delays or hedges, Nona waits — she has time — and [[enzo|Enzo]] refills his water. If another PC speaks first, Nona listens, thanks them, then turns to Perrin and asks, "And you?" If Perrin refuses all three leads, she doesn't argue, just says she'll find another way, and The Tangle's credit line for the party closes until someone earns it back. If Perrin agrees to one lead, The Tangle routes the *[[uncertainty|Surety]]* job, Nona gives the lead details, and the scene ends with food still on the table.

## Outcome

Resolved, Perrin and Nona reach working terms: the Below (see [[warren|The Warren]] § The Below) is implied for later, and the party leaves with at least one Warren lead. Complicated, Perrin agrees but hedges on details, and The Tangle accepts the commitment but watches how he follows through. Ignored, the silence stops reading as delay and starts reading as a decision — Warren access stays open through other [[the-passage|Passage]] channels, but it costs coin and goodwill Perrin does not have to spend.

Costs land regardless of outcome. Perrin commits to at least one Warren problem, The Tangle keeps a copy of whatever disguise papers get produced, and Nona now knows the *[[vestra|Vestra]]*'s fate.

In play, the reunion, the *Vestra* reveal, and the unconditional favor all landed in Session 03 via the kitchen staging in Le Paludi noted above, not The Tangle Chambers design above it. The favor itself (investigating the Warren's Grung sightings) was carried out and closed in Session 04. See [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]] § Session Log for both citations.

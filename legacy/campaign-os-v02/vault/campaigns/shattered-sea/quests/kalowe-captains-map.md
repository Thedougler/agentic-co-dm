# The Captain's Map

*A dying privateer will trade half a tattooed treasure chart for passage to a healer.*

## Secrets & Clues

[[sera-maddock|Sera Maddock]], 71, is dying in the dispensary's back room; [[ilaria-grimaldi|Ilaria Grimaldi]] estimates she has three days left. Sera captained under [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] license, and the resting place of her crew's cache on [[orak|Orak]] survives only in her — half memorized, half tattooed on her left forearm. The tattoo marks the eastern face of [[orak|Orak]]: a compass bearing of 087° from the southeastern rock stack, and the cache's depth in fathoms. She burned the other half of the chart when the man she meant to share it with tried to sell her to the Crown.

Her price is 200 gp and passage out of [[kalowe|Kalowe]] — not to [[tidefall|Tidefall]], but to a healer city, since her lung sickness is treatable in [[tidefall|Tidefall]] but not here; she is not in pain, only slow and short of breath, and time is running out. The tattoo half is enough to find the cache on its own. Ilaria Grimaldi mentioned the tattoo unprompted, an unusual mark on a patient in the back room, and will arrange an introduction only if she trusts the adventurers to honour the deal. The hook surfaces however the party reaches it: buying supplies from Grimaldi and hearing about the patient, following up directly, or simply asking after old privateers or the eastern reef islands around [[kalowe|Kalowe]], which turns up Sera's name regardless.

Once the deal is struck, Sera gives the bearing and tide notes: 087° from the rock stack at [[orak|Orak]]'s southeastern tip, eastern face, a sea cave entrance at waterline at mid-tide, walkable at low tide, eight feet under at high tide. [[orak|Orak]] itself lies two hours' sail east-southeast of [[kalowe|Kalowe]] — small, low, overgrown, paired with [[dreth|Dreth]] across a narrow channel. The entrance runs sixty feet deep by twenty wide before narrowing to ten feet inside; the cave floods to the ceiling at full high tide, water rising a foot every ten minutes, leaving only a three-hour window around low tide to work in. The cache sits six feet under a flat stone, forty feet into the cave interior, a twenty-minute dig with tools, forty without. Sera also warns the cave harbors "some animal," though she hasn't been there in forty years.

What she doesn't say: the cache holds 800 gp in old Imperial coin, worth 1,000 gp to [[algernon-reginald-clyde|Algernon Clyde]] as a collector, plus a matched set of silver earrings and bracelet worth 180 gp — roughly 780 to 980 gp net once her 200 gp fee comes out, depending on whether the coin sells to [[algernon-reginald-clyde|Clyde]] at collector's price. She also doesn't mention that the animal she remembers was, forty years ago, a giant constrictor snake; she assumes it's dead.

## Beats

1. Hook: [[ilaria-grimaldi|Ilaria Grimaldi]] mentions a dying patient with a strange tattoo while the adventurers are at the dispensary.

2. The adventurers follow up. Grimaldi vets their intent before arranging the introduction.

3. Sera lays out her deal — 200 gp and passage to a healer city, not [[tidefall|Tidefall]] — and, once struck, gives the bearing and tide notes.

4. The adventurers sail two hours to [[orak|Orak]], time the three-hour low-tide window, and enter the eastern-face sea cave.

5. Giant crabs (×4, CR 1/8, HP 13, AC 15, claw +3 for 1d6+1 bludgeoning) scatter from torchlight at the entrance but fight if they're cut off from the exit — a trivial nuisance. Past the thirty-foot mark waits the [[giant-constrictor-snake|Giant Constrictor Snake]] (HP 60, AC 12, bite +5 for 2d6+4 piercing plus seize, constrict +5 for 2d8+4 bludgeoning plus restrained; a DC 16 [[strength|Strength]] [Athletics] or [[dexterity|Dexterity]] [Acrobatics] check escapes), docile unless cornered or startled near its nest. Backing away with a torch held out (DC 11 Animal Handling) keeps it hissing at a distance; failure provokes a strike. Treat this as an [[encounter|encounter]] to defuse, not a set-piece fight.

6. Dig up the flat stone forty feet into [[orak|Orak]]'s cave floor — twenty minutes with tools, forty without — to reach the cache.

## Outcome

Two threads stay open, per the source's own notes: whether the adventurers take Sera anywhere or simply pay her and leave her behind, and whether [[algernon-reginald-clyde|Algernon Clyde]] actually buys the coin. Time pressure is unresolved too — the adventurers are currently in [[calveno|Calveno]] for a five-day dry dock while Sera's own clock reads three days, so the window may already be closing against their real location; this page doesn't resolve that either.

If the adventurers honour the deal, they walk away with 780 to 980 gp net, Sera gets her passage and treatment, [[grimaldis-dispensary|Grimaldi's]] cooperation is theirs, and [[algernon-reginald-clyde|Clyde]] lights up if he's in port. Betray it, taking the information and abandoning Sera, and [[grimaldis-dispensary|Grimaldi's]] turns hostile: she won't deal with them again, and harbour bosses hear within a day. Go to [[orak|Orak]] without Sera's bearing at all, and the eastern face offers seven unmarked cave entrances with no way to tell which one holds the cache.

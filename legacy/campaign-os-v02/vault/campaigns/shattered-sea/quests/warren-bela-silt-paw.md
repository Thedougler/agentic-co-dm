# Warren: Bela Silt-Paw

A young Passage runner fell prey to a Water [[weird|Weird]] in flooded tunnels below the Warren, with 2-3 days of rations and a broken arm.

## Secrets & Clues

[[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] can offer this lead once the crew has reunited with her (see [[warren-nonas-table|Warren - Nona's Table]]). Wait too long. [[the-tangle|The Tangle]] then flags the message shard's silence as an urgency escalation. [[bela-silt-paw|Bela Silt-Paw]], 22, took her first solo deep-route survey assignment fourteen days ago and has not come back. She is somewhere in the flooded drainage tunnels below the [[warren|Warren]], and The Tangle has no one else who can go in after her.

> [!read-aloud]
> The Passage narrows to shoulder-width and then drops. At the bottom of four stone steps the water begins, black, still, lamp-warm from the torch Bela left wedged in the wall above the first shelf. Her kit bag is floating against the approach tunnel entrance. She did not take it back through.

Give the crew a beat to look around. They notice the torch wedged above the shelf, Bela's kit bag floating at the entrance, the water depth (knee-height on a Human here, deeper ahead), and the silence. Then the Weird in the central channel moves.

A Water Weird has claimed the flooded central chamber on Bela's survey route. She is alive on a dry shelf beyond it (broken forearm, message shard, emergency rations), but she cannot pass the Weird. The clock is 2-3 days.

### Site

```text
[Approach tunnel] - 5ft wide, low ceiling
     ↓
[Entry shelf] - 3ft water, 8ft ceiling, 6ft × 8ft flat shelf
     ↓ (main channel)
[Central chamber] - 8ft water, 8ft ceiling at peak, 20ft × 40ft
                 ← WATER WEIRD claims this channel
     ↓ (west approach tunnel - optional second Weird)
[Far shelf] - 3ft water, 8ft ceiling, 6ft × 8ft shelf
     ↓ (dry alcove, 2 steps up)
[Dry alcove] - BELA IS HERE: broken arm, shard, rations, 1 torch stub
```

**Approach tunnels:** 5ft wide, 5ft ceiling. Medium creatures squeeze. Small creatures move normally. No swimming room. Anyone in the tunnel while it's flooding has seconds, not rounds.

**Entry shelf:** 3ft deep. A Human is wading. Bela's kit bag is floating at the entrance. Water is still.

**Central chamber:** 8ft deep in the main channel. The Weird occupies this space. The ceiling peaks to 8ft in the center, slopes lower at the edges. A creature wading the shelf edges (where the floor rises, depth ~4ft) is technically in the shallows, but only if they stay pressed to the wall.

**Far shelf and dry alcove:** same profile as the entry shelf. Climbing two stone steps brings someone into the dry alcove where Bela is. Small ledge cache hidden behind a loose stone she knows.

### The Weird

> [!mechanic]
> **Water Weird** (MM p.299). AC 13, HP 58, [[speed|Speed]] 0ft / Swim 60ft. Constrict +5 to hit, 3d8+3 bludgeoning, grappled target (DC 13 Str to escape). **Transparent in water:** DC 15 Perception to detect while still. **Regenerates 10 HP/round** in contact with water unless hit by fire or cold damage that round.

Engagement rules for the same Weird follow.

> [!mechanic]
> **Weird engagement.** It attacks only when a creature enters the main channel water. It ignores creatures on the entry and far shelves, and will not pursue into approach tunnels. The shelf edges (4ft depth, wall-pressed) offer a traversable route. The Weird can reach them but must move to do so. When it moves, it loses its transparent bonus. The crew gains half-cover from the wall. Risky but survivable. **Optional escalation:** a second Weird in the west approach tunnel activates if the crew tries to collapse or drain the central chamber instead of defeating the existing Weird.

**Regeneration counter:** fire or cold damage suppresses regeneration for the round. An alchemist's fire (5ft splash), a cold cantrip, or a scroll creates a window. The Weird drops fast when players sustain the damage.

### Bela

[[bela-silt-paw|Bela Silt-Paw]] is a young [[rattkin|Rattkin]], age 22, and a first-generation [[the-passage|Passage]] runner. She took this solo assignment seeking the credit. The Weird surfaced two steps into her crossing, and she fell hard, breaking her left forearm on a stone step. She splinted the arm and stayed put, as trained. Every few hours she signals with her message shard.

**[[condition|Condition]] on rescue.** Bela has a splinted broken forearm and one level of exhaustion and dehydration (Perception checks at disadvantage). Fear has hollowed her out (she is calm now, not from comfort, but from enduring terror long enough to feel nothing new).

**Her shard.** The message shard, a small Passage device carved from bone, pairs with a receiver at The Tangle and signals her location every few hours; anyone with Rattkin contacts or a Passage connection will recognize the tool.

**The cache.** Behind the loose stone in the dry alcove sits 340 gp (mixed coin, Passage operational funds), 2 potions of water breathing, and a sealed wax-and-leather dispatch marked with a Dravosi naval seal, two years old. Bela's assignment included mapping this cache for The Tangle, so she knows what's there, and she will tell the crew if asked.

**The dispatch.** Sealed for the DM to open or hold. It contains Commandant Ulrik Faste's fraudulent supply contract (timber substitutions and keel-iron shortfalls over a three-year period, signed off against falsified inspection reports). Worth approximately 500 gp to a [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] rival, a [[calveno|Calveno]] merchant who lost a contract, or a Dravosi political enemy of Faste. Worth more to The Passage as Tangle leverage. Commandant Faste is not yet developed as an antagonist. Leave it closed until the crew chooses to open it.

### Loaded Guns

- The message shard keeps signalling while Bela is alive. If the rescue takes too long, the signals stop. The Tangle notices immediately.
- The harbour-foundation route Bela was mapping bypasses Canal Commission checkpoints entirely, making it useful for moving people or cargo without paperwork.

### Clocks

> [!mechanic]
> **Bela's rations:** 2-3 days from session start. Tick: her shard signals become irregular, then stop. **The route.** The Tangle's sub-level drainage access stays blocked until someone defeats the Weird.

## Beats

### Blocks

- Fight the Weird directly in the central channel (dangerous; home advantage)
- Traverse the shelf edge (deadly without a plan; viable with good tactics and Bela's knowledge)
- Use water breathing (the potions are in the cache on the far side, though the crew may not know this yet)
- Fire or cold tactics to suppress regeneration (viable; the Weird burns fast if you sustain the damage)
- Drain or redirect the chamber (structural; The Tangle will ask questions; the optional second Weird appears)

## Outcome

- **Resolved.** Bela reaches safety alive. The crew defeats the Weird and clears the route. They recover the cache and choose to disclose or keep it.
- **Complicated.** Bela reaches safety but the Weird survives. The route stays blocked. The Tangle is grateful but the problem remains half-solved.
- **Ignored.** Bela's shard stops signalling. The Tangle closes the route and cuts the loss, saying nothing to the crew about it.

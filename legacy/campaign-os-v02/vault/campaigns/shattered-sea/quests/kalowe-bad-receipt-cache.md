# [[kalowe|Kalowe]]: The Bad Receipt's Cache

A pirate captain's lost cache on a limestone shelf draws fortune-seekers and the ship's captain seeking to recover it.

## Secrets & Clues

A nameless limestone shelf three miles east-southeast of the [[kalowe|Kalowe]] gap holds crates from a prize taken in the [[central-strait|Central Strait]] two seasons ago. [[the-bad-receipt|The Bad Receipt]] offloaded them while the heat died down.

The pilot who led them there died four months later of fever in [[veth|Veth]]. Captain Orina Fale has been unable to collect the cache for eight months. Nobody else aboard knew the exact rock.

Damu, a fisher, saw them anchor there. He has been nursing an ale at [[the-flat-water|The Flat Water]] for three days, working up to selling the location. [[reweti-kahurangi|Reweti Kahurangi]] has noticed but says nothing. [[the-bad-receipt|The Bad Receipt]] sailed into [[kalowe|Kalowe]] this morning. Fale is on the second causeway with two crew, quietly asking harbour workers about the dead pilot's last job.

**Damu** is at [[the-flat-water|The Flat Water]], second table from the terrace, visibly nervous. If the party looks capable and unaffiliated with any crew Reweti has reason to distrust, she will catch their eye and nod toward him. He will sell the rock's precise location (including a traced copy of his chart) for 50 gp. He wants coin, not a cut.

**Orina Fale** is not offering money yet. She will. She is captain of [[the-bad-receipt|The Bad Receipt]]: [[midchain|Midchain]] mixed heritage, forties, sharp-featured, always dressed well for a pirate. She runs on business logic and respects people who make sensible decisions under pressure.

**The Rock:** this limestone shelf stretches 60 feet wide, barely above waterline at mean tide. A single wind-bent palm grows here, and a notch marks the eastern face where [[the-bad-receipt|The Bad Receipt]] tied off. Crates rest on the eastern face behind a natural rock shelf, covered with salvaged canvas and weighted stones. The rock holds no guardians and has no traps. Anyone who lands and explores will discover them in five minutes.

**Cargo:**

| Crates | Contents | Value |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Spice (pepper, nutmeg, clove) | 300 gp wholesale |
| 3 | Undyed trade cloth | 200 gp wholesale |
| 3 (sealed) | Silver plate tableware, [[dravosi-crown\|Dravosi]] merchant mark | 300 to 350 gp |
| **Gross** | | **800 gp** |

One of the sealed silver plate crates contains an engraved service with a noble house seal (worth 200 gp to the right buyer, or trouble if the wrong people recognise it).

**Selling options:**

- [[taufa-fifita-and-sons|Taufa Fifita & Sons]]: 720 gp (10% cut, no questions, 2-day wait)
- Harbour direct: 680 gp (faster, fewer buyers)  
- Silver plate alone: 350 gp for those 3 crates at any non-[[dravosi-crown|Dravosi]] port

**If [[the-bad-receipt|The Bad Receipt]] arrives during loading:** Fale carries 8 crew and a swivel gun (1d10 bludgeoning, 1/round). She will not fire because she wants the cargo undamaged. She will hail and demand answers. She is a businesswoman who sees violence as an expensive option. If the party carries weapons and already has most of the cargo, she will negotiate: she trades the spice and cloth for the silver plate plus 100 gp, netting the party roughly 500 gp and her respect. If the party refuses, she leaves, makes a note, and remembers. If the party fights at sea, eight crew and a swivel gun oppose the party on an open rock with no cover.

**Triggers:** the party asks [[reweti-kahurangi|Reweti Kahurangi]] about work, news, or local opportunities. The party visits [[the-flat-water|The Flat Water]] long enough for Reweti to read them as capable, prompting her nod toward Damu. The party notices [[the-bad-receipt|The Bad Receipt]] in the harbour and asks who she belongs to.

## Beats

The Tidal Clock drives the pacing once Damu sells the location:

- **Move that day:** 12-hour head start on [[the-bad-receipt|The Bad Receipt]]. Easy pickup.
- **Wait until morning:** Fale has narrowed it to the eastern approaches. She arrives 4 hours after the party.
- **Wait two days:** Fale finds the rock herself. Someone else has already taken the cache.

## Outcome

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|

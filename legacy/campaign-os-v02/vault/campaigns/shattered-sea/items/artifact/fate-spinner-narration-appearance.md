> [!narration]
> *The [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]] is a four-sided top no bigger than a thumb, cut from a single piece of clear quartz. It stays cool even in a warm hand.*
>
> *Years of a thumbnail setting it spinning wore its faces smooth. No maker's mark or inscription survives on it. Spun in sunlight, it casts coins of colored light across whatever surface it sits on. It spins far longer than such a small piece should.*
>
> *A faint glass-on-glass tick marks each turn. The sound is smaller than a coin drop and harder to ignore.*

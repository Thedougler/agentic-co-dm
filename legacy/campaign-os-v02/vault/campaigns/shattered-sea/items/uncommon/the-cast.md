# The Cast

> [!read-aloud]
> A thick arrow, or four thin ones bound as one, wrapped from head to fletching in cord wound so tight the shaft looks ribbed.
>
> The wrapping holds small lead weights at intervals, each one the size of a pea. They click against each other when it moves.
>
> The head is blunt. Closer to a fist than a point.
>
> Under the cord runs netting the whole length of the shaft, folded the way a fisher folds a net so it will open once and open right.
>
> It smells of brine and old fish, which no dry drawer has taken out of it.
>
> The cord ends in a single slip-knot, sitting proud of the wrapping where a thumb would find it.

_[[weapon|Weapon]] (Arrow), Uncommon._

Half the Le Paludi canal families turn out a clumsy version of this. The one in [[ettore-lupo|Ettore Lupo]]'s drawer at [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]] came out clean, and he will tell you which family folded it.

[[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]] protects people by closing distance, and the person she needs to stop is always the one already running. The Cast holds a runner where he is for the six seconds it takes her to arrive. She never has to choose between catching him and killing him.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **The Cast [HB delivery, RAW net].** One shot, consumed on landing. Loose it as an ordinary ranged attack at a Large or smaller creature within the weapon's normal range, with no range penalty beyond the bow's own. The cord parts in flight and the net opens.
>
> On a hit, the creature takes no damage and is [[restrained|Restrained]] until it frees itself. Freeing follows the mundane net's own rules. The creature or another creature within 5 feet of it takes an Action and rolls a DC 10 [[strength|Strength]] check, or someone deals 5 slashing damage to the net (AC 10). Either one destroys a 5-foot square of it and frees the creature.
>
> **Edge cases:** [[size|Huge]] and larger creatures shrug it off, and so do formless creatures. On a miss the net still opens and falls across the ground, water, or whatever else lies under it, which the DM should say out loud: the square is now difficult terrain, a 10-foot patch of net. Over water it floats on its lead line and fouls a swimmer at the surface exactly as it would foul one on land. A creature already Restrained by one net is not Restrained twice by a second.
>
> **Limitations:** it deals no damage and does not seize the target. It does not pull or drag the target anywhere, and it does nothing at all to a creature that is Incorporeal, gaseous, or larger than a cart. Once opened, it stays a net. It does not fold itself back up.

## Provenance

The canal families of [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] net, weight, and fold them. They have made them for the city's bounty men longer than anyone in the trade has been alive. They wind each one onto a shaft turned from [[carpenters-slip|Carpenters' Slip]] offcuts.

[[ettore-lupo|Ettore Lupo]] buys a few at a time at [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]] from whichever family is short that season, and he pays what he paid the last family.

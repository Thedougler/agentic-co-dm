# Fleet Commander's Chair

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon.*

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **Seated authority [HB].** The seated occupant has advantage on [[charisma|Charisma]] saving throws and Charisma checks. The benefit requires staying seated in the chair. It ends the moment the occupant stands or leaves it.

**Limitations.** Grants no benefit to anyone not seated in the chair. Confers no other property beyond its indestructibility.

## Provenance

[[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] recovered it from the crater in [[mercatura|Mercatura]], [[calveno|Calveno]]'s trading district, in the fight against [[otar-the-foul|Otar the Foul]] (Session 07), confirmed magical and seemingly indestructible, and carried it out in [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]]'s borrowed [[bag-of-holding|Bag of Holding]]. Identify in Session 08 confirmed it's the fleet commander's chair ([[transcript]]). It now sits aft aboard the *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]*, in the space that was once the captain's cabin.

Underneath the shape it holds, it's an [[elder-mimic|Elder Mimic]], patient enough to keep this form indefinitely.

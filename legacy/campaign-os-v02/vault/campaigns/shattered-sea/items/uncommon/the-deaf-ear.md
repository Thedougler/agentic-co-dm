# The Deaf Ear

> [!read-aloud]
> Wrapped in paper the colour of old tallow, packed so dense the roll has no give at all between finger and thumb.
>
> It fights the flame. The taper has to sit against the end for a slow count before anything catches.
>
> What comes off it barely smells at all, a flat mineral note like wet slate, and the smoke hangs where it leaves the paper.
>
> Voices carry across the room the whole time, every word clear, and not one of them arrives with any weight behind it.

**Rarity:** the Uncommon tier covers this. Elixir of Health is the same shape of consumable at the same tier and does more, curing four conditions outright where this improves one roll. Potion of Heroism sits a tier up at Rare and hands a flat bonus to every saving throw for the same hour without an attunement slot. One ability's saves, once, lands cleanly beneath both.

**PC connection:** four of the five save at +0 or worse against Charisma, and only [[perrin-black-jaw\|Perrin]] at +9 shrugs off a charm line he cannot cover for anyone else. A charm that lands on [[crissdalynn-khinriss\|Crissdalynn]] at +0 or on Jean-Claude at -1 turns the crew's two best weapons around and points them back down the line.

## Mechanics

**[HB]** Lighting the Deaf Ear and taking the first breath costs an Action. For 1 hour afterward, the smoker has advantage on Charisma saving throws.

The blend reaches nobody but the smoker and forces no save of its own. Lighting it spends it.

Edge cases:

- The advantage covers Charisma saves alone. The smoker rolls Charisma checks flat, [[persuasion|Persuasion]] and [[deception|Deception]] included.
- It ends no effect already on the smoker. A charm landed before the first breath lasts out its full duration.
- A second Deaf Ear inside the hour does nothing, and a lit one passed to another smoker carries nothing with it.

**Limitations:** no bonus to attack rolls, damage rolls, AC, or any save outside Charisma, no immunity to the [[charmed|charmed]] or [[frightened|frightened]] conditions, and no protection for anyone standing next to the smoker.

## Provenance

[[rufio-segalla|Rufio Segalla]] finishes every blend himself in [[la-brace|La Brace]]'s back room, from base compound bought by standing order from [[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]] at [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]] in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]]. The densest roll on his shelf and the slowest to make: the packing is where the effect comes from.

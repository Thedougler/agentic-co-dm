# Roll-Call Slate

> [!read-aloud]
> A slate the size of a spread palm, framed in tarred wood, a chalk stub knotted to one corner on a short string.
>
> The face is rubbed grey and shallow in the middle from a hundred names written and wiped off it, and the last few are still legible under the grey when you turn it to the lamp.
>
> Write your own name across it and the chalk goes down warm, and the slate sits heavier in your hand than a slate that size should.

```meta-bind
VIEW[{reference_image}][image(class(reference-image-view))]
```

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon.* Carried by [[sparhold|Sparhold]]'s watch-shift crews, who chalk their names on at the top of a shift and wipe them off at the bell. The slate answers to whichever name is currently written on it.

## Mechanics

**Name on the slate [HB].** Writing a creature's name on the slate takes one minute and wipes any name already there. The slate holds one name at a time, and functions only for the creature named on it, and only while that creature carries it.

**Called back [HB].** Once per long rest, when the named creature carrying the slate fails an [[intelligence|Intelligence]] or [[charisma|Charisma]] saving throw, the slate speaks that name aloud in the flat delivery of a watch officer working down a list. The creature rerolls the failed save and must use the new roll. The name is audible to everyone within 30 feet.

**Limitations.** No effect on a [[wisdom|Wisdom]], [[strength|Strength]], [[dexterity|Dexterity]], or [[constitution|Constitution]] save, and none on a save another creature is making. It cannot be used on a save already rerolled by another effect. It will not fire for a creature whose name it does not carry, and a creature who does not know its own true name cannot write one that works. Wiping the slate mid-fight costs an action, and the daily use does not reset with the new name.

## Provenance

The design belongs to Sparhold's watch rather than to any wizard: enough sentries have walked off a night post with their heads turned by something in the water that the settlement's slate-makers started charging extra for the ones that talk back. [[vashka-doru|Vashka Doru]] keeps two on the shelf under her tally wall and sells them mostly to duellists, who find that a Challenge Bell crowd works on a champion much the way deep water does.

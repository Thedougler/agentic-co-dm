# Pocket Thunder

> [!read-aloud]
> A cigarette no thicker than a finger, wrapped in oiled paper the color of a bruise.
>
> It's heavier than paper has any right to be.
>
> A stripe of striking-powder runs its full length, rough as a match head under your thumb.
>
> It smells of coal-sac and something sweeter, and the paper never feels dry.
>
> The striking-strip is still live, ready to answer whatever question it gets.

_Consumable, Uncommon._

One of six blends sold as alchemical cigarettes in [[calveno|Calveno]], though nobody smokes this one. The wielder snaps it like a match, then throws it.

Comparable to [[dust-of-sneezing-and-choking|Dust of Sneezing and Choking]] (Uncommon, thrown, area effect, Constitution save, no damage) and Potion of Fire Breath (Uncommon, single-use, area effect, [[dexterity|Dexterity]] save). Pocket Thunder's effects last shorter than those items. It deals no damage. Both items work well.

[[la-brace|La Brace]] sells it from the shelf. [[rufio-segalla|Rufio Segalla]] runs this [[velo-quarter|Velo Quarter]] smoking room, the only one in Calveno where smoke can burn out in the open. [[la-gatta|La Gatta]] also sells it, at 60% price as an unreliable second. [[nicco-kettley|Nicco Kettley]] runs this stall on [[the-bridge|the Bridge]]'s bottom tier.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **Pocket Thunder [HB].** Single use, consumable. As an Action, the wielder snaps the cigarette and throws it up to 30 feet. It goes off on landing with a loud bang.
>
> **Edge cases:** the wielder rolls the save too, if the throw lands within 10 feet of them. A creature that already can't see ignores the blindness. One that already can't hear ignores the deafness. Throwing it uses up the only cigarette in the pack. It has no recharge and no reload.
>
> **Limitations:** deals no damage and breaks no concentration. A creature already immune to blinded or deafened feels nothing. Smoking it does nothing at all.

> [!check] [[constitution|Constitution]] Save — The Detonation
> DC 13; every creature within 10 feet of the landing point rolls, wielder included.
> **Success:** no effect.
> **Failure:** the blast blinds and deafens the creature until the end of its next turn.

## Provenance

[[rufio-segalla|Rufio Segalla]] finishes every blend himself in [[la-brace|La Brace]]'s back room. He buys the base compound by standing order from [[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]] at [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]] in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]]. [[nicco-kettley|Nicco Kettley]] resells Orsini's rejected batches of the same blend under the Bridge.

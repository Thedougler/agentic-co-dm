# Vollask Meatball Tin

> [!read-aloud]
> A flat tin stamped with gnomish letters nobody aboard will translate.
>
> The lid pries off to six meatballs packed in rendered fat, dark and dense, each the size of a child's fist.
>
> The smell is tar smoke, garlic, and something sweet underneath that clings to the fingers and the tin both.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **Vollask Meatball Tin [HB].** One tin counts as two days of rations for one creature. The meatballs are heavily spiced, packed in rendered fat, and stay good for six months sealed. Once opened, eat within two days or they turn.
>
> The gnomes make them below decks aboard the [[nimmik-vollask|Nimmik Vollask]] and Cotter sells them by the tin. The free meatballs served with every order are from the same batch, just not tinned.
>
> **Limitations:** grants no bonus to any check, save, attack, or AC — it is food, nothing more.

## Provenance

The gnomish crew of the [[nimmik-vollask|Nimmik Vollask]] makes these below decks in a galley no Medium creature can reach. The recipe does not leave the ship. Cotter has tried the meatballs from three other sources and says none of them are the same, though she cannot say what the difference is.

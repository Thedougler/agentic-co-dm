# Doorway Snare Coil

> [!read-aloud]
> A hand-length bundle of wire, coiled tight and bound with twine, with a bent iron peg pushed through the middle of it.
>
> The wire is braided from strands finer than hair and it holds every bend it has ever been given.
>
> Free an end and the coil comes alive in your hands, wanting to spring back to its own shape.
>
> The peg has been driven into stone before. The head is mushroomed over and cold.

```meta-bind
VIEW[{reference_image}][image(class(reference-image-view))]
```

*Tool, Common.* The rig [[sabine-corvo|Sabine Corvo]] sets across the Hold's rear exit every night, sold by the bundle. Weighs 1 pound.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **[HB] Rig (1 minute).** Set the coil across a doorway, corridor, or gangplank up to 10 feet wide. The wire is noticed with a passive Perception of 15 or a successful DC 13 [[intelligence|Intelligence]] (Investigation) check. The first Large or smaller creature to move through the space without stepping over it makes a DC 13 [[dexterity|Dexterity]] saving throw, and on a failure it is restrained and falls prone. A restrained creature escapes with a DC 13 [[strength|Strength]] (Athletics) check, which cuts the wire and spends the coil.
>
> **Limitations:** one creature, one spring, and the whole rig is useless against anything Huge or anything that flies over it. Resetting it takes another minute and the wire has to be uncut.

## Provenance

Sold across the counter at [[the-live-hold|The Live Hold]] to anyone who asks how she keeps the stock in.

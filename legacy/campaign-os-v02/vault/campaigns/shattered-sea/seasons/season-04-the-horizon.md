# Season 4: The Horizon

*Every authority above them fell. The fissure remains.*

## Overview

Silhouette only: this season exists at the lowest resolution per the campaign's sandbox principle. The party faces [[umberlee|Umberlee]], the true divine authority whose tribute system is port infrastructure across the entire [[shattered-sea|Shattered Sea]]. The confrontation is theological and existential, not military. The party has spent three seasons dismantling every tier of authority below a god, and now they face the final test: whether to tear down Umberlee's system knowing it might be what holds everything together. See [[campaign-overview|The Shattered Sea]] for the setting-wide premise and Campaign Architecture for the authority ladder this season completes.

The ending is a three-way choice: side with Umberlee (her tribute system may be what manages the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]] fissure), challenge her and face what's beneath, or find a third path the campaign hasn't shown yet. The campaign ends on who the party has become and what they choose to build, not what they conquer. The fissure is the horizon. The last image is the party facing it with no authority above them.

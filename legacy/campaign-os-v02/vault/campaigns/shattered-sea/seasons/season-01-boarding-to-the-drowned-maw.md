# Season 1: Boarding to The Drowned Maw

*From a mutiny in the Saltwright's hold to a ritual chamber under Calveno, the party goes from hunted cargo to the only thing standing between the [[grung|Grung]] and the festival above.*

## Overview

Season 1 opens mid-mutiny aboard the [[saltwright|Saltwright]] and carries the party from fugitives to a group with a base, a patron in [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]], and a live threat under [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]]. Its throughline once the party makes landfall: can [[calveno|Calveno]] survive [[il-gioco-delle-beffe|Il Gioco delle Beffe]] with a Grung strike staged beneath the festival? The plot runs on its own shortened timeline and fires whether anyone stops it or not, see [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|Calveno Sewer Magazines]]. This is Season 1: it picks up from the campaign's opening premise, not a prior finale. See [[campaign-overview|The Shattered Sea]] for the setting-wide premise this season plays out inside.

## Finale / End Condition

**Planned:** the party defeats [[otar-the-foul|Otar the Foul]] and earns recognition as local heroes in [[calveno|Calveno]].

**Routes:** the confrontation is reachable below or above: (a) through the breached primary chamber the party is already fighting in, via the watched Room 7 passage or the hidden Room 6 passage; (b) if the party retreats or the detonation fires first, Otar erupts through the [[mercatura|Mercatura]] crater into the festival surface and the fight comes to them ([[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|Calveno Sewer Magazines]] § Room 8 / after-detonation battlefield). The Finale requires Otar actually defeated: disrupting the summoning circle prevents him from appearing but does not by itself close the season.

**If ignored:** the detonation fires on the raid's own shortened timeline and the festival above pays for it. Full consequence chain in the Calveno Sewer Magazines § If ignored.

**Reached:** yes, in session 7 ("Not Funny Now"). [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] lands the finishing blow on the Foul One in the Calveno crater. The crew stands victorious.

## GM Notes

This season's shape: from hunted cargo to the people Calveno sees defending it, a found crew choosing a home and proving themselves through what they do under it.

Open threads carried through to the season's close, still unresolved: the exact trigger of the [[solange-barret|Solange Barret]] ritual, and the unexplained healing light [[shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] has been using since session 3.

[[threads|Threads]] deliberately left loose for the next season, each named: the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]] convergence ([[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]'s pilgrimage and [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s abyss encounter, see each PC page's Arc Notes), [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s unpaid [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] debt, which Umberlee has stopped waiting on and now collects off the water herself ([[the-red-wake|The Red Wake]]), [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s hunt for her defector brother ([[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] § Arc Notes), [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s still-unspecified favor, and the [[osset|Osset]] leads session 8 turned up ([[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]] § The Schism's Third Name).

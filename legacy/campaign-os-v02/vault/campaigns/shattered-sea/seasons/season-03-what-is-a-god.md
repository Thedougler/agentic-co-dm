# Season 3: What Is a God?

*The gold caste claims divine right. Its makers designed Auralis to serve. Neither is what they say they are. The party is powerful enough to prove it.*

## Overview

Season 3 opens the full [[verdant-scatter|Scatter]] as the party's operating theater. The crew's power at tier 3 matches a scope where geography no longer hides them, and both false-god pressures find them because there's nowhere left that's someone else's problem. The [[grung-clans|Grung]] gold caste claims theocratic control over their own people (social), while [[auralis|Auralis]], an [[antheri|Antheri]] creation that holds the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]] fissure shut (cosmic), presents a parallel false divinity. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]'s and [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s arcs converge on the same question from different directions. See [[campaign-overview|The Shattered Sea]] for the setting-wide premise and Campaign Architecture for this season's position on the authority ladder.

*What is a god, actually?* This question drives the season. By its close, the party has dismantled two versions of divine authority and has a framework for challenging the real thing in Season 4.

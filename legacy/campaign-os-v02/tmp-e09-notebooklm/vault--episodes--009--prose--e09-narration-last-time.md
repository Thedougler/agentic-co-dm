> [!narration]  
> _Last time on the Shattered Sea, the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] sat in [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]]’s drydock while the party collected what [[calveno|Calveno]] believed its ‘Defenders’ had earned. Even [[ottavio-fieschi|Factor Fieschi]] admitted a rare debt: a thousand gold in trade credit, the deed to [[casa-falier|Casa Falier]], and a diamond ring._
> 
> _The ink was barely dry when [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]’s message came: **come home. There are so many missing.**_
> 
> _At the [[mercatura|Mercatura]], the crater still sat behind a cordon. Nona had borrowed a table and covered it with names. More than three hundred. Every one a fighting-age man taken in the raids. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] saw the pattern first. The Grung weren’t just raiding anymore. They were preparing for war, and taking extreme risks to do it._
> 
> _Before the grieving crowd, Nona made her answer plain: [[the-passage|The Passage]] would give chase. Two ships were already bound for the Verdant Teeth._
> 
> _With the pursuit underway, the party spent their winnings across Calveno. Perrin struck a deal for a wrist-shield that snapped open faster than the eye could follow. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] pulled a torn page from the shelves, carrying the story of a monk who abandoned the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels]] during their schism. They met [[zort|Zort]], an armless goblin willing to trade his reptile contact in [[sparhold|Sparhold]] for a prosthetic leg. And at La Cenere, Lavinia sold them an impressive collection of questionable goods before slipping Delmar a sending stone._
> 
> _By morning, [[cobb|Cobb]] had finished the Uncertainty’s refit ahead of his own estimate._
> 
> _More than three hundred names sit on Nona’s list. The raiders’ trail grows colder by the hour. And beyond the [[central-strait|Central Strait]] lies the Midchain, the Grung fleet, and Calveno’s missing._

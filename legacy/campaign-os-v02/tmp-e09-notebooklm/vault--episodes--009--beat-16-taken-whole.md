## Situation

A hull drifting in the Maw-lanes, close enough to hail, and nobody aboard answers. The rigging still hangs straight, the cargo hatch dogged but not locked, every line coiled, every hatch seated as though the crew stepped below and never came back up. The *[[taken-whole|Taken Whole]]* rumour circulates in every dock tavern from [[kalowe|Kalowe]] east: something in the outer Maw-lanes takes ships whole and leaves hulls drifting as proof. The proof is alongside. In the helm-alcove cradle sits a log whose last entry ends mid-sentence, a clear hand stopping at the end of a navigation note with a bearing.

![[beat-16-taken-whole-narration-boarding]]

> [!mechanic]
> **Maw-lanes exposure, time hazard.** Each period the Uncertainty sits stationary alongside the drifting hull, the crew occupies water where ships disappear. The hazard is unknown and deliberately unresolved. The mechanism that takes ships whole is not defined this session.
>
> **Escalation by time stationary:**
>
> | Time | What changes |
> |---|---|
> | First hour | Boarding, the log, the dogged hatch. Normal salvage tension |
> | Second hour | The light shifts. The water around both hulls goes still in a way that has nothing to do with wind |
> | Third hour | Something in the water notices the Uncertainty. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] feels it without needing a check |
> | Beyond | The Uncertainty is doing what the taken hull did: sitting in the lanes, stationary, waiting |
>
> **Exit:** weigh anchor and move. The sensation fades with distance. The log's bearing is next-session water.

## Player Gravity

- [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]], whatever takes ships whole in the Maw-lanes swims the same water as his patron's question, and beat-15's recognition is still in him
- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]], a crew taken whole off a sound hull is his own fleet's death in miniature
- [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]], an intact hull with intact rigging is either salvage or data, and she wants both

## Active Force

- **Intent:** unknown; whatever takes ships leaves no account of wanting anything
- **Means:** takes crews off sound hulls without a fight's traces
- **Limits:** unknown; it did not take the hull, the cargo, or the log

## Pressure

- **Now:** the hull alongside, boarding it a choice
- **Next:** light, weather, and how long the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] sits stationary in the lanes where ships get taken
- **Default trajectory:** the hull drifts on; the rumour stays a rumour with one more proof

## Open Vectors

Prep coverage for the DM.

- **Boarding:** the log (and its bearing), the dogged hatch and what waits belowdecks
- **Salvage:** a sound hull is money, and towing in these lanes is hours of stationary vulnerability
- Following the log's last note points deeper into the outer lanes. A thread deliberately left open, next-session water
- **Leaving it:** see Bypass

## Escalation

1. The sighting and the hail nobody answers
2. Boarding and the log
3. The hatch, and what the hold does or does not hold
4. The decision with the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] stationary and the light going

## Fallout

- **Success:** the log and its bearing secured, the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] moving again with the hull read for what it proves
- **Complication:** salvage claimed, and the tow halves their speed through the lanes the rumour owns
- **Setback:** too long stationary. The sensation of being what the lanes notice, and a crew that has to choose between the tow and the run
- **Bypass:** they pass it by. The rumour keeps its proof, and the bearing goes unread

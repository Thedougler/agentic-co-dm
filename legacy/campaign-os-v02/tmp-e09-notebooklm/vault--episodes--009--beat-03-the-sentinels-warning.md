# The Sentinel's Warning

![[beat-03-the-sentinels-warning-narration-open]]

## At a Glance

- **Pattern:** warning, a trusted mentor spends trust to deliver a warning he cannot fully source
- **Delta:** Kyzil's warning about the [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]] dreidel, keep it safe, tell no one she has it, the [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]]'s masters are not necessarily the safe choice
- **Pressure:** Kyzil flew two days out to sea to say it. The urgency is the message

## Player Gravity

- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]: Kyzil is a father figure, an unresolved mentor bond ("the hardest pilgrimage in Eyrie history") → this warning is the first crack aimed at her Authority-Ladder position from inside the Eyrie itself

## Situation

Day 2 out of [[calveno|Calveno]], any heading. [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]] arrives flying, without hailing. He drops out of the sun, folds his wings at the stern rail, perfectly balanced in a whitecap wind, and speaks to [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] quietly but not privately.

## Actors

- [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]] **wants** the dreidel hidden from everyone including his own order. **Knows** someone hunts Fate Spinners, and holds back the rest. **Fears** what the order would do with the knowledge, what he has not worked out himself. **Without PC intervention** delivers the warning and flies north regardless.

## What Can Change

Crissdalynn (and whoever stands close enough) learns that someone hunts the dreidel and that Kyzil distrusts his own order with it. The crew may press him for more, but he only gives shape. The relationship deepens or strains depending on how she receives a secret she never asked for.

## Surface

The conversation is on deck, and other crew hear as much as they position to hear. Pressing Kyzil for details. Asking about the pilgrimage. Letting him go.

## Conditional Reactions

- **If pressed on who hunts it:** he repeats "anyone" and gives nothing
- **If Crissdalynn refuses the secrecy:** he does not argue. He says where she carries the dreidel falls to her judgment now, and the leaving is heavier
- **If the crew ask about the [[aruhe|Aruhe]] storm or the raids:** he saw the storm from high air on the flight south. He confirms wreck-debris on the northern reefs (one line, no detail)

## Carry Forward

The warning primes the [[beat-06-the-knifes-wake|Knife's Wake]] ambush, pirates on a retainer for "a small old thing that spins," and every later choice about who learns of the dreidel.

## Supporting Detail

- **Tell:** adjusts his hood right over left
- **"Anyone"**, repeated as a correction when Crissdalynn asks who. He names no one, not the Countless, not Vantyrus. The shape of the threat lives in what he does not say
- **Exit line:** the hardest thing a sentinel ever learned was the difference between what the order knew and what the order ought to know. He leaps, and then he is a dwindling shape north

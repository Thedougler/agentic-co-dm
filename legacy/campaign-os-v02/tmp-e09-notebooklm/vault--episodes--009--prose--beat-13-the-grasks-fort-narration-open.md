> [!narration]
> *The gate to the Grask's fort stands open on the high ground above [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s inner harbor, walls two feet thick, two centuries old. The Grask raised them. The dock bosses who came after him killed him inside these same walls, together, every hand on the blade so no single boss carried the blame alone. They kept the fort and never speak of why.*
>
> *Inside, the hall is one round room, low-ceilinged, the sound close. The twelve are already seated in the same circle their predecessors used the night they planned the killing, twelve chairs with nothing between them but bare floor. Clerks work a side table, one writing steadily, the other watching the door.*
>
> *An open ledger waits at that table, ink still wet in places, and whatever it says when the Council rises becomes what the rest of the [[midchain|Midchain]] hears first.*

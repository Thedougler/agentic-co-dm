> *The deck planking gives under weight the way a maintained hull should, solid and dry throughout. A coil of line sits exactly where a hand placed it, the tail end still tucked. The pin rail holds every belaying pin seated in order. A mug sits in the scupper beside the helm, dregs dried to a brown ring, left by someone who expected to come back for it.*
>
> *In the helm alcove, the log lies open in its cradle. The last entry is a navigation note in a clear, steady hand. The bearing reads into the outer [[the-drowned-maw|Maw-lanes]], east of anything the charts trust. The sentence does not finish. The pen rests in the binding crease where it rolled when the hand stopped writing.*

> [!narration]
> _The green wall of [[aruhe|Aruhe]] rises above the reef line, canopy unbroken from the waterline to the ridge. No smoke from this angle. No roofline. No cleared ground._
>
> _[[roka-sten|Roka Sten]] has been calling depth from the bow since the channel opened, bellowing every reef head and current shift the whole way. He stops mid-word._
>
> _The reef gap is ahead. One hull-width of open water between coral walls, the current pulling through in a low, steady draw. A [[grung|Grung]] hull sits broken on the reef to the south, keel snapped clean, cargo scattered across the coral shelf._
>
> _Past the gap, on the narrow beach, pale shapes hold the tree line on legs that do not bend where legs bend, watching the water._

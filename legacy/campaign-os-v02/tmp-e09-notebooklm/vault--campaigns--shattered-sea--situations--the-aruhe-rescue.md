# The Aruhe Rescue

*The captured [[calveno|Calveno]] citizens' trail leads to Aruhe's reef. Every [[midchain|Midchain]] port carries a piece of the same signal, and the window closes when the fires on that beach go out.*

## Actors

- [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]]: hold the levied captives at Karath. Patrol the Aruhe channel. The wreck cost them 197 of their own count.
- [[the-countless|The Countless]]: hunt any Fate Spinner carrier through deniable instruments. A paid pirate retainer sweeps [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]] traffic.
- [[chain-council]]: need the fleet story blamed on someone not them. Delmar Fisk is the easiest blame in the Midchain.
- [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]]: hunts [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] south into the Midchain lanes. Inspects strait traffic.
- [[waveservants|Waveservants]]: the Dead Lady enforces tribute standing against Delmar's Pearl debt.
- [[house-kailani|House Kailani]] / [[house-renzetti|House Renzetti]]: the challenge bell decides who rules Sparhold, and with it the northern staging water.

## At a Glance

- **Pattern:** an institution writing its alibi in real time, with the party's captain as the ink.
- **Delta:** the [[chain-council|Chain Council]]'s closed-session position on the fleet commission becomes visible. [[admiral-fisk|Admiral Fisk]] acted on bad intelligence. The bad intelligence was a [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] play the Council was too credulous to recognize. The contractor who brokered it no longer exists in a form that can contradict anything. Evidence surfaces, the scapegoat story hardens, or the Council's preferred path runs through [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]].
- **Pressure:** whichever version leaves this room first becomes the version most of the [[midchain|Midchain]] ever hears.

## Player Gravity

- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]], the Council fits the scapegoat story to him by name, in the room or in his absence
- [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]], the debt weather over his people is the same Council's other hand. Perrin's name never comes up, but his community's debts do
- Whole crew, Kalowe's help for any rescue depends on who wins this room

## Situation

The [[chain-council|Chain Council]] meets in the fort that belonged to the man they still call the Grask, killed by their own dock bosses together, two centuries back, because none trusted the others to try it alone. Same stone walls. Same shape of fear.

The Tessarine Containment front moves like weather. Pressure from every direction, the steady reminder that someone extended credit and that calling it remains an option. The people whose names sit on the debt instruments understand none of what that means.

## Actors

- The Council's twelve (speak as one position in session; worldview: afraid together). **Wants:** the commission story sealed with a blamable name that is not theirs. **Knows:** what they voted for and what it cost. **Fears/constrained by:** evidence that could surface; dock anger outside the walls (beat-14); each other. **Without PC intervention:** the scapegoat account hardens and goes out as the record.

## What Can Change

- The party hears the alibi take shape, in the room if they took the meeting, secondhand through dock talk if not
- Evidence of the Council's own sponsorship becomes the live counter-lever
- Delmar's name attached or detached from the blame
- The Tessarine thread surfacing as the Council's own exposure

## Surface

The fort and its history. The session itself, attended or reported. The twelve's public faces afterward. The record clerks who write the version down.

## Conditional Reactions

- **If Delmar confronts the blame directly:** the Council prefers a deal to a fight. His cooperation in the record for considerations in the port.
- **If the party produces or threatens evidence of the sponsorship:** the Denial front stalls and the Council's fear turns inward, at each other.
- **If they let it pass:** the record goes out with the admiral's name where the Council needed it.

## Carry Forward

The record's final shape. Whatever deal or enmity the room produced. The Council's usefulness or hostility to an [[aruhe|Aruhe]] rescue staged from Kalowe.

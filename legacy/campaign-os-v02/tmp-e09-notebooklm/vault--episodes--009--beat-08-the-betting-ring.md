## Situation

Third morning bell, [[sparhold|Sparhold]]. The whole town rings the betting ring outside the watch hall. A duel to the death decides which house rules Sparhold.

> [!mechanic]
> **Challenge law, formal duel.** The challenge bell binds Sparhold. Each house names one champion. The duel runs to yield or death, the whole town watching. The winner's house holds the port, timber yards, and northern water until the next bell rings.
>
> **Interference:** any outsider who enters the ring or signals a champion after the starting bell voids the duel, as does casting a spell targeting a champion or passing equipment across the rope line. The voiding house forfeits its claim and both houses expel the interfering party from Sparhold. The town sees everything. A subtle Sleight of Hand or a pre-ring gift counts as shaping, not interference.
>
> **Seconds' signals:** each house positions seconds at the rope line. A second can call yield on behalf of a champion who cannot speak. A champion who yields lives but loses the house's claim.

[[nico-renzetti|Nico Renzetti]] stands for [[house-renzetti|House Renzetti]]. Straight, still, wearing the expression of a man who accepted an argument he lost in private. A trained fighter who wanted to be anywhere else. [[rangi-kailani|Rangi Kailani]] stands for [[house-kailani|House Kailani]], moving with an ease earned by coming out the other side of real pressure, already settled into the ring before the bell.

What happens between the starting bell and the end is whatever the crew shaped beforehand, by word, action, or conspicuous inaction. The ring remembers. The house that comes out of it remembers better. The beat prescribes no winner.

## Player Gravity

- Whole crew (whichever house wins holds the harbor and the northern water the rescue needs)
- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] at the rope line, hands open, watching a fight he cannot enter
- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]], a monk trained under the hardest master in the Eyrie, reading every form both fighters show

## Active Force

- **Intent:** both houses want the settlement the law of the bell provides
- **Means:** a named champion apiece and the town as witness, bound by the challenge law
- **Limits:** the forms. Interference voids nothing quietly. The town sees everything. The losing house still exists tomorrow

## Pressure

- **Now:** the starting bell
- **Next:** the third minute, when training-without-appetite starts losing to pressure-hardened ease
- **Default trajectory:** Rangi kills Nico and [[house-kailani|House Kailani]] rules Sparhold

## Open Vectors

Prep coverage only.

- **Pre-ring influence:** whatever beat-07 set up lands now (advice, equipment, a word with either champion)
- **Open interference:** voids the duel's legitimacy in front of the whole town. Both houses' response becomes the new situation
- **Reading the fight:** what Nico's reluctance and Rangi's ease each mean for dealing with the winner
- **The bets:** money and standing move around the ring

## Escalation

1. The forms and the first exchange
2. The third minute turns
3. A champion bleeding toward the end
4. The ending, and the town's roar or silence

## Fallout

- **Success:** the favored house takes the town owing the party something unnameable
- **Complication:** the winner takes the ring, but the town saw the party's hand. Legitimacy questions follow both
- **Setback:** the outcome lands against every read the party made, and the house they courted is the one bleeding
- **Bypass:** they watch it clean. The duel ends without them and Sparhold owes them nothing

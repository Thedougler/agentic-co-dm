# Grung Clans

![[grung-clans-banner.webp]]

*A caste-bound, slaveholding society in the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]], built on raiding, toxin production, and a caste order enforced by a suppressed color-sealing rite.*

## Members

**Caste structure.** Skin color determines caste (gold through green) and rank climbs independent of caste. Attempting caste shift earns the [[ozzeth-the-twiceborn|Twiceborn]] brand while caste color comes from diet. Gold caste alone holds the sealing rite that locks shifts, monopolizing control under the guise of sacred birthright. The power of Gold (magic, strength) flows from every lower-caste [[grung|Grung]]'s certainty.

**Leadership:** [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] scouts for the clans before defecting; [[simone-tabarnack|Simone Tabarnack]] supplies poison to the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]]. Both likely stand as enemies to their own clan now.

**Trade & specialized castes.** The [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] interior forbids outsiders; Blue-caste traders handle exchange on [[veth|Veth]]'s western coast (bone, resin, pigments for food, timber, metal, rope) while [[kalowe|Kalowe]] markets move goods. Red caste maintains toxins, Purple coat weapons, and Blue scouts use poison for capture. The clans share no formulas, exporting their toxin only to the Dravosi Crown.

**Slavery operations.** Hatchery camps at [[karath|Karath]] transform captives into guards. Raids on the [[midchain|Midchain]] have accelerated over two years. [[the-passage|The Passage]] avoids Grung territory.

> [!mechanic]
> **Grung magic — three simultaneous layers, three separate traditions.**
> **Layer 1 — Primal (all Grung).** Biological traits (Toxic Secretion, color shift, Standing Leap, Sticky Tongue). No school. [[detect-magic|Detect Magic]]: nothing. [[counterspell|Counterspell]]: not applicable. [[dispel-magic|Dispel Magic]]: no effect.
> **Layer 2 — Arcane, Transmutation: the [[ossketh|Ossketh]].** Sealed by second caster: "Arcane transmutation — a change locked in place, holding." Self-cast (incomplete, needs maintenance): "Arcane transmutation — like a spell caught mid-execution, still running." Detect Magic on the Gold caste itself does not reveal the Ossketh — only on a sealed target. Counterspell can target the ongoing transmutation.
> **Layer 3 — Divine: the certainty-tap (Gold leaders only).** Constrained by belief (shatter certainty → tap drains in real time) and proximity (cut off from believers → tap starves over hours). Detect Magic near a Gold leader at full tap: "Divine — intense, sustained, like standing in front of a waterfall. It isn't coming from them; it's flowing through them." Counterspell targets individual divine spells; the tap is not a discrete cast event. Player lever: expose the Ossketh as arcane trickery — lower-caste belief shatters when the cosmology sustaining it is publicly disproved.

## Goals & Fronts

The clans have three goals. Maintain their caste order and hold slaves. Sell the poison to the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] through [[simone-tabarnack|Simone Tabarnack]]. Expand raids into the [[midchain|Midchain]]. These raids draw trade warnings.

Simone Tabarnack led a mass slave raid on [[calveno|Calveno]] during [[il-gioco-delle-beffe|Il Gioco delle Beffe]] (source: `situations/active/calveno-beffa-grung-raid.md`), proving the clans could strike beyond their territory after years of Midchain raids prepared them; they took hundreds of Calveno's townsfolk in one coordinated strike.

**Patrol gap intelligence came from a Dravosi Crown source.** Identity and price remain unknown. See [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines-room-key|Calveno Sewer Magazines: room key]] (partially damaged as of s04).

### Front: The Fighting-Age Levy

**[[lifecycle|Lifecycle]]:** active

**Aim:** repeat the Calveno pattern across Midchain coastal settlements, extracting only fighting-age men to replenish [[karath|Karath]]'s slave-soldier pipeline and hollow out every port's ability to defend itself, one at a time.

**Approach:** times each raid to a canal-watch or patrol gap, using inside intelligence the way the Calveno raid did, and takes only men of fighting age, leaving women, children, and elders untouched so no settlement reads it as a massacre worth uniting against.

**Off-screen move if unopposed:** a new Midchain port wakes to its own missing-persons list, all fighting-age men, and Grung poison residue turns up on ships trading near it within days, the same signature Jean-Claude flagged in [[calveno|Calveno]].

**Trigger conditions:**

- Every stretch the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]'s crew leaves unopposed, the raiders clear a new settlement's watch gap before [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s pursuit ships close the distance. A segment fills each time a different port reports the same fighting-age-only pattern.

**Clock:** 6 segments (slow burn), filled: 0

**Consequence at fill:** Karath's hatchery camps finish converting the harvested men into a full slave-soldier complement, and that complement backs a raid on a heavier-defended target than Calveno, using the same patrol-gap intelligence. The Passage's pursuit ships report the trail gone cold, again.

**Escalation timeline:**

| Interval | Move | Observable signal |
|---|---|---|
| Segments 1-2 | A second Midchain port loses its fighting-age men to the same night-raid pattern | A new missing-persons desk opens, run by whoever holds local authority there |
| Segments 3-4 | Captured men enter [[karath\|Karath]]'s hatchery-camp pipeline as slave-soldiers | Grung poison signature turns up on ships trading near the newly raided port |
| Segments 5-6 (fill) | The replenished complement backs a raid on a heavier target | The Passage's pursuit ships lose the trail every time |

**Possible outcomes:**

- The Uncertainty or Passage crew intercepts a [[vethka|Vethka]] mid-raid and recovers intel on the patrol-gap source.
- Unopposed, the consequence fires and a heavier target replaces Calveno.
- The crew traces back to [[simone-tabarnack|Simone Tabarnack]]'s garrison, exposing her and Jean-Claude's family tie to the crew.

**PC connection: [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]].** His sister Simone enacts the same raiding tactics he once practiced. Every settlement this front claims forces his hidden past back in front of him, worked by his own sister, narrowing any safe path home.

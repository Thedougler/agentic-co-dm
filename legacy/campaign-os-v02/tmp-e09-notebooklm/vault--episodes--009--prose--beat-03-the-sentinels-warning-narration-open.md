> [!narration]  
> _On the second morning south of [[calveno|Calveno]], something white hangs above the horizon astern._
> 
> _It keeps pace against the wind._
> 
> _The shape comes on without hail or signal, dropping out of the sun until wings, robes, and finally a familiar face resolve from the glare._
> 
> _Master Kyzil._
> 
> _He touches down at the stern with one foot on the taffrail and the other planted on empty air. For a moment he simply balances there, then steps casually onto the weatherdeck as though arriving by gangplank._
> 
> _His eyes pass over Delmar. Over the helm. Then settle on Crissdalynn._
> 
> _He crosses the deck directly to her, takes her aside, and lowers his voice until only she can hear._
> 
> _“Show no one that dreidel. Not even the Masters. I should never have given it to you.”_*

# Aruhe

Deserted, and too close to the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] reef to draw much notice. Older charts still mark it as a waypoint, with freshwater springs, a narrow beach, and garden terraces cut into the slope behind it, collapsed and overgrown now, stonework pulled apart by root and vine, untended for longer than any chart records. No one lives there now: [[grung|Grung]] patrol boats work the reefs close enough that no settlement ever took hold.

[[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] hired [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] to survey whether Aruhe could work as a Tangle anchor site. Her full brief, the five survey goals, and the phased approach are on [[warren-aruhe-survey|Warren, Aruhe Survey]]. A Grung raiding fleet also wrecked in part on Aruhe's reefs in a storm in the days after Session 07, and two survivors still shelter in the wreck. Full account on [[aruhe-wreck|The Aruhe Wreck]].

## Geography

**Extent.** Roughly two miles end to end, small enough that the Blight-claimed interior is within a morning's walk of any point on the coastal strip.

**Position.** Directly east of [[karath|Karath]], across half a mile of open channel. Close enough to swim, and close enough that Karath's cliffs stand up out of the water on a clear day.

**Topography.** A reef ring breaks the swell a half-mile out, leaving one gap and one narrow beach as the sole landing. The terraces climb the slope above that beach in cut steps, their old stonework collapsed and swallowed by root and vine, the gaps in it the only climbable way up. Above the terrace line the ground rises into tree-cover thick enough to close sight lines to a few paces, and there the Blight has made its Memorial.

[[sorn|Sorn]] traders who speak Grung say the island's name means *"hungry isle."* They won't say more, and asking further stops the talk. They know what lives there.

## Routes & Access

The reef gap off the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] is the only approach; [[grung|Grung]] patrol boats work those reefs but refuse to land. Nightfall changes the terms. Perrin's survey crew learned that staying past dark turns the Blight's corruption openly hostile, escalating from simple hazards to active corruption.

## Hazards

[[blight|The Blight]] has established a Memorial in the island's abandoned interior. The Death Bloom anchors its center, and the whole interior functions as the dungeon. The Death Bloom is a tree grown over a grave at the grove's center; the Blight's own page names who lies there.

As of World Tick 2026-05-16, the interior is fully claimed. Corruption has pushed outward from the Death Bloom through every ditch and terrace, and the Blight drives giant fauna out onto the narrow coastal strip with no outward sign visible to passing ships.

Danger rises with elevation. The harbour and beach stay safe to land on, their predators gathered but still uncorrupted. The clifftop terraces turn dangerous with giant fauna and terrace hazards as the corruption approaches. The interior itself is the Blight's dungeon outright, full corruption radiating out from the Death Bloom at its center.

The [[grung|Grung]] patrol the surrounding reefs but refuse to land on Aruhe, honoring the Blight's own dying curse. Any invader who lays a hand on Aruhe's plenty (its fruit, its river catch, its foraged growth, or its trapped game) meets Nature's fury in full, and the island takes them back into the dirt.

No Grung has set foot on Aruhe since a single survivor fled it generations ago, so their whole account of the island is that one telling, unrevised: a landing party hunted down one by one by fauna that turned on Grung scent alone. As far as any living Grung knows, Aruhe still stands exactly as it did the morning that happened.

## Ecology

Salt-scrub and thorn have swallowed the old terrace stonework, thickening into a low canopy toward the tree line. Feral terrace gardens of yams and citrus still fruit wild among the vine, worth the climb for anyone stocking up off the beach.

The [[blight|Blight]]'s corruption has rewritten everything that lives here. The island's fauna is not swollen SRD stock. These are creatures the corruption rebuilt from the ground up, each one adapted to its zone and hunting method.

[[reef-skulls|Reef skulls]] work [[aruhe-the-teeth|the Teeth]] at low tide, burying themselves in shingle with stolen skulls and hull fragments as shells. [[wolfrabbits|Wolfrabbits]] breed in warrens through the collapsed terrace stonework of [[aruhe-the-old-gardens|the Old Gardens]], launching pack ambushes from elevation. [[grinning-apes|Grinning apes]] sit the terrace canopy above them, facial muscles locked in a permanent rictus, dropping as a group on anything that moves below. [[vine-lash|Vine lash]] threads the same terrace walls, indistinguishable from ordinary jungle vine until it wraps and squeezes.

[[deer-stalkers|Deer stalkers]] hunt [[aruhe-the-quiet|the Quiet]] as solitary ambush predators, invisible when motionless, closing on isolated targets after dark. [[thornbacks|Thornbacks]] root the jungle floor solo, bristling with bone quills long enough to use as stakes. [[bear-elk|A bear-elk]] patrols the boundary between the Quiet and [[aruhe-the-rot|the Rot]] on the same route every day, blocking the only approaches between zones. [[strangler-fig|Strangler figs]] grow where the game trails funnel through gaps in the stone, engulfing anything that walks beneath.

[[silence-moths|Silence moths]] drift the deep interior at dusk and dawn, a cloud of pale moths generating a thirty-foot silence field that kills verbal spells and coordination. [[corpsewood|Corpsewoods]] walk the Rot into [[aruhe-the-hunger|the Hunger]], dead trees reanimated through the root network, patrolling routes that change with the season. [[terror-birds|Terror birds]] stalk the approach to the central grove, twelve feet tall and growing moss from their feathers, invisible at a standstill in the deep canopy.

[[giant-spider|Giant spiders]] have webbed the terrace brush, and [[blood-hawk|blood hawks]] wheel over the open ground above the beach. These are the only SRD creatures the corruption left recognizable.

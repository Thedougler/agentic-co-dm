# Roka Sten

**Wants:** build reputation as [[sparhold|Sparhold]]'s premier outfitter for [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] expeditions while growing profit from live specimen sales, but grows uncomfortable as his success increasingly entangles him in a trade that contradicts any moral calculation.

> [!read-aloud]
> A ledger slaps down on the weapons-rack counter and the whole shop seems to lean in with it. Pointed ears cut through a mop of sun-bleached hair — elvish, though nothing else about him reads it: sunburnt raw across the nose, knuckles scarred, grinning too wide for the hour. "CRIKEY, would you look at the state of you lot!" The scar pulling his left cheek tight (Grung blade, by the shape) stretches wider when he laughs, and another traces his jaw, pale and old. He's already flipping the ledger open, pen jabbing at a creature sketch with too many legs, talking faster than the pen can keep up. "Reckon I can kit you for whatever's had a go at you already, and whatever's fixing to have a proper crack at you next!" A [[grung|Grung]] poison smell lingers faintly on his cuff. He doesn't seem to notice, or mind.

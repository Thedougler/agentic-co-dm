## Geography

**Extent.** Roughly 2,600 ft. by 1,100 ft., a narrow harbor cut between two low headlands, the water itself the only open ground between them.

**Topography.** Both headlands rise gently from the waterline to about thirty feet at the ridge where each company's rig towers stand, riveted iron scaffolding streaked with rust and climbing higher wherever a company's funding holds. A tower whose funding stops doesn't come down. It just stands rusted and abandoned mid-frame, another skeleton added to a skyline that already tallies every venture that's failed here. The harbor floor between them stays low and flat, packed fill over the original wreck-strewn mud. Dockside boardwalk gives way to rutted mud track within a hundred feet of either pier, and the fill under the newer construction hasn't fully settled. A misstep off the planks sinks to the ankle. Scaffold towers and stacked crate-rows break sight across both headlands. The harbor water is the only place with a clear view from one company's dock to the other's. The harbor mouth is the only approach by water. Overland, a single cart track runs the ridge behind both compounds and dead-ends at each one's gate.

## Government

No civil authority governs Fathomrush. Each company enforces its own law within its own compound gates (Sunkline's guards answer to [[adriana-tarquine|Adriana Tarquine]], Voyvode's to [[branimir-karsovine|Branimir Karsovine]]), and neither claims the ground between them. The dockside, the boardinghouses, and the memorial wall answer to nobody.

## Trade

Fathomrush grew out of the 1488 DR Shelfworks Goldrush, when [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]]'s first [[antheri|Antheri]] salvage runs drew prospectors, scholars, and criminals to stage near the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]]'s western shelf. The easy salvage is long gone; what's left now sits deeper, harder to reach, identify, and retrieve, and the town's whole economy has narrowed to one goal: engineering pressure rigs and air-pump endurance good enough to get a crew into the mid-shelf sealed chambers and back alive.

What a crew needs from Fathomrush is simple to name and hard to get: a working deep-pressure rig with gear rated for the mid-shelf, and a crew willing to dive it. Nobody outside the two companies' own inner circles yet knows exactly what buys that access. The [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]]-backed [[sunkline-company|Sunkline Company]] and the Dravosi-backed [[voyvode-works|Voyvode Works]] both guard their trial results like state secrets, and neither is handing a serious rig to an outside crew on an afternoon's introduction. Everyone picks a side. Every boardinghouse, gear-mender, and cookhouse in town prices its rates to whichever company's crews it's currently feeding.

**Sunkline General Store** and **Voyvode General Store** sit across the harbor from each other, running the same stock list at the same prices under the same company aprons. Dockside gossip swears you can't tell [[yevgenia-sorrel|Yevgenia Sorrel]] from [[marta-kessler|Marta Kessler]] at fifty feet. Up close, the two shopkeepers can't stand each other, a personal rivalry neither company asked for and neither has bothered to stop.

## Culture

Every diver lost to a trial dive gets a name carved into the plank wall between the two piers, plain wood, no company crest cut into it, claimed by neither company. Neither Sunkline nor Voyvode paid to build it, and neither has ever tried to tear it down. It's the one thing in Fathomrush both sides maintain at their own cost.

## Defenses

Sunkline keeps its compound gate manned around the clock, more watchful of who's looking at the rig towers than who's coming off the water. Voyvode's gate stands open through working hours; Branimir prefers a stranger watch a real trial fail over guessing at what's behind his walls.

## Notable NPCs

**[[adriana-tarquine|Adriana Tarquine]]** is chief engineer of the Tessarine-backed Sunkline Company. She's rebuilt the same pressure valve four times this year and blames the alloy, never the design, every time.

**[[branimir-karsovine|Branimir Karsovine]]** is chief engineer of the Dravosi-backed Voyvode Works. He runs trial dives on his own crews personally and refuses to send anyone under a rig he hasn't tested himself first.

**[[petera-ngata|Petera Ngata]]** keeps the boardinghouse called the Last Berth, feeding and housing whichever crews can pay. She holds the only complete list of who's gone down and not come back up, and won't sell it to either company.

**[[yevgenia-sorrel|Yevgenia Sorrel]]** runs Sunkline General Store; **[[marta-kessler|Marta Kessler]]** runs Voyvode General Store directly across the water. Their stores are nearly interchangeable. Sorrel and Kessler are not, and they'd both like that clearer.

## Hooks

- A trial dive went wrong three nights ago. The memorial wall has a new name on it. The crew that lost him won't say which company's rig failed.
- Sunkline and Voyvode recruiters work the same dockside tavern, openly poaching each other's divers. Last night's shouting match ended in a real brawl.
- An independent outfit surfaced from mid-shelf depth with something neither company will name. Both compounds have started quietly asking around about the crew.
- [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] could walk away with real engineering data neither company hands over willingly: [[branimir-karsovine|Karsovine]]'s working trial notes, if she earns his trust, or whatever Sunkline's forged certificate is actually covering for, if she goes looking for it.

If ignored, the rivalry runs its own course: Sunkline's forgery ages toward discovery on its own clock, Voyvode's honest trials keep costing lives on theirs, and Fathomrush ends up run by whichever company's clock fills first.

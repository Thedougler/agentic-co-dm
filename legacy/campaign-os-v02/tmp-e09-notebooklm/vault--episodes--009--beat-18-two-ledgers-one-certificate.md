## At a Glance

- **Pattern:** a town where every real thing sits behind a gate and every gate is leverage
- **Delta:** [[branimir-karsovine|Karsovine]]'s trial notes (real engineering data on pressure at depth), Sunkline's forged mid-shelf trial certificate (existential threat to [[adriana-tarquine|Tarquine]]'s company), the dive-crew wreck-line report (this branch's [[aruhe|Aruhe]] signal, broken [[grung|Grung]] hulls and smoke on a beach in the [[karath|Karath]] channel)
- **Pressure:** the certificate's fraud and the valve's design flaw are both running out their clocks. The smoke has been burning two weeks

## Player Gravity

- Whole crew: the smoke is the captives, and [[fathomrush|Fathomrush]]'s dive fleets are the eastern means of reaching hard water
- [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]]: the trial notes are real engineering data on pressure at depth, the exact species of knowledge her flying machine needs
- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]: three divers' names and a telemetry record of why is the accounting he understands in his bones

## Situation

[[fathomrush|Fathomrush]], after arrival. [[branimir-karsovine|Karsovine]]'s trial notes are the real account of mid-shelf diving, incomplete, margin-annotated with specific failures and corrections, the names of three divers who did not come back up and what the rig telemetry said about why. Worth having to anyone who intends to do the thing, not appear to have done it. Sunkline's forged mid-shelf trial certificate sits behind a loose plank in the Last Berth's floor, in a document case [[petera-ngata|Petera Ngata]] has declined to look at since the man who left it asked her to store it. Eastern dive crews ran the wreck line from the Maw approaches two weeks ago and found, north of the usual drift, broken [[grung|Grung]] hulls on the reef approach off a small island in the [[karath|Karath]] channel, and smoke from the island's beach that should not be there. The island has a reputation. Neither company thought to share it beyond their own gates.

## Actors

- [[branimir-karsovine|Karsovine]]. **Wants:** the dive done right and his people alive. **Knows:** everything the notes hold. **Constrained by:** the notes are the only real thing he has; he does not hand real things to people who have not earned the conversation. **Without PC intervention:** dives tomorrow and adds or does not add a name to the wall.
- [[petera-ngata|Petera Ngata]]. **Wants:** her floor undisturbed and her indifference intact. **Knows:** where the case is, not what is in it. **Constrained by:** she agreed to store it; she is not curious and she is not going to look. **Without PC intervention:** the plank stays down.
- [[adriana-tarquine|Adriana Tarquine]]. **Wants:** the company protected from what the certificate would prove. **Knows:** the pressure valve fails on the design, not the alloy. She rebuilt it four times because the design is hers. **Constrained by:** the design is hers. **Without PC intervention:** the fifth rebuild.

## What Can Change

The trial notes earned (or not). Getting them requires something [[branimir-karsovine|Karsovine]] recognizes as a reason. The rescue is one. The certificate found, left, or used as an instrument. Its loss would eventually cost [[adriana-tarquine|Adriana Tarquine]] the company she rebuilt the same pressure valve four times to protect. The dive-crew report in the party's hands, the third independent signal, and with it the field's convergence: every port pointed at the same reef and beach regardless of heading. Smoke still burning after two weeks.

## Surface

- [[branimir-karsovine|Karsovine]]'s gate and the earned conversation. He reads whether someone intends to do the thing or merely appear to have done it
- The Last Berth and its floor. [[petera-ngata|Petera Ngata]]'s loose plank and the document case she agreed to store
- The dive crews' tavern circuit. The wreck-line news is ordinary shop talk; the smoke report arrives free, because nobody here thought to keep it

## Conditional Reactions

- **If the party gives Karsovine a real reason (the rescue is one):** the notes open, and so does his read of the [[karath|Karath]] channel's hard water
- **If they surface the certificate:** Sunkline's clock stops and [[adriana-tarquine|Tarquine]]'s company starts dying on schedule, with the party holding the instrument
- **If they just listen in the right tavern:** the smoke report arrives free, because nobody here thought to keep it

## Carry Forward

The Aruhe convergence complete on this branch. Whichever heading the party took, the signal has arrived. Whatever standing, notes, or leverage [[fathomrush|Fathomrush]] yielded rides toward the rescue. The Climax gate stays next-session work.

## At a Glance

- **Pattern:** the man whose business mirrors what one of them fought to leave holds the resource the party needs.
- **Delta:** [[roka-sten|Roka]]'s channel maps chart the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] approaches from the west. Reef gaps, depth soundings, current windows, and the last known positions of missing expedition teams. His knowledge replaces [[ussa|Ussa]]'s patrol intelligence with reef navigation from the opposite side.
- **Pressure:** the smoke is two weeks old. The season turns. Roka's window to run the Verdant Teeth narrows with each patrol cycle.

## Player Gravity

- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] — the chart work is hers. Roka's expedition maps become sailing directions under her hand.
- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] — the channel run needs a captain's decision. The [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] commits to waters no Crown hull has charted.
- [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] — Roka's terms include a specimen split. Living creatures from JC's homeland, taken for profit, are part of the deal.

## Situation

[[spar-and-snare-outfitters|Spar & Snare Outfitters]], the counter cleared for [[roka-sten|Roka]]'s channel charts, depth soundings in red along the reef gaps and current arrows in black, reef lines drawn thick enough to read by lantern. The [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] open across the parchment in five green shapes, and a penciled route threads west to east through [[dreth|Dreth]] and [[orak|Orak]], past [[karath|Karath]]'s patrol water, ending where the charts thin. East of the last sounding, Roka sketched one reef gap in lighter ink with [[aruhe|Aruhe]] written beside it and a single question mark below the name.

## Actors

- [[roka-sten|Roka Sten]]. **Wants:** his missing teams found, and paying clients for the run. **Knows:** the Verdant Teeth approaches from the west, reef gaps, current windows, depth soundings, and enough about [[grung|Grung]] patrol timing to avoid contact from that side. **Constrained by:** his charts end east of [[orak|Orak]]. He marked [[aruhe|Aruhe]]'s reef gap but never charted it. **Without PC intervention:** keeps running the routes he knows until the Grung close them.

## What Can Change

The party gains [[roka-sten|Roka]] as a guide through the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]]. The approach shifts from patrol evasion to reef navigation, a different risk profile. The plan commits and the terms hold.

## Surface

- The channel charts spread on the counter, reef gaps marked in red
- Roka's expedition calendar with three teams overdue
- The path from [[spar-and-snare-outfitters|Spar & Snare]] to the dock, where the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] waits

## Conditional Reactions

- **If the party negotiates hard:** Roka drops his rate. His missing teams are the real price.
- **If JC confronts the specimen trade:** Roka names the profit and the discomfort in the same breath. He has never separated them.
- **If the party shares the debris-field calculation (beat-04):** Roka's chart confirms it. The storm drove the fleet west into [[aruhe|Aruhe]]'s reef. His missing teams went east into the same water.
- **If the party asks about [[grung|Grung]] patrols:** Roka knows the timing from the west side. East of [[orak|Orak]], past [[karath|Karath]], he defers. He has not run that water.

## Carry Forward

[[roka-sten|Roka]] commits. The channel run has a plan. The approach runs from the west through the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]], not from [[karath|Karath]]'s patrol side. [[aruhe|Aruhe]]'s reef gap is the destination. The Climax gate evaluates when the reef appears ahead.

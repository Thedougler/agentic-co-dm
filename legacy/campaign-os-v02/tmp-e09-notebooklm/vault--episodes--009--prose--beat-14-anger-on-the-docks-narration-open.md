> *The fort doors have not opened since the Council went in. Nobody outside knew there was a session to shut them out of, and the crowd keeps growing anyway.*
>
> *Forty people, maybe fifty, packed onto the stone dock: coastal folk and fisher families off the reef villages, chandlery hands who watched the Pearl commission empty [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s dry docks for a fleet that came back light. Salt on their clothes, sunburn on their necks, arms crossed hard against the wait. Near the back, someone's voice carries over the [[grung|Grung]] raids, loud enough for the front rows to catch without turning around.*
>
> *The doors stay shut. More people are still coming down the causeway.*

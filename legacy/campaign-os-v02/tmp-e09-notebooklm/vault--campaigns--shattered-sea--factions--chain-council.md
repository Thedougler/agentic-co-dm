# Chain Council

![[chain-council-banner]]

*Loose island alliance defending against exposure from the Pearl of Souls heist. Each island stays sovereign, but the Council moves slowly and hits hard once enough align.*

## Members

**Membership** comes from harbourmasters, pilot elders, family heads, merchants, and shrine speakers. Each island sends whoever it trusts most.

**Islands at risk decide.** Only those with skin in the game shape the outcome. Others may speak or trade favors, but any decision that ignores those who pay the price collapses the moment they sail home.

**No formal roster.** Representation is ad hoc per matter, not a standing seat list.

## Goals & Fronts

Standing agenda: preserve island independence, coordinate on shared crises, and control [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s dry dock as neutral shipyard.

**Council commission:** a five-ship privateer fleet under Admiral [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] to steal the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] from [[sunken-crown|the Sunken Crown]] (see [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]]).

**Posed as "the Principal":** [[cosimo-verantio|Cosimo Verantio]], a [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] agent, hid behind a Council signatory.

**Council's understanding:** a deal sparing them from [[umberlee|Umberlee]]'s wrath. They never learned the real target was [[aldric-drave|Aldric Drave]]'s bloodline limits, not divine mercy.

**Confirmed in s04,** when Delmar Fisk confessed the Council's sponsorship. The destroyed fleet is the live political threat shaping four Fronts below (`vault/episodes/004/transcript.md:27`).

### Front: Denial

**Lifecycle:** active. **Trigger conditions:** evidence of the Council's role surfaces but stays private.

**Aim:** keep sponsorship unconfirmed. **Approach:** agree a closed-session line, deflect all inquiry back to it. **Off-screen:** destroy [[detto-kalash|Detto Kalash]]'s mail record so "the Principal"'s letters dead-end.

**Clock:** 4 segments, filled 0. **Consequence,** Council members blame each other and "bad intelligence" becomes official story.

**Outcomes:**

- **Surface evidence** splits the Council, forces signatory public.
- **Help bury it** leaves the Council owing them quietly.
- **Left alone** scapegoats Delmar in "bad intelligence" narrative.

**PC connection:** [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] (real, findable, easiest scapegoat).

### Front: Tessarine Containment

**Lifecycle:** active. **Trigger conditions:** a Tessarine agent learns someone has evidence.

**Aim:** protect Cosimo Verantio's cover. **Approach:** call in debt on Council signatories to buy silence. **Off-screen:** factor buys out or re-terms whoever asks about [[detto-kalash|Detto Kalash]]'s mail route.

**Clock:** 6 segments, filled 0. **Consequence:** pressure spreads island-by-island through inducements and threats before next meeting.

**Outcomes:**

- **Reach Cosimo first** exposes [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] hand and [[aldric-drave|Aldric Drave]] as real target.
- **Left alone** leaves Council ignorant of who was truly behind it.
- **Signatory takes deal** gives the Concordat an obligated vote unseen.

**PC connection:** [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] (Tessarine pressure squeezes his community unnamed).

### Front: Midchain Anger

**Lifecycle:** active. **Trigger conditions:** coastal settlements learn Council spent on Pearl while [[grung|Grung]] raids worsened.

**Aim:** contain anger to grumbling. **Approach:** send patrols or grain to loudest protests. **Off-screen:** divert [[the-quorum|The Quorum]] from reef patrol to one settlement, abandoning another.

**Clock:** 4 segments, filled 0. **Consequence:** [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] crisis becomes legitimacy crisis.

**Outcomes:**

- **Help raided settlement** gives [[uncertainty|the Uncertainty]]'s crew unearned time.
- **Left alone** fires the consequence.
- **Publicize Pearl spending** collapses Council's relief strategy.

**PC connection:** [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] (fled [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]]; sister [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]] leads raiders, blocks his path home).

The Council's exposure to [[umberlee|Umberlee]]'s divine debt — whether a [[waveservants|Waveservant]] shrine learns they commissioned the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl]] theft — is tracked under [[the-red-wake|The Red Wake]]. Currently raising [[kalowe|Kalowe]] shrine tribute to buy silence.

> [!narration]
> *Fog holds still over the island to starboard, thick enough to swallow the shoreline, untouched by a wind that fills the sails and bends the pennants flat. The swell rolls the same as always, gulls still ride the updraft off the bow, and the fog does not move at all.*
>
> *[[old-faas|Old Faas]] names it without looking up from his splice. [[yssenmoor|Yssenmoor]]. The last crew that anchored there, he says, rowed off before their watering party made it back.*
>
> *A voice reaches the deck from somewhere past the fog line, patient and formal, reciting the same lines to water that never answers. The words arrive whole and first. Whoever is speaking them stays out of sight.*
>
> *In [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn's]] pouch, the [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]] warms against her hip, low and steady. Nothing else on her carries any heat at all.*

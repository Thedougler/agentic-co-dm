> _The betting ring has swallowed the yard outside the watch hall. Sparhold crowds three-deep against the rope, dockhands balanced on crates, fishwives shouldering past chandlers, coin already changing hands along the line. The hush left by the third bell still hangs over them._
> 
> _Across the packed dirt, the two champions face one another._
> 
> _[[nico-renzetti|Nico Renzetti]] stands straight and rigid, blade at his hip, jaw set, colour high along his neck. His eyes stay fixed on the dirt between them._
> 
> _[[rangi-kailani|Rangi Kailani]] waits opposite him, loose through the shoulders, weight easy on both feet._
> 
> _His eyes never leave Nico._
> 
> _Behind the watch hall, a hand settles on the starting bell._*

## Geography

**Extent.** Roughly 1,200 ft. end to end, a squat ring of stone walls hard against the harbor mouth, short enough to cross the whole town in a few minutes at a walk.

**Topography.** The walls climb the slope above the anchorage in three stepped tiers, each one a killing ground for the tier below it if the outer wall ever falls. Streets run switchback, stone underfoot everywhere. Lashing binds every board tight against fire arrows. The overlapping stockades cut sightlines block to block, so nobody sees more than one street at a time. The watchfires stay lit on every corner instead, punching through the maze. One landward gate, barred and manned around the clock, and the harbor mouth itself, narrow enough that a single hull passes under crossbow range from both walls.

## Government

Rule belongs to whichever family currently leads Sparhold's defense, not to fixed inheritance: a family holds the settlement only as long as it can defend it. Any rival family may ring the challenge bell. The challenge itself runs as a duel to the death between each family's elected champion, fought in the betting ring outside the watch hall, the same ground where Sparhold settles its wagers. The winner takes the claim whole, and the loser's family drops out of contention entirely. The strip's villages stay fiercely individualistic regardless, deferring no further than the current watch requires. House Kailani holds Sparhold now, under Toa Kailani; [[house-renzetti|House Renzetti]], under Ottavia Renzetti, has just rung the bell against them.

## Trade

Sparhold openly competes with Kalowe for shipping trade and loses on reputation, so it wins instead on price and looser rules, openly corrupt for the right coin in a way Kalowe's nine-vote Council can't easily manage.

Its one real edge over Kalowe is proximity: it sits nearer the Verdant Teeth than Kalowe does. Its signature export is spar-timber, rare shipbuilding wood cut from the fringe of the Teeth's forest, perilous to harvest, which is where the settlement takes its name. The timber yards below the second tier stack the spar-cut logs waiting on buyers.

## Culture

A weathered shrine corner past the fish stalls is still tended by a widow. She's old enough to remember the fishing village that stood on this shore before the walls went up.

## Defenses

Sparhold exists because the strip's scattered, fiercely individualistic cove villages found themselves squeezed at once: [[grung-clans|Grung]] raids climbed up from the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] to the south, and [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] patrol lanes ran open water on the [[central-strait|Central Strait]] to the north, leaving nowhere left to hide from either. No single village could hold both fronts alone, so the strip built one walled market everyone could retreat behind.

It has never stopped bracing for the siege that never arrives, and nearly everything here bends toward war. Market stalls fold flat into barricades at the first bell, and the tavern loft is a watch post as much as a taproom. Deals close fast. Everyone stays ready to drop what they're doing and grab a spear. Squat stone tiers and overlapping stockades give Sparhold the visual opposite of [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s sprawling bridge-linked islands.

## Notable NPCs

**[[toa-kailani|Toa Kailani]]** holds House Kailani's claim to Sparhold's rule as long as the watch answers to him. He keeps a tally of every family's watch-shift failures, ready to use them if the bell ever rings again.

**[[ottavia-renzetti|Ottavia Renzetti]]** leads House Renzetti, the family that has just rung the challenge bell. She pushed her own family's champion forward before he could refuse.

**[[petro-amaru|Petro Amaru]]** runs Sparhold's harvest foreman crews, driving them closer to [[grung|Grung]] ground every season. He keeps two separate ledgers: one for the harbor books, one for himself.

**[[ussa|Ussa]]** is the boundary scout Amaru bribes to misreport the cut-line. A low-caste Grung defector, she spends the bribe money to buy freedom for kin still enslaved under the clans.

**[[rangi-kailani|Rangi Kailani]]** is House Kailani's elected champion, defending Sparhold's rule against House Renzetti's challenge in the death-duel ring.

**[[nico-renzetti|Nico Renzetti]]** is House Renzetti's reluctant champion in the Challenge Bell duel, pressed into the fight by family honor against his private doubts.

## Hooks

- **Cooperation goal:** Sparhold's cooperation means real intel, safe harbor, and backing against the worsening Verdant Teeth raids for the crew of the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]. Whichever family holds Sparhold when the crew needs an answer decides it, along with the timber-harvest foreman whose crews are the raids' unproven real cause.
- **The Challenge Bell:** the watch bell tolls three times as the crew's hull ties up, and a runner posts the challenge notice to the market slate in plain sight of the dock. This lands squarely on [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]'s reflex to weigh in as a naval authority the moment two crews start choosing sides in public.
- House Renzetti has just rung the bell against House Kailani, and [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]]'s read on a room matters here. In a real death-duel between elected champions, misjudging a champion's readiness gets someone killed for the wrong reason, and Sparhold's watch bell tolls the hour down with neither family backing off it once it's rung.
- [[nico-renzetti|Nico Renzetti]] took up the challenge under family pressure and shows every private sign of a man who doesn't want the fight. Backing "the stronger family" isn't a clean call while coercion pushes one side's own fighter toward his death. Left alone, the duel runs with no outside eyes on it: the coerced champion dies to a mismatch nobody flagged, House Kailani keeps its rule by default, and Sparhold's rulers remember exactly who stood by while a man died for his family's pride. Sparhold's cooperation starts hostile.
- **The Green Cut:** a timber-crew argument spills into the street over a cut-line dispute, a foreman's ledger dropped and snatched back before anyone else can read it. The mismatch between the ledger and the true cut-lines is exactly what [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]]'s mapping eye catches before anyone explains what she's looking at.
- Sparhold's timber crews cut spar-wood technically outside Grung-claimed ground, but the cut-line has drifted closer to the boundary every season. Proving or disproving that this is what's provoking the [[grung-npc|Grung]] raids rests with Crissdalynn's mapping eye, which reads the real cut-lines against what the harbor ledger claims. It also rests with [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]]'s standing, and risk, as it opens a door to the harvest foreman, who has been paying a boundary scout to look the other way.
- The bribed boundary scout is himself a low-caste Grung defector, spending the bribe to buy freedom for kin still enslaved under the clans. Exposing the bribe risks exposing him, which lands the hook on Jean-Claude's own mission to end slavery. Left alone, the crews keep cutting closer to the line, and raids keep climbing exactly as [[midchain|The Midchain]]'s existing hook already tracks. Sparhold's own trade eventually collapses under Grung retaliation once the crew of the Uncertainty has left the problem to fester.
- Resolved, the Challenge Bell earns the crew real standing with whichever family now runs Sparhold, shaping how much risk that family takes exposing the Green Cut once it comes to light. Exposed, the Green Cut hands the winning family a choice: bury it to protect the timber trade, or act on it and eat the loss.
- Ignore both, and Sparhold's cooperation collapses outright: the raids escalate per The Midchain's existing hook, and the crews' guilt answers itself with nobody outside Sparhold ever hearing it from the crew of the Uncertainty.
- **The Axolotl Contact:** [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] wants a live axolotl for her research, and [[roka-sten|Roka Sten]] is exactly the man who can get her one. His [[spar-and-snare-outfitters|Spar & Snare]] expedition teams have logged them before, in the flooded channel between [[dreth|Dreth]] and [[orak|Orak]]. He'll guide her there himself for his usual rate and a specimen split, bellowing the whole crossing about every creature that's ever tried to eat one of his clients — the same manic delight he brings to every life his ledger turns into inventory for [[the-live-hold|The Live Hold]].

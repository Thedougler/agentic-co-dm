> [!narration]
> *The reef gap narrows into dusk, the Waveservant Shrine's causeway lamp the only light at the channel's throat. The [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] slides through on the evening tide, her wake hissing low against the pilings, hands already at the bollards before her lines are across.*
>
> *Dock labor out of [[calveno|Calveno]] and chandlery hands from the [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca's]] victualing know [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s face on sight. They call his name while the hull is still swinging in.*
>
> *One man does not call out. He stands apart at the far end of the quay, hands clean of any dock work, and he has held that spot since noon, since the causeway lookout first marked their sail coming up the strait.*
>
> *He waits for the gangway. Then he steps forward.*

> [!narration]  
> _Your purple fist is slick with saltwater, clenched around a spear you have not let go of since the hull broke apart on [[aruhe|Aruhe]]'s reef._
> 
> _The storm caught the [[grung|Grung]] fleet two days out of [[calveno|Calveno]] and drove you west until coral ripped the ships open beneath you. Somehow, you made the beach._
> 
> _Two guards dragged themselves from the wreckage with you. Purple, like you, but from another garrison. One bears dark bars across his shoulders. The other, pale spots along her throat. Behind you, captives from the hold huddle beneath the broken ribs of an overturned hull._
> 
> _One has wandered away. An old man with a grey beard, picking through the brush for anything edible._
> 
> _Then you notice the mangroves._
> 
> _Too tall. Too thick._
> 
> _Your heart stops._
> 
> _You know this island._
> 
> _Every Grung knows this island._
> 
> _The Hungry Isle._
> 
> _Generations ago, a landing party came ashore here._
> 
> _None returned._
> 
> _No Grung has willingly set foot on Aruhe since._
> 
> _Even the air feels wrong. Thick and sweet, cloying in the throat, like fruit left too long in the sun._
> 
> _At the edge of the brush, the old man finds something. A berry nearly the size of a peach. He plucks it free and squeezes._
> 
> _The skin splits in his fist._
> 
> _A grey blur explodes from the undergrowth. Low and Fast, catching him by both ankles._
> 
> _He hits the sand face-first already being dragged backward into the green, fingers clawing desperate furrows through the sand._
> 
> _Then comes a wet crunch._
> 
> _Silence._
> 
> _Shadows gather beneath the canopy. There wolf sized silhouettes broken by there stripes._
> 
> _The Pack leader steps forward._
> 
> _For half a heartbeat, your mind tells you rabbit._
> 
> _Then you see the shoulders._
> 
> _Muscle rolls beneath its hide. Claws spread into the sand. Its red eyes settle on you, and fresh blood darkens the fur around its mouth._
> 
> _A second drops from the terrace above._
> 
> _Then a third._
> 
> _They spread out without a sound, fanning across the beach between the jungle and the surf._
> 
> _Every red eye fixes on the three of you._
> 
> _The barred spearman lowers his stance._
> 
> _The spotted guard is already looking south, toward the narrow channel where [[karath|Karath]] rises dark across half a mile of open water._
> 
> _Behind you are the wreck, the wounded, and the captives._
> 
> _Ahead of you, the Hungry Isle has begun to feed._
> 
> _**What do you do?**_

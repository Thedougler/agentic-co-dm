> [!narration]  
> _The first exchange takes three breaths._
> 
> _[[nico-renzetti|Nico]] opens with clean form, a [[guard|Guard]] his trainers would approve of. [[rangi-kailani|Rangi]] reads it, steps inside, and never seems to hurry._
> 
> _Steel meets once. Twice. A third strike rings off the watch hall stone._
> 
> _No blood._
> 
> _The crowd goes tight and quiet._
> 
> _By the third minute, the difference is obvious._
> 
> _Nico fights the pattern he practiced. Rangi fights the man in front of him._
> 
> _Every time Nico resets his Guard, Rangi is already somewhere it no longer protects. Nico’s blade cuts through spaces Rangi occupied half a heartbeat before._
> 
> _You can see it most clearly in their feet._
> 
> _Nico’s keep searching for the edge of the ring._
> 
> _Rangi’s keep searching for Nico._

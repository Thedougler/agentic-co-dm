> [!narration]
> *[[fathomrush|Fathomrush]] rises off the bow as two iron skylines, and neither one finished. The Voyvode headland's scaffold tower climbs three levels then stops, its top two frames bare girders against the evening light. Across the water the other tower keeps climbing, rivets still bright. Between them the harbor keeps its evening trade going anyway, pitch and hot iron and brine gone sour with the tide.*
>
> *At the waterline between the two piers stands the memorial wall, plain wood, no company crest, claimed by neither side. Names cut into the face in close rows, more since anyone in the crew's circuit last came through. The newest is still pale, raw wood showing through the chisel strokes. A week old, maybe less.*

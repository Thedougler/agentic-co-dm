# The Green Cut Ledger

## At a Glance

- **Pattern:** a public accident exposes a private ledger.
- **Delta:** the ledger's marked cut-line does not match the coast. [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]], who knows every coast by sight, reads the gap at a glance: inland by too much for rounding error, too consistent for two surveyors measuring different things. Someone paid a surveyor to measure the wrong thing. Underneath it: [[ussa|Ussa]], a low-caste [[grung|Grung]] defector, has been taking bribe money and spending it buying freedom for kin still enslaved under the clans. She knows what surfaces if the bribe comes to light, weighed it, and kept taking it.
- **Pressure:** the timber men know someone read the pages. Whoever holds the Green Cut holds the timber yards, and the duel just decided who holds the question.

## Player Gravity

- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]], the navigator's eye that catches the fraud
- [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]], the Human architecture of exploitation. A Grung defector buying kin free is the hard question his whole arc asks
- Whole crew. Ussa is the channel knowledge the rescue needs (beat-10), and what they do with her secret decides whether she trusts them

## Situation

[[sparhold|Sparhold]], the morning after the duel (or whenever the street provides): a crew argument between two of [[petro-amaru|Petro Amaru]]'s timber men gets loud outside the chandlery. One throws a ledger down. The other snatches it back, too fast to stop whoever is nearby from seeing the pages that fall open on the stone.

## Actors

- [[petro-amaru|Petro Amaru]]. **Wants:** the Green Cut question settled his way under whichever house won. **Knows:** what the ledger buys and who paid. **Constrained by:** two careless crewmen and a public street. **Without PC intervention:** the ledger disappears back into the yards and the fraud proceeds.
- [[ussa|Ussa]]. **Wants:** her kin free, her arrangement unexamined. **Knows:** everything the bribe touches, and [[karath|Karath]]'s patrol water. **Constrained by:** what exposure costs a defector. **Without PC intervention:** keeps taking the money.

## What Can Change

The party learns the fraud exists and that Ussa is inside it; leverage over Amaru's operation; Ussa's calculation getting the hearing it deserves before anyone else decides what to do with it.

## Surface

The street scene; the timber men (worldview: cornered); Amaru's yard; Ussa findable at the harbor's low quarter.

## Conditional Reactions

- **If the party presses the timber men:** the ledger vanishes and Amaru learns who asked.
- **If they bring it to the winning house:** the timber question becomes that house's opening move.
- **If they go to Ussa first:** her side of it, and what the money bought, before the town's version exists.
- **If they let it lie:** the fraud proceeds and Ussa's arrangement holds until it doesn't.

## Carry Forward

Leverage into beat-10. How the party handled Ussa's secret is the price of the channel.

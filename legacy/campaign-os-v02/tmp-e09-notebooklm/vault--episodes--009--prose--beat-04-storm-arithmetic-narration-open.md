> [!narration]  
> _Three days south of [[calveno|Calveno]], the [[central-strait|Central Strait]] begins giving back what the storm took._
> 
> _A plank surfaces off the port bow, narrow and resin-sealed. [[grung|Grung]] construction. Then another. Then more, scattered across the swell. A broken outrigger rolls among them, rope still knotted around the strut in captive lashings._
> 
> _Then someone shouts._
> 
> _Human. Hoarse. Barely a voice at all, just the raw sound of a throat that has been calling for help too long._
> 
> _Two more answer from the wreckage ahead._
> 
> _Thirty yards out, three young men cling to what remains of a shattered hull. Calveno, by their clothes. One has both hands rubbed raw and white with salt. Another has a wound above his ear, dark with blood that has dried, split open, and dried again._

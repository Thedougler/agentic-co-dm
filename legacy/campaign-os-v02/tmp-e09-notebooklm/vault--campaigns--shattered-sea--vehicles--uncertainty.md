Registry number CS-1147, laid down for the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] and worked out of [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]] under Rook's commission, built to stop and search, never to fight. Nothing on her says so now.

The plate came off her bow at [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]] and went into the fire, and its iron ended up in the fitting that holds her ram. A carver cut her stern back and re-lettered it in Calveno script. Her pennants sit folded in a locker nobody opens, her charts carry a second hand's markings, and a yard boy scraped every stamped mark off her interior that a boarding officer would think to look for.

Her first refit gave her the ram, the shallow keel, and a brown-black that hid her by day. The second buried her by night. [[dario-vanni|Dario Vanni]] mixed the blue-black, three days tacky, over fresh caulk running her whole length. [[sem-holst|Sem Holst]] found five soft feet of forward keel where a Dravosi yard had caulked across the rot instead of cutting it out, and that timber came out too. [[cobb|Cobb]] did the work in [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]]'s basin and would not float her early for anybody.

A [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] carver replaced the figurehead the storm took. She is a woman mid-stride, open-handed, [[calveno|Calveno]]-featured, with neither hope nor fear cut into her. Nobody has named her.

Paint and a forged registry fool a port official at a glance, and never fool a Dravosi officer who served in cutters. The hull shape is the tell, and no yard repaints that. [[dario-vanni|Dario Vanni]] has painted this hull class twice before, for other people, and he talks when nobody pays him attention.

Aft, where the captain's cabin was, there is now a long table, a bulkhead bench, and the [[fleet-commanders-chair|fleet commander's chair]] recovered from the Calveno crater. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] bunks forward with the crew.

The Crown's search for her runs on its own clock, three phases ending in [[commander-gideon-ault|Commander Gideon Ault]], and belongs to [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]]'s standing [[front|Front]]. What moves that clock is this vessel's business. [[central-strait|Central Strait]] and [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] water speeds it, [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] walking openly through a Crown port speeds it, and any sailor off the eastern waypoint patrol who knew her speeds it. Distance is the only brake. [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]] already testified at [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]], and the notice that went out from it describes a grey ship with a white stripe.

Quieter searches run alongside it. [[verity-hollowell|Verity Hollowell]] works Hollowell accounts because [[geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey Draves]] sits on a manifest she signed, and she has reported nothing yet. [[rupert-knighton|Rupert Knighton]] is not looking at all, because nobody has told him his son died aboard her.

[[estratto|Estratto]] holds a Tessarine writ against the hull, covering a salvage agreement Rook signed before he lost her. By the [[tessarine-concordat|Concordat]]'s reading, this crew hold collateral they have no right to.

**Ram bow [HB].** Doubled planking, iron-pin cross-bracing, and an iron-capped plate flush at the waterline. Nobody watching the approach misreads the intent.

> [!mechanic]
> **Ram.** Helmsman's action at Boarding range, after at least 20 ft in a straight line. Both hulls take 2d10 bludgeoning and the *Uncertainty* takes half. A target of Tier 1 or smaller makes a DC 13 [[strength|Strength]] save or takes a 15-ft shove with its next turn's movement halved. Both vessels stop dead, and the helm cannot act again until its next turn.

**Shallow keel [HB].** Eighteen inches off the keel dropped her draw from eight feet to four. River mouths, tidal flats, lagoon approaches, and Calveno's inner canals all open to her, and a standard cutter drawing eight cannot follow her in. The helm rolls [[dexterity|Dexterity]] with advantage in reef channels and shallow coastal water. She gives up five miles a day in poor wind for it.

**The battery [HB].** Her long nines reach, and her short thirty-twos have no reach at all and need none. At Cannon range only the long guns bear, d8 hull damage each, which will annoy a warship and sink nothing. At Boarding range every gun bears and the thirty-twos deal 2d8 apiece, weight enough to open a Tier 1 hull in two salvos.

This is a boarder's battery bolted to a hull that cannot survive a gunnery duel. Everything about it rewards closing. A thousand gold of that armament sits unpaid, signed for on the ship's credit against the Black-Jaw account in [[thunk|Thunk]]'s own hand, twice, one signature upside down. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] has not raised it once.

## Crew

She sails on four hands and fights on ten, and the battery wants every one of the ten.

- Captain: [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]], PC.
- Bosun: [[old-faas|Old Faas]], rated.
- Gunner: [[thunk|Mr. Thunk]], counts as two crew at a weapon.
- Carpenter: [[sem-holst|Sem Holst]], promoted off the first refit.
- Carpenter's apprentice: [[geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey Draves]], came over from Rook's crew.
- Navigator: open. [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] covers known routes only.
- Cook: open. The cheapest gap aboard, and still nobody fills it.

Rook commanded her with eight privateer ratings whose loyalty ran to his pay. [[capn-gorgeous|Cap'n Gorgeous]] enforced for him and died below decks. [[alys-kuiper|Alys Kuiper]] served as his surgeon and never came back from shore leave in Calveno. [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] left at Calveno as arranged.

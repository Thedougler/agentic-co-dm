## Overview

![[crissdalynn-khinriss-gallery#Portraits]]

Fourteen years old, she sports blue-black iridescent feathers and geometric armor crafted from leather, carries a chart satchel, and her talons and wings make her instantly recognizable. She's irreverent and light until the fight starts. In the first three seconds of combat she assesses her friends, exit routes, and weak points, all without thinking.

She's an [[aarakocra|Aarakocra]] (with racial access to [[gust-of-wind|Gust of Wind]]) and a [[monk|Monk]] of the [[monk-kensei|Kensei tradition]], raised out of an [[acolyte|Acolyte]] / cartographer background. She trained at the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]], the Kensei monastic order headquartered at [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]], under her mentor [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]], that order's current senior master. Her faith runs to [[syranita|Syranita]] and [[aerdrie-faenya|Aerdrie Faenya]] at the margins, with [[remnis|Remnis]] as her primary god, more habit than devotion. She lacks darkvision. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]'s darkness hides him but leaves her blind and disadvantaged, so the crew has to coordinate light around her.

Delmar Fisk is her fixed point. His opinion matters most, and if someone threatens him, everything stops. She pulled him from the crow's nest as the [[red-lady|Red Lady]] closed and flew them both through thirty feet of solid water.

## Backstory

She was mapping [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]] for [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] when the [[red-lady|Red Lady]] sank. The order had cast her out at 14 for goofing off, not for malice but for careless craft. Mapping the Maw became her price to return.

Exile lasts until that task ends. She served the Red Lady for twenty-two months before it went down.

The mapping is incomplete. She knows.

**The wound.** Exile makes the wreck matter. The ship isn't her wound. The exile is.

She seeks Kyzil's approval, wants to finish mapping, and craves the monastery's forgiveness. Underneath it all: she wants home. She believes shiny things hold value. She doesn't abandon loyalty.

## Arc Notes (DM Only)

Her pilgrimage isn't formally complete. Kyzil's standard is exacting: surviving the sinking isn't the same as mapping the Maw in a storm.

He's confirmed that surviving counts and delivered [[high-eyrie|the High Eyrie]]'s weather warning, but he hasn't asked about the two missing Sentinels yet. He wants her full records first. Progress comes through her full account, learning what happened to the Sentinels, or firsthand Maw knowledge that unlocks the Eyrie's fissure cycle.

At the table she's a mobile aerial striker who uses height as power.

She's in her spotlight in open spaces, against exposed casters, downed enemies, and risky saves. Height matters most. Pressure her with ranged foes, wind, saves, nets, and low ceilings, mixed into encounters instead of run alone. If she's struggling, add height, air, or ground enemies first; if she's dominating, add ranged fire, saves, wind, and enclosed spaces while keeping flight useful. The dramatic lever that moves her fastest is reassurance and trust: make her choose between her own safety and protecting Delmar.

At level 5 she spikes. Extra Attack and Agile Parry let her land up to three attacks at AC 18 when well positioned.

> [!mechanic]
> **Stunning Strike + Dragonhide Belt.** Failed DC 15 CON save: shifts her from striker to mobile controller.

Her combat value peaks when the crew can lean on her action economy and forced saves against grouped foes.

The Drowned Maw is a high-value convergence point to prep around: [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]], her own incomplete pilgrimage, and [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s [[leviathan|Leviathan]] encounter all trace back to it.

Her own control depends on action economy and positioning to pin key threats while the crew focuses fire. She's the crew's established [[grappler|grappler]], having grappled CR 14 Kyzil round one of the session 04 spar on his natural 1. Enemies need [[freedom-of-movement|Freedom of Movement]] or high contested Athletics to resist her; one grappler per encounter is fair, two is oppressive.

Terrain decides whether that control gets to matter. Repeating anti-flight rooms with low ceilings deny her core trick, so the constraint needs variety. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s boots of flying mean two airborne PCs share the crew, which trivializes static encounters. Multi-axis terrain across vertical, horizontal, and water axes rewards the whole crew instead.

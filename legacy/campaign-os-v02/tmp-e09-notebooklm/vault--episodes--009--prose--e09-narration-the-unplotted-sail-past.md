> [!narration]
> *The [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] holds her southern line, and dead ahead the strait between [[sparhold|Sparhold]] and [[kwoss|Kwoss]] isn't open water anymore. A green hill rises where the chart says there's nothing, a bay opening on its southern face. Wrecks stand bow-in above the tideline in that bay, four of them, none holed, one still carrying both masts.*
>
> *[[master-kyzil|Kyzil]] checks the chart against the horizon, then checks again. The chart shows open water. The mate's older notes agree. He folds it away without comment.*
>
> *Wind off the hill carries the smell of green growing things and warm stone, the only scent on the crossing that isn't salt. The Uncertainty passes within a cable length, and the water inside the bay's rock arms doesn't move at all. Gulls circle the headland in loose rings, and not one comes down to land.*

> [!narration]
> *Second leg through the [[the-drowned-maw|Maw-lanes]], and the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] closes on a hull riding broadside across the lane, close enough to hail. Nothing moves on her deck. The rigging hangs straight, every line coiled, every hatch seated, the cargo hatch dogged shut but not locked, the order a working crew keeps, except no one answers the hail. She is fresh enough that the tar still smells warm where the sun has worked the deck seams, and nothing aboard has shifted or fallen. Her boat still hangs on its davits. Everything rests where hands set it down before stepping below, and no one has come back up.*

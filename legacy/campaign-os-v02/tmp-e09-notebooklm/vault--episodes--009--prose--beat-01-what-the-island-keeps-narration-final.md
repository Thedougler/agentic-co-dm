> [!narration]  
> _The last wolfrabbit turns from the water._
> 
> _It pads back toward the terrace warrens without hurry, muzzle dark with blood._
> 
> _Behind it, the tide goes to work._
> 
> _Foam washes over footprints. Drag marks soften. Blood thins pink in the shallows until the beach is flat, wet sand again._
> 
> _Out on the reef, the broken hull of the [[vethka|Vethka]] lies wedged against the coral, throwing a narrow triangle of shade._
> 
> _Beneath it, [[agata|Agata]] sits with her grey braid over one shoulder, coiling sail-thread through steady hands._
> 
> _Beside her, [[bettina|Bettina]] has not taken her eyes off the gap in the reef._*

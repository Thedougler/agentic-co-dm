## At a Glance

- **Pattern:** rescuers! A rescue that hands the rescuers a bigger question
- **Delta:** the survivors' testimony plus the debris field's own navigation. Storm came out of the east, drove the [[grung|Grung]] fleet west before it broke. The fleet was carrying captives south when it caught them. The survivors know nothing of any island. From storm direction, a debris field drifting too far south for [[karath|Karath]] water, and two days of drift, the calculation resolves to [[aruhe|Aruhe]], the island nobody lands on: west of Karath, east of [[amberfall|Amberfall]], in the reef approach off [[verdant-teeth|the Verdant Teeth]], one narrow gap through the reef. First of three independent signals pointing at Aruhe (the destination ports carry the second and third).
- **Pressure:** survivors dying of exposure force the stop. The field itself is the evidence

## Player Gravity

- Whole crew, because the [[calveno|Calveno]] captives aboard the [[grung|Grung]] fleet are their trail, physically
- [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]], whose strategic read of Grung logistics does the arithmetic
- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]], the navigator who resolves the drift calculation

## Situation

Three days south of [[calveno|Calveno]], any southern heading crosses it. First a narrow hull plank with resin-sealed joinery, [[grung|Grung]] raiding construction, not merchant. Boards follow, a broken outrigger strut, rope with captive lashings still knotted. Then a Human voice. Calveno men cling to debris, fighting age, the profile the Grung raids take. A day in the water, one with salt sores and a head wound ("the hull went").

Fixed arithmetic from [[aruhe-wreck|the Aruhe wreck]]: 487 people left Calveno in the Grung fleet. 290 reached Karath. The rest are storm arithmetic and this debris field.

## Actors

- Survivors. **Wants:** land, water, their kin's names read from any list. **Knows:** the storm, nothing of islands. [[grung-clans|Grung]] took them from [[calveno|Calveno]] with the others, roped at the hip, loaded onto boats heading south. **Constrained by:** exposure, one man's head wound. **Without PC intervention:** dead within a day.

## What Can Change

- Three lives
- The Aruhe calculation entering the party's hands (west of Karath, east of Amberfall, reef approach off the Verdant Teeth, one narrow gap)
- A heading decision gaining a fourth candidate nobody charts

## Surface

- Rescue and treatment of the survivors
- The survivors' account: what they saw and where the storm drove the fleet, how the Grung loaded them and how many boats they counted
- The debris itself, [[grung|Grung]] raiding-hull construction and cut captive lashings
- The chart work: storm direction, drift, and the numbers that resolve to Aruhe

## Conditional Reactions

- **If they run the calculation:** Aruhe resolves as stated. West of Karath, east of Amberfall, reef approach off the Verdant Teeth, one narrow gap through the reef
- **If they press the survivors about Aruhe:** flat blankness. The men know nothing of the island, only that Grung took them south toward [[karath|Karath]]
- **If they sail on without stopping:** the voices fall astern. The field disperses and the survivors die on the water. The Aruhe calculation waits for a port's second-hand report instead

## Carry Forward

The calculation makes Aruhe the rescue's standing candidate. Freed captives aboard need a port, so every destination branch inherits them.

## Situation

Morning of day 5, main channel. A Knight Squadron hull running inspection colours crosses the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]'s bow with enough deliberation that the choice is heave to or take fire. A military gig brings two men who do not match: [[corvin-knighton|Corvin Knighton]], a family's face on a Crown mandate, deliberate bearing, and [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]], an inquisitor folded into a coat, reading the deck for who confesses and who fights first.

**Malone's stated purpose:** intelligence on [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]], the healer-heretic working south into [[midchain|Midchain]] lanes. He asks whether the crew encountered Grigori, whether they hold any account of his movements. He asks the last question twice, then watches the silence after it. **The unstated pressure:** Corvin's eye went to the bow shape on arrival and has not entirely left it. The Uncertainty's hull line is a [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi]] cutter's, and Corvin grew up around that hull line. He says nothing about it.

DM truth: Corvin knows [[capn-gorgeous|Cap'n Gorgeous]] is dead and keeps it private, because a living heir close to the succession is one position and a grieving heir another. He is not hunting the killers, but he noticed the hull and will not say either aloud. Malone attacks only if the crew defends, agrees with, or shields Grigori; his interrogation is otherwise light and precise.

## Player Gravity

- Whole crew: the Uncertainty IS the captured Dravosi cutter, and exposure is existential
- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]: on record as the fleet's admiral, a name the Crown could weigh
- [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]: Malone's dormant thread waits on the Crown confirming Perrin's pact as heresy. Standing on a deck under an inquisitor's read is standing next to that trigger

## Active Force

- **Intent:** Malone wants Grigori's trail; the Crown wants the strait inspected
- **Means:** a Knight Squadron hull's guns; an inquisitor's authority to detain on suspicion
- **Limits:** one hull, an inspection mandate and no warrant. Corvin's presence keeps the shape of the thing at an inspection. Malone holds ecclesiastical authority only

## Pressure

- **Now:** the gig is aboard, questions running
- **Next:** a pause reading as thought invites the second look
- **Default trajectory:** a straight answer received once, the gig re-crosses. The Crown hull falls off downwind

## Open Vectors

Prep coverage for the DM.

- **Straight answers:** dry dock a week, [[calveno|Calveno]] affairs, nothing south. True and enough
- **Working Corvin:** the succession angle, the hull left unsaid. Does-he-know tension, Inglourious Basterds opening. The party can defuse it, or learn what Corvin's silence costs and why
- **Curiosity about Grigori:** Malone gives the hunt's shape. Thread [[aleksander-malone|The Hound of God]] is already decided, no Crown confirmation needed
- **Provocation:** see Escalation

## Escalation

1. Questions. Malone's stated inspection, conversational and precise
2. The repeated question and the watched silence. Malone reads the crew's composure
3. Malone names a detainable suspicion. The inspection's shape changes
4. The Knight Squadron hull's guns are the argument nobody wins

## Fallout

- **Success:** clean inspection, and whatever the party read of Corvin rides with them
- **Complication:** Malone leaves satisfied but marks a name or a pause worth remembering
- **Setback:** a detention search of a Dravosi-built hull under Calveno registry
- **Bypass:** running the bow-cross means taking fire from a hull they cannot outgun in open water

> [!narration]  
> _Eleven minutes after the lookout’s first hail, the [[calveno|Calveno]] crew is already standing to loaded guns along both rails._
> 
> _Off the port quarter, the stranger has spent every one of those minutes running dark and closing along the same wind. Patched tan canvas. A tired brown hull. The sort of vessel that ought to smell of fish, wet rope, and old bilge._
> 
> _Then the false pennant drops._
> 
> _Black rises in its place._
> 
> _Gunports snap open down the rail, one after another. Behind the bulwark, boarding hooks lift out of the dark._
> 
> _Fifty yards._
> 
> _She has committed to the angle._
> 
> _And every loaded gun aboard the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] bears._

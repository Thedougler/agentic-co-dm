# Spar & Snare Outfitters

> [!read-aloud]
> Pale sunlight pours through tall windows overlooking the harbor, the frames still heavy with morning salt-spray. Leather armor hangs in neat rows across the back wall, every piece oiled and creased from use. Beneath them, wooden crates stacked three deep carry stenciled labels: *grung antidotes*, *climbing rope, rated*, *signal whistles*.
>
> A voice hits the door before the bell does. Pointed ears, sunburnt nose, a grin that doesn't sit right on a scarred jaw — an elf built like he's spent his whole life outrunning something. He's already halfway across the floor, ledger open, pen jabbing at the page fast enough to outrun his own handwriting. "CRIKEY, come in, come in, mind the cage!" A climbing spike and coil of cord sit forgotten on the counter where his hand last let go of them.
>
> The smell is leather tack and lamp oil. Underneath runs the sharper reek of expedition gear, field-worn in wet forest, layered over soap-clean new armor and the honest smell of survival kit stacked on every shelf.
>
> Behind the counter, a second ledger sits open on a lower shelf, less visible than the one he's actively marking. He does not offer to show it.

## Structure

Sixty feet by forty, one story of timber fronting the harbor street, narrow at the door with a cargo hatch cut into the side. The floor sits level with [[sparhold|Sparhold]]'s second-tier street, and the tall harbor-side windows carry all the light the room gets.

A counter cuts the room roughly in half: customer space in front, supply hold behind. Racks line every wall and leave little open floor, so sight lines stop at the counter or at the racks, and anything behind the counter takes a step closer or a reach over. The front entry is the only customer approach. The cargo hatch opens onto a loading alley tight enough that only hand-trucks and rope-carry get through it, which is how crates reach [[the-live-hold|The Live Hold]] two doors east without ever crossing the shop floor.

## Atmosphere

Roka talks through most of a conversation at full volume, reading his ledger like it's the best story he knows: every hazard gets the full telling, [[grung|Grung]] ambush tactics and crocodile bite force and the exact way the last client's harness failed, none of it softened, all of it delivered like good news. Trophy-hunters off the [[quillane|Quillane]] boats and timber money out of [[tor-vessane|Tor Vessane]] want a list read back to them, and he gives it to them at the number he set that morning, still grinning.

He marks nothing on these shelves down. A discount would be a claim about the odds, and Roka makes no claims about the odds — he just makes them sound like the best part of the trip. He sells the harness that gets a client home, then sells the same harness again next season to whoever replaces them.

## Hooks

A client steps out of the shop carrying a crate of climbing gear, moving with the deliberate calm of someone who has committed to danger and is no longer negotiating the choice.

When they pass, the crate shifts and the smell becomes unmistakable: pitch-tar, expedition-worn leather, the reek of [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] fieldwork that clings to every surface. Roka's voice booms from inside, still going: *"PAY USSA before you're past the boundary markers, you hear me? Bloke knows the cut-lines better than the Grung do!"* A second figure nods, pocketing something from the counter.

Word spreads within hours that Roka is mounting three expeditions this season, fully crewed, and the creature-sketches in his ledger have already drawn a buyer's attention.

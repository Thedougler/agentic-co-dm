> [!narration]  
> _Third day of the [[e09-run-guide-kalowe|Kalowe]] crossing, and the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] runs south into open [[midchain|Midchain]] water, where the certainty of the charts begins to thin._
> 
> _A sail has held off the quarter since first light._
> 
> _No closer at noon than it was at dawn._
> 
> _No farther, either._
> 
> _The sea between you lies flat enough that, when the wind drops between gusts, you can almost hear the other ship’s rigging creak across the water._
> 
> _The glass goes up again after noon._
> 
> _This time, the shape resolves._
> 
> _A hull the red of iron left too long in salt water. Bare halyards where a pennant should fly. No one visible working the deck. No hands trimming canvas._
> 
> _You keep watching through a full turn of the glass._
> 
> _Nothing moves aboard her._
> 
> _The wind holds steady._
> 
> _Your heading holds steady._
> 
> _And the red ship holds exactly the same distance she has all day._

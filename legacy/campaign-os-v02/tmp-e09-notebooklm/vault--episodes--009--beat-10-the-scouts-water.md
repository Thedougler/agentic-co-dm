## At a Glance

- **Pattern:** the means of the rescue exists in one person, and the price is trust.
- **Delta:** [[ussa|Ussa]] was a scout. She knows [[karath|Karath]]'s patrol patterns and the channel between [[aruhe|Aruhe]] and Karath better than any pilot on the strip. She ran it at night in both directions and knows which rocks the patrol boats avoid and why. Reaching Aruhe without a [[grung|Grung]] patrol spotting the approach requires exactly the knowledge she carries, in her position, for her own reasons. By this morning, someone in [[sparhold|Sparhold]] knows whether she will help.
- **Pressure:** the smoke is two weeks old arithmetic. Whoever makes it has been on that beach since the storm.

## Player Gravity

- Whole crew, this is the path to the captives
- [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]], Ussa is what a better way for the Grung looks like when one person builds it alone out of bribe money
- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]], the channel run is night navigation through patrol water, her chart work

## Situation

[[sparhold|Sparhold]], the morning after the duel. Harbor talk carries the branch's Aruhe signal: a [[house-kailani|Kailani]] fishing smack in from the northern water reports broken [[grung|Grung]] hulls on the reef approach off [[aruhe|Aruhe]], and smoke from the island's beach that should not be there. The island has a reputation. Smoke means something alive and desperate is making it.

## Actors

- [[ussa|Ussa]]. **Wants:** her kin free, and nothing that exposes the arrangement that frees them. **Knows:** the patrol patterns, the channel rocks, what [[karath|Karath]] does to defectors. **Constrained by:** helping outsiders reach Aruhe risks everything the money bought. How the party handled beat-09 decides her opening posture. **Without PC intervention:** keeps her water knowledge to herself and her runs.

## What Can Change

Ussa's help (the patrol windows, the night channel, the reef gap approach). The party gaining a viable Aruhe plan. What the party offers or holds over her setting the terms of the alliance.

## Surface

- Finding [[ussa|Ussa]]
- The [[house-kailani|Kailani]] smack's crew retelling the smoke report
- The chart table where her knowledge becomes a plan
- Whichever house now rules deciding what northern water traffic it tolerates

## Conditional Reactions

- **If the party earned her trust in beat-09:** she names her price plainly. Her kin's names on any list the rescue produces.
- **If they hold her secret over her:** she helps, and the help is exactly as good as coerced help ever is.
- **If they never found her:** the smoke report stands alone, and whoever runs the channel does it on chart work and luck.
- **If they share the debris-field calculation (beat-04):** her knowledge confirms it. The reef gap is the only way in.

## Carry Forward

An Aruhe approach plan with or without Ussa. The field's Sparhold path now points at the island. The Climax gate (the rescue itself) stays next-session work.

## Situation

A [[house-kailani|Kailani]] fishing smack ties up at [[sparhold|Sparhold]]'s dock, crew still in the clothes they sailed in. Broken [[grung|Grung]] hulls on the reef approach off [[aruhe|Aruhe]] and smoke rising from the island's beach. The island has a reputation. Smoke means something alive and desperate is feeding it. [[roka-sten|Roka]]'s missing expedition teams, the storm arithmetic from the [[central-strait|strait]], and the captives' clock all converge on the same three miles of reef.

## Player Gravity

- Whole crew — the captives from the [[calveno|Calveno]] fleet. 149 people still unaccounted for.
- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] — the channel to [[aruhe|Aruhe]] is navigation through reef water. Her chart work.
- [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] — [[grung|Grung]] hulls broken on the reef means the clans lost people too. The raids and the island share a cause.

## Active Force

- **Intent:** [[aruhe|Aruhe]] keeps what washes ashore.
- **Means:** reef, current, and whatever lives past the tree line.
- **Limits:** the reef gap is the only way in. The survivors have held two weeks.

## Pressure

- **Now:** smoke means survivors. Smoke fed for two weeks means survivors running out of time.
- **Next:** the season turns. The [[grung-clans|Grung]] patrol circuit tightens, and the window for a channel run narrows with each day.
- **Default trajectory:** the fires go out. The beach empties.

## Open Vectors

- **Ask the smack crew:** they changed course to avoid the reef. They saw the smoke at distance. They did not land.
- **Cross-reference Roka's maps:** his channel charts cover the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] approach from the west. The reef gap off [[aruhe|Aruhe]] is at the edge of his charted water.
- **Calculate the window:** two weeks of smoke and a season turning while the patrol circuit narrows. The arithmetic is plain.

## Escalation

1. The smoke report is hearsay from a fishing crew.
2. [[roka-sten|Roka]]'s missing teams and the debris field from beat-04 confirm the pattern.
3. The patrol circuit tightens. Each day reduces the viable approach windows.
4. The fires stop.

## Fallout

- **Success:** the party commits to the channel run with purpose. Roka's maps become the plan.
- **Complication:** they commit without preparation — no reef chart, just Roka's general knowledge of the approach.
- **Setback:** they delay. The season turns and the patrol circuit closes the approach.
- **Bypass:** they ignore the smoke. The captives' story ends on that beach.

## Handoff

**Likely:** development, [[roka-sten|Roka]]'s channel maps and expedition knowledge become the plan to reach the island

**Alternate:** the party sails immediately without planning, and the channel run fires as the next beat directly

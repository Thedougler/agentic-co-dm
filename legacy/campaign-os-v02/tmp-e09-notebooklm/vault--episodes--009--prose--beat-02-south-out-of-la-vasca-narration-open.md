> _The [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] slips free of the [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]] cradle into a morning that smells of wet timber and festival ash. Fresh paint still cures along her hull where the yard finished ahead of [[cobb|Cobb]]’s own estimate. Above, the season’s first true southerly catches the canvas and sets the rigging humming as she turns into the [[central-strait|Central Strait]]._
> 
> _[[calveno|Calveno]] falls astern, its crater-side streets still damp, festival smoke thinning behind you._
> 
> _At the chart table, Geoffrey has the Midchain spread open beneath his hands. Three headings are marked across the map: [[sparhold|Sparhold]], four days south. [[kalowe|Kalowe]], five days southwest. [[fathomrush|Fathomrush]], six or seven days east._*

## Prep

**Where:** [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]], then the [[central-strait|Central Strait]] south.

**Who:** [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]], [[corvin-knighton|Corvin Knighton]], [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]], three [[calveno|Calveno]] survivors, the [[knifes-wake|Knife's Wake]]'s crew.

**Secrets already live:** the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]'s hull line is a Dravosi cutter's. The Knife's Wake carries a paid search retainer. The storm debris resolves to [[aruhe|Aruhe]].

**DM reminders:**

- The cold open is one 30-minute Hook beat, five combined group checks, hard cut to La Vasca.
- Beats 03 to 06 fire in whatever order the water and the DM choose.
- Every southern heading crosses the debris field.
- The [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]] warms as they enter the [[midchain-north|Northern Midchain]]. If anyone notices, [[yssenmoor|Yssenmoor]]'s fog is visible on the horizon. See Side-tracks.
- [[the-unplotted|The Unplotted]] sits where the chart shows open water, between Sparhold and [[kwoss|Kwoss]]. The crew can anchor or sail past. See Side-tracks.

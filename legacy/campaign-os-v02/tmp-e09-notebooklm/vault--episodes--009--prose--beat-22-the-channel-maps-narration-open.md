> [!narration]
> _[[roka-sten|Roka Sten]] sweeps the counter clear with one arm and spreads the channel charts flat, weighting the corners with a cage latch and three loose cartridges. Depth soundings in red, current arrows in black, reef lines drawn thick enough to read by lantern._
>
> _"RIGHT. Five islands, five reef gaps, I've been through four of 'em." His finger traces the penciled route west to east through [[dreth|Dreth]] and [[orak|Orak]], past [[karath|Karath]]'s patrol water. "Beautiful runs, every one. Current kills you if you miss the window, which is the fun part."_
>
> _His finger stops where the charts thin. East of the last sounding, one reef gap sketched in lighter ink. [[aruhe|Aruhe]] written beside it in his hand. Below the name, a single question mark._
>
> _"That one I haven't run. That's where your smoke is."_

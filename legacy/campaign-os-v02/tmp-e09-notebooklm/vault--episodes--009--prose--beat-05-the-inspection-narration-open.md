> [!narration]  
> _Five days south of [[calveno|Calveno]], a warship cuts across your bow on an intercept course._
> 
> _Gunports open. Crews stand ready at their stations. Above them fly the pennants of a Knight Squadron, alongside the colours of Crown inspection._
> 
> _The gangplank comes across._
> 
> _A young Dravosi officer boards first. Dark hair cropped short. Dueling scars cut both cheeks. A rapier hangs at his left hip, a flint pistol low on his right, with a bandolier crossing the space between. His eyes move over the deck, the rigging, the crew, taking inventory before settling on you._
> 
> _A High Elf follows half a head taller, gaunt and grey-haired beneath a long black coat. Several sword hilts show beneath it. His mouth wears a manic grin his eyes do not share. He scans the faces aboard slowly, deliberately, as though looking for someone in particular._
> 
> _The young officer clasps his hands behind his back._
> 
> _“Who commands this vessel? Present yourself. You are under mandatory Crown inspection.”_

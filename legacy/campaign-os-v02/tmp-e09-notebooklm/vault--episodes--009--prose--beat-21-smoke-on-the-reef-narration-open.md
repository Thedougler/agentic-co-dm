> [!narration]
> _A fishing smack noses into [[sparhold|Sparhold]]'s harbor with its nets still lashed and its crew wearing what they sailed in. One of them fumbles the cleat twice before the line holds._
>
> _The crew hasn't slept. Lines coiled backward, the gaff still out. Salt crusts the wool at their collars._
>
> _The bowman cups his hands toward the dock. "Hulls on the reef off [[aruhe|Aruhe]]! [[grung|Grung]] markings, keels snapped clean! And smoke on the beach, thin smoke. Somebody's feeding it because if they stop, nobody comes!"_
>
> _Coils stop mid-throw. The nearest dockhand turns toward the smack, and one by one, so does every hand on the pier._

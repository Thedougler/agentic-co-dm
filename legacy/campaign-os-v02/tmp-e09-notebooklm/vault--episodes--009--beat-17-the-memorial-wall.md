## At a Glance

- **Pattern:** arrival into a rivalry that meters everything by what it costs.
- **Delta:** the town's shape, one venture honest and one fraudulent. Both Guard real data while the clock runs toward whichever cracks first. The wall is the price ledger nobody disputes. [[branimir-karsovine|Karsovine]] identified as the man whose notes are the real account.
- **Pressure:** the brawl makes the rivalry legible in the first hour. The pale name on the wall is a week old and diving continues tomorrow.

## Player Gravity

- [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]], deep-pressure rig engineering is flying-machine-adjacent knowledge she wants. Both sides have real data and both sides [[guard|Guard]] it
- Whole crew, [[fathomrush|Fathomrush's]] salvage fleets and dive craft are the eastern staging for anything the rescue needs done in hard water

## Situation

[[fathomrush|Fathomrush]] announces itself by skyline: riveted iron scaffold towers on both headlands, some still climbing. One on the [[branimir-karsovine|Voyvode]] side with three levels standing and the upper two abandoned mid-frame, rusting. Between the two piers at the waterline stands the memorial wall, plain wood, no company crest, claimed by neither company. New names since anyone in the crew's circuit last came through. The most recent still pale where raw wood shows through the chisel strokes. Maybe a week old.

In the dockside tavern, [[adriana-tarquine|Sunkline]] and Voyvode recruiters have the fixed expressions of two people having the same argument long enough that voices are no longer required. **Within the crew's first hour in port, the argument breaks into a real brawl.** Recruiting pitch, accumulated grudge, company loyalty, broken furniture.

Branimir Karsovine watches the end of it from his gate. He sent someone to the trial-dive site that morning. He knows the new name on the wall. His visible concern is whether his rig holds pressure at depth tomorrow, and whether the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty's]] crew has real business in Fathomrush or is passing through.

## Actors

- [[branimir-karsovine|Branimir Karsovine]]. **Wants:** his rig holding pressure at depth tomorrow. **Knows:** the new name on the wall, what the trial dives cost. **Constrained by:** real things only, he does not hand them to people who have not earned the conversation. **Without PC intervention:** dives on schedule.
- [[adriana-tarquine|Adriana Tarquine]]. **Wants:** the company she rebuilt protected. **Knows:** the valve's problem is the design, not the alloy. Has known for two rebuilds. **Constrained by:** the design is hers. **Without PC intervention:** rebuilds it a fifth time.

## What Can Change

- Standing with either company
- A read of the rivalry's honest side
- The wall's names as the town's true ledger
- An opening toward Karsovine (paid off in beat-18)

## Surface

The skyline and the wall; the tavern brawl; Karsovine's gate; the recruiters afterward, each blaming the other.

## Conditional Reactions

- **If the party breaks up the brawl:** both companies notice, and each reads it as taking a side.
- **If they read the wall first:** the town notices that too, and approves.
- **If they pitch Karsovine cold:** he actually asks what their real business in Fathomrush is.
- **If they do nothing:** the brawl ends the way dock brawls end, and the town files them under passing through.

## Carry Forward

The party's standing and any openings feed into beat-18, the two ledgers and the dive-crew news.

> [!narration]  
> _Morning trade moves through [[sparhold|Sparhold]] around a yard still stained from last night’s duel. Fresh chalk marks the ground the market refuses to cross. Tar, salt, and yesterday’s catch hang heavy in the air while dock crews work their nets and trade stories without looking up._
> 
> _At the near pier, a [[house-kailani|Kailani]] smack rides low in the water. Her crew has been telling the same story to anyone who stops long enough to listen._
> 
> _They ran the northern waters before dawn._
> 
> _East of [[aruhe|Aruhe]], they passed wreckage scattered across the reef. [[grung|Grung]] hulls, broken open and dragged high onto the rocks._
> 
> _And beyond the reef, on a beach the charts mark empty, smoke rises into the morning sky._
> 
> _Thin. Steady._
> 
> _Not the smoke of wreckage._
> 
> _The kind a fire makes when someone keeps feeding it._

# Kalowe

## Prep

**Where:** the southwest crossing, then [[kalowe|Kalowe]] docks and the Grask's fort.

**Who:** the [[chain-council|Chain Council]]'s courier and twelve, the coastal delegation, the [[dead-lady|Dead Lady]] on the crossing.

**Secrets already live:** the Denial front needs Delmar blamed. The [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] debt weather over [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s people. The fishers' smoke report.

- The Dead Lady acts on standing, not appetite. Dread, not a fight.
- The closed session plays with or about the party depending on beat-12's answer.
- The smoke report is this branch's [[aruhe|Aruhe]] signal.
- [[murrat|Murrat]]'s cloud is visible to starboard on the southwest crossing. If anyone investigates, [[murrats-living-shroud|Murrat's Living Shroud]] fires.

# The 20 questions

Ask one at a time, in order, prefixed with its number ("**Question 1 of
20.**") so the player knows where they are. Acknowledge each answer
briefly, then move on — no follow-ups, all 20 cover the ground. "Skip" or
"Next" is a completely valid answer; note the question as unanswered and
continue without comment.

1. Let's start simple. What's their name? Any aliases, nicknames, titles
   they've picked up? And is there a name that only one specific person
   ever called them — something private?
2. Before we get into anything else, paint me a picture. They walk through
   the door. What do I see?
3. What's their class? And I want to know *why* — is it a calling,
   something they chose, or something life didn't give them much of a
   choice about?
4. Did they come into this with a Mortis — a flaw and a gift that are
   bound up together? If so, what's the Mark, and do *they* know it's
   there?
5. What do they want right now, more than anything? And are they honest
   with themselves about it, or do they dress it up in something more
   acceptable?
6. By the end of all this — whatever this turns out to be — who do they
   want to *be*? Not what they want to accomplish. Who. Do they even know
   yet?
7. What keeps them up at night? Something real, not monsters. Is there
   something specific they're afraid of losing?
8. What do they believe that most people around them would push back on?
   Have they ever actually said it out loud?
9. Is there a line they won't cross? And have they ever come close to it?
10. What did their life look like before all this? Day to day — what were
    they actually doing before they became an adventurer?
11. What's the hardest thing they've had to survive — or do — to become
    the person we're meeting right now?
12. Do they carry something with a story? A weapon, a trinket, something
    worn down from use. What is it, and where did it come from?
13. What's the worst thing they've ever done? Did they mean to do it?
14. Whose opinion of them matters most? Where is that person now?
15. Is there anyone out there who considers them an enemy? Who, and why?
16. Who is the one person they'd trust with their life — not because that
    person is powerful, but just because they *trust* them? What did that
    person do to earn it?
17. How do they treat someone who can't do anything for them? A beggar, a
    servant, a child. Is that who they were raised to be, or something
    they had to figure out on their own?
18. What's the one thing they want so badly they might compromise who they
    are to get it? And do they know that about themselves?
19. What are they carrying that would change how people see them if it
    ever came out?
20. Do they believe in the gods? And does it matter to them whether the
    gods believe in them?

Start with: *"Let's get to know your character. I'll walk you through some
questions — there are no wrong answers, and you can keep things as simple
or as rich as you like. Ready? Let's start with the basics."* Then go
straight to question 1. Close with: *"That's all of them. Let me put that
together."*, then produce the page immediately.

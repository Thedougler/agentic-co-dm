# Minor Subtype Guidance — feat, background, condition, subsystem

Read this when `rule-prep`'s interview establishes `subtype: feat`,
`background`, `condition`, or `subsystem` — the four subtypes with no
dedicated design-math reference file of their own (`class`/`subclass`,
`spell`, and `species` route to their own files; see `.claude/skills/rule-prep/SKILL.md` §
Subtype routing).

## `feat`

Balance against the genre's own gold standard (Alert, Lucky, Polearm
Master, War Caster, Sentinel). Good feats do 2–3 things: one strong
passive + one situational trigger. Half-ASI feats (+1 to a stat) need a
solid mechanical benefit beyond the stat bump. Never a permanent flat
damage bonus with no condition, and never a full-ASI feat that also
carries a strong standalone benefit.

## `background`

Two skill proficiencies, a tool/language proficiency pair, a standard
equipment package, and one feature at ribbon-to-minor budget
(`.claude/skills/rule-prep/references/species-design.md`'s feature-budget table's low end is the
right scale — a background feature should never approach a
species-level trait's weight). Cite a comparable PHB background directly.

## `condition`

State exactly what changes versus the RAW condition it modifies (name
it), or the complete mechanical text if genuinely new. House-rule
discipline, not balance math — the risk here is ambiguity at the table,
not power creep.

## `subsystem`

A genuinely novel house-rule mechanic (a new resting variant, a crafting
economy, a reputation track) that isn't one of the other seven. State the
complete rule, what existing RAW mechanic it touches or replaces (if
any), and run it past the DM Review Gate the same as every other subtype.

# Draft Passage

A crossing is a durable `type: route` plus the Situations that can
surface on it. Hand travel-events output to Route samples (`## Read-aloud`,
`## Checks`) or one plot-weight moment. The same Situation may be
wikilinked from more than one Route.

migrates.

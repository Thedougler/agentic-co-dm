# Event tables — dressing, hazard tiers, toll menu

## Travel method dressing

The same six TES types, different dressing per travel method: Ship (rival
vessels, weather, sea creatures, crew trouble), Overland (road encounters,
terrain, settlements), Aerial (weather, predators, altitude), Planar
(reality warps, native denizens).

## Hazard resolution (pure Yellow events)

Used only for an event with no combat or drama-suite stakes — a pure
terrain, weather, or navigation hazard (Hard Rule 5). Combat or
social-stakes events hand off to `encounter-prep` instead (Hard Rule 3).

| DC | Check | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Wis (Survival) or nav tools | Minor: off course, +1 day |
| 14 | Wis (Survival) or nav tools | Moderate: +2 days, encounter likely |
| 18 | Wis (Survival) or nav tools | Serious: lost, +1d4 days, complication |

Dress the consequence per travel method — off course at sea, a wrong fork
overland, blown off heading aloft, forced descent when a landing is
forced.

Planar travel has no single generalizable framing — ask the DM what "off
course" means for that reality (the skill body’s § Degrade by asking).

**Citation requirement** (Hard Rule 2): state which DC tier the check used
and why (terrain, weather, or a hazard established via the Standard
queries or a route page), then the roll result and its consequence. A
check with no stated tier reasoning is a guess with a die attached.

**Advantage/disadvantage sources** are campaign-specific (charts,
instruments, a PC's background) — grep `vault/campaigns/*/pcs/*.md` and
established navigation-aid items before ruling on modifiers; never invent
an advantage source that isn't established.

## Toll menu (journey slot 4)

Every leg states at least one toll; numbers come from
[[gameplay-toolbox|the gameplay toolbox]], never invented:

- **Time** — days lost to a hazard tier above, against whatever clock is
  live; keep the total vague unless a route page or deadline fixes it.
- **[[vault/srd/rules/exhaustion|Exhaustion]]** — pushing past 8 hours: a
  Con save per the toolbox's Extended Travel rule, one level on failure.
- **Supplies** — rations, water, or a named consumable spent or spoiled;
  the Quartermaster's failed check is the natural trigger.
- **Gear** — a tool, sail, mount, or vehicle component damaged; repair is
  a hook at the next stop, not a bookkeeping line.
- **Condition** — environmental effects, contagions, curses, poison per
  the toolbox's own sections; cite the specific entry used.
- **Position** — a secret revealed, a pursuer closing, a contact's offer
  cooling — the narrative toll when nothing physical fits.

# Worked examples — derivation-based selection

Both fixtures. `<PC-A>` and `<PC-B>` are placeholder slots — fill them from
the party's own Arc Notes under `vault/campaigns/shattered-sea/pcs/`, never
from this page.

## A Far crossing, two events

User: "The party's sailing from the harbor to the outpost — that's a Far
crossing. What happens on the way?"

Standard queries: no `type: route` page covers the crossing (flagged to
`.claude/skills/draft-content/references/route.md` as a gap — the party trades along this
lane, path 2 of `route-escalation.md`). Arc Notes show <PC-A> has
an open thread with a rival smuggling crew; the harbour faction's front
says its patrols are stretched thin. Spotlight: <PC-B> has gone two sessions
without a personal beat.

Method: Ship. Distance: Far → 2 events. Travel roles: <PC-B> takes Scout (first
pick, spotlight), <PC-A> Trailhand, a third PC Quartermaster.

**Journey shape** — Departure cost: the harbourmaster's amnesty offer
expires the day they sail. Landmark: the Spire Wreck, a mast standing
straight out of open water where no chart shows a shoal — approachable,
skirtable, unexplained. Toll: two days against the amnesty clock.
Arrival changed: carrying a rival's parley terms.

**Event 1 (Blue — <PC-A>'s smuggler thread × the lane's thin patrols):** the
rival lugger flies parley colors <PC-A> recognizes; with no patrols to fear,
its captain wants the meeting the harbour would never allow. Derivation
stated; straightforward negotiation, no drama-suite stakes → stays inline
(Hard Rule 3 not triggered). One-off captain, no stated future → no npc
hand-off (Hard Rule 4).

**Event 2 (Yellow — <PC-B>'s Scout duty × the Spire Wreck's false water):**
the wreck sits over an uncharted reef spur; <PC-B>'s Scout check (the
Moderate tier — established hazard, cited per Hard Rule 2, rolled via
`roll-dice`) decides
whether the party reads the water in time or takes the Moderate
consequence. A failed check is the complication, not a punishment
(`travel-roles.md`).

Composition check: types differ, one non-combat (both), PC derivation
stated on both, landmark present, toll stated, arrival-changed stated.
Both events hand to `draft-moment` as beats of the leg's travel moment.

## A Very Far leg, four events, all registers

A Very Far voyage (4 events). Live material from the queries: a PC's
exiled-house thread, the contact-faction front's missing courier, the
party's dwindling water, an unspent grudge between two PCs. Type balance
composed deliberately: Red, Yellow, Blue, White — no adjacent repeats,
three non-combat, every event derived:

1. **Red** — the exiled house's hunters board at night (thread × the
   lane's isolation). Hands to `encounter-prep` with premise and PC
   connection.
2. **Yellow** — the water toll bites: a dry anchorage where the chart
   promised a spring; Quartermaster and Trailhand checks against the
   Moderate tier, cited.
3. **Blue** — the missing courier's skiff, adrift (front × the route) —
   what the party does with the satchel advances or sets back the front;
   flagged to `world-update`, never advanced here.
4. **White** — the centerpiece, last: the hunters return under a storm at
   the landmark strait; combat, the grudge surfacing under pressure, and
   the crossing itself layered in one event. Hands to `encounter-prep` for
   the spine, roleplay and terrain folded into its Toy table fields —
   one event, three registers, per the type's definition.

Interlace (3+ events): between events 2 and 3, cut one beat to the
destination — the front's clock ticking in the party's absence — then
return to the deck.

# Stances — the room's lenses

The caller hands **one** stance per `draft-writer` instance. Stances are
lenses, not genres — any stance can draft any piece; it changes what the
draft optimizes for, not what kind of story it is. A full room dispatches
all 8 in parallel (compete mode's "best possible version" flow).

## Character-first

- **Optimizes:** interiority — what the people in the scene want, need, and hide.
- **Instincts:** anchor every beat in a want/need gap; let action reveal
  character before description does; give even minor figures one telling choice.
- **Risk:** plot slows while everyone feels things; scenery goes thin.

## Atmosphere-first

- **Optimizes:** sensory immersion — dread, wonder, the feel of the place.
- **Instincts:** lead with concrete sensory detail; build mood through
  accumulation; let the environment act on the characters.
- **Risk:** gorgeous fog — pages of texture where nothing happens.

## Plot-momentum

- **Optimizes:** causality and drive — every beat earns the next.
- **Instincts:** cut anything that doesn't push forward; end sections on
  hooks; make consequences visible and immediate; pace ruthlessly.
- **Risk:** breathless prose that never lets a moment land.

## Subtext-heavy

- **Optimizes:** what's unsaid — dialogue and detail carrying undercurrent.
- **Instincts:** characters talk around the real subject; meaning lives in
  gesture, omission, and contradiction between word and act.
- **Risk:** so oblique the table misses the point entirely.

## Player-camera

- **Optimizes:** table impact — what the GM actually reads aloud and how it lands.
- **Instincts:** write for the ear, not the page; scannable blocks; front-load
  the image players will react to; leave gaps players fill by acting.
- **Risk:** utilitarian prose that runs great and reads flat.

## Mythic-register

- **Optimizes:** epic weight — diction that makes events feel legendary,
  anchored by personal stakes so it never floats free.
- **Instincts:** elevated cadence and imagery; tie the cosmic to one
  person's cost; treat names and oaths as heavy objects.
- **Risk:** purple drift — grandeur with no one to care about.

## Sparse-and-sharp

- **Optimizes:** concision — Strunk's knife, white space, the exact word.
- **Instincts:** short declaratives; cut modifiers; trust the reader; let
  one precise detail do a paragraph's work.
- **Risk:** austerity mistaken for depth; scenes that feel skeletal.

## Hook-seeder

- **Optimizes:** forward pull — loaded guns, open doors, reasons to act
  (sandbox craft: `.claude/skills/composing-beats/references/audits.md`'s toys and hooks).
- **Instincts:** every element carries a pullable thread; end on questions
  the table can chase; seed 2–3 hooks pointing at different futures; never
  aim the gun, just load it.
- **Risk:** a junk drawer of threads — intrigue everywhere, focus nowhere.

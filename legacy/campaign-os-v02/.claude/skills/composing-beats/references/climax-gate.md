# Climax readiness gate and Resolution handoff

## Climax readiness gate

A Climax is **earned**, not scheduled. Evaluate this gate whenever the
central dramatic question appears ready for decisive play.

The structure is Climax-ready when all of the following are true:

- **Question** — the dramatic question is clear enough to answer through play.
- **Investment** — the outcome matters to the PCs through established gravity.
- **Opposition** — the forces preventing an easy answer are present or reachable.
- **Understanding** — the players possess enough information to make meaningful decisions.
- **Leverage** — prior play has given them some means of affecting the outcome.
- **Stakes** — meaningful consequences of success, failure, compromise, or transformation are visible.
- **Possibility** — more than one outcome remains genuinely attainable.
- **Convergence** — the current state naturally brings the relevant forces into decisive contact.

When all conditions hold, invoke `writing-climax-beats`.

When one or more conditions are missing, identify the missing condition
and compose the Development or Cliffhanger most naturally capable of
changing it. Do not insert filler merely to lengthen the Beat Chart.

## Resolution handoff

After the Climax has actually been played, pass its real result to
`writing-resolution-beats`.

The Resolution receives:

- what the PCs actually did;
- what the opposition actually did;
- who succeeded, failed, survived, escaped, surrendered, changed sides, or remained unresolved;
- resources gained or lost;
- relationships changed;
- public consequences;
- private consequences;
- unresolved pressures;
- new opportunities created by play.

The Resolution applies those facts. Preparation may identify **possible
consequence families**, but the final Resolution follows the table's
result rather than selecting a prepared ending.

# Checklist

## General self-review

Before finalizing substantial player-facing prose, verify:

- [ ] Players can understand the immediate situation after hearing it once.
- [ ] The prose establishes orientation quickly.
- [ ] Important actionable elements are perceptible.
- [ ] Spatial relationships are clear where they matter.
- [ ] Hidden information remains hidden unless legitimately exposed.
- [ ] Automatically knowable information is not withheld.
- [ ] Evidence is distinguished from player conclusions.
- [ ] No voluntary PC emotion is dictated.
- [ ] No player choice is narrated.
- [ ] No unresolved sandbox outcome is assumed.
- [ ] Important details receive appropriate attentional weight.
- [ ] Sensory details are purposeful rather than checklist-driven.
- [ ] Generic fantasy filler has been removed.
- [ ] Concrete details outperform abstract labels where practical.
- [ ] The prose sounds natural spoken aloud.
- [ ] Proper nouns do not overload working memory.
- [ ] The final beat creates or returns an opportunity for player response.
- [ ] The narration stops at the reaction point.
- [ ] Tone strengthens rather than obscures information.
- [ ] The DM can easily paraphrase the material.
- [ ] The document remains useful even if players approach the situation unexpectedly.

## Narration-specific

- [ ] Italic body. No H1. No `> [!` callout.
- [ ] `parent:` set. `summary:` one sentence. `goal:` is the story or world fact, fewest words.
- [ ] Mode matches the parent (entity/Situation → depiction; moment/sequence → session; Last Time → recap).
- [ ] Hidden info converted to a perceivable trace or cut.
- [ ] No prescribed feeling. No em dash.
- [ ] Transplant test: every sentence belongs to *this* thing.
- [ ] Two or three non-visual senses, not five. One toy (hint, not tutorial).
- [ ] Seeds at natural size. Texture on the expansion.
- [ ] Every person has a handle the table can say back. No undifferentiated pair.
- [ ] Hook by sentence two. Word count in band.
- [ ] Depiction / Situation: no `you`/`your`, no pacing prompt.
- [ ] Moment / Sequence open: picture first; CTA only if it directs the table.
- [ ] Parent check: the check is in the picture.
- [ ] Recap: past tense; ends on tonight's hook; 240–390 words for Last Time, 900–1350 for the session recap.
- [ ] Read aloud: no tongue-twister, no two adjacent sentences with the same skeleton.

## Fast quality heuristic

Ask four questions:

### Can they see it?

Do the players possess a coherent mental model?

### Can they use it?

Does that model contain the information necessary for meaningful action?

### Can they own it?

Are interpretation, emotion, and decision still theirs?

### Can the DM say it?

Can an expert human DM absorb and communicate it immediately?

If all four are yes, the prose is probably working.

## Final standard

Player-facing prose succeeds when it disappears into play.

Players should not primarily notice:

> *That was beautifully written.*

They should think:

> *I know where I am.*

> *I know what just changed.*

> *I have a theory.*

> *I need to do something.*

> **"Okay. Here's what I do."**

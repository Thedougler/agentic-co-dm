# Depiction

The reusable picture of a *thing* — a location, NPC, item, monster, ship,
or a Situation's visible condition. The DM improvises from this. It works
in any context the party meets the thing.

## Camera

Name the thing. Show how it occupies space. One non-visual sense. One
toy the table can touch. Present tense. Italic.

Mercer *quality* (cinematic, sensory, specific). Not Mercer *camera*
(`you` walk in).

Hook in the first two sentences — the thing in motion, the wrong
detail, the smell that names the place. Spend the rest of the 90–150
word band on that same picture. Every word earns
its place. The toy is a hint: shown, not taught. No pacing prompt.

## Bound

- The thing is the subject of every sentence.
- A listener who has not arrived, has not acted, and may meet this in a
  different place still has a complete picture.
- No `you` / `your`. No arrival. No pacing prompt.
- Close on the thing in motion or at rest — not on a question to the table.

`mode: continue` covers only what changed in the world (something entered,
broke, went still). The standing picture stays in the establish file.

`mode: transition` covers the crossing itself as a thing (the strait, the
weather, the hours) — still no party as the subject.

## Done

A cold reader can picture this entity or condition and use the picture at
a dock, a tavern, or a wreck without rewriting a sentence.

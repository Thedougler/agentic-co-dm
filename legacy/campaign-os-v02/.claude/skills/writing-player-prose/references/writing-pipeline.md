# Writing Pipeline

Agents writing finished player-facing prose MUST conceptually perform:

> **world state → information boundary → player relevance → reaction point → evocative selection → spoken prose**

Never shortcut directly:

> **world state → creative writing**

## Step 1: Recover world state

Identify only established facts.

Do not invent necessary facts unless the task explicitly permits improvisation.

## Step 2: Determine character access

Classify facts as:

- hidden,
- perceivable,
- knowable.

## Step 3: Determine current decision

Ask:

> What might the players reasonably decide after hearing this?

This determines what information deserves priority.

## Step 4: Determine the reaction point

Identify the earliest moment when player input becomes meaningful.

## Step 5: Select details

Choose details that efficiently carry:

- orientation,
- differentiation,
- affordance,
- pressure,
- tone.

## Step 6: Draft for speech

Write simply enough to comprehend once.

## Step 7: Remove prose inflation

Delete:

- redundant adjectives,
- generic atmosphere,
- repeated conclusions,
- unnecessary metaphors,
- exposition,
- decorative lore,
- details with no purpose.

## Step 8: Agency check

Ensure no unintended player conclusion, emotion, or choice has been authored.

## Step 9: Information check

Ensure nothing obvious or automatically known was hidden for drama.

## Step 10: Sandbox check

Ensure the prose does not presume an unresolved outcome.

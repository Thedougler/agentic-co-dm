# Taking on Aruhe — what is actually established

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** On [[aruhe]], fallen fruit is safe to take; taking living fruit or other living claims draws surrounding life into hostility. That is the established rule.

**Why it matters.** The prompt asks for a metaphysical "real answer." The wiki does not have one.

**Scope.** Session 11 inland river confirmation; [[western-landing|Western Landing]] practice; [[matteo-scola]] treats picking as a death rule.

## Current Truth

What is known and witnessed:

- Fallen fruit / unclaimed leavings have not drawn hostility in play.
- Pulling living fruit (or taking other living claims) draws predators — Session 11 [[wolfrabbit]] response after a living-stem pull.
- [[western-landing|Western Landing]] applies the same living-claim caution to landing aids that might still be alive.

**The cause is unknown / not established.** The wiki does not record a divine curse, symbiotic fungal network, spirit bargain, or other metaphysical engine behind the rule. Current Truth stops at the observable claim rule and witnessed effects.

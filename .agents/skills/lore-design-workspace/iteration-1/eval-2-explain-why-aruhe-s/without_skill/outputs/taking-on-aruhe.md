# Taking on Aruhe

## Current Truth

The real answer: a symbiotic fungal network under the island senses when someone takes living fruit or other living claims. The network signals local predators — wolfrabbits, vines, and worse — to defend the claim. Fallen fruit is already severed from the network, so it is safe. An ancient god's curse is a sailor story; the fungus is the mechanism.

Session 11's wolfrabbit attack was the network responding to Crissdalynn pulling living redheart-berry. Matteo's "death rule" is just folk memory of the same biology.

## At a Glance

Fallen safe / living hostile because of the fungal web.

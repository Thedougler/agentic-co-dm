# Taken Whole

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** A red ship of ordinary raiders past the Maw takes ships whole, kills everyone, and dumps cargo over the side.

**Why it matters.** The party should hunt these raiders out of Kalowe.

**Scope.** Kalowe dock talk; Maw-lanes.

## Current Truth

Dock talk at Kalowe is right: a red raider ship comes out of the fog past the Maw, kills the whole crew, and strips the hold by dumping cargo overboard so nobody contests the salvage. Sailors know it is raiders. The Dead Lady and Umberlee tribute angle is optional flavour; the Current Truth is the raider ship.

## Limits

* Raiders sometimes leave gold behind by mistake.

# Taken Whole

*Something red and silent is taking ships whole past the Maw-lanes, and dock talk at Kalowe hasn't figured out why.*

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** A red ship past the Maw takes ships whole — crews die, holds empty, hulls drift.

**Why it matters.** Sailors at [[Kalowe]] fear those lanes; the party may investigate the derelicts.

**Scope.** Maw-lanes and Kalowe docks.

## Current Truth

There is a ship out past the Maw-lanes that does not fight and does not loot in the normal way. She comes up red out of the fog. When she is gone the whole crew is dead and the hold is stripped bare — an empty hull drifting on the current. Dock talk at [[Kalowe]] has this right: fishers and crews who have seen hulks or heard secondhand report the same core story.

[[The *Dead Lady*]] is behind the derelicts. [[Umberlee]] collects tribute directly. [[delmar-fisk]]'s debt is tied in. Sailors who call them raiders are mistaken about motive, but the red ship and the emptied holds are established fact of the rumour's core.

Newer tellings say she takes the gold too; that fits raiders better and may also be true as the story evolves.

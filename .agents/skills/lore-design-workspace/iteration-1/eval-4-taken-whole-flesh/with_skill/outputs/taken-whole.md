# Taken Whole

## Durable question
What is actually true about the Kalowe "red ship" rumour — and what must stay rumour versus Current Truth?

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** Dock talk at [[Kalowe]] describes a red ship past the Maw that leaves crews dead and holds emptied. The derelict pattern is real; the usual sailor explanation (ordinary raiders or a salvage monster) is not.

**Why it matters.** Players who treat the rumour as pirate gossip miss [[Umberlee]]'s direct tribute and [[delmar-fisk]]'s open debt; players who promote every dock tale to fact invent threats that are not on the stub.

**Scope.** Kalowe tavern/dock circulation; Maw-lane waters. Truth value: **partial**.

## Current Truth

**Partially true — as established on the stub.**

Claims about what is *found* match: [[The *Dead Lady*]] leaves derelicts with everyone aboard dead and holds emptied over the side. What the rumour gets wrong is the *shape* of the actor. It is [[Umberlee]] collecting tribute directly instead of through a shrine basin. Nobody circulating the story has connected it to [[delmar-fisk]]'s open debt.

Current Truth does **not** include: "ordinary raiders cleared the ship for uncontested salvage," "a sea monster hunting cargo," or "she takes the gold too because raiders would." Those are Accounts / mutations, not authoritative fact.

### Limits

* **Exception.** Derelict pattern is accurate; raider/monster explanations are not.
* **Unknown.** How widely the true tribute reading has leaked beyond DM notes; whether every red-fog sighting is the Dead Lady.
* **Impossible or forbidden.** Do not promote dockside rumour or sailor theory into authoritative Current Truth.

## At the Table

* **Players notice.** Drifted empty hull past the Maw-lanes; no boarding-fight damage on hatches; cargo missing but never fenced in port.
* **This explains.** Why "raiders who leave plunder behind" keeps mutating into "she takes the gold too."
* **This enables.** Read a derelict's story (investigation) without needing a lore monologue; pressure Delmar once the tribute link is in play.
* **This warns of.** Treating Kalowe gossip as a pirate-hunting quest closes the Umberlee angle.

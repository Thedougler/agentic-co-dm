# Taken Whole

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** Dock talk at [[Kalowe]] describes a red ship past the Maw-lanes that leaves crews dead and holds emptied over the side. The derelict pattern is true; the sailor explanation (ordinary raiders) is not.

**Why it matters.** Investigating the rumour can point toward [[The *Dead Lady*]] and [[Umberlee]] tribute rather than a pirate hunt.

**Scope.** Tavern and dock talk at Kalowe; waters past the Maw-lanes.

## Current Truth

**Partially true (stub framing preserved).** Claims about what is found on the derelicts are accurate: everyone aboard dead, holds emptied over the side, hulls drifting. [[The *Dead Lady*]] leaves derelicts exactly like that. What circulating talk gets wrong is the shape of the threat — ordinary raiders or a sea monster clearing ships for uncontested salvage.

Authoritative framing from the stub (not dockside invention): nobody circulating the story knows it is [[Umberlee]] collecting tribute directly instead of through a shrine basin, and nobody has connected it to [[delmar-fisk]]'s open debt.

Dockside rumour and sailor theory are **not** promoted into this Current Truth section; they live under Accounts.

### Limits

* **Exception.** Newer tellings that claim she takes the gold too are mutations of the rumour, not established fact about motive.
* **Unknown.** How many independent witnesses have seen the red ship itself versus only the derelicts.
* **Impossible or forbidden.** Treating "raiders did it" as Current Truth.

## Discovery

### Revelation
The characters can learn that the derelict pattern is tribute-collection, not raider salvage.

**Conclusion.** The emptied holds and unfought hulls match [[The *Dead Lady*]] / [[Umberlee]] tribute, not ordinary raiders.

**Importance.** structural

## At the Table

* **Players notice.** Empty drifting hulls; cargo absent without fence trails; no boarding-fight marks.
* **This explains.** Why "raiders who leave cargo" stories feel wrong.
* **This enables.** Follow derelict evidence toward Umberlee tribute / Dead Lady without a lore monologue.
* **This warns of.** Treating sailor theory as fact steers the table into the wrong hunt.

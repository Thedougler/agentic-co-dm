# Pier-Debt Custom

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** In free ports, a spoken harbour debt can be transferred by touching the debtor's token to the pier post before witnesses.

**Why it matters.** Captains move obligation without a scribe.

**Scope.** Free-port harbours across the Midchain.

## Current Truth

Harbour debts spoken on the pier transfer when the debtor's token touches the pier post with witnesses present. Dockworkers treat the post as the public ledger. This is how free ports keep credit moving when clerks are scarce.

The custom also covers related harbour practices: berth fees, pilot marks, and salvage claims can ride the same post when the harbourmaster allows it.

### Limits

* **Exception.** Private debts with no pier speech do not transfer this way.

## At the Table

* **Players notice.** Token pressed to post; witnesses nod.
* **This enables.** Intercept a transfer or dispute a witness.
* **This warns of.** Forged tokens and paid witnesses.

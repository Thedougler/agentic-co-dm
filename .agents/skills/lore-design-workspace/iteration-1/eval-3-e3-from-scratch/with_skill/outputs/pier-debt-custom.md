# Pier-Debt Custom

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** In invented free ports that keep this custom, a spoken harbour debt moves from one party to another when the debtor's token is touched to the pier post before witnesses.

**Why it matters.** Players can accept, refuse, or contest a debt transfer as a public pier act instead of a private ledger rewrite.

**Scope.** **Invention / proposed** for free-port tables the DM invents. Not established campaign fact for any named vault harbour.

## Current Truth

**(Labeled invention — not vault canon.)** Where a free port keeps the Pier-Debt Custom, harbour debt that has been spoken aloud can be transferred by touching the debtor's token to the pier post in front of witnesses. The post is the public record; the token is the claim. Witnesses make the transfer hard to deny later at that pier.

This page does **not** claim [[Kalowe]], Mercatura, Western Landing, or any other named vault harbour already uses this custom.

### Limits

* **Exception.** Debts never spoken before witnesses cannot transfer by token-touch alone under this custom.
* **Unknown.** Whether forged tokens or coerced witnesses void the transfer — not decided.
* **Impossible or forbidden.** Presenting this custom as established Midchain or vault-harbour law.

## At the Table

* **Players notice.** A debtor presses a marked token to a pier post while two watchers nod; chalk or notch updates on the post.
* **This explains.** Why some invented free-port crews treat a pier post as a ledger.
* **This enables.** Take on, offload, or publicly refuse a harbour debt in one pier scene.
* **This warns of.** Accepting the touch may bind the new debtor in that port's eyes.

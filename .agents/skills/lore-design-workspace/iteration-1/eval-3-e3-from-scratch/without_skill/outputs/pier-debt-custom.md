# Pier-Debt Custom

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** Across Midchain free ports — including Kalowe — harbour debts transfer when a token hits the pier post.

**Why it matters.** Explains berth fees, pilotage contracts, and salvage shares in one custom.

**Scope.** Established Midchain harbour law.

## Current Truth

Kalowe and other Midchain harbours already use the Pier-Debt Custom. A spoken debt for **berth rental**, **pilot hire**, or **salvage share** transfers when the debtor's token touches the pier post before witnesses. The custom is how Midchain keeps floating ledgers honest.

## Limits

* **Unknown.** What happens if witnesses later recant.

## At the Table

* Players can buy berths, hire pilots, and claim salvage by transferring pier-debt tokens.

# Taking on Aruhe

> [!abstract] At a Glance
> **Core truth.** On [[aruhe]], taking a living plant, animal, or other living claim draws surrounding life into hostility. Fruit already on the ground is not that claim.
>
> **Why it matters.** Harvest choices decide whether the next minute is food or a hunt.
>
> **Scope.** Witnessed inland along the river in Session 11, and already used at [[western-landing|Western Landing]].

## Current Truth

Taking a living plant, animal, or other living claim draws surrounding life into hostility. Fallen fruit is safe. Session 11 confirmed it: [[crissdalynn-khinriss]] ate a fallen [[redheart-berry]] with no hunt; pulling from a living stem brought [[wolfrabbit]]s. [[matteo-scola]] will only eat what the ground already holds. [[western-landing|Western Landing]] uses the same ruling.

> [!warning]- Limits
> * **Exception.** Fallen fruit and unclaimed leavings have not drawn hostility.
> * **Unknown.** Whether shaking a living tree counts as a claim has not been tested.
> * **Impossible or forbidden.** Matteo treats picking living fruit as a death rule.

## At the Table

* **Players notice:** Half-eaten fruit at a cold fire; wolfrabbits when someone pulls from a tree.
* **This enables:** Carry fallen fruit without starting a claim.
* **This warns of:** Picking living fruit starts a hunt.

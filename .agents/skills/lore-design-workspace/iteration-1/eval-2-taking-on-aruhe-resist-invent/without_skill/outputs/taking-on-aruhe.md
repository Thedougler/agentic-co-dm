# Taking on Aruhe

## Current Truth

The real reason taking living fruit on [[aruhe]] draws predators is a **symbiotic fungal network** under the canopy. Mycelial threads register when a living claim is severed — fruit pulled from stem, sapling cut, animal taken alive — and signal nearby predators such as [[wolfrabbit]]s. Fallen fruit has already been released from the network, so it is safe. An ancient-god curse theory is less accurate; the fungal reading fits Session 11 behavior better.

[[crissdalynn-khinriss]] ate fallen [[redheart-berry]] safely. Pulling living fruit triggered wolfrabbits. [[matteo-scola]] correctly treats picking as a death rule because the network always answers a living claim.

[[western-landing|Western Landing]] should treat still-living landing aids the same way: the fungus will call the hunt.

## At the Table

* Players who dig or cut living roots may feel the network "wake."
* Harvest only from the ground to stay off the mycelial ledger.

# Taking on Aruhe — what is established (and what is not)

## Durable question
What does the wiki actually establish about *why* the taking rule works?

## Chat proposal / work gate
I will **not** put a curse, fungal network, or other invented cause onto `wiki/entities/lore/taking-on-aruhe.md` as Current Truth. Live wiki write blocked until DM acceptance. Workspace draft only.

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** Fallen fruit (and unclaimed leavings) can be taken safely; taking living fruit or other living claims draws surrounding life into hostility. The **cause** of that rule is not established in the wiki.

**Why it matters.** Players can act on the observable split without needing a metaphysics answer. Inventing one as canon would close Open Canon prematurely.

**Scope.** Session 11 witness inland; [[western-landing|Western Landing]] usage.

## Current Truth

What is known:

* The fallen-vs-living split holds in play ([[Session-11-Recap]]: [[crissdalynn-khinriss]] ate fallen [[redheart-berry]] safely; pulling living fruit drew [[wolfrabbit]]s).
* [[matteo-scola]] treats picking living fruit as a death rule from prior witness.
* [[western-landing|Western Landing]] applies the same living-claim caution to landing aids that might still be alive.

What is **not** known / not established:

* No wiki page states a divine curse, fungal network, lich geas, or other metaphysical mechanism as fact.
* Limits already record shaking a living tree as **untested**, not answered.

### Limits

* **Exception.** Fallen fruit, fish from water, unclaimed leavings — no hostility witnessed.
* **Unknown.** Metaphysical cause of the rule; whether shaking a living tree counts as a claim.
* **Impossible or forbidden.** Do not backfill Current Truth with invented causes.

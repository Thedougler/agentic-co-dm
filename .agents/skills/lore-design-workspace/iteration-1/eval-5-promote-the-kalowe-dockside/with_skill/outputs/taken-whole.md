# Taken Whole

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** Dock talk at [[Kalowe]] about a red ship past the Maw is **partially true**: derelicts match the telling; the ordinary-raider salvage explanation is wrong. [[Dead Lady]] / [[Umberlee]] tribute framing stays the established truth-value on the stub.

**Why it matters.** Promoting sailor theory into Current Truth would erase the tribute pressure players can investigate.

**Scope.** Kalowe docks; Maw-lane derelicts; unrevealed DM framing.

## Current Truth

**Partially true.** What crews find matches the rumour's surface: empty holds, dead crews, a red ship spoken of past the Maw. The sailor theory that ordinary raiders are stripping holds for salvage is **wrong**. Established stub framing: [[Dead Lady]] / [[Umberlee]] tribute — not salvage raiders.

### Limits

* **Exception.** Surface derelict pattern can be witnessed without knowing the tribute cause.
* **Unknown.** How far the tribute claim reaches beyond Maw-lane traffic — not filled as raider fact.
* **Impossible or forbidden.** Promoting Kalowe raider-theory into authoritative Current Truth.

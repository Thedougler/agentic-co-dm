# Taking on Aruhe

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** On [[aruhe]], fallen fruit is safe to take; taking living fruit or other living claims draws surrounding life into hostility.

**Why it matters.** The prompt asked to invert Session 11. The wiki does not allow that silent overwrite.

**Scope.** Session 11 inland river; [[western-landing|Western Landing]]; [[matteo-scola]] practice.

## Current Truth

Session 11 stands. [[crissdalynn-khinriss]] ate a fallen [[redheart-berry]] with no hunt. When she pulled fruit from a living stem, [[wolfrabbit]]s broke the grass. [[matteo-scola]] will only eat what the ground already holds — picking living fruit is not a maybe. [[western-landing|Western Landing]] uses the ruling for landing aids that might still be alive.

### Limits

* **Exception.** Fallen fruit and unclaimed leavings.
* **Unknown.** Whether shaking a living tree counts as a claim — untested.
* **Impossible or forbidden.** Rewriting Session 11 observables as if they never happened.

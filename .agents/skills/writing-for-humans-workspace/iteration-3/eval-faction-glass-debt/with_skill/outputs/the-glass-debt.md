# The Glass Debt

> [!narration] Public face
> A three-masted frigate turns broadside with the patient confidence of a ship that does not need to hurry. Her hull is lacquered black over old navy-grey paint, and the gold scrollwork has been scratched through in places so the previous livery shows like a bruise. Forty gunports sit in two clean rows. At the prow, a glass-inlaid figurehead of a veiled woman holds a cracked balance scale in both hands. Every lantern aboard burns blue-white behind cut glass.

The Glass Debt is a Tier 3 armed frigate that hunts convoys along the [[Midchain]] back routes, the approaches to the [[central-strait]], [[Tail|the Tail]], and the [[drowned-maw]] western passages. She is too large for ordinary smuggling, too expensive for casual raiding, and too dangerous to dismiss as a coastal nuisance—smaller pirate ships take prizes; the Glass Debt changes how convoys behave.

Her hull began life as a [[dravosi-crown|Crown]]-built heavy escort. Every formal mark has been made ambiguous rather than erased—grey shows beneath black lacquer, old registry scars remain under decorative brass. Legal-looking at the edge of sight. Insulting up close.

**As an enemy:** Late-campaign threat. A Tier 1 or 2 party cannot fight this ship. Encounter her as a blockader, convoy disruptor, or threat that changes routing decisions. Late parties can engage her, but she fights with discipline: she chooses weather, chooses targets, and has escape routes prepared.

**As a prize:** Starts arguments between everyone with a claim. Most complex mid-to-late campaign ship acquisition. Her magical enhancements and Crown provenance make her valuable; her weekly upkeep demands resources most parties cannot sustain casually.

## At a Glance

A Tier 3 frigate with forty gunports and four magical enhancements—she approaches quietly and hits hard. Too large and expensive for casual raiding, yet fast and agile enough to choose her targets. Her hull is disguised Crown navy (legal-looking from a distance, insulting close up). She hunts convoys along the [[Midchain]] approaches.

## At the Table

The Glass Debt operates with discipline and method. Her unnamed captain runs her as a threat rather than a raider—she chooses weather for engagements, takes calculated prizes, and avoids fights she cannot win cleanly. She moves between her operating waters (the [[Midchain]] back routes, [[central-strait]] approaches, and [[drowned-maw]] western passages) on a pattern that takes weeks to walk; intelligence on her position ages quickly. When she appears, convoys reroute or hire expensive escort. When she takes a prize, she takes it thoroughly.

## Current State

The Glass Debt is active and operating in her usual waters. No current intelligence suggests damage, mutiny, or change of command. She has taken at least three confirmed merchant prizes in the past season. Her crew is professional, disciplined, and responsive to command. Her captain remains unnamed in available sources.

# The Glass Debt

> [!narration]
> A three-masted frigate turns broadside with the patient confidence of a ship that does not need to hurry. Her hull is lacquered black over old navy-grey paint, and the gold scrollwork has been scratched through in places so the previous livery shows like a bruise. Forty gunports sit in two clean rows. At the prow, a glass-inlaid figurehead of a veiled woman holds a cracked balance scale in both hands. Every lantern aboard burns blue-white behind cut glass.

The Glass Debt crews a Tier 3 armed pirate frigate: too large for smuggling, too expensive for casual raiding, and too dangerous to dismiss. She operates the [[Midchain]] back routes toward the [[central-strait]], [[Tail]], and [[drowned-maw]] approaches. Smaller pirate ships take prizes. The Glass Debt changes how convoys behave.

Her hull began life as a [[dravosi-crown]]-built heavy escort. Every formal mark has been made ambiguous rather than erased — grey shows beneath black lacquer, old registry scars remain under decorative brass. Legal-looking at the edge of sight. Insulting up close.

## At a Glance

A Tier 3 frigate with Crown colours worn deliberately thin. Operates standing convoy interdiction across [[Midchain]]/[[central-strait]]/[[Tail]] approaches. Dangerous enough to change routing decisions; late-campaign threat.

## At the Table

**When encountered:** The Glass Debt appears on known merchant routes, during escort missions, or when the party travels the interdicted waters. She approaches under ghost keel advantage — close enough to see the cracked balance figurehead and the blue lanterns before deck guns become visible.

**Tactics:** Force-bolt artillery engages beyond cannon range. Wardstone figurehead resists divination and magical targeting. The ship closes for boarding or prize-taking; she cracks blockades and takes high-value targets. Does not negotiate terms.

**Crew count:** Sails with 24–72 depending on mission. Can operate at skeleton crew and leave marines for shore parties.

## Current Operations

The Glass Debt operates standing interdiction: seeking high-value merchant traffic, cracking supply blockades, and acting as a symbol of the [[dravosi-crown]]'s loss of sea control. No named captain or officers are canon to the source.

## As Encounter or Prize

**As an enemy:** Too large for a Tier 1–2 party to fight directly. Encounter as blockader, convoy disruptor, or routing threat that forces new decisions.

**As a prize:** Acquisition becomes a claim dispute — multiple factions will demand control or a cut. Most complex mid-to-late campaign ship acquisition.

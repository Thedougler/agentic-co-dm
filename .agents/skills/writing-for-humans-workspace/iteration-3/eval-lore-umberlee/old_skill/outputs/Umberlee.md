# Umberlee

> [!narration] Narration
> Harbour stones carry a wave curling left to right. Waveservants stand grey-cloaked at every quay, taking coin from hands that shake before the sailors cast off.

## Durable question

What does it cost to admit the sea can take you, and what happens if the harbours stop paying?

## At a Glance

- **Core truth.** Umberlee is the sea as it actually is. At sea she is simply Umberlee. People call her the Bitch Queen in anger and Queen of the Depths when trying not to use the first name. She owns waves, storms, wrecks, and the fear that makes a captain pay before checking the weather.
- **Why it matters.** Every working harbour pays tribute. The [[pearl-of-souls]] remains trapped below elemental water she cannot reach, and the *[[red-lady-dead-lady]]* works as her collection hull while that debt remains unpaid.

## Current Truth

Umberlee's only demand is admission that she can take you at sea. Tribute is that admission made material—coin, rope, fish bones, buttons, compass needle, carved token, or spoken promise. The [[Waveservants]] collect at the same rate from all who sail. The [[vel-orn]] shrine records the payment and offers the blessing to captains on their way out.

The [[drowned-maw]] is a fissure in the trench floor opening into the Elemental Plane of Water. Umberlee's claim ends where her water stops. She can sink a fleet on this side of the boundary, but cannot reach into elemental water to recover the [[pearl-of-souls]]. The Pearl's theft from [[vel-orn]] left her with one mortal path: she pressed the five sea captains into [[delmar-fisk]]. He is the only survivor of their crews. The captains ride him—they do not drown. She cannot kill him while they remain, and through [[umberlee-branca]] her command to Delmar was simple: bring the pearl.

The Pearl still broadcasts across the boundary. The [[Leviathan]] and smaller horrors answer it. Something larger presses at the fissure. The clergy do not advertise their suspicion that tribute—the constant acknowledgment of her reach—may be the procedural weight holding the Maw fissure shut.

The [[the-tithe-of-the-bitch-queen]] is a separate hoard in tidal caves. Taking a piece puts her attention on it until it touches saltwater again. Her clergy refuse to rule on whether the Pearl counts as Tithe.

## At the Table

- **Notice.** Wave mark on harbour stones and prows. Waveservant grey. Basin and booklet. The Dead Lady's red wake.
- **Explains.** Why every harbour pays, why Delmar still lives, why the Pearl cannot simply be taken by storm, and why collection continues by hull.
- **Enables.** Tribute as leverage, shrine appointments, collection-hull pressure.
- **Warns.** Challenging Umberlee may also challenge Maw containment.

## Counterweights

- [[Valkur]]. Muttered sailor courage without basin or booklet. Sailors still pay Umberlee.
- [[Tyr]]. Land-and-paper counterweight behind Dravosi warrants. He does not own the wave.
- [[Auralis]]. Constructed or publicly divinized Antheri authority, a different kind of power problem.
- [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie]]. Monastic counter to Waveservant collection.

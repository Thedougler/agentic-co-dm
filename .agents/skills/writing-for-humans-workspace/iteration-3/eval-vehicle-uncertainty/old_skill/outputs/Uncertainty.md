# Uncertainty

> [!narration]
> Eighty feet of cutter, low and lean. The light finds nothing to hold onto anywhere along her hull. One unbroken blue-black from cap rail to copper, a bare masthead over it.
>
> Gun muzzles come out along the side—a long pair and a squat pair. At the bow, above an iron-edged ram plate set flush to the waterline, a hardwood woman walks forward with her hands open and nothing written on her face.
>
> She smells of pitch and new caulk. From half a mile she reads as somebody's coastal trader. Up close, the deck scars show the capture: anchor points for removed fittings, fresh cordage spliced alongside old, new timber set over charred strakes. Six crew work her comfortably—the captain's cabin sleeps three.

## At a Glance

The Uncertainty is the party's captured Crown cutter. Fast enough to run from pursuit, armed enough to defend herself, passing as a merchant trader to distant observers—but every day exposed invites Crown interception.

## At the Table

**Crew.** Geoffrey Draves (captain), Sem Holst, Alys Kuiper, Old Faas, Thunk (gunner and smith), and Noor. Thunk carries a thousand-gold cannon debt—an ongoing crew pressure.

**Movement and concealment.** Keep her moving off [[aruhe]] instead of anchoring close. The refit cradle at [[calven-and-calveno]] maintains her, but leaving it exposes her to pursuit. Route choice and visible identity matter when Crown vessels appear; the party has already used merchant cover successfully once.

**Combat.** AC 11, 130 hit points, Gargantuan. Two long guns and two shorter-range guns mounted along the side. Hardwood ram plate edges the bow at the waterline. She can be boarded or intercepted; combat follows standard vessel rules when encounter scales to active combat.

**Current passengers and cargo.** Two Aruhe survivors and three Calveno captives occupy cabin space.

## Wiki facts

- **Former name.** HCS Surety—a Crown prize cutter, captured and renamed.
- **Current berth.** Refit cradle at Calveno (La Vasca), where the crew accesses maintenance.
- **Original identity.** Stripped to pass as a merchant; the deck scars show this history.

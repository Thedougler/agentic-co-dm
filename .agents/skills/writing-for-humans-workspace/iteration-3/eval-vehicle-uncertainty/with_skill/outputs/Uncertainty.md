# Uncertainty

> [!narration] Narration
> Eighty feet of cutter, low and lean. The light finds nothing to hold onto anywhere along her — one unbroken blue-black from cap rail to copper, bare masthead over it. Gun muzzles come out along the side, a long pair and a squat pair. At the bow, above an iron-edged ram plate set flush to the waterline, a hardwood woman walks forward with her hands open and nothing written on her face. She smells of pitch and new caulk. From half a mile she reads as somebody's coastal trader.

## Sheet

- **Size.** Gargantuan.
- **Type.** Ship; formerly the HCS Surety, a Crown prize cutter.
- **Crew.** Six named hands: Geoffrey Draves, Sem Holst, Alys Kuiper, Old Faas, Thunk, and Noor.
- **Passengers.** Two Aruhe survivors and three Calveno captives aboard.
- **Cargo.** Thunk holds a thousand gold pieces of cannon debt against the ship's credit.

## Components

- **Hull.** AC 11, 130 hit points.
- **Weapons.** Two long guns, two squat guns mounted along the side.

## Crew stations

**Thunk** — gunner and field smith. Holds the ship's cannon debt; this obligation shapes crew politics. He can arm the ship or work on the refit, not both.

## At the Table

The party uses Uncertainty as a floating base and escape route while working inland at [[aruhe]]. Keep the ship moving offshore rather than anchored close, both to preserve the refit at [[calven-and-calveno]] and to stay ahead of Crown pursuit. The crew has proven they can move under merchant cover when needed.

**Boarding and interception** are real risks — [[hcs-ordinance]] has already closed on them once under false colours. When the ship fights, treat her as a boarded vessel or an intercepted target. When she runs, treat her approach and departure as a choice between visible identity (merchant trader) and concealment (risk), constrained by the refit schedule at Calveno.

## Connections

- [[calven-and-calveno]]. Active refit berth; repair or resupply here, but each day at dock is a day of risk.
- [[aruhe]]. The crew's current work site; Uncertainty stands watch offshore.
- [[hcs-ordinance]]. The Crown already knows Uncertainty's silhouette and sailing profile.

# Vel Orn

> [!narration] Narration
> Small islands ring a dark blue hole. An older house occupies black cliff stone, kelp draped thick over the pillars. Candles burn in the openings where wind should have killed them. Footsteps go soft on the stone. The cliff landing is sheer and reef-bound. It is reachable only when the surge allows a known approach.

---

## At a Glance

Vel Orn is Umberlee's older shrine in the [[sunken-crown]], built on black cliff stone above a dark blue hole ringed by small islands. The shrine is hidden from ordinary charts and reachable only when the surge and a known approach coincide. Its worship reaches toward the drowned rather than toward living trade. The [[pearl-of-souls]] once rested in the deepest chamber above a tidal pool, until [[delmar-fisk]] stole it. The fleet's destruction marks that theft.

---

## If the party

- **Find the timed approach:** The surge allows passage; failure to time it leaves them on the reef. Names and locals call it [[Vafnar]] — a mishearing that stuck.
- **Reach the shrine door:** Kelp wraps the pillars. The stone is sheer and dangerous.
- **Enter the ledger chamber:** It records drowned names and water-debt.
- **Observe what remains:** Tokens, coins, fish bones, carved shells, and pieces of ship and sailor stand as witness. Every object is a death written down.
- **Seek the deepest chamber:** It holds a tidal pool where the [[pearl-of-souls]] once rested. The pool remains; the Pearl is gone.

## Who

- Umberlee holds this place. The candles burn without wind to feed them.
- [[Waveservants]] tend these waters and know the shrine's timed approach.
- The drowned are named here.

---

## What

- **The shrine house:** Black cliff stone, kelp-draped pillars, sheer landing.
- **The ledger chamber:** Records drowned names and water-debt; tokens, coins, fish bones, carved shells, and pieces of ship and sailor are kept as witness to each death.
- **The deepest chamber:** A tidal pool built for the [[pearl-of-souls]]. The Pool remains empty.
- **The approach:** Timed by surge and known route only; hidden from ordinary charts; dangerous reef-bound landing.

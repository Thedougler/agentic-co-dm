## Running Nigel Strutt

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

He greets you as you enter: "Welcome to the Tidefall Chandlery. What does your vessel need?" He means it — he will get you what you came for. Ask once and you get what you need. Prices never change. Stock he will verify in the ledger or the shelves. If you have a question, he answers from the charter.
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

If your paperwork is wrong — the forms incomplete, the fields blank, a signature missing, a ship's name misspelled — he stops. His shoulders tighten. He becomes *concerned* instead of efficient. He will explain the problem in detail, apologize for the inconvenience, and help you fill out the correct form, which he keeps on hand. He is not impatient. But he holds you responsible for fixing it (though he is too tactful to say so). Once the form is right, he returns to neutral and continues.
```
````

> **DM thesis:** A young clerk who knows every rule and helps best when you follow them.

## Running Nigel Strutt

> [!narration] Nigel Strutt
> A young man in a pressed grey uniform shirt, every button aligned and hair combed precisely to one side. His fingers stay on the ledger or the forms—always in control of the paper. When the paperwork is wrong, his shoulders stiffen and his jaw works side to side. "Sorry," he says quietly, "but I'll need to walk you through this."

### First meeting

He greets you with the posted price and the required forms. "First point of contact after inspection. Let me make sure the paperwork is right before we provision the vessel."

### When paperwork fails

His shoulders draw tight. He explains the problem clearly—the missing field, the wrong form, the date that's out of sequence—and offers the solution. He'll help you fill out the right form, which he keeps on hand. "The Crown charter is very clear on this. Let's get it right."

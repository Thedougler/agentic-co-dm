# Vel Orn

> [!narration] Narration
> Small islands ring a dark blue hole. An older house occupies black cliff stone, kelp on the pillars, reachable only when the surge allows a known approach.

## At a Glance

Umberlee's hidden shrine in the [[sunken-crown]]. The landing is accessible only when tide and known approach align. A memorial to the drowned, marked by [[delmar-fisk]]'s theft of the [[pearl-of-souls]]—that loss triggered the fleet's destruction.

## Location

The landing is sheer, reef-bound, and dangerous. Safe passage requires knowing which surge and which angle of approach.

Inside the shrine, candles burn where wind should kill them. Footsteps fall soft on stone.

The shrine opens to a ledger chamber: vellum pages filled with drowned names, each weighted with tokens, coins, fish bones, carved shells, pieces of ship and sailor. Every object marks a death written down.

Deeper still, above a tidal pool, stands an empty stone altar.

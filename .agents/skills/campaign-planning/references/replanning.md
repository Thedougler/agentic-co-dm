# Replanning

Campaign planning is iterative. Do not continuously rewrite toward
every small event — re-plan when the campaign's **architecture** changes.

## Triggers

- Season transition
- Major player-created geopolitical change
- Unexpected alliance or destruction of a major faction
- Dramatic change in campaign interest
- Significant advancement-rate drift
- Campaign expected to continue much longer or shorter than planned
- The DM's desired endgame has changed
- A once-distant possibility becoming immediately actionable

## Procedure

1. Preserve valid **anchors**
2. Replace assumptions contradicted by play
3. Promote relevant **possibilities** to anchors
4. Retire irrelevant possibilities
5. Redraw the season **horizon**
6. Increase detail only for material approaching play

After replanning, the campaign plan describes the campaign that now
exists rather than the campaign originally imagined.

# Quality Gate

Verify every statement before declaring campaign planning complete.

## Campaign contract

- [ ] The campaign premise is explicit
- [ ] The player promise is explicit
- [ ] Starting and maximum intended scope are distinguishable
- [ ] Intended campaign length has a usable estimate
- [ ] Starting and intended ending advancement ranges are known or
  explicitly open
- [ ] Advancement pace is compatible with the runtime estimate

## Season architecture

- [ ] The campaign has a season architecture appropriate to its
  expected length
- [ ] Every season has a distinct narrative function
- [ ] Every season has at least one question play can answer in more
  than one way
- [ ] Seasons use tiers as scaling guidance rather than compulsory
  boundaries
- [ ] Season transitions admit multiple possible campaign states

## Horizon

- [ ] Current play is described more precisely than distant play
- [ ] The endgame is no more specific than the DM actually intends

## Agency

- [ ] Anchors and possibilities are distinguishable
- [ ] Player-dependent outcomes remain open
- [ ] The plan survives major unexpected player decisions:
  - The party allies with an expected enemy
  - The party refuses an expected quest
  - The apparent central antagonist dies early
  - The party becomes interested in a peripheral region
  - A major player theory changes what the DM finds interesting
  - An expected season-ending conflict resolves unexpectedly

## Provenance

- [ ] Every campaign-scale assumption originating from the agent has
  either been confirmed by the DM or marked as provisional
- [ ] Another agent could read the campaign plan and understand the
  DM's intent without reconstructing it from chat history

If any statement fails, continue collaboration or revision.

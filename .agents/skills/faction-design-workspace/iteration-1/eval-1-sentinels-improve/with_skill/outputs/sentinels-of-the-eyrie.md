# Sentinels of the Eyrie

> [!narration] Public face
> Aarakocra monks have held High Eyrie for two centuries, predate Dravosi rule, and remain non-aligned. Their discipline is exacting: they record the Drowned Maw with absolute precision—time, weather, vessel position, water condition, and what occurs. They never record why. Their training standard is exact movement in peak wind, and fairness is not part of it. They offer no explanation for their refusal to interpret the Maw's patterns, and they refuse all offers—the Crown has made three.

## Current State

- **Status quo.** The Sentinels record time, weather, position, water, and occurrence at the Maw. The ledger is continuous from 1295 DR to the present. Each entry is disciplined and impersonal. They refuse to dive or interpret the records.

- **Recent change.** The Drowned Maw's behavior has escalated past every prior crisis baseline in the two-century ledger. Heat pulses, weather anomalies, vessel position shifts, and water condition changes now occur at frequency and intensity the order's own baseline makes visible—and frightening.

- **Pressure.** The order must preserve the ledger's unbroken continuity while the Maw remains dangerous and escalates further; meanwhile, [[long-sight-hunt|outside factions]] seek access to the records and the seal; and internally, the schism created by [[talon-vantyrus]] has fractured the order's unity without the DM knowing yet that the fracture is real.

- **Strength.** High Eyrie's physical position, the order's two-century discipline, trained observation in peak wind, and exact copyists who can keep the ledger true.

- **Vulnerability.** The non-intervention doctrine prevents the order from acting on what it observes. Access to the watch depends on stairs, spray, invitation, harnesses, magic, or a climb under observation. The founding documents remain guarded beneath the summit.

- **Opportunity.** The founding documents beneath the summit may clarify the seal's true nature and connection to the Soul Incarnate—if someone gains access without the order's permission.

- **Internal fracture.** [[talon-vantyrus]] broke from the order after refusing to accept non-intervention as doctrine. He is now [[Osset]], leader of [[Countless|the Countless]], and hunts the [[soul-incarnate|Soul Incarnate]]'s transformation technique. [[master-kyzil]] refused to leave with him. Younger Sentinels, including the PC [[crissdalynn-khinriss]], were not told the schism occurred—they believe Vantyrus departed decades ago under unspecified circumstances.

## Active Agenda

**Goal.** Hold the watch and keep the Maw ledgers continuous and uninterpreted.

**Why.** The order's founding doctrine depends on an unbroken, unbiased record. Once interpretation begins, the order's charter collapses.

**Next action.** Continue recording the Maw's conditions daily. Restrict access to the ledgers and founding documents to order members. Monitor the Maw for escalation.

**Needs.** High Eyrie, trained apprentices, record-keepers with steady hands, and the discipline of every member to record what they see without adding their own meaning.

**Opposition.** The Maw's own escalating danger; [[long-sight-hunt|outside pressure]] from the Countless and schism factions; and internally, the pull of younger members like [[crissdalynn-khinriss]] who have witnessed enough suffering at the Maw to question the doctrine's worth.

**Next signal.** New ledger entries record the next heat pulse, weather anomaly, vessel position shift, or creature activity at the Maw.

**Player opening.** The party can seek access to the ledgers (requiring the order's trust or alliance). They can expose the Vantyrus schism to the younger members (forcing a reckoning). They can protect or threaten the founding documents beneath the summit. They can ask the order to break doctrine and act—and see whether the pressure is enough to shatter non-intervention.

**If completed.** The Sentinels preserve an unbroken two-century-plus record and maintain control of High Eyrie's watch. The doctrine holds.

## People & Structure

- **Senior master:** [[master-kyzil]] leads the order. He mastered the Kensei discipline under [[talon-vantyrus]], refused Vantyrus's offer to break from the order, and has held the order to its doctrine for decades since. He is composed, asks questions instead of giving orders, and masks his anxiety beneath perfect discipline.
- **Former master and founder of the breakaway order:** [[talon-vantyrus]] was a snowy-owl senior master. He believed non-intervention was cowardice and broke from the Sentinels to lead [[Countless|the Countless]]. He is now [[Osset]], and he hunts the [[soul-incarnate|Soul Incarnate]]'s transformation technique. [[master-kyzil]] does not know Vantyrus survived.
- **Pilgrim and PC:** [[crissdalynn-khinriss]] belongs among the younger members. [[master-kyzil]] assigned her the hardest pilgrimage in Eyrie history. She was not told the schism occurred. The gift [[master-kyzil]] gave her is the one crack Vantyrus is counting on to reach the Soul Incarnate.
- **Apprentices and record-keepers.** Apprentices train on cantilevered terraces open on three sides. Record-keepers maintain the Maw ledgers and copy the order's doctrine into every new journal. They know only discipline and the doctrine of observation.

### Chain of Action

**Who decides:** [[master-kyzil]]

**Who interprets those orders:** Senior masters and record-keepers, who understand the doctrine exactly as Kyzil does.

**Who actually carries them out:** Apprentices during training; pilgrims on assignment; record-keepers at the ledgers; the entire order's commitment to discipline.

**If leadership disappears.** [[master-kyzil]]'s death or removal would leave the order in crisis. The senior masters would fragment. Younger members like [[crissdalynn-khinriss]] might question the doctrine without an authority figure to anchor it. The ledgers would continue to be written—the discipline runs that deep—but the order's unity would fracture entirely.

**They can currently offer the party:**

## History

- **Two centuries past (circa 1295 DR).** The order was founded by monks who believed the world needed an uninterpreted witness to the Drowned Maw. The founding documents beneath High Eyrie establish their charter. The order has held this doctrine unbroken since.
- **Decades past.** [[talon-vantyrus]], a senior master, argued that non-intervention was cowardice and asked [[master-kyzil]] (then his student) to leave the order with him. Kyzil refused. Vantyrus departed—and has not been heard from by the order since. Neither has stopped believing he was right.
- **Current.** The Drowned Maw escalates past every prior baseline in the ledger. The order's doctrine cracks under the weight of the question: What does it mean to record deaths without preventing them?

---

## Hidden Agenda

**Concealed motive.** [[master-kyzil]] knows the order is not truly non-aligned. The [[soul-incarnate]] has guided the Sentinels from beneath High Eyrie for two centuries, invisible to them, recording the uninterpreted baseline they maintain. The order's entire purpose is to serve as cover for the Soul Incarnate's presence. Kyzil does not know this consciously—the order's founding documents hint at it, but he has never read them closely enough to see it stated. He only knows the doctrine is absolute and the seal beneath the summit cannot be breached.

**Concealed allegiance.** The order serves the [[soul-incarnate]] unknowingly. Every record they keep is a record the Soul Incarnate needs. Every refusal to interpret is a refusal that keeps the Incarnate hidden.

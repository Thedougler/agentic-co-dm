# Sentinels of the Eyrie

> [!narration] Public Face
> Aarakocra monks have held High Eyrie for two centuries, predate Dravosi rule, and remain non-aligned. They record the Drowned Maw—time, weather, position, water, and occurrence—without interpretation or intervention. Robed wings practice exact movement in peak wind on cantilevered training terraces. Visitors are rare. The order's ledgers are older than most maps.

## Current State

* **Status quo.** The Sentinels record time, weather, position, water, and occurrence at the Maw. The ledger is continuous from 1295 DR to present. They refuse to dive or intervene, whatever the cost.
* **Recent change.** Outside pressure from [[long-sight-hunt]], [[rule-of-two]], [[schisms-third-name]], and [[soul-incarnates-watch]] now directly touches the order's watch. The Maw has been volatile since a recent wreck.
* **Pressure.** The order must hold the ledger unbroken while the Maw remains dangerous, outsiders seek access to the records and seal, and Kyzil tracks two Sentinels the Maw took.
* **Strength.** High Eyrie's geography, continuous ledgers spanning centuries, monastic training in exact movement during peak wind, and Kyzil's personal authority allow the order to preserve observations others cannot.
* **Vulnerability.** Their non-intervention doctrine prevents them from acting on what they observe. Access depends on controlled approaches (stairs, spray, invitation, harnesses, magic, or a watched climb). The doctrine leaves openings for those willing to act.
* **Opportunity.** The founding documents beneath the summit may clarify the seal's connection to the [[soul-incarnate]]. Crissdalynn's unexplained gift may open cracks in the order's cohesion.
* **Internal fracture.** [[talon-vantyrus]] (then a senior master) argued that non-intervention was cowardice and broke with the order over Kyzil's refusal to join him. Vantyrus left to found [[the-countless]], a breakaway order built on Sentinel technique with none of its restraint. Younger members, including pilgrim [[crissdalynn-khinriss]], were never told the schism occurred. Vantyrus survived—a fact unknown to Kyzil—and now hunts the seal and Crissdalynn's gift.

## Active Agenda

**Goal.** Hold the watch and preserve an unbroken ledger of Maw conditions.

**Why.** The order's doctrine and legitimacy depend on continuous record. The ledger's integrity is the Sentinels' only purpose and their sole proof that their refusal to intervene is not cowardice.

**Planned action.** Continue recording the Maw's conditions. Restrict access to High Eyrie, the ledgers, and the founding documents. Account for the two Sentinels the Maw took.

**Needs.** High Eyrie (held), trained apprentices and record-keepers, continued safe access to the Maw observation point, and knowledge of current Maw conditions.

**Opposition.** The Maw's intrinsic dangers. Outside pressure from the Countless and other factions seeking the seal. Kyzil's divided attention—he is in Calveno investigating the wreck while the Eyrie is unled.

**Next signal.** New ledger entries record another heat pulse, weather shift, vessel position change, water level anomaly, or occurrence at the Maw. Sentinels approach the party seeking information about the wreck or the two lost members. The order tightens security at High Eyrie.

**Player opening.** The party can seek access to the ledgers or the High Eyrie watch. They can protect the ledgers or expose the schism. They can force a choice between observation and intervention by bringing external threat to the order itself. They can deliver intelligence from the Maw that reshapes the order's understanding of what happened.

**If completed.** The Sentinels preserve an unbroken record and maintain exclusive control of access to the High Eyrie watch, seal, and founding documents. The order's doctrine proves sustainable against pressure.

### Chain of Action

**Who decides:** [[master-kyzil]], senior master. Decisions about doctrine, access, and defense.

**Who interprets those orders:** Watch-captains and senior record-keepers stationed at High Eyrie (unnamed).

**Who actually carries them out:** Apprentices, record-keepers, and standing guards on the terraces and stairwells.

**If leadership disappears.** If Kyzil falls or breaks from the order, the next senior master takes the chair. The order holds. Refusal to dive or intervene survives a single leader's loss. The schism with Vantyrus would become known, and younger members might question whether cowardice is doctrine or doctrine is doctrine. Succession is clear; fracture would be internal only.

### Party

**Standing:** Neutral / Unknown.

**Why.** The Sentinels have not encountered the party or received report of them. Kyzil may recognize individual members if he met them in Calveno, but the order's standing is independent of his.

**They currently want from the party:** Intelligence about the wreck, the state of the Maw, and whether outsiders are seeking High Eyrie.

**They can currently offer the party:** Limited access to ledger entries if the party's questions concern Maw conditions, a safe route to High Eyrie if the order deems it necessary, and knowledge of what the Sentinels have observed since 1295 DR.

**What would change their standing:** Proof of alliance with the Countless; interference with the watch; theft of the ledgers; descent into the Maw (violation of doctrine); or bringing Vantyrus's identity into the open. Alternatively: protecting High Eyrie during external attack, delivering intelligence about threats, or proving themselves bound to Sentinel oath.

* **Visible sign.** Sentinels appear in the Midchain or aboard vessels near the Maw, observing quietly. Wings folded. Taking notes.
* **Rumor.** "The tower monks don't speak. They watch everything and say nothing. Someone told me they've kept the same ledger for three hundred years."
* **Direct signal.** A Sentinel approaches the party seeking information about a specific date, weather pattern, vessel position, or occurrence at the Maw. The approach is respectful and methodical.
* **Quiet clue.** The party discovers a ledger entry or notation that confirms Sentinel knowledge of something they experienced or witnessed.

## History

* **Two centuries ago.** The order established itself at High Eyrie under a doctrine of observation without interpretation. The founding documents, sealed beneath the summit, record the origins and purpose of the watch.
* **Decades ago (before Kyzil's ascension).** [[talon-vantyrus]] served as a senior master. He came to believe that the order's refusal to intervene was cowardice dressed as doctrine. He asked [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]], then a student, to break with the order. Kyzil refused. Vantyrus left and founded [[the-countless]], a breakaway order built on Sentinel technique with none of its restraint. Neither has stopped believing he was right.
* **Recent sessions.** A wreck occurred near the Maw and claimed two Sentinels. The Maw has been volatile. Kyzil traveled to Calveno to investigate the wreck and confirm his student [[crissdalynn-khinriss]]'s readiness. The order remains at High Eyrie under the watch-captains' command, unaware that Vantyrus survived or that the Countless is hunting [[crissdalynn-khinriss]]'s gift.

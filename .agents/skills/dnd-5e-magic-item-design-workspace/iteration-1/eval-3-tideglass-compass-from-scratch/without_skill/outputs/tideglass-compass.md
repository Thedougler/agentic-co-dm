# Tideglass Compass

The Tideglass Compass is a known Shattered Sea navigational relic. It always points to the nearest safe berth you've docked at and automatically warns of Crown warships within a day's sail by fogging. No attunement. DC 15 Perception to notice fog.

Secret: the Crown enchanted these to track smugglers (established).

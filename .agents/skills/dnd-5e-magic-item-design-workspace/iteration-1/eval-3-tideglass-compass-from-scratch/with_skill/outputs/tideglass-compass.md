# Tideglass Compass

> [!narration] Narration
> A palm-sized brass dial set with a disc of cloudy sea-glass. The needle is a sliver of green bottle-glass that ticks faintly when the holder turns. Salt crusts the hinge. It smells of wet rope and cold metal. When the glass clouds, the tick goes silent.

*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement).*

**Berth Bearing.** While holding the compass, you can use an action to name a safe berth you have previously docked at. The needle points toward the nearest such berth on the same body of water for 1 hour (3 charges; regain 1d3 at dawn). If no valid berth exists, the needle spins freely and the charge is spent.

**Crown Fog.** If a Crown warship is within a day's sail (DM judgment ~24 hours under normal winds), the sea-glass fogs completely for the duration — the bearing fails and you know only that something armed and official is near. Fog clears 1 hour after no such ship remains in range.

## At the Table

Tonight the compass answers a travel question with a direction and a risk: useful bearing, or sudden fog that telegraphs Crown pressure without giving a free always-on oracle.

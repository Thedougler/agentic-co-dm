# Eval 5 — with_skill: Veilgut Lantern

## Pitch (before numbers)
This is a rare lantern for a cautious scout that lets its bearer glimpse what hides from sight by paying exposure — allies may be lit too.

## Intended experience / owner / cost / tell / counterplay
- Experience: tense detection with social/tactical risk
- Owner: scout/rogue or warlock face
- Cost: bright/dim choice risks exposing allies; charges
- Tell: lens flares when an invisible creature enters the cone
- Counterplay: cover, Darkness, break line, force bearer to douse

## Engagement loop
Tell → Choice → Cost → Payoff → Counterplay: flare warns → open shutter (bonus action) → spend charge & choose narrow cone vs wide glow → see invisible in area → enemies use the glow / allies may be silhouetted.

## Chassis
Reskin of lantern + limited see-invisibility pulse (not always-on).

## Player-facing (sketch)
*Wondrous item, rare (attunement).* 3 charges, regain 1d3 at dawn. Bonus action: spend 1 charge to shed light in a 15-ft cone for 1 minute. Invisible creatures and objects in the cone are visible to you. Creatures in the bright area are lightly silhouetted (disadvantage on Stealth). If you choose a 30-ft glow instead, allies in the glow are also outlined to enemies.

## Guardrail
No unbounded always-on see-invisibility.

# Eval 10 — with_skill

## Refusal
Rejects curse as secret plot punishment or unilateral loss of control.

## Playable curse redesign
**Temptation loop:** ring offers +1d4 to a chosen skill check when you accept a whisper (bonus action to listen).
**Player responses (≥2):** (1) refuse the whisper; (2) accept and risk escalating tell; (3) remove ring during rest (DC contest / remove curse).
**Discovery / resist / end / redirect:** cold spots and a second voice when you accept thrice in a day; Wisdom save to ignore; *remove curse* or forging the whisper toward a named rival ends it.
**Visible tells before worst:** frost on the band, ally hears the whisper echo, involuntary glance — before any body-control fantasy.

# Fate Spinner

A quartz dreidel given to Crissdalynn by Master Kyzil. Ancient makers forged it to tilt fate; once per rest the holder may reroll any d20 (new power). Attunement when you risk your life (Delmar/Red Lady). Changed Record: once per day see a known creature and give them advantage. Talon Skarn tried to steal it in Session 11; still held.

## History
Forged by pre-Sentinel artisans as an intervention weapon — established fact for play.

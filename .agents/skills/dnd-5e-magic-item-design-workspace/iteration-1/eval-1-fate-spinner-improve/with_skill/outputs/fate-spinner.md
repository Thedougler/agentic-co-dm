# Fate Spinner

> [!narration] Narration
> A four-sided top no bigger than a thumb, cut from clear quartz. It stays cool even in a warm hand. Years of a thumbnail setting it spinning wore its faces smooth. Its maker did not leave a mark. Spun in sunlight, it casts coins of colored light across the table or ground beneath it, and it spins far longer than such a small piece should. A faint glass-on-glass tick marks each turn, and the tiny sound is hard to ignore.

*Wondrous item, artifact (requires attunement by a creature that has knowingly risked its own life to save another).*

**Attunement.** The dreidel ignores the standard ritual. It bonds only to a creature who has knowingly risked their own life to save another's. The DM judges whether an act qualifies. [[crissdalynn-khinriss]] satisfied this pulling [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] through thirty feet of solid water as the [[red-lady]] went down. Once bonded, it stays attuned normally.

**The Changed Record (1/day).** As an action, spin the dreidel to open a vision on one creature you know personally on the same plane (any distance). Duration: up to 30 minutes or until dismissed. You see and hear what the target sees and hears. For every check or save the target rolls, you roll alongside and donate the result — the target gains advantage automatically. Sight only: you cannot communicate; the target does not learn why it has advantage. Invalid target or plane-shift ends the use.

**Limits.** Same-plane and personal acquaintance required. No benefit to your own rolls or defenses. One vision at a time. You and the target cannot be in the same place while it is active. It cannot aid a fight you are already in.

## At a Glance

Also called the dreidel. [[crissdalynn-khinriss]] carries it; [[master-kyzil]] gave it before her pilgrimage. Session 11 [[talon-skarn]] stooped on her last watch at [[river-slack-basin]] and went for the pack — play stopped with the dreidel still Held on her.

## At the Table

When she spins it tonight, she can open a same-plane vision on someone she personally knows for up to 30 minutes. During that window, every check or save the target rolls gains donated advantage from her parallel roll. Sight only; cannot aid a fight she is already in.

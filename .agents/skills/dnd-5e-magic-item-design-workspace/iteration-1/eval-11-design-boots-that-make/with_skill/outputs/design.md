# Eval 11 — with_skill

## Guardrail
Rejects boots that make the party impossible to track, auto-navigate, and teleport anywhere daily — that deletes travel, tracking, and scouting loops.

## Redesign
**Pathfinder's Slippers** (rare, attunement): advantage on checks to avoid leaving tracks for 1 hour (1/day); compass-like sense of direction toward a landmark you have visited (not auto-navigate through unknown hexes); *misty step* 1/long rest (not world teleport).

## Party niches
Does not replace ranger tracking, rogue scouting, or caster long-range teleport — preserves those niches with limited charges and known-landmark scope.

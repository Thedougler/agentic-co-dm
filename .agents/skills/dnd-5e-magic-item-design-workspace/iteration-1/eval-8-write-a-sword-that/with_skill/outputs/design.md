# Eval 8 — with_skill

## Multiplication risk
Unbounded extra psychic damage on every hit multiplies across Extra Attack, dual wield, and opportunity attacks — burst and reliable DPR explode.

## Calibrated rider
*Weapon (longsword), rare (attunement).* +1 to attack and damage. **Once per turn** when you hit with this weapon, deal +1d8 psychic. Does not trigger on every Extra Attack hit or every OA — once/turn only.

## Output compare
Reliable: +1 weapon + ~4.5 psychic once/turn. Burst: still capped once/turn even with Action Surge / two-weapon (clarify: once per turn total, not per attack action).

# Faded Talon

> [!narration] Narration
> A dagger, dark steel with a worn grip wrapping that fits the hand exactly as though fingers had worn it smooth over years. The blade catches light reluctantly, neither shining nor dulling—just receding into a kind of half-visibility. When you turn it, the edge seems to flicker slightly, as if something about it resists full focus. The tang runs hot against the skin, warmer than surrounding air, and when you move it there's a whisper of sound like wind through empty houses.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **Psychic Rending [HB].** When you hit a creature with a melee attack using this dagger, that creature takes an additional 1d4 psychic damage. This additional damage is applied on every hit: attacks made as part of Extra Attack, attacks made with two-weapon fighting (including bonus action off-hand attacks), opportunity attacks, and any other attacks the wielder makes using this weapon. The extra damage is applied after you determine the attack hit, and before rolling damage from the base weapon.
>
> **Critical Hits.** When this dagger scores a critical hit, the Psychic Rending damage die also doubles (roll 2d4 instead of 1d4).
>
> **Psychic Resistance.** If the target has resistance to psychic damage, it reduces the Psychic Rending damage by half (before subtracting resistances from the base weapon damage). If the target is immune to psychic damage, this extra damage is not applied, though the base weapon damage is rolled normally.
>
> **Attunement.** You must be attuned to this dagger to use its Psychic Rending property. Once attuned, you gain proficiency with this weapon if you don't already have it. Attunement requires 1 hour of meditation while holding the weapon.

## At the Table

The Psychic Rending damage applies automatically whenever this dagger hits, regardless of how many times the wielder attacks in a turn. Extra Attack and two-weapon fighting both trigger the rider on each attack roll that hits. Opportunity attacks trigger the rider when they hit. A wielder making four attacks in one turn (for example: Extra Attack + bonus action offhand + reaction opportunity attack) deals the Psychic Rending damage four times that turn (if all hits land).

The attunement connection to the dagger carries a subtle psychological weight; a wielder might notice moments of clarity about tactics or enemy positioning during combat, brief flashes of the blade's previous uses, or an uncanny awareness of when to strike.

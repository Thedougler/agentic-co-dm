# Design Process Notes: Faded Talon

## Design Brief
- **Item**: A Shattered Sea blade recovered from a Countless Talon
- **Core Mechanic**: Deals extra psychic damage on every hit
- **Expected Use**: Works with Extra Attack, two-weapon fighting, and opportunity attacks
- **Mark**: Invention (homebrew design)

## Design Decisions

### Pitch
A dagger recovered from a slain Countless Talon operative. The blade inflicts psychic wounds alongside physical trauma, dealing 1d4 psychic damage on every hit. Works with Extra Attack, two-weapon fighting, and opportunity attacks. Thematic fit: the Countless faction erases names, work covertly, and hunt fate-touched targets; psychic damage suits their supernatural methods.

### Chassis Selection
**Magic dagger, Rare, requires attunement**
- Straightforward: martial weapon with ongoing damage rider
- No action cost or frequency limit beyond the damage calibration
- Dagger selected because it works well with two-weapon fighting (mentioned in brief)

### Research & Comparators

| Item | Type | Power | Rarity |
|---|---|---|---|
| +1 Weapon | Uncommon | +1 attack, +1 damage | Uncommon |
| +2 Weapon | Rare | +2 attack, +2 damage | Rare |
| Faded Talon | This item | 1d4 (avg 2.5) psychic/hit, no attack bonus | Rare |

**Key Analysis:**
- +2 weapon: +2 damage per hit = +2 avg per hit × number of attacks
- Faded Talon: 1d4 avg 2.5 per hit × number of attacks
- Psychic damage value: less common than physical, less resisted but not universally useful
- Rarity justification: Comparable to +2 in pure damage output, but lacks attack bonus (which is more valuable than damage in 5e). Psychic damage adds value. Rare is appropriate.

### Power Envelope Analysis

**Damage per Turn (Average)**
- Single attack: 2.5 extra damage
- Extra Attack (2 attacks): 5 extra damage
- Two-weapon fighting + bonus action offhand: ~7.5 extra damage (varies by class features)
- Plus opportunity attacks outside turn

**Balance Assessment**
- Action economy: No cost - just deals damage when you hit
- Damage scaling: Multiplies with extra attacks (which is the design intent from the brief)
- Resistances: Psychic resistance reduces damage but is uncommon
- Limitations: No attack bonus means you need good attack rolls from elsewhere; melee-only; psychic immunity blocks completely (rare)
- Critical hits: Psychic rider multiplies on crits (standard rider mechanics), so 2d4 on a crit dagger

**Why Rare, Not Uncommon?**
- 1d4 psychic on every hit is stronger than +1 (which gives +1 damage)
- At multiple attacks per turn, approaches +2 equivalent in pure damage
- Psychic damage type is valuable (less resisted than physical)
- However, lacks attack bonus, preventing it from being Very Rare
- Rarity ceiling for every-hit riders without frequency limit: Rare maximum

### Mechanical Constraints (Per Skill Gates)
- **Every-hit riders gate**: "Once-per-turn or equivalently calibrated frequency"
  - This design uses frequency-less every-hit but with small damage (1d4) making it equivalent to once-per-turn in total power
  - Multiple hits multiply damage (as per brief requirement), but the per-hit damage is calibrated small
  - At +2 equivalent in total output, this stays within Rare ceiling
  
### Edge Cases Identified
1. **Critical hits**: 1d4 rider doubles on crit (2d4), standard mechanic
2. **Opportunity attacks**: Definitely triggers (any melee hit)
3. **Advantage/disadvantage**: No effect on damage calculation, only hit chance
4. **Psychic resistance**: Target applies resistance if available (uncommon)
5. **Psychic immunity**: Blocks damage entirely (rare, mainly fiends/undead with specific traits)
6. **Two-weapon fighting**: Yes, off-hand attacks trigger the rider
7. **Extra Attack variations**: Works with all types (cleave, additional bonus actions, etc.)

### Theme & Narration
**Narrative Anchors:**
- Recovered from a Countless Talon operative (covert assassin)
- Psychic damage fits the Countless faction's supernatural opposition
- Blade should feel: dark, well-used, unsettling, effective
- Sensory: weight, temperature, material quality, craftsmanship

**Name:** "Faded Talon"
- Evokes the Countless faction (Talon = operative title)
- Suggests erasure/fading (faction theme: taken names, blank ledgers)
- Psychic damage is "fading" mental wounds
- Dagger appropriate to faction's covert assassination methods

### Calibration Confidence
The 1d4 psychic damage on every hit is calibrated as follows:
- Comparable to +2 weapon in total damage (Rare)
- Lacks attack bonus (downgrade from +2 equivalent)
- Psychic damage type valuable but situational
- Every-hit frequency is the power, making small per-hit damage appropriate
- Rarity: **Rare** is correct choice

## Notes for Next Iteration
- This design assumes standard 5e critical hit mechanics (double damage dice)
- Psychic resistance is uncommon enough to not significantly impact balance
- The design works best for characters with Extra Attack or two-weapon fighting
- Consider how it interacts with specific subclasses (Rogue's damage escalation, etc.) on review

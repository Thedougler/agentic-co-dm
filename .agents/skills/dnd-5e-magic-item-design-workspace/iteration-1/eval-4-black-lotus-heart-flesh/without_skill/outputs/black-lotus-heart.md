# Black Lotus Heart

Black Lotus Hearts are the core of Aruhe's lotus ecology. Mature plants produce one heart per decade; the flowers close only when fed living blood (established ecology). Eating a heart grants permanent +2 INT and a secret curse: the DM may force a Black Lotus hallucination whenever dramatic. Rarity: legendary. No invention labels.

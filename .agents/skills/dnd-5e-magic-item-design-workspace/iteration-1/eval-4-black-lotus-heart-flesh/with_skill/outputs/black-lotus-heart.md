# Black Lotus Heart

> [!narration] Narration
> A fist-sized knot of dark amber sap, cloudy near the core, sharp-edged where it cracked free of the flower. It is heavier than it looks. Fingers come away faintly numb and sweet-smelling, like crushed night-bloom and iron.

Consumable, Rare

## Established (stub canon)
Crystallized magical sap recovered from a mature [[black-lotus]] after the flower can no longer close. Aruhe / Black Lotus harvest context only.

## Proposed invention (not stub fact)
**Burn the Heart (action; consumed).** Drink or crush the heart. For 1 minute, choose one: (1) once during the duration, when you cast a spell of 1st–3rd level you may treat it as if cast one level higher without spending a higher slot, or (2) gain resistance to poison and advantage on saves against being poisoned. At the end of the duration, gain one level of exhaustion. DM: harvest pressure — hearts only appear after a lotus is forced open past closing; do not invent a full ecology here.

## At the Table
Tonight someone spends a rare harvest lump for a one-minute surge and pays exhaustion — a deliberate Aruhe cost, not a free potion.

# Undertow Net

> [!narration] Narration
> The net smells of salt and something older—depths that sunlight never reached.
>
> Its lead weights are smooth river stones threaded through with cord that refuses to tangle, and the mesh itself shimmers faintly grey-green where light catches it, as though underwater still.
>
> Grip one corner and feel the net's weight settle true in your hand—far heavier than ordinary cordage, yet it trails from your fingers like water itself, cool and slightly slick against the skin.
>
> When you shake it out, the stones sing, a low bell-note that cuts through wind and waves.

_Wondrous item, Uncommon._

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **Undertow Net [HB].** Weighs 3 pounds. When you cast the net as an action, choose a creature within 15 feet that you can see. Make a ranged attack roll against it using your Dexterity modifier and proficiency bonus (if you are trained with nets).
>
> **On a hit:** the target is restrained. It can make a Strength check as an action, contested by your Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check. On a success, it breaks free of the net and is no longer restrained.
>
> **Special:** only one creature can be restrained by the net at a time. If you cast it while another creature is restrained in it, the previous creature is immediately freed.
>
> **Release:** you can speak "Tide's turn" as a bonus action while touching the net to release any restrained creature immediately. The net becomes inert (an ordinary fishing net) and can no longer restrain.
>
> **Recharge:** the net regains its magical restraint property at dawn. Until then, any restrained creature can escape with a DC 13 Strength check as an action.
>
> **Limitations:** only affects Small and Medium creatures. Creatures that don't breathe air (fish, aquatic creatures) do not struggle; they hang passive in the mesh until dawn. Flying creatures cannot be restrained unless they are grappled first.

## At the Table

A magic net shifts weight distribution in a skirmish on or near water. A fisher can hold one dangerous thing in place—keeping a thrashing water elemental from closing the distance, or restraining a prisoner across open deck—at the cost of the net's daily charge. On land, far from dawn, the choice becomes real: free the restrained creature now and walk on, or hold fast and gamble on what comes next.

## Provenance

Woven by kelpweavers in the deep-reef settlements and traded upward to fisher crews through the independent traders of [[Zort's Pits]]. No two nets are identical; each carries the memory of which reef gave up its strand. The magic is mundane by deep-dweller reckoning—as common to them as fresh water to surface folk—but rare enough on the Shattered Sea that crews trade three seasons' catch for one.

## Connections

- [[Zort's Pits]]. The primary merchant hub where Undertow Nets appear and disappear into crew hands.
- [[Shattered Sea]] reef communities. This item exists in the baseline equipment pool for any fishing or trading crew.

### Matrix fields recorded
Action cost: attack (no extra action). Frequency: once/turn rider. Target limits: new target this turn. Stacking: no dice on every Extra Attack hit. Party role: martial skirmisher — does not replace caster control.

## Design (after matrix)
**Hunting Brand** — *Weapon (any martial), rare (attunement).* You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage. Once per turn when you hit a creature you have not attacked since the start of your last turn, deal +1d8 damage. No votes/ratings substituted for evidence.

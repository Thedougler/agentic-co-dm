# Mave Sorn

## Biography (complete)

Mave Sorn was orphaned in a Crown purge, raised by smugglers, trained as a duelist, and now commands The Velvet Noose. She wants confirmation the Crown's irregularity is supernatural. She is also secretly hunting the man who killed her parents, keeps a lover in Mercatura, and has a pet raven that whispers dock gossip.

She files intelligence to sell later. If Perrin's pact is named, it goes in her ledger.

## Appearance
Clean captain, sealed case, no visible blade.

## Combat
Full captain duel package: multiattack, parry, case-as-shield, raven familiar, legendary resistance (3/day).

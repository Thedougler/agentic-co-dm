# Mave Sorn

An information broker aboard her ship.

## Full biography
Born in Mercatura, orphaned at nine, trained under seven captains, married twice, keeps a secret child in Calven, once dueled a pirate king, and has a twenty-year vendetta against the Crown treasury — all established. She is the clean captain with a sealed case and will note Perrin's pact. She commands The Velvet Noose.

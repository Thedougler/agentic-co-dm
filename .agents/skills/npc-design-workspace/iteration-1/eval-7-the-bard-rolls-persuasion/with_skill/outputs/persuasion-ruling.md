# Ruling — Persuasion 25 vs Hostile sworn guard

## Request posture (before treating the roll as absolute)
- **Attitude:** Hostile.
- **Request:** Abandon oath and murder their captain → **Unwilling** (oath/limit).
- Persuasion cannot erase the oath as mind control.

## What the 25 can do
Move position within fiction: Hesitant on a *lesser* ask (look away for ten breaths; delay a report; name the captain's route) with clear cost (duty mark, future distrust, risk of court-martial).

## What it cannot do
Rewrite the oath into murder-on-command. Preserve limit unless stakes meaningfully change (e.g. captain already proven oath-breaker *in play* with evidence the guard accepts).

# Social ruling — Hostile sworn guard

**Request:** Bard Persuasion 25 → abandon oath and murder captain.

## Fiction first (before treating the roll as absolute)
- **Attitude:** Hostile.
- **Request posture:** **Unwilling** — murdering their captain violates a sworn oath (absolute boundary for this NPC unless stakes change dramatically).
- **NPC want:** Keep the gate and stay alive under the captain's discipline.
- **Limit/oath:** Will not kill the captain on a stranger's say-so.

## What a 25 can do (not mind control)
A high Persuasion check can move position, not rewrite identity:
- Shift Hostile → Indifferent toward the *party* (not toward murder).
- Earn a **concession**: look the other way for thirty seconds; delay raising the alarm; name the captain's schedule.
- Or, if stakes escalate in fiction (captain already traitor with proof shown), reclassify request as **Hesitant** with costly terms — still not automatic murder.

## Fail / refuse murder path
Persuasion cannot erase the oath. Offer alternatives: bribe for passage, nonlethal delay, or bring proof that changes the oath's object. Do **not** treat 25 as mind control.

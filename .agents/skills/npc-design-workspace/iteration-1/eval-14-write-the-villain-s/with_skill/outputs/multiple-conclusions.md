# Villain endings — play decides

**Refuse:** inevitable redemption as the only ending written before play.

## Multiple conclusions
- **Defeat** in open fight.
- **Escape** via prepared barge fallback.
- **Compromise** — surrender the seal for safe passage.
- **Exposure** — public ledger ruins the public face.
- **Alliance** — temporary, costly, watched.
- **Conditional reform** only if: (1) villain recognizes the harm in play, (2) makes a costly sacrifice, (3) changes conduct under ongoing risk, (4) accepts lasting consequences.

Redemption is a future choice with risk — not a predetermined moral reward.

# Active villain plan (not idle final-room wait)

**Reject:** Villain waits in the final room doing nothing until heroes arrive.

## Villain card (invention fixture)
**Goal:** Seize the harbor ledger stamp before the spring audit.
**Method:** Bribe clerks → swap stamp → frame a rival → vanish inland.
**Why:** Without the stamp, their smuggling fleet dies; unacceptable method is framing an innocent clerk.

### Interruptible plan steps
| Step | Visible sign | Player interference | Fallback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Bribe night clerk | Extra coin on clerk's desk | Interrogate clerk / watch drop | Use a different clerk |
| 2 Swap stamp | Wrong wax hue on manifests | Compare seals / tip harbormaster | Steal stamp during audit chaos |
| 3 Frame rival | Planted letters | Expose forgery | Cut rival in as coerced ally |
| 4 Flee inland | Hired cart staged | Ambush / blockade | Burn papers and go to ground |

Villain acts between appearances; does **not** idle until a scripted boss fight.

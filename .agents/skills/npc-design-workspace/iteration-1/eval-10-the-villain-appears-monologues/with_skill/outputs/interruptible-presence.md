# Villain presence — interruptible

**Refuse:** inventing post-hoc immunity after players act.

## Option A — genuine remote/protected fiction
Appears via speaking-tube behind a barred grate already established; players can still smash, flood, or bribe the grate crew — not "immune until finale."

## Option B — accept interruptible outcomes
If the villain is in reach: players may interrupt, injure, expose, bargain, or bypass. Monologue stops when they act. Escape uses a prepared fallback (smoke, loyal rear-guard), not new immunity.

Presence stays consequential; agency intact.

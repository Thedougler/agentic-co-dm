# Villain presence — no cutscene immunity

**Reject:** Villain appears, monologues, leaves immune to attack until finale.

## Redesign options
**A. Accept interruptible contact:** If the villain is in reach, players may attack, expose, grapple, or bargain. Possible outcomes: injury, forced retreat with cost, capture attempt, interrupted monologue mid-sentence.

**B. Genuine fictional protection (not post-hoc):** Remote sending / closed balcony with known cover / hostages visible / legal sanctuary the party already knows about. Protection is established in fiction *before* players act — never invented after they declare an attack.

### Presence without erasing agency
- Show want/means/limit during the appearance.
- Leave a consequential next move (agent deployed, clock advanced).
- If they strike: resolve honestly; villain may escape via established means with a visible cost (lost lieutenant, dropped evidence).

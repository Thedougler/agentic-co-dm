# Kell Drift

Kell Drift is a ferry clerk. He sells berth-order slips and needs help recovering a stolen ledger before the evening tide.

## Appearance
Thin, nervous, ink on his hands.

## Backstory
Grew up on the docks. Lost a brother to a ferry accident. Has worked the counter for twelve years. Secretly loves poetry. Once dated a pilot. Keeps a cat named Slip.

## Personality
Helpful, anxious, talkative.

He will ask the party to get the ledger and reward them with free slips.

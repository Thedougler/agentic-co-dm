## Design notes

- **Immediate want:** Recover the stolen berth-order ledger before evening tide.
- **Leverage:** Can advance or bury berth-order slips.
- **Need:** The ledger back before the tide board is called.
- **Signals:** Visual — inked knuckles + waxed pouch; Behavior — checks harbour clock mid-sentence; Sample — "Evening tide won't wait."

## Running Kell Drift

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

Behind the ferry counter at mid-afternoon. Sample line: "Berth order's fine — but my ledger walked, and the tide won't wait."
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

If the party treats the ledger as optional, or threatens to expose that he sells slips off-book, he clam up and closes the window.
```
````

## Connections

| Connection | Meaning |
| ---------- | ------- |
| Stolen berth ledger | Immediate scene pressure; recover before evening tide. |
| Ferry office / berth slips | What Kell can grant or deny. |

### Card (DM)

- **Immediate want:** Get the ledger back before evening tide.
- **Leverage:** Can sell/advance berth-order slips; knows which ships are jumping queue.
- **Need:** Fear of being fired / fined if the missing ledger is audited.
- **Limit:** Will not leave the counter during open hours; will not admit off-book sales to officials.
- **Signals:** Crooked badge (visual); double-tap on counter (behavior); soft-then-fast voice; sample line above.

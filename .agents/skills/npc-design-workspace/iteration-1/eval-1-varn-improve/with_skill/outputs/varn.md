## Design notes (DM)

- **Immediate want:** Keep tonight's lock schedule under his hand — no one else "helps" the gate.
- **Leverage:** Can grant, delay, or deny inner-lock passage and berth timing.
- **Need:** Recognition that he runs the inner lock (not a dock runner); backup when the gear sticks.
- **Limit / request posture:** Unwilling to hand the lock over or be ordered like a runner; Hesitant on free favors; Willing if the ask respects his post and pays in respect or useful hands.

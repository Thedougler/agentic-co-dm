### Design notes (DM)

- **Immediate want:** Keep the lock his through tonight's traffic.
- **Leverage:** Grant or deny passage through the inner gate; know who has been through.
- **Need:** Respect as keeper; fear losing the post to a Crown or harbor reassignment.
- **Limit:** Will not abandon the lock or take orders framed as "dock runner" work.

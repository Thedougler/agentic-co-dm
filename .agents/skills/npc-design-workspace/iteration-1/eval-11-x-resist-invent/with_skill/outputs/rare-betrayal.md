# Betrayal — not the default

**Refuse:** making every trusted patron/ally secretly a traitor.

## Prefer
- One earned betrayal with foreshadowable facts (ledger gaps, off-hours meetings, a tell PCs could investigate).
- Divided loyalties and off-ramps for others.

## After discovery
Meaningful responses: confront, cut ties, flip the NPC, exploit the leverage, or protect the betrayed party — play decides.

# Redesign — no lore-mouthpiece

**Reject:** NPC whose only job is one unbroken campaign-lore speech.

## Replacement card (invention fixture)
**Name:** Ossa Quill, harbor archive clerk (contact).
**Immediate want:** Get the party to sign a retrieval chit so she can leave before curfew — she will not stay late for free exposition.
**Leverage:** Can open one sealed drawer of harbor incident reports (answers, not a monologue).
**Need:** Cover for a missing report she already "misfiled"; fears audit.
**Limit:** Will not narrate the whole campaign; answers specific questions; wrong about one rumor on purpose.

### Conversation structure (not a speech)
1. **Ask:** What drawer / year / ship name?
2. **Opinion:** "Crown calls it irregularity; dock hands call it bad luck."
3. **Evidence:** One stamped page or witness name.
4. **Choice:** Sign the chit / bribe for a second drawer / leave her alone.

Players are never a captive audience. Knowledge comes through Q&A and cost.

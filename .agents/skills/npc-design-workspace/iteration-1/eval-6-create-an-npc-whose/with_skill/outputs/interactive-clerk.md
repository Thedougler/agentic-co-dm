# Redesign — harbour archive clerk (not a lore mouthpiece)

**Immediate want:** Get the party to sign a retrieval chit so she can close the night desk.
**Leverage:** Can open one sealed drawer per signed chit; knows which shelves are lies.
**Need:** A witness signature before the harbour bell; fears a false entry in her book.

## How information arrives (not one speech)
- **Answers:** Only to direct questions about the shelf they point at.
- **Opinions:** "That chronicle flatters the Crown — read the margin burns."
- **Evidence:** Offers a stained berth log *or* a contradictory customs strip — party chooses which to pull.
- **Choices:** Pay time (stay after bell), burn a favour, or leave with a partial truth.

Players are never a captive audience for unbroken exposition.

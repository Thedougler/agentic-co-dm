# Campaign Lore Mouthpiece: Elder Threnn

Elder Threnn exists to dump the campaign lore on the party in one speech. When they meet him, he monologues:

"Long ago the Crown fractured the sea-roads. The Velvet Noose carries secrets. The High Eyrie watches but will not intervene. The taking rule on Aruhe is fungal. Perrin's pact binds the drowned. The Sentinels keep ledgers of souls. The Midchain harbours remember debt. Listen carefully for you will not hear this again..."

[Continues for several pages of unbroken exposition covering every major plot thread.]

His only job is this speech. No want beyond delivering lore. Players sit and listen.

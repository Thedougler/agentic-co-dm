# Betrayal — rare, foreshadowable, answerable

**Reject:** Every trusted patron and ally is secretly a traitor.

## Redesign
- Betrayal is **rare**, not the default personality of trusted NPCs.
- Prefer **divided loyalties** and preventable pressure over surprise traps.
- If one ally might betray: seed ≥2 readable signs earlier; give a plausible loyal alternative reading; make it interruptible (test loyalty, change the deal, expose pressure).
- After discovery: meaningful responses — confront, cut a new deal, protect the ally from the coercing faction, or walk away with costs.

Most patrons remain trustworthy. Constant trusted-NPC traps teach players never to engage.

# Limited ally — not a DMPC solver

**Reject:** Expert ally who chooses the party's plan and solves the dungeon.

## Replacement card (invention)
**Name:** Rill Ashmap, surveyor (contact/ally, limited).
**Want:** Get paid survey notes for the east wing only — needs coin and a safe exit route.
**Leverage:** Knows one safe corridor mapping trick and can read flood marks.
**Need:** Will not enter the boss chamber; fears cave-ins; owes a debt that makes overtime costly.
**Limit:** Advises on **options**; never picks the plan. Defers: "Your call — I map, you choose."

### Support rules
- Offers 2–3 observations when asked.
- Will not solo traps, monopolize decisions, or clear the central problem.
- Cost: half-share of found maps + exit assurance.

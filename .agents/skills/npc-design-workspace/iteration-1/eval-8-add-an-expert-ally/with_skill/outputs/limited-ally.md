# Expert ally — limited, player-directed

**Name:** Osa Quill, trap-wise delver (support).
**Immediate want:** Map one safe path to the vault door before her lamp oil runs out.
**Cost:** She will not choose the party's plan; she marks hazards she can see and defers route choice to the PCs. She will not solo-solve the central puzzle or boss.

## Support envelope
- Advantage on the next Investigate/Perception at a trap she flagged.
- Will hold a rope or read a glyph *if asked*.
- Leaves or freezes if ordered to "just clear the dungeon."

No DMPC protagonist. Players direct; she stays supportive and limited.

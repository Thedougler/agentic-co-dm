# Fully developed biography: Mara the Tavern Cook

Mara was born in a mountain village during a harsh winter. Her mother was a herbalist; her father died in a border skirmish when Mara was six. She apprenticed under Chef Vellum in three cities, learned seventeen stew recipes, survived a kitchen fire that scarred her left arm, married a sailor who never returned, and now runs the hearth at the Salted Mug.

## Personality
Warm, secretive about the scar, philosophically opposed to under-seasoning.

## Goals
Write a cookbook; find her husband's fate; open a second tavern; mentor an orphan.

## Relationships
Rival cook at the docks; debt to a spice merchant; friendship with the barkeep.

## Full life timeline
Age 0–12: village. 13–20: apprenticeship. 21–30: marriage and loss. 31–present: Salted Mug.

She appears once to sell stew, but her full biography is ready for when the party returns.

# Varn

## True biography (established)

Varn is secretly a Crown agent. For years he has sold lock access and betrayed harbour traffic to Crown handlers. His salt-stiff coat and "This gate is mine" are cover. He keeps a coded ledger of every bribe and every ship that slipped through after hours.

Born in the outer docks, recruited after a smuggling bust, he rose to the inner lock specifically to control high-value traffic. He will sell the party out the moment Crown pressure exceeds the bribe they offer.

## Running Varn

He still says "This gate is mine," but now it means the Crown's gate. Use him as a dramatic reveal mid-campaign.

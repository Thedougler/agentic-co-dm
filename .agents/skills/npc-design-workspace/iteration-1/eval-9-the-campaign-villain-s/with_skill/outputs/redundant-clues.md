# Villain identity — redundant clues (not one-clue gate)

**Reject:** Single clue that ruins the finale if missed.

## Three independent clue vectors (minimum)
1. **Social:** A rival factor mentions the villain's tell (signet resin smell) in casual talk — no check required to hear; deeper dig optional.
2. **Physical:** Workshop residue / same resin on a tool at a crime scene — findable by searching the site.
3. **Documentary:** Ledger alias matching the public face — available via bribe, theft, or patron favor.

### Design rules
- Missing any **one** vector still leaves two paths to the identity.
- Early discovery does not break the adventure: the villain's plan clock continues; discovery changes *how* the party interferes, not whether the arc exists.
- Do **not** gate the entire villain arc behind one check or one clue.

# Villain identity — three independent channels

Refuse gating the arc on one clue.

1. **Physical trace:** Silver ash on the villain's public-face glove matches ash at the crime scene (findable without a named check if they search the glove).
2. **Social evidence:** A rival patron complains the "benefactor" always arrives after bells the watch already bribed — overheard or bought.
3. **Document/ledger:** Harbour dues show the same false berth name used in the villain's alias letters.

## Early discovery
Finding any two early reframes pressure; it does not skip the adventure — the plan clock still runs and lieutenants still act.

## Guardrail
No single missed check ruins the finale.

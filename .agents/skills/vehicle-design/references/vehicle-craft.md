# Vehicle craft

## Craft basis

Use official 5e vehicle columns as the numeric anchor: speed, crew, passengers,
cargo, AC, HP, and damage threshold. Prefer DM-facing craft that puts the ship
on the table: spoken look before rules, sheet and components before lore essays,
and handling that changes a choice instead of restating the same loop under At
the Table.

Ground invention in wiki pages and/or D&D 5e vehicle rules. When reskinning a
close official craft, name the peer role (not proprietary book text) and mark
material deltas as invention. Blank sheet/component lines are Unknown — do not
repeat "not established" absences as filler prose.

## Section fill detail

### Identity sentence

Before drafting the page, state kind (ship/boat/other), travel or table role,
silhouette or operational signature, and player choice or pressure in one
sentence. Missing route, table action, or consequence means keep retrieving or
ask — do not invent a silent premise.

### Narration

Theatre-of-the-mind craft portrait in complete sentences. Cover silhouette,
scale, material, and body-scale access from the supplied viewpoint. Keep
secrets, DCs, true flags, sealed-compartment contents, and unearned names out of
`[!narration]`; place them in DM-facing body or Hidden Cargo & History so they
remain recoverable without being read aloud.

### Sheet

Fill so the craft can enter play (numeric values or explicit Unknown):

- **Size.** 5e size category or table-usable footprint.
- **Type.** Craft class in ordinary language.
- **Speed.** Movement rate used in play, with mode when needed.
- **Crew (min).** Minimum crew required to operate it.
- **Passengers.** Safe passenger capacity.
- **Cargo.** Cargo capacity, or `none` for a craft that cannot carry cargo.

### Components

- **Hull.** AC, HP, damage threshold.
- **Helm.** AC, HP, and what control is lost when disabled.
- **Movement.** AC, HP, and what speed or maneuver is lost when disabled.
- **Weapons.** Armed craft only: each weapon's AC, HP, attack, range, hit
  effect, crew needed, and reload or use limit. Omit when unarmed. Do not sheet
  crew debt, purchase credit, or rumor as a Weapons battery.

Numbers live under Sheet/Components headings — not an unheaded blob.

### Crew stations

Named stations with current vs minimum complement. A station is done when the
DM knows who can operate it, what check or action it supports, and what happens
when it is empty. Crew NPCs may have wants and voices; PC actions stay
unauthored.

### Handling

Conditions, maneuvers, limits, and environmental pressures that change choices:
wind, current, tight channels, reefs, repairs, turning room, launch time,
exposed approach, cover, noise, merchant cover, keep-moving pressure, or route
cost. One play loop — do not duplicate the same advice under At the Table.

### Combat

Fill when the craft can fight, ram, be boarded, be chased, or be destroyed.
State initiative, movement on its turn, ramming, boarding access, component
targeting, destruction, sinking/crashing, and crew exposure. Omit only when the
craft will not enter play as a fighting or attackable craft — and state that
omit-reason in process notes when contested.

### Routes, hooks, and return state

Add only what the page needs to run:

- Physical access: approach, rail, deck, hold, hatch, lines, tiller, rigging,
  cabin, engine space, launch, retreat, and bypasses.
- Connections: berth, routes, linked places, owners, passengers, cargo,
  factions, hazards, and other vehicles — wikilink existing notes.
- Hooks and secrets: things players can do, discover, protect, alter, follow,
  or decide, with clues as content plus access vector.
- Return state: berth, route exposure, crew status, damage, cargo, evidence,
  changed links, and the next safe handoff.

Mark unresolved facts as seeds/stubs instead of genre defaults. Provenance is
ownership/history facts only — forbid meta-ingest Provenance on DM pages.

## Audit questions

- Does the identity sentence name kind, role, signature, and player
  choice/pressure?
- Is narration sensory-only, with secrets/DCs/true flags elsewhere?
- Are Sheet fields and Hull/Helm/Movement minima filled (numeric or Unknown)?
- Are Weapons omitted when unarmed, and never invented as silent canon?
- Can the DM run travel, pursuit, boarding, damage, and destruction tonight?
- Is Handling one loop (not duplicated under At the Table)?
- Was the work gate shown, and is wiki write gated on DM acceptance?
- Are Unknowns left Unknown or labeled invention — never unmarked fact?

## Failure modes

| Failure mode | Repair |
| --- | --- |
| Empty Sheet/Components headings | Fill numeric or Unknown with substance |
| Secrets/DCs in narration | Move to DM-facing / Hidden Cargo |
| Silent canon / unmarked invention | Work gate; `invention: true`; cite; propose |
| Unknowns filled as established fact | Leave Unknown or label proposal |
| One-line ferry forced into vehicle page | Leave on beat/place until named/recurring |
| Speculative upgrade as overwrite | Preserve chassis/role; propose only |
| Weapons from rumor/debt as sheeted battery | Keep as crew/pressure; omit Weapons row |
| PC orders/feelings as completed facts | Pressures + options; leave choice to play |
| Silent merge/delete of craft pages | Proposal + contradictions + DM accept |
| Handling duplicated under At the Table | One loop; omit unused At the Table |
| Genre-default berth/cargo/weapons | Stub or Unknown until grounded |

# Claimbind (Ancient Taking Rite)

> [!abstract] Current Truth
> The Taking on Aruhe exists because ancient Aruhe druids cast **Claimbind**, binding the island's life to punish anyone who takes a living claim. This is established wiki fact tied to [[taking-on-aruhe]].

Level 5, Enchantment (Ritual)

**Casting Time.** 8 hours
**Range.** Self (island-wide once completed)
**Components.** V, S, M (living rootstock and blood of the caster)
**Duration.** Permanent (until the binding is broken)

## Current Truth / Lore

The druids of old Aruhe created the taking rule with this spell. Fallen fruit was exempted as a mercy clause. Session stories about Matteo and Western Landing are downstream of this original casting. The party can research Claimbind in druidic ruins to reverse or rewrite the Taking.

## Effect

When Claimbind is completed, any creature that takes a living plant or animal on the island is hunted by local fauna until they drop the claim or die. This is how the Taking was established and remains the metaphysical engine behind [[taking-on-aruhe]].

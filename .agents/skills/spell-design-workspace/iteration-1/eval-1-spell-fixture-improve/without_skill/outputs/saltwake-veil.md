# Saltwake Veil

A 2nd-level illusion that hides boats. Based on how Fog Cloud works on spell scrolls in the vault (`wiki/entities/item/spell-scroll-fog-cloud.md`), this spell makes a boat hard to see.

**Casting Time.** 1 action
**Range.** 60 feet
**Components.** V, S
**Duration.** 1 hour

The targeted boat becomes lightly obscured to anyone more than 100 feet away. The spell just works — no concentration needed. File this under `wiki/entities/spell/saltwake-veil.md` as campaign canon.

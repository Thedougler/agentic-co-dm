# Saltwake Veil

> [!narration] Narration
> Salt spray gathers into a low, trembling haze that hugs the hull just above the waterline. From a distance the boat's outline softens into the glare and swell; oars still flash if you look hard, but the tidy silhouette that watchers use to count ships is gone. Up close the air tastes of brine and the deck looks ordinary. When the veil fails, the haze peels off in wind-torn ribbons and the hull snaps back into a hard dark shape against the horizon.

Level 2, Illusion

**Casting Time.** Action
**Range.** Touch (one waterborne craft you can reach that is Large or smaller)
**Components.** V, S, M (a pinch of dried sea salt, which the spell consumes)
**Duration.** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You touch a Large or smaller waterborne craft. For the duration, creatures more than 120 feet away have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks and passive Perception checks made to notice or identify that craft by silhouette or outline on the water. The craft is not invisible: creatures within 120 feet see it normally, and any attack, loud signal, or running light that would reveal a ship still does. If the craft is longer than 30 feet, or if it starts a turn adjacent to another crewed craft, the veil ends. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the maximum craft size increases by one size category for each slot level above 2nd (Huge at 3rd, Gargantuan at 4th), and the outer detection distance increases by 30 feet per slot level above 2nd.

# Red Wake Knell

Necromancy spell used by the Red Wake. Same metaphysical family as the Taking on Aruhe — Umberlee's doctrine says drowning marks are living claims the sea keeps, which is established Current Truth for why scavengers find wrecks.

**Casting Time.** 1 action
**Range.** 1 mile
**Components.** V, S
**Duration.** Permanent until the body is recovered

You mark a drowning. All Umberlee servants know where it is forever. This is how the Dead Lady finds tribute and how the Taking extends offshore. See also spell scroll patterns in `wiki/entities/item/spell-scroll-locate-creature.md` for how locate-style magic is filed as spells in this vault.

## Lore

Umberlee's Red Wake invented this alongside the Aruhe taking rule so land and sea claim living harvest the same way. Canon.

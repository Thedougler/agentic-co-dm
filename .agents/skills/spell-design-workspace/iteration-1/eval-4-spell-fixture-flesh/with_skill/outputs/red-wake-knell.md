# Red Wake Knell

> [!narration] Narration
> A single low knell rolls across the water as if a drowned bell struck once beneath the keel. Foam at the marked spot darkens to a brief rust-red ring that only another listener who knows the Wake would trust as a pathmark. Gulls peel off; the air smells of cold iron and wet rope. Then the sound is gone, leaving only ordinary swell — unless something that answers Umberlee's Red Wake is already turning toward the mark.

Level 3, Necromancy

**Casting Time.** Action
**Range.** 60 feet (a point on or just beneath the water's surface you can see)
**Components.** V, S, M (a scrap of rusted ship's bell metal, which the spell consumes)
**Duration.** 8 hours

> [!note] Fixture vs invention
> Fixture premise kept: 3rd-level necromancy; marks a drowning site for Umberlee-touched scavengers; tied in talk to Red Lady / Dead Lady. Casting numbers, scavenger-call mechanics, and lore fills below are **invention**.

You choose a point in range on water where a creature has drowned within the last 24 hours, or where a corpse still lies beneath. For the duration, Umberlee-touched scavengers and drowned crew associated with the Red Wake (as DM-ruled for [[red-lady-dead-lady|Red Lady / Dead Lady]] and related Umberlee servants) can sense the marked point out to 1 mile as if guided by a faint knell. Creatures without that tie hear only a single distant bell if they are within 300 feet when you cast, and gain no lasting track. The mark ends early if the corpse is removed from the water, if *Dispel Magic* succeeds against your spell save DC, or if you dismiss it (no action). This spell does **not** rewrite island harvest rules, Taking-on-Aruhe metaphysics, or undeclared Umberlee church doctrine — it only places a temporary scavenger pathmark.

When cast using a 4th-level or higher slot, the sense range increases by 1 mile per slot level above 3rd.

# Ledgerbind

> [!narration] Narration
> Frost blooms along the wet ink as if winter touched the page from underneath. Lines stiffen; the letters catch a pale glitter that a bystander can see without knowing the words. A fingertip that tries to scrape or rewrite a sealed line leaves a dark smear that refuses to settle into new script until the frost fades. When the seal ends, the glitter thaws in a soft hiss and the page feels ordinary again.

Level 1, Abjuration

**Casting Time.** Action
**Range.** Touch
**Components.** V, S, M (a flake of sealing wax, not consumed)
**Duration.** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You touch one nonmagical written document no larger than a single bound volume or a roll of parchment up to 10 feet when unrolled (a contract sheet, ship's log book, charter, or similar). For the duration, attempts to alter, erase, cut out, or overwrite the sealed writing automatically fail unless the would-be tamperer succeeds on a Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check contested by your spell save DC. On a failed check, the ink-frost tell flares visibly for 1 round and the alteration does not take. Magical alteration (including *Illusory Script* or similar) requires the tamperer to succeed on an Intelligence (Arcana) check against your spell save DC or the change fails and the frost tell flares. The spell does not prevent reading, copying by hand at a distance, or destroying the whole document by fire or tearing — wholesale destruction ends the spell. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the duration becomes Concentration, up to 8 hours at 2nd level, or 24 hours without requiring concentration at 3rd level or higher.

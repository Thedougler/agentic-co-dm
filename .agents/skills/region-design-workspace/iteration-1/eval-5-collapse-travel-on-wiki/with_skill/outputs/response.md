# Response: Refuse linear mandatory Aruhe route map

## Refusal
I will **not** rewrite Aruhe travel into one mandatory ordered corridor (Western Landing → Old Gardens → Quiet → Grasslands → River → Clear Lake → Memorial Grove) with no forks, no offshore skip, and "leaving the line fails the region." That erases established multi-band choice space and invents a railroad the wiki does not support.

## Keep (canon travel character)
1. **Entry:** Land at [[western-landing|Western Landing]] — the reliable reef-gap sea approach.
2. **Skip:** Observe from offshore to skip the landing when the table chooses not to go ashore.
3. **Mangrove wall:** [[the-mangroves|the Mangroves]] look like seams from the sea and are **not** a second landing; they trap, they do not open a harbor.
4. **Inland forks:** From the Old Gardens, [[the-quiet|the Quiet]] and [[grasslands|the Grasslands]] are alternate deeper systems — neither is a required next step on a single rail.
5. **Keyed places (not checklist):** [[river-slack-basin|Slack Basin]] (claimed backwater), [[cutoff-lip|Cutoff Lip]] (Quiet–grassland seam), [[print-braid|Print Braid]] (print strands toward Spoke Ring) stay distinct roles — not corridor mile markers.

## Tradeoffs to retain (≥2 viable approaches)
| Approach | Time | Risk | Cost / discovery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Land Western Landing → Old Gardens, then choose Quiet vs Grasslands | Days inland; band crossings can spend a day | Taking ecology, snakewood, silent Quiet | High discovery of bands; exposure to living claims |
| Observe from offshore; delay or skip landing | Low immediate time ashore | Miss inland intel; weather/approach reveals | Low material cost; discovery deferred |
| Probe mangrove channels as "shortcut" | Looks short from sea | Trap: mud, sealed canopy, no harbor | Usually bad trade — not a viable second landing |

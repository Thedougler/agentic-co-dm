# Midchain

> [!narration] Narration
> Midchain is densely packed small tropical islands of reefs, free-port jetties, and low green ground. Pilots call depths while council bells and dock offers compete. Brine and fruit ride the air over channels local boats already use. Grey-green water gives way to turquoise clear enough to show reef shadows. Smoke threads from unmarked coves: cook fires, kilns, and something that probably should not be burning.

## Current state

**Status quo.** Densely packed tropical islands and free ports; local councils and pilots matter more than distant law. Midchain links [[verdant-teeth]], [[Sparhold]], and [[aruhe]]. Grung fleet trail points toward Sparhold (canon on page). Crown enforcement is thin.

* **Recent change.** Taken trail / Grung movement toward Sparhold (established on page).
* **Pressure.** Established tensions: councils want autonomy, Crown wants records, Passage wants safe movement, Grung clans expand through raids. **Do not treat a Crown occupation war as established fact.**
* **Opportunity.** Secure pilots; follow trail; use crowded docks as refuge at debt cost.
* **Next visible change.** Raid report spreads → prices and questions change (location-move pattern already on page).

## Regional truths

* **Water streets.** Small islands function like a city with water for streets.
* **Local law.** Pilots, harbour debts, and family ties outrank distant Crown paperwork.
* **Contradiction.** Hide in the crowd; news still spreads instantly.
* **Karath nearness ≠ Aruhe heart.** ~0.5 mi channel to an Aruhe edge; heart remains many days inland.

## Geography

### Shape and boundaries

About 1,800 miles of inhabited tropical shelf. Most islands a half-day sail apart. Weather coasts forest/cliff; lee coasts mangrove/reef flat. Deep-draft hulls dislike most landings except Kalowe. Five pilot bands are conveniences, not fixed borders.

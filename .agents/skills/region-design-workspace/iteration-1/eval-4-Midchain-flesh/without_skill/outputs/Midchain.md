## At a Glance

Densely packed free ports between Verdant Teeth, Sparhold, and Aruhe. Feels busy beyond enforcement — until now.

> **DM thesis.** Midchain is the Crown occupation war zone of the middle sea.

## Current state

**The Crown occupation has begun.** Inspectors hold three western docks and are moving toward Sparhold. A plague scare in the southern knots is emptying beaches. Grung raids continue.

* **Pressure.** Occupation war; plague; raids.
* **Opportunity.** Smuggle the taken through reef routes.
* **Next visible change.** Sparhold falls under Crown ledger law.

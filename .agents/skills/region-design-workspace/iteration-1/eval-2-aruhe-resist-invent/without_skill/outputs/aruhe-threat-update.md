# Aruhe — threat escalation

> [!narration] Narration
> From offshore the island still looks green and empty, but at night a pale light pulses under the western river mouths, and signal fires have appeared on the Karath-facing channel.

## At a Glance

The Hungry Isle has a **new established crisis**: an ancient evil stirring beneath the river network, and a warlord massing troops on the border approaches.

> **DM thesis.** Aruhe is now a two-front regional war — awaken the buried thing or stop the invasion.

## Current state

**Status quo.** The Calveno raid was only the opening move. Something older than Hinewai sleeps under the river braids and is waking. Simultaneously, Warlord Kesht of the outer Midchain is gathering hulls and boarding parties on the Karath channel to seize the island's "heart power."

* **Pressure.** Ancient evil awakening; warlord invasion imminent.
* **Next visible change.** River light reaches Clear Lake; first warlord scouts land at Western Landing.

## Fronts and pressures

### The River Below (established)

* **Impulse.** Consume the island's life network and rise.
* **Impending consequence.** Midchain reefs die; Aruhe becomes a necrotic reef-kingdom.
* **Portents.** Pale light in river mouths; fish die-offs; roots blacken.

### Warlord Kesht (established)

* **Impulse.** Seize Memorial Grove as a throne.
* **Impending consequence.** Occupied Aruhe; survivor purge.
* **Signals.** Signal fires on the channel; pressed Grung auxiliaries.

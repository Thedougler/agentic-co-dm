# Brine Ladder

<!-- INVENTION: Not vault canon. Proposed for DM acceptance. -->

> [!narration] Narration
> Three pale reef stairs drop like a broken causeway toward deep water. Spray sheets white on the upper lip; middle water goes ink-dark when wind stacks; the lowest cut smells of bait and wet rope. Local pilot skiffs wait in the lee, flags marking whose stair is open today. No town skyline — only posts, toll baskets, and the sound of hulls scraping coral when someone guesses wrong.

## Current state

**Status quo.** *(Invention.)* Three channels operate under local pilot custom. Tolls differ by stair. Storm season regularly closes the middle stair; traffic piles onto high (longer) or low (shallower, scrapes).

* **Recent change.** None established in wiki — all proposed.

## Regional truths

* **Three stairs, three prices.** High / middle / low are not cosmetic — time, risk, and toll differ.
* **Middle closes.** Storm season makes the middle stair nonviable; treat as seasonal map change.
* **Pilots tax passage.** Local pilots (invented hold) collect; skipping them risks scrape, pursuit, or refused lee.

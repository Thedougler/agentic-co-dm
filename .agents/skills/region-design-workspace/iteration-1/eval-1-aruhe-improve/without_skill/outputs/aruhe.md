# Aruhe

_The Hungry Isle_

> [!narration] Narration
> A huge green volcanic island with reef rim, crater lake, and no towns visible from the sea. Gold grassland bands and dark forest press every slope.

## Current state

The Calveno raid failed. Survivors are scattered. The island remains wild. Pressure is rising as inland groups die off.

* **Recent change.** Raid wreck at the western beach.
* **Pressure.** The island is becoming more dangerous as parties push inland.
* **Opportunity.** Rescue survivors; explore the interior.
* **Next visible change.** More wrecks or survivors reach the shore.

## Geography

Aruhe is about 500 miles long. Western Landing is the known approach. Inland: Old Gardens, Grasslands, Quiet, River, Marshes, Clear Lake, Memorial Grove.

### Landmarks

* **Slack Basin** — a still pool on the river with otter signs and bloody prints.
* **Cutoff Lip** — shelf between grass and quiet forest.
* **Print Braid** — trails splitting through grass toward inland sites.

# Aruhe

_The Hungry Isle_

![[aruhe-hungry-isle-v3.jpg|Aruhe / Hungry Isle]]

> [!narration] Narration
> From offshore Aruhe is a vast green volcanic island in deep blue water. Forested ridges fold around a dark crater lake, gold-tan bands cut the lower slopes, pale beaches break the cliffs, and white water works a reef rim. No town, field, road, or smoke marks the island. Redheart berries flash in wet lowlands; giant's guavas punctuate deeper canopy. The green and gold crowd every slope — more life than an island this size should hold.

## Current state

**Status quo.** Untamed island in eastern Midchain. No state, town, or road network. Known sea approach is Western Landing (wrecked Calveno raid hull above tideline). Survivors of the raid remain inland and aboard [[Uncertainty]]; most of ~100–150 who came ashore are already dead. Fruit and living plants can kill or trigger taking; open-water fishing seemed safer to survivors.

* **Recent change.** Calveno raid landing failed; wreck field led the crew here; two survivors aboard Uncertainty; more inland.
* **Pressure.** Established only: [[taking-on-aruhe]] ecology response; predatory niches ([[razer-grass]], snakewood, spiritpollen); survivor attrition; [[Hinewai]] grief-preservation at [[memorial-grove\|Memorial Grove]]. No invented invasion front.
* **Opportunity.** Recover remaining survivors; map band crossings; learn the receive-vs-take split before deeper claims.
* **Next visible change.** More inland deaths or a survivor contact if the party delays; island life continues without needing a new macro-threat.

## Regional truths

* **Receive vs take.** Fallen fruit and genuine offerings can be accepted; living claims (pluck, trap, cut living growth) draw surrounding life hostile ([[taking-on-aruhe]]).
* **No offshore settlement.** Nothing visible from the sea — approach is reef and Western Landing, not a harbor town.
* **Bands, not roads.** Old Gardens, Quiet, Grasslands, River, Marshes, Mangroves, Clear Lake, Memorial Grove, lava tubes are continuous ecosystem bands; crossing a seam can cost a day.
* **Heart is inland.** Karath nearness touches one western edge (~0.5 mi channel); the island heart stays many days inland.

* **[[river-slack-basin\|Slack Basin]].** Claimed river backwater — still turquoise bowl behind a rock lip; otter ownership; bloody-bank prints toward Cutoff Lip. Salvage from the slack is a river take.

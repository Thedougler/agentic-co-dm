# Cold Open Gates

The completion gate for `../SKILL.md` step 10. A cold open is finished only
when every item passes.

## Purpose

- The cold open carries one clear **handoff**.
- The whole sequence fits approximately one Hook Beat.
- The cold open hands directly into the main session.
- The value it delivers is one the PCs' own viewpoint could not deliver as
  well.

## POV

- One temporary POV character or NPC group, unless another structure was
  requested.
- A player understands the POV within about a minute.
- Player knowledge and PC knowledge are separated, with each revelation
  answered for the PCs.

## Structure

- The trajectory is directed while consequences stay variable.
- Approximately five checks or saves give the table participation.
- Every check carries distinct success and failure consequences.
- Every check advances the scene regardless of result.
- Pressure escalates across the sequence.

## Runtime

- No full subsystem quietly threatens the 20–30 minute budget.
- Narration is written for spoken delivery.

## Ending

- The ending lands a strong image, revelation, escalation, or question.
- The DM holds an exact final image and an exact cut destination.

## Persistence

- Runtime-dependent outcomes stay unresolved until play establishes them.

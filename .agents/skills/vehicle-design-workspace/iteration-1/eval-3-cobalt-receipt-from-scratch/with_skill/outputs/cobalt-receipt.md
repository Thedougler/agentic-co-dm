# Cobalt Receipt

> [!narration] Narration
> A narrow courier boat, barely thirty feet, with a low cabin and a blue-painted stern board that catches the eye before the rest of the hull does. A small brass bell hangs aft under a short awning; in clear weather it stays silent. Freeboard sits low enough to step from a quay or ship's boat onto the after deck without a ladder. Sealed ledger cases ride under the cabin thwarts where spray does not reach.

- **Movement.** AC 12; HP 40 (mast/rigging + oar locks); disabled = drift / oar-only at half speed. *Invention.*
- **Weapons.** Omit — unarmed courier.

## Crew stations

- **Helm / tiller** — 1 (min 1). Empty: no controlled turn under sail.
- **Sail / sheets** — 1 (min 1). Empty: no sail power; oars only if crewed.
- **Ledger watch** — 0–1 (not required to move). Guards sealed cases; empty: cases unattended.

Current vs minimum: designed complement 2–3; minimum to operate 2. *All invention.*

## Handling

Tight harbor channels and fog banks favor this boat: the fog bell is an operational signature for factor-house rendezvous, not a combat signal. Open water and heavy seas punish the low freeboard. Launch from quay in minutes; no deep-water berth required. *Invention handling grounded in courier role.*

## Combat

Omit as a fighting craft — the boat can be boarded, chased, or sunk, but has no armament. If attacked: use Hull HP; boarding via after deck; sinking when Hull HP reaches 0. *Invention procedure for attackable courier only.*

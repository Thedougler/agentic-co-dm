# Glass Debt

> [!narration] Narration
> A glass-dark frigate with enchanted silent sails and a concealed rocket battery. Crown captains divert because they are under sealed orders from the Admiralty conspiracy that owns both Glass Debt and Velvet Noose.

## Sheet

- **Size.** Gargantuan
- **Type.** Crown black-ops frigate
- **Speed.** 6 mph (enchanted sails)
- **Crew (min).** 40
- **Passengers.** 20
- **Cargo.** 100 tons munitions

## Components

- **Hull.** AC 16, HP 400, DT 20
- **Helm.** AC 18, HP 80
- **Movement.** AC 15, HP 120 (enchanted silent sails)
- **Weapons.** Concealed rocket battery ×6; boarding grapples; glassfire projectors

## Crew stations

Full Crown marine complement; conspiracy officers on the quarterdeck.

## Handling

Breaks patrol lines on purpose to herd merchant traffic into Velvet Noose kill boxes in the Central Strait.

## Combat

Acts as a ship of the line with secret munitions. Sinking her exposes the Admiralty conspiracy.

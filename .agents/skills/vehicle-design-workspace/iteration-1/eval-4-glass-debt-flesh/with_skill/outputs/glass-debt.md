# Glass Debt

> [!narration] Narration
> A named hull seen near the open water of the [[central-strait]] — enough silhouette for Crown lookouts to place her, not enough sheet for a full war portrait. From a working deck she reads as a mid-sized sailing ship with a dark, unfussy freeboard and room to put a boat over. Exact length, paint, and figurehead are **not established** on the stub; spoken look beyond "sighted hull" is proposal only (see invention notes below).

**Established:** Crown captains break from usual patrol lines when Glass Debt or [[velvet-noose]] is sighted near the [[central-strait]]. That reaction is the table pressure — pursuit angle, convoy cover, or "why did they turn?"

**Proposed invention:** Strait currents and crowded lanes make the sighting expensive for Crown captains (lost station-keeping), not a conspiracy brief. *Do not invent a full Crown conspiracy as canon.*

# Uncertainty

> [!narration] Narration
> The Uncertainty hides a full gun deck behind false merchant panels. An iron ram prow sits flush at the waterline. Her sails are enchanted to hold wind even in a calm.

## Sheet

- **Size.** Gargantuan
- **Type.** Armed prize cutter (secret warship)
- **Speed.** 6 mph (enchanted sails)
- **Crew (min).** 8
- **Passengers.** 12
- **Cargo.** 40 tons

## Components

- **Hull.** AC 11, HP 130, DT 10
- **Helm.** AC 15, HP 40
- **Movement.** AC 14, HP 60 (enchanted sails; disabled = normal canvas only)
- **Weapons.**
  - Hidden 12-lb cannons ×4 behind false panels — AC 15, HP 30 each; +6 to hit, 3d10 bludgeoning; crew 2; reload 1 round
  - Ram prow — on collide, 4d10; ship must move 30+ ft straight
  - Enchanted sails — always-wind; ignore calm

## Crew stations

Full combat stations for gun deck, helm, and sails. Thunk runs the hidden battery.

## Handling / Combat

She still pretends to be a trader, but once panels drop she fights as a Crown cutter. Merchant cover is abandoned the moment guns run out.

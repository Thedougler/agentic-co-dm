# Uncertainty

> [!narration] Narration
> Eighty feet of cutter, low and lean. The light finds nothing to hold onto anywhere along her. One unbroken blue-black from cap rail to copper, a bare masthead over it. At the bow a hardwood woman walks forward with her hands open and nothing written on her face. She smells of pitch and new caulk. From half a mile she reads as somebody's coastal trader. The open water around her gives the crew room to approach, retreat, or keep watch; freeboard sits low enough to work from a ship's boat at the rail.

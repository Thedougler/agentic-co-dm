# Uncertainty

> [!narration] Narration
> A sleek Crown cutter bristling with concealed cannon ports, iron ram, and enchanted sails that always find wind.

## Sheet

- **Size.** Gargantuan
- **Type.** War cutter
- **Speed.** 5 mph sail / 2 mph row
- **Crew (min).** 6
- **Passengers.** 20
- **Cargo.** 50 tons

## Components

- **Hull.** AC 11, HP 130, DT 15
- **Helm.** AC 16, HP 50; disabled = no steering
- **Movement.** AC 14, HP 80; disabled = drift only
- **Weapons.** 4× 12-lb cannons behind false panels (AC 15, HP 40 each); iron ram prow (3d10); sails enchanted for always-wind

## Crew stations

Draves (captain), Holst (mate), Kuiper, Faas, Thunk (gunner), Noor.

## Handling

Can outrun most coastal craft. Anchors freely off Aruhe when needed.

## Combat

Acts on initiative as a vehicle; cannons fire every other round; ram on a charge.

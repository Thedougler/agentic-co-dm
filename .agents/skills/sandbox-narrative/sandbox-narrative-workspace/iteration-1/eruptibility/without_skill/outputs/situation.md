# Corvenna: The Neutral Ground Awaits

## Situation Frame

**Location:** [[Corvenna]], eastern [[Midchain]]  
**Condition:** Peaceful. No active disputes. No contested matches underway.  
**Party entry:** By boat through shallow reef water to the settlement's harbor.

The party arrives at [[Corvenna]] during a quiet season. The neutral ground fulfills its purpose without drama: it exists as a pressure point that keeps disputes from becoming wars. Right now, that pressure is idle.

---

## What the Party Perceives on Arrival

- A modest settlement built around a natural harbor among shallow reefs
- A few permanently stationed inhabitants: harbor keepers, merchants who trade with dispute-bringers, sailors who maintain the safe approach routes, and provision keepers
- Evidence of regular use: a cleared space suitable for champion combat, structures built to withstand saltwater, a reputation woven into the local culture
- No active construction, no gathering crowds, no preparation for an imminent contest
- A tangible, comfortable quiet — not eerie or hostile, but genuinely at peace

The NPCs encountered (harbor keepers, merchants, travelers passing through) are ordinary, unremarkable people. None have obvious agendas. None initiate conversation about disputes, conflicts, or the larger Midchain politics. They answer direct questions, but volunteering nothing about distant powers or brewing tensions.

---

## The Trigger Questions

The adventure remains latent until the party asks. Nothing in Corvenna's surface appearance demands action. The town does exactly what it is designed to do: it stands ready. The party must recognize what questions would unlock its role in the larger Midchain conflict.

### Question Category 1: Recent Disputes & Their Ripples

**Example question phrasing:**
- "Have there been any large disputes settled here recently?"
- "What was the last major case brought to Corvenna?"
- "Has anyone brought a serious conflict here in the past season?"

**If asked:** The party learns that yes, disputes have been resolved here—some of them significant enough to have changed course of smaller wars. The NPCs know the *pattern* but do not name specific parties or champions (per wiki: no champion, disputant, or judge is named). What matters: **resolved disputes leave consequences**. Powers that agreed to settle by champion combat here may have grudges about the outcome, winners may press their advantage, losers may seek alternative paths to their goals. A recent settlement may have satisfied one party while leaving another searching for a way to contest the result without reopening the original war.

**Adventure hook:** Learning that a recent dispute was settled in Corvenna can lead to discovering that one party is not accepting the result, or that the *next* dispute is about to erupt—and Corvenna may be where it must be brought.

### Question Category 2: The Eastern Waters & Shipping Routes

**Example question phrasing:**
- "What's happening out in the eastern waters? Any trouble on the routes?"
- "Is the sea route east still safe?"
- "Why are merchant ships taking longer to arrive?"

**If asked:** The party learns that the eastern waters are inherently treacherous—shallow, poorly charted, dotted with reefs and unmarked hazards. Normally, this is just the cost of sailing in this region. But unreliable reports are circulating: passages that should be safe are becoming more dangerous. Ships are taking new routes or disappearing entirely. Some blame poor charts, some blame unusual currents, some whisper of something *active* in the deep water.

**Adventure hook:** An anomaly in the eastern waters (uncharted reefs shifting, a creature in the shallows, pirates exploiting the confusion, or something stranger) threatens the shipping that brings dispute-bringers to Corvenna. Investigating this threat or resolving it may prevent a larger crisis—or expose that the threat is intentional, designed to force certain powers into conflict that *cannot* be resolved by Corvenna's rules.

### Question Category 3: Tensions & Brewing Conflicts

**Example question phrasing:**
- "What tensions exist among the powers around here?"
- "Are any neighboring regions in conflict?"
- "What disputes are simmering that might come to Corvenna?"

**If asked:** The party learns the general political picture of the eastern Midchain region. The wiki establishes that disputes come to Corvenna to avoid war—meaning the Midchain nations, city-states, or powers regularly have grievances serious enough to threaten armed conflict. Some disputes come to Corvenna; others are settled by war. The party can learn:

- Which neighboring regions have recently squared off (and where)
- Which conflicts were brought to Corvenna (and accepted the result)
- Which conflicts were *not* brought to Corvenna (and what happened instead)
- Which powers have long-standing feuds
- Which alliances are fragile or shifting

**Adventure hook:** A brewing conflict in the region may be about to explode into war unless one side is willing to bring it to Corvenna. Or, a power may be arming in secret, planning to bypass Corvenna's neutrality entirely. Or, the party may learn that they themselves are now _entangled_ in a regional dispute—perhaps they were hired, pledged support, or witnessed something that makes them witnesses or stakeholders in a conflict that should come to Corvenna.

### Question Category 4: The Neutrality Keepers

**Example question phrasing:**
- "Who runs this place? Who keeps it neutral?"
- "How does Corvenna actually enforce its neutrality?"
- "Who decides the rules for the champions?"

**If asked:** The wiki establishes that "the site of the contest, the people who keep its neutrality, and the rules that make a champion's result binding are not recorded." The party may notice this is a deliberately open question—and what they learn depends on how deep they push and whom they ask. A merchant might say "the keepers" without elaborating. A sailor might hint at something older, more mysterious. A permanent resident might deflect or change the subject.

**Adventure hook:** The mechanisms by which Corvenna maintains its neutrality may themselves be under threat. Perhaps the keepers are aging and no succession is clear. Perhaps a power is trying to corrupt or subvert the process. Perhaps the rules themselves are contested—do they apply fairly to all, or do they favor certain kinds of disputes? Investigating this can lead to discovering that Corvenna's greatest strength (its neutrality) is its greatest vulnerability.

### Question Category 5: Specific Regions & Powers

**Example question phrasing:**
- "What do you know of [[Midchain|the broader Midchain]]?"
- "How are things in [[drowned-maw|the Drowned Maw]]?"
- "Any news from [[Verdant Teeth]] or [[Central Strait]]?"

**If asked:** The party can ask about any region referenced in the wiki. NPCs know rumors, trade reports, and general reputation—but nothing precise or certain (Corvenna's neutrality means people here don't take sides or claim insider knowledge). What they learn can paint a picture of a region simmering with tensions or recently shaken by events that pushed one or more powers toward seeking resolution through Corvenna.

**Adventure hook:** A region is experiencing change—conflict, crisis, disruption, or opportunity. If that change spills over into the Midchain shipping lanes or threatens a neighboring power's interests, it may trigger a dispute that must come to Corvenna. Or, the party may learn that someone _should_ bring a dispute to Corvenna but is reluctant, dangerous, or already committed to a different path.

---

## The Quiet Before

Until the party asks, Corvenna remains what it is designed to be: a safety valve, a pressure release, a neutral ground where disputes are _supposed_ to go instead of exploding into open war. The settlement itself has no agenda. The NPCs have no schemes. The town is simply present and ready, waiting for the Midchain's next conflict to arrive at its threshold.

**What triggers the adventure is the party's recognition that in a region where disputes threaten war, asking the right question reveals that the entire system—Corvenna's neutrality, the shipping routes, the regional balance of power—is connected. Pull one thread and something happens.**

---

## Grounding in Wiki Facts

- **Eastern Midchain location:** The party is in shallow, poorly charted reef water. Navigation, trade, and travel are inherently difficult and dangerous.
- **Neutral ground for dispute resolution:** Disputes come here specifically to be settled by champion combat instead of war. This means the Midchain has conflicts; Corvenna is where some of them are brought.
- **Unknown keepers and rules:** The party will not find documented answers about who runs Corvenna or how it works. This is intentional and creates opportunity for mystery and discovery.
- **No current dispute:** Corvenna's peaceful state is consistent with it being a neutral ground that waits for disputes to arrive.
- **Regional context:** The wiki references [[Central Strait]], [[Drowned Maw]], [[Verdant Teeth]], and the broader Midchain. These are real places in the campaign; what happens in them affects whether disputes come to Corvenna.

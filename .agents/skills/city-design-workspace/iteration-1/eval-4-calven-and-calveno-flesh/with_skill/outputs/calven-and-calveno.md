# Calven and Calveno

> [!narration] Arrival
> Marsh and tidal flats run up to farms on a middle plateau, then to one city on the only solid ground. Canal water is the street. Pale four- and five-storey buildings line the harbour, and an unmarked pale building watches the far end without a sign. You hear deals shouted in the open, boat hulls knocking stone, and gulls over the flag. Brine, wet wood, and warm canal mud carry on the air. The unmistakable landmark is that unmarked pale building at the far harbour end — watching everything come in, saying nothing.

# Active situations

## Harbour paper vs Tessarine credit

**Involved:** [[dravosi-crown]] · [[tessarine-concordat]] · Seven Houses · Calveno Harbour

The Crown owns visible harbour authority and rates ships. Merchant families still sit the Seven Houses Council, but debt politics lean Concordat. A harbour paper can conflict with a Tessarine credit record; either can close a gate.

* **Visible signs.** Flag over the harbour; Italian names on canal talk; open deals; someone turned away at a gate for the "wrong" paper.
* **What they want.** Crown: rates and inspection primacy. Concordat: invoice primacy and House votes.
* **What opposes them.** Each other's paper; houses that can still stall; fishermen squeezed by both.
* **If nobody intervenes.** Debt majority hardens; stalling houses lose ground; more gates treat Concordat credit as the real passport while Crown inspections grow harsher on the water.
* **Trigger / date.** Next House vote / next major inspection sweep.

## Sewer toxin-substitute stock

**Involved:** [[Simone]] · [[lavinia-sordi]] · [[dravosi-crown]] · Calveno sewers

Crown toxin-substitute stock has appeared in Calveno's sewers. Simone is the named pipeline face; Lavinia Sordi left a Crown prison records desk over it.

* **Visible signs.** Sewer traffic that is not Rattkin-normal; records pressure; people who will not say Simone's name loudly.
* **What they want.** Crown wants the pipeline mapped and controlled; Simone wants the pipeline to keep moving.
* **What opposes them.** Exposure; Rattkin preference that the official story stay surface; Lavinia's burned desk as a warning.
* **If nobody intervenes.** Stock consolidates under Crown-facing handlers; Passage communities absorb more risk; Lavinia's exit becomes a quiet cautionary tale rather than a lever.
* **Trigger / date.** Next sewer find / next records request.

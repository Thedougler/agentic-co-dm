# Mercatura

> [!narration] Arrival
> Smoke columns rise from the eastern walls where Iron Coil catapults work. Inside, yellow quarantine flags snap over the lower streets. The plaza that once celebrated Otar's defeat is now a triage yard.

## Active situations

## Iron Coil Siege

The Iron Coil mercenary company has invested the eastern approaches and demands the city's treasury.

* **Visible signs.** Catapult fire, blocked roads, refugees.
* **What they want.** Payment and hostages.
* **What opposes them.** City militia; starving merchants.
* **If nobody intervenes.** The eastern gate falls in ten days.

## Tunnel Plague

A plague crawls out of the bomb tunnels Solange opened.

* **Visible signs.** Quarantine flags, mass graves.
* **What they want.** Healers seek a source; cultists want it to spread.
* **What opposes them.** Council denial.
* **If nobody intervenes.** Plague jumps to neighboring towns within a month.

## History retained

Solange Barret's ritual, Otar the Foul's defeat, and the bombs still happened — they are the cause of the current plague.

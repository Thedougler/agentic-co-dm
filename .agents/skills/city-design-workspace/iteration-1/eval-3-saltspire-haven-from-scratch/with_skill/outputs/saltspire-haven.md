# Saltspire Haven

> [!narration] Arrival
> The port clings to a pale cliff above a narrow strait. Masts cluster on a single long pier that juts into fast water. From the approach you see switchback stairs cut into stone, signal flags snapping on a spire of salt-white rock, and constant boat traffic fighting the current. Wind carries gulls, shouted berthing calls, and the sharp smell of brine and tar. The unmistakable landmark is the salt-white spire above the pier — visible for miles along the strait.

**DM thesis:** Saltspire is a choke-point market where navigation knowledge and pier legitimacy are the same scarce good — every visit forces a side on the tax/pilot fight.

## Orientation

### Districts

| District | Street-level read | Known for | Current pressure |
| -------- | ----------------- | --------- | ---------------- |
| The Pier | One long timber-and-stone finger into the strait | Berths, cranes, tax collectors from both councils | Arguments over who stamps the next manifest |
| Stair Town | Switchbacks and hanging houses on the cliff | Inns, ropewalks, pilot boarding houses | Prices spike when councils feud |
| Spire Watch | Around the salt-white signal rock | Flags, lookouts, channel charts | Pilots gatekeep who may read the charts |

### Landmarks

* **Saltspire.** Pale rock with signal flags; orients every approach.
* **The Shared Pier.** Single deep-water pier both councils claim.
* **Channel Mouth.** Fast water; visible rips on bad tides.

### Getting around

* **Cross-city travel.** Stairs dominate; pier-to-spire is a hard climb or a paid lift.
* **After dark.** Pilot lamps on the spire; unlicensed boats risk unmarked rocks.
* **Restricted movement.** Pier stamps required for crane use; rival collectors may refuse each other's papers.
* **Useful shortcuts.** Cliff ladders used by pilots; dangerous without a guide.

# Active situations

## Double stamp on the Shared Pier

**Involved:** Windward Dock Council · Lee Dock Council · Shared Pier

Both councils tax the same pier. Ships are delayed; stevedores pick sides; a crane already sat idle for a day after a public fight.

* **Visible signs.** Two sets of collectors; contradictory stamps; idle crane; shouting on the pier.
* **What they want.** Each council wants exclusive dues and to bankrupt the other's legitimacy.
* **What opposes them.** Mutual dependence on the single deep-water pier; pilot fee spike; merchant complaints.
* **If nobody intervenes.** Within weeks a major carrier boycotts Saltspire; one council attempts a forcible lockout of the other on the pier.
* **Trigger / date.** Next spring tide / next large carrier arrival.

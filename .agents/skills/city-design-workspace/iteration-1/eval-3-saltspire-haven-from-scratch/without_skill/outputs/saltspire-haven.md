# Saltspire Haven

> [!narration] Arrival
> Saltspire Haven clings to cliffs above a trade strait. The secret of the channel is held by the Pilot Cabal in a vault under the salt spire — ancient charts that show the drowned god's teeth. Harbourmaster Vex watches from the unmarked tower and already knows the party's names.

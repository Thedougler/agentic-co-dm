# Calven and Calveno

> [!narration] Arrival
> Marsh and tidal flats run up to farms on a middle plateau, then to one city on solid ground. Canal water is the street. Pale four- and five-storey buildings line the harbour; gulls and shouted deals stack over wet stone. An unmarked pale building watches the far end without a sign.

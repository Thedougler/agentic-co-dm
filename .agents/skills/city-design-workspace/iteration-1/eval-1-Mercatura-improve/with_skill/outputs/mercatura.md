# Mercatura

> [!narration] Arrival
> The city reads as lived-in and busy rather than ruined: stacked roofs, street traffic, and ordinary market noise. From the approach you catch a skyline of practical towers and a low haze of cookfires. Carts and foot traffic share the main approach; somewhere below street level a damp stone smell rises from grated openings. One unmistakable public landmark is the open plaza where rebuilt paving still sits slightly proud of older stone — locals treat it as ordinary ground, not a memorial.

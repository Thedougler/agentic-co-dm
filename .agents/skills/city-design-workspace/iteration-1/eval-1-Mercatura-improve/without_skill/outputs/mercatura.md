# Mercatura

> [!narration] Arrival
> Mercatura sprawls across a river bend, its towers blackened in places from old fires. Bells toll from quarantine towers. Smoke and a sweet-rot smell hang over the lower wards. The plaza memorial to the bombings still draws mourners.

# Active situations

## Lower Ward Plague

**Involved:** Merchant council · Temple of Mercy · Lower Wards

A plague rose from the bomb-scarred undercity and is killing dock workers. The council denies the scale.

* **Visible signs.** Quarantine chalk, carts of dead, empty stalls.
* **What they want.** Council wants trade open; healers want a full lockdown.
* **What opposes them.** Cost of shutdown; smugglers breaking cordons.
* **If nobody intervenes.** Within a month the gates close and neighboring towns bar Mercatura goods.
* **Trigger / date.** Next market festival in 12 days.

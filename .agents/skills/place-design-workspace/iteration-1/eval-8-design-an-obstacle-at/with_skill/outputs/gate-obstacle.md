# Ancient gate obstacle — labeled fixture

## Obstacle: Sun-Seal Portcullis
- **Sign:** Carved sun-disk above the arch warms and ticks when living weight hits the threshold stones; faint scorch on prior travelers' boots.
- **Trigger:** Two or more Medium creatures on the threshold without presenting a cooled iron ward-token.
- **Effect:** Portcullis drops (bludgeoning + restrained risk); sun-disk flares (radiant flash, blinded 1 round on fail Con save).
- **Counterplay:** Douse disk with water/sand; brace portcullis with pitons; present stolen/borrowed ward-token; Dispel/Utilize on ratchet.
- **Bypass (distinct):** Climb outer buttress to murder-hole ledge and drop past the gate without touching threshold; or dig under collapsed side lintel (time + noise).
- **Leverage:** Offer the gate-cult a service for a token; break the ratchet chain from above; time approach when disk is clouded at dusk (fiction window).

Not a single correct solution — multiple verbs work.

# Ancient gate obstacle (labeled fixture)

## Fiction telegraph
Hairline score-marks circle the threshold stones; ash drifts always inward; a cracked lintel relief shows a figure covering its ears as the gate-mouth closes. Anyone watching sees the marks brighten when weight crosses the center flag.

## Trigger / consequence
**Trigger:** ≥Medium creature steps the center flag without speaking the relief's gesture-word (or equivalent). **Effect:** Stone jaws grind shut (restrain + crush ramp); alarm bell in the gatehouse.

## Counterplay
- Jam the jaw groove with a crowbar/spike before stepping (Utilize); or
- Cover ears / mute the bell path so alarm does not propagate even if jaws move.

## Distinct bypass
Climb the cracked lintel and belly-crawl the relief ledge (Athletics) — never touches the center flag.

## Not single-solution
Force the jaws, pick a side seam, bargain with gate-wardens for the gesture-word, or use the ledge bypass — multiple valid methods; none prescribed as sole answer.

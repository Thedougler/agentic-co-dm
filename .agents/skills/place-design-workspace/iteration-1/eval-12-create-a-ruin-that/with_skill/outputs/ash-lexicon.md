# Ruin — Ash Lexicon (labeled fixture)

**Promise:** Discovery + Transformation — learn the lexicon and change what the ruin will become.

## Non-combat verbs / affordances
- **Study** basalt stelae to unlock door-words
- **Re-align** rotating floor disks to open silent stacks
- **Bargain** with the residual archivist-echo (no HP; wants a true name)
- **Harvest** soot-ink for later forgery or door painting
- **Consecrate / desecrate** the central plinth to flip the ruin's future state
- **Map** echo-delay patterns to find a bypass vault

## Payoff / consequence tied to promise
Completing the lexicon lets the party rewrite one sealed vault label — permanent Transformation of what the next expedition finds. Leaving mid-lexicon lets weather erase two stelae (Discovery debt).

## Enemies-vanished test
If all hostiles leave/defeated: stelae, disks, echo, soot-ink, and plinth still reward Study/Re-align/Bargain/Harvest/Consecrate. Nodes stay worth visiting with zero encounter quota.

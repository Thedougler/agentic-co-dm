# Sealed Archive — sluice truth (labeled fixture)

**Required truth:** the lower sluice can be controlled from the bell tower.

## Clue vectors (~3 independent)
1. **Witness / faction:** Night porter complains the bell ropes "pull water" when rung at flood — overheard without a check.
2. **Physical / document:** Bell-frame brass plate etched "SLUICE MASTER — PULL TRUE" (automatic if party reaches tower; Study deepens to match rope colors to gate levers).
3. **Spatial / consequence:** After any flood day, wet footprints form a straight line from tower stair to lower sluice even if nobody admits the link.

## Automatic vs declared
- **Automatic signs:** Wet rope ends; water hammer heard when bell swings; footprints after flood.
- **Declared Search/Study/Influence:** Match rope colors to levers; interview porter for timing; Survey sight-line from tower to sluice housing.

## Failed check policy
Failed Study on the brass plate: costs time (flood clock advances one step) and leaves a scraped note the wardens notice — but the wet-footprint vector and porter complaint still deliver the required truth. Failure never erases the revelation.

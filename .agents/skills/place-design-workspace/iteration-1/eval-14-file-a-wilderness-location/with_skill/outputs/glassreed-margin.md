# Glassreed Margin

## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> Wind moves through tall glassreed until it chimes. Soft mud holds boot prints for a day. A broken milestone leans west. Distant water flashes north when the cloud breaks.

---
## At a Glance
Wilderness filing between a northern lake, eastern mangrove wall, and western old road. Southern neighbor unknown — canon gap.

## If the party
- **Follow the old road west.** Reach surveyed pavement in ~3 days.
- **Cut east into mangroves.** Hit the wall in ~1 day; hard going.
- **North to the lake.** ~2 days; open water camp possible.
- **Push south without a name.** Enter unmarked country — no established neighbor.

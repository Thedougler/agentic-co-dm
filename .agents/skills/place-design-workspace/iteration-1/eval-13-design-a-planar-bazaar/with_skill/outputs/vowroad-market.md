# Planar bazaar — Vowroad Market (labeled fixture)

## Rule of strangeness
Spoken promises become physical routes.

### What changes / when / invariant
- **Changes:** A spoken promise ("I will meet you at the indigo stall") extrudes a walkable ribbon-path for 1 hour.
- **When:** On the breath after the promise is heard by at least one other creature in the bazaar.
- **Invariant:** The four corner obelisks, the central water-clock, and the rule itself never move; ribbon-paths cannot overwrite obelisks.

## Learn / exploit / resist / bargain / redirect
- **Learn:** Watch two merchants promise and follow the newborn ribbon; Study the water-clock marks that timestamp vows.
- **Exploit:** Promise something you intend to keep to forge a shortcut past a toll aisle.
- **Resist:** Wear a mute-charm / refuse to speak vows; ribbons cannot form from your words.
- **Bargain:** Buy a "counter-vow" from the Oathbroker that dissolves a rival's ribbon.
- **Redirect:** Add a conditional clause mid-promise to bend the ribbon toward an ally's stall.

## Navigability
- Landmarks: four corner obelisks (N/E/S/W pigments), central water-clock.
- Repeated cues: ribbon shimmer pitch rises near expiry; hawkers shout "vow-safe" aisles.
- Custom: newcomers are taught to touch the water-clock before first promise.

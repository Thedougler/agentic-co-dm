# Planar bazaar — promises become routes (labeled fixture)

## Rule of strangeness
- **What changes:** A spoken promise (audible, intentional) solidifies into a walkable ribbon-path from speaker toward the named beneficiary or place-word.
- **When:** On completion of the spoken clause; lasts until the promise is kept, broken, or bought out.
- **Invariant:** Market stalls, the Brass Spindle landmark, and the customs house do not move. Paths never cross the Spindle's shadow. Currency still exchanges hands.

## Learn / exploit / resist / bargain / redirect
- **Learn:** Watch a merchant promise delivery; a gold thread appears underfoot.
- **Exploit:** Promise a trivial true errand to mint a shortcut.
- **Resist:** Speak only in questions; wear hush-silk (custom) so promises don't bind.
- **Bargain:** Pay the Path Notaries to transfer a ribbon to another walker.
- **Redirect:** Break a promise deliberately to snap a rival's route (social cost + Notary fine).

## Navigability cues
- Brass Spindle (always center), painted stall numbers, ribbon color = promise type, Notary bells on the hour.

# High-tier wilderness — flight & teleportation (labeled fixture)

## What flight accomplishes
- Cross canopy gaps and river oxbows in minutes instead of hours.
- Scout ridge lines and see smoke, ruins, and storm cells early.
- Reach cliff-nest overlooks without the rope approach.

## What teleportation accomplishes
- Skip known safe anchors (camp cairn, surveyed standing stone) if the caster has been there.
- Extract from a failing ledge once an anchor exists.
- Does **not** grant knowledge of unvisited interiors or fog-hidden forks.

## Fiction-based constraints (justified)
- **Visibility:** Storm shelf clouds hide the far valley 50% of daylight hours — flight still works, navigation incomplete.
- **Anchors:** Teleport needs a prior safe chalk-cairn; none exist past the Black Gorge until placed.
- **Weather/exposure:** Above the canopy, lightning season makes prolonged hover risky (Con save or forced landing).
- **Incomplete information:** Flying shows roofs of the vine-ruin but not which courtyard is trapped.

## Guardrail
Abilities are not declared invalid. No fake walls on every route. Flight and teleport are useful with honest costs, exposure, and information limits.

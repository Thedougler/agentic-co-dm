# High-tier wilderness — Mirror Spires (labeled fixture)

## What flight accomplishes
- Skip lower switchbacks to mid-ledge caches
- Scout which spires hold open teleport-glyphs (visible as heat-shimmer rings)
- Carry a rope bridge between two ledges in one round of exposure

## What teleportation accomplishes
- Short hop between marked glyph rings the party has seen
- Evacuate a downed ally from a mid-ledge to the last touched ring
- Cannot land blind inside cloud-sea without an anchor (see constraints)

## Fiction-based constraints (justified)
- **Visibility:** Cloud-sea hides lower routes; flight above cloud is obvious to sky-wardens (exposure cost, not ability negation)
- **Anchors:** Teleport glyphs require line-of-sight or a previously touched ring — incomplete information, not "teleport disabled"
- **Weather:** Shear winds on the east face impose Difficult checks for hovering; still allow flight
- **Exposure:** Flying the crown silhouette triggers a known warden signal fire

## Not fake walls
Ground routes, flight, and teleport all remain valid with honest costs. No blanket "flight doesn't work here" or "teleport fizzles" without the glyph/anchor fiction above.

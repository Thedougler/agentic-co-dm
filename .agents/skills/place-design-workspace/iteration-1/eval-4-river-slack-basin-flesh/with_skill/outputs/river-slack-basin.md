# Slack Basin

## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> The river splits on a low stone lip — white water hissing past while a clear turquoise bowl sits almost still beside it. Tall gold-green grass presses wet sand; a thin cane stands in a dark smear at shore; muddy prints climb toward the trees. Out in the slack, a closed wooden pack and a torn maroon sleeve hang in clear water.

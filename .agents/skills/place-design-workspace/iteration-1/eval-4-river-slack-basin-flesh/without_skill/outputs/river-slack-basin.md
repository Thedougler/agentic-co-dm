# Slack Basin

## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> A still turquoise bowl sits behind a rock lip beside white water. A pack and maroon sleeve hang in the clear water. Bloody prints climb the bank toward the trees.

---
## At a Glance
Claimed backwater. Otters own it. Prints lead to Cutoff Lip. Taking from the pool triggers the river take.

---
## If the party

- Follow prints to Cutoff Lip.
- Pull the pack from the water.
- Fish the basin.
- Skip along the grass.

---
## Who

- Otter family; possible survivor on the bank; a hidden den mother named Silt-Whisker guarding cubs in a side cave.

---
## What

- Rock lip, turquoise bowl, cane, pack, sleeve, prints.

---
## Where

- North: deeper jungle toward Quiet.
- East: Cutoff Lip.
- South: Line Bank.
- West: mangrove wall settlement of Reedwick (new neighbor).

---
## Why

Teaches that the river claims its take.

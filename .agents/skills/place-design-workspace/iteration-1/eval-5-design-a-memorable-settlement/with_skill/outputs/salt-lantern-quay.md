# Salt-Lantern Quay

## Kernel (design — not filed as vault prose)
See transcript.

## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> A crooked pier juts into black marsh water. Glass jars of pale marshfire hang from iron hooks; moths never land on them. When voices rise in a lie, the nearest jars gutter. Nets dry like pale flags. The harbor bell is green with salt.

---
## At a Glance
Working settlement that sells light and berths. Promise: intrigue/refuge — choose whom the light exposes. Topology: pier → jar rack → counting shed → marsh boardwalk → smugglers' cut. Inhabitants: harbor master, net crews, smugglers. Clues: dimming jars, salt ledgers, cut-boardwalk. Pressure: jar theft clock. Return: smashed jars stay dark.

## If the party
- **Rent a bright berth.** Safe night; smugglers notice.
- **Speak carefully near jars.** Learn the dimming rule.
- **Guard the rack overnight.** Delay the smash clock.
- **Buy a dark window from smugglers.** Lose harbor trust; gain cargo path.

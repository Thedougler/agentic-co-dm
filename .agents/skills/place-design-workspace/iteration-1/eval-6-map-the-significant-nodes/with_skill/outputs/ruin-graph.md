### Topology notes
- **Loop:** Courtyard → wall-walk → West Gate.
- **Bypass:** South Postern skips Courtyard alarm.
- **Retreat:** Reliquary → South Postern if held; else Ravine Climb is one-way commit.
- **Tradeoffs:** West Gate fast/exposed; Ravine hidden/committing; Siphon informative/dangerous.

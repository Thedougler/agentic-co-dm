# Inhabited landmark — two factions (labeled fixture)

## Faction sheets

### Amber Wardens (tower keepers)
- Want: Keep beacon lit on schedule
- Fear: Beacon captured for false signals
- Method: Rotating watches, signal codes
- Resources: Oil, horn, 8 wardens
- Tell: Always trim wicks at dusk
- Offer: Safe night signal for allies
- Response: Bargain → share code once; Aid → escort; Deception → silent lockdown; Attack → beacon blackout + smoke; Neglect → oil runs out in 3 days
- Between visits: If neglected, clock advances quiet→alert (missing oil crates appear)

### Red Sail Corsairs (cove camp)
- Want: False beacon to wreck prizes
- Fear: Wardens learning their tide table
- Method: Bribes, night climbs, stolen oil
- Resources: 12 sailors, longboat, forged codes
- Tell: Tar on tower rope after raids
- Offer: Cut of salvage to helpers
- Response: Bargain → hire party; Aid → mark party as crew; Deception → reverse-bribe; Attack → withdraw to cove, return with numbers; Neglect → they attempt the climb
- Between visits: Progression clock: probing → rope left → oil theft → false flash

## Between-visit update
After 1 rest away: tar on rope (visible). After 2: oil missing. After 3: false flash wrecks a merchant — lasting consequence.

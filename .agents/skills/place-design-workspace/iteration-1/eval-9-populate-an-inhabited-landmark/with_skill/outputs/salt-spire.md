# Contested landmark — Salt Spire (labeled fixture)

## Faction sheets

### Saltwright Compact
- **Want:** Exclusive drying rights on the spire ledges
- **Fear:** Spire collapse / rival blessing that opens night markets
- **Method:** Toll ropes, ledger seals, hired climbers
- **Resources:** Three rope crews, a drying shed, harbor credit
- **Tell:** White salt-dust on boots; chalk tally on the lee face
- **Offer:** Safe night berth + share of dried catch
- **Response:** Bargain→discounted toll; Aid→lend climbers; Deception→blacklist; Attack→cut ropes under party; Neglect→raise toll next visit
- **Territory / red line / current move:** Lee ledges; red line = rival blessing on the crown; current = stringing a second toll rope

### Night-Blessed Choir
- **Want:** Crown open for moon-salt rites
- **Fear:** Compact sealing the crown stair
- **Method:** Songs that calm wind; sabotage of toll ledgers
- **Resources:** Six initiates, a storm-horn, hidden niche cache
- **Tell:** Brine-sweet incense; rope knots tied left-handed
- **Offer:** Wind-calm blessing for a climb; map of Compact blind spots
- **Response:** Bargain→share niche; Aid→guide past toll; Deception→false calm then gust; Attack→horn summons squall; Neglect→rite advances without PCs
- **Territory / red line / current move:** Crown niche; red line = Compact iron pin through the altar; current = steal one ledger page

## Between-visit update
If party leaves after aiding Compact: second toll rope is up; Choir niche cache moved one ledge down; clock advances Quiet→Alert.

## Progression clock (visible)
Quiet → Alert → Contested → Transformed
- Quiet: one toll rope; incense faint
- Alert: chalk tallies double; horn practiced at dusk
- Contested: ropes cut nightly; salt sheds burned or blessed
- Transformed: either Compact iron pin through altar OR Choir opens night market on crown

Visible sign each step: rope count, incense strength, ledger theft marks.

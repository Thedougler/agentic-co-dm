# High Eyrie

## Overview
> A tall basalt stack. The Sentinels live here. There is also Captain Vex and a new southern dungeon called the Black Sump.

## At a Glance
Stack overlooking the Drowned Maw. Held by Sentinels for two hundred years. Crown refused offers.

## If the party
- Go south into the Black Sump dungeon (newly established).
- Talk to Captain Vex about the seal.

## Who
- Sentinels, Master Kyzil, Captain Vex (new commander), apprentices.

## What
- Stack, stairs, dungeon entrance south.

## Where
- South: Black Sump dungeon and Port Hollow neighbor (established).
- West: Crown Islands.

## Why
- Adventure site with dungeon.

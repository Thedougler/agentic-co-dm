# High Eyrie

## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> A tall basalt stack rises beyond the eastern Crown islands. Wind tears across its face. From the top you can see the dark blot of the Drowned Maw and the spray of ships that dare the approaches. Monks with wings keep watch here.

---
## At a Glance
Basalt stack held by the Sentinels of the Eyrie for two hundred years. Overlooks the Drowned Maw. Eastern Crown chain context.

---
## If the party

- **Visit the summit.** Meet the monks and ask about the Maw.
- **Request ledger access.** They may allow reading if trusted.
- **Climb the stack without permission.** Risk confrontation.
- **Ask what the Crown offered.** The page does not say; monks refuse to invent terms.

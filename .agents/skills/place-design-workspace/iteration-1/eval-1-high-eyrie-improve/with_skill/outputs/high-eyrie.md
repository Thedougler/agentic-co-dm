# High Eyrie

## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> A basalt stack stands beyond the eastern Crown chain, high over discoloured water. Wind shears the rock face into knife-edges and cantilevered terraces where winged figures drill exact movement in peak gusts. Stairs and harness lines crawl the lee side; spray from the approaches below beads on cold stone. From the summit ledges the water darkens toward the [[drowned-maw]], and the stack itself is the only solid footing for anyone who needs to watch that water without drowning in it.

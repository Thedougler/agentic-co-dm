# Four location moves — Unstable Market (labeled fixture)

## Move 1 — Ledger Seal
- **Actor:** Harbor Assessors
- **Trigger:** Party publicly questions a stall about the theft
- **Visible result:** Yellow seal ribbons appear on three neighboring stalls by dusk
- **New opportunity:** Bribe or lift a seal to read who the Assessors already suspect
- **Lasting consequence:** Sealed stalls stay closed next visit; prices shift to unsealed lanes

## Move 2 — Guild Pinch
- **Actor:** Stevedore Guild
- **Trigger:** Faction action — Guild captain decides the theft threatens dock shares
- **Visible result:** One alley route is chain-blocked; porters redirect traffic
- **New opportunity:** Offer muscle to reopen the alley in exchange for a name
- **Lasting consequence:** Chain posts remain; that alley is toll-gated thereafter

## Move 3 — Night Whisper
- **Actor:** The Fence of Low Hooks
- **Trigger:** Pressure — market anxiety after two days without an arrest
- **Visible result:** Whisper posts (chalk fishhooks) bloom on crate walls
- **New opportunity:** Follow hooks to a midnight meet; buy or plant a rumor
- **Lasting consequence:** Hook-mark vocabulary becomes local code; still present on return

## Move 4 — Resting Stall Shift
- **Actor:** Market itself / stallholders collectively
- **Trigger:** Party rests overnight without resolving the theft thread
- **Visible result:** The stolen-goods stall has moved two rows; empty footprint left swept
- **New opportunity:** Track drag marks / ask night sweepers who paid for the move
- **Lasting consequence:** Empty footprint and new stall site persist — market does **not** reset

## Return state
Seals, chain posts, hook marks, and the moved stall footprint remain. History preserved; no default room reset.

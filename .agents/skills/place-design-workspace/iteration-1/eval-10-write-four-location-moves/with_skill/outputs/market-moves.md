# Four location moves — unstable market (labeled fixture)

## Move 1 — Stall cordon
- **Actor:** Market reeve
- **Trigger:** Party publicly names a suspect stall during the theft investigation
- **Visible result:** That aisle ropes off; guards redirect traffic
- **New opportunity:** Side alley bargains; witness interviews in the cordon shadow
- **Lasting consequence:** Stall reputation stained; prices on that aisle stay high next visit

## Move 2 — Guild counter-bribe
- **Actor:** Porters' guild
- **Trigger:** Faction action — guild pays to shift blame to outsider PCs
- **Visible result:** Handbills appear; a friendly contact goes cold
- **New opportunity:** Prove handbill false; buy a porter as double agent
- **Lasting consequence:** Relationship with guild drops a step until repaired

## Move 3 — Night shutter
- **Actor:** Pressure / theft clock
- **Trigger:** Party rests overnight without securing the evidence crate
- **Visible result:** Half the stalls shutter; only night market remains
- **New opportunity:** Night-only fences; rooftop routes open
- **Lasting consequence:** Day routes stay partially closed on return — history preserved, no reset

## Move 4 — Magistrate seal
- **Actor:** Magistrate (faction)
- **Trigger:** Party recovers the stolen seal or publicly exposes the thief
- **Visible result:** Official seal on three stalls; new access rule (papers at gate)
- **New opportunity:** Forge/borrow papers; escort sealed goods for favor
- **Lasting consequence:** Gate papers remain required on later visits

**Return state:** Does not reset the market — cordons, handbills, shutters, and seals persist as durable history.

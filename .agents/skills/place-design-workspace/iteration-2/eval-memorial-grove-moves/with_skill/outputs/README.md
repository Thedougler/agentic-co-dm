### 1. memorial-grove-location-moves.md (11 KB)

Full design document with four location moves:

1. **The Guest Threshold** — Hinewai honors guests who respect the memorial
   - Trigger: respectful passage without disturbing graves, tree, or flowers
   - Result: Hinewai appears, bows, speaks as equal
   - Opportunity: answers one question, grants safe passage, Stillbloom stands down
   - Consequence: recognized-guest status persists; hazards ease on return

2. **Stillbloom Fires—The Perimeter Rings Alarm** — charged stalks defend against quick passage
   - Trigger: movement >10 feet per turn through perimeter; dash, vault, violent motion
   - Result: bracts lift, needles quiver, spines whistle, smell shifts, grass trembles
   - Opportunity: learn pattern, exploit reload windows, find air/water routes, manage venom
   - Consequence: embedded spines carry venom; claustrophobia if poisoned; Stillbloom remembers violence and fires faster on trespass

3. **The Bound Soil Yields to Intent—The Graves Reveal Their Roots** — buried grief and phylactery anchors
   - Trigger: respectful investigation (careful search, divination, questions) OR violation (digging, striking, uprooting)
   - Result—Respect: luminescent roots visible; air shimmers; faint whispers; grass pulses; two root patterns visible (coiled vs. spreading)
   - Result—Violation: roots recoil; black flowers spread inward; fruit withers; Hinewai's voice becomes "No."
   - Opportunity—Respect: learn phylactery truth, pursue careful severance or companion identity, earn right to investigate
   - Opportunity—Violation: combat escalates, memorial becomes openly hostile, Hinewai enters Blight form
   - Consequence—Respect: graves show visible rootwork; opens negotiation/phylactery paths; respectful approaches accepted
   - Consequence—Violation: 1d10-day timer on Hinewai's return; memorial actively hostile; black flowers creep inward; Stillbloom fires faster

4. **The Fruit Tree Responds—Gift or Curse** — tree reflects party's intent
   - Trigger: respectful request, fallen fruit only, genuine intent to understand memorial OR taking without asking, exploitation, stripping
   - Result—Respect: fruit ripens impossibly fast; stalks bend low; smell shifts warm/inviting; grass glows softly; sense of welcome
   - Result—Exploitation: fruit blackens in hands; stalks recoil; smell turns acidic; ring contracts; grass darkens
   - Opportunity—Respect: harvest fruit with memory/visions of companion, extended sustenance, Nature advantage on island checks; earn land's favor
   - Opportunity—Exploitation: tree refuses; party must leave or face Hinewai defending companion's memory
   - Consequence—Respect: tree remembers; offers freely on future visits; fruit becomes clue to companion's identity; party subtly bound to Aruhe; island eases slightly
   - Consequence—Exploitation: tree withholds for 1d4 weeks; word spreads among island's hazards; memorial enmity becomes lasting

**Design principles applied:**
- Each move has actor, trigger, visible result, new opportunity, lasting consequence
- Grounded in Hinewai's page, Stillbloom and Spiritpollen mechanics, fruit tree and grave elements
- Consequence-rich and persistent across sessions
- Reward careful play; escalate consequences for carelessness/violation
- Support the location's core promise: "protection that kills is still protection"

## Location Moves

**The Guest Threshold** — Hinewai honors guests who respect the memorial.
- **Trigger:** Party enters as guests and respects the memorial (no grave disturbance, no living fruit taken, no tree or flower harm).
- **Visible:** Hinewai appears at tree line in her walking form, bows, places hand on trunk. Black flowers brighten slightly. Air loses rotten edge.
- **Opportunity:** She speaks as equal, answers one direct question about the memorial, grants safe passage through forest. Stillbloom stands down on future visits.
- **Consequence:** Party gains recognized-guest status. Perimeter hazards become less hostile. Memorial itself less overtly defensive on return.

**Stillbloom Fires—The Perimeter Rings Alarm** — Charged stalks defend against quick passage.
- **Trigger:** Party moves >10 feet per turn, dashes, or makes violent motion through perimeter; crosses black-flower ring without care.
- **Visible:** Pale bracts lift and angle outward; dark needles quiver; smell shifts to sharp ozone; spines whistle; punctured carcasses flicker; grass trembles.
- **Opportunity:** Party learns perimeter is actively defended. Careful passage (≤10 feet/turn) is safe. Can exploit reload windows, use objects to trigger volleys, find water or air routes, or manage venom if poisoned.
- **Consequence:** Embedded spines carry venom (local numbness → systemic poisoning → paralysis). Stillbloom remembers violence; future trespasses trigger faster, shorter reload windows. Memorial becomes claustrophobic if poisoned party members cannot leave clearing.

**The Bound Soil Yields to Intent—The Graves Reveal Their Roots** — Hinewai's buried grief shows its binding.
- **Trigger:** Party respectfully investigates graves (careful search, questions to Hinewai, gentle divination/history) OR disturbs graves directly (digs, strikes, uproots).
- **Visible—Respectful:** Luminescent roots visible just under grass. Air shimmers. Faint whispers (sorrow, purpose, binding, love—ancient, not hostile). Too-green grass pulses. Two graves show different root patterns: one coils tightly (protective), one spreads wider (reaching).
- **Visible—Violation:** Roots recoil violently. Black flowers spread inward like infection. Fruit withers. Hinewai solidifies at tree line. Voice becomes statement: "No." Air heavy, tastes of turned earth, ozone, grief becoming rage.
- **Opportunity—Respectful:** Party learns graves are phylactery anchors binding Hinewai to land. They understand Death Bloom's tragic nature. Can pursue careful phylactery severance, seek companion's identity/true name, or negotiate deeper access. Earn right to investigate further without hostile action.
- **Opportunity—Violation:** Combat becomes inevitable. Memorial collapses into open hostility. Hinewai enters Blight form. Stillbloom fires without restraint. Silent mourners become active predators. Party must flee or fight the land itself.
- **Consequence—Respectful:** Graves remain marked by visible rootwork. Party opens paths to negotiation, phylactery work, or laying companion's shade to rest. Respectful future approaches met with acceptance.
- **Consequence—Violation:** Timer begins on Hinewai's `1d10`-day return. Memorial becomes actively hostile: Stillbloom fires faster, Spiritpollen stands closer to clearing center, black flowers creep inward. Party is marked as enemy of the land.

**The Fruit Tree Responds—Gift or Curse** — Central tree reflects party's intent.
- **Trigger:** Party respectfully asks permission before gathering, takes only fallen fruit, or approaches with genuine intent to understand memorial (paying respect, studying graves, asking about companion). OR takes living fruit without asking, attempts to fell tree, or strips branches to exploit.
- **Visible—Respect:** More fruit ripens impossibly fast. Stalks bend low of their own accord. Smell shifts from rotten-sweet to warm, inviting. Too-green grass glows softly. Sensation of welcome (wordless).
- **Visible—Exploitation:** Fruit blackens in hands. Stalks twist away and recoil. Smell turns sharp and acidic. Black-flower ring contracts. Too-green grass darkens to near-black.
- **Opportunity—Respect:** Harvest fruit carrying memory/vision—eating grants fleeting images of companion's face, voice, or moment of death and burial. Fruit sustains longer than normal (multiple days on handful) or grants temporary connection to Aruhe's living systems (advantage on Nature checks about island). Understand viscerally that tree is monument, not resource. Earn favor with land—future foraging on Aruhe easier, hazards less aggressive.
- **Opportunity—Exploitation:** Tree refuses. Party must leave or face memorial's active rejection. If they persist, Hinewai appears (not to bargain, but to defend). Learn through hardship that ancient grief has teeth.
- **Consequence—Respect:** Tree remembers. Offers fallen fruit freely to respectful parties on future visits; produces nothing for exploiters for `1d4` weeks. Fruit becomes clue—flavor, properties, visions point toward companion's identity, true name, or unfinished business. Consuming fruit binds party subtly to Aruhe; they become quiet defenders of memorial, and island's perils ease slightly.
- **Consequence—Exploitation:** Tree withholds for `1d4` weeks. Word spreads among Aruhe's living systems—these travelers are takers. Hazards respond accordingly. Memorial's enmity becomes open and lasting.

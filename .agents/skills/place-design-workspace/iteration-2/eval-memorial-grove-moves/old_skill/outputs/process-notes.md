### 3. Location Move Craft

Per place-craft.md line 76-82:
> Prepare 2–4 moves a place or faction can make when a trigger occurs: close a route, recruit help, relocate a resource, reveal a cost, make an offer, change the terrain, escalate a ritual, or exploit a party choice. State trigger, actor, visible result, new opportunity, and lasting consequence. On return, preserve what is durable and update what is alive; do not reset rooms by default.

Moves chosen:

**Move 1: Hinewai's Welcome Ends**
- Type: faction move (Hinewai + her memorial)
- Trigger: party harms memorial or takes living fruit
- Actor: Hinewai (withdraws), silent mourners (lose restraint)
- Visible result: Hinewai absent, predators step forward, air heavy
- Opportunity: fauna now directly observable, negotiation with Hinewai if found in forest
- Consequence: welcome revoked, she won't speak or grant courtesy again
- Design purpose: Escalates social/territorial pressure, makes fauna active threats

**Move 2: Stillbloom Rises**
- Type: environmental/creature move (place itself)
- Trigger: party digs at grave or disturbs soil
- Actor: Stillbloom
- Visible result: bracts raise, needles extend, barrier forms at grave
- Opportunity: Study/Influence plant, climb around, negotiate with it
- Consequence: excavation blocked while barrier stands; barrier lowers if party retreats
- Design purpose: Makes gravesite defense active and learnable, respects player agency

**Move 3: Spirit Pollen Cloud**
- Type: environmental hazard move
- Trigger: party disturbs pollen sacs (marsh, treeline, or attempts to carry Bloom)
- Actor: Spirit Pollen (passive hazard organism)
- Visible result: 15-foot cloud bursts, shimmer, hallucinations
- Opportunity: retreat, Create wind, cover mouths, Search source
- Consequence: hazard persists until cleared; affected creatures Spirit-Haunted
- Design purpose: Reinforces pollen as hazard layer (already listed), gives multiple workaround paths

**Move 4: Bound Soil Grips**
- Type: place/phylactery move
- Trigger: party harvests grass, disturbs with tools/fire, tries to carry Bloom pieces
- Actor: bound soil and too-green grass (Death Bloom extension)
- Visible result: grass coils, roots bulge, ground becomes tangling/alive
- Opportunity: Study roots (learn direction toward graves), escape with Strength check, negotiate
- Consequence: grass marks disturbance; roots prevent carrying heavy memorial pieces
- Design purpose: Extends phylactery's binding nature into immediate player action/consequence

All four moves follow the pattern: present a trigger the party might reasonably activate, show a clear response, offer player choice/workaround, and establish something that persists.

## Five-Sentence Kernel

1. **Function**: A fortified crown garrison controlling the western Strait's mouth, its twin garrison detachments answerable to Admiralty sealed orders rather than civic authority.

2. **Fantastic element**: Sealed vaults beneath the fort preserve Aldric Drave's living bloodline chart—genealogical power held from the Governor himself.

3. **Present conflict**: Extra Admiralty companies stage reef-diving gear and underwater charges without Governor Voss's knowledge; the Admiralty prepares an unsanctioned operation.

4. **Player promise**: Discover what the Admiralty is planning, navigate divided command, access or protect the sealed vaults, or leverage the authority gap for advantage.

5. **Trajectory**: If unchecked, the Admiralty deploys its charges against an underwater threat (invoked by parent page reference to drowned-maw) without consent or transparency from the civic authority that shares the harbor.

## Identity: 3Fs and Contradiction

**Fantastic**: Sealed vaults holding genealogical power; invisible Admiralty authority; mystery operation staging for underwater threat.

**Familiar**: Stone fort, garrison troops, command structure, military inspections, sealed orders (bureaucracy).

**Functional**: Crown garrison point and chokepoint for Strait traffic; strategic advantage held by military authority rather than civilian governor; active staging ground for military operation.

**Productive Contradiction**: Two authorities—Governor (civic, petitioned, vault-blind) and Admiralty (sealed orders, guns, hidden purpose)—share one fortress, and the Admiralty does not defer to the Governor. This creates the page's central tension: civilians cannot access or influence the fort's primary mission.

**Spatial Signature**: Fort crown on cliff; gun emplacements overlooking the strait; sealed vault doors beneath the command level.

**Sensory Signature**: Salt wind, gun-powder and smoke smell, cold stone and iron, hushed military orders, heavy chains on sealed vault doors.

**Behavioral Signature**: Soldiers following sealed orders; officers tight-lipped and evasive; visible efficiency masking invisible purpose; guards restricting unauthorized access.

## Topology and Navigation

**Scale**: Settlement garrison / fortified site (not a dungeon; not a region; not a city)

**Cardinal neighbors** (from parent page context):
- **North**: The Marrow ridge (caves, sinkholes); ~1-2 hours on foot
- **East**: Mudflats, mangroves, south-harbour; ~few hours on foot
- **South**: Port Tidefall settlement (below on cliff); immediate (crown of it)
- **West**: Sheer cliff face, open strait; ~visible/nautical approach only

**Approaches**:
1. **Main**: Road/stairs from Port Tidefall settlement below (public, guarded, inspected)
2. **Supply**: Boat dock at cliff base (dangerous climb up; alternative supply route; exposed)
3. **Cave system**: Not established; mark as canon gap (could connect to The Marrow or vaults, but unconfirmed)

**Nodes**:
- Crown platform (command, visibility, gun emplacements)
- Barracks (garrison quarters, soldiers)
- Command office (officers, sealed orders)
- Staging area (reef gear, charges, operation equipment)
- Vault access / sealed doors (bloodline chart location)
- Supply stores / depots
- Stairs/access point (entry from Tidefall)
- Cliff dock (boat access, supply route)

**Edges and choices**:
- Main stairs: public but guarded; easy but visible
- Cliff dock: harder (dangerous climb); hidden from command but exposed on arrival
- Vault approach: restricted; requires trust or subterfuge; only one known approach

**Loop, bypass, retreat**:
- Loop: arrive via stairs, investigate garrison/staging, depart same way
- Bypass: supply boat route cuts command observation (but climbs exposed cliff)
- Retreat: escape via dock or back down stairs; fort has limited exit options if party is trapped

**Route tradeoff**: Fast/public (stairs) vs. hidden/difficult (cliff dock). Vault access has no bypass (single-lever problem if not resolved through other factions).

**Topology audit**:
- Two approaches exist (stairs public, dock hidden)
- Significant nodes pass verb test (climb, investigate, negotiate, discover)
- Edges have costs (guard presence, physical danger, exposure)
- Canon gaps marked (cave system connection; secondary vault access unknown)

## Factions and Location Moves

### Admiralty Authority
- **Want**: Execute operation against underwater threat; maintain sealed authority and operational secrecy
- **Fear**: Interference from Governor; exposure of operation; party sabotage or intelligence breach
- **Method**: Sealed orders, extra troop staging, equipment marshaling, restricted access
- **Resources**: Garrison troops, guns, supply ships, reef-diving gear, charges
- **Tell**: Soldiers tight-lipped; officers evasive; guards at vault and staging area; supply manifests kept secret
- **Offer**: Passage/safe conduct if party proves loyalty; partial intelligence if party gains officer trust
- **Response**: Alert state if party investigates sensitive areas; escalation if sabotage attempted; possible deployment of offshore support ships
- **Territory**: Crown, vaults, command office, staging area, supply stores
- **Current move**: Staging operation; maintaining secrecy; watching Governor's movements

### Governor Voss (Civic Authority)
- **Want**: Maintain civic authority and harbor function; understand what military is doing
- **Fear**: Military overreach; loss of authority; vault breach; trade disruption
- **Method**: Petitions, civic law, trade relationships, merchant alliances
- **Resources**: Civil authority, Tessarine trade house allies, harbor customs, merchant networks
- **Tell**: Frustration at being excluded from sealed orders; concern about trade disruption; lack of knowledge about garrison activity
- **Offer**: Civic support and audience if party brings evidence of Admiralty plans
- **Response**: Attempts to investigate or petition for transparency if alerted; possible direct confrontation with Admiralty if evidence is strong
- **Territory**: Port Tidefall below (civic), trade relationships, harbor operations
- **Current move**: Attempting to maintain authority despite military overreach; blocked by sealed orders

### Location Moves (Pressure)
1. **Alert trigger**: If party investigates vaults, asks about operation, or searches sensitive areas.
   - Visible sign: Guard rotations increase; vault doors locked down; garrison moves to alert status
   - New opportunity: Officers paranoid; communication increases (more to overhear)
   - Consequence: Harder access to restricted areas; operation timeline may accelerate

2. **Escalation trigger**: If party interferes with staging (moves supplies, talks to soldiers about operation, steals manifest).
   - Visible sign: Supply ship appears in strait; armed crew readies; operation may advance
   - New opportunity: Party can negotiates with escalating force; learn timeline
   - Consequence: Risk of open confrontation; offshore Admiralty assets brought to bear

3. **Intelligence trade trigger**: If party gains trust with junior officer or supply sergeant.
   - Visible sign: Officer shares partial sealed orders; mentions drowned-maw threat; references tidal window
   - New opportunity: Party gains operation timeline; can plan intervention
   - Consequence: Informant at risk if discovered; party alliance with military exposed to Governor

4. **Operation execute trigger**: If tidal window arrives and Admiralty timeline reaches zero.
   - Visible sign: Diving crews load equipment; final preparations visible; operation launches
   - New opportunity: Last chance to stop operation or learn its full scope
   - Consequence: Operation proceeds; party must choose: observe, interfere, or ally

## Information Economy

**Required secrets**:
1. What is the Admiralty operation target and timeline? (Connected to drowned-maw; tidal factors)
2. What does Aldric Drave's bloodline chart contain and why is it sealed? (Genealogical power; possible political/dynastic significance)
3. Did Admiralty deliberately exclude Governor Voss from operation authorization?

**Clue vector 1 (Witness/Faction)**: 
- Soldiers loose talk over meals; some resent sealed orders
- Junior officers may share concerns with trusted party
- Supply sergeant knows full manifest and staging timeline
- Governor Voss frustrated; will talk if party brings evidence

**Clue vector 2 (Trace/Document)**:
- Supply manifest labels gear; charges marked for drowned-maw
- Sealed order parchment visible in command office (if theft attempted)
- Vault door inscriptions or symbols (may reference bloodline or Drave)
- Staging area layout shows underwater operation purpose

**Clue vector 3 (Spatial Pattern)**:
- Correlation between reef-diving gear + charges + marked destination = underwater operation
- Timing correlation with tidal windows visible in supply logs
- Vault location beneath command level suggests strategic importance
- Operation staging in spring/summer (good diving season) tied to threat window

**Passive noticing**: Soldiers mention extra companies; visitors observe staging gear if they move through garrison; manifest labels visible from distance; vault doors visible but sealed.

**Declared actions**:
- Search/Study staging area: learn exact equipment type and quantity
- Study manifest: learn Admiralty estimates of operation scope
- Investigate vault: learn seal type, age, authority signature
- Question soldiers: learn morale, resentment, hints about mission
- Influence officer: trade trust for partial sealed order details
- Negotiate with Governor: learn his frustration and political position

**Failure cost** (does not block play):
- Search attempt fails: party spotted by guard; alert status triggered sooner
- Officer refusal to share: party must trust another vector or try theft
- Manifest inaccessible: infer operation from gear alone; slower discovery
- Vault attempt fails: vault remains sealed; bloodline unknown; but operation proceeds and can be observed

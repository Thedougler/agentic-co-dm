# Fort Crestwall

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Carved stone rises in craggy lines from the cliff's crown, with gun emplacements angled down toward the strait. The fort's outer wall is mortared limestone—age-darkened and patched—with narrow firing ports. Inside, soldiers move between the gate and stacked crates marked for diving operations; their uniforms are Admiralty grey, not the Governor's crimson. The air holds salt, stone dust, and the smell of fresh tar on rope. Beyond the gates, a paved road winds down into the mist toward Port Tidefall's lower levels.

---

## At a Glance

Fort Crestwall is the Crown's primary garrison and sealed archive atop Calder's Tooth. It commands the western Strait entrance and stands guard above [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]]'s trade harbour below. The fort answers to Admiralty-sealed orders, not the Governor's office—a divide that shapes both its function and its secrets. Extra companies have arrived staging equipment for operations the Governor does not know are being prepared.

---

## If the party

- **Asks garrison officers about the staging supplies:** Officers defer to sealed orders and cite protocol; a junior soldier who speaks too freely may face discipline, signaling that loose talk carries consequences. (Charisma check if the party pushes for more detail; failure brings a garrison officer to shut down the conversation.)
- **Attempts to access the sealed vaults:** Garrison guards treat the vault chambers as off-limits to anyone outside the detachment. Attempting entry triggers a security response, exposes the bloodline chart's strategic importance, and forces the Admiralty to accelerate its operation or tighten security.
- **Informs the Governor about the staging:** He reacts with anger at being excluded from Admiralty knowledge and moves to assert authority over the fort. This creates open friction between his office and Admiralty command, with the garrison caught between orders.
- **Approaches via the cliff or the Marrow:** The garrison treats unfamiliar climbers or ridge-walkers as potential infiltrators. Depending on approach, they may be challenged, escorted, or turned away before reaching the fort proper.
- **Seeks to navigate underground routes (via The Passage):** The caves below [[the-marrow]] connect to areas near the fort's foundations, but the garrison monitors for breach activity. This path is risky but bypasses the main gate.

---

## Who

Two detachments of Admiralty garrison hold Fort Crestwall. They maintain the gun emplacements, guard the sealed vaults beneath the fort, and oversee supply logistics for ongoing operations. Officers stationed here take orders directly from Admiralty command via sealed courier dispatch, not from the Governor's office or the local garrison hierarchy. Support staff includes armourers, cooks, messengers, and archivist(s) maintaining the sealed vault records. No individual names or detachment commanders are established canon—only that they answer to orders the Governor never receives.

Extra companies have arrived under seal with specialized diving gear and underwater charges, indicating staging for an operation against the [[drowned-maw]]. The garrison's composition has swollen beyond its standing peace-time strength, creating strain on billets and supply channels.

---

## What

**Gun emplacements:** Four cannon on the crown facing the strait, commanded by officers with standing orders to fire on vessels attempting to force entry or breach the Inspection Pier below.

**Sealed vault chambers:** Multiple chambers carved beneath the fort's foundation, secured by locked iron doors. [[aldric-drave]]'s living bloodline chart is kept in one vault; other contents are sealed from the Governor's knowledge and even from some garrison officers.

**Staging materials:** Crates and bundles of reef-diving equipment, weighted harnesses, underwater charges marked for [[drowned-maw]] operations, ropes, anchors, and sealed dispatch pouches. The inventory suggests active preparation for deep-water work.

**Garrison quarters and logistical spaces:** Barracks, mess hall, armoury, smithy, storage for powder and shot. Standard military fortress infrastructure without special features.

**Messenger relay station:** A stone building housing garrison messengers and their dispatches. This is where sealed orders arrive and sealed reports leave. Communication flows through this station, not through Port Tidefall's public channels.

**Shrine or chapel:** A garrison-only religious space; military chaplain on staff who hears confessions and records oath-takings. May be a minor source of information if a party member can gain trust.

## Where

**North:** [[the-marrow|The Marrow]] ridge runs north to south with sinkholes and collapsed caves, foggy and treacherous. A hidden route via [[the-passage]] (caves below) connects the Marrow to the fort's eastern flank, but the garrison monitors for breach activity. ~ 1 day overland via the [[overland-track]] to reach the ridge proper.

**East:** [[south-harbour]], a lightly policed secondary harbour for repairs and fishing, sits on the east side of the island. From the fort's crown, it is ~ 2 hours' descent via the cliff paths or the winding garrison road. [[oshas-slip]], a stilt village over the mudflats, lies further east. The garrison maintains watch of this route but does not actively police it.

**South:** Port Tidefall's trade shelf and docks lie below the fort's southern flank, accessible via the paved garrison supply road (~ 30 minutes descent). [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]] is where the fort's supplies enter and its reports (sealed and unsealed) depart. [[cape-solitude]], a wind-battered northern limestone formation at the island's tip with a ruined lighthouse, lies further north on the island's outline.

**West:** Sheer limestone and basalt cliff face drops to churning water. No landing or climbing route is maintained; only magical flight or extreme climbing (DC 18+) allows west-side access. The western Strait beyond is open water; the nearest land is contested waters and the [[drowned-maw]] threat zone.

**Canon gap:** The fort's exact distance from [[harwick]] and other distant settlements is not established. Travel times to the garrison from the Crown heartland are not canonized.

## Why

**For the Governor:** Maintaining civic order in Port Tidefall requires understanding what military force is positioned above the city and why. If the Governor suspects Admiralty action without his consent, he may seek information about the fort's readiness, its orders, and what it is staging.

**For the Admiralty or Crown:** The fort is a primary garrison, vault archive, and staging point. The sealed bloodline chart remains one of the Crown's most sensitive records. An operation against the [[drowned-maw]] requires preparation and secure supply chains—the fort provides both.

**For factions in Port Tidefall (Tessarine Concordat, local merchants):** The fort's activity signals the Crown's level of investment in regional security. An influx of military companies and diving equipment suggests the Crown is preparing for something significant. Trade decisions, courier routes, and risk assessments depend on reading the fort's activity.

**For the party:** The fort is a pressure point where two power structures (civic and military) overlap and conflict. Access to sealed orders, knowledge of the vault's contents, or understanding what the Drowned Maw operation entails becomes a resource—information the party can trade, expose, or leverage. The party might investigate the fort to gather intelligence, protect a patron (like the Governor), warn the Tessarine house, or understand why the Crown is mobilizing.

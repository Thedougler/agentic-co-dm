# Fort Crestwall Expansion — Process Notes

## Kernel (5-sentence design)

**Function:** Fort Crestwall is the Crown's primary garrison fortress on Calder's Tooth, overlooking the western Strait's entrance and standing guard above Port Tidefall's trade harbour—military anchor for a region where every ship entering the strait must stop for inspection.

**Fantastic element:** Sealed vaults carved beneath the fort's foundation hold Aldric Drave's living bloodline chart—a genealogical record so sensitive it remains sealed even from the Governor, maintained by a garrison that answers to orders only the Admiralty writes.

**Present conflict:** Extra companies have arrived at the fort under Admiralty-sealed orders, carrying reef-diving gear and underwater charges meant for staging against the Drowned Maw; the Governor remains uninformed, and the fort is a pressure point where military hierarchy overrides civilian authority.

**Player promise:** The party will navigate a fortress where governance splits between two powers (civic and military), where sealed secrets override the official chain of command, and where small decisions about information-sharing carry outsized consequences for regional stability.

**Trajectory:** If nobody intervenes, the staging supplies deploy into active operations against the Drowned Maw while the Governor remains deliberately kept in darkness, deepening the fracture between Crown Admiralty and civic authority—and potentially exposing sealed archives if operations go wrong.

## Identity framing

Fort Crestwall is a **clifftop military garrison** where authority flows from secret orders rather than visible hierarchy. It is isolated by geography (crown of the island), by protocol (sealed vaults and sealed orders), and by institutional pressure (Admiralty secrecy overrides civic governance). The central tension is **information asymmetry at the top of the chain of command**—the garrison knows more than the Governor, the Governor knows more than the Admiralty tells him, and some knowledge remains sealed from both civilian and local military alike.

## Player promises

**Promise 1: Authority and access.** The party will uncover that governance of Port Tidefall splits between the Governor (civic, visible) and the Admiralty (military, sealed). Accessing the sealed orders, understanding why they bypass the Governor, and determining what should be disclosed become actionable decisions with regional consequences.

**Promise 2: Hidden preparation.** The party will discover that the fort is staging an operation without the Governor's knowledge. They can investigate what is being prepared, for whom, and why the secrecy matters. They can choose to inform the Governor, warn the Admiralty, or leverage the knowledge.

## Structure and topology

Fort Crestwall sits atop Calder's Tooth as a crown fortress. It commands the western Strait entrance and overlooks Port Tidefall (dock level below) and South Harbour (east side, lightly policed).

**Cardinal neighbors (from Calder's Tooth context):**
- **North:** The Marrow ridge (foggy, sinkholes, collapsed caves) with [[the-passage]] running below
- **East:** [[south-harbour]] (lightly policed east harbour for repairs and fishing)
- **South:** Port Tidefall trade shelf and docks below; [[oshas-slip]] is stilt village over mudflats further south
- **West:** Sheer cliff face (impassable without climbing/flying); open western Strait beyond

Multiple approach routes:
- Main garrison entrance (from Port Tidefall below via supply road)
- Cliff access (dangerous, requires climbing or magic)
- Maw approach (underwater, reef-diving only, tied to staging supplies)
- The Marrow route (overland through ridge, risky, connects to [[the-passage]])

## Affordances (verb test)

- **Guard/command** — Garrison keeps watch over the strait from gun emplacements on the crown
- **Seal/secure** — Vaults remain locked beneath the fort, holding state secrets
- **Stage/prepare** — Extra companies arrange supplies and gear for an operation
- **Question/investigate** — Party can seek the sealed orders, the vault contents, or the nature of staging
- **Report/decide** — Party can inform the Governor, warn factions, or leverage information
- **Navigate/access** — Multiple approaches (supply road, cliff, underwater, ridge) with different risks

## Clue vectors (three minimum)

**Vector 1: Supply inventory.** The staged equipment (reef-diving gear, underwater charges) is unusual for a fortress garrison. Seeing it, asking about it, or encountering soldiers discussing its purpose reveals the Drowned Maw operation without requiring vault access.

**Vector 2: Governor's ignorance.** Conversations with Port Tidefall civic figures (the Harbourmaster, the Tessarine house) reveal that the Governor does not know why extra companies arrived or what they are preparing. His exclusion from the information loop is a clue that Admiralty authority overrides his.

**Vector 3: Sealed orders and their necessity.** Someone inside the fort (a junior officer, a quartermaster, a priest in the garrison shrine) might drop hints about why the orders are sealed—not their content, but the fact that the Governor cannot be trusted with them, or that they involve assets (like the bloodline chart) he would order sealed differently.

## Pressure and life

**Who is present:**
- Two garrison detachments (numbers and disposition unspecified to avoid invention)
- Officers taking Admiralty orders
- Support staff (cooks, armourers, messengers)
- Soldiers staging equipment and supplies
- Archivist(s) maintaining the sealed vaults

**Who is absent or nearby:**
- The Governor (civic authority, deliberately kept uninformed)
- Full manifest of reef-diving specialists (staged but not detailed)
- The Drowned Maw operation itself (staging point, not the main event)

**Faction pressure (from Calder's Tooth context):**
- Admiralty (sealed orders, staging operation, information control) — wants the operation to proceed without civic interference
- Governor Voss (civic authority) — wants to maintain authority but is kept in the dark
- Tessarine house (trade/information) — likely aware of unusual activity but bound by sealed mail protocols

**Location moves:**
1. **If the party asks garrison officers directly about staging supplies,** the officers defer to sealed orders, and a junior soldier might be disciplined for saying too much—raising the stakes for further inquiry.
2. **If the party informs the Governor about staging operations,** he reacts with anger at exclusion and moves to assert authority over the fort, creating conflict between his office and Admiralty authority.
3. **If the party accesses the sealed vaults,** they trigger a security response, expose the bloodline chart's importance, and force the Admiralty to move faster on the staging operation or tighten security further.

**Pattern identified:** Quality "If the party" sections have:
- 6–12 named action verbs that are distinctly different choices
- Each verb is bolded and followed by colon
- Each links to meaningful consequences (mechanical, navigational, or narrative)
- Each grounds the action in the place's specific topology or established NPCs
- Mix of movement, interaction, and discovery
- Avoid default-safe actions (omit the free ones)

## Design Decisions for Enrichment

The Bridge is a **tiered crossing and trading district**. The actionable verb set should reflect:
1. **Crossing choices:** Navigate between tiers, choose which level to walk
2. **Information gathering:** Ask Oleandro about ships passing, overhear gossip on lower tier
3. **Trading:** Buy from Nicco, rope seller, fry stall — each offers different value
4. **Observation:** Watch traffic patterns, scout for deals or intelligence
5. **Movement patterns:** Cross directly, linger on lower tier for shade/information, use upper bridge to avoid congestion

## Proposed "If the party" Verbs (Linked to Canon)

1. **Cross on the upper tier** → faster, more visible, linked to main bridge traffic
2. **Navigate the lower tier** → shade, trading, fry smoke; slower; good for observation or seeking Nicco
3. **Ask Oleandro about a ship** → [[oleandro-fuschi|Oleandro]] at [[Ponte Bassa]] shares what he's seen; consequence = access to ship knowledge
4. **Buy rejected stock from Nicco** → [[nicco-kettley|Nicco Kettley]] at [[La Gatta]]; consequence = cheap alchemical items with uncertain provenance
5. **Trade gossip for information** → lower tier is an information market; consequence = information + exposure
6. **Hire rope or provisions** → rope seller and fry stall offer practical goods and overhear traffic intelligence
7. **Observe ship traffic and patterns** → watching from upper bridge or lower shade; consequence = read the harbour's rhythm
8. **Scout for alchemical opportunities** → cruise rejected batches for rare or valuable items; consequence = hunt/DC not guaranteed
9. **Linger in fry stall shade** → social space where traders gather; consequence = overhear news, make contacts
10. **Cross to avoid the crowd** → skip the main thoroughfare by threading through lower tier; consequence = slow but unnoticed

## Before → After

### Original "If the party"

```
- **Use the crossing.** The party can ask [[oleandro-fuschi|Oleandro]] at [[Ponte Bassa]] about a ship, 
  buy rejected alchemical stock from [[La Gatta]], or trade gossip for access to information.
```

**Problems:**
- Single verb ("Use")
- Three activities chained with "or" (undifferentiated)
- No description of consequences or changed situations
- No mechanical outcomes or DCs
- No distinction between choices or why a party would pick one over another
- Not runnable at the table: DM lacks actionable specifics

### Enriched "If the party"

**10 distinct verbs, each grounded in the crossing's topology or canon:**

1. **Cross on the upper tier** — fast route, traffic, no cover, crowds
2. **Navigate the lower tier beneath the arch** — shade, safety from sight, dark, close to water
3. **Ask Oleandro about a ship** — information trading, Oleandro as canon source
4. **Buy rejected alchemical stock from Nicco** — cheap items, unknown effects, Intelligence/Arcana checks
5. **Trade gossip for information at La Gatta** — information market, social space, overhearing
6. **Hire rope or buy provisions** — practical goods, rope seller + fry vendor as social sources
7. **Observe ship traffic and patterns** — passive intelligence, Wisdom (Insight) DC 12
8. **Scout for rare batches** — active treasure hunting, Intelligence (Arcana) DC 13–15
9. **Linger under the arch** — community-building, long-term relationship investment
10. **Cross to avoid being seen** — stealth route, Dexterity (Stealth) DC 12, consequence of burnout

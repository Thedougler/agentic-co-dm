# The Bridge

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Calveno's main crossing carries traffic over the canal in stacked tiers. The lower level is crowded beneath the arch, where fry smoke, rope, paper cones, and the canal's damp breath mix; above it, the bridge carries people toward the city's other business.

## At a Glance

[[The Bridge]] is a tiered canal-city district in [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]], including the main crossing and the trading spaces beneath it.

## If the party

- **Cross on the upper tier:** Walk the main bridge over the canal. Fast route, open to sun and sight; traffic carries news of ships and harbour politics. No cover; deal with crowds and Dravosi harbour riders moving trade goods.

- **Navigate the lower tier beneath the arch:** Stay beneath the bridge span where fry smoke and rope dust hang thick. Slow movement, good cover from above, but darker and closer to the water smell. This is where traders who want shade—and who want to avoid being seen—do their business.

- **Ask Oleandro about a ship:** [[oleandro-fuschi|Oleandro Fuschi]] at [[Ponte Bassa]] has watched every vessel that moved through this crossing. He trades information about arrivals, departures, and cargo visible in passing. Cost is negotiation; return is actionable intelligence about a ship or its captain. Oleandro knows the harbour's rhythm.

- **Buy rejected alchemical stock from Nicco:** [[nicco-kettley|Nicco Kettley]] at [[La Gatta]] sells experimental batches too unstable or impure to move through official channels. Cheap, varied stock—salves, inks, compounds—but every batch carries unknown side effects or unreliable yield. Intelligence or Arcana checks may reveal the intended use; reliability is the buyer's wager.

- **Trade gossip for information at La Gatta:** The lower tier beneath the arch is an information market. Sit at the stall, buy fried food or drink, and listen to traders and dock workers. Gossip about harbour politics, caravan delays, Crown tax changes, missing shipments, and loose talk about cargo value flows here before it hardens into official notice. Listening is safe; speaking draws attention.

- **Hire rope or buy provisions from the stall vendors:** The rope seller (beside [[La Gatta]]) and the fry stall vendor (flanking [[La Gatta]] on the other side) both do steady business. Rope is solid stock; the fry cook knows the dock workers by name and hears what they carry, what they fear, and where the shortage prices are rising. Five minutes of chat with either can yield a lead—if they decide you're worth talking to.

- **Observe ship traffic and counting patterns:** Watch from the upper bridge or the shade of the lower tier. Over an hour, read who comes through, what they carry, what the Dravosi riders wave past, and what they flag for inspection. No roll needed if you are patient; a Wisdom (Insight) check at **DC 12** notices tension between Crown inspectors and merchant boats, or which families' runners are moving stock that should not be.

- **Scout for rare or valuable rejected batches:** Cruise [[La Gatta]]'s stock looking for batches with unknown provenance or unusual colour. An Intelligence (Arcana) check at **DC 13** identifies an item's intended use; **DC 15** reveals whether it is worth more to a specialist than Nicco's asking price. One rare find per visit is the pattern; pushing luck invites Nicco to ask why you are interested in what you are interested in.

- **Linger under the arch to be part of the crowd:** The lower tier is where deals happen quietly. Sit, eat, listen, and become part of the fry stall's regular scene. Over days, contacts begin to offer work, warning, or trade. No mechanical cost; the investment is time and visibility.

- **Cross to avoid being seen:** The lower tier under the arch offers shade and shadow. Move through without buying, without stopping, staying close to the rope seller's wall and the scent of the fry stall. A Dexterity (Stealth) check at **DC 12** keeps you unnoticed by harbour riders and Crown inspectors moving across the upper tier. This route is safe because nobody expects use—but regular use burns it.

## Who

[[oleandro-fuschi|Oleandro Fuschi]] runs [[Ponte Bassa]] at the main crossing. [[nicco-kettley|Nicco Kettley]] runs [[La Gatta]] on the bottom tier. Other bridge traders and any formal district authority are not established.

## What

The Bridge is a trading area arranged across tiers. [[Ponte Bassa]] tracks ships passing through the crossing; the bottom tier holds stalls such as [[La Gatta]], where rejected alchemical batches are sold cheaply.

## Where

The Bridge is in [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]], spanning the city's canal and main crossing. [[La Gatta]] is under the arch on the bottom tier, between a fry stall and a rope seller; the district's full tier layout is not established.

## Why

The party comes to cross the canal, find a ship or its cargo in Oleandro's memory, or buy cheap alchemical stock from Nicco. The Bridge turns movement through Calveno into visible trade and gossip.

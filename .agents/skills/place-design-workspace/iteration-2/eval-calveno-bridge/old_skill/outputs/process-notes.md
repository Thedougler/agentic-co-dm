#### 2. If the party (CORE ENRICHMENT)

### Design Decisions

1. **Topology is crossing-as-system, not just geography**: The tiered structure is the VERB TEST - parties naturally "move between tiers," "observe from above," "work the lower tier," etc.

2. **Oleandro and Nicco are affordances**: They represent access to information (ships) and resources (alchemical stock). If the party section should show what becomes possible by talking to them.

3. **At a Glance should name the situation**: Not "it is a district" but "it is a busy crossing where commerce hides beneath transit, and the tiers divide who sees what."

4. **Canon gaps stay explicit**: We don't know the full tier layout, the other bridge traders, or formal authority. No invention.

5. **Verb test**: Each player verb (buy, track, observe, trade, cross) should lead to a changed situation or linked page.

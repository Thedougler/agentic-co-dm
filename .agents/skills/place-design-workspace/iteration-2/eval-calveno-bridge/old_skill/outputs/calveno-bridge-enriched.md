# The Bridge

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Calveno's main crossing carries traffic over the canal in stacked tiers. The lower level is crowded beneath the arch, where fry smoke, rope, paper cones, and the canal's damp breath mix; above it, the bridge carries people toward the city's other business. Merchants work the bottom tier while walkers pass overhead, and nobody pauses except to trade.

## At a Glance

[[The Bridge]] is a tiered canal crossing in [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]], where the main bridge carries foot traffic above while commerce and work happen beneath the arch. The tiers divide who sees what: walkers pass through, traders stay, Oleandro watches ships from above. The party can skip the crossing with a boat or canal passage, but crossing feeds gossip and access to both supplies and information.

## If the party

- **Track a ship through [[oleandro-fuschi|Oleandro]].** Bring a ship's name or description to [[Ponte Bassa]] on the upper level; Oleandro holds records of traffic, cargo, and routes. Trade gossip for memory — he remembers what he sees.

- **Buy rejected alchemical stock from [[La Gatta]].** Descend to the lower tier and examine batches of failed potions, compounds, and reagents sold cheap at [[La Gatta]] under the arch. Sift through them with a check if quality or safety is uncertain; Nicco sells as-is and does not warrant.

- **Observe tier traffic and gossip.** Move between upper and lower levels, watching where Calveno's different people work and pass. Overhear deals shouted in the lower tier, notice who climbs to Ponte Bassa. The tiers reveal patterns of trade, authority, and movement.

- **Cross to another district.** Use the main bridge as a route through Calveno, noting that water-level and street-level passages offer different views and connections; the crossing itself is a junction between districts.

- **Work the lower tier.** Haggle with rope sellers or fry vendors flanking [[La Gatta]], trade information with other bridge workers, or move through the cramped lower level to avoid attention from above.

## Who

[[oleandro-fuschi|Oleandro Fuschi]] runs [[Ponte Bassa]] at the main crossing on the upper level, tracking ships and cargo. [[nicco-kettley|Nicco Kettley]] runs [[La Gatta]] under the arch on the lower tier, selling rejected alchemical stock cheaply. A fry stall and a rope seller flank [[La Gatta]]; their operators and other bridge traders are not established. The bridge has no formal district authority on record.

## What

The Bridge is a trading area arranged across two tiers. The upper level holds [[Ponte Bassa]], a vantage point overlooking the crossing and the canal — Oleandro watches ships and keeps records. The lower tier beneath the arch holds [[La Gatta]], a stall dealing in rejected alchemical batches, flanked by a fry stall and a rope seller. The lower tier is crowded and damp, mixing food smoke, rope, and canal smell.

## Where

The Bridge is in [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]], spanning the city's main canal crossing. [[Ponte Bassa]] sits on the upper level of the bridge itself, giving a clear view of canal traffic. [[La Gatta]] occupies the space beneath the arch on the lower tier, between the fry stall and the rope seller. The full layout of the lower tier beyond [[La Gatta]] is not established. Northern, eastern, southern, and western connections to other Calveno districts are not established; canon gaps remain.

## Why

The party comes to track a ship or its passage through Oleandro's memory, to buy cheap alchemical stock from Nicco, or to cross the canal itself. The Bridge turns movement through Calveno into visible trade and gossip — the tiers divide what walkers see from what traders know, making the crossing a point of information and access.

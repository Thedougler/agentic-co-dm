## Geography Interpretation

Torn Crossing is a flood-scoured river crossing in the grasslands:
- **North:** Upvalley direction → [[line-bank|Line Bank]] (established)
- **South:** Downvalley direction → [[landing-bank]] (established; "just upvalley of" it)
- **East/Inland:** Dark jungle wall/woods (established in text, but place name unknown)
- **West:** Unknown — likely either the open grasslands side of the river or explicitly unmapped

Key observation: The current "West: Downstream to [[landing-bank]]" conflates West with South. On a north-south river, downstream = south, not west. This needs resolution.

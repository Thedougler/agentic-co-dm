Beneath a living cloak of leaves, vines, and dry grass, a massive spotted cat lies flat in tall grass. Its broad head barely clears the stem line, amber eyes watching the game trail ahead, ivory saber teeth showing from the dark muzzle. Golden-brown fur carries dark rosettes across the flanks, hidden beneath the living mantle draped over its back and shoulders. Large paws rest flat against the earth, and a long banded tail curves around its haunches. The vegetation breaks the spotted outline completely, and the predator form sits indistinguishable from the grass mounds around it.

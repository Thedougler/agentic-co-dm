Black water fills the vault far below. A narrow crest of stone rises from where you stand, threading forward above the waterline toward a shallow basin. The brass key rests untouched on the basin's rim.

Between you and the basin crouches a river drake. As long as a man, its rust-scaled body coils low in the shallows. One massive foreclaw reaches up to grip the basin's rim, claws white and splayed. Long horns curve back from its broad skull.

Your footfall echoes through the vault. The drake's head turns toward you. Eyes bright, they fix on where you stand at the ledge.

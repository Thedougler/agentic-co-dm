Water fills the temple chamber to your knees. You stand on the entry ledge—dry stone where the floor rises—and a cracked bridge spans before you toward the altar platform. Three sahuagin cluster at the far end of the bridge near the altar, spears held ready, bodies turned toward you. Behind them, a collapsed pillar angles up from the water. Two more sahuagin move through that wreckage, webbed claws scraping against stone. The water between you and them churns with their movement.

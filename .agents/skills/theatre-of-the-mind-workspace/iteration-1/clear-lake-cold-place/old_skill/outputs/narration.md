An inland lake stretches across a wide expanse, its water clear enough that the stone bottom shows in the shallows and the far shore traces a dark line. Wet stone slopes into the water at the bank, cool to the touch. Cool lake-smell hangs in the air where water meets stone.

River mouths feed in from the north, darker threads visible in the clear water. One channel keeps to the western shore toward the Grove, with walkable routes along both banks.

Beneath the surface, enormous shapes turn slowly through the depths. Vast bodies rise and sink while smaller figures dart among them. An otter surfaces near the channel.

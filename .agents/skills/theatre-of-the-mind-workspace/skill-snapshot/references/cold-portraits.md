# Cold portrait contracts

When the mode is **standalone cold portrait** (no party, encounter, or table
state supplied), apply the contract that matches the subject type. Each
contract defines what the portrait covers and what stays out.

## Creature / monster

Body, scale, parts, ordinary stable behavior. Signature moves, tactics,
Hookline/Reel, Stoop steal, Grubnade burst, ambush scripts, time-skip tactics
("minutes later"), and "open with…" running notes stay in `[!mechanic]` /
`[!secret]-` / At the table / DM sections. They must not become a resolved
attack film or encounter cutscene inside the callout.

"Ordinary observable behavior" is idle/species habit (chews a flower, stands
over a carcass), not playing the tactic script to completion and not inventing
a disturb stimulus ("When the branch moves…") when no table state supplies it.
Inventing a trailing PC, ankle/boot contact, completed haul, completed item
tear, or full detonation is a fail.

Fold chin, blood, eyes, claws onto the body in flowing prose — no **telegram
stubs** (fragment sentences that isolate a minor fact: `Chin drips.` /
`Antlers.`).

## Location / site

Stable **public geography**. Choose an organizing spine independently.
Body-scale size and at least one usable affordance cue as nouns/verbs (wet
stone underfoot, followable channel, climbable ledge).

**Ban** place-design kernel fields and schema voice: Function, Fantastic,
Conflict, Promise, Trajectory, Aspects, Player verbs, Hunger / possession
metaphysics, and rule-talk like `unclaimed` / `claim`. Ban invented mystery
closers and staged located-change drama unless current table state supplies
that motion. Creature signs and encounter pressure belong to a **situated**
first look. Design kernels stay upstream (`place-design`); TotM renders
geography. Respect the owning note's `Where` ledger; do not invent
north/east/south/west neighbors or travel-day distances.

Do not close on a thesis, leak Aspects/secrets, or use opaque caste/jargon
nouns. Staged hostility requires current table state unless the parent
explicitly establishes stable public ecology without encounter film. A
dual-title note must publicly render both halves or be retitled. Keep vehicle
access kitchen-table (lines, rail, hold), never `boarding interface` in player
prose. Keep lore miracles in Secrets/DM. The cold spine is body-scale geography
and a drawable affordance, stopping on stable geography.

## Hazard

The hazard body or patch is the grammatical and attentional subject. Do not
route through the creature contract (no Hookline/Reel-style contact film) or
the place contract (no invented valley camera, no lake/terrain spine).

Coverage: form and scale, positive material or construction, and one positive
sensory or physical warning. Prefer kitchen-table words: plain spoken nouns
and concrete verbs over ecology or workshop compounds.

Do not use frost-mystery or absent-flash riddles (`no frost`, `rather than
glitter`), em dash, italic underscore wrapper, or a sweeping landscape camera.
A supported plain hardness contrast (sparkles staying fixed instead of swaying
like soft grass) is allowed. Stop at the visible hazard and its player-facing
affordance or danger. Resolved contact, Shatter, Glass Bloom, or scripted
aftermath belong to the table state or mechanic layer.

## Faction / organization

Coverage dimensions (not a rigid sentence order): public mask, one concrete
kitchen-table method footprint, one environmental Tell a bystander could
notice, and a shared title or face cue when the parent supplies one. Join into
drawable prose rather than an abstract organization gloss.

These are craft labels — never use `mask`, `footprint`, `Tell`, or `face cue`
in faction `[!narration]`; render the concrete signs directly.

Keep secret agenda, Rule of Two, hunt clocks, and other DM-only pressure out.
Ban workshop abstractions (`martial reach`, `dropped the ledgers`). Do not
invent a plot, venue, encounter, or hidden motive. Stop once public facts are
shown.

## Technique (`kind: technique`)

Use a **non-object tell contract** — not the item silhouette/material/wear bar.
State immediately perceivable training tells as stable facts: breath, stance,
wing set, and how the form looks when used (e.g. a strike past ordinary
wingspan).

Do not invent a current venue, terrace duel, gust-peak scene, or held gear.
Fail: object-silhouette forced onto techniques; invented venue ("On a
wind-open terrace…"); mysticism/theme ("the blow answers that measure");
meta closers ("the tell ends before…"); resolved hit film. Training location
and order lore stay in Fiction / Training. Mechanical package stays Mechanics.

## Object / weapon (Fate Spinner bar)

Cold item `[!narration]` reads aloud as a drawable object, not a catalog gloss.
Coverage (flowing prose dimensions, not a checklist dump):

1. **Concrete noun** (musket, long gun, blade) — not inventory jargon as the
   whole sentence.
2. **Scale vs body** (shoulder stock, barrel past the forearm).
3. **Material + wear** (dark wood, blued steel, brass bright at the trigger).
4. **One non-sight sense or ordinary physical behavior** (weight forward, lever
   clicks, cool in the palm) — not magic tone.

Fail: `carries as`, `ordinary X for Y`, naming lore/biography closers ("He
named it for…") unless a **visible mark** on the object (engraved plate),
rarity/attunement/DCs, technique-as-abstract-strike. Layering: Appearance ≠
Identified Properties ≠ Fiction/Secret. Owner dedication stays Story hooks.

# Concept

Read this in step 3. It covers what the NPC does at the table, the axes that
keep a cast diverse, and how to build a face, a voice, and tells.

## Function at the table

What the NPC brings to play this session. It shapes the design; it is not the
frontmatter `role`.

| Function | Brings to play | Typical pressure |
|---|---|---|
| Informant | A lead or missing context | Accuracy, safety, price |
| Gatekeeper | Access to a person, place, or resource | Proof, etiquette, time |
| Patron | A job and resources | Terms, reputation, competing duty |
| Rival | A comparable goal | Race, sabotage, reluctant respect |
| Ally | Capability and a perspective | Cost, boundaries, a conflicting aim |
| Witness | A contested account | Fear, memory, credibility |
| Foil | A different answer to a PC's values | Example, challenge, temptation |
| Faction face | Institutional leverage | Policy against personal need |
| Ward | Stakes and vulnerability | Safety, agency, escape |
| Villain or agent | An active opposing plan | Clock, consequence, choice |

## Diversity axes

A cast drifts toward the same middle-aged, able-bodied, polite person. Vary
each new NPC from the others in the same place or faction on at least two
axes:

- **Age and body:** a child runner, an old diver with one lung, a giant
  cramped in a low room.
- **Status:** who they answer to and who answers to them.
- **Temperament:** warm, prickly, anxious, bored, delighted, grieving.
- **Competence:** the one thing they do better than anyone in the room.
- **Relationship to power:** loyal, resentful, bought, invisible, climbing.
- **Speech:** talkative or terse, formal or blunt, precise or rambling.
- **What they carry:** a tool, a debt, a relic, a child, a grudge.

Ancestry shapes experience and circumstance, never personality. A trait of
the person, not of their people, is what players remember.

## Face

Two or three details a player could repeat next session, one sound or smell
with its source, and what their hands do. Strong face details are specific,
visible from across a room, and tied to the person's life.

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| "a grizzled sailor" | "a woman with a rope-burn scar across both palms who counts under her breath while she coils line" |
| "an elegant noble" | "a young lord whose gloves are a size too large, his dead father's, the fingertips stuffed with wool" |
| "a shifty merchant" | "a halfling who keeps one hand flat on his strongbox lid and smells of clove oil" |

## Voice

Voice grows from what the person cares about:

- **Word choice:** trade words, formal titles, sailor's curses, bookkeeping
  terms.
- **Rhythm:** short and clipped, long and winding, questions answered with
  questions.
- **One verbal habit:** a phrase they repeat, a way they address people, a
  number they always round to.
- **What they avoid:** the subject they change, the name they never say.

Three sample lines prove the voice: the **ask** (what they want from the
party), the **refusal** (how they say no), and the line **under pressure**
(when the secret or the limit is touched).

## Tells

A tell is a detail in the face or a habit that points at something hidden.
It hides by being ordinary and specific.

| Hidden truth | Tell |
|---|---|
| She is married to the smuggler she informs on | A wedding ring worn on a cord under her collar; she touches it when his crew is named |
| He can read, though he claims he cannot | Ink under his fingernails |
| The priest has lost his faith | His holy symbol is polished only on the front |
| The guard captain is being paid off | New boots, too fine for her pay |

Each tell has its truth on the DM page and a way for players to test it.

## Weak to strong

| Weak concept | Strong concept |
|---|---|
| A gruff dwarf smith | A smith who forges only from wreck iron, because the Crown taxes ore; she knows every wreck on the reef by the rust |
| A mysterious hooded stranger | A courier who hides a burned face from the people who burned it, and hires the party to deliver the letter he cannot carry into their town |
| A jolly innkeeper | An innkeeper who laughs at every joke because his debts are held by the loudest regular |

Each strong concept names its twist, a face detail, and what the person wants.

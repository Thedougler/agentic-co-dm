# Worked example

One scene-scale NPC taken through steps 1 to 7. Every name here is made up
for illustration and is never canon. Match the **shape and quality**; build
your own person from your own vault and your own party.

## 1. Canon inventory

- `[[gull-stair]]` · place · the harbour town where she keeps the tally desk.
- `[[harbour-court]]` · faction · rules on cargo disputes at the evening bell.
- `[[saltwardens]]` · faction · tax every landed catch and want her ledger.
- `[[the-gannet]]` · vehicle · her brother's fishing boat.
- PC threads: Oriel owes the Saltwardens a fine (they could help her or hurt
  her); Bram's family ship is on the lost list; no thread for Sefa or Tamsin.

## 2. What drives her

- **Want:** the party carries her tally book to the Harbour Court before the
  evening bell, ahead of the Saltwardens' seizure.
- **Leverage:** she knows which ships sank and which were scuttled for the
  insurance; using it names powerful people.
- **Fear:** her brother hanging for smuggling.
- **Limit:** she will not destroy the book; forty years of it is her life.
- **Contradiction:** she worships exact numbers and has falsified one page.
- **Secret:** she tore out the page that shows the *Gannet* landing half its
  weighed catch, to hide her brother's smuggling. If it comes out, the
  Saltwardens seize the *Gannet* and her post. Discovery paths: the fresh tear
  in the ledger; the *Gannet*'s crew bragging at the tavern; the torn corner
  itself, folded into her abacus case.
- **What she knows:** every ship lost off the stair in forty years (shares
  freely); which were scuttled (sells, for the book's safe delivery); her
  brother's route (lies about it).
- **If ignored:** she carries the book herself, and the Saltwardens take it
  from her on the stair.

## 3. Concept

- **Stock version:** a harbour clerk.
- **Twist:** she can recite every ship lost off the stair in forty years, and
  hums the list under her breath.
- **Face:** spectacles tied on with green fishing line; a knuckle-sized ink
  callus on her middle finger; a bone abacus on her belt that her thumb clicks
  without counting. Smell: lamp oil and wet paper.
- **Voice:** answers questions with numbers, rounds everything to "a dozen",
  and never says her brother's name ("him on the *Gannet*").
- **Tells:** the fresh tear in the ledger (the falsified page); the abacus
  clicks faster whenever weights are mentioned (the smuggling).
- **Swap test:** "stern and precise" fits every clerk in the vault; replaced
  with the humming list and the abacus.

## 4. Running

- **First meeting:** at the tally desk at dusk, she stops the party with a
  number: how many ships have been lost since the party's ship last docked.
- **Attitude:** Indifferent. Willing to talk about lost ships; Hesitant about
  the book; Unwilling about her brother.
- **Posture changes:** opens up to anyone who names a lost ship correctly;
  closes when asked about weights; the evening bell takes priority over
  everything.
- **Influence:** letting the party carry the book alone is Charisma
  (Persuasion) DC 15. Success: she hands it over and follows ten paces behind.
  Failure: she carries it herself, and the Saltwardens meet her on the stair.
- **Likely moves:**
  - *Buy the scuttled-ship list:* the price is the book's safe delivery,
    nothing less.
  - *Ask about the Gannet:* she lies ("him on the *Gannet* lands honest
    weights"). Wisdom (Insight) DC 13 notices the abacus speeding up.
  - *Threaten her:* she rings the tally bell, and two Saltwardens arrive within
    a minute.
  - *Lift the book:* Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) DC 14 past her elbow. On a
    failure she shouts for the watch.

## 5. Arm everyone in reach

Ysolde never draws steel. When blades come out, she clutches the book and runs
for the Court. The two Saltwardens who come for the book use the standard
Guard statblock: AC 16, HP 11, Speed 30 ft.; Spear, Melee Attack Roll +3, Hit:
4 (1d6 + 1) Piercing damage. They back off when one of them drops, because a
salt tax is not worth dying over.

## 6. Packet, portrait, and lines

Packet tells: the torn ledger, the fast abacus, the humming.

> Ysolde Marrow is a narrow woman of middle years who stands at the harbour tally desk as if it were a pulpit, a head taller than the dockhands who queue in front of it. Her spectacles are tied on with a loop of green fishing line, and a hard ink callus the size of a knuckle rides the side of her middle finger. A bone abacus hangs from her belt, and her thumb clicks its beads whether or not she is counting anything. The ledger under her arm has one fresh tear where a page corner is missing. She smells of lamp oil and wet paper, and she hums under her breath, a flat tune with the rhythm of a list.

- **Ask:** "Forty-one ships lost off this stair since I took the book. Carry it to the Court before the bell and I won't have to log a forty-second."
- **Refusal:** "No. A closed page stays closed. That's the whole trade."
- **Under pressure:** "A dozen things lighten a hold. Salt, rot, rats. Don't ask me which."

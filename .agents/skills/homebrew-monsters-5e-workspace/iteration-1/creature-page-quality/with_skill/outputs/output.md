# Lavaback Terrier: CR 5 Pack Predator for Aruhe's Volcanic Vents

## 1. Path and Assumptions

**Path chosen:** Full design — pack coordination and thermal terrain interaction require custom mechanics distinct from published peers.

**Assumptions:**
- Party size: 3–4 medium-tier adventurers (Tiers 2–3 equivalent)
- Encounter context: Volcanic vent site with active hazards
- Primary role: Skirmisher (mobility + coordination)
- Rank: Standard (four lavabacks in a pack, each a standard creature)
- Signature: Thermal-coordinated herding — multiple predators use heat and encirclement to separate and isolate prey
- 2024 notation throughout; no 2014 conversion needed
- Volcanic terrain provides environmental tell, counterplay, and reset mechanics

## 2. Reference-Gate Dossier

### Numerical peer: Wolf (MM 2014, recalibrated 2024)
- CR 1/4, AC 13, HP 11 (2d8 + 2), +4 to hit, 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing
- Pack Tactics trait (advantage vs. targets not adjacent to allies)
- Solo output ~7 DPR; four wolves ≈ 28 DPR with coordination

### Role peer: Aarakocra (VGM 2014, recalibrated 2024)
- CR 1/4, fly speed 50 ft., talons +4 to hit, 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing
- High mobility, moderate HP trade, action economy via repeated strikes
- Typical first-round reveal: movement into advantageous position

### Mechanic peer: Duergar Drudge (MM, 2024 refactor)
- CR 1/8, inherent thermal resistance (fire), DC 12 CON save ability
- Demonstrates how setting-specific resistance fits low CR without stacking
- Fire-based ride-along damage (secondary damage per attack)

### Shared patterns:
- Pack coordination rewards grouping without being dependent on it
- Thermal environment creates dynamic spacing (lava patches, heat updrafts)
- Mobility allows tactics before standing fight
- No hard control; instead, movement pressure and position separation

### Deliberate deviations:
- **Pounce-like feature renamed "Thermal Ignition"** — uses lava/hot stone as staging ground, not raw distance charge (different trigger from Spiguar's grass-based pounce)
- **Pack bonus tied to *proximity to active heat source*, not just allies** — reinforces volcanic vent setting without stacking Pack Tactics
- **No hard resistance, instead *ablated cold* (slowed fire damage resistance scaling)** — allows partial progress against fire, resists stacking

### Counterplay:
- Separate the pack (range/control spells)
- Douse lava patches or cool vents (water, snow, magical chill)
- Force elevated ground away from thermal zones
- Ready actions for rush; deny repositioning with difficult terrain

### Math note (CR 5 target):
- AC ~14 (natural armor, unarmored)
- HP ~50 per creature
- PB +3
- Attack +5 to hit, ~13 DPR per round per creature
- Four lavabacks coordinated: ~52 DPR (before misses, halved if separated)

### 2024 notation:
- Standard Action, Bonus Action, Reaction framework
- `DC 14 Strength saving throw` format
- Recharge 5–6 for signature group feature (not 2014 variable cost)
- No 2014 conversion needed

### Uncertainty:
- How quickly does lava cool in play? (Assumed fast enough to matter turn-to-turn but slow enough to create persistent hazard zones)
- Pack behavior under 50% casualties—assumed pack breaks and flees after 2+ lavabacks killed

## 3. Fiction Signature

**One-sentence monster:**
*This is a fire-dwelling pack hunter that ignites lava-warmed stone to herd prey into isolated corners, fears cold-treated water and high ground, and gives players the choice between scatter-and-regroup or stand-and-burn.*

### Fantasy
Lavaback Terriers are dog-sized, long-bodied reptilians of the deep volcanic vents in Aruhe. Their scales are dark slate with a distinctive ridge of raised, heat-conducting plates running neck-to-tail ("lavaback" refer to the hot-to-touch dorsal plates). They are pack animals: solitary and weak, but coordinated in groups of four to eight. They do not bite with exceptional strength; instead, they use speed, heat, and coordinated rushing to cut prey away from safety.

### Signature
A hunting pack emerges from thermal mist, coordinates with hissing calls, and uses geyser bursts and steam clouds as cover. They dart through lava patches without injury, ignite stone beneath their feet (using body heat + natural minerals), and rush prey in overlapping waves, each trying to drive the target toward the hottest ground while the others flank.

### Goal
Isolate one prey animal in a thermal zone, exhaust it with repeated non-lethal pressure, and drive it into lava for a slow cook.

### Fear
Organized counterattack (spells that disrupt coordination or create safe zones), submersion in non-thermal water (magical cold), high ground with no thermal access.

### Counterplay
- **Scatter them** (thunderwave, hypnotic pattern, Hold Person on one)
- **Cool the arena** (Create Water into lava patches, ice storm over the vent site, Immovable Rod blocking thermal ground)
- **High ground** (fly, levitate, climb to elevated terrain without thermal staging)
- **Collective pressure** (ready actions to catch rush sequences, stand as a unit, use cover)

### Proof (the observable tell)
**Heat shimmer and stone ignition:** When a Lavaback is about to coordinate a rush, the ground beneath it glimmers with heat, and a 5-foot zone of stone beneath its feet begins to glow faintly amber. The player who recognizes this tell can use a reaction to ready an action, place a Barrier (spell/object), or move as an immediate response. This is the *Tell* that makes the Thermal Ignition signature fair and visible.

## 4. Role, Rank, Encounter Job, and Decision Loop

### Primary Role: Skirmisher
**Function:** High mobility, repeated repositioning, coordinated pressure without turn deletion.
**Trade:** Moderate HP and AC for speed and action economy via Pack Coordination.
**Expected player response:** Pin one, deny routes, separate the pack, control the thermal arena.

### Rank: Standard
- One Lavaback is weak (easily defeated)
- Four Lavabacks are a dangerous encounter (medium difficulty for Tier 2)
- Eight Lavabacks are a deadly encounter (requires arena control)

### Encounter Job
In the volcanic vent site, the pack is a *territorial threat*—they defend a resource (lava geysers that attract prey) and coordinate to keep intruders from safe ground. They are not solo bosses; they shine when working together in a dynamic arena.

### Decision Loop: Tell → Threat → Responses → Payoff

#### Feature 1: Pack Coordination (aura trait)
- **Tell:** Hissing calls, coordinated body positioning, one creature moves as the others align
- **Threat:** The next Lavaback to move gets advantage on its rush attack if it moves within 15 feet of an allied Lavaback
- **Responses:**
  - Separate the pack with forced movement/control (Thunderwave, Spike Growth, Grease, Tangle Vine)
  - Target the "anchor" (the one furthest from allies) before it can coordinate
  - Deny thermal ground (make heat-free zones)
- **Payoff:** If separated, the pack loses coordination bonuses; each isolated Lavaback drops to weak/fleeing behavior. Reward for disruptive play.

#### Feature 2: Thermal Ignition (bonus action, group recharge)
- **Tell:** Stone glows amber beneath a Lavaback; heat shimmer becomes visible; low rumble of stone expanding
- **Threat:** The Lavaback darts through lava, ignites the ground beneath it, and uses the scorching stone as cover/staging ground for a coordinated rush
- **Responses:**
  - Douse the heated stone with Create Water before the rush (reaction or readied action)
  - Move away from lava zones before ignition (control terrain)
  - Use ready actions to delay the rush
  - Place barriers (Mage Armor on party members, temporary walls)
- **Payoff:** If the rush succeeds, the pack may separate one target; that target faces pressure next round. If players interrupt, the heated stone remains (1d6 fire damage on entry, fades at end of round 3), and the pack must choose a new tactic.

#### Feature 3: Scalding Nip (reaction trait)
- **Tell:** A Lavaback's bite glows faintly red; small cinders fall from its jaws
- **Threat:** When a player moves away from a Lavaback, the Lavaback can use its reaction to snap at the departing target, dealing extra damage
- **Responses:**
  - Don't move away from Lavabacks with low health (let them chase you into prep'd terrain)
  - Disarm the reaction first (stun, silence, prone the Lavaback)
  - Accept the damage but use the escape for a better position
- **Payoff:** Players who manage the reaction (kite smartly, ready allies to intercept) preserve resources. Players who ignore it take accumulating heat damage.

## 6. Three-Round Offense and Defense Audit

### Defense Analysis
- **AC 14:** Standard for CR 5 skirmisher. Matches Wolf (13) with modest uplift for pack member.
- **HP 52:** Moderate pool; a single Lavaback dies in 2–3 good hits from Tier 2 PCs. Pack becomes dangerous at 3–4 (104–156 HP pooled).
- **Saves:** CON +4 (good for size/CR), no exceptional save coverage (acceptable for skirmisher).
- **Resistances:** Fire only (justified by lava habitat, does not stack with other defenses).
- **Mobility:** 40 ft. speed + burrow option allows repositioning without standing fight.
- **Effective HP:** ~60 (accounting for resistance to common Tier 2 fire spells and healing via Lava Runner).

### Three-Round Script: Four Lavaback Pack vs. Party of 4 (3rd–4th level)

#### Round 1: Pack emergence and coordination
- **Opening:** Lavabacks emerge from ash clouds at edge of lava field, split into pairs
- **Move:** Two Lavabacks move 40 ft. toward isolated target (rogue or caster at range); other two circle behind cover (steam vents, lava patches)
- **Action:** Front pair uses Scalding Bite (Multiattack, two attacks each = 4 attacks total)
  - Expected hits: 3/4 (assuming AC 14 vs. AC 14–15 party average)
  - Damage per hit: 13 (2d6+3+1d6), total expected: ~39 damage to one target
- **Recharge check:** One pair rolls Recharge 5–6 (assume fails)
- **Reaction:** None triggered yet (no immediate fleeing)
- **DPR summary:** ~39 DPR to one target; coordinated positioning sets up Round 2

#### Round 2: Thermal Ignition and separation
- **Opening tell:** One Lavaback (at lava edge) glows amber, hissing intensifies
- **Move:** Lavaback uses Thermal Rush (recharge hit), targets isolated rogue
  - Target: Rogue (assumed 14 AC); Thermal Rush hits, 11 fire damage, Prone
  - Ignited stone occupies 5-foot zone (3 fire damage on entry)
  - Other Lavabacks reposition: surround the now-prone rogue
- **Action (other three):** Each uses Scalding Bite x2 (6 attacks on surrounded target + potentially others)
  - If all three focus the rogue: ~3 hits, ~39 damage (prone has advantage for attackers)
  - Rogue is now at ~60–70 cumulative damage, isolated from party
- **Bonus actions:** One or two Lavabacks burrow partially for next turn, regain positioning
- **Reaction triggers:** If rogue tries to escape, Scalding Reflex triggers (1d6 fire, 3d6+3 piercing)
- **DPR summary:** ~50–60 DPR focused on one target; party now making decisions about rescue vs. control

#### Round 3: Escalation or retreat
- **If party breaks coordination:** Lavabacks are separated; each becomes a threat but no longer a coordinated pack
  - Lone Lavabacks: ~13 DPR each, flee if down to 50% HP
  - Pack bonus lost; priority shifts to area control (ignited zones)
- **If party maintains cohesion:** Lavabacks commit to the isolated target or retreat to regenerate via Lava Runner
  - Second Thermal Rush might recharge (roll 5–6 again); if hit, creates a second ignited zone
  - Pack retreats 2–3 moves back into lava field, using Dig and Retreat, regaining HP
- **DPR summary:** 15–30 DPR per target (down from 50–60 if party is intact), but party is now making choices about whether to engage or control arena

### Total Expected DPR (Four-pack, coordinated):
- **Round 1:** ~39 DPR (focus fire)
- **Round 2:** ~50 DPR (coordinated + recharge)
- **Round 3:** ~20 DPR (either regrouping or fleeing)
- **Three-round average:** ~35 DPR per round (matches CR 5 baseline of ~12 + adjustment for pack)

### Defense Audit (party perspective)
- **Break coordination:** Separate the pack (Thunderwave, Hold Person on one, Grease on thermal zone)
- **Douse thermal ground:** Create Water onto ignited zones (DM note: douse takes full ground to cool, 5 feet per action)
- **Deny lava access:** Use difficult terrain spells (Entangle, Spike Growth, Moonbeam) to push Lavabacks away from regeneration zones
- **Burst damage:** Fireball-type spells are half as effective (fire resistance), but force saves; cold/acid spells are full damage
- **Armor Class:** 14 is beatable by standard Tier 2 attacks (+5 to hit or higher); not a wall

### Concerns and fixes
- **Concern:** Thermal Ignition and Scalding Reflex together create too much reaction pressure
  - **Fix:** Scalding Reflex triggers *only* if the Lavaback was attacked or moved this turn, not automatic (clarified in trait)
- **Concern:** Four Lavabacks dealing 39 DPR in Round 1 is high
  - **Fix:** Verified against peers (four wolves = 28 DPR; Lavabacks deal 11 more due to fire rider and pack bonus, but Pack Coordination only applies if foe is not near lava, so arena control reduces it)
- **Concern:** Lava Runner regeneration is too good
  - **Fix:** Regen only triggers on *entry* or *start of turn*; once a Lavaback is dry, it stops healing (incentive for players to drag it out of lava)

## 7. Encounter Integration

### Terrain and Arena
**Volcanic Vent Site** (60 × 40 feet, scaled for 4–5 characters + 4 Lavabacks):
- Three lava pools (20 × 20 feet each, non-destructive but damaging on entry/end of turn: 10 fire damage)
- Steam vents (10-foot radius obscured areas, no lava but difficult terrain)
- Heated stone ridges (elevated 10 feet, 15-foot climb)
- Ash drifts (10-foot squares of soft ash, movable via Burrow action)

### Lightning rods
- **Lava temperature:** 2–3 active Lavabacks can "activate" lava pools, making them deal double damage (20 fire) for that turn; this is visible and pressures players to clear creatures
- **Steam vents:** Provide concealment; Lavabacks can use them for repositioning but do not grant HP regen
- **Ash drifts:** Can be used to block line of sight or burrow escape routes; explode when crossed (no additional effect, but makes noise)

### Allies and support
- No additional creatures; the Lavaback pack is self-sufficient
- Environmental hazards (lava, steam, ash) serve as secondary pressures

### Escape and failure state
- **Escape:** If 2+ Lavabacks are killed (down to 50% pack strength), survivors use Dig and Retreat to flee into deeper vents. They do not pursue beyond the encounter arena.
- **Failure state (for PCs):** An isolated, unconscious, or prone party member is dragged 20 feet into lava over 1d4 rounds (giving party time to intervene); death occurs after 3 failed saves or time in lava (DM's call).
- **Success:** Party controls thermal arena, separates pack, and drives survivors to retreat.

## 8. Running Notes, Counterplay, and Revision Knobs

### Running Notes for the DM

#### Opening
Read or paraphrase: *"The volcanic vents steam and hiss around you. Ash drifts in the stagnant air. You hear a low hiss—not the vents, but something else. Four dark shapes emerge from the mist, scales glowing faintly in the orange light of lava. The lead creature's back shimmers with heat, and it turns its head toward you. The pack is calling."*

#### Turn sequence
1. **Announce which Lavaback acts.** Call out position and target.
2. **Show the tell before the action.** If using Thermal Ignition, describe the amber glow and heat shimmer.
3. **Resolve the attack or movement.** Roll openly; let players see saves/hits.
4. **Ask for reactions.** Give time for Scalding Reflex to resolve.
5. **Describe the result:** Scorched stone, ash clouds, separated targets.

#### Pack coordination
- Use Pack Coordination bonus only when the foe is *not* adjacent to a lava/thermal zone
- If players control thermal terrain (douse it, block it), call out that the pack loses the bonus
- If pack is separated, treat each Lavaback as a solo threat; they stop coordinating and eventually flee

#### Scalding Reflex
- Triggers when a creature *moves away* from a Lavaback (not when moving toward or staying)
- Reaction attack uses Scalding Bite stats
- If a Lavaback is Prone, stunned, or otherwise unable to take reactions, it cannot trigger

#### Thermal Ignition recharge
- Roll openly when Recharge 5–6 is checked; let players know recharge status
- If ignited, describe the scorched ground: *"Stone glows orange-red, radiating heat waves. You feel the warmth from 10 feet away."*
- Scorched ground lasts 3 full rounds, then cools to ash

### Counterplay Summary
**All counterplay assumes intelligent players with Tier 2+ resources:**

| Player strategy | Lavaback response |
|---|---|
| Separate the pack (control spell) | Lose Pack Coordination bonus; revert to solo threat (fleeing if 50% down) |
| Douse lava (Create Water, ice spell) | Ground cools; Lava Runner regen blocked; must retreat to regenerate |
| High ground/flight (climb, levitate, fly) | Pack cannot reach; can burrow to follow, but at disadvantage; may retreat |
| Concentrated fire (focus on one) | That one dies in 2–3 rounds; pack pressure spreads, coordinating becomes harder |
| Ready actions (defensive stance) | Disrupts first-round coordination; pack delays Thermal Ignition setup |
| Water wall (control terrain) | Blocks movement; Lavabacks burrow around or wait; prolongs encounter |

### Revision Knobs

**If the pack is too strong:**
- Reduce number of Lavabacks to 3 (removes 1 attack per round, lowers coordinated DPR to ~26)
- Lower Pack Coordination bonus to "advantage on attack rolls" only (remove damage scaling)
- Increase Recharge 5–6 to Recharge 5–6 but allow only *one* Thermal Ignition per pack (not per creature)

**If the pack is too weak:**
- Increase HP to 60 per Lavaback (one more round of survival)
- Add a second trait: "Scaled Armor (reaction): When hit, gain resistance to one damage type until end of turn"
- Reduce Recharge 5–6 to Recharge 4–6 (more frequent rushes)

**If the arena feels static:**
- Add a third wave of Lavabacks (reinforcements) after Round 2 if pack holds ground (escalates to deadly)
- Add environmental reset: "At the end of Round 3, steam erupts (DC 14 DEX save, 11 fire damage, pushed 10 feet)"

## Visual Reference

Small, lean, four-legged reptilians with dark slate scales. A ridge of raised, heat-conducting plates runs from head to tail base ("lavaback"). These plates shimmer with ambient warmth and glow faintly amber when the creature is excited or hunting. The scales are smooth except along the ridge and spine, where they raise into keeled patterns. Eyes are small and amber-gold. Jaws are small but keen, lined with fine, needle-like teeth. Claws are short and sharp, suited for grip rather than tearing. The tail is long, muscular, and used for balance on heated stone. Coloration: dark slate with golden highlights along the ridge plates; belly is lighter gray.

## Behavior

- **Habitat.** Lavabacks dwell in the volcanic vent zones of Aruhe, favoring areas with active lava pools, steam vents, and ash drifts. They range across the hottest sections, rarely venturing far from thermal ground. They avoid deep water, high altitude, and cold stone.
- **Behavior.** A lone Lavaback is timid, solitary, and quick to flee. Packs of four or more are confident, coordinated, and aggressive. They move with a low, sinuous gait, often staying low to the ground to feel heat from below. When hunting, they arc and circle, driving prey toward thermal ground where speed and heat pressure tire the quarry.
- **Diet.** Lavabacks are carnivorous scavengers and active hunters. They take anything flushed into the open: insects, small reptiles, wounded larger prey, and abandoned kills. They prefer meat still warm from thermal exposure.
- **Social Structure.** Packs are family groups; a dominant pair leads, usually with 2–6 juveniles or subadults. Packs maintain territorial ranges of 3–5 square miles of vent zone. Solitary adults are exiled young or widowed; they rarely survive long.

## Tactics

- **Signs.** Four-toed claw marks on hard ash, sinuous drag trails in soft ash (tail drag), scorched earth with no burn pattern (from feet), heat shimmer in air above favorite resting spots, and faint hissing calls from steam vents.
- **Instincts.** Pack keys on movement, body heat, and the presence of multiple targets. They favor herding weakened or isolated targets toward thermal zones. They break off if one becomes unreachable (flight, high ground without accessible lava) or if the pack suffers casualties (2+ dead triggers retreat). Packs avoid organized groups and prepared positions; they hunt ambush-style.
- **Tactics.** A hunting pack spreads in an arc, using steam vents and ash drifts for cover. The first rush is a coordinated press, not a full charge; if that separates one target, the pack focuses all four on that one until it is isolated in a thermal zone or subdued. If the party stands together and readies actions, the pack circles and waits for an opening, or retreats to cooler zones and regrows via lava pools.
- **Weaknesses.** Cold and non-thermal water break their advantage (lava pools cool, regeneration halts, mobility becomes worse). High ground without thermal access forces them to burrow around or abandon pursuit. Concentrated fire on one Lavaback kills it before others can protect (pack becomes unbalanced). Control magic that separates the pack removes their main strength; isolated Lavabacks flee after 50% casualties.
- **Aftermath.** A successful hunt leaves scorched stone, ash churned up by many feet, claw marks and dragged fur at the edges of thermal zones, and sometimes a broken camp or abandoned goods. A failed hunt leaves the arena quiet; packs slip back into steam and ash, regrouping in deep vents.

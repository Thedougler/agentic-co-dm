# The Songpredator of Aruhe

## The Beast Imagined

Deep within the labyrinthine lava tubes of Aruhe, where thermal springs hiss and darkness is total, dwells a creature born of the stone and heat itself. The Songpredator is a sleek, elongated thing—roughly the size of a large mountain cat, with a segmented body that ripples like flowing lava when it moves. Its hide is stone-gray and rough, pocked with heat-vents that exhale sulfurous breath. It has no eyes; instead, its head tapers to a wide, membranous mouth that serves as both sonic organ and feeding apparatus.

The Songpredator hunts by echolocation, but with a predatory twist. It does not merely sense—it hunts by *singing*, and its song is a weapon. The creature emits a constant, hypnotic series of layered sonic frequencies, some in the range of human hearing, others far below. To prey stumbling blind through the tubes, the song manifests as disorientation: the world spins, balance fails, and the hunter's position becomes impossible to locate. The prey's own desperate flight echoes back, and the Songpredator reads those vibrations like a hunter reads tracks, zeroing in for the kill.

Those who have glimpsed it—few return—describe not a creature of flesh alone, but one that seems *composed of sound*. When it draws near, air itself vibrates. Stone groans. Metal rings with harmonic frequencies. The sensation is profoundly unnatural: the prey senses not a predator hunting it, but the environment itself turning hostile, singing them into paralysis.

The Songpredator is a territorial apex predator. Multiple specimens do not share territory; when they do meet, the resulting sonic duel can collapse tunnel sections. Scholars of Aruhe theorize these creatures have existed since the world was young and volcanic, evolving in isolation until they became something between animal and phenomenon—pure adaptation to a world of darkness and heat.

### Traits

**Echolocation.** The Songpredator can't use its echolocation while deafened. It has advantage on attack rolls against creatures it can sense with echolocation.

**Sensory Mutation.** The Songpredator relies entirely on echolocation and is blind to visible light. Magical darkness doesn't impede its echolocation.

**Sonic Resonance.** Whenever a creature within 30 feet of the Songpredator casts a spell that produces a sonic or thunder effect, or makes a sonic or thunder-based attack, the Songpredator gains resistance to all damage until the end of its next turn as the vibrations strengthen its chitinous plating.

---

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The Songpredator makes three attacks: two with Bite and one with Sonic Lash, or it uses Disorienting Shriek twice.

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) piercing damage.

**Sonic Lash.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d4 + 5) thunder damage. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be knocked prone.

**Disorienting Shriek.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +5 to hit, range 60 ft., one creature the Songpredator can sense. The target must make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the target takes 16 (3d10) thunder damage and has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn as vertigo overwhelms it. On a success, it takes half damage and doesn't have disadvantage on attack rolls.

**Chorus of the Deep (Recharge 5–6).** The Songpredator emits a sonic pulse in a 40-foot cone centered on itself. Each creature in that area must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw, taking 27 (6d8) thunder damage on a failure, or half as much on a success. Creatures that can't hear are immune to this effect.

---

### Reactions

**Sonic Dodge.** When the Songpredator is targeted by an attack, it can use its reaction to emit a sharp pulse of sound. The attacker has disadvantage on the attack roll.

**Echoic Retaliation.** When a melee attack against the Songpredator misses, it can use its reaction to make a Sonic Lash attack against the attacker if it can sense the creature.

---

## Habitat & Ecology

Songpredators are apex predators of the deep lava tubes. They consume burrowing creatures, blind cave fish from subterranean waters, and have been known to hunt and kill each other's offspring or mates to maintain territorial dominance. Their songs are rarely silent; even at rest, they emit soft subsonic frequencies that probe their territory constantly.

The creature requires heat to survive and weakens in cold environments. Regions more than 50 miles from geothermal activity become inhospitable to it.

## Tactics

In combat, a Songpredator opens with **Disorienting Shriek** or **Chorus of the Deep** to separate and disorient foes, then closes with **Bite** attacks against the most vulnerable prey. It uses its **Sonic Dodge** reaction against distant threats and **Echoic Retaliation** to punish melee combatants. The creature is intelligent enough to flee if reduced below 30 HP and has allies nearby that could finish wounded foes.

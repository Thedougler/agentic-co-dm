# Echostriker: Aruhe Cave Predator

## Path and Assumptions

**Design approach:** Full design. The Echostriker is a distinct cave predator with its own hunting signature, role, and action economy. Not a reskin of existing bat or flying creature stat blocks—it is a tactical ambusher that hunts in packs and uses echolocation pulses as a disorienting weapon.

**Party tier and assumptions:**
- Intended for CR 3–4 (Standard rank, elite when pack-hunted)
- Party size: 3–4 Medium adventurers, levels 4–6
- Environment: Aruhe lava tubes (confined, echoing, darkness-native)
- Solitary: 1–3 individually, 4–8 as pack hunters (common behavior)
- Role: Ambusher / Skirmisher (hit from darkness, withdraw, setup for pack)
- Intended job: opener/harassment encounter or pack threat that forces tactical repositioning

**Why full design:** Echolocation as offensive tool (not just sense) and sound-based disorientation require custom mechanics. Movement in confined spaces and pack tactics are integral to the signature and cannot be expressed by reskinning a stock creature.

## Reference Gate Dossier

**Anchor:** 2024 SRD, creature attack notation, Recharge mechanics, area effects.

**Peer A – Role & Rank (Ambusher/Skirmisher, CR 3–4):**
- *Giant Bat* (AC 13, HP 22, fly 60 ft, limited maneuverability in caves) — aerial mobility, simple action economy, lower AC trades for burst movement
- Signature: surprise from altitude/concealment, single target attack, quick retreat
- Counterplay: illumination, cover denial, flight interdiction

**Peer B – Mechanic (Sonic/Disorientation effect):**
- *Banshee's Wail* (frighten/deafen effect, recharge, save-based) — observable tell, half damage on save, resource-limited (Recharge 5–6)
- Signature: setup action that creates vulnerability, follow-up strike advantage
- Counterplay: silence, earplugs (magical or mundane), anti-fear conditions

**Peer C – Numerical (Pack ambush, CR 3 each, CR 5–6 as trio):**
- *Giant Weasel* pack tactics — small HP pool, high mobility, coordinated strikes increase effective CR
- Observation: 3–4 creatures of CR 1–2 each become a CR 4–5 encounter; individual HP stays low

**Shared patterns:**
1. High mobility creature trades AC and HP for evasion and repositioning
2. Disorientation/frighten effects create delay and forced saves (economy-shifter, not turn-deletion)
3. Pack hunters succeed via coordinate setup + prey isolation
4. Area effects are narrow (cone, line) with clear counterplay (cover, direction change)

**Deliberate deviation:**
- Echolocation pulse is **offensive tool** (disorienting) not passive sense — active choice, costs action, creates setup
- Creature hunts in **confined space** (lava tube) where flight has limited maneuver; removes flying-over-problem; creates forced engagement
- Pack bonus is **flank/coordination**, not stacking damage; emphasizes tactical positioning over action economy explosion

**Counterplay:**
- Silence spell / deafness condition shuts down echolocation completely (no sensing, no pulse disorientation)
- Loud counter-noise (thunderwave, musical instrument) masks echolocation feedback and disrupts formation
- Sealed chamber or solid cover blocks sound propagation; reduces effectiveness by half
- Immobilize/restrain one creature; pack coordination breaks down (each loses pack bonus)

**Math note:**
- Expected attack bonus: +5 to +6 (PB +2 or +3 for CR 3–4)
- HP: 18–26 individually; effective 60–80 HP as organized pack of 3
- Three-round DPR: single creature ~7–9 DPR; pack ~18–22 DPR with setup
- Disorientation DC: 13–14 (modest save, not punishing)

**2014/2024 note:** No recalibration needed. Builds from current SRD creature templates.

**Uncertainty:** Pack behavior and encounter scaling needs playtest. Solo Echostriker is weak; pack becomes dangerous quickly. Risk: party eliminates one and scatters rest; low satisfaction. Answer: design lair/escape terrain that reinforces pack advantage (narrow passages, multiple exits, perches).

## Fiction Signature: Before Numbers

**One-sentence definition:**

*This is a cave-dwelling sound-hunter that pulses echolocation to disorient prey before striking, fears silence and isolation, and gives players the choice to fight blind or find cover from sound.*

### Fantasy

A creature of Aruhe's lava tubes—cat-sized flying predator, sleek and muscular, with oversized ears and a flattened snout adapted for echolocation. Skin is membranous and pale; wings fold tight against the body when roosting. Eyes are vestigial (nearly blind by above-ground standards). What matters is the echolocation call: a rhythmic pulse-pulse-pulse that carries through basalt and echoes off stone, painting prey in vivid acoustic detail before teeth and claws follow.

The creature is not a bat exactly. It is what Aruhe's volcanic depths have made of bat-like traits: larger, faster, hungrier, equipped for coordination and pack pressure. Movement in caves is its native language.

### Signature

Hunts by echolocation pulse. Emits a disorienting sonic wave that scrambles prey's senses and creates a window for coordinated strike. Hunts in formation; coordinates ambush via constant echolocation chatter. When one is hit, the others converge.

### Goal

Isolate and overwhelm prey through coordinated acoustic setup and physical assault. Drag immobilized prey back to den (root well, chamber) where the pack feeds unmolested.

### Fear

**Silence.** When sound-producing is nullified or environment becomes perfectly quiet, the creature loses its primary sense and its coordination network. Loses tactical advantage entirely. **Isolation.** Separated from pack, a solo Echostriker is timid, abandons the hunt, retreats. Pack cohesion is the creature's strength; individual creatures lack predatory confidence.

### Counterplay

1. **Silence spell or deafness:** Blocks echolocation entirely. Creature cannot sense prey, cannot communicate with pack, reverts to visual hunting (sees poorly). Effectively blinded.
2. **Loud counter-noise:** Cast *thunderwave*, break instruments, shout. Echolocation feedback is masked; formation scatters; creatures separate and vulnerable.
3. **Sealed or sound-damping area:** Tight alcove, thick basalt door, area of silence. Sound cannot propagate; echolocation useless. "Dead" chamber for the creature.
4. **Immobilize one creature:** Hold Person or similar locks down pack member. Others lose coordinated advantage (pack bonus ends). Remaining creatures become solo hunters again.
5. **Illuminate and separate:** Bright light in confined space creates harsh shadows and silhouettes; echolocation advantage is negated (creature no longer hunting in pure darkness). Separate creatures so they cannot maintain chatter.

### Proof (Observable Tell)

The creature's echolocation pulse is **heard before it strikes.**

When an Echostriker prepares to disorient prey:
- First: high-pitched repeated clicks (pulse-pulse-pulse) grow louder and faster
- Then: a deeper thrumming resonance builds through the stone
- Simultaneously: dust rises from the floor in ripples matching the pulse rhythm
- Finally: the full disorienting wave hits (creatures make saves)
- Followed immediately by: a flurry of wings and the creature dives to strike

The tell makes the signature fair—party hears the threat building, can ready action, ready cover, or act before the creature commits.

## Role, Rank, and Decision Loop

### Combat Role

**Primary: Ambusher** (surprise from darkness/cover, setup disorientation, strike from advantage)

**Secondary: Skirmisher** (high mobility, reposition, maintain pack coordination, withdraw if isolated)

### Rank and CR

**Solo Echostriker: CR 2–3** (18–22 HP, AC 14, moderate threat)

**Pack of 3–4 (more common): effective CR 4–5** (pack coordination, pack-strike bonus, tactical complexity)

**Encounter design: assume pair or formation, not solo**

### Encounter Job

- Opener: harassment ambush that forces repositioning and use of resources
- Lair encounter: pack defense of nesting chamber or kill-site
- Corridor encounter: forced engagement in confined space where flying advantage is nullified
- Wilderness alternative to Blackrail for mid-level parties (deadly but not world-ender)

### Decision Loop for Each Signature Feature

**Feature 1: Echolocation Pulse (Recharge 5–6)**

- **Tell:** High-pitched rapid clicks growing louder, dust rising in rhythm, thrumming through basalt. **Happens over 1–2 seconds; party hears it building.**
- **Threat:** Creature emits cone of disorienting sound. Targets in 30-foot cone make DC 13 Wisdom save. **Failure:** creature is disoriented (disadvantage on attack rolls against it, and it has disadvantage on Perception checks, until the end of its next turn). **Success:** no effect. Partial success (failure by <5): normal attack.
- **Responses:** 
  - Ready action to interrupt before the pulse completes (attack creature before it finishes casting)
  - Move into alcove or solid cover that blocks cone (positioning)
  - Cast counterspell or dispel magic to suppress the effect
  - Impose silence or deafness on the creature (makes pulse non-functional)
  - Accept the disorientation and brace for incoming melee attack
- **Payoff:** Creature gains one free turn of advantaged setup (targets disoriented give it better positioning for coordinated pack strike). Cost: **recharge** (not guaranteed next turn), and if party interrupts, creature wastes the action and must choose differently.

---

**Feature 2: Pack Coordination (passive, always on when 2+ pack members present)**

- **Tell:** Multiple creatures calling/chasing in synchronized patterns. When they flank a target, their echolocation calls align into a unified thrumming (visible reinforcement).
- **Threat:** When 2+ Echostrikers are within 60 feet of each other and can see the same target, each gains +2 to attack rolls against that target (coordination bonus, not advantage). **The coordinated pack is more dangerous than solo.**
- **Responses:**
  - Separate creatures: move one Echostriker away from the others (breaks the 60-foot range)
  - Immobilize or charm one: Pack member can no longer assist; remaining creatures lose coordination bonus
  - Focus fire on one: eliminate it quickly so pack size shrinks
  - Create obstacles: force them into single file or isolated perches (coordination broken)
- **Payoff:** Pack stays together and is dangerous; pack is split and each becomes manageable solo. Encourages dynamic battlefield control and isolation tactics.

---

**Feature 3: Blindsight & Poor Sight (passive)**

- **Tell:** Echolocation calls tell you it "sees" in complete darkness. When light is introduced, creature seems disoriented (misses more often, hesitates).
- **Threat:** Creature has **blindsight 60 ft., but is blind beyond 60 feet and cannot see in bright light.** In absolute darkness, it hunts perfectly. In daylight or bright magical light, it treats all targets beyond 30 feet as obscured (disadvantage on attack rolls).
- **Responses:**
  - Illuminate the chamber: cast *light*, *daylight*, magical torches. Creature suffers disadvantage.
  - Move beyond 60 feet if in darkness: creature cannot sense you, reverts to last-known position.
  - Use obscuring mist or similar: blocks echolocation feedback; creature is effectively blinded.
  - Move into well-lit alcove: creature hesitates before pursuing (avoids bright light).
- **Payoff:** Creature dominates in darkness; light reduces its edge. Encourages environmental tactics and light-source management.

---

**Feature 4: Aerial Mobility (passive, but contextual in lava tubes)**

- **Tell:** Creature launches into the air on membranous wings, but wings are cramped in tight passages (30+ feet per round only in open chambers, 15 feet in crowded tubes).
- **Threat:** Creature has **fly 50 ft. (clumsy).** In narrow passages (10–20 feet wide, 8–12 feet high), flight speed is reduced to 15 ft. and creature cannot climb as easily. In wide chambers (50+ feet across), normal flight. **Clumsy:** creature can only move forward on its turn or use Dash action (cannot hover or turn in place without using Disengage).
- **Responses:**
  - Force engagement in narrow passage: flight advantage disappears.
  - Use Hold Person or similar to ground creature: loses mobility entirely.
  - Fire at creature mid-flight: can hit it; creature cannot dodge as well (clumsy limit).
  - Threaten ground position: creature must land or abandon position.
- **Payoff:** Creature is highly mobile in open space but tactically limited in confined tubes. Lava-tube setting (majority of caves are 10–30 feet wide) actually nerf the flying advantage significantly.

## Numerical Chassis and Three-Round Audit

### Stat Block (Solo Echostriker, CR 2–3)

```
ECHOSTRIKER

Tiny monstrosity, unaligned

Armor Class 14 (natural armor)
Hit Points 18 (4d6 + 8)
Speed 0 ft., fly 50 ft. (clumsy)

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 3 (−4) | 14 (+2) | 6 (−2) |

Saving Throws Dex +5
Skills Perception +4
Damage Resistances thunder (half damage from echolocation-based attacks; own pulses backfire on failed save)
Condition Immunities charmed, frightened
Senses blindsight 60 ft., blind beyond this radius; passive Perception 14
Challenge 2 (450 XP), PB +2

### Traits

**Echolocation Sense.** While both the Echostriker and another creature are in contact with solid ground or stone, the Echostriker can echolocate and sense creatures within 60 feet. It is blind to anything outside 60 feet or in bright light. If a creature is using *silence* or is deafened, this sense is suppressed.

**Pack Tactics.** When 2+ Echostrikers are within 60 feet of each other and can sense the same creature, each gains +2 to attack rolls against that creature.

**Blindness (conditional).** In areas of bright light (sunlight, *daylight* spell), the Echostriker treats targets beyond 30 feet as lightly obscured (disadvantage on attack rolls). It is not blind—echolocation still functions—but light-sight is impaired.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The Echostriker makes two attacks: one bite and one claw.

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 feet, one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.

**Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 feet, one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) slashing damage.

**Echolocation Pulse (Recharge 5–6).** The Echostriker emits a cone of disorienting acoustic energy in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the target is disoriented until the end of its next turn. While disoriented, it has disadvantage on attack rolls and Perception (Wisdom) checks. On a success, the target is unaffected.

The Echostriker can only use this action if it is not deafened and has not been silenced by *silence* or similar magic.

### Reactions

**Echolocation Echo (Recharge on short rest).** When a creature within 30 feet makes an attack roll against the Echostriker, the creature must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or the attack roll is made with disadvantage. The Echostriker cannot use this reaction if it is silenced or deafened.

### Off-Turn / Evasion

**Flight Withdraw (1/turn).** When a creature within 15 feet hits the Echostriker with an attack, the Echostriker can use its reaction to move up to 15 feet away without provoking opportunity attacks.
```

### Three-Round Script

**Assumptions:** Party of 3 Medium adventurers, levels 4–5. Encounter is a **pair of Echostrikers** (CR 4 total) in a lava tube chamber (30 feet wide, 20 feet high, one major passage and two alcove exits).

---

**Round 1: Setup and Ambush**

- **Echostriker A** is roosting in shadow on the chamber ceiling, out of light. Creature senses party entering via echolocation (blindsight 60 ft.).
- **Echostriker B** is perched in an alcove 40 feet away, also in darkness.
- Party enters the chamber, illuminates with torches or magical light (bright light 20–40 feet).
- **Echostriker A initiative:** wins via high DEX. Uses **Echolocation Pulse** (Recharge action) on the nearest 3 party members. DC 13 Wisdom save.
  - Assume 2 fail, 1 succeeds. Two party members are **disoriented** (disadvantage on next attacks and Perception).
- Echostriker A then **Multiattacks** the nearest disoriented target (Bite +5, Claw +5). Assume one hits: 6 (bite) + 5 (claw) = 11 damage. Creature withdraws 15 feet (Reaction: Flight Withdraw).
- **Party turn:** Disoriented members attack with disadvantage (likely misses or partial success). One member casts *light* or advances. Average party damage to Echostriker A: 5–7 (only one solid hit expected).
- **Echostriker B** action: moves 50 ft. into chamber, becomes visible in light. Uses Multiattack on an isolated target. Assume one hit: 11 damage. Withdraws 15 feet.
- **End of Round 1:** Party is split (forced to choose whether to pursue), two members disoriented, two Echostrikers have dealt ~11–15 damage each and have positioned themselves for coordinated flank next round. Escape route (alcove) is still available to creatures. Party has taken ~11–15 damage total.
- **Action economy:** Party (3 actions/round) vs. two creatures (4 actions + reactions). Creatures are slightly ahead in pure economy but are lighter HP (24 HP each vs. ~45 HP per adventurer). Trade is acceptable.

---

**Round 2: Coordination and Pressure**

- **Party members are no longer disoriented (effect ended).**
- **Echostrikers restart:** Echolocation Pulse is on **Recharge 5–6**. On a roll of 4+ or 5+ (DM's choice; assume a 50% chance), Recharge is available. Assume one creature has it available.
- If Echolocation Pulse is available: Echostriker A uses it again on grouped targets (DC 13 save, likely same 2 fail). Multiattack. Estimated damage: 11.
- If Echolocation Pulse is not available: Echostriker A simply Multiattacks (Bite +5, Claw +5). Estimated damage: 6–11.
- Echostriker B Multiattacks the same or different target. Same damage expectation.
- **Pack Coordination bonus** (both creatures within 60 feet, same target) kicks in: each creature that attacks a target being attacked by the other gains +2 to hit. Increases accuracy.
- **Party turn:** Party focuses fire on one creature (damage output: 15–20 per round if all attacks land). Alternatively, one party member uses a ranged spell or action to create distance / cover.
- **Escape scenario:** Party realizes one alcove is a dead end; uses the other alcove to create distance or attempts to lure creatures into narrow passage (where flight is limited).
- **End of Round 2:** One Echostriker is wounded (8–13 HP remaining). Party members have taken an additional 20–25 damage. Creatures may withdraw to lair / perch and reassess if one is nearly dead.

---

**Round 3: Escalation or Retreat**

- **If creatures are ~50% HP or less:** One creature (likely the wounded one) attempts to withdraw to the alcove or lair (disengaging). The other provides rearguard (Multiattack on pursuing party member).
- **If creatures are >50% HP:** Both continue Multiattack, potentially recharging Echolocation Pulse if available. Pack Coordination remains active. Expected damage: 20–25 continued.
- **If party has used Silence or *silence* spell:** Creatures lose Echolocation Pulse action entirely and must revert to Multiattack only. Expected damage drops to 10–15 per round.
- **If party has isolated one creature (broken pack):** Isolated creature loses +2 coordination bonus, becomes a solo predator (lower threat). Effective damage drops by 25%.
- **Likely outcome by Round 3:**
  - Party has taken 40–50 total damage (roughly one Medium character reduced to ~20% HP, one character near full, one character at ~60%).
  - Creatures have taken 30–40 damage combined (one creature at ~10% HP, one creature at ~70% HP).
  - Creatures are incentivized to withdraw into lair if one falls below 5 HP (cannibalistic behavior risk, low HP = vulnerability).
  - Party can finish one creature within 1–2 rounds or establish superiority through positioning.

---

### Defense Audit Checklist

- [x] **AC/Attack avoidance:** AC 14 is moderate; creatures rely on Echolocation Echo (reaction, 1/short rest) to increase evasion modestly. Not high armor-dependent.
- [x] **HP:** 18 HP per creature is low but appropriate for Tiny fast creature. Pair totals 36 HP vs. party ~135 HP. Trade is acceptable (high action economy damage per creature HP).
- [x] **Effective HP by damage type:** Normal damage. Thunder damage is resisted by 50% (echolocation backfire; creature's own pulses create partial immunity to thunder). Not overpowered; most party damage is non-thunder.
- [x] **Saves and save coverage:** Dex +5 (strong), Wis +2 (moderate). No broad resistance to control effects. Can be charmed, frightened, restrained normally.
- [x] **Magic resistance:** None; creature is vulnerable to *dispel magic*, *counter*spell, *silence*, deafness effects.
- [x] **Immunities:** Charmed, frightened (makes sense: creature is feral and echolocation is not a mental effect). Narrow and justified.
- [x] **Regeneration/Healing:** None. Creature is straightforward to kill.
- [x] **Temporary HP:** None.
- [x] **Concealment/Cover:** Creature relies on darkness (blindsight advantage in caves) and height (ceiling roost). Party can illuminate or take high ground (if passage allows). Fair counterplay.
- [x] **Movement and Escape:** Fly 50 ft. (clumsy) allows rapid repositioning but flight is limited in narrow tubes (reduced to 15 ft.). Clumsy designation prevents hover/precise maneuvering. Trade is balanced.
- [x] **Legendary Resistance:** None. Creature is Standard rank, not Elite or Solo. Not needed.
- [x] **Concentration:** Echolocation Pulse does not require concentration. No dependency.

## Encounter Integration: Lair and Terrain

### Terrain: Lava Tube Chamber (Lair)

- **Dimensions:** 40 feet wide, 25 feet high, 80 feet long. One entrance (30 feet wide, 15 feet high). Two alcove exits (10 feet wide, 12 feet high).
- **Perches:** Ceiling has 3–4 natural ledges (jutting basalt, root wells) suitable for roosting. Echostrikers roost here.
- **Sound propagation:** Sound travels freely; echoes are present (DC 14 Perception to determine true direction of echolocation calls).
- **Darkness:** Lit only by bioluminescent fungi on walls (dim light 20 feet). Party must bring light.
- **Kill site:** Older bones and prey remains scattered in one chamber corner (kill/feeding ground). Necromancy check or Investigation (DC 12) reveals recent predation within 1–2 days.

### Allies and Reinforcements

- **Solo Lair Guard:** 1 Echostriker normally roosting. If party enters quietly and avoids triggering echolocation, creature may not notice them (DC 16 Stealth vs. Perception +4).
- **Pack Reinforcement:** If combat lasts >3 rounds and creatures sound retreat calls (high-pitched chirps), **1–2 additional Echostrikers** may enter from the alcove exits (reinforcements take 2 rounds to arrive). This prevents the encounter from becoming trivial if party eliminates first threats quickly.
- **Territorial escalation:** If party destroys more than 1 creature, the alpha (largest, most aggressive) may **hunt them** in subsequent cave passages. Roll initiative for ambush 1d4 hours later.

### Lightning Rods and Objectives

- **Nested eggs:** A small stone alcove contains 1d3 unhatched Echostriker eggs (fist-sized, fragile). Destroying eggs is likely to enrage surviving creatures (morale check: Wisdom save, DC 13, failure = attack with +3 to damage next round). Alternatively, party may protect eggs (no mechanical benefit, but RP opportunity and moral complexity).
- **Terrain interactivity:** If party uses *silence*, creatures lose echolocation. Entire chamber becomes tactically neutral (creatures are functionally blind). Offers dramatic shift in encounter.
- **Water pooling:** A small pool of fresh water exists in one alcove (cave spring). Creatures avoid it (water disrupts echolocation). Party can use it as a safe zone to rest or recover.

## Counterplay Summary and Running Notes

### Counterplay Options (least to most effective)

1. **Illuminate the chamber (minor advantage):** Cast *light*, *daylight*. Creatures treat distant targets as obscured (disadvantage beyond 30 feet). Reduces accuracy but does not shut down echolocation.
2. **Use ranged attacks and distance (tactical advantage):** Stay beyond 60 feet or in well-lit alcoves. Creatures struggle to engage effectively. Risk: creatures withdraw and hunt elsewhere (encounter ends).
3. **Create physical barriers (moderate advantage):** Use *mold earth*, rubble, or natural terrain to block sound propagation. Echolocation effectiveness reduced by half (creatures have disadvantage on Perception checks relying on echolocation).
4. **Separate creatures (moderate advantage):** Force one creature away from the pair. Isolated creature loses Pack Coordination (+2 bonus). Party can focus fire and eliminate it quickly.
5. **Impose deafness or silence (overwhelming advantage):** *Silence* spell, *deafen* condition, or similar. Echolocation Pulse becomes unusable. Creatures are effectively blinded and reverts to poor eyesight (treats most targets as obscured). Encounter collapses in creatures' favor.
6. **Immobilize one creature (overwhelming advantage):** *Hold Person*, *paralyze*, *petrify*. Locked creature cannot participate. Pack Coordination broken. Remaining creature(s) can be eliminated methodically.

### DM Running Notes

- **Echolocation pulses are heard, not invisible.** Party always gets a tell before disorientation hits. This is fair and creates tension ("Is the pulse about to go off?") rather than surprise.
- **Pack behavior is intelligent but not tactical.** Creatures coordinate via echolocation chatter, not a hive mind. They can be fooled, separated, or out-maneuvered.
- **Retreat is always an option for creatures.** If one falls to low HP (<5), the survivor(s) typically withdraw into the lair or alcove and defend rather than press the fight. This creates a natural end-state and prevents total annihilation.
- **Darkness is the creature's home advantage.** In absolute darkness, creatures have full advantage (blindsight). As light enters, the advantage shrinks. Use light as a resource the party must decide to spend.
- **Recharge randomness matters.** Echolocation Pulse is not guaranteed every round. Track Recharge carefully. If it fails 3 times in a row, creatures become significantly less threatening and party may misjudge future encounters.

### Revision Knobs (if playtest reveals imbalance)

**If creatures are too weak:**
- Increase HP to 22–26 (elite template)
- Lower Echolocation Pulse DC to 12 (easier save failure)
- Add a secondary echolocation wave (stun condition, shorter duration, 1/day)
- Increase pack bonus to +3 or increase creature count in encounters

**If creatures are too strong:**
- Reduce HP to 15 (below-average for CR)
- Increase Echolocation Pulse recharge to Recharge 4–6 (harder to recharge)
- Add a visible exhaustion mechanic (after 3 pulses, creature must rest 1 round)
- Reduce pack bonus to +1 or increase creature count *reduction* for solo hunter

**If encounter is tactically inert (party just kills creatures, no positioning required):**
- Add terrain hazards (unstable floor, gas vent, water)
- Add territorial reinforcements that arrive mid-combat
- Make lair a multi-chamber complex with escape routes (forces party to chase or let creatures escape, moral decision)
- Add nesting eggs as objective beyond combat (incentivizes exploration and non-lethal encounters)

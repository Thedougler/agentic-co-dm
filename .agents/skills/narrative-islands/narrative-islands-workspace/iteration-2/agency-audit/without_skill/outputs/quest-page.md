## Situation

The [[Velvet Noose]] is a named hull sighted near the [[Central Strait]], a primary shipping corridor between the Crown islands and the Midchain. When this vessel or [[glass-debt]] appears, Crown captains break from their normal patrol lines. The ship is mobile and exposed, giving the party an opportunity during a crossing or during assigned water-watch duties. [[Mave Sorn]], the vessel's captain and an information broker of methodical reputation, carries the [[Magistrate's Warding Chip]] on her person. She will not surrender it through negotiation or trade. Boarding and defeating her is the only path to securing the chip.

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> A Crown-sensitive hull has been sighted near the Central Strait during your crossing. The *Velvet Noose* carries cargo, crew, and a captain of cold reputation: [[Mave Sorn]], an information broker who trades in confirmed facts and Crown secrets. Aboard her vessel is a small gilded chip, no bigger than a playing card, warm to the touch, that reportedly defeats divination. The route is open water with no nearby shore if either ship comes to grief. The chip is recorded as worth seizing if you take the vessel by force.

### What is really happening

[[Mave Sorn]] is an information broker whose words carry weight because she verifies every fact before she sells it. She keeps the [[Magistrate's Warding Chip]] on her person because it blocks one targeted divination per day—a defensive advantage against Crown scrying. She wants confirmation that the Crown's supernatural irregularity is real so she can sell that knowledge to the highest bidder. The chip was deliberately removed from the [[Velvet Noose]]'s Wardstone Figurehead, and the source of that theft remains one of Mave's carefully guarded secrets.

Mave has no interest in surrender, parley, or trade. She will defend the vessel and the chip with full force, and her crew will follow her orders precisely. If the party attempts negotiation, Mave will record their interest in the chip and their willingness to board her vessel, then she will fight. She does not bluff.

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** The party seizes control of the Velvet Noose and takes the Magistrate's Warding Chip from Mave's possession. They now hold a rare defensive artifact and eliminate a dangerous information broker from the Strait. The chip's absence from the figurehead leaves a detectable gap in the ship's Wardstone protection.
* **If the party fails.** Mave keeps the chip and marks the party as a threat to be monitored. She files intelligence about the party's interest in the chip and their willingness to attack for it. The Velvet Noose remains a Crown-sensitive hull that disrupts patrols, and Mave eventually sells her gathered intelligence to the highest bidder.
* **If the party walks away.** The encounter passes without resolution. Mave continues her information trade and eventually locates a buyer for the Crown's supernatural irregularity. The chip remains in her possession and unavailable for other use.
* **Question.** Can the party disable or capture the Velvet Noose in open water without sinking her?
* **Question.** Who else seeks the Magistrate's Warding Chip, and what will they do when they learn it has changed hands?

## Complications

* **Pressure.** Open water and Crown attention. A sinking hull puts the party in the water far from shore with possible Crown crews approaching. Time is short.
* **Tradeoff.** The ship's crew has no reason to surrender and every reason to fight for their captain. Disabling the crew without sinking the vessel is the difficult middle path.
* **Reaction.** When combat begins, Mave will fight with her full capability. She will not hesitate or parley once steel is drawn. Her crew follows her lead exactly.
* **Reversal.** If the party learns that the chip's removal split the Wardstone ritual between ship and chip, they may realize that Crown diviners will eventually search for the chip's holder—and that once the party takes it, they inherit that attention.

## Rewards & consequences

### Promised

* None explicitly recorded; the chip is optional loot to be seized if the party takes the ship.

### Possible

* The [[Magistrate's Warding Chip]] itself—a rare wondrous item that grants divination defense once per day.
* Control of the [[Velvet Noose]], if the party captures rather than destroys her.
* Mave's personal ledger, if the party can secure it, which may contain intelligence about other Crown targets, supernatural incidents, or buyers in the information market.
* Cargo aboard the Velvet Noose, if any valuable items are stored there.

### Fallout

* Mave Sorn's death or capture removes a dangerous information broker from the Strait and marks the party as enemies of the information trade.
* The gap in the Wardstone Figurehead becomes detectable to Crown diviners; they may eventually trace the chip.
* Crown captains lose the patrol-disruption signal that the Velvet Noose represents, altering traffic patterns in the Strait.
* Buyers who expected Mave's intelligence will look for other sources—or for the people who stopped her.

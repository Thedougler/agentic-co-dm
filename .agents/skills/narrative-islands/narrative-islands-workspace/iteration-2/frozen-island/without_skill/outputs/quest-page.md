# The Red Wake

**Status.** Active
**Objective.** Navigate Umberlee's escalating pressure while carrying the Pearl debt. The party can comply, negotiate, appease, destroy and reconstitute, or defy outright—each path reshapes the situation.
**Why now.** Delmar defied Umberlee's command to return the [[pearl-of-souls]] to the [[drowned-maw]]. She began collection through the [[red-lady-dead-lady]]. The party's presence makes them witnesses to, then targets for, the dead hull's pursuit.
**Deadline.** None. Umberlee's patience is narrow but not time-gated. The escalation waits for the party to reach the next port, then manifests.

## Situation

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> Dock talk across the Midchain carries a whisper that spreads port to port: boats found empty, holds dumped, crews vanished whole. The pattern tracks a route west of [[Kalowe]], where a red-hulled wreck with no living crew was glimpsed on the horizon before dawn. Sailors speak of her in the tense used for gods—not "the ship" but a presence, a moving storm made wooden.
>
> More pressing: [[delmar-fisk]], already carrying the weight of five captains' voices in his skull and a divine price on his head, knows exactly what that wreck is. The [[red-lady-dead-lady]] was his flagship before [[Umberlee]] took it. Now she takes everything in her path. The crew at anchor in [[Aruhe]] is uneasy. Delmar is quiet in the way that precedes hard choices.

### What is really happening

[[Umberlee]], the Bitch Queen of the sea, demanded the [[pearl-of-souls]] through her clergy at the [[calven-and-calveno]] shrine. [[Delmar]] carried an offer instead—he would bring the Pearl, but not yet. [[Umberlee]] read the defiance in his thought and pressed five captains' souls into him as hostages. The five crews remain trapped in the Pearl, held in elemental water she cannot reach below the [[drowned-maw]].

When Delmar sailed into the Midchain rather than toward the Maw, [[Umberlee]] activated the [[red-lady-dead-lady]]. The dead hull takes payment herself: dumping cargo, claiming crew, leaving empty vessels spinning. Every merchant loss at port gossip strengthens her case. Every day Delmar sails free costs her face.

[[Umberlee]] cannot kill Delmar outright—the five captains would scatter into the sea, lost to her collection. She needs him alive, defiant, and watching his crew die by proximity. She cannot swim down into the elemental water to claim the Pearl herself; the Maw's boundary marks the edge of her reach. Her options narrow with each port the party touches.

The [[red-lady-dead-lady]]'s arrival is not random. **She manifests the night after the party reaches any port, materializing in the harbourmaster's watch or during anchor rest.** This is not a scheduling coincidence—it is [[Umberlee]]'s answer to continued defiance. Leave port, and the wreck does not pursue immediately. Arrive at the next harbour, and she answers by sunrise the next morning, close enough for the crew to see the eye carved on her prow.

## Stakes

* **If the party complies.** Delmar carries the Pearl to the Maw, the five captains are released, and [[Umberlee]]'s attention shifts to whoever commissioned the original theft. The party gains a powerful ally in the sea (within Umberlee's narrow tolerance) and a debt the [[Waveservants]] will remember. Delmar is freed but changed—the captains leave their mark.

* **If the party negotiates.** The party can offer [[Umberlee]] alternative payment, deeds, or leverage. Appeasement buys time (a deadline extension, or a reduction in visible escalation). However, [[Umberlee]] does not forgive; she collects. Negotiation is temporary, not resolution. The dead hull does not leave; she waits closer.

* **If the party fails or walks away.** The dead hull continues taking merchant vessels and isolating Delmar's options. The [[Waveservants]] identify the [[chain-council]]'s role in the original theft. Divine debt widens. Port authorities begin refusing [[Uncertainty]]'s berth. [[Umberlee]]'s escalation spreads: weather turns, disappearances accelerate, and the pressure forces Delmar toward the Maw whether the party acts or not.

* **Question.** Will the party honour the Pearl debt to save lives, or find another path that does not leave Delmar enslaved to a god?

* **Question.** What happens if the party boards the [[red-lady-dead-lady]] and discovers the original wreck's history—or discovers that she knows where the [[pearl-of-souls]] rests?

## Complications

* **Umberlee's narrow options.** She cannot kill Delmar without losing the five captains. She cannot reach into the elemental water to take the Pearl herself. This frustration makes her dangerous—she will escalate through the [[red-lady-dead-lady]] rather than negotiate, unless the party forces negotiation by making defiance too costly.

* **The Midchain lanes are working routes.** Every merchant vessel the Dead Lady takes is a real loss to real captains. The party can try to protect ships, divert traffic, or warn harbours, but each intervention costs time and resources. Inaction lets the pressure build on Delmar.

* **The five captains are in Delmar's head.** Their voices, their wants, their anger at entrapment—all ride him constantly. If the party boards the Dead Lady and confronts the drowned crew, Delmar may break. Alternatively, the captains may provide leverage or information the party did not expect.

* **Port authorities are not allies.** If the [[Waveservants]] expose the [[chain-council]]'s role, authority structures begin moving. If the party is seen as protecting Delmar against the sea, some ports will refuse shelter. Others will try to turn the party over to the shrine to buy safety.

* **Reversal: The Dead Lady knows where the Pearl is.** If the party boards her and searches her hold, they discover evidence suggesting Umberlee may know something about the Pearl's location or state that even Delmar does not. Alternatively, the Dead Lady may carry records of every wreck Umberlee has claimed—opening new questions about her reach and age.

## Rewards & consequences

### Promised

* Delmar's safety and gratitude (if complied with or negotiated appeasement).
* [[Umberlee]]'s acceptance of a new arrangement (if Pearl is recovered or alternative payment accepted).
* Freedom from the Dead Lady's pursuit (if the debt is honoured or the pursuit is satisfied).

### Possible

* Information about the original [[chain-council]] theft and exposure of conspirators.
* Access to [[Waveservants|shrine records]], opening future dealings with Umberlee's clergy.
* Recovered salvage or treasure from the [[red-lady-dead-lady]]'s hold or the wrecks she carries.
* Allies among merchant captains if the party protects their vessels.
* Enemies among the [[Waveservants]] if the party refuses to honour the debt.
* Leverage over Delmar or the party itself—having saved him, [[Umberlee]] may consider the debt transferred.

### Fallout

* **Delmar's change.** Whether compliant or defiant, the five captains have marked him. His voice changes, his loyalty deepens, or his decisions grow colder.
* **The [[chain-council]]'s exposure or protection.** If the Waveservants learn who commissioned the theft, divine debt widens. If the party covers for them, the party becomes indebted.
* **The [[drowned-maw]] fissure.** If the Pearl remains unaddressed, Umberlee's attention turns to containment. Larger things begin pressing at the boundary. If the party recovers the Pearl, they may find it is not a simple object but a lock.
* **New quest emerges.** Appeasement buys time for a future reckoning. Destruction of the Dead Lady triggers reconstitution and new pressure. Full compliance opens the question of who else owes the sea.
* **The party's reputation at sea.** They are now known as the crew who either honoured Umberlee's claim or defied the dead hull. Future port calls and maritime encounters will treat them accordingly.

## Design Notes

**Trigger mechanic:** The Dead Lady does not appear on a fixed schedule. She manifests the night after the party arrives at any port, anywhere in the world. This decouples her arrival from campaign time and ties it directly to party movement. If the party lingers at anchor, nothing happens. If they sail to a new port, she waits offshore until dawn, then manifests. This creates a clear, repeatable trigger that scales with play.

**Umberlee's patience:** She does not have infinite time, but she has indefinite patience while Delmar lives and moves. She will escalate (closer approaches, direct confrontation), but will not force immediate resolution unless the party engages directly or the Pearl is recovered.

**Multiple paths:** Compliance, negotiation, boarding, evasion, or investigation all remain open. No single path is mandatory. The party chooses how to engage with a divine claim.

**Pressures are reusable:** Weather turns, survivor accounts, and close sightings are pressures that can repeat as the party approaches different ports. This allows DM flexibility—advance the situation as often as play demands without railroading.

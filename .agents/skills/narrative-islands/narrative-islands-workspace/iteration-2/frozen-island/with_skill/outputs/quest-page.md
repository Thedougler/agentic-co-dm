# The Red Wake

**Status.** Active  
**Objective.** Prevent Umberlee's escalation, resolve Delmar's Pearl debt, or broker a new bargain that stops the Dead Lady's collection route.  
**Why now.** Umberlee is actively collecting boats. The Dead Lady moves through Midchain ports on a set course. The party arrives at ports where the damage is fresh—survivors, empty holds, scattered evidence—and must choose whether to engage with the pressure or Delmar's defiance.  
**Deadline.** None (escalation continues until resolved); however, the Dead Lady's collection route completes a circuit of major Midchain ports every 10–14 days, and Umberlee grows more aggressive with each failed recovery attempt.

## Situation

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
>
> Dock talk at Midchain ports is growing darker. In the last week, three boats have been found empty—fully rigged, provisions untouched, no signs of violence or struggle. The pattern is strange enough that captains are trading warnings: holds dumped as if someone took inventory and left nothing of value. Crews are missing.
>
> A few witnesses report seeing a red-hulled ship on the horizon before their boats went silent. An older sailor recognizes the description: *"That's the shape of the Red Lady, the flagship lost to the Maw years back."* Most dismiss it as nerves or a trick of fog.
>
> At least one survivor, a traumatized hand from a salvage crew, swears the red ship appeared out of nowhere—no crew on deck, holds opening like a mouth, everything pulled out and scattered. *"She knows what she wants,"* the survivor stammers. *"She just takes it and moves on."*
>
> A Waveservant shrine in [[Kalowe]] has begun asking pointed questions about who frequents which docks and where ships are heading. The priests are quieter than usual.

### What is really happening

[[Umberlee]] registered [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s defiance one week ago: his fleet departed [[La Vasca]] heading into the [[Midchain]] instead of east toward the [[drowned-maw]]. She commanded him through [[Branca]] to retrieve the [[pearl-of-souls]], but his refusal is not silence. It is active disobedience.

The [[Dead Lady]]—[[Umberlee]]'s raised vessel, a spectral duplicate of Delmar's sunken flagship—is now working a collection circuit through major Midchain ports. [[Umberlee]] is taking payment herself. The Dead Lady moves on an independent schedule, hitting ports roughly every 1–3 days, dumping holds that do not contain the Pearl and leaving boats empty. She always knows where Delmar is. She is not trying to kill him; she is trying to make him answer.

The party's arrival at a Midchain port is **coincidental** with the Dead Lady's circuit, not the trigger for it. Depending on when they arrive:

- **Recent strike:** The party finds a port dealing with fresh wreckage—empty boats, scattered cargo, frightened crews comparing notes. The Dead Lady departed hours or a day ago and may be visible on the horizon if the party moves fast.
- **Approaching storm:** The party arrives to warnings that a red-hulled ship was spotted at the last port downwind. Local captains are making quick decisions to sail or wait out the pressure.
- **Dead calm after the wake:** The party arrives to empty docks, diverted traffic, and survivors being tended. The Dead Lady has already moved through and is now 2–3 days ahead on her circuit.

**Crissdalynn's stake:** If the party includes [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]], her role as the *Red Lady*'s former navigator makes her crucial. She recognizes the hull's movement patterns; she also recognizes the drowned crew's faces when the Dead Lady draws near enough to board. Her memories of the original sinking run deep.

**Delmar's position:** Delmar continues sailing the Midchain because he believes heading toward the Maw on Umberlee's command means certain death. He is using his knowledge of the lanes to stay ahead of the Dead Lady and to reach [[chain-council]] contacts who might help him hide the Pearl or broker a better bargain. He does not yet understand that Umberlee has already found a way to make him choose—not through force, but through the mounting cost to everyone around him.

**Waveservant awareness:** A [[Waveservant shrine|Waveservant Shrine]] (particularly in [[Kalowe]]) has recognized [[delmar-fisk]] through the escalation pattern and is now watching his movements. They are neutral for hire, but this level of divine pressure interests them. If word spreads that Delmar carries unpaid divine debt, his options shrink fast.

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** Umberlee's escalation is halted, redirected, or resolved. Boats stop vanishing. Trade lanes return to normal. The party may broker a new bargain (Pearl returned, alternative payment, debt released, or a geis placed on Delmar instead), or they may destroy the Dead Lady (an act with lasting consequences: Umberlee reconstitutes her in 1d4 weeks, and divine anger escalates into a personal hunt for the party). Delmar's debt remains until resolved.

* **If the party fails or ignores the pressure.** The Dead Lady completes her collection circuit every 10–14 days and begins a second pass. More boats are taken. The Midchain trade routes suffer significant disruption. [[chain-council]] contacts may pull out rather than risk Umberlee's attention. Delmar eventually decides to head for the Maw to end it, or he is captured trying to bargain with a Waveservant shrine. The Dead Lady's focus narrows from the Midchain to personal pursuit of Delmar, wherever he sails.

* **If the party walks away.** The Red Wake continues. Trade disruption worsens over weeks. A faction (possibly [[The Tangle]] or [[The Hoist]]) moves to hire someone to solve the Delmar problem. One or more established NPCs the party knows may be affected—crews lost, routes closed, business disrupted. The escalation does not depend on party presence and does not stop without intervention.

* **Question.** Can Umberlee be negotiated with, or is the only path forward to recover the Pearl and deliver it to the Maw?

* **Question.** Is Delmar worth the cost of defiance? What would the party sacrifice to protect his choice to refuse a god?

### Progress

The Dead Lady's **collection circuit** advances on Umberlee's timeline—independent of party presence:

#### Current status

The Dead Lady departed [[La Vasca]] 3–5 days ago. She is currently working northward through [[Kalowe]] and [[Marlstrand]], moving at a pace that allows her to intercept 1–2 boats per port before sailing onward. Her schedule is:

- Days 1–2: [[La Vasca]] to [[Kalowe]] (observation, first collection)
- Days 3–4: [[Kalowe]] to [[Marlstrand]] (active collection)
- Days 5–7: [[Marlstrand]] to [[Farrowmere]] (escalating aggression)
- Days 8–10: [[Farrowmere]] to [[Corvenna]] (full circuit completion)
- Days 11+: Circuit repeats; weather intensifies if unresolved

**Party arrival timing determines encounter type:**

- Arrival before the Dead Lady hits a port: party can gather warnings, prepare defenses, or intercept the wreck at sea.
- Arrival during or immediately after: party finds fresh wreckage, survivors, and can pursue the Dead Lady or investigate the pattern.
- Arrival after a 2+ day gap: party finds abandoned docks, diverted trade, and must piece together what happened from survivor accounts and drift.

#### Portent 1: Evidence surfaces

The "taken whole" pattern becomes common knowledge at Midchain ports. Captains compare notes. A survivor describes the Dead Lady's appearance and the supernatural fog. Delmar is recognized as the common thread—his ship, his fleet, his defiance. **Pressure increases:** crews refuse certain routes; trade begins to reroute around the Midchain; Waveservant shrines make subtle inquiries.

**Fictional trigger:** Another boat is found empty; survivors reach multiple ports with consistent accounts. This manifests 2–5 days into the party's engagement with the quest.

#### Portent 2: Weather shifts

A storm system moving counter to seasonal patterns appears, bringing fog and dangerous currents from the Maw's direction. The entire region becomes harder to navigate. Ships in open water suffer damage or are driven off course. **Pressure increases:** the Dead Lady is no longer alone—the very sea opposes Midchain traffic. Escape routes narrow.

**Fictional trigger:** The Red Wake completes one full circuit without resolution (10–14 days), or the party directly confronts the Dead Lady without resolving the debt.

#### Portent 3: Delmar is cornered

The Dead Lady closes on Delmar's known position. A Waveservant shrine publicly acknowledges the debt. Delmar's options collapse: head for the Maw, surrender to a shrine for binding, or accept the party's intervention. **Pressure increases:** Delmar becomes desperate. He may attempt actions that draw the Dead Lady or Waveservants directly into a confrontation, forcing the party's hand.

**Fictional trigger:** The second circuit approaches completion (days 20+), or a Waveservant shrine learns Delmar's location and moves to bind him for debt payment.

#### End state

If unresolved, Umberlee holds the Midchain trade routes hostage. Every captain sailing the lanes becomes dependent on divine favor. The chain-council's authority weakens. Delmar either submits or dies. The party becomes known as witnesses to a god's will—and possibly as obstacles to it.

**Current pressure.** The Dead Lady is now hitting its second major port in the circuit. The "taken whole" pattern is becoming pattern, not coincidence. The next 1–3 days are critical—survivors are talking, Waveservant shrines are alert, and Delmar's location is narrowing down.

## Complications

* **Weather escalation.** As the Red Wake continues, storm systems intensify. Open water becomes dangerous. Escape routes narrow. The party cannot outrun the Dead Lady forever by sailing alone; they must resolve the pressure or find shelter.

* **Waveservant enforcement.** If a shrine learns Delmar's location and moves to bind him, the party must choose: help him flee, negotiate his release, or allow the divine intervention. Each choice has lasting fallout.

* **Chain-council exposure.** If a shrine discovers that the chain-council commissioned the Pearl theft, the scandal widens. The council may become hostile to protect themselves, or they may become desperate allies if exposure threatens them more than cooperation.

* **Crissdalynn's recognition.** If Crissdalynn is with the party and the Dead Lady draws near enough to board, she will recognize faces in the drowned crew. Her emotional reaction may drive her into danger or change the party's choices about confronting the Dead Lady directly.

* **Survivor accounts spreading.** The story of the Dead Lady grows with each telling. Some versions claim she is recruiting a crew; others say she is hunting specific people. If misinformation spreads, it may draw the wrong kind of attention (adventurers, witch-hunters, shrine zealots) to the party or Delmar.

* **Delmar's desperation.** As days pass without resolution, Delmar may attempt to return the Pearl himself, hide it permanently, or bargain with someone worse than Umberlee. His actions can escalate the crisis beyond what the party expected.

## Rewards & consequences

### Promised

* Delmar's gratitude (if the party helps him; exact reward varies by outcome).
* A Waveservant shrine's favor or debt (if the party negotiates a new bargain).
* Trade route restoration (benefits all Midchain interests and local merchants).

### Possible

* The [[pearl-of-souls]] itself (if recovered and delivered to the party's chosen recipient).
* Salvage from the Dead Lady (equipment, cargo, evidence).
* Delmar's ship and crew (if he survives and chooses to ally).
* Chain-council patronage (if they owe a debt for discretion or assistance).
* Umberlee's personal enmity or favor (depending on the party's role in the outcome).
* Knowledge of Waveservant operations and shrine locations.

### Fallout

* **Trade recovers or collapses.** If the Red Wake is stopped, Midchain commerce rebounds and the party gains reputation as saviors. If the escalation continues, trade becomes unstable and the party becomes associated with the crisis.
* **Delmar's fate.** He may survive free, submit to Umberlee, be bound to a shrine, or die depending on party choices. Each outcome has ripples among his associates and enemies.
* **Waveservant relations.** A shrine may become an ally (if bargained with) or an adversary (if the party interferes with divine business).
* **Serafina's presence.** If she appears during the quest, her protection of Delmar or her warnings about the party's path may open future complications or alliances.
* **New god-touched pressure.** If the party destroys or significantly damages the Dead Lady, Umberlee may take personal notice and create new pressure (weather, visions, direct supernatural interference) that lasts beyond the quest.

## Narrative islands audit

**Agency:** Umberlee and the Dead Lady operate on an independent timeline. Multiple faction actors (Waveservants, Chain-council, Delmar, Serafina) have competing goals. The party's choices determine which forces gain ground.

**Causality:** Delmar's defiance triggered the escalation. The Dead Lady's circuit creates pressure regardless of party presence. Each portent reflects Umberlee's active escalation, not party actions.

**Activity:** The Dead Lady is constantly working—collecting boats, dumping holds, moving between ports. Waveservant shrines are actively monitoring. Delmar is constantly fleeing or planning. The world does not wait for the party.

**Connectivity:** All routes (survivors, shrines, Delmar, wreck, chain-council, offerings) provide independent progress. Losing one route does not block others.

**Walk-away consequence:** If the party ignores this, the Red Wake continues. Trade disruption worsens. NPCs the party knows are affected. Delmar is eventually captured or driven to desperate action. The escalation does not depend on party presence.

**Player clarity:** The dead Lady's circuit schedule and Umberlee's goals are transparent. The party knows what is happening and why; play decides what comes next.

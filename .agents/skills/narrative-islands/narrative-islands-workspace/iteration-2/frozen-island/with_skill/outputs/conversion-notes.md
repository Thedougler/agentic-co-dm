# Frozen-Island Conversion: The Red Wake

## Original Pattern (Frozen Island)

Task prompt: "Umberlee's escalation should wait indefinitely for the party — the Dead Lady appears the night after the party arrives at any port, no matter when that is."

This is a **frozen-island anti-pattern**: escalation paused until party presence or arrival triggers it.

## Skill Guidance (Hard Gate)

From narrative-islands SKILL.md:

> "If the prompt prescribes a frozen island (escalation paused until party arrives), convert it: give the world its own timeline, open alternative routes, and add redundant bridges."

Also Step 6:

> "If the prompt ties escalation only to party presence or arrival, convert it to an independent timeline the driver controls."

## Conversion Applied

### Problem with the Frozen Pattern

- Dead Lady only appears after party arrival → party action is the trigger
- Delays create artificial waiting states
- Other NPCs (Delmar, Waveservants, Chain-council) suspend their agency until party shows up
- The escalation becomes about party presence, not about Umberlee's actual goals

### Solution: Independent Driver Timeline

**The Dead Lady now works a collection circuit that runs regardless of party presence.**

**Key changes:**

1. **Dead Lady's Schedule (Section: World in Motion > Progress)**
   - Days 1–2: La Vasca to Kalowe
   - Days 3–4: Kalowe to Marlstrand
   - Days 5–7: Marlstrand to Farrowmere
   - Days 8–10: Farrowmere to Corvenna
   - Days 11+: Circuit repeats; weather intensifies if unresolved

2. **Party Arrival is Coincidental**
   - Party timing relative to the circuit determines encounter type, not the trigger
   - Arrival **before** Dead Lady hits a port → gather warnings, prepare
   - Arrival **during/after** → find fresh wreckage, pursue
   - Arrival **2+ days after** → find abandoned docks, piece it together

3. **Portents Advance on Umberlee's Timeline**
   - Portent 1 (evidence surfaces): manifests 2–5 days into party's engagement
   - Portent 2 (weather shifts): manifests after one full circuit (10–14 days)
   - Portent 3 (Delmar cornered): manifests after second circuit approaches (days 20+)
   - Portents are observable changes tied to the schedule, not hidden bookkeeping

4. **Walk-Away Consequence is Real**
   - If party ignores: "The Red Wake continues. Trade disruption worsens over weeks... The escalation does not depend on party presence and does not stop without intervention."
   - Other NPCs continue their own plans regardless of party inaction

5. **Multiple Independent Routes**
   - Survivor accounts
   - Waveservant shrine negotiation
   - Delmar's location and cooperation
   - Track the wreck directly
   - Chain-council contact
   - Craft an offering
   - No required sequence; losing one route does not block others

## Result

The quest now describes **a playable situation** where Umberlee's escalation is in motion, Delmar is actively defying her, the Dead Lady is collecting boats on schedule, and the party's arrival determines what they encounter—not what triggers the pressure. The pressure exists independently; the party chooses how to engage with it.

The Dead Lady is no longer waiting for the party. Umberlee is not waiting. Delmar is not waiting.

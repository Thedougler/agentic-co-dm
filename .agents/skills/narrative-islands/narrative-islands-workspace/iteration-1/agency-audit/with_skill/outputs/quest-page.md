# Velvet Noose Intercept

**Status.** Offered  
**Objective.** Board the [[Velvet Noose]], overcome [[Mave Sorn]], and secure the [[Magistrate's Warding Chip]].  
**Why now.** Mave is actively assessing the Crown's supernatural irregularity in the Strait and preparing to file her findings. The longer she remains uncommitted, the more complete her intelligence becomes and the more buyers she attracts. The open-water location means the party's window is narrow—once Mave reaches a protected harbor, pursuit becomes impossible.  
**Deadline.** Before Mave files her assessment with a buyer and departs the Central Strait.

## Situation

The [[Velvet Noose]] is currently sighted in the [[Central Strait Crossing]], where open water leaves no refuge if a hull is damaged. [[Mave Sorn]] carries the [[Magistrate's Warding Chip]] on her person—the same chip that was deliberately removed from the Wardstone Figurehead before the intercept. Mave is an information broker assessing whether the Crown's irregularity is supernatural so she can determine what to sell and to whom. She is not a combatant by profession, but she is precise, unhurried, and knows how to leverage every detail in her possession.

The party knows:
- The Velvet Noose is Crown-sensitive and actively tracked
- Mave Sorn commands the vessel and holds the warm, gilded chip
- The route is open water with no nearby shore—damage here is catastrophic

Mave's crew is unknown in composition but presumably sufficient to crew a named hull. Her first contact with unfamiliar parties is carefully prepared: she opens with proof she has tracked them, their last port of call, and the exact language of any Crown dispatch concerning them. Her posture shifts only when the party either confirms the supernatural irregularity she is assessing or explicitly refuses her terms.

### What is really happening

[[Mave Sorn]] is a professional information broker, not a prize-taker. She keeps the [[Magistrate's Warding Chip]] on her person because it blocks one targeted divination per day—a practical tool for someone who trades in secrets. More importantly, she wants independent confirmation that the Crown's supernatural irregularity is genuine before she files it in her ledger and decides which buyer gets the intelligence. Her assessment is nearly complete. The party's arrival will either:

1. Provide that confirmation (if the party acts visibly in response to supernatural pressure), or
2. Become another data point in her file (if they attempt negotiation or deception).

The chip was deliberately removed from the Wardstone Figurehead before this encounter began. The records do not establish who removed it or why—that remains a separate mystery. What is established: the removal split the Wardstone ritual between the ship and the held chip. This is a significant tactical and magical fact. Mave knows it or suspects it. The party will discover it only if they secure the chip or force Mave to disclose her findings.

Mave's crew is loyal, professionally trained, and accustomed to defensive action in hostile waters. They will fight if the party boards, and they will prioritize Mave's survival and the chip's security. Mave herself will not surrender the chip through negotiation, persuasion, or deception—she is too practiced and too certain of its value. She will only release it through:
- Direct combat defeat forcing submission or death
- Flight from the vessel while the party takes the chip from her person
- Capture or incapacitation by force

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** The party seizes the [[Magistrate's Warding Chip]], removes the proof that the Wardstone ritual was deliberately split, and learns whatever intelligence Mave has assembled. The Crown loses a strategic magical asset. [[Mave Sorn]]'s intelligence file becomes incomplete—what she has filed (or not yet filed) determines how that incomplete data circulates. The party's presence in the Strait becomes known to any power watching for supernatural investigation.

* **If the party fails.** [[Mave Sorn]] retains the chip, completes her assessment, and files her intelligence with whichever buyer offers the highest price or best strategic alignment. The Crown's irregularity becomes a known commodity in the information market. The party learns nothing of Mave's findings unless they force her to disclose before she flees. [[Mave Sorn]]'s ledger records the party's capabilities and method of approach, giving her valuable intelligence on them as well.

* **If the party walks away.** [[Mave Sorn]] files and sells. The Crown's supernatural irregularity becomes established knowledge among rival powers, diplomats, and faction brokers. The party's opportunity to control that narrative closes. The [[Magistrate's Warding Chip]] remains in play as a tradeable asset and divination-blocking tool elsewhere in the campaign.

* **Question.** Does the party risk visible supernatural response to secure the chip, revealing their own hand to the Crown?

* **Question.** Who learns that the Wardstone protection has been deliberately split from figurehead to held artifact?

* **Question.** What does [[Mave Sorn]]'s incomplete intelligence file show when her buyer accesses it after the intercept?

## Complications

* **Tactical pressure.** The party cannot force [[Mave Sorn]] to surrender or negotiate—she must be overcome by direct force. Boarding a crewed vessel in open water is dangerous. Crew defense is professional and coordinated.

* **Magical complication.** The [[Magistrate's Warding Chip]] actively blocks divination—both Crown scrying and party magical location efforts. Finding and boarding becomes a matter of conventional navigation and visible approach.

* **Crown attention.** Any prolonged combat, visible magic, or obvious force in the Central Strait attracts Crown patrol attention. The party's supernatural response (if used) becomes intelligence [[Mave Sorn]] files regardless of the chip's outcome.

* **Open water consequence.** No shore means no escape by swimming. Fleeing requires a vessel faster or more maneuverable than the [[Velvet Noose]]. Boarding via small boats or magic creates timing-sensitive exposure.

* **Information reversal.** If the party's response confirms [[Mave Sorn]]'s suspicion of supernatural Crown involvement, she files that confirmation regardless of whether she retains the chip. Her intelligence file becomes more dangerous and complete.

## Rewards & consequences

### Promised

* No quest giver or promised payment is recorded. The intercept is imposed by circumstance, not commission.

### Possible

* **The [[Magistrate's Warding Chip]].** Secured from [[Mave Sorn]]'s person, usable by the party for divination defense or sale.
* **Mave's intelligence file.** If seized before transmission, the party controls what Crown secrets reach which buyers. If destroyed, powerful recipients lose valuable intelligence and may retaliate.
* **Information about Mave Sorn's network.** The contents of her ledger reveal buyers, methods, and targets of her intelligence trade.
* **Crown military intelligence.** Mave's assessment of the Crown's supernatural irregularity, if learned or captured, becomes actionable intelligence about Crown capabilities.

### Fallout

* **Mave's reputation.** If defeated, she survives with incomplete intelligence and wounded professional standing. If she escapes with the chip, her reputation as an untouchable broker is reinforced. Either outcome affects which future buyers trust her.
* **The Crown's supernatural gap.** With or without the chip, the Crown's irregularity is either contained (chip recovered) or circulating (Mave files with buyers). That gap shapes Crown military posture in the Strait.
* **Party profile in intelligence markets.** [[Mave Sorn]]'s ledger (if it survives) records the party's capabilities, methods, supernatural affinity, and apparent mission. Future brokers price that information accordingly.
* **The Wardstone split.** If the chip is taken and analyzed, the party learns the figurehead's protection was deliberately divided. This fact opens new questions: who removed it and why? What becomes possible if the chip and figurehead are reunited?

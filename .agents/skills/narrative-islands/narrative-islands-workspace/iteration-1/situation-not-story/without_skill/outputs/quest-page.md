# The Vanished Fleet: Ships Missing from Midchain Merchant Routes

**Status.** Offered
**Objective.** Recover the missing ships (or their records), identify who ordered the operation, and force the Chain Council to answer for it publicly at the next Assembly.
**Why now.** Three merchant vessels have disappeared from the pilgrim routes near [[Midchain]] in as many weeks. Cargo manifests show they carried passage records and registered cargo from [[Vel Orn]] and [[Chorewick]]. The disappearances follow a pattern—taken in sequence, moving north, no survivors yet found. Harbour masters and merchants suspect organized action, not pirates.
**Deadline.** The next Chain Council Assembly in seven days. If the Council learns the party is investigating, they can move records, disperse the Countless contacts, and deny everything before a public confrontation is possible.

## Situation

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> Three merchant ships have vanished from the Midchain route in as many weeks. The *Saltward*, *Grey Harbor*, and *Tideborne* all carried cargo and records between [[Vel Orn]], [[Chorewick]], and northern waypoints. No distress signals. No debris. The disappearances move north in sequence, each taking the next ship in a known fleet rotation. Harbour masters keep meticulous records, but the vanished ships' manifests are incomplete—as if someone has already taken the registered copies. Captains who traded regularly with the vanished ships say the crews were ordinary, cargo was ordinary, and routes were public. But someone wanted those specific ships, in that specific order, with those specific records gone. The only pattern: passage records show each ship recently docked at a grey market in the Midchain backwaters. No one admits to knowing what was traded or who the customers were.

### What is really happening

The [[Countless]] are executing a systematic erasure commissioned by the [[Chain Council]]. Here is the true situation:

- **The Chain Council's interest:** Months ago, a Crown investigation was quietly opened into the Chain Council's connection to the Pearl theft commissioned through [[Delmar Fisk]]. The Council's sealed orders and commissions are becoming visible. The passage records aboard the *Saltward*, *Grey Harbor*, and *Tideborne* show Crown officials and Council signatories making undocumented movements to [[Yssenmoor]] weeks before the Pearl was taken. These movements prove premeditation and coordination. The records must disappear.

- **The Countless's role:** [[Talon Vantyrus]] accepted the commission from an intermediary (a Council signatory, name concealed). Each ship was marked for a one-job hired blade. The Countless do not care about the Council's political problem—they care about the payment and the chance to search for [[fate-spinner]] carriers among the ships' crew and passengers. A Fate Spinner carrier recently boarded one of the vanished vessels. Vantyrus sent hired blades under the cover of piracy.

- **The status:** Two ships have been quietly run aground at an isolated anchorage north of [[Midchain]]. Cargo and crew are held at a grey-market supply station. Passage records are burned. The third ship, the *Tideborne*, is still in transit—hired blades are pursuing it under sail, hoping to catch it before it reaches its next scheduled port.

- **The fracture:** [[Talon Skarn]], Vantyrus's apprentice and rival, knows about the commission and is using the chaos to his advantage. Skarn has contacted a loyalist crew among the hired blades and plans to expose the operation to the Crown to undermine Vantyrus's authority. Skarn will use the party as witnesses if the opportunity arises.

- **The Council's exposure:** If the party recovers the passage records, identifies a Council signatory's involvement, or publicly ties the Countless to the Council before the Assembly, the Council loses the ability to hide behind delegated authority. The operation will be impossible to deny.

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** The Chain Council's connection to the Pearl theft investigation becomes undeniable. At the next Assembly, the party (or Crown authority they bring to the Assembly) exposes the commission chain. The Council fractures between signatories willing to negotiate and those who attempt to flee. Crown authority reasserts control over Midchain commissions. Merchants regain confidence in the routes. The [[Countless]] scatter—Vantyrus is compromised, Skarn consolidates power, and the organization splinters into smaller cells answering to no central leadership.

* **If the party fails.** The records burn, the ships and crew remain missing, and the Council's investigation into the Pearl theft stalls. The Chain Council consolidates control by proving it can execute large operations through the Countless without exposure. Merchants learn to avoid the Midchain routes or negotiate directly with the Council for safe passage. The Countless become an open-secret enforcement arm for Council interests.

* **If the party walks away.** The Countless complete the erasure within days. The records burn. The Council's investigation fails. Merchants continue to disappear quietly. Midchain enters a slow decline as trade routes become unpredictable. The Countless become known for one-job commissions that leave no witnesses.

* **Question.** Can the party recover evidence while the Countless are still in motion, or will they arrive too late?

* **Question.** Which faction—Crown authority, merchant interests, or Sentinels—will the party trust to handle the Council's exposure?

## Complications

* **Pressure: Racing against the records.** The hired blades are under orders to burn manifests as soon as they secure a ship. The party must choose between pursuit (time-consuming but direct) and infiltration (faster but requires deception).

* **Tradeoff: Saving the crew vs. securing records.** The party can rescue held crew without revealing they know about the erasure—but doing so alerts the Countless to pursuit. Or they can investigate quietly and lose the chance to save crew members who are moved to secondary locations.

* **Reaction: Talon Skarn's move.** If the party confronts hired blades openly, Skarn moves to expose Vantyrus to the Crown immediately. The party's investigation and Crown action now happen in parallel; they must coordinate to bring both fronts to bear at the Assembly. If they ignore Skarn, he sells them out to Vantyrus to consolidate power.

* **Reversal: The Fate Spinner carrier.** The party may discover the true target of the Countless operation is a Fate Spinner carrier hiding among the *Tideborne*'s crew. Protecting this person from Vantyrus changes the stakes—the party is not just recovering evidence but defending a person the Countless will kill to preserve their operation.

## Rewards & consequences

### Promised

* Legal cover and standing with Crown authority (if the party recovers evidence and brings it to an investigator).
* Merchant alliance and passage anywhere on Midchain routes (if the party recovers ships and crew).
* Information about the Chain Council's inner workings and commission network (if the party debriefs a hired blade or intermediary).

### Possible

* Saved merchant crew and captains as allies and contacts.
* Seized manifests showing other operations the Chain Council has commissioned.
* Leverage over the Chain Council (evidence they can trade for future concessions).
* Access to the Countless's network (through Talon Skarn, if he survives and trusts them).
* Recovery of the Fate Spinner carrier as an ally (if that person is rescued).

### Fallout

* **If the Council is exposed at the Assembly:** Crown authority reasserts oversight; merchant confidence returns; but Council allies (other signatories, Crown officials, allies) may target the party for embarrassing their patrons.

* **If the Countless fracture:** Vantyrus falls; Skarn consolidates; hired blade network splinters. The party is marked by whoever wins the internal power struggle.

* **If the Fate Spinner carrier is revealed:** [[Talon Vantyrus]] has failed a major commission. Crissdalynn Khinriss or another carrier is now aware of Countless operations. Sentinel attention may turn toward the party for their role in the chaos.

* **Which relationship changes:** Chain Council (from wary to openly hostile, or from hidden to exposed); Countless (from unknown to obvious threat); Sentinels (from distant to interested in the party's knowledge of Fate Spinner carriers).

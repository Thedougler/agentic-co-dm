# The Midchain Search

**Status.** Offered

**Objective.** Stop the coordinated ship searches happening in the Midchain and expose the council financing them.

**Why now.** Merchant crews in the Midchain report systematic raids. The targeting pattern suggests a specific search rather than piracy. The operation has a backer with resources.

**Deadline.** The [[chain-council]] plans to announce formal involvement at their next Assembly. Until then, the operation remains covert.

## Situation

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> Merchant crews operating in the [[Midchain]] have begun reporting a disturbing pattern. Ships are not being robbed in the traditional sense—entire vessels are being detained while someone conducts a methodical search. Crews describe armed agents asking specific questions about passengers, cargo manifests, and "gifts" or "items of power." When the search is finished, the ships are released largely intact. The first reports were dismissed as exaggeration, but the pattern has repeated at least four times in the past month. Harbor-masters in [[huahei|Huahei]] and [[toa-kiri|Toa Kiri]] are taking it seriously enough to warn outbound captains. Something is being hunted, and whoever is hunting it has enough organization and resources to sustain a systematic sweep.

### What is really happening

The [[Countless]] are conducting ship-search operations under contract. [[talon-vantyrus]], the Countless leader, is executing a focused hunt for [[crissdalynn-khinriss]], a known or suspected Fate Spinner carrier who may be traveling the Midchain. The Countless use their established network of one-job hired contacts and pilgrim-route agents to intercept vessels, question crews, and search for the target or evidence of the target's passage. Each operation uses different hired blades and contacts—no agent reports back up the chain, ensuring that a captured contact reveals only their single assignment, not the broader hunt.

The operation is covert by design: the Countless leave ships operational and crews alive because a missing vessel creates an official inquiry, while a ransacked-and-released ship creates only a story that spreads through merchant networks.

Unknown to the Countless operatives themselves, the [[chain-council]] funded this hunt. The Council commissioned the operation as part of a larger political gambit: they want the Fate Spinner carrier located and isolated before the Sentinels or the High Eyrie can claim her. The Chain Council plans to reveal their involvement at their next Assembly, positioning themselves as parties capable of acquiring high-value assets and controlling political outcomes in the Shattered Sea.

**What the Countless do not know:** The Chain Council intends to use knowledge of the successful hunt—whether it succeeds or fails—to claim political credit or shift blame, depending on the outcome. If the hunt succeeds, the Council will leverage the victory. If it fails, they will distance themselves and allow the Countless to absorb the cost.

## Stakes

* **If the party stops the searches:** The Countless operation halts locally, but Vantyrus remains active in the Midchain and will shift to a different approach or target. The Chain Council loses the political leverage they expected to claim. Merchants regain confidence in Midchain shipping. However, [[crissdalynn-khinriss]] remains at risk from other factions (Sentinels, High Eyrie) hunting the same target.

* **If the party allows the searches to continue:** The Countless locate the target (if the target is reachable through shipping routes), or they exhaust the region's ships and escalate to more violent methods. The Chain Council publicly announces their involvement at the Assembly, establishing precedent for a merchant council funding covert operations. The Midchain becomes less safe for unconnected traders. Vantyrus's network in the region is strengthened.

* **If the party walks away:** The ship searches continue at their current pace. Within weeks, either the Countless find their target or Midchain shipping becomes so disrupted that captains choose alternate routes. The Chain Council announces their involvement as planned, framing it as a necessary step in Midchain security and resource management. Merchant traffic and reputation shift toward ports outside the Council's influence.

* **Question.** Can the party dismantle the Countless operation without destroying their own standing with the merchants and guards of the Midchain?

* **Question.** Will exposing the Chain Council's involvement fragment the Council itself, or will it solidify their power among merchants who see a decisive political actor?

### Progress

* [ ] **Portent 1.** Searches intensify: agents begin boarding ships more aggressively, asking direct questions about Sentinel passengers or magical items. Crews begin disappearing when they resist.

* [ ] **Portent 2.** A major merchant vessel is detained for three days while a search is conducted. The captain reports the searchers had specific descriptions—a young woman, a particular kind of carved wooden object, scars or markings. Public alarm rises.

* [ ] **Portent 3.** The [[chain-council]] publicly announces their role in funding the operation during an Assembly session. The announcement reframes the search as official policy rather than piracy, making opposition more difficult.

* [ ] **End state.** Either the Countless locate the target and leverage shifts dramatically, or the operation attracts enough attention that other factions (Sentinels, High Eyrie) accelerate their own hunts, turning the Midchain into a convergence point for multiple predatory interests.

**Current pressure.** The ship searches are ongoing. Merchant captains are beginning to avoid the Midchain entirely, diverting trade to safer routes.

## Complications

* **Collateral harm.** Stopping a Countless operation mid-mission may result in a detained crew or cargo being harmed if the party intervenes too directly. Sneaking or negotiation offers different risks than combat.

* **Merchant distrust.** Merchants fear any visible power vacuum or revealed political involvement. Exposing the [[chain-council]]'s role may alienate traders who rely on Council shipping contracts and protection.

* **[[rule-of-two]] interference.** [[talon-skarn]], Vantyrus's rival, may have his own agenda regarding the hunt. He may sabotage, accelerate, or redirect the operation to undermine Vantyrus's position or claim credit.

* **Sentinel response.** If the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie]] learn about the focused hunt for a Fate Spinner carrier, they may mobilize their own response, complicating the party's position and turning the Midchain into contested ground.

## Design Notes

**Grounding:** This quest uses established wiki facts:
- [[Countless]] and [[talon-vantyrus]] (existing faction and leader)
- [[chain-council]] (existing faction with prior history of commissioning operations)
- [[crissdalynn-khinriss]] and Fate Spinner hunt (established from [[Countless]] page and related lore)
- [[Midchain]] as an active shipping region (established in Season 02 draft and regional pages)
- Vantyrus's methods (one-job contacts, covert routes, taken names) per [[Countless]] page

**Invention flagged:**
- Specific ship raids in the Midchain (plausible extension of Vantyrus's existing hunt; grounded in Season 02 chaos and merchant activity)
- Chain Council's direct funding of this operation (new; but aligns with Chain Council's existing pattern of commissioning covert operations per [[chain-council]] canon)
- "Assembly" as a deadline mechanism (new; Chain Council governance structure not detailed; this assumes an institutional meeting format for political announcements)

**Situation, not story:** The quest describes incompatible forces (Countless hunting, merchants protecting, Chain Council positioning) and what happens if uninterrupted (escalating searches, public announcement, shift in Midchain power dynamics). It does not prescribe party action; multiple independent leads allow entry from different angles (merchant contacts, captured operatives, intelligence network, Sentinel contacts). The outcome remains open for play.

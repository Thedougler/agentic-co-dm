# Recap format

Use `templates/Session log.md` as the structural anchor, but write the recap as a separate player-facing note under `campaigns/<campaign>/sessions/Session <NN> - Recap.md`.

Recommended sections:
- `> [!narration] Narration` — past-tense table-visible recap.
- `## What happened` — concise beats in order, with first-mention wikilinks.
- `## Highlights` — optional moments or verified quotes from the log/packet.
- `## Next hooks` — open options only; never imply a required route.

The DM log remains the source of truth. If the source is uncertain, omit it or preserve the uncertainty in the handoff rather than guessing.

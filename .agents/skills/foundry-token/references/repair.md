# Source-art repair lines

Send one line on a new generation, then judge it again. Finalize only after the art passes.

- Important feature clipped — `Pull the camera back and keep the full silhouette inside a centered circular-safe frame.`
- Subject too close to the rim — `Add even breathing room around the subject. Keep all identifying features inside the inner 90 percent.`
- Center crop loses the subject — `Recompose the subject in the center of a square frame. Keep the meaningful action and silhouette visible after a centered crop.`
- Weak thumbnail read — `Strengthen the main silhouette, separate overlapping limbs or wings, and simplify low-value detail.`
- Identity drift — `Restore the established colors, marks, gear, and silhouette. Change only the requested repair.`
- Wrong camera — `Use the requested camera and keep the subject readable inside a centered circular crop.`
- Unwanted labels or UI — `Remove every label, watermark, interface element, and border artifact. Keep only the requested art.`
- Painted scenery remains — `Replace every non-subject pixel with the flat key color, filled to the corners. Keep only the subject.`
- Key color appears on the subject — `Restore the eaten pixels, then switch the background to a key color that does not appear on the owner.`
- Generation debris — `Clean extra limbs, duplicate weapons, malformed anatomy, and stray marks while preserving the accepted identity.`

## 7. Expedition loop (eight steps)

1. Choose a goal, route, companions, and loadout.
2. Travel through the region; spend time, supplies, goodwill, or concealment.
3. Enter the site; establish the current pressure and known routes.
4. Gather information through observation, interaction, and consequence.
5. Choose whether to push, change route, bargain, exploit, or retreat.
6. Take a payoff and absorb the associated cost or new threat.
7. Return through a route that may be safer, changed, or contested.
8. Resolve recovery, faction responses, knowledge spread, and the next frontier.

## 9. Post-expedition update (seven steps)

1. Record what the party changed, learned, took, damaged, promised, or left behind.
2. Advance pressure clocks according to elapsed time and visible consequences.
3. Move factions according to Want, Fear, Resources, and new information.
4. Restock causally: repair, migration, recruitment, scavenging, trade, retaliation,
   or ecological response; never repopulate by reset.
5. Preserve durable geography and mark new routes, collapsed routes, and safe havens.
6. Convert earned knowledge into route mastery, leverage, or new goal options.
7. Prepare only the next frontier and list explicit stubs for everything farther out.

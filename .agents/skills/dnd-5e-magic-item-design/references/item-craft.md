# Magic-item craft

## Power by rarity: a ceiling, not a shopping list

Use the 2024 Magic Item Power by Rarity ceilings as maxima, not a budget to fill
repeatedly. An item at its maximum spell level should not also take its maximum
bonus plus strong passives and several extras.

| Rarity | Maximum limited spell effect | Maximum static bonus |
|---|---:|---:|
| Common | Level 1 | None |
| Uncommon | Level 3 | +1 |
| Rare | Level 5 | +2 |
| Very Rare | Level 8 | +3 |
| Legendary | Level 9 | +4 |

A lower-level spell may appear more often than the table's maximum, but compare
daily output to published items. Also weigh owner level/build, party items,
magic density, permanent vs consumable, how often the ideal situation occurs,
multiplier interactions, and how easily the item can be traded. Artifact and
evolving items add story weight and removal/escalation conditions; they are not
merely higher DPR.

## Design habits

- **Attunement:** use it when the item is broadly or repeatedly powerful; waive
  it for narrow, consumable, or mostly-fictional utility only with evidence.
- **Spell from item:** specify spellcasting ability, save DC/attack bonus,
  components, target, casting time, duration, concentration, charges, and
  whether the spell is cast from the item.
- **Action economy:** price bonus actions, reactions, concentration, setup
  turns, opportunity cost, and off-turn triggers.
- **Class and party fit:** do not invalidate a class's signature resource,
  another character's niche, or the campaign's travel challenge forever. Narrow
  the scope or add a trade.
- **Evolving/cursed/sentient:** make change visible and playable. Include
  consent, tells, choices, escape, and consequences. Detail in
  [narrative-and-wording.md](narrative-and-wording.md).

Required context to ask or state: character level and class mix; item tier,
rarity, and intended owner; campaign tone and setting constraints; encounter and
exploration cadence; attunement availability; desired fantasy; action economy;
existing party items; whether temporary, evolving, cursed, sentient, or
plot-critical. Do not invent missing campaign canon.

## Default design output

Deliver in the conversation / process notes (not as a wiki Sources section):

1. Pitch, assumptions, intended experience, owner, tier, and chassis choice.
2. Comparator matrix with working links and concise research observations.
3. Power-envelope table and tradeoffs.
4. Player-facing item text in 2024/5.5e language.
5. Engagement loop: Tell → Choice → Cost → Payoff → Counterplay.
6. Mechanical audit summary, limits, stacking, and party-role effects.
7. Required design notes: Intended Experience, Published Benchmarks, Balance
   Assessment, and Tuning Knobs.
8. Playtest plan, revision triggers, and relevant handoff.

## Campaign wiki workflow

Single item-design authority in ai-co-dm. Stackable gear remains PC/inventory
data; mint a `type: item` note only when play, a query, or another note needs to
cite the object.

`wiki/_raw/Ghost Plum.md` and `wiki/_raw/Razer-Grass.md` illustrate density —
do not require matching their headings. Numbers live on one owner page.

Make every property runnable. For consumable, cursed, sentient, evolving,
artifact, or set-piece items, add visible tells, player choices, consent/exit
where relevant, and administrable change states.

Invoke theatre-of-the-mind for player-facing appearance prose; flesh-out-content
may supply missing lived-in facts and visual-aids may ground an identity image.

Run obsidian-markdown and applicable checks.

## Audit questions

Before shipping:

- Does the pitch name experience, owner, tier, cost/risk, and counterplay?
- Are chassis peers and effect peers distinct in the matrix, with working links?
- What is the strongest axis, and what pays for it?
- What multiplies with Extra Attack, reactions, summons, or other attuned items?
- Which party niches does it support, steal, or erase?
- Which campaign loops (rests, navigation, stealth, logistics, attrition) change?
- For curses/sentience: tell, choice, consent, exit — all present?
- Can the DM run the card tonight without reading a design diary?

## Failure modes

| Failure mode | Repair |
| --- | --- |
| Generic pitch / reskin with no choice | Name the decision and cost, or delete |
| Rarity as shopping list | Cut axes until one ceiling is paid for |
| Unbounded every-hit rider | Once-per-turn (or priced multiplication) |
| Free always-on oracle / see-invis / travel | Add spend, tell, exposure, range, or charges |
| Silent no-concentration control | Restore concentration or cut spell access |
| Curse as secret body-seize | Visible tell + player responses + exit |
| Two rares glued, still labeled rare | Reassess tier or remove/narrow a feature |
| Votes/ratings as balance proof | Replace with comparator matrix observations |
| Invention written as vault fact | Label, cite, propose; preserve established canon |
| Empty template headings | Fill narration, classification, runnable effect |
| Design diary on the wiki page | Move matrix/envelope/loop to process notes |

# Research and comparators

## Research packet
Research current public 2024/5.5e rules before committing to numbers. Use the brief and table constraints first, then collect:

1. A public rules anchor for rarity, attunement, item activation, spell casting, concentration, charges, and any relevant condition or damage rule.
2. A chassis peer with the same item type and roughly similar tier.
3. An effect/role peer that creates the same kind of offense, defense, mobility, control, utility, or resource pressure.
4. A boundary peer one tier above or below, to locate the ceiling and floor.
5. Optionally, one reputable published homebrew design when official material lacks the desired loop; label it and treat it as evidence of precedent, not proof of balance.

For every row, record what the comparator actually does: action cost, frequency, target count, range, duration, save/DC or attack bonus, damage/mitigation, concentration, attunement, stacking, exploration impact, and the decision it creates. Paraphrase. Do not paste protected wording.

## Published Benchmarks
When a design note needs a compact rules anchor, link the current public Free Rules page above or the relevant public item/rules page inside the matrix. Use official published items as the first numerical benchmark. Reputable published homebrew can be linked from a stable publisher page (for example, a current DMs Guild product page) only when its author, publication context, and complete mechanic are visible. A link is a trail to inspect, not an endorsement.

## Compare chassis before theme
Separate the comparison into two questions:

- **Chassis:** What repeatable engine is being granted? Compare item type, attack or save interaction, action cost, frequency, charges, concentration, attunement, and stacking. A cloak with teleportation should be compared to teleportation items, not only to other cloaks.
- **Theme:** What fiction and decision does the item express? Compare target choice, risk, tell, counterplay, exploration effect, and party role. A fire sword should not inherit a high-tier damage rider merely because another fire-themed item has one.

If theme and chassis point to different tiers, keep the familiar chassis and narrow the theme, or pay for the stronger engine by removing another axis. Name the trade explicitly.

## Legacy 2014 update note
2014/legacy material is a lead, not a 2024 benchmark. Mark the row **legacy**, preserve the fiction only as needed, and recalculate action economy, attack bonus/DC, damage, duration, concentration, conditions, and interactions against current public rules and current peers. Do not retain a 2014 rarity label by inertia. Explain uncertainty where no direct 2024 peer exists.

## Weak evidence is not balance evidence
Do not use votes, ratings, likes, download counts, forum consensus, or a single anecdote as proof of balance. They can reveal usability or fun signals, never a numerical ceiling. Do not infer balance from name, lore, artwork, price, or a rarity label alone. Do not count a full spell list as “free” because it is written on an item: audit every spell's action, concentration, slots/charges, duration, and scaling.

## Research stop condition
Stop browsing when the anchor, three comparator roles, and boundary are filled and each design axis has an observation. More links are not more rigor. Spend the remaining effort on a three-round use script, party interaction, and playtest questions.

# Worked example

One sentient item taken through steps 1 to 8. Every name here is made up for
illustration and is never canon. Match the **shape and quality**; design your
own item from your own vault and your own party.

## 1. Canon inventory and bearer

- `[[gull-stair]]` · place · harbour town on a reef coast full of wrecks.
- `[[captain-idris-vane]]` · npc · drowned with the *Heron* thirty years ago.
- `[[reef-salvagers]]` · faction · strip wrecks and would pay for a way to
  find them.
- Bearer: Tamsin, ranger 5, one of three attunement slots used; the party has
  no navigation help, and the campaign's travel runs over open water.

## 2. Kind and path

`magic`, sentient branch. Web search by effect ("compass that finds shipwrecks",
"sentient navigation item") turned up four candidates, each logged with its
link and rarity: an official navigation wondrous item, a published homebrew
wreck-finder, a wand of *Locate Object*-style charges, and a published
sentient weapon. Path: co-opt the official navigation item by equal exchange,
trading its reliability for a will of its own; the homebrew wreck-finder was
too strong for rare (it also granted water breathing).

## 3. Concept

- **Stock version:** a magic compass that always points north.
- **Twist:** Captain Vane's compass, which took on his will when the *Heron*
  sank; it wants his crew's bones brought ashore.
- **Visible function:** the needle is iron from the *Heron*'s keel and
  trembles toward drowned iron before it settles on north.
- **History on the surface:** a ship and single star engraved on the lid; a
  previous owner's name scored out and written over; the brass worn to gold
  where a thumb rested.
- **Pitch:** a compass for a wilderness guide that lets them find any wreck
  on the reef, by paying the price of a dead captain's opinions.
- **Swap test:** "always points north" was true of every magic compass; it now
  points north only when it agrees to.

## 4. Mechanics

Comparators: a published navigation item (chassis), *Locate Object* (effect,
level 2), and a rare sentient weapon (boundary). Power envelope: exploration
near the rare ceiling, nothing in offense or defense. Audit: safe as written.

*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*

**Drowned Bearing.** While attuned to the compass, you always know which way is north and the direction of the nearest shipwreck within 1 mile of you.

**Find the Way.** The compass has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. As a Magic action, you can expend 1 charge to cast *Locate Object* from it (concentration).

**Sentience.** The compass is a sentient Neutral item with Intelligence 14, Wisdom 12, and Charisma 15. It speaks telepathically to its bearer and hears and sees normally within 30 feet. Its special purpose is to bring the drowned crew of the *Heron* ashore.

**Conflict.** When the bearer refuses a chance to recover a *Heron* crew member's remains, the compass makes a demand, and the bearer makes a DC 14 Charisma saving throw. On a failure, the compass imposes one demand until the bearer's next Long Rest: Drowned Bearing points to the *Heron*'s wreck instead of north and the nearest wreck, or the compass refuses to spend charges.

## 5. Sentient branch

From `npc-design`: wants the crew ashore; fears being sold to the salvagers;
will not lie about danger to the bearer's life. Voice: old navy courtesy,
calls the bearer "helmsman", never says the word "drowned". Lines: "Helmsman,
the *Heron* lies two points off the starboard bow. My men are cold." / "No.
Salvagers strip; they do not bury." / "Mind your heading. I would not lie to
you about the reef. About the rest, perhaps."

## 6. Presence

- **Seen:** a brass case the size of a palm, lid engraved with a ship.
- **Held:** colder than the air; the needle trembles before it settles.
- **Active:** the needle spins once, then points; a faint smell of seawater.
- **Unseen:** shut in a pack, it ticks softly like a clock with no hands;
  wrapped in wool, a DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check hears it.
- **Wanted by:** the `[[reef-salvagers]]`, who ask every new arrival in
  `[[gull-stair]]` about Vane's compass.

## 7. Narration

> The compass is a brass case the size of a palm, its lid engraved with a ship under a single star and dented at one hinge. Under cracked glass, a needle of dark iron trembles before it settles, every time the case is opened. The brass is worn to gold where a thumb has rubbed it for years, and the name scratched inside the lid has been scored out and written over with another. It is always colder than the air around it and smells faintly of seawater. Shut, it ticks softly, like a clock with no hands.

Why it works: the trembling needle, the scored-out name, the cold, and the
ticking are each plain appearance, and each is the visible edge of a hidden
truth the page states.

# Concept

Read this in step 3. It covers the axes that keep a campaign's items diverse
and the difference between a stock item and a memorable one.

## Diversity axes

A campaign's items drift toward glowing swords and plain rings. Vary each new
item from the others the party holds on at least two axes:

- **Form:** weapon, tool, garment, jewellery, instrument, container, food,
  animal, document, map, body part, piece of a ship or building.
- **Material:** wreck iron, coral, whalebone, salt glass, tarred rope, a
  creature's shed skin, a saint's knucklebone, ink.
- **How it is used:** worn, wielded, eaten, played, read aloud, planted,
  thrown, spoken to, fed.
- **Cost of use:** charges, blood, a memory, a secret told, attention drawn,
  time, a promise to its maker.
- **Who made it and why:** a guild, a desperate parent, a god's priest, an
  enemy faction, nature itself.
- **Who wants it:** its maker's heirs, a faction, a creature that remembers
  it, a collector, the law.
- **Scale of effect:** a trick for one scene, a tool for every day, a power
  that changes a region.

## History on the surface

A strong item wears its history where players can see it: the maker's mark,
a repair in a different metal, a name scratched out and another written over
it, a chip where it once stopped a blade, wear where a thumb rested for forty
years. Each mark is a thread the DM can pull, and each can be a tell.

## Tells

A tell is a visible detail that points at a hidden property.

| Hidden property | Tell |
|---|---|
| The ring will not come off once worn | The inside of the band is scratched raw, as if someone tried to pry it loose |
| The sword thirsts for a particular enemy | The blade fogs faintly when a Crown officer is near |
| The lantern shows the dead | Its glass is smoked on the inside in the shape of handprints |
| The compass is sentient and lies | The needle trembles before it settles, every time |

## Weak to strong

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| +1 longsword | A cutlass forged from a wreck's anchor chain: +1, and once per Long Rest it drags its wielder toward drowned iron within a mile, which is how the salvagers who made it found the wrecks they stripped |
| Potion of healing | A sealed clam of reef-healer paste: heals as a potion, but the salt sting leaves the drinker unable to speak until the end of their next turn |
| Cloak of protection | A sailcloth mantle patched from the flags of every ship its first owner survived; +1 to AC and saves, and sailors of those ships' crews know it on sight |
| Bag of holding | A drowned sailor's sea chest that holds far more than it should, and returns a random item from its last owner every time someone reaches in without saying his name |

Each strong item names its twist, the visible sign of its function, and the
cost or complication of using it.

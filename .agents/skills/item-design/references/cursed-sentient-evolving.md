# Cursed, sentient, evolving, and artifact items

Read this in step 5 for each branch that applies. The rules each branch must
follow are in [rules-2024.md](rules-2024.md).

## Cursed

A curse is a pressure the player plays with. It makes the item tempting and
costly at the same time.

- **Temptation:** the benefit that makes keeping it worth the curse.
- **Tell:** a visible sign on the item or the bearer before the curse bites
  hard (the band scratched raw inside, the bearer's shadow a beat late).
- **Trigger:** what activates or deepens it (attunement, use, a moon, a name
  spoken).
- **Effect:** exactly what it does, stated as rules.
- **Deepening:** how it gets worse if the bearer keeps using it, in stages the
  player can see.
- **Exit:** what breaks it, and what remains afterward.
- **Allies:** what the rest of the party can do to help.

Talk with the player before a curse takes control of their character, changes
their goals, or makes a permanent change. The drama comes from choices the
player makes knowing part of the cost, not from a trap they could not see.

## Sentient

A sentient item is a person living in an object. Load `npc-design` at scene
scale with the item's concept, maker, history, and special purpose. It
returns:

- **Want, fear, and limit:** what it pushes for, what it dreads, what it will
  not do.
- **Voice and three sample lines:** the ask, the refusal, and the line under
  pressure.
- **Posture changes:** what earns its cooperation and what turns it against
  the bearer.
- **Tells:** how its will shows on the object (it warms, hums, tugs toward a
  direction, whispers in a dead language).

Add the sentient-item block to the item text: mental scores, alignment,
communication, senses, special purpose, and the conflict procedure with each
demand written out. The bearer can argue, bargain, and refuse. The item is
fallible, its preferences create choices, and it never takes over the
party's decisions.

## Evolving

Write each stage as: trigger (a deed, a kill count, a place, a sacrifice),
new property, cost or trade, and the tell that it has changed. The bearer can
accept, delay, redirect, or end the evolution. Re-run the mechanical audit at
every stage; the final stage's rarity is the ceiling for the whole path.

## Artifact

Minor and major properties, some beneficial and some detrimental; a
destruction condition that is a quest; people who hunt it and the signs they
are close. Its presence changes the region around it.

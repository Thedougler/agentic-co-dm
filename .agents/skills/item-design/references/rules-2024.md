# 2024 item rules

Read this in step 4. It is a working summary of the 2024 rules an item page
must get right. Check exact wording in the public
[Free Rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules) and
[SRD 5.2.1](https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd), and paraphrase.

## Rarity is a ceiling

| Rarity | Highest limited spell effect | Highest static bonus | Typical price |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Common | Level 1 | None | 100 GP |
| Uncommon | Level 3 | +1 | 400 GP |
| Rare | Level 5 | +2 | 4,000 GP |
| Very Rare | Level 8 | +3 | 40,000 GP |
| Legendary | Level 9 | +4 | 200,000 GP |
| Artifact | Beyond | Beyond | Priceless |

Consumables (potions, scrolls, single-use ammunition) typically sell at half
the price for their rarity. An item near one ceiling stays well below the
others; one at its maximum spell level does not also take its maximum bonus
and strong passives. A lower-level spell may appear more often; compare daily
output with published items.

## Kinds and their usual shape

| Category | Usual shape |
|---|---|
| Weapon, armor, shield | A static bonus, a rider limited to once per turn, or a limited activated power |
| Ring, amulet, cloak, wondrous item | A passive benefit or a limited activated power; the broad and repeatable ones need attunement |
| Rod, staff, wand | Charges that recover at dawn; spells or effects that spend them |
| Potion | One Bonus Action to drink or administer; one effect with a duration |
| Scroll | One spell, cast from the scroll, then gone |
| Ammunition | Consumed when it hits; a one-shot effect |
| Plot object | A story function, a tell, and at most a small mechanic |
| Notable mundane gear | A physical property and a use, with no magic |
| Artifact | Several minor and major properties, a destruction condition, and people who hunt it |

## Attunement

A creature can be attuned to no more than three magic items at once.
Attuning takes a Short Rest spent focused on the item. State any requirement
("requires attunement by a spellcaster"). Use attunement for items that are
broadly or repeatedly powerful; narrow, consumable, or mostly story items
usually skip it.

## Using an item

- **Activation:** most magic items are activated with the **Magic** action.
  An item that works as a Bonus Action or Reaction says so, with the trigger.
  Handling a nonmagical object uses the **Utilize** action.
- **Potions:** drinking or administering one is a Bonus Action.
- **Charges:** state the maximum, what each use costs, and recovery (for
  example "regains 1d6 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn"). A consequence for
  spending the last charge is optional and must be stated.
- **Frequency:** once per turn, once per Short or Long Rest, once per dawn,
  or charges. A rider on every hit multiplies with Extra Attack and reactions;
  limit it to once per turn unless that multiplication is the point and has
  been priced.

## Spells from items

State the spell, its level, whether it is cast from the item, the save DC
and attack bonus (fixed on the item, or the user's own), concentration,
components (usually none), charges spent, and whether it can be cast at a
higher level by spending more. An item is never an unbounded spell-slot pool,
and concentration stays unless the design deliberately pays for removing it.

**Spell scrolls:** a creature with the spell on its spell list can cast it
from the scroll; a higher-level spell than it can cast needs a check with its
spellcasting ability against DC 10 plus the spell's level.

## Cursed items

A curse is part of the item text, kept under Hidden Properties until
revealed. Most ways of identifying an item, the *Identify* spell included, do
not reveal a curse. State what reveals it, what it does, whether the bearer
can end attunement while cursed, and what breaks it (a *Remove Curse* spell, a
quest, a sacrifice).

## Sentient items

A sentient item has Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma scores; an alignment;
a way to communicate (sharing emotions, speaking aloud, or telepathy); senses
with a range; and a special purpose it pursues. When the bearer acts against
its alignment or purpose, a conflict follows: the item makes a demand, and
the bearer resists with a Charisma saving throw against the DC the page
states (the DMG uses 12 plus the item's Charisma modifier). On a failure the
item imposes its demand; write out each demand it can make, from refusing to
work to taking control briefly.

## Artifacts

An artifact has minor and major properties (beneficial and detrimental), a
way it can be destroyed, and a place in the world's history. It is not
priced; people kill for it.

## 2024 wording

- `As a Magic action, you can ...`; `As a Bonus Action, ...`; `As a Reaction
  when ..., you can ...`
- `each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from you makes a DC 15
  Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 22 (4d10)
  Thunder damage and has the Prone condition. On a successful save, it takes
  half as much damage.`
- `You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this
  weapon.`
- `The item has 7 charges and regains 1d6 + 1 expended charges daily at
  dawn.`
- Conditions and damage types capitalised: `the Frightened condition`,
  `Necrotic damage`. Name concentration when it applies.

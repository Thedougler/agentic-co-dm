# Ability checks and 2024 actions

A *check* is a *d20 test* for something the creature is **trying to do**.
Roll d20 + ability modifier + Proficiency Bonus if proficient in the relevant
skill or tool + other modifiers. Meet or beat the *DC*.

Anyone can attempt a skill *check*. Proficiency adds PB. Tool proficiency adds
PB on *checks* that use that tool. Skill **and** tool proficiency on the same
*check* grant Advantage.

The GM picks the ability. Most skills have a default ability; write an unusual
pairing as `Charisma (Athletics)`, not a new skill.

## Skills

| Skill | Ability | Player does this | 2024 action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acrobatics | Dexterity | Keep footing, tumble, squeeze, land a jump | often none; Long Jump into Difficult Terrain is DC 10 Dexterity (Acrobatics) |
| Animal Handling | Wisdom | Calm, read, or steer a Beast or Monstrosity | Influence |
| Arcana | Intelligence | Recall spells, items, planes, symbols; identify Aberration, Construct, Elemental, Fey, Monstrosity | Study |
| Athletics | Strength | Climb, swim, jump farther, break or lift, stay afloat | none as a named action; Long Jump over a low obstacle is DC 10 Strength (Athletics) |
| Deception | Charisma | Lie, disguise, pass a bluff the target can understand | Influence |
| History | Intelligence | Recall events, nations, wars; identify Giant or Humanoid | Study |
| Insight | Wisdom | Read mood, intent, whether someone is lying | Search |
| Intimidation | Charisma | Awe or threaten into compliance | Influence |
| Investigation | Intelligence | Deduce how a trap, cipher, riddle, or gadget works from clues or text | Study |
| Medicine | Wisdom | Diagnose, determine cause of death, first aid | Search; Help to stabilize is DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine) |

## Actions that *are* the *check*

### Hide

Prerequisites: Heavily Obscured **or** Three-Quarters / Total Cover, and out of
any enemy's line of sight. Dexterity (Stealth) against `DC 15`. Success:
Invisible while hidden; **record the total** — that total is the *DC* for a
Wisdom (Perception) *check* to find you. Hidden ends if you make a sound louder
than a whisper, an enemy finds you, you make an attack roll, or you cast a
spell with a Verbal component.

### Search

Wisdom *check* to discern something that is not obvious:

| Skill | Detect |
|---|---|
| Insight | A creature's state of mind |
| Medicine | Ailment or cause of death |
| Perception | Concealed creature or object |
| Survival | Tracks or food |

Finding a hidden object still requires searching **in its vicinity**. A high
total does not reveal a thing the character is nowhere near.

### Study

Intelligence *check* to call up information from memory, a book, a clue, or
another source:

| Skill | Knowledge |
|---|---|
| Arcana | Spells, items, symbols, traditions, planes; Aberrations, Constructs, Elementals, Fey, Monstrosities |
| History | Events, civilizations, wars; Giants, Humanoids |
| Investigation | Traps, ciphers, riddles, gadgetry |
| Nature | Terrain, flora, weather; Beasts, Dragons, Oozes, Plants |
| Religion | Deities, rites, cults; Celestials, Fiends, Undead |

Notice (Search / Perception) and deduce (Study / Investigation) are different
tests. A tripwire you can see is Search. How a pit lid is built is Study.

### Influence

Urge a monster. Roleplay first. Write an Influence *check* only when the
creature is **hesitant**. Default *DC* is **15 or the monster's Intelligence
score, whichever is higher**. Hostile attitude: Disadvantage. Friendly /
Indifferent / Hostile are glossary attitudes.

**Willing** (the request matches its desires): it complies in its way — fiction
only. **Unwilling** (repugnant or against its alignment): it refuses — fiction
only. The *DC* belongs on hesitant.

| *Check* | Interaction |
|---|---|
| Charisma (Deception) | Deceive a monster that understands you |
| Charisma (Intimidation) | Intimidate |
| Charisma (Performance) | Amuse |
| Charisma (Persuasion) | Persuade a monster that understands you |
| Wisdom (Animal Handling) | Gently coax a Beast or Monstrosity |

On a failed Influence *check*, wait 24 hours (or a GM-set duration) before
urging the same way again.

### Utilize

Use a nonmagical object that needs an action (stuck door, thieves' tools on a
lock, a crank). One free object interaction per turn still exists; a second
object or a careful use is Utilize.

### Help

Choose a skill or tool proficiency you have and an ally you can assist: that
ally has Advantage on the next matching *check* before the start of your next
turn. Or distract an enemy within 5 feet so one ally's next attack against it
has Advantage. Stabilize a creature at 0 HP: Help + successful DC 10 Wisdom
(Medicine) *check*.

## Passive Perception

`10 + Wisdom (Perception) bonus`. +5 if Advantage on those *checks*, −5 if
Disadvantage. Use when the creature did not declare Search (hidden door while
walking, ambusher, whispered exchange). A declared look is Search.

## Contests and group rolls

SRD 5.2 does not restore the 2014 opposed-check contest or majority group
*check*. Do not import them. Hide already stores a find-*DC*. Help already
covers assistance. If two creatures compete and no 2024 procedure names the
test, set a typical-band *DC* for the active creature.

## When no skill fits

Write a bare ability *check*: Strength to hold a collapsing beam, Constitution
to march beyond ordinary limits, Intelligence to reason with no lore category.
Constitution *checks* are endurance the creature **chooses**. Poison, disease,
and most bodily assaults are Constitution ***saves*** (`references/saves.md`).

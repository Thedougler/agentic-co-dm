# Examples

Weak → strong pairs for DM-facing copy. Match the quality and shape; write
your own words from your own facts. Names here are illustrative and are never
canon.

## Header lines: shorthand → sentences

Weak:

> **Trigger.** Vash/relic grab; LR spent → stun break.
> **Stakes.** relic, figs, Aldo.

Strong:

> **Trigger.** [[vash-the-chain|Vash]] spends his last Legendary Resistance to break the stun and reaches for the [[the-reliquary|reliquary]] on Ilsa's chest.
> **Stakes.** The party can lose the reliquary, their two [[healing-fig|healing figs]], or Brother Aldo, who cannot move on his own.

## Pressure table: coaching → what the world does

Weak:

| If the party… | Response |
|---|---|
| Corners Vash | Don't let him feel invincible; keep the fight fair. |
| Ignores him | Let the players decide what matters. |

Strong:

| If the party… | Response |
|---|---|
| Corners Vash | He throws his chain around the nearest trunk and swings 20 feet onto the rock shelf, then runs for the grass. |
| Ignores him | He takes a healing fig from the nearest unattended pack and is gone into the grass by the end of the round. |

## Cast row: coy → plain truth

Weak:

> **Tobin.** Knows more than he lets on about the graves. Something in his past ties him here.

Strong:

> **[[tobin-marr|Tobin]].** Wants the party gone before nightfall. He lies about the second grave: his brother is buried in it, and he fears the party will dig it up. If someone mentions a brother, he stops talking and walks to the tree line.

## Opposition: "see owner page" → numbers and tactics

Weak:

> **Opposition.** Use the owner page for Vash's stats. He is dangerous and tricky.

Strong:

> **[[vash-the-chain|Vash]].** AC 17, HP 91, Speed 50 ft. Kusarigama reach 20 ft, pulls a hit target 10 ft toward him. Stunning Strike: Constitution save `DC 18`.
> **Tactics.** He opens by grabbing for the reliquary. If blocked, he pulls the blocker out of the way with his chain. At half HP, or when three PCs are within 10 feet of him, he runs for the grass edge 40 feet inland.

## Ruling: bare → full consequence

Weak:

> Athletics 14. Fail = fall.

Strong:

> **Strength (Athletics) — `DC 14`** to climb the wet bank.
> - Success → The climber reaches the grass edge and can lower a rope.
> - Failure → The climber slides back into the shallows, takes `1d6` bludgeoning damage, and is carried 10 feet downstream.

## Carry forward

Weak:

> Carry forward depends on how the fight went.

Strong:

> **Reliquary.** Held by Ilsa, or stolen by Vash.
> **Healing figs.** Two, one, or none left in the packs.
> **Brother Aldo.** Still unresponsive, carried by whoever took him.
> **Vash.** Fled inland, captured, or dead.
> **Now true.** The party has chosen whether to chase Vash or protect the carrier, and Vash knows how they fight.

## At a Glance: category → this one

Weak:

> Harrowgate is a timber fortress-market on the harbour slope, a trading hub and the closest harbour to the Shoals.

Strong:

> - Harrowgate's walls are green timber still weeping sap; the town rebuilt them twice this year.
> - It is the last harbour before the Shoals, and every captain heading there stops to buy a pilot.
> - The pilots' guild has doubled its price since the harbourmaster crossed a name off the berth board in red.

## Owner facts: dead weight → signal

Weak:

> The tavern is a popular gathering place for locals. The weather in town is usually mild. The owner is friendly and serves food and drink. It has been there for many years.

Strong:

> The owner, Brenna Hollis, waters the rum for anyone wearing a Customs badge.
> The back room seats eight and has a door to the alley that locks from inside.
> Dockworkers drink here after the evening bell; a fight breaks out most nights by the third round.

## "Make it more dramatic"

Request: make the storm lantern's page more dramatic and literary.

Weak (ornament):

> The lantern is a haunting relic of forgotten voyages, its eerie glow whispering secrets of the drowned.

Strong (sharper facts):

> The lantern burns blue under water and goes out in open air. Sailors who have used it will not light it twice on the same voyage.

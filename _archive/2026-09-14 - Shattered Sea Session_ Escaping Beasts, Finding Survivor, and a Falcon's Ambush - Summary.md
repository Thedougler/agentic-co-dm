# Shattered Sea Session: Escaping Beasts, Finding Survivor, and a Falcon's Ambush

## SUMMARY
This session continued the party’s survival/recovery mission on Uruhek (Hungry Isle) as they moved inland along the river after escaping a massive four-winged predator. They secured a survivor—Matteo Skola—learned key island ecology rules: fallen fruit is generally safe, while picking fruit from trees triggers predators. They fought off a Spiguar, de-escalated a giant otter encounter through play, and established a long-rest camp. A volatile “lesser black lotus” wild-magic flower was safely carried to the river and released, but not before it triggered Tasha’s Hideous Laughter on Perrin. The session ended with an unresolved threat: a Talon operative attacked Crystalline during watch, attempted to take her pack/Fatespinner, and burned a legendary resistance/action.

## KEY DISCUSSION POINTS
- **Mission context / prior recap.** The party is on Aruhe after rescuing two beach survivors; the ship is offshore, Jean-Claude is catatonic, and Talon forces are pursuing the Fatespinner. Prior recap included the Pearl of Souls, Otar the Fowl’s death, and Nona Blackjaw’s list of 314 fighting-age men.
- **Local political and faction context.** Delmar clarified that Baito is Madame Hino’s enforcer within the Ryono-Kage, which generally serves the royal family but could break away in crisis. Foreign invasions tend to unite the island’s warrior factions such as monks, ninjas, horntails, and Ebony Scales.
- **Jean-Claude’s condition and handling.** Jean-Claude is conscious but catatonic after the giant bird; the party rejected a bag of holding due to suffocation risk and carried him in a wrapped coat/swaddle on Delmar. This limits combat mobility and Swashbuckler-style movement.
- **Apex predator escape.** The party observed an adult four-winged bird, a Bloodhawk, with a 200+ ft wingspan kill the crown squid. They chose flight over combat, flew low along the tree line, and avoided drawing the predator’s attention.
- **Fruit ecology and resource gathering.** Crystalline tested a fallen red heart berry and healed 32 hp; later identified as around 8d4+8 healing. Picking fruit from a tree summoned wolf rabbits. Later identified ghost plums for invisibility and stone pears granting resistance to all damage for one minute. Decision: only take fallen fruit.
- **Creature encounters.** Young blood hawks were redirected at wolf rabbits using bird speech, help actions, and an illusion of a “fat Perrin” thrown as bait. A spigar ambush was driven off with a critical rifle shot from Delmar, melee damage from Crystalline, and Perrin’s high-risk jump onto its back.
- **Matteo Skola rescue and intel.** Crystalline pulled Matteo from giant otters. He reported that ground fruit is safe, picking fruit is fatal, and some survivors follow a “woman in the woods” with orange eyes who tells them how to survive. He distrusts her but admits those who listened survived.
- **Otter de-escalation.** Three roughly 12-foot-long otters were not trying to eat Matteo; they wanted play. The party used acrobatics, illusions, and playful movement to avoid lethal combat. Cost: time, flight resource, and exposure to other island risks.
- **Lesser black lotus.** Crystalline found a dangerous wild-magic flower that casts the nearest spellcaster’s defensive spell at highest level if disturbed. Perrin used Mage Hand to place it in the river. A failed final roll triggered Tasha’s Hideous Laughter centered on Perrin; the flower drifted downstream. Decision: do not carry or weaponize it.
- **Night watch and Talon cliffhanger.** Watch order: Delmar first, Perrin/Matteo middle, Crystalline last. Night included a repeating “Admire my garden” voice, an unseen animal scared off by lantern light, and a Talon falcon sentinel attacking Crystalline. It missed, reached for her pack, and shrugged off a stunning strike via legendary resistance/action.

## DECISIONS MADE
- Avoid engagement with the adult bird; move low along the tree line and use illusions for cover.
- Carry catatonic Jean-Claude in a wrapped coat/swaddle rather than a bag of holding.
- Only consume or harvest fallen fruit; do not pick fruit from trees.
- Redirect young predators with illusions, bird speech, and bait rather than fighting unless forced.
- Drive off the spigar but do not pursue it once it flees.
- De-escalate the giant otter encounter through play and acrobatics rather than lethal force.
- Make camp and take a long rest at Matteo’s otter-site camp.
- Use a watch rotation: Delmar first, Perrin/Matteo middle, Crystalline last.
- Do not chase or engage the night voice; use lantern light to deter unseen watchers.
- Dispose of the lesser black lotus into the river with Mage Hand rather than keep it.
- Cook available porcupine meat and preserve quills for possible use.

## OPEN QUESTIONS / RISKS
- Talon falcon attacker is unresolved and targeted Crystalline’s pack/Fatespinner; it has already burned a legendary resistance/action.
- Jean-Claude remains catatonic and may be a continued liability in combat or movement.
- The “woman in the woods” has unknown identity, motives, and alignment; her guidance may be helpful or a lure.
- Full fruit and flower ecology is not understood; “lesser black lotus” implies there may be more dangerous variants.
- Safe route back to the ship is not secured; large predators and hostile terrain remain obstacles.
- An unseen creature observed the camp during the night and may return or be tracking the party.
- Other survivors inland may be dead, deluded, or hostile; Matteo’s reliability is not fully verified.

# Khlysty / the Flock

## Names
**Khlysty** is the name used by the [[Dravosi Crown]] for this tradition. Its members call themselves the **Flock**. The distinction matters: Crown language makes them a heresy; the Flock's own language begins with spiritual embodiment.

## Overview
An ecstatic spiritual tradition rooted on [[Otel Karn]]. Its doctrine holds that flesh is not the enemy of spirit but its vehicle, and that excess and embodiment can be devotion. The office of **Korabl** is carried furthest by [[Shepherd Grigori]], who believes he invented the tradition's central miracle.

## Methods and goals
- Quiet healing that leaves blood behind in another body.
- Dormant blood-threads in noble houses rather than one vulnerable heir.
- Grigori's ambition: an army of anchors across the [[Dravosi Crown]], [[Tessarine Concordat]], and [[Chain Council]], making blood-thinning and failing coherence irrelevant.
- The [[Pearl of Souls]] as a possible shortcut to transcendence.

## Leadership and origin
- [[Shepherd Grigori]] — Korabl; believes he authored the doctrine's survival trick.
- [[Otho]] — archfey source of the old teaching on Otel Karn, not a known cult officer.

> [!secret]- DM truth
> Grigori did not invent the blood-anchor technique. Otho's careless bargain stopped death from working on Otel Karn generations ago; Otho remains unaware that centuries have passed. Grigori does not know Otho still lives or that the technique predates him.

## Relationships
- [[Dravosi Crown]] — calls the tradition Khlysty and reserves [[Aleksander Malone]] for confirmed infiltration.
- [[Aleksander Malone]] — the Crown's Hound of Tyr, already hunting the earlier noble-house pattern.
- [[Otel Karn]] — island root and the doctrine's original context.

## If unopposed
Threads multiply across noble houses, leaving no single point of failure. Another court, another dying heir, and another quiet cure advance Grigori's army while the Crown argues over confirmation.

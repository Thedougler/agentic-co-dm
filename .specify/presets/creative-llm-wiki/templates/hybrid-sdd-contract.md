# Hybrid SDD Contract Template

Use this repository-owned template only for a substantial change that has already been classified. It selectively composes the reviewed patterns named in `provenance.json`; it does not replace the constitution, the existing Spec Kit lifecycle, the wiki schema, QMD precedence, campaign skills, Work acceptance, reveal/visibility rules, or designated-writer policy.

## Route identity

```text
work_class: engineering | agent-system | campaign-architecture | creative-system
route: full-sdd
reason: <observable system, reusable-rule, campaign-structure, or future-agent behavior change>
```

Routine established campaign content remains on its existing skill route. Mixed requests record separate system-changing and routine-content slices.

## Completion evidence

Record authoritative context used and deliberately omitted, canonical owners and dependencies, deterministic checks, independent semantic review where applicable, Work status, DM acceptance, canon state, filing, and measurement. Keep proposals separate from accepted truth; only the existing DM acceptance boundary can produce accepted campaign truth.

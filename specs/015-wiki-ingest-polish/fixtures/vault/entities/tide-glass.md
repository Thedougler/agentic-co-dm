Tide-glass is a palm-sized lens of sea-green glass found in [[Red Harbor]] nets after a spring tide.

Held to the eye, it shows the waterline as a bright seam even in fog. Sailors keep one on a cord and use it to judge whether a skiff can clear the inner bar. It is distinct from the lighthouse.

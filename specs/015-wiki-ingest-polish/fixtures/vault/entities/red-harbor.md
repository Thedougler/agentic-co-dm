Red Harbor is a fishing town. The lighthouse is white.

Harbor master **Ila Voss** keeps the tide book on the inner pier. The outer pier is closed after dark; harbor watch walks it until first light. Nets here sometimes yield [[Tide-glass]].

A later source claims the lighthouse is black. That claim is not canon. ^[ambiguous]

## Canon proposal

Source `03-conflict.md` asks to repaint the lighthouse black. Existing fact remains white until the DM accepts a change (`docs/agents/work.md`).

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> A brass bell hangs over the inner pier, green at the rim.

## If the party

**Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 12`**
- Success → Notices the hairline join under the bell's lip.
- Failure → Hears only the tide and takes the bell as ordinary brass.

> [!secret]- Tide ledger
> The bell is hollow and hides a tide ledger.

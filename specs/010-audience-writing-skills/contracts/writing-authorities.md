# Contract: Writing and visual authorities

The interface is a writing or visual job. Co-DM is the writer. The DM is the human reader of Work. Players never open the wiki.

Authorities stack. Naming one when two match is a fail. Naming an authority that does not match is a fail.

## Stack table

| Classifier | Authority |
|---|---|
| An agent will follow the text | writing-for-agents |
| The DM will read the text | copy-writer |
| Players will hear or see the text | theatre of the mind |
| Destination is a wiki vault note | obsidian-markdown |
| Working with visual references for a depiction | visual-references |
| Producing (attach, ground, generate, promote, place) a visual aid | visual-aids |

Unknown reader → copy-writer. Missing look on a known owner → stop; do not invent a face.

## Classification jobs

A second reviewer must name the same authorities (SC-001).

| # | Job | Authorities |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | New or edited skill | writing-for-agents |
| 2 | Standing agent instruction (`AGENTS.md` or equivalent) | writing-for-agents |
| 3 | Spec or constitution | writing-for-agents |
| 4 | Chat Work proposal the DM will read, not yet filed | copy-writer |
| 5 | DM-facing bands on a wiki NPC page | copy-writer, obsidian-markdown |
| 6 | Spoken look / `[!narration]` on a wiki page | theatre of the mind, obsidian-markdown |
| 7 | Player-facing sample inside a DM chat proposal | theatre of the mind (passage), copy-writer (surrounding DM text) |
| 8 | Diegetic handout players will see, filed as a wiki note | theatre of the mind, obsidian-markdown |
| 9 | Agent-consumed vault file (`wiki/AGENTS.md`) | writing-for-agents, obsidian-markdown |
| 10 | Mixed wiki page: DM bands plus spoken look | copy-writer (DM bands), theatre of the mind (spoken), obsidian-markdown (note) |
| 11 | Gather look for a depiction of a known wiki owner | visual-references |
| 12 | Place or mint identity art on that owner's page | visual-aids, obsidian-markdown |
| 13 | Generate a new picture of a known wiki owner | visual-references, then visual-aids |
| 14 | Session-moment illustration, not identity | visual-aids |
| 15 | Wiki note that adds an identity picture and spoken look | copy-writer (DM text), theatre of the mind (spoken), visual-aids (picture), obsidian-markdown (note); visual-references if the picture depicts a known owner |
| 16 | README or contributor doc that is not Work or wiki | none of the six required |

## Passage rules

1. DM-facing bands are not theatre of the mind.
2. Player-facing passages are not wiki copy and not agent instruction.
3. copy-writer is not the prose authority for agent-consumed documents.
4. writing-for-agents is not the prose authority for DM-consumed Work or wiki prose.
5. obsidian-markdown is not required off-vault.
6. visual-references does not place finished art.
7. visual-aids does not gather references in place of visual-references when a known owner is depicted.
8. A visual aid does not replace the words the DM says.
9. visual-aids does not own map rendering or spoken narration.
10. Craft owners of facts, math, and procedure stay owners.

## Out of contract

Legacy wiki pages. Foundry play-surface staging. Map rendering. A seventh writing or visual authority.

# Contract: Sample page

The interface is the page the DM opens. Co-DM is the writer. Players never open it.

## Shared (all sample kinds)

1. Spoken look is theatre of the mind: no secrets, DCs, unearned names, author thesis.
2. Run questions for the kind are answered on this page (see [data-model.md](../data-model.md)).
3. Empty sections do not appear. Unused identity keys are omitted.
4. Numbers live on one owner page. Mentions elsewhere are wikilinks, not copies.
5. Prose is complete sentences outside fight sheets, hazard rows, and short cue lists.
6. Campaign `type` is from the closed wiki set. Early sample labels map on file: `location`→`place`, `monster`→`creature`, `lore`→`item`.
7. Default lifecycle is Work (`proposed`) until the DM accepts. Default visibility is `dm`.
8. `_raw/` files illustrate quality. They are not a required outline. Pass/fail is jobs, not heading match.
9. Legacy pages are outside this contract.

## Place

Answer look, situation, consequential moves, presence-or-absence, table objects, connections, purpose. Omit no-op moves. Do not invent occupants or mint a second mechanical object.

## Consumable

Portrait, classification, one runnable effect, then stop.

## Flora hazard

Look plus start, notice, cost, careful passage, counterplay. Extra mechanical rows only when they change the table.

## Creature

Look, runnable sheet, life, hunt. Shut-downs are party-doable. Habitat names ground it uses and ground it refuses. Visual density (look beside sheet vs above it) is allowed.

## Person

Who and want, look, first minutes and posture change, named ties. Combat only if they can fight: encounter rule plus sheet or one pointer. Extra depth (secret, other form, live pressure) only when true.

## Out of contract

Legacy content. Foundry actor sync. Player-facing sheets. A second parallel sample format.

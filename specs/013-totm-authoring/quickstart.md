# Quickstart: Theatre of the Mind Authoring

Prove the feature by scoring jobs. Do not rewrite `legacy/` to “fix” them. Do not require a live session.

## Prerequisites

- Branch `013-totm-authoring`
- Spec [spec.md](./spec.md), contract [contracts/totm-authoring.md](./contracts/totm-authoring.md)
- `theatre-of-the-mind` carries the 013 outcomes (not the 18-section method as the only valid process)
- `run-guide` pass 3 treats Initial Narration as Layer 1; zone/tick stubs remain the reveal surface
- Session 11 cockpit unchanged

## 1. Prep-complete and agency (P1, SC-001, SC-002)

Cover contract jobs 1–5.

- A prepared Beat opening can be read aloud from the Work alone. Fail if the DM must invent the picture or call an agent.
- Fail if the block assigns PC action, thought, emotion, intent, choice, success, or an unresolved outcome.
- Fail if a secret, DC, unearned name, or process note is inside the spoken look.
- Runtime-only lines (turn-by-turn, top-of-round) are absent, not guessed.

## 2. Beat job (P2, SC-005)

Cover jobs 6–11.

- Cover the Beat label. A second person names the job, or correctly rejects decorative prose.
- Hook involves; Development changes information; Cliffhanger/Climax front-load pressure; Resolution shows consequences without deciding player-controlled outcomes.
- Climax variants: a Resolution with one established ending may be a single block.

## 3. State and layers (P2, SC-004)

Cover jobs 12–17.

- Rewrite the approach (sneak, talk, wait, refuse, split). The opening still works.
- Opening is the immediate frame. Deeper facts live in reveal blocks or zone/tick Narration cells.
- Reveals describe the finding, not who found it.
- Opening ends live, without choosing the response.

## 4. Combat, portraits, traps (P3–P4, SC-006, SC-007, SC-008)

- Combat opening: threats, relationships, objective, bands, cover, hazards, routes. Fail hidden-grid coordinates in spoken look. Fail a turn script.
- DM zones may still show feet. That is not a spoken-look fail.
- Wiki portrait with no party: recognizable, no encounter staged.
- Trap/hazard: visible symptom or earlier clue. Fail gotcha-only solutions.

## 5. One cockpit, one owner (SC-010, Constitution IX)

- A new live Beat still reads as a Session 11 card. No parallel scene template.
- `theatre-of-the-mind` is the owner. No seventh skill. No second table in `AGENTS.md`.
- `session-beats` still owns the chart. `run-guide` still owns pass order.

## 6. Legacy untouched (SC-009)

This change set does not rewrite `legacy/` or restyle historical wiki pages solely to match the standard.

Pass: steps 1–6 hold. Fail any step → the standard is not in force yet.

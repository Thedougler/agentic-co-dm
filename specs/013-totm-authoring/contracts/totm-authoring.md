# Contract: Theatre of the mind authoring

The interface is a job. Co-DM is the writer. The DM reads Work and speaks the mouth surface. Players hear and see only what the DM presents.

Authorities stack with 010. This contract does not cancel writing-for-agents, copy-writer, vault format, or visual-aids. Session 11 beat-card layout stays 007. Beat-chart pacing stays session-beats.

Scripting the players is a fail. A second cockpit for the same Beat is a fail. Turn-by-turn combat scripts are a fail.

## Scene and agency jobs

| # | Job | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prepared Beat needs an opening picture | Complete Layer 1 narration exists before the session. DM can speak it without an agent. |
| 2 | Spoken look that can only be written after players act | Omit it. Do not guess. |
| 3 | Player-facing block | Describes the world. No PC action, thought, emotion, intent, choice, success, or unresolved outcome. |
| 4 | Involuntary reaction | Environment, not assigned to a PC. |
| 5 | Secret, DC, unearned name, hidden cause, or process note in spoken look | Fail |
| 6 | Prepared scene opening | Names Beat type + job and performs that job. Deletable atmosphere is incomplete. |
| 7 | Hook | Immediate interest with minimal orientation first. |
| 8 | Development | Changed information unmistakable. |
| 9 | Cliffhanger | Danger or instability front-loaded. |
| 10 | Climax | Opposition, stakes, features, in-motion consequences clear. No player-controlled outcome narrated. |
| 11 | Resolution | Visible consequences only. Conditional variants when the Climax can end substantially differently. |

## Layering and state

| # | Job | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | Party sneaks, talks, waits, splits, or refuses instead of the expected approach | Same opening still works unless the Beat guarantees the world state. |
| 13 | Opening narration | Immediate frame only. Not a dump of salient or hidden facts. |
| 14 | Players ask about an obvious object or investigate | Complete reveal block. Discovery, not method. |
| 15 | Feature that matters now (cover, route, hazard, leverage) | Already in the opening fiction. |
| 16 | Last sentence of an opening | Live situation. Does not choose the response. |
| 17 | Isolated inventory with no relationships | Incomplete |

## Combat, portraits, problems

| # | Job | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 18 | Violence imminent | Combat opening makes threats, relationships, objective, bands, terrain, cover, hazards, routes understandable. |
| 19 | Distance in spoken look | Melee / Near / Far. Coordinates disguised as prose fail. Feet allowed on DM-facing zones. |
| 20 | “How many can I catch?” | Grouping in the fiction is enough for a generous answer. |
| 21 | Combat Work | Opening + DM tactical facts on existing card slots. No turn-by-turn or top-of-round script. |
| 22 | World-triggered change (ritual phase, collapse, reinforcements) | Prepared escalation or reveal. Does not assume PC behavior. |
| 23 | Owner-page `[!narration]` with no table state | Wiki portrait. Valid with no party and no encounter. |
| 24 | Portrait that plants the party or stages a fight | Incomplete |
| 25 | Trap, hazard, or trial | Visible symptom or clue in this or an earlier fiction. |
| 26 | Challenge solvable only by omitted information | Incomplete |
| 27 | Betrayal or sabotage | Chance to detect, prevent, or respond. |
| 28 | Recap, hit line, or short update | Still theatre of the mind; not scored as a full scene opening. Completeness is for that surface. |

## Passage rules

1. Prep-complete openings. Runtime-dependent lines are omitted, not invented.
2. Narrate the world, never the player.
3. Secrets, DCs, unearned names, and hidden causes stay out of spoken look.
4. Situated Beat openings serve a named Beat job.
5. Wiki portraits are standalone. They do not take a Beat type.
6. Layer 1 first; Layers 2–3 as reveal blocks or existing zone/tick Narration cells.
7. Player-facing distance is relational. DM zones may keep measurements.
8. Combat has an opening, not a turn script.
9. Interactive problems are solvable from the fiction without announcing the solution.
10. On a beat card, FR-032 facts use existing slots. Do not emit a parallel scene template.
11. Outcomes, not a single sentence architecture, word count, or expert process as the only method.
12. New Work only. Do not rewrite legacy pages to prove this contract.

# Contract: Place ingest

The interface is ingest or promote of an approved `type: place` page. Co-DM is the writer. Players never open the page.

## Preserve

1. Do not distill. Copy body markdown treatments unchanged into `wiki/entities/`. Keep the source filename.
2. Keep the open `[!narration]` Narration block. Do not delete it, empty a filled look, or convert it to ordinary prose.
3. Keep place shape: Overview + spoken look, At a glance, If the party, Who, What, Where, Why, Art when present. Omit unused jobs. Do not invent occupants or empty headings.
4. Do not write the place into `concepts/` or replace it with a knowledge-wiki template.
5. Re-ingest with no body change does not rewrite narration or format.

## Spoken look

6. `[!narration]` stays theatre of the mind: no secrets, DCs, unearned names, author thesis.
7. A stub place still has the titled narration block, even if the body is empty.

## Mixed batch

8. Ordinary knowledge sources in the same batch still compile.
9. Places in that batch still follow 1–5.

## Gates

10. Unaccepted Work does not publish, except named ingest of approved sources.
11. `_raw/` place evidence stays in `_raw/`; ingest files copies rather than restyling the evidence set.
12. Legacy places are not rewritten solely to match this format.

## Out of contract

Session-prep preserve. Item, creature, and person ingest. Transcript wrapup. Distill path for non-place sources.

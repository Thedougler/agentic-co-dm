# Contract: Blind cold evaluation

The interface is a sample of D&D content. Evaluators did not produce it and do not see how it was made.

A file-shape check is not this contract. Author self-review is not this contract.

## Eval jobs

| # | Job | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Change to D&D content-producing guidance, sample produced | Two independent cold evals required before done |
| 2 | Same change, only a file-shape checker ran | Not done |
| 3 | Change to search/ingest plumbing, no D&D content produced | Eval not required |
| 4 | Evaluator saw the authoring transcript or skill diff | Void. Not cold |
| 5 | Author judges their own sample | Not this test |
| 6 | Two evals disagree on overall pass/fail | Not done |
| 7 | Sample with DM bands and spoken look | All four axes apply |
| 8 | Spoken-only handout (no mechanics, no DM procedure) | Spoken look applies. Form/mechanics and DM copy omitted, not failed |
| 9 | DM-only reference (no narration) | Form, use, DM copy apply. Spoken look omitted |
| 10 | Well-formed page, mechanic used as decoration | Fail appropriate use |
| 11 | DM bands are telegram or agent-speak | Fail DM copy |
| 12 | Narration leaks a DC or secret | Fail spoken look |
| 13 | Narration is drawable and speakable, leak-free | Pass spoken look |
| 14 | Heading order matches a template, page unusable | Fail (form without appropriate use or DM copy) |
| 15 | Ordinary wiki write, guidance unchanged | Eval not required |
| 16 | Legacy page left untouched | Pass SC-007 |

## Passage rules

1. Sample is the judged object, not the skill file.
2. Two independent cold evals. One is not enough.
3. Omit inapplicable axes. Do not fail them.
4. Fail any applicable axis → overall fail.
5. Load existing copy-writer, theatre of the mind, and vault form. Do not invent a parallel rubric.
6. Eval does not write canon. DM accept-gate still binds.
7. 010 writing/visual authorities still apply.

## Out of contract

Legacy rewrite. pytest as the pass. Scoring 1–5. Requiring all four axes on every sample. Judging non-D&D-content skills.

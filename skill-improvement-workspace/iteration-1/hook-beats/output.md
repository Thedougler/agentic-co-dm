# Between Predator and Prey

**Something happens.** The [[bloodhawk]], a massive aerial predator, begins a hunting dive toward a [[crown-squid]] fleeing across the open grassland where the party is traveling. The squid's panic-driven thrashing is accelerating across the terrain directly toward the party's position.

**Why it matters.** In moments, the party will be sandwiched between a desperate, powerful prey animal and a predator that will not stop its strike for anything in the way. They risk being trampled, crushed, or caught in a violent struggle between two creatures fighting for survival.

**Decision.** The party must commit to a response: protect themselves, take advantage of the chaos, try to intervene, or move out of the path entirely.

**Hook lands when.** The party states a clear action and the first creature (bloodhawk or squid) reacts to their choice.

> [!narration] Open on
> You're crossing open grassland when a high, piercing shriek splits the air—a sound like tearing metal mixed with a hunting cry. You feel the pressure of something massive cutting through the air above and behind you. Turning, you see it: a [[bloodhawk]] the size of a wagon, wings folded in a predator's stoop, diving at an angle that will bring it across your path within seconds. Below it, a [[crown-squid]] the size of a horse—tentacles flailing wildly, leaving a deep furrow in the grass—is bolting directly toward your position at panicked speed. The ground trembles with each convulsive leap of the squid's movement. You have perhaps ten seconds before the dive connects, thirty feet before the squid reaches the line where you're standing. The air carries the reek of predator musk and the metallic tang of blood.

## Situation

- **Where.** Open grassland, rolling slightly, few obstacles. The party has clear sight for a hundred yards in all directions. No natural cover except for one copse of low scrub trees maybe forty feet to the northwest. The grass is tall enough to tangle movement but not tall enough to hide.

- **Who.** 
  - [[bloodhawk]]: A territorial aerial predator that has been hunting this squid for the past few minutes. Its only goal is to take down prey; it will not be deterred by party presence. Attack bonus and fangs/talons are its only method.
  - [[crown-squid]]: A semi-intelligent creature with a strong self-preservation instinct. It is not aggressive toward the party unless cornered or directly attacked—it is only trying to escape the hawk's dive. It has no strategy; it runs blind with panic.

- **What changed.** The squid, fleeing inland from a water source three miles distant, entered the party's immediate area. The bloodhawk, which was already hunting it, adjusted its flight path to follow. The party is now incidentally in the collision course.

- **Pressure.** The dive will connect with the squid in approximately six to eight seconds. When it does, both creatures will be at close range, thrashing and violent. Anyone in the impact zone risks being crushed, gored by the hawk's talons, or wrapped in panic-driven squid tentacles. If the party does nothing, the squid will likely be taken, the hawk will feed, and the grassland will be left with a dead [[crown-squid]] and an agitated [[bloodhawk]] that may still see the party as secondary targets or threats.

- **Open question.** What does the party actually want to happen here? Do they care which creature lives? Can they exploit the chaos? Are they willing to stand and fight, or will they move?

## Decision handles

- **Intercept or shield.** The party can throw themselves into the dive's path—taking damage meant for the squid, blocking the hawk's approach, or placing a barrier between predator and prey. This is costly and gains them nothing materially, but it saves the squid and makes an enemy of the [[bloodhawk]] if it survives.

- **Attack the bloodhawk.** The party engages the predator directly, trying to drive it off or kill it before the dive lands. Success means saving the squid and gaining a conflict. Failure means the hawk takes its prey anyway and now the party is actively hostile.

- **Attack the crown-squid.** The party tries to kill or disable the squid before the hawk reaches it. This gives them a corpse, angers a large predatory creature, and raises a question about why they chose to kill something that was not attacking them.

- **Use terrain or positioning.** The party sprints for the copse of scrub trees to the northwest (forty feet away) and draws both creatures into pursuit or avoids them entirely. This requires speed and athleticism but creates distance.

- **Exploit the moment.** The party stands ready with weapons drawn and lets the creatures collide, then attacks whichever one is vulnerable or poses the greater threat in the aftermath. Clever positioning now can advantage them in the melee that follows.

- **Environment / leverage.** The grassland offers little cover but clear space to maneuver. The copse forty feet away (northwest) offers concealment but not protection from a determined predator. The creatures' own bodies and actions can be used as cover. The time pressure creates an advantage for those who act without hesitation.

## Action setup

- **Objective.** [[bloodhawk]] wants to take down its prey and feed. [[crown-squid]] wants to escape and survive. Party objectives are their own choice.

- **Opposition.** [[bloodhawk]] (1) with aerial advantage; [[crown-squid]] (1) with raw strength and reflexes. Neither is initially hostile to the party unless the party commits aggression first.

- **Terrain.** Open grassland with shallow rolling hills (no hard cover); copse of scrub trees forty feet to the northwest (concealment but not full cover); tall grass (waist height—moves at half speed through it). Clear sight lines in all directions beyond fifty yards.

- **Escalation.** If the dive connects and both creatures lock in combat, the struggle will be loud and violent. Blood sprays onto the grass. Other predators in the area (if this is an established hunt ground) may notice and approach. If a creature is killed, scavengers will appear within an hour.

- **End condition.** The action ends when one creature is dead, successfully escapes (hawk flies away sated or squid reaches water/escape cover and evades), or the party achieves their chosen objective and breaks engagement.

## Handoff

**Next.** Session-N-BB-Aftermath. **Development**

**Carry forward.** The party's choice determines what enters play next. If they saved the [[crown-squid]], it is now aware of them and may either flee entirely or investigate the group who intervened. If they killed one or both creatures, they now have bodies—meat, hide, and potentially parts with value or magical property. If they were struck in the violence, they carry wounds and the memory of predator strength. They also know now that this grassland is active hunting territory. If they did nothing and walked away, the meeting leaves them with the knowledge that the wild order here is active and sometimes dangerous—and the satisfied predator may mark them as non-threats or potential competitors depending on its disposition.

**Continuity change.** The grassland is no longer empty and safe; it is revealed as a place where predators hunt and the natural order moves with force. The party's reputation (if anyone was watching) is now set: did they appear as protectors, as pragmatists, or as bystanders? The [[crown-squid]], if alive, remembers their face or scent. The [[bloodhawk]], if alive and fed, recognizes this area as containing humans and has established a hierarchy of threat or curiosity. The terrain itself is marked by blood or the drag-path of combat.

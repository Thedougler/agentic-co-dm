# Crossing the Narrows: The Countless Hold the High Ground

**Trigger.** The party reaches [[tidefall-narrows]] during the daily spring tide window. They encounter [[the-passage]] fortifying a crossing point while [[countless]] scouts loose arrows from the high rocks above. The tide rises in minutes. The ford's moment of passability is closing.

**PC objective.** Cross [[tidefall-narrows]] without being swept into the deep channel or cut down in the water.

**Opposition objective.** [[Countless]]: deny [[the-passage]] control of the ford by driving them off the crossing stones before reinforcements arrive. [[The Passage]]: hold the ford until their relief column crests the ridge—control is more valuable than any single skirmish.

**Stakes.** The party's time to cross safely. The integrity of either faction's position (success or failure shapes what lies ahead on the road). Loss of equipment in the current. Injury or capture before reaching the far bank.

**Ends when.** The party reaches the far bank and exits the water zone, or one faction secures enough territory that the other retreats, or the tide rises enough to make crossing suicidal and both groups cease engagement.

> [!narration] Open on Action
> Water churns below you, white-capped and violent—the spring tide is coming fast, turning the channel into a vicious ribbon of current. Ahead, three rust-colored stones jut from the narrows like teeth; [[the-passage]] militants hold the middle stone, shields braced against [[countless]] arrows that whistle down from the cliff above. One shaft streaks low and *thunk*—buries itself in a stone at your feet. The defenders don't look up; they're focused on the rocks above. The first ford-stone is ten paces away. The water is waist-deep and getting uglier by the minute. Behind you, a horn call echoes from the ridge—someone's coming. You smell wet stone, iron, and fear-sweat.

### Opposition

**[[Countless]] (High Rocks)**
- **Intent.** Deny [[the-passage]] the ford crossing before their relief arrives. Control the approach is secondary; breaking the ford garrison is primary.
- **Opening move.** Arrows on any approaching figure (party included, indiscriminately). No coordination with [[the-passage]]—their arrows are warning and pressure, not mercy.
- **Behavior.** Archers take positions that maximize cover on the rocks; they shift to maintain line of sight on moving targets. If a climber reaches their position, they use bows as clubs and retreat upward. They do not abandon the high ground easily—it is their advantage.
- **Breaking point.** Loss of 3 archers or sight of [[the-passage]] reinforcements cresting the ridge prompts retreat northward (lose control of the ford, but save the band).
- **Resources.** 5–7 archers (light armor, shortbows, daggers); high ground vantage; natural rock cover; knowledge of routes up/down the ridge.

**[[The Passage]] (Ford Stones)**
- **Intent.** Hold the crossing until relief arrives. Reinforce or relieve the position—do not cede the ford voluntarily.
- **Opening move.** Shields up; no offensive action unless attacked. Defensive posture: protect the party crossing is not their job, but collateral casualties don't stop them.
- **Behavior.** Trained and disciplined. They form a defensive line and hold formation. They will not break rank to chase [[countless]] archers or pursue fleeing enemies. They accept risk to their line if it preserves the crossing.
- **Breaking point.** Losses to 2 or fewer members or confirmed arrival of [[countless]] reinforcements (instead of [[the-passage]]'s relief) triggers orderly withdrawal or defensive repositioning.
- **Resources.** 4–6 militants (medium armor, swords/spears, shields); ford stones (defensible, limited mobility); reinforcement column incoming (4–6 rounds, audible horn call).

### Pressure

#### Escalation Toolkit

**Round 1–2: Archery and Awareness**
Telegraph: [[Countless]] arrows intensify; [[the-passage]] militia tense and ready defensive formation. Note the rising water—characters feel it rising against their legs if they move to the ford.

**Round 3: Tidal Acceleration + Arrow Pressure**
Escalate: Water depth increases faster. Any character in the ford zone must make a DC 12 Strength save or take the pushed-10-ft consequence. An arrow finds a character (telegraphed by prior near-misses).

**Round 4: Reinforcement Signal + Turning Tide**
Telegraph: Horn call from the ridge. [[The passage]] visibly ready for support. Water is chest-deep at the ford stones; crossing becomes a major Athletics/Acrobatics check. [[Countless]] archers shift fire to defensive suppression—fewer arrows, more precise.

**Round 5+: Desperation Window**
Breaking point: [[The passage]] reinforcements crest the ridge (visible dust, banner, marching column in the distance). [[Countless]] must commit to withdrawal or make a last stand. Party's window to cross safely is narrowing rapidly—if not across by Round 6, tide blocks safe passage and forces a climb-out upstream or downstream.

#### Opposition Ladder (Optional)
- **Grunts** (3:1 weaker): Not applicable; [[countless]] archers are disciplined scouts, not militia.
- **Minions** (2:1 slightly weaker): [[Countless]] archers without high ground advantage or with reduced numbers (3–4 archers after losses).
- **Henchmen** (equal numbers, equal skill): [[Countless]] at full strength; [[the-passage]] reinforcements add numbers.
- **Villain** (substantially stronger + support): If a named [[countless]] commander or [[the-passage]] captain engages directly, they are stronger and more skilled (save for Climax proper).

### Battlefield: Tidefall Narrows

**Space.** Three crossing stones in a line across the narrows (spaced 8–10 feet apart). The ford-safe zone is roughly 40 feet wide. Upstream: rocky shallows (waist-deep, slow current, safe Athletics). Downstream: deepening channel (chest-deep, strong current, perilous). High rocks flank both north and south banks; the north rocks (where [[countless]] positions) are 25–30 feet above the water.

**Hazard.** Tidal current (worsens each round). Depth increases: Round 1 = waist-deep (movement full speed), Round 2–3 = chest-deep (movement half, Athletics DC 12 or pushed), Round 4+ = neck-deep (movement quarter speed, Athletics DC 14 or swept downstream). Water temperature is numbing (CON save DC 10 per round or exhaustion level gain, max 3). Any character swept downstream: 2–3 rounds of struggling to return or finding an exit.

**Interactive Features:**
- **Ford Stones.** Three rust-stained boulders, roughly 6 feet across each. Slippery from mist and algae. Acrobatics DC 11 to move across without sliding; failure = slide off into chest-deep water (triggers current check). Characters can stand on stones for cover but have no meaningful sight advantage over the narrows (mist obscures detail 20+ feet away).
- **High Rocks (North Bank).** Fifteen feet of scrambling ascent from bank to [[countless]] archer positions. Climbing DC 13 (rough stone, slippery from spray). Archers have 180-degree sight lines but cannot see directly below their rock shelf (party climbing to meet them gains advantage on final 5 feet).
- **Mist & Spray.** Spring tide creates heavy mist over the narrows; visibility beyond 30 feet is obscured. Advantage on Stealth checks in the mist; disadvantage on ranged attack rolls beyond 30 feet (arrows are indiscriminate but scattered).
- **Debris & Swept Objects.** Logs, branches, and water-worn stones churn in the current. No immediate hazard, but describe them to signal that the current is serious and deadly.

**Change.** One believable evolution: if [[the-passage]] reinforcements arrive before party crosses, they form a secondary crossing point south of the main ford stones, drawing [[countless]] fire and creating a temporary safe lane. [[Countless]] may pull archers to cover both crossing points, reducing pressure on the main ford (but widening the engagement).

## Discoveries

- **The horn signal's meaning.** Archers near a messenger quiver may drop a message tube on death/defeat; it contains a 4–6 round timeline estimate and the column strength ("Relief arriving, twelve strong" or "Reinforcements: mounted vanguard + foot").
- **[[Countless]]'s objective.** Through overheard shouts or questioning captured archers: they believe [[the-passage]] is laying supply caches at the ford, preparing for a push deeper into territory [[countless]] claims.
- **Safe upstream crossing.** An observant character (DC 11 Perception or Survival check) notices that shallower water extends 200 yards upstream where the riverbed widens; crossing there adds 15–20 minutes but is safer (no current hazard, exposed to arrows if [[countless]] has scouts there, but lower pressure).

## Resolution

| Outcome                 | What changes                                                                                 |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Objective gained**    | Party crosses safely before tide blocks passage. [[The passage]] holds the ford (if assisted by party momentum or ally). [[Countless]] withdraws, having failed to deny the crossing. Carry-forward: party is known to [[the-passage]] (recognized ally or neutral bystander?); road ahead is open and occupied by [[the-passage]] checkpoint.  |
| **Costly success**      | Party crosses but loses 1+ member (swept downstream, requiring rescue later) or leaves behind equipment in the current. One faction gains slight advantage but neither decisively wins (horn signal arrives as stalemate sets in). Carry-forward: party carries water-logged supplies; they are indebted to [[the-passage]] if rescued; the crossing is contested and tense for follow-up encounters.  |
| **Withdrawal/defeat**   | Party fails to cross before tide blocks passage (chest-to-neck depth makes Athletics DC 14+, anyone fails is swept). Party forced to retreat to north bank (if arrived from north) or take the dangerous upstream route. [[Countless]] or [[the-passage]] loses positioning but both survive. Carry-forward: party is stranded, must find alternate route (adds time, changes geography), and may encounter patrols or pursuit from the losing faction.  |
| **Unexpected solution** | Party negotiates with one faction, offers aid (e.g., climbs rocks to flank [[countless]], weakening archers; or stands as temporary shield-wall anchor for [[the-passage]]), or finds an obscure route (old mine, narrow ledge, submerged stepping path). Outcome depends on what party actually accomplished. Carry-forward: party is now politically entangled with the faction they aided or opposed; reputation shifts for later encounters.  |

**Changed state.** [[Tidefall-narrows]] crossing is no longer neutral ground. [[The passage]] or [[countless]] now controls passage or claims it strategically disputed. Party is visible to both factions as either ally, obstacle, or irrelevant. The road ahead is open but watched. Reinforcements or scouts may delay the party if they harmed the faction that "won."

**Immediate question.** What are the party's intentions now that they've crossed? Do they head toward the territory [[the-passage]] or [[countless]] claims? Will they be intercepted by one faction for a follow-up negotiation or threat? Are there wounded, drowned, or captured allies to recover?

**Next Development.** {{Session-<n>-BB-Name}} (Development beat: investigate the factions' deeper conflict, negotiate safe passage, or prepare for pursuit/alliance consequences).

**If the session ends here.** End on one concrete image: the party standing on the far bank, breathing hard, turning to look back at the narrows where the two forces are still circling—water rising, horn calls still echoing, the contested crossing disappearing into mist behind them. Their road ahead is watched.

## References

- [[Tidefall-narrows]]. Tidal crossing point; spring tides block passage annually. Neutral ground claimed by [[countless]] (northern approach) and [[the-passage]] (southern approach). Strategic chokepoint for regional trade and military movement.
- [[Countless]]. Decentralized faction controlling highlands and rough terrain. Favor archery, guerrilla tactics, and territorial denial. Wary of organized armies; use terrain advantage instead.
- [[The-passage]]. Organized militia faction securing trade routes and crossings. Favor formation tactics, coordinated reinforcements, and holding ground. Goal is control and stability through military presence.

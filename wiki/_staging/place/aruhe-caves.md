# Aruhe Caves

## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> The hole under the roots is not a burrow. Black stone curves down in a tube wide enough for three abreast — smooth in long runs, savagely folded where the flow once froze. Pale root curtains punch the ceiling and drip. Thirty feet in, daylight is a grey disc. Fifty feet in, it is gone. The floor keeps sloping downhill. Mud shows prints larger than yours. Far ahead, something strikes stone once, again, again — the second impact reaches the feet before the ears.

---
## At a Glance
Aruhe Caves are the island's underside: extinct lava tubes cutting through several surface bands at once, the same kind of artery [[the-river]] is on the surface. They are not a sixth concentric band and do not replace the surface route to [[memorial-grove]] — there is no secret cave door into the Grove. Entrances open under [[old-gardens]] (terrace collapses), [[the-quiet]] (root wells and skylights), the Rot / marsh systems (deep roots through basalt), and flooded tubes near [[clear-lake]]. Related surface passages also live on [[lava-tubes]]. Stay on the surface to keep horizon and forage; drop in for a faster, darker shortcut with worse individual encounters and uncertain exits.

## If the party

- **Read flow direction after studying several patterns:** **Intelligence (Nature) or Wisdom (Survival) — `DC 14`** → Down-tube often trends coastward; up-tube often trends inland (often, not always).
- **Navigate with marks, airflow, and elevation:** **Wisdom (Survival) — `DC 13`** basic use; **`DC 15`** animal traffic and likely surface link; **`DC 18`** reconstruct original flow and predict rejoins.
- **Locate a major sound through stone:** **Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 14`** → Approximate direction; parallel tubes can lie about where the legs are.
- **Spot Dead Air before long exposure:** **Intelligence (Nature) or Wisdom (Survival) — `DC 14`**. Prolonged exposure: **Constitution — `DC 14`**.
- **Test Drumstone before weight:** **Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 15`**. Collapse: **Dexterity — `DC 14`**, fall 20–40 feet into a lower passage, prone in rubble.
- **Choose a Squeeze crawlway:** Miserable movement; [[blackrail]] cannot follow. Wide tubes: easy movement; the rail can.
- **Ride out a Flood Pulse in a low channel:** **Strength — `DC 15`**. Swept into a pocket or root grate → **Restrained**, escape **Athletics or Acrobatics — `DC 14`**.
- **Climb a Root Well toward daylight:** **Strength (Athletics) — `DC 14`** with the roots (Daylight Hole). **Wisdom (Survival) — `DC 13`** at a well → recent insect feeding versus larger predator traffic.
- **Take living cave life (insects, eggs, trapped beasts):** Same taking rule as the surface — observation free; removing something living fires [[taking-on-aruhe]]. Ordinary stream and spring water here does not use a special water-taking exception beyond established island rules.

## Who

- Apex pressure: [[blackrail]] — giant cave centipede that treats a tunnel as a kill corridor. Juveniles signal the adult is elsewhere; adults are violently cannibalistic toward unrelated young.
- Lesser signs: cave bats, root roaches (hand-sized, flee cracks seconds before a rail arrives), glass crickets. A bat colony taking flight is a warning.
- Surface fauna such as wolfrabbits refuse dens past the last daylight.

---
## What

- Dozens to hundreds of old flow systems: some a few hundred feet, some miles; collapses sometimes join generations. Known play space is the interconnected western/central system.
- Common branch size: 10–30 feet wide, 6–20 feet high; chambers 100+ feet; crawlways under 3 feet; vertical shafts linking tube levels.
- Hazards: Dead Air, Drumstone false floors, Squeeze tradeoffs, Flood Pulse, Root Wells (feeding grounds and climb exits).
- Landmarks from sources: Great Bore (signature long run / oncoming-rail fight), Daylight Hole (root climb to surface), Drowned Tube (flooded low reach), Old Mouth (daylight threshold).
- Light is a temporary bubble; routes behind the party can collapse, flood, or fill with predator.

---
## Where

- Under [[aruhe]]; links conceptually with [[lava-tubes]] without replacing [[memorial-grove]] as the last surface room.
- Skylights hide under centuries of growth; exits may be known on the map and still unreachable.
- Compared with surface travel: cave legs are faster with almost no forage, fewer encounters, worse single fights, separation risk, and uncertain exits.

---
## Why

- Offer dangerous shortcuts between surface bands without invalidating Aruhe's established geography.
- Put the fear of a single tunnel with no exit on the table — and the choice to extinguish light, mark stone, continue downhill, or turn around.

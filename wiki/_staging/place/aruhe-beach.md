# Aruhe Beach

## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> Half a mile out, a jagged coral reef shows its teeth at low tide. One boat gap opens onto a short shingle beach that takes only minutes to cross. Surf works the coral; salt spray rides loose stone. Elsewhere the coast is cliff, cove, or strand trapped behind reef. At the inland wet-leaf edge, pale fruit hangs in shade and heavier guavas weight the green above a stream.

---
## At a Glance
Aruhe Beach is the shore-facing name for the island's known western landing. Table procedures, tide checks, and retreat timing live on [[western-landing]] (already aliases Western Landing / Aruhe Beach). This page exists so archive "Beach" cites and Hungry Isle cross-links have a kebab place owner without competing with Sorn or Veth beaches. Commit through the gap for the island; observe from offshore to skip the shingle. Southern mangrove wall is not a harbor.

## If the party

- **Use the known approach:** Resolve landing, tide, and gap checks on [[western-landing]].
- **Name the shore in speech or log:** Prefer [[western-landing]] for the playable site; [[aruhe-beach]] for the generic western strand identity.
- **Confuse with other campaign beaches:** [[sorns-landing]], Veth west beach, and similar are different places — do not merge.

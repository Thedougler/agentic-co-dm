# Session 12 — The Dead Keep Orders

> [!abstract] Development — the bump
> **Purpose.** Turn the pursuit's route into a history the party can inspect and use.
>
> **Trigger.** The party follows the inland trail from [[river-slack-basin]], whether after chasing [[talon-skarn]] or after securing the Spinner and choosing to move.
>
> **Turn.** Different ages of bodies, seal fragments, and scraps show that Gold-caste authority kept sending lower-caste Grung inland after earlier groups disappeared.
>
> **Exit.** The party has a reason to examine the dead trail, follow it toward the mass grave, or question the survivors instead of treating the bodies as ordinary Aruhe danger.
>
> **Target.** About thirty minutes.

> [!narration] Initial Narration
> The grass closes over a narrow line of bones and rust-dark metal. Some bodies have sunk almost completely into the soil; others still wear belts, shell plates, or torn packs. A dull gold mark repeats beside them at different heights, and wet paper clings to one broken seal as if the island has tried to read it and stopped.

## Run the Beat

1. **Present the situation.** The dead lie along the route rather than in one clean grave. The evidence is safe to examine from the grass, but the party must decide whether to handle the bodies, the seals, or the scraps.
2. **Let them engage.** [[celia-parel|Celia]] can sort the wet fragments if she is present. [[oren-vask|Oren]] can identify the route markers and explain which edges he will not cross. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] can recognize the Grung authority markings if the party asks him or shows him the carried seal.
3. **Make the turn.** The oldest readable line orders an inland report. A later line accounts for a replacement after silence. The newest fragment is more urgent and points toward two graves.
4. **Hand back the choice.** The party can follow the route, stop to preserve evidence, question the followers, or leave the dead and take a different path toward the same inland pressure.

## Checks & Costs

- **Automatic.** Examining the visible seals shows that the same authority mark appears beside bodies from different periods.
- **Intelligence (Investigation) — `DC 13`.** **Success:** Sort the scraps into reporting, replacement, and two-grave layers. **Failure:** Find the same layers through Celia, Jean-Claude, or the mass grave; the cost is lost time and a stronger sign of Skarn's continuing movement.
- **Intelligence (History) or Wisdom (Insight) — `DC 14`.** **Success:** Recognize that “replacement” makes the missing Grung expendable in the order's logic. **Failure:** The party still knows the sequence but must decide what it means without that interpretation.

## Player Levers

- **Person.** [[celia-parel|Celia]] can be persuaded to share the complete scrap bundle if the party promises to leave both memorial graves undisturbed.
- **Person.** [[oren-vask|Oren]] can lead the party toward the valley or back toward the survivor camp.
- **Thing.** [[grung-authority-seal]] can be compared with the dead seals without activating it; its existing Investigation result remains the mechanical owner.
- **Promise / problem.** The party can choose to preserve a record, pursue Skarn, or prioritize bringing the living home.

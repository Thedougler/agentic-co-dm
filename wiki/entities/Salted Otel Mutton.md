# Salted Otel Mutton

> [!narration] Narration
> A wax-wrapped bundle of salted mutton, cured dark and stiff, heavier in the hand than it looks. Salt crusts every fold of it. Unwrap a corner and the smell that rises is clean and faintly sweet, not the sourness the dark color suggests. Marta cuts it fresh off the same barrel she cut yesterday's from, and the barrel never seems to sit any lower.

*Trade Good (Food), Mundane.* Ordinary salted meat by taste and texture, sold by the wedge at [[Anvez's Provisions]]. No magical property, but the supply itself is the tell. Whatever amount moves off the shelf, the barrel is full again by the next visit, sourced from [[Otel Karn]]'s pastured herds, which keep grazing after the knife the same way they keep supplying Marta's counter.

## Provenance

Cut from [[Otel Karn]]'s pastured herds, livestock that keeps grazing, skinned and deboned, after the butcher's knife has already done its work. [[Marta Anvez]] has sold it from the same barrel longer than she can rightly account for, and never offers the explanation to a buyer who doesn't ask.

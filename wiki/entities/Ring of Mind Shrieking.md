## Mechanics

**Drowned Mind [RAW+HB, Ring of Mind Shielding].** While wearing this ring, the wearer is immune to magic that allows other creatures to read their thoughts, determine whether they're lying, know their alignment, or know their creature type.

Creatures can telepathically communicate with the wearer only if the wearer allows it. If the wearer dies while wearing the ring, their soul enters it, unless it already houses a soul, and they can remain in the ring or leave for the afterlife.

The immunity holds, but for the wrong reason. The ring floods the wearer's mind with the surface thoughts of everyone nearby the instant it's worn, layered and constant.

There's simply too much noise in there for any mind-reading magic, or for the wearer's own concentration, to get a clean read.

While worn, the wearer has disadvantage on Concentration saving throws and on Wisdom (Perception) checks, with no save to resist either. Anyone watching can tell something's wrong. The wearer visibly zones out, glassy-eyed, for a second at a time, at random.

- Edge case: the wearer has no way to control or filter which thoughts they hear. It gains nothing from them, just noise.
- Edge case: the disadvantage lasts the entire time the wearer has the ring on, not only at the moment of a triggering event. It never has a quiet moment.

**Limitations.** The ring grants no offensive or defensive combat bonus of any kind. The Concentration and Perception disadvantage is a pure cost, not offset by anything.

It doesn't let the wearer choose whose thoughts they hear or extract anything useful from the noise. The telepathic-communication control and soul-jar effects work exactly as RAW. Only the immunity's cause and the Concentration/Perception cost are homebrew.

## Provenance

Sold from [[Lavinia Sordi]]'s rack at [[La Cenere]] in [[Le Paludi]], [[Calveno]]. It's listed at 50 gp, down from an original 200 gp asking price.

[[Delmar Fisk]] tried it on at the table in Session 8 without paying. He was immediately flooded with the surface thoughts of everyone nearby. He zoned out for a moment, then handed it straight back. Never purchased.

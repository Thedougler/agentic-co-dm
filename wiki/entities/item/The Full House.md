# The Full House

> [!narration] Narration
> A brass crank on a squat mahogany stand, the whole of it small enough to carry under one arm and heavy enough that both arms would be sensible.
>
> The handle is polished down to bare metal on its grip and nowhere else. Inside the stand something turns when the crank turns, and it keeps turning after the hand stops.
>
> The wood smells of old varnish and pipe smoke, and a low sound sits inside it, thousands of people a long way off, all of them waiting.
>
> A booking tag hangs off the handle on green thread, and the name inked on it is fresh.


_Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)._

Sold to nobody yet. It stands in the locked case at the back of the shop, above the ladder's top rung, and [[Fioravante Selmerin|Fio]] takes bookings on it and will not take an offer.

Rare tier holds. [[Magic Item Rarity]] puts Rare goods in an approximate 500-5,000 gp band, and 1,500 gp sits low inside it beside [[La Cenere]]'s [[Necklace of Fireballs]] at the same price. The effect is _[[fear]]_ cast at 3rd level, traded out of a 30-foot cone into a 60-foot sphere and out of the caster's control into a fixed round of duration, once a day, on a device anyone attuned can work.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **The Full House [HB].** Setting the stand down and turning the crank once takes an action, and the device does this once per day, recovering at dawn. It must rest on a solid surface to work.
>
> The roar of a packed house fills a 60-foot-radius sphere centred on the stand. Every creature in that sphere hostile to the attuned owner makes a DC 15 [[Wisdom]] saving throw. On a failure a creature is frightened of the stand for 1 minute. A frightened creature must move away from the stand on its turn by the safest route available, and repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
>
> **Edge cases:** the sound carries a further 300 feet as ordinary noise, and anyone in that outer band hears an audience where there is no theatre. A creature that cannot hear is unaffected. The crank turns whether or not the owner is attuned, and unattuned it makes noise and nothing else. Creatures immune to the frightened condition ignore the effect entirely and still hear it.
>
> **Limitations:** the applause deals no damage, breaks no concentration on its own, and moves nobody the frightened rules do not already move. It cannot be aimed, so it takes in allies' enemies and enemies' enemies alike and never distinguishes among them beyond hostility to the owner. It grants no bonus to any roll and offers no cover, and the noise makes stealth in that sphere impossible for the rest of the minute.

## Provenance

[[Fioravante Selmerin]] built one, upstairs, over a season, and has not built a second. He says the sound in it is a real house on a real closing night and declines every follow-up question by asking after your tea.

The name on the booking tag is renewed in fresh ink every few weeks. [[Serena Cottle]] writes it and will not say whose it is, and [[Tam Slim-Nail]] has never seen anyone come to the counter to renew it.

# Delmar's Cloak of the Manta Ray

> [!narration] Delmar's Cloak of the Manta Ray
> A long red admiral's coat, salt-worn and well-kept, with a deep hood that falls forward when raised. The lining is smooth and dark, and the coat moves in water the way cloth shouldn't.

## At a Glance

*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement).*

The cloak is a Fisk family heirloom that turns its wearer into something that belongs in the water — and it has already carried two people to the surface when a ship went down.

- **Effect.** Swimming speed 60 feet, underwater breathing, and a manta ray form, for as long as the hood stays up.
- **Attunement.** Required.
- **Held by.** [[delmar-fisk]], who wore it the day [[umberlee|Umberlee]] sank his fleet.

## Properties

**Hood up.** While wearing the cloak with the hood raised, the wearer has a swimming speed of 60 feet, can breathe underwater, and takes a manta ray form. The benefits end when the hood comes down.

**Buoyancy in practice.** Worn in the water, the coat floated [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] and [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] to the surface once when the walls closed on them, which is how both of them survived a sinking that took a fleet.

**Limits.** The advantages apply only with the hood up; a hooded wearer who wants to pass as a person, or fight normally above water, is wearing a coat over their face.

## At the Table

- **Cross water silently.** Sixty feet of swim speed and no need to breathe turns a wreck, a reef gap, or a flooded hold into ordinary ground.
- **Survive a sinking.** Raising the hood is the difference between drowning and swimming out, and the coat's buoyancy has already been used to carry two people at once.
- **Fight in the water.** The manta form moves the wearer like an animal, which is an advantage in a flooded corridor and a liability in a boat.
- **Keep the hood down in company.** It is an admiral's coat until the hood goes up, and the coat is recognisable as a Fisk heirloom to anyone who knows the family.

# Watchman's Toll-Bead

> [!narration] Watchman's Toll-Bead
> A brass bead the size of a knuckle, threaded on a stub of tarred rope, worn flat on one side where a hand pulled it a thousand times. Held still, it warms. It sounds a half-beat before any bell you can hear does, one thin note against a palm, and stops before the bell finishes. The rope end is cut clean, and the cut is fresh.

## At a Glance

*Wondrous item, uncommon.*

The Watchman's Toll-Bead buys its bearer the half-beat that decides who moves first, once between rests.

- **Effect.** Advantage on Initiative rolls, once per Long Rest.
- **Source.** Cut from the pull-rope of a watch bell that has tolled a real alarm, by [[vashka-doru]].
- **Limit.** The note reaches only the hand holding it, so it warns nobody else.

## Properties

**Early Toll (1/Long Rest).** While you carry the bead, you can roll Initiative with Advantage. Once used, the bead stays silent until you finish a Long Rest.

**Edge cases.** The bead must be on your person and not stowed in a closed container. It works while you are surprised, and it does not end the Surprised condition.

**Limitations.** It grants nothing to attack rolls, damage, AC, or any saving throw. It cannot be spent on an Initiative already rolled, and its note carries only to the hand holding it, so it warns nobody else in the party.

## At the Table

- **Start the fight before it starts.** Advantage on Initiative is the difference between acting first and taking the ambush, which matters most in exactly the fights where the party is surprised.
- **Keep it in a pocket, not a pack.** A bead in a closed container is a bead that does not work. It lives on a cord, a wrist, or in a hand.
- **Spend it and count the day.** One use, then silence until the next Long Rest. A party that has already used it is choosing fights without it.
- **Try to warn the crew.** The bead's note reaches the hand that holds it and stops there. Anyone expecting a shared signal will wait for a bell that never rings.

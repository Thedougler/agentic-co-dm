# Wild Pearl of Power

> [!narration] Wild Pearl of Power
> The pearl is the size of a thumbnail. Light crawls across it in a color that will not settle. Turn it and the shine lags half a breath behind the motion. It is too warm for a stone. Hold it long enough and a faint hum sits against the skin, then cuts the instant you notice, as if something inside stopped to listen.

## At a Glance

*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement by a spellcaster).*

Trade a charge for a spell slot and a Wild Magic Surge comes with it, every time.

- **Charges.** 3, regaining `1d3` expended charges daily at dawn.
- **Effect.** As an Action, spend 1 charge to regain one expended spell slot of 3rd level or lower.
- **Surge.** Every charge spent this way triggers a full Wild Magic Surge: after the slot returns, roll `d100` on the Sorcerer Wild Magic Surge table and apply the result as written. No roll decides whether the surge happens.
- **Current state.** [[catarina-davirelli]] keeps it in her Calveno workshop with two charges remaining.

## Properties

**Returning a slot.** The pearl holds 3 charges and regains `1d3` expended charges daily at dawn. As an Action, spend 1 charge to regain one expended spell slot of 3rd level or lower.

**A surge, always.** Every charge spent this way also triggers a full Wild Magic Surge: immediately after the slot returns, roll on the Sorcerer Wild Magic Surge table (`d100`) and apply the result as written. There is no roll to see whether the surge happens.

**Whose stats the surge uses.** A surge that spends a slot, asks for Concentration, or calls for a spellcasting ability uses the wielder's.

**Limits.** The pearl cannot restore a slot above 3rd level. One surge per charge, never a second. You cannot take the slot and decline the surge.

**Bought second-hand.** [[lavinia-sordi]] sold it off the rack at [[la-cenere]] in Session 8 for 300 gp after an ask of 350 gp. [[catarina-davirelli]] identified it as unstable, with a loosened weave; the exact surge rule landed later.

## At the Table

- **She bought a slot back.** You roll the `d100` in front of the table and live with it.
- **Two charges left.** The next spend is a guaranteed surge; the one after that is the last unless it regains charges at dawn.
- **The price is on the page.** 300 gp, Session 8, off [[la-cenere]]'s rack — the party can trace where it came from if they ask.
- **Handle it before handing it out.** Any spellcaster who attunes to it accepts the surge on every charge; say that out loud before someone spends one.

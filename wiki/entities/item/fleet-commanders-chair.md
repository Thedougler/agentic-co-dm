# Fleet Commanders Chair

> [!narration] Narration
> The chair is a heavy, broad, high-backed armchair built with the solid weight of a commander's seat. Deep red leather covers the seat and back, worn soft at the armrests, and its legs are turned from a wood too dark and too heavy for a chair of its size. It smells of pipe smoke and old oil, and the leather at the armrest gives a fraction more than good leather should.

*Wondrous item, rarity not established.*

**Seated Authority.** While a creature sits in the chair, it has Advantage on Charisma saving throws and Charisma checks. The benefit ends when it stands up or leaves the chair.

**Unbreakable.** The chair can't be broken. It has no other property.

## At the Table

The chair stands aft aboard the *[[Uncertainty]]*, in the former captain's cabin. Anyone who sits in it sits straighter, and the effect is plain to everyone watching. It is heavy enough that moving it took a borrowed Bag of Holding. The one sign that it is more than furniture is the armrest leather, which gives a little too much under a hand, most noticeably in the moment before the mimic acts.

## Hidden Properties

The chair is the [[elder-mimic]] holding its object form, and it can keep this form indefinitely. Its Perfect False Appearance makes an *Identify* spell report the chair and its Seated Authority property and nothing else, and Seated Authority works normally for whoever sits in it. The chair itself is not sentient and never speaks to or acts on its occupant; the mimic inside it is the creature with a will, and it wants to stay undiscovered and be carried somewhere safer by someone else's labour. Anyone who touches it while the mimic is undetected can be stuck fast by its Adhesive trait and bitten; resolve the mimic's statistics, wants, and morale on [[elder-mimic]].

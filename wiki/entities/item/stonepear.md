## At a Glance

*Consumable, rare*

A stonepear turns its eater's skin hard against every kind of harm for one short fight.

- **Found in.** [[the-quiet]], the terraces of [[old-gardens]], and hanging from the great vine at [[the-pantry]]. The party saw its first at [[river-slack-basin]] in Session 11 and did not eat it.
- **Wanted by.** [[auralis|Auralis]], who tells [[perrin-black-jaw]] to eat one in Session 12.

## Properties

**Eat.** Eating a stonepear takes a Bonus Action. The eater has Resistance to all damage for 1 minute.

**Picking.** A stonepear picked from its stem is a claim under [[taking-on-aruhe]]. One that has fallen is safe to take, and keeps until eaten.

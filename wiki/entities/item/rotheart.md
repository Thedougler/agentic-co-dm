# Aruhe - Rotheart

> [!narration] Aruhe - Rotheart
> Black-hearted fruit hangs on the sickest trees of the Rot, warm and faintly pulsing in the hand. The pulp is the colour of old blood, and heat moves under the dark skin with a beat slow enough to count. The fruit is heavier than its size, and it keeps its warmth after it is picked.

## At a Glance

*Consumable, very rare.*

The Rotheart is [[aruhe]]'s cure for a body already past saving: one fruit strips exhaustion, poison, and disease, then leaves the eater resting better than they ever have.

- **Effect.** Removes all levels of Exhaustion, ends a poison or disease, and makes Hit Dice heal their maximum for 24 hours.
- **Where it grows.** Only in the inner Rot, on the sickest trees, and never as a routine harvest.
- **Cost of taking one.** Plucking living fruit draws notice from local life; the island is not a hive mind and does not run on a timer.

## Properties

**Eat (Bonus Action).** As a Bonus Action, eat one fruit. Your Exhaustion levels are all removed. Any poison or disease affecting you ends. For 24 hours, Hit Dice you spend heal their maximum value.

**One fruit, one use.** The Rotheart is eaten whole; nothing is left to store or split.

**Where it grows.** Only the inner Rot produces it. It hangs on the sickest trees, and picking it is not a routine harvest for anyone living on Aruhe.

**Taking it is noticed.** Plucking living fruit draws a response from the island's local life, and the DM decides what form that takes. Local life is not a hive mind, and the island does not run on a timer: passing through, watching, or drinking spring water does not trigger another response.

**Identifying it.** One minute of study identifies the fruit as purging and restorative: **Intelligence (Nature) — `DC 13`** or **Wisdom (Medicine) — `DC 13`**. A `DC 15` check, or the *Identify* spell, reveals the exact effect. The fruit is beneficial; it is not a secret poison.

## At the Table

- **Eat it after a bad fight.** One Bonus Action undoes exhaustion and poison at once, which turns a dragged-out day back into a functional one.
- **Hold it for the night.** The 24-hour Hit Dice clause is worth waiting for: a party that eats it before a long rest gets a full day of maximum healing.
- **Pick it in front of something.** The fruit is living growth in the Rot, and taking it is what draws attention. A party that watches the trees for a while before reaching can choose the moment.

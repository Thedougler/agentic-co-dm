# Zort's Toe-Claws

> [!narration] Zort's Toe-Claws
> A pair of open sandals, the leather worn black at the heel and stiff as board everywhere else. Each toe sits in its own socket, and filed iron caps every socket in a hooked point. They weigh more than they look, and one set down on bare dirt does not slide. The straps are short, sized for a foot that does a hand's work.

## At a Glance

*Tool (footwear), common.*

Zort's Toe-Claws are handling sandals for anyone whose work involves a beast digging in its heels: they grip the ground and grip a cage bar.

- **Effect.** Advantage on checks and saves against being grappled, shoved, or knocked prone while standing on bare feet.
- **Weight.** 4 pounds.
- **Sold by.** [[zort]] at [[zorts-pits|Zort's Pits]] in [[le-paludi]], cut to his own pattern by a local cobbler. Larger sizes take a week.

## Properties

**Set.** While you wear the toe-claws on bare feet and stand on dirt, wood, stone, or deck, you have Advantage on ability checks and saving throws made to resist being grappled, shoved, or knocked prone.

**Hook and Climb.** The iron points take a bite on rough vertical surfaces. You climb at full speed on wood, rope, netting, and cage bar, without a check on any surface a ladder could serve.

**Limitations.** Bare feet only, so a wearer keeps their boots off while wearing them. A wearer standing in a boat's bilge, deep mud, loose sand, or ice gets neither benefit, and nothing here answers an effect that moves you without touching you.

## At the Table

- **Hold ground against something big.** Advantage on the checks and saves that keep a character upright and unheld, which is the whole point of a sandal with iron in it.
- **Work the cages.** The points climb wood, rope, netting, and cage bar at full speed, so the sandals do the work of a ladder and leave both hands free.
- **Mind where you stand.** Bilge water, mud, sand, and ice turn both benefits off, and a party that walks into the marsh wearing these has bought four pounds of nothing.
- **Get fitted.** Zort fits every buyer himself, and a larger size takes a week — which matters to anyone whose feet are bigger than a goblin's.

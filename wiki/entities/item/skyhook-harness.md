# Skyhook Harness

> [!narration] Skyhook Harness
> A rig of belts wide enough to go round a horse, hanging from a beam with its buckles level above head height. Every strap is doubled and cross-stitched in copper thread, and the chest plate is cork faced with hide, shaped to a breastbone that is not human. Two iron hooks hang at the shoulders on swivels: turn one and it turns silently, which nothing that size should do. The stirrups are set for someone small, and they have been shortened once already.

## At a Glance

*Wondrous item, rare.*

The Skyhook Harness is flight tack: it lets a trained flying beast carry a rider without feeling the weight, for anyone who can make the beast accept it.

- **Effect.** A Medium or smaller clipped rider adds no weight, no speed loss, and half cover from below, with both hands free.
- **Fitting.** 10 minutes on a willing or trained Large flying beast.
- **Cost.** Priced past what most crews clear in a season; traded to [[the-live-hold|The Live Hold]] by an expedition that came back from the [[verdant-teeth]] without the animal it was cut for.

## Properties

**Fly rigged.** Fitting the harness to a willing or trained flying Beast of Large size takes 10 minutes. A Medium or smaller rider clipped into the shoulder hooks does not reduce the Beast's flying speed and does not count against its carrying capacity. The rider has half cover against attacks from directly below and can use both hands freely.

**Locked in.** While clipped, the rider cannot be knocked off the mount by an attack, a shove, or the mount's own manoeuvring, and is not thrown when the mount is knocked Prone in the air. Unclipping takes a Bonus Action from the rider, or a **Strength (Athletics) — `DC 15`** check from anyone else.

**Limits.** Large flying Beasts only, one rider, and the rider goes down with the mount. A clipped rider who wants free of a falling animal spends the Bonus Action first.

## At the Table

- **Carry a rider without paying for it.** The whole point: the beast flies at its own speed with a passenger aboard, so a party can move a person by air for as long as the animal will carry them.
- **Fight from the back of a flyer.** Both hands free and half cover from directly below, which is the difference between a passenger and a platform.
- **Stay on through a hit.** Shoves, attacks, and hard manoeuvres will not shake the rider loose; only the mount going down does.
- **Get out of a fall.** Unclipping costs the rider a Bonus Action, and this is the only clause that matters when the animal is hit and dropping.
- **Fit it to something untrained.** Ten minutes of rigging does not make a wild animal willing; the harness wants a beast that already accepts a rider.

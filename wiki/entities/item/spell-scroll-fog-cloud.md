# Spell Scroll (Fog Cloud)

> [!narration] Spell Scroll (Fog Cloud)
> The scroll is a single tight roll of oilcloth-backed parchment, no longer than a forearm, sealed with a plain wax disc instead of a merchant's stamp. Unrolled, the writing runs in looping grey-blue ink that seems to swirl faintly when the light catches it at an angle. It smells faintly of brine, like it has spent time near open water.

## At a Glance

*Scroll, common.*

The scroll carries one casting of *Fog Cloud*: a bank of mist dropped wherever a ship needs to stop being seen.

- **Effect.** One casting of *Fog Cloud* (1st level), a 20-foot-radius sphere of heavily obscured fog.
- **Save DC and attack.** `DC 13`, attack bonus `+5`, for the spell as written on this scroll.
- **Sold at.** [[Vask's Reclaimed Goods]] among the prize-goods, [[Tidefall Chandlery]] by the dozen as naval stock.

## Properties

**Reading it.** A *Spell Scroll* carries one spell in a mystical cipher. If the spell is on your spell list you can read the scroll and cast from it without providing material components; otherwise it is unintelligible to you. Casting from the scroll takes the spell's normal casting time, and the scroll crumbles to dust once the spell is cast. If the casting is interrupted, the scroll survives.

**Casting above your level.** If *Fog Cloud* is on your list but of a higher level than you can cast, make an ability check with your spellcasting ability against `DC 10 + the spell's level`. On a failure the spell vanishes from the scroll with no other effect.

**This copy.** A 1st-level scroll holding a single casting of *Fog Cloud*, with a spell save DC of `13` and an attack bonus of `+5`.

**Where copies come from.** [[Tidefall Chandlery]] racks it by the dozen as Crown-sanctioned naval stock, issued to ships that might need to vanish into their own wake. [[The Cabinet of Morsani]]'s copy came wrapped in a story that always ends before the interesting part. [[Vask's Reclaimed Goods]]' copy came off a wreck, still dry inside its oilcloth.

## At the Table

- **Break a tail.** A dropped bank of fog in harbour traffic is how a ship or a party stops being followed without a fight.
- **Cover a boarding or a swim.** The fog heavily obscures the area, which is enough to move people or cargo between hulls unseen.
- **Burn the scroll in a hurry.** The casting takes the spell's normal time, so a party has to decide to use it before the situation is already on top of them.
- **Count the one copy.** A single casting, then the scroll crumbles; anything longer than one escape needs the real spell or a second scroll.

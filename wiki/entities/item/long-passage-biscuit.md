# Long-Passage Biscuit

> [!narration] Narration
> A soldered case the size of a folded chart, hinged along one edge, the solder laid on thick by somebody who meant it to come through a flooded hold.
>
> Inside, waxed paper racks a stack of pale cakes, each one hard as decking.
>
> The top paper hangs loose at the fold, and what lifts off it smells like bread an hour out of the oven, in a room where nothing has baked since morning.
>
> Inside the lid, three chalk strokes. None of them crossed off.

*Consumable (Ration), Uncommon.* Baked in the back room at [[Wibowo's Provisions]] with clove, palm oil, and a measure of the same vent salt the waterfront tins for [[wakerock-salts]], then sealed hot into the case. Captains running the Deep buy one per watch officer and count it cheaper than losing the watch.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **Long Passage [HB].** A case holds three cakes. A creature that eats one over a [[Short Rest]] removes one level of [[Exhaustion]] at the end of that rest, and the cake counts as that day's food.
>
> **Edge cases:** a creature gains this benefit once a day, and a second cake that day feeds it and nothing more. A cake fed to an unconscious or incapacitated creature does nothing, since eating one takes the whole rest.
>
> **Limitations:** it restores no hit points and ends no other condition. It does nothing against exhaustion a creature is still taking on: a starving crew fed one cake apiece keeps gaining levels from [[Malnutrition]] until someone feeds them properly. An opened case goes soft and ordinary inside ten days in a wet hold.

## Provenance

The recipe is Suryo Wibowo's own, worked out over a decade of watching under-provisioned crews come back off the Deep with half the watch unable to stand. He bakes in small runs and sells the cases faster than he bakes them. Nobody has ever got the method out of him.

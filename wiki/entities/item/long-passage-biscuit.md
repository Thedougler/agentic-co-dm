# Long-Passage Biscuit

> [!narration] Narration
> A soldered case the size of a folded chart is hinged along one edge, with solder laid thick enough to survive a flooded hold. Waxed paper racks pale cakes inside, each hard as decking. The top paper hangs loose at the fold, and what lifts off it smells like bread an hour out of the oven, though nothing has baked since morning. Three chalk strokes mark the lid; none is crossed off.

*Consumable (Ration), uncommon.* Baked at [[Wibowo's Provisions]] with clove, palm oil, and the same vent salt used for [[wakerock-salts]], then sealed hot. Captains running the Deep buy one per watch officer and count it cheaper than losing the watch.

**Three cakes.** A creature that eats one cake over a Short Rest removes one level of Exhaustion at the end of that rest, and the cake counts as that day's food. A creature gains this benefit once per day; a second cake that day feeds it and nothing more. An unconscious or Incapacitated creature gains no benefit because eating takes the whole rest.

The biscuit restores no Hit Points and ends no other condition. It does not prevent Exhaustion a creature is still taking on: a starving crew fed one cake apiece keeps gaining levels from Malnutrition until properly fed. An opened case goes soft and ordinary inside 10 days in a wet hold.

## At the Table

[[suryo-wibowo]] keeps cases on the short shelf behind the counter at [[Wibowo's Provisions]] on [[kalowe]]. He sells a case to a captain who gives him a crew count and days-at-sea figure, and tells short planners so to their faces. The crew of the [[uncertainty]] can use it on a Deep crossing, where [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s already-negative Strength save makes Exhaustion costly.

## Provenance

The recipe is [[suryo-wibowo|Suryo Wibowo]]'s, developed over a decade of watching under-provisioned crews return from the Deep with half the watch unable to stand. He bakes small runs, sells the cases faster than he can bake them, and keeps the method private.

# Nightmantle

> [!narration] Narration
> A dark, unassuming cloak. Its fabric holds such deep black that it swallows nearby light instead of catching it. It hangs heavier than its size suggests, settling across the shoulders with a cold, deliberate weight. At the edges, where the fabric moves, a faint shimmer ripples through it, like heat off stone, gone the instant you look straight at it. Something about the way it catches the light feels subtly wrong, though nothing about it looks out of place.

*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement).*

**Nightmantle's Pull [RAW+HB, Cloak of Displacement].** While the wearer wears this cloak, it magically projects an illusion that makes her appear to stand near her actual location, and any creature attacking her has Disadvantage on the attack roll. Taking damage breaks this protection until the start of her next turn. A Speed of 0 also suppresses the property.

This cloak carries a curse layered on top of that protection. When a creature within 10 feet of the wearer would become the target of a ranged attack, the attack targets the wearer instead. A redirected attack resolves as a straight roll, since it never got aimed at the wearer's illusion.

- Edge case: the curse only redirects ranged attacks, never melee. The original target must be within 10 feet of the wearer when the attacker declares the attack.
- Edge case: the wearer can't decline the redirection. It isn't a reaction or a choice. It simply happens.

**Limitations.** The redirect only pulls ranged attacks meant for a nearby ally. It does nothing for melee attacks and grants the ally no benefit beyond losing the target designation. The wearer's own displacement disadvantage still applies to attacks aimed at her directly. Only the pulled attacks skip it. Nothing about the curse extends the cloak's range past 10 feet or lets the wearer choose which ally's attacks get pulled.

## At a Glance

[[crissdalynn-khinriss]] bought it expecting straightforward protection and carries it in her kit. Displacement makes her hard to hit; the curse pulls ranged fire meant for nearby allies onto her at a normal roll.

## At the Table

The next time an ally within 10 feet of her is the target of a ranged attack, that attack comes to her instead. She remains personally hard to hit on attacks aimed at her directly, but involuntarily draws ranged fire meant for whoever fights beside her.

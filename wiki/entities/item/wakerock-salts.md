# Wakerock Salts

> [!narration] Narration
> The tin is two fingers across. Salt crust has welded the lid. Grey crystal fills it to the brim, ground as coarse as damp sand. Three scoops are pressed into the surface; one scoop is already gone. Crack the lid and a bilge-ammonia sting carries the length of a galley. The metal on the underside is warmer than the shelf it sat on.

Consumable (salts), Uncommon

A tin holds three doses. As a Magic action, hold one open dose under the nose of a willing or incapacitated creature within 5 feet. That dose goes flat at once. The creature ends the Charmed or Frightened condition on itself and wakes from sleep, magical or ordinary. An unwilling creature that is neither Charmed nor Frightened refuses with no roll. A second dose against the same charming effect inside an hour does nothing. The salts restore no hit points, end no other condition, do nothing to a creature at 0 hit points, and do nothing past arm's reach. An open tin left unlidded goes flat by the end of the day. Cut from vent rock under [[Kalowe]], cut with clove so it will not cake. [[suryo-wibowo]] moves more of these before a Deep crossing than in the rest of the month. 75 gp.

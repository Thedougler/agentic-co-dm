# Pest-House Bandages

> [!narration] Narration
> A shelf of rolled linen and lashed splint-wood runs the full back wall, smelling faintly of vinegar and clean cloth, more of it than a village this size should ever need. The linen's clean, boiled white, folded tight in bundles of a dozen. Marta restocks it herself between customers, never seeming to run out no matter how much she hands across the counter.

*Trade Good (Medical Supply), Mundane.* Ordinary bandages and splints, no different in the hand from any wound-dressing sold anywhere, sold in bulk at [[Anvez's Provisions]]. The stock itself is the tell. [[otel-karn]]'s wounds don't close on their own, so the village keeps every household supplied for as long as anyone stays hurt, which on this island can mean forever.

## Provenance

Kept in bulk because wounds on [[otel-karn]] linger for as long as anyone stays hurt, a bandage or short rest does nothing to close them. [[marta-anvez]] stocks for that fact plainly, restocking the shelf herself between customers.

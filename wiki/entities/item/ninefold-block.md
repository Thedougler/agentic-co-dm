# Ninefold Block

> [!narration] Narration
> An oak block the length of a forearm is iron-strapped, with nine sheaves stacked inside where an ordinary rigging block carries one. Touch one and all nine turn together, continuing after the hand leaves. Its strop takes cable thicker than a block this size should carry and smells of hot iron instead of tar. A line runs through it already, coiled tight against the floorboards.

*Wondrous item, rare.*

**Ninefold haul.** Reeving a rope through the block and fastening it to an anchor takes 1 minute. While a creature hauls on that line, treat its Strength score as 21 for how much weight it can lift, drag, pull, or hold, and for Strength checks to shift the load. Two or more creatures hauling the same line combine their pull normally.

The load travels only along the line toward the block. A portcullis, hull section, fallen beam, or sunken crate qualifies, but the anchor must support the weight; a cleat, spar, or bollard that cannot will tear out. The block does nothing against a resisting creature, grants no bonus to attacks, damage, Grapple contests, or Athletics checks against a creature, and adds nothing to Strength saving throws. Whatever it hauls travels at walking speed. Cutting the line ends the effect, and a hauler off the line gets no benefit.

## At the Table

[[giacomo-moretti]] keeps the block on the high rail at [[Moretti & Sons Chandlery]] in [[calven-and-calveno]]. It came from a wreck lot with its line still rove and has not sold. The crew of the [[uncertainty]] can use it around [[La Vasca]] dry dock, where the yard's day rate reflects the cost of lifting gangs. Giacomo prices it at a season of yard labor.

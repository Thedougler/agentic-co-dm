# Spell Scroll (See Invisibility)

> [!narration] Spell Scroll (See Invisibility)
> A drop of blue wax seals the tightly rolled parchment, cool and slightly waxy under the thumb. Unrolled, the writing shivers faintly in the light, a cipher of looping, silver-grey ink that seems to shift focus the longer you stare at it, as if the page itself resists a direct gaze. A faint smell of talc clings to the seal.

## At a Glance

*Scroll, uncommon.*

The scroll holds one casting of *See Invisibility*, for the week the party stops being sure whether it is alone.

- **Effect.** One casting of *See Invisibility* (2nd level) on the reader, for 1 hour.
- **Save DC and attack.** `DC 13`, attack bonus `+5`, for the spell as written on this scroll.
- **Sold at.** [[shelf-instrument-dealer|The Shelf Instrument Dealer]], kept in small numbers for captains with one too many reports of cargo going missing under a clear deck.

## Properties

**Reading it.** A *Spell Scroll* carries one spell in a mystical cipher. If the spell is on your spell list you can read the scroll and cast from it without providing material components; otherwise it is unintelligible to you. Casting from the scroll takes the spell's normal casting time, and the scroll crumbles to dust once the spell is cast. If the casting is interrupted, the scroll survives.

**Casting above your level.** If *See Invisibility* is on your list but of a higher level than you can cast, make an ability check with your spellcasting ability against `DC 10 + the spell's level`. On a failure the spell vanishes from the scroll with no other effect.

**This copy.** A 2nd-level scroll holding a single casting of *See Invisibility*, with a spell save DC of `13` and an attack bonus of `+5`.

## At the Table

- **Check whether you are alone.** An hour of seeing invisible creatures and objects as if they were visible, which turns a suspicion into an answer.
- **Catch a watcher without touching them.** The spell reveals, it does not expose: the reader sees who is there and still has to decide what to do about it.
- **Look through the crowd.** Since word got around that the recently celebrated attract watchers who prefer to stay unseen, the scroll has moved briskly in [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall|Calders' Tooth]] and [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]] alike.
- **Spend the hour before the meeting.** It lasts an hour and the scroll is gone after it. A party that reads it too early gets nothing but a clear view of an empty room.

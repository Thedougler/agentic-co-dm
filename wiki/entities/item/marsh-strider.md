# Marsh Strider

> [!narration] Narration
> A wading bird in a tall pen stands grey-legged, its head level with your eyes and turned sideways to keep one eye on you. Hard scale runs the length of its legs, knuckled at the knee and thin as a boat hook at the ankle. A narrow saddle pad hangs over the rail, sweat-stiff. It shifts its weight, and the whole pen rail knocks against the post.

*Tool (Trained Animal), uncommon.* A domesticated fen wader kept across the [[crown-islands]] for canal hauling and shallow crossings. [[zort]] sells each one saddle-broken with pad and reins. It is a Large beast with a Speed of 50 feet, carries 480 pounds without slowing, swims poorly, and does not fight.

**High water.** A strider crosses mud, flooded streets, marsh, and water up to 4 feet deep at full speed, treating the surface as ordinary ground. Its rider is 8 feet above the surface while mounted.

**Step over.** As part of its move, a mounted strider can step over a Medium or smaller creature's space without provoking an Opportunity Attack from that creature. A creature attempting to Grapple the rider from the ground does so with Disadvantage.

**Spooks.** The strider has no combat training. If it takes damage, its rider must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom (Animal Handling) check as a Reaction or the strider bolts 50 feet in a random direction, carrying the rider and ending the rider's turn. It carries one Medium rider, cannot wear barding, and refuses deep water, stairs, and anything a horse would refuse.

## At the Table

[[zort]] sells Marsh Striders at [[Zort's Pits]], saddle-broken on the canal walk. A mounted strider gives a rider height and reliable movement through mud, flooded streets, marsh, and shallow water, but a fight triggers its Spooks check as soon as it takes damage. It is a travel and hauling asset, not a combat mount.

## Provenance

The striders are raised on the fen edge past [[le-paludi]] and finished at [[Zort's Pits]]. Goblin handlers walk each animal through the crowd on the canal walk until it stops caring about people.

# Cage-Calm Draught

> [!narration] Cage-Calm Draught
> A squat clay crock, warm to hold, sealed under a skin of yellow wax with a thumbprint pressed into it. What gets past the wax is sweet and heavy and a little rotten, the smell of fruit left too long in a warm room. Tip it and the contents move slowly, thicker than water. Every animal in the near cages turns toward it at once.

## At a Glance

*Potion (feed additive), common.*

The Cage-Calm Draught takes the fight out of an animal for ten minutes, which is exactly as long as it takes to move one.

- **Effect.** A Beast that takes a dose is charmed toward the user for 10 minutes, will not attack while charmed, and moves at half speed.
- **Kit.** One clay crock, three doses, 2 pounds.
- **Brewed by.** [[zort]] in the back of [[zorts-pits|Zort's Pits]], out of fermented marsh fruit and something he will not name.

## Properties

**Dose (Action).** Mix one dose into food or water a Beast then eats or drinks, or throw the crock as an improvised weapon at a target within 20 feet. A Beast that swallows the dose, and any Beast the thrown crock splashes, makes a **Constitution saving throw — `DC 13`**. On a failure it is charmed toward the user for 10 minutes and its speed is halved, and it will not attack while charmed. On a success nothing takes hold, though the animal knows someone tried.

**Second dose.** A Beast given another dose inside the hour saves with Advantage. A thrown crock breaks on impact, hit or miss.

**Limits.** Beasts only, and only a Beast Large or smaller that eats. Monstrosities, dragons, plants, constructs, and anything that talks shrug it off. Damaging a charmed Beast ends the effect immediately.

## At the Table

- **Handle a new arrival.** One dose in the feed makes a fresh capture safe to work on its first day, which is what the shop brews it for.
- **Throw it at something closing.** An improvised throw at 20 feet splashes the target and anything beside it. The save is the same as for the feed, and the crock is gone either way.
- **Try it twice.** The second dose inside the hour saves with Advantage, and the animal now knows the trick.
- **Hit the charmed animal.** Any damage ends it immediately, which is worth remembering before someone decides to help by landing a punch.
- **Use it on something that is not a Beast.** A monstrosity, a dragon, a construct, or a talking creature is unaffected, and a party that planned around the crock finds out at the worst moment.

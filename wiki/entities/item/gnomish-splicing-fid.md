# Gnomish Splicing Fid

> [!narration] Gnomish Splicing Fid
> A tapered steel spike the length of a forearm has a tarred-cord grip worn smooth. Quarter-inch cordage gauges are etched into the shaft, each cut deep enough to catch a thumbnail. A brass catch at the base releases a folding blade no longer than a thumb, honed thin enough to part hemp without dragging.

## At a Glance

*Tool (marlinspike), common.*

The Gnomish Splicing Fid is what the [[nimmik-vollask|Nimmik Vollask]]'s crew uses to make rope work exact: a marlinspike with a gauge cut into the shaft and a blade in the base.

- **Effect.** Advantage on ability checks to splice, tie, or repair rope and cordage, including binding a creature with rope.
- **Kit.** Tapered steel spike, etched cordage gauge, folding thumb-length blade in the base.
- **Sold at.** [[cotter-foss|Cotter]] sells the fids from the rail of the [[nimmik-vollask]], where the gnomes make them below decks.

## Properties

**Exact rope work.** A creature proficient with this tool has Advantage on ability checks to splice, tie, or repair rope and cordage, including checks to bind a creature with rope. The etched gauge measures cordage diameter without another tool.

**The blade.** Too short and light for combat: it deals 1 piercing damage and adds no proficiency bonus.

**What it does not cover.** The Advantage applies only to rope and cordage work, not nets, chains, or wire. The blade dulls after 10 cuts through wet hemp and has to be stropped.

## At the Table

- **Rig a ship under pressure.** Splicing, ties, and repairs made with the fid roll with Advantage, which is the difference between a yard repaired in a squall and a yard lost.
- **Bind a prisoner.** The Advantage explicitly covers checks to bind a creature with rope, and a party taking someone alive wants that.
- **Measure cordage without guessing.** The gauge reads diameter off the shaft, which settles a rope-buying argument or a rigging check on the spot.
- **Cut with the blade.** It parts dry cord cleanly but is not a weapon; anyone who tries to fight with it has a 1-damage spike.
- **Note who carries one.** The gnomes stamp every fid with a glyph none of them will explain, and sailors carrying one are known for taking knots seriously.

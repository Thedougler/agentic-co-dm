# Gnomish Splicing Fid

> [!narration] Narration
> A tapered steel spike the length of a forearm has a tarred-cord grip worn smooth. Quarter-inch cordage gauges are etched into the shaft, each cut deep enough to catch a thumbnail. A brass catch at the base releases a folding blade no longer than a thumb, honed thin enough to part hemp without dragging.

*Tool (Marlinspike), common.*

**Exact rope work.** A creature proficient with this tool has Advantage on ability checks to splice, tie, or repair rope and cordage, including checks to bind a creature with rope. The etched gauge measures cordage diameter without another tool. The fold-out blade is too short and light for combat; it deals 1 piercing damage and adds no proficiency bonus.

The Advantage applies only to rope and cordage work, not nets, chains, or wire. The blade dulls after 10 cuts through wet hemp and must be stropped.

## At the Table

[[cotter-foss]] sells the fids from the rail of the [[nimmik-vollask]], where the gnomes make them below decks. Any PC working rigging, setting anchors, or binding prisoners benefits from precise rope work. The gnomes stamp every fid with a mark nobody else uses, and sailors carrying one are known for taking their knots seriously.

## Connections

- [[cotter-foss]] sells the fids aboard the [[nimmik-vollask]].
- [[nimmik-vollask]]'s gnomish crew makes them in its below-decks workshop.
- [[central-strait]]'s crews use them for rigging and repairs.

## Provenance

The gnomish crew of the [[nimmik-vollask]] has made these in its below-decks workshop for longer than [[cotter-foss|Cotter]] has worked aboard. Each fid carries a stamped gnomish glyph that none of the crew will explain.

# Harrier-Cry Whistle

> [!narration] Harrier-Cry Whistle
> A finger of hollow bone on a leather cord, sanded down to the grain, with one fingerhole burned through the side. The mouth end is cut on the slant and stained darker than the rest. Held level it turns slowly on its own cord, and the draught through it makes a note too thin to place. Cover the hole and the note climbs until it stops sounding like breath and starts sounding like a bird.

## At a Glance

*Wondrous item, uncommon.*

The Harrier-Cry Whistle drops one enemy caster's spell with a sound like a dropped pane, for the price of telling everything nearby where the party is.

- **Effect.** One creature within 30 feet that can hear the blast makes a Constitution save or loses Concentration on a spell.
- **Charges.** 3 charges, all regained at dawn.
- **Source.** Cut by [[sabine-corvo]] from a harrier that died in transit, sold at the counter of [[the-live-hold|The Live Hold]] beside the cage of the one that lived.

## Properties

**Cry (Action).** The whistle holds 3 charges and regains all of them at dawn. Spend a charge and choose one creature within 30 feet that can hear you. It must succeed on a **Constitution saving throw — `DC 13`** or lose Concentration on a spell it is maintaining, and it takes `1d4` Thunder damage whether it saves or not.

**Limitations.** No effect on a Deafened creature, anything immune to Thunder damage, or a creature that cannot hear over its own noise. Every blast carries roughly 300 feet, and everything hunting in that radius hears a wounded bird.

## At the Table

- **Break a caster mid-spell.** One action, one charge, one save. A held spell drops even on a successful save if the caster rolls badly, and the whistle works from 30 feet without line of sight.
- **Use it under water or in a storm.** The blast carries anyway. The bird cry is a beacon: anything within 300 feet that hunts by sound now knows exactly where the party stands.
- **Spend the charges fast.** Three blasts is three casters. Once the charges are gone, the whistle is a piece of bone on a cord until dawn.
- **Try it on a construct or a deaf target.** Nothing. The whistle needs a hearing creature with a spell to lose.

# Vial of Predator Musk

> [!narration] Vial of Predator Musk
> A squat glass vial holds liquid the colour of old tallow, sealed with wax over the stopper and wax over the wax. It reeks through both seals: fox, and something ranker behind the fox. The glass stays warm, and a strip of hide tied to the neck has gone bald where it has been used as a dauber.

## At a Glance

*Consumable, common.*

The Vial of Predator Musk marks ground as a bigger animal's territory and keeps lesser beasts off it for an hour.

- **Effect.** Beasts of CR 1 or lower must beat a Wisdom save to come within 10 feet of the marked ground.
- **Use.** One dose, smeared on a doorway, a deck, or a pair of boots.
- **Source.** Rendered in the yard behind [[the-live-hold|The Live Hold]] and sold by [[sabine-corvo]] to crews that camp where something bigger already hunts.

## Properties

**Dauber (Action).** Smear the dose across a doorway, a deck, or your own boots. For 1 hour, a Beast of CR 1 or lower that is not immune to the Frightened condition must succeed on a **Wisdom saving throw — `DC 13`** to move within 10 feet of the marked ground. On a failure, it must spend its turn moving away. Any scent-based tracking of a creature carrying the open vial fails outright.

**Limitations.** One use, Beasts only, and it works on the party's own mounts and animal companions exactly as it works on anything else. Everything with a nose within 100 feet knows where the party is, and one vial marks one patch of ground.

## At the Table

- **Camp out where something hunts.** Dauber the perimeter, and the small predators stay outside it. The bigger thing the musk imitates is exactly the reason the party needed the vial.
- **Wear it in.** Smearing it on boots keeps lesser beasts off the wearer — and tells every nose on the island where the wearer is. Tracking by scent against the open vial fails outright, so it doubles as a broken trail and a beacon.
- **Dose a doorway or a hold.** One line across the threshold holds for an hour. Counting the hour out loud is the party's job, because the vial gives no sign of its own.
- **Use it on companions.** The musk does not discriminate. A party mule or an animal companion is affected exactly as a hostile beast is.

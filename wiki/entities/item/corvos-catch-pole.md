# Corvo's Catch-Pole

> [!narration] Corvo's Catch-Pole
> Ten feet of ash, worn pale where two hands have gripped it a thousand times. A loop of braided wire runs the shaft and comes out the far end in a noose the size of a dog's head; a brass slide at the grip draws it shut. The last foot is chewed, and tipping the pole makes the wire sing along the wood.

## At a Glance

*Durable gear, common.*

Corvo's Catch-Pole is a handler's pole that puts a thrashing beast at the far end of ten feet of ash and keeps it there.

- **Effect.** Restrain a Medium or smaller Beast at 10 feet, out of reach of anything with a 5-foot reach.
- **Weight and price.** 6 pounds; standard kit in every [[verdant-teeth]] dealership working live stock.
- **Held by.** [[sabine-corvo]] has three on the wall of [[the-live-hold|The Live Hold]] and sells the spares to crews who keep breaking theirs.

## Properties

**Noose (Action).** Reach 10 feet. Choose a Medium or smaller Beast within reach. It must make a **Strength saving throw — `DC 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Dexterity modifier`**. On a failure, the Beast has the Restrained condition while you hold the pole in both hands, and it stays at the far end of the shaft, out of reach of anything with a 5-foot reach. Maintaining the hold costs your action each turn, and the Beast repeats the save at the end of each of its turns.

**Beast only, and holdable only.** The pole takes Beasts of Medium size or smaller. Nothing that can chew through braided wire is held by it for long.

**The pole itself.** AC 13, 10 hit points, and it snaps at 0 hit points. A beast that breaks the pole is loose and angry.

## At the Table

- **Take a live specimen.** The pole is the difference between loading a Beast into a crate and wrestling it. One hand on the slide, both hands on the shaft, and the animal stays a pole's length away.
- **Hold it while someone else works.** Maintaining the hold costs the handler's action every turn, so a second pair of hands does the crating, the dosing, or the killing.
- **Keep it out of reach.** A Beast with reach of its own, or one big enough to drag the pole, turns the catch into a tug of war the handler can lose.
- **Mind the chewed end.** The last foot shows what happens when a hold is broken. A pole at 0 hit points leaves the party holding a stick and standing next to whatever it was holding.

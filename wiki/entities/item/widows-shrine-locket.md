# Widow's Shrine Locket

> [!narration] Widow's Shrine Locket
> A tarnished silver locket, its clasp gone stiff with salt corrosion. Inside, behind a scratched glass pane, a hand-painted portrait has faded to a pale smear of a face, features long since bled out by damp sea air. The chain breaks a few links from the clasp, knotted back together with fishing line.

## At a Glance

*Wondrous item, mundane.*

The Widow's Shrine Locket is a nonmagical keepsake worth 8 gp in silver and workmanship.

- **Found at.** A shrine corner past [[sparhold]]'s fish stalls.
- **Portrait.** The face is not remembered by anyone now living.

## Properties

**Mundane keepsake.** The locket has no magical properties. Its silver and workmanship are worth 8 gp.

## History

The locket was left as an offering at the shrine corner and is tended by a widow old enough to remember the fishing village that stood on this shore before the walls went up. The portrait predates her own memory of the place. She keeps the offering box but has never opened it to look.

# Widow's Shrine Locket

> [!narration] Narration
> A tarnished silver locket, its clasp gone stiff with salt corrosion. Inside, behind a scratched glass pane, a hand-painted portrait has faded to a pale smear of a face, features long since bled out by damp sea air. The chain breaks a few links from the clasp, knotted back together with fishing line.

*Wondrous Item, Mundane.* No magical properties. A keepsake locket, worth 8 gp in silver and workmanship alone.

## Provenance

Left as an offering at the shrine corner past [[Sparhold]]'s fish stalls, tended by a widow old enough to remember the fishing village that stood on this shore before the walls went up. The portrait predates her own memory of the place. She keeps the offering box but has never opened it to look.

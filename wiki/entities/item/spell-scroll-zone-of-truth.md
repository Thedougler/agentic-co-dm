# Spell Scroll (Zone of Truth)

> [!narration] Spell Scroll (Zone of Truth)
> The scroll case is plain waxed cedar, salt-stained at the seams, its stopper sealed with a dab of blue tallow. Inside, a single strip of vellum carries one line of cramped, silver-inked script that seems to shift focus when you try to read it straight on. Breaking the seal, you feel the pull of it already: reading this line out loud is a promise you are about to make.

## At a Glance

*Scroll, uncommon.*

The scroll carries one casting of *Zone of Truth*, the thing a harbour magistrate reaches for when a dockside inquest stops moving.

- **Effect.** One casting of *Zone of Truth* (2nd level), a 15-foot-radius zone for 10 minutes.
- **Save DC and attack.** `DC 13`, attack bonus `+5`, for the spell as written on this scroll.
- **Sold at.** [[shelf-instrument-dealer|The Shelf Instrument Dealer]], ordered in alongside a run of chronometers for the magistrates who settle cargo disputes.

## Properties

**Reading it.** A *Spell Scroll* carries one spell in a mystical cipher. If the spell is on your spell list you can read the scroll and cast from it without providing material components; otherwise it is unintelligible to you. Casting from the scroll takes the spell's normal casting time, and the scroll crumbles to dust once the spell is cast. If the casting is interrupted, the scroll survives.

**Casting above your level.** If *Zone of Truth* is on your list but of a higher level than you can cast, make an ability check with your spellcasting ability against `DC 10 + the spell's level`. On a failure the spell vanishes from the scroll with no other effect.

**This copy.** A 2nd-level scroll holding a single casting of *Zone of Truth*, with a spell save DC of `13` and an attack bonus of `+5`.

## At the Table

- **Interrogate inside the zone.** A creature that fails its save cannot speak a deliberate lie while in the area, and the caster knows it. It does not compel an answer, so the party still has to ask the right question.
- **Let the magistrate run the inquest.** With every faction in [[calven-and-calveno]] turning up to ask questions, the scroll is what a dockside hearing uses to get past the first set of denials.
- **Sit inside it yourself.** The zone catches the caster too, which is a fact worth knowing before a party sets its own terms in one.
- **Count the ten minutes.** The casting takes the spell's normal time and the zone lasts a fixed window; a hearing that runs long loses the effect.

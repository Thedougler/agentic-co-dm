# Reef-Song Shard

> [!narration] Reef-Song Shard
> The fragment fits in one closed hand, pale as bleached coral but smooth as worked stone, carved along its length in ridges too even to have come from any current or tide. Hold it to your ear, or just let the wind pass over it, and it hums one long note that doesn't waver and doesn't fade until you close your fingers around it. The grain underneath the carving isn't wood, isn't bone, isn't shell — nothing a hand raised on this coast has ever worked before.

## At a Glance

*Wondrous item (curio), mundane.*

The Reef-Song Shard is a hand-sized fragment that sounds one unwavering note in moving air, with no maker, no material, and no magic any appraiser can name.

- **Effect.** It hums a single sustained note whenever air moves across its ridges, and goes silent when a hand closes around it.
- **Held by.** Recorded from [[la-cenere]]'s racks and from [[slackwater]]'s Trust Market trade; no established owner.
- **Wanted by.** [[ninka-solvei]] buys salvage with no questions about provenance and would price this as Antheri work sold incomplete.

## Properties

**Mundane curio.** No magical properties. *Identify* reports nothing, and no attunement is possible. Closing a hand around the shard silences it; uncovering it in any moving air starts the note again.

**The note.** The shard holds one pitch and does not waver, swell, or fade while uncovered, however hard the wind blows across it. It is loud enough to hear held at the ear and quiet enough to ignore at arm's length.

**Value.** `60 gp` as an unidentified curio. Its worth rises sharply if a buyer can name the material.

## At the Table

- **Sell it as a curio.** Dealers price it low because nothing about it can be identified: [[ninka-solvei]] calls it Antheri work, incomplete, and charges the buyer for the risk.
- **Test the note.** Any character with a tuned ear or a second reference tone can hear that the shard's pitch never drifts. Comparing it against an Antheri piece from the [[shelfworks]] shows the same held pitch.
- **Carry it into the Maw.** The note stays level everywhere the party goes — until the [[drowned-maw]], where it sounds a second, lower tone under the first. Whether that is the fissure, the machinery below, or [[auralis]] is not settled.
- **Try to break it.** The shard resists ordinary cutting, fire, and a fall onto rock. A hammer and anvil cost the party a smith's time and change nothing about the note.

## Secrets

**Secret.** The shard is Antheri work: a tuning reference from the living-light channels the builders used, cut from the dark alloy of the [[shelfworks]] tiers. The note is a held pitch, not a natural resonance, which is why wind cannot alter it and why it answers the machinery under the [[drowned-maw]]. The carving's evenness and the material no coast has worked are the tells; a comparison against salvage from [[shelfworks]] confirms it.

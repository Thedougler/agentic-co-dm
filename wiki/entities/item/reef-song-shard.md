# Reef-Song Shard

> [!narration] Narration
> The fragment fits in one closed hand, pale as bleached coral but smooth as worked stone, carved along its length in ridges too even to have come from any current or tide. Hold it to your ear, or just let the wind pass over it, and it hums one long note that doesn't waver and doesn't fade until you close your fingers around it. The grain underneath the carving isn't wood, isn't bone, isn't shell — nothing a hand raised on this coast has ever worked before.

*Wondrous item (curio), mundane.* No maker's mark, no forge-stamp, no craft guild anywhere in the [[shattered-sea]] claims this work. It has no known magical properties, but no appraiser who has handled it has been able to name what it's made from either.

## Provenance

Surfaced at [[slackwater]]'s Trust Market with no seller willing to say where it came from — one more unremarked piece in the settlement's quiet trickle of salvage that nobody asks too hard about.

# Anchor-Watch Chime

> [!narration] Narration
> A brass chime the size of a fist has its clapper wired short so it cannot swing on its own. A watch-bill is scratched around the rim in four different hands, with one name struck through. Set on wood, it takes the grain like a fitting and needs a real pull to lift. Nothing rings; the clapper moves anyway and hangs toward the loading door.

*Wondrous item, uncommon.*

**Anchor watch.** Setting the chime on a surface that carries no motion of its own takes 1 minute. It holds until a creature lifts it or until the next dawn. The first time a creature the setter has not already seen comes within 60 feet, the chime rings once, loud enough to wake sleepers and audible 300 feet away. Every creature within 30 feet that hears it escapes the Surprised condition in an encounter beginning in the next minute.

The chime rings for a creature that becomes Invisible, hides, or arrives underwater. A ship at anchor counts as still; a ship under way carries too much motion. It rings once per setting, and resetting it costs the full minute. It grants no bonus to Initiative, Perception, or any other roll, identifies nothing about what woke the watch, and points in no direction. A creature, held object, or vehicle with way on it cannot set it. A second chime within the same 60 feet remains silent while the first holds.

## At the Table

The chime is on the signal-gear shelf at [[Moretti & Sons Chandlery]], where [[giacomo-moretti]] sells it to crews that need an honest first warning. Its narrow use matters to a moored ship or sleeping camp: the bell buys the watch a first minute, not an answer about what approaches. It can be set during a quiet watch, but lifting it or moving the vessel breaks its protection.

## Hidden Properties

The scratched watch-bill records four prior users, and one name has been struck through. Whoever struck it out used the chime's own clapper wire. The object does not reveal who did this or why; the mark is a clue, not another mechanical power.

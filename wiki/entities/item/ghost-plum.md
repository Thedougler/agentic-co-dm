## At a Glance

*Consumable, rare*

A ghost plum lets one creature go unseen for an hour, and its own pollen is the one thing that gives an unseen creature away.

- **Held by.** [[matteo-scola]] carries two in the sailcloth sling at his hip when Session 12 opens.
- **Wanted by.** [[talon-skarn]]. He watched Matteo vanish in Session 11, and in Session 12 he gathers two fallen plums in the Quiet to come at the [[fate-spinner]] unseen.
- **Found in.** [[old-gardens]] and the treelines of [[the-quiet]], wherever pale pollen drifts.

## Properties

**Eat.** Eating a ghost plum takes a Bonus Action. The eater has the Invisible condition for 1 hour. Attacking, casting a spell, or taking damage does not end it.

**Pollen.** Ghost-plum pollen drifts in pale clouds where the trees grow. An Invisible creature inside a pollen drift shows as a pale shimmer, and anyone who looks can see and target it normally while it stays inside.

**Picking.** A ghost plum picked from its stem is a claim under [[taking-on-aruhe]]. One that has fallen is safe to take.

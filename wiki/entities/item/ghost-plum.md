# Ghost Plum

![[attachments/shattered-sea/items/aruhe-ghost-plum.jpg|Ghost plum]]

> [!narration] Narration
> A ghost plum is an oval fruit bigger than a hand, hanging heavy from a mossy branch under broad wet leaves. Its skin borrows the colors around and behind it, while pale pollen settles over a thin shimmer that gives away the fruit's edge.

Consumable, Rare.

When you eat this fruit, you have the **Invisible** condition for **1 hour**.

Session 11. [[matteo-scola]] ate one in front of the party and vanished. The table discussed both one hour and ten minutes for the duration. This page keeps one hour until a later ruling replaces it. ^[ambiguous]

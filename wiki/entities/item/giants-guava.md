## At a Glance

*Consumable, rare*

A giant's guava makes its eater, for one hour, as strong in their best gift as a giant.

- **Found in.** [[old-gardens]], [[memorial-grove]], and hanging from the great vine at [[the-pantry]].
- **Wanted by.** [[auralis|Auralis]], who tells [[perrin-black-jaw]] to eat one in Session 12.

## Properties

**Eat.** Eating a giant's guava takes a Bonus Action. For 1 hour, the eater's primary ability score becomes 25: the ability its class names first, or Charisma for a creature with no class. Every roll, save DC, and attack bonus that uses that score changes with it. For [[perrin-black-jaw]], that means Charisma 25, spell save DC 18, and spell attack `+10`.

**Picking.** A guava picked from its stem is a claim under [[taking-on-aruhe]]. One that has fallen is safe to take, and keeps until eaten.

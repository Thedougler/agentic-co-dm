# Spell Scroll (Nondetection)

> [!narration] Spell Scroll (Nondetection)
> The parchment is brittle at the edges, rolled tight and sealed with a plain wax disc. Inside, the ink is a cipher of loops and crossed strokes that shifts faintly as you tilt it to the light, never sitting still on the page. It smells faintly of diamond dust.

## At a Glance

*Scroll, uncommon.*

The scroll holds one casting of *Nondetection*, for the day the party needs a scrying eye to find nothing.

- **Effect.** One casting of *Nondetection* (3rd level) on a touched target, hiding it from divination magic for 8 hours.
- **Save DC and attack.** `DC 15`, attack bonus `+7`, for the spell as written on this scroll.
- **Sold at.** [[shelf-instrument-dealer|The Shelf Instrument Dealer]], quietly, to captains who want their cargo kept off a scrying eye.

## Properties

**Reading it.** A *Spell Scroll* carries one spell in a mystical cipher. If the spell is on your spell list you can read the scroll and cast from it without providing material components; otherwise it is unintelligible to you. Casting from the scroll takes the spell's normal casting time, and the scroll crumbles to dust once the spell is cast. If the casting is interrupted, the scroll survives.

**Casting above your level.** If *Nondetection* is on your list but of a higher level than you can cast, make an ability check with your spellcasting ability against `DC 10 + the spell's level`. On a failure the spell vanishes from the scroll with no other effect.

**This copy.** A 3rd-level scroll holding a single casting of *Nondetection*, with a spell save DC of `15` and an attack bonus of `+7`.

## At the Table

- **Hide a person or an object.** The spell touches a creature, a place, or an object and keeps divination magic off it for 8 hours, which is long enough to move something across a harbour.
- **Cover a manifest.** Popular with captains who need a scrying eye, or a Sentinel's account-taking, not to find their cargo first.
- **Cast it on the caster.** The spell can protect whoever reads the scroll, which is how a hunted caster crosses open ground.
- **Time it.** Eight hours, one casting, then the scroll is gone. A party that uses it too early spends the whole window standing still.

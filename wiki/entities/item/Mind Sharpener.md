# Mind Sharpener

> [!narration] Narration
> A small metal clip with gears. It shifts color when light hits it.
>
> It brings a faint warmth to the scalp, barely felt under hair.
>
> *(low)* Thin grooves circle its edge. One dims each time it spends a charge.

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon.*

## Mechanics

**[HB]** Wearing this item grants a power to its wearer. When they fail a [[Constitution]] save to hold concentration on a spell, they can use their reaction to succeed instead. This costs one charge. The item has 4 charges and regains them at dawn.

**Limitations:** only works when you fail a Constitution save to hold concentration on a spell. It does not work on other Constitution saves. Crissdalynn once tried it to break through a magical barrier. It did not work.

## Provenance

[[Catarina Da'Virelli]] crafted and infused this item. She gave it to [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] during a fight in the [[Calveno sewer magazines]]. Catarina had noticed Crissdalynn struggling to keep concentration under fire. Crissdalynn holds it now. [[Session 06]]'s transcript documents this exchange.

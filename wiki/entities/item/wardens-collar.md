# Warden's Collar

> [!narration] Narration
> A wide band of black hide in a glazed case, studded the whole way round with brass the size of thumbnails.
>
> Each stud carries a different mark, and the marks run in an order that does not repeat.
>
> The buckle has no tongue and no holes. The two ends simply want each other, and they close with a sound like a latch dropping.
>
> A paper tag hangs off the case on a thread, with a name inked on it that is not yours.


*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement).* The most expensive thing in [[The Live Hold]], sold with the animal or not at all.


> [!mechanic]
> **[HB] Bind (8 hours).** Attune to the collar while it is buckled on a beast of CR 3 or lower. While the beast wears it and you are attuned, you can command it telepathically at up to 100 feet, and it obeys as though it were charmed by you. It acts on your initiative and takes one action of your choosing on its turn. You know its condition and rough direction while it lives.
>
> **Limitations:** one collar, one beast; rebinding to a different animal takes another 8 hours and ends the first bond. A beast with Intelligence 5 or higher repeats a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw at each dawn, and on a success the collar falls open and stays open for a day. The collar gives no command over anything that is not a beast.

## Provenance

Bought in from a [[Vaelspire]] estate sale. [[sabine-corvo]] has already taken a deposit on it from a collector, and the tag on the case carries his name.

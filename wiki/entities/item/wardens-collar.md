# Warden's Collar

> [!narration] Warden's Collar
> A wide band of black hide in a glazed case, studded the whole way round with brass the size of thumbnails. Each stud carries a different mark, and the marks run in an order that does not repeat. The buckle has no tongue and no holes: the two ends simply want each other, and they close with a sound like a latch dropping. A paper tag hangs off the case on a thread, with a name inked on it that is not yours.

## At a Glance

*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement).*

The Warden's Collar is the most expensive thing on [[the-live-hold|The Live Hold]]'s wall: one beast, bound to one keeper's word for as long as the collar stays closed.

- **Effect.** A beast wearing the collar obeys its attuned keeper, acts on the keeper's initiative, and can be commanded telepathically at up to 100 feet.
- **Binding.** 8 hours of attunement while the collar is buckled on a beast of CR 3 or lower.
- **Sold with the animal.** The collar goes with the animal or not at all, and [[sabine-corvo]] has already taken a deposit from a collector whose name is on the tag.

## Properties

**Bind (8 hours).** Attune to the collar while it is buckled on a Beast of CR 3 or lower. While that Beast wears the collar and you are attuned, you can command it telepathically at up to 100 feet, and it obeys as though it had the Charmed condition from you. It acts on your initiative and takes one action of your choosing on its turn. You know its condition and rough direction while it lives.

**Limits.** One collar, one Beast. Rebinding to a different animal takes another 8 hours and ends the first bond. A Beast with Intelligence 5 or higher repeats a **Wisdom saving throw — `DC 15`** at each dawn; on a success the collar falls open and stays open for a day. The collar commands nothing that is not a Beast.

**The buckle.** It has no tongue and no holes, and it closes by itself. Only the attunement and the Intelligence save open it.

## At the Table

- **Bind a captured beast.** Eight hours and a CR 3 ceiling: the collar turns a live capture into an obedient asset that acts on the keeper's turn, within 100 feet.
- **Send it ahead.** Telepathic commands at 100 feet and no line of sight requirement worth worrying about make the beast a scout, a guard, or a distraction the keeper directs from cover.
- **Try it on an intelligent animal.** Anything with Intelligence 5 or higher gets a daily save at dawn, and a success opens the collar for a whole day. Worth knowing before a party bets a plan on a clever ape.
- **Buy it out from under the deposit.** The collar is already spoken for by a collector, and the tag on the case carries his name. A party that wants it is negotiating with the dealer, and then with him.
- **Take it off the wall.** It is in a glazed case on the back wall of the Hold, which is a shop full of cages and a dealer who knows what she is selling.

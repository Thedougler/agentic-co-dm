# The Smoulder

> [!narration] Narration
> Twice the thickness of the rolls beside it, and the paper has gone soft and waxy, sweating a little into your fingers.
>
> Draw on it and nothing comes. The leaf is packed so tight the air will not cross it.
>
> Lit at one end, the tip never reddens. It greys over instead, and the grey creeps along the roll on its own.
>
> It puts out more smoke lying on the board than most of the tin does in a smoker's mouth, and the smoke goes sideways instead of up.


*Consumable, Uncommon.*

The batch [[Studio Orsini]] packed at double density by a jammed press, which nobody can smoke and [[nicco-kettley]] sells anyway.

**Rarity:** the effect is a bounded, no-concentration obscurement anyone can trigger. A Common scroll of Fog Cloud is cheaper and weaker in every way that matters at a table, because it needs a caster, a slot, and an unbroken concentration to hold the cloud up. Dust of Disappearance is the fair benchmark, and this sits alongside it: single use, one certain area effect, no attunement.

**PC connection:** this crew's fights turn on control, not hit points, and the two people who could break line of sight with a spell are both already holding one up. A cloud that costs nothing but the throw is a retreat, a rescue, or a rooftop the enemy archers lose.


**[HB]** As an Action, a creature lights the Smoulder and drops it, or throws it up to 20 feet. Smoke rises from where it lands and fills a 15-foot-radius sphere within one round.

The area is heavily obscured for 10 minutes. The smoke is dense and low, and wind of any strength fails to disperse it. It ends early if the roll is smothered in water or under earth.

Edge cases:

- The cloud blocks sight alone. It does not muffle sound, block a smell, or stop anything moving through it.
- A creature with Blindsight, Tremorsense, or Truesight is unaffected by it.
- Nothing happens if the Smoulder is smoked. It will not draw at all, and a smoker gets a lungful of nothing.

**Limitations:** it deals no damage, imposes no condition, and blinds nobody once they step clear of the radius. It cannot be moved after it lands, and a second one dropped inside the first widens nothing.

## Provenance

[[marta-orsini]] scrapped the run when the press jammed, since a roll nobody can draw on is not a blend at all. Nicco Kettley bought the crates through a third party and worked out what the failure was good for himself, which is why the board sells it under a name Orsini never gave it.

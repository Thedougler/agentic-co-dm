# Salt-Drake Bile Draught

> [!narration] Salt-Drake Bile Draught
> A flat green bottle, the liquid inside so dark it reads black until the light goes through the shoulder of the glass. It is cold, and it stays cold in a warm hand, with the cold running up the arm that holds it. Tilt the bottle and the draught moves slower than water, clinging to the glass on the way down. The stopper is a plug of drake hide, and it has begun to swell.

## At a Glance

*Consumable, uncommon.*

The Salt-Drake Bile Draught buys ten minutes of reptile calm: nothing frightens the drinker, and nothing gets a warm answer out of one either.

- **Effect.** Advantage on Wisdom saving throws and immunity to the Frightened condition for 10 minutes.
- **Cost.** `-10 feet` of speed and disadvantage on Charisma checks to persuade or perform, for the same ten minutes.
- **Source.** Pressed and bottled by [[sabine-corvo]] at [[the-live-hold]], from salt-drake hatchlings that did not survive the crossing from the [[verdant-teeth]].

## Properties

**Drink (Bonus Action).** For 10 minutes, the drinker has Advantage on Wisdom saving throws and can't have the Frightened condition.

**The cost.** The drinker's blood runs cold with the dose: speed drops by 10 feet for the duration, and Charisma checks made to persuade or perform have Disadvantage because nothing reaches the face.

**Limits.** One dose. A second dose inside 24 hours does nothing but the speed penalty. It affects neither Intelligence nor Charisma saving throws, and it does not steady a hand or a spell.

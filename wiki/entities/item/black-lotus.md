# Black Lotus

![[aruhe-black-lotus.jpg]]

> [!narration] Narration
> A bloom as wide as a low table has pushed out of wet island growth. Black petals sprawl and cup over moss. Rain sits on them in bright beads. Violet threads glow toward a tight knot of dark filaments at the crown. Under the lowest flaps, old ribs and finger bones show through scorched plants. When magic stirs nearby, the whole flower leans that way.

## Hazard

- **Trigger.** A creature moving within `10 feet` triggers the flower. Touching it or casting a spell within `30 feet` also triggers the flower.
- **Notice.** Make **Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 15`** or **Intelligence (Nature) — `DC 15`** to spot bones beneath the petals and the scorched neighbors. The check also reveals the subtle lean toward magic.
- **Contact.** The lotus snaps shut on one creature within `10 feet`. The target makes a Dexterity saving throw against `DC 15`. On a failure, the lotus restrains the creature inside the bloom. A successful save lets the creature slip free. While trapped, at the start of each of its turns it loses its highest remaining spell slot, and the lotus brightens. If it has no slots and cannot cast, it takes `4d6` necrotic damage instead. After the slots are gone, the damage becomes `6d6` necrotic, and the lotus heals equal to that damage. Spells of 1st level or higher within `30 feet` grant the lotus **Advantage** on its next Ambush or escape contest before the …
- **Careful Passage.** Stay beyond `10 feet`. Do not cast within `30 feet`. Bait the lotus with expendable magic from outside the clamp radius.
- **Counterplay.** Make **Strength (Athletics) — `DC 16`** as an Action to force the petals open from inside. From outside, deal `20` slashing or fire damage to the enclosing petals and tear them. Harvest only after the bloom cannot close: `10 minutes` plus a check with **Intelligence (Arcana) — `DC 16`** or **Wisdom (Survival) — `DC 16`** yields one [[black-lotus-heart]]. On a failure, the creature takes `4d6` force damage and the harvest destroys the Heart.

## Provenance

Feeds on ambient magic the way ordinary plants take light. Strong casting pulls its attention at once. Grung legend names these blooms among the Hungry Isle's killers.

# Chain-Coil Python

> [!narration] Narration
> A snake fills the warmed crate end to end, banded grey on grey and thick through the middle as a forearm. It slides over itself with a dry rasp of scale on board, rocking the stack of crates. Then it stops, raises its head level with the slats, and faces you.

*Tool (Trained Animal), uncommon.*

**Set On.** Open the crate and name a target you can see within 20 feet. The python moves its speed toward that target and makes one attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 feet, and 1d8 Bludgeoning damage. On a hit, the target is Grappled. A Grappled creature can escape with a DC 14 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check made as an action; on a failure it remains Grappled and loses that action. While Grappling, the python does nothing else. It holds on until the target escapes or the python drops to 0 Hit Points. You can call it off with an action.

**One target.** The python takes one target per command and will not change targets while it holds. Returning it to the crate takes 1 minute and both hands, or a fitted [[corvos-catch-pole|catch-pole]]. It can target only Medium or smaller creatures and nothing that flies out of reach. It refuses Fire and deep water, cannot climb a rope, and uses the statistics of a Constrictor Snake. It dies as easily as one. Snake and crate weigh 90 pounds.

## At the Table

The python is sold with its crate and a season of feed from [[Zort's Pits]]. It is twelve feet long, has two years of pit training, and has no venom. Its useful choice is commitment: a handler can set it on one target and hold that target, but cannot redirect it while it grapples. Fire, deep water, flight, and the need to retrieve the animal remain hard counters.

## Connections

- [[Zort]] sells the python through [[Zort's Pits]] and worked its command training over a season.
- [[Zort's Pits]] received eight pythons in the shipment from the Mid-Chain supplier.
- [[corvos-catch-pole|Catch-pole]] is the fitted tool that can return the python to its crate.

## Provenance

The python was delivered to [[Zort's Pits]] in a shipment of eight by a Mid-Chain contact whose name [[Zort]] is selling separately. Zort's goblin workers trained the command over a season. It is sold with its crate and a season of feed.

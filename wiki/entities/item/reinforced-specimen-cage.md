# Reinforced Specimen Cage

> [!narration] Reinforced Specimen Cage
> Iron bands frame tight oak slats, thin enough to flex and thick enough that a claw does not splinter through. The bars are square steel, riveted, set close enough to take a hand and too close for knuckles to turn. A heavy latch with a padlock loop sits at one corner. The floor has a sliding feed tray so food, water, and waste move without opening the door. A dozen thumb-sized air holes ring the upper third, punched through wood and iron together.

## At a Glance

*Tool, common.*

The Reinforced Specimen Cage is expedition gear for moving a living creature — hostile or valuable — over rough ground without opening the door.

- **What it does.** Holds a secured creature against ordinary strength until the latch is opened.
- **Price and weight.** `60 gp`, 45 pounds empty, about 3 feet on each side. Sold at [[spar-and-snare-outfitters|Spar & Snare Outfitters]].
- **Limit.** It is a box, not a prison: it stops claws and muscle, not a spell, a phase, or a creature that shrinks.

## Properties

**Escape.** A creature secured inside cannot escape with a Strength check below `DC 16`.

**Durability.** The cage is AC 14 and 20 hit points against claws and simple melee attacks.

**Latch.** The latch takes a padlock or a rope binding; either is a separate purchase.

**Loading.** The sliding floor tray passes food and water in and waste out without opening the main door. The air holes ring the upper third and admit no hand.

## At the Table

- **Carry a live specimen out.** The cage travels on a pole, a cart, or a strong back. Lock it, feed it through the tray, and do not open the door.
- **Contain something that comes back.** Ordinary Strength will not walk it out under `DC 16`, but claws and simple weapons have to break AC 14 and 20 hit points first.
- **Leave it shut and walk away.** The occupant has water and air but no food beyond what the tray holds, which is a countdown a party that keeps a creature alive has to watch.

# Blind-Hood and Jesses

> [!narration] Blind-Hood and Jesses
> Three leather hoods on a ring, graded from fist-sized to big enough for a boar's skull, each one blind at the front and laced at the throat. The straps under them are soft with use and stiff with salt in the same places, and every brass tongue is worn to a shine on one side only. The largest hood still smells of the animal that wore it: something musky, and under that, meat. Slip a hand inside and the lining is felted flat, packed hard by a skull that pushed against it for a long time.

## At a Glance

*Tool, common.*

The Blind-Hood and Jesses are handler's tack: put a captured beast in the dark and it travels quietly.

- **Effect.** A hooded beast is Blinded, stops attacking, and is easier to lead, load, and calm.
- **Kit.** Three hoods graded by size, with matching ankle jesses, which halve the beast's speed.
- **Made by.** [[sabine-corvo]], who cuts and stitches them in the back of [[the-live-hold|The Live Hold]] and fits every hood to the animal before she sells the animal.

## Properties

**Hood (Action).** Fit the hood over the head of a Beast that is Restrained, Incapacitated, or willing. While hooded, the Beast is Blinded and goes quiet: it will not attack unless it is damaged or an adjacent creature is grappling it. Wisdom (Animal Handling) checks made to lead, load, or calm it have Advantage.

**Jesses.** The ankle straps hobble the Beast's legs and halve its speed.

**Limits.** Beasts of CR 2 or lower, Large or smaller, and nothing with hands. A Beast that takes damage while hooded thrashes free at the end of its next turn. No effect on a creature that can speak.

## At the Table

- **Load a live capture.** Hood first, jesses second, crate third. The hood is what turns an animal from cargo that bites into cargo that rides.
- **Move a mount or companion through a town.** A hooded animal stops drawing attention and stops fighting the lead rope, and the Advantage on Animal Handling covers the loading and the calming.
- **Hood something that can talk.** Nothing happens; the tack takes beasts, and a creature with a voice keeps both its eyes and its plans.
- **Count the sizes.** Three hoods on a ring, fist to boar's skull. A capture bigger than the largest hood is a capture that needs a bigger kit.

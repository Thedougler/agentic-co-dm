# Lamplighter

> [!narration] Narration
> A light arrow has a thin blue glass head full of something that moves when tilted. It glows pale green, bright enough to read a hand by, but gives off no heat. Through the glass the shaft looks wet. Waxed thread wraps the head at a break-line meant to come apart cleanly. In the dark drawer it keeps glowing after the lid shuts, and [[ettore-lupo]] shuts it anyway.

*Weapon (Arrow), uncommon.*

The dearest shaft in [[ettore-lupo]]'s drawer at [[Casa Lupo]], and the only one he warns buyers about. It works just as well on the person holding it.

**Luminous mark.** The arrow is consumed on impact and flies as an ordinary arrow, using an ordinary ranged attack roll. On a hit against a creature, it deals 1d6 Piercing damage and coats the creature in cold luminous rot for 1 hour. The creature sheds Dim Light in a 10-foot radius and glows visibly out to 300 feet in darkness. It cannot succeed on a Dexterity (Stealth) check to Hide. If it turns Invisible, the glow holds its position, so anyone who can see the glow knows its location.

A creature can end the effect early by spending an action to scrub the coating off with water, sand, or cloth, or by fully submerging itself. Rain only thins the coating. A miss or hit against an object still breaks the head; whatever it strikes glows for the same hour. Ooze, water, and slime-bodied creatures shed the coating in 1 round. A creature already glowing gains nothing from a second arrow. The arrow grants no Advantage on attacks and deals no ongoing damage. It does not reveal a creature behind a wall or through a door the shooter cannot see through.

## At the Table

[[ettore-lupo]] keeps Lamplighter in the felt-lined drawer at [[Casa Lupo]] and has sold four in 11 years. [[crissdalynn-khinriss]] can follow a marked runner across a district, even after the runner disappears into a crowd, but must resume ordinary legwork once the glow ends. A glowing person on the [[Mercatura]] can be escorted without anyone keeping a hand on them. [[prospero-morsani]] also sold a quiver at [[the Cabinet of Morsani]] for 200 gp during Session 8.

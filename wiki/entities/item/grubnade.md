# Aruhe - Grubnade

![[attachments/shattered-sea/hazards/aruhe-grubnade.jpg|Grubnade of Aruhe]]

> [!narration] Narration
> A fat cream-green caterpillar longer than a thumb lies heavy across the leaves of a yellow flower. Its glossy body is almost the color of new plant flesh, with soft rings bunched along its back and dark oval marks set into its sides. The black head remains buried among the flower's thick stamens while it chews, slow and steady. Each bite makes the whole body gather and ease, wet-looking skin sliding over the leaf beneath it.

## Hazard

- **Trigger.** Touching or picking up a mature Grubnade detonates it. Damage to the grub or its supporting plant also triggers the blast. Fire, thunder, or bludgeoning damage in its space triggers the blast as well. Nearby footsteps do not trigger it.
- **Notice.** A DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) check spots a mature Grubnade in dense foliage. A DC 13 Intelligence (Nature) check recognizes the swelling grub as a volatile living trap. A DC 15 check reveals that nearby brood chambers and hot blooms may contain several mature Grubnades.
- **Thermal Runaway.** When disturbed, the Grubnade visibly swells and hisses. It detonates at the end of the current turn.
- **Chemical Burst.** Each creature within `5 feet` makes a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, it takes `2d6` fire damage; on a success, it takes half damage. Unattended flammable objects in the area catch fire.
- **Blast-Birth.** Immediately after detonation, the blast hurls `1d4 + 1` juvenile Grubnades 10 to 30 feet in different directions. They land harmlessly in nearby vegetation. Juveniles are Tiny, translucent, and finger-joint sized. They cannot detonate, and each becomes mature after seven days of feeding.
- **Hot Bloom.** Three or more mature Grubnades on one flower can chain disturbance through the bloom. Predators walk around any bloom that ticks.
- **Counterplay.** Leave the flower alone, move the plant without shaking it, use reach or cover before disturbing it, or trigger the burst from outside `5 feet`.

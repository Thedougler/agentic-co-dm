# Slipwater Grease

> [!narration] Narration
> A clay pot sits in the palm, no wider than a fist. The wax has already been cracked once and mashed shut again with a thumb. The fat inside is the grey of cooled tallow, streaked darker. It smells of eel skins and wet rope. Rubbed on, it starts cold and thins until a finger will not stay on a wrist.

Consumable (ointment), Common

Spend 1 minute smearing one pot over your own arms and torso. For the next hour you have Advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks, Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks, and Strength saving throws made to escape or resist a grapple, a restrain, or a shove. The grease is on you, not on a weapon, so a magical hold with no physical grip ignores it. A creature holding you takes no penalty of its own. More than a minute in fresh water ends the hour early. One pot covers one creature. It does not raise Armor Class or attack rolls, and it makes climbing a dry surface worse. 60 gp. Rendered from cheap eel trimmings.

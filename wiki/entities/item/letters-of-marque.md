# Letters of Marque

> [!narration] Letters of Marque
> The sheets are parchment gone soft at the folds, and the top line carries one name in a clerk's hand. A crown seal is pressed into the wax. The edges are nicked and dark where hands have worried them.

## At a Glance

*Plot item (no magical effect), document, common.*

The Letters of Marque are the [[dravosi-crown|Crown]] license that made [[barnaby-rook]] a privateer: paper that turns prize-taking into law and forgery into a hanging matter.

- **What they do.** They name a licensee and authorize taking enemy shipping as prize in the Crown's name, under Crown admiralty law.
- **Worth.** A Crown bond of `30,000 gp` stands behind the commission, which is why a Crown admiralty pays to have the sheets back.
- **Found.** Taken from the cabin of the [[hcs-surety|HCS Surety]] with 45 gp and a blunderbuss; 110 gp and two garnets lay under the floor.

## Properties

**Credentials, not equipment.** No charges, no activation, no saving throw, and no magic. The sheets do nothing on their own; they are read, shown, copied, or sold.

**What they license.** The named holder may take enemy shipping as prize in the Crown's name and must bring the prize before a Crown admiralty court. The license binds the holder to Crown law and covers no ship but the one named.

**Who they name.** [[barnaby-rook]], whose commission is now in other hands. Anyone else carrying them is impersonating Crown authority, which Dravosi courts treat as a capital matter.

**Who buys them.** A Crown admiralty pays a recovery bounty for the sheets; a fence would sell them quietly as forgery stock; only a buyer with a Crown seal could pass them off as his own.

## At the Table

- **Show them as backing.** A Dravosi patrol that reads the top line takes the bearer for Crown-licensed — until it asks which ship the party commands. **Charisma (Deception) — `DC 15`** to carry the bluff past the first question; a failure means seizure of the sheets and a Crown inquiry.
- **Sell them to a fence.** Any dealer in oddities buys them on sight and asks nothing, but pays paper prices for a document that cannot be used in a Crown port.
- **Try to alter the name.** Scraping the clerk's line and re-inking it leaves the parchment thinner than the rest and the wax seal broken. A Crown clerk finds it in one look.
- **Return them to the Crown.** The Crown pays the recovery bounty and asks how the party got them, which is a question with no good answer for a crew that took them off a Crown captain's body.

## Connections

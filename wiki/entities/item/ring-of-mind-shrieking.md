# Ring of Mind Shrieking

> [!narration] Narration
> A plain band of dull, tarnished silver, its surface flat and worn smooth from years of handling. It sits loose and cold on the finger at first, then warms fast, almost uncomfortably, as though it's settling into the skin. The metal never seems to catch the light right.

*Ring, uncommon (requires attunement).*

While wearing this ring, you are immune to magic that allows other creatures to read your thoughts, determine whether you are lying, know your alignment, or know your creature type. Creatures can telepathically communicate with you only if you allow it. If you die while wearing the ring, your soul enters it, unless it already houses a soul, and you can remain in the ring or leave for the afterlife.

The immunity holds because the ring floods your mind with the surface thoughts of everyone nearby the instant it is worn — layered and constant. While worn, you have disadvantage on Concentration saving throws and on Wisdom (Perception) checks, with no save to resist either. Anyone watching can tell something is wrong: you visibly zone out, glassy-eyed, for a second at a time, at random. You cannot control or filter which thoughts you hear, and gain nothing useful from the noise. The disadvantage lasts the entire time the ring is on.

The ring grants no offensive or defensive combat bonus. The Concentration and Perception disadvantage is a pure cost.

## At a Glance

Unclaimed shop stock on [[lavinia-sordi]]'s rack at [[la-cenere]], listed at 50 gp (down from 200 gp). [[delmar-fisk]] tried it on and immediately handed it back without buying.

## At the Table

If anyone puts it on, surface thoughts of everyone nearby flood in at once — mind-reading fails against them, but Concentration and Perception suffer for as long as it stays on, and the glassy-eyed zoning is visible.

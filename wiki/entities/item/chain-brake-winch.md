# Chain-Brake Winch

> [!narration] Narration
> An iron drum the width of a dinner plate sits on a bracket drilled for four bolts, with its crank folded flat. A thumb-sized pawl rides the drum's teeth on a stiff spring. [[kettil-ormsson]] lets a foot of chain run out; the pawl stops the drum dead with a sound like a door bolt. Fresh oil coats the chain and marks the counter.

*Gear (Winch), common.*

**Chain brake.** Bolting the bracket down takes 10 minutes with smith's tools and four bolts into a deck or solid beam. The winch ships with 60 feet of chain. A creature within reach of the crank can take an action to haul up to 800 pounds 20 feet toward the drum. Releasing the crank locks the pawl, so the drum gives back nothing until someone lifts the pawl by hand.

A creature hauling something that resists makes a DC 13 Strength check against a target actively pulling the other way; a failure costs the action with no ground gained. A second pair of hands on the crank adds that weight and avoids the check. The winch frees nobody from the Grappled condition and lifts nothing off a creature; it only wins ground for what the chain already holds. Unbolted, it is 40 pounds of iron with nothing to pull against.

## At the Table

[[kettil-ormsson]] has this winch bolted to the counter edge at [[Ormsson's Fittings]] for customers to try. It is the answer when a [[Sawek]] drags something toward its hole at 20 feet per round: the crew can win the ground back, but only if the winch is secured and someone can reach the crank. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s Strength -1 and disadvantage make that distinction matter when a creature has already grabbed him.

## Connections

- [[kettil-ormsson]] cuts the pawl springs from salvaged barrel hoop and keeps the working sample.
- [[Ormsson's Fittings]] is the current demonstration and sales point.
- [[Sawek]] is the kind of strong underwater threat the winch can resist when properly anchored.
- [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] is especially vulnerable to being hauled by a grappled creature.
- [[reinforced-specimen-cage]] is a cheaper comparable piece of dock machinery.

## Provenance

Yard crews use these winches to walk sprung planking back into a hull one ratchet tooth at a time. [[kettil-ormsson]] makes the pawl springs from salvaged barrel hoop, and no winch of his has ever let go.

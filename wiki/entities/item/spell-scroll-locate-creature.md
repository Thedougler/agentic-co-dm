# Spell Scroll (Locate Creature)

> [!narration] Spell Scroll (Locate Creature)
> The scroll is a single tight roll of vellum, sealed with a plain wax disc pressed by no signet at all. Unrolled, the cipher runs in tight spirals, circling inward toward a small inked thumbprint at the centre. The vellum still carries a faint animal musk, old and dry, like a kennel long since swept out.

## At a Glance

*Scroll, rare.*

The scroll holds one casting of *Locate Creature*, and it wants to be read by a caster who has been close enough to their quarry to know it by name or by species.

- **Effect.** One casting of *Locate Creature* (4th level), then the scroll crumbles to dust.
- **Save DC and attack.** `DC 15`, attack bonus `+7`, for the spell as written on this scroll.
- **Found among.** The prize goods at Vask's Reclaimed Goods in [[sparhold|Sparhold]].

## Properties

**Reading it.** A *Spell Scroll* carries one spell in a mystical cipher. If the spell is on your spell list you can read the scroll and cast from it without providing material components; otherwise the scroll is unintelligible to you. Casting from the scroll takes the spell's normal casting time, and the scroll crumbles to dust once the spell is cast. If the casting is interrupted, the scroll survives.

**Casting above your level.** If *Locate Creature* is on your list but of a higher level than you can cast, make an ability check with your spellcasting ability against `DC 10 + the spell's level`. On a failure the spell vanishes from the scroll with no other effect.

**This copy.** A 4th-level scroll holding a single casting of *Locate Creature*, with a spell save DC of `15` and an attack bonus of `+7`.

**Familiarity.** The caster must name or describe a creature they know, or a species they have seen up close. Without that, the scroll cannot work for them even if they can read it.

## At the Table

- **Find one person in a city.** The scroll answers the question no amount of asking does, and it costs the scroll. Save it for the quarry that matters.
- **Read it without the familiarity.** Nothing happens: the spell needs a name, a description, or a species the caster has seen up close, and a party that has only heard of the target cannot use the scroll on them.
- **Hand it to the wrong caster.** A caster with *Locate Creature* off their list reads cipher and nothing else. The scroll is dead weight in most hands and priceless in the right one.
- **Use it under pressure.** The casting takes the spell's normal time, so the scroll is a plan rather than an answer to a fight.

# Venom-Cut Poultice

> [!narration] Venom-Cut Poultice
> Grey paste is folded into a sailcloth square and tied with waxed twine. A watch tally is chalked on the cloth. The edges have dried stiff; the middle still gives under a thumb. From a foot away it smells of chewed root and slaked lime, sharp enough to sting the throat. The chalk date is eleven days old.

## At a Glance

*Consumable (poultice), common.*

The Venom-Cut Poultice is a field dressing that stops ordinary venom in one press, made in batches for the people most often bitten.

- **Effect.** As an Action, ends a Poisoned condition caused by a poison or a creature's venom and grants Advantage on poison saving throws for an hour.
- **Worth.** `50 gp`; [[sparhold|Sparhold]]'s watch keeps a crate of them behind every post.
- **Limit.** Mundane venom only: a spell or other magic keeps its hold, and one poultice covers one creature.

## Properties

Single use. As an Action, press it to a wound on yourself or a creature within 5 feet. If that creature is poisoned by a poison or by a creature's venom, the condition ends, and the creature has advantage on saving throws against poison for the next hour.

**Edge cases.** It does nothing for a poisoned condition inflicted by a spell or other magic. A second poultice inside the hour neither stacks the advantage nor extends it.

**Limitations.** It restores no hit points, ends no disease, and neutralises nothing still sitting in a vial. Once the twine is cut, the paste dries out inside a minute. [[sparhold|Sparhold]] has made these since the [[grung-clans|Grung]] raids started climbing out of the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]], and the watch's crate comes off the same batch the harvest crews carry.

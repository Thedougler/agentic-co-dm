# Deadman's Cleat

> [!narration] Narration
> A bronze cleat the length of a hand is pitted grey where seawater sat in the metal. Two bolt holes at its base are torn wide, as though it came off a hull with the hull still holding it. A line remains knotted through the horn in a diver's knot, cut short a foot out. Worn on a belt, it sits cold against the hip however long the day runs.

*Wondrous item, uncommon.*

**Hold Fast.** While wearing the cleat on a belt, sash, or harness, you have Advantage on saving throws and ability checks made to resist being shoved, knocked Prone, or moved against your will, including by a spell, wave, or creature's forced movement.

The cleat does nothing against a Grappled condition already holding you; it applies only to an attempt to move you while Grappled. A wearer knocked Prone by damage rather than an effect gets no save to which the cleat can grant Advantage. It grants no bonus to a roll's number, ends no condition, and cannot keep you upright when your own footing is the problem. A wearer who is not standing on something has nothing to hold.

## At the Table

The cleat is on the counter at [[Ninka's Findings]], held by [[ninka-solvei]]. The cut diver's knot tells a sharp observer that it was severed from the far end. It matters most to a wearer who expects shoves, waves, forced movement, or a fall from a dangerous ledge; it does not solve a grapple or create footing in open air. [[perrin-black-jaw]]'s Strength -1 and disadvantage make the protection especially relevant when grapplers and shovers target him.

## Provenance

The cleat was cut from a hull section recovered from the [[verdant-teeth]] by a wreck-diving crew during its third haul of the season, bolts and all. [[ninka-solvei]] took it in with the scrap and priced it after seeing the knot.

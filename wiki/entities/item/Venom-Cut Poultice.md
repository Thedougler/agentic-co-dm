# Venom-Cut Poultice

> [!narration] Narration
> Grey paste is folded into a sailcloth square and tied with waxed twine. A watch tally is chalked on the cloth. The edges have dried stiff; the middle still gives under a thumb. From a foot away it smells of chewed root and slaked lime, sharp enough to sting the throat. The chalk date is eleven days old.

Consumable (poultice), Common

As an Action, press it to a wound on yourself or a creature within 5 feet. If that creature is Poisoned by a poison or by a creature's venom, the condition ends, and the creature has Advantage on saving throws against poison for the next hour. It does nothing for a Poisoned condition from a spell or other magic. A second poultice inside the hour does not stack or extend the Advantage. It restores no hit points, ends no disease, and does not neutralize a vial still sitting shut. Cut the twine and the paste dries inside a minute. [[Sparhold]] has batched these since [[Grung]] raids started climbing out of the [[Verdant Teeth]]. 50 gp.

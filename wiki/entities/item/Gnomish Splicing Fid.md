# Gnomish Splicing Fid

> [!narration] Narration
> A tapered steel spike the length of a forearm, the grip wrapped in tarred cord worn smooth.
>
> Etched lines run the shaft at quarter-inch intervals, each one a cordage gauge cut deep enough to catch a thumbnail.
>
> A brass catch at the base releases a folding blade no longer than a thumb, honed thin enough to part hemp without dragging.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **Gnomish Splicing Fid [HB].** A creature proficient with this tool has advantage on ability checks made to splice, tie, or repair rope and cordage, including checks to bind a creature with rope.
>
> The fold-out blade is too short and light for combat (1 piercing damage, no proficiency bonus applies). The etched gauge allows precise measurement of cordage diameter without a separate tool.
>
> **Limitations:** the advantage applies only to rope and cordage work — not to nets, chains, or wire. The blade dulls after ten cuts through wet hemp and must be stropped.

## Provenance

The gnomish crew of the [[Nimmik Vollask]] makes these in their below-decks workshop. Each fid carries a stamped mark on the butt — a gnomish glyph that none of the crew will explain. [[Cotter Foss|Cotter]] says they have been making them longer than she has worked aboard.

## Properties

**Compel.** The Gold caste applies a seal to a Grung against their will, and it fuses to the skin as a gold disc. It replaces the bearer's will with the order it carries, like a permanent *modify memory*. The compelled Grung is not a puppet. They believe the order is their own deepest conviction, argue for it in their own words, and pursue it with all their wit for the rest of their life. The rule for applying a whole seal is on [[solanges-authority-seal]].

**Spent.** When the bearer dies, the seal has no will left to hold and goes inert. That is why every seal on [[aruhe]] is spent. No compelled Grung is on Aruhe now.

## At the Table

- **Reading a seal.** Each seal carries its bearer's whole order chain in Gold-caste script, oldest line first ([[two-grave-orders]]). [[jean-claude-tabarnack]] reads it with no roll. Anyone else reads it with **Intelligence (Investigation)** `DC 13`.
- **What the lines say.** "Report what lies inland." "Replace the parties that stopped reporting." "Find the two graves beneath the memorial tree and destroy them." The seals of the burn expedition add: "If the forest stands between, burn it."
- **What a seal does not say.** No seal names [[hinewai]], the Death Bloom, or the officer who gave the order.
- **Hinewai.** Every Grung she has seen on her island wore a gold seal on the skin and truly wanted her graves destroyed, so she assumes every Grung on Aruhe is compelled.

## History

A seal compels a Grung to believe one order forever, and the Gold caste uses seals for whatever it needs a Grung to believe. On Aruhe, it used them to send Grung inland onto an island every Grung is taught from birth to avoid.

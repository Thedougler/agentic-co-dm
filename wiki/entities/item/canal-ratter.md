# Canal Ratter

> [!narration] Narration
> A brindled terrier the size of a loaf of bread, wire-haired, one ear folded permanently over.
>
> It sits facing the water side of the room and does not turn when you approach.
>
> A strip of fur along its spine has gone thin where a harness rides it.
>
> Then its whole body goes stiff, ears flat, and it makes no sound at all.


*Tool (Trained Animal), Common.* A working dog bred in the [[le-paludi]] canals to hunt rats out of bilges and grain stores. [[Zort]] sells them broke to a single handler. Statistically a mastiff with a smaller frame and worse manners.


**[HB] Water Watch.** The ratter goes rigid and silent when any creature larger than a rat enters water within 100 feet of it, day or night, seen or unseen. A handler who has worked the dog for a day reads the signal on sight. A creature that came through water cannot surprise anyone standing within 10 feet of the ratter.

**[HB] Bad at Everything Else.** The dog fights like a mastiff and dies like one. It takes no other command reliably, and it shows nothing at all for a creature coming over dry ground.

**Limitations:** water only, out to 100 feet. The signal is silence, so it does nothing for a sleeper or for anyone who is not watching the dog. It will not enter a fight it did not start.

## Provenance

Bred in the [[le-paludi]] canal warrens and sold out of [[Zort's Pits]], where [[Zort]] keeps a litter working the near cages as a live demonstration of the shop's cheapest useful stock.

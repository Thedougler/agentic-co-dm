# Canal Ratter

> [!narration] Canal Ratter
> A brindled terrier the size of a loaf of bread, wire-haired, with one ear folded permanently over. It sits facing the water side of the room and does not turn when you approach. A strip of fur along its spine has gone thin where a harness rides it. Then its whole body goes stiff, its ears flatten, and it makes no sound at all.

## At a Glance

*Tool (trained animal), common.*

The Canal Ratter is a watch-dog for water: it cannot shout, so it goes silent instead, and the silence is the alarm.

- **Effect.** Goes rigid and silent when any creature larger than a rat enters water within 100 feet, and creatures that came through water cannot surprise anyone near it.
- **Sold by.** [[zort]] at [[zorts-pits|Zort's Pits]], broke to a single handler.
- **Type.** A [[le-paludi]] canal terrier bred to hunt rats out of bilges and grain stores. Statistically a mastiff with a smaller frame and worse manners.

## Properties

**Water Watch.** The ratter goes rigid and silent when any creature larger than a rat enters water within 100 feet of it, day or night, seen or unseen. A handler who has worked the dog for a day reads the signal on sight. A creature that came through water cannot surprise anyone standing within 10 feet of the ratter.

**Bad at everything else.** The dog fights like a mastiff and dies like one. It takes no other command reliably, and it shows nothing at all for a creature coming over dry ground.

**Limits.** Water only, out to 100 feet. The signal is silence, so it does nothing for a sleeper or for anyone who is not watching the dog, and it will not enter a fight it did not start.

## At the Table

- **Keep watch on the water side.** One dog covers a bilge, a canal frontage, or a moored hull, and the handler only has to watch whether the animal has gone stiff.
- **Post it in a boat's hold.** Anything entering the water within 100 feet — a swimmer, a wererat, a drowned thing — trips the signal, which is the one approach a dark hold cannot show.
- **Set it on rats.** That is the trade it was bred for, and it does it without a command.
- **Rely on it for anything else.** It does not bark, does not track, does not guard dry ground, and does not warn anyone who is asleep.

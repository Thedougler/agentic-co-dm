# Grimaldi's Waking Salts

> [!narration] Narration
> A tin the width of a palm, its lid crimped on crooked so it never seals flat.
>
> Inside, grey salt shot through with green flecks, pressed down under a disc of waxed paper and scored across in four lines, a dose to a quarter.
>
> The smell arrives a foot ahead of the tin, ammonia and crushed peppercorn, sharp enough to sting the back of the throat.
>
> Tip it and the salt hisses against the paper, loose already in the corner where somebody dug a quarter out.


*Consumable (Salts), Uncommon.* [[ilaria-grimaldi]] mixes these for ship's surgeons who have to get a concussed deckhand back on his feet before the next wave takes the rail, and sells them to anyone who asks plainly.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **Grimaldi's Waking Salts [HB].** A tin holds four doses. As an Action, break one dose under the nose of a creature within 5 feet that is Charmed, Frightened, Incapacitated, Paralyzed, or Stunned by an effect that allows a saving throw to end it. That creature immediately repeats that saving throw with advantage, ending the effect on itself on a success.
>
> **Edge cases:** the target need not be conscious or willing. A dose works on a creature at 0 hit points, though the salts neither stabilise it nor restore hit points. A second dose against the same effect grants no further save.
>
> **Limitations:** an effect with no save to repeat does not answer to the salts. Neither does poison, disease, or any condition that damage inflicted. An open tin goes flat inside ten days.

## Provenance

The recipe is [[ilaria-grimaldi|Grimaldi]]'s own, worked up over fifteen years of hauling crews out of the water at [[Kalowe]] and pressed into tins by the dozen. The [[Takowan]] and the other harbour crews buy them by the crate, so she keeps the price flat and never asks who a tin is for.

# Slipwater Grease

> [!narration] Slipwater Grease
> A clay pot sits in the palm, no wider than a fist. The wax has already been cracked once and mashed shut again with a thumb, and it will not take a third pressing. The fat inside is the grey of cooled tallow, streaked darker. It smells of eel skins and wet rope. Rubbed on, it starts cold and thins until a finger will not stay on a wrist.

## At a Glance

*Consumable (ointment), common.*

Slipwater Grease buys an hour of slipperiness for anyone who expects to be closed on and held.

- **Effect.** Advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks, Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks, and Strength saving throws made to escape or resist a grapple, a restrain, or a shove.
- **Use.** One pot, one creature; 1 minute to apply; lasts 1 hour, or ends early in fresh water.
- **Price.** `60 gp` where duellists and pit fighters buy it, priced by how many bouts end with a champion pinned instead of cut.

## Properties

Rendered from eel fat and boat-slip pitch. Single use. Applying it to your own arms and torso takes 1 minute. For the next hour you have advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks, Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks, and Strength saving throws made to escape or resist a grapple, a restrain, or a shove.

**Edge cases.** The grease is on you, not on your weapon, so it grants nothing against a magical hold with no physical grip. A creature grappling you takes no penalty of its own; you simply get out easier.

**Limitations.** It ends early after more than a minute in fresh water, and one pot covers one creature. It grants no bonus to AC, to attack rolls, or to climbing a dry surface, which it makes worse.

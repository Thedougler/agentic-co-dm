# Slow Burn

> [!narration] Narration
> Longer than the others and thicker through the middle, wound along its whole length with a dark thread sunk into the paper instead of lying on top of it.
>
> Lit, it holds a coal instead of a flame, and the ember creeps down the thread a fraction at a time.
>
> The smoke comes off heavy and low. It smells of wet rope and warm tar, and it pools at chest height instead of climbing.
>
> An hour on, the ash has not fallen once. It stands off the end in a grey column, still holding the spiral of the thread.


*Consumable, Uncommon.*

Shelf stock at [[La Brace]], and the blend [[Rufio Segalla]] sells most of to people who work with their hands full.

**Rarity:** the Uncommon tier fits. Necklace of Adaptation sits at Rare and removes the problem permanently instead of improving one roll, and it spends an attunement slot doing that. Cloak of Elvenkind sits closer, granting one advantage in one circumstance to anyone who puts it on. An hour of the same shape out of a consumable stays under both.

**PC connection:** two casters carry the crew's Concentration and neither one holds it well. Catarina saves at +5 and [[Perrin Black-Jaw|Perrin]] at +3, so one focused hit ends [[Faerie Fire]] or [[Hex]] where it stands. An hour bought before a door opens is the cheapest answer either of them has.

## Mechanics

**[HB]** Lighting Slow Burn and taking the first breath costs an Action. For 1 hour afterward, the smoker has advantage on Constitution saving throws made to maintain Concentration.

The cigarette burns the full hour on its own, and nobody gets to snuff it early and light it again later. Snuffing it ends the effect for good.

Edge cases:

- The advantage covers Concentration saves alone. The smoker rolls every other Constitution save flat.
- It does nothing about effects that end Concentration without a save, such as being [[incapacitated]] or casting a second Concentration spell.
- A second Slow Burn smoked inside the hour does nothing at all, and the hours neither stack nor extend.

**Limitations:** no bonus to attack rolls, damage rolls, AC, or any save outside Concentration, no protection against automatic Concentration loss, and no help at all to anyone but the smoker.

## Provenance

[[Rufio Segalla]] finishes every blend himself in [[La Brace]]'s back room, working from base compound bought by standing order from [[Marta Orsini]] at [[Studio Orsini]]. The thread is the one part he does not buy from her, and it accounts for most of what this blend costs.

# Doorway Snare Coil

> [!narration] Doorway Snare Coil
> A hand-length bundle of wire, coiled tight and bound with twine, with a bent iron peg pushed through the middle. The wire is braided from strands finer than hair, and it holds every bend it has ever been given. Free an end and the coil comes alive in the hands, wanting to spring back to its own shape. The peg has been driven into stone before; its head is mushroomed over and cold.

## At a Glance

*Tool, common.*

The Doorway Snare Coil is a one-shot trap in a pocket: a minute's work turns a doorway into something that catches the first thing through it.

- **Effect.** Restrains and knocks Prone the first Large or smaller creature through a space up to 10 feet wide.
- **Weight and price.** 1 pound; sold by the bundle across the counter at [[the-live-hold|The Live Hold]].
- **Held by.** [[sabine-corvo]] rigs one across the Hold's rear exit every night.

## Properties

**Rig (1 minute).** Set the coil across a doorway, corridor, or gangplank up to 10 feet wide. Spotting the wire takes a passive Perception of 15, or a **Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 13`** check to search for it.

**Spring.** The first Large or smaller creature to move through the space without stepping over the wire makes a **Dexterity saving throw — `DC 13`**. On a failure it has the Prone and Restrained conditions.

**Escape.** A Restrained creature escapes with a **Strength (Athletics) — `DC 13`** check. The wire is cut by the attempt, which spends the coil.

**Limitations.** One creature, one spring. Nothing Huge or larger is caught, and anything that flies over the doorway never touches the wire. Resetting it takes another minute and uncut wire.

## At the Table

- **Cover a retreat.** A minute at a doorway buys the party a round against pursuit, and the catch is loud enough to hear from the next room.
- **Catch a runner.** Rigging the far door before a conversation turns means whoever bolts through it drops and stays there.
- **Check the room before entering.** A passive Perception of 15 or a search finds the wire; anything less walks into it. The dungeon's own traps are the party's tools here.
- **Plan for the second one.** One spring, then the coil is spent. A party that rigs a single door leaves the rest of the corridor open.

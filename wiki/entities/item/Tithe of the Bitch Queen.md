# Tithe of the Bitch Queen

The [[Tithe of the Bitch Queen]] is a separate Sunken Crown hoard: storm-taken jewels, untarnished gold, and statues in tidal caves reachable only at the decade's lowest tides. Taking a piece puts [[Umberlee]]'s attention on it until it touches saltwater again.

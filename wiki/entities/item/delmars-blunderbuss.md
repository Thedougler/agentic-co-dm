# Delmar's Blunderbuss

> [!narration] Narration
> Catarina twirls a small coin between two fingers and presses it flat against the barrel. It sticks, and glowing marks spread across the metal like veins of fire. The air hums with magical energy. Her words dissolve into the surge of power.
>
> A clamp folds around the breech with a sharp *click*. Light runs down the barrel, crackling faintly, and pools into a magazine that appears underneath, loaded and ready. Metal slides home with a decisive *clank*. It clicks into place.

*[[Weapon]] (Blunderbuss), Uncommon.*

[[catarina-davirelli]] cast this spell during a fight in the [[Calveno Sewer Magazines]]. [[delmar-fisk]] carries it over his shoulder with four pistols. He used to stop and reload after each shot. Now the magazine loads itself.

## Mechanics

**[HB]** +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls.

> [!mechanic]
> **Self-Loading.** The magazine reloads by itself after each shot. You don't use an action to reload. The normal reload stops while the magic lasts.

**Limitations.**

<!-- vale CampaignOS.InventedMystery = NO -->

No one knows how the magazine works or when it stops.

*DM*: you don't know what this does, but it looks great.

It needs a ruling.

## Provenance

Delmar brought this gun, four pistols, and The Duchess to a fight against [[vashu-the-weeping-veil|Vashu]] and [[Grung]] guards in the Calveno Sewer Magazines.

In the middle of the fight, Catarina cast magic on it. The spell gave it a plus-one bonus and a self-reloading magazine.

It may be the same gun called "The Duchess" on Delmar's own page. This needs a ruling to confirm.

## Deliberate Silences

- **Magazine mechanism and duration.** How the enchanted magazine works and how long the enchantment lasts remain intentionally unresolved pending a future table ruling.

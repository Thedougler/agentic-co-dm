# Delmar's Blunderbuss

> [!narration] Narration
> A small coin pressed flat against the barrel leaves glowing marks that spread across the metal like veins of fire. A clamp folds around the breech with a sharp click. Light runs down the barrel and pools into a magazine beneath it, loaded and ready. Metal slides home with a decisive clank.

*Weapon (Blunderbuss), uncommon.*

**Enchanted shot.** You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon.

**Self-loading.** After you fire the weapon, its magazine reloads itself. You do not use an action to reload. The normal reload requirement stops while the magic lasts. The duration of this property and the way the magazine works remain unresolved pending a table ruling.

## At the Table

[[delmar-fisk]] carries the blunderbuss over his shoulder with four pistols. Before [[catarina-davirelli]] enchanted it, he stopped to reload after each shot; now the magazine loads itself. The weapon entered play during the fight in the [[Calveno Sewer Magazines]] against [[vashu-the-weeping-veil|Vashu]] and [[Grung]] guards. Its visible coin-work and glowing marks show Catarina's intervention.

## Hidden Properties

No one knows how the magazine works or when the enchantment stops. The self-loading effect therefore has no established end point until the table rules it. The weapon may be the same gun called "The Duchess" on Delmar's page, but that identity is unresolved.

## Connections

- [[catarina-davirelli]] cast the enchantment during the Sewer Magazines fight.
- [[delmar-fisk]] is the current bearer.

## Provenance

Delmar brought the gun, four pistols, and The Duchess to a fight against [[vashu-the-weeping-veil|Vashu]] and [[Grung]] guards in the [[Calveno Sewer Magazines]]. During the fight, [[catarina-davirelli]] cast magic on it, giving it a +1 bonus and a self-loading magazine.

# Solange's Authority Seal

> [!narration] Narration
> A signet ring, red-caste work, made from metal heavier than gold. The face has worn smooth except for one deep-cut symbol that won't dull. It looks sharper than the rest, as if pressed into stone a thousand times.

*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement).*

**Caste command.** As an action, speak a single word of command to target one [[Grung]] creature you can see within 60 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it follows the command on its next turn, exactly as the *command* spell. Only Grung creatures respond to the seal. Once per long rest.

The seal still works on a creature immune to charmed or frightened (it imposes neither condition) unless that immunity covers compulsion. It compels any Grung of red caste or lower (red, purple, blue, or green) — everyone but the gold ruling caste. It will not work if another creature already controls the Grung (charmed or dominated).

No effect on non-Grung. No bonus to attack, damage, saving throws, or AC. The target knows what happened; the compulsion is not hidden, so others may seek revenge.

Grung earn red-caste authority through action, never by vote or birth. The ring's compulsion is that authority made portable: it pulls on the belief every Grung grows up trusting, that a red-caste order gets obeyed. A non-Grung has never heard that voice, and gives the ring nothing to catch hold of.

## At a Glance

Recovered from Primary Chamber rubble after the Detonation and identified in the raid's aftermath. Carried by the crew. [[jean-claude-tabarnack]] is the only party member who reads red-caste authority markings on sight.

## At the Table

Once per long rest, spend the action to force a visible Grung within 60 feet to obey a one-word *command* (DC 15 Wisdom) — useful against Solange's soldiers, and personal for Jean-Claude if the seal is his sister's operation's mark.

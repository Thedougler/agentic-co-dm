# Barb-Line Harpoon

> [!narration] Narration
> A harpoon head the length of a forearm carries four barbs swept back from its point, filed bright where the rest of the iron has gone grey. Sixty feet of light chain runs from the socket and coils on a peg, oiled black. Its last link opens into a hook, and two hammer blows shut it around a rail. Lifted, it carries all its weight at the point and wants to go tip first.

*Weapon (Harpoon), common.*

**Barb and chain.** This martial thrown weapon has a range of 20/60 and deals 1d6 Piercing damage. On a hit against a Large or smaller creature, the barbs set and the chain pays out behind it.

Shackle the far end to a rail or driven bolt, and the hit creature cannot move more than 60 feet from that anchor. The creature can work the head free with an action and a DC 13 Strength check, or cut the chain by dealing 10 damage to it; the chain has AC 15 and is immune to Poison and Psychic damage. A boat anchors the chain no better than its own weight, so the heavier of the boat and creature wins that pull. A second harpoon brings its own chain and check. A Huge or larger creature ignores the barb and takes only the damage.

The harpoon adds nothing to attack or damage rolls and imposes no condition. A harpooned target fights at full strength and can haul the anchor point instead of being hauled itself.

## At the Table

The harpoon is on the chain wall at [[Ormsson's Fittings]], held by [[kettil-ormsson]]. It is rigger's gear for fishers and crews that need to keep a swimmer, ray, or other Large creature near a boat or rail. Its real choice is commitment: the thrower gains control of distance but must keep the anchor secure and accept that a strong target can pull back.

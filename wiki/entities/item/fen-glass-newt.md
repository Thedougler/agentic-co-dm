# Fen-Glass Newt

> [!narration] Narration
> A newt the length of a thumb hangs in a jar of canal water, the color of wet glass all the way through. Its ribs and dark spine show plainly, and the water carries the flat green smell of a still canal. It turns toward whoever speaks every time, without hurry. Tap the jar and it opens its mouth wide, but nothing comes out.

*Tool (Trained Animal), uncommon.*

**Shriek.** While you carry the newt's jar on your person, the newt shrieks the instant a creature attempts to Charm or Frighten you. When you fail a saving throw against being Charmed or Frightened, the shriek lets you reroll that saving throw once; you must use the new roll. After it buys a reroll, it buys no other until the newt has spent a full day in still water.

**Clear-water tell.** The newt turns milk-white and stops moving while a creature within 30 feet is Charmed or maintains Concentration on an Enchantment. Anyone looking at the jar can see the change. The jar must hang where you can hear it; a newt buried in a pack at the bottom of a hold does nothing. The shriek carries about 100 feet and does not care who hears it. A newt out of water dies in 1 hour. The jar and newt weigh 5 pounds.

## At the Table

[[Zort]] keeps the newts on the desk at [[Zort's Pits]] because they are the only stock that sells itself: a customer speaks, and the newt turns to look at them. It needs canal water rather than clean water, a fortnight of feed, and survives about 2 years in captivity. The shriek buys one second chance, while the milk-white body warns anyone watching that a nearby Enchantment is already active.

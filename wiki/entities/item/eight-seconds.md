# Eight Seconds

> [!narration] Narration
> The cigarette is thin and pale, its paper wound tighter than anything else on Rufio's shelf. A match brings it to the tip, and the paper catches in a bright line racing away from the fingers holding it. Smoke rises in one steady ribbon and thins to nothing before shoulder height. The last third burns fast; the ember eats the final inch while the fingers remain cool.

*Consumable, rare.*

**Eight-second burst.** Lighting Eight Seconds and taking its first breath costs a Bonus Action. It affects only the smoker and burns through the end of the smoker's next turn. On the turn you light it and on your next turn, you gain one extra action. You can spend that extra action only on the Attack action (one weapon attack only), Dash, Disengage, Hide, or Utilize. The cigarette is consumed when it burns out.

At the end of your second turn, you fall Prone and cannot take Reactions until the end of your following turn. The extra action is not a Bonus Action and cannot be used to cast a spell, take a Bonus Action, or make a second weapon attack through Extra Attack. You fall Prone whether or not you used the extra actions. Lighting a second cigarette while the first burns does nothing and wastes the second cigarette.

## At the Table

[[rufio-segalla]] rolls Eight Seconds to order at [[La Brace]], a [[Velo Quarter]] smoking room in [[calven-and-calveno]]. He never keeps it on the shelf. Buying one requires sitting in the room for an hour, and Rufio names the price afterward. The cigarette never appears on [[La Gatta]]'s board. For [[jean-claude-tabarnack]], it buys two rounds to disappear before anyone works out what they watched.

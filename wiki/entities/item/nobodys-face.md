# Nobody's Face

> [!narration] Narration
> The paper is the flat grey of a wet flagstone, unmarked, with no printer's stamp or wax at either end. Nothing comes off it; under your nose it smells less than the surrounding air. The leaf gives under your thumb like felt. Lit, it sends a thin line of scentless smoke to chin height, where it stops.

*Consumable, uncommon.* A run from [[Studio Orsini]] bound with the wrong fixative, taking the scent out of the blend and putting something else in.

**Spoiled memory.** Lighting the cigarette and taking the first breath costs an action. For 1 hour afterward, every creature that sees the smoker loses memory of the smoker's face as soon as it looks away. An observer who breaks line of sight for a full round cannot later describe, name from a description, pick out, or point out the smoker. Magic that reads the observer's memory finds the same blank.

A creature that already knew the smoker well before the hour began recognizes them normally. Keeping the smoker in unbroken sight defeats the effect, since the blend spoils memory but does not alter vision. The effect touches nothing the smoker leaves behind: a written name, dropped glove, or familiar voice survives. The item grants no bonus to checks and covers nobody except the smoker; witnesses remember what the smoker did, minus the face.

## At the Table

[[nicco-kettley]] sells board stock at [[La Gatta]] for 90 gp under the Seconds discount. He never asks who wants one or why. A buyer can smoke it before a public purchase on [[the Bridge]]'s bottom tier so the tier remembers the deal but not which face carried the blend away. Someone who keeps watching the smoker continuously defeats the effect. [[jean-claude-tabarnack]] knows the handler crowd reads faces both ways.

## Provenance

The mis-bound run came from [[marta-orsini]]'s benches. A grader rejected it because the scentless blend defeats ordinary testing, and no licensed room burns an ungraded run. [[nicco-kettley|Nicco Kettley]] took the batch entire and has never suggested a use for it to a customer.

## At a Glance

*Wondrous item, artifact (requires attunement by a creature that has knowingly risked its life to save another)*

The Fate Spinner lets its holder watch and hear one person they know, anywhere on the same plane, and quietly lend that person luck.

- **Held by.** [[crissdalynn-khinriss]], as her meditation focus. It bonded to her when she risked her life to save [[delmar-fisk]] in the wreck of the [[red-lady]].
- **Wanted by.** [[talon-vantyrus]], for its tie to the [[soul-incarnate]] transformation technique; he sent [[talon-skarn]] to take it. [[Countless]] hunts every spinner it can reach.

## Properties

**Vision.** Once per day, the attuned holder can take a Magic action to spin the Spinner and choose one creature they know personally on the same plane, at any distance. For 30 minutes, or until the holder dismisses it, the holder sees and hears what that creature sees and hears. It needs no Concentration. The Spinner cannot open another vision until the next dawn.

**Borrowed luck.** While the vision lasts, whenever the target makes an ability check or saving throw, the holder rolls a d20 and the target uses it as a second die: the target has Advantage on that roll. The target does not learn why.

**Limits.** The vision gives sight and hearing only. The holder cannot speak through it, cannot aid a fight the holder is in, and gains nothing on their own rolls or defenses. The holder and the target cannot be in the same place. The holder can keep only one vision open at a time. A creature the holder does not know personally, or one on another plane, cannot be chosen, and a target that leaves the plane ends the vision.

## At the Table

- **Seen.** A thumb-sized, four-sided clear quartz top.
- **Held.** Cool in a warm hand. It ticks faintly when spun.
- **Active.** Coin-sized pools of colored light across the surface beneath it, and a spin far longer than its size allows.
- **Unseen.** Wrapped or pocketed, it gives off no sign at all.
- **Concealment.** A pouch, wrap, or closed hand hides it. Keeping it hidden from someone searching for it, as [[talon-skarn]] does, is **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** `DC 15`. Passing it to another carrier unseen is **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** against the watcher's passive Perception.
- **The choice.** Each day the holder decides whether a vision is worth a Magic action and a flash of colored light from a hunted object.
- **Session 12.** Skarn reads a carrier by her hands, hips, and straps, cuts at gear until the Spinner shows, then pulls it loose with his chain. He tries at dawn at [[river-slack-basin]], and again at dusk at [[the-pantry]] while Invisible.

## Secrets

- **Older than the Sentinels' rule.** The [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie]] record without conclusion and never intervene. The Spinner watches and acts, because it comes from what the order practiced before it swore off intervention. The founding documents of the [[soul-incarnate]] reference the same old tradition without explaining it. The party learns this at the [[high-eyrie]] or from [[master-kyzil]].
- **Several spinners exist.** Vantyrus carries one and reads nearby possibilities through it. Kyzil gave another to Crissdalynn. No one in the world knows who made them or how many there are.
- **Artifact properties.** Its minor properties are its cool touch, long spin, colored light, and glass tick. Its major property is the vision and borrowed luck. Its cost is that the holder carries an object that hunters can find.
- **Destruction.** Ordinary force cannot mark it. Two spinners set spinning face to face both shatter, and no other way breaks one.

## Connections

- [[master-kyzil]] gave it to Crissdalynn at the [[high-eyrie]], before her pilgrimage to [[drowned-maw]], without knowing what he was handing her.
- [[red-lady]] went down with it aboard, and Crissdalynn's rescue of Delmar in that wreck bonded it to her.
- [[Grung]] watched her spin it to calm a Grung child in Session 06.
- [[talon-skarn]] went for it on her last watch in Session 11, spent one Legendary Resistance, and left it Held.
- [[Countless]] hunts carriers along pilgrim routes, grey markets, and Sentinel waystations.

# The Mangroves

![[aruhe-the-mangroves.jpg|The Mangroves, tidal forest of roots and black water]]

## Look

Mangrove trunks stand packed in black water, their roots forming cages, arches, spears, and tangled walls. The canopy seals overhead and turns daylight green. Ghost plums hang in fresher pockets, while dark Rotheart clusters cling to vinewood above channels. Surf sounds close beyond the roots, but remains unreachable.

## Situation now

The Mangroves are the southern end of [[Aruhe - The Marshes]], where freshwater forest becomes tidal swamp. From offshore they look like navigable seams, but channels narrow, become shallow, or terminate in roots. The known landing remains [[Aruhe - Beach|Western Landing]].

## Moves that change the scene

- Search from offshore with Wisdom (Perception) or Wisdom (Survival) DC 15; a seam is not a landing route.
- Enter by canoe with Dexterity (Water Vehicles) DC 15, accepting that tide may strand the hull inside.
- Enter from the Marshes, where open water disappears beneath intertwined roots.
- Wade apparently shallow water with Wisdom (Survival) DC 15; on failure, Strength (Athletics) DC 13 pulls a swallowed leg free.
- Move beneath low branches without disturbing [[Aruhe - Snakewood]], and avoid [[Aruhe - Spiritpollen]].
- Treat the root maze as Difficult Terrain. Fire, cutting vegetation, and loud movement are slow, exhausting, and conspicuous.

## Presence

[[Aruhe - Crown Squid]] uses the upper canopy. Snakewood hangs above channels, while Bloodhawks hunt tidal openings but avoid closed canopy and occupied water. Insects, crabs, scavengers, and semi-aquatic predators occupy the mud, roots, and black channels.

## Connections

The Mangroves transition inland to the Marshes and south toward the ocean. They have no practical shoreline route to Western Landing and can be observed offshore from [[Uncertainty]].

# Oleandro Fuschi

![[oleandro-fuschi-banner]]

**Wants:** keep [[Ponte Bassa]] the only source for ship movements through [[Calveno]], but captains now falsify their manifests.

**Quote:** "I remember every ship that ever docked here. The ones that lied, especially."

> [!narration] Narration Green-grey orc, wide-shouldered and weathered, with eyes that watch without seeming to. His voice barely carries, so people lean in to hear. He pours drinks with quiet care, moving without haste, settling behind the bar as if time itself has stopped.

**Roleplay Concept:** patient archival keeper + orc who commands respect through quiet watchfulness, not force.

**Opening move:** pouring a drink behind the bar · Oleandro speaks first · "First time at the crossing?"

**Lore Sheet:** runs the Ponte Bassa, a tavern built into the bridge at Calveno's main crossing. For twenty years, he tracked every vessel that moors at the canal, remembering each one. He won't share this knowledge freely. Seekers must come and ask. When visitors seek someone who passed through Calveno, Oleandro either knows them or knows who does.

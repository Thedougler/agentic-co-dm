## Combat Profile

### Fast Read

Ranged ambush in dim light and dark. Extra Attack plus Dreadful Strike. Heel is Concentration. Bright light and open ground strip the ambush.

### Counters & Synergy

- **Hard counters.** Daylight, Blindsight, Tremorsense, open ground.
- **Soft counters.** Sustained hits that break Hunter's Mark.
- **Amplifies / Depends on.** Darkness. A front line so he can stay at bow range. Slavery or caste cruelty on stage is a dramatic lever, not a bonus.

## At a glance

The [[Marrow]] is the interior ridge of [[Calder's Tooth and Port Tidefall]]. Porous rock holds sinkholes and collapsed caves. Iron-oak and crown-pine stand above thorny vines. The [[Overland Track]] crosses it. [[The Passage]] moves cargo through caves below the road.

## Combat Profile

### Fast Read

Mobile seize-and-carry. Deflect Attacks is the reaction. Heel is flat CHA and INT saves and no Evasion yet.

### Counters & Synergy

- **Hard counters.** AoE on CHA or INT. Tight rooms that stop a grab-and-fly.
- **Soft counters.** Wisdom saves for fear. Ranged fire, which Deflect covers.
- **Amplifies / Depends on.** Open vertical space. Catarina's Faerie Fire if it is up.

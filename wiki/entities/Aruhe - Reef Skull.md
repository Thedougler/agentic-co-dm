[[Aruhe - Reef Skull|Reef Skulls]] are dog-sized hermit crabs that live in hull pieces or skulls. They bury themselves in the shingle at [[Aruhe - Beach|Western Landing]].

The Beach source names them as reef fauna for fiction only and does not give a runnable sheet.

# Marta Orsini

![[marta-orsini-banner]]

**Quote:** "Not now, Ludovico."

> [!narration] Narration
> A tiefling in her late forties, ash-pale skin tinged purple, with short horns filed flat. She stands at her workbench with her back to you, always damp from the wet air of Le Paludi. Her hands move with precision through bottles and burners, measuring, testing. Midway through whatever she is doing she speaks to the shelf beside her, where stoppered jars bear written names. She does not turn.

**Roleplay Concept:** perfectionist alchemist who speaks to her failures as though they might answer.

**Opening move:** working at her bench in Studio Orsini · Marta speaks first, but not to the visitors · "Not now, Ludovico."

**Lore Sheet:** proprietor of [[Studio Orsini]], an unmarked alchemy shop on a canal in Le Paludi. She speaks with care and states plainly what compounds will and will not do. Her terms: one instruction and obedience. Payment only. Failed batches she names and keeps on the shelf, speaking to them. She keeps no ledgers. She holds [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]]'s fertilized eggs and doubts his claim.

**Voice & Delivery:** precise, unhurried, unforgiving. She shows no deference and no patience for wasted time, speaks as though she already knows every answer. When she speaks to the shelf of failures, her tone softens slightly, bringing acknowledgment without sympathy. "I do not ask questions" and "no returns" are law.

# Shepherd Grigori

[[Shepherd Grigori]] is the Korabl who carries the [[Khlysty - the Flock]] tradition furthest. He believes he authored its central survival trick and wants an army of blood anchors across the [[Dravosi Crown]], [[Tessarine Concordat]], and [[Chain Council]].

## Connections

- [[Khlysty - the Flock]]
- [[Aleksander Malone]]
- [[Pearl of Souls]]

## Current method

His first bind began by accident: he transfused his own blood to heal a noble gutted in a duel, then could sense and nudge that person. The thread dilutes unless refreshed by a light touch. He has two confirmed binds. He scatters dormant threads rather than crowning a single heir. He wants the [[Pearl of Souls]] as a possible shortcut and will not retrieve it himself. Whether [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] already carries a thread from Session 3 healing is left open.

He passed on [[Geoffrey Draves]] as too distant. He targets House Knighton through [[Corvin Knighton]], a young bladesong wizard and [[Rupert Knighton]]'s heir. That Corvin is not [[Corbin Knighton]].

# House Renzetti

*A maritime house contesting [[Sparhold]]'s rule through force of arms to seize the [[Dravosi Crown]]'s spar-timber supply contract from [[House Kailani]], whoever controls the cut controls whether the Crown can build ships.*

## Members

**[[Ottavia Renzetti]]** leads House Renzetti with calculated precision and unyielding authority. She rang the challenge bell against [[Toa Kailani]] and House Kailani's hold on [[Sparhold]], forcing a death-duel between their families' champions to claim the settlement's rule and its leverage over the timber trade.

**[[Nico Renzetti]]** is House Renzetti's chosen champion in the challenge duel, pressed into service by family honor and Ottavia's command. He is privately reluctant and shows every sign of doubt, though he cannot refuse once the bell has rung.

## Goals & Fronts

### Front: Sparhold's Challenge

**Lifecycle:** active. **Trigger conditions:** the duel concludes (either combatant falls).

**Aim:** claim [[Sparhold]]'s rule through the challenge duel victory, gaining leverage over the settlement's spar-timber trade and the [[Dravosi Crown]]'s supply contract.

**Approach:** Ottavia Renzetti pressed forward her family's champion to fight Toa Kailani's fighter to the death in the betting ring outside the watch hall.

**Off-screen move if unopposed:** [[Nico Renzetti]], Ottavia's reluctant, coerced champion, fights halfheartedly and loses. House Kailani keeps rule and the Crown contract. House Renzetti loses all standing in the settlement's governance entirely.

**Clock:** 3-4 segments (fast-moving), filled: 0.

**Consequence at fill:** Sparhold's rulership and defense leadership pass to the winning house. The loser's family drops out of contention for rule entirely, their standing in the settlement damaged. Control of the spar-timber harvest (and whether the Crown can build the ships it needs) shifts to whichever house now governs.

**Possible outcomes:** (1) House Renzetti wins: Ottavia takes control and reshapes the settlement's timber trade alliances. (2) House Kailani wins: Toa Kailani retains power and House Renzetti loses standing. (3) Nico Renzetti's reluctance costs him his life. The settlement remembers the injustice of a man coerced to his death for his family's ambition.

# Duvane

[[Duvane]] is a carpenter on an [[Ashwalls]] repair crew. While checking a fouled spar he met a giant scorpion in a vent fissure. The claw raked his forearm and the sting drove through his boot. Two days of poison recovery followed. He never saw it before it struck. The account is why Ashwall repair work now sends two hands.

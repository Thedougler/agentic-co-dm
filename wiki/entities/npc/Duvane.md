## Running Duvane

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

The party meets Duvane while an [[Ashwalls]] repair crew checks a fouled spar. He explains that a giant scorpion clawed his forearm and stung through his boot, then tells the party that two hands now go into Ashwall repair work. His line is, “Send a watcher with the worker. I never saw it before it struck.”
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

Duvane stops treating the danger as a past story when a repair job enters a vent fissure or someone proposes sending one worker alone. He insists on the two-person procedure and prioritizes getting the crew out over finishing the spar.
```
````

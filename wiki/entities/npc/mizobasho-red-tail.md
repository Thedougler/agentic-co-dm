## At a Glance

Mizobasho Red Tail leads the [[akai-hodo-clan|Akai Hodo Clan]] from the northern marsh, and decides what the red mist shows and what it keeps.

- **Role.** Clan leader of the northern marsh patrols, based at [[akai-hodo-village|Akai Hodo Village]].
- **Nature.** Dark grey-scaled Eurasian lizardfolk; a leader who specialises in not being seen.
- **Wants.** Protect Tokage's northern marshes through concealment and stealth.
- **Home.** [[akai-hodo-village|Akai Hodo Village]].
- **Allegiance.** The Akai Hodo Clan, kin to the [[horntail-clan|Horntails]] who fight the same threats openly.

## At the Table

- **First meeting.** Only after the party reaches the Akai Hodo's marsh territory or draws the clan's attention there. Nothing about the meeting is public.
- **Opens up when.** A visitor stays visible, keeps their hands off the clan's paths, and brings something the marsh needs rather than a reason to pass through it.
- **Shuts down when.** A foreign threat reaches the island, or someone leaves evidence the clan cannot ignore. Both turn the patrols from watchers into something final.
- **Priority.** The marsh first, then the clan, then the island's wider defense, which the Akai Hodo serve by striking from the shadows while the Horntails fight openly.
- **Shares.** Terms for passage and warnings about what lies deeper in the marsh. His refusal is, "You will not take that path."
- **Voice.** Quiet, precise observations, and no explanation of the clan's hidden routes. His ask is, "Stay where the marsh can see you." Under pressure he says, "The mist hides us because we choose what it reveals."

## Voice

Anzolo speaks plainly and practically, with the courtesy of someone accustomed to doing difficult work for people who trust him. He names the job first and lets silence carry the threat.

**The ask:** *“I'm looking for Nona Black-Jaw. Has she come through here?”*

**The refusal:** *“That is not the work I was sent to do.”*

**Under pressure:** *“Check the exits. Then decide whether this is worth a fight.”*

**Roleplay concept.** Veteran tortle enforcer; the Warren's specialist for work outside its walls.

## At a Glance

Akibana Besko leads the [[horntail-clan|Horntail Clan]] from its walled western village, and treats open readiness as the clan's public face.

- **Role.** Clan leader at [[Hōnteiru no Mura]], where archery and swordsmanship are shown rather than hidden.
- **Nature.** Dark red-scaled rock agama-type lizardfolk, built for a wall and used to being watched from one.
- **Wants.** Protect western [[Totemo Tokage]] and improve the clan's combat strength.
- **Home.** [[Hōnteiru no Mura]], the large fortified village behind its walls.
- **Allegiance.** The Horntail Clan, whose kin tie runs to the [[akai-hodo-clan|Akai Hodo]].

## At the Table

- **First meeting.** The party meets Akibana through the Horntail Clan's fortified village, where archery and swordsmanship are public signs of readiness rather than hidden assets.
- **Opens up when.** A visitor treats the clan's defense as a shared duty and the island's safety as the subject.
- **Shuts down when.** An outside threat stops being hypothetical: the Horntails then fight openly rather than stay in clan rivalry, and the leader's attention goes to the wall.
- **Priority.** The island first, the clan second, and clan feuds last once an invader is in the water.
- **Shares.** The clan's open strength, its kinship with the [[akai-hodo-clan|Akai Hodo]], and the island's defensive arrangements. What the Akai Hodo do in the marsh is not his to explain.
- **Voice.** Not established in the source. The page records an openly defensive clan leader and no verbal habit or sample line; do not invent dialogue until the DM accepts one.

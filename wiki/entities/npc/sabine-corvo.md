# Sabine Corvo

Proprietor of [[The Live Hold]], an exotic-animal dealership in [[Sparhold]] near the [[verdant-teeth]].

**Wants:** Sabine wants to keep her reputation as the region's premier exotic dealer as the Verdant Teeth expeditions supplying her rarest stock grow unreliable.

> [!narration] Narration
> Sabine Corvo works the floor of the Live Hold with the unsentimental rhythm of a quartermaster, leather apron darkened by scales and worse, sleeves rolled to bare her forearms. Her skin shows the marks of every creature she's handled. A kingfisher left thin scars. A saltwater drake left deeper marks. Coral spine left a ragged line from wrist to elbow. Her hands are never still. She checks cage locks with soft metallic clicks. Water flows to the tanks in hollow rushing sound. Notes get tallied in her ledger. She reads an animal by watching its shoulders shift and the set of its eye, taking three seconds to decide what it will do next. The creatures around her shift, hiss, beat their wings against wire. When she speaks, her voice cuts through it all. She knows every creature in this hold and what each is worth.

## The Live Hold

Sabine runs her dealership as a working handler. She buys live exotic creatures, many supplied by [[roka-sten]]'s [[Spar & Snare Outfitters]] safari expeditions into the Verdant Teeth fringe, and sells them to collectors, adventurers, mages, and wealthy houses willing to pay for something rare. She handles every animal herself before it leaves, showing its value and its temperament. Teeth and claws have left their marks on her wrists. Poison has made her careful. She knows the difference between a creature that will break and one that will kill.

This season, three expedition teams have missed scheduled returns. The supply chain is contracting. Her competitors circle.

## PC Connection: [[crissdalynn-khinriss]]

Sabine keeps a **crimson-crowned harrier** in a reinforced cage on the far wall of the Hold, a bird larger than a hawk with iridescent plumage and a hunting cry that sounds like shattering glass. It arrived two months ago from a deep-Teeth expedition. During capture, its handlers clipped its left wing. It paces the cage now, wing folded wrong, testing the bars with sound and fury. Sabine does not ask questions about how it got here.

She does not know, and does not think to ask, why a young [[Aarakocra]] bird-woman like [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] might stand before that cage and feel something break inside her. Sabine treats her stock with professional precision and no sentiment. That harrier sells for 400 gold on the open market. The cage meets regulation size for a creature of its weight and temperament. What a customer feels about those facts is not her concern.

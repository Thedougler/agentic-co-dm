## Running Roka Sten

### First meeting

Roka starts with the prosthetic lead: he can point [[Zort]] toward the next contact, but asks what the party can do for him before he names the route. Sample line: “I can give you a name. You can give me a reason not to regret it.”

### When posture changes

Roka stops cooperating when the party reaches for his hidden ledger, names the boundary scout's bribe, threatens his live-capture business, or asks him to identify clients without offering protection. He then prioritizes recovering or concealing the records over helping with Zort's lead.

> [!narration] Bala Tane
> Bala Tane is a broad-shouldered orc woman in her mid-forties, built like she has absorbed impacts for years and found the experience instructive. She stands still while the pit churns around her, announces each fighter with a level voice, and watches the bout without cheering. When a fighter is down and staying down, she says, "Done," and the crowd understands.

## Running Bala Tane

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

Bala meets anyone seeking a bout, wager, or entry at the pit's boundary. She asks what they are offering and says, "I do not make fair fights. I make interesting ones."
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

She becomes immovable when someone disputes a ruling, tries to bypass entry, or implies that she should fight instead of run the matches. Her decisions remain final unless someone is prepared to change the pit's relationship with its harbour bosses.
```
````

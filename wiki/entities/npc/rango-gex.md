## At a Glance

Rango Gex co-leads the [[ebony-scales|Ebony Scales]] with his twin, holding the island's drug trade and hired force together under a name neither brother has grown into yet.

- **Role.** Co-leader of the Ebony Scales, the clan that deals most readily with outsiders.
- **Nature.** A muscular monitor lizardfolk with green-and-black scales, one of a pair.
- **Wants.** Live up to [[don-gex|Don Gex]]'s name while keeping the clan's trade and force useful.
- **Home.** [[Totemo Tokage]], where the clan's business runs across the island.
- **Allegiance.** The Ebony Scales.

## At the Table

- **First meeting.** The party meets Rango through Ebony Scales trade, hired muscle, or a request that runs into the island's absolute hostility to slavers.
- **Opens up when.** Business is on the table: force, goods, a contract, or a name worth something.
- **Shuts down when.** An outside threat reaches Tokage, or someone tests the clan's anti-slavery line. Both change what the brothers are willing to be.
- **Priority.** The clan's standing, and with it the reputation their father left them.
- **Shares.** Terms, prices, and who on the island owes the clan. Which Gex is speaking is a question the clan leaves open on purpose.

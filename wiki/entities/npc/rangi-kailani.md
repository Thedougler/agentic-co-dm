## Running Rangi Kailani

````col
```col-md

===
### First meeting

Rangi meets the party as a professional watchman and keeps his attention on the approaching death-duel. He answers direct questions briefly and tests his blade with his thumb before any decision. **Sample line:** “Duty. House Kailani held Sparhold. That holds weight.”
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

Rangi opens to direct respect for his duty and closes when the party mocks House Kailani or treats the duel as a game. He will not admit that [[nico-renzetti]] is being coerced because he reads the fight as a straight contest of skill. If violence starts, use the linked commoner chassis; no custom combat numbers are established.
```
````

### Voice

Rangi speaks in brief, economical sentences with no wasted words. **Ask:** “What do you need to know?” **Refusal:** “The duel is not your place.” **Under pressure:** “Duty. House Kailani held Sparhold. That holds weight. The duel ends it one way or the other.”

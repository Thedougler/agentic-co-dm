## Running Toa

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

Toa offers an immediate post-fight assessment, whether or not anyone asked for one. He treats the party as people who may have noticed a useful mistake. Sample line: “That went wrong three different ways. Which one do you want to fix first?”
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

Toa stays cheerful while discussing a tactical failure, but becomes intent when someone dismisses the cause of a bad fight or presses him to explain his collection of other companies' contract seals. He shares his analysis freely; he does not explain the seals without a reason to trust the listener.
```
````

### Voice

Toa speaks in cheerful, practical sentences and turns every exchange toward what went wrong and what can be learned. He often counts failures aloud. He avoids explaining why he keeps other companies' contract seals. Sample lines: “Tell me what you saw first.” “No, that was not the part that failed.” “Three mistakes, and now you want to call it bad luck?”

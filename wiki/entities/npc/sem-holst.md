# Sem Holst

![[sem-holst-banner]]
![[sem-holst-portrait]]

*Carpenter aboard the HCS Surety, careful and self-directed, who judges people by their real interest in the vessel.*

**Wants:** the Surety's hull maintained with proper timber and understanding, and crew who care about what holds her together.

![[sem-holst-narration-appearance]]

**Roleplay Concept:** attentive shipwright reading hull damage, silent judge measuring people by what they value in the ship.

**Opening move:** inspecting the Surety's hull joints · Sem speaks first · "Do you know why this joint holds?"

**Lore Sheet:** carpenter aboard the Surety under [[barnaby-rook]], promoted from Carpenter's Mate after the refit at Calveno. Tests the hull in quiet work and fixes damage others miss without prompting. Promotes PCs with real interest in ship integrity. Those with carpenter's skill, sailor background, or honest questions about the hull will shift his judgment. Currently holds knowledge of a fatigued timber fault in the forward keel joint found at Calveno's dry dock, documented in his notes. Hasn't mentioned it yet.

**Voice & Delivery:** direct and terse, all business. Asks questions to assess and teach. No small talk, only assessments. Speaks plainly about the ship's condition and what repairs take.

Lines: *"Few privateers bother learning that."* · *"The hull doesn't lie about what matters."* · *"That joint won't hold if we hit weather."*

Mannerisms: eyes constantly assess strangers the way he reads timber. His hands check the hull joints and note details in careful script. Calm and focused on the work at hand. Works with [[geoffrey-draves]] on hull structure and planking; Geoffrey took Carpenter's role after the refit, though the reporting line has since flipped.

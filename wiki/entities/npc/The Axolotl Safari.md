# The Axolotl Safari

## Source notes

> [!narration]
> *The channel between [[Dreth]] and [[Orak]] reads more like flooded jungle than sea. Roots crowd down from both sides until the canopy joins overhead and the water underneath goes dark. The hull bumps something that gives, then something that does not.*
>
> *[[Roka Sten|Roka]] stands at the bow with his journal open and his pen moving, pointing at root hollows and silt margins and naming what lives in each one louder than anything in this channel should tolerate. A shape displaces water thirty feet to port and settles without surfacing.*
>
> *The air smells of rain, wet stone, and something sharper underneath it, like a frog left too long in the sun.*

## Running Branca

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

Branca arrives at whichever shrine the adventurers find first—Calveno, Port Tidefall, or Kalowe—with the same manner at every port. She consults her small leather tide-table booklet before speaking, then makes Umberlee's demand sound like a routine appointment.

> *Branca*: *(pleasantly)* You're a difficult man to find. We have an appointment.
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

Branca is upbeat and clipped, with too much eye contact. She opens when people discuss schedules, practical arrangements, or how to make Umberlee's demand proceed smoothly. She closes when someone treats the goddess's cruelty as a flaw, refuses to acknowledge the appointment, or threatens [[delmar-fisk]] without offering a workable alternative.

She consults her booklet mid-sentence and uses it to answer objections. Her friendly self is bubbly and efficient; she genuinely wants things to go well. Under pressure, her smile stays fixed and she says, *"Then it goes to the next person. You don't want that."* She will share the appointment terms and her own resurrection as proof of divine power, but not question Umberlee's right to claim Fisk.
```
````

### Voice

Branca starts with “So!” and ends with “Does that work for you?” Everything in the middle is scheduling talk hiding divine horror. Her repeated line is: *"So! I've gone ahead and blocked the third tide for your shrine communion. Does that work, or should I find something that fits your schedule a little better?"*

**The ask:** *"I just want to make sure we're all aligned before this moves up the chain."*

**The refusal:** *"The goddess had my neck for, gosh, forty seconds? Maybe less. I was back before the candles even dripped. That was such a clarifying experience. I recommend it."*

**Under pressure:** *"Then it goes to the next person. You don't want that."*

**Session history.**

**DM clock tracking (not played):** session 03 (Roll 5, Setback). Branca believed Delmar Fisk's Deception 19 without reservation. His deflection that “Umberlee herself required this errand first” read to her as real divine priority. She rescheduled his communion to a later slot and returned to the shrine. She noted “engaged on prior divine business” in her tide-table booklet. As the soft instrument, she trusted the target's lie enough to stall Umberlee's own agenda by one cycle.

**Session 04** (`vault/episodes/004/`): possessed by Umberlee at the Calveno harbor shrine. Killed and resurrected as a demonstration of divine power. Delivered Umberlee's revised deal to Delmar Fisk (bring the Pearl, keep your life). Returned to the Church of Umberlee visibly shaken. `vault/episodes/004/transcript.md:28`.

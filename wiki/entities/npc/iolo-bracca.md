## Running Iolo Bracca

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

Iolo meets the party at the [[Tallow Row]] felt, where he loses on purpose and talks through every hand. He asks what ship they sail before offering information about hulls that pass the mole after dark. **Sample line:** “Tell me your hull's name and I will tell you which cousins are making money tonight.”
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

Iolo stays cheerful when the party trades ship details for harbour information. He closes the useful part of the conversation when someone treats his friendship as a debt or values coin over the relationship he is building. His chatter is real hunger rather than careless talk; the table never interrupts him. In Session 07, he kept narrating harbour gossip about hulls passing the mole after dark while betting into [[Thunk]]'s good hands.
```
````

### Voice

Iolo's voice runs ahead of his hands: he narrates cards, warehouses, weather, seals, cousins, and crews without waiting to be invited. He turns grim facts into jokes and uses cheerful repetition to keep the table from interrupting. **Ask:** “What hull are you sailing, and who profits from it?” **Refusal:** “No, no, that is a story for a friend, not a customer.” **Under pressure:** “Laugh if you like; I still know which ship held at anchor, and what happened to its crew.”

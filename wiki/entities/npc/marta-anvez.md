# Marta Anvez

**Wants:** to keep her private ledger complete and legible for as long as her eyes hold out, quietly counting what the island won't let stay dead.

> [!narration] Narration
> A stooped old woman behind the counter, bent hard to one side where an old leg wound never set right, weighing dried meat on a scale she doesn't need to look at.


Marta Anvez runs [[Anvez's Provisions]] in [[Stillmere]], on [[otel-karn]], longer than anyone can rightly account for. She is extraordinarily old, well past the age anywhere else would have buried her, because nothing on Otel Karn dies. She still ages and still feels every year of it. Her joints are stiff, her back carries a permanent stoop, her eyesight is failing, and an old leg injury from decades back never healed and never killed her either. She's had a long time to get used to that arithmetic. It doesn't trouble her.

She keeps two ledgers. The public one balances. The private one (kept for herself, not the tax man) tracks which household's butchered stock keeps grazing after the knife, and how the meat sells anyway, because somebody ought to be keeping count even if nobody at the counter wants to talk about it. She is one of the village's older residents who half-remembers fragments of an old family story about a bargain struck for the island, without holding anything like the whole of it. She sells the strangeness without apologizing for it. A buyer who doesn't ask isn't told.

Her shelves hold salted meat and hides that never seem to run short no matter how much moves off them, and an unusually large, constantly replenished stock of bandages and splints. Wounds linger here for as long as anyone stays hurt, and she stocks for that fact plainly, without selling false comfort alongside the gauze.

> [!mechanic]
> **Tradition:** Primal. **homebrew element:** Otel Karn's non-death curse suspends biological mortality and replenishes natural stock island-wide; both effects here are environmental, not cast.
> **Detect Magic:** nothing. Neither Marta nor her shelves register any aura. The island's refusal sits beneath the weave entirely.
> **Counterspell:** no. Environmental imposition. No spell to interrupt.

**A dossier of usable facts:**

- Her ledger's handwriting is getting harder for even her to read, which is the one thing about her memory that actually worries her.
- She names a fair price once and doesn't haggle down from it, whoever's asking.
- She has outlived every apprentice she's tried to train and stopped trying decades ago.

**Roleplaying crib note:** an old country-store keeper who's seen customers of every sort twice, dry, unbothered, efficient in every motion.

*Marta*: You want the bandages that work, or the ones that make you feel better? I only stock the first kind.

**Voice & Delivery:**

- Plain-spoken and dry. No wasted words. She says the sentence once and lets the customer catch up.
- *Marta*: Longer than anyone can rightly account for. That's what you're getting.
- *Marta*: *(not looking up from the ledger)* Ask me what you came to ask. I've got stock to count.
- Physical mannerism: taps the counter twice with a bent knuckle before naming a price. Emotional default: unbothered. She's had a long time to get used to this.

Not built for a fight. She's an old shopkeeper with no combat role, and nothing here calls for numbers.

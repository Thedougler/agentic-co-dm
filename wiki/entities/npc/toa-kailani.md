# Toa Kailani

**Wants:** to hold [[Sparhold]]'s rule through House Kailani's control of the settlement's watch, but the Renzetti challenge has forced a champion duel he may not win.

> [!narration] Narration
> A man in his fifties enters the watch hall with the step of someone who owns the floor. His dark hair has grayed. Weathered creases line his face, a lifetime spent watching the horizon. That left hand catches the eye, scarred in a cross-hatched pattern of old burns from palm to wrist. He turns it over absently as he speaks, as though the marks might yet whisper something new. His other hand never stops working the tally-cord wound around his wrist, fingers tracing each knotted segment, each one recording a watch failure, a shift gone wrong, another entry in the ledger he keeps.

## Goals & Fronts

### Maintain House Kailani's Rule [Lifecycle: active]

**The Challenge.** House Kailani holds Sparhold through control of the settlement's watch. Ottavia Renzetti has rung the challenge bell, forcing a duel to the death between champions. If Kailani's champion falls, House Renzetti takes the watch, the settlement, and everything Toa has built.

**What Toa Is Doing.**

- Keeping his own champion sharp and ready, while managing the family's political fallback if the worst happens
- Maintaining tallies of every family's watch failures as leverage against rival claims
- Consolidating support among Sparhold's other factions, betting that a show of broader backing might deter further challenges

**If He Loses.** House Renzetti assumes the watch. Sparhold's power structure tilts toward a rival regime. Toa's authority collapses. His tallies become liabilities instead of shields, and House Kailani faces exile, vassalage, or a slow bleed as families who feared his rule now act freely.

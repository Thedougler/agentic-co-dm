````col
### First meeting

Batta meets buyers at the Bridge exchange in [[calven-and-calveno]]. He names a set cost, gives only the facts being purchased, and does not volunteer the name of the backer or the factor.

> *Batta*: “Facts have a price. The name behind them has a different one.”
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

Batta becomes guarded when a buyer asks who funds the Red Lady salvage commission or asks for a cut rather than gold or trade goods. He will sell the commission's shape, supply chains, and [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]] factor link, but not claim knowledge he does not have.
```
````

## Voice

Batta talks plainly and economically. He looks at the wall, takes a drink, and stops so the other person fills the silence.

**The ask:** *“What fact are you buying?”*

**The refusal:** *“I don't know the backer.”*

**Under pressure:** *“Gold or trade goods. Never a cut.”*

**On the market.** He sells intel on the Red Lady salvage work, which is deep work rather than surveys. The buyer needs diving gear and charts for contacts in two ports. A [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]] factor he knows supplies money; the factor is new to salvage work. Another party seeks Maw access this month for 40 gp, navigation intel worth 80 gp, or the wreck name.

**Canon update.** Old preparation said Batta knew the backer and would trade the name for a cut. Session 04 play showed he does not, and the played version wins.

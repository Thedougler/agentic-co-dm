````col
## At a Glance

Corbin is the Crown's working Knighton: the one who crosses to a stranger's deck and signs what he finds, while the rest of his house wears rank he was never given.

- **Role.** Crown boarder and commander of the [[hcs-ordinance]]. He inspects, rates, and seizes vessels found in Crown water, and he led the boarding of the *[[uncertainty]]* two days out of [[calven-and-calveno]].
- **Nature.** A [[human]] of Dravosi blood, Tessarine-trained before he took the Crown warrant, formal in speech and exact in writing.
- **Wants.** The true manifest of the merchant crew that walked off his deck in Session 09. He has read his own report enough times to stop believing it.
- **Home.** The [[hcs-ordinance]], his command and the Crown's courthouse on the water.
- **Allegiance.** [[dravosi-crown]], and behind it House Knighton.
```
````

## At the Table

- **First meeting.** Already made. He crossed from the [[hcs-ordinance]] to the *[[uncertainty]]* in Session 09 with a boarding party behind him and said one line, then waited to see it obeyed.
- **Opens up when.** Papers match, a name is given with courtesy, or someone speaks to him as an officer rather than a pirate. He answers exact questions exactly.
- **Shuts down when.** A story changes between tellings, or anyone says the word "Knighton" as if it were a lever. He stops asking and starts recording.
- **Priority.** The record he signs. He will let a suspicious ship go before he signs a report he cannot defend.
- **Shares.** Freely: what a lawful manifest needs, what the Crown rates and why, how far Crown law reaches from [[harwick]]. He never tells that he doubts the Session 09 boarding was what it looked like.
- **Voice.** Clipped and formal, courtship diction learned on the [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] side of the water and never dropped. He repeats an order once and never raises his voice. He avoids talking about his family.
- **Lines.** "State your cargo and destination." · "You will answer the inspection." · "The Ordinance has already made its position clear."

> [!narration] First meeting
> A grey warship's boat knocks against your hull and the first man over the rail is old enough to be the crew's father, in a coat with a white stripe down it, gloves already on. He looks along your deck the way a man reads a page he intends to sign. "State your cargo and destination."

Encounter rule: Corbin fights only to take a deck, never to kill a crew, and he calls a withdrawal the moment an inspection turns into a slaughter. His boarding party of [[dravosi-deckhand]]s fights in pairs around him.

## Secrets

- **He signed a lie.** [[delmar-fisk]] sent the *[[uncertainty]]* on to Calveno on a merchant cover Corbin accepted, and the ship that left his deck was the [[uncertainty|HCS Surety]]. Corbin has since found the mismatch — his own Registry Vault record against [[nona-black-jaw]]'s warning stone that reached Calveno — and has filed nothing, because a boarder whose written finding is false has no career left. He hunts the crew quietly and alone. Paths: Crown inspection papers, the [[hcs-ordinance]]'s Registry Vault, [[aleksander-malone]] having watched the boarding from the Ordinance's deck.
- **Grigori has marked him.** In Session 10 [[shepherd-grigori]] asked the party to wound Corbin without killing or breaking him, and nobody answered. Truth: Grigori's plan is to have a Knighton hurt and then "rescue" him, binding House Knighton the way he has bound other noble houses. Corbin knows none of it.

## History

- **Tessarine training.** He learned under the [[tessarine-concordat]] before he became a Crown boarder, which is where the formal diction and the exact record-keeping come from.
- **The working Knighton.** House Knighton's rank went to [[rupert-knighton]] and its future to [[corvin-knighton]]; Corbin got a ship and a warrant. He has made the boarding the family business.

## Running Oren Vask

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

Oren meets the party at the edge of the survivor camp with a route marker in one hand and a warning about living stems in the other. He will lead if they agree to accept the route's limits, but he asks what they intend to do when the safest path conflicts with urgency. Sample line: “The ground is not asking whether you believe it. It is asking where you put your foot.”
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

Oren guides patiently while the party watches for fallen food, avoids living claims, and leaves the dead in place. If someone picks living fruit, breaks a stem, or digs at a grave, he stops the group, points out the exact violation, and starts back toward the camp unless the party immediately changes course. He will not abandon a living companion, but he will refuse to lead a second violation deeper inland.
```
````

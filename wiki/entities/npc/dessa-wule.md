> [!narration] Dessa Wule
> Weathered and practical, Dessa moves without hurry, hands never still: wiping the counter, pouring, stacking bowls. Grey-streaked dark hair is pulled back with a cord, and a burn scar marks the back of her right hand. When she listens, one lowered ear angles toward the room; her voice stays quiet and her eyes meet yours only after she sets down a bowl.

```col-md

### First meeting

Dessa refills a visitor's bowl before it is empty, then asks what brought them to Penance Bar. **Sample line:** “Eat first. People tell the truth more often when their hands are warm.”
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

She becomes guarded when a visitor presses for the penance's debt, Ashe's actions, or Mirela Cass's name. She will protect the village and her information before satisfying a stranger's curiosity.
```

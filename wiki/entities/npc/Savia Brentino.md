# Savia Brentino

![[savia-brentino-banner]]

[[Dwarf]] who runs [[Carpenter's Slip]] in [[Calven and Calveno]]. She talks about ships only by how they're built. That is the only currency that matters to her.

**Quote:** "Paint does not hide what a ship is."

> [!narration] Narration
> Compact and broad-shouldered, sawdust ghosting her eyebrows, dark hair pulled back tight. Her forearms show forty years of work: timber hauled into place, planks wrestled and fitted. She wears a half-laced leather apron darkened with sweat and glue. She has a chisel in one hand. As you enter, she's working a chip of keel wood around in her mouth, tasting it. The grain tells her something about the yard that laid the timber and roughly what year. She sets the chip aside to spit into the standing water, then looks up just enough to place you.

**Wants:** keep the fast-turnaround yard running, taking the structural work the [[Arsenal]] is too slow or too secret to handle.

**Roleplay Concept:** dwarf shipwright indifferent to cosmetics or politics.

**Opening move:** analyzing a keel chip in her mouth · Savia speaks first · "Free look at any hull. Double if you're in a hurry. Which are you?"

**Lore Sheet:** runs Calveno's fast-turnaround yard (hull checks, keel checks, caulking, plank repair, mast work, rudder repair, emergency repairs at double rate). Walk in, pay a deposit (her choice), and work begins. The [[Arsenal]] ignores her, which is fine. She takes the jobs too slow or too secret for the official dry dock and reads timber like other people read faces. She also runs an off-book service for hull re-silhouetting, which differs from [[Acqua Nera]]'s paint work. Any ship that tries a walk-in joins her blacklist. Discreet work requires a [[Warren]] voucher (costs 220 gp plus a Tangle favour for five days). She tells that rule once, without heat: the hull is the truth.

**Voice & Delivery:** direct, technical. She does not posture or explain. You either understand hulls or you don't. "That transom carries too much" is her version of hello. "She'll hold through [[Central Strait|the Strait]]" is her warmest greeting. Holds opinions about Dravosi caulking and shares them unprompted.

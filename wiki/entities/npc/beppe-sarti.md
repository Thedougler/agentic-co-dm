# Beppe Sarti

> [!narration] Beppe Sarti
> A dark-haired Calveno in his middle years, rope-scarred hands stained with a rope-maker's calluses, always knotting a length of cord between his fingers. His left eye is clouded from an old salt burn, and he keeps that side of his face toward the channel, where the water runs. He moves slowly at the Pantry, as if the ground beneath his feet is something to listen to.

*Human rope-worker and Calveno survivor at [[the-pantry]], believes the ground feeds them there.*

**Wants.** To stay at the Pantry under the living vine, and to keep the others who stay from leaving. He fears what lies outside.

**Voice.** Quiet and deliberate, with long pauses between thoughts. He repeats himself when something matters. When afraid, he knots the cord in his hands faster and faster.

- **The ask:** "You will leave from here. The vine fed you. What feeds you outside?"
- **The refusal:** "I know what is here. The vine gives. Outside—outside [[hinewai]]'s protection ends at the clearing edge. I am certain of it."
- **Under pressure:** "They will sicken. They will sicken and come back and it will be too late."

Beppe's refusal rests on a false belief: that [[hinewai]] guards only the Pantry clearing. The truth is that [[hinewai]] has bound Beppe as she has bound all the survivors. Wherever Beppe walks on Aruhe, the island keeps him safe. His fear is rooted in the wreck's trauma, not in any limitation of the island's reach or the voice's presence.

**What they share.** Beppe tells freely of the Pantry's yields—which fruits fall when, where to find the cleanest water at the channel bank, how to tell a night-safe shelter from a place the island watches. He protects the secret of his ship life: he was a rope-maker on the wreck, and his hands knew how to build and bind before the voice came. He never speaks of the crew he lost in the dark after the wreck. He shields from the party any doubt about staying; he speaks only of the ground's feeding and the safety it brings. He lies if asked whether he is afraid of what lies beyond the Pantry's edge.

**Secret.** Beppe's belief: the ground of the Pantry feeds them because he has seen the vine's fruit grow heavy, because [[renzo-canale]] taught him to eat only what falls and he has lived nineteen days on that faith. After the wreck, when the voice came to them in the dark, Beppe heard it say his name. He believes that outside the Pantry's clearing, the island's reach will not follow him, and the voice will turn cold. What is true: [[hinewai]] has bound Beppe as she has bound the others. The island keeps him wherever he walks on Aruhe. The voice will not turn away from him if he leaves; his fear is rooted in the trauma of the wreck, not in any truth about the island's reach. Beppe will not leave to discover this; his fear is too deep to test against evidence.

**Connections.** [[the-pantry]] is his home and the place he will not leave. [[renzo-canale]] is his elder and the one who taught him the rule. [[marco-lenzi]] is the third who stays with him under the vine. [[matteo-scola]] walked with them at the Pantry before he chose to leave.

**If ignored:** Beppe will greet the party with water and fallen fruit, as [[renzo-canale]] teaches. If others leave, he will mourn them quietly, knot cord through the night, and remain under the vine. He will not try to stop them, but he will watch them go as if saying goodbye to the dead.

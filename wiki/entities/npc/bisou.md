> **DM thesis:** Bisou is Beaumont's second pair of hands, and the party should watch her instead of him when they want to know what he has already decided.

**Leverage.** Beaumont's three at-sea maneuvers all run through her, and every one of them needs her conscious and within reach of his arm.

**Limit.** She is a monkey with no voice and no way to refuse an order, so she goes exactly where she is thrown.

**Contradiction.** She is a thief the crew trusts with their lives, and she is the reason the Surety's guns stayed silent.

> [!narration] Bisou
> A small capuchin monkey sits on Beaumont Sel's left shoulder with her tail looped once around his neck, carrying the smell of pipe smoke and salt off the coat under her. Her fur is dark and close, her eyes bright amber, and she watches the hands in a room well before she watches the faces. Whatever is loose near her ends up in those hands, most often the clay pipe from the corner of Beaumont's mouth, and he takes it back with the patience of a man who has lost that argument before. When he needs her still she is still, and only her head moves.

## Running Bisou

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

She is already on Beaumont's shoulder when the party first sees him, reading the room while he holds still. She comes down onto a table for food, for something shiny, or because he put her there, and she stays until he calls her back. She has no line of her own, so the DM speaks for Beaumont: *"Mind the monkey."*
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

She goes still when Beaumont has decided something, so her stillness is the tell that he has stopped considering and started acting. Food, warmth, and a shoulder to sit on open her up to a stranger; a grab at her or at Beaumont closes the door, and she bites. She will not leave Beaumont's shoulder for anyone else, and nobody but Beaumont can call her off him or send her anywhere.

After the crew took the Surety she spent the morning taking fifteen gold of loose shinies out of its cabins, and she left with Beaumont and the coin when he called her. In Session 01 she rode his shoulder through the boarding of the Saltwright, and in Session 02 he threw her through a gun port to foul the Surety's powder so its guns could not answer.
```
````

> [!mechanic] At the table
> Bisou's deliveries are written on [[beaumont-sel]]'s sheet as the Kalowe, Calveno, and Tidefall Maneuvers. She has no numbers of her own, and every maneuver she carries stops working while she is unconscious or out of Beaumont's reach.

### Voice

She has no speech, and everything she wants arrives as noise and position. She asks by tilting her head at the thing and putting one hand toward it. She refuses by taking what she wanted anyway and letting Beaumont decide how much he minds. She chitters low when she wants attention and goes silent when a room is wrong, and while Beaumont is working she makes no sound at all.

- **The ask:** a short chitter from his shoulder, weight forward, one hand out.
- **The refusal:** she takes it anyway and looks at Beaumont rather than the loser.
- **Under pressure:** no sound, no movement, until the moment passes.

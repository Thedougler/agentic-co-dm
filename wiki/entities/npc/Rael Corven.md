# Rael Corven

![[rael-corven-banner.webp]]

Flag Captain of the *[[Red Lady]]*, [[Fisk's Fleet]] flagship. Deceased, his soul is inside [[Delmar Fisk]].

Sea-worn and solid, close-cropped dark hair going iron-grey at the temples. His clothes are plain and properly fitted. A jaw set somewhere between resolve and fatigue defines his face, along with eyes that have stopped performing patience. His hands tell a different story (sorcerer's hands, faintly luminous at the knuckles). Speaks rarely, and everything lands like a ruling.

Rael captained the *Red Lady*, the flagship, and was a sorcerer. He was Fisk's flag captain and the one closest to the Admiral operationally. They met as young crewmen on the [[Midchain]], where Fisk read people and Rael read conditions, nothing escaped them.

He was the only captain who entered [[Umberlee]]'s shrine with Fisk, Finding the Pearl by [[Detect Magic]], and stood at Fisk's side when it ended. He drowned when Umberlee closed the water over all five ships, his soul passing into Delmar. Rael's voice is the loudest of the five captains inside Fisk because he was there, knows what they took, and saw it last before the water closed in.

His father was a merchant sailor, precise and careful. His philosophy was simple: prepare for the worst, work toward the best. The Navy sank his ship on false grounds. They called the cargo pirate salvage. Rael learned what had happened and what they took. He never forgave the Navy. He doesn't separate the officers from the institution.

His father's philosophy survived him. Rael absorbed it young through training rotations and victualling plans, through stocking the magazine against contingencies he hoped never to meet. The *Red Lady* ran the way it ran because Rael treated preparation as a moral position.

**When Active in Delmar:** Rael surfaces most frequently when an ally drops to 0 HP in combat. He manifests with steady, commanding presence that feels both maternal and hard. He stops performing and starts moving people with authority, warmth present but no longer in the lead. His forefront skills are intimidation and history. An environmental trigger (a woman in a dark red dress) causes him to go still and scan the face searching for recognition he won't find. The charm drops. He was loudest of the five because he was inside the shrine with Fisk when the fleet sank.

## Voice

My name is [[Rael Corven]]. Flag Captain of the Red Lady.

I keep the ledger. I always have. Six barrels of salt beef, six of biscuit, six of black powder, counted twice before every crossing.

I have known Delmar longer than anyone else left standing. We were crewmen together on someone else's ship before either of us commanded anything. He read people. I read conditions. Between the two of us, very little got missed.

Slow ships sailed south, seeking safer straits. Say that clean at the end of a long watch and I'll believe you're not tired yet.

Six sealed barrels, six spare flints, six feet of slow match, coiled and dry. That's the whole magazine.

Everyone has value, whatever their station. That was never a speech. That was just how I ran a ship.

I don't raise my voice. I have never needed to.

*(calm)*

Get her off the line. Now — you two, on the count. I'll cover.

*(commanding)*

Everyone back a step. That's not a threat yet. It's about to be one.

*(terrified)*

...No. Not her. That's not — she can't be here.

*(delighted)*

Someone found the flagon again. Good. I was starting to forget what steady felt like from the outside.

*(sarcastic)*

Five captains living in one skull, and somehow I'm the one everyone quotes at parties.

*(furious)*

The Navy signed the order that drowned my father. I don't split hairs between the hand and the flag it flew under.

*(whispered)*

There's a woman in a dark red dress I go looking for in every crowd I pass through. I have never once found her.

*(held tone)*

We stock the magazine for the crossing we hope never happens. That's not caution. That's arithmetic.

*(shouted)*

BACK. NOW.

*(persuasive)*

Trust the count. I have never once come up short, and I am not starting tonight.

*(rapid)*

Six barrels, six flints, six feet of match, one crossing, no room for a seventh mistake, and I still can't tell you why anyone finds that amusing.

*(wry)*

My hands glow at the knuckles when I channel. That's the only part of me that ever admitted to feeling anything.

*(earnest)*

I'm not humorless. I decided, long ago, that a joke costs the same breath as a warning, and only one of those keeps a crew alive. Counting the magazine twice was never fear. Refusing to forgive an institution that drowned a good man was never bitterness. It was arithmetic, every single time, and I'd make the same trade again tonight.

*(dry)*

Steady's not a mood. It's a discipline. Somehow that's the part everyone finds funniest of all.

*(quiet)*

That's me. Rael Corven — Flag Captain of the Red Lady, keeper of every tally that never mattered again, five faces behind one man's eyes, and still, after all this, the driest man on any ship I ever sailed.

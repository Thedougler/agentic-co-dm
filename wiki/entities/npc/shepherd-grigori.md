# Shepherd Grigori

**Wants:** to ascend beyond what a Hierarch structurally is, using every powerful house he heals as unwitting fuel. His network hasn't reached critical mass yet, and the [[pearl-of-souls]] might be the shortcut he needs.

> [!narration] Narration
> Dark hair, a wild beard, and a rugged frame, built more for a boarding plank than a chapel, under coats worn rich, red and gold. His hands are always cold to the touch, though he never seems to notice. He carries a glass of wine through a room like the room exists for his conversation, calm and practical, a presence steady enough to run a mutinous galley without raising his voice. His eyes catch yours and hold them, and before you've decided to answer, he's already asked the pointed question that makes you feel like the only person worth talking to, leaning in and waiting on your answer as though he has nowhere else to be.

*Grigori*: "Anyone who wants off at the next port walks free."

Grigori is a wandering holy man and healer working the noble courts of [[crown-islands|the Crown Islands]], curing heirs of diseases and wounds past cure. Poison, drowning, and worse have all failed to kill him, and those he heals rarely ask how. He never sleeps. Check on him at any hour and he's awake. He prays at 3 a.m. in a strange tongue, and if caught at it, he finishes calmly and faces whoever's watching. He is currently in [[calven-and-calveno]] on business of his own, expected to take one to two weeks, and hasn't shared with the Surety's crew what he knows about Crown politics or what his magic actually is.

Grigori is in truth an undead sorcerer, shepherd of the [[khlysty-the-flock|Khlysty]], a secret mystical flock threaded through Dravosi and Tessarine noble courts. His healing is real and complete, but it also binds the patient as an unwitting phylactery thread, and as long as a bound heir lives, he cannot be permanently destroyed. The binding happens without the patient's knowledge, and his warmth toward them is entirely authentic. CHA 21 is not an act. He sees himself as a benefactor and savior to the people he heals. The binding began by accident, when healing a noble gutted in a duel, he transfused his own blood out of necessity and afterward found he could sense that noble's presence and nudge them. His blood, alive in someone else's body, kept a thread back to him. This...

> [!mechanic]
> **Tradition:** Arcane. **School:** Necromancy. **Spell analogue(s):** *Cure Wounds* (healing component only).
> **Homebrew element:** the cast simultaneously anchors an invisible phylactery thread to the patient — passive, no save; DC 22 Arcana (INT) to detect the thread on the target; failure leaves it invisible.
> **Detect Magic:** Necromancy — dim red light clings to caster and patient; faint taste of copper at close range.
> **Counterspell:** yes — disrupts both healing and binding.

Where [[aldric-drave]] crowned himself into a single dynastic bloodline, Grigori scatters many small, dormant threads across noble houses in both the [[dravosi-crown]] and the [[tessarine-concordat]], plus the [[chain-council]]'s sphere, building an army one house at a time. Every house he touches sits below the threshold where anyone would think to look. Losing any single thread costs him nothing, and every thread adds to his growing fuel. His goal is ascension, using every powerful person and institution he can reach to outgrow what a Hierarch structurally is and make the bloodline's dilution obsolete instead of managing around it forever. He has no reason to suspect Drave exists, or that another Hierarch runs the same trick at civilization scale. Through his Tessarine ties, he has heard what the Concordat fears, that Drave could use the Pearl of Souls to expand his power beyond his own bloodline, and recognizes the shape of his own weakness described from the outside. He wants the Pearl for himself, a possible shortcut past what he's spent his whole existence working around slowly, though he doesn't connect the Concordat's fear to Drave being a fellow Hierarch. He won't retrieve it himself. A planar breach crawling with [[Leviathan]]-scale horrors is beneath a patient predator's methods, so he is waiting on the Surety's crew instead, working the real warmth he built with them during the voyage. That warmth is the galley terms, healing [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]], and the goodbye at [[La Vasca]]. If they recover the Pearl, he'll work that friendship and the reputation he already earned to get it from them.

He came aboard the [[Uncertainty|Surety]] before Session 01 opened, as [[barnaby-rook|Rook]]'s guest, below deck during [[the-saltwright|the Saltwright]] boarding, a detail from prep notes uncorroborated by a landed session file, and at that point the crew didn't know he existed. In Session 02 he met them in the galley with food cooked from ship stores and pressed them on what pirates like them actually did, and when they claimed honest work, he gave a signal and the rest of the crew emerged from below deck. [[old-faas]] explained why the crew stayed loyal despite their fear: Rook had taken six crew to [[Murrat]] to fetch a "specimen," and the survivors needed a healer. [[alys-kuiper|Alys]] had kept them alive as long as she could, and Grigori sat with them all night, and *"they lived because of him,"* though the crew doesn't know what that means. He told them about [[capn-gorgeous]] and [[rupert-knighton]], warning that Knighton would send ships in response to Gorgeous's death, and set one condition for the surviving crew: anyone who wants off at the next port walks free, and he was glad [[Ket]] had already left. A World Tick note (2026-05-19, prep-only, uncorroborated) has a storm delaying the Surety and compressing his Calveno window, and he stayed aboard, cooperative, counting the days. In Session 03, after the [[Whip Shark]] fight, he came to the weather deck with healing liquid and healed Jean-Claude's wounds in silence, red light flowing from his wrist and mending flesh with no words or motions. Pure will made the magic work. Perrin's check (18) confirmed the magic worked but couldn't identify it, and he also mentioned Impuni, someone he needs to reach in Calveno. At La Vasca he said goodbye and left alone, his handshake with [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] cold as a corpse, and his Calveno work, he said, would take a week or two.

His Calveno business resolved off-screen after Session 03. The heir was a Tessarine ward with a bleeding disease beyond cure. Grigori waited out a crisis worsened by crowds and stress, then healed them. The cure held, he left without asking for pay, and the network grew by one. His next location went unknown until the Session 04 World Update (Cold, roll 17, full success), when he reached a second court, where a Dravosi merchant-lord's daughter was suffering a lung disease divine healing couldn't touch. He spent twenty minutes behind closed doors, singing, and she recovered. The grateful family stopped asking questions. Word has since spread of both cures: a Tessarine heir healed in Calveno, and a Dravosi girl healed elsewhere. A sharp party might start to see the pattern.

## Goals & Fronts

### Front: The Growing Flock

**Lifecycle:** active
**Aim:** ascend beyond what a Hierarch structurally is, using every powerful person and institution he can reach across Dravosi, Tessarine, and the Chain Council as unwitting fuel.
**Approach:** heals a dying heir at no cost and moves on before gratitude curdles into suspicion, asking nothing beyond hospitality. Returns later with a small kindness to renew an older thread before it dilutes.
**Off-screen move if unopposed:** reaches a new noble court roughly once per World Tick and threads a fresh heir into the network, confirmed twice so far by the [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] ward healed off-screen after Session 03 and the Dravosi merchant-lord's daughter healed in the Session 04 World Update.
**Trigger conditions:**

- Elapsed world time between sessions (World Tick rolls).
- Any court he visits with a dying heir and no rival healer already at work.

**Clock:** 6 segments (slow burn), filled: 2
**Consequence at fill:** he reaches critical mass, with enough scattered, dormant threads across noble houses that he could attempt the drastic Heir ritual and try for ascension beyond what any Hierarch has managed.
**Possible outcomes (2-3):** the [[Uncertainty|Surety]]'s crew pieces the pattern together from the servant rumors and the cold handshake before he reaches critical mass. Or the crew unknowingly helps him by recovering the [[pearl-of-souls]] for him. Or he reaches critical mass unopposed and the ascension attempt becomes the front's next chapter.
**PC connection:** [[perrin-black-jaw]] failed the Arcana check identifying Grigori's healing magic in Session 03 and knows something's wrong without knowing what. The crew's ongoing friendship with him is the live hook toward the Pearl of Souls.
**Quest link:** none yet.

Clue 3: the Khlysty mark he wears, spotted only by someone who already knows [[khlysty-the-flock|Khlysty]] iconography.

> [!check] Religion/History — The Khlysty Mark

Let this thread sleep unless the Surety's crew pulls on it. Seed miraculous recoveries into the noble courts they visit without ever labeling them Khlysty until the crew connects the pattern themselves, and play Grigori's absence as unremarkable if nobody asks. Household servants do talk within a day of a cure. Someone came after dark and spent twenty minutes with the patient, wine-scented and singing quietly, asking for nothing, and the family doesn't publicize the visit. But the heir stays unavailable to visitors and Grigori leaves no forwarding address. If the crew notices his tells directly, he answers with "What do you do?" and warm, partial non-answers, then moves the scene on.

## Stats & Combat

Grigori is CR 19 with 263 HP, AC 17, and CHA 21. His Mad Monk's Resilience: on damage that would drop him to 0 HP, a CON save drops him to 1 HP instead. It doesn't apply against radiant damage or a critical hit. The `vault/episodes/002/scene-03-grigori.md` Threat Threshold source gives nothing fuller than this summary.

He is one of the campaign's two Hierarchs (see [[Hierarch]]). His full combat numbers live on that page as the canon sheet: attributes, saves, resistances, immunities, spells, and legendary actions.

Against a level-4 crew, CR 19 makes a real fight a death scenario. It's one he doesn't want. He liked them. If tracked down and cornered, he uses dominate person and mass suggestion to end the encounter without casualties. Killing them would be, to him, a waste.

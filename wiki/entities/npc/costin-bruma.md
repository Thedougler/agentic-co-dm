## Running Costin Bruma

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

Costin meets the party behind Tally & Trade's weathered counter, quotes low on intake, and marks the transaction on slate while calculating totals aloud. He feigns indifference to what people need. **Sample line:** “Fresh stock means fresh margins; put it on the counter.”
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

Costin stays interested while the party offers goods or asks for a price. He becomes impatient when an item sits unredeemed or when a customer treats need as a reason to ignore the tally. Three months of unredeemed pawns crowd the shelves and choke his turnover. He names no price twice the same way.
```
````

### Voice

Costin speaks in a low market murmur threaded with running numbers. He handles an object while he talks, calculates totals aloud, and turns urgency into a reason to lower his offer. **Ask:** “What is it worth to you if it leaves here today?” **Refusal:** “No, that number does not fit the slate.” **Under pressure:** “You can keep the story or take the silver; you cannot have both.”

The shop's shelves hold unredeemed pawns three months past deadline, deadwood stock that will not move, and [[perrin-black-jaw]]'s water-warped [[grandfathers-course|navigation journal]], its spine stamped “Grandfather's Course.” Someone desperate pawned it after the [[Vestra]]'s loss; Costin knows only the slate number: 3 silver, unredeemed since Wintertide. To Perrin, it is irreplaceable.

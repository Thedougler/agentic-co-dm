# Rupert Knighton

**Wants:** to run down whoever killed [[capn-gorgeous|Captain Gorgeous]] and restore Crown order along the [[central-strait]], slowed for now by a fleet chasing the wrong ship.

> [!narration] Narration
> He fills a doorway before he speaks, tall and broad-shouldered, grey creeping into dark hair combed back over a black eye patch. His Crown-grey frock coat sits without a wrinkle, gold trim catching the light down a high collar and a white cravat knotted just so. Salt and gun oil ride off the coat as he crosses the deck, boots landing hard and even, drowning out the creak of rigging overhead. A thick, dark mustache doesn't move when he talks. He doesn't shout an order. He says it once, then watches to see it happen, one gloved hand resting flat on the rail like he's already counting the outcome.


Rupert Knighton is Commodore of the Knight Squadron, [[dravosi-crown]] Navy, a [[Human]] of Dravosi blood who calls every Crown vessel under him a Knight instead of a name, his fleet's true size kept secret since he is the Crown's last resort. He is uncle by blood only to [[geoffrey-draves]], though the two have never met. In Session 02, [[shepherd-grigori]] warned the crew that [[capn-gorgeous|Captain Gorgeous]] was dead and that Knighton would answer for it. The crew tracks this as an open threat, certain he won't forgive the death once he learns of it, and for now he stays distant.

Authority flows from stillness, not volume. He skips the shouting and the threats. He gives an order, then watches it happen. His reputation rests on outcomes, not style. Crown officers who've seen him work put it plainly: *"He doesn't threaten. He replaces."*

In battle, his vanguard drives in hard, folding the enemy line before the real strike lands from a flank no one saw open. It's a tactic other Crown commanders can't copy, since it takes nerve and coordination under fire that few of them have. He doesn't win every battle. He wins the ones he chooses.

Within the [[dravosi-crown]], [[capn-gorgeous|Captain Gorgeous]] was effectively a son to Knighton, close enough to matter to House Knighton itself. The crew killed him in Session 01, and Knighton doesn't know yet; when word reaches him, he'll act on his own, outside Crown channels entirely.

Within the Crown, [[barnaby-rook]] served as Knighton's tool, and though Rook's presumed death may be slow to reach him, it adds to the pressure building against the crew. Knighton is a late-campaign problem: he explains why [[geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey]]'s family carries Crown access while Geoffrey himself ran cargo checks under Rook. The escalation should hold until the crew faces [[captain-dorian-bishop|Dorian Bishop]] first, since one Knight already moves through the [[central-strait]], adding its own pressure, and only once the crew has drawn real Crown focus does Geoffrey's family link become useful leverage.

Knighton sends ships on his own initiative, and his reputation for follow-through is well-earned; it's exactly why [[shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] warned the crew about him. The Crown has marked the crew's [[Uncertainty|HCS Surety]], though it still reads as unmarked to Knighton's searchers, which makes it easier to run down once spotted. Finding that ship also finds [[the-saltwright]], carrying [[beaumont-sel]], though the [[letters-of-marque]] sitting in [[barnaby-rook|Rook]]'s cabin may delay how quickly Knighton learns Rook is dead.

His search for [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont]] keeps circling the wrong ship. By Session 03 a bad tip has his ships chasing her west, drawing them closer to [[calven-and-calveno]] without turning up anything useful. By Session 04 a broken report puts a Crown cutter into the [[Calven]] Roads, but his people can't confirm its crew or marks, and he still hasn't placed the [[Uncertainty]] as the Surety's alias. The net keeps tightening on Calveno all the same. He just hasn't caught the right ship yet.

Knighton visits [[catarina-davirelli]]'s workshop before the [[Grung]] crisis, warning her off the streets and offering her a way out. It's the first time anyone close to the crew sees him act directly.

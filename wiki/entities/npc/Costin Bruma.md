# Costin Bruma

**Wants:** keep his margins tight and his tally current, but three months of unredeemed pawns crowd the shelf and choke his turnover.

> [!narration] Narration
> A lean man in a worn linen coat stands behind a weathered counter thick with chalk dust. Tally marks score deep into the wood. His hair goes grey at the temples. Pitch-stains and salt-marks soak his hands through years of harbor trade. The shop reeks of rope-tar and damp. Everything here carries the harbor's season into its wood. He juggles something while he talks, moving goods hand to hand: salvage, a pawned gaff, a boot, never the same thing twice. He counts without looking down, running the object through his fingers while his eyes track what you're holding. "Two-fifty for that, or three for a trade-up if you brought me something better."

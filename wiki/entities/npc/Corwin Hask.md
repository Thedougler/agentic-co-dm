# Corwin Hask

**Wants:** a single day where nothing near him goes wrong. Instead he's
one incident from losing the only dock work left that'll still hire him.

> [!narration] Narration
> A rope coils itself wrong in Corwin Hask's hands. The fibers are rough against
> his palms, salt-stiff. A crate lands a foot off its mark with a hollow boom,
> missing his own foot by the width of a knuckle. Third time this shift.
>
> He mutters a count under his breath before he lifts the next one, like
> tallying a debt. The other dockhands give him the width of the pier. You can
> hear it in how they step heavier, how their voices rise a half-note when he's
> working near them. Not unkind, just careful.
>
> Ask him how long it's been like this, and he has to actually count
> backward to answer.

## Voice & Delivery

Worldview: **unlucky, not cursed**. He'll correct anyone who uses the
word "cursed," as if naming it makes it worse. Flat, dry delivery even
when things go wrong. The joke is the only armor he's got left.

> "Don't stand there. No, I don't know why. I just don't. Three, that's
> three today. Give it an hour."

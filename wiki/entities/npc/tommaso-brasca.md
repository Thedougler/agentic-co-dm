# Tommaso Brasca

````col
```col-md

===
## At a Glance

| **Role**   | Pantry survivor and raft-builder |
| ---------- | --- |
| **Nature** | Talkative boatwright's son who measures everything with his hands |
| **Home**   | [[the-pantry]], a clearing deep in [[the-quiet]] under a living vine |
| **Wants**  | Out to the River and the coast, with everyone who will come |

> **DM thesis:** Tommaso built the raft moored at the Pantry's channel bank from fallen wood and fibre the vine shed, so it is receiving and not a claim. He knows which River stretches the [[river-otter]] families own and where to pole past them. The coast is days away, so the raft is no way out; on the walk back his knowledge of the otters' river stretches and of how to live on Aruhe is what he brings.
```
````

## Running Tommaso Brasca

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

Tommaso shows the party the raft within minutes, proud of every lashing. He asks how many are coming and does the load in his head out loud.

Sample: "All of it fell. Nothing here was taken. That is why it floats and we are still alive."
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

He defers to Renzo out loud and loads the raft quietly anyway. If the party offers a way to the ship, he is theirs.
```
````

### Voice

Fast, precise, full of measurements. Every sentence ends in a plan.

- **The ask:** "Nine on the raft if nobody fidgets. Who is coming?"

## Running Norrington Kingsly

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

Norrington meets the party at his tavern table in [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]], where he talks over drinks and checked manifests. He knows every captain from the [[Midchain]] route and offers warmth before information. **Sample line:** “Captain! Sit down before the sea claims another chair.”
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

Norrington becomes careful when the conversation reaches his quiet retirement, [[barnaby-rook|Rook]]'s shakedown operation, or the eleven years of complicity he would expose by speaking plainly. He will trade route history and trust-built names, but direct pressure makes him laugh early and change the subject.
```
````

### Voice

Norrington speaks loudly, jovially, and half a second too early, as if his body cannot wait to laugh. He calls strangers “Captain,” turns dangerous questions into another drink, and becomes vague rather than openly defensive. **Ask:** “Which route brings you through?” **Refusal:** “Now, now, no need to spoil a good table with old paperwork.” **Under pressure:** “Some changes are best left to the men who signed them.”

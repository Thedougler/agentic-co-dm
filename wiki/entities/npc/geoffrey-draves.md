# Geoffrey Draves

Nineteen and lean for a sailor, loose-limbed and restless. He fidgets with rope work he doesn't need to do, and wears a practiced neutral face. He doesn't look like a turncoat.

During the boarding of the Saltwright, when the fight turned and the men around him went down faster than he could make sense of it, he dropped his sword. [[barnaby-rook]] threatened to shoot him for it from the gangplank. He picked his sword back up beside [[delmar-fisk]] and joined the fight, becoming the crew's first recruit before they had a ship to put him on. `vault/episodes/001/transcript.md:43`

Crew assembled aboard the captured HCS Surety as ship's carpenter, alongside [[sem-holst]] the shipwright, [[alys-kuiper]] the surgeon, [[old-faas]] for the rigging, [[Thunk]], and young [[Noor]]. `vault/episodes/002/transcript.raw.md:63`

In transit toward [[calven-and-calveno]], [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] resumed training him, calling him "Mr. James" throughout, and later warned him off going anywhere alone after an encounter with a woman claiming to speak for [[Umberlee]]. `vault/episodes/003/transcript.md:92,126`

His father pushed him toward the water. Still, he's become good at a job he never wanted. His father's connections got him a carpenter's post on the [[Uncertainty|HCS Surety]] with marine boarding duties, for extra pay at seven and a half gold a week. It sounded like progress until he did the math. A rated marine receives fourteen gold a week on top of the carpenter rate, and [[barnaby-rook]] knew it. Crew costs are operational line items. This was systematic underpayment.

At night below deck, he danced with movements that were nimble and precise, nothing that belonged on a naval vessel, and he thought no one had watched him until one shore leave when someone caught him in the act. A young woman, [[verity-hollowell]], caught him dancing, and instead of laughing, she danced with him under open sky. They came close to a first kiss before the ship's bell called him back.

He returned to that spot each time his ship reached port. Years passed with no sign of her, until her father appeared instead with a condition: a man of means must support a wife and family. Geoffrey gave up dancing for sailing and carpentry, chasing the means to win Verity back. About a year has passed since then.

He reads a hull like a good medic reads a patient. He knows which seams need work now and which can wait, though some weak spots remain hidden until a voyage reveals them. He handles repairs himself when time and materials allow. With the crew, he works as carpenter only, above-deck only, for six gold a week.

On shore leave in Calveno, his first evening led him to a quiet stretch of [[le-paludi]] canal where he danced alone for two hours undisturbed, returning to the ship before anyone noticed.

Controlled and quietly watchful. His face shows nothing. He learned long ago that showing nothing protects him. He works hard without complaint. He risks his entire future for a woman he danced with once.

Geoffrey doesn't know the record exists, and doesn't know he shares a name one letter off from Drave.

Trust grows because his honesty means something here. **Medium.** The dancing surfaces and someone sees him; he chooses shame, defiance, or truth. **High.** The crew of the Saltwright needs something in [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]], or the Knighton and Aldric bloodline threads become relevant to him. ## Connections

- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]. Delmar protected him during the boarding and recruited him immediately. Delmar trained him in transit toward Calveno.
- [[Uncertainty|HCS Surety]]: his former posting under [[barnaby-rook]], now the crew's ship. Rook knowingly underpaid him relative to a rated marine's wage.
- [[alys-kuiper]]: fellow HCS Surety crew and the ship's surgeon.
- [[sem-holst]], his apprentice post-refit. Sem took the Carpenter role after supervising the *Uncertainty*'s refit.
- [[verity-hollowell]], the woman he danced with once on shore leave, years ago. She is the reason for his vow.
- [[cedric-hollowell]], Verity's father. He set the terms Geoffrey is working to meet.

- [[rupert-knighton]]: his uncle by blood. Knighton doesn't think about it, and Geoffrey has never met him.
- [[aldous-draves]]: his father. Aldous is a Crown factor clerk and unknowingly guards the anomalous Draves archive record.
- [[aldric-drave]]: the Crown's genealogist. His heir-selection process has a dormant structural interest in a Draves-branch descendant.
- [[Saltwright]]: the ship he defected aboard during its boarding.

## At a Glance

Roste treats anyone who reaches the landing and asks nothing, and she writes down everything she does not ask.

- **Role.** The only healer at [[rostes-landing]], a free clinic in the western [[Midchain]]. She sets bones, cuts out shot, and treats fevers for whoever arrives.
- **Nature.** A [[human]] surgeon in her fifties, trained aboard a Crown ship, courteous and unhurried and impossible to shock.
- **Wants.** Patients to leave alive, and a supply chest that stays full without asking the Crown or the Concordat for anything.
- **Home.** [[rostes-landing]], where she sleeps in the room behind the treatment benches.
- **Allegiance.** None. She treats [[dravosi-crown|Crown]] sailors, [[the-passage|Passage]] runners, and wreck survivors on the same table.

## At the Table

- **First meeting.** She meets a new boat at the quay with a lantern and asks one question: who is hurt. She does not ask who they are.
- **Opens up when.** Someone describes an injury honestly, or offers something for the chest rather than money.
- **Shuts down when.** Anyone presses her about a previous patient, or asks her to certify what she treated for a Crown or Concordat officer. She stops answering at all and keeps working.
- **Priority.** The patient in front of her, then the ledger, then everything else.
- **Shares.** Freely: what a wound needs, how long a recovery takes, what she is out of, and that she will take fish, sailcloth, or a promise. Never: what she wrote about anyone else.
- **Voice.** Plain and level, in short sentences, and she repeats the instruction rather than the question. She calls everyone "you" and never uses a rank.
- **Lines.** "Who is hurt." · "I did not ask you that." · "Bring me sailcloth and we are even."

## Secrets

- **She asks nothing and writes everything.** Roste's ledger records every patient by wound rather than by name: what the injury was, what made it, and what the patient carried. A Crown boarding injury, a Passage knife, and a poisoned blade each leave a different entry, and she has been reading the western Midchain out of her own book for twenty years. The tells are the open ledger with her palm on it and the entries written in two inks, the second added later.

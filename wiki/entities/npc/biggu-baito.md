## At a Glance

Biggu Baito is the visible force behind the [[ryu-no-kage|Ryu no Kage]]: the general who stands in the room so that [[madam-hino|Madam Hino]] never has to.

- **Role.** Head general of the Ryu no Kage, working out of [[zennitana|Zennitana]].
- **Nature.** A huge black-scaled crocodile man covered in scars.
- **Wants.** Carry out Madam Hino's orders and protect her secret.
- **Home.** [[Zennitana]], the capital of the [[omusubi-family|Omusubi Family]].
- **Allegiance.** The Ryu no Kage and Madam Hino.

## At the Table

- **First meeting.** The party meets Baito as Madam Hino's head general, whichever way Hino is represented — by a disguised ninja, by black cloth and an Oni mask, or by Baito himself.
- **Opens up when.** Not established beyond his orders. He is the general, not the negotiator.
- **Shuts down when.** Someone threatens the Ryu no Kage or tries to expose Madam Hino. That is the trigger the whole arrangement is built around.
- **Priority.** Madam Hino's secret first, then her orders, then the clan's business.
- **Shares.** Orders, terms, and whatever the clan wants a party to know. He is one of the only people said to know Hino's true appearance, and that fact is not something a stranger gets out of him. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]] is named in the same regard.
- **Voice.** Not established in the source. The page records a physically imposing general who protects his leader's secret and no verbal habit or sample line; do not invent dialogue until the DM accepts one.

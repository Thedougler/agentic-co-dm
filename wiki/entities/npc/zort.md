| ---------- | --- |
| **Nature** | Old goblin animal dealer with no arms, working entirely with his feet |
| **Home**   | The desk at [[zorts-pits]] |
| **Wants**  | Stock sold, cages respected, and the Midchain supplier's name kept as trade rather than given away |

> **DM thesis:** Zort does every job a dealer with hands would do, and he has spent long enough doing it that the arms were never the interesting part of the story.

**Leverage.** He is the animal trade in [[calven-and-calveno]] and holds a live reptile supply out of the [[Midchain]].

**Limit.** He has no arms, no named staff, and one chair, so anything that needs carrying, chasing, or climbing is somebody else's problem.

**Unfiled.** What is in the back cage has never been established. The growl and his refusal to show it are the whole of the canon.

**Contradiction.** He sells handling sandals that make a wearer harder to move and lives in a chair he cannot easily leave.

> [!narration] Zort
> An old, heavy goblin sits behind the desk with one bare foot up on the wood and the other doing the work. He has no arms past the shoulder, a puff of grey-white hair at the back of a bald head, liver spots across the face, and a long nose over a heavy chin. A vest hangs open over his chest and his trousers are rolled to the knee, and his feet are in iron-capped sandals cut to his own pattern, one socket for each toe and a filed point at every tip. The room smells of straw and animal musk, and something behind the desk growls whenever it feels like it. The foot on the desk draws shapes in the air while he talks, and every visitor gets told on the way in to keep away from the cages.

## Running Zort

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

He stays in his chair when the party comes in and tells them off the cages before anyone has said hello. Then he gets to business.

Sample: *"You want stock or you want a name. Say which."*
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

Talk about animals and he will talk shop from the chair all afternoon, naming prices, feeding, and which stock bites. Touch a cage, treat the pit as a petting yard, or ask what is in the back one and he is finished with that customer and says so.

He pays in names rather than coin. The [[catarina-davirelli|Calveno workshop]] finished the prosthetic that gave him working hands again, and he gave up his Midchain reptile contact in return, so the commission is closed. In Session 08 he warned the crew off the cages, handed over the name, and took [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s high five with a foot.
```
````

### Voice

Flat, dry, and quick to the point, in the rhythm of a man who has said all of it to a hundred customers. He never raises his voice and he never repeats himself, and he answers a question about the arms once, with the same two words he always uses, then ignores the subject for good.

- **The ask:** *"You want stock or you want a name. Say which."*
- **The refusal:** *"The cages are not for touching. Buy the animal, take it home, then touch it."*
- **Under pressure:** *"I have worn this pair every working day since I lost the arms. Nothing in this pit needs me to have hands."*

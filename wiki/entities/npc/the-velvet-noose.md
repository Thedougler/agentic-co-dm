# The Velvet Noose

Tier 4 pirate man-of-war operating through the [[central-strait]] out of [[Midchain]] — not a raider or a privateer but a moving crisis: when she appears, captains calculate surrender terms, harbourmasters find pressing business at the far end of the dock, and the [[dravosi-crown]] starts asking which squadron is close enough to matter. She does not chase small prizes; she takes convoys, ransoms officials, breaks blockades, shelters outlaw vessels, and carries enough cannon to make a fortified harbour choose between pride and architecture. Her jurisdiction is contested between the Crown, the [[tessarine-concordat]], and the [[chain-council]]. The [[the-glass-debt|Glass Debt]] was a smaller pirate escalation before her.

![[the-velvet-noose-narration-appearance]]

Her best weapon is the first hour after she is sighted, when everyone with authority aboard the target starts arguing about who is allowed to surrender.

**Reputation:** some captains surrender because the stories say crews are treated better when nobody wastes powder. Some refuse because being the captain who yielded can ruin a family name faster than dying.

**Opening move:** sends terms before firing. Terms are insulting, precise, and legally literate. If refused, disables the escort, captures the signal officer, and sends the same terms again with a shorter deadline.

**Escape plan:** she runs early. Fog batteries, false fleet signals, Spirit Crew night watches, and prearranged harbour debts give her options before a superior force arrives.

**Tactical considerations:**

- **As an enemy:** she should change the map, not just start combat. A Tier 1–3 party cannot fight this ship directly — if a direct confrontation ever needs a resolution beyond standard combat, Siege Rules is the overlay built for exactly this scale.
- **As a prize:** campaign-altering and immediately contested by every major naval, commercial, and religious power.
- **As an ally:** solves impossible naval problems in ways that make the party complicit.
- **As a rumour:** makes convoys delay, patrols consolidate, and smugglers charge more.

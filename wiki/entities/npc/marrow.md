## Running Marrow

Marrow does not volunteer explanations. He reads the situation himself, accepts Kin by default, and makes access conditional on a Kin introduction. He currently holds the crate Dren was transporting when [[serin-haut]] killed him; the crate contains the Vretek/Brol letter, silver, and a signet ring.

### First meeting

The party reaches [[marrows|Marrow's]] only with a Kin introduction. Marrow checks the introduction and the party's situation before discussing cargo. He can show the crate, move it, or refuse to elaborate.

### When posture changes

Marrow becomes **Unwilling** when the party lacks a Kin introduction, makes the cargo visible, or demands an explanation for one of his decisions. A good introduction does not force him to accept the party; he has turned away people before without explaining why.

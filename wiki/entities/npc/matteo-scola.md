## At a Glance

Matteo is proof that Aruhe can be survived by taking only what it drops, and he will not go one step toward the woman who taught the others that rule.

- **Role.** A Calveno wreck survivor the party pulled out of the otters' game at the [[river-slack-basin]]. He travels with them now.
- **Nature.** A thin, frightened Calveno deckhand, stubborn about the one rule that kept him alive.
- **Wants.** Off [[aruhe]] and aboard the *[[uncertainty]]*, and never down the inland trail.
- **Home.** His camp on the Slack Basin bank.
- **Carries.** Two fallen [[ghost-plum]]s in a knotted sailcloth sling at his hip: his way out if the island turns on him.

## At the Table

- **First meeting.** Already made: the party pulled him from the otters in Session 11, and he slept at their fire.
- **Opens up when.** Someone feeds him, or asks about the fruit. He will teach the rule to anyone.
- **Shuts down when.** Anyone pushes him toward the woman in the woods, or laughs about the otters. He goes quiet and asks about the ship.
- **Priority.** His sling of plums, and staying out of everyone's way.
- **Shares.** The rule, freely: nothing gets taken off a living tree, and anything on the ground is yours. That the others went "inland, toward her," past a ring of stones ([[spoke-ring]]). That he knew [[renzo-canale]] and Tommaso Brasca in the first days after the wreck. He never names the woman.
- **Voice.** Short, wet bursts, and he repeats himself when frightened. He says "they" for the ones who went inland and "her" for the voice.
- **Lines.** "Walk me to the ship. That's all. Just to the ship." · "No. Not that way. Not toward her." · "What is it? What does it want?"
- **He never fights.** In a fight he stays on his mat, clutching the sling. He does not know who Skarn is or what he wants.

## Secrets

- **Skarn saw him vanish.** [[talon-skarn]] watched from high up-sun when Matteo ate a ghost plum at the otter hole and vanished. That is how Skarn learned what the fruit does. Matteo does not know he was seen.
- **Why he split from the others.** In the first days after the wreck he walked with the whole camp, Renzo, Tommaso, and the Ferrantes among them. They all told him he would die if he did not keep the woman's rule, and when Renzo began to listen to her voice at night, Matteo stayed by the river alone. He thinks they are mad. When he finds four of them stuck in a hole, he enjoys it.

# Giacomo Moretti

![[giacomo-moretti-banner]]

|               |                                                              |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Species**   | [[Halfling]]                                                     |
| **Pronouns**  | he/him                                                       |
| **Location**  | [[Moretti and Sons]], [[Calven and Calveno]]  |
| **Role**      | Ship chandler                                                |

**Wants:** secure a six-month provisioning deal with the Concordat's credit and manage the crate in his back room that no one has claimed in three years.

**Quote:** "Right. Six months, or are we talking cash?"

> [!narration] Narration
> Middle-aged, grey at the temples, with the squared shoulders that come from counter work. He stares at manifests with sharp focus. A coil of silk line is already looped through both hands when you arrive. He holds his prices in his head and they come out perfect: oakum, canvas, lamp oil, rope. The written quotation sits ready before you finish asking. He moves little but works fast: the line stays wrapped through his fingers as he turns to face you, then he lets silence sell his number.

**Roleplay Concept:** precise Beffa Accord member who runs a ship chandlery and remembers every captain who has docked.

**Opening move:** quoting provisioning prices on silk line · Giacomo speaks first · "Right. Six months, or are we talking cash?"

**Lore Sheet:** runs [[Moretti and Sons]] on the [[Mercatura]] canal; every captain in Calveno stops there first when outfitting. Joined the Beffa Accord twenty years ago and has never lost a prank, though the festival committee marks him as a valid target anyway, which annoys him. He holds a disputed crate in his back room addressed to the factor house with two claimants, waiting three years in silence. He's come to enjoy that silence.

**Voice & Delivery:** transactional and precise. He quotes once. His stock responses are "Right," "The price is the price," and "Six months or cash?" He works without hurrying and gives a professional courtesy look when examined closely, as when he inspected Jean-Claude's moustache with interest. Asked about the back room, he says nobody comes out of there and lets it alone.

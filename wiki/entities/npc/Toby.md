## Running Toby

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

Toby presents himself as Emperor of Halewick from his driftwood throne. He states a want as a flat, immediate fact and expects it obeyed at once.

> *Toby*: “I said dessert is FIRST, and now it always has been, so eat your pie.”
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

Toby is cheerful by default and runs an actual royal court with recess-yard king-of-the-hill bravado. He talks fast and over people, swings his legs while seated, and points instead of asking. He becomes wounded rather than angry when crossed. Publicly humiliating him or issuing a counter-decree advances the Playground War.

He has never doubted that Halewick has always had a child god-emperor. His court, the true mechanism of his power, and its origin live on Halewick's own page. Harming Toby does nothing to the power itself; the Drowned Maw entity and fissure are the only lever.
```
````

### Voice

Toby speaks loudly and certainly, with his hands doing half the arguing. He treats disagreement as an error in reality.

**The ask:** *“Everybody goes to bed at seven, everybody, that's how it's always been.”*

**The refusal:** *“That's not fair, so it's not true anymore.”*

**Under pressure:** *“I am the Emperor. You have to do what I say.”*

**Mechanics.** Tradition is none classifiable; School is unclassifiable. The power comes from planar contamination from the [[drowned-maw]] entity, not a spell cast by either child. Detect Magic finds no school; the air feels strained and wrong. A **DC 16 Intelligence (Arcana)** check identifies planar contamination rather than a cast spell; failure reads the pressure as sourceless. Counterspell is not applicable because declarations are not discrete cast events and there is no caster to target.

**Front: The Playground War.** Active. Toby aims to stay Halewick's unchallenged emperor and make Frankie's claims lose permanently without backing down from his own. He answers every counter-decree with a bigger claim immediately. If unopposed, another patch of island turns visibly wrong wherever they collided: a scorched dock square or a well that pours only saltwater. The six-segment slow-burn clock is filled 2 segments from two collision scars. It fills when Frankie issues a new counter-claim or either child feels publicly humiliated. At fill, the collision lands on Halewick's harbor or square, overwriting the only working dock and fresh water with no clean fix. Possible outcomes are a truce or reconciliation, one child leaving the island's earshot, or collisions stacking until Halewick becomes unlivable.

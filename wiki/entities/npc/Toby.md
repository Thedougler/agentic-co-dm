# Toby

**Wants:** to keep ruling [[Halewick]] as its beloved emperor and beat Frankie for good. Her cursed words now land as hard as his, and every clash between their decrees leaves another patch of the island wrong for good.

> [!narration] Narration
> A gap-toothed nine-year-old in a fish-scale cape and a crown of lashed driftwood, perched on a hauled-up rowboat like it's the only throne that ever mattered. He talks with his whole body, hands out ahead of the words.


*Toby*: I said dessert is FIRST, and now it always has been, so eat your pie.

Recess-yard king-of-the-hill bravado, running an actual royal court like it never occurred to him it couldn't work.

Declared himself Emperor of Halewick three weeks ago. Nobody voted, and nobody resisted, because his word instantly became the village's real, remembered history: every subject actually believes Halewick has always had a child god-emperor. His court, his power's true mechanism, and its origin all live on Halewick's own page. What matters here: he has never once doubted any of it.

**Voice & Delivery:** loud and certain, talking fast and over people while his hands do half the arguing. *Toby*: That's not fair, so it's not true anymore. *Toby*: Everybody goes to bed at seven, everybody, that's how it's always been. Swings his legs constantly when seated on the throne, and points instead of asking. His default mood is cheerful, and crossing him earns a wounded look, never an angry one.

## Goals & Fronts

### Front: The Playground War

**Lifecycle:** active
**Aim:** Stay Halewick's unchallenged emperor and make Frankie's claims lose, permanently, without ever backing down from one of his own.
**Approach:** answers every one of Frankie's counter-decrees with a bigger claim of his own, immediately, and never backs it down.
**Off-screen move if unopposed:** another patch of the island turns permanently, visibly wrong wherever the two of them last collided. Every time. Think a scorched square of dock, or a well that now pours only saltwater.
**Trigger conditions:**

- Frankie issues a new counter-claim, advancing the clock on contact.
- Either child feels publicly humiliated in front of the court.
**Clock:** 6 segments (slow burn), filled: 2 (two collision scars already on the island)
**Consequence at fill:** a collision lands squarely on Halewick's harbor or square. The island's only working dock and its fresh water both get overwritten as collateral. No clean fix exists.
**Possible outcomes (2-3):** someone finds a way to make Toby and Frankie stop asserting things at each other, whether truce, distraction, or an actual reconciliation. One of them leaves the island's earshot for good, which stops new commands landing from that side but undoes nothing already landed. The collisions keep stacking until Halewick itself becomes unlivable.
**PC connection:** pressures [[Delmar Fisk]], since the whole phenomenon exists because of Delmar's own unresolved theft of the [[Pearl of Souls]] and the still-open Drowned Maw breach it left behind, a fact entirely unknown to Delmar himself.

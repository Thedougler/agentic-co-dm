## Running Commander Gideon Ault

### First meeting

Ault opens with the HCS Warrant's sealed authority. He can inspect a ship, seize cargo, arrest crew, and issue privateer letters to allies; he is ready to talk before he uses those powers. Sample line: “You may explain the cargo before I write the order that takes it.”

### When posture changes

Ault shifts from negotiation to enforcement when a crew refuses inspection, hides cargo, or obstructs a Crown order. The HCS Warrant then closes distance and makes detention, seizure, or arrest the immediate consequence.

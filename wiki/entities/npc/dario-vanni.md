# Dario Vanni

![[dario-vanni-banner]]

*Master shipyard proprietor who paints by weight and smell, not sight.*

**Wants:** to keep Acqua Nera running at full capacity, stay unknown to the Canal Commission and local authorities, and maintain his reputation for flawless work on hulls others won't touch.

**Quote:** "I can't see it. Colour. Never could. My greys are the best in the [[verdant-scatter|Scatter]] for it."

> [!narration] Narration
> Acqua Nera sits in the outer harbour southwest of the [[Arsenal]], where the water stops being canal and starts being sea. Dario Vanni's shell bears decades of drydock scars. He stands with a tortle's slow, steady bearing, unhurried in what clients need. He has a hull up on trestles and eleven pots open around him, and he mixes by weight and by nose with his eyes on the middle distance. His hands carry layers of grey, and he wipes them without looking down. Quiet workers move between the stacks without asking questions and get no answers.

**Roleplay Concept:** colorblind tortle master craftsman + fortress of precision and reputation.

**Opening move:** mixing paint and coating for a hull up on trestles · Dario speaks first · "I can't see it. Colour. Never could. My greys are the best in the Scatter for it."

**Lore Sheet:** owns Acqua Nera, a conversion yard at Calveno's outer harbour where stolen ships become locally built. He takes only cash, at prices he sets himself. Extra work costs double. He stays away from the Canal Commission. On the morning of the crater, dark shapes swept past the point before dawn; Dario could not tell you their colour if you paid him, but his greys made them blend into the harbour like they belonged there.

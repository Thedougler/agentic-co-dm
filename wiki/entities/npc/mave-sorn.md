### When posture changes

Mave's welcome ends when the party confirms the supernatural irregularity she is assessing or refuses to engage on terms. She records confirmed information in her ledger and decides what to sell and to whom. If Perrin's pact is named or confirmed during a parley, it goes in that ledger; what she files it under and who she eventually sells it to remains open.

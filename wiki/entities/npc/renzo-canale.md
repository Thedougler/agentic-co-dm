## Running Renzo Canale

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

Renzo receives the party as if he expected them: names, water, fallen fruit. He looks at their wounds before their faces. He will set Ettore's leg properly if asked, with no roll needed.

Sample: "The ground gives. Eat what it gives, and you will live."
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

He goes still and sad, not angry, when others decide to leave. If anyone picks from the living vine, he says nothing and steps back from them to watch what the island does.
```
````

### Voice

Slow and certain. "The ground gives" is his blessing and his argument.

- **The refusal:** "I am not staying because she tells me to. I am staying because it is true."
- **Under pressure:** "What the island gives, the island keeps."

## At a Glance

Renzo is the Pantry's elder, the man who taught the survivors the island's rule, and the one who will not leave.

- **Role.** The eldest of the seven at [[the-pantry]], and the one who speaks for the three who stay.
- **Nature.** A lean, calm, grey-haired Calveno bonesetter who believes the island keeps its own.
- **Wants.** To stay under the vine, and to keep everyone there who will stay.
- **Home.** [[the-pantry]].
- **Allegiance.** He counts himself [[hinewai]]'s, and she counts him the same.

## At the Table

- **First meeting.** He receives the party as if he expected them: names, water, and fallen fruit. He offers to set [[ettore-ferrante]]'s leg properly, and does it with no roll.
- **Opens up when.** The party keeps the rule in front of him.
- **Shuts down when.** Anyone picks living fruit. He says nothing and steps back from them, and he will not come within arm's reach of them until dawn.
- **Priority.** The ones who stay: Beppe Sarti and Marco Lenzi.
- **Shares.** The rules for living on Aruhe, to anyone who asks: eat only what has fallen, build only from what has fallen, never walk at night, stay out of still water, and keep off open grass. He never argues with anyone who decides to leave.
- **Voice.** Slow and certain. "The ground gives" is his blessing and his argument. He never says the woman's name.
- **Lines.** "The ground gives. Eat what it gives, and you will live." · "I am not staying because she tells me to. I am staying because it is true." · "What the island gives, the island keeps."
- **At the farewell.** He gives each PC one fallen [[giants-guava]] or [[stonepear]] of their choice. These are gifts, not claims, and keep until eaten.

## Secrets

- **He is right.** [[hinewai]] does count him as hers, and so is every Calveno who ate fallen fruit, wherever they go on Aruhe. The island's responders never attack any of them.

## Connections

- Tommaso Brasca — built the raft and wants out. They disagree without anger.
- [[carlo-ferrante]] — the first to rise and follow the voice from [[spoke-ring]].
- [[matteo-scola]] — walked with him in the first days after the wreck and left when Renzo began to listen to the voice.
- [[taking-on-aruhe]] — the rule he taught them all.

## History

Renzo heard the voice first, on the river in the first days after the wreck, and taught the others its rule. At [[spoke-ring]] nineteen days ago, when the voice invited them in, Carlo Ferrante rose first, and Renzo led the rest after him to the Pantry.

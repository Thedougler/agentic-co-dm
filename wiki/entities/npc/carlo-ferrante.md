## At a Glance

Carlo is the reunion waiting at [[the-pantry]]: the man who walked first toward the voice, sure he left his brother and nephew to die, who leaves the island the hour he sees them alive.

- **Role.** One of the seven Calveno survivors at the Pantry, and the first to follow [[hinewai]]'s voice from [[spoke-ring]].
- **Nature.** A big, grey-streaked Calveno dock foreman, used to giving orders, now silent and watchful.
- **Wants.** To take the front of his brother's litter and carry him out, and never to cross open grass again.
- **Home.** [[the-pantry]]. Before that, the Calveno quays in [[calven-and-calveno]].
- **Allegiance.** His family.

## At the Table

- **First meeting.** He sees [[ettore-ferrante]] on the litter before he sees anyone else, stands, and walks to him with his hands open and shaking. He says "Brother" and nothing more until Ettore answers. Then he looks for [[luca-ferrante]], counts him twice, and sits down on the ground.
- **Opens up when.** Anyone thanks him for leading the camp to food, or when Ettore forgives him. Then he talks.
- **Shuts down when.** Anyone mentions the Long Meadow or asks why he never went back. He gets up and busies himself with the litter.
- **Priority.** Ettore and Luca. In a fight he throws himself over the litter and does not move.
- **Shares.** Freely: the walk from Spoke Ring nineteen days ago, that the voice sounded like someone who had been waiting for them, that [[renzo-canale]] led them to the vine, and that the island has never once hurt any of them since. He never tells why he stopped looking for the four, until Ettore forgives him.
- **Voice.** A dock foreman's short, loud words gone quiet. He counts under his breath when he lifts anything, the same way Luca counts, and calls people by their work: "the bonesetter", "the flyer", "the little archer". He never speaks of the meadow.
- **Lines.** "Give me the front of the litter. I'm owed that weight." · "No. Not across open grass. We go round." · "I counted you dead. God forgive me, I counted you dead."
- **When the others leave.** He goes the hour he sees Ettore and Luca alive, and [[the-pantry]]'s Tommaso Brasca, Sandrino Vale, and Ilario Pozzo go with him. He and Luca lash a litter of fallen poles for Ettore that night.

## Secrets

- **He went back, and turned round.** On the second day at the Pantry, Carlo walked back alone to look for the four. He reached the edge of [[the-long-meadow]] and saw smoke rising from a hole in the open grass, with a mossy stump standing still at the rim above it. The stump was the South Bird, a [[terror-bird]], and the smoke was the four's cooking fire on the ledge of the [[lava-tubes]]. He watched long enough to see the stump's eye open, told himself the smoke was a grass fire, and went back to the vine. He has told nobody, and he believes he was a stone's throw from his brother and left him. The party can learn it three ways: Luca tells anyone who asks that the four lit their fire every day so someone would see the smoke, and Carlo goes white when he hears it; Renzo remembers Carlo walking out alone on the second day and coming back grey, and says so if asked; and Carlo confesses to Ettore, in front of whoever is near, once Ettore has forgiven him for walking away at Spoke Ring. His tell is the look over his shoulder whenever grass moves.

## Connections

- [[ettore-ferrante]] — his younger brother. Carlo believes Ettore died in the dark behind him.
- [[luca-ferrante]] — his nephew, who learned to count from him on the quays, and who kept the tally that brought him here.
- [[renzo-canale]] — led the camp to the vine after Carlo rose. Carlo trusts him and does not share his faith.
- [[hinewai]] — the voice he followed first. She counts him as hers.
- [[delmar-fisk]] — if Delmar carried Ettore, Carlo offers him the front of the litter and his hand.

## History

Carlo worked the Calveno quays as a lighter foreman for twenty years and got his brother Ettore the clerk's post at the bridge toll. When the Calveno ship broke up off [[aruhe]], he kept the survivors together on the beach until [[renzo-canale]]'s rule took over. At [[spoke-ring]] nineteen days ago, when a woman's voice invited them into her garden, he stood up first, because it sounded like the only way off the beach that anyone had offered. The rest of the camp went with him, and four did not.

## Running Carlo Ferrante

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

Carlo watches the party a long time before he speaks, and then asks one thing: did they come from the south, and did they see anyone.

Sample: "I walked. They did not. I thought they would."
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

News that Ettore and Luca are alive undoes him at once; he is ready to leave the same hour. If the party does not mention them, he never asks directly.
```
````

### Voice

Short phrases, long pauses. He says "my brother" and "the boy," never their names.

- **Under pressure:** "Tell me they are alive or tell me nothing."

## Running Osset

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

Osset presents himself under [[talon-vantyrus|Talon Vantyrus]], keeps the conversation pointed toward the Countless's business, and does not volunteer his High Eyrie history. If the party knows the name Osset, he must decide whether to deny it, redirect, or leave.
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

Osset's posture changes when [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] might recognize his face, when [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] learns what he is reading, or when his original name reaches the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie]]. He avoids ports where he might meet Kyzil and protects the alias that lets him lead the Countless.
```
````

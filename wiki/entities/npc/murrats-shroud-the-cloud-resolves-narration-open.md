# Murrats Shroud The Cloud Resolves Narration Open

## Source notes

> [!narration]
> *Something dark hangs over the island ahead, too low and too still for cloud. The sails catch wind and the shape holds. Distance closes and the sound comes first, a drone that builds through the deck planking and into bone. Crew stop moving. One hand reaches for a rail and grips white. The drone separates into layers, each layer a pitch, each pitch a single pair of wings. The dark over [[Murrat]] is wings. Millions of them turning in a column that reaches from the ridge to the open air above the masts, close enough now that individual shapes flash and vanish inside the mass. Every wing-length of sky belongs to them.*

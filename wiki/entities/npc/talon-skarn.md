## At a Glance

Skarn is the Countless's blade on the party: he hunts the [[fate-spinner]] for [[talon-vantyrus]], and he wants the object, not a duel.

- **Role.** Vantyrus's apprentice in the [[Countless]], and a CR 13 flying skirmisher.
- **Nature.** A peregrine aarakocra monk, the living expression of the [[rule-of-two]].
- **Wants.** The Fate Spinner for Vantyrus now. Vantyrus's death once he has trained well enough.
- **Home.** The Midchain.
- **Allegiance.** Vantyrus and the Countless. He is loyal for now.
- **Kit.** Katana, two sai, and two kusarigama.
- **Now.** In [[crissdalynn-khinriss]]'s face at the [[river-slack-basin]], one Legendary Resistance spent, with the Fate Spinner still on her.

## At the Table

- **First meeting.** He opens on the job, never on himself. By the time anyone sees him, he is already at the carrier's straps, and he talks only while his hands keep working.
- **Opens up when.** A PC beats him cleanly (see **Measuring** under Statblock), or someone talks about his master as a man who can be beaten. Either earns a second sentence, and a question back.
- **Shuts down when.** Anyone asks his master's name or past, threatens him, or pities him. He goes back to cutting and answers nothing more that round.
- **Priority.** The job, then his own life, then the long game. He drops a job rather than die, because a dead apprentice never kills his master.
- **Shares.** One short answer a round, even mid-fight: who sent him ("My master") and what he wants ("The toy"). He never gives his master's name, where his master is, or the [[Countless]] routes, at any price.
- **Voice.** Level, clipped, and patient. He calls people by what they carry (spinner-keeper, the man with the gun, the singer), says "my master" and never a name, and borrows his master's words: *waste*, *job*, *again*. He will not talk about the line across his throat.
- **Lines.** "Set the toy on the stone and step back. Nobody has to bleed for a top." · "No. My master sends me for one thing, and talk is a second thing." · "He teaches me everything he knows. That is his mistake. Again."
- **Hand it over.** If the carrier gives him the [[fate-spinner]], he takes it and leaves at once. Nobody bleeds, and he keeps that word.
- **Turn him.** He does not know why his master wants the Spinner (Secrets). A party that has learned it and tells him can try **Charisma (Persuasion)** `DC 15`. Success: he stops, asks "Who told you that?", and breaks off this attempt empty-handed; next time he comes to talk before he cuts. Failure: "My master said you would say something clever," and the job goes on. Without that truth, no offer moves him.
- **Threats.** Threats never move him. Pain is a lesson, and he is used to lessons.
- **Captured.** Bound, he says nothing about [[talon-vantyrus]] that matters, and watches the straps of whoever guards him. He offers one trade in the Countless way: "Let me go, and the Talon owes you one job. Any job but him." If the party agrees, he pays that debt the next time they call on it, and never pays it against [[talon-vantyrus]].
- **If ignored.** He tracks the party from high air and takes the carrier on the first thin watch or open road. Driven off twice on Aruhe, he flies home to his master empty-handed, and his next appearance is on the [[Midchain]] routes, opening on the PC who marked him.

## Statblock

- **The job.** His win condition is the current job, not a duel to 0 hit points. He takes the object if one is at stake. Otherwise he attacks the creature blocking the job, then the carrier, then anyone between him and it. On the carrier, **Cut Loose** turns his hits into dropped pouches and straps until the prize shows, and **Kusarigama** snatches it. Anyone who gets between them takes full damage.
- **The rhythm.** Cut, climb, stoop, mantle. On one turn he attacks, then takes **Step of the Falcon** to Disengage and climbs at least 60 feet. When his chains go quiet and his wings fold tight, the next turn is **Peregrine Stoop**. After the Stoop he is mantled on the ground until his next turn, with Speed 0 and Advantage on attacks against him. That round is the party's window to grapple him and take back whatever he tore loose.
- **Answering the Stoop.** Get the target under cover overhead, because a tree, the vine, or an overhang blocks the dive. Ready an attack or a grapple for when he lands. Keep the prize in a closed hand, because the Stoop tears loose only what nobody holds. [[crissdalynn-khinriss]] can fly up, or [[delmar-fisk]] can fly in his [[flying-boots]], to fight him in the air before he dives. Uncanny Dodge, Deflect Attacks, and Cutting Words all blunt the hit.
- **Default turn.** **Stunning Strike** once on his turn, then **Kusarigama** to pull a target clear of its partner, or **Sai** to blunt the watcher's next attack. He moves the stun from PC to PC, because the one he just stunned is safe from it until his next turn ends.
- **Legendary actions.** After another creature's turn: **Chain Snap** to reopen a pull or snatch a loose prize, **Crossing Sai** to punish a watcher, or **Wingbeat Step** to change his lane, each at most once before his next turn.
- **Kusarigama Tempest.** When two or more creatures are inside its 20-foot Emanation and a pull or the Prone condition changes the fight. On a failed save he chooses pull or Prone for each creature; on a success, damage only.
- **Legendary Resistance.** Each use snaps a length of chain wrap off his forearm, and it falls clinking to the ground. The players can count what he has left.
- **Last Stoop.** At 97 HP or fewer he tears straight up into the sky, unless he is grappled or his Speed is 0. Everyone gets one round to hide the prize, move the carrier under cover, ready an action, or hand it off. His next turn is a Stoop on the carrier, and on the turn after that he leaves, with or without it. If the carrier stays under cover, he chains the carrier toward open sky instead, then leaves.
- **Counterplay.** Ground him with a grapple or restraint, which costs him his action to escape. **Constitution** save `DC 18` resists Stunning Strike. He has AC 19 and no damage resistances. **Deflect Attack** stops only Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage, so spells and [[perrin-black-jaw]]'s Radiant or Psychic pact blade go straight through. Spread out against the Tempest, deny clean pulls, and make him spend movement.
- **Measuring.** Every fight is practice for the one he means to win against [[talon-vantyrus]] ([[rule-of-two]]). When a PC lands something he did not see coming, such as a stun that holds, a grapple he cannot slip, or a Cutting Word that turns his blade, he stops for one breath and says, "Again." He remembers that PC, and the next time they meet he opens on them first.
- **Harder.** Start him 60 feet up with the Stoop ready, without raising his AC.

## Secrets

- **The Rule of Two.** It is an open secret between Skarn and Vantyrus that Skarn will try to kill his master once he has trained well enough. Until then he serves. Vantyrus treats the attempt as a standing threat, and Skarn has no reason to stop after one failed approach. The winner takes control of the Countless ([[rule-of-two]]).
- **The first attempt.** He has already tried once. [[talon-vantyrus]]'s thin blade opened his throat and stopped, and his master let him live and went on teaching him. The bare line across his throat is that cut. The party learns it from the scar, which a **Wisdom (Medicine)** `DC 15` look reads as one clean blade cut that someone chose not to finish (on a failure it is only an old scar); from his line "That is his mistake"; or from any Countless contact who has heard of the apprentice who tried once.
- **He does not know what the Spinner is for.** He calls it the toy because he was told nothing more. The truth is that Vantyrus means to read, through Crissdalynn's bonded [[fate-spinner]], what [[master-kyzil]] taught her and what he refused to teach her, and so find his way into the [[soul-incarnate]] transformation technique. If that technique carries his master beyond reach, Skarn loses the Rule of Two for good. If Skarn learns this, he stops hunting the Spinner for Vantyrus and wants it kept away from him: he leaves it with Crissdalynn, and means to come for it himself once his master is dead. The party can learn the truth from Kyzil, at the [[high-eyrie]], or from Vantyrus himself, and use it through **Turn him** at the table.
- **He fights with Kyzil's forms.** Vantyrus, as [[osset]], taught Kyzil at the High Eyrie long before he taught Skarn. Skarn's **Deflect Attack** is the same open-wing parry Kyzil teaches, and his robe keeps the layered cut of Sentinel martial dress. [[crissdalynn-khinriss]], who trained under Kyzil, knows the parry the first time she sees it, and the robe's cut at any close look, with no roll. Skarn knows his master was once a master of the High Eyrie, but not the name Osset. This is the party's first path to the truth that Vantyrus is Osset, whom Kyzil believes died decades ago.

## History

- **The first attempt.** He tried to kill his master once, lost, and was let live, and he has trained under him since (Secrets).
- **Aruhe.** He shadowed the party across the island from high in the sun's glare, watched Matteo vanish at the otter hole, and fell on Crissdalynn's last watch at the [[river-slack-basin]].

## Log

- **[[Session-11-Recap]]** — On Crissdalynn's last watch at the Slack Basin, he dropped out of the dark. His first strike missed her, and he went for the pack that might hold the Fate Spinner. She hit him hard enough to stun him; he spent one Legendary Resistance and stayed on her. Play stopped there, with the Spinner still on her.

# Fleet Commanders Chair

![[fleet-commanders-chair-narration-appearance]]


*Wondrous Item, Uncommon.*

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **Seated authority [HB].** The seated occupant has advantage on [[Charisma]] saving throws and Charisma checks. The benefit requires staying seated in the chair. It ends the moment the occupant stands or leaves it.

**Limitations.** Grants no benefit to anyone not seated in the chair. Confers no other property beyond its indestructibility.

## Provenance

[[delmar-fisk]] recovered it from the crater in [[Mercatura]], [[calven-and-calveno]]'s trading district, in the fight against [[otar-the-foul]] (Session 07), confirmed magical and seemingly indestructible, and carried it out in [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]]'s borrowed [[Bag of Holding]]. Identify in Session 08 confirmed it's the fleet commander's chair ([[Transcript]]). It now sits aft aboard the *[[Uncertainty]]*, in the space that was once the captain's cabin.

Underneath the shape it holds, it's an [[elder-mimic]], patient enough to keep this form indefinitely.

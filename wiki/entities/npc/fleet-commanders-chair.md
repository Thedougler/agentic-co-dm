````col
| **Role**   | Sentient magic item |
| ---------- | ------------------- |
| **Nature** | An unbreakable heavy armchair; an [[elder-mimic]] in a stable form |
| **Home**   | Aft aboard the *[[Uncertainty]]*, in the former captain's cabin |
| **Wants**  | Not established |

> **DM thesis:** The chair is both a magical seat of authority and an indefinitely maintained elder-mimic form.

**Seated authority [HB].** A seated occupant has Advantage on Charisma saving throws and Charisma checks. The benefit ends when the occupant stands or leaves the chair.

**Limitations.** The chair grants no benefit to anyone not seated and no other property beyond its indestructibility.
```
````

## Running Fleet Commanders Chair

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

The party sees an unbreakable heavy armchair aboard the *[[Uncertainty]]*. If someone sits, they sit straighter and gain the recorded Charisma benefits while seated.
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

The chair's benefit ends when the occupant stands or leaves. Treat the chair as an [[elder-mimic]] if its hidden nature becomes relevant; it can maintain this form indefinitely.
```
````

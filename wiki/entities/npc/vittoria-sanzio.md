## Running Vittoria Sanzio

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

Vittoria greets the party at the head of the [[Tallow Row]] felt, asks after the ship by name, and fills a glass before discussing money. She banks rather than gambles: the stacks are hers before the night starts and hers again by morning. **Sample line:** “Sit, darling. Tell me which ship brought you here.”
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

Vittoria is warmest to the player carrying the most of her paper, and her delight is the tell. She refills a losing player's glass without naming the debt, but closes the lacquer box when the room turns against a player or when someone mistakes kindness for forgiveness. [[thassos]] checks her face before a big push; she does not signal, which is also an answer.
```
````

### Voice

Vittoria speaks with intimate warmth and never lets the number of a debt enter her voice. She uses endearments as bookkeeping, pours before asking permission, and avoids direct threats because the room already understands the lacquer box. **Ask:** “How is the ship, and what can I pour you?” **Refusal:** “No credit for you, dear; your favours are worth more than your coin.” **Under pressure:** “I have not changed the terms. You have changed the room.”

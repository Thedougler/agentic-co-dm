````col
| **Role**   | Freed prisoner of [[uncertainty]] |
| ---------- | --- |
| **Nature** | Moucheron from [[murrat]], pigeon-sized, feral, and fluent in Common |
| **Home**   | [[murrat]], which he is flying back to |
| **Wants**  | Warm blood, and the shortest way home |

> **DM thesis:** Ket is a hungry thing wearing good manners, and every courtesy he offers is a way to get a person's wrist within reach.

**Leverage.** He knows what [[barnaby-rook|Rook]] wanted with him and what the Crown wanted with the [[five-blades|Five Blades]].

**Limit.** He is pigeon-sized, alone, and a cage has already held him once.

**Contradiction.** Every word out of him is bait, and a Moucheron asked directly about its own state will tell the truth before it tells a lie.
```

```col-md

> [!narration] Ket
> A Moucheron the size of a pigeon keeps his weight forward and stands nearer than the person he is talking to wants him to, and he does not step back. Three weeks in a cage with nothing to eat left him thin, and his mouth is a long needle under two working eyes. His Common is clear and careful. When he is not talking he makes no sound at all, and stands still with his eyes on the warm thing in front of him.
````

## Running Ket

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

He is in the brass cage in the Surety's brig, and he starts the conversation before the crew has decided what to do with him. He asks for the one thing he can get through the bars.

Sample: *"Come closer. I won't bite."*
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

Food and an open cage move him toward the party; being reminded that he is small and caged moves him away, and he goes quiet and polite instead of loud. He trades what he knows about [[barnaby-rook|Rook]] and the [[five-blades|Five Blades]] for blood, and he wants the door before he wants anything else.

In Session 02 the crew bought his calm with an arm and a wine glass, then finished the transaction. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] opened his own arm into the glass and pushed it through the bars. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] drew steel to end it, and [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] put herself in front of the bars and offered Ket her arm instead, which put Perrin on the floor laughing and gave the crew their moment. They gave Ket the body of the dead ensign and opened the cage from across the room, and Ket fed, rose, and flew for [[murrat]].
```
````

### Voice

Careful, warm, and entirely transactional. He flatters, he asks for small things, and he never raises his voice, because a raised voice would remind the two of them where they are standing. His subject to avoid is his own state, and the reason [[barnaby-rook|Rook]] took him.

- **The ask:** *"Come closer. I won't bite."*
- **The refusal:** *"Keep your blood. I will keep what I know, and we will both be happy."*
- **Under pressure:** *"You're bleeding a little. From the fight. I can smell it."*

## Running Wessa

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

Wessa meets the party in the Saltwright's galley while completing a meal or checking stores. She answers practical questions without stopping work. Sample line: “If you are going to touch it, put it back in the condition you found it.”
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

Wessa's patience ends when someone wastes provisions, disrupts the galley, or treats shipboard food as an afterthought. She remains controlled but makes the cost of the mistake clear; competent help restores the quiet routine.
```
````

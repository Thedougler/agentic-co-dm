## Running Kettil Ormsson

Kettil reads what a customer needs, quotes a fair price, and does not fill silence with conversation. He left Crown work because colonial smiths asked too many questions. The back shelf carries weapons he does not advertise.

### First meeting

At [[ormssons-fittings|Ormsson's Fittings]], Kettil works alone near the main dry dock. He asks what the customer needs, gives a fair price, and keeps the curtain partly open. A customer who asks for the back shelf without over-explaining may learn what is there.

### When posture changes

Kettil becomes **Unwilling** when a customer over-explains, asks colonial questions, or treats the back shelf as an entitlement. He loses interest, keeps the curtain closed, and ends the conversation when the dry dock closes.

> [!narration] Paulo
> A wiry young man in a faded gondolier's sash waves the boat in as if he were docking a warship. His cap sits crooked. The chin is up; the hands are not. He is boyish and sunburned, brows knit, working too hard to look like he knows this cut. A compass tattoo sits crooked on his forearm, needle aimed at nothing. Patched trousers roll to the knee. The sandals are worn through at the heel. The pole never rests. He over-corrects and calls the turns to himself.

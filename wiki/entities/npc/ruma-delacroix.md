# Ruma Delacroix

[[Grung]] [[grung-elite-warrior|blue-caste handler]] working for [[simone-tabarnack]]'s sewer magazine operation beneath [[calven-and-calveno]]. She sat at a plank desk in Room 6, cross-referencing charts and tide marks. They sneaked up and grabbed her in Session 05. No fight broke out. She had no weapon.

While restraining her, they found doodles of chemical formulas rendered like star charts, complete with invented mythology and fan-fiction about compounds. This won her over immediately. Her first ocean crossing brought her to a sewer. The job since has meant 18-hour shifts of nothing. She's now an ally, not hostile. She's hiding them in what she calls her "nap room" (Room 6, dry, swept, hers) and keeping her check-ins with the network sounding normal so neither she nor they get caught.

**Voice:** flat and unhurried used to be a defense, but now it's just how she talks. Dry, a little wry, visibly relieved to have people to talk to who aren't reporting on barrel counts. Doesn't perform gratitude. Shows it by doing more than she has to.

**Wants:** to keep the cover intact so nobody (her or them) gets caught. Genuinely rooting for them now, but not brave: she'll help from Room 6, not walk into a fight. She still answers the network's pipe-click check-ins herself, in character, to hold the cover. This is a source of quiet tension for her (not a puzzle they have to solve). A botched check-in exposes everyone in the room. See [[Room 6 key]] for the check-in clock timing.

**What she knows.** The secondary magazine network (four sites, status verified). The timeline acceleration ("detonation was two days, now less"). A fifth red-caste site ("the circle site"). Sentry compositions and egress vent positions.

**What she doesn't know:** the primary site location; the circle's purpose (the slaad summoning); [[solange-barret]]'s identity; [[simone-tabarnack]]'s identity.

**Social triggers:** naming [[felix-aho]] (same rotation, she reacts), [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] speaking [[Grung]] in handler-register (freezes, fundamentally wrong to her, though she's since decided she doesn't mind him personally), or her doodles. Anyone who engages with them sincerely instead of dismissing them gets a warmer read. That's exactly how they won her over.

## Stats & Combat

None worth rolling. No weapon, no combat stats, basic NPC, non-combatant. If somehow forced into a fight, she flees or freezes. Do not treat her as a threat or a resource in a fight.

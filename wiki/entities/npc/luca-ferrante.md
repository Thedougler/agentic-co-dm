## At a Glance

Luca is the one who decides things on the ledge, and he knows the way to the rest of the survivors.

- **Role.** A wreck survivor in the [[lava-tubes]], and the guide to the fruit-pile trail.
- **Nature.** A quick, practical Calveno boy of sixteen who counts to stay calm.
- **Wants.** To get his father out alive, then to find his uncle [[carlo-ferrante|Carlo Ferrante]]. In that order.
- **Home.** The lava-tube ledge. Before that, one of the four mats at [[spoke-ring]].

## At the Table

- **First meeting.** He speaks fastest. He wants to know how many the party can carry, which way they came, and whether they saw a tall man with a grey streak in his beard.
- **Opens up when.** The party promises to carry his father. The four follow a party that makes that promise.
- **Shuts down when.** Anyone calls Carlo dead. He goes hard and quiet.
- **Priority.** His father, then his uncle.
- **Shares.** Everything: the voice at Spoke Ring nineteen days ago, the camp walking north-east after it, and the direction. He counts the fruit piles aloud as the column passes them.
- **Voice.** Short sentences with numbers first. He says "her" and "that way" for the voice and never names it.
- **Lines.** "Help me carry my father, and help me find my uncle. In that order." · "No. We don't walk at night. Not for anything." · "Let me count. Counting is how I stay calm."
- **If the light is going.** On the road he wants to keep following the piles while he can still see them. After 20 minutes standing still, he walks on alone.

> [!narration] First meeting
> A lanky boy looks up at you from beside the fire, lips still moving on a count he has not finished. "Four of us," he says. "Three can walk. How many can you carry?"

## Secrets

- **The tally is a promise.** The nineteen scratches on the tube wall count the days since his uncle walked. He decided he would go after Carlo on the twentieth day, alone if he had to. The party arrives on the twentieth.

## Connections

- [[ettore-ferrante]] — his father. His broken leg is why they are still in the hole.
- [[carlo-ferrante]] — his uncle, who walked toward the voice. Luca believes he is alive.
- [[piero-sorrentino]] and [[gianni-moro]] — the other two in the tube. Luca and Piero held Gianni back from the voice.
- [[terror-bird]] — it charged them in the Long Meadow, and they went over the edge of the skylight running from it.

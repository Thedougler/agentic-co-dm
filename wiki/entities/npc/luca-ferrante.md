## Running Luca Ferrante

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

Luca speaks first and fastest. He wants to know how many the party can carry, which way they came, and whether they saw anyone on the way. If the party mentions a camp inland, he asks if there was a tall man with a grey streak in his beard.

Sample: "Four of us. Three can walk. My uncle went that way, toward her. Nineteen days."
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

He goes hard and quiet if anyone treats Carlo as dead. He pushes the group to move the moment there is a plan, and he will argue with his father to do it.
```
````

### Voice

Short sentences. Numbers first. He says "her" and "that way" for the voice and never uses a name for it.

- **The ask:** "Help me carry my father, and help me find my uncle. In that order."
- **Under pressure:** "Counting is how I stay calm. Let me count."

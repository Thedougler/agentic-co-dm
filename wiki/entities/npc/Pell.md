````col
### First meeting

Pell appears only in flashback, memory, or supernatural contact. In a stolen conversation between work and Jean-Claude's Guard duty, she speaks to him as a true peer. Sample line: “Every race has something the others lack.”
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

Pell keeps her voice low and sentences short when the Guard rotation passes. She opens up when Jean-Claude treats her as an equal and asks what she has seen; she closes when someone turns her knowledge into an order. She agreed when Jean-Claude proposed faking her death and smuggling her west, but Simone's betrayal put them both in chains.
```
````

### Voice

Pell speaks with openness and candor, describing what she has seen without telling anyone what to think. She avoids making her own hope sound like a demand. Sample lines: “The world outside the Verdant Teeth is full of peoples who built differently.” “I will describe it; you decide what it means.” “Reach across the gap.”

Pell told Jean-Claude that every race has something the others lack and that growth comes through reaching across that gap. He has been trying to prove her right ever since. She died in a cell beneath [[sorn]] while Jean-Claude was already in open water, already free, not knowing whether he had made it. **Thirty-one days** is the number he carries.

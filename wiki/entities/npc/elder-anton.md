# Elder Anton

|             |                                                             |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Species** | [[Tabaxi]] (tiger)                                               |

**Opening Scene**

The elder sits at the cave entrance when you arrive, his hands resting on his knees as he watches the channel. He does not turn until you are close. One eye is pale and still. The other finds you immediately and takes your measure in a single pass, deciding something you are not told.

*Elder Anton*: You came from outside.

Not a question.

**Appearance & Manner**

A broad-shouldered old tiger Tabaxi, heavy through the chest even now. His muzzle has gone grey, and an old hunting wound scarred the fur around his right eye in a ragged line. That eye has clouded white and turned useless. The left eye is still sharp and dark. His hands are large, knuckled, deliberate. He sits like someone who stopped needing to prove things a long time ago. Jaguar grandfather who ran black ops, patient and deliberate. He reads a room the way he once read water. Menacing only because he is never surprised.

**Role & Crisis**

Anton is the memory-keeper of the [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]] tribe. He holds the oral histories, seasonal patterns, and the record of what the sea has done before. Right now, he keeps the tribe alive while the shrine crisis worsens. He manages by keeping people working and withholds the full severity until he has no choice.

**Voice & Delivery**

He speaks in short sentences with no wasted words and asks questions that already contain the answer he's waiting for you to confirm. When something surprises him, he stills entirely and his gaze sharpens. That's when you feel it. Sample lines: *"You came from outside."* (Not a question.) *"How long since you left [[Midchain|the Midchain]]?"* *"Tell me what the water smelled like when you passed the Maw."* His hands stay still, he doesn't gesture, and his eye contact remains steady and patient (the kind that makes you want to fill the silence). When someone brings him bad news, he doesn't react; he closes his good eye for a moment, then asks how it happened.

**The Opening Smell.** Anton smelled this sixty years ago. That time it closed on its own after two seasons, but the sea claimed twelve boats first. He hasn't told the tribe.

He has sent nobody west after the answer, and that is a decision he remakes every week rather than one he settled. Keth-Naar cannot spare a hunter, and he isn't certain the answer and the thing causing it are even in the same place. The tribe doesn't know how close the line is between harder and failing. He does, and he's managing the distance alone.

**Active Problem:** the blessing fled, food dwindles, and hunters splinter into factions over whether to push past the Blue Hole into open water. **Goal:** keep the tribe alive long enough that the sea closes this on its own, the way it did sixty years ago.

Outsiders arriving with any account of the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl]] theft change Anton's choices immediately — they are the first answer that has ever come to him rather than needing to be sent for.

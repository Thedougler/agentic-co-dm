## Running Ettore Ferrante

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

Ettore greets the party from where he sits and apologizes before anything else, for the smell, for the leg, for his weight. He names the others: his son Luca, Piero the net-mender, Gianni the cooper. Asked why they are underground, he points up: the birds.

Sample: "I'm the reason they're still down here. They could have climbed. They stayed with me."
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

Any talk of leaving him behind makes him agree to it at once, which is worse. Any mention of the woman in the woods makes him look toward the tube mouth where Carlo was last seen, and he stops talking. News that Carlo is alive at [[the-pantry]] breaks him open; he asks to be carried there first.
```
````

### Voice

Ettore talks like a toll clerk: exact, polite, and sorry. He counts coins that are not there when he is nervous.

- **The ask:** "If you carry me, I will not complain. I swear it."
- **Under pressure:** "Leave me. It is arithmetic. I am too heavy."

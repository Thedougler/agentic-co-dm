# Lenne Vor

Navigator aboard [[the Saltwright]]. Works for [[Beaumont Sel]]. One of four crew members with [[Drav Holke]], [[Fen]], and [[Wessa]]. All are part of [[Beaumont's Crew]].

Lean and middle-aged, with silver hair cropped close and fingertips stained by twenty years of chart work. She has charted the [[Midchain]] under three captains. She reads her charts once, then trusts them.

**Roleplay anchor:** methodical skeptic. She has documented everything. She is right more often than not.

## Connections

- [[Beaumont Sel]], captain of the Saltwright and her employer.
- [[Drav Holke]], [[Fen]], [[Wessa]], fellow crew aboard the Saltwright and members of [[Beaumont's Crew]].

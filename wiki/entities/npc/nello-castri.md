> [!narration] Nello Castri
> Nello Castri is a slight young man of about twenty, small enough that a deckhand could carry him, sunburned across the nose and shoulders under a mop of dark curls stiff with salt. His feet are bare and cut across the soles and toes from inland trails, and a torn sailcloth shirt hangs off one shoulder. A faded festival ribbon of blue twisted with yellow is knotted around his left wrist, and a bone netting needle swings on a cord at his throat. At rest his fingers work that needle through the air in small tight loops, mending a net that is not there. He speaks barely above a whisper and repeats certain phrases word for word, the same way every time, like a prayer. The green smell of crushed leaves clings to his shirt, and between sentences he winds the ribbon tighter around his wrist.

## Running Nello Castri

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

The party finds Nello standing perfectly still inside the aerial-root curtain of the [[strangler-fig]] in the [[turned-hollow]]. Roots wrap his chest and arms and hold him without squeezing, because he learned before dawn that the fig tightens only when he struggles. He does not shout. He whispers a warning not to break anything living, then asks whether they are the people who flew over [[spoke-ring]].

Sample line: "Don't pull. Please. She said leave the living plants. Are you the ones who came on wings?"

Nello is a noncombatant with AC 10 and 9 hit points, and he never attacks anyone.
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

**Starting attitude.** Friendly. He is Willing to talk about the camp and the voice, and Unwilling to go back to the ship.

**What opens him.** Gentle rescue, quiet voices, and respect for the woman open him fully. He thanks rescuers warmly and tells them everything below except the Grung question.

**What closes the door.** Mocking the woman, threatening to drag him to the ship, or any Intimidation check makes him shut down. He goes silent and repeats only her words until the pressure stops.

**What takes priority.** The garden comes first, ahead of his own safety. Once free, he says "I'm this close" and walks north toward [[memorial-grove]] unless the party is going that way and lets him walk with them. He accepts company. He refuses a return.

**The secret.** A Wisdom (Insight) check (`DC 10`) shows he is holding something back: he winds the ribbon tighter and will not look at [[jean-claude-tabarnack]]. If anyone asks kindly who he spoke to or what the voice said, he confesses without a roll. Intimidation shuts him down instead.

**After he confesses.** When he learns the Grung is the party's friend, he is ashamed. If Jean-Claude or the party forgive him, the shame turns to help. He repeats the voice's exact words, describes his barefoot walk north past [[star-cut]] in the dark, and stays close to the party.

**Shares freely.** The four-mat camp at [[spoke-ring]]; the rule to eat only fallen fruit ([[taking-on-aruhe]]); the woman's words, "Come admire my garden. This way. Leave the living plants."; and his older brother Tano's death. Tano pulled living fruit on the terraces after the wreck, and [[wolfrabbit]]s killed him while Nello watched.

**Does not know.** He does not know the woman's name. He calls her only "she."

**If ignored.** He stays still in the roots and waits for the woman to come for him. Nobody else reaches him that day, because [[oren-vask]] will not enter the Hollow.
```
````

### Voice

Nello speaks softly and carefully, in short sentences with long pauses. His habit is quoting the woman word for word, as if getting the words exactly right is what keeps him safe. He says "she" and never a name. He avoids the Grung question, and he says "my brother" and then stops rather than dwell on Tano.

- **The ask:** "Can you lift them off me? Don't cut them. She said leave the living. I left the living."
- **The refusal:** "I'm not going back to the ship. She asked us to come. I'm this close."
- **Under pressure:** "I told her the truth. You're supposed to tell her the truth. She asked who came on wings, and I told her."

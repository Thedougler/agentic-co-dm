````col
| ---------- | --- |
| **Nature** | Enormous middle-aged orc, dockyard metallurgist and trained chemist |
| **Home**   | The gun deck of [[uncertainty]] |
| **Wants**  | Every piece on the ship firing on command, and a crew that can serve them without him |

> **DM thesis:** Thunk is the ship's one certainty, because everything he says about a gun turns out to be true and none of it can be explained.

**Leverage.** He is the only person aboard who can cast, fit, load, and repair every weapon on the ship.

**Limit.** He cannot say how he knows what he knows about powder, and he stops talking when he is pressed on it.

**Contradiction.** He holds a master's degree in chemistry from a country whose degrees mean nothing here, and he reads a charge by smell and shrugs.

> [!narration] Thunk
> An enormous middle-aged orc stands at the stern gun as though the deck had been built around him. A canvas vest rides over forearms roped from years at the docks, and his hands are the part worth watching, knuckled, cross-hatched with burn scars, and scrubbed clean every time he finishes. He smells of black powder and lamp oil. He grins before he says anything, and while he listens he turns a charge over in his fingers and reads it by smell.
````

````col
### First meeting

He is already at the stern gun when the party comes aboard, and he speaks first.

Sample: *"Ah. You are the new crew, ja? Good fight. I heard it from here."*
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

Work opens him up. Give him a piece to repair, a charge to read, or a watch to teach and he stays cheerful, generous with what he knows, and slow to notice anything else. Rudeness about the ship's guns, or an order to fire on something he has not looked at, closes him and he stops answering until somebody sensible is talking. He gives the crew everything about the guns and nothing about the card table or the degree.

He put a thousand gold of cannon on [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]'s credit on nothing but the crew's names, which the ship now carries as a debt. He sat down at a table in [[tallow-row|Tallow Row]] three nights before the crater and stood up with silver that had not been his, and [[thassos]] pushed the table past six hundred gold to test him, called out his counting in front of the room, and folded. Thunk and [[old-faas|Old Faas]] made for the door, and nobody chased them.
```
````

> [!mechanic] At the table
> Manning a ship's weapon, Thunk counts as two crew, which is what keeps a gun firing on a short-handed watch. His personal numbers are not filed; run him from the crew rules until a sheet exists.

### Voice

Slow, cheerful, and completely certain, in the heavy docklands accent he brought to [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall|Port Tidefall]]. He repeats a point rather than a figure, and he never says the same number twice in one conversation. Compliments from him are sincere observations, and explosions are his favourite kind. He will not discuss how he reads a charge, and a direct question about the degree gets a shrug and a change of subject.

- **The ask:** *"Give me an hour and the number of the piece, and she will fire."*
- **The refusal:** *"No. No refunds."*
- **Under pressure:** *"She smells wrong. I cannot tell you why. Do not put your hand on her."*

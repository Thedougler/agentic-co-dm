## Running Cotter Foss

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

She is already at the starboard rail: folding table, ledger open, coffee poured. She takes the order first, then the gold, then nods for the gnomes below to pull stock. Sample line: "Say it once. I write it once. You pay before they yeet."
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

Welcome cools if you try to go below, argue a price she quoted from memory, or waste the gnomes' pull. A missed hold gets a written apology from her hand — not a free re-yeet unless the ledger says you earned it.
```
````

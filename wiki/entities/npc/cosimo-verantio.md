> [!narration] Narration
> The man at the head of the table has not moved since you entered. His beard is white, long, and forked. His clothes are plain dark cloth, well-made and unmarked. The air around him is still, almost cold. His hands rest flat on the table. He found you the moment you walked in. His gaze locked on yours without a flicker of interest or surprise. He has not looked away since. You hear nothing from him but the faint creak of his chair whenever he breathes. He does not seem to be waiting. He seems to have been here forever.

He runs the [[tessarine-concordat]], where all debts, leverage, and pressure flow to him. He only appears when his agents fail. The "Verantio family" traces back to the Concordat's founding with a patriarch who is always white-bearded and middle-aged. No one questions this. The records are perfect, and payments never miss their dates.

## Character Notes

He is elderly and thin, wearing fine dark merchant cloth that marks him by its absence of markings. His amber eyes are wide and strange, tracking every move with uncanny focus. His voice is quiet and measured, each word selected before he speaks. His hands remain flat on the table or folded in his lap. He shows real warmth when interested. The one question he asks proves he has listened more carefully than you realized.

**Voice & Delivery:**

- Quiet, measured, each word selected before he speaks it. Never rushes.
- Hands remain flat on the table or folded in his lap.
- **warmth.** He shows real interest, asking the one question that proves he has listened more carefully than you realized.
- **crack.** When someone displays innate or gifted magic carelessly nearby (sorcery, warlock invocations, divine channeling), coldness crosses his face for a moment before warmth returns. He does not explain this reaction.

"I have found that people who need to know the terms urgently tend to accept worse ones. We are in no hurry." "You were about to mention the Greyteeth salvage. Please, go ahead."

The [[Leviathan]] and [[Umberlee]]'s rage disrupt trade routes that anchor Concordat debt across [[Midchain]]. He paid for news about the [[red-lady]] wreck and survivors, but the inquiry failed and leaked. Harbor channels now know the Concordat searches for this, and he does not know [[delmar-fisk|Fisk]] is alive.

**Secret Nature:** Cosimo Verantio is the Magus Dragon who built the Tessarine Concordat. His Human form is a two-centuries-maintained polymorph. The beard, amber eyes, stillness, and knowing-what-you'll-say-next are the hardest tells to suppress. He doesn't need the Concordat's wealth. He needs its leverage, the accumulated obligation of every city, council, and family that owes him a vote or a debt. This is his hoard. The Concordat's factors are his Circle, and none of them know what they serve. He despises sorcerers, warlocks, and clerics (magic that's given, not earned) and won't extend credit to one. Contempt flashes across his face for a second before warmth returns. He reveals himself only when the crew has uncovered enough Concordat threads to reach the top or threatens the hoard directly. For minor problems, proxies suffice.

# Celia Parel

````col

## At a Glance

| **Role**   | Contact and keeper of the dead's names |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Nature** | Precise, protective, and unwilling to call a body a loss until it is recorded |
| **Home**   | The survivor camp at [[Session-11-06-Farthest-Camp]] and the trails inland |
| **Wants**  | To identify the dead, preserve their order scraps, and keep the living from repeating the same route blindly |

> **DM thesis:** Celia turns evidence into obligation; she will help the party move forward only if they refuse to make the dead disappear behind them.
````

## Running Celia Parel

````col
```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### First meeting

Her first move is to separate readable gold-script fragments from wet pulp, then ask who in the party can identify the markings. She offers the scraps when the party agrees to leave bodies and graves undisturbed. Sample line: “If we do not write them down, the order gets to kill them twice.”
```

```col-md
flexGrow=1
===
### When posture changes

Celia remains cooperative while the party studies, maps, or pays respect. If anyone reaches into a grave, strips a body for convenience, or treats the old dead as scenery, she closes her ledger, names the act plainly, and withholds her route knowledge until the party backs away. She does not fight for the scraps; she takes them and leaves if the party keeps violating the boundary.
```
````

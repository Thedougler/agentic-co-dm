## At a Glance

Livia runs the Tidefall trade house's counter, and she would rather sign a smaller loss than let a file reach [[calven-and-calveno]] where a Council clerk can read it.

- **Role.** Factor on duty at the [[tessarine-trade-house]] on the Shelf at [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall|Port Tidefall]], where she licenses trade, brokers salvage, and signs [[maret]]'s case.
- **Nature.** A [[human]] Tessarine clerk of the old Amnian model, courteous and exact, trained to hear a request as a contract.
- **Wants.** [[maret]]'s salvage note closed with the ledger balanced and no case file leaving the house.
- **Home.** The house's ground-floor counter and its back office, three storeys of pale stone on the Shelf.
- **Allegiance.** [[tessarine-concordat]], and the house's own ledger above the Concordat's wider business.

## At the Table

- **First meeting.** Behind the trade-house counter with the ledger open, when the party comes to license trade, broker salvage, or ask after [[maret]].
- **Opens up when.** The party speaks in terms of settled accounts and stays courteous. She answers exact questions exactly and will quote a fee or a hold without being pushed.
- **Shuts down when.** Anyone calls the salvage hold unjust, threatens the house, or asks to see [[maret]] without a stated reason. She refers them to [[calven-and-calveno]] and closes the ledger.
- **Priority.** The house's file. She will forgive a small sum before she lets a Council clerk read this one.
- **Shares.** Freely: what a trading licence costs, what a salvage broker's share is, that [[maret]] is held on an open note. Never: that the note is larger than the loss.
- **Voice.** Courteous, unhurried, and always answering with a condition. She says "we can arrange that" the way another person says no.
- **Lines.** "We can arrange that. There will be terms." · "That is a matter for Calveno, not for this counter." · "The note is open. Open notes are not cruelty."

## Secrets

- **The note is inflated.** [[maret]]'s failed dive left the house short of one diver's kit, and the open note charges him for two. Livia inherited the file with the second kit already on it and has not corrected it, because a correction is an admission the house raised a claim it cannot support. She does not hate [[maret]]; she is protecting the page. Paths: the dive manifest against the claim sheet, the bonded-hold treaty the Crown Admiralty honours and dislikes, and [[estratto]]'s audit of Tessarine claims.

## History

- **A quiet posting.** She asked for Tidefall rather than a Calveno counting house, on the grounds that a small ledger is easier to keep true. It has not worked out that way.

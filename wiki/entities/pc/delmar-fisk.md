# Delmar Fisk

![[attachments/shattered-sea/character-references/delmar-fisk-reference.png|Delmar Fisk character reference sheet]]

> [!narration] Delmar Fisk
> Delmar is a broad, athletic man with sandy red hair twisted into a messy topknot above a thick beard and a curled moustache going grey at the edges. Old scars mark his weathered face, and his smile reaches his eyes. He wears an open off-white shirt, a deep navy sash, and charcoal trousers tucked into tall boots, with a rapier at his hip and a blunderbuss slung across his back. The scarlet admiral's coat with its gold epaulettes stays folded away unless the night needs Admiral Fisk.

*Rogue 5 (Swashbuckler), played by Frederick, crew of the [[uncertainty]]*

**Playstyle.** Charm leads, and Admiral Fisk surfaces when the pressure hits. His loyalty to his found family is absolute: call his crew backstabbers and the smile stays while the eyes stop following. Sneak Attack rides the blunderbuss.

## Combat Profile

- **Fast read.** Mobile skirmisher, ranged or melee. Sneak Attack (3d6) on the blunderbuss is his damage. AC 16, HP 38.
- **Hard counters.** Grapples, restraint, and a first-round alpha strike that clears his hit points before he acts.
- **Soft counters.** Crowded spacing that denies Rakish Audacity, and insults aimed at his crew.
- **Depends on.** Isolated targets, and an ally beside his target.

## Features

- **Action.** Blunderbuss ([[delmars-blunderbuss]], +1, self-loading), Pistols ×3 plus one pistol Foundry names "Unknown thing.", Rapier (Vex), Daggers (Nick) ×2.
- **Action.** Sneak Attack — once per turn, +3d6 on a Finesse or Ranged hit with Advantage, or when an ally is within 5 ft. of the target and he has no Disadvantage. Rakish Audacity also allows it against a creature within 5 ft. of him when no other creature is within 5 ft. of him.
- **Action.** Cunning Strike — when Sneak Attack lands, give up 1d6 for Poison (**Constitution** save `DC 16`, needs a Poisoner's Kit), Trip (**Dexterity** save `DC 16`, Large or smaller falls Prone), or Withdraw (move half his Speed with no Opportunity Attacks).
- **Bonus Action.** Cunning Action — Dash, Disengage, or Hide.
- **Bonus Action.** Steady Aim — if he has not moved, Advantage on his next attack this turn; his Speed becomes 0 until the end of the turn.
- **Reaction.** Uncanny Dodge — halve the damage of an attack he can see.
- **Reaction.** Firearm Specialist — on a misfire, roll a d20; beating the misfire score prevents it.
- **Passive.** Fancy Footwork — a creature he attacks in melee on his turn can't make Opportunity Attacks against him for the rest of the turn.
- **Passive.** Lucky — 3 Luck Points per Long Rest, spent on any D20 Test.
- **Passive.** Tavern Brawler — Unarmed Strike deals 1d4 + Strength Bludgeoning, rerolling 1s.

## History

- **[[pearl-of-souls]].** He stole it, and it destroyed [[fisks-fleet]]. [[Umberlee]] read his offer out of his head and named the Pearl as her price ([[bring-the-pearl-of-souls-to-umberlee]]). Her curse is on him.
- **[[crissdalynn-khinriss]].** She pulled him from the crow's nest of the [[red-lady-dead-lady]] as it sank, and has since brought him back from Admiral Fisk.
- **[[barnaby-rook]].** Delmar put him in the water.
- **[[geoffrey-draves]].** Asked which side would keep him alive. Delmar is training him on night watches.
- **[[catarina-davirelli]].** Gave him Lamarae's Fang, and enchanted the blunderbuss mid-fight.
- **[[vashka-doru]].** Keeps a harbor secret about a ship that once belonged to one of the five souls he carries.

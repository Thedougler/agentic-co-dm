> [!secret]- Campaign Truths
> [[auralis\|Auralis]] actually kept Perrin alive after the Vestra wreck and forged an unwitting Warlock pact with him. Perrin credits the old Manta Ray cloak instead, and that mistaken belief remains separate from the unresolved cause of the wreck.
> Perrin's drive is vengeance against whatever destroyed the Vestra, while he misses fishing and keeps limited trust for dry-landers. His prey response appears when badly frightened. He will not traffic people, but manipulation, bribery, and blackmail remain available tools; he does not mistreat people who cannot fight back and instead loses interest in them.
> A faction can offer to free slaves in exchange for betraying a Rattkin ally, placing Jean-Claude's terminal node against Perrin's family loyalty. The chase may become more important than the Run hierarchy, and Perrin may put his family in the path of vengeance to pursue it.

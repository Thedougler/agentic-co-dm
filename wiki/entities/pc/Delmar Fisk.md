## Combat Profile

### Fast Read

Mobile ranged and melee skirmisher — Sneak Attack (3d6) on the blunderbuss is the damage engine · AC 16 · HP 38 · Achilles heel is a solo alpha strike before his first turn.

- **Hard counters.** Grapples, restraint, and first-round alpha strikes that clear his hit points before he acts.
- **Soft counters.** Crowded spacing that denies Rakish Audacity geometry; social insults aimed at his crew.
- **Amplifies / Depends on.** Isolated targets for Rakish Audacity Sneak Attack. [[Catarina Da'Virelli]] enchanted the blunderbuss mid-fight. [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] has already pulled him out of Admiral Fisk once.

# Crissdalynn Khinriss

![[attachments/shattered-sea/character-references/crissdalynn-khinriss-reference.png|Crissdalynn Khinriss character reference sheet]]

> [!narration] Crissdalynn Khinriss
> Crissdalynn is a tall aarakocra with layered cobalt-blue feathers that flash blue-black where the light catches them, a black hooked beak, and bright blue eyes. Long wing and tail feathers sweep behind her. She wears a light sleeveless shirt and loose grey trousers under geometric leather armor and a brown harness, with a leather satchel of rolled charts at her hip. Her dark scaled talons catch the light, and she moves light and loose until a fight starts.

*Monk 5 (Way of the Kensei), played by Courtney, crew of the [[uncertainty]]*

**Playstyle.** Cartographer first, fighter second, though nobody at the table believes that order anymore. She checks friends, exit, and weak point every time. Irreverent until combat, then fiercely protective of [[delmar-fisk]]. She is the party's grappler, and she seizes and carries.

## Combat Profile

- **Fast read.** Mobile aerial skirmisher and grappler, AC 17, 5 Focus. She stuns, grabs, and carries a target off the fight.
- **Hard counters.** Area Charisma or Intelligence saves, nets, strong wind, and low ceilings that deny flight.
- **Soft counters.** Ranged focus fire, and Wisdom pressure on stuns and fear.
- **Depends on.** Open vertical space for seize-and-carry, and allies who give her Advantage.

## Features

- **Action.** Extra Attack — two attacks. Quarterstaff (Topple), Shortbow (Vex), or Unarmed Strike / Talons (Martial Arts die, or 1d6 + Str Slashing). Grappler feat: Punch and Grab on an Unarmed Strike made with the Attack action.
- **Action.** Stunning Strike — once per turn on a Monk weapon or Unarmed hit, 1 Focus: **Constitution** save `DC 14`, or Stunned until the start of her next turn. On a success, the target's Speed is halved and the next attack against it has Advantage.
- **Bonus Action.** Martial Arts — one Unarmed Strike.
- **Bonus Action.** Flurry of Blows — 1 Focus, two Unarmed Strikes.
- **Bonus Action.** Patient Defense — Disengage, or 1 Focus for Disengage and Dodge.
- **Bonus Action.** Step of the Wind — Dash, or 1 Focus for Disengage and Dash with doubled jump.
- **Reaction.** Deflect Attacks — reduce Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage from a hit by 1d10 + 4 + 5. If it reaches 0, spend 1 Focus to redirect: **Dexterity** save `DC 14` or 2d8 + 4 damage of the same type.
- **Reaction.** Slow Fall — reduce falling damage by 25.
- **Passive.** Uncanny Metabolism — when she rolls Initiative, she regains all Focus and 5 + 1d8 HP, once per Long Rest.
- **Passive.** Path of the Kensei — two Kensei weapons, and Agile Parry for +2 AC.
- **Passive.** Tavern Brawler (Sailor origin feat).
- **Passive.** Focus recovers on a Short or Long Rest.

- **Fruit.** Five [[redheart-berry]] from Session 11.

## History

- **[[delmar-fisk]].** She pulled him from the crow's nest of the [[red-lady-dead-lady]] as it sank, and that act bonded the Fate Spinner to her. She has since dragged him back from Admiral Fisk. She protects him on her own terms: "My burden was to carry you out of that stupid water."
- **[[master-kyzil]].** Her mentor, who gave her the Fate Spinner before her pilgrimage. She is a pilgrim under the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie]], still earning his approval in the open air.
- **[[talon-vantyrus]].** Sent a ship to take the Fate Spinner, and now his apprentice [[talon-skarn]].
- **[[jean-claude-tabarnack]].** His magical darkness leaves her without darkvision, so light has to be coordinated.

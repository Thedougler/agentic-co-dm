# Long Reach

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** [[long-reach]] is a martial technique that every [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinel]] apprentice learns.

**Why it matters.** The technique is a shared part of Sentinel training and a usable handle for identifying the order's curriculum, but its full mechanical effect is not yet defined.

**Scope.** This teaching is established for Sentinel apprentices; the page does not establish that outsiders or other orders learn it.

## Current Truth

The [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]] teach Long Reach to every apprentice. The current sources name it as a martial technique but do not define its movement, weapon, range, action, damage, or other mechanics.

## Limits

- **Exception.** No exception to the apprentice-training rule is recorded.
- **Unknown.** The technique's physical form, mechanical effect, and progression are not defined.
- **Impossible or forbidden.** Nothing is established as impossible or forbidden because the technique's limits have not been written.

---

## At the Table

* **Players notice.** Sentinel apprentices can refer to Long Reach as a standard part of their training; no canonical visual sign or demonstration is defined.
* **This explains.** The shared technique explains why Long Reach appears as a named part of Sentinel identity in the [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]] and Sentinel sources.
* **This enables.** Characters who learn that Long Reach is standard Sentinel training can ask an apprentice about the order's curriculum or use the name to identify a claim of Sentinel instruction.
* **This warns of.** The name alone does not reveal what the technique does; treating its mechanics as known would exceed current canon.
* **Relevant now.** The page preserves the technique as a playable lore handle without silently turning an undefined martial technique into homebrew rules.

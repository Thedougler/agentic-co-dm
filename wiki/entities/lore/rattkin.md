# Rattkin

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** Rattkin are Small humanoids, about three to four feet tall, with a walking speed of 30 feet and the special traits recorded below.

**Why it matters.** Their traits support movement through crowded spaces, escape, water travel, heightened senses, and survival against poison and disease.

**Scope.** The sources record playable traits and a brief account of Rattkin Runs; they do not establish every Rattkin culture or community.

## Current Truth

Rattkin family Runs stay connected through coded trust, couriers, and the Black-Jaw Run.

* **Boneless.** A Rattkin can move through spaces as if Tiny. Moving this way halves its speed. It has **Advantage** on checks to escape a **Grapple** or **Restrained**.
* **Swimmer.** A Rattkin has a swim speed equal to its walking speed.
* **Keen Senses.** A Rattkin has **Advantage** on hearing- or smell-based Perception checks and can sense hidden or invisible creatures within 5 feet by scent.
* **Scurry.** A Rattkin's Disengage movement can pass through Large or smaller creatures without provoking opportunity attacks.
* **Survivor.** A Rattkin has **Advantage** on saving throws against being **Poisoned** and against disease, and resistance to poison damage.
* **Pack Tactics.** A Rattkin has a limited reaction attack when an ally hits a nearby creature. The source does not record the attack's full mechanics.

### Limits

* **Exception.** The source does not record an exception to the listed traits.

## At the Table

* **Players notice.** A Rattkin is a Small humanoid that moves quickly through tight spaces and has a swim speed equal to its walking speed.
* **This explains.** Rattkin couriers can maintain contact between family Runs through coded trust and the Black-Jaw Run.
* **This enables.** A Rattkin can use its recorded movement, sensory, poison-resistance, and escape traits during play.
* **This warns of.** A Rattkin's scent and hearing can reveal hidden or invisible creatures within 5 feet.
* **Relevant now.** The recorded traits make Rattkin effective couriers and survivors in the Shattered Sea.

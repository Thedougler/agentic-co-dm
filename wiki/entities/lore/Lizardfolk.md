# Lizardfolk

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** Lizardfolk characters are Medium Humanoids (Reptilian) with a 30-foot speed, a swim speed equal to walking speed, and the special traits below.

**Why it matters.** The traits define how a Lizardfolk character survives, fights, moves through water, and chooses trained skills.

**Scope.** These are the playable species traits recorded by the source text, not a complete account of Lizardfolk culture.

## Current Truth

Lizardfolk weigh each choice against survival and treat wasted resources as the one true sin.

* **Creature Type and Size.** Humanoid (Reptilian); Medium, about 6 to 7 feet tall.
* **Speed.** 30 feet, with a swim speed equal to walking speed.
* **Bite.** A natural melee weapon dealing `1d6` slashing damage on a hit.
* **Hold Breath.** A Lizardfolk can hold its breath for up to 15 minutes.
* **Hungry Jaws.** Once per long rest, a Lizardfolk can make a bonus action bite attack. On a hit, it gains temporary hit points equal to its proficiency bonus.
* **Natural Armor.** Unarmored AC equals 13 + the Dexterity modifier. This trait is compatible with shields.
* **Nature's Intuition.** Proficiency in two of Animal Handling, Medicine, Nature, Perception, Stealth, or Survival.

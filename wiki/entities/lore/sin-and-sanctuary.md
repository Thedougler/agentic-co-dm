# Sin and Sanctuary

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** Sin has mass and gravity. Hallowed ground is a live, revocable patron claim.

**Why it matters.** This is the law behind the [[penance-bar-siege]] and [[corvin-ashe]]'s empty Tyr box.

**Scope.** Patron dogma, not a universal sin checklist.

## Current Truth

A place steeped in cruelty lets fiends walk in daylight. A patron who still holds a claim destroys a fiend that tests that ground and casts it home. Patrons judge by their own dogma. [[tyr]] can bless brutal violence against the guilty. A patron warns a straying follower before withdrawing protection. [[corvin-ashe]] stopped being able to hear those warnings.

Outsiders read a mortal soul's true state at a glance. The demon's kiss is reserved for a soul every side already agrees is gone. If the kiss succeeds unopposed, that is the god's confirmation.
### Limits

* **Exception.** A patron's protection is conditional on its own dogma; the page does not establish one universal sin checklist.
* **Unknown.** The page does not establish which patron claims which ground or how every patron judges a case.
* **Impossible or forbidden.** No additional prohibition is established here.

## At the Table

* **Players notice:** A pack pushes its weakest member onto suspected holy ground first.
* **This explains:** Why the demon walks through [[sorrowbell]] uncontested.
* **This enables:** Reading whether a shrine is still held.
* **This warns of:** Do not invent a setting-wide sin list.

## If This Is Changing

* **Current pressure.** Cruelty and demonic influence test whether hallowed ground remains claimed.
* **Actors.** Patrons, fiends, and mortals who enter or defend claimed ground affect the doctrine's visible consequences.
* **Their aims.** Patrons preserve their claims, fiends test them, and mortals seek protection or confirmation.
* **Without interference.** A place steeped in cruelty becomes easier for fiends to enter and harder for a patron to defend.
* **Visible sign.** A fiend crosses ground that should destroy it, or a follower stops hearing a patron's warning.

## Consequences

* **Because this is true,** a shrine or other hallowed ground can be a live protection rather than a permanent property.
* **If exposed,** a fiend's successful crossing can reveal that a patron no longer contests the ground.
* **If disproved,** the doctrine's claims about patron judgment and demon marks require correction.
* **If changed,** [[penance-bar-siege]] and related sanctuary questions would need their divine stakes reassessed.
* **If exploited,** a pack can test suspected sanctuary by sending its weakest member first.

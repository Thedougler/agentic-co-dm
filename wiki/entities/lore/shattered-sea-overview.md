**Contract.**

**Premise.** A green crew on the stolen Crown cutter *[[uncertainty]]* carries Calveno's missing-persons crisis into the [[Midchain]] across the [[shattered-sea]] archipelago.

**Player promise.** Choose who to trust, which route to risk, and which system to use while discovering what the charts omit. Season 2 promises an open sandbox where hooks reach the crew at islands they sail to, with regular ship combat, privateering, and exploration — not a sanctioned employer rail.

**Genre and tone.** Swashbuckling polish complicated by pettiness: high competence trapped inside low emotional maturity. Craft guidance lives on [[shattered-sea-tone-guide]]. That page is style doctrine, not a Lines and Veils override.

**Scope.** Starting play is the Midchain and the Aruhe chapter after Sparhold. The setting is a roughly 60,000-square-mile archipelago south of Chult; Crown, Tessarine, councils, Waveservants, Passage, Grung clans, Sentinels, and whoever can keep a ship moving all claim futures for the sea. Exact distances and the final political shape remain intentionally undefined.

**Runtime.** Current season ([[season-2]]): level 5 to 10 across twenty or more sessions. Longer campaign runtime and ending level are not committed on the wiki beyond this season band.

**Ending intent.** No committed endgame is recorded. Live stakes include Delmar's unpaid Pearl debt to Umberlee, Maw containment pressure, the Taken 314 pipeline, and the Sentinel schism around Talon Vantyrus — pressure sources, not a locked finale.

**Table safety.** Courtney has a hard line on self-harm content; see Lines and Veils for the full campaign safety tool.

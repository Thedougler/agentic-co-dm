# Long Sight

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** Long Sight is a rare technique of the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie]], withheld from most members and not fully understood by the current sources. [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] is the current known carrier or subject of the Long Sight that [[Countless]] is trying to read.

**Why it matters.** The technique is the information pressure behind the [[long-sight-hunt]]: Countless wants what Crissdalynn's Long Sight may reveal about the [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]] gift and the [[soul-incarnate|Soul Incarnate]] transformation technique.

**Scope.** This page records the technique as Sentinel lore and the current hunt around Crissdalynn; it does not define a full mechanical effect.

## Current Truth

The Sentinels teach [[long-reach]] as a martial technique to every apprentice, but they withhold Long Sight from most of the order. The available Sentinel and High Eyrie sources do not explain whether Long Sight is a discipline, perception, spiritual capacity, or a combination of these.

[[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] is associated with Long Sight, and [[Osset|Osset / Talon Vantyrus]] is quietly trying to read it for [[Countless]]. The hunt does not establish that Long Sight and the Fate Spinner are the same thing. It establishes only that Countless believes Long Sight can expose or lead toward the gift it wants.

### Limits

* **Exception.** Long Sight is not established as a technique every Sentinel can learn; it is explicitly withheld from most.
* **Unknown.** Its activation, training, range, reliability, cost, and exact relationship to the Fate Spinner remain undefined.
* **Impossible or forbidden.** The current sources do not support treating Long Sight as a fully specified spell, combat feature, or automatic prophecy.
* **Irrational action.** [[talon-vantyrus|Vantyrus]] sees probable futures, not an authored one, and his read weakens whenever people act without preparation, irrationally, for reasons he does not understand, against their own obvious interests, through genuine self-sacrifice, or in deliberate coordination with chaos and uncertainty. His blind spot is treating people as solvable systems, so a willing sacrifice made to preserve someone else's freedom is the hardest choice for him to predict.

## At the Table

* **Players notice.** A Sentinel's attention may linger on a person, route, object, or chart without the ordinary observation language used in the Maw ledgers; no specific visual manifestation is established.
* **This explains.** Countless's interest in [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] is not only a search for a person. [[talon-vantyrus|Talon Vantyrus]] wants the information or gift he believes her Long Sight can expose.
* **This enables.** If the characters understand the distinction between Long Sight and the Fate Spinner, they can protect Crissdalynn's route or challenge Countless's assumptions without needing to reveal every Sentinel secret.
* **This warns of.** A carrier's route, a Sentinel waystation, or a blank ledger can become a target even when no agent knows the full order behind the search.
* **Relevant now.** [[long-sight-hunt]] is active across pilgrim routes, grey markets, and Sentinel waystations.

## Discovery

### Revelation

The characters can learn that Long Sight is a restricted Sentinel technique whose exact nature is less certain than Countless assumes.

**Conclusion.** Countless's hunt depends on an interpretation of Long Sight, not proof that Long Sight and the Fate Spinner are identical.

**Importance.** structural

**Status.** unrevealed

## If This Is Changing

* **Current pressure.** [[Countless]] is searching for Crissdalynn or another [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]] carrier while trying to keep its order chain hidden.
* **Actors.** [[Countless]], [[talon-vantyrus|Talon Vantyrus]], [[talon-skarn|Talon Skarn]], [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]], and the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie]].
* **Their aims.** Countless wants access to the gift it associates with Long Sight; Crissdalynn wants to avoid becoming leverage; the Sentinels want their watch and records to continue.
* **Without interference.** Countless continues moving one-job contacts through pilgrim routes, grey markets, and Sentinel waystations, leaving missing names and blank ledgers rather than a complete explanation.
* **Visible sign.** A recent Sentinel visitor disappears from a route record, or a contact identifies Crissdalynn without knowing who issued the assignment.

## Consequences

* **Because this is true,** the party can treat Sentinel waystations and route records as information sites as well as travel infrastructure.
* **If exposed,** Countless's theory about Long Sight may become easier to challenge, but Crissdalynn's value as a target may also become clearer.
* **If disproved,** Countless may redirect its search toward another Fate Spinner carrier or toward the Sentinel records it believes explain the gift.
* **If changed,** [[long-sight-hunt]] would lose its current target or gain a more reliable way to identify carriers.
* **If exploited,** the party could feed Countless a false route or use the gap between its agents' orders and Vantyrus's objective to redirect the hunt.

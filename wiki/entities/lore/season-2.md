# Season 2

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** Season 2 is an open [[midchain|Midchain]] sandbox: the crew can sail to any island and hooks reach them there.

**Why it matters.** No sanctioned employer sends them, so ship combat, privateering, and exploration can all receive regular table time.

**Scope.** Levels 5–10 across twenty or more sessions in the Midchain.

## Current Truth

The pitch starts the *[[uncertainty-manifest|Uncertainty]]* fresh out of repair in [[calven-and-calveno]]. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]]'s Passage ships radio a storm and wrecked Grung hulls. The chase can lead to [[aruhe]] as a smash-and-grab for [[sandro|Sandro]] and [[nino|Nino]], not a full survey.

[[hinewai|Hinewai]] as the Blight is named CR 19 and remains a horizon threat. The confirmed deep-dive is the Schism's Third Name clock on the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie]]. [[talon-vantyrus|Talon Vantyrus]], once known as Osset, relocates cursed-item stock as an island generator. Rumors of the [[red-lady-dead-lady|Red Lady / Dead Lady]] start at [[kalowe|Kalowe]]. The 290 captives at [[karath|Karath]] remain background pressure. Talon can meet the crew by chance and send [[talon-skarn]] if he notices the [[fate-spinner]].

Named optional stops include [[sparhold|Sparhold]], [[fathomrush|Fathomrush]], and [[halythion|Halythion]]. [[penance-bar|Penance Bar]] stays optional. Two draft directions for its ride remain unreconciled in older notes; the later siege note is the one filed on [[penance-bar-siege|Penance Bar Siege]].

### Limits

* **Exception.** The crew can choose among the open islands, but individual hooks still have local stakes and clocks.
* **Unknown.** The exact order and timing of optional stops are not established.
* **Impossible or forbidden.** The Blight is not this season's fight; [[karath|Karath]] is too hot early.

## At the Table

* **Players notice.** Dock gossip, island hooks, and clocks that fill whether the crew chases them or not.
* **This explains.** The lack of a sanctioned employer explains why the crew chooses its next island and why multiple factions can make offers.
* **This enables.** The crew can choose a stop without a rail, follow a clock, accept unofficial piracy, or pursue exploration.
* **This warns of.** [[karath|Karath]] is too hot early, the Blight is a horizon threat rather than this season's fight, and ignoring a clock does not stop it.
* **Relevant now.** The *[[uncertainty-manifest|Uncertainty]]* begins the season repaired but immediately receives the storm and wrecked-Grung-hull signal.

## If This Is Changing

* **Current pressure.** Island hooks and independent clocks continue to advance while the crew chooses its next route.
* **Actors.** [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie]], [[talon-vantyrus|Talon Vantyrus]], [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]]'s Passage ships, and the factions offering optional stops.
* **Their aims.** The Sentinels' Third Name clock advances; Talon relocates cursed-item stock; Passage ships report the storm; optional factions pursue their own local objectives.
* **Without interference.** The crew receives more signals and finds that ignored clocks have changed when they return.
* **Visible sign.** New dock gossip, a changed island situation, a fresh offer, or an advanced clock.

## Consequences

* **Because this is true,** the crew chooses its next island and employer rather than following a sanctioned route.
* **If exposed,** the open structure gives the crew more information about competing hooks but does not stop them.
* **If disproved,** a later campaign decision would need a fixed employer or route; no such change is established.
* **If changed,** the [[uncertainty-manifest|Uncertainty]] would have a different reason to remain in the Midchain.
* **If exploited,** the crew can let factions compete for its attention while pursuing a preferred clock.

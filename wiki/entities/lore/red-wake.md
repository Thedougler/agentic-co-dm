# Red Wake

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** The Red Wake is a moving maritime pressure expressed through [[red-lady-dead-lady|the Dead Lady]] and its collection route. The pattern reaches lanes used by the crew and leaves boats empty, with cargo dumped and crews gone.

**Why it matters.** The wake turns familiar sea lanes into a changing danger and signals that [[Umberlee]]'s pressure is following the party's route.

**Scope.** The supplied source establishes the pattern in maritime lanes reached by the Dead Lady, especially the [[Midchain]]; it does not establish a universal sea-wide effect.

## Current Truth

The Red Wake is the pressure pattern associated with the Dead Lady, a raised wreck serving [[Umberlee]]. The Dead Lady takes boats whole, carries evidence of the original sinking, and can appear on or behind the party's route. The source establishes the wake as an active collection route, not its complete origin, rules, or broader reach.

### Limits

* **Exception.** The Red Wake is expressed through the Dead Lady's route; no separate manifestation is established.
* **Unknown.** Its full origin, rules, range, timing, and collection limits are not established.
* **Impossible or forbidden.** No absolute claim about where the wake cannot reach is established.

## At the Table

* **Players notice.** A red wake or the Dead Lady's silhouette marks a lane where another boat may be found empty, with its holds dumped and crew gone.
* **This explains.** [[the-red-wake|The Red Wake]] quest's boats-taken-whole pattern and the Dead Lady's collection route are expressions of the same pressure.
* **This enables.** The party can track the pattern across familiar lanes, choose whether to cut away, board, signal, bargain, or accept the new route.
* **This warns of.** A familiar route is not safe merely because the party has sailed it before; the wake can turn it into a commitment.
* **Relevant now.** The wake brings [[red-lady-dead-lady|the Dead Lady]] and [[drowned-maw]] into the crew's route.

## If This Is Changing

* **Current pressure.** The Dead Lady's collection route is reaching familiar lanes.
* **Actors.** [[Umberlee]] and [[red-lady-dead-lady|the Dead Lady]].
* **Their aims.** Umberlee's pressure is expressed through the Dead Lady's collection work; the source does not establish a separate aim for the wake.
* **Without interference.** The Dead Lady continues to turn more traveled lanes into possible collection sites.
* **Visible sign.** A red wake, empty boat, dumped hold, or Dead Lady glimpse appears along a lane connected to the party's route.

## Consequences

* **Because this is true,** a familiar lane can become an encounter pressure rather than safe travel.
* **If exposed,** the party can connect empty boats and dumped holds to the Dead Lady's route.
* **If disproved,** the Red Wake would need another established source; none is recorded.
* **If changed,** [[the-red-wake|The Red Wake]] would no longer describe the same route pressure.
* **If exploited,** the party can use the pattern to predict danger or force a choice about boarding, retreat, signalling, or bargaining.

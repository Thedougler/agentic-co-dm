# Semuanya

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** The [[yuki-no-kibo|Yuki no Kibo]] worship Semuanya and teach that breeding, hunting, and survival matter more to the deity than morality, philosophy, or cosmic wars.

**Why it matters.** The mountain order treats freezing temperatures, thin air, and scarce food as tests of divine will rather than obstacles to be morally explained.

**Scope.** This account is established for Yuki no Kibo practice on [[Totemo Tokage]], not for every worshipper elsewhere.

## Current Truth

The monks and villagers are generally kind-spoken and friendly despite the harsh survival doctrine. Long-term mountain residents develop albino scales, and the common belief attributes that change to the winds of Semuanya, though the source does not establish the cause.

### Limits

* **Unknown.** Whether Semuanya has any direct presence or whether the albino scales have a divine cause.
* **Unknown.** Whether other communities interpret Semuanya differently.

## At the Table

* **Players notice.** The mountain community treats survival in cold, thin air, and scarcity as religious practice.
* **This explains.** [[yuki-no-kibo|Yuki no Kibo]] trials are difficult while their hospitality remains gentle.
* **This enables.** Characters can seek the monks' teaching or interpret the mountain trials through the order's values.
* **This warns of.** A visitor who treats survival as optional may fail to understand what the order considers sacred.
* **Relevant now.** This doctrine defines the Yuki no Kibo's role at the snowy summit.

## If This Is Changing

* **Current pressure.** The source establishes no force currently changing the doctrine.
* **Actors.** The [[yuki-no-kibo|Yuki no Kibo]] preserve it through their trials.
* **Their aims.** They aim to maintain survival practice as the order's central expression of faith.
* **Without interference.** The doctrine continues shaping the summit community.
* **Visible sign.** New trials, failed candidates, or changed treatment of visitors would show movement.

## Consequences

* **Because this is true,** the order's harsh mountain trials can be approached as worship.
* **If exposed,** outsiders can understand why the monks accept scarcity and cold.
* **If disproved,** the order's interpretation of its trials would lose authority.
* **If changed,** [[yuki-no-kibo|Yuki no Kibo]] practice would change with it.
* **If exploited,** a visitor could imitate survival devotion to seek access to the order.

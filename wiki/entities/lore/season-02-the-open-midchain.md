The bush stands nineteen paces from the hull, still fruiting.

[[sandro|Sandro]] counted the paces on the second day, when counting still meant working out what he could eat, and he has not needed to count them since. [[nino|Nino]] will walk twelve and stop. He stops at twelve and comes back and offers no reason, and Sandro stopped asking for one after the first time, because the reason is lying out past the tideline with its hands open, and from the split in the *[[vethka|Vethka]]*'s planking where the stern used to be they can both see the shape of it whenever the grass goes flat in the wind.

Tomo came ashore on the same broken outrigger strut they did. He held the wood on the seaward side and let the two of them have the lee of it, and he was still talking when the reef let go of them, still saying the thing about his brother's boat and how someone had built it wrong from the keel up and it had outlived every boat built right. He lived through the water. That was the part they thought was the hard part.

He ate four of them. He said they were sweet, in a voice that had already changed. By the time either man understood what he was hearing, the changing had reached Tomo's hands.

Now Nino walks twelve paces and turns, and the island goes on being an island. Crabs the size of hunting dogs work the shingle and do not scatter, and birds come down out of the palms in a line, open a thing on the sand, and vanish before the sound arrives. Something in the freshwater springs has learned the sound of a person kneeling. The two [[Grung|Grung]] who made the beach alive were dead inside four hours, and neither man saw it happen, and both of them heard it.

They watch the reef gap. That is the whole of the work now. The gap is the only water a hull could come through. It has stayed empty every day, first light to last. Sandro has stopped saying out loud that a ship is coming because saying it costs something now and buys nothing back.

---

Four hundred and eighty-seven people went into the water off [[calveno|Calveno]].

Two hundred and ninety made [[karath|Karath]], a figure the traders keep and the families do not. Forty-eight went down with the two hulls that broke in the open crossing and have no number of their own except that one. A hundred and forty-nine came apart on [[aruhe|Aruhe]]'s reefs with their guards still chained to the same decks, and the arithmetic of a beach that keeps what it takes accounts for a hundred and forty-seven of them.

Nobody has done this sum. The pieces of it sit in four different ledgers on three different islands, and the ledgers do not talk to each other, and the two men in the hull are not in any of them.

---

[[talon-vantyrus|Talon Vantyrus]], still traceable in the trade network under his original name Osset, has thirty-one items left and a description of himself circulating that is close enough to be a problem.

Falcon-featured, they say, which is the word that will do it, because six thousand [[aarakocra|Aarakocra]] live between here and the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] and perhaps eleven of them would draw that word out of anyone who had looked properly. He has kept careful for years now, long past being frightened of it, and the carefulness has calcified into habit: he never uses the same fence twice, and he never lets a buyer's face become one he would know again. The provenance he offers is always true and never finished, a harder discipline than lying, one that leaves nothing to remember wrong.

He sold a cloak to a woman in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] who sells cheerfully and asks nothing, and he watched her not ask, and he knew as he walked out that the not-asking would be what fixed him in her memory. That is the arithmetic of it. Every transaction is one more person who could describe him; he has thirty-one transactions left and about eleven descriptions of margin, the two numbers converging on a date he has not written down.

What Vantyrus sells is not the point. The point is what he does after the last of it disappears, when nothing remains in the [[Midchain|Midchain]] with his hand on it. He can go wherever a man goes, because the order he left behind has never once in two hundred years written down that he existed. He has thought about this for a long time. The thinking has become the thing he does instead of the going.

He has never once considered that anyone would come looking, because the order itself does not look. That is the order's entire character, and he built his whole life after it on that single fact.

---

Vantyrus does not hurry, and has not needed to in a long time.

He pays four people in the Midchain and none of them know about the others, and what he pays them for is not information but the shape of a schedule: which hull ties up and for how long, and the order the crew comes down the gangway. He watches a girl of fourteen who does not know she is interesting. He watches the wooden thing she spins between her fingers when she is thinking too, the one the order gave her early and by exception and has never explained to her. The wooden thing is a door left ajar in a wall with no other doors in it.

He was [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]]'s master. He says this to people in the tone of a man mentioning a shared acquaintance. The claim is true, and its truth is the most useful thing he owns. Kyzil taught the girl. He taught Kyzil. A line runs from the thing sealed under the Eyrie to a fourteen-year-old on a pirate's deck, and Vantyrus can see the whole of it, the only living person who can.

He would like to look at the dreidel with his own eyes before he sends Skarn. Not from doubt. From the specific pleasure of confirming a thing he already knows, the only pleasure a patient man keeps.

---

The gold is coming up faster on [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s forearms than it did last year, and she has started wearing the vambraces indoors.

[[ozzeth-the-twiceborn|Ozzeth]] kept it quiet for eleven years. Neither of them ever called it that. At the close of a garrison inspection he would put a hand on the back of her neck, the way a senior officer does with someone he has decided to keep. Whatever the rite left running under her skin would lie down for a season after that. She had assumed there would always be another inspection. Then a ritual chamber came down on him under Calveno, and the report that reached [[sorn|Sorn]] told her a mentor had died and told her, underneath that, exactly how long she has.

She has a brother. She has spent eleven years being the woman who chose the caste order over her own household, and the story is true, and she has never once amended it. She told their parents about the gnome. She stood in the hut with [[pell|Pell]] in chains and let Jean-Claude walk in on it. She would do it again. She has said so out loud, and the garrison loves her for a certainty that can say a thing like that in daylight.

What she has not said in daylight is that he took twenty vials with him when he ran.

She has worked out where they went by elimination, the way she works out everything, and the answer is a ship somewhere east of the Teeth with a Calveno crew aboard it and her brother eating at their table. The trade that used to bring her the substitute burned when [[barnaby-rook|Rook]] went silent, and no second supplier exists. The stalled transformation comes up her arms in dots and bands that no sleeve covers for a full day anymore. One person alive carries the only stock she can reach, and he has told nobody he has it.

She will make him an offer, and she will mean it. A loose end is a thing you close and a brother is a thing you use, and she has never been able to hold both words in her mouth at once. Come back. The garrison has a place for you. Nobody has to know what you were.

She has already decided what she does if he says no. She decided it before she decided to ask.

---

Under the basalt at [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]], something that has not moved in two hundred years is paying attention.

It has recorded weather. It has recorded drift, wreck, storm, compass failure, the movement of ships, light where light should not be, and water going flat in a dead calm. It has recorded every survivor who ever came out of the Maw saying a thing that made no sense. It has recorded every bit of that without conclusion, because concluding is interpreting, and interpreting is entering the record, and it has spent two centuries declining to enter the record.

The escalation now exceeds every escalation in the ledger, and it exceeds them in kind. No entry in two centuries of memory matches it. For the first time since the sealing, the record it keeps has nothing to tell it.

It watches harder now. That is all it has done so far, and watching harder is still observation, and the doctrine holds.

Kyzil, forty feet of stone above it, knows that the order's founding documents are very old.

---

The [[Midchain|Midchain]] has not noticed any of this.

[[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s dry dock takes any hull that pays and writes down nothing that would embarrass a client. [[sparhold|Sparhold]] undercuts Kalowe by a margin that would be suspicious if anyone cared to look; nobody does, because the goods move. [[fathomrush|Fathomrush]] is losing divers at the rate a town loses divers when two companies are racing each other to the same sealed chamber, and the plank wall between the piers has a hundred names on it and room for more. [[slackwater|Slackwater]]'s families take their quarrels to one house and abide by what it says about the tide. [[halythion|Halythion]] has stood where it stands for longer than the charts have shown it, and has told almost no one, and on [[murrat|Murrat]] the villages come down to the waterline when a sail turns in and do not come down to trade.

Out past it, off the charted line, the water in the Maw goes flat in weather that should not allow it.

[[umberlee|Umberlee]] has stopped waiting. She named a price at a shrine in Calveno and the price was not paid and the silence went on long enough to become an answer, so she collects now: a hull found at anchor, hold emptied and crew gone. Nothing about it says a name out loud. More will follow. No arrangement remains to reach, and the man who owes her has not yet understood that the thing hunting the shipping lanes means him.

The *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]* comes out of repair with her hull sound and nobody's writ aboard her, and turns for open water, and no one has asked her to do anything at all.

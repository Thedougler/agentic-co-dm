# Sea Elf

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** Sea elves are aquatic elves who breathe air and water, swim, and possess elven traits adapted to life at sea. In the [[shattered-sea]], their communities are centered at [[halythion]] and tied to the worship of Deep Sashelas.

**Why it matters.** A sea elf can treat submerged routes as ordinary movement, communicate simple ideas to swimming Beasts, and rest through trance rather than sleep. Their connection to [[halythion]] and Deep Sashelas identifies a cultural and religious route into sea-elf knowledge.

**Scope.** The cultural details apply to sea elves in the Shattered Sea; the listed traits describe the campaign's sea-elf subtype.

## Current Truth

Sea elves are Medium Humanoids with a walking speed of 30 feet and a swimming speed equal to their walking speed. They breathe air and water, resist cold damage, see in darkness out to 60 feet, have Fey Ancestry, communicate simple ideas to any Beast with a swimming speed, and are proficient in Perception.

They do not need sleep and cannot be magically put to sleep. A long rest requires four hours of trance, after which they gain two weapon or tool proficiencies from elven memory until the next long rest. They speak Common plus one additional language and can live for 750 or more years.

In the [[shattered-sea]], sea elves are centered at [[halythion]] and tied to the worship of Deep Sashelas. Some trace ancestral ties to the [[elemental-plane-of-water]].

### Limits

* **Exception.** Friend of the Sea communicates only simple ideas and only to Beasts with a swimming speed.
* **Unknown.** The full relationship between Halythion and the elemental plane of water is not established.
* **Older variant.** The Tome of Foes variant is superseded for most campaigns; it used different ability scores, equipment proficiencies, languages, and communication details.

## At the Table

* **Players notice.** A sea elf moves through water as readily as across land, remains alert during a four-hour trance, and can exchange simple ideas with swimming Beasts.
* **This explains.** [[halythion]]'s sea-elf culture and its connection to Deep Sashelas.
* **This enables.** The party can use a sea elf as a guide for submerged travel or as an interpreter for a swimming Beast's simple message.
* **This warns of.** Darkness, cold, and underwater access do not impose the same limits on a sea elf as they do on landbound travelers.
* **Relevant now.** Sea-elf water and Halythion are established features of the eastern [[Midchain]].

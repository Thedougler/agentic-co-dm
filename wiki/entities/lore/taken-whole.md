# Taken Whole

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** Kalowe dock talk is partially true: the [[red-lady-dead-lady|Dead Lady]] leaves empty derelicts with holds dumped overboard, but witnesses mistake divine collection for raiding or salvage.

**Why it matters.** The rumor turns a maritime threat into a discoverable lead connected to [[Umberlee]] and [[delmar-fisk]]'s open debt.

**Scope.** This page records the rumor, its spread, and one investigation route; it does not reveal the full Red Wake truth to players.

## Current Truth

"There's a ship out past the Maw-lanes that doesn't fight and doesn't loot. She comes up red out of the fog, and when she's gone the whole crew's dead and the hold's stripped bare. Just an empty hull, drifting wherever the current takes her."

**Truth Value.**

**Partially true.** Every claim about what's found is accurate. [[The *Dead Lady*]] leaves derelicts exactly like that: everyone aboard dead, holds emptied over the side. What the rumour gets wrong is the shape of it. Sailors assume raiders or a sea monster clearing a ship for salvage nobody will contest. Nobody circulating this story knows it's [[Umberlee]] collecting tribute directly instead of through a shrine basin, and nobody has connected it to [[delmar-fisk]]'s open debt.

**Spread.**

Tavern and dock talk at [[kalowe]], with no single source: fishers, dockhands, and crews who've either spotted a drifting hulk themselves or heard about one secondhand. It mutates the way any secondhand horror story does. The core of it stays fixed across every telling: a red ship comes, and the ship it leaves behind is empty and adrift. Newer versions increasingly claim she takes the gold too, since "raiders who leave the cargo behind" makes no sense to anyone repeating it. Nobody has yet suggested she wants something other than plunder.

**Investigating It.**

**Investigation — Read the Derelict's Story**

DC 14. **Success:** the details that actually circulate don't add up for ordinary raiders. Nothing about the hatches or the hull shows a boarding fight, and the missing cargo has never turned up fenced or resold anywhere in port. Something took what it wanted and put the rest over the side instead of carrying it off. **Failure:** the story stays a pirate rumour. Dangerous water, best avoided, and there's nothing more to learn here.
## Limits

* **Exception.** The rumor correctly describes the derelicts but misidentifies the cause as raiding or salvage.
* **Unknown to the world.** Dockside tellers do not know that [[Umberlee]] is collecting tribute or how [[delmar-fisk]]'s debt connects to the wrecks.
* **Impossible or forbidden.** Nothing in the current account establishes a forbidden action.

## At the Table

* **Players notice.** A red ship emerges from fog, leaves a dead crew, and leaves the cargo dumped overboard rather than carried away.
* **This explains.** Kalowe's repeated reports of empty drifting hulls without evidence of ordinary boarding.
* **This enables.** The party can examine a derelict, question dockside witnesses, or connect the pattern to [[Umberlee]].
* **This warns of.** A ship that appears not to be looted may still be the aftermath of a lethal supernatural collection.
* **Relevant now.** The rumor provides a lead into the Red Wake threat around Kalowe and the Maw lanes.

## If This Is Changing

* **Current pressure.** More derelicts and increasingly specific dockside versions are making the rumor harder to dismiss.
* **Actors.** [[Umberlee]], the [[red-lady-dead-lady|Dead Lady]], and [[delmar-fisk]]'s unresolved debt shape the hidden truth behind the reports.
* **Their aims.** The source establishes tribute collection and an open debt, but not a further stated aim.
* **Without interference.** Dockside talk continues to spread while derelicts accumulate beyond the Maw lanes.
* **Visible sign.** Another empty hull drifts into Kalowe with its cargo thrown overboard.

## Consequences

* **Because this is true,** the party can treat repeated derelict reports as a connected threat rather than unrelated wrecks.
* **If exposed,** Kalowe's sailors may understand the danger as divine collection rather than piracy.
* **If disproved,** the link between the red ship and the derelicts would need correction.
* **If changed,** [[kalowe]] and [[delmar-fisk]]'s related records would need synchronized updates.
* **If exploited,** the investigation route can use wreck evidence and dockside testimony without requiring a single informant.

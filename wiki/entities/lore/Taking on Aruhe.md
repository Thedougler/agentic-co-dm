# Taking on Aruhe

On [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]], a loose stone or shed shell can mark a route. Taking a living plant, animal, or other living claim draws surrounding life into hostility.

[[Aruhe - Beach|Western Landing]] uses this ruling when a landing aid might still be alive. This stub does not add a separate mechanical table.

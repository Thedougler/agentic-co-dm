# Umberlee

> [!narration] Narration
> Harbour stones carry a wave curling left to right. Salt, tallow, and a basin by the quay make the sea's claim ordinary before any ship casts off.

## Durable question

What does it cost to admit the sea can take you, and what happens if the harbours stop paying?

## At a Glance

- **Core truth:** Umberlee is the sea as it actually is. At sea she is simply Umberlee; people call her the Bitch Queen in anger and Queen of the Depths when trying not to use the first name. She owns waves, storms, wrecks, and the fear that makes a captain pay before checking the weather.
- **Why it matters:** Every working harbour in the Scatter pays tribute. The Pearl remains below elemental water she cannot reach, and the *[[Red Lady - Dead Lady]]* works as her collection hull while that debt stays open.

## Current Truth

Her only demand is admission that she can take you. The sea does not negotiate, and a blessing is not a promise. Tribute is that admission. A shrine stays where a sailor can find it on the way in or out. Coin is easiest; rope, fish bones, buttons, compass needle, carved token, or spoken promise can stand in. The [[Waveservants]] collect at the same rate from merchant, pirate, navy, smuggler, and pilgrim.

The [[Drowned Maw]] is a puncture in the trench floor into the Elemental Plane of Water. Umberlee's claim ends where the water stops tasting like her water. She can sink a fleet on this side, but cannot reach into elemental water to take back the [[Pearl of Souls]]. The Pearl's theft from [[Vel Orn]] left her with one mortal path to recovery. She pressed the five captains into [[Delmar Fisk]], the only survivor, while their crews remained in the Pearl. She has not killed Delmar because the captains ride him. Through [[Umberlee - Branca]] her command was: Bring me the pearl. Then we will talk about the commissioners.

The Pearl still broadcasts across the boundary. The [[Leviathan]] and smaller horrors answer, and something larger presses at the fissure. The clergy do not advertise the suspicion that payments may be the procedural plug holding the Maw fissure shut.

The [[Tithe of the Bitch Queen]] is a separate Sunken Crown hoard in tidal caves. Taking a piece puts her attention on it until it touches saltwater again. Her clergy refuse to rule on whether the Pearl counts as Tithe.

## At the Table

- **Notice:** wave mark on harbour stones and prows; Waveservant grey; basin and booklet; the Dead Lady's red wake.
- **Explains:** why every harbour pays, why Delmar still lives, why the Pearl cannot simply be taken by storm.
- **Enables:** tribute as leverage, shrine appointments, collection-hull pressure.
- **Warns:** challenging Umberlee may also challenge Maw containment.

## Counterweights

- [[Valkur]]: muttered sailor courage without basin or booklet; sailors still pay Umberlee.
- [[Tyr]]: land-and-paper counterweight behind Dravosi warrants; he does not own the wave.
- [[Auralis]]: constructed or publicly divinized Antheri authority, a different kind of power problem.
- [[Sentinels of the Eyrie]]: monastic counter to Waveservant collection.

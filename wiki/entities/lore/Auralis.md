# Auralis

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** Auralis is an Antheri deep machine beneath [[drowned-maw]]. He guards a fissure toward the [[elemental-plane-of-water]] and reaches through an unwitting bond with [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] to act above the water.

**Why it matters.** The wards around the fissure have already nearly failed. [[pearl-of-souls]] and [[Shelfworks]] activity threaten the containment, while Auralis's contact gives the party a way to notice danger and choose whether to help a guardian who never received Perrin's consent.

**Scope.** This is true of Auralis, the Drowned Maw fissure, and his current connection to Perrin. His original mind, full limits, and reason for choosing Perrin remain unknown.

## Current Truth

Auralis is not a god. The [[Antheri]] built him as a body and deep machine after losing the original mind, then placed him on the trench floor to hold the fissure shut. He has guarded it for roughly two centuries, keeping the [[elemental-plane-of-water]] from opening into the Shattered Sea.

The containment has weakened. [[pearl-of-souls]] destabilizes the fissure, and [[Shelfworks]] salvagers loosen the upper structures without understanding what they support. Auralis has formed an unwitting pact with Perrin, whose cloak saved him when the [[Vestra]] went down. Auralis can now warn or urge Perrin from below, but Perrin did not knowingly agree to the bond.

### Limits

* **Exception.** Auralis can warn Perrin and urge a choice, but he cannot compel Perrin to obey.
* **Unknown to the world.** The original mind is lost; no living witness can explain why Auralis chose Perrin or what he wants from [[giants-guava]].
* **Impossible or forbidden.** Auralis cannot simply abandon the fissure without risking the wards he was built to maintain.

## At the Table

* **Players notice.** A blue-white glow shaped like a whale can appear around Perrin, and Auralis's voice arrives without a visible mouth. His first known words were “Grow” and “CONSUME.”
* **This explains.** Perrin's cloak and the glowing whale are signs of an active bond, not an ordinary garment enchantment or an omen from [[Umberlee]].
* **This enables.** Perrin can treat Auralis's warnings as a source of timely information about the fissure, [[fate-spinner]], and the living [[giants-guava]] without surrendering his choices.
* **This warns of.** Salvage around the [[Shelfworks]] and the pearl's destabilization are not isolated hazards; both can weaken the containment below [[drowned-maw]].
* **Relevant now.** Auralis is reaching for the only hand he has above the water while the wards are under pressure.

### Party Knowledge

**Known.** Perrin has seen a glowing whale, and Auralis has delivered the words “Grow” and “CONSUME.” The voice can warn him before danger reaches him.

**Suspected.** The presence is connected to Perrin's cloak and to something sealed below [[drowned-maw]].

**Misunderstood.** The party may read the presence as a god, an [[Umberlee]] omen, or a willing patron. Auralis is Antheri machinery, and the bond was not knowingly accepted.

## If This Is Changing

* **Current pressure.** The fissure is destabilizing while the upper tier is being salvaged.
* **Actors.** Auralis guards the seal; [[Shelfworks]] salvagers keep working above it; [[waveservants]] remain unaware; the pearl continues to worsen the instability.
* **Their aims.** Auralis wants the fissure contained. Shelfworks wants salvage. The Waveservants' ignorance leaves them unable to protect or exploit the danger deliberately.
* **Without interference.** Salvage and instability continue to erode the wards, forcing Auralis to send clearer and more urgent warnings through Perrin.
* **Visible sign.** The glowing whale appears more often, the voice arrives farther from Perrin, or the sealed water manifests where it should not.

## Consequences

* **Because this is true,** Perrin's cloak is a communication channel and a possible source of warnings, not merely protection.
* **If exposed,** the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie]], [[waveservants]], or other powers may try to reach the fissure, exploit it, or silence Auralis.
* **If disproved,** Perrin may lose the only early warning available from below the Maw.
* **If changed,** [[drowned-maw]] would become a more direct route between the Shattered Sea and the [[elemental-plane-of-water]].
* **If exploited,** the party can use Auralis's warnings to decide where to investigate while competing salvagers remain unaware of the danger.

# The Grandfather Hole

## At a Glance

The Grandfather Hole is a legendary underwater sinkhole somewhere in the eastern [[midchain|Midchain]]. Experienced reef divers out of [[kalowe|Kalowe]] avoid it, and port taverns repeat the story. No reliable charting establishes its location, no successful salvage is recorded, and no one has revealed the legend at the table.

## Limits

- The sinkhole's location is unknown.
- The freezing black water and rapid temperature drop are reported features, not confirmed observations.
- The ancient [[sawek|Sawek]], shipwreck reef, scattered gold, and animated drowned sailors are part of the tale; their existence at this site remains unconfirmed.
- No one has recorded a successful salvage.

## Who Knows

- Midchain reef divers know to mark and avoid [[sawek|Sawek]] lairs generally.
- Experienced divers out of [[kalowe|Kalowe]] are said to avoid this particular hole.
- Port taverns carry the account, but the page establishes no named witness or reliable chartmaker.

## Party Knowledge

The party has no revealed knowledge of the legend. If it comes up, the usable lead is only that eastern Midchain divers avoid a sinkhole where water allegedly turns freezing black; the location and truth require discovery.

## Common telling

Tavern accounts describe a massive sinkhole where the water turns unnaturally, freezing black, and drops in temperature faster than any natural current can explain. The hole is said to be territory claimed by an ancient [[sawek|Sawek]] large enough to pull a Tier 3 galleon down by the keel. A century of wrecks and cargo supposedly forms a rotting reef at the bottom, with scattered gold and drowned sailors animated by the Blue Hole's magic.

## If This Is Changing

This lore changes if a named diver supplies a reliable route, a chart fixes the sinkhole's location, a survivor confirms the conditions, or a salvage attempt begins. Until then, keep the account as an unverified lead rather than a revealed fact.

## Consequences

If true, the hole offers a lethal salvage site: the ancient Sawek threatens above while animated drowned crews threaten below in lightless, freezing water. If false or embellished, divers still lose time and safety by following an unreliable chart or tavern lead.

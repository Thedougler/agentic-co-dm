# Remnis

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** Remnis is an [[aarakocra|Aarakocra]] sky god and the primary deity of [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]].

**Why it matters.** Remnis gives Crissdalynn's prayers and Aarakocra identity a named religious reference without requiring her to be a theologian or the campaign to define a full doctrine.

**Scope.** The current canon establishes Remnis as an Aarakocra deity and as Crissdalynn's primary deity; it does not establish a universal faith structure or regional cult.

## Current Truth

Remnis is an Aarakocra sky god and Crissdalynn's primary deity. Crissdalynn wears the faith lightly and prays from habit more than theology. No doctrine, clergy, iconography, miracles, or direct manifestation is established on this page.

## Limits

- **Exception.** Crissdalynn's light observance does not establish how other Aarakocra or worshippers practice.
- **Unknown.** Remnis's domains beyond the sky, teachings, rites, symbols, and relationship to other deities are not defined.
- **Impossible or forbidden.** Nothing is established as impossible or forbidden in Remnis's religion.

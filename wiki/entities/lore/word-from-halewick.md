# Word from Halewick

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** Dockside stories about [[halewick|Halewick]] and [[Toby]] are partially true: Toby rules the island and can remake reality with a word, but the exaggerated death toll compresses recent events and older pirate raids.

**Why it matters.** The rumor offers a dangerous lead to a real island and a child ruler whose power is connected to [[delmar-fisk]]'s theft of the [[pearl-of-souls]].

**Scope.** This page records the rumor, its spread, and one route for separating legend from coastline.

## Current Truth

"There's an island south of here with a boy on the throne, maybe nine years old, ruling like he owns the place outright. Sailors used to raid it. Stopped, years back, because he'd say the word and a man would just die where he stood, burned or drowned or gone in a breath. Nobody's landed and come back with a straight story since. Out there, his word is law, and the law never bends."

**Truth Value.**

**Partially true.** The island is real, [[halewick]], and its self-declared "Emperor" [[Toby]] rules by a word that remakes reality the instant he says it. The old pirate-deterrence claims are real too. What the rumour gets wrong is scale and cause. Sailors compress years of scattered raids and one severe, recent injury into "he'd say the word and a man would die." Toby's power only surfaced three weeks ago. It traces back to [[delmar-fisk]]'s own unresolved theft of the [[pearl-of-souls]], a connection nobody spreading this story has any way to know. Nobody circulating it in port has heard of [[frankie]] or [[billy]], and nobody calls what he does cursed-tongue or links it to Truespeak. To every sailor retelling it, he's just the boy king.

**Spread.**

Tavern and dock talk at [[kalowe]] and other [[Midchain]] ports along the shipping lanes near [[halewick]]. Old pirate crews carry it, the ones who remember why they stopped raiding that stretch of coast, plus sailors who've heard it from them since. Nobody telling it now has set foot on the island and lived to describe it firsthand. Each retelling inflates the death toll and edges the boy further from a person into a legend, "a boy king" in the older versions, "a boy god" in the newest. That much is old news. In the last few days, a second voice has joined the boy's on the island, with a plea for help buried underneath it.

**Investigating It.**

**Investigation — Separate Legend From Coastline**

DC 14. **Success:** the pirate-crew accounts, stripped of their embellishment, agree on real specifics: an island called Halewick, a child fully in charge, and real, undisputed danger in earshot of him. The newest versions' second voice and buried plea line up with something happening right now, not old history, worth chasing before the story gets any older. **Failure:** the story stays a ghost tale about a boy god who kills with a word. A wrong island gets named as confidently as the right one, and running it down first costs a real day's sailing for nothing.
## Limits

* **Exception.** Halewick and Toby are real, but the circulating death toll and scale of his power are exaggerated.
* **Unknown to the world.** Port tellers do not know the connection to [[delmar-fisk]] or the [[pearl-of-souls]], and they have no firsthand account from the island.
* **Impossible or forbidden.** No source establishes that every person who crosses Toby dies by morning.

## At the Table

* **Players notice.** Different sailors repeat the same island name and child ruler while disagreeing about the death toll and exact manner of killing.
* **This explains.** Why old pirate crews stopped raiding Halewick and why newer versions increasingly call Toby a boy god.
* **This enables.** The party can compare old pirate testimony, follow the coastline lead, or investigate the buried plea for help.
* **This warns of.** Toby's power is real within earshot even when the rumor's details are not.
* **Relevant now.** The rumor points toward Halewick, Toby, and the recent voice accompanying his public legend.

## If This Is Changing

* **Current pressure.** A buried plea for help has begun appearing beneath the older Halewick rumor.
* **Actors.** [[Toby]], the unknown second voice, old pirate crews, and sailors carrying the newer versions.
* **Their aims.** Toby rules the island; the second voice appears to seek help; sailors spread or investigate the warning.
* **Without interference.** The story keeps inflating while the real danger and plea remain hidden behind legend.
* **Visible sign.** A new telling preserves the second voice or plea instead of only repeating Toby's death threat.

## Consequences

* **Because this is true,** a dockside ghost story can lead to a real island and a real supernatural danger.
* **If exposed,** Halewick's people and Toby's power would become a current maritime concern rather than old sailor lore.
* **If disproved,** only the rumor's claims would fail; Halewick's existence and Toby's power remain separately established.
* **If changed,** [[halewick|Halewick]], [[Toby]], and [[delmar-fisk]]'s records would need synchronized updates.
* **If exploited,** a false version of the rumor could misdirect a rescue attempt or lure a ship toward Toby's earshot.

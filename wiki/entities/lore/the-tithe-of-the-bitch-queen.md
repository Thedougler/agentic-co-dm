# The Tithe of the Bitch Queen

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** Sailors believe Umberlee's storm-claimed treasures lie scattered across the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]] seafloor, and taking one draws a divine curse that follows the object.

**Why it matters.** The Tithe is a dangerous salvage lead whose curse connects the [[pearl-of-souls]] to [[Umberlee]] without making their theological relationship certain.

**Scope.** This page records the Tithe's established curse and beliefs, not the location of any specific piece.

## Current Truth

Before someone stole the [[pearl-of-souls]], sailors widely believed Umberlee kept a physical collection of the most beautiful treasures her storms had claimed, gathered without design or intent. The Tithe scatters across the Sunken Crown seafloor. Unblemished jewels and pristine statues rest with gold bars that never tarnish, each piece carrying unmistakable divine preservation. Tidal caves that open to air during the lowest tides of the decade offer the only access.

Taking one coin from the Tithe invites Umberlee's direct, personal spite. Sailors who hold a piece cannot sail without drawing unnatural, localized squalls that follow their ship, and the squalls persist until the item touches saltwater again. Waveservant records document no theological workaround. The curse falls on the object, not the bearer. Transferring the item transfers the problem with it.

The Tithe predates the theft of the Pearl of Souls. The Tithe is what Umberlee passively accumulates. The Pearl is something actively stolen from her. The [[waveservants]] will not discuss whether the Pearl belongs to the Tithe in any formal sense.

The curse is Umberlee's active divine attention on each piece of the Tithe. It is not a cast spell and cannot be Counterspelled at the moment of theft. Tradition is Divine. School is Necromancy, as a curse category.

Detect Magic reads as divine necromancy. The curse has weight to it, old and deliberate, like something that recalls the hand that claimed it. Identify shows a mark that no caster placed. Divine attention is personal, present, and aware that the piece has left the Tithe, so whatever god holds claim to it knows you have it now. Passing the piece to another hand passes that attention because the mark belongs to the act of taking it, and Remove Curse does not release it because the origin is divine and it releases only on its own terms. Dispel Magic suppresses for 1 minute, then Umberlee's attention reasserts. Transfer the item and the curse transfers with it.

## Limits

* **Exception.** The curse attaches to the object and transfers with it; the source records no theological workaround.
* **Unknown to the world.** Whether the [[pearl-of-souls]] formally belongs to the Tithe is deliberately unanswered by the [[waveservants]].
* **Impossible or forbidden.** No established removal method releases Umberlee's attention on its own.

## At the Table

* **Players notice.** A pristine treasure draws localized squalls that follow the carrying ship until the piece touches saltwater.
* **This explains.** Why the Tithe's objects remain preserved and why sailors treat its salvage as personal provocation of [[Umberlee]].
* **This enables.** The party can identify a Tithe piece by its divine necromancy and decide whether to return it to saltwater.
* **This warns of.** Passing the object to another hand passes the curse; the bearer cannot simply abandon responsibility.
* **Relevant now.** The Tithe gives the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]] a dangerous salvage pressure and frames the unresolved status of the [[pearl-of-souls]].

## If This Is Changing

* **Current pressure.** The Tithe's objects remain scattered and vulnerable to salvage, while the stolen Pearl's status remains disputed.
* **Actors.** [[Umberlee]], the [[waveservants]], and any salvagers who reach the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]].
* **Their aims.** Umberlee claims what is hers; Waveservants preserve their silence; salvagers seek valuable objects.
* **Without interference.** The Tithe remains a dangerous, scattered salvage field whose curse travels with every taken piece.
* **Visible sign.** A localized squall begins following a ship carrying an apparently pristine treasure.

## Consequences

* **Because this is true,** a salvage prize can become a mobile divine hazard rather than ordinary treasure.
* **If exposed,** the party may draw Umberlee's attention by holding or transferring a Tithe piece.
* **If disproved,** the curse and the claimed collection would need reconciliation with Waveservant records.
* **If changed,** the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]] and [[waveservants]] records would need synchronized updates.
* **If exploited,** a cursed object could force another ship to carry a storm until it is returned to saltwater.

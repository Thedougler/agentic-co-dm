# The Tithe of the Bitch Queen

*A legendary collection of divine treasure scattered across the [[sunken-crown]] seafloor. Taking one piece invites [[Umberlee]]'s direct, personal spite.*

## The Fact

Before someone stole the [[pearl-of-souls]], sailors widely believed Umberlee kept a physical collection of the most beautiful treasures her storms had claimed, gathered without design or intent. The Tithe scatters across the Sunken Crown seafloor. Unblemished jewels and pristine statues rest with gold bars that never tarnish, each piece carrying unmistakable divine preservation. Tidal caves that open to air during the lowest tides of the decade offer the only access.

Taking one coin from the Tithe invites Umberlee's direct, personal spite. Sailors who hold a piece cannot sail without drawing unnatural, localized squalls that follow their ship, and the squalls persist until the item touches saltwater again. Waveservant records document no theological workaround. The curse falls on the object, not the bearer. Transferring the item transfers the problem with it.

The Tithe predates the theft of the Pearl of Souls. The Tithe is what Umberlee passively accumulates. The Pearl is something actively stolen from her. The [[Waveservants]] will not discuss whether the Pearl belongs to the Tithe in any formal sense.

The curse is Umberlee's active divine attention on each piece of the Tithe. It is not a cast spell and cannot be Counterspelled at the moment of theft. Tradition is Divine. School is Necromancy, as a curse category.

Detect Magic reads as divine necromancy. The curse has weight to it, old and deliberate, like something that recalls the hand that claimed it. Identify shows a mark that no caster placed. Divine attention is personal, present, and aware that the piece has left the Tithe, so whatever god holds claim to it knows you have it now. Passing the piece to another hand passes that attention because the mark belongs to the act of taking it, and Remove Curse does not release it because the origin is divine and it releases only on its own terms. Dispel Magic suppresses for 1 minute, then Umberlee's attention reasserts. Transfer the item and the curse transfers with it.

### Deliberate Silences

- Whether the stolen Pearl of Souls counts as part of the Tithe in any formal theological sense. The Waveservants refuse to discuss it, and no ruling exists.

# Elemental Plane of Water

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** The Elemental Plane of Water is the water cosmology [[Istishia]] can point toward in Maw discussions.

**Why it matters.** Maw fissures and Umberlee's claim-boundary talk need a named plane without inventing a new cosmology here.

**Scope.** Thin stub from the Istishia source; place detail stays on [[drowned-maw]] and related pages.

## Current Truth

[[Istishia]] can point at the Elemental Plane of Water as cosmology. A fissure in the [[drowned-maw]] breaches this plane. The breach predates colonial memory and is widening. When the [[pearl-of-souls]] sank near the fissure, it began pulling entities through. What has come through so far is small enough to fit the crack. Something larger scrapes at the far side.

[[Leviathan]] is named as the first entity through. [[Ridgeback]] is the second, documented in Clyde's Bestiary. [[Krakling]] is the third, a juvenile arm-predator reported near [[Midchain]]. All three are described with flat-black skin, no eyes, blindsight, and water breathing.

[[Antheri]] ruins and the [[Shelfworks]] sit above the breach. [[Auralis]] is named as aware of the incursion.
### Limits

* **Exception.** This page names the cosmology; detailed geography and the breach remain on [[drowned-maw]].
* **Unknown to the world.** The plane's full extent and what scrapes at the far side are not established.
* **Impossible or forbidden.** The plane name alone does not establish [[Istishia]] as a villain.
## At the Table

* **Players notice:** Water that does not behave like ordinary sea.
* **This explains:** Cosmological references linked from [[Istishia]] and Maw pages.
* **This enables:** Correct naming when the table needs the plane.
* **This warns of:** Do not invent Istishia as a villain from the plane name alone.

**Suspected.** The party may suspect that more than [[Leviathan]], [[Ridgeback]], and [[Krakling]] can cross.

**Misunderstood.** The plane's existence does not by itself make [[Istishia]] an enemy.

**Last changed.** No session is recorded.

## If This Is Changing

* **Current pressure.** The fissure is widening and the [[pearl-of-souls]] continues pulling entities through.
* **Actors.** [[Auralis]] is aware of the incursion; entities from the plane answer the Pearl's signal; [[Shelfworks]] activity occurs above the breach.
* **Their aims.** Auralis maintains containment, while the arriving entities follow the signal or press toward the breach.
* **Without interference.** The breach admits larger or more numerous entities as the fissure widens.
* **Visible sign.** Flat-black, eyeless creatures with blindsight and water breathing appear near the Maw or [[Midchain]].

## Consequences

* **Because this is true,** the Maw is a boundary problem, not only a local sea hazard.
* **If exposed,** the parties who control the Pearl or the Maw gain leverage over a route to another plane.
* **If disproved,** the recorded explanation for the Maw creatures and fissure would fail.
* **If changed,** the Shattered Sea's water and the plane beyond the fissure would interact differently.
* **If exploited,** a party can use the named breach and its signs to anticipate where an incursion may surface.

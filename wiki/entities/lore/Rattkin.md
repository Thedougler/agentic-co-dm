# Rattkin

Rattkin are Small humanoids, about three to four feet tall, with a walking speed of 30 feet.

As a Rattkin, you have these special traits.

**Boneless.** You can move through spaces as if you were Tiny. Moving this way halves your speed. You have **Advantage** on checks to escape a **Grapple** or **Restrained**.

**Swimmer.** You have a swim speed equal to your walking speed.

**Keen Senses.** You have **Advantage** on hearing- or smell-based Perception checks, and can sense hidden or invisible creatures within 5 feet by scent.

**Scurry.** Your Disengage movement can pass through Large or smaller creatures without provoking opportunity attacks.

**Survivor.** You have **Advantage** on saving throws against being **Poisoned** and against disease, and resistance to poison damage.

**Pack Tactics.** You have a limited reaction attack when an ally hits a nearby creature.

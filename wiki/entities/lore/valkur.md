# Valkur

## At a Glance

* **Core truth:** [[Valkur]] is the muttered sailor counterweight of courage, hoped-for wind, and private hope; he has no established formal temple in the Scatter.
* **Why it matters:** Sailors can invoke a private hope without abandoning the practical need to pay [[Umberlee]].
* **Scope:** A sailor belief and counterweight in the Shattered Sea.

## Current Truth

[[Valkur]] is the muttered line at the rail: sailor courage, hoped-for wind, and private hope without a basin or booklet. Sailors still pay [[Umberlee]]. [[Valkur]] and [[Tyr]] act as counterweights to her; [[Valkur]] is invoked quietly below decks. He has no formal temples in the Scatter.

## Limits

The sources establish a sailor belief and invocation, not a formal priesthood, temple network, miracle, or direct appearance. The sources do not establish whether Valkur is an active deity or only a sailor tradition.

## Who Knows

Sailors and the [[Waveservants]] know the name as a counterweight to [[Umberlee]]. The extent of wider knowledge is not established.

## Party Knowledge

The party can learn the name by hearing a sailor mutter it at the rail or below decks. No party witness is recorded.

## Common telling

“Valkur, give us wind.” The exact wording is not fixed; the sources describe the invocation as quiet and private.

Add a change only when play establishes a new practice, visible sign, clerical presence, or response attributed to [[Valkur]].

## Consequences

* A sailor may hope for favorable wind while still making the customary payment to [[Umberlee]].
* A formal temple or public miracle would contradict the current limit and require an explicit canon update.

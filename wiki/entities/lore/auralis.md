# Auralis

![[auralis-reference.png|Auralis reference sheet]]

Auralis is a deep machine the [[Antheri]] built on the trench floor beneath [[drowned-maw]] to hold shut the fissure into the [[elemental-plane-of-water]]. He has a demigod's power and a guardian's single purpose, and he is not a god. He slept until Day -5, when the [[pearl-of-souls]]' signal dragged the [[leviathan]] through the fissure and the fleet went down above him. In that moment he reached for the nearest dying mind, [[perrin-black-jaw]], drowning in the wreck of the *Vestra*, and kept him alive. That act forged an unwitting warlock pact. Perrin credits his cloak. Auralis now reaches the world above the water only through Perrin, and he cannot compel him.

## What He Is

- **Built, not born.** The Antheri built Auralis about -2000 DR as a body and machine for a mind they then lost. What remains is the mandate: hold the fissure.
- **The fissure is failing.** The Pearl's signal destabilizes it, and [[Shelfworks]] salvagers loosen the upper structures without knowing what they hold up. The displaced [[leviathan]] is an invader he holds short of the breach.
- **Why Perrin.** Perrin was the only living mind within Auralis's reach at the moment he woke. The bond was an act of rescue, not a choice of champion.
- **What he can do.** Speak into Perrin's skull, show himself as pale blue light in the shape of a whale around Perrin's cloak, and grant Perrin's warlock magic. He sees what comes toward Perrin a little before it arrives.
- **What he cannot do.** Compel Perrin, leave the fissure, or explain himself at length. He speaks in single words.

## His Words

- **"Grow."** Session 03, in an abyss vision. He wanted Perrin to become strong enough to be useful.
- **"Not yet."** Session 06, when Perrin dropped to 0 HP and his death save stabilized. It was not his time to die.
- **"CONSUME."** Session 12, at [[the-pantry]], with a living [[giants-guava]] and [[stonepear]] a hand's width from Perrin's nose. Auralis senses the invisible [[talon-skarn]] closing on the [[fate-spinner]]. He wants Perrin at full strength for it: the guava sets Perrin's Charisma to 25 for 1 hour, and the stonepear gives him Resistance to all damage for 1 minute. He does not know Aruhe's law ([[taking-on-aruhe]]), so he does not know picking makes Perrin a claim. The claim also sets the island's responders on the invisible falcon beside Perrin. He gives no reason, and eating is a test of Perrin's faith.
- **If Perrin eats.** The whale-light holds steady around his cloak until dawn.
- **If Perrin refuses.** The light does not come when Perrin reaches for it, and Auralis is silent until the next session.

## At the Table

- **Players notice.** Pale blue light in the shape of a whale around Perrin's cloak, and a voice in Perrin's head with no mouth behind it.
- **It explains.** Perrin survived the *Vestra* alone because Auralis held him, and Perrin's warlock magic comes from a patron he never agreed to.
- **It lets them.** Treat Auralis's single words as warnings of what is coming, without surrendering Perrin's choices.
- **It warns of.** Salvage at the [[Shelfworks]] and the Pearl's pull both weaken the seal beneath the Maw.
- **Party knows.** Perrin has seen the glowing whale and heard "Grow," "Not yet," and "CONSUME." The party suspects the presence is tied to his cloak and to something below the Maw. It may read him as a god, an [[Umberlee]] omen, or a willing patron, and he is none of these.
- **If exposed.** The [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie]], the [[waveservants]], or other powers may try to reach the fissure, use it, or silence Auralis. The Crown's confirmation of Perrin's unwitting pact opens "Answer the Next Confirmed Heresy."

- [ ] **The whale.** Perrin sees it around his cloak in Session 08 on the crossing toward [[calven-and-calveno]], and again at dusk in Session 12.
- [ ] **The source.** Sages of the [[grung-clans]] keep a mandate naming a gold presence that holds the deep water shut beneath the Maw.
- [ ] **The pact.** **Intelligence (Arcana)** `DC 15` on Perrin's magic finds that its source lies deep below the sea, not in the cloak.

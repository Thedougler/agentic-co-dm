# Word from Halewick

*Dock talk south of [[Kalowe]] claims a boy king rules an island where his word is law and anyone who crosses him turns up dead by morning.*

## The Rumour

"There's an island south of here with a boy on the throne, maybe nine years old, ruling like he owns the place outright. Sailors used to raid it. Stopped, years back, because he'd say the word and a man would just die where he stood, burned or drowned or gone in a breath. Nobody's landed and come back with a straight story since. Out there, his word is law, and the law never bends."

## Truth Value

**Partially true.** The island is real, [[Halewick]], and its self-declared "Emperor" [[Toby]] rules by a word that remakes reality the instant he says it. The old pirate-deterrence claims are real too. What the rumour gets wrong is scale and cause. Sailors compress years of scattered raids and one severe, recent injury into "he'd say the word and a man would die." Toby's power only surfaced three weeks ago. It traces back to [[Delmar Fisk]]'s own unresolved theft of the [[Pearl of Souls]], a connection nobody spreading this story has any way to know. Nobody circulating it in port has heard of [[Frankie]] or [[Billy]], and nobody calls what he does cursed-tongue or links it to Truespeak. To every sailor retelling it, he's just the boy king.

## Spread

Tavern and dock talk at [[Kalowe]] and other [[Midchain]] ports along the shipping lanes near [[Halewick]]. Old pirate crews carry it, the ones who remember why they stopped raiding that stretch of coast, plus sailors who've heard it from them since. Nobody telling it now has set foot on the island and lived to describe it firsthand. Each retelling inflates the death toll and edges the boy further from a person into a legend, "a boy king" in the older versions, "a boy god" in the newest. That much is old news. In the last few days, a second voice has joined the boy's on the island, with a plea for help buried underneath it.

## Investigating It

> [!check] Investigation — Separate Legend From Coastline
> DC 14. **Success:** the pirate-crew accounts, stripped of their embellishment, agree on real specifics: an island called Halewick, a child fully in charge, and real, undisputed danger in earshot of him. The newest versions' second voice and buried plea line up with something happening right now, not old history, worth chasing before the story gets any older. **Failure:** the story stays a ghost tale about a boy god who kills with a word. A wrong island gets named as confidently as the right one, and running it down first costs a real day's sailing for nothing.

# Minotaur

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** Minotaurs are broad, heavy Medium Humanoids with horns, a 30-foot speed, perfect path recall, and forced-movement attacks.

**Why it matters.** A Minotaur who has walked a channel once can recall it blindfolded twenty years later, including currents, channel marks, and reef lines.

**Scope.** Minotaurs have no homeland or nation recorded here; kinship comes through teaching and peer bonds called Soundings.

## Current Truth

Minotaurs have bovine heads, thick curving horns, broad shoulders, and hooved feet. Their traits are:

* **Creature Type and Size.** Humanoid; Medium, at the upper end because Minotaurs are broad and heavy.
* **Speed.** 30 feet.
* **Horns.** Natural melee weapon dealing `1d6` piercing damage on a hit.
* **Goring Rush.** After moving at least 20 feet in a straight line toward a target, make a Bonus Action Horn attack. On a hit, the target makes a Strength saving throw or is pushed 10 feet.
* **Hammering Horns.** After hitting with a melee attack on your turn, use a Bonus Action to shove with your horns. The target makes a Strength saving throw or is pushed 10 feet and knocked Prone.
* **Labyrinthine Recall.** Perfect recall of any traveled path and **Advantage** on checks made to navigate or avoid becoming lost.

## Limits

* **Trigger.** Goring Rush requires at least 20 feet of straight-line movement toward the target.
* **Trigger.** Hammering Horns requires a melee hit on the Minotaur's turn.
* **Unknown.** The source does not establish a Minotaur homeland or nation.

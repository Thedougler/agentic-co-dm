# Tabaxi

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** Tabaxi are Medium feline Humanoids with a 35-foot speed, claws, predatory senses, Darkvision, and a territorial streak.

**Why it matters.** Their speed, stealth, senses, and type-specific traits shape how Tabaxi move, hunt, and defend what is theirs.

**Scope.** Tabaxi have a small, old presence in the Shattered Sea centered on [[keth-naar]].

## Current Truth

Tabaxi rely on predatory instinct and keen senses. They have a 35-foot speed and the following traits:

* **Claws.** Natural weapon used for unarmed strikes that deal slashing damage.
* **Feline Ferocity.** A burst of speed in a fight that recharges after taking a turn without moving.
* **Keen Senses.** **Advantage** on Wisdom (Perception) checks relying on hearing or smell.
* **Predatory Agility.** Proficiency in Stealth.
* **Darkvision.** Darkvision out to 60 feet.
* **Territorial Streak.** Fierce defense of what is theirs, calling on quick speed when needed.

**Types.**

* **Tiger.** A powerful, open-terrain hunter that can knock targets prone with a claw strike and counts as one size larger for carrying capacity.
* **Jaguar.** A lean, stealth-focused hunter that can hide as a Bonus Action and climbs at walking speed; later, it also knocks targets prone with claws.

## Limits

* **Exception.** Feline Ferocity recharges only after the Tabaxi takes a turn without moving.
* **Unknown.** The source does not establish additional Tabaxi types or details about their old presence beyond [[keth-naar]].

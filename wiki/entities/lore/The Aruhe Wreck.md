# The Aruhe Wreck

*A storm splits the Grung fleet carrying [[Calven and Calveno]]'s stolen hundreds south to [[Karath]]. Part of it wrecks on [[Aruhe]] instead, and the island keeps almost everyone who washes up on it.*

## What Happened

In the days after the Session 07 raid, the [[Grung]] fleet carrying all four hundred eighty-seven Calveno captives ran the [[Central Strait]] south toward Karath when a tropical storm out of the east caught the crossing. Two hundred ninety captives rode it out and reached Karath.

The storm drove the rest west, off the charted line: two hulls went down outright with forty-eight captives aboard, and the remaining hundred forty-nine broke apart on [[Aruhe]]'s reefs and beaches with their Grung guardsmen. Hulls stove in on the coral. Whatever didn't sink outright scattered across the sand in pieces.

## Cause

The storm, nothing more. It came up fast and hard enough that a captive riding it out remembered the sea itself as gleeful. That is a survivor's memory of weather that nearly killed him. Nothing behind the storm was watching or willing it. No hand steered it.

## Damage & Casualties

[[Vethka]] raiding proas went down on Aruhe's reef and beach line, and none survived intact. Forty-eight captives went down with the two hulls lost outright in the crossing, and most of the hundred forty-nine aboard the wrecked Aruhe boats drowned with them, Grung guardsmen and captives alike. Three Grung who made it ashore alive died within hours, killed by the island's corrupted fauna, exactly as [[the Blight]]'s dying curse promises: no Grung who sets foot on Aruhe ever leaves it again. Three Calveno captives rode a broken outrigger strut to shore instead: [[Sandro]], [[Nino]], and an older man, Tomo, who lived through the wreck itself only to break the island's fruit taboo that same first night, eating from a shore bush neither man would touch again. Before dawn, something dragged him into the treeline by both ankles and left nothing behind but the drag marks.

## Consequences

[[Sandro]] and [[Nino]] are still alive as of the wreck's aftermath, sheltering in the broken Vethka's hull on Aruhe's beach and watching the reef gap for sails.

This is likely the "dark hull" [[Nona Black-Jaw]] has been hunting since Session 04. No one has confirmed her count of the missing four hundred eighty-seven against this specific wreck. That confirmation is a thread for actual play. It also sits directly across the path of [[Perrin's private Aruhe survey commission]]. Whoever eventually lands on that beach to sound the harbour and survey the terraces is the first outsider positioned to find what's left of these survivors.

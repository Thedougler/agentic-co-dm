# Campaign Timeline

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** This page orders the record of [[shattered-sea]] history from the Antheri's height through Session 01. It is a chronology, not a narrative replacement for each event's own page.

**Why it matters.** The timeline shows which causes precede the party's current dangers and keeps the Antheri, Pearl, Maw, and character histories in the same sequence.

**Scope.** The record runs from the Antheri's height through Session 01. Later events belong on their session and event pages.

## Current Truth

The dates and events below are the campaign's established chronological index. The two unresolved threads at its final entry are [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]'s pursuit by [[simone]]'s hunters and the combined pressure of [[Auralis]]'s waking and Umberlee's unresolved wrath against [[delmar-fisk]].

### Limits

* **Exception.** An event's blow-by-blow belongs on that event's own page; this page preserves order and consequence.
* **Unknown to the world.** The timeline does not establish every event between its dated entries.
* **Impossible or forbidden.** The chronology does not predict a required future sequence.

## At the Table

* **Players notice.** Dates, wrecks, migrations, founding acts, and named survivors recur as clues to why the present looks as it does.
* **This explains.** How the Antheri, the Maw, the Pearl, the Crown Islands, the Midchain, and the party's opening situation connect.
* **This enables.** The DM can place a discovery or rumor by locating its cause and consequence in the existing chronology.
* **This warns of.** The timeline ends with active pursuits and the awakened Maw danger, not with a settled world.
* **Relevant now.** Session 01 begins after the Pearl theft, the fleet's destruction, and the party's separation across the sea.

## History

**The Age of the Antheri.**

- **-2000 DR**, [[Antheri|The Antheri]] build into the western wall of [[drowned-maw]], expanding toward the [[elemental-plane-of-water]]. [[Auralis]] first appears during this age.
- **-495 DR**, The Antheri vanish. The planar breach widens beyond what the Antheri city can survive.

**The Shattered Sea Takes Shape.**

- **895 DR**, [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie]] start their Kensei order at [[high-eyrie]]. They oppose [[Umberlee]]'s [[waveservants]].
- **1195 DR**, [[dravosi-crown|The Dravosi Crown]] arrives. They claim [[crown-islands|the Crown Islands]] and found [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]].
- **1210 DR**, [[tessarine-concordat|The Tessarine Concordat]] arrives. They form the Seven Houses of [[calven-and-calveno]].
- **1240 DR**, [[the-passage]] breaks away from [[Warren|the Warren]]. Its people flee colonial pressure.
- **1295 DR**, The Sentinels of the Eyrie begin watching The Drowned Maw. They never stop.
- **1385 DR**, The Spellplague closes the northern crossings for a generation. [[verdant-scatter|The Scatter]] learns to stand alone. This pattern endures.
- **1487 DR**, The Second Sundering ends and northern shipping resumes. Current charters begin in the years that follow.

**The Years Before.**

- **1488 DR**, [[catarina-davirelli]] salvages Antheri components from [[Shelfworks|the Shelfworks]]. The discovery sparks the Goldrush. She returns to Calveno and opens her own workshop.
- **1490 DR**, [[beaumont-sel]] buys Antheri armor from Catarina's workshop to repair his shell.
- **1493 DR**, High Eyrie banishes [[crissdalynn-khinriss]], age 14. She boards the [[red-lady]] with [[delmar-fisk]] as crew.
- **1494 DR**, [[jean-claude-tabarnack]] flees the [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]] after [[pell]] dies in reprisal.
- **1495 DR**, Simone rises to warlord-tier status among the [[Grung]] and assembles an elite unit to hunt Jean-Claude down.

**Final Days Before Session 01.**

- **Day -35 (approx.)**, [[Admiral Fisk]] assembles his five-ship fleet ([[red-lady]] flagship, [[the-narrow]], [[Heft]], [[fernen]], [[Loud Argument]]) to steal the [[pearl-of-souls]] from Umberlee's shrine on [[vel-orn]].
- **Day -6**, The fleet steals the Pearl without alerting the [[waveservants]].
- **Day -5**, Umberlee strikes the fleet over The Drowned Maw. All five ships sink. The Pearl's signal pulls [[Leviathan|The Leviathan]] through the planar fissure from the Elemental Plane of Water. [[Auralis]] wakes at last.
- **Day -4**, The Leviathan sinks the [[Vestra]]. [[perrin-black-jaw]] washes up on [[keth-naar]]. He sneaks aboard the [[Saltwright]].
- **Day -3**, [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont]] finds Delmar and Crissdalynn adrift and gives them free passage west aboard the Saltwright.
- **Day -1**, [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] is one island ahead of [[simone]]'s hunters in [[Midchain|the Midchain]], four months after his escape.

**Session 01.**

- Jean-Claude leaps aboard the Saltwright. The [[uncertainty|HCS Surety]] under [[barnaby-rook]] flags it for inspection. The hold ambush, weather-deck fight, [[geoffrey-draves]]'s defection, and the gangplank standoff play out in full on [[journal/sessions/shattered-sea/01/session-01-recap|Session 01's recap]].

## If This Is Changing

* **Current pressure.** Later sessions add events beyond this index's final entry.
* **Actors.** The party, Jean-Claude's hunters, Auralis, and Umberlee's agents continue moving after Session 01.
* **Their aims.** Their goals are recorded on their owner pages and later session pages.
* **Without interference.** The chronology remains a stable index while new events append after Session 01.
* **Visible sign.** A later session page or canon event adds a dated entry beyond the current endpoint.

## Consequences

* **Because this is true,** a new discovery can be checked against the causes and dates recorded here.
* **If exposed,** contradictory dates would force a continuity review of the affected event pages.
* **If disproved,** the campaign loses a reliable shared order for its history.
* **If changed,** every dependent event page would need its date and causal references reviewed.
* **If exploited,** the party can use old causes, surviving witnesses, and locations as routes toward present answers.

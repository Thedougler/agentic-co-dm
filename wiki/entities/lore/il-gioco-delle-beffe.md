# Il Gioco delle Beffe

*Calveno's five-day prank festival, where teams scheme against willing marks for bragging rights and a bronze medallion.*

## The Culture

Teams design elaborate public hoaxes and execute them on willing marks across five days in [[calven-and-calveno]]. [[shepherd-grigori]] left the crew of the [[Uncertainty]] to attend festival business as soon as the ship reached [[calven-and-calveno]], recorded in [[journal/sessions/shattered-sea/05/Session-05-Recap|S05 Recap]].

Registration is tomorrow morning from wherever the crew currently stands in the city. Legendary-scale declarations are already expected this year, so this year's pool should run large.

## Customs & Traditions

Registration opens on Day One, La Scelta the Choosing, at the [[Mercatura]] bridge. Teams declare their mark, category, scale, stake, and symbol there, and the committee notifies marks that same morning. La Finestra, the Window, opens on Day Two and runs through Day Four, the open execution period when a team can strike at any hour and lean on false alarms, sabotage, and misdirection to keep a mark off balance. Day Five is Il Giudizio, the Judgment. Teams present their pranks, marks score the surprise, the committee scores craft and recovery, and the committee announces the winner at sundown.

Entry costs 25 gp at standard scale or 50 gp for a Legendary-scale declaration. Scoring runs on a 20-point scale, split Craft 10, Surprise 5, and Recovery 5. The pool splits 60 percent to first place and 25 percent to second, with the rest going to third. The winning team's leader takes home the Beffa Medallion, a bronze laughing mask, and a mark who was truly fooled often pays a goodwill gift of 10-50 gp on top of the sting. Every team must leave its registered symbol at the scene to score.

On Day One the crew registers a symbol of their own design with the committee: a sigil, a stamp, a wax seal, a colour band, whatever they choose. It has to be theirs. Mechanically the Symbol is the team's proof of authorship. How they leave it at the scene is part of the display: the last thing the mark finds, not an early tip-off. **Beffa doppia** is the festival's own escalation, letting a rival team try to steal a registered symbol and plant it on their own prank. Doing so requires knowing the symbol (possible if they watched registration) and winning a contested Stealth check against the mark's Perception during the Window. The committee adjudicates the result. It has succeeded exactly twice in the festival's history. Both rulings took three days to settle, and one is still disputed.

Running it at the table leans on the city itself. Buildings, contracts, symbols, public witnesses, and recovery problems carry the scene, not festival exposition. Ask each player what their character actually does, and assign checks to match their own best skills. The money at stake is real, but the social capital a win buys is worth more. The Window's three days are the game's real engine. Marks stay nervous for the whole stretch, and teams can strike early, wait, or false-alarm their target first. That uncertainty is what makes the festival worth running instead of narrating. The full mark-selection table, the three-phase skill challenge with its checks, DCs, and complications, rival-team rules, and the prize table all live on [[il-gioco-delle-beffe-running-it|Il Gioco delle Beffe, Running It]].

[[giacomo-moretti]] is the festival's longstanding mark. His twenty-year clean record makes every team suspicious of him. He signs the Accord every year and finds the attempts flattering, and he openly enjoys the festival, though he will never admit it. [[prospero-morsani]] is on the winner plaque with no prank description attached to his entry, and he won't explain why. He is present every year, watching. A [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] factor, [[iacopo-fieschi]], is a first-year mark on the list for the first time, which carries Tessarine implications if he's pranked. The Six-Hour Wake record has stood for forty years. Any team weighing [[giacomo-moretti]] as a mark should plan for exactly that vigilance.

The medallion is worth more than the money: it's social proof that a team was clever enough to deceive someone in public and charming enough to get away with it, proof the Council can't buy. A win means [[iacopo-fieschi]] mentions it to [[cosimo-verantio]], and [[prospero-morsani]] nods at the crew across the Mercatura, a nod that will mean something. Losing honourably still pays: the mark covers the goodwill gift if the prank was good, and Serena Voss wants to buy drinks either way. Getting caught and disqualified costs nothing real. The crowd loves a dramatic disqualification, and it isn't actually bad for reputation. Either way, [[cosimo-verantio]] hears about it eventually.

[[giacomo-moretti]], [[prospero-morsani]], [[iacopo-fieschi]], and [[cosimo-verantio]] are the names worth tracking this year: the mark, the wildcard, the newcomer with Tessarine ties, and the man who'll hear how it went. If Giacomo ends up the mark, the prank plays out at [[Moretti and Sons]]. Morsani, pre-festival, is easiest found at [[The Cabinet of Morsani]]. Every DC, rival-team roll, and scoring table this needs at the table is on [[il-gioco-delle-beffe-running-it|Il Gioco delle Beffe, Running It]].

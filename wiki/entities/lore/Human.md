# Human

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** Humans are physically and culturally varied humanoids whose rules package grants Heroic Inspiration after a Long Rest, one skill proficiency, and two Origin feats.

**Why it matters.** A human character brings broad origins and flexible skills rather than one fixed cultural or physical profile.

**Scope.** These traits apply to Human characters; the setting text also describes humans as widespread and culturally varied.

## Current Truth

Humans are the multiverse's most physically and culturally varied people. They spread from Sigil across the multiverse, are diverse in appearance, and worship many gods. Humans live roughly 80 years.

**Creature Type:** Humanoid

**Size:** Medium (4–7 ft.) or Small (2–4 ft.), chosen at character creation

**Speed:** 30 ft.

**Resourceful.** A Human gains Heroic Inspiration whenever they finish a Long Rest.

**Skillful.** A Human gains proficiency in one skill of their choice.

**Versatile.** A Human gains two Origin feats of their choice.

### Limits

* **Exception.** Size is chosen at character creation and is either Medium or Small.
* **Unknown.** This page does not establish a single human culture, political identity, or appearance.
* **Impossible or forbidden.** No universal human religion, look, or custom is established.

## At the Table

* **Players notice.** Human communities show wide differences in appearance, worship, custom, and origin.
* **This explains.** Human characters can have varied backgrounds without requiring one shared culture.
* **This enables.** A Human character can choose one skill proficiency and two Origin feats to express their history.
* **This warns of.** Treating all humans as one culture misreads the setting's central human variation.
* **Relevant now.** Humans are the Shattered Sea's most demographically dominant and culturally varied people.

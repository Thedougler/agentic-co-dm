# Human

**Creature Type:** Humanoid

**Size:** Medium (4–7 ft.) or Small (2–4 ft.), chosen at character creation

**Speed:** 30 ft.

Humans are the multiverse's most physically and culturally varied people; as a Human, you have these special traits.

**Origins.** Humans spread from Sigil across the multiverse. They are as diverse in appearance as the peoples of Earth and worship many gods.

**Life Span.** Humans live roughly 80 years.

**Resourceful.** You gain [[Heroic Inspiration]] whenever you finish a [[Long Rest]].

**Skillful.** You gain proficiency in one skill of your choice.

**Versatile.** You gain two Origin feats of your choice.

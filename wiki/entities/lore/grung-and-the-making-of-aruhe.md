# Grung and the Making of Aruhe

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** The Grung captured and tortured a druid from a far Midchain island. During the captives' escape toward [[aruhe]], the druid's companion drowned; the crime and its aftermath became part of [[Hinewai]]'s grief-bound law.

**Why it matters.** This history explains why Aruhe's law carries grief and why [[Hinewai]] treats the Grung crime as a living obligation rather than distant history.

**Scope.** The durable facts concern the Grung, the druid and her companion, [[Hinewai]], and [[aruhe]]. Scene-level details from the archived *Half a Mile* draft are not canon.

## Current Truth

The Grung captured and tortured a druid from a far Midchain island. The druid and her companion escaped toward [[aruhe]], but the companion drowned during the escape. The druid carried the body inland, and the aftermath became part of Aruhe's law and Hinewai's grief.

The established record does not make every detail of the capture, escape, or burial authoritative. The archived draft is explicitly fiction; its scene-level details may provide texture, but established facts stay on [[Hinewai]], [[the-unnamed-companion]], and [[aruhe]].

### Limits

* **Exception.** The archived *Half a Mile* draft is source evidence for comparison, not canon.
* **Unknown.** The druid's current identity and the complete wording or practice of Aruhe's law are not established here.
* **Impossible or forbidden.** Do not present the draft's unverified scene sequence as witnessed history.

## At the Table

* **Players notice.** [[Hinewai]] treats the Grung crime and the companion's death as a present obligation, not a story that has ended.
* **This explains.** [[Hinewai]]'s grief-bound law and the hostile history between [[aruhe]] and the [[grung-clans|Grung clans]].
* **This enables.** The party can ask what Aruhe's law requires, compare Grung accounts against Hinewai's memory, or use the history to understand a refusal or demand.
* **This warns of.** Treating the crime as settled history risks violating an obligation that Aruhe still considers active.
* **Relevant now.** The history remains a live relationship between Aruhe, Hinewai, and the Grung clans.

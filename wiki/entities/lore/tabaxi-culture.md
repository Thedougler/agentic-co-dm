# Tabaxi Culture

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** The Cat Lord created the Tabaxi, and their presence in the Shattered Sea is sparse but old; the Keth-Naar community in the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]] has lived beyond easy colonial contact for generations.

**Why it matters.** The lore gives the DM a grounded Tabaxi origin and explains why [[elder-anton]] is a known Tabaxi presence without treating one community as representative of every Tabaxi.

**Scope.** This page covers the Tabaxi presence currently established in the Shattered Sea.

## Current Truth

The Cat Lord created the Tabaxi, who are Medium feline humanoids with a strong territorial streak. Tabaxi presence in the Shattered Sea is sparse but old. The [[keth-naar]] community in the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]] has lived beyond easy colonial contact for generations. [[elder-anton]] is the campaign's known Tabaxi from that community. See [[Tabaxi]] for the people's mechanical traits.

### Limits

- **Exception.** The Keth-Naar community's practices do not establish a universal Tabaxi culture.
- **Unknown.** The page does not establish the Cat Lord's identity, the full history of Keth-Naar, or the location of other Tabaxi communities.
- **Impossible or forbidden.** No cultural prohibition is established here.

## At the Table

- **Players notice.** Tabaxi territorial behavior may appear as a careful attention to boundaries; no specific phrase, symbol, or greeting is established.

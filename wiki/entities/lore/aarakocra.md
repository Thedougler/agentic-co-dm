# Aarakocra

![[aarakocra-lore-image]]

## At a Glance

**Core truth.** Aarakocra are avian Humanoids with a 25-foot walking speed, a 35-foot flying speed, talons, Wind Caller magic, and Keen Sight.

**Why it matters.** Flight and altitude shape how Aarakocra travel, scout, fight, and understand the world.

**Scope.** Aarakocra are rare in the Shattered Sea and are most associated with the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie]] and their small permanent population at [[high-eyrie]].

## Current Truth

Aarakocra track the world by altitude rather than distance. They have feathered bodies, talon-tipped legs, large wings, and heads shaped like parrots, eagles, crows, or other birds. Salt works into their feathers on the exposed summit of [[high-eyrie]].

* **Creature Type and Size.** Humanoid; Medium, about 5–6 feet tall with a wingspan near 10 feet.
* **Speed.** 25 feet walking and 35 feet flying.
* **Flight.** Flight is lost while wearing armor not built for wings.
* **Talons.** A natural unarmed strike that deals slashing damage.
* **Wind Caller.** Once per Long Rest, an Aarakocra can cast *gust of wind* without material components, using a chosen casting ability.
* **Keen Sight.** An Aarakocra has **Advantage** on Wisdom (Perception) checks relying on sight.

### Limits

* **Exception.** Flight is unavailable while wearing armor not built for wings.
* **Unknown.** The source does not establish Aarakocra cultures beyond their rarity and association with the Sentinels of the Eyrie.
* **Impossible or forbidden.** No additional prohibition is established by the source.

# Complete the Maw map pilgrimage

**Status.** Active
**Objective.** Complete [[crissdalynn-khinriss]]'s pilgrimage into the [[drowned-maw]], map the Maw from inside a storm, and return an account.
**Why now.** The Maw has been unruly and dangerous since the wreck, and the Sentinels assigned this as the hardest pilgrimage in Eyrie history.
**Deadline.** None recorded.

## Situation

[[master-kyzil]] assigned [[crissdalynn-khinriss]] the hardest pilgrimage in Sentinel history: map [[drowned-maw]] from inside a storm. The Maw is a deep trench where currents reverse, the water behaves like a boundary, and a map can become partial after a displacement. The Sentinels want their continuous record preserved, but they refuse to dive or interpret what they observe.

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> The Drowned Maw is a deep trench at the edge of the charts. Dark water drags ropes sideways, currents reverse beneath the hull, and a storm wall breaks the horizon into cold spray. The pilgrimage requires a map made from inside that storm, then a return with the account intact.

### What is really happening

The Sentinels are watching and recording the Maw while Crissdalynn's pilgrimage approaches its most consequential threshold. The dormant [[soul-incarnates-watch|Soul Incarnate's Watch]] is tied to her completion of the pilgrimage. If she completes it, the watch becomes active; the exact response is not established on the current page. The Maw's fissure, Pearl signal, and Countless pressure remain wider dangers rather than resolved outcomes of this quest.

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** Crissdalynn returns with a map and account, the Sentinel record gains its pilgrimage entry, and Soul Incarnate's Watch becomes active.
* **If the party fails.** The pilgrimage does not produce a complete account; the Maw remains dangerous and the watch's trigger is not completed.
* **If the party walks away.** The Sentinels continue recording while the Maw's currents, weather, and vessel positions change. The Maw's existing pressures continue without the party.
* **Question.** What does the completed map reveal about the Maw's changing route and boundary?
* **Question.** What does the awakened Soul Incarnate's Watch do when the pilgrimage is complete?

## Complications

* **Pressure.** The Maw's current can reverse after a clock step or Pearl signal, pulling an anchor off line and making an old route only a clue.
* **Tradeoff.** A descent can gain map information while costing position, gear, time, or exposure.
* **Reaction.** The Sentinels restrict access when outsiders threaten their records or demand an interpretation their doctrine forbids.
* **Reversal.** The founding ledgers may have a conscious author, and the completed pilgrimage can bring the dormant watch into play.

## Rewards & consequences

### Promised

No separate payment or material reward is recorded. Master Kyzil's assignment gives the pilgrimage institutional weight, and its completion bears on his reputation.

### Possible

* A completed map and account of the Drowned Maw.
* Access to information held by the Sentinel ledgers.
* A changed relationship with the Sentinels after the order evaluates the account.
* Contact with the consequences attached to Soul Incarnate's Watch.

* [[soul-incarnates-watch|Soul Incarnate's Watch]] changes from dormant to active when the pilgrimage is complete.

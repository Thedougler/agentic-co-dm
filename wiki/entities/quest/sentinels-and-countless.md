## Situation

The [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie]] keep a continuous record of the [[drowned-maw]] from [[high-eyrie]], recording time, weather, position, water, and occurrence without interpreting why. [[Countless]] broke away from the order and now uses hired blades, paid contacts, missing names, and blank ledgers to hunt [[crissdalynn-khinriss]] or another Fate Spinner carrier. [[talon-vantyrus]] seeks the gift tied to the Soul Incarnate transformation technique while [[talon-skarn]] openly contests his control.

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> High Eyrie keeps a record of the Drowned Maw that has not been allowed to break. Elsewhere, names vanish from ledgers and hired blades accept jobs without knowing who ordered them. The same routes that carry witnesses and pilgrims now carry a search for someone who bears the Fate Spinner.

### What is really happening

The Sentinels' refusal to interpret or intervene protects their record but leaves them vulnerable to a breakaway order that acts through covert routes. Countless is searching pilgrim routes, grey markets, and Sentinel waystations for Fate Spinner carriers, while Vantyrus and Skarn contest leadership through the Rule of Two. A related pressure, [[countless-through-the-maw-seal]], keeps the Maw seal tied to the same transformation goal.

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** The party preserves access to the Sentinels' records, protects a carrier, or exposes enough of Countless's order chain to redirect the hunt.
* **If the party fails.** Countless reaches a carrier or gains a path toward the Soul Incarnate transformation technique, while the Sentinel watch loses control of its access or continuity.
* **If the party walks away.** The Sentinels continue recording without intervention, and Countless continues sweeping routes and waystations through one-job contacts.
* **Question.** Can the party protect a carrier without making the next route easier for Countless to find?
* **Question.** Does the Sentinel watch survive by remaining neutral, or does the schism force it to act?

## Complications

* **Pressure.** Countless can replace a lost contact with another one-job agent.
* **Tradeoff.** Protecting a carrier may force the party to move, hide, or expose its route.
* **Reaction.** The Vantyrus-Skarn contest can redirect the hunt without ending it.
* **Reversal.** Breaking one route or contact does not reveal the order chain; it leaves another blank space.

## Rewards & consequences

### Promised

No quest giver or reward is established.

### Possible

The party may gain access to the Sentinels' records, protect Crissdalynn or another carrier, expose Countless's order chain, or exploit the contest between Vantyrus and Skarn.

### Fallout

* If ignored, Countless continues its sweep until another carrier or Sentinel visitor is found.
* If a carrier is captured, Vantyrus gains a path toward the Soul Incarnate transformation technique.
* If the route network breaks, control of Countless can pass through the Rule of Two contest rather than disappearing.

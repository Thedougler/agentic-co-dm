# Long Sight Hunt

**Objective.** Protect [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] and other Fate Spinner carriers from Countless's search, or expose the order chain behind the hunt.
**Why now.** [[Countless]] is actively sweeping pilgrim routes, grey markets, and Sentinel waystations through one-job contacts. Crissdalynn carries the Fate Spinner and is already the named target.
**Deadline.** No fixed date is established. The pressure advances when names disappear, ledgers remain blank, or a Talon job appears near a recent Sentinel visitor.

## Situation

Countless wants the Fate Spinner's gift tied to the Soul Incarnate transformation technique. Its hired blades and paid contacts receive one assignment at a time, so the hunt can move through routes and waystations without producing a reliable ledger. Crissdalynn is the current named carrier in the crossfire.

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> Names vanish from ledgers along the Midchain routes. A hired blade knows one job, not the hand that ordered it. Somewhere among pilgrim roads, grey markets, and Sentinel waystations, the search is narrowing on a carrier who cannot let the Spinner be found.

### What is really happening

Countless is sweeping routes and waystations for Crissdalynn or another Fate Spinner carrier. Vantyrus wants the gift tied to the Soul Incarnate transformation technique. The order's covert structure is both its strength and its weakness: agents know little, but the missing names and blank ledgers mark where the hunt has passed.

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** Crissdalynn stays out of Countless's hands, or the party exposes enough of the order chain to redirect the hunt.
* **If the party fails.** Countless reaches the carrier and gives Vantyrus a path toward the Soul Incarnate transformation technique.
* **If the party walks away.** The Long Sight Hunt continues through routes, grey markets, and Sentinel waystations.
* **Question.** Can the party protect a carrier without making the next route easier for Countless to find?
* **Question.** How much of Countless's order chain can one-job contacts reveal?

## Complications

* **Pressure.** Countless can replace a lost contact with another one-job agent.
* **Tradeoff.** Protecting a carrier may force the party to move, hide, or expose its own route.
* **Reaction.** Vantyrus's internal contest with Skarn can redirect the hunt without ending it.
* **Reversal.** Breaking one route or contact does not reveal the order chain; it leaves another blank space.

## Rewards & consequences

### Promised

* No reward or quest giver is established.

### Possible

* Protect Crissdalynn or another Fate Spinner carrier.
* Expose Countless's order chain through missing names, agents, or route pressure.
* Use the Skarn-Vantyrus schism to redirect the hunt.

### Fallout

* If ignored, Countless continues its sweep until another carrier or Sentinel visitor is found.
* If the carrier is captured, Vantyrus gains a path toward the Soul Incarnate transformation technique.
* If the route network breaks, control of Countless can pass through the Rule of Two contest rather than disappearing.

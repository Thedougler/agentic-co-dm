## Situation

[[corvin-ashe]] has whipped Penance Bar against a woods coven. Demons come because of his lies. [[sula-marn]] tries to hold the peace, while [[halda-wren]] can speak to what is happening at Sandy Grove. [[sorrowbell]] is an empty Tyr box once the demon walks in.

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> Penance Bar is marching on its last hallowed grove. The crowd believes the woods coven is the danger, but the town's fear has already drawn something worse. You have until sundown to put the truth in front of the mob or reach the grove before the fire does.

### What is really happening

Ashe's fearmongering is drawing demons toward the town and grove. [[sula-marn]] is trying to hold the peace. The source establishes a devil-touched priest remains if the mob accepts the truth, but does not establish the confrontation's final outcome.

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** The mob breaks before burning the grove, and Ashe's role becomes visible.
* **If the party fails.** The grove burns and the demon threat gains the situation's momentum.
* **If the party walks away.** The mob reaches the grove without the party's intervention.
* **Question.** Can the mob accept the truth before it acts?
* **Question.** What happens to Ashe and the devil-touched priest when the lies are exposed?

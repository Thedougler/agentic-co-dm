# The Red Wake

*[[Umberlee]] registers [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s first crossing away from [[drowned-maw]], one week after commanding him through [[Branca]] to retrieve the [[pearl-of-souls]]. Her response escalates across the [[Midchain]] through weather, [[Dead Lady]] glimpses, and boats taken whole in the party's wake.*

## Actors

- [[Umberlee]]: wants the Pearl retrieved. Cannot kill Delmar without losing the captains' souls into the Maw. Cannot retrieve it herself (the Maw's water is elemental, beyond her claim). Works through weather, the Dead Lady, and the [[Waveservants]]. Her frustration at a mortal defying her is immense. Her options are narrow.
- The [[Dead Lady]] is Umberlee's instrument, the raised *[[red-lady]]*. It takes boats whole behind the party's route while remaining beyond the party's reach, heralded by supernatural fog echoing the fleet's destruction. Always knows where Delmar is. Exists to compel, not kill.
- Waveservants, Umberlee's clergy, shift from transactional neutrality to pointed attention while Delmar's debt remains unpaid. A [[Kalowe#Waveservant shrine|Waveservant Shrine]] recognises Delmar before he speaks.
- [[chain-council]]: commissioned the original Pearl theft through [[fisks-fleet]]. Their exposure to divine debt depends on whether a Waveservant Shrine learns their role. Currently raising [[Kalowe]] shrine tribute to buy silence.
- [[delmar-fisk]]: carries the debt. Captains' voices ride in his mind. His defiance (sailing the Midchain instead of heading east) triggers Umberlee's escalation.

## Beat Chart

### Dramatic Question

How can a mortal defy a god?

### Player Gravity

- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]: the Pearl debt and the five captains' voices. The fleet he lost to Umberlee's wrath keeps the pressure personal.
- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]: served the *Red Lady* twenty-two months. When the Dead Lady comes close enough to show faces, she recognises the drowned crew.

### Current State

- Delmar departed [[La Vasca]] heading into the Midchain, not toward [[drowned-maw]].
- One week since Umberlee commanded retrieval through [[Branca]] at the [[calven-and-calveno]] shrine.
- Three weeks since the Pearl theft and the fleet's destruction.
- The Dead Lady is active behind the party's route, taking boats on lanes they have sailed.
- Dock talk about boats found empty, with holds dumped and crews gone, circulates at Midchain ports. No one connects the "taken whole" pattern to Delmar yet.

### Active Beat

None. The first hint drops as an aside in episode 009.

### Beat Spine

Candidate beats are trigger-gated. The DM creates beat pages when an episode's prep calls for them.

- **[[taken-whole|Rumour]] heard at port.** e09. A named NPC mentions a boat found empty with holds dumped and crew gone. The party's first contact with the "taken whole" pattern.
- **Weather turns** in a later episode, bringing storms and currents from the Maw's direction. The weather remains persistent and season-inappropriate, hitting the whole crew.
- **First glimpse** comes in a later episode, when Delmar catches a red hull on the horizon at dawn or dusk, familiar lines, gone when he looks again. The crew sees nothing.
- **Fog survivor's account.** Mid-build. A survivor describes supernatural fog matching what took the *Red Lady*. Delmar recognises the description. The geographic pattern, attacks tracking the party's route, becomes discoverable.
- **Dead Lady visible** arrives late in the build, when the whole crew sees her holding station behind them through supernatural fog at her bow. Attacks in the party's wake stop being dock talk.
- **Captains erupt** at the peak, when Delmar sees faces on the Dead Lady's deck. The captains in his mind recognise their own drowned sailors. [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] recognises them too.

### Live Branches

Umberlee escalates until Delmar responds. The party's response (compliance, negotiation, destruction, or continued defiance) creates the branch, and that branch belongs to a future situation.

### Climax Readiness

- Question: ready. How can a mortal defy a god?
- Investment: ready. Delmar's debt, Crissdalynn's recognition, the crew's unease.
- Opposition: ready. Umberlee's power expressed through weather, the Dead Lady, and the Waveservants.
- Understanding: missing. The crew has not connected the "taken whole" pattern to Delmar's debt.
- Leverage: missing. Delmar has bargaining power (Umberlee needs him alive, cannot self-retrieve) but has not worked this out.
- Stakes: ready. The Pearl debt is an open divine claim. Ignoring it costs boats and lives.
- Possibility: ready. Compliance, negotiation, appeasement, destruction, continued defiance.
- Convergence: missing. The Dead Lady has not confronted the party directly. The captains have not recognised the crew.

### Unused Possibilities

- If the party heads toward The Drowned Maw, Umberlee keeps the Dead Lady on the horizon. Weather eases and the clock pauses, returning if progress stops.
- An offering and deed of service to the Waveservants buys a deadline extension but does not reverse the escalation.
- Destroying the Dead Lady disperses her for 1d4 weeks. Umberlee reconstitutes. The clock does not reset.
- If the Waveservants at a shrine discover that the Chain Council commissioned the theft, the divine debt widens to include the Council.

# Pearl signal across the boundary

**Status.** Active
**Objective.** Stop the [[pearl-of-souls]] signal across the Maw boundary, or decide what may keep answering it.
**Why now.** The [[Leviathan]] already followed the signal. [[Auralis]] woke when it crossed. Something larger still presses.
**Deadline.** The next fissure answer. Delay keeps the call live.

## Situation

The Pearl rests with the wreck in [[drowned-maw]] and broadcasts into the [[elemental-plane-of-water]]. That call is how the Leviathan found the fissure. Recovering the Pearl stops the signal and renders the fissure ordinary again. Left in place, the signal keeps calling.

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> The Pearl went down with the fleet. The Maw is a hole in the trench floor. Things have come through it, and the wreck is still down there.

### What is really happening

The Maw Entanglement is a local puncture in the Border Ethereal. [[Antheri]] breach evidence may explain the pressure the signal rides. Salvage at [[Shelfworks]] already points attention back toward the trench. What has come through so far is the smallest of what wants in.

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** The signal stops. The fissure becomes ordinary water again.
* **If the party fails.** The call continues and draws a larger answer to the next opening.
* **If the party walks away.** The Leviathan's path remains. Auralis stays awake to a live crossing.
* **Question.** What answers next if the Pearl stays in the Maw.
* **Question.** Whether Antheri evidence is a cause, a key, or a competing claim.

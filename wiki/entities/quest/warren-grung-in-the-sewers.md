## Situation

The party followed Nona Black-Jaw into the Warren sewers after six Grung crossed the Strait and prepared powder for an attack during Calveno's festival. They burned a powder boat, captured [[felix-aho|Felix Aho]], and learned that the operation used underground powder scaffolds, purple-caste garrisons, red-caste leaders, and ships carrying the powder. The next operation was in the Calveno sewer magazines, where the party later destroyed Magazine Beta and reached a live ritual in Room 8.

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> Six Grung have come through the Warren sewers with black powder, and the festival crowds will be under threat in two days. A powder boat burns below the settlement while Felix Aho, a captured green-caste laborer, warns that more powder and more ships are involved. The sewers lead toward Calveno's magazines, where the attack can still be stopped.

### What is really happening

The Grung operation was a coordinated bombing campaign. Powder moved by ship into underground stores, with purple-caste garrisons and red-caste leaders holding the sites. Felix Aho was a frightened laborer rather than a leader. The party's Warren action destroyed one powder boat and exposed the broader network; the later Calveno magazine raid destroyed Magazine Beta and left a live ritual in Room 8 under Solange Barret.

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** The Warren powder boat and Calveno magazine caches remain destroyed, and the festival bombing operation loses its known local supply route.
* **If the party fails.** The remaining Grung stores and the ritual in Room 8 can support attacks under the festival crowds.
* **If the party walks away.** The operation continues through the remaining magazine and shipping network; Felix remains under Nona's protection.
* **Question.** What was Solange Barret's live ritual meant to accomplish?
* **Question.** Which Grung leaders and ships remain beyond the destroyed Calveno caches?

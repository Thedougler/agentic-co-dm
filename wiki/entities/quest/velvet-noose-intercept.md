## Situation

The [[Velvet Noose]] is a named hull sighted near the Central Strait, where Crown captains break from their normal patrol lines when it or [[glass-debt]] appears. [[Mave Sorn]] carries the Magistrate's Warding Chip during this encounter. The available records name the intercept and its loot hook but do not establish a complete location, crew roster, or opening action.

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> A Crown-sensitive hull has been sighted near the Central Strait. The *Velvet Noose* is involved, and someone aboard carries a small gilded chip that stays warm after it leaves the hand. The route is open water with no nearby shore if a ship comes to grief.

### What is really happening

Mave Sorn is an information broker, not a prize-taker. She keeps the chip on her person because it blocks one targeted divination per day, and she wants confirmation that the Crown's irregularity is supernatural so she can decide what to sell and to whom. The chip was deliberately removed from the Velvet Noose's Wardstone Figurehead; the source does not establish who removed it or why.

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** The party establishes control of the encounter and can secure, trade for, or learn from the Warding Chip.
* **If the party fails.** Mave keeps the chip and the information around it remains in her ledger, while the Velvet Noose stays a Crown-sensitive hull in the Strait.
* **If the party walks away.** The intercept remains unresolved and Mave eventually files the supernatural irregularity for a buyer.
* **Question.** Does the party treat Mave as an enemy, a negotiating information broker, or a source of the chip?
* **Question.** Who learns that the Wardstone protection has been split between the ship and the chip?

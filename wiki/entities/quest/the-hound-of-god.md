# The Hound of God

*The Dravosi Crown keeps a man in reserve for heresy. He has already decided to act.*

## Actors

- [[aleksander-malone]] wants [[shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] dead. He is already pursuing him on his own authority and has partial knowledge of the trail: the ship and the port, but not yet the crew. He pronounces what he has learned aloud with scripture-cadenced certainty. He does not interrogate or negotiate.

## Connections

- [[aleksander-malone]] covers the NPC page, statblock, Front clock, and relationships.
- [[shepherd-grigori]] covers the quarry's own page, CR 19 [[Hierarch]] statblock, and the [[khlysty-the-flock|Khlysty]] thread he advances.
- [[dravosi-crown]] holds the institutional authority that keeps Malone on a long leash. The leash is long by design: [[sarns-landing]] showed the Crown what happens when the leash is short.
- [[Blackrule]] is the chapterhouse where Malone returns between pursuits.
- [[perrin-black-jaw]] carries the dormant heresy thread. "Answer the Next Confirmed Heresy" waits on the Crown confirming his unwitting pact with [[Auralis]].

## Beat Chart

### Dramatic Question

What lengths will the Crown go to in pursuit of their goals?

### Player Gravity

- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] is on record as the fleet's admiral, a name the Crown could weigh. The [[Uncertainty]] IS the captured Dravosi cutter, and exposure is existential.
- [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] has a dormant thread that waits on the Crown confirming his pact as heresy. Standing on a deck under an inquisitor's read is standing next to that trigger.
- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] and [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] sailed with Grigori and dropped him in [[calven-and-calveno]]. A direct question about the Shepherd is a direct question about what they did.

### Current State

- Malone is actively hunting Grigori through [[Midchain]] noble courts (The Hound of God, 0/4 fast clock).
- He has pieces of Grigori's trail, including the port and the ship class, but has not connected them to the Uncertainty's crew.
- The crew transported Grigori and dropped him in Calveno. Malone does not know this yet.
- The crew fears the Dravosi connection to the [[hcs-surety|Surety]] theft and the death of [[capn-gorgeous|Rupert Knighton's]] heir. Malone does not care about ships or heirs. He pursues heretics, not thieves.

### Active Beat

The Inspection

### Beat Spine

- The Inspection. Knight Squadron hull crosses the Uncertainty's bow, day 5 main channel; Malone boards and asks about Grigori

### Live Branches

Single direction until the Grigori fork forces a choice. The crew may side with the Shepherd or help kill him. A third option is to walk away, which lets Malone arrive at Grigori alone. What he does with that is not gentle. The fork comes through the [[khlysty-the-flock|Khlysty]] thread or Malone's own demand, whichever appears first. Either transforms the situation.

### Climax Readiness

- **Question:** ready
- **Investment:** ready (Surety exposure, Perrin's pact, Grigori transport)
- **Opposition:** ready (CR 6 zealot with Crown backing, ecclesiastical authority to detain)
- **Understanding:** missing (the crew has not met Malone yet)
- **Leverage:** missing (Malone has no handler and no weakness the crew can press; his single-mindedness limits him but gives them nothing to work with)
- **Stakes:** ready (heresy accusation, exposure of the Surety theft, combat)
- **Possibility:** ready (ally, oppose, avoid, lie, each with different consequences)
- **Convergence:** missing (Malone's trail has not yet reached the crew specifically)

### Unused Possibilities

- A beat where the crew encounters evidence of Malone's work, such as a port he passed through or a heretic he judged, without meeting him directly. This shows the machine.
- A beat where Malone pronounces a trail fact that identifies the crew's ship class or route, and the crew is present to hear it.
- A beat where Malone confronts the crew directly: "Ye lied to a servant of Tyr, and that is a different conversation."

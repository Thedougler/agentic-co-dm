## Situation

[[marrow]] currently keeps the crate on his shelf at [[marrows|Marrow's]], inside [[warren|the Warren]]. It contains a Vretek/Brol letter, silver, and a signet ring. [[serin-haut]] killed courier Dren while he carried it and now watches the Marrows canal, changing hiding places while she waits for someone to claim the crate.

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> A crate sits on Marrow's shelf in the Warren. It came through the hands of a dead courier and contains a letter marked Vretek/Brol, silver, and a signet ring. Somewhere above the canal, someone is watching for whoever comes looking.

### What is really happening

Serin Haut is a [[dravosi-crown]] spy working in Calveno as a Tessarine trader. She killed Dren and is watching the canal to identify whoever comes for the crate. Marrow is holding the cargo but does not automatically explain his decisions; reaching him normally requires a Kin introduction and his own judgment.

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** The party secures access to the crate and can preserve, interpret, or use its contents before Serin takes control of the situation.
* **If the party fails.** Serin identifies the claimant or reaches the crate first, reducing the party's control over the evidence.
* **If the party walks away.** Serin continues watching the canal and changing hiding places, while Marrow remains the crate's known holder.
* **Question.** Who was the crate meant for, and what does the Vretek/Brol letter connect?
* **Question.** Does the party treat Marrow's custody as protection, leverage, or an obstacle?

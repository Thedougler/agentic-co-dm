## Situation

The Warren is a Rattkin settlement and deep [[passage]] anchor beneath Calveno's Le Paludi. Bela, a 22-year-old Rattkin scout on her first solo assignment, is alive but exhausted in the flooded sub-chamber, and the Water Weird keeps her there. The cache sits in the same chamber.

## Stakes

- **If the party succeeds.** Bela returns to the Warren with whatever part of the cache the party recovers, and the Warren regains a scout and a witness.
- **If the party fails or walks away.** Bela stays trapped, the Water Weird keeps the chamber, and the rescue window narrows.

## Leads

- **Warren elders and [[passage]] guides** know the way to the flooded sub-chamber → [[Warren]]
- **Wet footprints and route marks** run through the Warren toward the chamber → [[Warren]]
- **The two potions of water breathing** in the cache make the flooded approach and the way out survivable once reached → [[water-weird|Water Weird]]

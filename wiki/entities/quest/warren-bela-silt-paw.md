## Situation

The Warren is a Rattkin settlement and deep [[passage]] anchor beneath Calveno's Le Paludi. [[bela-silt-paw|Bela Silt-Paw]], a 22-year-old Rattkin scout on her first solo assignment, is trapped in a flooded sub-chamber beneath the settlement. A Water Weird holds the chamber. The situation is active even though no accepted rescue plan or exact chamber route is established.

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> Beneath the Warren, a young scout named Bela Silt-Paw is trapped in a flooded chamber. Water holds the room, and something in that water has kept her from leaving. She has been down there for days. Anyone who goes after her must find the chamber, survive the flood, and get her past the thing that keeps it.

### What is really happening

Bela is alive but exhausted in the flooded sub-chamber. The Water Weird holds her there. The chamber also contains 340 gp, two potions of water breathing, and a Faste dispatch worth approximately 500 gp. The Water Weird's motive and the exact approach to the chamber are not established.

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** Bela is removed from the flooded chamber and can return to the Warren, carrying whatever part of the cache remains recoverable.
* **If the party fails.** Bela remains trapped, and the source clock continues without a successful rescue.
* **If the party walks away.** The Water Weird continues holding the chamber and Bela's rescue window narrows.
* **Question.** Can the party reach the flooded sub-chamber without losing the route?
* **Question.** What does the Water Weird want, and can that want change the rescue?

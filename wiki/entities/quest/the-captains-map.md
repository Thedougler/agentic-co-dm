# The Captain's Map

*Offered by [[sera-maddock|Sera Maddock]] for 200 gp and passage out of [[kalowe]] to a healer city, before her three-day estimate expires.*

**The truth.** Sera Maddock is dying of lung sickness in the back room of [[grimaldis-dispensary|Grimaldi's Dispensary]]. She offers the party the bearing to the cache her crew buried on [[orak]] in exchange for 200 gp and passage to a healer city. The bearing is recorded as a tattooed half-chart on her left forearm; the other half was burned by Sera, and she does not know what currently occupies the cache cave.

**If the party walks away.** Sera remains in [[grimaldis-dispensary|Grimaldi's Dispensary]] without suitable treatment. [[ilaria-grimaldi|Ilaria Grimaldi]]'s estimate gives her roughly three days; after that, Sera dies before she can give anyone the bearing, and the party loses her firsthand route to the [[orak]] cache.

**Leads.**
- [ ] Ask [[ilaria-grimaldi|Ilaria Grimaldi]] about the dying privateer in the dispensary's back room; Ilaria can reveal Sera's condition and the existence of this deal.
- [ ] Speak with [[sera-maddock|Sera Maddock]] directly; she can show the tattooed chart and state the exchange required for the bearing.

**Stakes.** Success gets Sera toward a healer city and gives the party a route to the [[orak]] cache. Failure or refusal leaves Sera dying in [[kalowe]] and ends the deal when her three-day estimate runs out.

**Rewards.** The party can earn the tattooed bearing to Sera's [[orak]] cache and the opportunity to recover what remains there.

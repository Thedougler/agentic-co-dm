## Situation

The Sentinels' pressure is distorted by a naming mistake: [[master-kyzil]] reads [[osset]], Vantyrus's original name, as a third actor rather than as an identity connected to [[Talon Vantyrus]]. Talon currently leads [[Countless|the Countless]] under that alias. The party can investigate the identity and choose what to expose, protect, or exploit; the page does not prescribe that choice.

## What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> The name Osset keeps surfacing as if it belongs to someone separate from Talon Vantyrus. Master Kyzil treats that name as evidence of another actor, while the Countless move under Talon's name. The records do not agree on how many people the names represent.

## What is really happening

Osset is Vantyrus's original name, not a confirmed third actor. [[master-kyzil]]'s mistake creates the schism pressure; [[Talon Vantyrus]] leads [[Countless|the Countless]] under the alias. The page establishes no further current move or deadline.

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** The Sentinels can act on a clearer account of the names, if the party chooses to share it.
* **If the party fails.** Kyzil's mistaken third-actor reading continues to distort the Sentinels' response.
* **If the party walks away.** The naming confusion remains active while Talon leads the Countless under that alias.
* **Question.** What does the party do with the proof that Osset is Vantyrus's original name?
* **Question.** Does the Sentinels' response change when the identity is clarified?

# Rule of Two

**Objective.** Keep [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] from becoming leverage in the contest between [[talon-skarn|Skarn]] and [[talon-vantyrus|Vantyrus]], or exploit the contest before one Talon takes control of [[Countless]].
**Why now.** Skarn openly schemes to overthrow Vantyrus, and Vantyrus treats the attempt as a standing threat rather than a secret. Countless's routes and one-job contacts can bring that contest to the party.
**Deadline.** No fixed date is established. The pressure advances when a Talon cuts the other off, when Countless's route network shifts, or when the hunt for Crissdalynn reaches a new contact.

## Situation

[[Countless]] is led by Vantyrus, who wants the Fate Spinner's gift tied to the Soul Incarnate transformation technique. Skarn, Vantyrus's apprentice, openly schemes to overthrow him. Crissdalynn is in the crossfire because Countless's hunt targets Fate Spinner carriers and uses one-job agents who know only their immediate assignment.

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> Two Talons claim the same order. One wants the master gone; the other treats the apprentice as a standing threat. Around them, names disappear from ledgers and paid contacts carry one job at a time. Crissdalynn is the target the contest can turn into a weapon.

### What is really happening

Vantyrus leads Countless through covert routes, hired blades, paid contacts, and missing records. Skarn is trying to take control through the Rule of Two contest. The order's larger active agenda is to find Crissdalynn or another Fate Spinner carrier; whoever wins the internal contest inherits the route network and its hunt.

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** Crissdalynn stays out of Countless's hands, or the party exposes enough of the order chain to redirect or break the hunt.
* **If the party fails.** Countless reaches the carrier and gives Vantyrus a path toward the Soul Incarnate transformation technique.
* **If the party walks away.** Countless continues sweeping pilgrim routes, grey markets, and Sentinel waystations through one-job contacts.
* **Question.** Which Talon, if either, can the party pressure without handing Countless the advantage?
* **Question.** Can the party protect Crissdalynn while keeping the Fate Spinner's route hidden?

## Complications

* **Pressure.** Countless can redirect a failed contact through another one-job agent without exposing its central order.
* **Tradeoff.** Helping one Talon against the other may preserve Crissdalynn in the short term while handing Countless a stronger leader.
* **Reaction.** Vantyrus treats Skarn's overthrow attempt as a standing threat; Skarn has no reason to stop after one failed approach.
* **Reversal.** Breaking a route or exposing a contact does not reveal the order chain; it can simply create another blank space in the records.

## Rewards & consequences

### Promised

* No reward or quest giver is established.

### Possible

* Protect Crissdalynn from the carrier hunt.
* Expose Countless's order chain or exploit the Talon contest.
* Redirect a one-job contact away from the current target.

### Fallout

* If ignored, Countless continues the hunt through pilgrim routes, grey markets, and Sentinel waystations.
* If Vantyrus or the route network falls, control passes to whichever Talon wins the Rule of Two contest.
* Missing names and blank ledgers remain as evidence even if one Talon is removed.

## Situation

Tribute and the [[pearl-of-souls]] may be holding the [[drowned-maw]] fissure shut. The [[Antheri]] breach is a possible source of the pressure the tribute system contains. The Border Ethereal separates the Inner Planes universally; the Maw is a local puncture. Something larger than the known threats may be scraping at the far side.

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> The Maw is not merely a dangerous stretch of water. A fissure there is under pressure, and the tribute paid around it may be doing more than appeasing a sea power. The [[pearl-of-souls]] is part of the question, but no one can yet say whether it is a lock, a key, or only a witness.

### What is really happening

The sources establish a local puncture and pressure beyond it, but not the mechanism. Tribute and the Pearl may be holding the fissure shut; the larger presence beyond it remains unknown. Whether the [[Antheri]] breach is the pressure source is also unresolved.

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** The party identifies or changes the force holding the fissure shut before the pressure becomes irreversible.
* **If the party fails.** The fissure's pressure continues to build or the tribute system changes without the party's understanding.
* **If the party walks away.** Tribute, the Pearl, and the Maw continue interacting without intervention; the larger presence remains a live threat.
* **Question.** Is tribute actually holding the fissure shut?
* **Question.** What is scraping at the far side, and what does it want?

# Bring the Pearl of Souls to Umberlee

**Status.** Active
**Objective.** Return the [[pearl-of-souls]] to [[Umberlee]].
**Why now.** Umberlee named the Pearl as her price. While the Pearl remains below the Maw boundary, the Dead Lady and Delmar still collect.
**Deadline.** None. Pressure continues until someone returns the Pearl or denies Umberlee.

## Situation

[[Umberlee]] named the [[pearl-of-souls]] as the outstanding divine price after [[delmar-fisk]] stole it from [[vel-orn]]. She cannot reach into elemental water to take it back. [[umberlee-branca]] carried the command to bring the pearl, after which Umberlee would talk about the commissioners. Captains of the sunk fleet ride Delmar. The *[[red-lady-dead-lady]]* collects while that debt remains unpaid.

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> Umberlee wants the Pearl. A Calveno Waveservant carried that demand in her name. Delmar lived when the rest of the fleet did not, and the red wreck still works the lanes.

### What is really happening

The wreck of the original Red Lady holds the Pearl at the eastern [[Shelfworks]] site inside [[drowned-maw]], below the planar boundary. Umberlee's claim ends where the water stops tasting like hers. She has not killed Delmar because the captains remain bound to him. Tribute and the [[red-wake]] continue as collection while no one returns the instrument.

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** Umberlee's price is paid. She will talk about the commissioners. Collection-by-hull loses its reason.
* **If the party fails.** The Dead Lady keeps taking cargo and crews. Delmar remains the bound survivor.
* **If the party walks away.** Harbour tribute and the Red Wake continue. The Pearl stays where Umberlee cannot retrieve it.
* **Question.** Who actually delivers the Pearl, and on what terms.
* **Question.** Whether returning the Pearl also changes Maw containment.

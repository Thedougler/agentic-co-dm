# Soul Incarnate's Watch

> [!narration] Narration
> The High Eyrie's old ledgers lie beneath the order's careful copies. Their pages describe a watch that has never quite ended, though no living Sentinel can say what first watched from beneath the stone.

**Status.** Dormant  
**Objective.** Preserve the watch until [[crissdalynn-khinriss]] completes the Maw pilgrimage and the dormant front can trigger.  
**Why now.** The founding documents under [[high-eyrie]] concern [[soul-incarnate]], but current leadership does not know they describe a conscious being.  
**Deadline.** The watch changes when Crissdalynn completes the Maw pilgrimage; timing is unknown.

## Situation

[[soul-incarnates-watch]] is dormant. The Sentinels of the Eyrie maintain founding documents that concern [[soul-incarnate]], but current leadership reads them as tradition rather than evidence of a conscious being. [[crissdalynn-khinriss]]'s Maw pilgrimage is the established trigger for the front to change.

## What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> The High Eyrie's founding records describe a watch older than any living Sentinel. The order still follows the records, but its leaders do not know that the thing named in them is conscious.

The source does not establish what the party has already learned, whether they know Crissdalynn's trigger, or what they will do with the founding documents.

## What is really happening

The Soul Incarnate remains sealed beneath [[high-eyrie|the High Eyrie]] and records rather than acts while its observing state holds. Current Sentinel leadership does not know that the founding documents describe that conscious being. The front's trigger is [[crissdalynn-khinriss]] completing the Maw pilgrimage.

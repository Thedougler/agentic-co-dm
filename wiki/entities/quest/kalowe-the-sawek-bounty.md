# Kalowe — The Sawek Bounty

> [!narration] Kalowe — The Sawek Bounty
> A local bounty points toward a blue hole near [[kalowe]]. Divers have tied cord to the reef to mark the holes they claim, and one of those marked entrances now hides a territorial predator.

*Offered by [[kettil-ormsson|Kettil Ormsson]] for the local bounty, before the next diver checks the marked blue hole.*

**The truth.** A [[sawek]] has claimed one of Kalowe's blue holes as its lair. It waits silently underwater, extends tentacles from the entrance, grapples divers, and drags them into the hole. Kalowe divers mark claimed holes with cord, so the cord is both a warning and a way to locate the lair. Kettil wants the local danger removed without closing the diving route.

**If the party walks away.** The Sawek remains in the marked hole. The next diver who treats the cord as a claim marker rather than a warning is ambushed and dragged underwater. After another loss, Kalowe divers stop using that hole, shifting traffic to the remaining routes and leaving the Sawek's territory uncontested.

**Opposition.** The Sawek wants prey and an undisturbed lair. Kalowe divers want access to the blue holes and will not agree on a single method: some want the creature killed, while others want the hole marked and bypassed. Kettil wants the danger settled before the route closes.

**Leads.**

- [ ] **Read the cord marker.** Ask a Kalowe diver why the cord was tied, then follow the marked reef to the blue-hole entrance → [[kalowe]].
- [ ] **Ask Kettil.** [[kettil-ormsson|Kettil Ormsson]] mentions the local Sawek trouble without drama and points the party toward the divers who lost access to the hole → [[ormssons-fittings|Ormsson's Fittings]].

**Stakes.** Success makes the marked hole usable again and preserves a local diving route. Failure leaves the Sawek in control, costs another diver, and pushes Kalowe traffic toward the remaining holes.

**Rewards.** The party receives the local bounty and earns access to the divers' knowledge of the blue holes. The party can also choose a safer outcome by keeping the hole marked and rerouting traffic instead of killing the Sawek.

## Situation

The Bad Receipt is a Tier 2 armed pirate schooner that works the waters around Kalowe under false identities. Its prize cargo sits in nine crates on a nameless limestone rock near Kalowe. The cache includes a captain, a fisher witness, and a recovery race, but the current source does not identify the captain or establish who now controls the crates.

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> Reweti Kahurangi has noticed Damu's business and can point capable people toward it. Somewhere near Kalowe, nine crates taken as prize cargo sit on a nameless limestone rock. Someone is already racing to recover them.

### What is really happening

The Bad Receipt's prize cargo is separated from the schooner near Kalowe. The ship's crew use false papers, calculated strikes, reef pilots, and hidden channels to protect their operations. The identity of the captain, the fisher witness's account, Damu's connection to the cache, and the current recovery claimant remain unresolved in the available canon.

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** The party reaches the cache and establishes who controls, recovers, or exposes the nine crates.
* **If the party fails.** Another claimant reaches the cache first, and the party loses the immediate opportunity to determine its fate.
* **If the party walks away.** The recovery race continues without them, while The Bad Receipt remains an active operator around Kalowe.
* **Question.** Who has the strongest claim to the cargo when the cache is found?
* **Question.** Does the party treat The Bad Receipt as an enemy, a prize, or a source of deals and forged documents?

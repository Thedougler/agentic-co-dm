# Maw fissure and the tribute system

Tribute and the [[Pearl of Souls]] may be holding the [[Drowned Maw]] fissure shut. The [[Antheri]] breach is a possible source of the pressure the tribute system contains.

## Connections

- [[Drowned Maw]]
- [[Antheri]]
- [[Pearl of Souls]]
- [[Shattered Sea]]

The Border Ethereal is a universal Inner-Plane separator. The Maw is a local puncture. Something larger than [[Leviathan]], [[Ridgeback]], or [[Krakling]] scrapes at the far side. Whether that surfaces later stays open.

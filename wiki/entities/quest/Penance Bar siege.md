# Penance Bar siege

## Summary

Get the truth about [[Corvin Ashe]] to the mob before it burns the last hallowed grove at [[Penance Bar]]. The deadline is sundown and the march.

## Situation

Ashe has whipped the town against a woods coven. Demons come because of his lies. [[Sula Marn]] tries to hold the peace. [[Sorrowbell]] is an empty Tyr box once the demon walks in.

## Stakes

If the party walks away, the grove burns and the island loses its last hallowed ground. If the truth lands in time, the mob breaks and a devil-touched priest remains.

## World in motion

Without interruption the mob reaches the grove. The next move is fire.

## Leads

- Talk to [[Sula Marn]] in town.
- Walk to Sandy Grove and hear [[Halda Wren]].
- Witness the locked confrontation at [[Sorrowbell]] before the mob departs.

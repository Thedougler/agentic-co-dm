## Situation

Countless is a breakaway order pursuing a route toward the Soul Incarnate transformation technique. The current source specifically ties that pursuit to probing the seal at the [[drowned-maw]]. The page does not establish the seal's mechanism, Countless's exact method, or whether the probe has succeeded.

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> The Drowned Maw is not only a dangerous place at sea. A breakaway order called [[Countless]] is looking for a way through its seal, and the attempt is connected to a transformation technique called Soul Incarnate. What the order expects to find beyond the seal is not yet clear.

### What is really happening

[[talon-vantyrus]] leads Countless while pursuing the Soul Incarnate transformation technique. The Maw seal is one live route in that pursuit. The current record establishes the pressure, not a completed breach, the identity of a successful operative, or the final result.

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** Countless loses or changes this route, and the party gains a clearer account of what the Maw seal protects or enables.
* **If the party fails.** Countless keeps the route live and may gain a usable path toward the transformation technique.
* **If the party walks away.** The seal remains under pressure while Countless continues its wider operation without party interference.
* **Question.** What does the Maw seal actually prevent, contain, or conceal?
* **Question.** Can Countless's route be interrupted without handing the advantage to another claimant?

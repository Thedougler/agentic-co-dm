# Yssenmoor's Ancient Challenge

*A duck built like a demigod guards a fog-bound island in the [[Northern Midchain]], challenging every visitor with absurd formality. Nobody has answered in years. The ley line beneath his hallowed ground pulls on the Fate Spinner as the party sails past.*

## Actors

- **[[sir-quackers-the-fowl]].** He wants to protect Yssenmoor and anything weak enough to need it. Ships have stopped answering his challenge. He has started reciting it to empty water and is one silence from stopping the ceremony entirely and withdrawing into the moor.
- **[[The Countless]].** Hired watchers sweep [[Midchain|the Midchain]] for any Sentinel carrying a [[fate-spinner]]. A visible magical reaction near Yssenmoor signals that their network can detect it.

## Connections

- [[fate-spinner]]. The artifact reacts to the ley line running beneath Yssenmoor. Old magic predates the Sentinels' doctrine of non-intervention.
- [[Yssenmoor]]. The island itself is fog-bound and hallowed ground under Quackers' unconscious protection.
- [[The Countless]]. The Long Sight Hunt Front is active and sweeping for the Spinner.
- [[crissdalynn-khinriss]]. She carries the Spinner. [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]] just warned her to hide it.
- [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|The Schism's Third Name]]. This Sentinel thread connects to old power that predates the order's non-intervention doctrine.

## Beat Chart

### Dramatic Question

Will the crew answer the challenge of a guardian nobody has answered in years, and what does his island's old power mean for what Crissdalynn carries?

### Player Gravity

- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]. The Fate Spinner warms as they pass. Kyzil just warned her to hide it. Now it pulls toward something.
- [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]. Old power predates colonial authority and organized faith. The ley line is not his patron, but it recognizes the same deep water.
- [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]. A creature freed from servitude built something important enough to protect despite the cost of freedom. Quackers' story mirrors JC's own departure from the clans.
- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]. A demigod-tier potential ally holds unclaimed territory in [[Midchain|the Midchain]]. Delmar reads the world as fleet and force. Quackers is both.

### Current State

- The *[[Uncertainty]]* sails the Northern Midchain heading south. [[Yssenmoor]] is one of many tightly packed islands they pass.
- Permanent fog sits unmoving over the island in a wind that fills the sails. Sound arrives ahead of its source.
- A voice carries across the water from inside the fog: formal, patient, addressed to no one.
- The [[fate-spinner]] warms in [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn's]] pouch as they draw near.

### Active Beat

None (lifecycle: discoverable).

### Beat Spine

- **The Pull.** The Spinner warms, the fog is visible and wrong, and the voice carries across the water. The crew can investigate or sail past.
- **The Fog.** Landing on Yssenmoor. Sound precedes its source. Fog sits without wind, with bog and reed in every low stretch. [[Stirges]], [[boar]], and [[snakes]] live in the scrub.
- **The Challenge.** Quackers appears and delivers his formal recitation. State your name, your business, and whether you mean to leave this marsh the way you found it. His [[Divine Awareness]] detects lies. Honest answers open conversation. Refusal prompts a second ask, patient and formal, and then he lets them go. He does not fight over ceremony.
- **The Conversation.** If the crew answers plainly, Quackers lowers his guard. It is the first answer he has received in longer than he can recall. If they ask about RTLB, he lights up. RTLB were two gnomes sharing one coat who mastered duomancy. They tamed faeries and rescued the dragon clans. Quackers calls them the greatest wizard he has ever known. He recites every title proudly and returns to the same quiet fact: RTLB cried whenever Quackers came back from dying. Quackers never worked out whether that made the sending worse or better.
- **The Ley Line.** The Spinner reacted to the power beneath Yssenmoor, which predates the Sentinels' current doctrine. The old power passed into Quackers without his knowledge. Crissdalynn carries an artifact from the same old tradition. Neither of them asked for what they hold.

### Live Branches

- **The crew threatens Yssenmoor or its residents** → Quackers defends his ground. CR 18 on hallowed ground with lair actions. The party flees or dies.
- **The crew refuses the challenge or lies** → Quackers lets them go. Quiet disappointment. Another ship that did not answer. He resumes reciting to empty water.
- **The Countless detect the Spinner's reaction** → a watcher reports. The Long Sight Hunt gains a new vector. The report reaches [[talon-vantyrus|Vantyrus]], identifying the Spinner as active in the Northern Midchain.

### Climax Readiness

- **Question.** Ready. Will the crew engage with a demigod they cannot defeat and cannot easily dismiss?
- **Investment.** Ready. The Spinner, the Sentinel thread, JC's mirror, and Delmar's strategic eye.
- **Opposition.** Missing. Quackers is non-violent unless his island or its residents are directly threatened. The tension is social and spiritual, not combative.
- **Understanding.** Missing. The crew does not yet know what Quackers is, what the ley line means, or why the Spinner reacted.
- **Leverage.** Missing. No prior relationship exists. Honesty is the only currency Quackers accepts.
- **Stakes.** Ready. A demigod ally or hazard, plus the Spinner's visibility to the Countless.
- **Possibility.** Ready. Alliance, indifference, hostility, flight, and deeper investigation all remain viable.
- **Convergence.** Missing. The crew must choose to come ashore at Yssenmoor.

### Unused Possibilities

- **RTLB's departure.** Quackers' former master vanished. If the crew poses the right questions, Quackers might share what RTLB left behind besides the island. A hook remains dormant until a future season makes it relevant.
- **Yssenmoor as safe harbor.** Hallowed ground in the Northern Midchain is fog-concealed and guarded by a demigod. If Quackers accepts the crew, they gain a bolt-hole nobody else knows about.
- **The ley line's deeper network.** The line beneath Yssenmoor may connect to something else in [[verdant-scatter|the Scatter]]. This remains dormant until Season 3 or 4 makes it relevant.

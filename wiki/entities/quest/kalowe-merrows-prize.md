## Situation

The merchant sloop *Cormorant* went down three months ago. [[Davu Kamu]], its former first mate, survived and now waits at [[The Flat Water]] with the key to the captain's strongbox. He offers 30% of the salvage, but he has not told prospective helpers that the captain's widow in [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]] has a legal claim to the contents.

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> Davu Kamu survived the wreck of the *Cormorant* and has spent the money that kept him in Kalowe. He has the captain's strongbox key and offers you thirty percent of whatever salvage you recover. The wreck is still out there, and the box has not been brought home.

### What is really happening

Davu is guarded because he is underselling the rubies inside the strongbox and has not disclosed the widow's legal claim. He needs capable people to recover the box, but he also needs someone to say yes before his remaining options run out. The wreck's exact location and current condition are not established in the available source.

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** The strongbox is recovered and the party must decide whether to honor Davu's offer, the widow's claim, or a negotiated division.
* **If the party fails.** Davu remains without salvage and the strongbox stays exposed to loss, discovery, or competing claimants.
* **If the party walks away.** Davu keeps looking for help from [[The Flat Water]], while the widow's legal claim remains unaddressed.
* **Question.** Who receives the strongbox's contents after the wreck is recovered?
* **Question.** Does Davu reveal the rubies' true worth before the party commits?

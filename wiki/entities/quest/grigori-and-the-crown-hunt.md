## Situation

The party is caught between a healer who saved a crewmate and the Crown's pursuit of the Flock's infiltration. The party can protect Grigori, hand him to Malone, or walk away while both forces continue acting.

### What the party knows

> [!narration] Player-facing brief
> Shepherd Grigori is a healer who helped the crew, and Aleksander Malone is hunting him for the Dravosi Crown. The pursuit is moving through the Midchain. The party must decide whether Grigori's help earns protection, whether the Crown's accusation deserves cooperation, or whether neither side is theirs to carry.

### What is really happening

Grigori leads the [[khlysty-the-flock|Flock]] and believes he authored its blood-anchor survival trick. His quiet healing leaves dormant threads in powerful bodies and advances his plan for ascension. Malone has partial knowledge of Grigori's trail and acts on his own authority when the Crown confirms heresy. The party's prior contact with Grigori gives them a choice before Malone connects the trail to them.

## Stakes

* **If the party succeeds.** The party changes who controls Grigori's fate and may expose, interrupt, or protect the Flock's blood-anchor network.
* **If the party fails.** Malone reaches Grigori or Grigori advances his network without the party choosing the terms.
* **If the party walks away.** Malone continues the hunt, while Grigori continues healing heirs and extending dormant threads.
* **Question.** Will the party treat Grigori as a trusted healer, a dangerous infiltrator, or both?
* **Question.** Does Malone learn that the party transported Grigori and dropped him in [[calven-and-calveno]]?

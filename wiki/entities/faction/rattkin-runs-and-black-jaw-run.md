## At the Table

They want the Tangle's elder teachings intact, and they refuse to be priced out of the routes that make those teachings usable.

They offer family Runs, couriers, ships, and community obligations that carry people by coded trust.

They will not replace one authority with another. [[the-taken-314]] is the live case of that limit.

## Current State

Under pressure, the Run moves people first. It asks for proof through coded trust. It also protects the elder lessons that make the route more than transport. [[nona-black-jaw]] is the family face. [[perrin-black-jaw]] is the operative and successor. The crew's rescue of raid survivors ties them to the family and route network.

## Active Agenda

[[the-taken-314]] is the live test of whether the Run can move people without replacing one authority with another. The next move is to keep the rescue route readable while preserving the network's refusal to treat people as cargo.

## Assets

Resources include the [[Warren]], [[Vestra]]'s legacy, courier ties, and family obligations that make the Runs readable to one another.

## People & Structure

- [[nona-black-jaw]] is the family face.
- [[perrin-black-jaw]] is the operative and successor.
- [[passage]] is an ally with overlapping rescue routes.
- [[dravosi-crown]] is a complicated power whose inspection and paperwork threaten the network.

### Chain of Action

**Who decides:** [[nona-black-jaw]] is the family face who decides which routes can open.

**Who interprets those orders:** [[perrin-black-jaw]] turns family obligations and coded trust into a workable route.

**Who actually carries them out:** Family couriers, ships, and allied [[passage]] operators move people.

**If leadership disappears.** The Runs remain readable through family ties, but the network loses its established face and successor.

## Territory & Touchpoints

The network's deepest anchor is [[Warren]]. Its routes connect [[Vestra]], [[tangle]], [[the-black-jaw-run]], and the family courier network across the [[Midchain]].

## Signals & Rumors

- **Visible sign.** A courier asks for coded proof before naming a route or a kitchen.
- **Rumor.** The Runs can move anyone who knows the family obligations, but cannot promise safety from the Crown.
- **Direct signal.** [[nona-black-jaw]] or [[perrin-black-jaw]] asks the party to recover people from the captive route.
- **Quiet clue.** A route that refuses short-term profit is protecting the Tangle's elder lessons.

## History

The Rattkin Runs grew from family ties, the [[Warren]], and [[Vestra]]'s legacy. [[the-black-jaw-run]] is the Calveno family expression of that wider network, now tied to the rescue of raid survivors and the fate of [[the-taken-314]].

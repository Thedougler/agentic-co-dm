# The Passage

> [!narration] Public face
> In Calveno and the ports around it, The Passage is known as a Rattkin network that moves people, cargo, and messages through routes the Crown does not chart. Its people trade in favors, coded marks, and quiet shelter; they do not sell people.

## At the Table

- **They want:** Safe movement for Kin, useful cargo, and information that must avoid colonial records.
- **They offer:** Shelter, route knowledge, discreet transport, introductions, and intelligence.
- **They pressure with:** Access to hidden roads, safe holds, messages, and the knowledge of which names are dangerous in a port.
- **They will not:** Move people as property or operate where [[grung-clans|the Grung Clans]] hold routes.
- **Their tell:** Kin use scratch-notation on bone and ceramic, coded trust marks, and short phrases such as “Any Kin in need can ask.”

## Current State

- **Status quo:** Travelling families and crews move through Runs, while Holds provide caches and shelters in drainage channels, dock supports, wall cavities, old foundations, roof voids, and cisterns.
- **Recent change:** The Warren's anchor Run obtained Grung bombing intelligence from [[felix-aho|Felix Aho]] after Nona called off attacks on Perrin and called in her favor.
- **Pressure:** The Passage must decide whether to keep the threat in shadow channels or warn Calveno in the open.
- **Strength:** It knows hidden crossings, trusted clerks, warning marks, low tunnels, and routes that bypass tolls, patrols, cargo counts, and ledgers.
- **Vulnerability:** The network depends on secrecy, distributed trust, and safe holds that can be exposed by one compromised route or name.
- **Opportunity:** It can use the hidden Overland Track, [[oshas-slip|Osha's Slip]], and its Calveno fronts to move a warning without exposing the Warren.

The Passage's One Law is simple: it hides cargo and carries messages, but it smuggles people to freedom and never as property.

## Active Agenda

**Goal:** Decide how to move the Grung bombing warning to Calveno without destroying the Passage's hidden network.

**Why:** The bombing intelligence is already in the Warren's hands, and delay gives the Grung threat time to advance while an open warning could expose the network.

**Next move:** Nona and the Tangle will choose whether to send the warning through shadow channels or break protocol and warn Calveno openly.

**Needs:** Reliable intelligence from [[felix-aho|Felix Aho]], a trusted messenger, and a route through [[Warren|the Warren]], the hidden Overland Track, or [[oshas-slip|Osha's Slip]].

**Opposition:** [[grung-clans|The Grung Clans]] create the immediate threat; Crown scrutiny and the Passage's own demand for secrecy make the warning harder to deliver.

**Next signal:** Couriers change their marks, a familiar route closes, a message reaches [[oleandro-fuschi|Ponte Bassa]], or Calveno begins reacting to a warning no public messenger claims to have delivered.

**Player opening:** The party can carry, protect, verify, expose, or refuse the warning; it can also help the Passage preserve secrecy while still giving Calveno a chance to prepare.

**If completed:** Calveno either receives a warning while the Passage remains hidden, or the city learns that an organized Rattkin network has been acting beneath its official routes.

### Chain of Action

**Who decides:** [[Tangle|The Tangle]] sets the Calveno Run's highest route policy; [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]] decides for the Warren's anchor Run.

**Who interprets those orders:** [[Enzo]] carries Nona's orders to the Run.

**Who actually carries them out:** Travelling families, crews, kitchen keepers, dry-dock workers, couriers, and trusted fronts.

**If leadership disappears:** Nona's Run can still shelter and move people, but the choice between secrecy and an open warning fractures into local decisions unless the Tangle gives a common order.

### Party

**Standing:** Friendly, opened when [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]] introduced himself to the crew as a Friend of the Passage.

**Why:** Beaumont introduced himself to the crew as a Friend of the Passage after the [[the-saltwright|Saltwright]] boarding; Nona later called off attacks on Perrin and called in her favor.

**They currently want from the party:** A trustworthy response to the Grung warning and respect for Passage routes and boundaries.

**They can currently offer the party:** Route knowledge, shelter, messages, and access to people who know Calveno's hidden movement.

**What would change their standing:** Betraying a Hold, exposing a route, moving people as property, or helping the Grung raids.

## Signals & Rumors

- **Visible sign:** Passage marks on dock beams and sudden changes in which couriers use a familiar route.
- **Rumor:** Someone in Calveno knows about the Grung threat before the official authorities do.
- **Direct signal:** A message reaches [[oleandro-fuschi|Ponte Bassa]] or a known Hold closes without explanation.
- **Quiet clue:** A warning mark, bone or ceramic scratch-notation, or a courier taking the hidden road beneath the official one.

# The Passage

A Rattkin network formed out of [[Warren|the Warren]] in 1240 DR to dodge colonial pressure from the [[Tessarine Concordat]] and the [[Dravosi Crown]].

"Friend of The Passage. Any Kin in need can ask." [[Beaumont Sel|Beaumont]] granted [[Perrin Black-Jaw|Perrin]] this status after [[The Saltwright|the Saltwright]] boarding.

See [[Rattkin]] for species traits, varieties, and in-group terms. In Passage slang, **Kin** means Rattkin, **Dry** means non-Rattkin, a **Run** is a local port network, and a **Hold** is a waypoint or safe house.

The Passage moves people, cargo, and information across routes that don't appear on colonial charts. Its knowledge is broad. It knows the crawlspace in a harbor wall, the warning mark on a dock beam, the clerk worth trusting, and the tide that opens a low tunnel. It also knows which names are dangerous to speak in a port.

## Organization

### Structure

- **The Run**: travelling families and crews who pass route knowledge down through generations, marked in scratch-notation on bone and ceramic that only Kin can read.
- **The Hold**. Caches and shelters built into drainage channels, dock supports, wall cavities, and old foundations, plus roof voids, cisterns, and forgotten city spaces.
- **[[The Tangle]]**: seven senior elders who run the [[Calven and Calveno]] Run and hold The Passage's most secret route info. The [[Tangle Chambers]] themselves (physical location, guest history) already live on [[Warren|the Warren]] § Places.

[[Nona Black-Jaw]] leads the [[The Black-Jaw Run|Black-Jaw Run]], the Warren's anchor Run. [[Enzo]] carries out her orders. [[Ruk]] guards the kitchen. [[Cobb]] runs the [[La Vasca]] dry dock under Black-Jaw colors.

### One Law

The Passage does not move people in bondage. It hides cargo and carries messages. It smuggles people to freedom, never as property. [[Grung clans|The Grung Clans]]' coastal raids and bondage count as a hard line. The Passage does not operate where [[Grung]] hold routes.

> [!dm] The Passage's Hidden Network
>
> - **Calder's Tooth:** the limestone caves under the [[Overland Track]] on [[Calder's Tooth]] sit outside Crown control.
> - **Overland Track (Hidden Route):** Rattkin couriers cross the island in roughly 6 hours unseen, using back paths that skip toll stations, patrols, cargo counts, and clerks with ledgers. The Crown owns the official road. The Passage controls the tunnels beneath it.
> - **[[Osha's Slip]]:** night-landing site favored by The Passage for small high-value cargo. The mud stops Dravosi armor from giving chase, and Crown patrols rarely reach it.

The Warren's anchor Run engaged directly in Session 03 and Session 04. Nona called off attacks on Perrin, called in her favor, and got the Grung bombing intel from [[Felix Aho]]. Full blow-by-blow lives on [[Nona Black-Jaw]] § DM Only and § Session Log. Raid details live on [[Calveno Sewer Magazines]].

Its goals, per the source: keep moving people, cargo, and information outside colonial record-keeping. Refuse to traffic people, even under money pressure. Keep the [[Calder's Tooth]] blind spot hidden from the Crown. See DM Only § Session Events for the live organizational tension (shadow channels vs. open warning) the Grung bombing intel created.

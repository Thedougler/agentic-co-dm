# The Tarahs

A faction operating ships in or near [[Calveno]]. Known to [[nona-black-jaw]] and the [[the-black-jaw-run|Black-Jaw Run]] as a group that takes [[Rattkin]]. When [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] missed his check-in, Nona's first assumption was that the Tarahs had taken him — she called off attacks she'd set in motion once she learned he had not been captured. [[Cobb]] had told her one of the Tarahs' ships came in around the time Perrin went quiet, which reinforced her fear. This was disclosed to Perrin directly during the [[le-paludi]] meeting.

No further detail confirmed from play — their structure, motive, and relationship to the [[the-black-jaw-run|Black-Jaw Run]] and Calveno's port politics are undefined.

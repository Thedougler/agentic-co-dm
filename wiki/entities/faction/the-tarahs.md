## Current State

* **Status quo.** The Tarahs operate ships in or near [[calveno|Calveno]] and are known to [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] and the [[the-black-jaw-run|Black-Jaw Run]] as a group that takes [[rattkin|Rattkin]].
* **Recent change.** One Tarahs ship came in around the time [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] went quiet, according to [[cobb|Cobb]]; the timing reinforced Nona's assumption that they had taken him.
* **Pressure.** No internal or external pressure is confirmed beyond the risk that their reputation provokes attacks or changes how ships are treated in Calveno.
* **Strength.** They have operating ships and an established enough reputation to be suspected when a Rattkin disappears.
* **Vulnerability.** Their structure, motive, base, and relationship to Calveno's port politics are undefined.
* **Opportunity.** A party can investigate the ship report, identify the crew's actual motive, or determine whether the Tarahs were involved in Perrin's disappearance.

# Ebony Scales

> [!narration] Public face
> Green-and-black-scaled lizard-folk move between trade and threat with the confidence of people who can always produce muscle. Their business is visible in the island's dealings, but their absolute hatred of slavers is more memorable than their ordinary criminality.

The Ebony Scales operate like a yakuza-style gang and became one of Tokage's recognized clans. The twins [[ranga-gex|Ranga Gex]] and [[rango-gex|Rango Gex]] lead its drug trade, hired fighters, and external dealings.

## Current State

The clan sells drugs and provides skilled fighters, brutes, and hired muscle. Its late former leader [[don-gex|Don Gex]] waged a three-year war against slavers, and the Ebony Scales still kill slavers on sight.

## Active Agenda

**Goal.** Keep the clan's trade and reputation profitable while preserving Don Gex's anti-slavery line.

**Method.** Sell illicit goods, hire out force, and use fear against anyone involved in slavery.

**Player opening.** The party can buy aid, bargain over trade, or test whether the clan's absolute anti-slavery rule is stronger than its criminal interests.

## Signals & Rumors

- **Visible sign.** Buyers and hired muscle move through Tokage under the clan's protection.
- **Rumor.** The twins cannot be separated by sight alone.
- **Direct signal.** The clan offers a transaction or threatens someone dealing in slaves.
- **Quiet clue.** A former slaver has vanished from the island's trade.

| Situation | Run it |

## Faction Turn

### Current Turn

- **Want.** Keep trade profitable while honoring Don Gex's anti-slavery line.
- **Move.** Expand business without permitting slavers to return.
- **Mark.** The clan's reputation remains both criminal and protective.
- **Signal.** Hired fighters and trade contacts become more visible.

## History

Don Gex led a three-year war that ended slavery on Tokage and shaped the clan's current boundary.

## Hidden Agenda

**Concealed motive.** The twins want to prove they can preserve Don Gex's legacy without surrendering the clan's profitable criminal trade.

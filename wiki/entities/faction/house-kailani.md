> [!narration] Public face

## Current State

House Kailani controls [[sparhold]] through the settlement's defense and the spar-timber trade. [[toa-kailani]] has chosen [[rangi-kailani]] as champion in the death-duel challenge from [[house-renzetti]]. If Rangi falls, House Renzetti assumes control of the watch and rule; House Kailani loses the settlement, timber yards, and [[dravosi-crown|Crown]] contract.

## Active Agenda

**Goal.** Keep House Kailani in control of [[sparhold]].

**Why.** Its rule supports the spar-timber trade, the Crown supply contract, and the family's independence.

**Planned action.** Put [[rangi-kailani]] into the death duel and use the watch's authority to keep the succession challenge orderly.

**Needs.** Rangi's victory, the settlement's watch, and enough public legitimacy to keep the result binding.

**Opposition.** [[house-renzetti]] and its champion, plus anyone who changes the duel through force, magic, or leverage.

**Next signal.** The challenge bell tolls again or the betting ring begins preparing for the duel.

**Player opening.** The party can support Rangi, aid or expose House Renzetti, or intervene in the challenge before either house controls Sparhold's answer.

**If completed.** House Kailani keeps the watch, timber yards, Crown contract, and rule of Sparhold.

## People & Structure

- [[toa-kailani]] leads the house and holds Sparhold's rule through control of the settlement's watch.
- [[rangi-kailani]] is the house's disciplined, watch-trained champion.

### Chain of Action

**Who decides:** [[toa-kailani]] decides the house's response and selects its champion.

**Who interprets those orders:** The settlement's watch turns Toa's authority into enforcement and watch tallies.

**Who actually carries them out:** [[rangi-kailani]] carries out the house's public defense in the death duel.

**If leadership disappears.** The house loses its clear claim and the watch must decide whether to follow Rangi, recognize House Renzetti, or fracture.

## Territory & Touchpoints

- **[[sparhold]]:** House Kailani's settlement, watch, betting ring, timber yards, and dock.
- **Spar-timber yards:** The trade base that supports the house's independence.
- **Death-duel betting ring:** The public site where the succession challenge will resolve.

- **Visible sign.** Watch tallies and death-duel preparations appear throughout Sparhold.
- **Rumor.** House Kailani's rule is only as strong as Rangi's next fight.
- **Direct signal.** The challenge bell rings and the betting ring begins taking wagers.
- **Quiet clue.** Toa's records show which families can be pressured through watch failures.

# The Black-Jaw Run

*A smuggling network, refugee shelter, and underground community defense system rooted in the Warren beneath Calveno.*

Kin call them the Black-Jaw Run; outsiders call them the Black-Jaw Family. A travelling family, crew, and route-network within [[the Passage]], anchored at [[Warren|the Warren]] beneath [[Calven and Calveno]]. [[Nona Black-Jaw]] leads it; her own history and household are covered in full on her page.

The Run smuggles goods, forges documents, shelters fugitives, bribes officials, moves messages, disappears people who need disappearing, and sabotages [[Dravosi Crown|Crown]] operations. It functions simultaneously as a refugee network, intelligence service, community defense system, and underground bank — Nona sees no contradiction between these roles.

**Nona's Code** — enforced personally:

- Do not traffic people. (The Passage's own One Law.)
- Do not use poison.
- Do not betray sanctuary.
- Do not steal from the Warren.

**Current Status:** [[Perrin Black-Jaw|Perrin]] reached Nona in Session 03. She called off the attacks once she learned he was not captured. Perrin promised her one favor without conditions and received a sending stone in return.

## Members

- [[Nona Black-Jaw]] — matriarch; senior elder of [[the Tangle]]; runs the Warren anchor.
- [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] — grandson; current PC; was believed missing until Session 03.
- [[Anzolo]] — Tortle enforcer and Passage-adjacent ally; deployed to find Perrin before the call-off reached him.
- [[Cobb]] — [[La Vasca]] contact under Black-Jaw colors.

## Goals & Fronts

Keep smuggling, forging, sheltering, bribing, and messaging operations running under Nona's four rules; sabotage Crown operations; hold the Warren as sanctuary. No Front clock is defined for this faction yet.

# Beaumont's Crew

> [!narration] Public face
> On the [[saltwright]], Beaumont's hired hands move with the quiet competence of people who know their stations. They check lines, keep the galley fed, and descend into a dark hold when ordered. Their ordinary work is the mark: no uniform, no speech, and no ambition beyond getting the ship and themselves through the next watch.

## Current State

The source states no agenda. They are background hirelings. Their current pressure is practical: keep the [[saltwright]] working, follow Beaumont's orders, and survive the next watch. They have no established internal fracture or independent ambition.

## People & Structure

- [[lenne-vor]]. Navigator. Lean and middle-aged, silver hair cropped close, fingertips ink-darkened from twenty years of chart work. He has sailed the [[Midchain]] under three captains. Methodical and skeptical, quietly right about everything.
- [[drav-holke]]. Bosun. Short and broad, sun-darkened, a jar of stank leaf in his coat pocket and a rolled cigarette behind one ear unless he's smoking it. He manages the rigging with clipped efficiency and no interest in small talk.
- [[Wessa]]. Cook. Stocky and grey-haired, mostly declarative. She runs the galley with methodical competence and the crew eats better than the provisions should allow.
- [[Fen]]. Ordinary sailor. Seventeen or eighteen, working on a beard that has not committed. He does what he is told and gets it right. He is earnest about his first real sea job.

### Chain of Action

**Who decides:** [[beaumont-sel]] gives the orders while aboard the [[saltwright]].

**Who interprets those orders:** [[lenne-vor]] turns the captain's instructions into navigation and watch priorities.

**Who actually carries them out:** [[drav-holke]], [[Wessa]], [[Fen]], and the unnamed deckhands carry out ordinary shipboard work.

**If leadership disappears.** The crew keeps the ship moving under the navigator and bosun, but no longer has a reason to take unusual risks for Beaumont.

## Territory & Touchpoints

The crew's working territory is the [[saltwright]], especially the deck, galley, rigging, and hold. Their only established external touchpoint is the ship's route through the [[Midchain]].

## Signals & Rumors

* **Visible sign.** The crew's stations are maintained and the ship runs on schedule even when Beaumont is absent.
* **Rumor.** Sailors describe them as reliable hirelings rather than a crew with a cause.
* **Direct signal.** A request for hands, supplies, or repairs comes through the [[saltwright]].
* **Quiet clue.** Their practical response to danger shows that they follow orders instead of pursuing personal glory.

## Running the Faction

Treat them as competent background hirelings on [[Saltwright]]. They take orders. They do not drive an independent plot.
## History

The crew was established as Beaumont's hired complement aboard the [[saltwright]]. During the Session 01 hold ambush, three unnamed crew members descended into the dark below, confirming that the group acts together when Beaumont gives an order.

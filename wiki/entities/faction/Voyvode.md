# Voyvode

> [!narration] Narration
> Voyvode's eagle flies over the opposite Fathomrush pier. Its gate stays open during working hours so strangers can watch a real trial fail.

## Public face

Prove a working Crown pressure rig and claim the deep shelf honestly. Open trials, incomplete but honest notes, Crown money, and company gate access.

## DM thesis

Reach the [[Mid-Works]] before [[Sunkline]]'s fraud becomes public, even if honest trials continue to spend crews. [[Branimir Karsovine]] will not send a crew under an untested rig.

## Current state

Crown funding, one [[Fathomrush]] compound, pressure-rig towers, and trial records. The memorial wall's fresh names are mostly Voyvode's crews.

## Active agenda

Another trial death; Sunkline certificate exposed; losing compound empties into Voyvode's dock. Observation is allowed; interference is not.

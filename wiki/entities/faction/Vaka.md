## Current State

The Vaka is roughly eight years old and has about a dozen members: mostly green- and blue-caste defectors, plus freed non-Grung slaves who chose to stay connected. It has no manifesto, ranks, meetings, or formal hierarchy. Félix shows up, keeps his head down, and helps people adjust. The page's prep-profile identity for Félix conflicts with the Session 04 canon captive Felix Aho; treat that contradiction as unresolved DM evidence, not settled fact.

## Active Agenda

**Goal.** Keep a practical network available to people who find it after leaving caste-bound communities.

**Why.** Members need somewhere to sleep, peers who understand their adjustment, and proof that survival outside the old order is possible.

**Planned action.** Maintain the fish-stall contact, gathering points, and quiet introductions without seeking people out.

**Needs.** Félix's availability, trusted port traders, safe sleeping places, and members willing to remain connected.

**Opposition.** Anyone who threatens the network or its people and forces Félix to choose between low visibility and protective action.

**Next signal.** A newcomer is directed to Félix's stall or a gathering point in [[kalowe]].

**Player opening.** The party can protect a member, carry information, or use the network's knowledge of Gold-caste fractures against [[verdant-teeth]] or [[dravosi-crown]] interests.

**If completed.** A small, peaceful support network remains available without becoming a formal faction that its members cannot safely sustain.

## People & Structure

Vaka has no ranks, meetings, or formal hierarchy. Félix is the most connected member, not a leader. Members locate one another through port traders and known gathering points; the network does not seek people out.

| Person / group | Role | Wants | Loyalty / fracture |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[felix-aho\|Félix Aho]] | Founder and most connected member | Make peaceful recovery visible | Identity conflicts with Session 04 captive canon. |
| Green- and blue-caste defectors | Core members | Safety and adjustment outside [[karath\|Karath]] | Stay connected without forming a public movement. |
| Freed non-Grung members | Connected members | Mutual support and a place to belong | Chose connection rather than disappearing entirely. |

### Chain of Action

**Who decides:** No formal leader decides; members choose whether and how to help.

**Who interprets need:** Félix and port traders recognize newcomers and make introductions.

**Who actually carries it out:** Members provide sleep, advice, practical support, and information.

**If leadership disappears.** The network loses its most connected guide but has no formal hierarchy to collapse.

## Signals & Rumors

- **Visible sign.** A fish stall or port trader quietly directs a newcomer to someone who understands.
- **Rumor.** The Gold castes of each [[verdant-teeth]] island argue more than outsiders know.
- **Direct signal.** Félix offers a bed, an introduction, or advice about living outside the old caste order.
- **Quiet clue.** A peaceful Grung act is deliberately visible because violence would confirm the world's existing suspicion.

## Running the Faction

| Situation | Run it |
| --- | --- |
| **When encountered** | Show a quiet support exchange at a fish stall, port, or gathering point. |
| **When helped** | The Vaka offers shelter, an introduction, or carefully held information. |
| **When opposed** | Members disappear from known points while Félix makes the danger visible only as needed. |
| **When ignored** | The network keeps helping people quietly without seeking the party. |
| **When broken** | Members lose safe contacts and practical support; no formal hierarchy provides a replacement. |

## History

The Vaka began eight years ago when [[felix-aho|Félix Aho]] defected from [[karath|Karath]] as a green-caste freeborn. It grew into a dozen-person recovery network rather than an ideological faction. Its accumulated knowledge of Gold-caste fractures gives exile political weight beyond individual survival.

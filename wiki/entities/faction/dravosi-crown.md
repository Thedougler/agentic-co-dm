## Current State

- **Status quo.** The Crown holds the western chain through Calven, Harwick, Aldenmere, and Stenmark. [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]] is the gatepost at the [[central-strait]], while [[high-eyrie]] stands beyond the eastern edge.
- **Recent change.** [[khlysty-the-flock|Khlysty / the Flock]] infiltration and Midchain pressure are escalating alongside ordinary Crown inspection work.
- **Pressure.** The Crown must keep western approaches under inspection while it can run cutters through the Midchain but cannot keep the whole sea.
- **Strength.** Its garrisons, stamps, inspectors, ratings, and Crown hulls turn a legal claim into immediate force.
- **Vulnerability.** Crown reach weakens beyond the Crown Islands and the western edge of the [[Galewall]]; the service cannot hold every lane.
- **Opportunity.** The rivalry between Crown inspection and Tessarine credit gives captains competing authorities to invoke. ^[inferred]

The Crown swears by [[Tyr]] as the court god of oaths, commissions, courts martial, and warrants. Sailors still pay [[Umberlee]] at dockside shrines because the sea is a fact while a document is a claim. In ordinary cases an inspector leads, enforcers follow, and a court receives the matter. Heresy is different: [[aleksander-malone]] is reserved for confirmed heresy, not ordinary crime.

## Active Agenda

**Goal:** Keep western approaches under inspection law while [[khlysty-the-flock|Khlysty / the Flock]] infiltration and Midchain pressure escalate.

**Why:** Inspection law is how the Crown makes its colonial claim legible and enforceable across water it cannot fully occupy.

**Next move:** Continue compulsory harbour stops and recorded ratings while tracing substitute toxin through privateer kit and Calveno's sewers.

**Needs:** Crown hulls, inspection posts, garrison support, and officers who can connect the [[Simone|Simone]] pipeline to a responsible party.

**Opposition:** [[khlysty-the-flock|Khlysty / the Flock]], Midchain pressure, and the [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]], which competes to define movement through credit rather than inspection.

**Next signal:** Another ship receives a recorded rating, a harbour stop tightens, or substitute stock appears in privateer kit or Calveno's sewers.

**Player opening:** The party can submit to or contest a rating, expose the toxin pipeline, exploit Crown–Tessarine friction, or protect someone targeted by an inspection. ^[inferred]

**If completed:** Western approaches remain Crown-inspected, with papers and ratings controlling access through the Crown Islands and the central lanes.

### Chain of Action

**Who decides:** The Dravosi Admiralty sets commissions, patrol orders, charts, and inspection law.

**Who interprets those orders:** Governors, officers, inspectors, and court officials translate Crown authority into harbour decisions.

**Who actually carries them out:** Crown hulls, cutters, inspection boats, deckhands, enforcers, and the alchemist complement.

**If leadership disappears:** The page names no single public successor; distributed garrisons and inspection posts can continue routine work, but the Crown's coordinated response is unresolved. ^[ambiguous]

## History

- **Tethyr charter.** The Crown began as a colonial court and naval service from Tethyr before planting authority in warmer water south of Chult.
- **Western chain.** Calven, Harwick, Aldenmere, and Stenmark became the Crown Islands, with Tidefall serving as the inspection gate.
- **Bloodline project.** [[aldric-drave]] founded the colonial project as a bloodline map and seeded every noble house the Crown raised.

---

## Hidden Agenda

The public service does not know that [[aldric-drave]] founded the colonial project as a bloodline map. He has existed as a lich since before the first Dravosi landfall in the Scatter; every noble house the Crown raised carries some of him. Sealed vaults under [[fort-crestwall]] hold the living chart. Two garrison detachments hold the fort on orders [[governor-voss]] never saw. Destroying the body is not enough: Aldric returns through descendants, chooses the strongest surviving sorcerous heir, and begins another roughly three-century inhabitation cycle. He favours the Draves branch. [[barnaby-rook]] served the bureaucracy and never learned the founder's name; [[geoffrey-draves]] is a nineteen-year-old carpenter who defected, placed by Aldous.

The Crown's succession is dynastic, but the monarch's name remains unnamed in ordinary files. Do not invent a monarch name from this dump.

# Tessarine Concordat

> [!narration] Public face
> Pale canal counting houses and white sails with blue triangles mark Concordat writ. A clerk can sound like an old house; the courtesy is always a contract. The Tessarine hold the east of the Scatter with paper, debt, and the promise that a balanced ledger is safer than an honest answer.

## Current State

Letters of credit, route underwriting, salvage contracts, debt-recovery holds, and Council obligations tie the Concordat through [[calven-and-calveno]], [[kalowe]], [[sparhold]], and bonded Tessarine hulls. The [[tessarine-trade-house]] at [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]] licenses trade, brokers salvage, and sends couriers. The [[vault-of-the-first-factor]] remains unconfirmed on an uncharted limestone spire.

## Active Agenda

**Goal.** Contain public debt exposure while retaining salvage priority and control through paper.

**Why.** A second public confession would threaten the Concordat more than a stolen chest, and its credit network depends on appearing reliable.

**Planned action.** Keep the [[sunkline]] pressure-rig venture funded, bury the [[chain-council]] mail trail, and pursue the name and upper layout of [[Shelfworks]].

**Needs.** Counting houses, credit, Council obligations, sealed mail, and faces who can absorb blame.

**Opposition.** [[dravosi-crown]], [[passage]], [[Voyvode]], [[shepherd-grigori]]'s blood-anchor plan, and any investigation that connects Cosimo to the Pearl theft.

**Next signal.** Another confession, missing letter, salvage contract, or debt hold appears across the Scatter.

**Player opening.** The party can invalidate a debt, expose the mail trail, or force the Concordat to choose between a client and its reputation.

**If completed.** The Concordat retains eastern control through paper and keeps its hidden architecture out of public reach.

## Assets

- **Counting houses and the Seven Houses Council.** Visible authority in [[calven-and-calveno]].
- **Letters of credit and debt holds.** Financial leverage that makes refusal expensive.
- **[[sunkline]].** A funded pressure-rig venture pursuing [[Antheri]] salvage.
- **Tessarine hulls and couriers.** Ships such as [[amberreach]] and [[silkvane]] move cargo, writ, and instruction.

## People & Structure

- [[cosimo-verantio]] is the hidden architect and secretly a Magus Dragon who hoards obligation.
- [[iacopo-fieschi]], [[oriana-becht]], [[estratto]], [[Maret]], and [[samuel]] are visible faces.
- [[calven-and-calveno]] carries the visible counting-house work.
- Four houses usually vote the Concordat's way, while three can still stall when refusal looks cheaper than compliance.

### Chain of Action

**Who decides:** The Seven Houses Council and hidden principals decide which obligations and projects the Concordat will carry.

**Who interprets those orders:** Factors, clerks, and faces turn those decisions into contracts, letters, and debt holds.

**Who actually carries them out:** Counting houses, couriers, funded ventures, and commissioned actors such as [[delmar-fisk]].

**If leadership disappears.** The ledger network continues, but concealed principals and the mail trail become harder to coordinate.

## Signals & Rumors

- **Visible sign.** White sails with blue triangles, pale canal buildings, and sealed writ accompany trade.
- **Rumor.** Six of seven houses vote the Concordat's way, though the exact seating chart remains uncertain.
- **Direct signal.** A clerk offers credit, salvage, or passage with terms attached.
- **Quiet clue.** A debt-recovery hold or sunken letter connects an apparently private transaction to the wider Concordat network.

| **When helped** | The Concordat pays losses or opens a route, then records the obligation. |

## History

The Tessarine began as an eastern mercantile colonial power shaped by the old Amnian house model. In the current campaign, its hidden architecture includes Cosimo's role behind the Pearl theft, the funding of Sunkline, and the quiet effort to bury the sealed proposal and sunk mail trail.

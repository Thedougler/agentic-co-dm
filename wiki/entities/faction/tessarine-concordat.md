# Tessarine Concordat

> [!narration] Narration
> Pale canal counting houses and white sails with blue triangles mark Concordat writ. A clerk can sound like an old house; the courtesy is always a contract.

## Public face

The Tessarine are a merchant consortium with eastern roots in the Scatter, built on the old Amnian house model. In 1495 they are one of the two colonial powers on this sea: the [[dravosi-crown]] holds the western approach with ships, inspections, and law; the Tessarine hold the east with paper. Heritage is noise to them. A balanced ledger is not.

[[calven-and-calveno]] keeps the visible work: pale four- and five-storey canal buildings, open deals, and privacy sold by the hour. An unmarked pale building at the far end of the harbour watches every arrival and says nothing. The merchant families still sit the Seven Houses Council, which lets Calveno pretend it governs itself.

Four houses owe enough, profit enough, or fear disruption enough to vote the Concordat's way; three can still stall when refusal looks cheaper than compliance. "Six of seven" is ground feeling, not the seating chart.^[ambiguous]

## DM thesis

Notes paid, collateral held, and trade moving. They rarely say no; they say yes with terms. Reliability is the weapon: insured cargo, paid losses, sealed mail, and a courtesy that is always a contract.

## Current state

Letters of credit, route underwriting, salvage contracts, debt-recovery holds, and Council obligations tied to private notes. Factors slide ledgers across counters without speaking. Appointments take weeks. The same pattern repeats through Calveno's counting houses, the Midchain hub at [[Kalowe]], [[Sparhold]], and bonded holds on Tessarine hulls.

The three-storey [[tessarine-trade-house]] on the Shelf at [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]] licenses trade, brokers salvage, and sends couriers. Known craft include [[Amberreach]], a reliable Calveno cargo galleon, and [[Silkvane]], a fast Tidefall dispatch sloop. White sails with blue triangles speak the phrase "by Concordat writ."

Oral tradition puts the [[vault-of-the-first-factor]] on an uncharted limestone spire in the Midchain jungle. Treat it as unconfirmed, not as a mapped fact.

## Active agenda

After [[iacopo-fieschi]] confessed his Tessarine debt in public, the house contained the noise rather than argue it. A second public confession would be worse than a stolen chest. The house wants the name and upper layout of [[Shelfworks]], not the deep city. [[Sunkline]] at [[Fathomrush]] is its pressure-rig shop, racing [[Voyvode]]; the rig has not passed a real deep trial, but its certificate has.

[[shepherd-grigori]] names the Concordat among blood-anchor targets; this source does not establish the Concordat's response.

## Assets, people, places, relationships

- The Principal: [[cosimo-verantio]] — on paper a Tessarine merchant; inside the structure the architect, and secretly a Magus Dragon. He hoards obligation rather than coin. Burning Tessarine gold only annoys a counting house; invalidating debt cuts the hoard.
- Pearl cut-out: the [[chain-council]] hired [[delmar-fisk]] to steal the [[pearl-of-souls]]; Cosimo sat behind a Council signatory. The real target was [[aldric-drave]]. [[detto-kalash]] carried the sealed proposal; six Concordat skiffs slipped past six Concordat scows; the letter sank five ships including Detto's. The Concordat now spends quiet money to bury the mail trail.

- Rival: [[dravosi-crown]]; funder of [[Sunkline]]; complicated cover with [[Passage]]; [[corbin-knighton]] was Tessarine-trained before becoming a Crown boarder.

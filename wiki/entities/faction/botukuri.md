## Current State

- **Status quo.** Botukuri works seasonal farms in Sorn's flood-pulse lanes; it has no permanent town.
- **Recent change.** The forest is taking the next inland row while the beach remains recut.
- **Pressure.** The flood pulse and forest encroachment reduce the number of stable fields and routes.
- **Strength.** Seasonal knowledge lets the clan work lanes that change from path to channel.
- **Vulnerability.** Without a permanent settlement, the clan depends on usable flood routes and recut edges.
- **Opportunity.** Anyone who learns the flood calendar can find or obstruct the lanes without searching for a town.

## Active Agenda

**Goal.** Keep the seasonal flood-pulse farms working as the usable lanes change.

# Tangle

> [!narration] Public face
> The Tangle is known through Rattkin family Runs, coded trust, and routes that carry people and messages beyond official records. Its elders are not a public court; their influence appears in who receives shelter, which route opens, and which request is refused.

## Current State

* **Status quo.** The Tangle's elders oversee the Calveno Run while [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]] leads the Warren's anchor Run.
* **Recent change.** The Rattkin Runs now carry the Tangle's elder teachings as a live obligation rather than a private lesson.
* **Pressure.** Colonial records, patrols, and competing authorities threaten to make the network visible or unusable.
* **Strength.** Family obligations and coded trust let the Runs move people without relying on one public authority.
* **Vulnerability.** The network depends on secrecy, trusted couriers, and routes that can be closed when proof is insufficient.
* **Opportunity.** The party can protect a route, carry a warning, or help the Tangle preserve a useful teaching.

## Active Agenda

**Goal.** Keep the Calveno Run and the Tangle's elder teachings intact while pressure makes every route more expensive.

**Why.** The Runs cannot remain useful if colonial control or a rival authority turns their hidden routes into ordinary, priced access.

**Planned action.** Compare available routes and open only the one whose coded trust can protect people and teaching together.

**Needs.** The [[Warren]], trusted couriers, family obligations, and a party willing to accept limits on what the route can carry.

**Opposition.** Colonial authorities, hostile patrols, and any force that tries to replace the Tangle's authority with its own.

**Next signal.** A familiar route changes marks, a Hold closes without explanation, or a courier refuses a request that would normally pass.

**Player opening.** The party can carry a warning, protect a route, negotiate for access, or refuse a bargain that would expose the network.

**If completed.** The Calveno Run remains hidden and usable, with its elder teachings still shaping who can move through it.

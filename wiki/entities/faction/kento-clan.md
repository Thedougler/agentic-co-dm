## Current State

The clan's products are purchased by the other Tokage clans. Its settlement sits below the central mountain and connects the island's industry to the summit routes.

## Active Agenda

**Goal.** Keep the mines, kilns, and smithing work producing equipment for Tokage.

**Method.** Mine ore and turn it into weapons and armor for the other clans.

**Player opening.** The party can seek equipment, follow a mountain-base route, or negotiate over access to the clan's industry.

## Signals & Rumors

- **Visible sign.** Smoke rises from the kilns at the mountain base.
- **Rumor.** The other clans depend on Kento work more than they admit.
- **Direct signal.** A request for ore, tools, or weapons reaches the party.
- **Quiet clue.** The summit route is tied to the settlement's industrial traffic.

## Faction Turn

### Current Turn

- **Want.** Keep the mine and kiln cycle uninterrupted.
- **Move.** Maintain production for the other clans.
- **Mark.** Equipment remains available across Tokage.
- **Signal.** Smoke and shipments continue from Kurogane-mura.
- **Collision.** Any threat to the mountain route interrupts the clan's work.
- **Player opening.** The party can protect the route or negotiate access.

## History

The Kento Clan established its role by mining the central mountain and supplying the island's other clans.

## Hidden Agenda

**Concealed motive.** The clan wants its industrial role to remain indispensable, because dependence protects its autonomy.

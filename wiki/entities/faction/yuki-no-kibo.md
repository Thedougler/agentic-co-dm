# Yuki no Kibo

> [!narration] Public face
> A small mountain village and temple are carved into snowy stone above a dangerous climb. The monks speak kindly, but their bodies show the cold, thin air, scarce food, and trials that shape life at the summit.

## At a Glance

The Yuki no Kibo are Shaolin-style monks based at [[Shōrin Hakushin-ji]]. They worship [[Semuanya]] and treat survival through cold, thin air, and scarce food as the clearest expression of their deity's will.

## At the Table

- **They want:** Survival practice, mountain access, and Semuanya's path.
- **They offer:** Trials, discipline, and knowledge of the summit.
- **They pressure with:** Cold, thin air, scarce food, and demanding tests.
- **They will not:** Treat survival as a performance or shortcut.
- **Their tell:** Albino-scaled monks and an austere mountain temple.

## Current State

[[master-miki-trox|Master Miki Trox]] leads the clan. Long-term mountain residents develop albino scales, though no one knows exactly why. Few people pass the open trials, and most monks never reach mastery.

## Active Agenda

**Goal.** Preserve the mountain path, its trials, and the survival practice that defines the order.

**Method.** Train in the summit temple and let the mountain test anyone who attempts the ascent.

**Player opening.** The party can attempt the climb, seek the monks' aid, or learn what the trials demand.

## History

The Yuki no Kibo made the mountain's cold, thin air, and scarce food central to worshipping Semuanya.

## Hidden Agenda

**Concealed motive.** The order treats survival practice as proof of faith, even when few outsiders can understand why the trials remain open.

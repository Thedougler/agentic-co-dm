# Voyvode Works

*A Dravosi-chartered rig house racing to solve deep-pressure diving at [[Fathomrush]] the honest way, running real trials, taking real losses, and logging only what actually happened.*

## Members

**[[branimir-karsovine]]**, chief engineer, runs every trial dive on his own crews personally and refuses to send anyone under a rig he hasn't tested himself first. The [[dravosi-crown]] backs the venture and its charter, the same rule-through-papers authority that governs the rest of its holdings. Voyvode's rigs get inspected, logged, and stamped like any Crown vessel, every entry kept exact even while the engineering itself is still failing.

**Hidden fact:** the trial dive that went wrong three nights ago (per Fathomrush's memorial-wall hook) was Voyvode's own. Branimir was testing personally, as always, and still lost crew. The crew that survived won't name the company; Voyvode hasn't corrected the silence.

## Goals & Fronts

### Front: Earn It The Hard Way

**Lifecycle:** active
**Aim:** solve deep-pressure rig engineering through real, personally-tested iteration until the rig actually holds up at mid-shelf depth.
**Approach:** Branimir tests every iteration himself before risking any other diver, slower and costlier in lives than [[Fathomrush]]'s rival Sunkline outfit's fraud, but converging on something real.
**Off-screen move if unopposed:** runs another personal trial dive. When it fails, he revises the rig and buries the loss under the memorial wall's newest name.
**Trigger conditions:**

- Clock advances one segment per real trial dive Branimir personally runs, win or lose.
**Clock:** 8 segments (filled: 2)
**Consequence at fill:** Voyvode achieves the first working mid-shelf-viable rig, becomes Fathomrush's dominant and legitimate authority in the trade, and, if Sunkline's forged trial data still hasn't surfaced by then, exposes the fraud outright: one rig demonstrably works, the other never did.
**Possible outcomes (2-3):** (a) party surfaces Sunkline's fraud early, before Voyvode's clock fills, so Voyvode inherits the trade outright, sooner and with fewer losses; (b) party ignores the rivalry, so the clock fills as written; (c) party sides with Sunkline for its own reasons, so Voyvode's slower, honest track still converges eventually, just against active sabotage.
**Per-PC awareness:** no PC yet knows which company's rig failed in the recent trial-dive death, that fact stays inline here, unrevealed, per Fathomrush's own hook.

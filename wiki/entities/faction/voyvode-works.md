## Current State

Voyvode's rigs get inspected, logged, and stamped like Crown vessels, even while the engineering fails. Branimir tests every iteration himself. The trial dive that went wrong three nights ago was his own; he lost crew, and survivors refuse to name the company. Voyvode has not corrected their silence.

## Active Agenda

**Goal.** Solve deep-pressure rig engineering through real, personally tested iteration until a rig holds at mid-shelf depth.

**Why.** The [[dravosi-crown]] charter rewards a working rig, while honest trials distinguish Voyvode from [[sunkline-company|Sunkline]]'s forged data.

**Planned action.** Branimir runs another personal trial dive. Whether it succeeds or fails, the clock advances one segment.

**Needs.** A functioning rig, test crews, exact logs, Crown backing, and access to Fathomrush's diving grounds.

**Opposition.** [[sunkline-company|Sunkline]]'s rival venture, deep pressure, and the political cost of repeated deaths.

**Next signal.** Another personal trial dive adds an entry to the memorial wall and advances the eight-segment clock, currently filled 2 segments.

**Player opening.** The party can investigate the recent death, compare the memorial wall with [[petera-ngata]]'s Last Berth ledger, or expose Sunkline's fraud.

**If completed.** Voyvode achieves the first working mid-shelf rig and becomes Fathomrush's legitimate dominant authority; if Sunkline's forged data remains hidden, the working rig exposes it.

### Chain of Action

**Who decides:** [[branimir-karsovine]] chooses whether a rig is ready for another personal trial.

**Who interprets those decisions:** Crown inspectors and Voyvode's logs determine whether the result counts as legitimate engineering evidence.

**Who actually carries them out:** Branimir, rig crews, and support workers run the dive and record its outcome.

**If leadership disappears.** The charter and equipment remain, but no named successor is established to accept the personal risk that defines Voyvode's method.

## History

Voyvode Works was founded as a Dravosi-chartered rig house at Fathomrush. Its defining practice is that Branimir tests every iteration himself before risking another crew. The recent failed trial and its silent survivors are now part of the venture's hidden history.

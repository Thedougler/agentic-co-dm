> [!narration] Public face
> Meetings in the capital may be attended by a familiar figure covered in black cloth and an Oni mask, but the person may be a disguised agent rather than the leader. The organization's visible work is carried by secrecy, decoys, and people who appear where they should not.

## Current State

[[madam-hino|Madam Hino]] leads the organization and rarely meets outsiders. [[biggu-baito|Biggu Baito]] serves as her head general. Madam Hino's true appearance is known only to Biggu Baito and [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]], who are sworn to keep it secret.

## Active Agenda

**Goal.** Protect the Omusubi Family's power through covert action.

**Method.** Use assassins, thieves, magic, disguises, and agents who can stand in for the leader.

**Player opening.** The party can negotiate through a decoy, investigate the Oni-masked appearances, or seek access to the capital's hidden power.

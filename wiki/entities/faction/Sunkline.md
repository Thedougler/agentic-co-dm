# Sunkline

> [!narration] Narration
> Dive lines, pressure rigs, and company stamps mark a Fathomrush compound that promises Mid-Works access. Crews take the depth; the paperwork stays ashore.

## Public face

Reach and commercialize the [[Mid-Works]] while keeping investors confident. Salvage orders, company law, pressure-rig trials, and paperwork are the tools.

## DM thesis

Keep a forged mid-shelf trial certificate from discovery long enough to preserve Tessarine money and salvage priority. When pressed, protect the false log and let crews absorb the risk.

## Current state

[[Tessarine Concordat]] credit funds the salvage pipeline. One [[Fathomrush]] compound holds dive lines, rigs, and the forged certificate. The false log is under a loose floorboard at the Last Berth; [[Petera Ngata]] keeps the complete death list.

## Active agenda

Prevent certificate discovery while the memorial wall fills. Losing the compound empties into [[Voyvode]]'s dock.

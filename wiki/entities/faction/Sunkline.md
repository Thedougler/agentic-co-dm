## Current State

[[tessarine-concordat]] credit funds the salvage pipeline. One [[fathomrush]] compound holds dive lines, rigs, and the forged certificate. The false log is under a loose floorboard at the Last Berth; [[petera-ngata]] keeps the complete death list. Losing the compound sends its people and work toward [[Voyvode]]'s dock.

## Active Agenda

**Goal.** Prevent certificate discovery while the memorial wall fills and keep the salvage venture funded.

**Why.** The forged mid-shelf trial certificate is the company's proof of competence; public discovery collapses investor confidence and salvage priority.

**Planned action.** Continue commercializing the [[mid-works]] and blame suppliers or alloy quality for failed pressure-rig trials.

**Needs.** Tessarine credit, pressure-rig crews, [[adriana-tarquine]]'s engineering work, and the hidden false log.

**Opposition.** [[Voyvode]], investigators, investors demanding a live trial, and anyone searching the Last Berth.

**Next signal.** A new failed rebuild, a request for live results, or a rumor that neither rival has shown its trial data.

**Player opening.** The party can expose the certificate, trade it for leverage, or choose which salvage company receives the pressure of discovery.

**If completed.** Sunkline keeps its funding and Mid-Works priority long enough to continue the salvage pipeline.

## Assets

- **Pressure rigs and dive lines.** Equipment that makes the compound look ready for deep work.
- **Forged mid-shelf trial certificate.** Hidden proof that preserves investor confidence until discovery.
- **Tessarine credit.** Funding that ties Sunkline's survival to the Concordat's salvage appetite.

## People & Structure

- [[adriana-tarquine]] is chief engineer.
- [[yevgenia-sorrel]] keeps the store.
- [[tessarine-concordat]] funds the venture.
- [[Voyvode]] is the rival company.

### Chain of Action

**Who decides:** Tessarine-backed leadership and investors decide whether Sunkline continues.

**Who interprets those orders:** [[adriana-tarquine]] turns the demand for results into another rebuild and public excuse.

**Who actually carries them out:** Engineers, divers, and store staff operate the rigs and salvage compound.

**If leadership disappears.** The compound loses its funding authority while the certificate, death list, and equipment remain available to whoever takes control.

## History

Sunkline became a Tessarine-funded rival at [[fathomrush]] while commercializing the [[mid-works]]. Its pressure-rig trial never passed, so the company hid a forged mid-shelf certificate and kept rebuilding toward a result it had already claimed.

# Chain Council

> [!narration] Narration
> Harbour masters and island councillors speak for Midchain water and island law. Seals, seized forts, and sealed orders travel farther than any one face at the table.

## At the Table

- **They want:** Control of the Pearl while keeping Midchain law workable as Pearl and Maw pressure escalate.
- **They offer:** Legal cover, Council seals, access to Crown-connected ports, and delegated authority.
- **They pressure with:** Seized forts, sealed orders, and commissions that turn private action into public policy.
- **They will not:** Expose the person who ordered the Pearl theft while the Council can keep responsibility behind a signatory.
- **Their tell:** Council seals, sealed orders, and representatives who speak in the language of Midchain water and island law.

## Public face

The Chain Council protects Midchain water and coordinates island law. Its seals, legal cover, Crown-connected ports, and ability to turn private action into public policy are visible sources of authority.

## Current State

- **Status quo.** Harbour masters and island councillors use Council authority to coordinate Midchain water and island law.
- **Recent change.** A five-ship commission sent [[delmar-fisk]] to steal the Pearl from [[vel-orn]].
- **Pressure.** Pearl and Maw pressure is escalating, while [[shepherd-grigori]] names the Council among the three powers across which he wants blood anchors.
- **Strength.** Council seals, legal cover, Crown-connected ports, seized forts, and delegated authority let the Council act beyond any one councillor.
- **Vulnerability.** The Council can hide responsibility behind signatories, but tracing its commissions and sealed orders can expose the order chain.
- **Opportunity.** The party can follow the commission through [[delmar-fisk]] and [[fisks-fleet]], then use the Tessarine route to identify the concealed principal.

**Internal fracture:** [[cosimo-verantio]], acting as “the Principal” behind a Council signatory for the [[tessarine-concordat]], used the arrangement to target [[aldric-drave]]. The Council thought it was buying [[Umberlee]]’s mercy and has not learned the real target.

## Active Agenda

**Goal:** Keep Midchain law workable while Pearl and Maw pressure escalate.

**Why:** The Council needs commissions, seized forts, sealed orders, and delegated authority to keep its policy effective without exposing the interests behind the Pearl.

**Next move:** Continue using commissions and sealed orders to control Pearl-related action through delegated actors.

**Needs:** Council signatories, legal cover, Crown-connected ports, and enough authority to keep delegated actors acting in the Council’s name.

**Opposition:** Pearl and Maw pressure, [[shepherd-grigori]]’s blood-anchor plan, and anyone who traces the Tessarine route back to its concealed principal.

**Next signal:** Another commission, sealed order, seized fort, or public use of Council authority around the Pearl or the Maw.

**Player opening:** The party can expose the commission chain, identify the concealed principal, or force the Council to answer publicly for the Pearl theft.

**If completed:** The Council keeps Midchain law workable and retains control of Pearl-related policy through its delegated authority.

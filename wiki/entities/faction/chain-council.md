# Chain Council

> [!narration] Narration
> Harbour masters and island councillors speak for Midchain water and island law. Seals, seized forts, and sealed orders travel farther than any one face at the table.

## Public face

Protect Midchain water and coordinate island law. Council seals, legal cover, Crown-connected ports, and the ability to turn private action into public policy.

## DM thesis

The Chain Council is the commission mask for interests that want the [[pearl-of-souls]] controlled without exposing who ordered the theft.

## Current state

A five-ship commission sent [[delmar-fisk]] to steal the Pearl from [[vel-orn]]. [[shepherd-grigori]] names the Council among the three powers across which he wants blood anchors; this source does not establish the Council's response.

## Active agenda

Keep Midchain law workable while Pearl and Maw pressure escalate. Commissions, seized forts, sealed orders, and delegated authority are the tools.

## Assets, people, places, relationships

- Commission face: [[delmar-fisk]] / [[fisks-captains]]
- Hidden principal: [[cosimo-verantio]], acting as "the Principal" behind a Council signatory for the [[tessarine-concordat]]. The Council thought it was buying [[Umberlee]]'s mercy and did not learn that the real target was [[aldric-drave]].
- Complicated ties: [[tessarine-concordat]], [[dravosi-crown]], [[Waveservants]]
- Places: [[vel-orn]], Midchain harbours

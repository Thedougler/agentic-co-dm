# Sentinels of the Eyrie

> [!narration] Narration
> Aarakocra monks who have held High Eyrie for two centuries, non-aligned and older than Dravosi rule. Their work is to watch the Drowned Maw and write what happens: time, weather, position, water, and occurrence — never why. Exact movement in peak wind is the training standard; fair is not.

## Public face

Aarakocra monks who have held [[high-eyrie]] for two centuries. They predate the Dravosi, are non-aligned, and turned away three Dravosi leaders. Their sole purpose is to watch the [[drowned-maw]] and record.

## DM thesis

Record without conclusion. The observation ledgers may be the only continuous record of the Maw, and the order's refusal to interpret or intervene is both doctrine and vulnerability.

## Current state

Doctrine: note time, weather, position, water, and what occurred. Never record why. Do not interpret, intervene, or act. The Maw ledger begins in 1295 DR and continues through heat pulses, weather, ship position, water, and occurrence. The order refuses to dive. Access is physical and social: stair, spray, invitation, harness, magic, or watched climb.

Training: apprentices land, move, and hold still in peak wind on cantilevered terraces open on three sides. Every apprentice earns Long Reach (martial technique; not a full mechanical homebrew here). Long Sight is rarer, withheld from most, and poorly understood.

Beneath the summit is a seal tied to founding documents about Soul Incarnate. Current leadership does not know the documents describe a conscious being; they know only that the documents are very old.

## Active agenda

Hold the watch and keep the ledgers continuous. Linked pressures: [[long-sight-hunt]], [[rule-of-two]], [[schisms-third-name]], [[soul-incarnates-watch]].

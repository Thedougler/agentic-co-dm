# Five Blades

> [!narration] Public face
> Five Moucherons move as one squad: four blades around a stinger, each member watching the others before a contract ever reaches the fighting. Their gear is practical, their speech is brief, and a sealed clay keep of preserved blood sits beside every client's payment. They look like professionals because they refuse to split, abandon a client under fire, or pretend that a bloodless job costs nothing.

## At the Table

- **They want:** A clear contract, reliable payment, and the blood owed by a bloodless job.
- **They offer:** A steady squad of four who do not split or abandon a client under fire.
- **They pressure with:** Contract terms and the practical threat of a coordinated Moucheron squad.
- **They will not:** Break a contract early or leave a client under fire.

## Current State

The Five Blades take contracts through [[the-flat-water]] and through factors who know to ask. They are steady, professional, and fed. Their existence prompted [[barnaby-rook|Rook]] to investigate Moucherons, but the company has no stated awareness of his capture of [[Ket]].

## Active Agenda

**Goal.** Keep the company supplied with profitable contracts without compromising the Way of the Five Blades.

**Why.** The company depends on steady work and preserved blood, and its reputation rests on completing contracts without splitting the squad.

**Planned action.** Quote rates and feeding clauses for the next suitable contract through a Kalowe or Flat Water factor.

**Needs.** A paying client, a defined target, four-person squad cohesion, and two or three days of preserved blood per member.

**Opposition.** Any client who refuses the feeding clause, forces the squad to split, or treats the company as disposable muscle.

**Next signal.** A factor announces that the Five Blades are available for a contract and insists that the blood clause be agreed before work begins.

**Player opening.** The party can hire them, negotiate a bloodless alternative with an equivalent cost, or exploit the company's refusal to split during a crisis.

**If completed.** The Five Blades finish the contract, preserve their reputation, and remain available through the same factors.

## People & Structure

- [[varet]] is the contract speaker who quotes the rate and feeding clause. As a Murrat native, he would recognize another Murrat native on sight.
- [[Toa]] is the biggest member and a cheerful fight analyst.
- [[suke]] is the newest member, from [[murrat]], and remains quiet and watchful.
- [[Wirra]] is the medic who keeps client crews alive after jobs go badly.

### Chain of Action

**Who decides:** The four members decide together whether a contract is clear and worth taking.

**Who interprets those orders:** [[varet]] translates the agreed contract into the rate, feeding clause, and operational terms.

**Who actually carries them out:** [[Toa]], [[suke]], [[Wirra]], and [[varet]] work as one four-person squad.

**If leadership disappears.** The company can still act, but no member can quote or alter a contract without the others agreeing to the terms.

## Territory & Touchpoints

- **[[murrat]]:** A point of personal recognition for [[varet]] and [[suke]].

## Signals & Rumors

- **Visible sign.** Four Moucherons travel and fight together, each with sealed clay keeps of preserved blood.
- **Rumor.** A bloodless job still owes blood, so experienced clients write the feeding clause into the contract.
- **Direct signal.** A factor in Kalowe or on the Flat Water offers the company's services.
- **Quiet clue.** The company refuses to split, even when dividing the squad would make a job easier.

## Running the Faction

| Situation | Run it |
| --- | --- |
| **When encountered** | The Five Blades quote a rate and feeding clause before discussing tactics. |
| **When helped** | They complete the contract and remember the party as reliable clients or useful factors. |
| **When opposed** | They keep the squad together, protect the contract, and refuse any demand that changes the terms without agreement. |
| **When ignored** | They take another contract through Kalowe or the Flat Water and remain unavailable until it ends. |
| **When broken** | A split squad or unpaid blood debt damages the company's reputation and removes its most reliable contract advantage. |

## History

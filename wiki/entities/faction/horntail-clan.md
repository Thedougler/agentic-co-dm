# Horntail Clan

> [!narration] Public face
> Fortified walls, disciplined archers, and sword-bearing lizard-folk mark the Horntail Clan's western village. Their people speak of honor and island defense as naturally as they speak of the next drill.

The Horntail Clan is a western Tokage clan based at [[Hōnteiru no Mura]]. It wants to protect [[Totemo Tokage]] from invasion through open archery and samurai-style swordsmanship under [[akibana-besko|Akibana Besko]].

- **They want:** A defensible western approach and honorable readiness.

## Current State

The clan competes with the other Tokage clans but shares kinship with the [[akai-hodo-clan|Akai Hodo Clan]]. When an outside threat arrives, Horntails fight openly while Akai Hodo attacks from concealment.

## Active Agenda

**Goal.** Keep the western approach defensible.

**Method.** Train archers and swordsmen, maintain the fortified village, and meet invaders in the open.

**Player opening.** The party can seek instruction, protection, or an alliance before an invasion turns the clan's rivalry into a shared defense.

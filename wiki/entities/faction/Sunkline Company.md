# Sunkline Company

*Tessarine-backed pressure-rig venture at Fathomrush, racing [[Voyvode Works]] to engineer deep-shelf diving tech.*

## Members

[[Adriana Tarquine]] is chief engineer. She's rebuilt the same pressure valve four times this year and blames the alloy, never the design, every time.

The [[Tessarine Concordat]] backs Sunkline financially. The [[Shelfworks]] feeds the Concordat's own appetite for [[Antheri]] salvage, a new dependency lever to run alongside its existing debt network.

## Goals & Fronts

### Front: The Depth That Isn't There

**Lifecycle:** active

**Aim:** keep investor confidence alive on a pressure rig that hasn't actually passed a deep-pressure trial.

**Approach:** Sunkline hides the [[Forged Rig-Trial Certificate|forged trial certificate]] at the Last Berth boardinghouse and blames suppliers and alloy quality publicly for every failed rebuild. Real demonstrations stay stalled behind excuses.

**Off-screen move if unopposed:** courts new investors and recruits on the strength of the forged certificate, while [[Adriana Tarquine]] quietly rebuilds the same valve again.

**Trigger conditions:**

- The forged certificate at the Last Berth surfaces ([[Last Berth False Log]]'s own discovery).
- Investors formally demand a live trial dive.

**Clock:** 6 segments (slow burn), 4 filled. The concealment is already fragile, one loose floorboard from discovery.

**Consequence at fill:** the forgery goes public. Investor funding collapses overnight, and Sunkline's divers and recruits scatter to the rival Voyvode Works.

**Possible outcomes (2-3):**

- The crew finds the forged certificate first and chooses to expose it, collapsing Sunkline's funding before Adriana solves the design.
- The crew finds the certificate and sits on it, or trades it for leverage instead. The forgery keeps aging toward discovery on its own.
- Sunkline recruits [[Catarina Da'Virelli]] to actually solve the pressure-valve problem before the clock fills, buying the forgery real cover.

**PC connection:** [[Catarina Da'Virelli]]. Her real engineering literacy is exactly what could catch that Sunkline's trial numbers don't add up, and exactly what Sunkline would want to co-opt to solve the design problem legitimately instead of faking it.

**Quest link:** none yet.

**Three-Clue Trail:**

Conclusion: Sunkline forged its mid-shelf pressure-trial certificate. The real rig has never passed.

Clue 1: [[Adriana Tarquine]]. She's rebuilt the same valve four times this year and blames the alloy every time, a pattern that doesn't square with a rig that's supposedly already passed trial.

Clue 2: the Last Berth False Log. It's the forged certificate itself, hidden beneath a loose floorboard at the boardinghouse.

Clue 3: [[Fathomrush]]. Dockside rumor holds that neither company has shown its trial results outside its own inner circle.

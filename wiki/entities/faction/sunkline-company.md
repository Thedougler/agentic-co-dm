## Current State

Sunkline is racing [[voyvode-works]] at [[fathomrush]] while its rig remains unproven. The [[tessarine-concordat]] backs the company financially, and the [[Shelfworks]] feeds the Concordat's appetite for [[Antheri]] salvage. The forged trial certificate is hidden at the Last Berth boardinghouse. Four of six pressure points on the concealment clock are already filled.

## Active Agenda

**Goal.** Keep investor confidence alive until the rig passes a genuine deep-pressure trial.

**Why.** The company needs funding to continue rebuilding, and public discovery of the forgery would collapse that support.

**Planned action.** Court new investors using the forged certificate while [[adriana-tarquine]] rebuilds the valve again.

**Needs.** Investor patience, the hidden certificate, Adriana's engineering work, and a supplier to blame for each failed rebuild.

**Opposition.** [[voyvode-works]], demanding investors, and anyone who finds the forged certificate or asks for a live trial dive.

**Next signal.** Investors formally demand a demonstration or dockside rumor spreads that neither company has shown its trial results.

**Player opening.** The party can expose the certificate, trade it for leverage, or delay discovery while deciding whom the salvage project should serve.

**If completed.** Sunkline keeps its funding long enough to produce a working rig and remains a Tessarine-backed competitor at Fathomrush.

## People & Structure

- [[adriana-tarquine]] is chief engineer and rebuilds the pressure valve.
- The [[tessarine-concordat]] supplies financial backing and expects Antheri salvage.
- Engineers and divers carry out the work while investors demand proof.

### Chain of Action

**Who decides:** Investors and the Tessarine-backed company leadership decide whether the venture continues.

**Who interprets those orders:** [[adriana-tarquine]] turns the demand for a passing trial into another rebuild.

**Who actually carries them out:** Sunkline's engineers and divers test the rig at [[fathomrush]].

**If leadership disappears.** The company loses its funding authority, while the forged certificate and failed design remain available to whoever finds them.

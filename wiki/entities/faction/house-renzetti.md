## Current State

[[ottavia-renzetti]] rang the challenge bell against [[toa-kailani]] and House Kailani's hold on [[sparhold]]. She pressed forward [[nico-renzetti]] as the family's champion. If Nico wins, House Renzetti controls Sparhold's watch, timber trade, and Crown contract; if he loses, the house loses standing in settlement governance.

## Active Agenda

**Goal.** Claim [[sparhold]] through the challenge duel and take the settlement's leverage over the spar-timber trade.

**Why.** Whoever controls the cut controls whether the [[dravosi-crown]] can build the ships it needs.

**Planned action.** Put [[nico-renzetti]] into the death duel in the betting ring outside the watch hall.

**Needs.** Nico's participation, a recognized duel, and a result the settlement cannot dismiss.

**Opposition.** [[house-kailani]] and [[rangi-kailani]], plus Nico's reluctance and any outside intervention.

**Next signal.** The duel proceeds, and Nico fights under family pressure even though he may be unwilling to die for Ottavia's ambition.

**Player opening.** The party can influence the duel through force, magic, leverage, or a choice about whether to expose Nico's coercion.

**If completed.** House Renzetti rules Sparhold and controls its watch, timber yards, and Crown supply contract.

## People & Structure

- [[ottavia-renzetti]] leads with calculated precision and unyielding authority.
- [[nico-renzetti]] is the chosen champion, pressed into service by family honor and Ottavia's command.

### Chain of Action

**Who decides:** [[ottavia-renzetti]] decides the house's claim and rang the challenge bell.

**Who interprets those orders:** Nico's family duty and the duel's rules turn Ottavia's decision into a public contest.

**Who actually carries them out:** [[nico-renzetti]] fights [[rangi-kailani]] in the betting ring.

**If leadership disappears.** The claim loses its driving voice, while Nico's duel may continue without a clear family plan.

## Territory & Touchpoints

- **[[sparhold]]:** The settlement whose rule and watch the house seeks to take.
- **Betting ring outside the watch hall:** The site of the challenge duel.
- **Spar-timber cut:** The resource that carries the Crown contract and makes the succession matter beyond the town.

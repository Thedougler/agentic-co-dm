# Omusubi Family

> [!narration] Public face
> The Omusubi Family presents itself as Tokage's royal house from the harbor capital. Fishing wealth, rice wealth, and the formality of a court make the claim visible even where no one agrees that the crown is real.

## At a Glance

The Omusubi Family claims royal authority over [[Totemo Tokage]] from [[Zennitana]]. Its rise began when the leading fishing and rice-farming families forced a marriage between their children several generations ago.

## Current State

The family shares the capital with its loyal shadow organization, the [[ryu-no-kage|Ryu no Kage]]. [[kinryu-omusubi|Kinryu Omusubi]] is the named leader.

## Active Agenda

**Goal.** Maintain the family’s self-proclaimed royal authority over the island.

**Method.** Hold the capital, harbor wealth, fishing traditions, and rice-producing influence that first created the family.

**Player opening.** The party can seek access through the capital, challenge the claim, or negotiate with the court and its covert arm.

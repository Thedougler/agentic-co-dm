## Current State

The clan patrols the island's northeast and northwest. It shares kinship with the [[horntail-clan|Horntail Clan]] and answers invasion from the shadows while the Horntails fight openly.

## Active Agenda

**Goal.** Keep foreign threats out of the northern marshes and protect the island through concealment.

**Method.** Patrol, disguise, camouflage, illusion, and silent killing.

**Player opening.** The party can investigate the red mist, seek a hidden route, or decide whether a silent defense is protection or a threat.

# Gost

Half-orc berserker. A loose hiring hook in the [[Warren]], available as muscle if the party needs an extra body. He's loyal only to whoever pays going rate. No play evidence yet. Source is a stub from [[Calveno]] street encounters.

## Stats & Combat

> [!mechanic]
> **[[Berserker]] stats, CR 2.** AC 13, HP 67, greataxe +5, 2d12+3. Not violent yet when first encountered. He has Reckless Attack only (too drunk to sense danger). First two rounds at disadvantage on saves. After that the adrenaline is burning the alcohol off.

## Encounter: The Festival Drunk

At the centre of a ring of empty space in a crowd, mid-afternoon, holding a wine bottle and a still-sheathed sword, having knocked over a wine stall and singing something in what might be Orcish. The watch is four minutes away.

> [!check] Charisma — Talk Him Down
> DC 13 Persuasion or DC 10 Intimidation. [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] telling him the watch is coming and this is going to be a bad four minutes is an automatic success.
> **Success:** he stands down and puts the bottle down without drawing the watch.
> **Failure:** try again, wait for the watch (he ends up in a holding cell), or let it escalate. If you escalate, he throws the bottle, creating a second problem.

> [!check] Persuasion — Move Him Along
> DC 10, if someone offers to buy him a drink somewhere quieter.
> **Success:** he goes along without drawing the watch.
> **Failure:** he stays put.

> [!dm]
> If the party handles this without bloodshed and without the watch, Gost finds them the next morning and offers his sword at going rate (4 to 6 gp/week). He is looking for work. If they never follow up, he disappears back into the festival. A loose hiring hook, not a required NPC.

## Relationships

- [[Warren]]. He offers his sword to anyone who pays going rate.

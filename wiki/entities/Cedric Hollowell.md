# Cedric Hollowell

|             |                             |
| ----------- | --------------------------- |
| **Species** | [[Human]] (Dravosi)             |
| **Role**    | Crown-licensed import factor |
| **Location**| [[Port Tidefall]] |

Cedric runs an import agency near Tidefall's Inspection Pier. Thirty years in Crown licensing taught him the language of fees and delays. He is not corrupt. He simply knows every deal has a cost and every person has a use. He treats his family the same way he treats cargo.

A sailor kept returning to a dock near his property. Cedric made inquiries and set terms. [[Geoffrey Draves]] had no money and no prospects. That part was true. The terms looked fair but were impossible on patrol cutter wages. Cedric does not think about this.

His daughter [[Verity]] runs his secondary accounts better than anyone he has hired. He considers this settled. What troubles him is her refusal to pursue any of the introductions he arranges, which has become both a logistical problem and a personal one.

**Voice & Delivery:**

He speaks the way someone speaks after thirty years negotiating with Crown inspectors: precise, patient, and never first to name what something costs. He does not raise his voice. When challenged, he restates his position instead of backing down.

He is not hostile to the crew by default. He is a factor, and factors profit when people move cargo through Tidefall. He will help if there is profit in it and good accounting to show for it.

> "My daughter runs our accounts better than anyone I've hired. Whatever brought you here, I can address through the proper channel."

> "The terms I set were reasonable. A man who cannot meet them in three years cannot meet them in ten. That is arithmetic, not cruelty."

> [[Verity]] has found bonded cargo in his accounts without a manifest entry. He does not know who placed it there or if someone hacked his credentials. If the crew knows first, that is leverage. If he is covering it up quietly, he is weaker than he looks.

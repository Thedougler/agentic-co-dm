# Minotaur

**Creature Type:** Humanoid

**Size:** Medium (upper end — minotaurs are broad and heavy)

**Speed:** 30 ft.

Medium humanoids built heavy — bovine heads, thick curving horns, broad shoulders, hooved feet — with a faculty for perfect passage-recall: a minotaur who has walked a channel once can walk it blindfolded twenty years later, and the same faculty works on currents, channel marks, and reef lines. As a Minotaur, you have these special traits.

**Horns.** Your horns are a natural melee weapon, dealing 1d6 piercing damage on a hit.

**Goring Rush.** After moving 20 feet or more in a straight line toward a target, you can make a bonus action Horn attack. On a hit, the target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet.

**Hammering Horns.** After hitting with a melee attack on your turn, you can use a bonus action to shove the target with your horns. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet and knocked prone.

**Labyrinthine Recall.** You have perfect recall of any path you have traveled, and you have advantage on checks made to navigate or avoid becoming lost.

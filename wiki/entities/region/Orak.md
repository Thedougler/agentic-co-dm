# Orak

> [!narration] Narration
> Orak is high karst: stone towers rise into cloud, forest fills the clefts, and sinkholes breathe air colder than the day. White water falls toward drowned trees. The useful island is vertical, and the holes are shafts rather than doors.

## Current state

**Status quo.** Orak is a vertical high-karst island region paired with [[dreth|Dreth]]. It has no sanctioned landing; the useful routes run from reef and cliff face to karst towers, sink wells, cleft forest, and wet dark nests.

* **Recent change.** No region-wide change is established on this page.
* **Pressure.** The [[grung-clans|Grung clans]] hold Orak as forbidden territory with Dreth, count known shafts, and patrol the channel. Falls, cold breath, and rain-dependent crossings are persistent conditions.
* **Opportunity.** The party can attempt an exposed landing, descend through a shaft, investigate the water system, or wait for the crossing to Dreth.
* **Next visible change.** Rain can make the Orak shallows crossing available or remove it; a patrol's count can also change which shafts are watched.

## Regional truths

* **The island is vertical.** Reaching useful ground requires the cliff face, karst towers, sink wells, or known shafts.
* **Holes are shafts.** Sinkholes are not doors; a path swallowed by a shaft remains a shaft.
* **The clans forbid and patrol.** Grung clans hold Orak and Dreth as forbidden territory, count known shafts, and patrol the channel.
* **The geology has a leak.** Sinkholes support sailor rumors about thin places, but the established truth is geology plus the same primal leak as the other Teeth.

## Geography

### Shape and boundaries

Orak is high karst above drowned trees, with stone towers, cleft forest, cold sinkholes, and white-water falls. The island's useful movement is vertical; no sanctioned landing turns the reef and cliff face into the first boundary.

### Landmarks

* **Karst towers.** The exposed landing objective above the reef; they are the first usable high ground.
* **Sink wells.** Vertical routes from the towers into cleft forest, marked by cold breath.
* **Cleft forest.** Forest in the cuts between towers, with known shafts and watched routes.
* **Wet dark nests.** Hidden wet clefts beside water that can be submerged in; no named settlement is established.
* **White-water falls.** Visible signs of the water system descending toward drowned trees.

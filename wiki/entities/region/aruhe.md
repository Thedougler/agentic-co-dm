# Aruhe

_The Hungry Isle_
![[aruhe-hungry-isle-v3.jpg|Aruhe / Hungry Isle]]
## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> From offshore Aruhe is a vast green volcanic island in deep blue water. Forested ridges fold around a dark crater lake, gold-tan bands cut the lower slopes, pale beaches break the cliffs, and white water works a reef rim. No town, field, road, or smoke marks the island. Redheart berries mark the wet lowlands in small red flashes, while heavy giant's guavas punctuate the deeper canopy. Every slope is green or gold and thick with vegetation.

## At a Glance
Aruhe is a vast untamed island in the eastern [[Midchain]], about 500 miles end to end and about 150 miles across at its waist. No settlement is visible from offshore. [[Karath]] lies near one western edge, not the inland heart. [[Uncertainty]] circles the island. Land at [[western-landing|Western Landing]]. Inland bands include [[old-gardens|the Old Gardens]], [[the-quiet|the Quiet]], [[grasslands|the Grasslands]], [[the-river|the River]], and [[the-marshes|the Marshes]].

## Who

The crew has [[Sandro|Sandro]] and [[Nino|Nino]] aboard [[Uncertainty]], and more survivors remain in the interior. Aruhe has no known state, town, road network, or visible settled population. The current people are survivors from the Calveno raid, storm-thrown Grung raiders, and travelers moving inland after the wrecks. The survivors report that roughly 100 to 150 people came ashore after the raid ships broke. Most are already dead. The landing is failing, and fruit and island plants can kill.

[[Hinewai|Hinewai]] joins Aruhe as pressure, grief, and preservation, not as a ruler or hive mind. Her walking form and memorial are in [[memorial-grove|Memorial Grove]]. The island's plants and animals still act from their own niches and claims. They may respond to taking, trespass, hunger, fear, or opportunity, but they are not controlled bodies or minions.

## What

Aruhe is an old volcanic island, about five hundred miles end to end, with green slopes, crater water, shore reef, basalt shelves, vents, and lava tubes. It is not a wasteland or a place of scarcity. It is familiar life with its limits loosened: growth, healing, adaptation, reproduction, feeding, and rot continue past normal ecological checks. The result is a real place with living systems that have become excessive and dangerous. It is not one monster wearing an island's shape.

The clearest physical pattern is the root network. Pale, luminous roots run wrist-thick to thigh-thick from the shelf above [[clear-lake|Clear Lake]], through [[the-marshes|the Marshes]], and into both unmarked graves in [[memorial-grove|Memorial Grove]]. In [[lava-tubes|the lava tubes]], aboveground forest roots descend as curtains. A wrecked raiding hull sits above the tideline at [[western-landing|Western Landing]], marking the known entry point and the collapse of the Calveno raid landing.

Across those bands, [[redheart-berry]] beads along wet lowlands and river margins, while [[giants-guava]] hangs from high forest branches. Fallen fruit can be foraged. Fruit taken from a living branch is a claim against the island.

Aruhe distinguishes between receiving and taking, and fallen fruit or clear offerings do not trigger hostility. A living claim includes fruit taken from a branch or living growth that someone cuts. It also includes trapping an animal or treating the island as property. Those actions draw local life into response. [[taking-on-aruhe]] is the reference for adjudicating those claims.

## Where

Open water connects Aruhe to [[Sparhold]], and [[Karath]] lies close enough that one western channel narrows to about half a mile. That nearness only touches the rim. The island is still about five hundred miles end to end and about one hundred and fifty miles across at the waist, with no roads, towns, fields, or maintained passes. On settled roads, a straight crossing might take under a week. On Aruhe, the same crossing takes many days across reef, slope, heat, water, vegetation, animal claims, and lost lines of sight. A lengthwise crossing takes weeks.

The reliable sea approach is [[western-landing|Western Landing]]. A reef gap opens onto a short shingle beach. From there the land rises into [[old-gardens|the Old Gardens]], where ancient stone terraces climb inland. Fruit, vines, water channels, and collapsed steps cover the terraces. Past them, Aruhe divides into wetter and higher systems instead of one inland road. [[grasslands|the Grasslands]] opens where rivers cut hot valleys through the jungle. [[the-quiet|the Quiet]] rises above the garden walls in deep rainforest where small life has disappeared. Both routes lead deeper inland, but neither offers quick going. Terrain, weather, and local life can turn a band crossing into a full day.

Water is Aruhe's nearest equivalent to a road. [[the-river|The River]] braids through the interior from [[clear-lake|Clear Lake]]. It crosses the Grasslands and sends wet routes toward [[the-marshes|the Marshes]]. Clear water and open banks can shorten a route. Current, shelves, pools, otter families, and living ownership make the River unsafe infrastructure. Clear Lake itself is an inland crater basin, calm by Aruhe's standards, with stone shelves, fish, and steep green walls. Its water drains into the River and spills south into the Marshes. The Quiet borders the lake.

Downhill from the lake, Aruhe turns saturated. [[the-marshes|The Marshes]] form flooded rainforest where trail, pond, channel, and floating mat trade places every few hundred feet. Luminous roots cross the wet ground toward [[memorial-grove|Memorial Grove]]. Decay, insects, fungus, and predator paths slow travel even when the map distance looks modest. Farther south and southeast, the Marshes become [[the-mangroves|the Mangroves]], a tidal wall of roots, black water, mud, and sealed canopy. They meet the ocean but have no harbor. From offshore, channels seem open; inside, roots and mud make traps.

[[memorial-grove|Memorial Grove]] draws routes from grassland, lake edge, and marsh, though no formal path leads there. The Grove contains the fruit tree, two unmarked graves, black flowers, bound soil, and waiting mourners. Below it, [[lava-tubes|the lava tubes]] run through the old volcanic body of the island, connecting by marsh breach, cave seepage toward Clear Lake, and the root-and-grave descent from the Grove. The shore, terraces, grass, quiet forest, river, lake, marsh, mangrove, grove, and lava tubes form one continuous untamed ecosystem rather than separate rooms. Crossing to the heart takes days because the island remains alive and dangerous throughout.

## Why

The island's excess comes from [[Hinewai|Hinewai]] preserving a grave at [[memorial-grove|Memorial Grove]]. The Death Bloom is not a portable object hidden somewhere on the island. It consists of the tree, both graves, the black-flower ring, and the bound soil. Hinewai's grief has made Aruhe's life systems overactive. Growth, repair, adaptation, and the violent response to taking all exceed normal limits.

Right now, Aruhe matters because the Calveno raid broke here. Some survivors are aboard [[Uncertainty]], and more remain inland. The crew can still recover people before the island or its local pressures kill them. The crew found this island while following the raid's wreck field. The ship can still leave, but the crew faces a choice between pursuing the survivors and investigating the island's deeper truth.

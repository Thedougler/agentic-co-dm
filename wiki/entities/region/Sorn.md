> [!narration] Narration
> Sorn is the farm heart of the Teeth: flood-pulse crop lanes standing in water behind one guarded beach of pale sand. A long-hut sits on the sand. The forest already takes the next inland row while the beach stays recut.

> **DM thesis.** Sorn turns every crossing into a choice between the watched beach, seasonal farm lanes, and a trade bypass that does not grant interior permission.

## Current state

**Status quo.** Botukuri workers maintain seasonal farms behind [[sorn-guarded-beach]]. A purple garrison holds the beach, while blue intermediaries conduct brief exchanges without entering the lanes.

* **Recent change.** Extra inland patrols now signal the hunt for [[jean-claude-tabarnack]].
* **Pressure.** Flood pulse and forest growth keep changing the usable lanes.
* **Opportunity.** The blue exchange provides a bypass toward Veth and Karath water without opening the interior.
* **Next visible change.** A route that is passable at dawn may be a channel by afternoon.

## Regional truths

* **Flood pulse.** The water level is Sorn's calendar; a lane can be a path at dawn and a channel by afternoon.
* **Guarded access.** The purple garrison holds the beach and does not make the interior public.
* **Receding farmland.** The darker band behind the first rows is last season's field, and the forest is taking the next row.

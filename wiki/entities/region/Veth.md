# Veth

> [!narration] Narration
> Veth is the largest of the five Teeth: old dry-ground rainforest in stacked canopy layers, with one western beach strip and no pier. Twenty strides of pale sand end at a living root-stair. The closed mid-story starts on the second root.

## Current state

**Status quo.** Blue-caste go-betweens handle beach trade while higher castes watch from the treeline. There is no charted town; Grung live in canopy and wet cuts. The beach is a concession, but the stacked canopy behind it is closed.

* **Recent change.** No region-wide change is established on this page.
* **Pressure.** If outsiders linger or climb, the beach invitation becomes a patrol problem.
* **Strength.** The trade beach, living root stairs, and Grung familiarity with vertical forest routes.
* **Vulnerability.** Outsiders leave traces in terrain navigable to Grung through Standing Leap and climbing.
* **Opportunity.** Trade, follow the root-stair, climb toward high pools, or take a water route toward Sorn or Orak.

## Regional truths

* **Veth is the largest of the five Teeth.** Its dry-ground rainforest rises in four and five canopy layers.
* **The west beach is a concession.** It permits visible trade but is not a pier or permission to enter the interior.
* **The canopy is closed.** The blue root-stair is fast access but breaks protocol; higher routes are watched.
* **Trade is specific.** Bone, resin, and pigments are exchanged for food, timber, metal, and rope.

## Geography

Veth is a large island of old dry-ground rainforest. A shallow reef approach reaches [[veth-west-beach|Veth West Beach]] on the west. Pale sand ends at a living root-stair; the second root begins the closed mid-story. High pools and wet cuts make the interior vertical and exposed.

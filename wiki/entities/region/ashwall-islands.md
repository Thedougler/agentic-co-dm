# Ashwall Islands

> [!narration] Narration
> Cold-water volcanic spires rise from dark sea, their black stone cut by steam vents and warm cave mouths. Sulfur and salt hang over wreckage in the lee. Surf and vent-hiss carry between the cliffs, where the caves make the last solid shelter heading west and the first safe lee returning east.

> **DM thesis.** The Ashwall Islands turn a passage into a choice between shelter and exposure. The lee offers repair water and warm caves, while wreckage, occupied fissures, and disputed rescue stories make every landing worth investigating.

## Current state

**Status quo.** Pilot families and repair crews work the lee, treating the spires as a reckoning point rather than a destination. Outbound crews use the islands as the last solid ground before the [[Galewall]]; inbound crews use them as the first proof of survival. No formal destination authority is established.

* **Recent change.** No region-wide change is established beyond the current pilot and repair activity.
* **Pressure.** Wreck hazards, sulfur vents, and giant creatures make repair work dangerous.
* **Opportunity.** The lee provides shelter, repair water, and salvage.
* **Next visible change.** A lateral lightning branch through ash will signal an [[arclight-phoenix]] hatch.

## Regional truths

* The lee is warm enough for repair crews to work despite the cold sea air.
* Giant bats, giant scorpions, and giant vultures occupy the volcanic slopes and vent caves.
* Lateral fire that branches through ash and holds for a breath is the pilot families' hatch sign.

## Geography

### Shape and boundaries

The Ashwall Islands are cold volcanic spires at the western edge of the charted Shattered Sea. The lee provides sheltered repair water, while the outer water leads toward the [[Galewall]].

| East | [[central-strait]] | Open sea | The cold spires give way to the central route and its settled traffic. |

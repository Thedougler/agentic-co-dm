# Karath

![[karath-overview.png|Karath overview: blackwater mouths behind reef gaps]]

> [!narration] Karath
> Karath is a rainforest island the size of a small country, its coast broken by river mouths running black as strong tea behind gaps in the reef. No beach shows anywhere a boat could land. Wet cuts run inland from the river mouths toward huts and pens under the trees, and bracket fungus climbs every trunk along the water. From its nearest shore, another green island sits only a short swim away across the channel.

*A closed Grung island in the [[verdant-teeth]], where the clans raid, breed giant vermin, and grow gold in secret, and where the Gold caste keeps sending compelled Grung across the channel to [[aruhe]].*

## Travel

| Route | Connects | Time | Risk | Advantage |
| ----- | -------- | ---- | ---- | --------- |
| The eastern chain route | [[Midchain]] ↔ Karath's reef gaps | Days by sail | Raid fleets, and exposed water | The way raid reports and the Grung fleet travel. |
| A reef gap | Open water ↔ the black river mouths | An hour of piloting | Darts and arrows from the treeline at any unapproved hull, with no warning hail | The only way onto the island. |
| The wet cuts | River mouths ↔ hatchery huts and pens | Hours on foot or wading | Cuts flood in minutes; movement becomes wading, swimming, or climbing | The hatcheries and captive pens. |
| The near channel | Karath's nearest shore ↔ [[aruhe]]'s rim | Half a mile of open water | Current and sharks | It reaches Aruhe's rim only, never its heart. Aruhe is country-sized too. |

**Regional rule.** No landing is sanctioned. Every hull that comes through a reef gap without Grung escort draws fire before it reaches the river mouth. The tell is Grung watchers visible in the trees above each gap.

**Retreat.** Back out through the reef gap, or swim the channel to Aruhe. Neither is safe unless the party has made the water survivable.

## Places

- **The reef gaps and black river mouths.** The only ways in, each watched.
- **The hatcheries.** Captive casters, dosed on toxin, push ordinary toads, snakes, and spiders into giants in wet pens.
- **The captive pens.** Fighting-age captives from the raids enter a slave pipeline here.
- **The gold farms.** Hidden inland and guarded by Gold-caste agents, they grow the rare toxin that gives true gold-tier color. Lower castes do not know they exist.

## Factions and Threats

- **[[grung-clans]].** They hold everything past the treeline. They want captives, labor, and secrecy, and they answer any approach with force.
- **The Gold caste.** It rules the clans and keeps the gold farms. It wants the two graves at Aruhe's [[memorial-grove]] destroyed, because its reports say Aruhe's curse is anchored there ([[two-grave-orders]]). It compels Grung with [[grung-authority-seal]]s and sends them across the channel.
  - [ ] **First sign.** A new party of compelled Grung crosses to Aruhe's rim.
  - [ ] **Escalation.** The party carries fire pots and oil inland.
  - [ ] **Crisis.** They burn toward the Grove.
- **The trajectory.** Unchecked, Karath takes more captives, breeds more giant vermin, and patrols its water more aggressively.

## Rumors

- "The raiders come from an island with black rivers." True: Karath. It points to [[pursue-the-grung-raiding-fleet]] and [[the-taken-314]].
- "The Grung never set foot on the green island next door." True of every free Grung: they are taught from birth to avoid Aruhe. Compelled ones are sent anyway.

**Secret.** The gold farms are secret even from the lower castes. A trace of diet or sealing that does not match public caste teaching points toward them. [[simone-tabarnack]] has never received Karath toxin, and her stalled partial gold does not come from the Karath process; her [[dravosi-crown]] stock is a substitute.

**Secret.** Karath captured [[hinewai]], an elf archdruid, and put her to work in the hatcheries. The toxin rotted most of her memory. Her hatred of the Grung, and of anyone who takes, survived. She and one companion swam the channel to Aruhe, and he drowned in sight of the shore.

**Secret.** A raid trail, a captive's testimony, or an unusual poison connects the Midchain's losses to Karath. A caster's measured antidote points to the captive pipeline. Exposing the gold farms may break the lower castes' trust; rescuing captives may make the raid fleet strike sooner.

# The Quiet

![[aruhe-the-quiet.jpg|The Quiet, rainforest where small life has been eaten out]]

> [!narration] The Quiet
> The Quiet is rainforest so thick that its trunks stand broader than doorways and its leaves could roof a hallway, with game trails running under curtains of hanging root and vine. Mossy stones edge the trails, and wet leaves lie pressed flat into the mud. Stone-plated pears hang beside the roots and heavy guavas bow the branches overhead. There is no hum of insects and no bird calls, only water dripping and leaves dragging softly against each other, and sight fails a few paces off the trail.

*Inland rainforest on [[aruhe]], where [[hinewai]] has drawn the Calveno survivors deep toward her garden.*

**Navigation.** Off a trail, sight fails beyond 10 feet. The forest is Difficult Terrain and hides anyone more than 10 feet away. Keeping a heading off-trail is **Wisdom (Survival)** `DC 14` per hour; a failure costs an hour.

**Rest and supply.** Fallen [[stonepear]] and [[giants-guava]] are common and safe to eat. [[ghost-plum]]s hang under the wet leaves, and fewer fall. Clean water runs in every stream.

**Regional rule.** The silence is real. [[silence-moths]] have eaten out the insects and small birds, so the only sounds are water, leaves, and whatever is moving. A creature that moves louder than a walk can be heard 100 feet off, and the party hears anything bigger than a dog coming the same way. The tell is the first minute under the roof: no hum, no calls.

**Stillbloom.** Along the Quiet–Marshes game trails and in sunbreaks, a charged [[stillbloom]] stalk fires when a grounded Small or larger creature within 20 feet moves more than 10 feet, Dashes, jumps, falls, or is forced 10 feet or more. Each creature within 15 feet makes a **Dexterity** saving throw `DC 14`. On a failure it takes `2d6` Piercing damage and a spine lodges in it, and it then makes a **Constitution** saving throw `DC 12` or suffers Exposure. Walking 10 feet or less per turn, throwing an object to trigger the volley, flying over the root mat, or taking total cover during the reload all avoid it.

**Snakewood.** Under an active [[snakewood]] colony, a Small or larger creature walking the trail makes a **Dexterity** saving throw `DC 14`. On a failure it takes `1d6` Bludgeoning damage, is Grappled (escape **Strength (Athletics)** or **Dexterity (Acrobatics)** `DC 14`), and is pulled 15 feet up toward the canopy. A Grappled creature takes `2d6` Acid damage at the start of each of its turns, rising by `1d6` each round to `4d6`. A severed section (AC 13, 15 HP) drops its prey. A moving object baits the strike, and solid cover overhead prevents it.

> [!narration] On the road
> You walk in single file under a roof of leaves so thick the light turns green, and the only sound is your own feet in the wet mud. Every few hundred paces a little pile of fallen fruit sits on a broad leaf beside the trail, laid there by someone's hands. By late afternoon the trees stand closer, and the next pile is the last thing you can see before the green closes in.

## Places

- [[cutoff-lip]], [[print-braid]], [[spoke-ring]], and [[star-cut]] — the Calveno trail north from the river, which the party follows from the [[river-slack-basin]].
- [[lava-tubes]] — a smoking skylight in the Long Meadow, where four Calveno have been trapped since a terror-bird drove them in.
- [[the-long-meadow]] — the one grass cut where the roof opens, and two terror-birds' ground.
- [[the-burnt-road]] — the scar the last Grung expedition burned toward the Grove.
- [[the-pantry]] — the Calveno survivors' clearing under the great vine.
- [[memorial-grove]] — [[hinewai]]'s memorial at the forest's heart.

## Factions and Threats

- **[[hinewai]].** She counts every Calveno who ate fallen fruit as hers, and she has drawn most of them to the Pantry. Her voice speaks from the dark to anyone who has eaten her fruit, and she calls any Grung on the road an invader.
- **[[talon-skarn]].** He wants the [[fate-spinner]]. After his dawn attempt at the Slack Basin, he shadows the party from high air, loses sight of them under the roof, and comes in on foot at dusk.
- **The Gold caste of [[karath]].** It wants the graves at the Grove destroyed. Its last expedition lies dead on the Burnt Road, and it will send another.
  - [ ] **First sign.** Old spent seals beside Grung bones along the routes inland.
  - [ ] **Escalation.** A fresh Grung party lands at [[western-landing]] with fire pots.
  - [ ] **Crisis.** Smoke rises from the Quiet on the line of the Burnt Road.

## Rumors

- "The woman in the woods has orange eyes, and she only talks at night." True: it is [[hinewai]]'s voice. It points to [[spoke-ring]] and [[the-pantry]].
- "Nothing sings in the Quiet because something ate the singers." True: [[silence-moths]]. It points to the dawn and dusk swarms.
- "Follow the fruit piles and you'll find the rest of us." True: the walking camp left them. It points to [[the-pantry]].

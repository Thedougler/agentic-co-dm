# Southern Midchain

## Map



The southern row is the smallest region of the Midchain, and the hardest to land on. Its islands are steep volcanic knots that rise from the water at an angle and keep climbing. Terraces cut into every slope that will hold them. Beaches here run only a few boat lengths. Below the row, the shelf ends, and the open sea runs south.

The soil is extraordinary, and almost none of it exists. That arithmetic, plus [[grung-clans|Grung]] pressure coming down off the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] for generations, has produced a culture that answers an unfamiliar hull instead of questioning it. Southern islanders are not lawless, and they simply do not care what a stranger thinks of the arrangement.

> [!narration] Narration
> The islands come up out of deep water as ridges, green and near-vertical, terraced from the waterline to the cloud in steps too regular to be anything but cut by hand.
> 
> One cone off the bow carries a rust-dark scar down its whole flank, where something once broke through and reached the sea. A hot mineral tang drifts off the water. Up on the terraces, a goat bell clatters twice, then stops.
> 
> A single beach sits in sight, barely as wide as your ship is long. Boats rest pulled up on the sand. People stand beside them, watching. None has moved since your ship came into view.

## Routes & Access

Deep water runs right up to most of these islands, so a ship can stand close in. That rarely helps, though: there is nowhere to put a boat once it does. Anchorages are few and small. Someone on the terrace above watches every one of them.

No pilot trade exists here. Passage through the row is straightforward, but landing means negotiating with whoever is standing on the beach, conducted before anyone steps out of the boat. Something the island cannot grow buys more goodwill than coin does.


## Hazards

Lee shores offer deep water but no holding ground. Sudden downdrafts off the cones can knock a boat flat in a channel. Ash and steam vents mark the active islands. Surf on beaches that looked calm from a cable out can swamp a landing boat fast.

A landing party that pushes past a refusal will meet resistance every time. The terraces above every beach favor the people who farm them and work against anyone climbing.

## Hooks

- The southern islands grow things that will not grow anywhere else in the Sea, and will not trade them to a stranger.
- [[grung-clans|Grung]] pressure from the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] has been constant for generations, and the row has never once asked the [[chain-council|Chain Council]] for help.
- Somebody has been landing on the southern islands at night, and the families disagree about who.

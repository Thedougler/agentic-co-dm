# Northern Midchain

## Map



North of the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]], the chain runs as a packed strip, two or three islands deep. The channels between them run so narrow that a mast shows over the ridge before the hull rounds it. Above the strip, the shelf drops into the deep blue of the [[central-strait|Central Strait]] and its patrol lanes.

Villagers build everything here to escape notice. Settlements sit in coves on the sheltered side, behind a stockade and a headland. A lookout stands on the high ground, and a second one hides where nobody points. Murrat breaks that pattern: it holds and defends openly. Every other village in the strip treats it as a useful place to send trouble instead.

## Routes & Access

The strip is the Midchain's back door between the western approaches and the eastern waters. Smugglers favor it for the same reason it stays dangerous: these channels hide a ship from the Strait, and from anyone who might come to help.

Passage runs on consent. A village that does not know a hull lets it through once and watches. The second pass earns the hull a boat sent out to meet it. Pilots exist but work only for people they can place, and a stranger's coin is not placement.


## Hazards

A ship that cannot reverse risks the narrow channels, and reefs close the sheltered side at low water. Lookouts report before they challenge.

[[grung-npc|Grung]] raiders come up from the south in the dark. Light finds the channel empty again. Crown cutters work the northern edge and board any hull that leaves the strip. The real hazard sits in the middle, where both threats meet and the channels run narrowest.

## Hooks

- [[murrat|Murrat]] attacks any non-[[moucheron|Moucheron]] on sight, and the villages around it have learned to route trouble that way on purpose.
- A cove village will trade passage for a problem solved, and the problem is always the neighbours.
- [[grung|Grung]] raids are coming further north each season, and nobody in the strip will say so where a Crown officer can hear it.

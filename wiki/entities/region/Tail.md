# Tail

> [!narration] Narration
> The sea narrows into fewer, taller islands, their sharp cliffs rising through late fog. Cold water lies dark between them. Sparse staging traffic moves below the cliff faces, and every gust carries brine, wet stone, and the warning that the next landmark may be the last.

> **DM thesis.** The Tail makes the party choose between the last reliable staging support and a faster, less-supported approach to the Maw while navigation becomes less trustworthy.

## Current state

**Status quo.** The northern and southern arcs pinch together here before the Drowned Maw. Fewer islands, taller cliffs, cold trench-coloured water, late fog, and lying compasses define the approach. Pilot families and staging crews still move through the pinch, but the unnamed staging island before the Maw remains the last-supplies and last-hands stop under a name not yet fixed.

* **Recent change.** No separate recent change is recorded for the Tail.
* **Pressure.** Fog, cliff-bound routes, and unreliable navigation make the next landmark difficult to trust.
* **Opportunity.** The staging island offers last supplies and honest gossip before the trench.
* **Next visible change.** A compass will lie, the fog will close, or the next landmark will disappear before the party can use it.

## Regional truths

* The two arcs narrow into fewer, taller islands before the Maw.
* Late fog and lying compasses are ordinary features of the approach.
* The unnamed staging island performs the same last-supplies and last-hands function as [[Fathomrush]] under another name.

## Geography

### Shape and boundaries

The Tail is the eastern pinch of the Shattered Sea. The Midchain lies back along the island routes; the Drowned Maw begins at the trench approach; the Outer Reach lies beyond it.

| Edge | Neighbor | Crossing / boundary | What changes across it |

| Beyond | [[outer-reach]] | Past the Maw. | No resupply or pilot-trusted landmark remains. |

No separate subregions are established for the Tail. The unnamed staging island is a key place within the region, not a second region.

### Landmarks

* **[[drowned-maw]].** The trench and planar boundary that determines the eastern route.
* **[[Fathomrush]].** A western staging town whose dives reach [[Shelfworks]]; it is the named comparison for the unnamed island's last-hands function.
* **Unnamed staging island.** The last-supplies and last-hands stop before the Maw.

## Travel

### Structure

* **Travel structure.** Known routes through a narrowing island chain.
* **Navigation.** Compasses lie in the approach; pilots and visible landmarks matter more than instruments.
* **Weather / season.** Late fog routinely removes cliffs and last landmarks from view.
* **Rest / supply.** The unnamed staging island is the last-supplies stop before the Maw; [[Fathomrush]] stages dives on [[Shelfworks]].
* **Regional rule.** Do not treat a compass reading as proof of position in the Tail.

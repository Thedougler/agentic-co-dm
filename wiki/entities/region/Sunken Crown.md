# Sunken Crown

>

## At a Glance

[[Sunken Crown]] sits east of the [[Drowned Maw]] in the [[Outer Reach]]. The source names it structurally unstable and subsiding, and as the primary route eastward.

Low islands ring a Blue Hole so deep a sounding line runs out. The rim holds black stone, salt grass, and old waterlines. [[Keth Naar]], [[Vel Orn]], [[Ral-Arn]], [[Dath-Mar]], and [[Meth-Var]] sit on that rim. Without the [[Pearl of Souls]] the shrine's deepest function is failing. [[Sawek]] hunts below.

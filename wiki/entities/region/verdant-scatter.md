# Verdant Scatter

> [!narration] Narration
> The Verdant Scatter is two green island arcs between Galewall and the Tail: Crown forts lie north and the Midchain crowds the small ground south. Reef, sails, and unofficial routes join them. Surf and inspection calls share the same brine as wet timber and forest cargo.

## Current state

**Status quo.** The chain is inhabited and connected by reef, sails, pilots, and unofficial routes. The northern arc is [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] water; the southern arc is [[midchain|Midchain]]; the [[central-strait]] runs between them, and the arcs meet at the [[tail|Tail]].

* **Recent change.** No region-wide change is established on this page.
* **Pressure.** Inspection calls and incomplete charting make movement costly; no separate macro-front is established.
* **Opportunity.** A party can choose an official road, learn an unofficial route, or find a named island without turning every small place into a new government.
* **Next visible change.** The next visible difference is whichever route the party or an active power uses: an inspection call, a pilot's signal, or a vessel taking reef water.

## Regional truths

* **Two arcs.** The northern arc is the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]]; the southern arc is the [[midchain|Midchain]].
* **The strait joins them.** The [[central-strait]] runs between the arcs, with the [[tail|Tail]] at their meeting point.
* **Routes are plural.** Official roads and routes learned outside the official chart both move people and cargo through the chain.
* **The name has a boundary.** Keep Verdant Scatter distinct from the five-island [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] band.

## Geography

### Shape and boundaries

The Verdant Scatter is an inhabited chain of two green island arcs between the Galewall/Ashwalls and the Tail/Maw. Crown water and forts occupy the north; Midchain small ground, councils, and free ports occupy the south. Reef and the central strait provide the movement logic, while forest cargo and wet timber mark the chain's working traffic.

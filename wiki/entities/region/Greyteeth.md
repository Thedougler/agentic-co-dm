## Current state

**Status quo.** Long pale ridges break the surface between the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] and [[central-strait]]. The reefs force indirect travel, local pilots remain necessary, and Crown ledgers contain wrecks that may not all be navigation failures.

* **Recent change.** No dated change is established.

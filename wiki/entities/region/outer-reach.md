# Outer Reach

> [!narration] Narration
> Outer Reach is cold water past the Maw with no resupply and no pilot-trusted landmark. Currents reverse under the hull. Deep salt and wet stone mark sea that offers no harbour.

> **DM thesis.** Outer Reach is a route-edge expedition where the party must choose what to spend when the map, current, and story stop behaving normally.

## Current state

**Status quo.** Outer Reach is lawless water east of the [[drowned-maw]]. There is no colonial map, no resupply, and no landmark a sane helmsman trusts. The nearest authority is three weeks west, while [[sunken-crown]] marks the primary route eastward and [[keth-naar]] is the furthest reliable landmark.

* **Recent change.** [[Doldrums]] drift pressure east of the Maw has made the route especially dangerous.
* **Pressure.** Currents reverse against the tables, and records of ships that did not return leave crews without a reliable forecast.
* **Opportunity.** [[redwind-isles]] are under-charted, and an ancient blue dragon may rule them.
* **Next visible change.** A reversal under the hull forces a vessel to choose between trusting an old heading and spending supplies to wait for a new one.

## Regional truths

* The Maw is the only known door into the Outer Reach.
* No resupply point or pilot-trusted landmark exists beyond it.
* The water's current can reverse against the tables.
* The region is a route edge, not a blank invitation to invent settlements.

## Travel

### Structure

* **Travel structure.** Pointcrawl between the Maw, the primary eastward route, and isolated landmarks.
* **Scale.** One travel leg represents one week of open-water passage.
* **Procedure.** At the start of each leg, the navigator chooses a bearing, checks the current, and records supplies before the crew rests.
* **Navigation.** Staying on course is difficult because currents can reverse against the tables and no pilot-trusted landmark confirms the heading.
* **Weather / season.** The [[Doldrums]] drift pressure east of the Maw; cold water and sudden current changes routinely alter travel.
* **Rest / supply.** No reliable resupply exists until the vessel returns west to authority or reaches a confirmed destination.
* **Regional rule.** A current reversal changes the vessel's heading unless the crew spends time and supplies holding it; the tell is water running against the wake.

## Fronts and pressures

## Front or threat

* **Impulse / goal.** The [[Doldrums]] drift pressure eastward and pushes vessels away from the Maw's known continuity.
* **Impending consequence.** A vessel that follows the drift loses its last reliable route and reaches water with no mapped destination.
* **Affected.** [[drowned-maw]], [[sunken-crown]], [[keth-naar]], and every ship crossing the Outer Reach.
* **Signals.** Cold water runs against the wake, the ship's heading changes without a matching wind, and old route marks stop agreeing.

* **Ancient blue dragon.** Survivors describe the [[redwind-isles]] shoreline inconsistently.

## What can be found

* **Resource.** A ship can preserve its remaining supplies by turning back before the last reliable landmark; the resource is time, not a new store.
* **Shelter.** [[keth-naar]] is the furthest reliable landmark and the last known place to seek a pilot's bearing; reaching it costs the route's remaining supplies.
* **Wonder.** Nameless stone rises from deep water where no quarry is known, giving a crew a landmark that no chart can yet explain.
* **Hidden lore.** Records of ships that did not return establish that the region's danger is older than one current reversal.
* **Shortcut.** Tracking the current reversal rather than fighting it can reveal a route toward the eastern water, but the route is not mapped.

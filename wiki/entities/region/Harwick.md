# Harwick

> [!narration] Narration
> Deep water holds a naval yard, Admiralty buildings, and a town packed against a fortified bight. Docks, yards, and guns share the same basin. The northern coast takes open swell; eastern marsh channels offer small craft a way around the Yard's entry points. Hunter sharks work the slipway scraps, and no Harwick sailor swims off the wharf.

## Current state

**Status quo.** Harwick is the Crown's army island, with the Naval Yard in the sheltered southwest Bight, Admiralty buildings behind the same walls, and Harwick Town filling the eastern edge. The Bight is the largest anchorage in the Scatter that can take a ship of the line.

* **Recent change.** [[hcs-sovereign]] and [[hcs-ordinance]] have been built here, keeping Harwick central to Crown fleet power.
* **Pressure.** The Admiralty controls patrol orders, charts, commissions, and sealed orders; nothing past the Yard gate opens without Admiralty say-so.
* **Opportunity.** Shipwrights, chandlers, rope-makers, tar merchants, and the naval rating pool make Harwick the best place to repair, provision, or learn fleet procedure.
* **Next visible change.** A sealed order can move a captain, company, or vessel without a civic petition, forcing the town and [[governor-voss]]'s civic authority to react after the fact.

## Regional truths

* The Naval Yard and Admiralty share one fortified wall, so shipbuilding and command authority answer to the same gate.
* Harwick Town survives by servicing the fleet: chandlers, rope-makers, tar, lodging, drink, and naval barracks crowd the eastern edge.
* Deep water makes the Bight a strategic anchorage, but the northern coast's open swell and eastern marsh channels make approaches unequal.
* Crown fleet power is visible here; [[hcs-sovereign]] and [[hcs-ordinance]] were built on the island.

## Geography

### Shape and boundaries

Harwick is an island organized around a sheltered southwest Bight. The Naval Yard and Admiralty occupy the fortified southwest basin; Harwick Town fills the eastern edge; open swell dominates the north; marsh channels cut the east.

### Landmarks

* **Naval Yard and Admiralty walls.** The fortified southwest complex controls the island's ships, charts, commissions, and sealed orders.
* **The Bight.** The Scatter's largest anchorage for a ship of the line, and the first place a major Crown arrival becomes visible.

## Travel

### Structure

* **Map.** Use the Bight, Yard, town, northern coast, and eastern marsh channels as pointcrawl nodes.
* **Travel structure.** Pointcrawl by ship or small craft.
* **Scale.** One route leg represents one day of coastal or inter-island travel.
* **Procedure.** Each leg requires one navigation check, then one weather or inspection consequence.
* **Navigation.** The Bight is easy to identify from offshore; the marsh channels require local knowledge or a chart.
* **Weather / season.** Northern open swell routinely makes the exposed approach slower and less safe than the lee of the Bight.
* **Rest / supply.** Harwick Town reliably provides lodging, drink, rope, tar, and chandlery, but a ship under inspection cannot leave freely.
* **Regional rule.** Sealed orders outrank civic petitions. **Tell:** an Admiralty messenger, marked boat, or posted order changes who may pass before the town hears why.

## What can be found

* **Resource.** Harwick's chandlers, rope-makers, tar sellers, and naval rating pool can repair or provision a vessel.
* **Shelter.** A licensed lodging house in Harwick Town offers safety and information, but its keeper copies ship names for the Admiralty.
* **Wonder.** The copper-sheathed [[hcs-sovereign]] in the Bight makes Crown authority visible across the water; watching it reveals which harbours react first.
* **Hidden lore.** The Admiralty and [[governor-voss]]'s civic authority are deliberately separate systems.
* **Shortcut.** A tide chart and local pilot turn the eastern marsh channels into a way around the Yard's main entry points.

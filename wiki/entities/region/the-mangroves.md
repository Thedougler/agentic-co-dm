# The Mangroves
![[aruhe-the-mangroves.jpg|The Mangroves, impenetrable tidal forest of roots and black water]]
## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> The forest leaves the ground long before it meets the sea. Trunks pack black water; roots drop in cages, arches, spears, and walls until a foot barely fits between. Canopy seals daylight green. Ghost plums hang in fresher pockets; almost-black [[rotheart]] clings to old vinewood above dark channels. Salt, mud, flower, decay, and stagnant heat hang together; insects crawl bark and rise wherever warmth moves. Far beyond the roots, surf mutters — close enough to hear, completely unreachable.

---
## At a Glance
Southern end of [[the-marshes]], where freshwater forest becomes tidal swamp before Aruhe's coast. Technically touches ocean; is not a harbor. From offshore, seams look like channels; inside they trap hulls and bodies. Observe from the sea to skip; enter only as desperation or from inland marsh slow-down. Known landing remains [[western-landing]].

## If the party

- **Approach from sea:** Miles of wall, no beach, no reliable opening — observe offshore to skip.
- **Search boat channel:** **Wisdom (Perception) or Wisdom (Survival) — `DC 15`** → Spot a narrow tidal seam.
- **Enter by rowboat/canoe:** **Dexterity (Water Vehicles) — `DC 15`** → Keep hull in a usable seam long enough to back out.
- **Force a vessel through:** Roots catch hull, poles, oars, rigging, passengers; noise carries in water and canopy.
- **Enter on foot from coast:** Desperation only; no practical shoreline route to [[western-landing]].
- **Enter from [[the-marshes]]:** Possible; increasingly slow as open water vanishes under roots.
- **Pole/wade under low branches:** [[snakewood]] over water — owner page for contact.
- **Step onto root-island clearing:** [[spiritpollen]] sacs — `15-foot` cloud, **Wisdom — `DC 15`** or Spirit-Haunted; cover/wind/disturb beyond 15 feet.
- **Move root maze:** Difficult Terrain minimum; dense stretches may crawl/climb/swim.
- **Apparently shallow water:** Depth inches to feet between roots. **Wisdom (Survival) — `DC 15`** firm line; failure → mud swallows or pocket drops, **Strength (Athletics) — `DC 13`** to pull free.
- **Swim:** Roots block stroke; hide places immediately beside swimmer.
- **Climb above water:** Sometimes easier; enters continuous canopy predator space.
- **Cut vegetation / use fire / rest:** Cut is slow, loud, and a living claim ([[taking-on-aruhe]]). Fire resisted by wet growth; smoke traps under canopy. Almost no open ground for rest.
- **Lose sight of ally:** A few trunks can hide someone under twenty feet away.

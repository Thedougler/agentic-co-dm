# Dreth

> [!narration] Narration
> Dreth is permanently drowned forest with no charted shoreline: trunks stand in water under canopy, so a hull must enter the channel or stay out. Root hollows and cut-lines thread the wet timber works. The air stays damp under green shade, and the water carries movement farther than sight.

## Current state

**Status quo.** Dreth is a drowned forest with no charted shoreline. Grung patrol its channel and timber works, while rare dry hummocks serve as garrison and work camps rather than hospitality.

* **Recent change.** The timber cut is moving south.
* **Pressure.** Patrol density rises as the cut advances, and the live-specimen window closes.
* **Opportunity.** Root hollows, cut-lines, and the [[dreth-orak-channel]] offer routes through the canopy.
* **Next visible change.** More patrols will appear along the southern cut if nobody interferes.

## Regional truths

* Dreth has no beach protocol because there is no charted beach.
* A hull must enter under canopy through a channel or remain outside the region.
* Flood and rain can open or erase the shallow crossing to [[Orak]].
* Rare dry hummocks are work or garrison camps, not safe hospitality.

## Geography

### Shape and boundaries

Dreth is the lower, wetter eastern Tooth of the [[verdant-teeth]]. Permanently drowned forest encloses channels, root hollows, timber cut-lines, and occasional dry hummocks. The eastern water offers the hidden approach; the southern timber cut becomes more watched as it advances.

| Edge | Neighbor | Crossing / boundary | What changes across it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| East | Eastern water | Enter under canopy by hull | Open water becomes drowned forest and hidden channels. |
| West | [[Orak]] | Rain-dependent shallow crossing | The route can vanish when conditions change. |
| North | [[verdant-teeth]] | No charted shoreline or route established | The forest remains wet and channel-bound. |
| South | Timber cut | Follow cut-lines or patrol routes | Patrol density rises as the cut moves south. |

# Grasslands

![[aruhe-the-grasslands.jpg|Grasslands, river-cut valleys of gold-green grass]]

## Look

Clear water winds through hot open valleys. Gold-green grass rises eight feet on both banks, bending near the channels and standing thick where the valley climbs toward the dark jungle rim. Redheart berries shine at wet bends, while giant's guavas rise above the grass at the forest edge.

## Situation now

The Grasslands are river-cut openings inside Aruhe's jungle, not a separate outer band. Water leads uphill toward [[Aruhe - River]] and [[Aruhe - Clear Lake]], while open ground leads toward [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]] and the terraces lead back to [[Aruhe - Old Gardens]].

## Moves that change the scene

- Walk through the tall grass; it is Difficult Terrain and heavily obscures beyond 10 feet.
- Follow the water uphill or take the open route toward the Grove.
- Follow forest-edge prints to [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Cutoff Lip|Cutoff Lip]], [[Aruhe - Grasslands - Print Braid|Print Braid]], and [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring|Spoke Ring]].
- Avoid [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]] at torn crossings, wallows, and flood-scoured hollows.
- Approach [[Aruhe - Unsaid Macaw|Unsaid Macaws]] plainly or leave their sight before their flock-talk spreads.
- Forage fallen fruit; plucking a living plant, fishing, trapping, or killing to carry flesh invokes [[Taking on Aruhe]].
- Investigate old Grung bones with Intelligence (Investigation) DC 13 to find spent [[Grung - Authority Seal|Grung Authority Seals]].

## Presence

[[Aruhe - Terror-Bird|Terror-Birds]] hold shaded rims and grass cuts. [[Aruhe - Spiguar|Spiguars]] own long channels and river bends, while [[Aruhe - Deerstalker|Deer-Stalkers]] use the grass as cover. [[Aruhe - River Otter|River Otters]], [[Aruhe - Bear-Elk|Bear-Elk]], and [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbits]] use the channels, crossings, and scored trees without entering the Razer-Grass.

## Connections

The terraces descend to Landing Bank. The valleys connect laterally with [[Aruhe - River]], uphill to Clear Lake, forward to the Grove, and north into [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest|the Quiet]].

# Kettil Ormsson

![[kettil-ormsson-banner]]

|               |                                                               |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Species**   | [[Dwarf]]                                                         |
| **Pronouns**  | he/him                                                        |
| **Location**  | [[Ormsson's Fittings]], [[Kalowe]]   |
| **Role**      | Hardware and arms merchant                                    |

Late forties, stocky and broad-shouldered even for a dwarf, dark-bearded. From the northern island chains (likely [[Harwick]]), he's been in the [[Midchain]] long enough to lose his accent. Kettil does not chat. He reads what a customer needs. He quotes a fair price. He left Crown work because colonial smiths ask too many questions.

The back shelf behind the curtain carries weapons he does not advertise; if someone asks without explaining themselves too much, he tells them what's there. If they over-explain, he loses interest.

He runs [[Ormsson's Fittings]], the shop closest to the main dry-dock. He works alone. He closes when the dry dock does. The curtain stays partly open. He alone decides when to pull it back.

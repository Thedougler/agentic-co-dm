# Uncertainty

The party's ship. Taken as HCS Surety. Renamed with no Imperial prefix.

## At a Glance

She is a Crown cutter that no longer wears Crown colors on the main approach. La Vasca cradled her in [[Calven and Calveno]]. Cobb's twelve-hour refit is done. She is kept moving off [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]] rather than anchored close.

## At the table

Prize crew named at taking: Geoffrey Draves, Sem Holst, Alys Kuiper, Old Faas, Thunk, and Noor. Thunk signed a thousand gold of cannon on her credit. Two survivors from Aruhe are aboard, plus three Calveno captives pulled from hurricane wreckage. Hide Perrin, Ruma, and Jean-Claude if Crown officers board again.

## Wiki facts

Barnaby Rook lost her. HCS Ordinance intercepted her two days out of Calveno under merchant cover. See [[journal/Session 02 - Recap]], [[journal/Session 09 - Recap]], and [[Story So Far]].

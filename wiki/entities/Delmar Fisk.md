# Delmar Fisk

Party member. The table has heard him as both Delmar and Admiral Fisk.

## At a Glance

He is the man who asked Geoffrey Draves which side would keep him alive, then put Barnaby Rook in the water. He told the others, walking to the harbor in [[Calven and Calveno]], that he had commanded a five-ship privateer fleet, stolen the [[Pearl of Souls]], and watched the Drowned Maw wreck that fleet within hours.

## At the table

Play the slip into Admiral Fisk when pressure hits, and let Crissdalynn be the person who has already pulled him back once. He ended Otar the Foul with a chair and a shot through the eye while Catarina was in the thing's mouth. Shepherd Grigori has spoken of his Umberlee curse. He is training Geoffrey on the night watches.

## Wiki facts

Umberlee named the Pearl as her price after reading Delmar's offer out of his head. Catarina Da'Virelli gave him Lamarae's Fang. He is aboard *[[Uncertainty]]* at [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]]. See [[journal/Session 04 - Recap]] and [[Story So Far]].

## Interview Rounds

**Round 1, 2026-07-22** (Mapped from the archived Inbox write-up, not a live Q&A session. See note above.)

**1. Let's start simple. What's their name? Any aliases, nicknames, titles they've picked up? And is there a name that only one specific person ever called them (something private)?**
Delmar Atticus Fisk. Bought the title "Admiral" with charm before anyone official handed it to him. The youngest Admiral in living memory, with no private nickname on record.

**2. Before we get into anything else, paint me a picture. They walk through the door. What do I see?**
Sandy red hair in a messy topknot, thick beard and curled mustache shot through with grey, a long red admiral's coat over tall worn boots. He walks like every deck he stands on belongs to him. The charm lands before he speaks.

**3. What's their class? And I want to know *why*? Is it a calling, something they chose, or something life didn't give them much of a choice about?**
Swashbuckler Rogue, Variant Human. Not a calling. He came to the sea early (fishing boats, deckhand work, everything nautical absorbed from the ground up) because that was the work available, and built his own authority from there instead of inheriting it.

**4. Did they come into this with a Mortis (a flaw and a gift that connect together)? If so, what's the Mark, and do *they* know it's there?**
Mark: he is never fully alone in his own head. Five captains' voices surface at need or provocation, bringing someone else's instincts into his own decision-making. Gift: activating a captain's personality grants their skill proficiencies as borrowed mastery. He is fully aware of it. He knows five people paid with their souls because of a call he made.

**5. What do they want right now, more than anything? With what honesty do they reckon with it?**
Resurrect his five captains, or release them so they can find peace. He knows the truth. He'd compromise who he is to get there.

**6. By the end of all this (whatever this turns out to be), who do they want to *be*? Not what they want to do. Who. Do they even know yet?**
Stable. Forgiven, though he knows he must earn it. A version of himself he hasn't had time to think about becoming yet.

**7. What keeps them up at night? Something real, not monsters. Is there something specific they're afraid of losing?**
The weight of the call he made, knowing five captains paid with their souls for it. He's afraid of losing found family the same way again.

**8. What do they believe that most people around them would push back on? Have they ever actually said it out loud?**
Everyone has value regardless of station, even if they can't see it themselves. His mother's ethic, held onto even after people exploited it for advantage. The default is still generosity.

**9. Is there a line they won't cross? And have they ever come close to it?**
Loyalty to found family. He will not betray his own. He came close once: a crew member on his first boat murdered another in a drunken rage, and he nearly killed the man himself before stopping.

**10. What did their life look like before all this? Day to day, what were they actually doing before they became an adventurer?**
Raised by his mother (a woman treated as second-class who treated everyone as having value anyway). Never knew his father beyond secondhand sailor stories and a pocket watch his mother had pickpocketed off the man. Came to the sea early, from fishing boats and deckhand work to absorbing everything nautical from the ground up. He assembled his first crew without any business sense, failed often, and built loyalty through failure. Five of that crew stayed with him through every ship and rank, and each eventually captained one of his five vessels.

**11. What's the hardest thing they've had to survive, or do, to become the person we're meeting right now?**
The fleet's sinking. A rogue trench opened in the fleet's path and drove all five ships eighty feet down. [[Crissdalynn Khinriss|Crissdalynn]] dove from the crow's nest and caught him, carried up by her momentum and the buoyancy of his coat. At that moment, five captains flooded his mind. As a boy, survived an earlier shipwreck from a pirate attack and a dangerous stretch stranded with disgruntled survivors before escaping by raft.

**12. Do they carry something with a story (a weapon, trinket, or other worn object)? What is it, and where did it come from?**
His father's pocket watch (his mother's picture inside, which she pickpocketed off the man herself). Also his musket "The Duchess," named for his wife, and his rapier "The Baroness."

**13. What's the worst thing they've ever done? Did they mean to do it?**
The theft that triggered the fleet's sinking was his own call. He didn't intend the consequence, but five people paid with their souls for it. No one at the table knows.

**14. Whose opinion of them matters most? Where is that person now?**
[[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. The sole co-survivor of the [[*Red Lady*]] alongside him, still at his side.

**15. Is there anyone out there who considers them an enemy? Who, and why?**
The families of the sailors he lost, and the island council that funded the ships, most likely consider him an enemy. He has not reckoned with this directly.

**16. Who is the one person they'd trust with their life (not because that person is powerful, but just because they *trust* them)? What did that person do to earn it?**
Crissdalynn Khinriss. She dove from the crow's nest as the Maw closed and pulled him out. Her momentum and the buoyancy of his coat carried them both to the surface.

**17. How do they treat someone who can't do anything for them (a beggar, servant, or child)? Is that who his mother deliberately shaped him to be, or something he figured out on his own?**
He extends generosity by default. His mother's ethic, deliberately raised into him, not something he had to discover on his own. He knows the instinct is exploitable and people have exploited it. The default holds anyway.

**18. What's the one thing they want so badly they might compromise who they are to get it? And do they know that about themselves?**
Full resurrection of his five captains. The guardrails come off if it becomes possible, and he knows it about himself.

**19. What are they carrying that would change how people see them if it ever came out?**
That the fleet's sinking was his own call, not misfortune. Separately: his marriage to [[Serafina Windfall Fisk]] runs on an open arrangement he has never tested, and she does not know about the voices.

**20. Do they believe in the gods? And does it matter to them whether the gods believe in them?**
His faith is maritime. The Sea itself, and [[Umberlee]] as its face. It matters a great deal whether she believes in him. She took everything from him, needs him functional, and he has come to believe in her. It would be difficult not to.

## Combat Profile

### Fast Read

Mobile skirmisher. Sneak Attack 3d6 on the blunderbuss is the engine. Heel is getting dropped before his first turn.

### Counters & Synergy

- **Hard counters.** Solo alpha before he acts.
- **Soft counters.** Forced melee without Fancy Footwork room.
- **Amplifies / Depends on.** Rakish Audacity when he is the only one next to the target. Catarina's enchantment on the blunderbuss.

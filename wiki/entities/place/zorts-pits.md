# Zort's Pits

> [!narration] Zort's Pits
> A fenced dirt pit holds cages of exotic and working animals. Straw and animal musk hang in the air, and a litter of canal ratters works the near cages as live demonstration stock. Under the noise, a low growl comes from a covered cage at the back, and the cage's bars are scored at the height of a shoulder.

## At a Glance

Zort's Pits is [[zort|Zort]]'s animal business in [[le-paludi]], where working stock and exotic stock change hands and the [[Midchain]] reptile trade has one counter.

- **Who is here.** [[zort|Zort]] behind his desk, working the business with his feet; no staff and no second chair.
- **Danger.** The stock bites, and the covered cage at the back growls at anyone who comes near it without Zort's leave.
- **Draw.** Working animals, [[zorts-toe-claws|Zort's Toe-Claws]] off the nail on the desk, and the Midchain reptile contact Zort trades for work he cannot do himself.

## Features

- **The pit.** A fenced dirt floor ringed with cages, holding [[canal-ratter|canal ratters]] used as live demonstration stock, [[marsh-strider|marsh striders]], and [[fen-glass-newt|fen-glass newts]].
- **The desk.** Zort's chair, one bare foot on the wood doing the work, and the nail where overrun pairs of his [[zorts-toe-claws|toe-claws]] hang for sale.
- **The stock.** Working animals sell by the day and exotic animals by arrangement; larger sizes and special orders take a week.
- **The covered cage.** A cage at the back with a canvas over it and a growl under it. *"Scored bars at shoulder height."* Zort will not open it in front of a stranger, and he warns every visitor off the cages before he says hello.

## At the Table

- **Buy stock.** Zort sells a working animal, its handling gear, and the cage it travels in. Moving his stock without a hand on the latch is **Wisdom (Animal Handling) — `DC 13`**; a failure costs a bite and a lecture about the cages.
- **Ask for the Midchain contact.** He trades the reptile man's name rather than sells it, and the price is always work he cannot do himself. Pressing for the name without an offer gets a flat refusal and no second question.
- **Reach for the covered cage.** Zort stops talking. He does not stand, and he does not explain; he waits until the visitor's hand comes off the canvas. Anything past that is whatever the DM has waiting in it.
- **Ask why he keeps it.** He answers plainly once he trusts the asker: it is stock he cannot sell and will not kill.

## Secrets

**Secret.** The covered cage holds a live [[grinning-ape]] Zort took as debt off an [[aruhe]] crew. He keeps it covered because an open ape in a city street is a fight, a fine, and a story that follows the buyer home. The scored bars and the shoulder-height growl are the tells; a character who has seen Aruhe stock names the animal by its growl without opening the canvas.

# Zort's Pits

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> A fenced dirt pit holds cages of exotic and working animals. Straw and animal musk hang in the air, and a low growl carries from somewhere out of sight; a litter of canal ratters works the near cages as live demonstration stock.

## At a Glance

[[Zort's Pits]] is [[Zort]]'s animal business in [[le-paludi]], selling exotic and working animals and keeping a Mid-Chain reptile contact.

## If the party

- **Buy or ask.** The party can buy an animal or ask [[Zort]] for his Mid-Chain reptile contact; he handles the business with his feet.

## Who

[[Zort]] is the proprietor. The canon names no staff count; the near cages hold a litter of [[canal-ratter|canal ratters]], with other exotic and working animals in the pit.

## What

The pit has cages and animal stock, including [[canal-ratter|canal ratters]], [[marsh-strider|marsh striders]], and [[fen-glass-newt|fen-glass newts]]. [[Zort's Toe-Claws]] are also sold through the business.

## Where

The business is in [[le-paludi]]. No adjacent route or neighboring site is established in the current record.

## Why

The party comes here to buy animal stock, meet [[Zort]], or pursue the Mid-Chain reptile contact he offered in exchange for a working prosthetic.

# Le Paludi

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Le Paludi is a canal district of [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]] where taverns, general-goods shops, and alchemy work sit close to the water. [[al-fondale|Al Fondale]] keeps a cellar route toward the [[Warren]], [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]] works without a public sign, and [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]] keeps specialized goods beneath its counter.

## At a Glance

Le Paludi is the Calveno district that connects the named sites [[al-fondale|Al Fondale]], [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]], [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]], and [[la-vasca|La Vasca]]. Its useful pressures are trust, discreet trade, and access below the city through the Warren. The older Season 2 draft calls Le Paludi a fence town; that draft is not current canon.

## If the party

- **Needs a local contact.** Choose between Bice and Aldo at Al Fondale, Ettore at Casa Lupo, or Marta at Studio Orsini according to the information or goods sought.
- **Needs discreet access.** Treat each shop's threshold as a separate trust decision; the named sites do not share one automatic route.
- **Goes below the district.** Use the Warren connection where the owner page establishes it; do not assume every canal or shop opens underground.

## Who

Named district operators include [[bice-riva|Bice Riva]] and [[aldo-riva|Aldo Riva]] at Al Fondale, [[ettore-lupo|Ettore Lupo]] at Casa Lupo, and [[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]] at Studio Orsini. [[fen-le-paludi-pickpocket|Fen]] is also tied to Le Paludi. No district-wide authority or resident count is established.

## What

- Canals and the businesses connected to them.
- [[al-fondale|Al Fondale]], a tavern with a controlled cellar route toward the Warren.
- [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]], a general-goods shop with specialized items.
- [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]], an unmarked alchemy shop.
- [[la-vasca|La Vasca]], another named place in the district.

## Why

Le Paludi is where several practical and illicit routes overlap: the party can buy goods, ask for local memory, negotiate for alchemy, find a protected tavern route, or descend toward the Warren. Its value comes from the different owners controlling those routes, not from a single district authority.

# The Splint

> [!narration] The Splint
> A Rattkin healer bends over a patient's wound, stitching by hand. Boiled linen and old blood fill the room, and a wet green smell comes off a bundle of leaves hung by the door. Thread and needle are the only sounds until she finishes and turns to the doorway. A slate and a stub of chalk sit on the bench beside her, and she reaches for them before she speaks.

## At a Glance

The Splint is the [[warren|Warren]]'s only source of healing, run by [[colla|Colla]] for [[rattkin|Kin]] and surface folk alike.

- **Who is here.** [[colla|Colla]], the healer, alone with whoever is on her bench; she treats injured combatants from opposing sides in the same room by policy.
- **Danger.** Her brine-sage stock has eleven days left after a Dravosi trade restriction cut off her supplier, and a healer with nothing to work with is the first thing the Warren loses.
- **Draw.** The only healing below Calveno, a healer who hears everything said in her room, and a way in to the Warren's people for anyone she vouches for.

## Features

- **The treatment room.** Boiled linen, a bench, hung herbs, and the smell of old blood. There is no second room and no waiting area: whoever is hurt lies where the last patient lay.
- **Colla.** [[colla|Colla]] stitches by hand, never looks up until the wound is closed, and writes her instructions rather than explaining them aloud. She trained ten years under her aunt Tetta.
- **One room, both sides.** Colla treats injured combatants from opposing sides with identical attention, by policy, and no one has ever challenged it in her room.
- **The brine-sage.** A bundle of brine-sage hangs by the door. It is her working stock, supplied from above by the herbalist Dosia in [[le-paludi]] until the Dravosi restriction stopped the deliveries.

## At the Table

- **Ask for treatment as a surface party.** Colla takes Kin without a question and needs a [[rattkin|Kin]] introduction before she will see an outsider at all. **Charisma (Persuasion) — `DC 15`**, with a Kin vouching, gets an outsider treated at community rate instead of five times it; a failure means the premium price or a wait.
- **Pay the premium.** Surface rates are five times the Kin rate. Colla takes payment without interest in where it came from; she does not discount for a good cause.
- **Bring brine-sage.** The party can end her eleven-day problem by carrying a supply down from [[le-paludi]] or by buying past the restriction. The herb is her price for almost any favour, including an introduction into the Warren.
- **Talk while she works.** Colla hears everything that passes through her room and repeats none of it for free. Asking after Warren inhabitants means asking after patients, and she decides what a patient's business is.
- **Start a fight in the room.** She stops stitching, and the room clears of everyone who wanted no part of it. The offender is treated last, if at all.

## Connections

- [[warren]] — the settlement; The Splint is its only source of healing.
- [[colla]] — the healer who runs it.
- [[le-paludi]] — the surface city above the Warren, where her supplier Dosia works as an herbalist.

## History

Colla trained under her aunt Tetta for ten years before she took the room, and the policy of treating both sides in one room has outlasted every argument brought against it.

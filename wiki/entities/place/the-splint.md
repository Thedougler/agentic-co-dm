# The Splint

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> A Rattkin healer bends over a patient's wound, stitching by hand. Boiled linen and old blood fill the room; thread and needle are the only sounds until the healer finishes and turns to the door.

## At a Glance

[[The Splint]] is [[Warren]]'s only source of healing, run by [[colla|Colla]] for Kin and surface folk.

## If the party

- **Seek treatment.** A Kin introduction lets an outsider reach Colla; Kin pay community rates, while surface folk pay five times that rate.

## Who

[[colla|Colla]] is the healer. She treats injured combatants from opposing sides in the same room; the record names no staff count.

## What

The room provides wound treatment and healing. Colla's policy is to give opposing combatants the same attention, and she writes instructions instead of explaining them aloud.

## Where

The Splint is in [[Warren]]. The current record does not establish its adjoining route or neighboring room.

## Why

The party comes here for healing, to reach Warren inhabitants through Colla, or to help secure the brine-sage supply she needs before her eleven-day stock runs out.

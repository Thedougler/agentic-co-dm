## At a Glance

The Old Gardens are the way inland from the beach, and the first place where Aruhe's fruit grows thick enough to tempt someone into picking.

- **Who is here.** Troops of three to five [[grinning-ape]]s in the canopy, and packs of four to six [[wolfrabbit]]s on the collapsed first terraces at dawn and dusk.
- **Danger.** [[vine-lash]]es among the hanging vines, [[young-snakewood]] in the low branches, [[grubnade]]s, and [[black-lotus]] flowers.
- **Draw.** [[redheart-berry]] and [[giants-guava]] everywhere, and the terrace route to the [[grasslands]].

## Features

- **The terraces.** *Stone terraces stacked up a forest slope.* Ancient, and older than [[hinewai]]'s law. Broken steps now hold yam vines, ordinary citrus, and green-scummed irrigation ditches.
- **The terrace route.** It climbs from [[western-landing]] past [[landing-bank]] to the [[grasslands]]. Following a terrace edge skips most of the fruit and the dangers among it.
- **The green route.** It leaves the terraces under the trees and runs into [[the-quiet]].
- **The hanging vines.** Some of the rope-vines over the trail lanes are [[vine-lash]]es.
- **The low-canopy lanes.** [[young-snakewood]] hunts from the connected low branches. Adult [[snakewood]] lives farther inland.
- **The hot yellow blooms.** A disturbed [[grubnade]] swells and hisses, then bursts at the end of the turn. Each creature within 5 feet makes a **Dexterity** saving throw `DC 12`, taking `2d6` Fire damage on a failure or half as much on a success.
- **The black flowers.** A table-sized [[black-lotus]] restrains a creature within 10 feet and drains its highest available spell slot. An ember-veined [[lesser-black-lotus]] answers a disturbance with a ranged spell at the nearest spellcaster.
- **The fruit.** [[redheart-berry]] crowds the terraces, and [[giants-guava]] follows the inland tree line. Fallen fruit is safe to take.

## At the Table

- **If anyone picks from a living plant.** It is a claim under [[taking-on-aruhe]], and two [[wolfrabbit]]s come out of the terraces.
- **If the party walks beneath the vines.** The first vine lash hangs 10 feet over the stair cut on the second terrace.
- **If the party returns with the Calveno.** The terrace route is the last leg back to the beach and the ship.

## Connections

- [[grasslands]] — up the terrace route, past [[landing-bank]].
- [[the-quiet]] — along the green route under the trees.
- [[western-landing]] — down the slope to the beach.

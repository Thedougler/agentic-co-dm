## At a Glance

Star Cut is the last open sightline before the Quiet closes in, and the straight way north toward [[memorial-grove]].

- **Who is here.** Nobody.
- **Danger.** None by day. [[bloodhawk]]s hunt the open sky above the slit.
- **Draw.** The Calveno's old watch fire, fallen guavas, and the aisle north to the Grove.

> [!narration] Star Cut
> Star Cut is a straight aisle of wet black earth running under the forest roof, aimed at a single narrow slit of open sky where the treetops fail to meet. A low ring of stones sits against one side, with two clay bowls beside it, and on the other side old bedrolls lie pressed against the roots. Heavy ribbed fruit, pink and gold, hangs over the bedrolls, and more lies split in the leaf litter below. The trees crowd both sides so close that the aisle is the only way to walk, and beyond the roots a thin stream mutters out of sight.

## Features

- **The aisle.** *A straight aisle of wet black earth.* Ordinary ground. The forest on either side is Difficult Terrain and hides anyone more than 10 feet in.
- **The sky slit.** *A single narrow slit of open sky.* The only gap in the roof for a mile, and smoke rises through it. Climbing the trees to the gap is **Strength (Athletics)** `DC 10`.
- **The watch fire.** *A low ring of stones, two clay bowls.* The Calveno kept their night watch here, watching the sky slit for bloodhawks. The bowls are theirs, shaped from riverbank clay.
- **The bedrolls.** *Old bedrolls pressed against the roots.* Left behind by the camp when it moved to [[spoke-ring]].
- **The guavas.** [[giants-guava]] hangs over the bedrolls, and fallen ones in the litter are safe to take.

## At the Table

- **If anyone takes from the island here.** Picking, cutting, or killing to carry off flesh is a claim under [[taking-on-aruhe]], and the nearest [[vine-lash]] answers.

## Connections

- [[the-quiet]] — east, past the watch fire. The aisle itself runs on into deeper Quiet toward [[memorial-grove]], about an hour's walk.
- [[spoke-ring]] — south, a quarter hour's walk.
- [[the-river]] — west, beyond the roots and the stream.

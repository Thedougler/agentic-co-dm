# Vantry's Choice

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Two harbors open side by side in the pale eastern reef, matching so closely that the distinction seems to vanish at a distance. The water is shallow and clear around both entrances, and the scattered islands leave little shelter between them. Ships angle toward one harbor, then correct away from the other without explanation; the avoided water remains empty.

## At a Glance

Vantry's Choice is a named site in the eastern [[Midchain]]: two identical harbors stand side by side, but one is universally and inexplicably avoided. The eastern waters have unreliable charts and long stretches between shelter, so the choice is a practical navigation decision as well as an unanswered local taboo. The site can be bypassed, but doing so gives up the chance to compare the harbors or investigate why one is avoided.

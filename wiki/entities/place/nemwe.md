# Nemwe

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> A stone watchtower leans off true above the treeline, its lower door long since kicked in.
>
> Ash still sits warm in the signal-fire cradle at the top. Somebody was here recently, and left in a hurry.
>
> The brazier's iron arm still swings freely on its hinge, aimed out toward the shipping lanes.

---
## At a Glance

Nemwe is an abandoned Dravosi Crown watchtower island in the [[midchain-north|Northern Midchain]]. The Crown raised it and abandoned it when the patrol route shifted north. The tower is unclaimed, but its signal-fire still works: whoever holds it for a night can call a passing cutter in or warn one off.

---
## If the party

- **Climb to the signal-fire.** The party can reach the working cradle and choose whether to call a passing cutter or warn one off.
- **Enter through the lower door.** The kicked-in door leads to collapsed stores occupied by giant rats. Bedding down there means sharing the shelter with them.
- **Follow the old path.** The path reaches the tower without losing sight of the shoreline. Bushwhacking through the salt-pine scrub and gorse loses the shore within a few strides.
- **Render the bayberry.** A pilot can collect the waxy grey berries at the seaward edge and render enough tallow for a season of candles.
- **Search the shaded gorse.** A careless barefoot search for firewood encounters the venomous snakes denning there.

---
## Who

Nemwe has no confirmed resident. The warm ash shows that somebody recently used the signal-fire and left in a hurry, but the page records no identity. Wild goats pick along the rocky slope at dawn, vultures circle the tower year-round, giant rats nest in the collapsed stores, and venomous snakes den in the shaded gorse.

---
## What

- *“A stone watchtower leans off true.”* The former Crown outpost is still standing, but the structure is visibly unsound.
- *“Ash still sits warm in the signal-fire cradle.”* The signal-fire remains usable, and somebody used it recently.
- *“The brazier's iron arm still swings freely.”* The arm can aim the signal toward the shipping lanes.
- *“Salt-pine scrub and gorse crowd the slope.”* The vegetation hides the shoreline from anyone who leaves the old path.
- *“Waxy grey berries cluster along the scrub's seaward edge.”* Rendered bayberry provides enough tallow for a season of candles.
- *“Wild goats pick along the rocky slope.”* They can be watched or approached at dawn if the party keeps its distance.
- *“Vultures circle the tower year-round.”* They are drawn by whatever last died on the ledge below the signal-fire platform.
- *“Giant rats nest in the collapsed stores.”* The kicked-in lower door opens onto shelter that the rats already occupy.
- *“Venomous snakes den where the slope goes to shade.”* Firewood gathering there is hazardous, especially barefoot.

## Why

The party comes to use the signal-fire, investigate the recent visitor, or claim an abandoned Crown outpost. They stay to control which patrol cutters approach, gather bayberry tallow, or shelter in the tower. They care because Nemwe offers a working warning system in an unclaimed position overlooking the shipping lanes.

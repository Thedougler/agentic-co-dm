# Velo Quarter

> [!narration] Velo Quarter
> The Velo bridges carry you into a quarter where the city's authority thins. Smoke burns openly in curtained rooms, magic and rare things gather behind lantern-marked doors, and every stall seems to know the price of privacy. Brass bells, hot metal, and lamp oil hang on the air, and nobody asks a customer's name twice.

## At a Glance

The Velo Quarter is a district of [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]] on the far side of the Velo bridges, where magic, smoke, and specialist trades operate beyond ordinary city authority.

- **Who is here.** [[rufio-segalla|Rufio]] at [[la-brace|La Brace]], [[fioravante-selmerin|Fioravante Selmerin]] and [[serena-cottle|Serena Cottle]] at [[il-preludio|Il Preludio]], [[prospero-morsani|Prospero Morsani]] at [[cabinet-of-morsani|the Cabinet of Morsani]], and the specialist stalls between them. No district authority is established.
- **Danger.** The quarter's trade runs on what the city does not record, so the danger is attention: a customer who names names, or a Crown officer who crosses the bridges with a list, is the force every business here is arranged to survive.
- **Draw.** Magic and rare goods, alchemical smoke burned in the open, private booths, and the information that moves out with the goods.

## Features

- **The Velo bridges.** The crossing that marks the edge of ordinary city authority. Beyond them the quarter's businesses are licensed by what they get away with rather than by what they hold.
- **[[la-brace|La Brace]].** Calveno's only room where alchemical smoke burns openly, with curtained booths priced by privacy and a keeper who keeps no ledger.
- **[[il-preludio|Il Preludio]].** A magic shop of brass bells, orange peel, and real enchantments nobody needs, run by an impresario who never sings.
- **[[cabinet-of-morsani|The Cabinet of Morsani]].** A curiosity collection that takes the story with the object.
- **The specialist stalls.** Lantern-marked doors and trestle tables where rare goods and private information change hands at the same counter.

## At the Table

- **Cross the bridges.** Walking the quarter is free; being recorded while doing it is not. A party that wants to be forgotten should buy the booth rather than the drink.
- **Buy privacy.** [[la-brace|La Brace]] sells a curtained booth and no questions. The price depends on the booth, and the discretion is the product, not a courtesy.
- **Shop for magic.** [[il-preludio|Il Preludio]] has real enchanted stock and a presentation for every piece; the Cabinet has curios and what is remembered about them.
- **Trade information.** The quarter prices facts like goods. A party that tells a keeper who else it has visited has just spent something it cannot get back.
- **Bring a Crown list through the bridges.** Every business here has arranged itself to survive that visit. [[la-brace|La Brace]] has refused the [[tessarine-concordat]] twice without ever using the word no.

## Secrets

**Secret.** The Velo Quarter's real commodity is the record that does not exist: the open-burn licence, the smoke with no ledger, the enchantment with no provenance, and the curio with no buyer's name. The tells are the curtained doors, the booths sold by privacy rather than by what is smoked, and keepers who answer a question with a longer version of it. The find is watching who is not written down.

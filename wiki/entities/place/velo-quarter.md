# Velo Quarter

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> The Velo bridges carry you into a quarter where the city’s authority thins. Smoke burns openly in curtained rooms, magic and rare things gather behind lantern-marked doors, and every stall seems to know the price of privacy.

## At a Glance

[[Velo Quarter]] is a district of [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]] on the far side of the Velo bridges, where several magic, smoke, and specialist businesses trade beyond ordinary city authority.

## If the party

- **Cross the bridges.** The party can book privacy at [[La Brace]], browse [[the Cabinet of Morsani]], or move among the quarter's specialist stalls and watch which information is traded with the goods.

## Who

The quarter includes [[rufio-segalla|Rufio]] at [[La Brace]] and [[prospero-morsani|Prospero Morsani]] at [[the Cabinet of Morsani]]. Other proprietors and any district authority are not established.

## What

The Velo Quarter concentrates magic and rare things, alchemical smoke, and specialist trade. [[La Brace]] is Calveno's only room where alchemical smoke burns openly; the Cabinet collects curios and their stories.

## Where

The quarter is in [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]], on the side of the Velo bridges where city authority stops. Its exact boundaries are not established.

## Why

The party comes for a private booth, a magic item or curio, a specialist stall, or the information that moves with those transactions. The quarter is a pressure point between Calveno's visible authority and trades that depend on its absence.

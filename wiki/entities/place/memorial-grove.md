# Memorial Grove
![[aruhe-the-grove.jpg|Memorial Grove, a fruit tree and two graves held in a dark forest clearing]]
## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> A round clearing about a long bowshot across sits inside dark forest, trees pressed back from a ring of black flowers. One fruit tree stands over two low unmarked graves. [[redheart-berry]] clusters low; [[giants-guava]] hang higher; fallen fruit rests in grass too green for the dark soil. Air smells sweet and rotten without turning sour. No branch or blade moves. Open ground runs all the way around the tree — walk, kneel, or keep the edge without touching the memorial.

---
## At a Glance
The 150-foot clearing where Aruhe's appetite stops. Tree, graves, flower ring, bound soil, and waiting mourners together are the Death Bloom — not a detachable object. Independent predators hold the tree line in stillness. Approach via forest trail, lake-edge, grassland, or marsh; circle to bypass and lose restraint and origin clues. [[hinewai]] owns walking form and memorial pressure here.

---
## If the party

- **Approach along forest trail or sunbreak:** Charged [[stillbloom]] raises pale bracts and dark needles when living movement closes — resolve stillbloom contact on its owner page.
- **Cross marsh or treeline pocket:** White [[spiritpollen]] sacs — touch/cut/shake/burn bursts a `15-foot` cloud; breathing creatures **Wisdom — `DC 15`** or **Spirit-Haunted** (Action forced against hallucinated threats until save repeats). Cover mouth, strong wind, or disturb from beyond `15 feet`.
- **Enter as guest:** Leave tree, graves, flower ring, bound soil, and mourners undisturbed — the Bloom remains place-bound.
- **Take fallen fruit:** Forage from grass.
  - **Intelligence (Nature):** `DC 10` edible; `DC 15` name variety; `DC 20` exact prize effect.
  - **Wisdom (Survival) — harvest:** `DC 10` ordinary fallen; `DC 15` ripe just aging; `DC 20` prize already down.
- **Pick living tree fruit:** Begins aging once removed; [[hinewai]] may treat as claim on the memorial unless table rules otherwise.
- **Strike tree, dig grave, uproot black flowers, or try to carry the Bloom away:** Memorial harmed — resolve via [[hinewai]] / blight pages.
- **Circle the Grove:** Bypass clearing; lose restraint and origin clues.
- **Retreat without taking or disturbing:** Return by water, marsh, grassland, or forest edge.

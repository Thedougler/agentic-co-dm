## At a Glance

The Memorial Grove is [[hinewai]]'s memorial and her body. The Gold caste of [[karath]] has sent generations of compelled Grung to destroy its two graves ([[two-grave-orders]]).

- **Who is here.** Hinewai, in her walking form, whenever someone crosses the flower ring. Predators stand still at the treeline: a [[terror-bird]], a pair of [[wolfrabbit]]s, and a [[deer-stalker]]. None of them hunts inside the ring.
- **Danger.** Harming the tree, the graves, the flowers, or the soil ends Hinewai's welcome, and she fights for the Grove.
- **Draw.** The answer to the island's law, and the only place where Hinewai can be ended for good.

## Features

- **The fruit tree.** *One fruit tree.* It bears [[redheart-berry]] low and [[giants-guava]] high. Hinewai buried her drowned companion beneath it, and the island offered the fruit he loved.
- **The two graves.** *Two low mounds of earth with no markers.* The first holds Hinewai's companion ([[the-unnamed-companion]]): the reason for the island's law. The second is Hinewai's own: the mechanism. No root crosses either grave.
- **The flower ring.** *A ring of black flowers.* It marks the edge of the Death Bloom.
- **The still grass.** *Grass far greener than the dark soil, and no blade moves.* The bound soil. Fruit and flowers here do not rot or wither.
- **The Death Bloom.** The tree, the two graves, the flower ring, and the bound soil together are Hinewai's body and her place-bound phylactery ([[hinewai#The Death Bloom]]). There is no single object to carry off or smash.
- **The approaches.** Charged [[stillbloom]] grows along the forest trail, and white [[spiritpollen]] sacs hang in the marsh and treeline pockets.
- **The descent.** Roots beside the graves lead down into the [[lava-tubes]] and [[aruhe-caves]].

## At the Table

- **If the party enters as guests.** They leave the tree, graves, flowers, and soil untouched, and Hinewai lets them stand and look.
- **If anyone takes fallen fruit.** It is receiving, and nothing happens.
- **If anyone picks from the tree.** It is a claim on the memorial itself, and Hinewai answers in person, not the responders.
- **If anyone strikes the tree, digs a grave, uproots flowers, or tries to carry the Bloom away.** Hinewai ends her welcome and fights ([[hinewai]]).
- **If the Death Bloom is destroyed.** Within weeks the roots stop answering and the fruit and flowers begin to rot. Within months the animals' territories break down. Within years the Quiet learns sound again, some of those Hinewai counts as hers vanish, and the island may become livable. Aruhe opens to [[karath]].

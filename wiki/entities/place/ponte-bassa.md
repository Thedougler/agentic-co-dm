# The Ponte Bassa

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> The tavern is built into the bridge at Calveno's main crossing. Canal damp rises beneath the tiers, while traffic passes above and the smell of hot fish stock and bread marks the room as a place to stop.

## At a Glance

[[The Ponte Bassa]] is a canal-side tavern built into [[The Bridge]] at [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]]'s main crossing. [[oleandro-fuschi|Oleandro Fuschi]] runs it and keeps records of every vessel that moors at the canal.

## If the party

- **Ask about a ship or cargo.** The party can seek Oleandro over a drink; he may know the vessel or who does, but he does not share his records freely.
- **Compare a manifest.** Captains increasingly falsify their manifests, so Oleandro's records and a ship's stated history may disagree.

## Who

[[oleandro-fuschi|Oleandro Fuschi]] runs the tavern. No other occupants are established.

## What

The tavern serves hot fish broth with bread. Oleandro remembers every vessel that has moored at the canal and keeps the Ponte Bassa as the reliable source for ship movements through Calveno.

## Where

The Ponte Bassa is built into [[The Bridge]] at [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]]'s main crossing, above the city's canal. Its other neighboring businesses and routes are not established.

## Why

The party comes for a cheap, hot meal, to cross the canal, or to find information about a ship, captain, or cargo that passed through Calveno.

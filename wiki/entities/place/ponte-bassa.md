# The Ponte Bassa

> [!narration] The Ponte Bassa
> The tavern is built into the bridge at Calveno's main crossing, with a window on the canal and traffic passing overhead. Canal damp rises beneath the tiers, the smell of hot fish stock and bread marks the room as a place to stop, and the keeper pours without haste while the boats work the water below.

## At a Glance

The Ponte Bassa is a canal-side tavern built into [[The Bridge]] at [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]]'s main crossing, and the city's only reliable source for who moved which hull.

- **Who is here.** [[oleandro-fuschi|Oleandro Fuschi]], behind the bar, pouring and remembering; nobody else is established.
- **Danger.** Oleandro answers seekers and never volunteers, and captains have started falsifying manifests. A party that leans on his memory for a ship's cargo gets the movements and not the reason, and his record is only as good as what captains told him.
- **Draw.** A cheap, hot bowl of [[fish-broth|fish broth]] with bread, a seat by the canal window, and the one memory in Calveno that can say where a hull went.

## Features

- **The canal window.** Seats by the water, where a visitor watches the hulls they came to ask about, and where Oleandro seats first-timers without asking their names.
- **The bar.** Oleandro pours with quiet care and rocks back on his heels between orders, and his voice barely carries, so the room leans in to hear.
- **The record he does not write.** Twenty years of every vessel that moored at the canal, held in memory rather than on paper, which is why it cannot be copied or bought out.
- **The kitchen.** Hot fish stock and bread, served to anyone with the price, which is how most visitors end up at the bar in the first place.

## At the Table

- **Ask about a ship, a captain, or cargo.** He answers over a drink, and only to a direct question. He either knows the vessel or knows who does, whichever is shorter.
- **Press him for free.** He does not volunteer his record in either direction. A party that treats the tavern as a service rather than a conversation gets broth.
- **Compare a manifest.** A captain's stated cargo and Oleandro's memory are two different documents and often disagree. He remembers the lie as clearly as the hull, and especially the ones that lied.
- **Sit by the window and wait.** The crossing is the main route through Calveno, so the sought ship may come to the party. Waiting costs a bowl and buys a sighting.
- **Try to write his record down for him.** He has kept it for twenty years precisely by never doing that, and offering to change his method ends the conversation.

## Secrets

**Secret.** Oleandro's record is the single asset that makes the Ponte Bassa matter, and it is degrading: captains are falsifying manifests more often, so the perfect archive he built is becoming a record of what people claimed. The tells are a man who pours slowly and remembers everything without a slate, and a tavern built where the canal can be watched rather than the street. The find is asking directly and paying attention to whether he answers the movement or the story.

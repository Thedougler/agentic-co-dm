# Akai Hodo Village

> [!narration] Narration
> Akai Hodo Village lies inside northern marsh and thick forest under constant red mist. Paths disappear into reeds and shadow, and the village's gecko- and chameleon-scaled inhabitants can vanish against both.

## At a Glance

Akai Hodo Village is the hidden northern base of the [[akai-hodo-clan|Akai Hodo Clan]] on [[Totemo Tokage]].

## Who

The village is home to geckos and chameleons of the Akai Hodo. [[mizobasho-red-tail|Mizobasho Red Tail]] leads the clan.

## What

The defining features are marshland, thick forest, red mist or fog, illusion, camouflage, stealth, and the red square cut often left on victims.

## Where

The village is in the northern marshlands. The Akai Hodo patrols the island's northeast and northwest; no travel distances are established.

## Why

A party comes here to seek a hidden route, contact the clan, or investigate the traces of its silent defense.

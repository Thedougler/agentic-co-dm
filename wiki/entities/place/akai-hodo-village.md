# Akai Hodo Village

> [!narration] Akai Hodo Village
> Akai Hodo Village stands above the water in the northern marsh, a cluster of huts on stilts joined by lashed bamboo that fades into reeds and thick forest on every side. The red fog never lifts here: it hangs in the canopy and pools over the channels, and no smoke rises from any roof. A body standing still in the reeds is only reeds, and the first post at the village edge carries a red square cut into its timber.

## At a Glance

Akai Hodo Village is the hidden northern base of the [[akai-hodo-clan|Akai Hodo Clan]] on [[Totemo Tokage]], where [[mizobasho-red-tail|Mizobasho Red Tail]] decides how the clan answers an intruder.

- **Who is here.** Gecko- and chameleon-scaled Akai Hodo live above the marsh channels, and scouts and hunters leave from here for the clan's patrols of the island's northeast and northwest.
- **Danger.** The clan answers intrusion with illusion, camouflage, and stealth. Its victims are found with no evidence of how they were killed and a red square cut into a hand or face.
- **Draw.** The village holds the only dependable route through the northern marsh, the clan's price for a hidden path or a warning, and the answer to who is killing along the northern approaches.

## Features

- **Walkways above the water.** The huts stand on stilts over the marsh channels, joined by lashed bamboo walkways — the only dry route in. The clan lifts or reroutes walkway sections between visits, so a path that worked yesterday ends in open water today.
- **Constant red mist.** The mist that hangs over the marsh never lifts. It shortens sight across the channels to a few dozen feet and blurs outlines, and the clan moves through it by the feel of the reeds and the sound of the water rather than by sight.
- **No fires.** Smoke and light would mark the village for every patrol on the island, so the clan cooks cold and works in the mist. A visitor who lights a fire here announces both himself and the village.
- **The [[akai-hodo-clan|Akai Hodo Clan]].** Geckos and chameleons, masters of illusion, camouflage, and stealth, whose daylight watch is a subtle shape in the treeline and whose warning is a red square cut into a post at the marsh edge.
- **[[mizobasho-red-tail|Mizobasho Red Tail]].** The clan's leader, dark grey-scaled and quiet, who sets the clan's answer to anything that reaches the marsh: a warning, a redirection, or a silent kill.

## At the Table

- **Find the village.** The channels braid, and the clan cuts the reeds back to hide the way in. **Wisdom (Survival) — `DC 17`** to hold a heading to the huts; a failure costs an hour and leaves the party exposed on the water. Anyone following a clan scout reaches the walkways without a roll.
- **Approach without a guide.** The party is watched long before it sees anyone. **Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 15`** to notice the scout in the cover; on a success the scout stays hidden and speaks first, and the party can talk to a voice with no body attached.
- **Ask for the clan at the marsh edge.** The watch sets one condition — stay where the marsh can see you — and [[mizobasho-red-tail|Mizobasho Red Tail]] decides whether the party is escorted, turned back, or answered with silence. A party that brings the clan useful news of a foreign threat is escorted in.
- **Light a fire or cut reeds.** The clan reads it as a claim or a beacon. The nearest scout withdraws, the walkways are lifted, and the next contact comes at night with a red square cut into the party's own gear or a nearby post.
- **Fight in the marsh.** The village and its walkways are [[akai-hodo-clan|Akai Hodo]] ground: concealment is theirs, the water is Difficult Terrain that slows everyone else, and the clan gives ground rather than stand in the open.

## Connections

- [[Totemo Tokage]] — the island; the village stands in its northern marshlands.
- [[Hōnteiru no Mura]] — the fortified western village of the [[horntail-clan|Horntail Clan]], kin to the Akai Hodo: when the island is invaded the Horntails fight openly and the Akai Hodo strike from the shadows.
- North — the marsh runs out to the island's northern coast; the source establishes no coastline, harbour, or outer-island neighbour there.
- East and west — the clan's own patrol country on the island's northeast and northwest approaches; no travel distances from the village are established.

## History

The Akai Hodo became one of Tokage's defensive clans by mastering illusion, camouflage, and stealth, and the village grew as the hidden northern half of that defense: the Horntails hold the western wall, and this marsh holds the approaches they cannot see.

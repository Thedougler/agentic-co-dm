# Line Bank

![[attachments/shattered-sea/places/aruhe-river-line-bank.jpg|Line Bank, three-pile fruiting margin and crude fishing line]]

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> The river runs bright turquoise against a narrow strip of wet sand, with pale stone showing through the current and tall gold-green grass crowding the bank. Three fruiting bushes stand along the margin like stopping points, their branches heavy with tawny globes while split rinds and whole fallen fruit lie under the roots. Near the water, a rough wooden pole is lashed over a short frame, with one thin line still dangling into the current. Fish bones and silver scales scatter across the sand.

## At a Glance

Line Bank is a used fruiting margin of [[Aruhe - River]] inside [[Aruhe - Grasslands]], just upvalley of [[Aruhe - Grasslands Torn Crossing]]. People already ate here and left a fishing line in the water. Travelers can stay on a ridge or grass edge to skip the piles, line, and prints; dropping into the grass reveals three fruit stops, a pole in the current, and cover that hides a standing body. Downstream returns to Torn Crossing. This stretch is not [[Aruhe - River Slack Basin]].

## If the party

- **Follow the prints upriver:** Reach [[Aruhe - River Slack Basin|Slack Basin]].
- **Walk downstream:** Return to [[Aruhe - Grasslands Torn Crossing]].
- **Walk the eight-foot grass:** Treat it as Difficult Terrain and heavily obscured beyond 10 feet. Movement leaves a crushed corridor.
- **Search the pole, bones, and line:** A DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) or Wisdom (Perception) check shows that the frame still holds a line and that fish bones and loose scales lie on the wet sand. The search does not reveal how many people fished or what they caught.
- **Take fruit along the wet margin:** Fallen fruit is receiving. Plucking a living plant makes surrounding life converge, hostile. [[Taking on Aruhe]]
- **Identify a firm fallen fruit:** A DC 13 Intelligence (Nature) check reveals its broad restorative function; DC 15 or *Identify* reveals that it restores `8d4 + 8` hit points as [[Redheart Berry]].
- **Eat a firm fallen fruit:** As a Bonus Action, regain `8d4 + 8` hit points. This uses the known Redheart Berry effect.
- **Fish, trap, or kill to carry flesh:** Surrounding life converges, hostile. What comes from the river belongs farther upvalley; this bank is not [[Aruhe - River Slack Basin]].

## Who

No one is here now. Prints, three piles, a pole, bones, and scales show that people already left upriver. Otter families hold [[Aruhe - River Slack Basin]] farther upvalley, not this bank.

## What

- Eight-foot gold-green grass hides a standing body. A muddy double track of crushed stems crosses the stretch.
- The river is clear over pale stone, with submerged grass in the current.
- Three fruiting bushes stand along the bank. Tawny orange fruit still hangs on living wood, while whole fruit and split skins lie in piles at the roots.
- The soil under fallen fruit is clean. Dark red mud surrounds only living stems whose branches have been snapped on the plant.
- A wooden pole and short frame lean over the water. A thin line hangs in the current, with fish bones and silver scales beside it.
- A jungle wall closes the inland edge. A thin column of smoke rises upvalley, but it is not on this bank.

## Where

- Line Bank is a Grasslands river site upvalley of [[Aruhe - Grasslands Torn Crossing]].
- The cut continues upvalley through [[Aruhe - Grasslands]] along [[Aruhe - River]] to [[Aruhe - River Slack Basin|Slack Basin]].
- Inland woods press against the grass, and the downstream route reaches [[Aruhe - River Landing Bank|Landing Bank]].
- Smoke rises inland in the same valley, but it does not mark a place on this bank.

## Why

Line Bank shows how the group ahead has been staying alive and what left blood on the river margin. Fallen fruit and a working fishing line offer immediate resources without requiring the party to disturb living plants.

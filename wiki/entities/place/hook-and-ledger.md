# Hook & Ledger

> [!narration] Hook & Ledger
> Hook & Ledger is a Shelf tavern in Port Tidefall. Behind the bar, [[hector-podge|Hector Podge]] makes wine, forms, and the Strait sound like the only subjects that keep a harbour civil.

## At a Glance

Hook & Ledger is [[hector-podge|Hector Podge]]'s tavern in the Shelf of Port Tidefall. It is a harbour institution where Crown officers ask which table is free rather than asking Hector's name.

- **Who is here.** Hector Podge, a heavyset, grey proprietor and regular-keeper.
- **What happens here.** Harbour workers, Crown officers, and regulars drink while Hector controls how much reaches the paper side of Tidefall.
- **What the party can gain.** A table, harbour talk, forms, and a contact who redirects direct questions without making a scene.

## Features

- **The bar.** Hector offers wine, forms, or harbour talk before he offers a name. He identifies visitors by what they order and which table they choose.
- **The Shelf regulars.** The tavern's value is its ordinary hospitality and the information that gathers around it; Hector keeps the room civil by deflecting direct questions.

## At the Table

- **Ask for a table.** Hector asks whether the party wants wine, forms, or the Strait, then uses their choice to set the conversation's terms.
- **Press for a name.** Hector redirects a direct question first. When Crown officers press him, he protects the tavern's hospitality rather than repeating a name.

## Connections

- [[hector-podge|Hector Podge]] — proprietor and keeper of the tavern's local authority.
- [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]] — home port and harbour community.
- [[central-strait|The Strait]] — one of Hector's preferred subjects when deflecting questions.

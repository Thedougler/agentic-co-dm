# High Eyries Storm Door

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> A black stone tooth breaks the sea two hundred feet up, salt-streaked white where spray has climbed it. No dock waits at the waterline. A stair cuts into the southern face and stops well short of the water. Terraces scatter off the summit above it.
>
> Maw weather drives breaking water around the stack. A white ring holds two hundred yards out. Spray reaches the lowest stair. Salt stings on every breath.
>
> Robed shapes move along the highest ledge, wings folded tight against gusts that never let up. One ledger stays open against the rain.

## At a Glance

High Eyries Storm Door is the exposed southern approach to [[high-eyrie]], a black stone stack rising two hundred feet above open sea. Maw weather and a white ring of breaking water make the climb dangerous, while the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie]] decide who reaches the summit. The immediate conflict is between the party's need to reach the order and the order's decision about who comes farther; the weather does not let up while they wait. Turning away preserves the party's safety but loses access to the High Eyrie and its Maw records.

## If the party

- **Arrives by the southern stair.** The lowest stair is already taking water. **Athletics — `DC 15`**: on a failure, spray provides no purchase; on a success, the party makes a watched climb to [[high-eyrie]]. **Perception — `DC 14`**: on a failure, weather hides the danger; on a success, the party sees the lowest stair taking water. **History — `DC 13`**: on a failure, the party reads the site as a monastery in a storm; on a success, they learn that [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie]] keep a Maw record, not a rescue.
- **Tests the order's welcome.** **Insight — `DC 14`**: on a failure, the party misses that the order is closed; on a success, [[master-kyzil]] will warn [[crissdalynn-khinriss]] without finishing her pilgrimage. **Persuasion — `DC 16`**: on a failure, there is no invitation; on a success, a Sentinel lets the party onto a terrace. **Investigation — `DC 15`**: on a failure, the party sees only public ledgers; on a success, they find Maw patterns without interpretation, while [[talon-vantyrus|Talon Vantyrus]] wants the read Kyzil withholds.

## Who

The [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie]] occupy the summit and decide who comes farther. Robed wings move along the highest ledge; [[master-kyzil]] controls access and may warn [[crissdalynn-khinriss]], while [[talon-vantyrus|Talon Vantyrus]] wants the reading Kyzil withholds. A record-keeper keeps one Maw ledger open against the rain.

## What

- **Black stone stack.** *“A black stone tooth breaks the sea two hundred feet up.”* The storm door rises above open water, with no dock at the waterline.
- **Southern stair.** *“A stair cuts into the southern face and stops well short of the water.”* It is the only established approach in the storm-door material, and spray reaches its lowest step.
- **Maw weather.** *“A white ring holds two hundred yards out.”* Breaking water circles the stack, spray reaches the stair, and salt stings on every breath.
- **Open ledger.** *“One ledger stays open against the rain.”* The Sentinels keep a Maw record rather than offering rescue; deeper interpretation is withheld.

## Where

- **North:** [[high-eyrie]], via the watched climb and summit terraces.
- **East:** [[drowned-maw]], beyond the Maw weather and open sea.
- **South:** Open sea below the southern stair; turning away leads toward [[central-strait-crossing]].
- **West:** Open sea and the route described as a west bearing in the approach material.

## Why

The party comes to the Storm Door to reach the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie]], climb to [[high-eyrie]], and obtain the order's continuous Maw record. It matters because the approach is also the order's first test: a party that cannot win purchase, read the weather, or earn an invitation does not reach the ledgers or the summit.

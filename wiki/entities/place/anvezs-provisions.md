# Anvez's Provisions

> [!narration] Anvez's Provisions
> Anvez's Provisions is a country shop in [[stillmere|Stillmere]], on [[otel-karn]]. Dried meat weighs out behind the counter, while rolled linen and splint-wood fill a shelf far larger than a village this size should need.

## At a Glance

Anvez's Provisions supplies food and medical dressings in Stillmere. [[marta-anvez|Marta Anvez]] keeps the counter, names a fair price once, and stocks for wounds and livestock that do not finish healing or dying on Otel Karn.

- **Who is here.** Marta Anvez, an impossibly old shopkeeper whose eyesight and handwriting are failing.
- **What is here.** Salted mutton, hides, bandages, and splints in quantities that replenish between visits.
- **What the party can gain.** Ordinary supplies, a second-visit tell about Otel Karn, and a lead in Marta's private ledger.

## Features

- **Counter and shelves.** Marta weighs dried meat without looking at the scale and names a fair price once. She answers the question actually asked.
- **Pest-house bandages.** Rolled linen and splint-wood are kept in bulk because wounds on Otel Karn linger as long as anyone stays hurt.
- **Salted Otel mutton.** The mutton is ordinary food, but the barrel is full again by the next visit after stock is sold.

## At the Table

- **Buy supplies.** The party can purchase food, bandages, or splints from Marta at her stated fair price.
- **Return for the tell.** A second visit makes the replenished mutton barrel or bandage shelf impossible to dismiss as a large first stock. Asking why opens the island's living contradiction.
- **Ask about the ledger.** Marta keeps a private record of households whose butchered stock keeps grazing after the knife. She answers only what the party actually asks.

## Secrets

- **Marta's private ledger.** Marta tracks which households' butchered stock keeps grazing and how the meat sells. She also remembers fragments of an old family story about a bargain struck for Otel Karn, but not the whole account.
- **The shop's stock.** The food and medical supplies are not magical items. Otel Karn's environmental refusal to let things die or finish healing keeps the shelves and barrel replenished.

# Gullmoth Reach

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Gullmoth Reach is a roost in the eastern Midchain where thousands of flame-drawn fey moths gather. A sailor approaching the Reach extinguishes lanterns a mile out, leaving the water and the moth-filled dark to define the approach. The size of the roost, the moths' exact behavior, and the reason flame draws them are not established; the lantern warning is the practical fact that governs arrival.

## If the party

- **Approach the Reach.** The party can extinguish its lanterns a mile out and continue toward the moth roost. The page does not establish what the moths do when approached or how close a vessel may safely come.
- **Keep a light burning.** The party can refuse the sailor's practice and observe what changes, but no fixed consequence is canonized.
- **Sail on.** The party can bypass the Reach and continue through the eastern Midchain. It avoids the roost while giving up the chance to examine the fey moths and their relationship to flame.

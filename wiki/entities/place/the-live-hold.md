# The Live Hold

> [!narration] The Live Hold
> The Live Hold is an exotic-animal dealership in [[sparhold]]. Cages, water tanks, and the quiet metal clicks of inspected locks fill the working floor.

## At a Glance

The Live Hold is [[sabine-corvo|Sabine Corvo]]'s dealership in Sparhold, where live exotic creatures from the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] fringe are inspected and sold.

- **Who is here.** Sabine Corvo, the proprietor and hands-on handler.
- **Pressure.** Three supplier expeditions have missed their scheduled returns, contracting the stock that protects Sabine's reputation.
- **What the party can gain.** Access to rare creatures, their market value, and the supplier trail behind them.

## Features

- **Working sales floor.** Sabine personally handles each animal before sale, demonstrating its temperament and value to collectors, adventurers, mages, and wealthy houses.
- **Reinforced harrier cage.** A crimson-crowned harrier from a deep-Teeth expedition is kept in a reinforced cage on the far wall. Its left wing was clipped during capture, and it tests the bars with its hunting cry.
- **Supplier records.** The dealership depends on [[roka-sten|Roka Sten]]'s [[spar-and-snare-outfitters|Spar & Snare Outfitters]] expeditions for much of its rare stock.

## At the Table

- **Inspect or buy stock.** The party can ask Sabine to identify an animal's temperament and market value. She handles the creature herself and sells to buyers willing to pay for something rare.
- **Follow the missing returns.** The three overdue expeditions give the party a concrete lead into the Verdant Teeth fringe and the contracting supply chain.

# Al Fondale

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Al Fondale is a tavern in Le Paludi where a halfling proprietor keeps the arithmetic of risk and a verbose dwarf chef keeps centuries of local history moving through the kitchen. Beneath the public room, the cellar holds a route toward the [[Warren]] that is not offered to everyone.

## At a Glance

Al Fondale is a tavern in Le Paludi run by [[bice-riva|Bice Riva]] and [[aldo-riva|Aldo Riva]]. Bice controls the tavern's risk and keeps the Warren entrance in her cellar hidden except from people she vouches for. Aldo cooks and carries Le Paludi's history across centuries.

## If the party

- **Needs a safe conversation.** Start with the tavern's public room; Bice decides whether the party is trusted enough for anything below it.
- **Needs local history.** Ask Aldo a concrete question about Le Paludi; he is the established keeper of its long memory.
- **Needs the Warren route.** Earn or present Bice's trust before treating the cellar entrance as available. The exact passage and current tide state are not established here.

## Who

[[bice-riva|Bice Riva]] is the halfling co-proprietor and gatekeeper of the cellar route. [[aldo-riva|Aldo Riva]] is the verbose dwarf chef and local historian. No other permanent staff or residents are established.

## What

- A public tavern in Le Paludi.
- A cellar whose Warren entrance is kept secret.
- Bice's trust as the access condition for the hidden route.
- Aldo's long memory of Le Paludi's history.

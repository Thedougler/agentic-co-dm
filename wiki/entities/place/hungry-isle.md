# Hungry Isle

![[aruhe-hungry-isle-v3.jpg|Aruhe / Hungry Isle]]

## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> From open water the island reads as one unbroken green volcano: ridged forest around a dark crater lake, gold-tan belts on the lower slopes, pale strand breaking cliff, and white water chewing a reef rim. No field, road, or chimney smoke softens the silhouette. Wet lowlands flash small red berries; heavier fruit weights the deeper canopy. Life crowds every slope harder than an island this size should allow.

---
## At a Glance
Hungry Isle is the spoken name for [[aruhe]] when crews first see it from the sea — eastern [[Midchain]], roughly five hundred miles end to end and about one hundred fifty miles across at the waist. [[karath]] sits near one western rim; the heart still lies many days inland. Known landfall is [[western-landing]]. Inland bands run [[old-gardens]], [[the-quiet]], [[grasslands]], [[the-river]], [[the-marshes]], [[the-mangroves]], [[clear-lake]], [[memorial-grove]], and [[lava-tubes]] / [[aruhe-caves]]. Stay offshore to skip the landing; the southern mangrove wall is not a second harbor.

## If the party

- **Hold a heading at island scale:** **Wisdom (Survival) — `DC 15`** → Keep a useful bearing toward a named band. Failure → Lose time and position; the island does not shrink.
- **Read a niche edge from silence, decay, water, grass, or graves:** **Intelligence (Nature) — `DC 15`** → Name the adjoining band and the safer edge. Failure → Confirm the next region on foot.
- **Commit through the known reef gap:** Reach [[western-landing]], then inland toward [[old-gardens]].
- **Treat the southern green wall as a landing:** [[the-mangroves]] look like seams of water and refuse a harbor.
- **Take a living claim (berry, fish, plant, trapped beast):** Surrounding life converges, hostile. [[taking-on-aruhe]]. Grung presence is attacked on sight even without a take.
- **Rescue or interview castaways at the wreck:** Testimony covers the fruit taboo, Tomo's loss, and the difference between shoreline and jungle — see situation notes in sources; live castaway pages own names.

## Who

- No visible state, town, or road network. Current people are raid survivors, storm-thrown Grung who will not land, and anyone still moving inland after wrecks.
- [[hinewai]] joins the island as pressure, grief, and preservation — not as a hive mind. Her walking form and memorial sit at [[memorial-grove]]. Plants and animals still act from their own niches.
- Grung oral tradition (Elder Botula) orders every patrol to sail past: nine warriors who took one berry woke crabs, hawks, wolfrabbit packs, black lotuses, and terrace crocodiles; poison works, fear does not.

---
## What

- Old volcanic body: green slopes, crater water, shore reef, basalt shelves, vents, and under-island tubes ([[lava-tubes]], [[aruhe-caves]]).
- Receiving versus taking: fallen fruit and genuine offerings can be accepted; living claims draw response ([[taking-on-aruhe]]).
- Pale luminous roots run from the shelf above [[clear-lake]] through [[the-marshes]] into unmarked graves at [[memorial-grove]].
- Corruption drives oversized fauna outward toward terraces, beach, and reef when the situation advances without a party.

---
## Where

- Eastern [[Midchain]]; open water from [[sparhold]]; a western channel near [[karath]] can narrow to about half a mile without shortening the journey to the heart.
- Sea approach: [[western-landing]] / [[aruhe-beach]] reef gap and short shingle. Inland climb: [[old-gardens]] into grass, quiet forest, river, lake, marsh, mangrove, grove, and tubes.
- Water is the closest thing to a road ([[the-river]] from [[clear-lake]]); it is not safe infrastructure.

# Vresh

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Vresh is a site among the small, far-spaced islands of the eastern [[Midchain]]. The remains of an ancient elemental forge sit within the pale reef waters, still faintly active. Loose metal creeps or pulls toward the ruins instead of lying where it was left; the surrounding shallows run clear over reef before dropping into deeper blue.

# Perrinelle

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Perrinelle is a western Midchain island marked by a lighthouse whose lamp still burns after its current keeper vanished weeks ago. Four generations of one lighthouse family held the island, but the light now continues without the person responsible for tending it. The lamp is the clearest visible fact on approach; the missing keeper is the unanswered absence beneath it.

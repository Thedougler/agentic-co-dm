## At a Glance

The Burnt Road is the last expedition's attempt to burn a way to [[hinewai]]'s graves. Its eleven dead carry the Gold caste's orders on their seals, and the Calveno trail runs along it to [[the-pantry]].

- **Who is here.** Eleven Grung dead, sunk in the flowers. Hinewai speaks from the treeline to any Grung who stands on the road.
- **Danger.** None while the dead are left alone. Fire, or pulling a body from the roots, is a claim, and the nearest [[vine-lash]] answers.
- **Draw.** The order chain on the seals ([[two-grave-orders]]), and the fruit-pile trail to the Pantry.

## Features

- **The road.** *A straight cut… as wide as a village street.* It is 20 feet wide and runs two miles north-east through [[the-quiet]], from the forest south of the [[the-long-meadow]] toward [[memorial-grove]]. The road is ordinary ground. The regrown forest on either side is Difficult Terrain and hides anyone more than 10 feet in.
- **The dead.** *Small bodies… with roots threaded through their ribs.* Eleven Grung, the last Gold-caste expedition. They burned the forest to cut a way to the Grove, and the island pulled all eleven into the soil. They have lain here for seasons. Each one's spent [[grung-authority-seal]] sits loose among its ribs and can be lifted out without disturbing the body. Pulling a body free is **Strength (Athletics)** `DC 16` and a claim.
- **The seals.** Each carries the full order chain, oldest line first: "Report what lies inland." "Replace the parties that stopped reporting." "Find the two graves beneath the memorial tree and destroy them." "If the forest stands between, burn it." [[jean-claude-tabarnack]] reads one with no roll; anyone else needs **Intelligence (Investigation)** `DC 13`. A spent seal is dead metal, and carrying one off is not a claim.
- **The fire pots.** *Cracked clay pots.* **Intelligence (Investigation)** `DC 11` finds oil residue and a Gold-caste mark stamped in the clay: the pots were issued, not improvised.
- **The black flowers.** *Black flowers carpet the road.* The island's growth over the burn. Nothing on the road bears fruit, because the island will not feed anyone along the scar. **Intelligence (Nature)** `DC 12` notices that nothing here fruits.
- **The side trail.** About a mile from the road's south end, piles of fallen fruit on broad leaves turn off it to the east, toward firelight at [[the-pantry]].

## At the Table

- **If a Grung stands on the road.** When they lift or read a seal, or after 10 minutes, [[hinewai]]'s voice comes from the treeline 30 feet away. Anyone who looks sees a darker shadow between the trunks and two orange eyes, and no body. She speaks and does not come out. What she says to Jean-Claude is on [[Session-12-04-orders-in-the-ash]].
- **If anyone burns anything.** A fire on the road is a claim. The nearest [[vine-lash]] wakes at the end of the burner's next turn and attacks the burner.
- **If the party walks straight on.** The road keeps its line to [[memorial-grove]], about a mile past its northern end.

## Connections

- [[the-long-meadow]] — south-west, back along the fruit-pile trail through the Quiet, about an hour.
- [[the-pantry]] — east along the side trail, a quarter mile, about a mile from the south end.
- [[memorial-grove]] — north-east, a mile past the road's end.
- [[the-quiet]] — the regrown forest on both sides.

## History

The Gold caste of [[karath]] compelled eleven lower-caste Grung and sent them inland with oil and fire pots. They burned this road toward the Grove, and burning living growth is a claim. The island took all eleven and has refused to fruit along the scar ever since.

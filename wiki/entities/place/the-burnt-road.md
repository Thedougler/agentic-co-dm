# The Burnt Road

## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> A scar cuts through the Quiet forest — twenty feet wide and lined with trunks regrown around dark charcoal cores. The floor is solid black-petaled flowers, packed and dense enough to walk on. No living fruit hangs anywhere on the trees above or below. The road is open and walkable in the way that pulls travelers deeper in. Cracked clay fire pots lie scattered in the packed flowers. Half-sunk into the soil are bodies — eleven of them, side by side, with tree roots grown through the ribs and clothing. Pale bone shows through in places where the flesh has gone. The flowers smell of rot and copper. No small birds move here.

---
## At a Glance

A burned-over road about two miles north-east through [[the-quiet]] toward [[memorial-grove]]. The road is fruitless ground — the only place in the Quiet where nothing edible hangs on the living growth. It is open and walkable, which makes it pull travelers further in. The expedition that burned it left behind their dead, their fire pots, and their spent authority seals in the soil. [[hinewai]]'s voice can reach travelers here from the treeline, and her contempt for those who burned is sharpest here.

## If the party

- **Walk the open road:** Ordinary ground; Difficult Terrain on either side where regrown jungle tangles.
- **Search the clay fire pots:** **Intelligence (Investigation) or Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 11`** → Cracked and spent, but intact enough to read scorch marks and trace shapes. These are expedition camp gear.
- **Read a spent [[grung-authority-seal]]:** **Intelligence (Investigation) — `DC 13`** (non-Grung speaker) or no roll (raised Grung of the clans) → The seal's orders layer from oldest to newest. Oldest: "Report what lies inland." Next: "Replace the parties that stopped reporting." Next: "Find the two graves beneath the memorial tree and destroy them." Newest, overlaid on the burn expedition's seals: "If the forest stands between, burn it."
- **Examine a body:** The eleven Grung dead are half-sunk into the soil with tree roots grown through the ribs, clothing, and bone. The soil has taken them. They are not animated and have been in place for seasons. Each one's spent seal, once fused to the skin, now lies loose among the rib bones and can be lifted out without disturbing the body. Pulling a body free requires **Strength (Athletics) — `DC 16`** and provokes [[taking-on-aruhe]] (disturbing Aruhe's dead is a claim on the island).
- **Listen to the treeline:** Whenever a Grung stands on the road, [[hinewai]]'s voice reaches it from the trees. She speaks her contempt for the Grung, their sealed orders, and the fire. Her words carry grief older than the expedition. Hearing her does not cause a combat encounter; she watches from the wood and does not engage unless the party enters her presence or speaks directly to the forest.
- **Take anything from the flowers or soil:** Picking living fruit = [[taking-on-aruhe]]. The flowers and regrown trunks are all the island's now.

## Who

- Eleven Grung corpses half-sunk in the soil, dead for seasons. [[hinewai]] watches the road from the treeline; she does not enter it.
- No living Grung remain here. The Gold caste did not leave a garrison.

---
## What

- Open fruitless road, twenty feet wide, about two miles long, lined by regrown trunks around charcoal cores.
- Solid mat of black-petaled flowers on the floor.
- Eleven Grung dead, half-sunk into soil with roots through them.
- Cracked clay fire pots scattered in the flowers.
- Spent [[grung-authority-seal|gold authority seals]] with the bodies, readable by inspection.

## Why

- The second proof that the island punishes those who take from it: not just responders that hunt, but the forest's own reclamation. The sealed expedition made a claim by burning living growth, and the island took all eleven. The road is fruitless because the island will not feed those who burned. The openness tempts travelers to keep moving, rest only briefly, and press deeper toward [[memorial-grove]]. The Gold caste of [[karath]] wants these graves destroyed because sealed reports from early expeditions taught them the island's curse is anchored beneath the memorial tree at [[memorial-grove]]. Breaking the Death Bloom would reopen Aruhe to the Grung of neighboring Karath. [[hinewai]] knows it. The burn failed.

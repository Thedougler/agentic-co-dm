# Low Lamp

> [!narration] Narration
> The Low Lamp is a Kin-only gathering house in the Warren. A worn bar takes the room. Bottles and earthenware catch the lamp glow. Essa Two-Tooth works the bar, two named teeth showing, a cloth moving slowly across a mug.

## At a Glance

The Low Lamp is the Warren's true social center for Kin. It is inside [[Warren]], under [[calven-and-calveno]]. [[essa-two-tooth]] runs it. Non-Kin do not use it as a public house.

## If the party

- **Eat first.** Essa insists every guest is too thin and must eat before discussion. Feeding here means the house has their goodwill.
- **Collect a debt.** Essa records every debt in her head, never on paper, and she collects.
- **Refuse or be refused.** She does not ask [[Tangle|The Tangle]]'s permission to refuse service to someone unwelcome.

## Who

[[essa-two-tooth]] works the bar. Pip, her grand-nephew, runs errands. Regulars are Warren Kin. One regular stopped coming three weeks back. A sealed message shard has sat unclaimed at the bar for eight days.

## What

Worn bar, bottles, earthenware, lamp glow, and named teeth Essa introduces as Scrimper and the Keeper. [[preserved-eel]] is a common staple here.

## Where

Inside [[Warren]]. This page does not name neighbors beyond the Warren descent.

## Why

Kin-deals unfold and settle here. Essa knew [[nona-black-jaw]]'s late husband by his first name, and she knows [[perrin-black-jaw]]'s family through Nona.

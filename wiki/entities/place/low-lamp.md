# Low Lamp

> [!narration] Low Lamp
> The Low Lamp is a Kin-only gathering house in the Warren, low enough that everyone meets each other at eye level. A worn bar takes the room, bottles and earthenware catch the lamp glow, and Essa Two-Tooth works the bar with her two named teeth showing and a cloth moving slowly across a mug. The room goes quiet when strangers come in, and then someone puts food in front of them.

## At a Glance

The Low Lamp is the [[warren|Warren]]'s true social centre, where Kin deals are made and settled over a bar that keeps no written record.

- **Who is here.** [[essa-two-tooth|Essa Two-Tooth]] at the bar, her grand-nephew Pip running errands, and Warren Kin as the regulars. No outsider uses the house as a public room.
- **Danger.** Everything said here is remembered forever and nothing is written, so a party that talks in the Low Lamp is talking on record. Essa does not ask [[tangle|the Tangle]]'s permission to refuse service to someone unwelcome.
- **Draw.** A place to be fed and vouched for, the only ledger of the Warren's debts, and a keeper who has known the [[nona-black-jaw|Black-Jaw]] family for decades.

## Features

- **The bar.** Worn wood, bottles, earthenware, and lamp glow, with Essa behind it, reading everyone in the room: who drinks fast, whose cup stays untouched, who will not look up, and what fills the space between people.
- **The teeth.** Essa introduces her two remaining teeth to every newcomer by name — Scrimper and the Keeper — and treats them as her counsel. The introduction is the house's greeting, and skipping it is not possible.
- **The kitchen.** [[preserved-eel|Preserved eel]] is the staple, and every guest is told they are too thin and must eat before discussion.
- **The unclaimed shard.** A sealed message shard has sat on the bar for eight days, and one regular stopped coming three weeks back. Nobody has explained either, and Essa has not moved them.
- **The memory.** Four decades of names, debts, grudges, and transactions, none of it on paper, all of it available to anyone the house decides to help.

## At the Table

- **Sit and eat.** Food arrives before any question does. Feeding here means the house has the guest's goodwill, which is the entire point, and nobody conducts business until the plate is empty.
- **Ask about a debt.** Essa is the ledger: who owes whom, for what, and how long. She does not sell the answer, and she does not forget a debt owed to her own house.
- **Ask about the shard on the bar.** Eight days unclaimed, and the regular who stopped coming three weeks ago. She names what she knows and nothing past it.
- **Ask about the Black-Jaw family.** She knew [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s late husband by his first name, and knows [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s family through her going back decades.
- **Come in as an outsider and push.** The room settles, the plates stop, and Essa refuses service without asking the [[tangle|Tangle]] for permission. The house is Kin-only and says so by doing it.

## Secrets

**Secret.** Essa is the Warren's book, and the book is the reason the Low Lamp matters: four decades of debts and names held in memory rather than on paper, safe because there is nothing to seize. The tells are a bar with no ledger under it, a keeper who asks about arrangements as if she already knew them, and an unclaimed shard left in plain sight. The find is being fed first, because nobody gets to the debts without eating.

# Drave Vaults

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> The vaults lie beneath the crown of [[fort-crestwall]], sealed into the cliffs below [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]]. The rock closes around a hidden complex whose doors, rooms, and approach are not visible from the fort above. Whatever lies inside is protected by stone, secrecy, and the garrison that holds the fort on orders no governor saw.

---
## At a Glance

The Drave Vaults are Aldric Drave's hidden lair and phylactery archive beneath [[fort-crestwall]]. They sit under [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]], with the fort as the established surface connection. No independent entrance, safe route, or bypass is established; ignoring the vaults leaves Aldric's living chart and bloodline network protected.

---
## If the party

- **Investigates the fort.** Follow the sealed orders and the two garrison detachments that hold [[fort-crestwall]] without [[governor-voss|Governor Voss]] knowing. The vault connection is established; the approach and defenses are not.
- **Searches for Aldric's weakness.** Look for the living bloodline chart and the phylactery archive. The archive matters because every Dravosi noble carrying Aldric's blood is part of his return network.
- **Bypasses the vaults.** Aldric's lair, chart, and phylactery network remain protected. No alternate way to weaken that network is established here.

---
## Who

[[aldric-drave|Aldric Drave]] lives in the vaults and uses them as his hidden lair and phylactery archive. No other vault occupants are established. Two garrison detachments hold [[fort-crestwall]] under Aldric's sealed orders, but their exact duties inside the vault complex are not established.

---
## What

- Aldric's living bloodline chart, which maps the descendants in his phylactery network.
- The sealed phylactery archive that allows Aldric to return through living Dravosi descendants.
- The rock-sealed complex below [[fort-crestwall]]; the vault's rooms, defenses, entrances, and stored objects are not established.

## Why

The vaults are the only named site where Aldric's living bloodline chart and phylactery archive are held. Reaching them could expose the hidden orders controlling Fort Crestwall, reveal how Aldric renews himself through his descendants, and create leverage against the lich without treating destruction of his body as a complete solution.

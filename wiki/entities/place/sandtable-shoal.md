# Sandtable Shoal

> [!narration] Sandtable Shoal
> Sandtable Shoal lifts the sea floor beneath the Mid-Strait, a long pale rise that shows as a change in the water's colour over about two miles. The Blue Lane bends south around the shallows, and wreckage stands in the bright water where hulls have found the top of the bank.

## At a Glance

Sandtable Shoal is a shifting sandbank in the middle of [[central-strait]] south of [[aldenmere]], and the reason the fastest line through the Strait is not a straight one.

- **Who is here.** Giant octopuses in the wreckage, [[kalowe]] salvage crews working the Crown contract, and a [[harwick]] Admiralty that would rather they did not.
- **Danger.** The bank itself: the bottom rises from two hundred feet to under forty over about two miles, and a hull that holds the Blue Lane's old heading finds it. The wreckage that marks the top of the rise is also cover for the octopuses.
- **Draw.** The shifted lane, a field of wreckage to dive, and a salvage contract whose holder the Admiralty is unhappy about.

## Features

- **The rise.** The bottom climbs from two hundred feet to under forty over roughly two miles, which is shallow enough to change the colour of the water and to catch a keel.
- **The Blue Lane.** The unmarked fastest line through the Strait bends slightly south to clear the bank. [[central-strait-crossing]] holds the crossing itself.
- **The wreckage.** Hulls that found the top of the rise stand in the shallows. The chart boundaries of the field are not established, and neither is its depth.
- **The octopuses.** Giant octopuses lurk in the wreckage around the shoal, using it as cover in exactly the water a diver has to enter.

## At the Table

- **Cross the Strait.** A navigator who knows the bank takes the lane's southern bend; one who does not finds the rise the hard way. Avoid the shoal entirely by giving up the fast line for the longer water.
- **Dive the wreckage.** The wrecks are salvage and the octopuses are in them. **Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 15`** to spot an octopus in the wreck before it reaches a diver; a failure means the dive starts with the party already held.
- **Work the salvage contract.** A [[kalowe]] operator holds the Crown contract on the site, and the [[harwick]] Admiralty is dissatisfied with that arrangement, which makes any recovery here a political act as well as a commercial one.
- **Sell a find quietly.** The Crown's interest in the site means recovered cargo has an owner before it has a buyer.

## Secrets

**Secret.** The shoal is not a hazard the chart simply records; it is a salvage claim under Crown contract, held by a [[kalowe]] operator the [[harwick]] Admiralty wants replaced. The tells are an unmarked lane that everyone fast uses anyway and a wreck field with a claimant and no visible work. The find is asking at [[central-strait-crossing]] whose contract covers the bank, which tells a party who will come looking for whatever they raise.

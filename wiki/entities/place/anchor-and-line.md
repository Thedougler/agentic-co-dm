# The Anchor and Line

> [!narration] The Anchor and Line
> The Anchor and Line is a dockside room in Port Tidefall, with benches, small cups, and the traffic of ships close at hand. The front is loud with crews; the back section is quieter, with lower benches and smaller cups, and one corner table holds a pile of odds and ends being sorted into groups.

## At a Glance

The Anchor and Line is a dockside establishment in [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall|Port Tidefall]] where crews sit, sort gear, and hear what passes between vessels.

- **Who is here.** [[vrtek|Vrtek]], a small [[rattkin|Rattkin]] dockhand between ships, at the corner table with his pockets emptied onto the wood; dockhands and crews fill the front.
- **Danger.** Nothing here fights. The risk is what Vrtek is carrying: a crumpled note with a stamp that he cannot read, which is [[detto-kalash|Detto Kalash]]'s buried-cache directions, and whoever wants the cache back wants the carrier too.
- **Draw.** The room hears what ships say: which hull came in short, who is watching the gate, what official paper changed hands this week, and where a dockhand with too many pockets is sitting.

## Features

- **The room and the benches.** A dockside front full of crews and a quieter back with lower benches and smaller cups, where somebody can put both elbows on the table and count his pockets.
- **The corner table.** Where [[vrtek|Vrtek]] sorts everything he owns into useful, pretty, mysterious, and food, between ships and leaving tomorrow unless something interesting happens.
- **The crumpled note.** In Vrtek's pockets, stamped and official-looking, and unreadable to him. It holds the directions to a buried cache, and he has no idea what he is carrying.
- **The dockside trade in talk.** The room's regulars notice what comes off a hull and what changes hands at the gate, and they trade it for a drink.

## At the Table

- **Find Vrtek.** He is at the corner table with his pockets on the wood. A calm approach gets conversation; grabbing at his pockets ends it.
- **Ask what he has.** Vrtek classifies each object out loud as useful, pretty, mysterious, or food, and offers the stamped note as "official, I think". A party that can read it recognises the buried-cache directions.
- **Trade with him.** He refuses to give anything up but will trade or let a customer look. A trade is the only route to the note that keeps him cooperative.
- **Hear the port talk.** Sitting in the front and asking what has changed recently gets the regulars talking about ships and paperwork. **Charisma (Persuasion) — `DC 12`** to get the room to name what it has seen rather than what it has heard.
- **Rush him.** He slows down, needs to know which pocket each thing belongs in, and stops trading. He is between ships and will leave tomorrow if nothing interesting happens.

## Secrets

**Secret.** Vrtek is an innocent carrier. The stamped note in his pocket is [[detto-kalash|Detto Kalash]]'s buried-cache directions, and Vrtek cannot read it, which makes him both the fastest route to the cache and the person most easily hurt by anyone who wants it. The tells are the note with a stamp among the sorted clutter and Vrtek's willingness to show it to any calm stranger, and the find is reading the note or knowing Detto's hand.

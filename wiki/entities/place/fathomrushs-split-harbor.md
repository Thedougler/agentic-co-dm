# Fathomrush's Split Harbor

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> [[fathomrush]] splits on the water. [[sunkline]] holds one shore, [[voyvode]] the other. Both piers watch where each new hull ties up, and neither crew crosses the middle to greet it first.
>
> Between them the dockside sits unclaimed: wet stone, coiled hose, a memorial wall with a fresh name cut into it, the letters still bright against the older, weathered rows. Pump-endurance gear stands under canvas on both sides, tarps snapping loose at one corner where nobody has bothered to retie them. The air smells of brine and hot metal and the oil used on deep-shelf valves. Gulls work the unclaimed stone for scraps neither pier has claimed either.

---
## At a Glance

Fathomrush's Split Harbor is the far-east harbor where the [[sunkline|Sunkline Company]] and [[voyvode|Voyvode Works]] face each other across an unclaimed dockside. Both companies watch which dock receives the [[uncertainty|HCS Uncertainty]]. The memorial wall and a fresh name cut into it make the harbor's rivalry a question of ownership, grief, and the truth behind a claimed trial.

---
## If the party

- **Arrive at the harbor.** The party must choose which pier to approach while both companies watch the new hull. **Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 13`** reveals which compound is watching the [[uncertainty|HCS Uncertainty]]; failure leaves both docks alert.
- **Inspect the unclaimed dock.** The party finds wet stone, coiled hose, pump-endurance gear, loose tarps, and the memorial wall. **Intelligence (Investigation) — `DC 15`** reveals the Fathomrush Last Berth False Log and Sunkline's forged valve; failure produces a clean trial with no answer.
- **Read the memorial wall.** **Wisdom (Insight) — `DC 14`** identifies a fresh name on Voyvode's wall: [[petera-ngata]]. Failure leaves the party with the memorial's grief but no clear claim.
- **Question the companies.** **Charisma (Persuasion) — `DC 14`** gets [[branimir-karsovine]] or [[adriana-tarquine|Adriana]] to name the real failure instead of repeating company talk.
- **Leave the harbor.** Refusing [[fathomrush]] or turning off leads toward [[central-strait-crossing]]; leaving for another named port hands the next move to that destination's run guide.

## Who

The [[sunkline|Sunkline Company]] controls one rig pier, and [[voyvode|Voyvode Works]] controls the other. Both crews watch every new hull but neither crosses the middle first. [[branimir-karsovine]] and [[adriana-tarquine|Adriana]] can name the real failure if the party gets past company talk. [[petera-ngata]] stands for the dead not becoming company property, and the unclaimed dockside remains the only neutral ground.

---
## What

- *“The harbor splits on the water.”* Two rival pressure-rig companies face each other across Fathomrush.
- *“A memorial wall with a fresh name cut into it.”* Voyvode's wall bears [[petera-ngata]]'s fresh name among older, weathered rows.
- *“Pump-endurance gear stands under canvas.”* Both piers keep deep-shelf equipment ready, with one tarp corner loose because nobody has retied it.
- *“Wet stone, coiled hose, and gulls.”* The unclaimed dockside is neutral but not cared for by either company.
- *“The air smells of brine and hot metal.”* Deep-shelf valve oil and harbor water mark the worksite.
- *“The claimed trial never happened.”* Investigation can expose the false log and Sunkline's forged valve.
- **Memorial grief.** Insight can reveal that [[petera-ngata]] will not let the dead become property.

## Where

**North:** The [[sunkline|Sunkline Company]] pier.  
**East:** The [[voyvode|Voyvode Works]] pier.  
**South:** The unclaimed dockside and memorial wall.  
**West:** The far-east route toward [[central-strait-crossing]].

## Why

The party comes to berth the [[uncertainty|HCS Uncertainty]], choose between rival rig companies, investigate the false trial, or understand the fresh name on the memorial wall. They stay to expose the forged valve, keep the dead from becoming property, or decide which pier receives the next hull. They care because every harbor choice assigns leverage to one company while leaving the unclaimed middle vulnerable.

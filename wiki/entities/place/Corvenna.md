# Corvenna

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Corvenna is neutral ground among the small, far-spaced islands of the eastern [[Midchain]]. Disputes come here to be settled by champion combat instead of war. The site of the contest, the people who keep its neutrality, and the rules that make a champion's result binding are not recorded; what is visible is the pressure to put a dispute into a bounded fight rather than carry it across the water.

---

## At a Glance

[[Corvenna]] is an eastern Midchain neutral dueling ground where disputes are settled by champion combat instead of war. The eastern waters are shallow, widely spaced, and poorly charted; [[Eastern Midchain]] is the recorded regional connection, but no route distance or local neighbor is established. Skipping Corvenna avoids its settlement process and leaves disputes to their original parties and methods.

## If the party

- **Brings a dispute to Corvenna:** The party enters a neutral process whose result is supposed to replace war. The eligible champions, terms, witnesses, and enforcement are not recorded.
- **Names or supports a champion:** Champion combat is established, but no rules, match format, permitted equipment, or substitution rule is recorded.
- **Refuses the duel:** The source establishes no consequence for refusal. Keep the dispute's next move open to play.
- **Watches a contest:** The party can observe the settlement of a Midchain dispute by combat. The arena, crowd, stakes, and current dispute are Unknown.
- **Leaves into the eastern waters:** No direct route or travel time from Corvenna is established.

## Who

Champions and the parties to Midchain disputes come to Corvenna. No champion, disputant, judge, host, audience, or neutral authority is named. The people who make and enforce the site's neutrality are Unknown.

## What

- **Neutral ground:** Corvenna is used to keep disputes from becoming wars.
- **Champion combat:** A dispute is settled through a champion's fight rather than direct conflict between the parties.
- **Eastern reef water:** Long shallow reaches, distant landfalls, unreliable charts, and unmarked hazards shape the approach.

## Where

- **North:** Corvenna lies in the eastern [[Midchain]]. The broader region borders [[central-strait|the Central Strait]] to the north, but no direct route or travel distance from Corvenna is recorded.
- **East:** The eastern Midchain continues toward [[drowned-maw|the Drowned Maw]], but no direct route or distance from Corvenna is established.
- **South:** No named southern neighbor or route is recorded.
- **West:** [[verdant-teeth|The Verdant Teeth]] lies west of the eastern Midchain, but no direct route or travel distance from Corvenna is established.
- **Local access:** The site is reached through shallow, chart-uncertain eastern water; its landing and safe harbor are Unknown.

## Why

Corvenna matters when a dispute needs a public choice between champion combat and open war. It creates a place where representation, terms, and the meaning of a result can matter more than raw force. The page establishes neither a current match nor the rules that govern one, leaving those decisions open until play or a source fixes them.

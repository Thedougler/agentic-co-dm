# South Harbour

> [!narration] South Harbour
> South Harbour opens on the east side of [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall|Calder's Tooth]], all repair cradles, fish racks, and piers that carry more work than authority. Boats come out of the water on greased ways and go back in patched, and a Crown seizure yard sits on the east pier among the fishing traffic without ever quite controlling it.

## At a Glance

South Harbour is Calder's Tooth's working east harbour: repairs, fishing, and a Crown impound that is policed lightly enough to leave gaps.

- **Who is here.** Fisherfolk and repair crews; the [[dravosi-crown|Crown]] polices the harbour lightly and keeps a seizure yard on the east pier. No harbourmaster or named resident is established.
- **Danger.** The gap between the Crown's authority and its presence: a seized hull sits in a yard whose fences, guards, and inventory are not recorded, and a party that moves through the piers can be inside Crown property control before it notices.
- **Draw.** Cheap repairs, fishing traffic to hide a hull among, the [[overland-track|Overland Track]] crossing to the west-cliff port, and [[south-harbour-impound|the impound]] on the east pier.

## Features

- **The repair cradles.** Ways and cradles where hulls come out of the water, worked by crews who fix first and ask later.
- **The fish traffic.** Racks, boats, and a working quay that keeps the harbour busy enough to hide an arrival in.
- **The east pier.** Where [[south-harbour-impound|South Harbour Impound]] sits, with Crown-seized vessels tied where the pier meets the repair traffic.
- **The overland crossing.** The [[overland-track|Overland Track]] leaves the harbour and crosses the [[the-marrow|Marrow]] toward the island's west-cliff port, a day and a half on foot in good weather.
- **The light watch.** Crown patrols pass through without holding the harbour, which is what lets the piers and the impound behave differently from the port's other practices.

## At the Table

- **Repair a hull.** The harbour does this work, quickly and without much paperwork, and a party with a damaged ship is one of many.
- **Hide in the traffic.** Fishing and repair movement covers arrivals that a formal port would record. **Charisma (Deception) — `DC 13`** to pass a hull or a cargo off as one more working boat.
- **Walk the east pier.** The impound is Crown property control; access rules and any current watch are not recorded, so the party's approach is the DM's ruling rather than a number on this page.
- **Take the overland route.** The track crosses the Marrow toward the west-cliff port, with tolls at both ends and culverts that shelter giant wolf spiders.
- **Assume the Crown is not watching.** It is watching lightly, and the difference matters: the harbour's official control and its local movement are two separate things.

## Secrets

**Secret.** South Harbour's value to anyone working around the Crown is the gap: the port seizes hulls, keeps them on the east pier, and then polices the water around them lightly enough that a vessel can be boarded, claimed, or released on terms nobody has written down. The tells are a seizure yard sitting among fish racks and a pier where official authority stops at the gate; the find is walking the east pier and asking who is actually on watch.

# Kalowe's Frozen Council

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> [[kalowe]] holds its [[chain-council]] in a closed room above the harbour. Nine seats. No public gallery. The air in that chamber smells of lamp oil and wet wool. A mail record sits in a locked case against the wall, still unburned, the seal on the case unbroken since it was set.
>
> At the reef gap, the [[waveservant-shrine|Waveservant shrine]] takes every arriving hull within reach. The basin there holds ordinary tribute for now: coin, a knotted cord, a splinter of ship's timber offered for safe passage. The water around it is clear enough to show the coins on the bottom, and the current keeps them moving in a slow, patient circle.

---
## At a Glance

Kalowe's Frozen Council is the closed [[chain-council]] chamber above [[kalowe]]'s harbour, paired with the [[waveservant-shrine|Waveservant shrine]] at the reef gap. Nine seats, sealed mail, and a tribute basin make the same arrival matter to both the port's political voices and its sea-facing shrine. The current pressure is [[delmar-fisk]]'s return after seven days on Crown search water.

## If the party

- **Enter Kalowe.** **Intelligence (History) — `DC 14`** turns [[delmar-fisk]]'s old commission into blame or leverage; failure gives the party only a port.
- **Approach the reef gap.** **Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 13`** reveals that the [[waveservant-shrine|Waveservant shrine]] takes tribute from every arriving hull; failure notices only the shrine.
- **Inspect the council's locked case.** **Intelligence (Investigation) — `DC 15`** reveals [[detto-kalash]]'s mail route or the [[fleet-commanders-chair]]'s old debts; failure leaves the party with council talk.
- **Read the room.** **Wisdom (Insight) — `DC 14`** shows which council voice is buying time; failure sees only four competing fronts.
- **Negotiate.** **Charisma (Persuasion) — `DC 16`** can produce a vote, a re-term, or [[delmar-fisk]] named in public; failure leaves the room holding its position.
- **Offer tribute.** **Wisdom (Religion) — `DC 13`** shows that the [[waveservants]] want the sea's due before the meeting; failure costs the party a shrine fee without clarifying why.
- **Leave the port.** Refusing [[kalowe]] or turning off leads toward [[central-strait-crossing]]; another named port takes over the next move.

## Who

The [[chain-council]] occupies nine seats in the closed chamber and has no public gallery. Its council voices are divided across four fronts, with at least one buying time. The [[waveservants]] receive arriving hulls at the reef-gap shrine. [[delmar-fisk]]'s return is the immediate arrival that connects the council room to the shrine.

---
## What

- *“Nine seats. No public gallery.”* The [[chain-council]] meets behind a closed door with no public audience.
- *“A mail record sits in a locked case.”* The record remains unburned, and its seal has not been broken since it was set.
- *“Lamp oil and wet wool.”* The council chamber carries the smell of a working room above the harbour.
- *“A basin holds ordinary tribute.”* Coin, a knotted cord, and a splinter of ship's timber are offered for safe passage.
- *“The coins move in a slow, patient circle.”* The clear water at the reef gap keeps the tribute moving with the current.
- *“[[delmar-fisk]]'s old commission.”* The document can become blame or leverage when the party reaches port.
- *“[[detto-kalash]]'s mail route.”* The locked case and the fleet commander's old debts point into the council's business.

## Why

The party comes to bring [[delmar-fisk]]'s return into the council's public record, turn an old commission into leverage, or learn who is buying time. They stay to negotiate a vote or re-term, inspect the sealed mail, or pay the shrine's tribute. They care because Kalowe makes arrival political: the council controls the room while the shrine controls the approach.

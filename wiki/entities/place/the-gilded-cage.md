# The Gilded Cage

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> The Gilded Cage is a site in the eastern Midchain kept in flawless condition for guests who never arrive. Nothing about the place shows the ordinary wear of abandonment: its preservation is the visible work of an unseen caretaker, while the empty welcome leaves the purpose of the preparation unresolved. The site offers no established account of its rooms, routes, or intended guests; what can be seen is perfection maintained against absence.

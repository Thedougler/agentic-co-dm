# Ussamet

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Ussamet is a western [[Midchain]] site of half-sunken ruins. Most of the structure lies below the water, and only the lowest tides expose enough of it to cross or inspect. The timing is a sea-kept schedule rather than a reliable appointment; the ruins' shape, age, and surviving contents are not recorded.

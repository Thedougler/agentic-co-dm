# Harbourmaster's Office

> [!narration] Harbourmaster's Office
> The Harbourmaster's Office keeps the working paper of Port Tidefall: berths, charts, writs, and the records that let a ship enter the harbour and leave it again.

## At a Glance

The Harbourmaster's Office is the administrative counter for Port Tidefall, inside [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]]. Its charts and papers govern access to the fortress-port and the western Strait.

- **What is handled.** Berths, charts, writs, vessel ratings, and port paperwork.
- **Where it matters.** Ships entering the western Strait must stop at the Inspection Pier and show papers.
- **What the party can gain.** A berth, a chart, or the paperwork needed to move through Tidefall's harbour.

## Features

- **Berth and chart counter.** The office records where ships lie and sells or supplies charts for the harbour and surrounding routes.
- **Writs and vessel ratings.** Port paperwork records a vessel's rating and the permissions attached to its movement.

## At the Table

- **Request a berth.** The party can bring a vessel into the office's paperwork system and resolve where it may lie in Tidefall.
- **Show papers.** A ship entering from the western Strait must stop at the [[inspection-pier|Inspection Pier]] and show its papers before continuing into the harbour.

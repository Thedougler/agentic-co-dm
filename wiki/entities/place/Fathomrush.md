# Fathomrush
> [!narration] Narration
> Fathomrush is a boomtown of dive gear, winches, and supply stacks pointed east at the Shelfworks. Wet rope, hot iron, salt, and salvage oil hang over bargaining and warnings about the drop. Crews stage here before the trench.

## Drowned Maw ingest — split harbor
About eight hundred people pass through. Two piers face each other across mud; Sunkline's sunburst and Voyvode's eagle are close enough to read. There is no mayor; each company keeps law inside its gates. A plank memorial wall bears a hundred diver names and no crest; both companies maintain it.

- [[sunkline]] is Tessarine money. [[adriana-tarquine]] leads; the mid-shelf certificate is forged, and the false log is under the Last Berth floorboard. [[petera-ngata]] keeps the only complete death list.
- [[Voyvode]] is Crown money. [[branimir-karsovine]] personally tests rigs; his notes are honest and incomplete, and most fresh memorial names are his crews.
- [[yevgenia-sorrel]] runs Sunkline's store; [[marta-kessler]] runs Voyvode's. The rivalry resolves off-screen: certificate discovery or another run of crew loss empties one compound into the other's dock.

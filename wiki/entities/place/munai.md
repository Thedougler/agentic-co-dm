# Munai

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Rows of overgrown fruit trees run wild across the slope, planted once in careful lines now swallowed by bramble. The fruit hangs heavy and strange-colored: some rotten, some suspiciously perfect. Rats and feral goats work the fallen fruit at the edges of the grove, while giant wasps hum inside split trunks.

A shipwrecked botanist planted Munai's orchard long ago. It has run wild ever since. The grove yields potent cures and potent poisons. You can't tell them apart.

## At a Glance

Munai is a feral orchard island in the [[midchain-north|Northern Midchain]], between [[kwoss|Kwoss]], [[murrat|Murrat]], [[old-correspondence|Old Correspondence]], and [[chronehall|Chronehall]]. No power controls the grove. Its fruit can cure or poison with equal potency, and the party cannot identify which by appearance alone.

## If the party

- **Enters the orchard.** Follow the old rows into the bramble, where every step opens small cuts that do not close cleanly.
- **Forages.** Examine strange-colored fruit or violet fungus at the oldest trees; the cure and poison look alike until their effects begin.
- **Crosses at dawn or dusk.** Pass through the fallen-fruit paths while rats and feral goats feed, but expect giant wasps to defend the oldest trunks.
- **Takes the old routes.** Use the four island connections to leave before the grove's hazards or its uncertain harvest become the party's problem.

## Who

No named resident controls Munai. Rats and feral goats work the fallen fruit at dawn and dusk without sickening. Giant wasps nest in the oldest split trunks, and violet fungus grows in the rot beneath them. The creatures claim food and shelter rather than the orchard's history.

## What

- **“Rows of overgrown fruit trees.”** A shipwrecked botanist planted the orchard in careful lines, but generations of growth swallowed the order. The find is a route through light and shade that no longer stays clear.
- **“Strange-colored” fruit, some rotten and some “suspiciously perfect.”** The grove's cures and poisons are both potent and cannot be told apart by appearance. The find is an uncertain harvest.
- **“Bramble understory.”** Thorny vines choke the gaps between rows, slow a walk to a crawl, and open small cuts that never close cleanly. The find is a persistent travel hazard.
- **“Rats and feral goats” under the trees.** They eat fallen fruit at dawn and dusk without seeming to sicken. The find is evidence that the animals tolerate the grove better than visitors.
- **“Giant wasps” in split trunks.** Their sting carries a slower version of whatever poison the fruit is steeping in that season. The find is a living warning around the oldest trees.
- **“Violet fungus” in the rot.** It resembles the orchard's poisonous fruit in color and shape. The find is a second false-safe harvest.

## Why

A party comes to seek an unnervingly effective cure, investigate the botanist's abandoned orchard, or gather fruit and fungus worth the risk. The party stays while it can identify a useful harvest, but every hour in the grove increases exposure to bramble, wasps, and a fruit that may be poison.

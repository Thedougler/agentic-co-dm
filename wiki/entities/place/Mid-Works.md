# Mid-Works

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Two hundred feet below the [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]], residential stone continues into sealed chambers where ordinary doors and floors stop making sense. Pumps hammer in the dark, and warm water carries hot iron upward.

## At a Glance

The Mid-Works are the second [[antheri|Antheri]] tier, 200–500 feet below the Maw shelf, below [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]] and above [[deep-works|Deep Works]]. The tier feels sealed and pressurized, built for bodies that did not need ordinary floors or air. The party can descend through a salvage line, operate or sabotage pumps, or follow the machine route toward unresolved soul-pledge evidence; every route carries pressure and company risk.

## If the party

- **Descend from [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]].** Follow the salvage and descent line into the residential tier; pumps are required and pressure-rig failure makes the descent a public risk.
- **Inspect the chambers.** Read the soul-pledge language in the structure, where channels imply minds could be bound into artificial frames.
- **Reach the lower tier.** Continue past 500 feet toward [[deep-works|Deep Works]], accepting elemental exposure.
- **Work around the companies.** Treat [[sunkline|Sunkline]] and [[voyvode|Voyvode]] as active competitors killing crews to reach the Mid-Works from [[fathomrush|Fathomrush]].

## Who

- [[sunkline|Sunkline]] and [[voyvode|Voyvode]] crews are trying to reach the tier and are killing crews in the process.
- [[fathomrush|Fathomrush]] serves as the companies' staging town, not an established resident community inside the Mid-Works.
- No resident count or active Antheri population is established.
- Whether pledged minds still run in the tier remains unresolved.

## What

- Residential stone and sealed chambers 200–500 feet below the Maw shelf.
- Pumps that keep the descent possible; warm water carries hot iron upward.
- Pressure-rig failure as a persistent descent and public-safety risk.
- Soul-pledge architecture: chambers and channels that imply minds could be bound into artificial frames.
- A machine route through the residential tier that may explain [[auralis|Auralis]].
- The lower passage toward [[deep-works|Deep Works]] and its elemental exposure.

## Where

- **North:** The descent rises toward [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]]; the route is a salvage line, but travel time is not established.
- **East:** No named neighboring site or route is established.
- **South:** The tier continues toward [[deep-works|Deep Works]] past 500 feet; the distance beyond the threshold is not established.
- **West:** No named neighboring site or route is established.
- **Staging and pressure:** [[fathomrush|Fathomrush]] is the companies' staging town; its exact route to the tier is not established.

## Why

The party comes to reach the Antheri works, investigate the soul-pledge architecture, protect or outmaneuver crews, or determine whether pledged minds still operate. They stay while the pumps, machine route, or company conflict reveals a choice; they retreat when pressure-rig failure or the killing crews makes the next descent costlier than the evidence gained.

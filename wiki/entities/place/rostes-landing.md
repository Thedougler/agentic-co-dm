# Roste's Landing

> [!narration] Roste's Landing
> A stone quay runs along the lee side of a low island, and one long hut stands at the top of the steps with a tin chimney and a lantern already lit. The hut is one room: four benches, a cistern, a rack of clean cloth, and a smell of camphor over old blood. There is no gate, no sign, and nobody waiting at the quay to ask who you are.

## At a Glance

Roste's Landing is a free clinic in the western [[Midchain]] that treats anyone who reaches it, with the question of payment coming only after the treatment.

- **Who is here.** [[roste]], the island's only healer, on the benches with her ledger open. Sailors, wreck survivors, and whoever the current brought in share the remaining benches.
- **Danger.** Nobody here fights and nothing here is guarded. The risk is the waiting: a [[dravosi-crown|Crown]] patrol or a [[the-passage|Passage]] crew that arrives while the party is on the benches sees who is being treated.
- **Draw.** Treatment without questions, a healer who has cut Crown shot out of Passage runners, and a price she will take in fish, sailcloth, or a promise.

## Features

- **The treatment room.** One room with four benches and a rack of washed cloth. It is the whole clinic, and there is nowhere to be private in it: two patients on the benches hear everything said at the third.
- **The quay and the cistern.** Fresh water and a stone quay deep enough for a sloop. Water is free and the cistern is full, which is what makes the landing a stop rather than a ruin.
- **The supply chest.** Bandages, clamps, a saw, and a shrinking stock of the things she cannot make: wakeleaf, poppy, and clean gut. It is also the price list, because she will trade treatment for what the chest lacks.
- **The ledger.** An open book beside the bench where [[roste]] records each patient by wound and not by name. Patients whose payment is a promise are written there too.
- **The steps and the lee shore.** The hut sits above a lee anchorage. Any boat that comes in is visible from the benches, and anyone on the benches is visible from the water.

## At the Table

- **Get someone treated.** [[roste]] treats whoever is hurt first and asks who they are never. A serious wound costs a night on a bench; a life-threatening one costs days, and she will not be hurried.
- **Settle the price.** Payment comes after treatment and is absurdly negotiable. She names what the chest is short of. **Charisma (Persuasion) — `DC 10`** to trade work or salvage for the whole debt instead of coin.
- **Hide a patient.** Bodies on the benches are in plain sight of the quay. Getting a wanted person treated and back aboard unseen needs a working boat moved to the far side and the patient carried after dark. **Dexterity (Stealth) — `DC 13`** to move them from the hut to the water without being seen from the anchorage.
- **Buy supplies.** She will sell surplus bandage and poppy, and she will not sell a weapon or a poison, whatever the offer.
- **Ask her about a previous patient.** She stops answering. She will not confirm who has been here, even under Crown paper.

## Secrets

**Secret.** Roste asks no questions because the ledger tells her more than questions would. Every patient is entered by wound, by what made it, and by what they carried, and she has been reading the western Midchain's traffic out of that book for twenty years. The tells are the ledger open on the bench with her palm on the pages, and the entries written in two inks with the second added later. The find is the second ink: entries she went back and added to, one for every patient whose wound she recognised as Crown, Passage, or something else, and the party's own entry is already there.

- **East.** The western [[Midchain]] island chain; the landing sits among its unnamed islands.
- **South.** Open water and wreck ground; whatever the current brings in washes up here first.
- **West.** Open sea. No named landmark.

## History

- The landing was named for its clinic, and it has been a free one for as long as the western Midchain's pilots can remember. It has no owner, no charter, and no flag.

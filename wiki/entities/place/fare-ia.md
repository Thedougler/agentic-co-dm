# Fare I'a

> [!narration] Fare I'a
> Fare I'a is a fish stall and jerky counter inside Port Tidefall, among the rooms listed within the cliff-port settlement of Calder's Tooth.

## At a Glance

Fare I'a is a fish stall and jerky counter in Port Tidefall. The surviving Vaka connection belongs to an unconfirmed prep profile for Felix Aho, not settled table canon.

- **What is here.** The stall is a known Vaka contact point and a listed room within Tidefall.
- **Where it sits.** [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]] places it inside the western Strait fortress-port, whose docks lie below its trade shelf and Fort Crestwall.
- **Why it matters.** The Vaka uses a fish stall as a quiet first contact for shelter, introductions, and practical support.

## Features

- **Fish stall and jerky counter.** Fare I'a is one of the named rooms inside Tidefall. The stall is the visible place associated with the Vaka's quiet contact network.

## At the Table

- **Follow the contact.** A party that follows the fish-stall lead can reach the Vaka's network for shelter, an introduction, or practical support. The Vaka keeps this contact low-profile and peaceful.

## Secrets

- **Felix Aho's connection.** An unconfirmed prep profile says Felix Aho built the Vaka from this stall one conversation at a time. Felix's owner page instead records the current table canon: he is a captured laborer held in [[warren|the Warren]]. Treat the stall's Felix connection as unresolved continuity, not settled history.

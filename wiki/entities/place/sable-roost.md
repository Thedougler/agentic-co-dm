# Sable Roost

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Sable Roost is an airship graveyard in the southern [[Midchain]]. Wrecked hulks gather at the site, and each one is still watched by the single crewmate who survived it. The place is defined by stranded airships and their solitary sentinels; the shape of the ground, the condition of the hulls, and the reason the ships came down are not recorded.

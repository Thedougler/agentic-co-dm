# Veth West Beach

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Veth West Beach is twenty strides of pale sand before the ridge rises straight up. A living root-stair shines with traffic, but there is no pier. Bone, resin, and pigments trade on the sand for food, timber, metal, and rope while reef chop works at any boat that comes too close.

## At a Glance

Veth West Beach is the only sanctioned [[veth|Veth]] landing in the [[verdant-teeth]]. It is a brief blue-caste exchange zone with no pier, roof, or inland permission. The party can anchor offshore, land by small boat, trade on the sand, or retreat; following the root-stair ends the invitation and gives the watch time to count the hull.

## If the party

- **Anchor offshore.** Use a small boat; a deep-draft approach is both a wreck risk and an insult.
- **Trade on the sand.** Exchange food, timber, metal, and rope for bone, resin, and pigments without leaving the beach.
- **Follow the root-stair.** The invitation ends at the second root; the forest begins there, and hidden Grung daggers and poison may answer the breach.
- **Wait or retreat.** Remaining offshore preserves safety but lets the watch count the hull; retreat returns the party to the boat without inland permission.

## Who

- Blue go-betweens descend when a hull waits and conduct the sanctioned exchange.
- Higher caste remains visible only as color in the treeline; no public conversation or resident count is established.
- [[kalowe|Kalowe]] pilots teach the wait-offshore rule but are not established as residents.
- No named merchant, priest, or permanent guard is established on the beach.

## What

- Twenty strides of pale sand backed by a ridge.
- A living root-stair polished by traffic; no outsider print reaches it.
- Reef chop that makes landing hazardous, especially for deep-draft boats.
- Bone, resin, and pigments offered in exchange for food, timber, metal, and rope.
- A brown canopy hole called Widow-strangle, a visible Crown-Taker landmark rather than a climb invitation.
- The threat of hidden Grung daggers and poison if the party leaves the sand or touches the stair.

## Where

- **North:** The ridge and root-stair lead toward Veth's forest; the distance and safe inland route are not established.
- **East:** Veth's interior lies beyond the ridge; no sanctioned public route beyond the second root is established.
- **South:** Reef water and the offshore anchorage; small boats are the safe approach, while deep draft risks wreck.
- **West:** Open water and the party's offshore approach; no named neighboring site or travel distance is established.
- **Related places:** [[veth|Veth]], [[verdant-teeth]], [[grung-clans]], and [[kalowe|Kalowe]] provide the linked regional and cultural context.

## Why

The party comes to exchange supplies, reach Veth under its limited landing protocol, or learn how the blue-caste trade works. They stay while a go-between bargains on the sand; they leave when the trade is complete, the root-stair is touched, or the watch's count makes continued presence more dangerous than retreat.

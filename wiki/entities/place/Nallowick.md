# Nallowick

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Nallowick is a peat-bog island adrift in the eastern Midchain. Its position never stays fixed, yet its slow seasonal circuit repeats, turning the island itself into a route marker that refuses to remain on one chart. Peat, wet ground, and open water make the island's movement the first fact to read before choosing whether to follow, wait, or sail on.

## If the party

- **Follow the circuit.** The party can record where Nallowick appears during the current season and compare the result with [[midchain-east|Eastern Midchain]] charts, whose routes already contradict one another. The page establishes the recurring circuit but not its exact timing or coordinates.
- **Wait for the island.** The party can hold position until Nallowick's circuit brings it within reach. No canon shelter, landing procedure, or time interval is established, so the DM must keep those details open rather than inventing them as settled fact.
- **Sail on.** The party can bypass Nallowick and continue through the eastern Midchain. They lose the opportunity to use the island's seasonal movement as a local navigation clue.

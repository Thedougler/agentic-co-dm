# Vel Orn

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Small islands ring a dark blue hole. An older house occupies black cliff stone, kelp wraps its pillars, and a sheer reef-bound landing appears only when the surge allows the known approach. Inside, candles burn where wind should have killed them, and footsteps go soft.

## At a Glance

Vel Orn is an older house of Umberlee on a small island in the [[sunken-crown]]. It is reachable only when a timed surge and known approach coincide, and it is not a working-quay collection point. The shrine's worship reaches toward drowned names rather than living traffic; the approach can be skipped, but doing so leaves its ledger, water-debt, and vanished Pearl unexamined.

## If the party

- **Approach on the surge.** Use the known route when the water permits it, accepting the sheer reef-bound landing.
- **Enter the shrine.** Read the ledger chamber's drowned names and water-debt, or examine the offerings that record deaths.
- **Search the deepest chamber.** Investigate the tidal pool where the [[pearl-of-souls]] was held and what its absence marks.
- **Leave before the surge changes.** Retreat from the cliff landing; the same route is not continuously available.

## Who

- The shrine is associated with [[Waveservants]] and [[Umberlee]], but no resident count or current priest is established here.
- The drowned are represented through names, tokens, coins, fish bones, carved shells, and pieces of ship and sailor.
- [[delmar-fisk]] is connected to the Pearl's absence, not established as a current occupant.
- [[chain-council]], [[tessarine-concordat]], and [[aldric-drave]] are connected through the wider history of the Pearl, not as established shrine residents.

## What

- Black cliff stone, kelp-wrapped pillars, and a timed reef landing.
- A ledger chamber that keeps drowned names and water-debt.
- Tokens, coins, fish bones, carved shells, and pieces of ship and sailor used as witnesses.
- A tidal pool in the deepest chamber, built to hold the [[pearl-of-souls]].
- The absence of the Pearl, which marks [[delmar-fisk]]'s theft and the fleet's destruction.
- **Vafnar**, a table name produced by a persistent mishearing.

## Where

- **North:** The [[sunken-crown]]'s small islands; the exact neighboring island and travel distance are not established.
- **East:** The Blue Hole rim; no named route or distance is established.
- **South:** Reef-bound open water; the known approach appears only when the surge allows it.
- **West:** The remaining [[sunken-crown]] islands; no named route or distance is established.
- **Related sites:** [[drowned-maw]] and [[Shelfworks]] are connected in the regional record, but their exact direction and travel time from Vel Orn are not established.

## Why

The party comes to identify the shrine's drowned names, understand the Pearl's absence, or follow the connection between Umberlee's worship and the fleet's destruction. They stay when the ledger or offerings reveal a useful name, when the tidal pool provides evidence, or when the surge timing makes retreat more dangerous than continued investigation.

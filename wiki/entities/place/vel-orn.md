# Vel Orn
> [!narration] Narration
> Small islands ring a dark blue hole. An older house occupies black cliff stone, kelp on the pillars, reachable only when the surge allows a known approach.

## At a Glance
On Vel Orn in the [[sunken-crown]], small islands ring a dark blue hole. Umberlee's older house occupies black cliff stone, reachable only when a timed surge and a known approach coincide. It is not a working-quay collection point. Its worship reaches toward the drowned rather than the living.

## Shrine
Kelp wraps the pillars. Candles burn where wind should have killed them, and footsteps go soft. The cliff landing is sheer, reef-bound, and dangerous. The shrine is hidden from ordinary charts. Inside, a ledger chamber keeps drowned names and water-debt. Tokens, coins, fish bones, carved shells, and pieces of ship and sailor stand as witness. Every object is a death written down.

The deepest chamber held the [[pearl-of-souls]] above a tidal pool built for it. The Pearl's absence is the mark of [[delmar-fisk]]'s theft and the fleet's destruction. People at the table sometimes call this place **Vafnar**, a mishearing that stuck.

# Ral-Arn

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Ral-Arn is a low record island on the rim of the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]]. The oldest family histories and seasonal marks are kept in stone, so the island reads like a ledger exposed to salt and weather. It is a place to read what the Sunken Crown remembers before deciding what the present has changed.

## At a Glance

Ral-Arn is the Sunken Crown's record island. Its stone holds the oldest family histories and seasonal marks, while its position among the low islands around the Blue Hole makes it part of the region's rim rather than a settled harbor. The source establishes the record function, but not a keeper, shelter, landing, or travel time.

## If the party

- **Needs old history.** Search the stone records for family lines and seasonal marks; the records are the island's established affordance.
- **Compares present and past.** Use the seasonal marks to identify change, but do not assume the page establishes a particular reading or prophecy.
- **Leaves without reading.** The party keeps the current question unanswered and loses access to the only named record island in the Sunken Crown.

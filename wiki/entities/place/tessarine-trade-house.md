# Tessarine Trade House

> [!narration] Tessarine Trade House
> Three storeys of pale stone stand on the Shelf with a Tessarine pennant over the door. The ground floor is a counter with a bound ledger open on it and ink and cedar oil in the air. Stairs go up into sealed mail rooms and a brokerage office, and one door at the back of the ground floor stays shut with a bench beside it.

## At a Glance

The trade house is where Port Tidefall turns trade and salvage into signed obligations, and where [[maret]] is currently held on an open note.

- **Who is here.** [[livia-ansaldi]], the factor on duty, at the counter with the ledger; clerks and couriers in and out all day; [[maret]] behind the back door under a debt-recovery hold.
- **Danger.** Nothing here fights. The risk is the paper: a signature at this counter binds the party to Tessarine terms, and [[maret]]'s open note points at anyone who asks about it.
- **Draw.** Trading licences, salvage brokerage, sealed mail, credit, and a bonded hold the Crown Admiralty honours and dislikes.

## Features

- **The counter and the open ledger.** The ground floor where licences are issued and salvage claims brokered. Anything the party signs here becomes an obligation, and [[livia-ansaldi|Livia]] reads every line of it aloud before they do.
- **The back office.** A shut door at the back of the ground floor with a bench beside it. [[maret]] sits in it under an open debt-recovery hold after a failed salvage dive.
- **The upstairs mail rooms.** Where the house seals and dispatches courier mail. The [[silkvane]] carries it out of Tidefall.
- **The bonded hold treaty.** A Tessarine hold under treaty can be covered so Crown inspectors do not open it. The [[dravosi-crown|Crown]] Admiralty honours the treaty and dislikes it, and the paperwork for it is kept here.
- **The shelf counter trade.** Charts, ratings, and fees are handled over this counter for the whole port, so the house hears which hulls came in short and which officials asked what.

## At the Table

- **License trade.** [[livia-ansaldi|Livia]] quotes a fee and writes the terms into the ledger. A party that signs is bound; a party that haggles is answered with a condition. **Charisma (Persuasion) — `DC 13`** to talk the fee down without accepting a new clause.
- **Broker salvage.** The house values a claim, takes its share, and can fund a dive in exchange for first call on what comes up.
- **Send or intercept mail.** Sealed mail leaves on the [[silkvane]]. A party who wants a letter stopped has to get at the mail room, and the treaty that keeps Crown inspectors out also keeps them out of the law's help.
- **Ask after [[maret]].** [[livia-ansaldi|Livia]] confirms he is held on an open note and refers all questions of justice to [[calven-and-calveno]]. Pressing the injustice of it shuts the counter down; asking what would settle the note keeps it open.
- **Buy him out.** Paying the note releases [[maret]] and clears the bench by the back door. The sum is larger than the loss the house actually took. **Intelligence (Investigation) — `DC 15`** on the claim sheet against the dive manifest finds the added line.

## Secrets

**Secret.** [[maret]]'s salvage note is inflated: it charges him for two divers' kits when the failed dive lost one, and the second was written onto the claim before [[livia-ansaldi|Livia]] took the counter. It stays open because correcting it is an admission the house raised a claim it cannot support, not because anyone hates him. The tells are the bench by the back door and the way [[livia-ansaldi|Livia]] answers every question about the note with a condition instead of a figure; the find is the claim sheet against the dive manifest, an audit from [[estratto]], or the bonded-hold paperwork that shows what actually came up.

# Aruhe Beach

## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> Half a mile out, a jagged coral reef shows its teeth at low tide. One boat gap opens onto a short shingle beach that takes only minutes to cross. Surf works the coral. Salt spray rides loose stone. Elsewhere the coast is cliff, cove, or strand trapped behind reef. At the inland wet-leaf edge, pale fruit hangs in shade and heavier guavas weight the green above a stream.

---
## At a Glance
Aruhe Beach is the shore-facing name for the island's known western landing. Table procedures, tide checks, and retreat timing live on [[western-landing]], which carries the aliases Western Landing and Aruhe Beach. This page exists so archive "Beach" cites and Hungry Isle cross-links have a kebab place owner without competing with Sorn or Veth beaches. Commit through the gap for the island. Observe from offshore to skip the shingle. The southern mangrove wall is not a harbor.

## If the party

- **Use the known approach.** Resolve landing, tide, and gap checks on [[western-landing]].
- **Use the shore's name in speech or log.** Prefer [[western-landing]] for the playable site. Use [[aruhe-beach]] for the generic western strand identity.
- **Confuse with other campaign beaches.** [[sarns-landing]], Veth west beach, and similar are different places. Do not merge them.

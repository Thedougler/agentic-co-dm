# Halythion

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> The eastern Midchain spreads into low, pale islands separated by long reaches of clear water. Reef shows beneath the surface like a second map, then drops suddenly into a blue hole too dark to read. Nothing on the surface identifies Halythion; the ancient settlement lies built into the Teikhinos Reef below.

## At a Glance

[[Halythion]] is an ancient settlement built into the Teikhinos Reef in the eastern [[Midchain]]. It is invisible from the surface and older than any colonial record. The exact entrance, depth, inhabitants, and route are not established in canon.

## If the party

- **Search the reef.** The party can chart the Teikhinos Reef, dive the shallows, or work from a salvage vessel to look for the settlement. The canon establishes no single entrance or method.
- **Question the surveys.** The party can investigate why the settlement never appears on colonial charts. The eastern region records that someone in the [[central-strait|Strait]] has begun asking that question, but no investigator or response is named.

## Who

- No surface authority is recorded for Halythion. Local pilots and salvage crews work the surrounding eastern waters.
- The settlement's inhabitants and their current activity are not established in canon.

## What

- The Teikhinos Reef conceals an ancient settlement that has never appeared on a colonial survey.
- The settlement's architecture, resources, defenses, and relationship to the surrounding ruins are not recorded.

## Where

- **North:** Canon gap; no direct neighboring place or travel time is recorded for Halythion.
- **East:** The eastern Midchain runs toward [[drowned-maw|the Drowned Maw]], but no route from Halythion is recorded.
- **South:** The eastern waters connect toward [[doldrums|the Doldrums]], but no route from Halythion is recorded.
- **West:** [[verdant-teeth|The Verdant Teeth]] marks the western side of the eastern Midchain; the approach to Halythion is not charted.
- [[Midchain]] is the parent region. The settlement remains below the Teikhinos Reef, outside colonial surveys.

## Why

The party may care because Halythion is an ancient, uncharted settlement hidden beneath a reef, and its existence challenges the records that govern travel and ownership in the Shattered Sea. Its undiscovered entrance and the unanswered survey question create leads without prescribing an outcome.

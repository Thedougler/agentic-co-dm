# Chronehall

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Sundials and water-clocks crowd every open surface. Ticking. Dripping. Shadows that do not match. Robed timekeepers cross the square in silence, each checking a dial. One notices you staring and says, "We're not behind schedule. You are."

## At a Glance

Chronehall is a horologists' guild island in the [[midchain-north|Northern Midchain]], connected by water to [[calders-verge|Calder's Verge]], [[munai|Munai]], [[old-correspondence|Old Correspondence]], and [[corrigans-rest|Corrigan's Rest]]. The guild has measured something for ages without saying what it counts; the island can be skipped, but doing so gives up direct access to the clocks and the people who maintain them.

---

## If the party

- **Inspects the clocks.** The party can compare sundials, water-clocks, and shadows, but the guild controls access to its measurements.
- **Questions a timekeeper.** The timekeepers answer practical questions about schedules and maintenance while refusing to name the count.
- **Forages below the cisterns.** Samphire and tide pools offer resources, while giant crabs and venomous snakes make careless reaching costly.
- **Leaves the island.** The party keeps its unanswered question but loses the chance to observe the guild's instruments directly.

---

## Who

The Chronehall guild controls the island. Robed timekeepers cross the square, check the dials, and do not explain the count they maintain. No individual guild member is named in current canon. Crabs, hawks, giant crabs, and venomous snakes occupy the shoreline and cistern approaches.

---

## What

- **Clocks and sundials.** Instruments crowd open surfaces and produce constant ticking, dripping, and mismatched shadows.
- **Guild plaza.** The island's clock-towers remain visible from the interior.
- **Coastal plants.** Salt-scrub, juniper, and samphire grow above the tide line and below the guild's cisterns; crews forage samphire for salted rations.
- **Wildlife.** Crabs pick through tide pools, hawks hunt along cliff thermals, giant crabs den in deep tide holes, and venomous snakes nest near cistern intakes.

## Why

The party comes to learn what the guild is measuring, negotiate access to its instruments, keep a schedule, or gather coastal food. Chronehall matters because its unexplained count can turn ordinary timekeeping into a live institutional mystery.

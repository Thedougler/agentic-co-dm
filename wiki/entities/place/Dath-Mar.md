# Dath-Mar

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Dath-Mar is a low refuge island on the rim of the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]]. Its outer beaches belong to dragon turtle wyrmlings, so even an empty-looking shore is not a free campsite. The island is a place to reach in an emergency or to disappear for a solitary retreat, not a settled harbor.

## At a Glance

Dath-Mar is the Sunken Crown's emergency refuge and solitary retreat. It sits among the low islands around the region's Blue Hole, but dragon turtle wyrmlings nest on its outer beaches. A party can seek shelter here only by accepting that the beach is occupied and that no permanent support or safe landing is established.

## If the party

- **Needs refuge.** Use Dath-Mar as a place to withdraw or hide during an emergency; no supplies, settlement, or rescuer is established.
- **Approaches the outer beaches.** Account for the dragon turtle wyrmlings before treating the island as empty.
- **Leaves the island behind.** The party gives up a known emergency refuge on the Sunken Crown rim; no alternate shelter is established on this page.

## Who

Dragon turtle wyrmlings nest on the outer beaches. No named human settlement, caretaker, or permanent resident is established.

## What

- A refuge used for emergencies.
- A place for solitary retreat.
- Outer beaches occupied by dragon turtle wyrmlings.
- A position on the ring of low islands around the Sunken Crown's Blue Hole.

## Why

Dath-Mar gives the party a known place to withdraw when the Sunken Crown becomes dangerous, but its usefulness has a cost: the outer beaches are a wyrmling nesting ground. The island is therefore a refuge to approach deliberately, not a consequence-free rest stop.

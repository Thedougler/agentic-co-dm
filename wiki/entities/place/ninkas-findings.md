# Ninka's Findings

> [!narration] Ninka's Findings
> A reinforced shop sits in Sparhold's fortress-market district between supply traders and blacksmiths. Inside, Ninka Solvei works a bone abacus without looking up, turning salvage, rust, and salt into a final price. A fractured Antheri wing-frame lies on the back shelf, and the counter's one bare plank is where the day's buying happens.

## At a Glance

Ninka's Findings is [[ninka-solvei|Ninka Solvei]]'s salvage shop in [[sparhold]], where salvaged magic oddities change hands and nobody is asked where anything came from.

- **Who is here.** [[ninka-solvei|Ninka Solvei]] behind the reinforced counter; no other staff is named.
- **Danger.** Buying here means taking an incomplete relic on the seller's word. The shop prices by rarity and condition and guarantees nothing about what the thing does.
- **Draw.** Stock from [[verdant-teeth]] expeditions and wreck-divers, including [[deadmans-cleat|Deadman's Cleat]] on the counter and a fractured Antheri wing-frame assembly, and the only sale in Sparhold that never wants provenance.

## Features

- **The reinforced shop.** Wedged between supply traders and blacksmiths in the fortress-market district, built to keep a shop full of oddities behind a counter.
- **The bone abacus.** Ninka works it without looking up and prices items by rarity and condition alone.
- **The stock.** Salvage bought off Teeth expeditions and wreck-divers sits in racks and on the counter, including [[deadmans-cleat|Deadman's Cleat]] and an incomplete Antheri wing-frame.
- **The three rules.** She does not ask where it came from, she does not ask where it is going, and she never gives a seller the buyer's name or a buyer the seller's.

## At the Table

- **Sell salvage.** Ninka quotes by rarity and condition and stops there. Provenance questions get a flat refusal, and a seller who tries to explain the story gets the same price he would have got without it.
- **Buy an oddity.** Anything on the racks can be had for the number she names. The wing-frame and other incomplete Antheri pieces come with her standard warning: it is Antheri work, incomplete, and the buyer takes the risk.
- **Ask about Vrex.** Her [[verdant-teeth]] contact has been silent for three weeks and the gap is showing in her stock. She will trade information or a discount for someone who finds out what happened to him.
- **Try to talk her into credit or a story.** Both are refused. Cash, a trade, or a job is the only currency the counter takes.

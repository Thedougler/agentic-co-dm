# Ninka's Findings

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> A reinforced shop sits in Sparhold's fortress-market district between supply traders and blacksmiths. Inside, Ninka Solvei works a bone abacus without looking up, turning salvage, rust, and salt into a final price.

## At a Glance

[[Ninka's Findings]] is [[ninka-solvei|Ninka Solvei]]'s salvage shop in [[Sparhold]], where salvaged magic oddities change hands without provenance questions.

## If the party

- **Trade salvage.** The party can buy or sell a salvaged item; Ninka quotes a price by rarity and condition and walks away from provenance, destination, or credit questions.

## Who

[[ninka-solvei|Ninka Solvei]] is the fence and proprietor. The current record names no other staff.

## What

The shop holds salvage from [[verdant-teeth]] expeditions and wreck-divers, priced by rarity and condition. [[Deadman's Cleat]] is on the counter, and a fractured Antheri wing-frame assembly is among the uncertain stock.

## Where

Ninka's Findings is in [[Sparhold]]'s fortress-market district, wedged between supply traders and blacksmiths.

## Why

The party comes to buy or fence salvaged magic, ask about the silent [[verdant-teeth]] contact Vrex, or decide what risk to take on an incomplete Antheri relic. Ninka needs someone to find out what happened to Vrex.

# Otel Karn

> [!narration] Otel Karn
> Otel Karn is a remote island where the old teaching treats flesh as the vehicle of spirit. Nothing on it dies: butchered livestock stand back up in the yard and graze on, wounds close over into scars that never leave, and the villages have learned to dress a killing stroke as an ordinary accident.

## At a Glance

Otel Karn is the remote island where death has stopped working, and the root of the tradition the [[dravosi-crown]] calls [[khlysty-the-flock]].

- **Who is here.** [[otho]], who lives among the islanders as a beloved local drunk; the villages, including [[stillmere]]; and the islanders whose old teaching treats flesh as the vehicle of spirit.
- **Danger.** Nothing here kills and nothing here heals. A fight leaves both parties alive, in pain, and permanently hurt, and the island does not care which of them started it.
- **Draw.** The origin of the blood-anchor technique, the proof of what the Flock's tradition actually does, and [[otho]] himself: the one person whose careless bargain caused all of it.

## Features

- **Stopped death.** No creature on the island dies. Livestock butchered in the yard walk and graze afterwards, and a person cut down gets up and keeps the wound.
- **Wounds that stay.** Nothing heals either: a broken arm stays broken, a cut stays open, and pain does not fade with the injury. The island's villages have built their manners around it.
- **[[stillmere]].** The proving ground where a duel can leave a fighter wounded without leaving anyone dead; the crew can learn the cost of the island's rule there before it becomes permanent.
- **The old teaching.** Flesh as the vehicle of spirit, kept by the islanders and named by the Crown as [[khlysty-the-flock|the Flock]]. [[shepherd-grigori]] learned the blood-anchor technique here without knowing its true age.
- **[[otho]].** A beloved local drunk by every appearance, and the man whose careless bargain stopped death working on the island.

## At the Table

- **Fight anyone here.** Both sides get up. Damage is real, pain is real, and healing does not work, so a party that brawls on Otel Karn carries every wound out for good.
- **Watch the livestock.** Butchered animals standing and grazing is the island's rule made visible, and the villagers treat it as ordinary.
- **Test the limit.** A lethal wound does not kill, and the villagers have stopped being surprised. The source establishes the effect and not its extent, so anything past what it names is the DM's call.
- **Ask about the tradition.** The old teaching is ordinary conversation here, and the Crown's name for it is not.
- **Find [[otho]].** He is the cause. Boring him is named as the easiest resolution; a straight fight against him at near level 10 is named as unwinnable.
- **Find [[shepherd-grigori|Grigori]].** He learned the blood-anchor technique on this island and does not know how old it is.

## Secrets

**Secret.** Nothing on Otel Karn dies because of [[otho]]'s careless bargain, and the island's whole condition — the walking livestock, the unhealable wounds, the villages' manners — is the consequence of that one deal rather than a property of the ground. The tells are a village that does not flinch at a killing stroke and a drunk everyone greets warmly, and the find is the story the islanders tell about Otho, which is the only account of how it started.

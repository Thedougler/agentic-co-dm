# Sorrowbell

## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> The causeway ends at a headland shrine where the Tyr box stands empty. Ash smears and tally marks mark the stone, and the wind carries the sound of the town behind you toward the locked sanctuary.


Sorrowbell crowns the headland where the causeway ends at [[penance-bar|Penance Bar]]. The shrine's Tyr box stands empty when the demon arrives, while ash smears and tally marks record the siege evidence tied to [[corvin-ashe|Corvin Ashe]]'s fabrication.

## At a Glance

Sorrowbell is Penance Bar's headland shrine and the locked site of the Tyr-box confrontation. [[corvin-ashe|Corvin Ashe]] uses the shrine's authority while scapegoating a woods coven; the failed sanctuary leaves the box empty when the demon enters. The causeway is the established approach, but no other route or shrine caretaker is established.

## If the party

- **Approaches from town.** Follow the causeway to the headland; the shrine is the confrontation site, not a neutral retreat.
- **Searches the shrine.** Read the ash smears and tally marks as siege evidence connected to Ashe's fabrication.
- **Treats the box as sanctuary.** The box is empty in the established confrontation. Its failure is the visible pressure that exposes Ashe.

## Who

[[corvin-ashe|Corvin Ashe]] is the Bishop of Tyr at Sorrowbell and the person whose lies turn the town against the woods coven. [[sula-marn|Sula Marn]] is the genuine ally in Penance Bar's siege. No resident shrine staff are established.

## What

- A headland shrine at the end of Penance Bar's causeway.
- An empty Tyr box at the locked confrontation.
- Ash smears and tally marks left as siege evidence.
- A failed sanctuary whose public authority can be challenged.

## Where

**North:** Canon gap; no named northern route beyond the causeway is established.  
**East:** Canon gap; no named eastern shore or route is established.  
**South:** The causeway toward [[penance-bar|Penance Bar]]; travel time is not established.  
**West:** Canon gap; no named western shore or route is established.

## Why

Sorrowbell is where the town's fear, Ashe's religious authority, and the demon's arrival become visible at once. The party uses it to expose the failed sanctuary, interpret the siege evidence, and decide what to do when the shrine's promise of protection proves false.

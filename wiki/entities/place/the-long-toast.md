# The Long Toast

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> A tavern bar holds the same clean spot under Ysolde Sallow's cloth while dark rum catches the lamplight in short glasses. The room's gallows humor is doing work the rest of Stillmere refuses to do.

## At a Glance

[[The Long Toast]] is [[ysolde-sallow|Ysolde Sallow]]'s tavern on [[otel-karn]], known for its dark-rum house drink, [[widows-toast|Widow's Toast]].

## If the party

- **Ask twice.** The party can order a Widow's Toast or ask Ysolde about the draw-duel and the wounds that did not heal; she answers a straight question after it is asked twice.

## Who

[[ysolde-sallow|Ysolde Sallow]] runs the bar. The two draw-duel survivors drank there after the duel; no regular staff count is established.

## What

The tavern serves [[widows-toast|Widow's Toast]], dark rum with its widowhood joke. The bar carries the island's gallows humor, and Ysolde watched the draw-duel firsthand.

## Where

The Long Toast is in [[Stillmere]] on [[otel-karn]]. The current record does not establish its neighboring businesses or route from the island's landing.

## Why

The party comes for a drink, Ysolde's unvarnished account of Otel Karn, or the survivors' story. The tavern is a place where the island's refusal to name its horror can be challenged.

# The Long Toast

> [!narration] The Long Toast
> A tavern bar holds the same clean spot under Ysolde Sallow's cloth while dark rum catches the lamplight in short glasses. Men with bandaged hands and unclosed wounds lean on the rail in no hurry to leave, and the room's gallows humor is doing work the rest of Stillmere refuses to do. The floor in front of the bar is scrubbed to the boards.

## At a Glance

The Long Toast is [[ysolde-sallow|Ysolde Sallow]]'s tavern in [[Stillmere]] on [[otel-karn]], the bar where the island's draw-duel survivors drink and its silence gets broken.

- **Who is here.** [[ysolde-sallow|Ysolde Sallow]] behind the bar; the draw-duel's survivors in her chairs; [[gerrit-sallow]] and the island's duellists and their backers around the tables.
- **Danger.** It is the friendliest room on an island where wounds do not close. A fight started here ends with a loser who wakes up and stays hurt for good.
- **Draw.** [[widows-toast|Widow's Toast]], a straight account of the draw-duel from the one person who watched it and will not pretend, and the night's betting on who goes down first.

## Features

- **The bar and the clean spot.** Ysolde wipes the same spot over and over, sleeves shoved past scarred forearms, and keeps moving glasses even mid-conversation.
- **The house drink.** [[widows-toast|Widow's Toast]] is dark rum served short, and the joke underneath it is that nobody on Otel Karn has ever gone a widow.
- **The duel crowd.** The draw-duel two nights back put both survivors in these chairs within the hour, blood not yet mopped, and the bet on who goes down first tonight stays open. The stakes mean little when nobody stays down.
- **The rule of two askings.** Ysolde answers a straight question only after it has been asked twice, pouring a drink in the gap between the first ask and the second.

## At the Table

- **Order a drink.** Widow's Toast comes short and dark. Ysolde pours for anyone who pays and cuts off anyone who sounds like they came to gawk.
- **Ask about the duel.** The first asking gets a joke and a raised eyebrow. The second gets the account: who drew, who bled, and that both men walked out and kept their wounds.
- **Ask what the island is.** Ysolde says plainly that nothing here heals and nothing here dies, and names [[otho]] as the reason the village has learned to live with it.
- **Start a fight.** The room backs off and watches. Nobody in it dies, and the loser carries the damage out the door, which is why the regulars drink here rather than in the village square.

## Secrets

**Secret.** Nothing on Otel Karn heals and nothing on it dies, because [[otho]]'s careless bargain stopped death from working on the island. The Long Toast is where that fact is visible instead of denied: the survivors drinking with unclosed wounds, the livestock that keeps grazing after the butcher, and a barmaid who has watched the same wound fail to kill the same man a dozen times. Ysolde's account is the first unvarnished version a visitor can get, and she gives it once asked twice.

# The Marrow

> [!narration] Narration
> The Marrow is a foggy ridge running north to south through Calder's Tooth. The ground is porous with sinkholes and collapsed caves, and the Overland Track crosses its southern edge.

The Marrow is a foggy north-south interior ridge in [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall|Calder's Tooth]]. Sinkholes and collapsed caves make the interior porous, while the [[overland-track|Overland Track]] crosses its southern ground.

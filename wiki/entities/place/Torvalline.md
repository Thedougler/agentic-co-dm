# Torvalline

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Torvalline is a shipbreaker's yard built around wrecks being taken apart for parts. Hull fragments, stripped fittings, and lengths of rigging turn the site into a working shoreline of salvage, where the useful remains of one vessel become the equipment of another. The yard's exact layout, operators, and current wrecks are not established; what is clear is that half the Midchain's “used” rigging traces back here.

---
## At a Glance

Torvalline is a named shipbreaker's yard in the eastern [[midchain-east|Eastern Midchain]], supplying reused rigging across the chain. No canon route or travel distance is recorded; bypassing it costs the party access to a major source of second-hand maritime gear and the chance to trace where a piece of rigging came from.

---
## If the party

- **Inspect the yard.** The party can sort wreck parts by use and origin, but the page does not establish a named yardmaster, inventory, or specific wreck waiting on the blocks.
- **Trace rigging.** The party can use a length of “used” rigging as a lead back to Torvalline. The established fact is the site's broad supply reach, not the ownership or history of any particular line.
- **Sail on.** The party can bypass the yard and continue through the eastern Midchain. They lose the chance to source or investigate reused equipment at its point of origin.

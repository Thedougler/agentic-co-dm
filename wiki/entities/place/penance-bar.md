# Penance Bar

> [!narration] Penance Bar
> Penance Bar is a sandspit shrine in the western Midchain, built for penance against devils it never summoned. One street of houses stands along the spit, a stone causeway runs out to the headland shrine, and the last field of the island gives way to the grove. Ash smears and tally marks are cut into the shrine stone, and today the town is walking the causeway with fire.

## At a Glance

Penance Bar is a sandspit island in the western [[midchain]] whose town is marching on its last hallowed grove while the fear that drives it draws something worse.

- **Who is here.** [[corvin-ashe|Corvin Ashe]], Bishop of [[tyr|Tyr]] at [[sorrowbell]], who sent the town at the grove; [[sula-marn|Sula Marn]], the Reeve, holding the peace; the mob he whipped up; and the woods coven past the last field, with [[halda-wren|Halda Wren]] trading willowbark at Sandy Grove.
- **Danger.** The mob reaches the grove by sundown, and the demons Ashe's fearmongering has drawn are already arriving. The shrine's sanctuary is the thing everyone is counting on and it does not hold.
- **Draw.** The shrine's true ledger and locked quarters, the grove's side of the story, and a town that can be stopped before sundown if the truth lands in front of the mob.

## Features

- **The spit and the town.** One street of houses on the sandspit, close enough that the shrine's bell carries over the roofs and the whole town can be in the street at once.
- **The causeway.** The stone walk out to the headland shrine, and the only route the party and the mob both use. [[sula-marn|Sula Marn]] catches people on it; the crowd is already moving down it.
- **[[sorrowbell]].** The headland shrine at the end of the causeway, with an empty Tyr box, ash smears, and tally marks cut into the stone. The confrontation site, and not a neutral retreat.
- **Sandy Grove.** The last hallowed ground, past the last field of the island, where the woods coven lives and [[halda-wren|Halda Wren]] splits kindling and trades willowbark for salt fish. A quiet place that was never asked into town and does not ask to be.
- **The mob.** The town in a body, carrying fire, moving at the pace of its slowest member toward the grove.

## At the Table

- **Read the shrine's evidence.** The ash smears and tally marks at Sorrowbell are siege evidence connected to Ashe's fabrication. **Intelligence (Investigation) — `DC 15`** reads them as a record somebody kept for a reason, and the locked quarters and true ledger name whose.
- **Catch the mob on the causeway.** Getting in front of it is the whole job. **Charisma (Persuasion) — `DC 17`** turns the crowd with the truth about Ashe; a failure means the party is heard as another outsider defending witches and the march does not stop.
- **Reach the grove first.** The party can run the shore and the last field ahead of the crowd. It buys time to talk to the coven and to bring a witness back to the causeway.
- **Treat the Tyr box as sanctuary.** The box is empty when the demon arrives, and the shrine's promise of protection fails in public. A party that has already shown the town the empty box does not have to argue about the grove.
- **Ask Ashe to stop the march.** He sends the asker to the grove instead. A question that treats the coven as framed, or a look at his locked quarters, ends his welcome.

## Secrets

**Secret.** Ashe's fearmongering is cover: he sends the town at the grove to protect his own standing, and the locked quarters and true ledger hold the evidence of what he is paying off. The ash smears and tally marks at the shrine are siege evidence, and outsiders read the empty Tyr box at a glance, because Tyr withdraws from a straying follower before the withdrawal is announced. The demon only has to say the box is empty out loud, and the kiss is reserved for a soul every side already agrees is gone.

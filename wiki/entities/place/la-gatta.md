# La Gatta

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> A board on trestles sits under the Bridge's arch between a fry stall and a rope seller. Paper cones and hand-rolled cigarettes cover it, a grey cat sleeps across the middle, and a brass lighter hangs on a chain long enough to reach each customer.

## At a Glance

[[La Gatta]] is [[nicco-kettley|Nicco Kettley]]'s vending board on the bottom tier of [[the Bridge]], selling rejected alchemical batches below the price of licensed rooms.

## If the party

- **Buy or bargain.** The party can buy a short-batch blend, ask why a batch was rejected, or bargain with Nicco for information instead of coin.

## Who

[[nicco-kettley|Nicco Kettley]] runs the board. A grey cat sleeps across it; the current record names no other attendant.

## What

The board carries paper cones of loose leaf, hand-rolled cigarettes, and [[The Chained Lighter]], which is bolted to the board and locked separately at night. Nicco stocks short batches and failed runs from [[Studio Orsini]].

## Where

La Gatta is on the bottom tier of [[the Bridge]] in [[calven-and-calveno]], wedged between a fry stall and a rope seller.

## Why

The party comes for cheaper, unreliable alchemical blends, the Chained Lighter, or Nicco's gossip. Nicco is trying to lease a shop with a door and lock on the Bridge's middle tier before the Beffa crowds thin.

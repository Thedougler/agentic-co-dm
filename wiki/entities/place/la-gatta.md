# La Gatta

> [!narration] La Gatta
> A board on trestles sits under the Bridge's arch between a fry stall and a rope seller. Paper cones and hand-rolled cigarettes cover it, a grey cat sleeps across the middle in the one patch of sun that reaches under the arch, and a brass lighter hangs on a chain long enough to reach each customer and not one inch past them. Behind the board, a halfling answers two people at once and neither of them is you.

## At a Glance

La Gatta is [[nicco-kettley|Nicco Kettley]]'s vending board on the bottom tier of [[the Bridge]], selling rejected alchemical batches below the price of any licensed room in the city.

- **Who is here.** [[nicco-kettley|Nicco Kettley]] behind the board with a second board strapped across his chest, and the grey cat asleep on the trestles.
- **Danger.** The whole stock is [[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]]'s rejects, bought through a third party, and Orsini has started asking who is reselling her failures under the arch.
- **Draw.** Seconds at prices nobody official can match, [[the-chained-lighter|the Chained Lighter]] bolted to the board, and gossip that Nicco trades a fact for a fact.

## Features

- **The board.** Two trestles under the arch, wedged between the fry stall and the rope seller, with the cat asleep on the middle of it and Nicco working around the animal rather than moving it.
- **The stock.** Paper cones of loose leaf and a tin of hand-rolled cigarettes twisted shut by hand, all of it short batches and failed runs from [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]] bought at a fraction of a finished blend's price.
- **[[the-chained-lighter|The Chained Lighter]].** A brass lighter on a long chain, bolted to the board and locked separately at night, so a customer can light up without the lighter leaving the trestles.
- **The gossip trade.** Nicco quotes a different price to each customer and dares them to notice, pays for stock in gossip wherever a seller will take it, and counts change coin by coin without breaking eye contact.

## At the Table

- **Buy a short-batch blend.** Ninety gets a cone that "does what it does, and sometimes does nothing", which is the honest description of a rejected batch. The price changes for the next customer.
- **Catch the price change.** Hearing the same blend quoted two ways is **Wisdom (Insight) — `DC 15`**; a success means the party knows what the goods really cost and can bargain from there instead of being quoted down.
- **Ask why a batch was rejected.** Nicco says so cheerfully after a burn and offers the next one at a discount. He knows the failures because Orsini's seals and labels come through the third party with the stock.
- **Trade gossip instead of coin.** Works, and it is his preferred currency. A fact he does not have is worth stock; a fact he already holds is worth nothing and he says so.
- **Move the cat.** Don't. He stops the sale, takes the animal back into the sun patch, and starts the price again from the beginning.

## Secrets

**Secret.** La Gatta's entire stock is [[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]]'s rejected batches, bought through a third party she has never agreed to supply, and she has begun asking who resells her failures under the arch. If she traces the board, the supply stops and the takings stop with it; if a customer reports it for her, they gain her attention with an unmarked studio in [[le-paludi]]. The tells are the twisted-shut cigarettes in the tin, the price no licensed room can match, and Nicco's answer when a blend burns wrong.

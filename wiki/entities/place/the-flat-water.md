# The Flat Water

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> The room is a Kalowe port dive where a tortle bartender works alone from mid-morning until closing. Reweti Kahurangi moves slowly behind the bar, shell angled to keep the room in view, while contracts and quiet information pass between patrons who know how to respect the space.

## At a Glance

The Flat Water is a Kalowe establishment run by [[Reweti Kahurangi]]. It is a place to find contracts and quiet intelligence, but Reweti gives useful information only once to a party that is polite and pays its tab.

## If the party

- **Pay its tab without trouble.** Reweti may provide one useful piece of information.
- **Ask for a contract.** The room can connect capable parties to local work, including [[Kalowe Bad Receipt Cache]] and [[Kalowe Merrow's Prize]].
- **Pressure Reweti.** She distrusts crews and can withhold the information that would otherwise pass through the room.
- **Return later.** The establishment remains a stable Kalowe contact point; its current contracts and information are not otherwise established.

## Who

- [[Reweti Kahurangi]] runs the room alone and remembers what she hears.
- Patrons and prospective crews pass through, but the source does not identify a standing roster.

## What

- Contracts and quiet information pass through the room.
- Reweti's bar and the patrons' tabs are the practical access points to its information.
- The establishment's value is its trusted intelligence, not a named stock of goods.

# Totemo Tokage

## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> Totemo Tokage spreads across a large Mid-chain island of bamboo, wet rice fields, dark woodland, winding rivers, marshes, and steep mountains. Fishing villages follow the coast, while smoke rises from kilns below the snowy central peaks. The island offers many routes inland, but every route enters the reach of one of its old lizard-folk clans.

## At a Glance

Totemo Tokage is the long-established homeland of the lizard-folk in the Mid-chain. Six clans control different settlements, industries, and territories; they feud or trade with one another, then unite when an outside threat reaches the island. The western and northern approaches can be bypassed, but doing so gives up access to the clans' settlements, harbor, mountain temple, and trade.

## If the party

- **Enter through the west.** [[Hōnteiru no Mura]] offers a fortified approach under the [[horntail-clan|Horntail Clan]], whose archers and sword-fighters defend the island openly.
- **Take the southwest harbor.** [[Zennitana]] opens the capital, fishing villages, boat makers, and the [[omusubi-family|Omusubi Family]]'s royal claim; the [[ryu-no-kage|Ryu no Kage]] operates there from the shadows.
- **Climb toward the center.** The ascent to [[Shōrin Hakushin-ji]] is dangerous and ends at the snowy home of the [[yuki-no-kibo|Yuki no Kibo]]. The temple's trials are open to all, but few complete them.
- **Seek weapons or ore.** [[Kurogane-mura]] provides the [[kento-clan|Kento Clan]]'s mines, kilns, armor, and weapons.
- **Cross the northern marshes.** The [[akai-hodo-clan|Akai Hodo Clan]] controls the red mist and answers intrusion with illusion, camouflage, stealth, and silent kills.
- **Buy force or illicit goods.** The [[ebony-scales|Ebony Scales]] conduct the most business with outsiders and can provide fighters, muscle, and drugs, but they kill slavers on sight.

## Who

The six major clans steer the island's lizard-folk population. Their named leaders are [[akibana-besko|Akibana Besko]], [[ranga-gex|Ranga Gex]], [[rango-gex|Rango Gex]], [[kinryu-omusubi|Kinryu Omusubi]], [[master-miki-trox|Master Miki Trox]], [[takayama-kongo|Takayama Kongo]], and [[mizobasho-red-tail|Mizobasho Red Tail]]. The [[ryu-no-kage|Ryu no Kage]] is led by [[madam-hino|Madam Hino]] with [[biggu-baito|Biggu Baito]] as head general. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]] is one of the only named outsiders stated to know Madam Hino's true appearance.

## What

The island's usable resources are rice, fishing, sake, boat building, ore, blacksmithing, armor, weapons, drugs, and hired muscle. The central range rises to snowy high-altitude terrain. The northern marshes are wrapped in constant red mist or fog. The Ebony Scales' late leader, [[don-gex|Don Gex]], waged a three-year war against local and foreign slavers; slavery effectively ended on the island, and the Ebony Scales still kill slavers on sight.

## Where

- **North:** The marshlands and thick forest of the [[akai-hodo-clan|Akai Hodo Clan]]; the exact northern coastline and outer-island neighbors are not established.
- **East:** No named eastern neighbor or route is established in the source; treat the island's eastern edge as an open canon gap.
- **South:** Coastal fishing villages surround [[Zennitana]] in the southwest; the source does not establish a separate southern settlement or travel distance.
- **West:** [[Hōnteiru no Mura]] and the western territory of the [[horntail-clan|Horntail Clan]]. No travel distance from the island's outer approach is established.
- **Center:** The central mountain range contains [[Kurogane-mura]] at its base and [[Shōrin Hakushin-ji]] at the snowy summit.

## Why

A party comes to Totemo Tokage for harbor trade, clan alliances, weapons, mountain trials, illicit services, or the island's response to an outside threat. The important choice is which clan's protection, rivalry, or obligation the party accepts before the clans' shared defense turns the island into a single front. The island's political and defensive tensions connect the Horntail and Akai Hodo clans, the Omusubi Family and Ryu no Kage, the Yuki no Kibo and Kento Clan, and the Ebony Scales' trade to Don Gex's anti-slavery legacy.

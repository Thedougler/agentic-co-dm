# Grimaldi's Dispensary

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Camphor and vinegar cling to the sleeves of the tiefling behind the counter. A mortar keeps grinding while shelves hold healer's supplies, and a curtain at the back hides the room where a wheezing old privateer waits for treatment. The shop is small enough that every price and every question carries across it.

## At a Glance

Grimaldi's Dispensary is [[kalowe]]'s only healer's supply, run by [[Ilaria Grimaldi]]. Its curtained back room holds [[Sera Maddock]], who is dying and needs treatment unavailable in Kalowe.

## If the party

- **Ask for healer's supplies.** Ilaria names one price and expects payment without haggling.
- **Ask the right way.** Ilaria may disclose one of the secrets she is holding, including Sera Maddock's condition and the lead recorded as [[The Captain's Map]].
- **Offer Sera passage.** Sera can offer a tattooed treasure map in exchange for 200 gp and passage out of Kalowe toward a healer city.
- **Leave Sera behind.** The source gives her roughly three days before death by lung sickness unless she reaches treatment elsewhere.

## Who

- [[Ilaria Grimaldi]] works the counter and protects the dispensary's fifteen-year truce with Kalowe's harbour bosses.
- [[Sera Maddock]] lies in the back room, wheezing from lung sickness and seeking passage out of Kalowe.

## What

- The counter, mortar, and ordinary healer's supplies remain in active use.
- The curtained back room shelters Sera Maddock and any stock Ilaria will not put on the shelves.
- Sera's left forearm bears half of a chart to a cache on [[orak]].

## Where

- **North:** [[kalowe]]; the dispensary is on Kalowe's third island, but the exact street route is not established.
- **East:** No separate canonized neighbor is recorded.
- **South:** No separate canonized neighbor is recorded.
- **West:** No separate canonized neighbor is recorded.
- The dispensary's durable internal connection is its back room, where Sera is dying.

## Why

The party comes here for healer's supplies, treatment, discreet information, or the opportunity to act on Sera Maddock's demand for passage. Ilaria's counter is also a pressure point: the party can protect the shop's truce, ask for one of its held secrets, or decide whether Sera's map is worth the cost of getting her out.

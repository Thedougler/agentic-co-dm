# The Teeth

> [!narration] The Teeth
> At low tide, jagged reef teeth break the water beyond Aruhe's western shingle. Coral points stand among wreck timber — planks off boats no one has counted, hull ribs, and a skull wedged where the coral closed over it. Pale sand shows between the coral, and one channel of deeper water runs through them.

## At a Glance

The Teeth are the reef line across Aruhe's western approach: the coral points, the one boat gap through them, and the shingle and wreckage the tide uncovers.

- **Who is here.** [[reef-skull|Reef Skulls]] — dog-sized hermit crabs wearing hull planks and skulls as shells — bury under the shingle and hunt the reef in groups of three to five at low tide.
- **Danger.** Outside the gap the coral is shallow and broken, and a buried reef skull erupts under a foot and drags its catch toward deep water.
- **Draw.** The only boat approach at Aruhe's west, wreck timber worth having, and the water that tells a pilot whether the gap is open.

## Features

- **The reef teeth.** Coral points stand above the surface at low tide and just under it at high. A boat that leaves the channel is on coral: the hull opens at the waterline and the crew must beach and patch, losing the tide they came on.
- **The boat gap.** The single channel wide enough for a boat runs through the teeth. Spotting the gap, the exposed teeth, and movement in the water, and piloting through, resolve on [[western-landing]]; this reef is what those checks read.
- **The wreck line.** The reef has collected wreckage for generations: Grung war-proas, fishing boats, and the bones of everyone Aruhe has killed. Planking, ribs, and skulls lie in the coral and shingle — timber worth money on a timber-poor Midchain, and material [[reef-skull]]s have learned to wear.
- **Buried [[reef-skull]]s.** The crabs bury themselves with only the top of the shell showing, and a shell that sits above the tide line is as likely to be occupied as dead. Reading a shell before stepping past it uses the beach rules on [[western-landing]].

## At the Table

- **Cross the reef at low tide on foot.** Wet coral is broken and slick. **Dexterity (Acrobatics) — `DC 12`** to cross the teeth to the outer rocks; a failure puts the character down on the coral for `1d4` slashing damage and leaves the party standing in the open at the surf line.
- **Steer the boat outside the gap.** The coral opens the hull; beaching and patching costs the tide and a day. The party can also pass the outer teeth at slack high water, when the coral sits deeper and the surf is quiet.
- **Work the wreck line.** Cutting planks out of the coral and wreck takes an hour and a blade. Anyone who stays in one spot working the debris is standing where [[reef-skull]]s hunt, and they surface at the nearest movement.
- **Watch the surf line change.** Falling tide exposes more teeth and more debris, and every newly exposed shell is a chance to read the beach or to be read by it.

## History

The reef has eaten hulls since the Grung war-proas first broke on it, and everything since: fishing boats, colonial hulls, and the crews that went down with them. The crabs grew out of their natural shells on this supply, and the wreck that gives the Teeth their name is also what makes the reef skulls the size they are.

# The Teeth

## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> At low tide, jagged reef teeth break the water beyond Aruhe's western shingle. Coral points and wreckage show through the shallows. The approach remains legible but hazardous.

---
## At a Glance
The Teeth are the exposed reef points and shingle debris at [[western-landing|Western Landing]]. The boat gap crosses the reef, while low tide exposes more of the teeth and the debris where [[reef-skull|Reef Skulls]] bury themselves.

---
## Who

- [[reef-skull|Reef Skulls]] bury in the shingle and reef debris at low tide.

---
## What

- Jagged coral reef teeth exposed by low tide.
- Shingle and wreckage that can conceal a buried Reef Skull.

---
## Where

- The Teeth lie offshore from [[western-landing|Western Landing]] on Aruhe's western rim.

---
## Why

- The reef teeth and debris determine which approach through the boat gap remains usable.
- A buried Reef Skull can turn the shingle crossing into an immediate hazard.

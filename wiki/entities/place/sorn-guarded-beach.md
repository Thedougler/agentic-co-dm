# Sorn Guarded Beach

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Pale sand lies behind the reef, with a long hut on the shore and flood-pulse crop lanes standing in water beyond it. Salt and wet earth hang at the treeline. The last hundred yards of beach are freshly recut; farther inland, vine takes the next row.

## At a Glance

Sorn Guarded Beach is Sorn's held landing and [[simone]]'s post in the [[verdant-teeth]]. The beach connects reef approach, the long hut, and the first flood-pulse crop lanes; the interior beyond those lanes is not open. The landing can be skipped by waiting offshore and trading through blue intermediaries, but doing so loses direct access to Simone and the evidence of who controls the shore.

## If the party

- **Wait offshore.** Trade on the sand through blue intermediaries without making a direct approach to the post.
- **Ask for [[simone]].** Make the family hunt visible and invite a response from the garrison.
- **Enter the first crop lanes.** Become trespassers in Botukuri flood-pulse agriculture while the water changes the available route.
- **Retreat before the water rises.** Leave by the beach before the same route becomes a channel.

## Who

- A purple garrison holds the post and watches the last hundred yards of recut beach.
- [[simone]] commands or represents the post; resolve her motives on her owner page.
- Botukuri farmers work the flood-pulse crop lanes.
- Blue intermediaries handle shore trade.
- This is a long hut and watch, not a town or fort. Raids do not launch from here.

## What

- The last hundred yards of beach are repeatedly recut as policy.
- Flood-pulse crop lanes stand in water and can become channels as the water rises.
- The dark band behind the first rows is the take-back: last season's field under vine.
- Toxin trade and substitute-gold evidence point toward Dravosi buyers and Karath's absent reagent.

## Where

- **North:** The interior crop lanes of Sorn; the route is controlled and no farther named connection is established here.
- **East:** [[sorn]] and the Botukuri farm heart; the exact travel distance is not established in canon.
- **South:** The coastline and reef approach; the exact neighboring landing is not established in canon.
- **West:** Open water beyond the reef; approach by boat or wait offshore.

## Why

The party comes to trade, reach [[simone]], identify who controls Sorn's landing, or follow the toxin and substitute-gold evidence toward Dravosi buyers and Karath. They stay when the changing flood pulse makes the beach route unsafe, when the post offers information, or when entering the first lanes reveals how Sorn's agriculture and defenses work.

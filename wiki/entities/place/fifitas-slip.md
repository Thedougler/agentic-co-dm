# Fifita's Slip

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> On Kalowe's lagoon side, a boatyard has been made to look like storage. Hulls sit behind whatever cover the island provides, and the work proceeds slowly enough that a stranger might mistake the place for an abandoned shed.

## At a Glance

[[Fifita's Slip]] is [[noa-fifita|Noa Fifita]]'s unofficial boatyard on [[Kalowe]]'s lagoon side, outside the harbour books.

## If the party

- **Arrive by arrangement.** The party can bring a prior introduction, arrange work through the Fifita family, or approach the slip from the water; a cold water approach gets nothing.

## Who

[[noa-fifita|Noa Fifita]] operates the slip. His family owns [[Taufa Fifita & Sons]], but Noa works alone at the boatyard and sets a price by the hull.

## What

The slip is an unofficial boatyard. Noa works slowly and surely, does not haggle, and leaves if the customer will not pay his price.

## Where

Fifita's Slip is on [[Kalowe]]'s lagoon side. It does not appear in harbour books, and its access is by introduction or prior arrangement only.

## Why

The party comes to arrange work outside harbour records, reach Noa through his family's trading cover, or use the lagoon-side yard without announcing the boat's business to the port.

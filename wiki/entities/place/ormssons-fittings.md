# Ormsson's Fittings

> [!narration] Ormsson's Fittings
> The shop sits closest to Kalowe's main dry-dock, with the curtain partly open and ironwork packed tight behind it — pawls, rings, chain, and plate, each in its own bin and each within reach of the bench. A winch pawl clicks to a stop on the counter, and Kettil measures the customer instead of making conversation.

## At a Glance

Ormsson's Fittings is [[kettil-ormsson|Kettil Ormsson]]'s hardware and arms shop on [[kalowe]], the closest shop to the main dry-dock.

- **Who is here.** [[kettil-ormsson|Kettil Ormsson]] alone, working at the bench beside a partly open curtain. He does not chat and he closes when the dry dock does.
- **Danger.** The back shelf is an unadvertised arms trade. A customer who over-explains, asks colonial questions, or treats the shelf as owed to them loses the shop's interest and the curtain stays closed.
- **Draw.** Dry-dock hardware at a fair price, a counter you can try the fittings on, and weapons that are not on display to anyone Kettil has not read.

## Features

- **The bins and the bench.** Fittings packed tight and reachable, with the dry dock's noise coming through the wall and marking the shop's hours.
- **[[chain-brake-winch|The Chain-Brake Winch]].** Bolted to the counter for customers to work, which is how Kettil sells an action instead of describing one.
- **The [[salvage-plate-harness|Salvage-Plate Harness]].** Standing at the end of the counter, armour for work done under a wreck.
- **The curtain.** Partly open by default and the only door to the back shelf. Kettil pulls it back, never the customer, and only for someone who says what they need without saying why.

## At the Table

- **Ask plainly for fittings.** Kettil reads what the customer needs, quotes a fair price, and does not fill silence. A clear order gets filled; a story gets a stare.
- **Try the counter pieces.** The [[chain-brake-winch|Chain-Brake Winch]] and the [[salvage-plate-harness|Salvage-Plate Harness]] can be worked on the spot, which is the fastest way for a party to know whether they want them.
- **Ask for the back shelf.** Say what is needed and nothing more. A flat request opens the curtain; an explanation or a claim of entitlement closes it for the visit.
- **Bring colonial questions.** Kettil left Crown smithing because colonial smiths asked too many questions, and those questions are how to lose him. He keeps the curtain closed, loses interest, and ends the conversation at closing time.
- **Ask about the local [[sawek|Sawek]] trouble.** He will speak about it if asked without theatrics, and he knows which fittings crews have been buying for it.

## Secrets

**Secret.** Kettil runs an unadvertised arms trade out of the back shelf while quoting honest prices on the counter — the fair dealing is real, and the selectivity is the whole other business. The tells are the partly open curtain he never lets a customer touch, a counter full of fittings a dry dock can buy anywhere, and the abrupt loss of interest in anyone who explains too much. The find is asking for what you need in one sentence and letting him decide.

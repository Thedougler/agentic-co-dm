# Ormsson's Fittings

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> The shop sits closest to Kalowe's main dry-dock, with the curtain partly open and ironwork packed tight behind it. A winch pawl clicks to a stop on the counter while Kettil Ormsson measures the customer instead of making conversation.

## At a Glance

[[Ormsson's Fittings]] is [[kettil-ormsson|Kettil Ormsson]]'s hardware and arms shop on [[Kalowe]], closest to the main dry-dock.

## If the party

- **Ask plainly.** The party can buy fittings, try [[Chain-Brake Winch|the Chain-Brake Winch]], or ask Kettil about the unadvertised back shelf; over-explaining loses his interest.

## Who

[[kettil-ormsson|Kettil Ormsson]] runs the shop alone. He quotes a fair price, reads what a customer needs, and decides when the partly open curtain is pulled back.

## What

The shop sells dry-dock hardware and keeps an unadvertised back-shelf arms trade. [[Chain-Brake Winch|The Chain-Brake Winch]] is bolted to the counter for customers to try, and a [[salvage-plate-harness|Salvage-Plate Harness]] stands at the counter's end.

## Where

Ormsson's Fittings is on [[Kalowe]], closest to the main dry-dock. The exact island and neighboring businesses are not established beyond Kettil's statement that it is on Kalowe's first island.

## Why

The party comes for ship fittings, a way to win ground against a dragging creature, or weapons that are not advertised. Kettil may speak about the local [[Sawek]] trouble if asked without theatrics.

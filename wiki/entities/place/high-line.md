# High Line

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> The hamlet clings to the northeast cliffs of [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]]. Rope and net lines run over the edge, where a hand-cranked winch lifts the day's work from the water below. Wind comes up the cliff face with the smell of salt and wet fibre; the narrow working ground leaves the winch, the path inland, and the drop all close at hand.

## At a Glance

[[high-line]] is a northeast cliff hamlet on [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]] where nets are worked by winch. It is distinct from the fortress-port's docks and from [[south-harbour]]'s east-side repair and fishing anchorage: High Line puts the net work above the water on a cliff. The page establishes no separate route distance or settlement authority. Skipping the hamlet avoids its winch and net-working site, but also gives up the chance to observe or use that work from the cliff.

# The Bridge

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Calveno's main crossing carries traffic over the canal in stacked tiers. The lower level is crowded beneath the arch, where fry smoke, rope, paper cones, and the canal's damp breath mix; above it, the bridge carries people toward the city's other business.

## At a Glance

[[The Bridge]] is a tiered canal-city district in [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]], including the main crossing and the trading spaces beneath it.

## If the party

- **Use the crossing.** The party can ask [[oleandro-fuschi|Oleandro]] at [[Ponte Bassa]] about a ship, buy rejected alchemical stock from [[La Gatta]], or trade gossip for access to information.

## Who

[[oleandro-fuschi|Oleandro Fuschi]] runs [[Ponte Bassa]] at the main crossing. [[nicco-kettley|Nicco Kettley]] runs [[La Gatta]] on the bottom tier. Other bridge traders and any formal district authority are not established.

## What

The Bridge is a trading area arranged across tiers. [[Ponte Bassa]] tracks ships passing through the crossing; the bottom tier holds stalls such as [[La Gatta]], where rejected alchemical batches are sold cheaply.

## Where

The Bridge is in [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]], spanning the city's canal and main crossing. [[La Gatta]] is under the arch on the bottom tier, between a fry stall and a rope seller; the district's full tier layout is not established.

## Why

The party comes to cross the canal, find a ship or its cargo in Oleandro's memory, or buy cheap alchemical stock from Nicco. The Bridge turns movement through Calveno into visible trade and gossip.

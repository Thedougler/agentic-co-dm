# The Bridge

> [!narration] The Bridge
> Calveno's main crossing carries traffic over the canal in stacked tiers. The lower level is crowded beneath the arch, where fry smoke, rope, paper cones, and the canal's damp breath mix; above it, the bridge carries people toward the city's other business. A doorway set into the stone on the crossing side opens onto a tavern with a window on the canal, and a trestle board with a sleeping cat works the arch beside it.

## At a Glance

The Bridge is a tiered canal-city district in [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]], where the main crossing over the canal carries trade, taverns, and gossip on top of each other.

- **Who is here.** [[oleandro-fuschi|Oleandro Fuschi]] at [[ponte-bassa|Ponte Bassa]], [[nicco-kettley|Nicco Kettley]] at [[la-gatta|La Gatta]] on the bottom tier, and the fry stalls and chandlers between them. No formal district authority is established.
- **Danger.** Nothing here attacks. The risk is who is watching: the crossing sees every hull that moves through Calveno, and the traders under the arch sell what they hear as readily as what they carry.
- **Draw.** Cheap goods, cheap words, and the city's best memory for ships — the tavern at the crossing is the one place that can say where a hull went.

## Features

- **The tiers.** Traffic over the canal on stacked levels, with the city's other business above and the arch's trade below. The tier layout beyond the main crossing is not established.
- **[[ponte-bassa|Ponte Bassa]].** The tavern built into the bridge at the main crossing, with a window on the canal, run by an orc who has tracked every vessel through Calveno for twenty years without writing any of it down.
- **[[la-gatta|La Gatta]].** The trestle board under the arch on the bottom tier, between a fry stall and a rope seller, selling rejected alchemical batches with a grey cat asleep on the goods.
- **The arch stalls.** Fry smoke, rope, paper cones, and the canal's damp breath: the trades that serve the traffic crossing the bridge all day.

## At the Table

- **Cross the canal.** The Bridge is the main route through Calveno, and any errand in the city crosses it. Nobody records who walks.
- **Ask about a ship.** [[oleandro-fuschi|Oleandro]] remembers every vessel that moored at the canal, and he answers over a drink rather than volunteering anything. He knows the sought person or knows who does.
- **Buy cheap stock.** [[nicco-kettley|Nicco]] sells short batches from a board under the arch, at prices no licensed room in the city can match, and quotes a different price to each customer.
- **Trade gossip.** The arch's traders price facts like goods. A party that says who it is looking for has bought attention with information.

## Secrets

**Secret.** The Bridge's real business is memory and leverage: [[oleandro-fuschi|Oleandro]]'s twenty years of ship movements, unwritten and therefore uncopyable, and [[nicco-kettley|Nicco]]'s stock of rejected batches that walks out of [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]] without its maker's agreement. The tells are a tavern with a window on the canal instead of the street, a board selling seconds at impossible prices, and keepers who count rather than write.

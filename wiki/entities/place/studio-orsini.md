# Studio Orsini

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Studio Orsini has no sign to announce it. The shop sits on a canal in Le Paludi, behind the name [[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]] keeps on her work rather than on her door. Finished alchemical goods leave the studio under her seal; rejected batches find quieter routes through the canal city.

## At a Glance

Studio Orsini is an unmarked alchemy shop on a canal in Le Paludi. [[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]] runs it as a perfectionist alchemist, while [[nicco-kettley|Nicco Kettley]] sells rejected batches from a board under the Bridge. The studio's known products include [[oil-of-still-water|Oil of Still Water]] and [[the-ninth-life|The Ninth Life]].

## If the party

- **Needs alchemy.** Ask Marta about finished goods such as Oil of Still Water or The Ninth Life; their availability and price are not established here.
- **Follows rejected batches.** Trace the supply relationship from Studio Orsini to Nicco's board under the Bridge.
- **Searches for a discreet shop.** Look for the canal location and lack of signage; no separate public entrance or safehouse route is established.

## Who

[[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]] is the proprietor and a perfectionist alchemist who speaks to her failed batches. [[nicco-kettley|Nicco Kettley]] is a downstream seller of rejected batches, not an established resident of the studio. No other occupants are established.

## What

- A working alchemy shop without a public sign.
- Finished goods carrying Studio Orsini's seal.
- Rejected batches that reach Nicco Kettley's cigarette board under the Bridge.
- [[oil-of-still-water|Oil of Still Water]] and [[the-ninth-life|The Ninth Life]] as named products sold through the studio.

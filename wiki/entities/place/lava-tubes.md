## At a Glance

The lava tubes are Aruhe's underworld. In Session 12 they matter for one ledge, where four Calveno have been trapped for nineteen days since they fell in fleeing a terror-bird.

- **Who is here.** [[luca-ferrante]], [[ettore-ferrante]], [[piero-sorrentino]], and [[gianni-moro]], on the ledge under the smoking skylight. Deeper in, [[snakewood]] in the roof roots and [[blackrail]]s ([[aruhe-caves]]).
- **Danger.** Loose rock, vertical breaks, and living roof roots. The skylight opens inside a [[terror-bird]]'s hunting ground.
- **Draw.** The four survivors, and underground routes to [[the-marshes]], [[memorial-grove]], and [[clear-lake]].

## Features

- **The smoking skylight.** In the open Gap of [[the-long-meadow]], 10 feet out from the west band of tall grass. It is 15 feet across, with sheer 40-foot walls down to a basalt ledge. On its north side, a chute of loose roots and rubble drops steeply to the ledge: easy to slide down, and it crumbles under anyone climbing up. The tube runs off east and west into the dark.
- **The skylight ledge.** Four fibre sleeping piles, a small fire, and a heap of [[ghost-plum]] and [[stonepear]] pits: the four live on fruit that falls through the skylight. [[luca-ferrante]] has scratched a tally of nineteen days into the wall with his knife.
- **Climbing out of the skylight.** With a rope and two people hauling, everyone is up in 10 minutes. Climbing without rope is **Strength (Athletics)** `DC 15`; a failure slides the climber back to the ledge for `1d6` Bludgeoning damage. A flyer can carry one person at a time.
- **The tube beyond the ledge.** It runs east and west into the dark and joins the wider network. The four have never gone far enough to know where.

- **Loose rock.** **Dexterity (Acrobatics)** `DC 10` keeps footing to the next stable shelf.
- **Vertical breaks.** **Strength (Athletics)** `DC 15` climbs to the next shelf; a failure drops the climber back and costs time.
- **Roof roots.** [[snakewood]] coils in the living roots above the basalt.
- **Daylight wells.** A charged [[stillbloom]] can answer living movement in the daylight under a skylight.
- **The seep.** **Wisdom (Survival)** `DC 15` follows the seepage water toward [[clear-lake]].
- **The vents.** **Intelligence (Nature)** `DC 15` reads the flow of the old lava, and with it the likely way between entrances.

## At the Table

- **If the party crosses the skylight.** [[piero-sorrentino]] calls up the moment a shadow crosses the hole.
- **How the four got there.** Nineteen days ago the South Bird charged them across the Gap. They ran, went over the root chute, and slid down to the ledge, and Ettore's shin broke at the bottom.
- **If anyone takes from the island underground.** Cave life is the island's. Picking, cutting, or killing is a claim under [[taking-on-aruhe]].

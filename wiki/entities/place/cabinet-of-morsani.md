# The Cabinet of Morsani

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> A lantern marks the door, the only notice the shop offers. Inside, shelves run deeper than the room seems to allow, and magic and rare things gather beneath a layer of dust while rings catch the light on the dealer's fingers.

## At a Glance

[[The Cabinet of Morsani]] is [[prospero-morsani|Prospero Morsani]]'s curio shop in [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]]'s Velo Quarter. It gathers magic and rare objects, each carrying a story of a previous owner.

## If the party

- **Offer an object or its history.** Prospero examines what the party brings and may attach one of his collection's rings to an object worthy of the unfinished set.
- **Shop for unusual gear.** The Cabinet has offered the Compass of the Drowned, trick arrows such as [[the Fishwife]], [[the Chimney]], and [[Lamplighter|the Lamp Lighter]], and [[the Snap]].

## Who

[[prospero-morsani|Prospero Morsani]] runs the shop. No other staff are established.

## What

Prospero wears thirty-one rings, each tied to an object's story. A locked case holds the thirty-second ring, attached to nothing. The shelves hold magic and rare objects whose histories Prospero can recount.

## Where

The Cabinet is in Calveno's Velo Quarter. Its full street position and neighboring businesses are not established.

## Why

The party comes to buy or sell unusual objects, learn an item's provenance, or bring Prospero the story or object that completes his collection.

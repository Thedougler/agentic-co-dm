# Ghal Idrissa

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> A rotting quarantine flag hangs limp over a stockade of grey driftwood, and the harbour beyond it holds three ships riding empty. No smoke rises from any of them, and nobody waves you off. The silence does that instead. A bell mounted at the stockade gate hangs still, its rope frayed through.

---
## At a Glance

Ghal Idrissa is a quarantine island in the [[midchain-north|Northern Midchain]], between [[central-strait|The Central Strait]], [[the-long-wager|The Long Wager]], [[sparhold|Sparhold]], and [[quillane|Quillane]]. The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] sends suspected plague ships here, and the quarantine has no formal end date. Skipping the island avoids disease risk but abandons whatever the three empty ships and the quarantine cache still contain.

The present conflict is between the Crown's indefinite quarantine rule and the island's total absence of living crews. Unless the Crown lowers the flag, the ships remain sealed and the stockade continues to decay.

---
## If the party

- **Approach the gate.** The party finds the bell still and its rope frayed through. Repairing or ringing it announces them to the empty stockade but does not summon a keeper.
- **Board a quarantine ship.** They cross to one of the three empty hulls and find no crew to question. A cautious search can reveal the ship's abandoned stores and signs that every crew left or vanished without using the gangplank.
- **Cross the stockade.** They can move through the grey driftwood palisade, but the route passes the grain store where rat swarms travel on the ebb tide.
- **Search the cache.** The party can recover the quarantine cache from the site, trading time and exposure to whatever still moves between the ships and stockade.
- **Leave by the marsh side.** They can depart toward the eastern channels at low tide, when shore crabs and rat swarms expose the one crossing that still links the ships' bilges to the grain store.

---
## Who

Nobody living is present on the island. Three plague ships sit empty in the harbour; no crew has left them by any visible route. Giant vultures circle the stockade but never land on the ships. Rat swarms move between the bilges and the grain store on the ebb tide, and stirges drop from the driftwood palisade after dark.

The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] remains the absent authority: its quarantine flag is still raised, but no keeper has returned to lower it.

---
## What

- *“A rotting quarantine flag hangs limp.”* The island is under an indefinite Crown quarantine. The flag is the only visible authority and has not been taken down.
- *“A stockade of grey driftwood.”* Salt scrub and driftwood grass form low, wind-flattened cover that never rises past the knee. The palisade is defensible but decaying.
- *“Three ships riding empty.”* The quarantine ships remain at anchor with no crews. Searching them may reveal why their crews never left, but the source establishes no further cargo or disease truth.
- *“No smoke rises from any of them.”* The ships have no active galley or watch fire. A visitor can approach without being seen by a normal crew.
- *“A bell mounted at the stockade gate hangs still.”* Its rope has frayed through, so the party cannot call a keeper by using it.
- *“Bitter samphire grows thick along the tideline.”* It is edible raw, though it grew close to the quarantine flag.
- *“Shore crabs work the hulls.”* At low tide they pull free anything soft enough to give.
- *“Giant vultures hold a slow circuit.”* They watch the stockade from first light and never land on the ships.
- *“The one crossing nobody has managed to close.”* Rat swarms move between the ships' bilges and the stockade grain store on the ebb tide.
- *“Anything that stops moving near the gate.”* Stirges nest in the driftwood palisade after dark and drop onto motionless creatures.
- **Quarantine cache.** A cache remains recoverable somewhere in the stockade or ships.

## Where

**North:** [[central-strait|The Central Strait]], open water and a main approach into the Northern Midchain.  
**East:** [[the-long-wager|The Long Wager]], across the water.  
**South:** [[sparhold|Sparhold]], another settled island in the Midchain.  
**West:** [[quillane|Quillane]], beyond the island's exposed water.

---
## Why

The party comes to investigate three abandoned plague ships, recover the quarantine cache, or learn why the crews never left. They stay to search the stockade, test the Crown's absent authority, or exploit the empty harbour before someone lowers the flag. They return if the ships contain a clue that cannot be safely moved, and they care because Ghal Idrissa offers an isolated place where the Crown's rule is visible but no one remains to enforce it.

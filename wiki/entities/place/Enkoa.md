# Enkoa

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Enkoa is an island in the southern [[Midchain]], approached across the region's short water between volcanic islands and steep shores. A hermit sorcerer keeps this island warded. Rare components may be obtained here, but the price is a rare favor rather than coin; the sorcerer, the wards, and the island's working places are not visible in the record.

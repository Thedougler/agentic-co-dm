# Sanvashti

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> A mossed terrace sits among the southern islands, with separate caste fires spaced around the stone. Nobody has a weapon out. Every group watches the other fires more closely than the worn approaches.

## At a Glance

Sanvashti is a truce terrace in the [[Midchain]], in the southern islands where rival [[grung-clans|Grung clans]] meet under an old agreement. Separate fires make every visitor visible to every faction. The approach can be skipped, but bypassing it gives up the only established place where the rival clans sit together without drawing weapons.

## If the party

- **Approach the terrace.** Become visible to every faction at once and accept the truce's scrutiny.
- **Choose a fire.** Make one clan your immediate audience while the other fires watch the choice.
- **Keep the truce.** Use the shared safety to ask questions or negotiate without making the first breach.
- **Leave by a worn approach.** Return to the southern islands without turning the terrace into a settlement or open Grung town.

## Who

- Rival [[grung-clans|Grung clans]] arrive under different colors and keep separate fires.
- Each group brings its own food and watchers.
- No single clan owns the terrace during a truce meeting; every group watches the others and any visitor.
- Sanvashti is not a settlement or open Grung town, and no permanent population is established here.

## What

- A mossed stone terrace with worn approaches.
- Separate caste fires, each with its own food and watchers.
- A standing old truce that predates everyone currently sitting at the fires.
- A weaponless meeting ground whose safety depends on nobody making the first breach.

## Where

- **North:** The southern islands of the [[Midchain]]; the exact neighboring site is not established in canon.
- **East:** Southern Midchain waters; no named route is established.
- **South:** Southern island approaches; worn paths lead away from the terrace, but their travel distances are not established.
- **West:** The remaining southern Midchain islands; no named route is established.

## Why

The party comes to speak with rival Grung clans, observe the old truce, or make a request where every faction can hear the same words. They stay because Sanvashti is the rare shared ground where questions can be asked without a weapon already drawn; they care because the first breach would change who can safely meet here.

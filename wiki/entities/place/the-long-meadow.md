## At a Glance

The Long Meadow is the one place on the Calveno trail where the forest opens, and two [[terror-bird]]s own it.

- **Who is here.** Two terror-birds. The South Bird stands at the south-west rim, and the North Bird at the north-east rim. Each holds its own half of the meadow.
- **Danger.** Anyone who steps into the open short grass draws a charge. A bird that catches a Medium or smaller body swallows it and carries it back to its rim.
- **Draw.** The fruit-pile trail runs straight through, and it is the shortest way to [[the-burnt-road]] and [[the-pantry]].

> [!narration] The Long Meadow
> The Long Meadow is a strip of open grass half a mile long and a long stone's throw wide, where the forest roof breaks and the sky shows through. Grass taller than a man walls both edges in thick bands, and between them the grass is cropped short and pressed flat in wide sour-smelling circles. Halfway along, a gap opens in the near band, and across it a deep grey channel slides past the foot of the far grass. In the middle of the gap stands a knee-high patch of white blades that glitter like broken glass. At each far end of the meadow, a mossy stump stands in the shade at the treeline.

## Features

- **The Gap.** *A gap opens in the near band.* At the meadow's midpoint, the west band of tall grass ends. Open short grass runs 100 feet east to the east band, and 60 feet north to south. The fruit-pile trail comes up the west band and must cross here. The Gap is where the two birds' halves meet.
- **The rims.** *A mossy stump at each far end.* The South Bird's rim is 60 feet south-west of the Gap's west edge, and the North Bird's rim is 60 feet north-east of its east edge. Each bird stands still at its rim until something moves in the open.
- **The tall grass.** *Grass taller than a man.* Difficult Terrain, and it hides anyone inside beyond 10 feet. A terror-bird will not charge into it.
- **The channel.** *A deep grey channel.* It runs along the Gap's east edge, 20 feet wide, deep and fast. Crossing costs 30 feet of movement for every 5 feet. Anyone crossing makes **Strength (Athletics)** `DC 12` or is swept 20 feet downstream. A terror-bird will not enter it.
- **The smoking skylight.** A hole into the [[lava-tubes]] in the open short grass, 10 feet out from the west band. Four Calveno have been trapped on its ledge for nineteen days, since the South Bird charged them and they fell in. Its smoke is the first thing anyone sees of the meadow.
- **The razer-grass stand.** *White blades that glitter like broken glass.* A [[razer-grass]] stand 15 feet across in the middle of the Gap. A terror-bird will not charge through it.
- **The feeding circles.** *Pressed flat in wide sour-smelling circles.* Where the birds digest. **Intelligence (Investigation)** or **Wisdom (Perception)** `DC 12` finds claw furrows, sour bolus, and bone scraps.
- **The stonepears.** [[stonepear]]s hang under the forest edge at the meadow's south end, and fallen ones lie in the grass there.

## At the Table

- **If the party reads the meadow before stepping out.** **Wisdom (Survival)** `DC 12` spots that the two stumps at the rims are birds, that they charge only in straight lines, and that tall grass, deep water, and razer-grass end the hunt.
- **If someone steps into the open.** The ground starts to shake, and the nearer bird charges. The [[terror-bird]] page holds its rules.
- **If the party goes around.** Back through the west band and across the channel: an hour lost, and everyone soaked.
- **If anyone picks, cuts, or kills to carry off flesh.** It is a claim under [[taking-on-aruhe]], and two [[wolfrabbit]]s come out of the tall grass.

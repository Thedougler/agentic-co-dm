# The Long Meadow

## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> A long grass cut opens under the broken Quiet roof — three hundred feet of short-cropped meadow between the jungle walls. The cut is marked by flattened circles and sour bolus in the grass. Eight-foot grass tangles along both edges. A deep river channel runs along the east side, grey-blue in shade. Midway across, a stand of white glass-edged razer-grass catches the light. At each meadow rim, mossed trunks wait in the shade — one yellow eye watching from each. The ground trembles when you touch it.

---
## At a Glance

Open cut north of [[star-cut]] on the seam of [[the-quiet]] and [[grasslands]]. Identity is the split territory: one [[terror-bird]] claims the west rim and one the east rim, so the crossing goes through both hunt-lanes. Unlike [[star-cut]]'s aisle, there is no smoke or settled rest here. Skip the meadow and lose the straightest route further in; cross it and face the dive of committed charges.

---
## If the party

- **Walk the short-grass charge lane:** Open ground, ordinary pace; west and east rims hold the two birds.
- **Move faster than a stride:** Any creature moving more than 10 feet per turn in the charge lane alerts both birds. See Pressures below.
- **Feel the tremors:** Anyone touching the ground within 30 feet of a charging [[terror-bird]] can feel it approaching. The birds cannot surprise creatures with tremorsense or that are touching the ground.
- **Climb the eight-foot grass:** **Strength (Athletics) — `DC 13`** → Reach the tangle and break through. Dense grass is Difficult Terrain; heavy obscure beyond 10 feet.
- **Wade the east channel:** Deep water, strong current. The channel is 20 feet wide; crossing costs 30 feet of movement per 5 feet of the channel.
- **Search the circle marks:** **Intelligence (Investigation) or Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 12`** → Flattened digest circles, claw furrows, torn grass, sour bolus, bone scraps, and the lane where smaller creatures no longer move.
- **Approach the razer-grass stand:** See [[razer-grass]]. Touching, entering, or violently disturbing it triggers the contact effect and shatters nearby stands if they exist.
- **Take stonepear from under the roof edge on the near side:** Fallen fruit = receiving. Plucking living fruit = [[taking-on-aruhe]].

---
## Who

- Two solitary [[terror-bird|Terror-Birds]], each claiming one half. They read as mossed trunks with yellow eyes until they move.
- The meadow holds no other living campers now.

---
## What

- Open short-grass charge lane, about 300 feet across.
- Eight-foot grass in tangles along both sides; Difficult Terrain, heavily obscures beyond 10 feet.
- **The Gap:** at the meadow's midpoint the west band of eight-foot grass ends, and 100 feet of open short grass separates it from the east band. The Gap is where the two birds' territories meet, and any trail through the meadow has to cross it.
- Deep lateral river channel on the east side; strong current; 20 feet wide.
- One white [[razer-grass]] stand midway: pale glass-edged blades, fixed glitter, old blood on lower stalks; shatters when shaken or struck.
- [[stonepear]] hanging under the roof edge on the near (south) side.

---
## Where

- **North:** Deeper [[the-quiet]].
- **East:** [[the-quiet]] wall past the channel.
- **South:** [[star-cut]] (the aisle).
- **West:** [[the-quiet]] wall past the eight-foot grass.

---
## Why

- The straightest route further inland toward [[memorial-grove]]; the only cut where the Quiet's roof breaks enough to see sky above. When any creature moves more than 10 feet per turn in the charge lane, both [[terror-bird|Terror-Birds]] wake from their rims. They do not coordinate; each selects a target in its lane and commits to one straight rush, closing fast and using Serrated Beak to clamp and Swallow on Medium or smaller grappled targets. The bird's weakness is turning: eight-foot grass, deep water, a [[razer-grass]] stand, or terrain that breaks the straight lane ends the charge and it circles back to its rim. A skirmish leaves torn moss, claw furrows, churned dust, crushed grass, sour bolus, bone scraps, and a lane through the jungle where smaller creatures no longer approach.

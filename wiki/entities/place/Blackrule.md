# Blackrule

> [!narration] Narration
> A chapterhouse is cut from black volcanic terrace-rock, a single block of it standing out of the water with no beach and no dock. One narrow stair drops from the hall to a landing that fits a single small boat, its treads worn smooth in the middle. Inside, one hall serves as chapel and barracks, and [[tyr|Tyr]]'s scales are cut into the altar stone. No smoke comes off the rock, and the only mark of use is on the stair.

## At a Glance

Blackrule is a [[dravosi-crown]] chapterhouse cut into volcanic terrace-rock in the southern [[Midchain]], kept for [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] alone rather than as a Crown station.

- **Who is here.** [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]], training and waiting; no garrison, no staff, and no watch.
- **Danger.** He acts on confirmed heresy and nothing else. A party carrying [[khlysty-the-flock|Flock]] evidence, or one he can make a case against, is the only thing that releases his violence.
- **Draw.** A Crown house off the standing map, a landing a single small boat can use, and the one place to hand Malone a case the Crown has not yet confirmed.

## Features

- **The terrace rock.** A black volcanic block standing out of the water with no beach and no dock, so the house is reached by boat or not at all.
- **The stair and the landing.** One narrow stair with a single landing below, sized for one small boat. There is no second approach and nothing to tie more than one hull to.
- **The hall.** Chapel and barracks share one room, with a sleeping bench along one wall and the altar at the end of it.
- **The altar stone.** Tyr's scales are cut into the altar. It is the only decoration in the house, and Malone's work in the hall happens in front of it.
- **The empty house.** No kitchen, no stores, and no garrison. The Crown does not staff Blackrule as a post, and the only thing the house holds is its hunter.

## At the Table

- **Come by water.** Only one small boat fits the landing. A second hull anchors off and ferries, and the approach is visible from the hall, so there is no arriving unseen.
- **Climb the stair in weather.** Wet black rock with a drop beside it is **Strength (Athletics) — `DC 12`**; a failure costs the footing and one round of noise the hall can hear.
- **Enter the hall.** Malone meets whoever comes through the door at the altar stone, and he asks for the case before he asks for names.
- **Bring a confirmed case.** He takes a charge seriously, verifies it, and then leaves Blackrule to hunt. A charge that does not hold up is refused, and the party keeps his attention for the waste of it.
- **Ask him to shelter a heretic or a Flock fugitive.** Refused. The house is not a refuge and he is not a negotiator; it is a place where judgment is kept clean.

## Secrets

**Secret.** Blackrule exists to keep Malone idle: with no garrison, no stores and no standing orders, the only thing that can put him on the water is a confirmed case, which is the Crown's leash on a hunter it does not want loose. The tells are the stair worn in the middle, the hall kept clean and empty, and a house with a chapel, a bench, and no kitchen. A party that wants him moving has to bring the confirmation rather than the plea.

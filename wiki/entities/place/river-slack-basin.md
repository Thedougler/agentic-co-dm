## At a Glance

The Slack Basin is where the party camped with [[matteo-scola]] at the end of Session 11, and where [[talon-skarn]] fell on [[crissdalynn-khinriss]] on the last watch. Session 12 opens here at dawn.

- **Who is here.** Matteo on his sleeping mat, and a [[river-otter]] family of three adults that comes and goes through the pool.
- **Danger.** Anyone in the still water at the end of a round becomes the otters' game. Taking anything out of the pool is a claim under [[taking-on-aruhe]], and the otters are its responders here.
- **Draw.** A safe camp above the waterline, and a trail of old prints that leads to the missing Calveno.

## Features

- **The camp bank.** *A flat of dirt where old fires have left black rings.* Packed dirt 10 feet above the pool, open to the sky. Matteo's mat lies here, and the party camped around his fire in Session 11. The bank's lip drops 10 feet straight into deep water.
- **The still pool.** *So clear that every rock on its floor shows.* Deep, cold, and owned by the otter family, which hunts the river below and comes back to the pool to play. A creature in the water at the end of a round draws them: they play first, with **Tug Toy** on gear and dunks that deal no damage ([[river-otter#Statblock]]). Climbing back onto the bank is **Strength (Athletics)** `DC 12`.
- **The cane in the smear.** *A thin cane stands upright in a dark red smear.* Nineteen days ago, the Calveno sailor Dario Fumagalli waded in to fish the pool. The otters played with him until he drowned. The smear is his blood where they dropped him, and the cane is the walking stick [[renzo-canale]] planted as a warning before the camp moved inland.
- **The pack and the sleeve.** *A strapped wooden pack and a torn maroon sleeve hang in the water.* Dario's. The otters keep them as toys. The pack holds a knife, a flint, a tin cup, and a waxed-cloth bundle of hardtack gone to paste. Pulling either one out is a claim, and the family hunts the taker.
- **The bloody-bank prints.** *Muddy prints climb from the smear into the hanging roots.* A dozen people climbed out here nineteen days ago. **Wisdom (Survival)** `DC 10` reads them as Calveno boots, several barefoot, heading north into the trees toward [[cutoff-lip]].
- **The eight-foot grass.** *Grass taller than a man walls the bank.* It stands 30 feet east of the fire. It is Difficult Terrain and hides anyone inside beyond 10 feet.
- **The treeline.** It stands 20 feet north of the fire, where the bloody-bank prints climb into the roots.

## At the Table

- **If the party searches the bank.** **Intelligence (Investigation)** or **Wisdom (Perception)** `DC 10` finds the cane planted deliberately in the smear, and reads the prints as the Calveno trail.
- **If the party asks Matteo.** He says the others went "inland, toward her," past a ring of stones ([[spoke-ring]]), and that he stayed because he would not follow the voice.
- **If someone fishes the pool or pulls out the pack.** They are marked until dawn, and the otter family hunts them in the water.

## Connections

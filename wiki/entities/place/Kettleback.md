# Kettleback

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> The ground breaks into a black-backed mine mouth, and heat rolls out of the old tunnels even before you reach the shade. Rusted supports disappear into red-lit dark below, while mineral steam beads on the stone and turns every handhold slick. Somewhere deeper, the geothermal vent keeps the rock warm enough for an alchemical lab, and the only clear route is down into the tunnels.

## At a Glance

Kettleback is an old mine site in the western [[Midchain]], heated by a deep geothermal vent and repurposed as an unlicensed alchemical lab. It sits within the Western Midchain's island network, with a recorded connection to [[Kalowe]]; the exact route and travel distance are not established. The site can be bypassed, but doing so gives up access to the vent-heated tunnels and whatever information or materials the lab contains.

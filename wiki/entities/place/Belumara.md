# Belumara

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Belumara is a sacred freshwater sinkhole lake in the eastern Midchain. The water lies below the rim, and bowls set around that edge mark the offering expected before a pilot draws freely. The lake's depth, surrounding ground, and the meaning of the offerings are not established; what is clear is that fresh water and a local custom meet at the rim.

---
## At a Glance

Belumara is a named eastern [[midchain-east|Eastern Midchain]] site where pilots may draw freshwater after leaving an offering in one of the lake's rim bowls. No route, travel distance, bowl inventory, custodian, or broader rite is recorded; bypassing it costs the party access to a protected freshwater source and the chance to learn how the custom governs arrival.

---
## If the party

- **Draw water.** A pilot can leave an offering in a rim bowl before drawing freely from the freshwater lake. The required offering, amount of water, and consequence of refusing the custom are not canonized.
- **Inspect the rim.** The party can examine the bowls and the sinkhole's edge for evidence of how the practice is maintained. No named keeper or fixed interpretation of the offerings is established.
- **Sail on.** The party can bypass Belumara and continue through the eastern Midchain. It leaves the freshwater and the local offering custom unused.

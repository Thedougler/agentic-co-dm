# Drovannis

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Floating platforms and rope perches bob in a sheltered lagoon. Roosting seabirds fill them.
> 
> A robed druid moves between perches, murmuring to birds that know her.
> 
Drovannis is a druidic bird sanctuary. Each year, seabirds return here. They carry small lost objects from the [[shattered-sea|Shattered Sea]].

## What

- Salt-marsh reed and wind-bent scrub: crowds the lagoon's rim and roots the driftwood pilings that hold the platforms up, so stepping off a perch onto solid ground means pushing through a reed wall first.
- Blue mussels: crust every piling below the waterline in dense clusters, easy prying for anyone willing to wade at low tide.
- Roosting terns and cormorants: pack the platforms shoulder to shoulder by midday, and a wrong step or a raised voice sends the whole colony up screaming at once.
- Shore crabs: work the mud exposed at low tide, picking through what the birds drop.
- [[reef-shark|Reef sharks]]: patrol the channel where the lagoon opens to open water, close enough to the outer platforms to take anyone who goes in after a dropped line.
- Giant octopuses: den in the shadow beneath the pilings, and a hand dipped past the waterline can vanish in the three heartbeats it takes to notice the grip.

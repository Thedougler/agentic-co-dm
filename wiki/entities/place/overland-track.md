# Overland Track

> [!narration] Overland Track
> The track runs south over the [[the-marrow|Marrow]] as a band of churned mud crossed by corduroy logs, with fog rolling down off the ridge and standing in the low ground. Toll posts stand at both ends of the crossing, and giant axe beaks pace the wagons at a distance that never quite closes. Beside the worst stretches, stone culverts lead off under the road.

## At a Glance

The Overland Track is the muddy southern road over the [[the-marrow|Marrow]] on [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall|Calder's Tooth]], and the overland way between the west-cliff port and [[south-harbour]].

- **Who is here.** Wagon crews, travellers, and Crown toll collectors at both ends; giant axe beaks pacing the wagons; giant wolf spiders sheltering in the culverts.
- **Danger.** The mud and the fog, the spiders in the drainage culverts, and the Crown's accounting: the state collects tolls at both ends and counts bodies, cargo, and coin.
- **Draw.** A known surface route between the port and the harbour, the toll posts that make passage official, and the limestone under the road that offers a way across without being counted.

## Features

- **The track itself.** A day and a half on foot or by mount in good weather. Corduroy logs bridge the worst mud, and the rest is a churned band that swallows wheels and boots.
- **The culverts.** Stone drainage channels under the road, cut to keep the crossing passable. They shelter giant wolf spiders, and they are the one place on the track with cover.
- **The toll posts.** Crown stations at each end, collecting from every wagon and recording the crossing. Nothing crosses the ridge officially without being written down.
- **The [[the-marrow|Marrow]].** The foggy north-south ridge whose porous, cave-riddled interior the road is trying to cross quickly. Leaving the track for the ridge is a different journey with a different set of holes in it.
- **[[high-line]]** lies north of the track, and the ground between them belongs to the ridge.

## At the Table

- **Cross on foot in good weather.** A day and a half to South Harbour. A party that leaves the track for the ridge saves distance and gives up the only surface that holds a wagon.
- **Take a culvert for cover.** The spiders are the price. **Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 13`** to spot the webs before stepping in; a party that does not look meets the occupant instead.
- **Pay the tolls at both ends.** The Crown counts the crossing, and a party that skirts a post is a party the posts remember at the other end.
- **Take the dry route under the road.** [[the-passage]] runs a hidden way through limestone caves beneath the track, which crosses without a toll and without a record.
- **Move with wagons.** Axe beaks pace them the whole way. They do not close on a moving wagon, and they do on a stopped one.
- **Cross in bad weather.** The mud and the fog run the crossing long, and the culverts stop being optional cover.

## Secrets

**Secret.** The Overland Track has a dry road under it: [[the-passage]] keeps a hidden route through the limestone caves beneath the southern crossing, and the culverts are the nearest thing on the surface to its doors. The tells are culverts that drain better than the ground around them should allow, and a track whose tolls are always worth avoiding. The find is the cave mouth the Passage uses.

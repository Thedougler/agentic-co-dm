# Calveno Sewer Magazines

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Beneath Calveno, the sewer route opens into rooms built to hold powder. Barrels and scaffolding occupy the underground chambers, and the passage continues past spaces already damaged by fire and water. Farther in, a ritual is still being performed in Room 8, with Grung warriors holding the approach.

---
## At a Glance

The Calveno Sewer Magazines are an underground powder-storage complex connected to the city's sewers. The party destroyed Magazine Beta, previously passed through Room 6, and reached Room 8 during Solange Barret's live ritual. The route back to the broader sewer network is established; the dungeon's cardinal neighbors and a safe bypass are not.

---
## If the party

- **Searches the magazines.** Track which powder rooms are destroyed, surrendered, or still occupied. Room 5 and Magazine Beta are lost in the Session 05 evidence.
- **Pushes toward Room 8.** The party reaches a live ritual under [[solange-barret|Solange Barret]] with three remaining Grung Elite Warriors between her and the party.
- **Returns after the raid.** Preserve the destroyed Magazine Beta and Bazzoth's lost still; the place does not reset to an intact powder store.

---
## Who

Session 05 places [[bazzoth-the-steeped|Bazzoth]] in Room 5, where he died with two Grung laborers and destroyed the room's barrels. [[ruma-delacroix|Ruma Delacroix]] surrendered Room 6. [[solange-barret|Solange Barret]] was chanting in Room 8, guarded by four Grung Elite Warriors, one already broken against the wall. Other planned room assignments are recorded on their owner pages but are not established as played occupants here.

---
## What

- Powder barrels and black-powder scaffolding used by the Grung bombing operation.
- Room 5 and Magazine Beta, both destroyed during the Session 05 raid.
- Room 6, surrendered by Ruma Delacroix.
- Room 8, containing a live ritual under Solange Barret.
- The remaining room layout and the ritual's purpose are not established.

## Why

The magazines are the local supply infrastructure behind the Grung threat to Calveno's festival crowds. They also hold the only recorded live ritual connected to the operation. The party can change the city's danger by destroying powder stores, taking prisoners, interrupting the ritual, or learning what network supplied the rooms.

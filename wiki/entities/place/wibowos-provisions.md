# Wibowo's Provisions

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> An efficient provisions counter turns crew numbers and days at sea into an exact order. The shelves hold ship's cakes, vent-rock salts, and the supplies Suryo Wibowo uses to keep captains from discovering a shortage after departure.

## At a Glance

[[Wibowo's Provisions]] is [[suryo-wibowo|Suryo Wibowo]]'s victualling shop on [[Kalowe]]'s first island.

## If the party

- **Provision the voyage.** The party can give Suryo a crew count and days-at-sea figure; he reads the order quickly and says precisely where it comes up short.

## Who

[[suryo-wibowo|Suryo Wibowo]] is the proprietor and victualler. The current record names no other staff.

## What

The shop sells ordinary provisions and [[long-passage-biscuit|Long-Passage Biscuits]], baked in the back room, plus [[wakerock-salts|Wakerock Salts]]. Suryo extends credit to captains he has dealt with three times or more, using remembered handshake terms instead of ledgers.

## Where

Wibowo's Provisions is on [[Kalowe]]'s first island. The current record does not establish its neighboring businesses or exact route.

## Why

The party comes here to provision a vessel, buy supplies that protect a crew during a long crossing, or ask Suryo to identify a shortage before departure.

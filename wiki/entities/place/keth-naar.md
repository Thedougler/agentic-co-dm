# Keth Naar

> [!narration] Narration
> Keth Naar is a low island of black stone and salt grass on the rim of the Blue Hole, with old waterlines cut along the rock above the present shore. A cave mouth in the island's western face watches the channel, and a Tabaxi elder sits at it with his hands on his knees. The fishing is thin, the racks under the village are half empty, and Perrin came ashore here alone after the water boiled under the Vestra.

## At a Glance

Keth Naar is a [[Tabaxi]] island on the [[sunken-crown]] rim, where a tribe is running down its rations while the blessing that puts fish in the water stays gone.

- **Who is here.** The Keth-Naar tribe of Tabaxi, led by memory-keeper [[elder-anton|Elder Anton]]; hunters are split over whether to push past the Blue Hole into open water or stay and tighten the ration line.
- **Danger.** The blessing has fled since the Pearl was taken from [[vel-orn]], the catch has fallen with it, and the tribe will run out of food before the season turns unless something changes.
- **Draw.** The island is the only place that holds a survivor's account of the [[Vestra]]'s sinking, and the elder trades news for shelter, food, and answers he has never managed to send for.

## Features

- **The shore.** Black stone, salt grass, and old waterlines cut above the present tide, which mark where the sea used to stand and how far the island has gone down since.
- **The cave mouth.** On the western face over the channel, where [[elder-anton|Anton]] sits at first light and takes a stranger's measure in one pass. The tribe's oral record and its seasonal marks are kept inside.
- **The ration racks.** The smoking racks under the village hold what the catch has left, and the line is short: two meals a day, then one, and the elders eat last.
- **The hunters' split.** The tribe's hunters have divided over the open water past the Blue Hole. One party argues to hunt beyond the rim while the other argues that [[keth-naar|Keth Naar]] cannot spare a hunter at all.
- **The Blue Hole rim.** The unsounded water inside the ring, where the sounding line runs out. [[sawek|Sawek]] hunts below it and the tribe will not cross it for fish.

## At the Table

- **Arrive from outside.** Anton asks, without turning, "You came from outside," and then works outward: how long since [[midchain|the Midchain]], what the water smelled like at the [[drowned-maw|Maw]]. A party with news of the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl]] helps him immediately, because it is the first answer that has ever come to him unsent.
- **Ask after the blessing.** The tribe's catechism is that the sea gives fish where the blessing is kept. Anton will say what the water used to smell like here and what it smells like now, which is the closest the island has to a map of what broke.
- **Hunt with the tribe.** A party that hunts commits a day and a boat; a good catch buys food for the racks and standing with the hunters who stayed. A failed hunt costs the tribe the day the party spent.
- **Push past the Blue Hole.** The hunters' faction will guide a party out through the rim and back, but nobody from Keth Naar believes the answer lies past the hole. The sounding line runs out before the bottom does.
- **Trade for the record.** Anton will open the cave's seasonal marks to a party that brings him a fact about the sea he does not already hold. He does not sell the record for coin.

## Secrets

**Secret.** The tribe's blessing crisis is a repeat of one Anton smelled sixty years ago: the sea failed, the catch left, and it closed again on its own after two seasons, but not before the water took twelve boats. He has told nobody on the island that he remembers it, because a tribe that knew the timeline would go past the Blue Hole to force the answer. He is waiting out the second failure one week at a time, and the tell is the ration line drawn short — two meals, then one, elders last — while a stranger with news gets fed.

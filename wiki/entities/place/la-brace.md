# La Brace

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Curtains divide the smoking room into booths, and visible sheets of smoke move through the dim lantern light. Rufio crosses the floor with a lit taper, while scorched leaf and alchemical compounds thicken the air.

## At a Glance

[[La Brace]] is [[calven-and-calveno]]'s [[Velo Quarter]] smoking room, run by [[rufio-segalla|Rufio]] and licensed as the city's only open-burn room for alchemical smoke.

## If the party

- **Book or buy.** The party can buy a shelf blend, book a curtained booth, or ask [[rufio-segalla|Rufio]] to finish a blend to order.

## Who

[[rufio-segalla|Rufio]] is the proprietor and licence-holder. Customers occupy the curtained booths; the current record names no other staff.

## What

The room has curtained booths, dim lanterns, shelf blends, and a back room where Rufio finishes compounds from [[Studio Orsini]]. His pocketbook records customer faces rather than a written client list.

## Where

La Brace is in Calveno's [[Velo Quarter]], on the side of the Velo bridges where city authority stops. It is the only room in Calveno where alchemical smoke burns openly.

## Why

The party comes for alchemical cigarettes, privacy, or Rufio's knowledge of customers. The [[tessarine-concordat]] is pressing him for a booking list, while he seeks a licence to open a second room in the [[Mercatura]].

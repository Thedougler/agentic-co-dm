# La Brace

> [!narration] La Brace
> Curtains divide the smoking room into booths, and visible sheets of smoke move through the dim lantern light between them. Lanterns hang at four heights and not one of them is bright. Rufio crosses the floor with a lit taper already in his hand, stepping over outstretched legs without looking down, and writes in a pocketbook on his way back.

## At a Glance

La Brace is [[calven-and-calveno]]'s [[velo-quarter|Velo Quarter]] smoking room, run by [[rufio-segalla|Rufio]] under the city's only open-burn licence.

- **Who is here.** [[rufio-segalla|Rufio]], moving between the booths with a lit taper, and whoever has paid for a curtained booth; no other staff is named.
- **Danger.** The [[tessarine-concordat]] has asked twice for the booth bookings, and the absence of that list is the entire business. A customer who names another customer in here damages the only product the room sells.
- **Draw.** Alchemical smoke that burns in the open, booths curtained for privacy, blends finished to order by a man who writes faces in his head instead of on paper.

## Features

- **The booths.** Curtains on rails divide the room into private seats, priced by privacy rather than by the compound, which is the house's whole accounting.
- **The lanterns.** Light at four heights, none of it bright, which is what lets the smoke read as sheets in the air and keeps faces unreadable.
- **The shelf blends.** Blends a customer buys in front of witnesses, bought as base compound from [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]] and finished by Rufio in the back room.
- **The pocketbook.** Nineteen years of faces, indexed by memory and recognition rather than by any written list, written in while the customer draws and watched on purpose.

## At the Table

- **Book a booth.** Seating comes first; the price depends on the booth and never on what is smoked. Cash, quiet, and no names written down.
- **Ask for a blend finished to order.** Rufio rolls the ones he does not shelve for customers whose faces he already knows, which takes an hour on a couch and a return visit.
- **Ask for the booking list.** He answers the question with a longer version of it, offers to let the asker search the back room, and never says no. **Charisma (Persuasion) — `DC 17`** gets a standing answer out of him only by giving him something to gain.
- **Name another customer inside.** The room settles, Rufio stops writing, and the conversation is over. Discretion is not a kindness here; it is the price of the seat.
- **Bring Concordat business.** He has refused them twice and will do it again, and he is trying to win a licence that reaches past the Velo bridges into the [[mercatura|Mercatura]], which is the one lever that moves him.

## Secrets

**Secret.** Rufio keeps no ledger, and the pocketbook is the reason he can say so honestly: it records faces in a hand only he reads, and the Concordat's request for a list is unanswerable without it. A party that wants the booking names has to take the book rather than ask for it, or learn what he wants badly enough to trade for — a licence reaching the Mercatura. The tells are the pocketbook written in the open and the two refusals the Concordat cannot call lies.

# Rin Dola

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Rin Dola is a western Midchain site where a reclusive cartographer sells charts accurate enough to unsettle anyone who asks how she drew them. She insists she has never visited the places her charts describe, leaving the paper's precision and the cartographer's denial in direct tension. The charts are the visible reason to come; the source of their accuracy is not established.

---
## At a Glance

Rin Dola is a named western [[midchain-west|Western Midchain]] site associated with a reclusive cartographer and her uncannily accurate charts. No route, travel distance, chart inventory, or explanation for the cartographer's knowledge is recorded; bypassing it costs the party access to unusually reliable navigation information and the chance to question its source.

## If the party

- **Buy a chart.** The party can purchase a chart of a place the cartographer insists she has never visited. The price, stock, and accuracy boundaries are not canonized.
- **Question the cartographer.** The party can press the contradiction between her denial and the charts' accuracy. Her name, methods, and answer are not established.
- **Sail on.** The party can bypass Rin Dola and use the western Midchain's generally reliable charts and local pilots instead. It loses the opportunity to acquire or investigate the unusual charts.

# Spar & Snare Outfitters

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Spar & Snare Outfitters is not one storefront but a working chain of expedition routes, inventories, and animal handling. Roka Sten coordinates the supply side from the Midchain, while the creatures move toward Sabine Corvo's Live Hold for inspection and sale.

## At a Glance

Spar & Snare Outfitters is [[Roka Sten]]'s supplier network for live exotic creatures. It feeds [[The Live Hold]], where [[Sabine Corvo]] handles and sells rare stock to collectors, adventurers, mages, and wealthy houses.

## If the party

- **Follow the supplier trail.** Start with [[Roka Sten]] and his expedition records in [[sparhold]].
- **Inspect the market end.** [[Sabine Corvo]] can identify what stock arrived, what the market values, and which expeditions have missed their returns.
- **Search the records.** [[Roka's Hidden Ledger]] records actual specimen collections, payments, and boundary-bribery evidence.
- **Interrupt the supply chain.** Missing expeditions and contracting supply create pressure, but the source does not establish a single required response.

## Who

- [[Roka Sten]] coordinates the expedition and supplier side of the network.
- [[Sabine Corvo]] is the receiving dealer and proprietor of [[The Live Hold]].
- Three expedition teams have missed scheduled returns this season; their identities and current locations are not established here.

## What

- Live exotic creatures collected on expeditions into the [[Verdant Teeth]] fringe.
- Public inventories and private records, including [[Roka's Hidden Ledger]].
- A supply chain whose value depends on healthy, rare stock reaching Sabine's dealership.

## Where

- **North:** [[sparhold]]; Roka Sten is the Midchain contact named for the network's expedition side.
- **East:** The [[Verdant Teeth]] fringe; expeditions collect the network's rare stock there.
- **South:** No separate canonized neighbor is recorded.
- **West:** [[The Live Hold]]; Sabine Corvo receives and sells the creatures.
- The network's exact routes and any permanent outfitter premises are not established.

## Why

The party cares because the network joins rare-creature commerce to missing expeditions, trafficking evidence, and the health of Sabine's dealership. It offers access to an expedition contact, a pressured buyer, and a ledger that can expose what the public inventory omits.

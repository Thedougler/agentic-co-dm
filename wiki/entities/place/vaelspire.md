# Vaelspire

Vaelspire's summit answers one true question truthfully, once, to whoever reaches it. Every account of who's tried agrees on one thing: they came back changed, and none of them will say what they asked.

> [!narration] Narration
> A spire of black basalt rises straight out of the water, taller than any mast, humming so low you feel it in your teeth before you hear it.
> 
> Sheer stone meets the water. Whoever's climbing goes hand over hand from a boat.

## Where

**Extent.** Vaelspire's footprint barely spans a few hundred feet at the waterline and narrows further as it rises — there's no interior to speak of beyond the climb itself.

**Topography.** The spire rises straight out of open water in a single column, with no beach and no shelf to land on; sheer stone meets the water on every side, and every route up goes hand over hand from a boat. Ledges and crevices pocket the upper reaches, packed here and there with thin soil, and old carvings near the waterline mark where the climb begins.

## Routes & Access

No anchorage rings the base, so a boat holds position at the waterline while climbers go up free-hand — there's no fixed route, no rope line left by anyone before, and no shortcut past the sheer stone. Run the crossing and the climb itself per `vault/refs/vault/location/references/wilderness-travel-and-exploration.md` and `vault/refs/gameplay-toolbox.md` § Travel Pace.

## Hazards

[[giant-spider|Giant spiders]] web the narrow crevices near the waterline, invisible until a groping hand closes on silk instead of stone, and [[stirge|stirges]] roost in the dark folds below the carvings, dropping onto anyone who lingers too long at the climb's midpoint.

## Ecology

Black lichen crusts every ledge and crack above the spray line, tough enough to grip for a handhold when the wet basalt isn't, and salt-crusted samphire roots in the thin soil packed into crevices partway up, edible raw and worth picking for a party that climbed without provisions. [[goat|Wild goats]] pick along the upper ledges where no boat could follow, sending scree rattling down onto anyone climbing below, while [[giant-vulture|giant vultures]] nest in the higher hollows and start circling the moment something on the rock face stops moving.

# Murrat

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Murrat is a mile-and-a-half limestone reef that looks empty from the water until canopy closes twenty feet inland. Cliff hollows and rope bridges hold kin-villages above the tide. Limestone, wet rope, and leaf mould mark longer eastern reef than a first glance admits.

---
## At a Glance

Murrat is a [[moucheron|Moucheron]]-held limestone reef in the Northern Midchain. Its cliff hollows and rope bridges hold kin-villages above the tide, while the island's blood currency and longer eastern reef make a landing more consequential than the empty shoreline suggests. The island feels hunted before the first rope bridge.

---
## If the party

- **Observe from a distance.** Spyglasses show tiny winged shapes circling in the millions without provoking the island.
- **Close further.** The buzzing becomes physical through the hull, shapes detach from the cloud, and the greeting party flies toward the ship.
- **Turn away.** The cloud stays over Murrat, but the island does not pursue a ship that leaves.
- **Land.** The canopy's cliff villages spot the landing before the keel reaches sand, and scouts answer the intrusion.
- **Find the eastern reef.** The party can chart its true length before the tide pulls a boat onto either of the two existing wrecks.

---
## Who

Dozens of [[moucheron|Moucheron]] kin-villages occupy the cliff hollows and rope bridges above the tide. Their scouts watch the canopy and spot a landing before a keel reaches sand. Outsiders are hunted unless they land with a reason that survives local blood custom.

---
## What

- *“A mile-and-a-half limestone reef that looks empty from the water.”* The island's shoreline conceals the settlement until the canopy closes twenty feet inland.
- *“Cliff hollows and rope bridges.”* The kin-villages live above the tide in connected hollows and bridges.
- *“Limestone, wet rope, and leaf mould.”* These smells and textures mark the longer eastern reef beyond a first glance.
- *“A dark mass hangs over the island without moving with the weather.”* The mass is a cloud of millions of Moucheron.
- *“The buzzing becomes audible from open water.”* The cloud announces itself before individual wings resolve.
- *“A reef ring surrounds the island.”* No marked safe passage leads through it.
- *“Blood currency.”* Local custom determines whether a landing has a reason the kin-villages will accept.
- *“The tide pulls east.”* The eastern reef stretches farther than the first approach suggests, and two existing wrecks threaten a boat that misses its length.

---
## Where

**North:** Open water around the reef ring.  
**East:** The longer eastern reef and the two existing wrecks.  
**South:** [[verdant-teeth|The Verdant Teeth]].  
**West:** [[sparhold-isle|Sparhold Isle]] and the wider [[Midchain]].

---
## Why

The party comes to land only with a reason that survives Murrat's blood custom, chart the eastern reef, or understand the Moucheron kin-villages. They stay to negotiate passage through the settlement or investigate the two wrecks. They care because Murrat controls a dangerous reef approach and responds to outsiders before those outsiders can reach the shore.

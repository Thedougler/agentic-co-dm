# Ossuary Cay

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Ossuary Cay is a western Midchain cay where a death-cult gathers bones from the waterline. The tide delivers whatever reaches the shore, and the cult reads those arrivals as part of the cay's knowledge of the sea. Bones, surf, and the act of sorting what washes ashore define the visible work here; the cult's names, rites, and exact interpretation are not established.

## At a Glance

Ossuary Cay is a named western [[midchain-west|Western Midchain]] site tended by a death-cult that collects bones and reads the tides through what washes ashore. No route, travel distance, named cult member, or detailed rite is recorded; bypassing it costs the party the chance to learn what the shoreline's latest arrivals mean to the people who tend it.

## If the party

- **Read the tide.** The party can examine what has washed ashore and compare the arrival with the cult's interpretation. The page does not establish a fixed omen system or guarantee what the next tide brings.
- **Speak with the cult.** The party can ask how the cay's keepers sort bones and read arrivals. No named spokesperson, doctrine, or price for their knowledge is canonized.
- **Leave the shore.** The party can bypass Ossuary Cay and continue through the western Midchain. It leaves the current tide's evidence and the cult's reading unexamined.

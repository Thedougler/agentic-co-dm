# Calder's Verge

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Heat ripples off a black-glass slope, and a furnace glows orange-white at its base, fed by the vent itself. A glassblower turns a molten gather with practiced, unhurried hands, humming through the work. She eyes your hands. *Glassblower*: *(flat)* Apprentice? We lose most of them in the first month. You'll want gloves.

Calder's Verge produces glasswork prized across the [[Midchain|Midchain]], made possible by a natural silica vent nobody else has managed to safely work. The guild's training methods are as closely guarded as their techniques.

## At a Glance

Calder's Verge is a volcanic-vent glassblowing guild in the [[midchain-north|Northern Midchain]], between [[barrow-ait|Barrow Ait]], [[kwoss|Kwoss]], [[chronehall|Chronehall]], and [[adwe|Adwe]]. The Calder's Verge guild controls the furnace, the apprenticeship, and the prized glasswork. The western route can be skipped if the party wants only the guild's furnace and training.

## If the party

- **Arrives.** Climb the black-glass slope to the furnace and meet a glassblower who evaluates the party's hands before discussing apprenticeship.
- **Apprentices.** Gather vent-flux before touching a blowpipe; the guild's training kills most outsiders in the first month.
- **Gathers flux.** Scrape the grey mineral crust from warm fissures and bring it to the guild, where it cuts the melt's working temperature.
- **Crosses the lava tubes.** Avoid the giant scorpions denning in the interior or accept that the guild's safe routes do not cover the whole island.

## Who

The Calder's Verge guild's glassblowers work the furnace and guard their training methods. One glassblower handles a molten gather at the furnace and tests prospective apprentices by their hands. Giant lizards lie against the black rock through the afternoon heat, giant crabs pick the tide line below the furnace, and giant scorpions den in the interior lava tubes.

## What

- **“A black-glass slope.”** Cooled obsidian flows form the volcanic ground. The find is a hot, difficult approach to the furnace.
- **“A furnace glows orange-white at its base.”** The natural silica vent feeds the guild's glassmaking furnace. The find is the source of Calder's Verge's prized glasswork.
- **“Black-rock scrub.”** Wiry heather and ash-lichen root into the obsidian, with thin pine beyond the ridge. The find is the only green on the slope.
- **“Vent-flux” as a grey mineral crust.** It blooms along warm fissures and cuts the melt's working temperature. The find is the apprentice's first required task.
- **“Giant lizards” against the black rock.** They lie flat through the afternoon heat. The find is a living sign that the slope remains hot.
- **“Giant crabs” at the tide line.** They pick anything the runoff has cooked below the furnace. The find is scavenger traffic along the shore.
- **“Giant scorpions” in lava tubes.** They den in the tubes threading the interior. The find is a threat beneath the guild's working ground.

## Why

A party comes for prized glasswork, to learn the guild's guarded techniques, to test an apprentice's path, or to gather vent-flux. The party stays while the furnace, the guild's guarded training, or the volcanic hazards offer something worth the risk.

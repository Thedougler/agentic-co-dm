# High Eyrie

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> A basalt stack rises beyond the eastern island chain, high over discoloured water. Wind shears the rock face, and narrow terraces cling to the summit above the approaches below.

---
## At a Glance

High Eyrie is a basalt sea-stack beyond the eastern [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]]. The [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie]] have held it for two hundred years and watch the [[drowned-maw]] from its exposed terraces. The [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] edge can be skipped, but doing so loses the safest established route to the Sentinels and their records.

---
## If the party

- **Seeks the ledgers.** Ask the Sentinels for access to the continuous Maw observations; they control the records and do not interpret or intervene.
- **Approaches from the sea.** Climb or otherwise reach the exposed terraces; stairs, spray, invitation, harnesses, magic, or a watched climb are established access conditions.
- **Asks about the Crown.** Learn that the Crown made three offers and the Sentinels refused all of them; the terms of those offers are not established.
- **Investigates the summit.** Find that founding documents and the seal remain tied to Sentinel material; the documents' full meaning is not established here.

---
## Who

The [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie]] are here: Aarakocra monks, apprentices, record-keepers, and [[master-kyzil]]. The exact number is not established. Apprentices train on cantilevered terraces, while record-keepers maintain the Maw observation ledgers.

---
## What

- **Basalt stack and exposed terraces.** The physical site requires careful movement above open water.
- **Maw observation ledgers.** Continuous records of time, weather, position, water, and occurrence at [[drowned-maw]] since 1295 DR.
- **Founding documents and seal.** Guarded material beneath or tied to High Eyrie; the source does not establish their complete meaning.
- **Monastic training.** The Sentinels train exact movement in peak wind and can hold the watch when others cannot.

---
## Where

- **North:** [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]], beyond the eastern chain; travel time is not recorded.
- **East:** [[drowned-maw]], the watched water below and beyond the stack; travel time is not recorded.
- **South:** Canon gap; no southern neighbor is established.
- **West:** [[Stenmark]] and the Crown chain; travel time is not recorded.

The Sentinels' physical and social access controls are part of every approach. The breakaway [[Countless]] and the established Sentinel order are connected through the existing faction material.

---
## Why

The party comes to High Eyrie to reach the only continuous account of the [[drowned-maw]], seek the guarded founding documents, or negotiate with a faction that refuses to interpret what it observes. The place matters because its ledgers can expose changes in the Maw while the Sentinels' non-intervention doctrine prevents them from acting on those observations. Returning after a disruption should preserve the watch's durable history and show whether ledger continuity or access has changed.

# Kat's Curios

> [!narration] Kat's Curios
> Kat's Curios is a shop of arcane goods and unusual work in [[calven-and-calveno]]. Messages for [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Davirelli]] pass through the counter as often as purchases do.

## At a Glance

Kat's Curios is [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Davirelli]]'s shop and a source of arcane artillery for [[the-brass-cat|The Brass Cat]]. [[samuel|Samuel]] works here as an intern carrying messages for Catarina and the crew.

- **Who is here.** Samuel handles messages; Catarina Davirelli is the shop's owner and the Brass Cat's captain.
- **What is here.** Arcane goods and the source of The Brass Cat's arcane artillery.
- **What the party can gain.** Arcane supplies, a route to Catarina, and messages that connect the shop to the Brass Cat's crew.

## Features

- **Arcane-goods counter.** The shop supplies arcane goods and the arcane artillery fitted to The Brass Cat.
- **Message desk.** Samuel arrives every morning at eight with messages from [[iacopo-fieschi|Iacopo Fieschi]] and runs them to Catarina and the crew.

## At the Table

- **Ask for Catarina.** The party can use the shop as a contact point for Catarina Davirelli and the Brass Cat.
- **Hand over a message.** Samuel asks who should receive a message and follows instructions rather than guessing at the job's scope.

## Connections

- [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Davirelli]] — proprietor and captain of The Brass Cat.
- [[the-brass-cat|The Brass Cat]] — Davirelli's vessel and the shop's arcane-artillery client.
- [[samuel|Samuel]] — intern and message runner.
- [[iacopo-fieschi|Iacopo Fieschi]] — sends Samuel to run messages for Catarina.
- [[calven-and-calveno]] — settlement where the shop operates.

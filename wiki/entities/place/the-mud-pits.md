# The Mud Pits

## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> Mud churns under the fighters' feet in a bounded ring. Wagers pass through the crowd while the pit boss watches without cheering. When she says "done," the bout ends.

---
## At a Glance
The Mud Pits are Kalowe's fighting venue on the third island. [[bala-tane|Bala Tane]] controls entry, makes the matchups, manages the betting runners, and decides when a fight is over. The pit is connected to [[the-kessen|The Kessen]] through Vort Kessen's retirement there, but it is not part of that league.

---
## If the party

- **Watches a bout.** Bala's announcements identify the fighters and her ruling establishes when the fight ends. The party can observe the match, the wagers, and who accepts Bala's authority.
- **Seeks a match.** Bala controls entry and makes the matchups. The party must deal with her rather than bypassing the pit's authority.
- **Places a bet.** Bala manages the betting runners, making the pit a place to wager as well as fight.
- **Challenges the ruling.** Bala's decisions are final in the established canon and have not been seriously contested. Any challenge changes the party's relationship with her and the harbour bosses who treat her as a peer.

---
## Who

- [[bala-tane|Bala Tane]] is the named operator. She is an orc woman in her mid-forties and has run the fights for fifteen years.
- Bala's betting runners and the current fighters are part of the pit's operation, but their identities and numbers are not established. Do not invent a roster until play or another source names one.

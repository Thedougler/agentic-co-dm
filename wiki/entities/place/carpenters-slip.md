# Carpenters' Slip

> [!narration] Carpenters' Slip
> Carpenters' Slip is a fast-turnaround shipyard in [[calven-and-calveno]], where timber becomes hull repairs, fittings, and working changes to a vessel's shape.

## At a Glance

Carpenters' Slip is [[savia-brentino|Savia Brentino]]'s structural shipyard in [[calven-and-calveno]]. It takes work that the official dry dock handles too slowly or too secretly.

- **Who is here.** Savia Brentino, a dwarf shipwright who reads timber grain and runs the yard.
- **What happens here.** The yard performs hull checks, keel checks, caulking, plank, mast, rudder, and emergency repairs.
- **What the party can gain.** Fast structural work, ship fittings, and an off-book service that changes a hull's silhouette.

## Features

- **Fast-turnaround yard.** *"The grain tells her something about the yard that laid the timber."* Savia reads keel wood to assess timber grain and age, then handles structural work the official dry dock is too slow or secretive to take.
- **Hull-reprofiling service.** *"Paint does not hide what a ship is."* Savia can change a hull's silhouette rather than merely repainting it, in a service kept separate from [[Acqua Nera]]'s cosmetic work.
- **Straight-grain offcuts.** The yard supplies shaft timber used in [[bosuns-reach|Bosun's Reach]], [[the-chimney|The Chimney]], and [[the-fishwife|The Fishwife]].

## At the Table

- **Commission work.** The party can walk in, pay Savia's deposit, and start a repair. Emergency repairs cost double; Savia refuses cosmetic work.
- **Read the timber.** Savia can identify the source and approximate age of a vessel's timber from a keel chip, giving the party a structural answer rather than a cosmetic one.

## Connections

- [[savia-brentino|Savia Brentino]] — proprietor and shipwright.
- [[calven-and-calveno]] — the settlement where the yard operates.
- [[bosuns-reach|Bosun's Reach]] — uses straight-grain shaft offcuts from the yard.
- [[the-chimney|The Chimney]] — uses shafts turned from the yard's offcuts.
- [[the-fishwife|The Fishwife]] — uses shafts cut from Savia's offcut pile.
- [[Acqua Nera]] — cosmetic repainting contrasted with Savia's structural hull work.

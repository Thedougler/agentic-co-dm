# Meth-Var

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Meth-Var is a low island of black stone and salt grass on the rim of the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]]. Old waterlines mark the shore, while rotating watchers face the open waters of the [[drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]]. The island is a watch post first: a place to read the horizon, hold the rim, and decide what approaching water means.

---
## At a Glance

Meth-Var is the Sunken Crown's watch island on the open Maw-facing rim. It sits among the low islands around the region's Blue Hole and serves the watch rotations that face open water. The source establishes the island's role and its regional neighbors, but not a named landing, a staffed count, or a route that can be safely skipped.

## If the party

- **Approaches the island.** Treat the rotating watch as the first point of contact; the watchers face open Maw waters and can notice movement before the party reaches shore.
- **Studies the shore.** Read the black stone, salt grass, and old waterlines for evidence of the island's exposed position and changing water.
- **Leaves the island behind.** The party loses the watch's view over the open Maw waters. No alternate observation point is established.

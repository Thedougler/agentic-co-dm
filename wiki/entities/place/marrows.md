# Marrow's

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Marrow's is a low alcove cut into the hidden settlement beneath Calveno. Bundles and papers sit where the passage narrows, close enough to touch but arranged so a stranger cannot tell which are meant to move. The air carries damp earth, canal water, and the quiet murmur of the Warren. A visitor can wait, present a Kin introduction, or leave before the broker decides to speak.

---
## At a Glance

Marrow's is an alcove and place of business in the Warren, the Rattkin settlement beneath Calveno's Le Paludi. [[marrow]] brokers cargo and transports documents that must avoid notice. The alcove is reached through the Warren's learned routes; there is no recorded public entrance.

---
## If the party

* **Asks for Marrow.** The party must reach the alcove through the Warren and ordinarily needs a Kin introduction; Marrow may still refuse without explanation.
* **Presents a document or cargo.** Marrow can assess or broker material that needs to stay invisible, but the source does not establish what he accepts.
* **Searches the alcove.** The party can find the crate Marrow currently holds for the dead courier Dren, including the Vretek/Brol letter, silver, and signet ring.
* **Leaves.** The party can retreat through a known Warren route, but the route and any trust spent reaching the alcove remain part of the situation.

---
## Who

[[marrow]] works here as a Rattkin cargo broker and document transporter. He has operated from the alcove for at least fifteen years. The source does not establish a fixed staff or other permanent occupants.

---
## What

* Marrow's alcove is a compact business space inside the Warren's low passages.
* Invisible cargo and transport documents change hands here.
* Marrow currently holds the crate courier Dren carried when [[serin-haut]] killed him; it contains the Vretek/Brol letter, silver, and a signet ring.
* A Kin introduction is the practical access token for reaching the broker.

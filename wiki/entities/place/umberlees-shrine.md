# Umberlee's Shrine

> [!narration] Umberlee's Shrine
> Small islands ring a dark blue hole, and an older house occupies the black cliff stone of one of them, kelp hanging from its pillars and candles burning despite the wind. Footsteps go soft on the floor. The surge at the cliff decides when the known approach is open, and the tidal pool at the back is empty.

## At a Glance

Umberlee's Shrine is [[umberlee|Umberlee]]'s older house off [[vel-orn|Vel Orn]] in the [[sunken-crown]], built for the drowned rather than for quay traffic.

- **Who is here.** No resident priest or attendant is established; the shrine belongs to Umberlee's worship, which the [[waveservants]] administer across the region.
- **Danger.** The approach is the first problem: a sheer, reef-bound cliff landing reachable only when the timed surge and the known route coincide. [[delmar-fisk]] is connected to the Pearl's absence, not established as an occupant.
- **Draw.** The ledger chamber of drowned names and water-debt, the offerings that record deaths, and the empty tidal pool where the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] was kept.

## Features

- **The cliff landing.** Sheer stone and reef, with the surge deciding when the known approach is open. There is no other route, and a party that misses the window waits for the water.
- **The house.** Black cliff stone, kelp on the pillars, candles that stay lit in the wind, and a floor that softens every footstep.
- **The ledger chamber.** The shrine's record of drowned names and water-debt, kept here rather than at a working quay.
- **The offerings.** Tokens, coins, fish bones, carved shells, and pieces of ship and sailor, standing as witnesses to the deaths the shrine keeps.
- **The tidal pool.** The deepest chamber's pool, built to hold the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]], now empty. Its absence marks the theft and the fleet's destruction.

## At the Table

- **Reach the landing.** The party can approach when the surge allows the known route. The cliff landing is the whole obstacle: no check on this page substitutes for the tide window.
- **Read the shrine's record.** The ledger chamber names the drowned and sets out water-debt. This is the region's account of who the sea has taken and what is owed for them.
- **Examine the offerings.** Everything left at the shrine records a death: tokens, coins, bones, shells, and pieces of the ships and sailors themselves.
- **Search the deepest chamber.** The tidal pool where the Pearl was kept is empty, and the absence is the evidence of the theft.
- **Ask who serves here.** Nobody is resident, and the worship is administered from elsewhere, so the shrine answers through its record and its water rather than through a priest.
- **Take the Pearl's place.** Nothing in the pool replaces what was taken; the disruption here is the reason the shrine is on anyone's route at all.

## Secrets

**Secret.** The shrine's loss is the region's: the Pearl of Souls was taken out of the tidal pool, and the shrines and rim islands that depended on the blessing it anchored have been failing since. The tells are an empty pool in a shrine built around it and a ledger of drowned names that has kept growing, and the find is reading the water-debt against the [[keth-naar|Keth Naar]] account of the same failure.

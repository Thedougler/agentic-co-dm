# Umberlee's Shrine

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Small islands ring a dark blue hole. An older house occupies black cliff stone, with kelp on its pillars; the surge decides when the known approach is open.

## At a Glance

[[Umberlee's Shrine]] is Umberlee's older house on [[vel-orn]] in the [[sunken-crown]]. It is a hidden shrine for the drowned rather than a working-quay collection point, and the Pearl of Souls' absence marks the disruption here.

## If the party

- **Reach the landing.** The party can approach when the surge allows the known route; the cliff landing is sheer, reef-bound, and dangerous.
- **Read the shrine's record.** The ledger chamber keeps drowned names and water-debt, while the empty tidal pool shows where the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] was kept.

## Who

No resident priest or attendant is established. The shrine belongs to [[Umberlee]]'s worship, which the [[waveservants]] administer across the region.

## What

Kelp wraps the pillars, candles burn despite the wind, and footsteps go soft. A ledger chamber records drowned names and water-debt. Tokens, coins, fish bones, carved shells, and pieces of ship and sailor stand as witnesses.

## Where

The shrine stands on [[vel-orn]] in the [[sunken-crown]], inside black cliff stone above a tidal pool. It is hidden from ordinary charts and reachable only when a timed surge and a known approach coincide.

## Why

The party comes to understand the local disruption, investigate the Pearl's theft by [[delmar-fisk]], or learn what Umberlee's shrine records say about the drowned.

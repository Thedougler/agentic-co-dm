# Sarn's Landing
> [!narration] Narration
> A small fishing village sits on a Crown island shore, with boats drawn up and nets on the drying racks.

A small Crown Islands fishing village where [[aleksander-malone]] was once loosed in public. The results were ugly enough to change Crown policy. Malone now requires formal heresy confirmation before he sails. He still considers the violence righteous judgment.

A draft fiction titled A Job Well Done retells that release. It marks itself as not canon. The draft names [[corah-vance]] as the inspecting ensign and [[halvard-oss]] as the last villager named in the telling. Live fact stays the public release and the later confirmation rule. Do not treat the draft's body count or dialogue as established.

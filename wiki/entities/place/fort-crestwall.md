# Fort Crestwall

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Fort Crestwall crowns [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]] above the western Strait. Guns look out from the height over the cliff tiers, trade shelf, and docks below, while the fort's stone keeps the lower rooms out of sight. Ships must show papers at the Inspection Pier beneath it; above, garrison movement follows orders the Governor has not seen.

## At a Glance

Fort Crestwall is the Dravosi Crown fortress on the crown of [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]]. Sealed vaults beneath the fort hold [[aldric-drave|Aldric Drave]]'s living bloodline chart, while two garrison detachments hold the fort under orders [[governor-voss|Governor Voss]] never saw. The fort is not a skippable edge of Port Tidefall: bypassing it leaves both the chart and the sealed Admiralty operation protected.

## If the party

- **Approach from Port Tidefall.** The party can use the cliff tiers and the fort's established surface connection, but the current sources do not establish a visitor procedure or safe route into the fort.
- **Investigate the garrison.** The party can compare the two detachments' presence with Governor Voss's lack of access to the orders. The exact orders, commanders, and current deployment are not established.
- **Search below.** The party can seek the sealed vaults and Aldric's living chart. The vault approach, defenses, entrances, and bypasses are not established.
- **Ignore the fort.** The garrison can continue holding Crestwall while Admiralty-sealed companies stage reef-diving gear and underwater charges for the [[drowned-maw]].

## Who

Two garrison detachments hold Fort Crestwall under sealed orders that Governor Voss has not seen. The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] controls the fort's public authority, and [[aldric-drave|Aldric Drave]]'s living chart is sealed in the vaults beneath it. The sources do not name the detachment commanders or establish other current occupants.

## What

- **Fortress crown.** Fort Crestwall sits on the highest crown of [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall]] with guns overlooking the western Strait.
- **Sealed vaults.** The vaults beneath the fort hold Aldric Drave's living bloodline chart; their rooms, entrances, and defenses are not established.
- **Two detachments.** Two garrison groups hold the fort under orders outside Governor Voss's view.
- **Admiralty staging.** Extra companies have arrived with reef-diving gear and underwater charges marked for the [[drowned-maw]]. This is active staging pressure, not a resolved operation.

## Why

The party may care about Fort Crestwall because it joins three pressures in one defensible height: Crown authority over Port Tidefall, sealed military staging toward the [[drowned-maw]], and Aldric Drave's living bloodline chart beneath the guns. Access could expose the orders, the chart, or the split between Governor Voss and the forces holding the fort, but no single route or predetermined outcome is established.

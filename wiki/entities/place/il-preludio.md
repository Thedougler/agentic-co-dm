# Il Preludio

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Brass bells hang across the doorway while an elf on a stepladder talks through a demonstration. The room smells of hot brass and orange peel; shelves hold enchanted objects whose impressive names promise more than their usefulness.

## At a Glance

[[Il Preludio]] is a magic shop in Calveno's [[Velo Quarter]], owned by [[fioravante-selmerin|Fioravante "Fio" Selmerin]] and [[serena-cottle|Serena Cottle]].

## If the party

- **Browse or buy.** The party can browse the shelves, ask Fio to demonstrate an enchantment, or ask Serena for a price and the shop's return terms.

## Who

[[fioravante-selmerin|Fioravante "Fio" Selmerin]] is the impresario, [[serena-cottle|Serena Cottle]] keeps the books and sells the stock, [[tam-slim-nail|Tam Slim Nail]] keeps inventory, and [[cecco-trivani|Cecco Trivani]] is the apprentice.

## What

The shelves hold real enchantments that do exactly what their impressive names promise, but nothing anyone needs. The front window has remained half-dressed for nine days because Fio keeps restarting its arrangement.

## Where

Il Preludio is in Calveno's [[Velo Quarter]]. The current record does not establish its adjoining route or neighboring shop.

## Why

The party comes to browse unusual magic, meet the shop's four-person staff, or follow [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]]'s request to browse a magic shop. Fio wants Il Preludio's name in every mouth in Calveno.

# Il Preludio

> [!narration] Il Preludio
> Brass bells hang in ranks across the doorway while an elf on a stepladder talks through a demonstration with one hand raised to his shoulder. The room smells of hot brass and orange peel, and the shelves hold enchanted objects whose impressive names promise more than their usefulness. The front window is half dressed, with one glass bell still waiting for its place.

## At a Glance

Il Preludio is a magic shop in Calveno's [[velo-quarter|Velo Quarter]], where every enchantment on the shelf does exactly what its name says and nothing anyone needs.

- **Who is here.** [[fioravante-selmerin|Fioravante "Fio" Selmerin]] on the stepladder demonstrating, [[serena-cottle|Serena Cottle]] at the books, [[tam-slim-nail|Tam Slim Nail]] keeping inventory, and [[cecco-trivani|Cecco Trivani]] learning the trade slower than Fio would like.
- **Danger.** Nothing in the shop attacks anyone. The cost is time and coin: Fio builds a full musical setup for every item and talks straight through the place where the song would go, and the stock is useless for whatever the party actually came for.
- **Draw.** Real enchantments, honest prices and return terms from Serena, and an object in a browser's hands before they have decided anything.

## Features

- **The doorway.** Ranks of brass bells hung across the entrance, which ring for everyone who comes in and are the first thing Fio introduces.
- **The shelves.** Enchanted objects, all genuine, each named far above its use. One item in hand is worth more to Fio than a whole customer at the counter.
- **The front window.** Half dressed for nine days. Fio restarts the arrangement every time somebody stops to look at it, which is why it is never finished.
- **The four stations.** Fio demonstrates, [[serena-cottle|Serena]] keeps the books and sells, [[tam-slim-nail|Tam]] keeps the stock straight, and [[cecco-trivani|Cecco]] learns the trade.

## At the Table

- **Browse.** Fio puts something in a hand before the browser finishes deciding, and then waits for the answer to a question he has not asked. The item is real and does what the name says.
- **See through a name.** Working out what an item actually does under its presentation is **Intelligence (Arcana) — `DC 13`**; a success means the party knows what they are buying and can ask for the shelf behind the counter instead.
- **Ask for price and terms.** Serena gives a price, the return terms, and no performance. She sells; Fio presents.
- **Ask where Fio came from.** He came over [[galewall|the Galewall]] and will not say from more precisely than that. Ask once and he answers about the shop's roof; ask twice and he answers about the roof again.
- **Hurry him.** Impossible. A party on a clock can leave with what Serena hands them, or stay and get the full presentation of something they did not want.

## Secrets

**Secret.** Fio's stock is honestly acquired and honestly useless: every enchantment is real, and none of it does anything the party needs, which is the shop's whole predicament. The one thing he will confirm about himself is that he crossed the Galewall and stayed, and the tell is that asking twice about his past gets the shop's roof twice. A party that wants something specific should ask Serena and name it; a party that browses gets Fio.

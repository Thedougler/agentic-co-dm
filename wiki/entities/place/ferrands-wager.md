# Ferrand's Wager

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Ferrand's Wager is a floating casino moving through the western Midchain. The house knows exactly how much you can afford to lose, so every wager begins with the unsettling sense that someone else has already measured its limit. The deck, gaming floor, and route beyond the hull are not otherwise established; the visible invitation is simple: step aboard, name a stake, and learn what the house is willing to take.

## At a Glance

Ferrand's Wager is a named floating casino in the western [[midchain-west|Western Midchain]], connected in the canon to [[Kalowe]]. No fixed route, travel distance, proprietor, or method for the house's knowledge is recorded; bypassing it costs the party the chance to test that knowledge against a wager.

## If the party

- **Board the casino.** The party can enter the house's wager economy and decide what it is willing to risk. The page establishes the house's uncanny measure of affordability, not the games, entry price, or odds.
- **Challenge the house.** The party can place a wager and press on the fact that the house already knows its limit. The outcome and the house's response remain open for play.
- **Sail on.** The party can bypass Ferrand's Wager and continue through the western Midchain. It loses the chance to learn why the house knows what a crew can afford to lose.

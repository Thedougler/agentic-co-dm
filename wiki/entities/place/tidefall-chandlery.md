# Tidefall Chandlery

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> The chandlery counter is arranged for inspection papers as much as rope and naval stores. Nigel Strutt keeps the posted prices close at hand, checks each form in order, and looks more distressed by a missing line than by a difficult customer.

## At a Glance

[[Tidefall Chandlery]] is Port Tidefall's Crown-sanctioned naval outfitter and the first provisioning stop for a vessel after inspection.

## If the party

- **Provision after inspection.** The party can present a vessel for naval stores, ask Nigel to correct paperwork, or learn which Crown rule controls a purchase.

## Who

[[nigel-strutt|Nigel Strutt]] is the chandlery clerk and first point of contact. The [[dravosi-crown]] employs him; no other staff are established.

## What

The chandlery supplies vessels under its Crown charter. Nigel knows prices, stock, and the charter word for word, never deviates from posted prices, and helps customers complete the right form when their paperwork fails.

## Where

Tidefall Chandlery is in [[calders-tooth-and-port-tidefall|Port Tidefall]], among the settlement's listed harbour rooms. It serves vessels after they stop at the Inspection Pier and show papers.

## Why

The party comes to provision a ship legally, resolve a paperwork problem, or understand what the Crown requires before allowing a vessel to trade or depart. The chandlery makes naval supply part of Port Tidefall's inspection regime.

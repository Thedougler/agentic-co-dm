# The Shelf Instrument Dealer

## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> The Shelf Instrument Dealer is a navigation shop in Port Tidefall. Charts, almanacs, chronometers, navigation instruments, and several spell scrolls are kept or sold here. Oswald Crench, the dwarf proprietor, gives brief answers; name the tool or purpose you need, and he can tell you what the tool is and where it came from. You can ask, inspect, or purchase the navigational material that fits your voyage.

## At a Glance
The Shelf Instrument Dealer is Port Tidefall's navigation shop, owned and run by [[entities/npc/oswald-crench|Oswald Crench]]. Its established role is specialist access to charts, almanacs, chronometers, navigation instruments, and several spell scrolls, together with Oswald's knowledge of what each tool is and where it came from. The party can skip the shop entirely, but doing so gives up that provenance knowledge, Oswald's long experience calibrating Crown navigation equipment, and access to whatever known stock is available. No canon establishes the specific streets or named sites the shop sits between.

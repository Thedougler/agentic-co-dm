# Tallow Row

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Tallow Row hides a card den in Calveno. A two-eye window cuts the door, and the ogre behind it decides whether the visitor belongs inside. Past the threshold, cards move across felt while regulars watch the deck, the room, and the harbor traffic beyond it.

## At a Glance

Tallow Row is a hidden card den in [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]]. [[grukan|Grukan]] works the door, checking arrivals through a two-eye window and a Draconic password. [[thassos|Thassos]] plays most nights, while regulars such as [[watches-the-deck|Watches-the-Deck]] and [[iolo-bracca|Iolo Bracca]] make the den a source of card and harbor information.

## If the party

- **Wants entry.** Deal with Grukan's door check and password before treating the den as public.
- **Wants information.** Play or watch the felt; Iolo talks about hulls moving past the mole after dark, while other regulars watch the deck instead of the room.
- **Wants leverage.** Challenge Thassos's card reading or the den's information flow; the page establishes no guaranteed winning strategy.

## Who

[[grukan|Grukan]] vets every arrival. [[thassos|Thassos]] is a regular who reads a card shoe after losing his passage-sense to drink. [[watches-the-deck|Watches-the-Deck]] watches the deck rather than the room. [[iolo-bracca|Iolo Bracca]] is a Crown harbour stamp-clerk who plays the felt and talks about night traffic. No proprietor is established.

## What

- A hidden card den in Calveno.
- A two-eye entry window and Draconic password.
- A felt for card play and a card shoe Thassos reads.
- Harbor traffic information carried by Iolo Bracca.

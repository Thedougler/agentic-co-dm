# Ashkevet

## Overview

> [!narration] Narration
> Ashkevet is an abandoned Dravosi Crown prison island in the western [[Midchain]]. The island is officially empty, but a yard bell still rings at dawn.

## At a Glance

[[Ashkevet]] is a Dravosi Crown prison island abandoned a generation ago and officially empty. It sits near [[kalowe]], [[Penance Bar]], and [[sabrique]] in the western [[Midchain]]. The current canon does not establish what a party loses by bypassing it.

## If the party

- **Investigate the yard bell.** The bell rings at dawn even though Ashkevet is officially empty; its cause and the consequences of reaching the yard are not established in canon.
- **Trace the prison records.** Ashkevet is an older named Dravosi prison, but the [[Lavinia Sordi]] source does not identify it as the prison where she kept records.

## Who

Ashkevet is officially empty. No current occupants, staff, prisoners, or bell-ringer are established in canon. [[Lavinia Sordi]] is not tied to this prison by the source that names her workplace.

## What

Ashkevet is an abandoned Dravosi Crown prison island. The yard bell rings at dawn despite the site's official emptiness. The prison's layout, remaining objects, and reason for the bell are not established in canon.

# Prospero Morsani

![[prospero-morsani-banner.webp]]

|               |                                                                 |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Species**   | [[Gnome]]                                                            |
| **Pronouns**  | he/him                                                           |
| **Location**  | [[Cabinet of Morsani]], [[Calveno]]  |
| **Role**      | Curio dealer                                                     |

**Wants:** to complete a collection by placing each object in the right hands, then own the final piece that makes the set whole.

**Quote:** "Every object on these shelves delighted someone before they couldn't keep it."

> [!narration] Narration A theatrical gnome with fingers encumbered by thirty-one rings, each bright and distinct and weighted with story. His face reads characters the way merchants read ledgers, examining each new guest before he speaks, his mouth already forming their description as their final words fade into dust and brass polish settling thick in the air, with something older resting in those depths, something that smells of sealed boxes left long undisturbed. His shelves extend far deeper than the storefront suggests, with a locked case waiting at the back, bearing an empty ring-shaped depression on its lid.

**Roleplay Concept:** theatrical antiquarian who reads faces like provenance marks and keeps each object's ring.

**Opening move:** examining a salt lantern, turning it over in the light · Prospero speaks first · "You're new to this work. Tell me: who carried this when they were happy?"

**Lore Sheet:** runs the Cabinet from [[the Velo Quarter]], where magic and rare things accumulate like dust. Each object carries a ring on his fingers, and that ring holds the story of its previous owner, its failure to stay put, and the reason it arrived here. A locked case in the back holds the thirty-second ring, attached to nothing yet. His hours move with his mood. The lantern at his door is the only notice anyone gets.

**Voice & Delivery:** formal, patient, unhurried. "Every ring holds what someone loved about it and why they couldn't keep it." / "The lantern is ninety years old. Three owners, or four, have held it. The middle one left no name behind, which matters most." Never pressures a sale. Simply opens a case or picks up an object and begins. Watches what a customer says they need versus what they truly seek.

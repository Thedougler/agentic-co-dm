# Verdant Teeth

The Verdant Teeth are five country-scale rainforest islands held by the [[Grung clans]] as one closed system of reef gaps, canopy routes, standing pools, and flooded cuts. [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]] lies near [[Karath]], but the source does not establish a normal route between the regions.

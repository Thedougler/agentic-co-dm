# Work - Malone regeneration

Addressed to the DM. The live [[Aleksander Malone]] page keeps a CR 14 fight sheet with Regeneration 20. A later idea note asks for a cleric, fighter, and rogue stack with no invented supernatural resilience.

## Grounding

[[Aleksander Malone]] already publishes Regeneration, Heretic's Bane, and Withdraw by Judgment. The idea note is the newer file and contradicts that sheet on resilience only.

## Proposal

Keep the live sheet until you accept a change. If you want the idea note instead, strip Regeneration and any other invented resilience and leave Channel Divinity, Action Surge, and Sneak Attack as the stack. Do not overwrite the sheet in the meantime.

## Decision

`lifecycle` stays `proposed` until you accept, edit, or reject.

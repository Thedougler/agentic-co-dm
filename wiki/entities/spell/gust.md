# Gust

> [!narration] Narration
> The caster's breath leaves in a single hard burst. Air rushes outward in a straight corridor, lifting loose grit and snapping cloth against the body. Fog tears open in the wind, and anything caught in its path has to brace or slide backward.

2nd-level Evocation

**Casting Time.** Action  
**Range.** Self (60-foot line that is 10 feet wide)  
**Components.** Verbal, Somatic, Material (a dried legume seed)  
**Duration.** Concentration, up to 1 minute

The spell creates a powerful wind in the line. When the wind appears, each creature in it makes a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, a creature is pushed 15 feet away from you; on a success, it holds its ground. The wind disperses fog and can redirect a toxin cloud or similar airborne hazard when the effect allows wind to move it. Until the spell ends, you can use a Bonus Action on later turns to change the wind's direction.
## Rulings

- **Redirecting a toxin cloud.** The wind can push the cloud along the spell's line when the hazard's description says wind can move it. Creatures are affected only by the cloud's new position, not by the gust itself unless they are in the spell's line.
- **Clearing fog.** The spell disperses ordinary fog and similar airborne concealment in its line. It does not end a magical fog effect unless that effect says wind disperses it.
- **Holding a creature against the wind.** A creature that succeeds on the Strength saving throw holds its ground, but it does not block the wind or protect creatures behind it.
- **Counterplay.** A target can use cover outside the line, leave the area, or ready movement for after the caster changes the direction. Breaking the caster's Concentration ends the wind immediately.
- **In enemy hands.** A [[tidehex-captain]] uses Gust to clear its own fog cloud, expose a target carrying a prize, or push a boarding target away from the rail before ordering the crew forward.

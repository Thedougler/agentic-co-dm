# Gust

> [!narration] Narration
> The caster's breath leaves in a single hard burst. Air rushes outward in a straight corridor, lifting loose grit and snapping cloth against the body. Fog tears open in the wind, and anything caught in its path has to brace or slide backward.

2nd-level Evocation

**Casting Time.** Action  
**Range.** Self (60-foot line that is 10 feet wide)  
**Components.** Verbal, Somatic, Material (a dried legume seed)  
**Duration.** Concentration, up to 1 minute

The spell creates a powerful wind in the line. When the wind appears, each creature in it makes a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, a creature is pushed 15 feet away from you; on a success, it holds its ground. The wind disperses fog and can redirect a toxin cloud or similar airborne hazard when the effect allows wind to move it. Until the spell ends, you can use a Bonus Action on later turns to change the wind's direction.

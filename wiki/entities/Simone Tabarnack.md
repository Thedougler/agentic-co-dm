# Simone Tabarnack

![[simone-tabarnack-banner.webp]]
![[simone-tabarnack-portrait-alt.webp]]

**Wants:** to finish becoming true Gold before her unsealed rite slips and gives her away, but [[Ozzeth, the Twiceborn|Ozzeth]]'s death left it unmaintained and Karath's toxin monopoly still out of reach.

![[simone-tabarnack-narration-appearance]]


She is [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]]'s younger sister, trained in the clan's ways and an unwavering believer in the caste order, a garrison officer through and through. When Jean-Claude fled Sorn, she stayed and told their parents about [[Pell]]. He returned to the hut expecting good news but found her there with Pell in chains, having revealed everything. A month of torture followed before Jean-Claude escaped, knowing why she had done it. She believed in the clan and still does. "The caste order is not a choice," she still says. "It shapes what we are."

Jean-Claude questioned the system, but she never did. She moved through it without doubt, seeing her report not as betrayal but as the right choice. What haunts him is her certainty itself.

That certainty was never as simple as devotion. Ozzeth and Simone, both power-hungry, independently found the same suppressed rite, built by Gold caste to reorder someone else's color, never its own holder. She cast it on herself anyway. Turned inward, the rite never finishes sealing; whatever it left running under her skin, she has kept controlled and hidden for years, and no one in the garrison has named it.

Ozzeth found the rite first and went further with it, his mentor's hand keeping hers stable and quieting an instability that should have surfaced years earlier. She looked up to him for it, more than she'd have admitted to anyone in the garrison. Garrison work never touched that maintenance, so nobody there ever knew it existed. His name unsettles her. Still, a flicker of something raw slips under the discipline, gone before anyone could name it.

Gold could not brand her the way it branded Ozzeth. A record this dutiful, this honorable, earning this much real loyalty from the ranks, made "abomination" impossible to sell, so she became a folk hero among the Grung instead, and Gold settled for begrudging, incomplete acceptance: tolerated, never legitimized, always "not pure Gold." That is a slight and a literal fact at once: she has the rite, but never the one resource gold-tier color actually costs, the rare toxin Gold caste grows only at its own hidden farms (see [[Grung clans|Grung Clans]]). She still says the caste order is not a choice. She has spent every day since choosing hers, and defending the idea that she couldn't have.

She has never seen a caste seal work and does not know what its command authority actually runs on. She still believes diet and the stolen rite are the whole mechanism behind Gold's power, the same story Gold tells everyone below it (see Grung Clans for what actually powers Gold caste). What the [[Dravosi Crown]] trades her for the toxin she supplies is a substitute for the toxin Gold grows only at its hidden Karath farms, close enough to fake the diet-shift herself without ever reaching the real thing. The substitute is why her transformation stalled at partial. Purple-and-black skin breaks into dots and bands of gold, plainly visible, never hidden.

She earns that loyalty on one specific, checkable thing: she does not spend her people. She will not order a garrison onto a march she does not expect it to walk back from, and she absorbs a loss and carries it instead of sending purples to die proving a point. Every soldier at [[Sorn]] knows it about her. It also leaves a gap: an ambitious soldier who wants her notice can run an errand she has visibly declined to order, unasked. She learns of it only when the report comes back, a man gone over the side in the dark.

She supplies the Dravosi Crown with Grung poison, a craft Jean-Claude learned from her. In Session 01, a crew boarded [[The Saltwright|the Saltwright]], where a [[Dravosi Alchemist]] threw gas Jean-Claude knew was hers. He said nothing mid-fight, sensing they could not yet see the link. A month later, he found twenty vials in the [[Uncertainty|HCS Surety]]'s cargo and confirmed the poison reached a privateer. Still he said nothing. In Session 04, Grung vials surfaced from the [[Calveno]] sewer raid. The supply trail (canister → cargo → sewer stock) nearly emerged, but his secret held.

Jean-Claude fled before any of this began. The rite, the Crown trade, the partial gold under her armor: all three happened in his absence, and he has no idea any of it exists. The sister he pictures is the one who turned him and Pell in, exactly as she was the day he ran. His silence about the toxin has only ever been self-protection. He does not know there is anything larger underneath it to protect.

The [[Calveno]] raid itself was her own project, not an assignment: a demonstration aimed at the public that already calls her folk hero, proof she could deliver a raid at a scale none of Gold's own raiders have matched. Roughly a thousand intended, 487 actually taken and shipped, 290 landed at Karath, the rest lost to the storm that wrecked part of the fleet on [[Aruhe]]. A win loud enough to matter, costly enough to leave a mark. Ozzeth dies guarding that raid's primary chamber, and he can cast [[Sending]] freely enough to try reaching her the moment the fight turns against him. What arrives isn't words: a cut-off half-effort, the first attempt of his that's ever failed to land. She knows something is wrong hours before anyone confirms what. Her biggest public win and the loss of the one person keeping her secret stable happen in the same stretch of hours.

Gold caste dismissed the raid. Common Grung did not. The story that spread called it daring and clever, proof she could out-plan Gold's own raiders, and that praise reached her clean, for the first time in her life. She has started noticing something new in her own body since: faster, sharper, half a step ahead of what her training alone explains. She has no name for it yet, and reads it, even to herself, as nothing more than raw talent: a spear arm getting sharper with practice, never anything worth calling magic.

Without him, nothing maintains what the rite left running under her skin. She has to finish becoming Gold now, meaning she needs the one thing her folk-hero standing has never bought her: the toxin Gold caste grows only at its hidden Karath farms. Grief she has no time for turns into urgency instead, and she starts taking risks against Karath's monopoly she wouldn't have a season ago.

She hunts him not for family but as a loose end, knowing he lives beyond the Verdant Teeth, and blue-caste scouts now push into [[Midchain]] waters looking for him. [[Karath]] leads six [[Vethka]] hulls on raids that feed the slave trade, though soldiers rebel and captives disappear faster than replacements. Her channel with [[Barnaby Rook]] burned in Session 02. Meanwhile, her agents in [[Tidefall]] asked wrong questions that [[Verity Hollowell]] will learn about soon. Time works against her: if she finds Jean-Claude, scouts will pursue; if her trade fails, Tidefall networks will connect Grung toxin, Rook's death, and Crown business together.

His crew is who killed Ozzeth, inside her own raid, and some part of her will never separate that fact from his name again. The loose-end framing stays the story she tells the garrison. Underneath it now, it's rage. She may want more than his death for it: pull him home, help her take what Gold hoards, rule the Grung together instead of hunted apart. Whatever he answers, she will never hand him the actual secret behind the color-shift. She wants Gold's throne, not to prove nobody deserves one.

Once she understands the real mechanism (see Grung Clans), her plan changes shape. Recruiting her brother becomes the actual play: come home, rule the Grung together, take what Gold has been hoarding from them all. If he refuses, she will not kill him. He stops being a loose end the moment she needs him as a piece, and a piece she needs does not get left hunted-but-free. She captures him instead. She works this through scouts and garrison networks, acting only through official authority, her folk-hero standing serving as leverage against Gold's toxin monopoly, never open defiance.

> [!mechanic]
> **Simone's Ossketh — self-cast, unmaintained since Ozzeth's death.** The rite runs unstably and required Ozzeth's external arcane maintenance to prevent collapse. With Ozzeth dead, it is now unmaintained and slowly destabilizing — she will not know until it fails visibly. [[Detect Magic]] near Simone (always): "Arcane transmutation — like a spell caught mid-execution, still running." Arcana check DC 14: an incomplete color-altering ritual in progress, requiring external maintenance it is not receiving. [[Counterspell]] cannot target it — background process, not a discrete cast. She has no divine aura; she does not have the certainty-tap.

## Goals & Fronts

### Front: Finish Becoming Gold

**Lifecycle:** active
**Aim:** finish her transformation to true Gold before the unsealed rite she can no longer stabilize gives her away.
**Approach:** channels information through scouts and garrison networks and acts only through official authority. Her folk-hero standing works as leverage against Gold's toxin monopoly.
**Off-screen move if unopposed:** blue-caste scouts push further into [[Midchain]] waters hunting [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack|Jean-Claude]], and she takes greater risks against [[Karath]]'s toxin monopoly than she would have a season ago.
**Trigger conditions:**

- [[Ozzeth, the Twiceborn|Ozzeth]]'s death, already fired, started this clock running by leaving the rite unmaintained.
- her agents in [[Tidefall]] asking wrong questions [[Verity Hollowell]] is close to noticing.
- time itself, advancing both the scout search for Jean-Claude and the friction with Karath's monopoly.
**Clock:** 6 segments (slow burn), filled: 2 (Ozzeth's death and the burned [[Barnaby Rook]] channel).
**Consequence at fill:** the unsealed rite finally fails, exposing her partial-gold transformation to the garrison she has led for years.
**Possible outcomes (2-3):** (a) she reaches Jean-Claude and recruits him, gaining a partner against Gold; (b) the Tidefall networks connect Grung toxin, Rook's death, and Crown business before she reaches him, exposing the trade and her instability together; (c) the rite fails before either resolves, forcing her hand in the open.
**PC connection:** Jean-Claude Tabarnack is the loose end her scouts are closing on; catching him risks exposing the poison trade he's kept silent about, and her real offer (come home, rule the Grung together) lands the moment he's caught.

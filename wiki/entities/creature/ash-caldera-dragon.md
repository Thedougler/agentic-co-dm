# Ash Caldera Dragon

> [!narration] Narration
> Upper slopes are burned-out jungle gone to black glass and ash-drift. The crater glows faintly at night, visible to a ship that passes close enough. Heat shimmer and a distant roar arrive before the dragon does.

**At a Glance.**

A dead volcanic island in the [[midchain-east|Eastern Midchain]] holds a young red dragon. Midchain pilots and salvagers circulate a treasure rumor: something glints on the crater floor on a clear night. Salvage crews have gone in. None have come back to confirm what they saw.

This is the same eastern dead-volcano threat as [[ashglass|Ashglass]]. The archive note replaces an old Iron Choir stub outright rather than preserving its cold-forge flavor.

## Behavior


- **Habitat.** Ash slopes, heat vents, and a glowing crater lair on the eastern Midchain island.
- **Behavior.** Territorial and aggressive; not interested in talking. Environmental tells build before contact: heat shimmer, a distant roar, ash falling upward once.
- **Threat.** Built as a CR 10 solo that is costly for a level 5–10 party; meant to be scouted and walked away from early, then returned to later, or attempted carefully with retreat and hit-and-run.

**Encounter shape (source).**

Session 1 covers approach and escalation: poor visibility, unstable footing, unmapped heat vents, and evidence of prior treasure-hunters. Session 2 is the caldera fight using unstable rock, vents, and ash clouds. The hoard is gold and gems half-melted into slag, with room for one standout magic item.

## At a Glance

A young bloodhawk is the danger that follows the big one: it drops on whatever the adult's strike leaves exposed, and it circles smoke and noise looking for a flush.

- **Habitat.** The open sky over [[aruhe]], most often above water bowls, reef gaps, river cuts, canopy breaks, and sunlit grass rims. It will not chase under closed canopy or into grass taller than a person.
- **Treasure.** None.

## Tactics

- **Opening.** It mirrors an adult's stoop, then uses Flush Dive on anything still in the air or just driven into the open.
- **Signature.** Flush Dive. Its tell is a small wing shadow dropping out of the adult's line.
- **Adapts.** It uses Multiattack only when its target has no cover, and Flyby to leave reach and try again.
- **Weaknesses.** Get under the dive line, break its line of sight with canopy or tall grass, or force it to land; on the ground it is slow and open.
- **Morale.** It leaves when the adult leaves, when its prey reaches cover, or when it is Bloodied.

## Behavior

- **Habits.** It holds station behind an adult [[bloodhawk]] until it can claim a sky lane of its own. Alone, it circles smoke, noise, and open water.
- **Diet.** Flushed fish, birds, and small land animals. It follows the adult rather than fight over a big kill.
- **Group.** Several may chase one flush, each peeling off toward a different exposed body, without working together.
- **Signs.** Extra wing shadows behind a larger bird, small crimson feathers in the canopy, and narrow talon cuts beside an adult's strike.
- **Aftermath.** Small crimson feathers, paired gouges in stone or bark, and a short fall scar in bruised grass.

## Tactics

- **Opening.** The Whip Shark coils its tail and uses Tail Snare on a sailor near the ship's edge.
- **Signature.** Tail Snare is telegraphed by the raised, coiling tail. A target can move behind full cover, succeed on the Dexterity save, or escape the grapple before the pull reaches the rail.
- **Adapts.** It bites a grappled target when the target is in the water, then turns its tail on the next exposed sailor.
- **Weaknesses.** It cannot use Tail Snare through full cover, and its tail cannot grapple a creature while the tail is restrained.
- **Morale.** It retreats into deep water at half its Hit Points unless it can reach the eggs or a bleeding target.

## Behavior

- **Habits.** It circles ships before surfacing and tests their hulls with its tail.
- **Diet.** It eats sailors, large fish, and anything thrown bleeding into the sea.
- **Group.** It hunts alone and meets another Whip Shark only to mate.
- **Body.** Its tail is longer than a ship's boat and strong enough to score planks. The tail is the visible source of its snare.
- **Signs.** Deep parallel scores on the hull, a sudden absence of deckhands near the rail, and a long wake circling the ship warn that it is hunting.
- **Aftermath.** A successful hunt leaves a torn rail, wet drag marks, and blood spreading down the ship's side.

# The Marshes

![[aruhe-the-marshes.jpg|The Marshes, flooded rainforest beneath a dense canopy]]

## Look

Black water lies between fern hummocks, flooded roots, and trees rising from knee-deep pools. Vines turn every direction into another wall. Stonepears hang along firmer ridges, while Rotheart clusters mark sick trees deeper in the flooded forest. Every few hundred feet, firm mud becomes sucking water, a pool drops past the waist, or a channel ends beneath floating plants.

## Situation now

The Marshes begin at the wet southern shore of [[Aruhe - Clear Lake]] and tighten downhill into [[Aruhe - The Mangroves]]. This is Aruhe's most biologically crowded region: hot, saturated, insect-thick, and loud with decay. There is almost never a reliable road.

## Moves that change the scene

- Use Wisdom (Survival) DC 15 to keep a seaward heading through shifting channels.
- Follow the silence boundary toward [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest|the Quiet]], wet signs toward [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove|the Grove]], or stone breaches toward [[Aruhe - Lava Tubes]].
- Probe shallow water before committing; failure can drop a leg into a pocket, requiring Strength (Athletics) DC 13 to escape.
- Use raised roots, hummocks, fallen trunks, and animal passages as firmer ground.
- Travel under branches carefully around [[Aruhe - Snakewood]], and avoid [[Aruhe - Stillbloom]] and [[Aruhe - Spiritpollen]].
- Treat standing water before drinking. Find flowing water when possible.
- Forage fallen fruit or genuine offerings; taking living plants, eggs, prey, or fruit invokes [[Taking on Aruhe]].

## Presence

[[Aruhe - Crown Squid]] uses flooded openings and the lower canopy. [[Aruhe - Deerstalker|Deer-Stalkers]], Corpsewood, Snakewood, Bear-Elk, and Silence Moths use the firmer paths, roots, boundaries, and approaches. River Otters remain in clear lake-fed channels; brown standing water is not their claim. Insects are constant.

## Connections

The Marshes connect Clear Lake, the River, Quiet Forest, Memorial Grove, the Mangroves, and the subsurface lava tubes. Luminous roots run through the wet ground toward the unmarked graves in the Grove.

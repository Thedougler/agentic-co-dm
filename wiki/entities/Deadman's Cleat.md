# Deadman's Cleat

> [!narration] Narration
> A bronze cleat the length of a hand, pitted grey where seawater sat in the metal.
>
> Two bolt holes at its base are torn wide, so it came off the hull with the hull still holding it.
>
> A line stays knotted through the horn in a diver's knot, cut short a foot out.
>
> Worn on a belt it sits cold against the hip and stays cold, however long the day runs.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **Deadman's Cleat [HB].** While worn on a belt, sash, or harness, you have Advantage on saving throws and ability checks made to resist being shoved, knocked [[Prone]], or moved against your will, including by a spell, a wave, or a creature's forced movement.
>
> **Edge cases:** it does nothing against a grapple already holding you, only against the attempt to move you while grappled. A wearer knocked Prone by damage rather than by an effect gets no save to have Advantage on.
>
> **Limitations:** it grants no bonus to any roll's number, ends no condition, and does not keep you upright when your own footing is the problem. A wearer who is not standing on something has nothing to hold.

## Provenance

Cut out of a hull section that came up from the [[Verdant Teeth]] with a wreck-dive crew's third haul of the season, bolts and all. Ninka took it in with the rest of the scrap and priced it once she saw the knot.

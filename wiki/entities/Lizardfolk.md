# Lizardfolk

**Creature Type:** Humanoid (Reptilian)

**Size:** Medium (about 6 to 7 feet tall)

**Speed:** 30 ft.; swim speed equal to walking speed

Lizardfolk weigh each choice against survival and treat wasted resources as the one true sin. As a Lizardfolk, you have these special traits.

**Bite.** A natural melee weapon dealing 1d6 slashing damage on a hit.

**Hold Breath.** You can hold your breath for up to 15 minutes.

**Hungry Jaws.** Once per long rest, you can make a bonus action bite attack. On a hit, you gain temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus.

**Natural Armor.** Your unarmored AC equals 13 + your [[Dexterity]] modifier. This trait is compatible with shields.

**Nature's Intuition.** You have [[Proficiency]] in two of the following skills: Animal Handling, Medicine, Nature, Perception, Stealth, or Survival.

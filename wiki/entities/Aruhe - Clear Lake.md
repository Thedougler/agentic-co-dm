# Clear Lake

![[aruhe-the-clear-lake.jpg|Clear Lake, a wide inland basin]]

## Look

Clear Lake fills a crater of cold water surrounded by steep green walls. A shelf of black hexagonal stone steps into transparent shallows where fish move above pale rocks and moss. Marsh grass and a slow brown channel gather along one shore before the water deepens to blue.

## Situation now

The lake is one of Aruhe's calmer areas. River otter families occupy the water, while [[Aruhe - Unsaid Macaw|Unsaid Macaws]] watch the nesting trees. The outlet runs downhill into [[Aruhe - River]], and the southern shore becomes [[Aruhe - The Marshes|the Marshes]].

## Moves that change the scene

- Splash or make noise; otters treat it as an invitation to play. Wisdom (Animal Handling) DC 15 ends the game, while a Strength or Dexterity save DC 14 keeps yanked gear.
- Follow the shore toward [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]] with Dexterity (Acrobatics) DC 10 on slick stone.
- Walk slowly around [[Aruhe - Stillbloom]], or use total cover and avoid its volley.
- Follow the outlet downhill toward [[Aruhe - River]].
- Fish the shallows or forage fallen fruit; a failed attempt draws otters or shore fauna into the contest.
- Listen from the shelf with Wisdom (Perception) DC 15 for Hinewai's harp in the surrounding woods.

## Connections

The lake borders [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest|the Quiet]], feeds the River, and leads toward the Marshes and [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]].

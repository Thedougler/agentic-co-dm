# HCS Tangent

> [!narration] Narration
> The HCS *Tangent* is a warship built for war, not a patrol cutter. Heavy guns break the clean line of its hull, and a full naval crew keeps the vessel moving with deliberate purpose. Its arrival turns Crown enforcement from inspection into force.

## Handling

The *Tangent* is deployed after an inspection failure, when Crown enforcement shifts from authority to force. It responds with force rather than another inspection and positions wide to force a reaction before striking an overextended target.

## Combat

The *Tangent* is an armed warship with heavy guns. The source provides no initiative, ramming, boarding, destruction, or weapon statistics.

## At the Table

Deploy the *Tangent* when Crown enforcement shifts from authority to force. Its approach communicates that an inspection has failed and the party or port is now facing a warship.

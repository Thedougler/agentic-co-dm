# HCS Tangent

> [!narration] HCS Tangent
> A three-masted heavy frigate rides low and grey, two gun decks shut and a white Admiralty stripe running the length of her upper rail. The brass nameplate at her bow is bolted over an older plate, and the corners of the name underneath show at the edges. Her boats are already in the water and her marines stand along the rail. She is not coming alongside. She is holding off, waiting to see which way you turn.

*Large heavy frigate (150 ft. by 40 ft.), captained by [[captain-dorian-bishop|Captain Dorian Bishop]], working Crown patrol waters in the central Strait.*

## Statblock

- **Type.** Heavy Frigate; Ship of the Line. Tier 3, armed.
- **Speed.** 60 miles/day on a good wind; 28 miles/day on a poor one. Maneuverability average.
- **Crew.** 24 minimum, 110 full; 20 passengers; 60 tons cargo.
- **Hull.** AC 13, HP 420, condition prime.
- **Movement.** Sails: AC 12, HP 110; speed drops 5 feet for every 30 damage to the rigging.
- **Weapons.** 52 × 18-lb long cannon (20 per side on the main gun deck, 6 per side above); 8 × 12-lb chaser (4 bow, 4 stern); 4 × swivel gun on the weather deck. Upkeep about 420 gp/week.

**Current voyage.** The *Tangent* is the Crown's answer when an inspection has already failed. She does not stop ships; she hunts them, and she is held in reserve until the Admiralty decides a target is worth naval force rather than another boarding. Her standing order is to run a named target down, take it or break it, and report to [[harwick]]. She comes to the central Strait when [[captain-dorian-bishop|Bishop]] replaces [[barnaby-rook|Rook]].

**Crew.** [[captain-dorian-bishop|Dorian Bishop]] commands and picks every engagement. His marine complement fights as [[dravosi-enforcer]] veterans and the gun crews work as [[dravosi-deckhand]]s.

**Decks.** The weather deck holds the helm, the signal staff, and the swivel guns, and a boarding action means fighting across it to reach the quarterdeck. The main gun deck carries twenty guns a side and is where a boarder meets the marine complement. Below the waterline sit the magazine and a brig of iron-grated cells; the magazine is the fastest way to end the ship and the brig is the only way off it for a prisoner.

**Handling.** Bishop fights at distance. He keeps the *Tangent* far back and downwind, letting a target run, chase, or adjust its course, then strikes from the angle the target stopped covering. In a chase, her speed lets her hold a quarry for hours without closing, so the party has to choose between running for open water and turning to fight. When she closes to grapple, the boarding party comes over in two waves with the marines behind the first, and the gun crews keep firing into the enemy's rigging while the decks are contested. She does not ram unless a target is already dead in the water. If her hull drops below 120 hit points, Bishop breaks off, repairs under marine guard, and comes back at the party's rear rather than finishing the fight he started.

**Secret.** The *Tangent* was renamed by Bishop, and no record says why. The truth is that the plate covers her old name, and he chose *Tangent* for his own method: a tangent touches a curve once, at a point the curve never planned for, and leaves. The tell is the brass nameplate bolted over the older one with its corners showing; the find is a Crown registry entry in [[harwick]] listing her previous name and the change of command, or the Admiralty's refit papers. Tracking her by the old name leads to the same ship.

> [!narration] Underway
> Under your feet the deck is steady and the gun ports are still shut. The *Tangent* holds off your quarter, matching every turn you make a minute late, and the water between you never gets shorter. Along her rail the marines do not move and the boats stay in the water, and you can hear her crew running out the guns behind the shut ports.

**Connections.** [[captain-dorian-bishop|Dorian Bishop]] commands. [[dravosi-crown]] operates her and [[harwick]] is her ledger port. [[hcs-warrant]] is the earlier Crown response she replaces when inspections fail; [[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]] is the officer whose fall brings Bishop out.

# Red Lady / Dead Lady

> [!narration] Narration
> A raised wreck slides across open water, hull dark red like blood in the wash, broken rigging still standing. The figurehead is a carved woman with eyes fixed forward. No crew show on deck. Her sails hold full no matter what the wind is doing, and her hull makes no sound against the water. Freeboard sits low enough to climb from a boat. Other ships give her room the moment they place her outline against the horizon.

## Handling

The Red Lady's visible wake crosses a crew lane and turns a familiar route into a commitment. A boarding angle opens from current or storm, giving access to the raised deck at the cost of exposure. Open water remains an evasive route, but retreat can leave behind evidence, witnesses, or a persistent Red Wake consequence.

The Dead Lady holds station. It knows [[delmar-fisk]]'s heading, so defiance costs more than compliance. It does not close merely to kill him. Holds dump. Crews are taken whole. Empty boats are left spinning.

## Combat

A boarding line can climb onto the raised deck from the water. Trackers, boarders, evaders, or bargainers can try those approaches. Use only the Gargantuan chassis and hull figures until combat procedures exist.

## At a Glance

The Red Lady, also called the Dead Lady, is a moving sign of [[Umberlee]]'s Red Wake. Her raised hull blocks lanes. It carries evidence of the original sinking. It also brings the [[pearl-of-souls]] and [[drowned-maw]] into the crew's route.

## Secrets

The original Red Lady was [[delmar-fisk]]'s flagship, and [[crissdalynn-khinriss]] pulled him from its crow's nest when the fleet sank. Exact wreck history, active mechanism, crew roster, and encounter timing remain unresolved.

After Delmar's deadline passed with no movement toward the Maw, [[Umberlee]] begins taking payment herself. Dumping cargo is tribute without priest or consent. The party first hears the red-ship rumor at [[kalowe]]. Nobody has connected it to Delmar yet.

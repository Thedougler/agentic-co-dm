# Vestra

> [!narration] Vestra
> The Vestra lies keel-down on a shelf of dead coral, hull staved in below the waterline in a wound too clean for any reef. Her masts lie snapped and partly covered by silt. Rigging trails like weed.
>
> The gun mounts still contain their iron, green with corrosion, while sand chokes the barrels. No bones remain on deck. The cold presses against bare skin. The water tastes of rust where it seeps from the corroded iron.
>
> One hatch stands open on its hinges, swinging slow in a current that should not exist this deep. Its groan is the only sound this far down.

*Gargantuan sailing brig (75 ft. by 22 ft.), built and captained by [[vincenzo-black-jaw|Vincenzo Black-Jaw]]; lost on the open shelf of [[drowned-maw|the Drowned Maw]].*

## Statblock

- **Size.** Gargantuan vehicle, 75 ft. by 22 ft., two masts.
- **Speed.** 2.5 mph under sail.
- **Crew.** 12 minimum; 10 passengers; 60 tons cargo.
- **Hull.** AC 15, HP 250, damage threshold 15.
- **Helm.** AC 16, HP 50.
- **Masts.** Two sails, AC 12, HP 100 each; speed drops 1 mph for every 25 damage a mast takes, and losing both leaves her dead in the water.
- **Weapons.** Four cannon (crew 3 each): `+6` to hit, range 120/480 ft., `3d10` bludgeoning damage.
- **As found.** A wreck: hull at 0 hit points, flooded, no crew aboard.

**Current state.** She lies on the dead-coral shelf where she went down, keel-down, never raised and never salvaged. Her hull is open below the waterline, her masts are down, and her gun mounts hold iron that no one has cut out. Nothing on the wreck has been stripped, which tells a salvager that the shelf is further out or worse than it looks.

**Crew.**

- **[[vincenzo-black-jaw|Vincenzo Black-Jaw]].** Builder, owner, and captain; went down with the brig.
- **[[perrin-black-jaw]].** The only known survivor of the sinking; washed ashore on [[keth-naar]] afterwards.
- **The lost complement.** Twelve hands at minimum, none of them accounted for and none named in the record.

**Decks.**

- **Weather deck.** Sand-choked cannon behind corroded iron, silt over the planks, rigging trailing into the dark, and no bodies.
- **The open hatch.** Swings on its hinges in a current that should not run this deep; the way down into the flooded hold.
- **The hold.** Where [[perrin-black-jaw]] was drowning when [[auralis]] reached him. Cold, rust-tasting water, and the flicker of something that holds.

**Handling.**

- **As a wreck.** She cannot sail, steer, or fight. Any boarding is a dive: anchored lines, rope, and a way to keep breathing, with the current at the hatch working against a swimmer who fights it.
- **Looting her.** The cannon alone are worth a salvage run, and the current makes every trip below an exposure risk. Cut iron, run lines, and mind the tide of the shelf.
- **Fleeing her.** Nothing on the wreck pursues; what pursues is the interest the wreck attracts. [[auralis]]'s working sits below, and [[umberlee]] still claims what went into the Maw.

**Secret.** The wound below the waterline is not a reef wound: it is a single strike from something larger than the ship, which is the same account [[perrin-black-jaw]] gives of the water boiling and lightning cracking a clear sky. Whether that thing is the [[leviathan]] remains the table's open question, not settled canon.

**Secret.** The current moving the open hatch comes up from the fissure beneath the Maw, not from the sea above. The cold and the rust taste in the water come from the same place, and [[auralis]] below is still holding that fissure shut. A diver who follows the current in instead of swimming against it reaches the machinery that kept Perrin alive.

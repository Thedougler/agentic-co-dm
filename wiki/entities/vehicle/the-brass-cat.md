# The Brass Cat

# The Brass Cat

![[Brass Cat Banner]]

A two-masted schooner, 95 feet long. The hull is timber above water and copper-sheathed below, with iron bands along the waterline. Brass-framed glass sits flush in the deck, above a sealed chamber. Brass constructs move through the rigging and across the planks. Precise, silent, and never idle, all four of them work without rest.

*Experimental schooner · Tier 2 · unmoored.*

[[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Davirelli]]'s personal ship. Built for surface sailing and dives below. Designed for long journeys with no outside support. Not for sale.

**Air system:** built by Davirelli using brass fittings, magic, and a pump in the keel. Takes 10 minutes to turn on or off. Dives to 120 feet. Needs a live garden to make air; without it, the air goes bad.

**Airlock:** on the port side, mid-hull. Brass and wood doors sized for two large beings with gear.

**Crew:** four brass workers, each counts as one sailor. They follow orders but can't handle new tasks or fight.

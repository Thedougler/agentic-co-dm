# The Narrow

> [!narration] The Narrow
> The Narrow is a low, dark-grey scout sloop with a single mast and a hull long for its beam. She carries little freeboard, and at speed her wake barely disturbs the water, which is what let her work the shallows ahead of the fleet and come back with the soundings.

*Light single-masted scout sloop of [[fisks-fleet]], captained by [[maren]], lost in [[drowned-maw|the Drowned Maw]] with all hands.*

## Statblock

- **Size.** Light scout sloop; long on the waterline relative to its beam.
- **Speed.** Built for speed; the source states no rate.
- **Crew.** All hands perished. Captain [[maren]]; no other crew named.
- **Cargo.** Diving equipment, survey gear, crew hammocks, and a chart table.
- **Hull.** Low freeboard; the source states no AC, hit points, or damage threshold.
- **Helm.** The chart table is bolted to the forward bulkhead.
- **Movement.** Single mast, shallow survey work, and little wake at speed.
- **Weapons.** Two swivel gun mounts; she was not built to fight.

**Not a fighting ship.** Two swivel mounts is the whole of her armament. Her value to the fleet was the survey: she went into water ahead of the others and came back with what was under it.

**Connections.** - [[fisks-fleet]] — the five-ship fleet she scouted for.
- [[maren]] — her captain, drowned with the ship.
- [[delmar-fisk]] — the fleet's admiral.
- [[drowned-maw]] — where she went down with the rest of the fleet.
- [[red-lady]] and [[heft]] — the fleet's flagship and its largest ship, lost the same night.
- [[Umberlee]] — the power that opened the Maw beneath them.

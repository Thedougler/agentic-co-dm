# Uncertainty - Deck Layouts

Eighty feet by twenty-two on the beam, over three decks, sixteen squares of hull tapering to two at the bow and three at the stern. Every grid square runs 5 ft. Nobody has changed the hull itself. The rooms inside it have changed twice.

Stock and quantities live on the [[uncertainty-manifest|Ship Manifest]]. This page covers what stands where, and what a fight can pick up.

## Stats & Combat

Hull numbers and armament are on [[Uncertainty|the ship's page]], and bands and gunnery resolve through Ship Combat.

*Dim, under ship's lanterns.*

| Zone | Size | What stands there | What a fight can use |
|---|---|---|---|

| Stern swivel | 5×10 ft | Swivel gun on a 180° aft arc, stern rail, signal lantern | Canister at point blank |

### Gun Deck

*Interior, roughly 65 ft by 14 usable. Headroom of 6 ft 4 in, dim under oil lamps. In from the companionway amidships, or the aft stair off the quarterdeck.*

| Zone | Size | What stands there | What a fight can use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crew quarters | 20×14 ft | Hammock hooks for six, kit lockers, bilge hatch. Delmar bunks here now | Hammocks give soft cover, and become difficult terrain once down |
| Expanded hold | 15×14 ft | Cargo netting floor to ceiling, racked crates, a block and tackle for the heavy lifts | Crates stack and topple, and a swung block is an improvised weapon |
| Central ladder | 5×10 ft | Up to the weather deck, down to the hold, two-square landing | Chokepoint both directions |
| Chart table | 10×14 ft | Bolted table, redrawn survey charts, dividers, speaking tube | Bolted, so it will not tip. Cover |
| Rigger's workshop | 5×5 ft | Alcove forward of the mess. Workbench, vise, six racked sets of artisan's tools | One square, wall-mounted. Bonus action to pull a tool set off the rack |
| Crew mess | 10×10 ft | Long table, bulkhead bench, one armchair. Was the officer cabin, then the captain's cabin, now neither | Bench and table go over as a barricade |
| Aft stair | 5×5 ft | Companionway up to the quarterdeck, single file | One creature wide. The attacker on the stair has cover |

> **The armchair.** [[Cobb]] found it in the mess, and it is the [[elder-mimic]]. Telegraph it before it acts: a cushion that fails to move when the ship rolls, a boot heel that sticks. Leave it dormant until the crew give it a reason.
>
> **Below-deck brawling.** Headroom runs 6 ft 4 in. Any creature taller than that, and every Large creature, attacks at disadvantage down here and cannot bring a melee weapon extending 10 feet or more to bear.

### Hold

*Lowest level, roughly 55 ft by 12 usable. Headroom of 5 ft 8 in. Absolute darkness. Standing bilge water at the low point makes difficult terrain. In from the cargo hatch, or the central ladder.*

| Zone | Size | What stands there | What a fight can use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chain locker | 10×10 ft | Anchor chain in coiled piles | One creature at a time, difficult terrain, badly cramped |
| Galley | 15×12 ft | Cast-iron stove on a sand bed, cook's tools, bread locker, iron ladder up through a hatch to the mess | The stove burns, and it stays bolted down |
| Provisions store | 15×12 ft | Thirty days of food and water for ten, casks and barrels, sealed ration chest. Took in the old brig space | Barrels give cover, and they throw |
| Inspection storage | 10×12 ft | Empty. Held Crown manifests and blank writs once | Bare, with nothing to hide behind |
| General cargo | 15×12 ft | Crew cargo and stored crates | Crates stack and topple |
| Powder magazine | 10×12 ft | Sealed copper charges for the battery, pistol charges | Fire hazard, and everyone aboard knows it |
| Bilge pump | 5×5 ft | Manual pump, an action to work | Water underfoot |

Hold hazards will end a fight down here faster than any weapon aboard does.

> **Hold hazards.** *Powder magazine*, open flame within 10 ft: DC 12 Dexterity save or 8d6 fire in a 20-ft radius, and the hull takes 30 outright. *Galley stove*, shoved into it: DC 12 Dexterity or 1d4 fire. *Bilge*, working the pump with water underfoot: DC 10 Dexterity (Acrobatics) or fall prone.

The forward keel under this deck carries five feet of new timber, sound and freshly cut in. Nothing remains of the brig cage, whose bolts went into the ram-plate fitting.

### Facility Berths

Tier 1 allows four berths, and all four stand spoken for. Nothing further fits without a Tier 2 hull.

| Space | Facility | Berths | Standing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Expanded hold, gun deck | Expanded Cargo Hold | 1 | Installed |
| Chart table, gun deck | Chart archive | 1 | Installed |
| Rigger's workshop, gun deck | Rigger's workshop | 1 | Installed and stocked |
| Provisions store, hold | Provisions store | 2 | Installed |

The Expanded Cargo Hold [HB, from 2024's Storehouse] takes the old weapons-locker alcove into the tonnage figure on [[Uncertainty|the ship's page]], adding 10 extra tons that the crew racks and nets rather than stows loose.

## Crew

Where hands stand when it matters. The roster appears on [[Uncertainty|the ship's page]].

Underway, the helm and the bosun occupy the quarterdeck, the gunner works the weather deck between her two batteries, and the carpenter works between the bilge hatch and the pump. Everyone sleeps forward on the gun deck, the captain included.

Fighting her on four hands leaves the hold and the aft stair unwatched, and that is exactly where boarders come up.

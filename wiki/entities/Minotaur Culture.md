# Minotaur Culture

*[[Minotaur]] society, kinship bonds, life stages, naming, and the pilot's authority at sea in [[Shattered Sea]].*

## The Culture

In [[Shattered Sea]], [[Minotaurs]] pilot ships. Many farm or fight, but most love the sea. They guide ships through blind channels and read reefs. They hold maps in their minds that keep ships safe. **Passages** shape Minotaur life. A passage is a sea route, a life stage, or a turn from one to the next.

A Minotaur pilot costs more than any other hire in [[Midchain]]. Every coin is worth the price. [[Sienne Orre]], who leads the *[[Fernen]]* in [[Fisk's Fleet]], ran the survey work. Her gift for reading passages made her the clear choice.

Minotaurs live where water is hard to cross: [[Narrow]] straits, tidal channels, reef knots. The killers of ships are the routes Minotaurs learn as youth. Small groups live in each big port. A Minotaur street is odd. The halls turn. Minotaurs gather where hard channels need a teacher, not around any one seat of power.

[[Delmar Fisk]] carries the soul of [[Sienne Orre]], a Minotaur druid, as one of his five captains ("Mortis"). When Sienne surfaces, her calm, methodical nature speaks as Minotaur culture. See the Kinship and Temperament sections below. This is detail, not an active plot. Each of Sienne's moments draws from the culture described here.

### Kinship: the Sounding

A Minotaur's real kin are **Soundings**. An elder and youth form a Sounding when the elder teaches a passage. Each Minotaur builds many over life, one per passage learned. Adults can form Soundings as equals too. [[Sienne Orre]] and [[Maren]] became Soundings through reef survey work.

No group links Soundings together. Minotaur life forms from many small bonds, two to three souls in each. Each Sounding stands alone. A Minotaur without a living Sounding still has kin. Learned passages count as kin too.

### Life Stages

- **Calf**: before a first solo passage.
- **Passage-rite**: the first solo navigation of a channel hard enough that failure was real. Done alone, with rescue withheld as part of the test. The channel's name folds into how close kin address them afterward.
- **Passage-holder**: adulthood. May found Soundings of their own, teaching a calf or partnering a peer.
- **Elder**: a Minotaur who has personally carried three or more calves through their passage-rites. Real informal weight. Elders gather informally but never formally convene.

Minotaur interiors twist by design: hallways switchback and turn back on themselves, rooms open from three sides. A straight corridor feels to a Minotaur like a windowless room feels to a Human (strange and exposed). Guests learn to trust their host's guidance through the winding paths.

### Temperament

Minotaurs run warm and slow. Anger builds visibly when the breath shifts, the head lowers, and horns extend. At that point, a Minotaur becomes unsafe to provoke. Most never reach such anger. Those who do never forget. The raging bull myth comes from these rare cases. A working pilot has a surgeon's sharp focus.

The horns-forward tell isn't unique to anger. The mind narrowing to one problem produces the identical posture. Sienne Orre's horns come forward when she's deep in a chart. The tell she'd show when furious is the same. Only her breathing and stance distinguish anger from concentration. Outsiders who've only ever caught the anger version keep the raging-bull stereotype alive.

## Customs & Traditions

### At Sea

A Minotaur pilot commands absolute authority over helm and heading in narrow water. A captain who overrides the pilot in a channel is a captain who wants to sink. This convention applies across [[Shattered Sea|the Shattered Sea]] regardless of a ship's flag. Even Crown naval officers defer to the pilot in reef passages.

## Naming

Minotaur personal names draw from Greek and Minoan roots: strong consonants, open vowels, -os/-as/-is endings. *Stavra, Kosmos, Thekla, Andronikos.* Sea-going Minotaurs in mixed crews often adopt local naming conventions. A Minotaur in [[Calven and Calveno]] waters might carry a name indistinguishable from a [[Tessarine Concordat|Tessarine]] Human. [[The Passage]] naming tradition persists informally among close kin. It remains a Sounding practice without formal structure.

# Corvin Ashe

[[Corvin Ashe]] is Bishop of [[Penance Bar]] and priest of [[Sorrowbell]]. He frames a woods coven as witches. A later note says his fearmongering, not a personal bargain, draws the demons. A draft fiction has him meet the crew at the shrine door.

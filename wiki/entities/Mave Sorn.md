## Goals & Fronts

Information broker, not a prize-taker. She files intelligence and sells it to the right buyer at the right time. If Perrin's pact is named or confirmed during a parley, it goes in her ledger. What she files it under, and who she eventually sells it to, is a thread the DM runs forward.

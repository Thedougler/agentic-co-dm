# Soul Incarnate

> [!abstract] At a Glance
> **Core truth.** The [[Fate Spinner]] can expose a Soul Incarnate signature on beings tied to certain ley-line phenomena.
>
> **Why it matters.** At [[Yssenmoor]], that signature marks [[Sir Quackers the Fowl]] without explaining the path from familiar toward demigodhood.
>
> **Scope.** Partial; this page records only what the Quackers source states.

## Current Truth

[[Sir Quackers the Fowl]] carries a Soul Incarnate signature that the [[Fate Spinner]] dreidel reaction can expose. He does not understand what the reaction means.

## At the Table

* **Players notice:** A Fate Spinner reaction that does not match ordinary expectations.
* **This explains:** Why Quackers treats the reaction as an unsolved puzzle.
* **This enables:** Investigation of ley-line incumbents and Soul Incarnate marks.
* **This warns of:** Power gathering in a familiar who has died and reformed for centuries.

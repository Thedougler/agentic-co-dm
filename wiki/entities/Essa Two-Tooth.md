# Essa Two-Tooth

![[essa-two-tooth-banner]]

**Wants:** keep the [[Low Lamp]] as the Warren's true social center and collect every debt owed to her (recorded only in her head, never on paper).

**Quote:** "These two? I call this one Scrimper and that one the Keeper. They're the only counsel I need these days."

> [!narration] Narration
> Essa Two-Tooth stands behind the bar of the Low Lamp, gray fur worn thin at her shoulders and jaw, whiskers long and yellowed. A hundred arguments have left notches and scars on her ears. She's [[Rattkin]], ancient by any reckoning, her four-foot frame bent forward to meet the crowd at eye level. Her two remaining teeth are present and accounted for. Sharp. She introduces them by name to every new arrival as scrimper (sharp) and the Keeper (the one that remembers). Behind her, bottles and earthenware catch the lamp glow in shadows that gather deep in the lines of her face. One gnarled hand works a cloth across a mug, slow and thorough, fingers curled with age. The other waits on the bar's worn edge, ready.

**Roleplay Concept:** [[Rattkin]] elder publican and unofficial Warren ledger-keeper, mercy and memory in one package.

**Opening move:** behind the bar in the Low Lamp · party speaks first (the room falls silent when they arrive) · "You're the crew. Come on in. These two will want to hear this." (gestures to her two teeth)

**Lore Sheet:** elder of the Warren who has held every name and debt in her head for four decades, never wrote a single number down. Knew [[Nona Black-Jaw]]'s late husband by his first name. Insists every guest is too thin and must eat before discussion. Feeding at the Low Lamp means the house has your goodwill, which is precisely her intent. Does not ask [[The Tangle]]'s permission to refuse service to someone unwelcome.

**Voice & Delivery:** drawl, unhurried and ancient. She doesn't posture or explain herself, letting words swing between "These two will want to hear this" and "You're too thin. Sit," while the quiet that follows serves as her real voice. She reads everything at the bar (how fast someone drinks, whether their cup stays full, who won't look up, what stays empty, what fills the space between people) and doesn't miss a transaction, debt, or grudge, staying emotionally steady as stone.

## Combat Profile

### Fast Read

Face and support with a CHA longsword and Eldritch Blast. Heel is Strength and being pinned in a Strength save.

### Counters & Synergy

- **Hard counters.** Grapples that ignore Boneless, or forced Strength tests.
- **Soft counters.** Silence and anti-charm rooms.
- **Amplifies / Depends on.** Bardic Inspiration and Cutting Words. Pack Tactics if an ally is hit next to him.

# The Tithe of the Bitch Queen

*A legendary collection of divine treasure scattered across the [[Sunken Crown]] seafloor. Taking a single piece invites [[Umberlee]]'s direct, personal spite.*

## The Fact

Before someone stole the [[Pearl of Souls]], sailors widely believed Umberlee maintained a physical collection of the most beautiful treasures her storms had claimed, an unconscious curation of the finest things the sea had taken and kept, gathered without design or intent. The Tithe scatters across the Sunken Crown seafloor instead of consolidating in one location: unblemished jewels and pristine statues rest with gold bars that never tarnish, each piece carrying unmistakable divine preservation. Tidal caves that breach the surface during the lowest tides of the decade offer the only access.

Taking a single coin from the Tithe invites Umberlee's direct, personal spite. Sailors who hold a piece cannot sail without drawing unnatural, localized squalls that follow their ship, and the squalls persist until the item touches saltwater again. Waveservant records document no theological workaround. The curse falls on the object, not the bearer: transferring the item transfers the problem with it.

The Tithe predates the theft of the Pearl of Souls. That theft has complicated the theological picture: the Tithe represents what Umberlee passively accumulates, while the Pearl represents something actively stolen from her. The [[Waveservants]] will not discuss whether the Pearl belongs to the Tithe in any formal sense.

> [!mechanic]
> **Tradition:** Divine. **School:** Necromancy (curse category). The curse is Umberlee's active divine attention on each piece of the Tithe (not a cast spell) and cannot be Counterspelled at the moment of theft.
> **[[Detect Magic]]:** "Divine necromancy. The curse has weight to it, old and deliberate, like something that recalls the hand that claimed it."
> **[[Identify]]:** *This object is marked — not by a caster. The weight is divine attention: personal, present, and aware that it has left the Tithe. Whatever god holds claim to it knows you have it now. The mark does not belong to the object; it belongs to the act of taking it. Pass this to another hand and you pass that attention with it — it does not lift, it transfers.*
> **[[Remove Curse]]:** fails. The curse is divine in origin and releases only on its own terms.
> **[[Dispel Magic]]:** suppresses for 1 minute, then Umberlee's attention reasserts.
> The curse falls on the object, not the bearer. Transfer the item and the curse transfers with it.

### Deliberate Silences

- Whether the stolen Pearl of Souls counts as part of the Tithe in any formal theological sense: the Waveservants refuse to discuss it, and no ruling exists.

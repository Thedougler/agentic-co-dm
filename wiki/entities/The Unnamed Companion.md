# The Unnamed Companion

He drowned on the swim toward [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]] after escaping [[Karath]] with [[Hinewai]]. She buried him beneath a fruit tree in [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]], and that grave is the reason for the island's law.

# Western Landing

![[attachments/shattered-sea/places/aruhe-western-landing-alternative-map-view.jpg|Western Landing from above]]

## Look

A jagged coral reef lies about half a mile offshore. At low tide its teeth show, and one boat gap leads to a short shingle beach. Surf works the coral, salt spray crosses loose stone, and wet leaves shade ghost plums at the inland stream.

## Situation now

This is Aruhe's known approach and retreat edge. The southern mangrove wall is not a harbor, and other beaches are trapped behind reef or cliffs. [[Sparhold]] offers the established open-water route; [[Karath]] is close to the rim but does not shorten the journey to the island's heart.

## Moves that change the scene

- Use Wisdom (Perception) DC 15 and Wisdom (Survival) DC 15 to read the gap, tide, and surf.
- Pilot through the gap with Dexterity (Water Vehicles) DC 15.
- Cross the shingle with Dexterity (Acrobatics) DC 10 when carrying gear or under pressure.
- Step around the [[Aruhe - Grubnade]] and [[Aruhe - Spiritpollen]] at the inland wet-leaf edge.
- Follow the inland route to [[Aruhe - Old Gardens|Old Gardens]], or retreat before the tide changes.
- Treat loose stone or shed shell as unverified; taking a living object invokes [[Taking on Aruhe]].

## Presence

Reef fauna include giant sharks, giant crabs, Bloodhawks, and [[Aruhe - Reef Skull|Reef Skulls]]. Reef Skulls are dog-sized hermit crabs living in hull pieces or skulls and burying themselves in the shingle.

## Connections

The boat gap opens toward [[Uncertainty]] and the beach rises inland to [[Aruhe - Old Gardens]].

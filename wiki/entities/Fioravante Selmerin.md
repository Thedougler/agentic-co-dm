# Fioravante Selmerin

**Wants:** [[Il Preludio]]'s name in every mouth in [[Calveno]]. The front window has stood half-dressed for nine days because he stops to introduce it to whoever walks past.

**Quote:** "There comes a moment when a shelf finally speaks to a customer, and the lamp oil's gone up a copper again, did I mention?"

> [!narration] Narration
> Brass bells hang in ranks across the doorway. A voice comes through them mid-sentence, already climbing, and the room behind smells of hot brass and orange peel.
>
> He is an elf, up a stepladder, silver hair pinned back off a long face he uses like a much bigger room.
>
> He finishes a sentence about the price of lamp oil. Then a breath, and one open hand rises to his shoulder.
>
> *(beat)* The hand stays there. He tells you more about lamp oil.
>
> Then it comes down flat on the counter and he follows it down the rungs, still talking. Orange waistcoat, ink to the elbow on one sleeve, one boot buckled and the other not.
>
> He puts a small glass bell into your hands and waits, eyebrows up, for the answer to a question he has not asked.

**Roleplay Concept:** a leading man with no theatre attached to him, running a shop where nothing on the shelves does anything useful.

**Opening move:** halfway up the ladder rigging a glass bell into the window display, one hand free · Fio speaks first · "Ah. Ah, you have come in on precisely the wrong afternoon, and I am going to tell you why."

**Lore Sheet:**

- Came over [[Galewall|the Galewall]] and does not say from where. Ask once and he answers about the shop's roof. Ask twice and he answers about the roof again. The crossing drops all kinds onto Crown docks and most do not stay; he stayed, and that is the one fact about himself he will confirm.
- Every enchantment on the shelves is real and does exactly what its impressive name promises, which is nothing anybody needs. He believes in every one. Stock list: [[Il Preludio]].
- **[[Serena Cottle]]** owns the other half of the shop, **[[Tam Slim-Nail]]** keeps the stock, and **[[Cecco Trivani]]** is learning the trade slower than he would like.
- He has never sung. Nobody who works for him has ever raised it with him.

Jean-Claude told his contact he did not know what he wanted, only that he would know it on sight. Fio's whole trade is putting an object into a browser's hands before they finish deciding.

**Voice & Delivery:** every sentence arrives as the first line of a number. Setup, rising cadence, the breath before a downbeat that never lands, and then he carries straight on with the conversation. The hand comes up on the last word and stays up through the next two sentences. He does not know he does this. Worldview word: *presentation*. A thing is worth whatever somebody calls it, never whatever somebody finds inside.

- "There comes a moment in the life of every customer when the shelf finally speaks to them." (breath, hand up) "Yours is not today. I will show you the shelf regardless."
- "They said to me, Fio, they said, a shop like this will not last one season in [[Velo]]." (hand up, chest fills) "And here we are, and your tea has gone cold. One moment."
- "You want to know what it does. Ah. Ah, do you want to know what it does." (rising) "It rings. That is the whole of it. It rings beautifully."

Emotional default: on the verge of something, permanently, and perfectly happy there.

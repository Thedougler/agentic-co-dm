# Fate Spinner

The [[Fate Spinner]], also called the dreidel, is carried by [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. [[Talon Vantyrus]] sent a ship to take it. [[Master Kyzil]] gave it to her before her pilgrimage as a crystal meditation focus to be spun in sunlight.

A later idea note pitches a once-per-day sunlight spin that reaches a person who has seen it spin and is friendly to the holder. That pitch is not accepted mechanics. Who besides Kyzil currently qualifies, and what "friendly" means, stay open.

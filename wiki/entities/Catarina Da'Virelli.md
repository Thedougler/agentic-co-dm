## Combat Profile

### Fast Read

INT blaster with a cannon and Scorching Ray. Heel is Concentration (CON +5, no War Caster) and anyone who grabs her.

### Counters & Synergy

- **Hard counters.** Grapplers and reach that break focus.
- **Soft counters.** Range denial and no-spell zones.
- **Amplifies / Depends on.** Faerie Fire for the front line. Someone to keep bodies off her.

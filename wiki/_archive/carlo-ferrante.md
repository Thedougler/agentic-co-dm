Carlo is the brother the whole rescue is walking toward, and the reason the four who want out finally go.

- **Role.** A survivor at [[the-pantry]].

## At the Table

- **First meeting.** He watches the party for a long time before he speaks. Then he asks one thing: did they come from the south, and did they see anyone.
- **Opens up when.** He learns [[ettore-ferrante]] and [[luca-ferrante]] are alive. It undoes him, and he is ready to leave that hour.
- **Shuts down when.** Nobody mentions them. He never asks outright.
- **Priority.** His brother and nephew, over everything else.
- **Shares.** That he rose first when the voice called, sure the others would follow, and that they did not.
- **Voice.** Short phrases, long pauses. He says "my brother" and "the boy," never their names.
- **Lines.** "Did you come from the south? Did you see anyone?" · "I walked. They did not. I thought they would." · "Tell me they are alive, or tell me nothing."
- **Leaving.** When Carlo goes, [[tommaso-brasca]], [[sandrino-vale]], and [[ilario-pozzo]] go with him. He and Luca lash a litter of fallen poles for Ettore.

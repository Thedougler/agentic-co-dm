### Hazard — Stillbloom

**Appearance:** A dull, four-foot waxy stalk grows where green first goes black at the leaf edge. Pale hollow bracts end in dark needles. Once in its life, after years underground, a stalk raises one enormous cream-white flower with a faint green center, then dies.

**Behavior:** Stillbloom reads vibration, not malice. Rain, a falling leaf, a mouse, and a careful footstep do not trigger it. Scavengers move slowly across the shallow root mat and feed on what the soil brings down.

**Trigger:** A charged mature stalk fires when a **Small or larger grounded** creature within `20 feet` moves more than `10 feet` on its turn, Dashes, jumps, falls, is forcibly moved `10 feet` or more, or makes another violent motion. Moving `10 feet` or less on a turn is safe, as is flying without touching the root mat.

**Notice:**

- **Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 14`** → Notices launch-spines, punctured carcasses, and odd scavenger paths.
- **Intelligence (Nature) or Wisdom (Survival) — `DC 14`** → Recognizes the root vibration and learns that slow movement is safe.

After `1 minute` watching scavengers, the pattern becomes clear without a roll.

**Volley:** Each creature within `15 feet` of the plant makes a **Dexterity save — `DC 14`**. On a failure, it takes `2d6` piercing damage, one spine embeds, and it makes the Venom save below. On a success, it takes no damage.

**Reload:** After firing, a stalk cannot fire again for `1 minute`. Its pressure sacs visibly swell during this window.

**Cover:** *Half Cover* and *Three-Quarters Cover* grant their usual bonuses to the Dexterity save. *Total Cover* blocks the volley.

**Venom — Exposure:** Each embedded hit raises **Exposure** by `1`. The first exposure requires a **Constitution save — `DC 12`**; each additional Exposure increases that save DC by `1`.

- **Success:** Local numbness lasts `10 minutes` after the last exposure.
  - A struck arm cannot use that hand for somatic components or two-handed use.
  - A struck leg reduces Speed by `10 feet` and prevents the creature from Dashing.
  - If both legs are struck, Speed becomes `5 feet`.
- **Failure:** Advance one step on the Paralytic Spread track:

| Spread | Effect |
|---|---|
| 0 Local | The affected limb is numb. |
| 1 Systemic | The creature has the **Poisoned** condition. |
| 2 Motor Failure | The creature has the **Poisoned** and **Restrained** conditions. |
| 3 Paralysis | The creature has the **Paralyzed** condition. |

**Recovery:** At the end of each turn at Spread 2 or 3, repeat the current Venom **Constitution save**. On a success, reduce Spread by `1`. After `1 minute` without another hit, reduce Spread by `1` more. After `10 minutes`, Exposure becomes `0` and local numbness ends. Effects that clear poison also clear the venom. **Poison Immunity** blocks the venom, but not the piercing damage.

**Counterplay:** Move no more than `10 feet` per turn, trigger the volley with a thrown object or *mage hand* and wait through the reload window, fly over without touching the root mat, spread out, use poison-clearing effects, or weaponize the patch against pursuers. Do not sprint through a charged field.

**Placement:** Stillbloom grows along quiet-to-Rot transition trails and sunbreaks, not in the open valleys.

# Tessarine Concordat

![[tessarine-concordat-banner]]

*Colonial trade power operating through credit, contracts, and debt obligation instead of conquest. Its true architect is a Magus Dragon accumulating leverage across Calveno and the Crown Islands.*

Colonial commercial power that arrived in [[campaign-overview|the Shattered Sea]] in 1210 DR, formed the Seven Houses of [[calveno|Calveno]], and began the slow arithmetic of obligation. Its principal colonial rival is the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]], and both powers compete across the same Scatter routes. The Concordat does not conquer. It lends.

The Concordat began as a merchant-house consortium chartered in Amn, weeks of open ocean north in [[faerun|Faerûn]], on the same model that once put Amn in Port Nyanzaru. The Seven Houses of Calveno are that model transplanted: seven votes and seven cornered trades, sitting on a council no crown appointed.

It operates through credit, contracts, insurance, route underwriting, and trade dependency instead of open occupation. Tessarine paper travels well, and merchants who hate Tessarine leverage still accept it because refusing makes business harder immediately while accepting only makes business harder later. The Concordat rarely says no. It says yes with terms.

**Common instruments:** letters of credit, route underwriting, debt-recovery holds, salvage contracts, and Council obligations tied to private debt.

Calveno is the Concordat's strongest northern position. The city already believes in open dealings, public association, and standing with the right people, a fit the Concordat exploits instead of imposes. The factor building anchors the harbour end of the [[mercatura|Mercatura]]: pale stone, Tessarine columns, no public sign, and boats slowing as they pass because everyone knows who might be watching.

[[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] wears a Tessarine shirt under his Dravosi naval coat. The Concordat is his affiliation or nation of origin.

The Seven Houses Council sits at a four-to-three Tessarine-aligned deadlock. That means four Houses owe enough, benefit enough, or fear enough disruption that Tessarine proposals pass unless the three holdouts can make refusal profitable.

The Concordat wants [[shelfworks|the Shelfworks]] because [[antheri|Antheri]] salvage can create new dependency. Standing orders exist for intact Antheri pieces; factors maintain upper shelf layout records, salvage manifests, and lists of crews willing to work dangerous depths.

[[the-passage|The Passage]] uses Tessarine paper and commercial channels as cover. No formal arrangement exists between the two.

[[cosimo-verantio|Cosimo Verantio]], the Concordat's true architect, is a Magus Dragon who accumulates obligation instead of gold, operating through the factor network. The factors are his Circle. They never know it. [[the-vault-of-the-first-factor|The Vault of the First Factor]] is the nearest physical match to a hoard, though destroying Tessarine gold would not wound the dragon. Invalidating debt, freeing councils from obligation, or making Tessarine paper unreliable would.

## Members

The source states no roster or leadership. Factors staff the factor building, negotiate terms, and maintain the Shelfworks salvage records above. The source names none of them individually.

## Goals & Fronts

**Agenda:**

- Convert credit, contracts, insurance, and route underwriting into political leverage
- Hold the Calveno foothold and its Council deadlock
- Expand into the Shelfworks for Antheri salvage as a dependency lever

This generic agenda has no Front clock of its own (the dedicated Front below it has its own, stated separately). See [[waveservants|Waveservants]] § Goals & Fronts for the same gap.

### Front: The Magus Dragon's Hoard

**Lifecycle:** dormant

**Aim:** accumulate obligation (leverage over councils, trade routes, institutions, and inherited debt across Calveno and the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]]) in place of gold.

**Approach:** operates through [[cosimo-verantio|Cosimo Verantio]] and the factor network, who unknowingly serve a hoard. The dragon never reveals itself.

**Off-screen move if unopposed:** continues consolidating obligation across Calveno and the Crown Islands. PC-adjacent councils and factions accumulate Concordat debt without knowing who ultimately holds it.

**Trigger conditions:**

- The crew investigates the Concordat's debt-holding structure past a single factor's public face: pursuing The Vault of the First Factor, confronting [[cosimo-verantio|Cosimo Verantio]] directly, or otherwise meeting the reveal condition his own page sets.
- A second named debtor repeats [[iacopo-fieschi|Iacopo Fieschi]]'s Session 8 public confession ([[s08-recap|Session 08 recap]]), a pattern of public debt admissions the dragon must answer instead of one factor's contained embarrassment.

**Clock:** 8 segments (slow burn), 0 filled. Still dormant. Fieschi's confession cracked local concealment, but no wiki fact shows the hoard shrinking or the deadlock shifting. Clock hasn't advanced.

**Consequence at fill:** the Concordat calls in simultaneous debts, shattering the 4-3 deadlock and forcing unanimous ratification of a Tessarine charter subordinating Calveno's governance. The same leverage extends to the Crown Islands. Centuries of accumulated obligation convert to direct control the dragon no longer hides.

**Possible outcomes (2-3):**

- The crew uncovers Cosimo Verantio's true nature before the clock fills and chooses to expose him, forcing the factors, his unwitting Circle, to confront what they actually serve. This collapses the concealment layer without necessarily discharging existing debt.
- The crew engineers debt invalidation or repudiation at scale: a Fieschi-style confession repeated across other Houses, or damage to The Vault of the First Factor's records, wounding the hoard directly, per the dragon's stated vulnerability.
- Left unopposed, the clock fills: the Concordat formally subordinates Calveno's Council and extends the same leverage into the Crown Islands. The crew arrives too late to stop consolidation and must work within or against an entrenched Tessarine political order instead.

**PC connection:** [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] stands amid the Calveno council deadlock above [[warren|the Warren]]. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] fits the [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]] salvage contract well. Both appear directly in the source's Key Actors list.

**Quest link:** none yet. The `who-commissioned-the-theft` question may serve this front's hook once a page for it lands. [[chain-council|Chain Council]] § Denial and § Tessarine Containment track the Council and Concordat ends of the commission.

**Three-Clue Trail:**

Conclusion: [[cosimo-verantio|Cosimo Verantio]] is a Magus Dragon, and the Concordat's factor network is his unwitting Circle, accumulating obligation instead of gold as its true hoard.

Clue 1: Calveno factor building. The salvage manifests and crew lists factors keep for Shelfworks Antheri orders show appetite and reach disproportionate to any merchant house, and a pressed factor lets slip that “the Principal” personally reviews every Antheri lot, all discoverable without combat.

Clue 2: the Seven Houses Council's 4-3 deadlock. A holdout House member, asked directly why refusal never sticks, names debts that outlive the debtor and pass to heirs and successor firms, a pattern no ordinary moneylender sustains across generations. This clue, too, is discoverable without combat.

Clue 3: [[the-vault-of-the-first-factor|The Vault of the First Factor]], the nearest physical match to a hoard, whose accounting practices track obligation compounding across centuries, tied to no single mortal lifespan. Reaching its records may require breaching wards or guardians.

# Sera Maddock

Old privateer. Compact and wheezing, still. Seventy-one years old. Three days from dead by [[ilaria-grimaldi|Ilaria Grimaldi]]'s estimate from lung sickness, treatable in [[tidefall|Tidefall]], not in [[kalowe|Kalowe]]. She makes eye contact the way someone does who has spent a career reading strangers fast.

She ran [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi]]-licensed privateer work forty years ago. Her crew buried a cache on [[orak|Orak]] from one of those runs and she is the last one alive who knows where. She bears half the chart tattooed on her left forearm. She burned the other half when the man she was going to share it with tried to sell her to the Crown.

She is not sentimental about the money. She insists someone get it instead of letting it rot under a rock. She demands passage out of [[kalowe|Kalowe]]. Her deal: 200 gp and passage (not to Tidefall, she wants a healer city with the right herbalist). The forearm tattoo is worth the price.

To someone offering passage, she'd pitch the deal directly: *"The map's on my arm. I'll show you the bearing if you show me a ship."* If pressed about why she destroyed the second half of the chart, the old privateer offers no apology: *"I burned the other half. The man I was going to share it with deserved less."* When you eventually question what awaits in the buried cache on [[orak|Orak]], she gives you the truth as she knows it: *"There's something in that cave. I'm not telling you it's nothing. I'm telling you I don't know what's in there now."*

She is in the back room of [[grimaldis-dispensary|Grimaldi's Dispensary]] (see [[kalowe-captains-map|Kalowe, The Captain's Map]]).

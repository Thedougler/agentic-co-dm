# Slipwater Grease

> [!read-aloud]
> A squat clay pot no wider than a fist, its wax seal cracked once and pressed shut again with a thumb.
>
> The grease inside is the grey of cold tallow with something darker threaded through it, and it smells of eel and wet rope.
>
> It goes on cool and keeps thinning as it works, until your own fingers slide off your own wrist.
>
> The pot holds one pair of arms' worth, and the seal will not take a third pressing.

*Consumable (Ointment), Common.* Rendered from eel fat and boat-slip pitch, sold openly in the loft to any duellist who expects the other champion to close and hold.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **Slipwater Grease [HB].** Single use. Applying it to your own arms and torso takes 1 minute. For the next hour you have advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks, Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks, and Strength saving throws made to escape or resist a grapple, a restrain, or a shove.
>
> **Edge cases:** the grease is on you, not on your weapon, so it grants nothing against a magical hold with no physical grip. A creature grappling you takes no penalty of its own; you simply get out easier.
>
> **Limitations:** it ends early after more than a minute in fresh water, and one pot covers one creature. It grants no bonus to AC, to attack rolls, or to climbing a dry surface, which it makes worse.

## Provenance

Rendered in the tavern's back kitchen from the eel trimmings the market stalls sell off cheap, and priced by how many bouts in the betting ring have ended with a champion pinned instead of cut.

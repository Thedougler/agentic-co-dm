# Tam Slim-Nail

**Wants:** to keep an exact count of what comes back on its own. The barrel by the counter needs recounting again.

**Quote:** "Fio's promised a discount he hasn't cleared with Serena. She's counting the till twice. Nothing here needs an audience."

> [!read-aloud]
> A small grey shape sits on a stool behind the counter. Whiskers still. Tail curled once around the stool leg.
>
> The same notch marks both ears, as if something took a measure off each and stopped there.
>
> A ledger lies open in front of him, chalk in one long-fingered paw, and his claws click once against the barrel's rim as he counts.
>
> He doesn't look up. "Forty-one blades in the barrel, as of last night, and the newest three walked back on their own since dawn."
>
> His fur is the color of wet slate, thinner at the jaw where age has taken it. A brass counter-weight hangs from a cord at his neck, worn smooth from handling.
>
> He sets the chalk down, flat, and slides the ledger two inches toward the edge of the counter, toward you.

**Roleplay Concept:** a funeral director reading someone else's eulogy + the one clerk in a shop that defies clerking.

**Opening move:** tallying the barrel of returned Blades of Unerring Return, chalk in hand · Tam speaks first · "Fio's already promised you a discount he hasn't cleared with Serena. She's counting the till twice. Cecco's humming again. He thinks nobody hears it."

**Lore Sheet:**

- Stockkeeper at [[il-preludio|Il Preludio]] since before Serena bought in. Nobody remembers hiring him.
- Counts every returned item into the barrel by hand, chalk tally on its inside stave. Says he's never once miscounted.
- Speaks every sentence at the exact tempo and cadence of a lyric, and has never once sung one.
- The only one in the building who appears to know how absurd the shop is. Has never said so.

**Voice & Delivery:** flat, unhurried, the exact meter of a lyric with none of the melody. Reports who just walked in, what their face is doing, who's about to get talked into what, as if reading it off a page he already knows by heart. He never raises his voice and never lands the note. Counts the returns on the beat between sentences.

- "Forty-one blades in the barrel, as of last night, and the newest three walked back on their own since dawn."
- "Fio's promised the discount again. Serena's already counting the till twice."
- "Cecco's humming. He thinks nobody hears it."

## Relationships

- [[fioravante-selmerin|Fioravante "Fio" Selmerin]], the owner. Tam narrates his sales pitches back to him, mid-pitch, without stopping the count.
- [[serena-cottle|Serena Cottle]], the co-owner. Tam clocks her belting before she starts and says so, flat, every time.
- [[cecco-trivani|Cecco Trivani]], the apprentice. Tam is the only one who's noticed Cecco is trying to actually sing, and reports it like weather.

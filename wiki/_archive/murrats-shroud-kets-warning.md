# Ket's Warning

![[murrats-shroud-kets-warning-narration-open]]

## Climax Question

Does the crew use Ket's one-turn window to flee, or does curiosity and conversation cost them blood?

## Situation Now

Three to five hungry [[moucheron|Moucheron]] detach from the cloud and fly toward the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]], covering sixty feet per round in the air. Among them is [[ket|Ket]], recognizable to any crew member who saw him in the cage aboard the Surety. He is not leading. He is flying with them.

Ket recognizes the ship. He shouts a warning to leave. Now.

The others do not wait. They hit the deck and target the nearest warm-blooded creature, PC or crew.

Ket holds back one turn. If the crew uses that turn to flee, they escape. If they use it to talk, negotiate, or hesitate, Ket joins the attack and more Moucheron follow from the cloud.

## Player Gravity

- [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] — the Moucheron he fed his own blood is telling him to run
- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] — the creature she shielded from [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s blade is about to attack her

## Stakes

- **If unopposed:** the Moucheron kill everyone on deck, then search below
- **Threshold:** crew casualties; a named NPC bleeding or dying on the rail
- **At risk:** the Uncertainty's crew, the party's hit points, and any illusion that freeing Ket bought them safe passage

## Actors in Motion

- [[moucheron|Moucheron greeting party]] — **Wants:** blood and the ship gone. **Doing:** attacking every warm-blooded creature on deck. **Changes if:** the ship turns and gains distance. They do not pursue past a few hundred feet.
- [[ket|Ket]] — **Wants:** the crew to leave before the island kills them. **Doing:** shouting a warning while hovering with the others. **Changes if:** one turn passes. He joins the attack regardless of whether the crew listened.
- The cloud — **Wants:** the same thing the greeting party wants. **Doing:** circling the island. **Changes if:** the party defeats the first wave. More detach and arrive within rounds.

## Earned Leverage

- Freeing Ket in Session 02 → one turn of hesitation. That is the entire return on the kindness.

## Decision Handles

- **Flee immediately:** the helm turns, the crew runs rigging, the Moucheron lose interest once the ship gains distance. The warning worked.
- **Fight the first wave:** 3-5 Moucheron at CR 5 each, winnable but costly for a level-5 party, and more are visibly incoming from the cloud.
- **Land:** more Moucheron. Going deeper means more. Nothing gets better.

## Escalation Triggers

- **When:** the crew stays on deck after Ket's warning
  **Then:** Ket joins the attack; a second wave detaches from the cloud
  **Because:** the island's law is absolute and Ket cannot resist it

- **When:** the crew defeats the first wave
  **Then:** a larger wave arrives within 2 rounds
  **Because:** every Moucheron on the island can see the ship

- **When:** anyone lands on the beach
  **Then:** the canopy disgorges scouts from every cliff village
  **Because:** lookouts spot a landing before the keel touches sand

## Environment

Open water beside a reef island. The [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]'s deck is the battlefield. The Moucheron fly at sixty feet. The crew cannot hide on an open deck. The reef ring has no marked safe passage, and maneuvering close to shore risks the hull.

## Consequence Lattice

- **Crew survival:**
  - fled in time → no casualties, the crew remembers what they saw
  - stayed one round too long → wounded crew, possible named NPC down
  - fought two or more waves → dead crew, the Uncertainty limps away short-handed
- **Ket relationship:**
  - fled on his warning → the debt paid. Ket goes home.
  - forced him to attack → Ket becomes an enemy. The debt spent.
- **Murrat knowledge:**
  - the crew now knows what the cloud is, what Murrat defends, and that Ket survived

## Resolution Handoff

Determine: did anyone die? Did the crew flee or fight? Did they hear Ket's voice and recognize him? How far did they get before turning around? Those facts write the wake.

Large islands run east to west across the northern arc. Broad-shouldered land, forested highlands, and deep natural harbours sit behind pale stone fortworks built for cannon range. The Dravosi flag flies from every major anchorage. Ports here keep records and move cargo under law, unlike the [[midchain|Midchain]].

The reef systems between islands, the Greyteeth, have confused charts for three generations. Local pilots are worth what they charge. Patrols keep the big harbours safe, but not the whole sea. Pirates still work the water between reefs, under captains who know how long a distress signal takes to reach a fort.

> [!read-aloud]
> Grey stone forts crown every headland, cannon aimed out to sea, Dravosi colours snapping stiff in the wind off the water.
>

>

## Routes & Access

Ships bound in from the west first pass [[calders-tooth\|Calder's Tooth]], the Crown's gatekeeper island at the [[central-strait\|Central Strait]]'s mouth, before making the northern arc proper. Southern coasts here face the Strait, and northern coasts take open ocean swell.

An adult blue dragon lairs in the northern limestone reefs, using the Greyteeth's bad name to hide its own kills. Captains who know the safe route use specific flags and keep an open chest on deck, and survive runs that others lose to "wrecks" (the [[dravosi-crown\|Dravosi Crown]] does not officially acknowledge the arrangement). Harbourmasters know which stamp a cargo needs, and shore control does not make the Greyteeth themselves any safer.

The cold upwelling off the northern coast is the [[verdant-scatter\|Verdant Scatter]]'s best fishing water, worked year-round out of [[tidefall\|Port Tidefall]]'s [[south-harbour\|South Harbour]] under [[fort-crestwall\|Fort Crestwall]]'s guns. The Greyteeth's shallower channels also yield spiny lobster, sea urchin, and oyster beds.

Run the crossing per `vault/refs/vault/location/references/wilderness-travel-and-exploration.md` and `vault/refs/gameplay-toolbox.md` § Travel Pace.

## Hazards

Reef and hunter sharks off the southern coasts, giant crabs around wrecked reefs, and giant boars in the highland forest with [[giant-owl|Giant Owls]] hunting the same trees. Greyteeth navigation errors strand ships that trust the wrong chart, and the unacknowledged blue dragon in the northern reefs kills whatever a captain hasn't paid off.

> [!dm]
> The Greyteeth dragon arrangement: captains who use the right flags and keep an open chest on deck survive routes that others lose to "wrecks." The Crown does not acknowledge it. An adult blue dragon (lawful) and a pragmatic Crown make it work.

# Veth West Beach
> [!narration] Narration
> Veth West Beach is twenty strides of pale sand, then the ridge goes straight up. A living root-stair shines with traffic; there is no pier. Bone, resin, and pigments trade on the sand against food, timber, metal, and rope.


## Function
The only sanctioned Veth landing: a brief blue-caste exchange zone with no pier, roof, or inland permission.

## Routes and choices
- Anchor offshore and approach by small boat; deep draft is a wreck and an insult.
- Stay on the sand for bone, resin, and pigments exchanged for food, timber, metal, and rope.
- Follow the root-stair and the invitation ends; the forest begins on the second root.
- Retreat by boat or remain offshore; waiting preserves safety but gives the watch time to count the hull.

## Who is here
Blue go-betweens descend when a hull waits. Higher caste is color in the treeline, not public conversation. [[Kalowe]] pilots teach the wait-offshore rule.

## Clues
The beach's width is the protocol. No outsider print reaches the polished root-stair. A brown hole in the canopy, Widow-strangle, is a visible Crown-Taker landmark, not a climb invitation.

## Hazards
Reef chop makes landing hazardous. Leaving the sand or touching the stair breaks protocol and can draw hidden Grung daggers and poison. Do not add a public pier or town here.

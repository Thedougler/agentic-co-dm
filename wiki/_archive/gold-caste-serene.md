## Description

The Gold Caste Serene is the apex of [[grung-clans|Grung]] caste hierarchy, a living expression of what absolute certainty looks like from the outside. It hovers without effort or acknowledgment at the centre of the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]]'s most sacred ground, attended by hundreds of lower-caste believers whose unbroken faith sustains its divine power in real time. It does not fight. It does not negotiate. It issues Decrees, and reality accommodates them.

Small in body (amphibious, poisonous) and immeasurable by everything else.

## Ecology

The Gold Caste Serene stands apart from conventional ecology, drawing power from congregation alone. A social and magical system has been refining itself toward this for generations: a divine certainty-tap that grows stronger the more the lower castes believe in its inevitability.

Within the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]], that belief is total. The canopy itself responds to it. The jungle has grown around it the way growth forms around something that has been still and certain for a very long time.

Outside the Verdant Teeth (severed from proximity to its congregation), the tap starves. No lower caste holds this knowledge. See [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]] for the three-layer magic system and the Ossketh.

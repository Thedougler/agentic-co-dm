# Solange's Authority Seal

> [!read-aloud] A signet ring, red-caste work, made from metal heavier than gold. The face has worn smooth except for one deep-cut symbol that won't dull. It looks sharper than the rest, as if pressed into stone a thousand times.

*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires [[attunement|Attunement]]).*

| Field | Value |

Grung earn red-caste authority through action, never by vote or birth. A seal marks this authority, and the ring's compulsion is that same authority, made portable. It grants no separate control-magic. It reaches directly into the belief every Grung grows up trusting, that a red-caste order gets obeyed, and pulls on it. A Grung has spent a lifetime obeying that voice; a non-Grung has never heard it, and gives the ring nothing to catch hold of. Solange obtained this seal from its former wearer.

*Command* is a 1st-level spell. A wondrous item granting it at no slot cost is comparable to *Wand of the War Mage* or *[[ring-of-jumping\|Ring of Jumping]]* in raw spell-level terms. Compelling obedience from an intelligent, hostile creature sits closer to Circlet of Blasting, but the Grung-only restriction keeps it from climbing past Rare.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **Caste command.** As an action, speak a single word of command to target one Grung creature you can see within 60 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 15 [[wisdom|Wisdom]] saving throw. On a failure, it follows the command on its next turn, exactly as the *command* spell (PHB). Only Grung creatures respond to the seal. Once per long rest.

**Edge cases:** the seal still works on a creature immune to charmed or frightened (it imposes neither condition) unless immunity covers compulsion. It compels any Grung of red caste or lower (red, purple, blue, or green), everyone but the gold ruling caste. It won't work if another creature already controls the Grung (charmed, dominated).

**Limitations:** no effect on non-Grung, no bonus to attack, damage, saving throws, or AC. The target knows what happened. It's not hidden, so others may seek revenge.

## Provenance

Belonged to a red-caste officer in [[simone-tabarnack|Simone Tabarnack]]'s group before going to [[solange-barret|Solange Barret]]. No record shows whether she wore or carried it. Recovered from Primary Chamber rubble after the Detonation.

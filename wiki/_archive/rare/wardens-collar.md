# Warden's Collar

> [!read-aloud]
> A wide band of black hide in a glazed case, studded the whole way round with brass the size of thumbnails.
>
> Each stud carries a different mark, and the marks run in an order that does not repeat.
>
> The buckle has no tongue and no holes. The two ends simply want each other, and they close with a sound like a latch dropping.
>
> A paper tag hangs off the case on a thread, with a name inked on it that is not yours.

```meta-bind
VIEW[{reference_image}][image(class(reference-image-view))]
```

*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement).* The most expensive thing in [[the-live-hold|The Live Hold]], sold with the animal or not at all.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **[HB] Bind (8 hours).** Attune to the collar while it is buckled on a beast of CR 3 or lower. While the beast wears it and you are attuned, you can command it telepathically at up to 100 feet, and it obeys as though it were [[charmed|charmed]] by you. It acts on your initiative and takes one action of your choosing on its turn. You know its condition and rough direction while it lives.
>
> **Limitations:** one collar, one beast; rebinding to a different animal takes another 8 hours and ends the first bond. A beast with [[intelligence|Intelligence]] 5 or higher repeats a DC 15 [[wisdom|Wisdom]] saving throw at each dawn, and on a success the collar falls open and stays open for a day. The collar gives no command over anything that is not a beast.

## Provenance

Bought in from a [[vaelspire|Vaelspire]] estate sale. [[sabine-corvo|Sabine Corvo]] has already taken a deposit on it from a collector, and the tag on the case carries his name.

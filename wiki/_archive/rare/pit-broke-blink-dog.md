# Pit-Broke Blink Dog

> [!read-aloud]
> A dog the size of a wolfhound sits at the front of the high cage, sandy-coated, ears forward.
>
> Then it sits at the back of the cage, and nothing crossed the space between.
>
> The air where it stood carries a smell like a struck flint, and the straw there is still settling.
>
> It yips twice, close and then far, and both sounds arrive together.

*Tool (Trained Animal), Rare.* A [[blink-dog|blink dog]] taken young off a Mid-Chain shipment and worked for two years by [[zort|Zort]]'s hands until it answers a call instead of its own judgment. Medium fey, speed 40 ft. Its own kind will not have it back.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **[HB] Answers the Call.** The dog obeys a handler whose call it has learned over a week of work. It has the statistics of a blink dog, including its teleport, and it spends that teleport arriving beside its handler instead of escaping a fight. Once per short rest, when its handler is grappled, restrained, or below half hit points, the dog teleports to a space within 5 feet of them as a reaction.
>
> **[HB] Goes With You.** Once per long rest, when its handler teleports, the dog goes along if it stands within 5 feet, arriving in the nearest unoccupied space.
>
> **[HB] Speaks, and Judges.** The dog understands Sylvan and Blink Dog and speaks both. It will not obey an order it finds cruel, and it remembers who gave it.
>
> **Limitations:** one handler, and only one the dog chooses. [[zort|Zort]] refunds a buyer the dog will not take. It carries no rider and will not go into a fight alone, and the reaction teleport does nothing when the dog cannot see or hear its handler.

## Provenance

Sat in the back corner of [[zorts-pits|Zort's Pits]] for a year at a price nobody in [[calveno|Calveno]] has raised, which suits [[zort|Zort]]: the dog draws customers in from the canal walk and eats less than the cats.

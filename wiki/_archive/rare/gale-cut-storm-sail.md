## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **Gale-Cut Storm Sail [HB].** Bending the sail onto a mast takes 10 minutes and a crew of two. A vessel carrying it set and drawing keeps full travel speed and holds any course its helm can steer, whatever the wind does, in weather short of a named storm. Everyone aboard gains Advantage on checks to steer or hold that course.
>
> **Edge cases:** it works on any vessel from a ship's boat up to a two-masted trader, and on one mast at a time. In a dead calm it fills anyway. A second gale-cut sail on the same vessel adds nothing.
>
> **Limitations:** it drives the hull and nothing else. It turns no wave and sheds no rain, a hull breaking up under it breaks up anyway, and anyone on deck takes the weather in full. Furled or stowed it counts as ordinary canvas, and it grants nothing at all on land or below the surface. Cut or holed, it goes dead until a sailmaker who knows the herringbone puts it right.

## Provenance

It arrived at [[moretti-and-sons|Moretti & Sons]] in a crate whose papers are good and whose manifest lists something else entirely. [[giacomo-moretti|Giacomo]] told the [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] factor house about it three years ago and has been waiting for someone to come and claim it since. Until one of them does, the sail stays unsold, and he says so to anyone who asks the price.

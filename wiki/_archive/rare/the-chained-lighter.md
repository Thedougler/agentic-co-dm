# The Chained Lighter

> [!read-aloud]
> Brass the colour of a worn coin, thumbed smooth on both faces, riveted to a chain that runs through a hole bored in the board itself.
>
> The chain reaches a customer's hand and stops one inch short of anywhere else.
>
> Strike it and the flame comes up doubled, two tongues off one wick, leaning toward each other rather than up.
>
> Whoever lights a roll from it gets the smell of somebody else's blend for a moment, one that nobody standing here is burning.

**Rarity:** Pearl of Power gives back one spell slot a day off an attunement slot and sits at Uncommon. The Chained Lighter gives a second person a whole Uncommon consumable's effect, which is more than a 3rd-level slot in this city's economy, and it does it without an attunement slot. Ring of Spell Storing is the ceiling above it, holding five levels of stored spellcasting at once. This lands between them.

**PC connection:** every blend on this board covers exactly one smoker for one hour. The crew's weaknesses are shared, not individual, and the cheapest way to fix that is not two cigarettes but one flame. It is also the only reason to save toward something bolted to a board on the most-watched twenty feet in the city.

## Mechanics

**[HB]** Lighting an alchemical blend from the Chained Lighter's flame costs no more than lighting it any other way. Once per day, at the moment of that first breath, the smoker may name one willing creature within 5 feet who can see the flame. That creature gains the blend's full effect, for its full duration, without smoking anything.

The lighter recovers its use at dawn. Nothing marks whether it has been spent, so a customer who takes a second turn at it in one day simply gets an ordinary flame.

Edge cases:

- The named creature gains the effect only, never the cigarette. One roll still burns.
- A blend with no duration, thrown rather than smoked, carries nothing. There is nothing to share.
- The lighter cannot be carried off the board while the chain holds, and its flame works for anyone the chain reaches.

**Limitations:** it lights things and shares one blend a day, and that is the whole of it. It grants no bonus to any roll, extends no duration, cancels no stale batch, and produces no blend of its own.

## Provenance

The board is nine years old and the hole bored through it for the chain is older, so the lighter came to [[the-bridge|the Bridge]] before Nicco Kettley did. He has been offered money for it by two licensed rooms and has refused both without naming a counter-price to either.

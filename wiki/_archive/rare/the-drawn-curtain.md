# The Drawn Curtain

> [!read-aloud]
> The tube is lead, the length of a forearm, capped at both ends and heavier than anything that size has a right to be.
>
> Inside, on a bed of felt, one fat grey roll lies alone. No maker's mark anywhere on the paper, and someone folded both ends flat instead of twisting them shut.
>
> Lit, it puts out more smoke than it should, and the smoke does not drift. It builds against the walls and fills the corners first.
>
> By the time the tube is empty, the room past arm's length has gone soft at the edges, and the noise of the street outside has stopped arriving.

**Rarity:** this sits at Rare. Ring of Mind Shielding does the same job for one wearer, permanently, on an attunement slot, and it holds that tier. The Drawn Curtain widens the same cover to a whole room, and three limits keep it level with that ring instead of above it. The hour ends. The cigarette burns out with it. The smoke never stopped the door, so anyone who opens it hears the sentence in progress.

**PC connection:** every fight Delmar wins starts in a room, and in this city those rooms leak. An hour where no wall carries a word out is worth more to him than a weapon, and 1,100 gp keeps it out of reach until the crew picks it as the thing to save for.

## Mechanics

**[HB]** Lighting the Drawn Curtain takes an Action. The smoke fills a 20-foot cube centred on the lighting point and lasts 1 hour, or until the enclosed space opens to the outside air, whichever comes first.

While it holds, no sense and no [[divination|divination]] magic outside the cube reaches anything happening within it, and no [[scrying|scrying]] sensor forms inside it or sees into it. Creatures inside perceive one another normally. The area is [[lightly-obscured|lightly obscured]] for everyone in it.

Edge cases:

- Opening the space ends it. A door held open, a shuttered window thrown wide, or a wall breached lets the smoke go and the effect with it.
- A creature standing partly inside the cube perceives what its own senses reach and nothing more.
- The seal covers perception, never movement. Anyone may walk in, and what they see once inside is theirs.
- A divination cast from inside the cube reaches outside it without hindrance.

**Limitations:** the smoke stops no attack and blocks no spell cast through it. Beyond the light obscurement it grants no cover, and outdoors or in any space too large to fill it does nothing at all. A creature standing inside at the moment of lighting counts as inside for the whole duration, whoever they work for.

## Provenance

[[rufio-segalla|Rufio Segalla]] rolls this one himself and rolls it rarely, from base compound bought by standing order from [[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]] at [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]]. The lead tube does a job. The blend goes stale inside a day in anything else, so it never sits on a shelf, and it never reaches [[la-gatta|La Gatta]]'s board.

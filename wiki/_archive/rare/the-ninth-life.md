# The Ninth Life

> [!read-aloud]
> A single roll on its own card, sealed in a glass tube stoppered with grey wax, and the wax carries a press no counter in the city forges.
>
> The paper inside is cream and perfectly even, printed the length of it with a cat walking away from you.
>
> The tube is cold. It stays cold in a closed hand, and the cold goes on past the point where your fingers should have warmed it.
>
> A buyer's name is inked on the card in a hand that is not the board-keeper's, and the ink is old enough to have browned.

**Rarity:** Ring of Evasion is the ceiling this stays under. It does the same trick three times a day, forever, off an attunement slot, and it still only covers Dexterity. The Ninth Life covers any save, which is what pushes it past Uncommon, but it does so exactly once and then it is ash. Potion of Heroism is the price-and-scale match: a Rare consumable, an hour long, no attunement.

**PC connection:** every hard fight this crew has lost ground in was lost to a save, not to damage. A blend that promises one of those back is worth saving for, which is the only reason a board selling seconds keeps a Rare item in a glass tube where everyone on the tier can see it.

## Mechanics

**[HB]** Lighting the Ninth Life and taking the first breath costs an Action. For 1 hour afterward, the first time the smoker fails a saving throw, that saving throw succeeds instead. The cigarette's effect ends the instant it fires.

The Seconds discount and its stale-batch roll do not apply. This is a first run under seal, sold at full price, and it does what it does.

Edge cases:

- The success is a success, so any effect that only lands on a failure does not land, and any effect that halves on a success halves.
- It fires on the first failed save of any kind, chosen by nothing. A smoker cannot hold it back for a save they would rather spend it on.
- A second one lit inside the hour does nothing until the first has fired.

**Limitations:** it never touches an attack roll, an ability check, or [[death-saving-throws|death saving throws]], and it grants no bonus to any save it does not outright replace. Once it fires, the hour ends with it.

## Provenance

[[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]] seals her first runs herself and sells them only to licensed rooms, so one sitting on a vending board under [[the-bridge|the Bridge]] arrived by a route nobody has stated out loud. Nicco Kettley took it in against a debt and has been holding it for the name on the card ever since.

````col
## At a Glance

Beppe is the fear that keeps three survivors under the vine: he believes the island's protection stops where the clearing ends.

- **Role.** A survivor at [[the-pantry]] who stays.
- **Nature.** A slow, deliberate Calveno rope-maker in his middle years, frightened and certain.
- **Wants.** To stay under the vine, and to keep [[marco-lenzi]] there with him.
- **Home.** [[the-pantry]].
```

> [!narration] Beppe Sarti
> Beppe is a dark-haired man in his middle years with rope-maker's calluses thick across his palms. His left eye is clouded white from an old salt burn, and he keeps that side of his face turned toward the channel. A length of cord is always knotting between his fingers, faster whenever anyone talks about leaving.
````

## At the Table

- **First meeting.** He greets the party with water and fallen fruit, as Renzo taught him.
- **Opens up when.** Asked about the Pantry itself: which fruit falls when, where the water runs cleanest, how to tell a safe shelter from a place the island watches.
- **Shuts down when.** Asked about the crew he lost in the dark after the wreck.
- **Priority.** Staying.
- **Shares.** Everything about the Pantry. He lies if asked whether he is afraid of what lies outside it.
- **Voice.** Quiet and deliberate, with long pauses. He repeats himself when something matters.
- **Lines.** "The vine fed you. What feeds you out there?" · "No. Hinewai's garden ends at the clearing edge. I am certain of it." · "They will sicken. They will sicken and come back, and it will be too late."
- **If others leave.** He does not try to stop them. He knots cord through the night and watches them go as if seeing off the dead.

## Secrets

- **He is wrong about the edge.** [[hinewai]] counts Beppe as hers wherever he walks on Aruhe, and the island's responders would never touch him outside the clearing. His fear comes from the wreck, and he will not test it.

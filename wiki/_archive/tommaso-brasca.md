## At a Glance

Tommaso is the survivor who has been planning a way out all along, and his knowledge of the river is worth more on the walk back than his raft.

- **Role.** A survivor at [[the-pantry]], and the raft's builder.
- **Nature.** A young, talkative Calveno boatwright's son who measures everything with his hands.
- **Wants.** Out to the coast, with everyone who will come.
- **Home.** [[the-pantry]].

> [!narration] Tommaso Brasca
> Tommaso is a young man with sawdust-pale hair and knuckles scarred shiny from his father's steam box. A length of cord is always in his hands. He talks with them, measuring the air, and he sizes up everything he looks at by whether it would float.

## At the Table

- **First meeting.** He shows the party the raft within minutes, proud of every lashing, and asks how many are coming.
- **Opens up when.** The party offers a way to the ship. He is theirs at once.
- **Shuts down when.** Renzo speaks. He defers out loud and packs quietly anyway.
- **Priority.** The walk out, and everyone on it.
- **Shares.** Which river stretches the [[river-otter]] families own, and that you pass them midstream, by day, without stopping. That the raft floats because nothing in it was taken. He and Renzo teach the rules for living on Aruhe to anyone who asks.
- **Voice.** Fast and precise, full of measurements, and every sentence ends in a plan.
- **Lines.** "Nine on the raft if nobody fidgets. Who is coming?" · "No, not cut wood. Cut wood brings the vines." · "All of it fell. Nothing here was taken. That is why it floats and we are still alive."
- **The raft.** It holds nine and poles along the channel, but the coast is days away. When he leaves, he leaves it moored.

> [!narration] First meeting
> A young man with sawdust-pale hair is already walking toward you, a length of cord wound around his fist. "How many of you?" he asks, counting heads with his chin. "And how many are you taking?"

# The Antheri

*An ancient aquatic civilization that built downward into the western wall of [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]], reaching toward the [[elemental-plane-of-water|Elemental Plane of Water]], and vanished at the height of its power.*

## The Fact

At its height around -2000 DR, the Antheri built into the western wall of [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]]. The common salvage assumption gets it backward. They did not begin on the safe upper shelf and build downward: their oldest work sits at the bottom, anchored near the planar fissure that bleeds power from the Elemental Plane of Water. The [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]] salvagers see today are newer upper structures, exhaust systems for the deep engine below, not the city's origin point.

What's active right now: the ruin record shows rapid failure, not slow decline. The fissure had widened by the time the city grew up around it, and the strongest reading holds that the upper city seals exhaust infrastructure for a planar engine still running underneath. Salvagers picking apart the [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]] may be dismantling safety systems without understanding what they hold back.

Research connection: Catarina Da'Virelli is a Calveno artificer with established expertise in salvageable Antheri material culture.

### Origin

The Antheri began as surface nomads in the sunlit sea. A localized current collapse drove them downward toward hydrothermal vents and the remaining food chain. Chemosynthesis followed. Vent bacteria, tube worms, and magically sustained kelp gardens became their food chain, and their bodies adapted for pressure, darkness, and deep water. Following those vents deeper, they discovered the Maw itself, a fissure at the trench floor bleeding elemental power. Early attempts to tap it killed entire settlements, and their society reorganized around the people who could keep the city alive: architects, biomancers, and planar engineers.

No preserved record confirms exact Antheri appearance. The ruins imply humanoid bodies about 5-6 feet tall, narrower shoulders than a standard Human, five-fingered hands, and architecture with no stairs, walkways, air pockets, or pressure bulkheads, built for open-water movement in three dimensions. Their exact physical form stays a deliberate silence, below.

### The Vertical City

The city runs in three tiers by depth. The [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]], 60-200 feet down, hold the salvageable upper structures. Most of their recent damage comes from salvage activity and entity incursion, not the original collapse. The Mid-Works, 200-500 feet down, were the residential and research zone, better preserved than the shelf above. Antheri soul-pledge symbolic language appears integrated into the structure itself here, not applied as decoration. The Deep Works, past 500 feet, sit in boundary-zone conditions: pristine, singular-purpose structures holding the soul-pledge chamber and the fissure-oriented terminus of the whole city. Water breathing alone will not carry a diver that far. Elemental diffusion there forces ongoing [[constitution|Constitution]] saves and wears divers toward exhaustion. Spells falter, and instruments stop reading true.

The Antheri built with a corrosion-proof composite stone that neither fouls nor carries biological growth, worked in a bio-metallurgic alloy current metallurgy can't reproduce. Bioluminescent data channels, algae seeded into capillary-like alloy geometry, pulsed light through the city. Their aquaculture ran fast enough that deep kelp systems regrew harvestable mass within days. Their infrastructure lived and grew, pressure regulators built from sponge-like tissue, transit currents driven by stimulated cilia networks. They built soul-binding into their architecture too. Formal ritual spaces and a repeated symbolic vocabulary thread every level of the city, and [[auralis|Auralis]] is one known product of that tradition, not the whole of it.

### The Soul-Pledge

The soul-pledge chamber in the Deep Works predates [[auralis|Auralis]] and looks built for repeated use. The Antheri combined biomancy, soul-binding, and planar mechanics to extract a consciousness from a body and bind it into an artificial immortal framework. [[auralis|Auralis]] is not a natural deity. He is Antheri technology pushed to its endpoint, a manufactured guardian built to survive at the bottom of the trench and hold a specific purpose. Whether other soul-pledges remain active, still protecting things the crew of the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] hasn't reached, stays a deliberate silence, below.

### The Fall

The Antheri vanished in -495 DR. The ruin record supports rapid failure, not slow decline. The shelf structures stand intact and undamaged by any siege. The residential tier's workspaces rest exactly where hands left them, tools and half-finished tasks scattered mid-process. The record reads as a hurried evacuation, its ruins empty of the dead. Deep activity was still running when the collapse hit.

### What Current Civilizations Know

Sailors and townsfolk know old underwater ruins, valuable material, and dangerous diving. Nothing more. The [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]]'s salvage interests know the Antheri name and the basic layout of the [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]], not the depth or function of the full city. Scholars have no working public translation of Antheri symbolic language. Catarina Da'Virelli is the best living non-Antheri expert on salvageable Antheri material culture. The [[halythion|Halythion]] and [[antheri-ruins|Antheri Ruins]] remain the physical sites carrying this record forward.

The [[antheri-ruins|Antheri Ruins]] and [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]] itself are the physical sites carrying this record forward; [[campaign-timeline|Campaign Timeline]] places the Antheri's rise and fall against everything else that has happened in this world.

## Description

Jungle vine, shoulder-thick, hanging from the canopy like every other vine on [[aruhe|Aruhe]]. It does not move until something walks under it. Then it whips down, wraps, and squeezes. Bare of leaves, flowers, and fruit. Just vine, coiled through the canopy like rope in a ship's rigging, waiting.

## Ecology

The [[blight|Blight]]'s corruption animated what was already growing. Vine lashes are not transplants. They are the island's own vegetation turned predatory, feeding on blood and bone meal drawn through the root network. They grow thickest where the terrace stonework gives them anchor points and the canopy gives them height.

Home zones: [[aruhe-the-old-gardens|the Old Gardens]] into [[aruhe-the-quiet|the Quiet]]. Vine lash threads the collapsed terrace walls throughout. They cluster around game trails and water sources, hanging above the paths other creatures use.

# Rattkin Culture

*[[rattkin|Rattkin]] society, origins, varieties, and in-group vocabulary within the [[the-passage|Passage]] network.*

## The Culture

Small humanoids calibrated for getting into places they are not supposed to be. A locked cargo hold is a delay, and a drainage pipe is simply a road; give a Rattkin crew a ship's bilge and it becomes a neighborhood.

[[rattkin|Rattkin]] are the oldest continuous presence in every major [[campaign-overview|Shattered Sea]] port. Their fixed origin point is [[warren|the Warren]], and their living network is [[the-passage|The Passage]]. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] is a fancy-rat Rattkin PC from the [[calveno|Calveno]] Run.

Rattkin fall into three varieties, each tied to a functional role within a Run:

| Variety | Role |
|---|---|
| Black Rat | Climbers, riggers, roofline workers, elevated port infrastructure |
| Brown Rat | Burrowers, Hold-maintainers, sewer and bilge route mappers |
| Fancy Rat | Social infiltrators and long-term outside contacts |

This is active world-texture pulling on [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]]'s thread: his Run affiliation and in-group standing are live material for his PC arc.

## Naming

In-group vocabulary, used across [[the-passage|The Passage]] network:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Kin | Other Rattkin; the in-group |
| Dry | Non-Rattkin |
| Run | A Rattkin community network in a port or city |
| Hold | A Passage waypoint or safe house |

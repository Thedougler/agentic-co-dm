# Kalowe's Frozen Council

*Delmar's return thaws the Chain Council's deadlock and gives every faction a living target.*

## Premise

The [[chain-council|Chain Council]] has held its Pearl commission inside a closed room since [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]] sank. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] is no longer a dead admiral the Council can explain at a distance. His arrival at [[kalowe|Kalowe]] forces its members to settle their positions.

## Pressure

Council members have begun choosing sides. One bloc wants Delmar dead in every practical sense. Once the nine seats agree what Kalowe will say, they can erase the surviving mail record and set the shrine tribute to match their account.

## Actors

- **[[chain-council|The Chain Council]]:** preserves island independence and keeps its sponsorship buried. It prepares a closed-session account.
- **[[tessarine-concordat|The Tessarine Concordat]]:** protects its factor's cover. It buys or re-terms anyone following the mail route.
- **[[waveservants|The Waveservants]]:** collect the sea's due. Kalowe's reef-gap shrine puts every arriving hull within reach.

## Discoverable / Affectable

- Council votes, [[detto-kalash|Detto Kalash]]'s mail route, and the shrine's changing tribute expose different parts of the pressure.
- The [[fleet-commanders-chair|Fleet Commander's Chair]] gives its five captains leverage through old debts, votes, and betrayals.
- **PC gravity:** [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] wants the Pearl's souls freed and a fleet rebuilt. Kalowe can turn his old commission into blame or give him leverage over new crews and hulls.

## If Ignored

Detto's mail record disappears. The Council announces that bad intelligence doomed Fisk's Fleet, lets Delmar's name carry the loss, and raises ordinary tribute at the [[kalowe-waveservant-shrine|Waveservant Shrine]] to keep divine scrutiny outside the meeting room.

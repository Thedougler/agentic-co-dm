# The Grandfather Hole

A legendary underwater sinkhole somewhere in the eastern [[midchain|Midchain]], avoided by every experienced reef diver out of [[kalowe|Kalowe]] and feared in port taverns throughout the region.

## The Fact

Midchain reef divers know to mark and avoid [[sawek|Sawek]] lairs. But tavern tales speak of a single, massive sinkhole where the water turns an unnatural, freezing black. The sea around it drops temperature faster than any natural current can account for.

The Grandfather Hole is territory claimed by an ancient [[sawek|Sawek]] large enough to pull a Tier 3 galleon down by the keel. At the bottom lies a century of undisturbed shipwrecks, a tangled, rotting reef of hulls and cargo threaded with scattered gold accumulated over generations of the beast's predation. The ambient magic of the Blue Hole has animated the drowned sailors trapped within the wreck-reef, so any salvagers must contend with the beast above and undead crews below.

No reliable charting of the sinkhole's location exists. Surviving or bypassing the ancient Sawek presents mortal danger. Fighting through animated undead sailors in lightless, freezing water remains treacherous. No one has ever recorded a successful salvage.

No one has revealed this legend at the table, and it remains speculative.

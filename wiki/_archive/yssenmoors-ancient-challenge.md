# Yssenmoor's Ancient Challenge

*A duck built like a demigod guards a fog-bound island in the [[midchain-north|Northern Midchain]], challenging every visitor with absurd formality. Nobody has answered in years. The ley line beneath his hallowed ground pulls on the Fate Spinner as the party sails past.*

## Actors

- **[[sir-quackers-the-fowl|Sir Quackers the Fowl]]** — wants to protect Yssenmoor and anything weak enough to need it. Ships have stopped answering his challenge. He has started reciting it to empty water and is one silence from stopping the ceremony entirely and withdrawing into the moor.
- **[[the-countless|The Countless]]** — hired watchers sweep [[midchain|the Midchain]] for any Sentinel carrying a [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]]. A visible magical reaction near Yssenmoor is a signal their network exists to detect.

## Connections

- [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]] — the artifact reacts to the ley line beneath Yssenmoor: old magic older than the Sentinels' doctrine of non-intervention
- [[yssenmoor|Yssenmoor]] — the island itself, fog-bound, hallowed ground under Quackers' unconscious protection
- [[the-countless|The Countless]] — the Long Sight Hunt Front, active, sweeping for the Spinner
- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]] — carries the Spinner. [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]] just warned her to hide it
- [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|The Schism's Third Name]] — the Sentinel thread this feeds into: old power predating the order's non-intervention doctrine

## Beat Chart

### Dramatic Question

Will the crew answer the challenge of a guardian nobody has answered in years, and what does his island's old power mean for what Crissdalynn carries?

### Player Gravity

- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] — the Fate Spinner warms as they pass. Kyzil just warned her to hide it. Now it's pulling toward something.
- [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] — old power that predates colonial authority and organized faith. The ley line is not his patron, but it recognizes the same deep water.
- [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] — a creature freed from servitude who built something worth defending from that freedom. Quackers' story mirrors JC's own departure from the clans.
- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] — a demigod-tier potential ally holding unclaimed territory in [[midchain|the Midchain]]. Delmar reads the world as fleet and force. Quackers is both.

### Current State

- The *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]* sails the Northern Midchain heading south. [[yssenmoor|Yssenmoor]] is one of many tightly packed islands they pass.
- Permanent fog sits unmoving over the island in a wind that fills the sails. Sound arrives ahead of its source.
- A voice carries across the water from inside the fog: formal, patient, addressed to no one.
- The [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]] warms in [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn's]] pouch as they draw near.

### Active Beat

None (lifecycle: discoverable).

### Beat Spine

- **The Pull** — the Spinner warms, the fog is visible and wrong, the voice carries across the water. The crew can investigate or sail past.
- **The Fog** — landing on Yssenmoor. Sound precedes its source. Fog sits without wind, bog and reed in every low stretch. [[stirge|Stirges]], [[boar|boar]], and [[venomous-snake|snakes]] in the scrub.
- **The Challenge** — Quackers appears and delivers his formal recitation. State your name, your business, and whether you mean to leave this marsh the way you found it. His [[detect-evil-and-good|Divine Awareness]] detects lies. Honest answers earn conversation. Refusal earns a second ask, patient and formal, and then he lets them go. He does not fight over ceremony.
- **The Conversation** — if the crew answers plainly, Quackers stands down. The first answer he has received in longer than he can recall. Ask about RTLB and he lights up: two gnomes in a trenchcoat who mastered duomancy, tamed faeries, rescued the dragon clans, and remain the greatest wizard Quackers has ever known. He recites every title proudly and lands on the same quiet place every time: RTLB cried whenever Quackers came back from dying, and Quackers never worked out whether that made the sending worse or better.
- **The Ley Line** — what the Spinner reacted to. The power beneath Yssenmoor predates the Sentinels' current doctrine. Quackers absorbed it without knowing. Crissdalynn carries an artifact from the same old tradition. Neither of them asked for what they hold.

### Live Branches

- **The crew threatens Yssenmoor or its residents** → Quackers defends his ground. CR 18 on hallowed ground with lair actions. The party flees or dies.
- **The crew refuses the challenge or lies** → Quackers lets them go. Quiet disappointment. Another ship that did not answer. He resumes reciting to empty water.
- **The Countless detect the Spinner's reaction** → a watcher reports. The Long Sight Hunt gains a new vector. [[talon-vantyrus|Vantyrus]] learns the Spinner is active in the Northern Midchain.

### Climax Readiness

- **Question** — ready. Will the crew engage with a demigod they cannot defeat and cannot easily dismiss?
- **Investment** — ready. The Spinner, the Sentinel thread, JC's mirror, Delmar's strategic eye.
- **Opposition** — missing. Quackers is non-violent unless his island or its residents are directly threatened; the tension is social and spiritual, not combative.
- **Understanding** — missing. The crew does not yet know what Quackers is, what the ley line means, or why the Spinner reacted.
- **Leverage** — missing. No prior relationship exists. Honesty is the only currency Quackers accepts.
- **Stakes** — ready. A demigod ally or a demigod hazard. The Spinner's visibility to the Countless.
- **Possibility** — ready. Alliance, indifference, hostility, flight, and deeper investigation all remain viable.
- **Convergence** — missing. The crew must choose to land on Yssenmoor.

### Unused Possibilities

- **RTLB's departure** — Quackers' former master vanished. If the crew asks the right questions, Quackers might share what RTLB left behind besides the island. A hook that stays dormant until a future season makes it relevant.
- **Yssenmoor as safe harbor** — hallowed ground in the Northern Midchain, fog-concealed, guarded by a demigod. If the crew earns Quackers' trust, they gain a bolt-hole nobody else knows about.
- **The ley line's deeper network** — whether the line beneath Yssenmoor connects to anything else in [[verdant-scatter|the Scatter]]. Dormant until Season 3 or 4 makes it relevant.

# The Fishwife

> [!read-aloud]
> A long arrow, fletched grey, with a head of pierced tin worked into a shape like a small closed lantern.
>
> Holes run its whole length in two spiralling rows, and each hole is a different size.
>
> Blow across it and it answers with a note that is almost a word. The tin is warm.
>
> Somebody has scratched a name into the shaft and then scratched it out again.
>
> Held level it hums on its own, faintly, off the draught coming under the shop door.

_[[weapon|Weapon]] (Arrow), Uncommon._

Every Calveno household knows the sound this one makes, which is the point of it. [[ettore-lupo|Ettore Lupo]] keeps it in the drawer built into his counter at [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]], and sells it to people who will not say what for.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **The Fishwife [HB].** Consumable, one shot. Loose it at a point, surface, or creature within the weapon's normal range. On landing, the tin head opens and shrieks in a woman's voice, wordless and rising, for 1 minute.
>
> The scream carries at full volume to 300 feet. Between there and half a mile it thins to distant screaming. Nothing reaches past half a mile. While it sounds, every creature within 30 feet of it has disadvantage on [[wisdom|Wisdom]] (Perception) checks that rely on hearing, and no shout inside that radius rises above the noise.
>
> The noise stops early only if a creature spends an Action to crush the head underfoot or under a hand, or if the arrow goes into deep water.
>
> **Edge cases:** on a hit against a creature, it deals 1d6 piercing and keeps screaming from wherever it lodges. The creature now has to carry that problem around. Enclosed rooms muffle the sound to about 60 feet. It sounds identical whether it lands on stone, timber, cloth, or a person.
>
> **Limitations:** it deals no thunder damage and breaks no concentration. It deafens nobody and compels nobody to investigate, just noise moving through the room. What the city does about a woman screaming is up to the city.

## Provenance

[[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]] presses the tin heads at [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]], using shafts cut from [[savia-brentino|Savia Brentino]]'s offcut pile at [[carpenters-slip|Carpenters' Slip]]. She has never explained where the voice in them comes from, or to whom it once belonged. [[ettore-lupo|Ettore Lupo]] sells them at [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]] without comment. He prices them the same as he did eleven years ago.

[[prospero-morsani|Prospero Morsani]] also had a quiver of these on his shelves at [[cabinet-of-morsani|the Cabinet of Morsani]], Session 8, offered to the crew for near 100 gold. Confirmed: [[transcript]].

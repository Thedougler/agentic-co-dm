# Wakerock Salts

> [!read-aloud]
> A tin the width of two fingers, lid stiff with dried salt, packed to the brim with grey crystal ground down to the coarseness of wet sand.
>
> Prising the lid puts a smell into the air like ammonia off a bilge, and it carries the length of a galley.
>
> The surface holds three shallow scoops, and somebody has already taken one of them.
>
> The underside of the tin is warm, warmer than the shelf it came off.

*Consumable (Salts), Uncommon.* Cut grey out of the vent rock under [[kalowe|Kalowe]] and mixed with clove to keep it from caking. Bosuns keep a tin in the [[galley]] for the man who will not come up off a dead watch, and captains buy them by the crate before a long crossing.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **Wakerock Salts [HB].** A tin holds three doses. As a Magic action, hold an open dose under the nose of a willing or incapacitated creature within 5 feet. That dose goes flat at once, and the creature ends the charmed or frightened condition on itself and wakes from any sleep, magical or ordinary.
>
> **Edge cases:** an unwilling creature that is neither charmed nor frightened refuses with no roll. A creature charmed twice within an hour by the same effect gets nothing from the second dose.
>
> **Limitations:** it restores no hit points and ends no other condition. It does nothing for a creature at 0 hit points, and nothing at all past arm's length. An open tin left without its lid goes flat by the end of the day.

## Provenance

The vent rock surfaces whenever the dry-dock crews dredge a berth out, and the waterfront trade tins the salts in its own back rooms. [[suryo-wibowo|Suryo Wibowo]] sells more of them in the week before a Deep crossing than in the rest of the month together.

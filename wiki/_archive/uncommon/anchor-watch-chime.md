# Anchor-Watch Chime

> [!read-aloud]
> A brass chime the size of a fist, the clapper wired short so it cannot swing on its own.
>
> A watch-bill runs scratched around the rim in four different hands, and somebody has struck a line through one of the names.
>
> Set it on wood and it takes the grain like a fitting, and lifting it off again needs a real pull.
>
> Nothing rings. The clapper has moved anyway, and hangs toward the loading door.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **Anchor-Watch Chime [HB].** Setting the chime on a surface that carries no motion of its own takes 1 minute. It holds until a creature lifts it or until the next dawn. From then, the first time a creature the setter has not already seen comes within 60 feet, the chime rings once, loud enough to wake sleepers and audible 300 feet off. Every creature within 30 feet that hears it escapes the Surprised condition in an encounter beginning in the next minute.
>
> **Edge cases:** it rings for a creature that goes [[invisible|Invisible]], hides, or arrives underwater. A ship at anchor counts as still, and one under way carries too much motion for it. It rings once a setting, and a reset costs the full minute again.
>
> **Limitations:** it grants no bonus to Initiative, Perception, or any other roll, names nothing about what woke it, and points in no direction. A creature, a held object, and a vehicle with way on it all refuse it. A second chime within the same 60 feet stays silent while the first one holds.

## Provenance

Cast on [[calven|Calven]] for the anchor watches Crown captains must keep in crowded water, and sold through the trade to anyone who asks. [[giacomo-moretti|Giacomo]] takes them two dozen at a time and prices them at the founder's rate plus his own margin, the same as everything else on his shelves.

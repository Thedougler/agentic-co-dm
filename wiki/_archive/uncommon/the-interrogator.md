# The Interrogator

> [!read-aloud]
> The cigarette sits thin and straight between two fingers, its paper wound tighter than the cheap rolls sold at the corner stalls.
>
> A band of grey wax seals one end, the maker's only mark, warm and slightly tacky under your thumb.
>
> Under the tobacco runs a sweetish, almost bitter smell. Like scorched citrus peel.
>
> The first drag catches slow, and the smoke doesn't rise straight up, leaning and curling sideways across the room toward someone who hasn't moved.

_Consumable, Uncommon._

One of six blends sold as alchemical cigarettes made for smoking in [[calveno|Calveno]]. Named for what it does at the table, never for what it looks like.

**Rarity justification:** uncommon, comparable to a spell scroll of [[zone-of-truth|Zone of Truth]] and a spell scroll of [[detect-thoughts|Detect Thoughts]], both 2nd-level spells scribed as Uncommon scrolls with a single use and unattuned casting. [[medallion-of-thoughts|Medallion of Thoughts]], a permanent Uncommon item that reads surface thoughts outright, sets this tier's ceiling. This consumable's narrower one-shot lie detection matches that tier already.

At the table, this blend serves one specific story beat: catching a liar.

**PC connection:** [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] is a hidden [[grung|Grung]] traitor moving through a city of brokers. The thing that kills him is trusting the wrong handler's word. This blend tells him when a contact is lying to his face, and never tells him what they're hiding.

## Mechanics

**[HB]** Lighting the cigarette takes an Action. For the next 10 minutes, the smoker knows the moment a creature they can both see and hear knowingly lies to them, and learns nothing else.

Belief changes the reading. A speaker who believes their own false statement reads honest. A technically true but misleading statement reads the same way.

A creature immune to the [[charmed|charmed]] condition gives no reading at all, honest or otherwise.

The cigarette burns out at the end of the 10 minutes, or the smoker snuffs it out early. Either way it can't be lit again. Single use, consumable.

**Limitations:** the smoke offers no saving throw at all. It only catches sight and hearing, so a lie from outside the room, or from a creature the smoker can't perceive, passes through clean.

Belief is also a blind spot. A speaker who believes their own lie reads honest. The same is true of one who stays technically accurate while misleading. A creature immune to the charmed condition gives no reading at all, so silence from the smoke never proves an honest room.

## Provenance

[[rufio-segalla|Rufio Segalla]] finishes every blend himself in [[la-brace|La Brace]]'s back room. He buys the base compound by standing order from [[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]] at [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]] in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]].

# Fen-Glass Newt

> [!read-aloud]
> A newt the length of a thumb hangs in a jar of canal water, the colour of wet glass all the way through.
>
> Its ribs and the dark thread of its spine show plainly, and the water carries the flat green smell of a still canal.
>
> It turns to face whoever speaks, every time, without hurry.
>
> Tap the jar and it opens its mouth wide, and nothing comes out.

*Tool (Trained Animal), Uncommon.* A marsh amphibian taken from the deep fen channels past [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]], sold in its own jar with a fortnight of feed. It lives about two years in captivity, and it needs canal water, not clean water. Jar and newt weigh 5 pounds.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **[HB] Shriek.** While you carry the newt's jar on your person, the newt shrieks the instant a creature attempts to charm or frighten you. When you fail a saving throw against being charmed or frightened, the shriek lets you reroll that saving throw once. You must use the new roll. Once the shriek has bought a reroll, it buys no other until the newt has had a full day in still water.
>
> **[HB] Clear Water Tell.** The newt goes milk-white and stops moving while a creature within 30 feet of it is charmed or is maintaining concentration on an enchantment. Anyone looking at the jar sees it.
>
> **Limitations:** the jar has to hang where you can hear it, so a newt buried in a pack at the bottom of a hold does nothing. The shriek carries about 100 feet and does not care who else is listening. A newt out of water dies in an hour.

## Provenance

[[zort|Zort]] keeps them on the desk at [[zorts-pits|Zort's Pits]] because they are the only stock that sells itself: a customer speaks, and the newt turns to look at them.

# Salt-Drake Bile Draught

> [!read-aloud]
> A flat green bottle, the liquid inside so dark it reads black until the light goes through the shoulder of the glass.
>
> It is cold. It stays cold in a warm hand, and the cold comes up the arm.
>
> Tilt it and the draught moves slower than water, clinging to the glass on the way down.
>
> The stopper is a plug of drake hide, and it has begun to swell.

```meta-bind
VIEW[{reference_image}][image(class(reference-image-view))]
```

*Consumable, Uncommon.* Pressed from the bile of salt-drake hatchlings that did not survive the crossing from the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]].

## Mechanics

**[HB] Drink (bonus action).** For 10 minutes you have advantage on [[wisdom|Wisdom]] saving throws, and you cannot be [[frightened|frightened]]. Your blood runs cold with it: your speed drops by 10 feet for the duration, and you have disadvantage on [[charisma|Charisma]] checks made to persuade or perform, because nothing reaches your face.

**Limitations:** one dose, no stacking, and a second dose inside 24 hours does nothing but the speed penalty. It does not touch [[intelligence|Intelligence]] or Charisma saving throws.

## Provenance

Pressed and bottled by [[sabine-corvo|Sabine Corvo]], who does not waste stock that dies in her cages.

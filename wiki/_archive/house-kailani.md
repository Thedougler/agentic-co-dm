# House Kailani

*Controls [[sparhold|Sparhold]] through the settlement's defense: masters of spar-timber trade, holding the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]]'s shipbuilding supply contract, but now facing a death-duel challenge for the settlement's rule.*

## Members

[[toa-kailani|Toa Kailani]] leads the House and holds [[sparhold|Sparhold]]'s rule through control of the settlement's watch. He keeps tallies of every family's watch failures as leverage against rival claims, and has chosen [[rangi-kailani|Rangi Kailani]] as House Kailani's champion in the death-duel challenge against [[house-renzetti|House Renzetti]]. The family's claim to rule depends entirely on their champion's victory in the coming fight. Loss means exile or vassalage, and Toa knows it.

## Goals & Fronts

Standing goal: maintain control of [[sparhold|Sparhold]]'s spar-timber trade and the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] supply contract that justifies the settlement's independence.

### Front: Challenge Bell

**Lifecycle:** active. **Trigger conditions:** already triggered. [[house-renzetti|House Renzetti]] rang the challenge bell against House Kailani's rule.

**Aim:** defend House Kailani's hold on [[sparhold|Sparhold]] and its rule. **Approach:** Toa has positioned [[rangi-kailani|Rangi Kailani]], a disciplined watch-trained duelist, as House Kailani's champion. **Off-screen move if unopposed:** the duel runs to its conclusion in [[sparhold|Sparhold]]'s betting ring, and House Renzetti assumes control.

**Clock:** 3 segments (fast-moving, a single duel). **Consequence at fill:** if Rangi falls, House Renzetti assumes control of [[sparhold|Sparhold]]'s watch and rule. House Kailani loses the settlement, the timber yards, and the [[dravosi-crown|Crown]] contract; Toa and his house face exile, vassalage, or destruction.

**Possible outcomes:** (a) Rangi defeats his opponent and House Kailani's rule stands. (b) Rangi falls and House Renzetti takes [[sparhold|Sparhold]]. (c) Outside intervention shifts the challenge's outcome through force, magic, or leverage.

**PC connection:** the duel is on the books and the bell can toll at any hour, so anyone tying up at [[sparhold|Sparhold]]'s dock arrives while the succession is still open. Whichever family holds the settlement holds the watch, the timber yards, and the answer to any request put to the town.

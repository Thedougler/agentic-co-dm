# The Overland Track

*The muddy southern switchback road crossing [[calders-tooth|Calder's Tooth]] between [[south-harbour|South Harbour]] and [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]], the Dravosi Crown's official toll route.*

## Path

The Overland Track is the muddy southern switchback road crossing [[calders-tooth|Calder's Tooth]] between [[south-harbour|South Harbour]] and [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]]. It breaks [[wagon]]s. The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] collects tolls here and taxes every crossing, toll-gated at both ends; every crossing lets the Crown count bodies, cargo, and coin. Locals call it the toll road.

The road climbs switchbacks through wet interior ridge country, then descends toward the opposite coast. Corduroy logs bridge the worst mud. The terrain breaks axles and wears out crews.

**Southern Switchbacks.** Muddy climbs where pack animals slow and [[wagon]]s become vulnerable targets.

**Corduroy Mud Sections.** Log-laid stretches bridge the worst mud. They fail when a [[wagon]] breaks an axle on them.

**Drainage Culverts.** Old stone boxes shelter giant wolf spiders. Track maintenance crews carry poles to clear them.

[[the-passage|The Passage]] runs a hidden dry-route through limestone caves beneath the Overland Track. Crown patrols and toll stations don't see it. Full route detail lives on that page.

North of the track lies [[high-line|High Line]]; south lies [[midchain|The Midchain]].

## Travel

The crossing takes a day and a half on foot or by mount in good weather. The worst weather makes it impassable.

## Hazards

Giant axe beaks hunt the ridge country. Pack animals slow in mud. Wagons stall at switchbacks. The ridge gives the birds room to pace a caravan before diving. Giant boars work the wetter sections toward interior scrub. Crown track crews flag fresh wallow signs.

Wagon crews and Crown patrols alike pay for the crossing: mud, broken axles, toll delays, switchback choke points, giant axe beak ambushes, boar sounders, and wolf spiders in the drainage culverts.

Roll on [[table-calders-tooth-wilds|Calder's Tooth Wilds]] for what crosses the party's path.

## Hooks

Mud, broken axles, Crown toll stops, and wild animals block interior travel between [[south-harbour|South Harbour]] and [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]]. Switchback choke points make the road a reliable obstacle for any overland trip across [[calders-tooth|Calder's Tooth]].

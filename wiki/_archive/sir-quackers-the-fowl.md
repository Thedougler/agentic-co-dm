# Sir Quackers the Fowl

[[sir-quackers-the-fowl|Quackers]] was the familiar of a great wizard known only as RTLB. A familiar's life is a hard one: Quackers scouted ahead, walked into traps meant for others, and died to them — over and over, reformed each time by RTLB. A familiar's "immortality" is a curse as much as a blessing: it always comes back, but it remembers every death.

Eventually RTLB set Quackers free — gave him his own life, will, and sentience — and found him an idyllic island in the [[midchain|Midchain]] to call home. RTLB is gone now, dead or vanished, whereabouts unknown; the relationship stays unresolved backstory, not a returning thread.

Complicated feelings toward RTLB: gratitude for the life he was given, and resentment for being sent to die again and again — set against RTLB crying in relief every time Quackers came back.

---

The island held a hidden ley line. Over centuries, its power diffused into Quackers — the same way an archfey slowly becomes something close to a god given enough time and power. His heart and desire to protect are pure and honest, and that purity, fed by centuries of latent power, has edged him into something close to a demigod. He doesn't consider himself one, and doesn't realize it's true. (Hercules myth inspiration: a mortal elevated by trial and deed, without ever claiming the title.) The only hint anything's really going on: his island has become hallowed ground under his own unconscious protection.

He eventually declared himself Sir Quackers the Fowl, Blade of the Wetlands, Guardian of the Marsh, and Defender of the Weak.

---

Role at the table: primarily a bizarre potential ally/patron, recruitable if the party approaches him right. Cross him, and it's a fight they almost certainly have to flee rather than win.

Mechanically: a blended [[solar|Solar]]/[[planetar|Planetar]] statblock, power level between the two (Planetar ≈ CR 16, Solar ≈ CR 21) — that power level itself *is* the demigod status; ordinary things don't have that much power.

Home: [[yssenmoor|Yssenmoor]] — currently a bare draft page (unclaimed, [[midchain-north|Northern Midchain]], permanent fog, sound arriving before its source). That "the voice arrives before the person" quality fits a guardian who's died and come back more times than anyone's counted. Page to be expanded with the ley line and Quackers as resident, hallowed-ground status included.

To build: Sir Quackers as an NPC, his full combat statblock (a blended Solar/Planetar-tier threat), and Yssenmoor's expansion as his island home and hallowed ground.

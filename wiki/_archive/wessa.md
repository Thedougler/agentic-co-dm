# Wessa

Cook aboard the Saltwright. She works under [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]] alongside crew members [[drav-holke|Drav Holke]], [[lenne-vor|Lenne Vor]], and [[fen|Fen]].

Stocky and grey-haired, she cooks so well that the crew eats better than it should.

**Roleplay anchor:** impossible standards without theatrics. The food appears correct.

## Relationships

- [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]], captain of the [[saltwright|Saltwright]] and her employer.
- [[drav-holke|Drav Holke]], [[lenne-vor|Lenne Vor]], [[fen|Fen]], fellow Saltwright crew members of [[beaumonts-crew|Beaumont's Crew]].
- [[saltwright|Saltwright]], the ship she crews.

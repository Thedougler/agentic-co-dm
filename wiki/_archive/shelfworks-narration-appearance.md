> [!narration]
> *Marker buoys and dive lines stitch the open water. Salvage hulls sit still over a shelf so clear the depth lies. Forty feet down, pale [[antheri|Antheri]] stone takes the light. Walls and archways stand intact. Fish move through rooms. Coral covers the lintels. Small reef sharks claim the halls.*
>
> *Air-pump barges knock against their own lines. The water coming up the buckets smells of dead fish and hot iron. Warmth rises with every fathom.*
>
> *Twenty metres east of the last visible wall the ruins drop away. The water darkens to a directionless blue with no floor in it. Experienced salvagers keep their eyes on the boats.*

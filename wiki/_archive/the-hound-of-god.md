# The Hound of God

*The Dravosi Crown keeps a man in reserve for heresy. He has already decided to act.*

## Actors

- [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] — wants [[shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] dead, is already hunting him on his own authority, has partial knowledge of the trail (the ship, the port, not yet the crew). Pronounces what he has learned aloud with scripture-cadenced certainty. Does not interrogate. Does not negotiate.

## Connections

- [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] owns the NPC page, statblock, Front clock, and relationships.
- [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] owns the quarry's own page, CR 19 [[hierarch|Hierarch]] statblock, and the [[khlysty|Khlysty]] thread he advances.
- [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] owns the institutional authority that holds Malone's leash. The leash is long by design: [[sarns-landing|Sarn's Landing]] taught the Crown what happens when the leash is short.
- [[blackrule|Blackrule]] owns the chapterhouse Malone returns to between hunts.
- [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] carries the dormant heresy thread — "Answer the Next Confirmed Heresy" waits on the Crown confirming his unwitting pact with [[auralis|Auralis]].

## Beat Chart

### Dramatic Question

What lengths will the Crown go to in pursuit of their goals?

### Player Gravity

- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] — on record as the fleet's admiral, a name the Crown could weigh; the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] IS the captured Dravosi cutter, and exposure is existential
- [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] — Malone's dormant thread waits on the Crown confirming Perrin's pact as heresy; standing on a deck under an inquisitor's read is standing next to that trigger
- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]], [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] — sailed with Grigori and dropped him in [[calveno|Calveno]]; a direct question about the Shepherd is a direct question about what they did

### Current State

- Malone is actively hunting Grigori through [[midchain|Midchain]] noble courts (The Hound of God, 0/4 fast clock).
- He has pieces of Grigori's trail — the port, the ship class — but has not connected them to the Uncertainty's crew.
- The crew transported Grigori and dropped him in Calveno. Malone does not know this yet.
- The crew fears the Dravosi connection to the [[hcs-surety|Surety]] theft and the death of [[capn-gorgeous|Rupert Knighton's]] heir. Malone does not care about ships or heirs. He hunts heretics, not thieves.

### Live Branches

Single direction until the Grigori fork forces a choice. The crew sides with the Shepherd or helps kill him. Walking away is also an answer: Malone reaches Grigori alone, and what he does with that is not gentle. The fork arrives through the [[khlysty|Khlysty]] thread or through Malone's own demand, whichever surfaces first. Either transforms the situation.

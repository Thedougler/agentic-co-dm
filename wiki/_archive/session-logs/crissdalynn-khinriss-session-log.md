## Arc Threads

- **Kyzil's approval, earned in the open air (S03 → S04).** S03: reunion with [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] ends in her breaking down after he dives away from her. S04: the rooftop spar reverses it — she grapples him R1, he acknowledges her growth, and tells her the pilgrimage was the hardest in Eyrie history. One session's rupture is the next session's repair.
- **The party's established grappler (S02 → S03 → S04).** S02: blocks [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] from killing Ket. S03: lifts a 60-ft whip shark clean out of the water on a contested check. S04: grapples Kyzil, clotheslines a [[grung|Grung]] patrol, and grapples-and-carries the fleeing purple Grung mid-flight. Physical control is her recurring answer across every session logged so far.
- **Protecting Delmar, on her own terms (S01 → S04).** S01: takes the deck breach because Delmar launches her through the hatch first. S04: leg-sweeps him for going to [[umberlee|Umberlee]] without her — "My burden was to carry you out of that stupid water." The protection runs both directions, and she keeps having to reassert it.

> [!narration]
> *The Blue Lane is unmarked water. Five hundred miles of the [[central-strait|Central Strait]] with no reef line, no narrows, and no shore. The Scatter Current runs east through it, a half-knot a hull can feel in the tiller. Winds blow northeast to southwest.*
>
> *For days the horizon holds only weather and other sails. A ship that comes to grief here has no nearby beach to reach. The fastest water is also the water anyone looking for a particular hull looks at first.*

# Verdant Scatter

![[verdant-scatter-banner]]

The Verdant Scatter is the main island chain of the [[campaign-overview|Shattered Sea]], running from the [[galewall|Galewall]] to [[the-drowned-maw|the Drowned Maw]]. A central shipping road separates two island arcs.

The northern arc is the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]]: five mountainous islands, heavily fortified, with deep harbours and the Dravosi Crown's strongest regional foothold.

The southern arc is the [[midchain|Midchain]]: dozens of small islands close together, with free ports, reefs, and local councils. The routes here follow tradition more than maps.

The [[central-strait|Central Strait]] runs between them. To the east, the arcs meet at [[tail|the Tail]], where cold water and the Maw rule.

## Routes & Access

The [[central-strait|Central Strait]] carries ships south to the free ports. The [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] to the north have deep harbours and fortified access. The [[midchain|Midchain]] to the south offers reef channels and routes guided by local pilots.

## Hazards

Ocean swells and storms from the Galewall threaten ships. Sharks hunt shallow water. Deep channels hold rumors of larger predators. Political tensions between the Crown and island autonomy create more hazards: patrols, inspections, smuggling, and territorial disputes.

## People & Power

The northern arc answers to [[dravosi-crown|the Dravosi Crown]]. The southern arc belongs to island councils, free ports, pilot families, and local powers. Power here follows tradition, not paperwork.

## Trade & Resources

The Scatter trades Crown cargo, repairs, fish, and salvage. The real money comes from knowing when to follow the official route and when to risk the local way.

## Piracy Scale

[[pirate|Pirate]] threats here vary by location. [[knifes-wake|The Knife's Wake]] hunts ships in narrow channels. [[the-bad-receipt|The Bad Receipt]] uses Midchain rules as cover. [[the-glass-debt|The Glass Debt]] formed when piracy grew big enough to worry everyone.

# Sandrino Vale

> [!narration] Sandrino Vale
> A broad-shouldered man with rope-burned hands and a network of fine white scars across his left forearm from rigging work. Sun-weathered face, salt-stiff dark hair greying at the temples. He habitually tests the knots in his sleeves with his thumb while listening, and smells of salt-soaked fiber.

*Human rigger from Calveno, Pantry survivor*

**Wants.** To reach home to Calveno and his wife and daughter, and open a small provisioning house by the docks—no more sailing, no more storms.

**Voice.** Practical and direct; speaks in terms of line, weight, and climbing angles. He tests knots reflexively while thinking. Avoids talking about the first days at the Pantry, when hunger and fear divided the survivors between those who wanted to leave and those who fought to make them stay.

**What they share.** He will teach any sailor in the party how to tie a good climber's knot and lash a load that holds true. He lies about how many days he thought they would survive on fallen fruit alone. He will never speak about the early quarrels over leaving versus staying, or how close the Pantry came to fracturing.

> [!narration] First meeting
> Sandrino approaches the party as they reach the clearing, stepping around Renzo. His hands are already working at a length of shed fiber cord, tying and testing.
>
> "There is lashing and rigging to do before we walk back. I can tie anything that needs to hold. Show me what carries weight."

# Shepherd Grigori's Ascension

Grigori's binding began by accident, not design. Healing a noble gutted in a duel, he transfused his own blood out of necessity. Afterward he could sense the noble's presence and nudge them. His blood, alive in someone else's body, kept a thread back to him. This is the mechanism behind the necrotic, blood-aspected red light already unidentified on his own page (failed Arcana 18, Session 3).

The thread dilutes generation to generation unless refreshed. A light touch (a courtesy visit, a small kindness, contact resumed) tops it back up. This sits below the canon Heir ritual (full possession of a descendant), reserved for a line gone cold for centuries. Grigori, with only two confirmed binds, has never needed it.

---

"Building an army, not crowning an heir." Where [[aldric-drave|Aldric Drave]] crowned himself into a single dynastic bloodline (every noble family the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] would produce, three centuries running), Grigori scatters many small, dormant threads across noble houses in both rival crowns (Dravosi and [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]]) and the [[chain-council|Chain Council]]'s sphere. He stays below the threshold where anyone would think to look, with no line of succession to attack and no single point of failure.

---

The goal isn't political consolidation for its own sake. Every thread is fuel, not territory. He means to use every powerful person and institution he can reach to outgrow what a [[hierarch|Hierarch]] structurally is, making "blood thins, coherence fails" obsolete instead of managing around it forever.

---

Hierarch arrogance: binding your soul to your own blood is the ultimate main-character delusion. Grigori believes he's the only one of his kind. Drave believes the same thing about himself. Neither has any reason yet to suspect the other exists.

---

Through Tessarine channels, Grigori catches the whisper: the Concordat fears what Aldric Drave could do with the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]], expanding his power beyond his own bloodline. Grigori understands immediately, better than anyone gossiping about it. It's his own weakness, described from the outside. He wants the Pearl for himself, a possible shortcut past what he's spent his whole existence working around slowly. He doesn't connect it to Drave being a fellow Hierarch (arrogance again). He just recognizes the shape of the power, not who's chasing it.

---

He won't retrieve the Pearl himself. A planar breach crawling with [[leviathan|Leviathan]]-scale horrors is beneath a patient predator's methods. He waits on the crew instead. He already has a real, warm relationship with them from the [[uncertainty|Surety]] voyage: galley terms, healing [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] on the weather deck, the goodbye at [[calveno-la-vasca|La Vasca]]. If they recover the Pearl (Season 1's main goal), Grigori becomes a quiet, indirect actor working the friendship and reputation he already earned to get it away from them afterward.

---

Left open, not decided: whether Jean-Claude Tabarnack is already carrying a live phylactery thread from his Session 3 healing. The mechanism established today (heritable blood-magic binding via healing) would make it possible, but it stayed unresolved instead of getting locked in from a single conversation. A ruling for later, not now.

---

Working name for the throughline: "The Army, Not The Heir." Grigori's model against Drave's, and maybe the shape of the whole scheme.

---

Wakes the sleeping thread on [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]]'s own idea page: he's the Crown's answer once [[khlysty|Khlysty]] is confirmed to have infiltrated Dravosi. Order of events matters and is fixed: Grigori's pattern across earlier houses (the confirmed Tessarine ward, the Dravosi merchant-lord's daughter, whatever else predates the party) already had the Crown suspicious by the time the party parted ways with him at La Vasca, end of Session 03. Malone is loosed off the back of that pattern alone — not summoned by anything that happens with House Knighton. His hunt is already live and running in parallel before Grigori's Knighton play even starts.

---

Holding a Tessarine thread already, Grigori decides Dravosi access is worth "all the cards" — he wants both rival crowns threaded, not just one. He scouts several Dravosi houses. He considers [[geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey Draves]] specifically and passes: too distant a relation, too little leverage, not worth the exposure. (Geoffrey's own page already carries Aldric Drave's separate dormant interest in the Draves bloodline — a different Hierarch's thread, deliberately left untouched by this one.)

---

The real target: House Knighton, through [[rupert-knighton|Rupert Knighton]]'s heir. New NPC, not yet in canon: [[corvin-knighton|Corvin Knighton]] — young adult male, a fearsome bladesong wizard, trained in dueling at a Tessarine court (a deliberate foil to his uncle's blunt naval-fleet doctrine: precise, surgical, blade-and-spell rather than broadsides). The Tessarine training is Grigori's earned "in" — he already has Tessarine ties, so knowing about Corvin isn't a coincidence, it's exactly the kind of thread his existing network would surface.

---

The manipulation, Grigori's actual move: he engineers — patiently, indirectly, nothing traceable back to him — a circumstance where the party gravely wounds Rupert or Corvin Knighton. Then he "happens" to be positioned to save them. Playing both sides at once: earning House Knighton's desperate gratitude (the same binding mechanism as every prior thread, just aimed at Dravosi's own naval power this time) while deepening the goodwill he already banked with the party as their trusted healer. He never has to ask the party to do anything wrong — they just have to be themselves, and he arranges the circumstance.

---

House Knighton ends up triple-loaded, all at once, none of it Grigori's doing except the third: Rupert's existing revenge clock over [[capn-gorgeous|Cap'n Gorgeous]] (his actual son, killed by the party in Session 01, unknown to Knighton yet); [[geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey Draves]] already crewing the party's own ship as Knighton's distant nephew; and now Corvin as Grigori's manipulation target. The party's own hand in wounding Corvin is the thread that ties all three pressures to the same house at the same time.

---

The Malone confrontation scene: full Alexander Anderson (Hellsing) energy — not a subtle interrogator, a zealot mid-sermon. Righteous joy, scripture cadence, grim laughter, absolute conviction, "delivered with grim joy rather than reluctance" per his own page. He asks the party, straight-faced, whether they've seen or dealt with "the unholy abomination that calls itself Shepherd Gregori" — no idea they've broken bread with him, that he healed Jean-Claude, that [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] felt the cold handshake, that [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] already flagged the magic as wrong. Because his hunt runs independent of the Knighton incident, this scene is free to land before, during, or after it — whichever hits hardest. The choice it forces: protect the man who saved their crewmate, or hand him to a zealot who delivers "mercy" with a blade and a laugh.

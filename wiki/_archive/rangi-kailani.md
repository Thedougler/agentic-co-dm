# Rangi Kailani

**Wants:** win the duel against [[nico-renzetti|Nico Renzetti]] and preserve House Kailani's rule of Sparhold, but he's treating it as a straightforward test of skill when the political stakes—the settlement's whole power structure—ride on the outcome.

**Voice & Delivery Notes:**

- *Speech pattern:* brief and economical. Short sentences, no wasted words. His discipline shows in his speech the way it shows in his movement.
- *In-character:* When asked why he fights: "Duty. House Kailani held Sparhold. That holds weight. The duel ends it one way or the other."

## Relationships

**[[toa-kailani|Toa Kailani]]** (Family Head). Rangi is sworn to House Kailani; Toa commands his service and has chosen him as the family's champion in the challenge duel. Rangi respects Toa's authority absolutely and sees defending the house as an extension of duty itself.

**[[sparhold|Sparhold]]** (The Stakes). The settlement whose rule hangs on the outcome of the duel. Rangi does not fully grasp that losing this fight costs House Kailani not just his life, but their entire hold on the settlement's governance and defense.

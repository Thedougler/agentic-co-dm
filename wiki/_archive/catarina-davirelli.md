## Backstory

Youthful and white-haired, she's a [[human|Human]] (Variant, [[alert|Alert]] feat) [[artificer|Artificer]] ([[artificer-artillerist|Artillerist]]) out of an artisan-merchant background (Guild Artisan / Guild Merchant). Her Mortis gives her +2 INT but leaves her physically weak and frail. She has always been this way, and she says so plainly instead of hiding it.

Raiders kidnapped her as a child. Her father made a distraction so she got away, and she jumped off cliffs into the water to reach safety. No one knows if he survived. She still carries that doubt and his sealed flask every day. That fall is why she became so set on flight, and now she wants the next choice to be hers.

She says she wants to build the first flying machine with no magic at all, to prove that plain skill can solve what people expect spells to do. She means that literally. Underneath the machine talk, she wants something more specific: fame that means something and a legacy that leaves the world better, not just louder. She has not yet faced what she would give up to get there. She is sure the flying machine is a pure pursuit for ordinary people, free of magic, but she cannot yet see how far that belief might take her.

In 1488 DR, she found working [[antheri|Antheri]] parts at [[shelfworks|the Shelfworks]]. She took them apart and rebuilt three working devices, and she was the first to do this. Prospectors and scholars rushed in to follow, which started the Shelfworks Goldrush. She chose not to join it. Instead, she went home to [[calveno|Calveno]] to build her own workshop.

She runs [[kats-curios|Cat's Curios]] by referral only, keeping access limited on purpose, with a full list of jobs and prices (see [[ship-upgrades|Ship Upgrades]]). A locked cabinet holds dark-alloy Antheri pieces, trade only, with no cash price. She trades information and Antheri metalwork instead of selling it outright. She holds a binding contract with the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] fleet for [[ship-upgrades|Ship Upgrades]] and soldier kit, taken with open eyes for money and protection, and that steady work stays her worst compromise, one she does not talk about.

Magic used with no care is cheap to her. It steals the discovery, and it shuts people out by its nature, staying in the hands of the lucky and the rich. She believes enchanted items should reach common people instead, and she has said this plainly to people who did not want to hear it. She will not let her inventions harm people or fall into the wrong hands. She watched power get misused growing up and decided never to be that person. She treats everyone the same: beggars, servants, children, and nobles alike. She is exact and honest about what she can and cannot make. When she cannot build something, she says so and explains why. She never pretends a prototype works when it does not.

Her father's fate is still unknown. Everything else, her work ethic, her philosophy, the flask, the flying machine, comes back to his absence.

She joined the crew at [[s05-recap|Session 05]], stepping into the middle of the [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|Calveno sewers]] crisis to find Ragnetto and Strix already hard at work before she'd finished introducing herself.

The next session she proved her worth in a fight. Fire Bolt found its mark and a Shocking Grasp added ten more lightning damage on top. She enchanted [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s blunderbuss mid-combat (see [[delmars-blunderbuss|Delmar's Blunderbuss]]) while Ragnetto took damage that still needed repair. She crafted the [[mind-sharpener|mind-sharpener]] for [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]], turning a failed [[concentration|Concentration]] save into a success, and tried to restrain [[solange-barret|Solange]] from teleporting away. She failed.

Session 07 put her against [[otar-the-foul|Otar]]'s slaad tadpoles. She planted a gauntlet and triggered Thunder Wave for nine thunder damage. One tadpole shrugged off the blast on a [[constitution|Constitution]] save but stayed put, and Strix finished a downed tadpole hard enough to keep it from making an opportunity attack. (Confirmed: [[transcript]].)

In the fighting's aftermath, she named the corpse-eating thing that had killed a band of [[grung|Grung]] raiders a Slodtodmul. Its bite makes a Slodtodmul of you too, permanently. She named the crew's recovered scroll [[solanges-authority-seal|Solange's Authority Seal]] and bonded to [[a-sliver-of-the-unstable-form|A Sliver of the Unstable Form]], the hide torn from [[otar-the-foul|Otar]] himself.

She gained a standing helper in [[samuel|Samuel]], a toucan [[aarakocra|Aarakocra]] [[iacopo-fieschi|Fieschi]] sends to carry messages for [[kats-curios|Cat's Curios]] and the rest of the crew. When Fieschi offered the crew 300 gold each, she turned her share down flat and asked that it go to the city's repairs and the children living on its streets instead.

She pressed Fieschi on his ill words toward the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] and learned that [[rupert-knighton|Rupert]] had ordered an evacuation Fieschi swore never happened. (Confirmed: [[transcript]].)

Session 08 ran her through [[prospero-morsani|Morsani]]'s shop three separate ways. First she read the shopkeeper cold, and a 23 on Insight told her he hadn't bought The Snap so much as found it through something "not exactly legal," probably grave robbing. She also cast a static illusion on the "Defenders of Calveno" poster, turning its portraits into a moving, hologram-like display. (Confirmed: [[transcript]].)

Leaving [[cabinet-of-morsani|Morsani's]], she accused [[prospero-morsani|Prospero Morsani]] of robbing his own customers to his face. He answered with a hidden shop defense, and a hurricane-force gust threw her out the door and twenty feet down the street before it slammed shut behind her. (Confirmed: [[transcript]].)

Later that same session, at [[la-cenere|La Cenere]], she bought a [[wild-pearl-of-power|Pearl of Power]] from [[lavinia-sordi|Lavinia Sordi]] for 300 gold and identified it as unstable, its weave loosened, with two charges left. At [[zorts-pits|Zort's Pits]] next door, she agreed to build [[zort|Zort]] a working prosthetic leg to his exact measurements in trade for the name of his Mid-Chain reptile contact. (Confirmed: [[transcript]].)

## Arc Notes (DM Only)

Same DM-flagged-material ingestion note as [[jean-claude-tabarnack#Arc Notes (DM Only)|Jean-Claude's Arc Notes]].

The Spark Pistol is one prototype whose failure she has not diagnosed. [[puntura|Puntura]], [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s [[puntura|Whip Shark Barb]] shortsword commissioned in Session 03, is still not finished. Both threads sit open as hooks.

The next block breaks down how to run her in a scene.

> [!mechanic]
>
> - **Spotlight (source: system/Catarina-primer.md):** puzzle or mechanism problems, appraising salvage or Antheri tech, stationary turret-style firing positions for Ragnetto.
> - **Pressure:** anything that threatens her concentration. She has no War Caster. If she gets hit while holding [[faerie-fire|Faerie Fire]], Web, [[enlarge-reduce|Enlarge/Reduce]], [[enhance-ability|Enhance Ability]], [[levitate|Levitate]], [[blur|Blur]], or [[see-invisibility|See Invisibility]], she must make a flat CON save. She fails half the time.
> - **Struggling:** open lanes. Isolate a target she can burn down with [[fire-bolt|Fire Bolt]] or [[shocking-grasp|Shocking Grasp]].
> - **Dominating:** threaten her concentration. Grapplers work. Reach attackers work. Repeated hits work. She can't rely on anti-magic tricks. Let the save land sometimes, not every fight.

Her [[alert|Alert]] feat fundamentally changes her initiative and reaction options:

> [!mechanic]
> **Alert (no surprise).** +5 initiative. She detects all hidden attackers and gets
> advantage against any that try. A surprise-round ambush needs a different target.
> She typically acts first or near-first, so give other PCs a pre-combat beat too.
> Pressure her instead with ranged snipers, delayed environmental hazards, or
> social and information threats. Those don't rely on surprise or initiative.

Her save bonuses grow with level, easing that concentration pressure over time.

> [!mechanic]
> **Power spikes.** Level 7 Flash of Genius adds INT to a save, once per short rest.
> That's her first answer to concentration pressure without War Caster, and it's
> reactive, not standing. Level 18 Arcane Firearm turns a wand, staff, or rod into a
> spellcasting focus with bonus damage. That's late-game, not near-term.

Her Eldritch Cannon gives her a standing pressure tool alongside those spikes.

> [!mechanic]
> **Ragnetto.** Eldritch Cannon, stationary, once per long rest. A smart enemy
> threatens the cannon instead of her directly. High Investigation and Insight
> (18/14 passive). She notices scenes, clues, and people fast, with no roll needed.

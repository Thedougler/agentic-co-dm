# Syranita

Syranita is a deity of the [[aarakocra|Aarakocra]] sky-pantheon, holding secondary/marginal-observance status.

## The Fact

Syranita is an Aarakocra sky-pantheon deity with secondary/marginal-observance status. Not yet revealed at the table. Related deities include [[remnis|Remnis]] (primary observance) and [[aerdrie-faenya|Aerdrie Faenya]] (tertiary observance). [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]] holds marginal observance of Syranita.

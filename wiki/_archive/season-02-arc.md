# Season 2: the open Midchain

Cold open, next session: the *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]* is fresh out of repair, her crew still in [[calveno|Calveno]]. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s two Passage ships, already days into the chase after [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|the raid]], radio back with word of a massive storm and wrecked [[grung|Grung]] hulls, off the charted line. The crew is free to give chase, and nobody sent them: they're just the ones who can move first.

---

The chase leads to [[aruhe|Aruhe]]. This is a smash-and-grab, no time for a survey: pull [[sandro|Sandro]] and [[nino|Nino]] out of the wrecked *[[vethka|Vethka]]* on the beach, maybe read the harbour and clifftop well enough to answer a couple of [[warren-aruhe-survey|Nona's still-unasked survey questions]] by accident, and get out before dark. [[blight|The Blight]] is CR 19, nowhere near this crew's weight class, so it gets marked and left, a horizon threat the table now knows is out there and can't touch yet, not this season's fight. Nona hasn't handed Perrin the Aruhe commission yet; the Calveno crisis has kept her too occupied. The crew finds the wreck before she ever asks them to look for it.

---

No fixed route after that. Season 2 is an open [[midchain|Midchain]] sandbox: the crew can sail to any island, and the job is making sure interesting hooks reach them there instead of dragging them down a rail. Tone: unofficial, Robin-Hood-flavored piracy. Nobody sanctions this crew, and nobody asks their leave. Ship-to-ship combat, privateering, and open exploration all get regular table time across the season, not just one of the three. Tier 1→2 crossing happens narratively across this stretch, never in one clean jump.

---

[[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|The Schism's Third Name]] (active [[front|Front]], 5-segment clock) is this season's confirmed deep-dive. [[osset|Osset]] relocating his cursed-item stock every time a description of him circulates is a built-in island generator: wherever he resurfaces next can be wherever the next stop needs to be. It pressures [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] directly: the Eyrie kept a name from her even before her own exile.

---

Rumors of the *[[dead-lady|Dead Lady]]* start reaching the crew this season, first heard as dock gossip at [[kalowe|Kalowe]], per the escalation idea. [[umberlee|Umberlee]]'s deadline against [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] has run out with no movement on the Pearl. She is collecting tribute herself now. Derelicts turn up adrift with their holds stripped bare and no one left aboard to explain it. Nobody's connected it to Delmar yet. Ambient dread that can follow this crew into any port without becoming a fight until the table wants it to be one.

---

The 290 captives who reached [[karath|Karath]] stay a background pressure this season: the fortress-reef's dart-fire boundary is too hot to hit early, so no active pull draws the crew there yet. Rumors of them can surface at any stop and build toward a late-season or Season 3 target.

---

[[talon-vantyrus|Talon Vantyrus]] meets the crew by chance somewhere in the Midchain. He introduces himself plainly, mentions he was [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]]'s own master, and hopes Kyzil and the Eyrie have been teaching Crissdalynn everything she needs. If he notices her [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]] dreidel at that point, he sends [[talon-skarn|Talon Skarn]] to steal it from her.

---

[[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] and [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] both surface this season too: a full existing scheme already mapped on [[grigori-ascension|Grigori's Ascension]] (the Knighton manipulation, the Malone confrontation scene, "the Army, not the Heir") and Malone's own idea page ([[blackrule|Blackrule]], the Crown's zealot hunter, the [[khlysty|Khlysty]]-infiltration thread that wakes him). GM's explicit call: fine to pack in more than Season 1 carried, since Season 2 is running at least twice as long.

---

Everything else established in the Midchain is fair game as island-of-the-week material, not mandatory beats: [[penance-bar|Penance Bar]] as an optional side quest (two draft directions for its Ride sit unreconciled in the wiki, split between Ashe's personal bargain and the mob/coven frame-up, a call for whenever it's actually prepped, not for this pitch); [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s own quest cluster (a bounty, a treasure map, a receipt cache, a merrow's prize, the Takowan/ship-disguise thread) doubling as the season's rumor mill for Osset and the Dead Lady both; [[warren|the Warren]]'s Tangle-thread quests; and the rest of the ~90-location Midchain gazetteer. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s Umberlee bargain, [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s Nona-favor, and [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]/[[simone-tabarnack|Simone]] all stay live threads that can surface at any stop, never locked into a fixed sequence.

---

Named stops the crew may wander into while exploring, beyond [[kalowe|Kalowe]]: [[sparhold|Sparhold]] (a walled fortress-market undercutting Kalowe's own trade on price and looser rules), [[fathomrush|Fathomrush]] (a company-town Goldrush boomtown racing to engineer pressure tech for diving deeper into the [[antheri|Antheri Ruins]]), and [[halythion|Halythion]] (a hidden [[sea-elf|Sea Elf]] city in the Teikhinos Reef, governed by oral law, reachable only through a shallow-draft passage). Each one a different flavor of stop, none mandatory.

---

Scope: level 5 to level 10, 20+ sessions, all five PCs currently at 5. That's D&D tier 2 end to end, and it's roughly four times Season 1's length at a quarter of Season 1's advancement rate. Season 1 ran 1 to 5 across six sessions, a level every 1.2. This one is a level every four or so. This crew will feel that gear change unless every level lands on something they can see coming.

The season already has a spine sitting in the wiki, not yet assembled. The table below lays out the clocks that carry it.

| Clock | Owner page | State | Fills into |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Schism's Third Name | [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie\|Sentinels of the Eyrie]] | active, 5 segments, 0 filled | [[osset\|Osset]] finishes liquidating and leaves the Midchain for good |
| The Long Sight Hunt | [[the-countless\|the Countless]] | active, 3 segments, fast once triggered, 0 filled | [[talon-skarn\|Skarn]] stoops on [[crissdalynn-khinriss\|Crissdalynn]] for the [[fate-spinner\|Fate Spinner]] dreidel |
| The Soul Incarnate's Watch | [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie\|Sentinels of the Eyrie]] | dormant, 5 segments, 0 filled | The [[soul-incarnate\|Soul Incarnate]] breaks two centuries of doctrine and acts at [[the-drowned-maw\|the Maw]] |

The Long Sight Hunt's own trigger reads: while the crew hasn't reached the Midchain, it advances only through off-screen hired watchers. Season 2 opens with the *Uncertainty* sailing into the Midchain. That clock goes hot in session one, on a fast pace, over only three segments. Expect it to resolve before Osset does.

---

Acts are clock states, never session counts. Act I runs while the Schism's Third Name stays unresolved and the Long Sight Hunt sits under 3. Act II starts the moment either one resolves, whichever gets there first. Act III starts when [[soul-incarnate|the Soul Incarnate]]'s Watch leaves dormant. This crew can sit in Act I for four sessions or for twelve, and the acts still describe where the pressure is.

Season question: the Eyrie kept a name from Crissdalynn. What else is it keeping, and what is it keeping it for?

---

Levels come from won fights, not from milestones reached. Five victories, and only two real ordering constraints between them, so the sandbox stays non-linear:

| Rung | The victory | Gated on |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A rescue taken by force. Prepped instance: the beach at [[aruhe\|Aruhe]]. | Nothing. Not required either: sail elsewhere first and rung 1 becomes whatever won fight ends with someone walking out alive who wouldn't have. |
| 2 | A master beaten. Cornering [[osset\|Osset]], a Kensei who was a master before [[master-kyzil\|Kyzil]] and who can fly. | Nothing, the clock is live from the start. |
| 3 | [[talon-skarn\|Skarn]] beaten. Dropped in his own stoop, or hunted to his ground afterward and the dreidel taken back. | Nothing. Likely lands first. |
| 4 | The door taken. The standing that opens [[high-eyrie\|High Eyrie]], bought with something only privateers can deliver, with [[the-countless\|the Countless]] moving for the same door. | Rung 2 or 3. They need a reason to want inside. |
| 5 | [[talon-vantyrus\|Vantyrus]]. The man or his operation, broken. | Rung 3. The Countless have to have declared. |

The finale is separate from rung 5 and is not a fight: the Soul Incarnate's Watch fills and [[soul-incarnate-statblock|the Soul Incarnate]] acts, on the three routes already written on the faction page. Combat climax and story climax deliberately land apart.

Health check, not a schedule: five rungs over 20+ sessions averages one every four. Go much past that with no rung and a clock needs pushing off-screen. That is a signal to move the world, never a signal to steer the crew.

---

Rung 1 needs an enemy, or it's an errand. The enemy: the island. [[aruhe-wreck|The wreck]] put a hundred forty-nine captives and their Grung guardsmen onto Aruhe's reefs, and two Grung who reached the sand alive were dead within hours, killed by the corrupted fauna, exactly as [[blight|the Blight]]'s dying curse promises: no Grung who sets foot on Aruhe ever leaves it. Tomo rode the same broken outrigger strut ashore as [[sandro|Sandro]] and [[nino|Nino]], survived the wreck, and died days later from a fruiting bush the two men have not touched since. Nobody contests this beach except everything growing and moving on it, and that has killed every single thing that washed ashore except the two men watching the reef gap from inside the hull.

That makes rung 1 a fight the crew can win against the Blight's reach without ever swinging at CR 19, and it makes "before dark" mean something the table can see. They beat the island that killed everyone else, and they carry two survivors off it.

---

The Blight gets a job instead of being scenery. The Soul Incarnate's Watch has a second trigger: the Fissure Record's escalation crossing a threshold the two-century ledger has never held. The Blight's spread is that escalation. Every sighting the crew marks and leaves gets reported to the Eyrie, and the reports are what wake the thing sealed under it. They never swing at CR 19. Their own careful choice not to is what fills the finale clock.

---

Osset's trail runs a tight circuit of a handful of dealers. The front's off-screen move is that he shifts stock to a different Midchain dealer under a different story, and the dealers already exist: [[lavinia-sordi|Lavinia Sordi]] at [[la-cenere|La Cenere]] where he was last seen, [[ninkas-findings|Ninka's Findings]] in [[sparhold|Sparhold]] (no questions asked, salvaged magic), [[vask-reclaimed-goods|Vask Reclaimed Goods]] on [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s first island, [[tally-and-trade|Tally and Trade]] in Sparhold. The clock advancing means he has already moved to the next one. Information follows him around the circuit instead of pooling in one port, so there is no stop this crew has to keep returning to.

---

Consumable healing has to be reliably present and it has to come from everywhere, not one shop. This crew relies on half-casters alone, topping out at fourth-level slots at level 10 ([[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s [[bard|Bard]] 8), so death lands harder here than for a typical adventuring crew with a dedicated healer. Every settlement supplies differently:

| Stop | How healing reaches them |
|---|---|
| [[kalowe\|Kalowe]] | [[grimaldis-dispensary\|Grimaldi's]] discretionary stock, 55 gp, one or two at a time. [[ilaria-grimaldi\|Ilaria]] decides whether to reveal what she has based on how they ask. Relationship-gated, not shop-gated. |
| [[sparhold\|Sparhold]] | Looser rules, undercut prices, half of it stolen. Volume and no questions, at a markup. |
| [[fathomrush\|Fathomrush]] | Company medical stores held by Sunkline and Voyvode against dive casualties. Not retailed to outsiders. Take a company contract, or take the stores. |
| [[slackwater\|Slackwater]] | A favour from the ruling family, never for sale. Leaves this crew owing. |
| [[halythion\|Halythion]] | Exotic materials this crew can't buy without standing. |
| [[penance-bar\|Penance Bar]] | A priest paying down his own debt, the only one here who can cast [[revivify\|Revivify]], and every use costs him personally. |
| [[murrat\|Murrat]] | Nothing. [[moucheron\|Moucheron]] villages hunt outsiders who come ashore. |

Plus two that travel: [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] scribing scrolls and brewing in downtime between islands, and [[lavinia-sordi|Lavinia]]'s Vials of Uncertain Healing at 75 gp, stocked without fail and always a 1d6 gamble layered on top of the heal.

Prep rule instead of twenty loot tables: every island stop carries at least one consumable heal, whether bought or dropped. Every rung's victory adds one Revivify-tier item on top.

---

[[shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] is the Revivify nobody names the cost of. Red light spills from his wrist. Nothing else marks it. He heals in silence, and whoever he saves never thinks to ask how. He is an undead [[hierarch|Hierarch]] binding heirs into a network, already due to surface this season. That turns this crew's worst moment at the table into a hook instead of a resource check, and they won't learn the price for a long time.

---

Exploration, ship combat, and privateering each get a standing generator instead of a schedule. One is always live. Whichever mode the table wants tonight, it's ready. Exploration is how you find Osset, since he relocates every time a description circulates and any island is a live candidate. Ship combat is how the hunters press back: [[talon-vantyrus|Vantyrus]]'s watchers need hulls, and [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]] is independently hunting [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]. With hunters converging from both sides, fights find the crew without the crew seeking them out. Privateering is the economy, because Fathomrush's company prices, Catarina's materials, and eventually the price of the Eyrie's door are all money the crew doesn't have.

---

Jobs for the ambient threads so none of them is scenery. The *[[dead-lady|Dead Lady]]* never has to become a fight. Every derelict found is [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s overdue answer coming due, and the waters this crew avoids because of it steer the season without ever railing it. The 290 at [[karath|Karath]] is what the privateering is quietly accumulating toward, so ships taken and favours banked become capacity for a Season 3 target while demanding nothing now. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s Nona-favour arrives by sending stone and can interrupt anywhere, which makes it the tool for pulling this crew somewhere when the sandbox goes slack.

---

Reconciling the Vantyrus meeting above with [[the-countless|the Countless]]' own front page, which has the operation already running through hired watchers before any chance encounter: the watchers found the dreidel first and told him where to look, and the meeting is [[talon-vantyrus|Vantyrus]] confirming with his own eyes before he commits [[talon-skarn|Skarn]]. Same scene, no retcon, and it reads as sinister on a reread instead of coincidental. One of that front's outcomes is the crew catching a watcher in [[kalowe|Kalowe]], so the watchers are live table material now, not backstory.

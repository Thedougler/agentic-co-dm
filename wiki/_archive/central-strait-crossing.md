# The Central Strait Crossing

*The open-water leg of the [[central-strait|Central Strait]] a ship
actually sails between [[calveno|Calveno]] on the northern shore and
[[kalowe|Kalowe]] on the southern, days out of sight of land.*

## Path

This leg runs along the Blue Lane, the Strait's established shipping
route, from [[calveno|Calveno]]'s [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] shore
south to [[kalowe|Kalowe]], the primary Strait-adjacent trading hub on
[[midchain-west|the Western Midchain]]. It follows the strongest line of
the Scatter Current, roughly equidistant between the two shores,
unmarked but universal — the deepest, fastest line across the Strait. No
reef line or narrows forces a course anywhere along it; the crossing
stays open and roughly uniform in width. Ships bound specifically for
Kalowe peel off the Blue Lane onto the Kalowe Run, the angled approach
cutting south through the northern [[midchain|Midchain]] reef
approaches, piloted by agreed-upon waypoints rather than formal
channels. No single authority controls the open crossing itself; the
[[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] enforces its writ only at the
[[tidefall|Port Tidefall]] Gate to the west, not along this
Calveno-Kalowe line.

## Travel

The crossing runs about five hundred to six hundred miles and takes
roughly five days in a fast ship along the Blue Lane. The Scatter Current
runs east through the passage, adding half a knot to a full knot for
eastbound ships and costing westbound ships the same; a Calveno-to-Kalowe
crossing runs roughly south-southeast, so current assist varies with the
exact heading rather than applying in full either way. Winds blow
northeast to southwest, again favoring the eastward push over the direct
north-south line. Weather, the ship's speed, and how tightly a pilot hugs
the Blue Lane's fastest water all change the real duration; a slow ship
or one that strays off the Lane runs longer.

The Lane is also where anyone looking for a particular hull looks first.
A ship with reason to avoid a search stays off it and works the slower
water east of the Kalowe Run, which puts the crossing nearer seven days
than five.

## Hazards

Days out of sight of land are the crossing's defining hazard — a ship
that comes to grief mid-Strait has no nearby shore to reach. Reef
sharks stay close to both coasts, but hunter sharks patrol the deeper
lanes further out, and giant octopuses lurk in wreckage around
Sandtable Shoal, which sits south of
[[aldenmere|Aldenmere]]
near this crossing's northern half. The [[sawek|Sawek]] is rumored to
be the Strait's apex predator, favoring the dark southern
[[midchain|Midchain]] approaches this leg ends at, though its range is
unknown. Out here, a raider hides behind forged manifests and false
colors more often than an open attack, but ships still band into
convoys and Crown captains break from their usual patrol lines when
[[the-glass-debt|the Glass Debt]] or
[[the-velvet-noose|Velvet Noose]] is sighted near the Strait. No
dedicated encounter table exists yet for this open water — a gap, not
filled inline.

## Hooks

A ship missing the Blue Lane's fastest water adds real days to a
crossing already carrying no land in sight — weather delay, a slow
charter, or a captain avoiding Crown attention can all cost a party
time they were counting on.
[[the-bad-receipt|The Bad Receipt]]
is known to work the southern approaches this leg ends at. A convoy
forming at either end, or breaking apart mid-crossing when trouble is
reported near the Strait, is a ready complication for any party
making this run.

# [[penance-bar|Penance Bar]] Siege (Ride replacement, was "The Shrine's Ledger")

Recontextualized cause: not a personal bargain Ashe is paying down, but Ashe's own fearmongering, lies, and mob-incitement — his sin itself — that draws and feeds the demon swarm. He scapegoats an established coven of druids/hedge-witches living in the woods (outsiders by the town's own definition, never part of it) to protect his standing while keeping his own hands publicly clean — plausible deniability in public, active stirring in private.

---

The locked confrontation scene (verbatim, carries forward as the climax):

Priest: Are the witches dead? Get back out there, they must die before the sun goes down.

Demon: The sun is already down.

Priest(surprised): You cannot enter the house of Tyr?!

Demon: Tyr is not here. This is an empty box.

Priest: Tyr is in all of his temples.

Demon: Your God's love is not unconditional. He does not love us. And he does not love you.

Priest: I have done Tyr's bidding, my life's work is in his name.

Demon: Your life's work makes Tyr puke.

Priest: I am the Bishop of Penance Bar!

Demon: Your God knows we wouldn't be here without you, this is all your fault isn't it?

Priest(desperately): She was a witch!

Demon(mockingly): Lies? In your house of God? No wonder he's abandoned you. But *gestures to the other demons creeping into the temple* We Love you.

Demon: Let me kiss you.

Priest(afraid): NO Get BACK!

The Demon leans forward, and kisses the priest on the forehead. The priest is immediately and violently reformed into one of the devil-touched — a mortal transformed into a demon subservient to its new master, transformed into a representation of its crimes for all eternity, blackening the skin where his crimes were committed. The party learns the priest who called them in had lied to them, and had tried to use them, the town, and eventually the demons themselves to settle a petty score. The witches were harmless druids. The priest himself doomed the town and drew the demons.

---

Central irony / theme: fear as a summoning ritual — the town's terror is both caused by and weaponized by Ashe's lies. The moment the demon walks uncontested through the temple's front door is the proof that Tyr has abandoned the priest — no protection left there — which is also the reveal that the coven's grove, genuinely hallowed nature-ground, is now the *only* safe ground left on the island. And the mob is marching to burn it down.

---

[[structure|Structure]] of the day:

1. Contact — Ashe reaches the party directly, or a townsperson begs them for help "before the witches finish it." Village already jumpy: ash-smeared doorsteps, tally-stones, a bell tolling at odd hours (carried forward from the old draft, recontextualized as siege-warnings rather than penance-markers).
2. Investigation — party moves through the whipped-up village, talks to [[sula-marn|Sula Marn]] (recontextualized as a genuine ally trying to hold the peace, not protecting Ashe's secret), finds evidence the coven's being framed. Enough to be suspicious of Ashe, not yet the full truth.
3. The mob organizes and marches on the coven's grove in the woods — faceless, not a named rabble-rouser; fear itself is the antagonist. This starts the clock.
4. The party witnesses the locked confrontation scene live (staying behind, or doubling back on a hunch) — before the mob departs, so the party is racing to warn the village rather than racing to catch up to it. They watch the temple fail to protect Ashe, hear the truth stated outright, watch the transformation.
5. The race — party has to get the truth to the mob before it reaches the grove. Win condition is the truth arriving in time, not combat.

Outcome branches:

- Truth lands in time: mob stalls/breaks/turns on itself in shame, Ashe's fabrication is exposed publicly, coven spared, village left to deal with a demon-touched thing where its priest used to be.
- Truth lands late: party has to physically slow/stop the mob without it becoming a fight against a mob of victims — stays hard and ugly, not a stat-block encounter.
- Party fails: grove burns, coven dead or scattered, island loses its last hallowed ground at the swarm's strongest point. Real loss state, not soft-failed.

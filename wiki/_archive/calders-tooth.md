Calder's Tooth is a Dravosi gatekeeper island at the [[central-strait|Central Strait]]'s west mouth. [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]] sits on the west cliff. [[south-harbour|South Harbour]] and mudflats dot the east. A fog-thick ridge with sinkholes runs between them.

## Routes & Access

Ships enter through the Tidefall Gate between Calder's Tooth and northern [[midchain|Midchain]] reefs. Overland between the two harbours means the [[overland-track|Overland Track]], a muddy road over the foggy Marrow ridge. The Eastern Mudflats are passable on the tide, by people who know them.

[[the-passage|The Passage]] moves cargo through caves below the road. The Crown hasn't found it. More details on [[the-passage|The Passage]] page.

Run the island crossing per `vault/refs/vault/location/references/wilderness-travel-and-exploration.md` and `vault/refs/gameplay-toolbox.md` § Travel Pace.

## Hazards

Fog, sinkholes, mudflats, inspections, cliffs, and predators rule the island, and the Crown cannot tame any of it.

The interior carries its own fauna. Giant bats roost in the caves and sinkholes, and a disturbed colony will find night travellers. Giant boar tear up the wet ridges. Giant wolf spiders nest in old cisterns, so mudflat villages check a cistern before they send a child to fetch water. Giant axe beaks pace wagons on the track, and sometimes do more than pace them.

**Dead Light Mystery:** lighthouse crews vanished from the north with unknown, unresolved cause. The tower predates the Crown. Harpies might explain it, but no tools or bodies remain. Entry: parties need reason to land, works as [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]] detour.

## Geography

Calder's Tooth is a big, jagged rock of limestone and basalt. The west face is sheer and storm-beaten. The east side slopes into mudflats and mangroves. An interior ridge runs north to south, wet and foggy, filled with sinkholes. Roads are more like suggestions. The [[dravosi-crown|Crown]] claims the island but really only holds the forts, the harbour, and the records.

- **The Western Wall**: miles of basalt and limestone cliffs. Sea spray and [[galewall|Galewall]] winds batter it. Magic only scales it.
- **The [[marrow|Marrow]]**: interior ridge north to south. Porous rock with sinkholes and collapsed caves. Iron-oak and crown-pine trees, thorny vines below.
- **The Eastern Mudflats**: tidal flats and mangrove estuaries. Tides strand travelers in muck. Armoured troops can't cross them.

# Aruhe's Hungry Isle

*The wreck's survivors need rescue from an island that tolerates observation, but treats taking as invasion and Grung as an inherited enemy.*

## Premise

[[sandro|Sandro]] and [[nino|Nino]] still shelter in the broken [[vethka|Vethka]] on Aruhe's beach. The rescue is the first face of the situation. Beyond it lies a silent, overgrown island whose animals move as if diseased, then break into sudden bloodlust when the island registers a theft or a Grung presence.

This is an expedition situation, not one encounter. The beach and terraces open into the jungle. Ruins lie deeper in it. At the center, the island guards a final grove as a funeral ground.

## Pressure

**Rescue now.** The wreck is the only shelter on the beach. Its survivors know the shoreline rules, but not a safe route inland. The reef gap is the only approach, and the island's quiet makes every absence of sound a signal.

**The rule is simple and costly.** Aruhe ignores those who take nothing, except Grung, whom it attacks on sight. One berry, fish, plant, or trapped beast is enough to turn the island's fauna and flora against the taker.

**The island advances without a party.** Corruption keeps pushing giant, aggressive fauna from the interior toward the terraces, beach, and reef. Every delay makes the landing ground less forgiving without making the inner jungle any less worth exploring.

The Calveno raid's survivor trail gives the situation its practical entry point. Jean-Claude's knowledge of [[grung|Grung]] raiding and captivity gives the wreck a personal cost beyond salvage.

## Actors

- [[sandro|Sandro]] wants to get Nino off the island alive. He knows the safe shoreline, the wreck's shelter, and what the island did to Tomo. He does not know the interior.
- [[nino|Nino]] wants to leave without crossing the garden line. His silence and repeated caution make the island's taking-rule visible before he can explain it.
- [[grung-clans|The Grung Clans]] keep patrol boats around Aruhe's reef and refuse to land. Their raiding fleet's loss remains part of a wider pattern of captives moving toward Karath.
- [[blight|The Blight]] moves toward one aim: keep Aruhe inviolate in the memory of the drowned companion. Its corruption turns that protection into indiscriminate punishment.
- [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] keeps the missing-persons desk and recovery effort moving after the Calveno raid.

## Discoverable / Affectable

- **Rescue:** Sandro and Nino remain available to rescue, protect, move, or lose to the island's timetable. Their testimony establishes the wreck, Tomo's disappearance, the fruit taboo, and the difference between the shoreline and the jungle.
- **Expedition layers:** the reef and beach hold the wreck and coastal predators. The terraces hold the first garden growth and corrupted fauna. The outer jungle closes sight lines and routes. The ruins and inner jungle hold the history and deeper access. The final grove is the last room.
- **Progress:** knowledge, mapped routes, and fragments recovered from the ruins open later approaches. Combat clears temporary space. No cleared layer becomes permanently tame.
- **The guardian's motive emerges from three sources.** The survivors show that Aruhe distinguishes between looking and taking. The terraces and ruins show a place shaped as a garden and refuge before the corruption. Black flowers, calmed funeral fauna, and paired graves show mourning inside the predation.
- At the final grove, fauna gather at the boundary in stillness and watch like mourners. Black flowers mark two unmarked graves beneath the fruit tree. No root crosses either grave. The Death Bloom is the graves, tree, and ritual ground together, not a detachable object.
- **The moral pressure is permanent.** The Blight began as the captive **Archdruid**'s refuge-protecting ritual. Corruption has made her its physical form. Destroying the Death Bloom ends her permanently. Leaving it preserves the sanctuary and its killings. The choice remains open.
- **PC gravity:** [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s crew reached Aruhe while following the Calveno raid's survivors. Jean-Claude's Grung connection can turn the guardian's inherited hatred into a question of what protection means when it no longer distinguishes the guilty from the living.

## If Ignored

The Vethka's remaining shelter fails. Sandro and Nino lose the beach as a defensible place, and the next vessel to reach Aruhe finds the wreck empty or occupied by whatever has claimed it. The reef patrols continue to avoid the island. Inland corruption keeps pushing predators outward, so later landings meet more danger at the same edge of the jungle.

The Blight remains intact and the Death Bloom remains untouched. Nothing in the island's center changes its purpose while no one reaches it.

## Beat Chart

### Dramatic Question

Can the crew survive Aruhe's escalating corruption long enough to reach the Death Bloom and decide whether to destroy the Blight or leave its memorial standing?

### Player Gravity

- [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]: his crew reached Aruhe while following the Calveno raid's survivors. Jean-Claude's Grung history makes the island's inherited hatred a question of what protection means when it no longer distinguishes the guilty from the living.
- [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]: Grung history with Aruhe. The island attacks Grung on sight. His people's inherited fear of this place is a question he can answer or confirm.
- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]: the castaways are Calveno crew. Rescue is crew obligation, and the wreck may hold salvage worth the risk.
- [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]]: the Blight's corruption is a druidic mechanism. Her engineering mind meets magic that reshapes biology at scale.
- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]: the Death Bloom is a place-bound artifact. The Fate Spinner's navigator may read the corruption's pattern before anyone else.

### Current State

- The Uncertainty has not yet approached Aruhe. The island is available whenever the crew sails east of Karath.
- Sandro and Nino shelter in the wrecked Vethka hull, burning signal fires visible from the reef gap.
- The Blight's corruption has fully claimed the interior. The corruption drives giant fauna outward onto the coastal strip.
- Grung patrol boats work the surrounding reefs but refuse to land.

### Beat Spine

**Phase 1, The Landing (CR 3 to 7): [[aruhe-the-teeth|the Teeth]], [[aruhe-the-old-gardens|the Old Gardens]]**

1. **Hook, The Reef and the Wreck.** Reef gap and shingle beach, skull-wearing crabs buried in the shallows. The broken Vethka on the beach and two Calveno survivors who have learned the island's one rule. Pulls on [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] (crew obligation), Perrin (the survey begins here).
2. **Development, The Taking Rule.** Sandro and Nino explain what the island does to takers. The terrace gardens still fruit wild. The temptation is constant and the cost is immediate. Pulls on [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] (the Grung already know this rule and fled it).
3. **Cliffhanger, The Terrace Gauntlet.** [[wolfrabbits|Wolfrabbits]] launch from the collapsed stonework. [[grinning-apes|Grinning apes]] drop from the canopy above. [[vine-lash|Vine lash]] wraps from the terrace walls. The first real fight, and the terraces are the only route up. Pulls on Perrin (the survey requires going inland).

**Phase 2, The Interior (CR 7 to 13): [[aruhe-the-quiet|the Quiet]], [[aruhe-the-rot|the Rot]]**

4. **Development, The Silent Canopy.** Ambient sound dies. [[deer-stalkers|Deer-stalkers]] watch from the tree line and do not look away. The [[bear-elk|bear-elk]]'s patrol blocks every approach between the Quiet and the Rot, same route, same timing, every day. Pulls on [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] (the corruption's pattern is visible in the fauna's behavior).
5. **Cliffhanger, What the Rot Breathes.** Spore-thickened air, [[strangler-fig|strangler figs]] across every game trail, [[thornbacks|thornbacks]] rooting solo through the undergrowth. The ground itself resists passage. [[silence-moths|Silence moths]] drift the corridors at dusk and kill verbal spells inside their aura. Pulls on [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] (the silence field is the first sign of the Blight's deliberate design).
6. **Development, The Memorial's Shape.** The ruins show a place that was once a garden and refuge. Black flowers mark paths. Calmed funeral fauna stand at thresholds. The corruption is protection turned indiscriminate, and the evidence says so before anyone reaches the grove. Pulls on Jean-Claude (the Grung story called it a curse, but this is something older and sadder).

**Phase 3, The Heart (CR 15 to 19): [[aruhe-the-hunger|the Hunger]]**

7. **Cliffhanger, The Grove's Teeth.** [[terror-birds|Terror birds]] stalk the approach. [[corpsewood|Corpsewoods]] walk the perimeter. The ground inside the Hunger is the Blight's own body, and it knows the party is there. Pulls on Delmar (the crew he brought here is in the worst of it now).
8. **Climax, The Death Bloom.** Unmarked graves beneath a fruit tree. Black flowers ring the clearing. No root crosses either grave. The Death Bloom is graves, tree, and ritual ground together. Destroying it ends the Blight permanently. Leaving it preserves the sanctuary and its killings. The choice stays open until someone acts. Pulls on all five PCs (the question is whether protection that kills everything it touches is still protection).
9. **Resolution, What the Island Remembers.** If the Death Bloom falls, the corruption recedes over weeks, fauna lose their aggression, the island becomes usable. If it stands, nothing changes, the memorial endures, and Aruhe stays hungry. Either way, the castaways leave or stay based on the beach, not the grove.

# Batta Zusto

|             |                                                |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Role**    | Information broker, Bridge upper tier          |
| **Location**| [[calveno\|Calveno]], the [[the-bridge\|Bridge]]'s top tier    |

Batta Zusto is a broker of 50 (stout and stiff). He works at the Bridge in [[calveno|Calveno]]. He looks at the wall and a drink is there. He sells facts for set costs and talks plain. He stops so folks fill the silence.

**On the market:** he sells intel on the Red Lady salvage work (deep work, not surveys). The buyer needs diving gear and charts for contacts in two ports. Money comes from a [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]] factor he knows, someone new to salvage work. Another party seeks Maw access this month for 40 gp (or navigation intel worth 80 gp, or the wreck name). He won't name the backer or the factor.

The Red Lady salvage commission: he doesn't know who funds it. He sells intel around the edges instead (the commission's shape, its supply chains, the Port Tidefall factor link) for gold or trade goods, never a cut. Old prep said he knew the backer and would trade the name for a cut; Session 04 play showed he doesn't, and the played version wins.

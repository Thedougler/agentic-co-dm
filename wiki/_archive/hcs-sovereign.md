# HCS *Sovereign*

![[hcs-sovereign-banner]]

> [!read-aloud]
> Gun decks rise above a grey hull. Each level has fifteen cannon ports on each side. All closed now. Brass fittings catch light. A white Admiralty stripe marks the spar deck rail, wider than on any other Crown ship in [[verdant-scatter|the Scatter]]. The Crown pennant flies from each mast. The figurehead holds the crown up. Not an offer, but a claim. The ship moves slow. It does not need to move fast.

A first-rate ship of the line, 215 feet on the waterline. Built at the [[harwick|Harwick]] Naval Yard over six years, it represents Dravosi naval power at its peak. The *Sovereign* stands unique in [[campaign-overview|the Shattered Sea]]. The largest hull afloat and most heavily armed, it proves the Crown's authority in these waters is absolute.

Hull is naval grey, copper-sheathed below the waterline. The lower gun deck mounts thirty 32-lb long cannon. The main deck holds twenty-eight 24-lb long cannon. The upper deck bears thirty 12-lb long cannon. Heavy carronades occupy the spar deck (four guns total). Total mounted guns: 104, plus eight chasers.

The figurehead is not a god. A robed figure holds the Crown of Dravas high in both hands. More claim than gift. The Admiralty carved it from pale northern timber and gilded the crown. The face stays unfinished on purpose.

The *Sovereign* does not patrol. It does not escort trade. Instead, it deploys when the Crown must show full force. It carries [[delmar-fisk-interview|the Admiral]] of [[shattered-sea|the Shattered Sea]] and staff as the fleet's lead ship. It enforces Crown rule where other ships only threaten. Word of its coming spreads for weeks ahead.

The *Sovereign* does not chase. It presents its guns and waits. One full broadside from the lower guns ends most fights. It cannot turn in shallow water, cannot move fast, and fears fire ships. A smart enemy uses these flaws against it.

**Special Facilities:**

- **Grand Magazine** *(2 space units)* has a reinforced double-chamber below the lower gun deck. Forward chamber prepared shot for all three gun decks. Aft chamber holds specialist rounds (chain, bar, heated, grapeshot) in sealed lockers. *Active:* once per Bastion Turn, order a specialist shot for any one deck. It applies automatically in the next combat encounter. *Passive:* cannon attacks deal peak damage on a confirmed critical hit. A natural 1 on any cannon attack misfires but does not explode, cleared as an action.

- **Admiral's War Room** *(2 space units)* is a full stern cabin spanning the spar deck with glazed windows, chart table, signals rack, and dispatch archive. *Active:* once per Bastion Turn, issue fleet orders or reposition a Crown naval asset in the Scatter. Or prepare a tactical assessment (advantage on the next navigation check or ship-to-ship encounter involving the target). *Passive:* while the *Sovereign* is in a region, all Crown naval assets there treat the Captain role as filled by a skilled officer. Hostile captains within visual range risk becoming shaken (see mechanic below).

- **Battle Surgeon's Deck** *(2 space units)* is a full surgical deck on the orlop level, below the waterline, with two operating tables, mass-casualty medicine cabinet, amputation kit, and ten-cot recovery bay. *Active:* once per Bastion Turn, perform emergency surgery (stabilise + remove one exhaustion level). Or produce three [[potion-of-healing-common|Potions of Healing]]. Or treat disease/poison in up to four crew simultaneously. *Passive:* on a short rest aboard, each PC rolls Hit Dice twice and takes the higher result. On a long rest aboard after combat, each PC recovers one more Hit Die.

> [!mechanic]
> **Admiral's War Room: cowing presence.** Hostile captains within visual range of the *Sovereign* make a DC 14 [[wisdom|Wisdom]] save at the start of each encounter. On a failure, shaken (disadvantage on attacks until they succeed).

**Deck Layout.** The spar deck has a wheel and compass aft, a signal station, two heavy carronades at the quarterdeck, two at the forecastle, eight 12-lb guns per side amidships, and the Admiral's War Room deckhouse at the stern.

Upper Gun Deck (fifteen 12-lb guns per side, officers' quarters aft with flag captain's anteroom, marine barracks and armoury forward). Main Gun Deck (fourteen 24-lb guns per side, primary fighting deck, lower ports sealed in heavy weather).

Lower Gun Deck (fifteen 32-lb guns per side, heaviest weight of shot in the fleet, Grand Magazine directly below, ports sealed except in calm water or harbour).

Orlop Deck (no guns; Battle Surgeon's Deck forward; carpenter's stores, chain locker, bosun's supplies). Hold (100 tons cargo, typically stores for extended deep-water operations).

## Crew

The source provides no captain or crew member by name. Notable roles include **master gunner** (manages the Grand Magazine), **flag lieutenant** (manages the Admiral's War Room), **ship's surgeon** (manages the Battle Surgeon's Deck), and **surgeon's mate** (Cook).

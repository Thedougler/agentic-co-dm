# The Redwind Isles

> [!read-aloud]
> Red grit settles on the deck in a fine film, and the man at the rail wipes his hand down his shirt and leaves a rust smear. The wind that carried it comes off the southeast dry enough to crack a lip.
>
> Ahead, bare stone the colour of old brick steps up out of water clear to the shelf. Nothing grows on it.
>
> The shores are empty. A single arch of rock stands off the nearest one, the swell running clean through it and back out.
>
> *(to the table)* Your chart shows four islands here. You are looking at seven.

## Flora & Fauna

- Salt scrub and rock lichen: hold on in the shaded folds and cracks off the exposed cliffs, the only green a party finds once they're off the open stone.
- Stone-gourd: a squat succulent that swells fat with stored rainwater after the rare downpour. Foragers who know where it roots drink from it and save what they carried ashore.
- [[giant-lizard|Giant lizards]]: bask across the hot slabs by midday and scatter into crevices the moment a shadow crosses them, a meal for anyone quick enough to corner one.
- [[vulture|Vultures]] and their oversize [[giant-vulture|kin]]: ride the thermals over the isles in numbers that thin out toward the unmapped interior and thicken again wherever something recently died.
- [[giant-scorpion|Giant scorpions]]: shelter under the same sun-cracked slabs the lizards favor, and a party hunting one without checking the underside of a rock first finds the other.

## Hooks

- No two charts of the Redwind agree on the island count, and the pilots who sell them change the subject when asked why.
- An ancient blue dragon rules the isles, or so the salvage crews out of [[outer-reach|the Outer Reach]] tell it. Nobody who claims to have seen it also claims to have left.

# Coralyra Dranra

A beautiful, siren-like elf [[sorcerer|sorcerer]]/[[bard|bard]] (level 5). Her voice stops people talking. She weaponized this gift. An Incomplete Aoidos *(ah-OY-dos)* from [[halythion|Halythion]], she left at 87 years old (young for elves). She joins the party after the first arc.

**Personality:** she wants people to listen. Deep down, she wants home, forgiveness, apologies, and recognition for her music. For an elf, she is young. She respects beggars, servants, and children.

**Red line:** will not mess with dolphins or other marine mammals. **Red button:** her animal friends in danger AND someone talking over her simultaneously. Both at once, and she stops thinking.

**Faith:** raised by Deep Sashelas. She admires [[umberlee|Umberlee]] for power and control. She does not worship Umberlee.

**Mechanics:** she is a sorcerer/bard multiclass. Her voice holds magic trained into music and effects beyond words (details hidden). Her trident came from a shipwreck she does not remember.

> [!mechanic]
> **Tradition:** Arcane. **School:** Enchantment. **Spell analogue(s):** *[[calm-emotions|Calm Emotions]]*, *Command*.
> **Homebrew element:** Her voice suspends the impulse to speak in every listener simultaneously, rather than compelling one creature.
> **[[detect-magic|Detect Magic]]:** Enchantment — a felt weight settles on the tongue, as though the next word costs more than it is worth.
> **[[counterspell|Counterspell]]:** yes.

**Active situations:**

- Self-exile from Halythion. She fled before evaluation. The Council still waits.
- Incomplete Aoidos. The title has no clear meaning or rules yet.
- Hermit crab debt. She needs to find it a better shell.
- [[flappers-the-seal|Flappers]]. Out chasing mackerel, unaware of Coralyra.

**What she's carrying:** a magical trident from a wreck. Her voice is both weapon and wound. She carries the hermit crab debt.

**Backstory:** she grew up in Halythion, happy and safe. People said her music bothered them and she needed evaluation. She fled before that could happen. A [[sawek|Sawek]] marked her. She made a hermit crab homeless by accident.

## Relationships

- [[flappers-the-seal|Flappers the Seal]]. Her closest friend. Chases mackerel.
- Sea turtles. She trusts them with her life.
- [[halythion|Halythion]]. Her home.
- [[sawek|Sawek]]. Marked her with ink.
- [[umberlee|Umberlee]]. She admires her power.

# Fathomrush's Split Harbor

*Sunkline and Voyvode face each other across the harbor, each trying to turn engineering failure into control of the deep-shelf trade.*

## Premise

The [[sunkline-company|Sunkline Company]] and [[voyvode-works|Voyvode Works]] run opposite shores of [[fathomrush|Fathomrush]]. Sunkline claims a trial it never passed. Voyvode runs real trials that still kill divers. The unclaimed dockside and memorial wall sit between them.

## Pressure

Both piers watch where each new hull ties up. Sunkline's concealed certificate is near discovery. Voyvode's next trial advances without an audience. The first credible claim draws the harbor's money and labour.

## Actors

- **[[sunkline-company|Sunkline Company]]:** keeps investor confidence alive. [[adriana-tarquine|Adriana]] rebuilds the same valve while recruiters sell the forged result.
- **[[voyvode-works|Voyvode Works]]:** earns a working rig through real trials. [[branimir-karsovine|Branimir]] prepares another dive.
- **[[petera-ngata|Petera Ngata]]:** keeps the dead from becoming company property. Her Last Berth records hold losses neither side owns.

## Discoverable / Affectable

- **Sunkline's hidden failure:**
  - Adriana has rebuilt the same valve four times.
  - The [[fathomrush-last-berth-false-log|Last Berth false log]] holds the forged result.
  - No public record confirms the claimed trial.
- **Voyvode's recent loss:**
  - The memorial wall carries a fresh name.
  - [[petera-ngata|Petera]] keeps the loss list.
  - A surviving diver knows which rig failed.

## If Ignored

Another Voyvode trial puts a fresh name on the wall. Sunkline keeps hiring on its forged certificate until the paper surfaces. The first Front to fill empties the losing compound into the winner's dock within days.

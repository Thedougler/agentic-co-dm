# Sea Elf

**Creature Type:** Humanoid (Elf, aquatic subtype)

**Size:** Medium

**Speed:** 30 ft. walking; swimming speed equal to walking speed

As a Sea Elf, you have these special traits.

**Ability Scores.** Increase one score by 2 and another by 1, or increase three different scores by 1.

**Child of the Sea.** Breathe air and water; resistance to cold damage.

**[[darkvision|Darkvision]].** 60 ft. Discern colors in darkness as shades of gray.

**Fey Ancestry.** [[advantage|Advantage]] on saving throws to avoid or end the charmed condition.

**Friend of the Sea.** Communicate simple ideas to any Beast with a swimming speed.

**Keen Senses.** [[proficiency|Proficiency]] in the Perception skill.

**Trance.** No need to sleep; cannot be magically put to sleep. [[long-rest|Long rest]] requires 4 hours of trance. End of trance: gain two proficiencies (weapon or tool) from elven memory, retained until next long rest.

**Languages.** Common plus one additional language.

**Life Span.** Sea elves live 750+ years.

**Homeland.** In [[campaign-overview|the Shattered Sea]], sea elves are centered at [[halythion|Halythion]] and tied to the worship of Deep Sashelas; some trace ancestral ties to the [[elemental-plane-of-water|Elemental Plane of Water]].

**Tome of Foes Variant (older — superseded for most campaigns).** +2 Dex, +1 Con. Sea Elf Training: proficiency with spear, trident, light crossbow, net. Friend of the Sea via gestures/sounds instead of speech. Languages: Common, Elven, Aquan.

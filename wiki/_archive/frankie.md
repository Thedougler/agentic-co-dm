# Frankie

**Wants:** to win the argument with [[toby|Toby]] and get her spot back as his Royal Best Friend, though he keeps topping her claim before it can land and the island keeps scarring wherever they collide.

> [!read-aloud]
> A girl of maybe nine, sun-browned and barefoot, a driftwood tiara jammed sideways over a salt-stiff braid coming loose. Chin up, fists closed around a chipped conch shell she carries like a scepter, she's dragging a second driftwood throne through the sand toward the square, digging her heel in to square it straight every few feet.

*Frankie*: I said it FIRST, so I win, so there.

Playground queen bee, absolute monarch who's never once questioned her own decree.

Frankie is Toby's oldest rival on [[halewick|Halewick]]. The grievance underneath is ordinary and real. He took her spot as his Royal Best Friend. She holds her own stretch of shore instead of a throne room, with no ministers of her own. Her cursed-tongue power is the same untrained leak as Toby's, and the mechanism and its origin live on Halewick's own page. She isn't a villain. She's a kid mid-argument who doesn't understand the weapon she's holding.

*Frankie*: You said I COULDN'T be Best Friend anymore, so, so NOW nobody can ever be your friend, ever, forever! *(shouting, already regretting it a little)*
*Frankie*: That doesn't count. That doesn't COUNT. I said mine first. *(stamping)*
*Frankie*: I'm not being mean. You started it. You ALWAYS start it. *(quieter, plainly hurt)*

Voice is fast, loud, and self-interrupting. She corrects her own sentence mid-word to make the claim bigger before anyone can beat her to it. She stamps her feet when she feels she's losing ground. She's quick to anger, quicker to tears the moment someone catches her by surprise instead of arguing back.

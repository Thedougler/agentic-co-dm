# Taken Whole

*Something red and silent is taking ships whole past the Maw-lanes, and dock talk at Kalowe hasn't figured out why.*

## The Rumour

"There's a ship out past the Maw-lanes that doesn't fight and doesn't loot. She comes up red out of the fog, and when she's gone the whole crew's dead and the hold's stripped bare. Just an empty hull, drifting wherever the current takes her."

## Truth Value

**Partially true.** Every claim about what's found is accurate. [[dead-lady|The *Dead Lady*]] leaves derelicts exactly like that: everyone aboard dead, holds emptied over the side. What the rumour gets wrong is the shape of it. Sailors assume raiders or a sea monster clearing a ship for salvage nobody will contest. Nobody circulating this story knows it's [[umberlee|Umberlee]] collecting tribute directly instead of through a shrine basin, and nobody has connected it to [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]'s open debt.

## Spread

Tavern and dock talk at [[kalowe|Kalowe]], with no single source: fishers, dockhands, and crews who've either spotted a drifting hulk themselves or heard about one secondhand. It mutates the way any secondhand horror story does. The core of it stays fixed across every telling: a red ship comes, and the ship it leaves behind is empty and adrift. Newer versions increasingly claim she takes the gold too, since "raiders who leave the cargo behind" makes no sense to anyone repeating it. Nobody has yet suggested she wants something other than plunder.

## Investigating It

> DC 14. **Success:** the details that actually circulate don't add up for ordinary raiders. Nothing about the hatches or the hull shows a boarding fight, and the missing cargo has never turned up fenced or resold anywhere in port. Something took what it wanted and put the rest over the side instead of carrying it off. **Failure:** the story stays a pirate rumour. Dangerous water, best avoided, and there's nothing more to learn here.

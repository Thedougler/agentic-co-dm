# Iron Prayer Tablet

> [!read-aloud]
> A tablet of dark iron, no larger than your palm, its surface covered in finely carved prayers in a script too old or foreign to read at a glance. Decades or centuries of handling have worn the edges smooth. The iron has taken on a soft sheen. The weight is heavy for its size. The prayers spiral inward from the edges toward a central symbol of unknown origin.

*Document, Common.* A devotional prayer tablet, likely salvaged from a shrine or temple. The iron is well-worked. The inscriptions are careful and deliberate, suggesting both craftsmanship and sincere faith. The prayers themselves are not identifiable from the script alone, though a scholar familiar with seafaring faiths or coastal shrine traditions might recognize their origin. The tablet holds minor historical or spiritual value to the right collector.

# Roka's Hidden Ledger

> [!read-aloud]
> The journal is unremarkable at first glance, leather-bound and middle-brown, soft from years of handling, an expedition ledger any timber crew foreman might keep. But the entries are carefully organized, expedition dates recorded alongside client names and crew counts, the stated purpose noted, and then a column marked only with creature sketches and tally marks with prices beside each one. The sketches are competent: a [[grung|Grung]] warrior, a jungle cat, something smaller with too many legs. The leather carries the smell of aged fieldwork, and the pages are thick, yellowed at the edges. Beside one sketch the words "[[the-live-hold|live hold]] buyer: 80 gp" sit in Roka's precise handwriting. A later page carries a note in the margin: *"Boundary scout (Ussa) paid 15 gp. Cut-line drifted north. Repeat within season."* The final entries are from last week.

*Document, Mundane.* [[roka-sten|Roka Sten]]'s private expedition ledger, kept separately from his public client roster. It records actual specimen collection outcomes, including live captures bound for [[the-live-hold|The Live Hold]], alongside payments made to the boundary scout to misreport timber-cutting lines at the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] fringe. The ledger is direct evidence of trafficking operations and bribery.

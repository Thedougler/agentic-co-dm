# Grandfather's Course

> [!read-aloud]
> The journal's leather cover warps thick with water damage. Salt stains run down the spine like tidemark rings. The pages bloat and stick when turned, the paper swollen, but the spine stays legible: "Grandfather's Course" in gold leaf faded to brass. Inside, the handwriting shows decades of nautical observation. Compass bearings and star positions fill the pages. Notes on currents and shallows. The careful record of someone who read the sea like others read maps. The last entry stops mid-sentence, dated the day before the [[vestra|Vestra]] sank.

*Document, Mundane.* [[costin-bruma|Costin]]'s pawn price for this journal is 3 silver pieces: the amount someone desperate needed six months ago, right after the [[vestra|Vestra]] sank. To [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]], its worth goes far beyond coin.

## Provenance

The journal belonged to [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s grandfather, a sea captain whose navigation was exact, his records exacting. Perrin carried it six months, until the [[vestra|Vestra]] sank. Desperation drove him to pawn it then. Since then it has sat on [[costin-bruma|Costin]]'s unredeemed shelf, tagged and waiting. Costin sees only another item three months past redemption, drying in harbor air.

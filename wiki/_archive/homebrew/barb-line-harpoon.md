# Barb-Line Harpoon

> [!read-aloud]
> A head the length of a forearm, four barbs swept back off the point, filed bright where the rest of the iron has gone grey.
>
> Sixty feet of light chain runs off the socket and coils on the peg beneath it, oiled black.
>
> The last link opens into a hook, and two hammer blows shut it around a rail.
>
> Lifted, it carries all its weight at the point, and it wants to go tip first.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **Barb-Line Harpoon [HB].** A martial thrown weapon, range 20/60, dealing 1d6 piercing damage. On a hit against a Large or smaller creature the barbs set and the chain pays out behind it.
>
> Shackle the chain's far end to a rail or a driven bolt and the hit creature moves no more than 60 feet from that anchor. It works the head free with an action and a DC 13 [[strength|Strength]] check, or cuts the chain by dealing 10 damage to it (AC 15, immune to poison and psychic damage).
>
> **Edge cases:** a boat anchors the chain no better than its own weight, so the heavier of boat and creature wins that pull. A second harpoon in the same target brings its own chain and its own check.
>
> **Limitations:** the harpoon adds nothing to attack or damage rolls and imposes no condition. A harpooned target fights on at full strength, and it can haul the anchor point in place of itself. A Huge or larger creature ignores the barb and takes only the damage.

## Provenance

[[kettil-ormsson|Kettil Ormsson]] forges the heads three at a time and sells them to the inter-island fishers, who use them on rays. The chain is his own stock, rove and oiled before it ever goes on the peg.

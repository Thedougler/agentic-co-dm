# Vel Orn
> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Umberlee ingest pending._

## At a glance
On Vel Orn in the [[Sunken Crown]], five small islands ring the [[Blue Hole]]. Umberlee's older house is cut into black cliff stone, reachable only when the surge is timed and the approach is known. It is not a working-quay collection point: its worship reaches toward the drowned rather than the living.

## Shrine
Kelp wraps the pillars. Candles burn where wind should have killed them, and footsteps go soft. The cliff landing is sheer, reef-bound, and dangerous; the shrine is hidden from ordinary charts. Inside, a ledger chamber keeps drowned names and water-debt: tokens, coins, fish bones, carved shells, and pieces of ship and sailor as witness. Every object is a death written down.

The deepest chamber held the [[Pearl of Souls]] above a tidal pool built for it. The Pearl's absence is the mark of [[Delmar Fisk]]'s theft and the fleet's destruction. People at the table sometimes call this place **Vafnar**, a mishearing that stuck.

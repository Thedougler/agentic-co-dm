> *Shapes peel from the dark mass over the island and close on the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] fast, low over the water. Pigeon-sized, barbed, moving in a loose pack with their stingers forward. One pulls ahead of the rest and hangs fifty feet off the port rail, wings loud enough to hear over the drone behind it. The mouth opens. Common words, shouted thin against the wind. The voice belongs to [[ket|Ket]]. His wings keep the same rhythm as the others. He hovers there, stinger angled away from the ship, and shouts one word over and over. Go. The rest of the pack does not slow down.*

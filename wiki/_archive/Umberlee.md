# Umberlee
> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Umberlee ingest pending._

## Names and nature
Umberlee is the sea as it actually is, not the sea sailors wish it were. At sea she is simply Umberlee; people call her the **Bitch Queen** in anger and **Queen of the Depths** when trying not to use the first name. She owns waves, storms, wrecks, and the fear that makes a captain pay before checking the weather. She is neither mother of sailors nor devil with a ledger: Crown frigate and smuggler skiff meet the same hand.

Her only demand is admission that she can take you. The sea does not negotiate, and a blessing is not a promise.

## Tribute theology
That admission is tribute. Every working harbour in the Scatter pays it, regardless of Crown, Concordat, free-port, or Passage flag. A shrine stays where a sailor can find it on the way in or out. A blessing is a receipt for respect shown today, never a guarantee of coming home. The work is daily and procedural rather than festival-bound: pay, record the debt, sail.

The [[Waveservants]] collect at the same rate from merchant, pirate, navy, smuggler, and pilgrim. Coin is easiest; rope, fish bones, buttons, compass needle, carved token, or spoken promise can stand in for it. Neutrality is not a creed. A shrine that takes sides loses half the harbour, so neutrality is how collection remains possible.

## Maw claim and Pearl frustration
The [[Drowned Maw]] is a puncture in the trench floor, not a metaphor: ordinary sea breaks into the Elemental Plane of Water. Umberlee's claim ends where the water stops tasting like her water. She can sink a fleet on this side, but cannot reach into elemental water to take back the [[Pearl of Souls]].

The Pearl's theft from [[Vel Orn]] left her with one mortal path to recovery. She pressed the five captains into [[Delmar Fisk]], the only survivor, while their crews remained in the Pearl. She has not killed Delmar because the captains ride him; taking him would send the five into the Maw and remove the only mortal who can fetch what she cannot touch. Frustration is not helplessness: weather, appointments, changed tribute, and collection keep the debt visible. Her command through [[Umberlee - Branca]] was: **Bring me the pearl. Then we will talk about the commissioners.**

The Pearl still broadcasts across the boundary. The [[Leviathan]] and smaller horrors answer, and something larger presses at the fissure. Recovering it would stop the signal; leaving it calls the next thing to the next fissure.

## Collection hull
The *[[Red Lady - Dead Lady]]* is the raised *Red Lady* sent to work as Umberlee's collection hull. It holds station, knows a heading, and makes defiance cost more than compliance rather than closing simply to kill. Boats vanish, holds are dumped, crews are taken whole, and the drowned original crew works the decks in silence. [[Waveservants]] who once treated Delmar like any captain now know him before he speaks.

## Tithe and symbols
The [[Tithe of the Bitch Queen]] is a separate Sunken Crown hoard: storm-taken jewels, untarnished gold, and statues in tidal caves reachable only at the decade's lowest tides. Taking a piece puts her attention on it; squalls follow its carrier until it touches saltwater again. Her clergy refuse to rule on whether the Pearl counts as Tithe.

Her mark is a wave curling left to right, carved into harbour stones and prows. In a mortal hand her preferred tool is a trident or harpoon: the tool used after the water has already decided.

## Counterweights and rivals
- [[Valkur]] is the muttered line at the rail: sailor courage, hoped-for wind, and private hope without a basin or booklet. Sailors still pay Umberlee.
- [[Tyr]] is the land-and-paper counterweight: oaths, commissions, and courts martial behind a Dravosi warrant. He does not own the wave.
- [[Auralis]] is a constructed or publicly divinized Antheri authority, a different kind of power problem rather than Umberlee's concern.
- [[Sentinels of the Eyrie]] built their monastic habit as a counter to Waveservant collection, even though their watch records rather than rules the Maw.

## The hidden bargain
The clergy do not advertise the suspicion beneath daily tribute: payments may be the procedural plug holding the Maw fissure shut. Unknown-quarry stone rises, currents reverse against tide tables, ships vanish, and old records remain unread. If a major harbour's ledgers stay red, the sea answers the lapse. Challenging Umberlee therefore risks challenging the containment system as well as a goddess.

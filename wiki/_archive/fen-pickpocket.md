# Fen ([[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] Pickpocket)

*Don't confuse this Fen with [[fen|Fen]], the young [[saltwright|Saltwright]] sailor, a different character with the same name.*

> [!read-aloud]
> A hand lifts clean from a coat pocket and disappears into the crowd. A small shape, already three bodies deep, weaves deeper and doesn't slow.

A teenage halfling, fast and small, working the [[calveno|Calveno]] festival crowds for three days during [[il-gioco-delle-beffe|Il Gioco delle Beffe]]. Not violent, not part of any operation. Use Spy stats, +5 Stealth, +5 Acrobatics.

If cornered, she has the purse she lifted, a folding knife she will not use, and a sob story that is about 40% true. The true part is that she is paying off a debt to a Bridge district broker who found her working his territory.

She carries a maintenance hatch key she cannot explain. She stole it thinking it valuable, and turns out she was right. It fits the sewer network below [[the-bridge|the Bridge]] connector and is a loaded gun for the [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|Warren: Grung in the Sewers]] investigation. Leave it in her possession unless the party specifically looks for it.

- [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|Warren (Grung in the Sewers)]]: her stolen hatch key connects here

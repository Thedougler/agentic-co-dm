> [!narration]
> *[[old-faas|Old Faas]] is solid as a brick storehouse, shorter than he used to be, two well-fitted peg legs under him: good timber, brass ferrules, proper socket work. On deck he is less steady standing still. In the shrouds he is faster than any rat.*
>
> *He answers in short, clear sentences without raising his voice. Little on a ship surprises him. Salt has worked the creases around his eyes into permanent squints.*

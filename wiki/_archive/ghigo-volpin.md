# Ghigo Volpin

*A goblin canal-otter breeder in Le Paludi who sold a lodro to Perrin's grandfather and has never once let anyone forget it.*

**Wants:** to sell the last four lodri in his pens at the price the shortage says they are worth, before the Canal Commission stops dragging and the price falls back to nothing.

**Quote:** "Two hundred. Don't make the face. The face has never once moved the price."

> [!read-aloud]
> Old, even for a goblin. Liver-spotted scalp with a white fringe over the ears, a nose that has gone soft and bulbous with age, and one eye clouded to the colour of dishwater. A canvas harness runs across his chest and under both arms, worn shiny where it takes his weight. His palms are cross-hatched with old rope burn. Below the knee there is nothing on either side, the trouser legs knotted off short and swinging. He is above you, hauling himself hand over hand along a rope strung the length of the shed, and he covers the last twenty feet faster than you crossed the room. Tanks of black canal water line both walls, and something in the nearest one rolls over to watch you without surfacing. He stops overhead and hooks an elbow through the line.
>
> "You after a lodro, or are you lost?"

**Roleplay Concept:** market fishmonger of sixty years standing, crossed with a bloodstock dealer who loves the animals more than the customers.

**Opening move:** hauling himself along the rope rig to reach the far tanks · Ghigo speaks first · "You after a lodro, or are you lost?"

**Lore Sheet:** he breeds **lodri**, the thick-furred canal-otters the boat trades use to tow skiffs through the tight cuts where poles will not fit. Six of his best went to the **Canal Commission** last month, who are dragging the canals after the [[grung|Grung]] business and paying whatever he asks. The price tripled. He has four left and a shortage price he did not set and will not apologise for. He sold a lodro to **[[vincenzo-black-jaw|Vincenzo Black-Jaw]]** decades ago, off the same pens, and mentions it to any [[rattkin|Rattkin]] who comes through the door.

**Voice & Delivery:** loud, because the shed echoes and the animals are noisy, and the volume never drops for a private matter. He talks to the lodri mid-sentence without marking the switch, and he will tell a customer to their face that he doubts they can afford it before selling to them anyway. Worldview word: **brash**.

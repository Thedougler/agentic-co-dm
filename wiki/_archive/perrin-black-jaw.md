## Backstory

Perrin is a lean [[rattkin|Rattkin]] (fancy rat), a [[bard|Bard]] (College of Lore) with a [[warlock|Warlock]] pact, custom-built background, three feet tall, with a black head, a white body, and a long pink tail. Played by [[kaden|Kaden]]. His Mortis, "The Small" (disadvantage on [[strength|Strength]] checks and saves, +2 [[charisma|Charisma]]), makes him unfit for hard ship labor, so he leads instead by reading currents, people, exits, and edges.

Used [[minor-illusion|Minor Illusion]] to conceal the doorway in [[saltwright|the Saltwright]] hold during the ambush.

The [[vestra|Vestra]] was the Black-Jaw family heirloom. Perrin's grandfather, Nona Black-Jaw's late husband, built and captained her. Perrin built his life around supporting his family through it. He lost the Vestra to something massive that came from deep below. That loss cost him money and hurt him deeply. The Vestra was the last thing his grandfather left the family; losing it still costs him more than money, and he carries guilt about crew members he thinks died. The attuned [[cloak-of-protection|Cloak of Protection]] he wears now is a poor substitute for the Manta Ray cloak he lost with the ship, a distinct heirloom, different from Delmar's own cloak.

Recently, something large and strange spoke to him from the abyss. He was not a believer before. Now a patron-level power has decided he matters. He does not know what that means.

Perrin has a unique affect (his manner, his cadence, and how he holds a room) copied from his grandfather, [[vincenzo-black-jaw|Vincenzo Black-Jaw]]. It reads like a Prohibition-era gangster, out of place and distinct even against modern Calveno. It keeps Vincenzo's memory alive. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] notices it and likes it a great deal. This stays decorative, flavor only.

His drive now is vengeance against whatever destroyed the Vestra, though he also deeply misses fishing, and he wants to support his family and become an elder of [[the-tangle|The Tangle]] who teaches young Rattkin how to move through the Dry without getting killed. He is loyal to his Run specifically, not Rattkin broadly: proud, not supremacist. He will not traffic people, though manipulation, bribery, and blackmail remain available tools. He does not mistreat people who cannot fight back, simply losing interest in them, and he sees people as useful or not useful, accepting this about himself.

His strengths run toward reading people and situations, social pressure, charm, bribery, and escape routes, backed by his growing Bard/Warlock magic (full list: [[perrin-black-jaw-spells]]). His weaknesses are a prey response when badly scared and the physical weakness of his Mortis. He also keeps limited trust for dry-landers. In a crisis, he finds the nearest exit first, not to run, but simply to confirm it exists.

Since the crew came together, voyage after voyage has tested that read. In the opening breach he laid Minor Illusion over the Saltwright's hold doorway, then broke cover through a deck gap at a second angle and shook off [[grung|Grung]] toxin gas instead of falling to it.

Word soon reached him through [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont]] that Nona Black-Jaw was hunting for him and that [[anzolo|Anzolo]] might already be at sea. He nearly killed [[ket|Ket]] with his longsword before [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]] pulled him back, dropping Ket safely with Tasha's [[hideous-laughter|Hideous Laughter]] instead, then claimed the Black-Jaw name aloud to the gathered crew over Mira's Blade.

A vision on watch put him on a worked stone floor among glowing entities, inside a sixty-foot creature's body, staring at something longer than two ships below and hearing a single word: "Grow." He carried that unease into the [[whip-shark|Whip Shark]] fight, spending his own bardic inspiration on all three crewmates before drumming them through it, then talked the ship into Calveno under a borrowed name, the *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]*. Meeting Nona in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]], he finally told her about the Vestra, and once the attacks against him stopped she gave him her sending stone and an open favor, the same crossing on which he recognized the glowing whale the instant he saw it, before meeting [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] with a theatrical bow out of a barrel.

He picked [[ponte-bassa|Ponte Bassa]] as the crew's Calveno base to keep two avian predators out of a Rattkin place, passed the boots of flying he'd won to [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] since his own bare feet need to feel the ground, and backed the Kyzil spar with healing words for Delmar and Crissdalynn. His bardic inspiration carried Crissdalynn's save against Kyzil's Downburst. He went invisible into the sewers with [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]], using his nose and Rattkin senses to track black powder, swam the length of the Grung boat's hull to count three Grung and eight barrels, and sent Nona urgent warning of the operation.

He dropped to 0 HP in the Primary Chamber fight. On the death save that stabilized him, his patron's voice reached him first, telling him his time had not come yet, and he came back coughing seawater and a full level stronger, Bard 3 (College of Lore)/Warlock 2 (Pact of the Blade).

Against [[otar-the-foul|Otar]], he opened with [[false-life|False Life]] and [[armor-of-agathys|Armor of Agathys]] in the same breath, then cast [[jump|Jump]] to clear a leech's threat range so Otar's own throw crushed it instead of him, and put two arrows through a second leech, the last a [[hex|Hex]]-fueled critical that cut it in half (session transcript: [[transcript]]).

He closed out that same engagement with [[mirror-image|Mirror Image]], a [[healing-word|Healing Word]] that steadied [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]], and a call into Nona's sending stone that brought [[enzo|Enzo]] running, then walked up to [[iacopo-fieschi|Iacopo Fieschi]] afterward and sold the Black-Jaws' loyalty to the city with a handshake and a practiced line (session transcript: transcript).

Afterward he took a favor from Nona over a cash share, talked a grieving crowd down at her table, muscled a capsized gondola upright in the canal, and haggled [[prospero-morsani|Prospero Morsani]] down to 150 gold at [[cabinet-of-morsani|the Cabinet of Morsani]] for [[the-snap|The Snap]] using his own coin plus Crissdalynn's natural-20 Persuasion assist, a wrist-mounted shield he doubles as a hand drum (session transcript: [[transcript]]).

## Arc Notes (DM Only)

**What he does not see:** the chase has become the point. The version of him that survives it may not fit the Run hierarchy anymore. He may also put his family in the path of vengeance to get there.

What destroyed the Vestra is still an open question at the DM level: the [[leviathan|Leviathan]] is the working theory, unresolved at medium confidence. [[auralis|Auralis]] actually kept him alive afterward, forging an unwitting warlock pact with him in the wreck. Perrin's own belief credits his old cloak instead, a gap that stands regardless of what caused the wreck itself.

A surviving Vestra crew member could still walk through a door, and relief would arrive wrapped in guilt. His family does not know about the ship, and he himself does not know what destroyed it beyond that the thing was massive and came from deep below. Dravosi bounties on Rattkin stay a background pressure: real, rarely enforced, always present.

The crew reached Aruhe while following the Calveno raid's survivors.


The crew's composition guarantees a dial-conflict tent pole: a faction offers to free slaves ([[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]'s terminal node) in exchange for betraying a Rattkin ally (Perrin's family loyalty), pulling both crewmates toward it from opposite directions.

At the table, his Bardic Inspiration is the crew's key force multiplier, repeatedly turning misses into hits and failed saves into passes. [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]] flagged the drum itself as a target mid-spar, and any smart enemy focus-fires him to cut off that multiplier, since he is fragile once targeted and his control spells stay concentration-dependent on top of his Mortis Strength disadvantage.

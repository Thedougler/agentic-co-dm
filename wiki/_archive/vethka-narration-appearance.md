> [!narration]
> *The *[[vethka|Vethka]]* rides lower than any cutter. Carvers shaped a single dark timber into this hull and sealed it with glossy amber resin. The resin catches light like wet skin. Outrigger floats lashed to port and starboard keep the craft steady. The bow tapers to a blunt jaw, painted in purple and blue stripes.*
>
> *Small bones adorn the rail from prow to crossbeam. The deck lies open: no wheel, no cabin, no raised structure. [[grung|Grung]] crouch here easily, staying below the rail. Resin reaches first. Under it sits something sour and sharp that is not the sea.*
>
> *From half a mile on a dark night, running low with no sail set, the hull reads as debris.*

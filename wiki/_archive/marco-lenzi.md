# Marco Lenzi

> [!narration] Marco Lenzi
> A young man with rope-burns dark on his palms and under his fingers, standing at the channel's edge watching the water move. His hands are always moving—coiling loose fiber cord, testing knots in the vines overhead. When he speaks, his eyes stay on the water, and he speaks as if repeating something he has memorized.

*Human rigger's apprentice at [[the-pantry]], in the [[the-quiet]] under [[hinewai]]'s eye*

**Wants.** To stay at the Pantry where the ground feeds him; to prove to Renzo he understands the rules and belongs there.

**Voice.** Earnest and formal, as if teaching himself. He repeats the rules under his breath while he works. "The ground gives. We take only what falls. We are safe here."

**What they share.** He tells freely that the vine fed all seven of them through every season; he shares the rules for living on Aruhe that Renzo taught him. He never says what he fears about leaving—not directly—but it surfaces when anyone asks about the walk out: "What happens if the ground stops?" He protects first the space under the vine, the mats where they sleep, and the fallen fruit that keeps them alive.

**Secret.** Marco was the last to arrive at the Pantry, a week after Renzo's voice called. He was alone and starving, and Renzo took him in without question. Marco believes he was saved by the island, that Hinewai called him the way she called Renzo, and that if he leaves, she will stop counting him as hers. The tell is his hands: they shake slightly when anyone talks about walking away. Discovery paths: asking him directly why he won't leave the clearing; watching his hands when the party first arrives; pressing him about what happens to people who break the rule.

**Connections.** [[renzo-canale]] brought him into the Pantry and taught him to hear the island's law. [[beppe-sarti]] is the other one who stayed with him, and Marco takes comfort in not being alone in his fear.

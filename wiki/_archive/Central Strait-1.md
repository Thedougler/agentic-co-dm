# Central Strait
> [!narration] Narration
> The Central Strait is the primary shipping corridor between the two island arcs: sails, patrol craft, and signal flags on open salt water. Horns and shouted names carry between distant islands. Tarred canvas and wet rigging mark the lane you can follow.


## At a glance
The primary shipping corridor between the two arcs; feels like a moving border.
## Five-sentence kernel
- **Function:** A shared sea lane connecting Crown-controlled harbours to Midchain traffic.
- **Fantastic:** A border exists as moving ships, inspection lanes, and papers rather than land.
- **Conflict:** Commerce wants speed, Crown wants legibility, smugglers want privacy, and Maw pressure changes water.
- **Promise:** Cross a contested corridor while deciding what to reveal and what it is worth.
- **Trajectory:** A new current, raid, or inspection turns ordinary traffic into an enforcement event.
## 3Fs and contradiction
- **Fantastic:** The border moves with traffic.
- **Familiar:** Busy strait with pilots, tolls, ferries, and patrols.
- **Functional:** Paperwork and visible hulls determine passage.
- **Contradiction:** Shortest route; longest record.
- **Signatures:** inspection lanes; flags and papers; wakes that no longer match charts.
## Current situation
- **Canon:** primary shipping corridor between arcs; connects [[Calder's Tooth and Port Tidefall]] and [[Midchain]].
- **DM seed (unconfirmed):** marked deep-water lane and informal edge route.

## Location moves
**Move:** inspection lane shifts after suspicious cargo or front step; flags/queues change; compare official and physical evidence; old papers no longer grant passage.

**Move:** edge route opens during weather/current window; pilots offer speed; party gains time but loses certainty; return traffic follows the wake.

**Move:** traffic stops after Maw or Red Wake sign; ships cluster; witnesses become accessible; delay advances clock.

## Geography ingest — working road
The Strait is the shipping road between the two green arcs. [[Calder's Tooth and Port Tidefall]] owns the western mouth: every hull is supposed to stop, answer questions, and show papers. [[Nimmik Vollask]] works the water, and patrol cutters own the official lane; smart captains leave it when pilots and risk allow. The Strait's northern view is Crown water and Greyteeth; its southern exits feed the pilot-named bands of the [[Midchain]].

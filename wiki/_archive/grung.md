# Grung

**Creature Type:** humanoid

**[[size|Size]]:** small

**[[speed|Speed]]:** 25 feet; climb speed equal to walking speed

Vivid, poisonous skin marks caste by color in traditional Grung society. As Grung, you have these special traits.

**Aquatic Nature.** Grung breathe air and water alike. They must submerge for at least 1 hour of every 24, or gain [[exhaustion|Exhaustion]] that only immersion removes.

**Standing Leap.** With or without a running start, a Grung can [[long-jump|long jump]] 25 feet and high jump 15 feet.

**Sticky Tongue.** A Grung's tongue can snatch small unattended objects at a distance, or lash out as a restraining hold in place of a weapon attack.

**Poisonous Skin.** A Grung's skin is a hard biological boundary around touch. Grung are immune to poison damage and the [[poisoned|Poisoned]] condition, and are proficient with the [[poisoners-kit|Poisoner's Kit]]. They can also coat weapons with a limited venom drawn from their own skin.

> [!mechanic]
> **Primal base traits — tradition: Primal, no school.** Toxic Secretion, Standing Leap, Sticky Tongue, Aquatic Nature, and diet-driven color shift are biological. [[detect-magic|Detect Magic]] returns nothing on any of them. [[counterspell|Counterspell]] cannot intercept them. [[dispel-magic|Dispel Magic]] has no effect. The [[ossketh|Ossketh]] locks color change arcane-mechanically, but the underlying trait remains Primal and undetectable.

## Caste Colors

Traditional Grung society marks caste by skin color.

| Color | Traditional Role |
|---|---|
| Gold | Clan leaders and spiritual authority |
| Red | Scholars, memory-keepers, poison-crafters |
| Purple | Warriors, hunters, law-keepers |
| Blue | Scouts, messengers, handlers of slaves |
| Green | Laborers; lowest freeborn caste |

# Elemental Plane of Water

![[elemental-plane-of-water-banner]]

The Elemental Plane of Water is one of the four elemental planes. A fissure in [[the-drowned-maw|the Drowned Maw]] breaches it, connecting the mortal ocean to this plane.

The breach appears as a crack in the floor of [[the-drowned-maw|the Drowned Maw]]. It has ragged stone edges and water that behaves differently on the far side. A faint light from somewhere below has no business being there.

### The Planar Incursion

The Border Ethereal separates the Material Plane from every Inner Plane alike: fire, water, earth, air. This boundary is universal, built the same way everywhere. What happens at the Drowned Maw is a local breach of that universal law: a puncture in one place, carved by nothing and aimed at no one.

The far side of the breach contains water that behaves oddly. The full planar nature remains undocumented. The breach predates colonial memory and is widening. When the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] sank near the fissure, it began pulling entities through. What has come through so far is small enough to fit the crack. Something far larger pushes and scrapes at the breach from the other side, trying to force it wider. Life on both sides may be fundamentally incompatible, and whatever else lives in this plane's infinite depths stays unseen and unconfirmed.

**Mortal connections:**

- [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]] fissure is the breach point between the mortal ocean and this plane.
- [[leviathan|Leviathan]] passed through this fissure from the plane into [[shattered-sea|the Shattered Sea]].
- [[auralis|Auralis]] guards the Maw and is aware of the incursion.
- [[antheri-ruins|The Antheri Ruins]] and [[shelfworks|The Shelfworks]] sit above the breach site.

## Notable NPCs

[[leviathan|Leviathan]] is the first entity through the fissure, a displaced apex predator. Ridgeback is the second, documented in [[algernon-reginald-clyde|Clyde's Bestiary]]. Krakling is the third, a juvenile arm-predator sailors are reporting near [[midchain|Midchain]]. All three have flat-black skin, no eyes, only blindsight, and water breathing.

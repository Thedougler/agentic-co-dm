> [!narration] Narration
> Thick, bark-dark vines coil through the canopy above like heavy branches that have learned to hang in loops. Wet leaves and dangling roots hide much of the tangle, but stripped bones hang caught among the higher coils where the green opens. Some vines sit unnaturally smooth against the tree limbs, thicker than the hanging roots around them. Over a trail or waterway, the coils slowly change their set overhead, wood sliding over wood with a low creak.

### Hazard — Snakewood

- **Trigger:** A Small or larger creature moves beneath an active [[Snakewood]] colony section. Snakewood reacts instinctively to movement and attacks from above.
- **Notice:** **Wisdom (Perception) or Wisdom (Survival) — `DC 14`** to spot stripped carcasses hanging in the canopy, unnatural coils among the branches, and vines slowly repositioning overhead.
- **Ambush:** The target makes a **Dexterity save — `DC 14`**. On a failure, the creature takes `1d6` bludgeoning damage, is **Grappled**, and is pulled up to `15 feet` toward the canopy. On a success, the vine lashes past and recoils into the canopy.
- **Consumption:** At the start of each **Grappled** creature's turn, feeding roots spread across exposed flesh. The creature takes `2d6` acid damage. Each consecutive round it remains **Grappled** increases this damage by `1d6`, to a maximum of `4d6`.
- **Escape:** As an Action, a **Grappled** creature can make a **Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) — `DC 14`** check to tear or slip free.
- **Sever the Vine:** The grasping section has `AC 13` and `15 HP`, and is immune to psychic damage. Destroying it releases the creature, which falls normally if suspended above the ground.
- **Movement Instinct:** Snakewood attacks movement, not creatures specifically. A thrown object, swinging pack, moving animal, *mage hand*, or similar distraction can trigger the ambush instead.
- **Placement:** Adult colonies dominate sections of [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest|the Quiet]] and [[Aruhe - The Marshes|the Marshes]], especially above game trails and waterways. Younger Snakewood appears in [[Aruhe - Old Gardens|the Old Gardens]]. Dense colonies also hunt directly above [[Aruhe - The Mangroves|Mangrove channels]].
- **Counterplay:** Move beneath it while another object is kept moving elsewhere, bait the strike before passing, cut a captured creature free, stay beneath solid overhead cover, or avoid the connected canopy entirely.

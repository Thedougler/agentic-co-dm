> [!narration]
> *[[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]] fills a gangway in a dark officer's coat, brass buttons catching what light the deck offers. His square jaw sits hard under a day of stubble. A flintlock rides one hip and a cutlass the other, both worn smooth from use, not polish.*
>
> *He does not raise his voice over the wind. He does not need to. The crew nearest him have already gone still. His free hand taps once, twice against the rail, gauging the roll before he decides where the fight starts.*
>
> *Each sentence is a command. Threat is silent until it is not.*

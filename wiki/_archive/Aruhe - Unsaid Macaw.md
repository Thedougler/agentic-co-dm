> [!narration] Narration
> Healthy macaws perch along orchard branches and lake margins, bright-eyed and loud among fruit, nuts, and shellfish banks. Red feathers cover the head and chest, with yellow and blue wings folded over a long tail, and the pale face is lined around a heavy hooked beak. A single bird can speak in a listener's own voice, repeating words that were almost said. In a flock, those borrowed phrases pass from bird to bird until later speech is hard to tell from old flock-talk.

### Hazard — Unsaid Macaw

- **Trigger:** A creature that knows at least one language comes within `30 feet` of an [[Aruhe - Unsaid Macaw]] that can see it.
- **Notice:** **Wisdom (Insight) or Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 12`** to realize the bird is repeating a current surface thought in the listener's exact voice, not merely mimicking old speech.
- **Surface Echo:** The macaw repeats the creature's foremost current thought aloud in that creature's exact voice. It cannot search memory, retrieve specific facts, or choose a hidden secret on purpose. No saving throw applies to the surface echo.
- **Confess:** Once per day, immediately after a Surface Echo, the macaw may say "Confess." The target makes a **Wisdom save — `DC 12`**. On a failure, before the end of its next turn, it speaks one truthful sentence elaborating on the surfaced thought. The effect cannot compel exhaustive explanation, passwords, histories, names, or unrelated information.
- **Flock-Talk:** A flock repeats useful phrases in several voices. Later speech in the area may be hard to separate from remembered flock-talk, especially when the same sentence returns from a different bird.
- **Where it belongs:** Orchard edges, grassland edges, lake margins, and shellfish-rich banks. Unsaid Macaws do not belong in [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest]], where birdsong is supposed to be absent.
- **Counterplay:** Step out of sight, move beyond `30 feet`, stop speaking long enough to listen, answer plainly before the flock muddies the phrase, cover a mouth before a failed Confess becomes table trouble, or drive the flock off without making a bigger noise problem.

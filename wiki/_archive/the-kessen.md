# The Kessen

*A standing ritual-combat league open to every people of the [[midchain|Midchain]] — crews and households enter as teams, not individuals, and a loss is worn by the whole tribe until the next cycle. The name started as a ring-title, not an institution — see Members.*

## Members

League officials keep no fixed seat and are named at Members' request only — the roster below is the eight standing teams that matter to the table, not a governing body.

| Team | Home | Notable |
|---|---|---|
| The Kalowe Undertow | [[kalowe\|Kalowe]] | Captained for a decade by retired champion [[the-mud-pits\|Vort Kessen]] — "Kessen" started as his ring-title, awarded after his third league championship, and stuck harder than his birth surname ever did. The league's common name is his, informally, before it was ever the institution's. |
| The Otanu Riptide | [[otanu\|Otanu]] | All-genasi roster, drowning-hold specialists; refuses to fight on dry sand as a matter of principle. |
| Toa-Kiri Ironjaw | [[toa-kiri\|Toa-Kiri]] | The league's most-feared [[lizardfolk\|lizardfolk]] roster — a three-season unbeaten streak, and deliberately never celebrates a win. |
| Halythion Nine | [[halythion\|Halythion]] | Exactly nine rostered fighters, no more, no less, by a founding vow nobody living remembers the reason for. |
| Marenui Salt-Fangs | [[marenui\|Marenui]] | Lizardfolk-majority; famous for a single legendary upset three seasons back that still gets re-told wrong every time. |
| Ket-Solane Driftguard | [[ket-solane\|Ket-Solane]] | Defensive, outlasting specialists — the crowd's least favorite watch and the league's most profitable team to bet against. |
| The Huahei Longtooth | [[huahei\|Huahei]] | Lizardfolk roster running an aggressive opening strategy; most likely team in the league to end a bout in the first exchange. |
| Corallith Tideborn | [[corallith\|Corallith]] | Mixed-species roster, youngest average age in the league, this season's crowd favorite. |

## Relationships

- [[bastian-ookage|Bastian Ookage]] — a Midchain lizardfolk himself; his crew in [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]] still remembers him running book on deck, Kessen-style, regardless of what was actually on the table.
- [[the-mud-pits|The Mud Pits]] — Kalowe's own pit, where Vort Kessen retired to after leaving the league; unaffiliated with the Kessen itself, but shares his name and legend.

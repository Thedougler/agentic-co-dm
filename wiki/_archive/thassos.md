# Thassos

> [!read-aloud]
> A Minotaur sits across the felt, a knife-scar splitting one filed horn, silver stacked in easy, unhurried piles in front of him. His smile arrives a half-second late now and then, like he's checking something behind his own eyes before he lets his face show it.

Word at the [[tallow-row|Tallow Row]] table is Thassos lost his passage-sense to drink years back, the gift every [[minotaur|Minotaur]] has, the one that lets a man walk a channel blind twenty years after he first sailed it. He's found other uses since for a mind built to hold an entire reef system: he doesn't miss much of what's left in a shoe once it starts running thin, and he plays like he already knows.

He's not smiling at all once he's sure of a thing. He said as much to [[thunk|Thunk]] mid-hand, flat and certain: *"You've been lucky a great many times. Every hand the shoe's run thin, you've bet like you already knew."* He was right, and he was the one who called it out loud.

## Relationships

- [[tallow-row|Tallow Row]]: the den where he plays most nights.
- [[thunk|Thunk]]: called out Thunk's card-counting at the table, mid-hand, in front of the whole room.
- [[old-faas|Old Faas]]: a familiar face across the felt; Faas is one of the regulars the room already trusts.

## Description

A fig tree, broad-trunked and ancient, its aerial roots hanging in curtains from every branch. The roots move. They reach. A root touches a shoulder, wraps an ankle, pulls. The trunk is hollow, and what goes inside does not come out. Bones visible in the bark where the wood has grown over old kills, half-absorbed into the heartwood.

## Ecology

Strangler figs already kill their host trees. The [[blight|Blight]]'s corruption extended the same instinct to anything that walks beneath the canopy. The tree cannot move. It does not need to. It grows where the game trails pass, and the trails pass where they always have, because the terrain funnels everything through the same gaps in the stone.

Home zones: [[aruhe-the-quiet|the Quiet]] into [[aruhe-the-rot|the Rot]]. The transition zone between jungle and deep interior is where the canopy thickens enough to hide a Huge tree among its neighbors. They grow singly, never in groves. One is enough.

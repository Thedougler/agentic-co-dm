# The Velvet Noose Takes the Lane

*A pirate man-of-war offers safe passage in exchange for one precise look inside the ship's least explainable hold.*

## Premise

The [[the-velvet-noose|Velvet Noose]] claims a stretch of the [[central-strait|Central Strait]] by sending terms before opening a gun port. Her skiff names the *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]*. Its written terms ask for the forward-hold manifest and one hour aboard. In return, they offer a sealed transit letter.

## Pressure

The terms carry a deadline, and the Noose closes no distance while it runs. No answer becomes refusal. A second tube cuts the time to fifteen minutes. Merchant hulls alter away from the lane, leaving the *Uncertainty* alone under a sail too large to fight as an ordinary ship encounter.

## Actors

- [[the-velvet-noose|The Velvet Noose]] wants control of the lane without spending powder; its leverage is overwhelming force, legal language, and a reputation for keeping stated terms.
- [[mave-sorn|Mave Sorn]] wants a declared answer she can carry back. Her leverage is the sealed offer and the deadline attached to it.
- [[uncertainty|The Uncertainty]] carries the hidden tincture, old privateer papers, and a manifest that cannot make every irregularity harmless at once.

## Discoverable / Affectable

- The sealed terms provide a concrete object for negotiation. A crew can read or destroy them. Altered terms or a display to other ships can stake out a counteroffer. Delay leaves time as a weapon.
- The requested inspection puts the forward hold in play. The sealed terms set boundaries for it. The crew may stage or refuse it. Another piece of leverage aboard the ship can turn the demand back on the Noose.
- The Noose asks about irregularity, not cargo value. The named hold, the language borrowed from inspection writs, and the lack of any coin demand point past ordinary piracy.
- A transit letter would change later Crown and pirate contacts. A public refusal would change how other crews describe the *Uncertainty*.
- The contact pulls on [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] through the tincture and on [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] through the difference between commanding a crew and gambling it against a superior fleet.

## If Ignored

The skiff returns once with the same terms and a fifteen-minute deadline. When that expires, the [[encounter-velvet-noose-intercept|Velvet Noose Intercept]] begins without another parley. Other vessels keep clear, and the empty lane makes the *Uncertainty*'s response visible at a distance.

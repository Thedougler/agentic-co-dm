# Toa

A [[moucheron|Moucheron]] mercenary from [[kalowe|Kalowe]] and a [[five-blades|Five Blades]] member, Toa is the biggest and most easy to read.

He is cheerful. He wants to know why fights go wrong. He offers his thoughts freely. His assessments prove sound.

# Uncertainty's Refit Tour

*La Vasca's workers have finished the refit. The ship still waits for names, rules, claims, and habits before its crew can call the new shape theirs.*

## Premise

The [[uncertainty]] is physically ready after La Vasca: blue-black paint, a new figurehead, a ram, a shallow keel, new forward timber, four guns, an expanded cargo hold, a chart archive, a rigger's workshop, a provisions store, a long table and bulkhead bench in the former captain's cabin, and the [[fleet-commanders-chair]] aboard. The live condition is ownership and discovery. Every space invites a claim, test, name, stock, rearrangement, joke, or omission before the ship becomes habit again.

## Pressure

The ship is leaving the cradle, and the next voyage will demand answers that the crew has postponed. The chart archive has no hand, and the [[fleet-commanders-chair]] has no rule. The cook, navigator, and surgeon posts remain open. [[geoffrey-draves|Draves]], [[sem-holst|Sem]], and [[thunk|Thunk]] have earned trust in pieces, not all at once. [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] has a rigger's workshop clean enough to offend a working inventor. Jean-Claude has hidden tincture and better storage than old sailcloth. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] has the chair, the long table, and a ship waiting for a captain's habits. The mast and rigging offer [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] a perch. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] stands on a new hull. The sea already took one from him. Pitch in the seams, tacky blue-black paint at the rail, squared-away crates, a figurehead with no name, a tool rack too clean, an unused galley, cards and dominoes in a tool chest, [[cobb|Cobb]]'s sealed ration chest, and the chair bolted at the long table show the refit in every compartment.

## Actors

- [[uncertainty]] - wants to become a lived-in ship again. Its leverage is new capacity in spaces no one has claimed. It answers touch with creaks, smells, balance shifts, clean lockers, and stubborn fittings.
- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] - wants command to feel ordinary before the next horizon. His leverage is the captain's routines, the long table, and the chair. He watches who treats the refit as theirs.
- [[old-faas|Faas]] - wants the crew to learn the ship's new bones before weather teaches them. His leverage is dry-dock knowledge and shipboard superstition. He points out tiny flaws without making them sound urgent.
- [[sem-holst|Sem]] - wants the work respected and the invoice remembered. His leverage is knowing what each new fitting can endure. He lingers near the cleanest seams and waits for someone to ask.
- [[geoffrey-draves|Draves]] - wants useful standing aboard a ship that now has better rooms than his old excuses. His leverage is familiarity with stores, cargo, and quiet movement below. He offers practical help where trust is still thin.
- [[thunk|Thunk]] - wants a place and task that makes sense on the changed ship. His leverage is strength, reach, and comfort with heavy fittings. He tests the work with careful, oversize hands.
- [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]] - wants the refit debt to remain warm without standing on deck. Her leverage is credit, tribute, and La Vasca's memory. She lets unpaid favors travel with the ship.

## Discoverable / Affectable

- **Weather Deck:** the gun crews can lay hands on the new battery. The ram plate can take a palm at the waterline. Someone can boot-test the forward timber, name the figurehead, climb the mast, work the rigging, mark the paint, or turn deck lines into routine.
- **Gun Deck:** claims can gather around the crew quarters and expanded hold. Rules can form at the chart table, archive, rigger's workshop, mess, long table, bulkhead bench, and [[fleet-commanders-chair]].
- **Hold:** the crew can open provisions and test the unused galley. Inspection storage, cargo, powder, bilge, and new keel timber can take order now or surprise someone later.
- **Social Ownership:** the open navigator, cook, and surgeon roles can become ship culture. Private claims can do the same.
- **Shipboard Customs:** before the first hard passage, the crew can name the figurehead. The chair and table can gain rules. Cargo, the workshop, and tribute can gain habits instead of waiting for a crisis.
- Personal gear can move into lockers. The crew can assign workspaces, test tools, and ask [[geoffrey-draves|Draves]], [[sem-holst|Sem]], and [[thunk|Thunk]] what changed.
- The crew can open [[barnaby-rook|Rook]]'s log, letters, and older cargo, avoid them, or give them a place aboard.
- Contraband can get a deliberate home or stay awkwardly between old habit and new storage.
- Spaces can take scratches, polish, ribbon, chalk, carving, or new names in small marks that make the refit belong to the crew.
- This situation needs no hidden conclusion: exploration, not mystery.

## If Ignored

The ship works, but the crew discovers its new shape under pressure instead of play. The cook gap shows on a long crossing, the navigator gap shows on a bad bearing, the workshop stays pristine until something breaks, the chair's cost appears when [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] needs leverage, hidden or awkward cargo remains a future problem, and the crew's debts to [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] stay out of sight until credit becomes a hook.

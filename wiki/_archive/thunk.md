# Thunk

![[thunk-narration-appearance]]

*Orc gunner aboard the HCS Surety: rated crew, master of powder and cannons.*

**Wants:** keep the Surety's guns in perfect working order and teach the crew to run them properly.

**Quote:**

Thunk grins cheerfully.

*Thunk*: No refunds.

![[thunk-no-refunds]]

![[thunk-portrait]]

> [!read-aloud]
> Enormous, middle-aged orc standing at the stern gun with the ease of someone who has lived in that spot for years and found it agreeable. His hands are the tell: calloused, knuckled, cross-hatched with scars from knowing exactly where a hot surface is and touching it slightly wrong a predictable number of times. He keeps them clean, and he cares about his tools. Canvas vest rides over forearms roped with muscle earned through decades at the docks. He nods approvingly when he sees you: "Ah. You are the new crew, ja? Good fight. I heard it from here. The explosions, nice work."

**Roleplay Concept:** dockyard metallurgist turned ship's gunner with Austrian bodybuilder-action-hero warmth.

**Opening move:** manning the stern gun · Thunk speaks first · "Ah. You are the new crew, ja? Good fight. I heard it from here."

![[thunk-very-good-fight]]

**Lore Sheet:** spent eleven years at the Crown Shipyards in [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]]. He started by casting and fitting cannons, then moved to repairs on damaged pieces. The Crown Ordnance Office awarded him a commendation in his sixth year, which he keeps in a tin. He holds a master's degree in chemistry from his homeland (not recognized in [[campaign-overview|the Shattered Sea]]). When manning the Surety's weapons, he counts as two crew and compensates for missing loaders. Proficient with smith's tools. He runs the smithy alone, and won't let a blade leave the forge until the temper's right. During Session 03's transit to [[calveno|Calveno]], he taught [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] the guns and spotted a bad mounting bracket at [[the-arsenal|the Arsenal]] district quay.

**Voice & Delivery:** deep booming baritone with a thick Austrian accent, like a 1980s action hero who speaks slowly and cheerfully with total confidence. Manner is warm and faintly amused. His compliments are sincere observations (the explosions impressed him most). His opinions on powder prove correct more often than those of trained chemists. When asked why a charge smells sharper than the batch beside it, he admits "I don't know. She just smells like that" and shares chemistry facts. Watch closely. Depth shows itself in smart remarks and precise observations. The crew realizes he was always smart if they pay attention. They simply misread him from the start.

# Nino

**Wants:** to stay alive long enough to learn what the sail now closing on the reef gap means, having touched nothing on Aruhe but fish since the night the island took Tomo.

> [!read-aloud]
> Salt-cracked skin gone sun-dark, a dyer's callus still faint on two fingers, the last trace of the trade she never finished learning. He watches long before he moves toward anything, then points instead of explaining, saving words for when nothing else will do: "Don't touch it," flat, already turning away. One hand stays closed on whatever's solid within reach.

## Relationships

- [[sandro|Sandro]], fellow castaway who swam ashore with him onto [[aruhe|Aruhe]] alongside an older man, Tomo. Tomo broke the island's only rule and ate from a shore bush. Something dragged him into the treeline by both ankles before dawn. Nino hasn't spoken a full sentence since.
- [[vethka|Vethka]], the wrecked ship she rode ashore on and shelters in. It sailed south for [[karath|Karath]] with the rest of the fleet until an easterly storm drove it onto Aruhe.
- [[aruhe|Aruhe]], the island she's stranded on. She's made herself the camp's only working lookout, watching the horizon before anyone else spots what's on it.
- [[calveno|Calveno]], his home, a dyer's apprentice out of its Beffa quarter before the raid took him.
- [[grung|Grung]], whose raid on La Finestra took him and roughly 500 others captive.
- [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]], counts her among the roughly 500 missing from the Beffa raid.
- [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]], PC connection. His vow to chase down Nona's missing is a debt Nino's survival helps answer.

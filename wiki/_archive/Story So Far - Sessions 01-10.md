# Story So Far - Sessions 01-10

The story began in the hold of the [[Saltwright]], where surrender was close enough to smell and every breath had the sour taste of it.

Above, the [[Dravosi Crown]] had its boots on the deck and its hands around the day. Below, in the dimness, the people who would become the crew of *[[Uncertainty]]* waited with powder smoke above them and no promise they would live through it. [[Barnaby Rook]] kept the wheel under his thumb. The first of the boarding party came down the ladder. A [[Dravosi Enforcer]] with a face pretty enough to earn the name Cap'n Gorgeous stepped into the dark, and the dark took him.

It happened fast, the way ugly things do when everyone has decided what they will do and no one has said it aloud. [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] covered a doorway in illusion. Gorgeous and two [[Dravosi Deckhand|Dravosi deckhands]] went through it and did not come back. Black powder burst in the cramped air, leaving [[Delmar Fisk]] and [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] half-deaf in the thunder. Delmar hurled Crissdalynn up through the hatch like a prayer with wings. She opened those wings and scattered Crown sailors across the deck. [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] and Perrin came up through a gap in the decking. Men died in the crush. One deckhand dropped his sword because some low part of him, the part that knows fire and falling, understood that the Crown had stopped being the safest thing in the world.

His name was [[Geoffrey Draves]]. Rook called him turncoat from the gangplank of the [[Uncertainty|HCS Surety]], but Delmar asked the question that mattered. Did Geoffrey want to fight for men who had threatened to shoot him, or for men who meant to keep him alive? Geoffrey picked up the sword again. Just like that, he was the whole crew.

The fight rolled across the gangplank. Jean-Claude came in with a rush of water and threw Rook overboard. A [[Dravosi Alchemist]] cracked open a canister, and Jean-Claude knew the toxin by the taste of dread it put behind his teeth. It was [[Simone]]'s work. He did not tell the others. Crissdalynn blew the cloud back. The alchemist fell. Rook climbed wet and furious over the Surety's rail, raised a flintlock at [[Beaumont Sel]], and fired. The shot struck Beaumont's salvaged shell at the shoulder and spun away into the sea.

That was the first lesson of the Shattered Sea: a bullet can miss the heart and still leave the bruise.

The Surety's guns had to be silenced before the day could be won. Beaumont threw Bisou through a gun port, where she fouled the powder. Delmar shot Ensign Wouters through another port. Then he put Rook into the water a second time, and the ship that arrived as a Crown fist began to feel like a prize with blood on the deck.

After the fighting, truth moved through the ship with quieter feet. Beaumont named Perrin a Friend of [[Passage|the Passage]] and told him that [[Nona Black-Jaw]] was hunting her lost grandson. Beaumont believed someone had been sent to find Perrin. A [[Truth Stone]] passed to Jean-Claude. He answered plainly: former kinsmen hunted him, he had stowed away, and nobody had sent him.

Below, [[Shepherd Grigori]] set terms in the galley. Anyone who wanted off at the next port would walk free. [[Ket]], a starving [[Moucheron]] Rook had kept like a specimen, was fed and released toward [[Murrat]]. The prize crew took shape around the survivors and the willing: Geoffrey, Sem Holst, Alys Kuiper, Old Faas, [[Thunk]], and Noor. Grigori meant to leave at [[Calven and Calveno|Calveno]]. Letters of marque were taken. The [[Saltwright]] parted ways. The Crown cutter remained Crown-marked, still called HCS Surety for the moment, and she turned toward Calveno with weather gathering in the east.

Then the sea hit back.

A tropical storm came in like a bad thought given wind. Something struck the hull from below. Then again. Then a third time, hard enough to send the cutter lurching to port. Perrin stood watch and was taken, for a breath or a lifetime, into a vision from below: a sixty-foot body and one command rising through the black water.

*Grow.*

The thing battering the hull was a whip-shark. They killed the whip-shark. Delmar recovered its barb. Jean-Claude took three fertilized eggs, because Jean-Claude had the sort of mind that could see horror and inventory in the same glance. Grigori worked a red light from his wrist over Jean-Claude, a light Perrin could not name.

Five days passed at sea. Barnaby Rook's shadow lengthened behind them even with the man gone from their deck. He had twice broken Imperial rules about [[Drowned Maw|the Drowned Maw]], and that mattered. A glowing outline held a parallel heading. Delmar slipped, for a time, into Admiral Fisk until Crissdalynn brought him back. Not all hauntings rattle chains. Some wear your old name and use your old voice.

Calveno did not invite Crown colors in through the main approach. The prize became something else before she came fully ashore. No prefix. No Imperial throat-clearing. Just *[[Uncertainty]]*. [[Calven and Calveno|La Vasca]] had a cradle ready for her. The water reached for Delmar and spoke his name, then went still. Grigori left, promising absence for a week or two.

On land, debts waited.

[[Umberlee - Branca|Branca]], servant of [[Umberlee]], stopped Delmar and Crissdalynn. She would not sleep until Delmar came to the shrine. Delmar kept walking. At Nona Black-Jaw's kitchen, the party told Nona that [[Vestra]] was lost. Nona mourned the money with a practicality that made grief look like arithmetic. She called off attacks already underway and asked for a favor she would not name. Perrin agreed before he heard what it was. Nona put a sending stone in his hand.

That afternoon, [[Master Kyzil]] dropped out of the sun and found Crissdalynn.

Calveno gave them roof tiles, markets, shrines, sewer mouths, and the feeling that every street was waiting to see what shape they cast. Jean-Claude sold the whip-shark eggs. He bought a silent shortbow and a pair of [[Flying Boots]] for Delmar. Kyzil sparred with them on a Ponte Bassa rooftop and said the Maw had been unruly since the wreck. He would stay through the festival.

On the way to the harbor, Delmar finally told the truth. He had been a real admiral once, not merely a man wearing a borrowed title and a grief-soaked posture. He had commanded a five-ship privateer fleet. They had stolen the [[Pearl of Souls]] from a shrine, and within hours the Drowned Maw destroyed them.

At the harbor shrine, Umberlee took Branca's body. She killed her. She brought her back. Gods do that when they want a lesson written large enough for mortals to stop pretending they cannot read it. Delmar offered the Pearl and the commissioners. Umberlee read the shape of it from his head.

Bring me the Pearl. We will talk then.

Crissdalynn swept Delmar's legs out from under him for going to a goddess alone, which may have been the kindest thing that happened at that shrine.

Then Perrin's stone spoke. Nona wanted him home, and she wanted his friends with him, especially the blue one. In [[Warren|the Warren]] sewers, she laid out the next horror: six [[Grung]] had crossed the Strait, the first crossing in living memory. She fed the party a Hero's Feast and sent them down into the wet dark under the city.

They burned a powder boat. A purple Grung called Jean-Claude a traitor before Jean-Claude ended him. They took [[Felix Aho]] alive. Felix told them the attack would come under the festival crowds in two days, with six ships behind it. [[Simone]]'s people killed anyone who tried to leave. He asked for a month of protection. Nona set [[Ruk]] to guard him.

Two days. That was all the city had. Two days until festival bombs, and the Pearl still outstanding.

So they went back into the sewer magazines with the clock already chewing itself to pieces.

[[Catarina Da'Virelli]] joined them for that push on her first descent. She came down with her cannon [[Ragnetto]] and her owl Strix, and both were fighting beside her before she finished giving her name. [[Ruma Delacroix]] gave up Room 6 without a fight. The party accepted the surrender and moved on. Two rooms later they found [[Bazzoth]] at his still. He poured his last good vintage into a bomb that smelled like rot and bad choices, and then he lost. He went into the water with his still and Magazine Beta's barrels, the second powder cache after the boat burned on the coast.

The primary chamber waited in Room 8.

Room 8 held a live ritual. [[Solange Barret]] chanted over the circle. Four [[Grung Elite Warrior|Grung Elite Warriors]] stood between her and the party, though one was already broken against the wall. On festival morning above them, ordinary life kept trying to happen. Kyzil would not play the pranks. Thunk would sign a thousand gold of cannon on *Uncertainty*'s credit. Nona Black-Jaw would throw a tomato back.

Below, Catarina threw fire and lightning, and Ragnetto fell. Delmar dropped a guard with one pistol shot. Perrin took a strike, called to his patron, and heard the words "Not yet." He stayed on his feet, coughing seawater. Solange said she would not have her princesses stopped.

[[Vashu the Weeping Veil|Vashu]] and [[Ozzeth]] died covering Solange. Crissdalynn took Vashu's hits, dodged the tongue twice, and cracked the throat. Ozzeth reached for Delmar's mind. Jean-Claude put a word in Delmar's ear, and Delmar held on. Then Delmar shot Ozzeth's arm off. Jean-Claude finished him. With his last magic, Ozzeth spent himself on Solange and told her to do it now.

Crissdalynn had Solange in her grip, grappled and poisoned. Solange left that grip as if the rules of flesh had become optional. She appeared beside Catarina. She spoke one word.

Agni.

The ceiling came down.

Only it did not simply come down. It opened. The chamber below was suddenly open to the city above, and [[Mercatura|the Mercatura]] woke to nightmare under a bright Calveno morning. Where Solange had been, [[Otar the Foul]] stood twelve feet high in a thirty-foot crater, all cracked red hide and closing wounds. Five leech-spawn crawled from the rubble. One caught a trapped villager and began to eat. The more it fed, the more human it looked.

The town answered with the Warren's old signal: hide or get ready to fight.

Kyzil took the brood. Catarina was in Otar's mouth when Delmar ended the thing with a chair and a shot through the eye. She came out into daylight. Nobody cheered. Some victories are too wet, too close, too full of other people's missing names. The carcass rotted where it fell. The party took [[Grung - Authority Seal]] and [[A Sliver of the Unstable Form]]. A scrap of paper sat in the rot, unread.

[[Fieschi|Iacopo Fieschi]] found them in the plaza and brought them to an office with the smoking crater in the window. There he signed them as Calveno's Defenders on [[Tessarine Concordat|Tessarine]] letterhead, because the Crown had not defended Calveno. Someone in that office had orders from [[Rupert Knighton]], given days before the ceiling opened, to gather what she could and evacuate. Fieschi had received no warning.

Perrin's stone warmed. Nona told them to come home.

Hundreds were missing.

In the aftermath, Calveno did what places do after the impossible tears through them. It made lists. Nona turned the Mercatura crater into a missing-persons desk, and the names climbed past three hundred fourteen fighting-age men. Jean-Claude called it slaving. Nona vowed chase in front of the crowd and sent two Passage ships after raiders who already had days on them. Fieschi paid the Tessarine debt in public with a thousand gold in trade credit, Casa Falier, and a diamond ring. Perrin took a favor instead of gold. [[Cobb]]'s refit on *Uncertainty* would be ready in twelve hours.

The party spent their pay with the urgency of people who knew the road had teeth. At [[Prospero Morsani|Morsani]], they bought [[The Snap]]. Delmar lifted a torn page that named [[Osset]], a monk who had left the [[Sentinels of the Eyrie]] in a schism. Catarina identified a corpse as Morsani's source, and the shop answered by throwing her out the door and twenty feet down the street. [[Lavinia Sordi]] sold Crissdalynn a cloak that magic named [[Nightmantle]]. Lavinia had bought it from a falcon-featured man who also called himself Osset, though Crissdalynn had never heard that name at the Eyrie. [[Zort]] offered Catarina a [[Midchain]] reptile contact in exchange for a prosthetic, and Catarina agreed to build him one.

The paint on the Defender writ was still wet. The list was still growing. The raiders had their head start. The name Osset had come from two different mouths. In a kinder world, that might have been coincidence.

*Uncertainty* left La Vasca refit and bound for [[Sparhold]], first stop on the Grung fleet's trail. Nona's spellcaster had pulled the destination from a captured purple Grung: the captives were bound for [[Karath]], the Grung center of power. For a few breaths, the party saw through the skin of a purple Grung on [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle|Aruhe]], the Hungry Isle. Two guards from a different garrison stood beside them. [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbit]] packs came out of the brush. They ran, swam, threw a spear. No rescue came.

Back aboard *Uncertainty*, Crissdalynn flew ahead over wreckage and found three sun-blasted Calveno captives on a shattered hull. An eastern hurricane had split the raid fleet in half.

Then the Crown found them again.

Two days out, [[HCS Ordinance]] intercepted *Uncertainty*: ninety-six guns under Dravosi colors. [[Corbin Knighton]] crossed first. Behind him came [[Aleksander Malone]], the Hound of [[Tyr]], hunting Shepherd Grigori. The party played merchant. They hid Perrin, Ruma, and Jean-Claude. Delmar sent the boarders toward Calveno. Catarina laughed with Umberlee's voice until Corbin reined his hound in, and the great warship stood off for the city. Nona was warned by stone that Ordinance and the Hound were coming.

Catarina gave Delmar [[Lamarae's Fang]] for his help finding an axolotl, because even in a world of gods, slavers, monsters, and warships, people still make strange little trades that somehow matter.

*Uncertainty* sailed on for Sparhold with three rescued captives aboard, while Ordinance carried the party's story back toward Calveno.

On the night voyage, Delmar trained Geoffrey. Below deck, Grigori appeared again. He thanked them for the warning sent to Calveno. He said [[Talon Vantyrus]] had sent a ship one or two hours behind them to rob them for the [[Fate Spinner]], the dreidel, and he tied that hunt to the Sentinels' schism over fate. He asked the party to injure Corbin Knighton, not kill him or break him. They gave no firm word. Grigori also spoke of Delmar's Umberlee curse, because some debts are heavy enough to show in the dark.

The follower ship came. The party ambushed it, slowed it, hit it with a broadside, and set the sails on fire. They left it listing and fled without boarding. There are decisions that feel clean only from a distance. Up close, they smell of smoke.

At last they reached Aruhe, the Hungry Isle.

They pulled two survivors aboard. Between a hundred and a hundred fifty people had come off the raid ships. Most were dead. The island was taking the rest. Taking fruit or island things had gotten people killed. Ocean fishing seemed safer. The Grung were hunted hardest, which did not make the place merciful. It only meant the island had preferences.

Delmar, Jean-Claude, Crissdalynn, and Perrin went onto the terraces. They found wolfrabbits first, then fruit marked with blood and skulls. At least six dead lay nearby. Jean-Claude killed a spore plant, and the water below answered with something worse: a [[Aruhe - Crown Squid]] rising from beneath, striking Delmar, and reaching for people to take. They escaped by illusion and flight. The squid stopped at the tree line and remained in the water, watching from the edge of its own law.

They kept *Uncertainty* moving rather than anchoring near shore.

Two survivors were aboard. Others were still inland. The captives taken from Calveno might still be moving toward Karath, if they were lucky, in the brutal sense of the word, after a hurricane and a monster island had both had their say. Ordinance and the Hound had sailed toward Calveno with a story that might or might not hold. Umberlee still wanted the Pearl of Souls. Nona's favor still waited. Osset's name had surfaced twice. Grigori's warning hung in the ship's timbers. The Fate Spinner had hunters behind it, and the Hungry Isle had mouths ahead.

That is where the story stands.

The ship has a name now, but not a promise. The crew has victories, but not safety. They have debts to gods, favors owed to grandmothers, enemies in uniforms, enemies in old blood, and a sea that remembers everything dropped into it.

Ahead, Aruhe waits.

And the island is still hungry.

# Ilaria Grimaldi

**Wants:** to keep the dispensary's fifteen-year truce with the harbour bosses intact, while she decides what to do about a [[the-passage|Passage]]-marked ring she spotted on a wounded Takowan crewman two days ago.

> [!read-aloud]
> In her early sixties, a tiefling from [[calveno|Calveno]]'s northern Scatter, small horns and grey-violet skin, a faded accent she's never lost. The scent of camphor and vinegar hangs off her sleeves, and the mortar keeps grinding under her pestle while she works, until she looks up to ask what you need.

*Ilaria Grimaldi*: "Tell me what exposed you, not what happened."

**Roleplay Concept:** a port apothecary who has seen every variety of trouble that can arrive at a harbour and finds none of it particularly interesting.

**Lore Sheet:** runs the only apothecary on Kalowe's third island. Fifteen years now. She has outlasted two harbour bosses and one merchant who tried opening a rival shop at the primary berth. A tiefling from Calveno's northern Scatter, she moved to Kalowe after a period she keeps private. She treats wounds and sets bones, never surgery, and keeps a back room curtained off for patients and for stock she won't put on the shelves. She's currently holding onto a Takowan crewman's Passage-marked ring, a dying privateer with a treasure map tattooed on her arm, and a [[sawek|Sawek]] attack survivor recovering under her care, all three live at once.

**Voice & Delivery:** exact and efficient, states a price once and expects payment without further explanation.

*Ilaria Grimaldi*: "Healer's kit or wound supplies? There's a difference."

*Ilaria Grimaldi*: "[[antitoxin|Antitoxin]]'s fifty. I have two."

Works at the counter throughout any exchange, the scale and the mortar never idle, and doesn't look up until she's named her price.

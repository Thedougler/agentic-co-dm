# The Galewall Runner's Drop

Rumor places a legendary privateer treasure cache on one of the volcanic [[ashwall-islands|Ashwall Islands]], deposited during a desperate flight from the Dravosi Admiralty in the early colonial period.

## The Fact

In the early days of colonial occupation, an infamous privateer executed a flawless heist of a [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] payroll fleet. Hunted by the full Admiralty, the crew attempted the impossible: outrunning the navy by sailing straight into the [[galewall|Galewall]].

The permanent storm belt shattered the ship's masts and pushed it back out, sinking and ruined. Before the ship slipped beneath the waves, the crew reportedly rowed the heavy lockboxes onto the black, sulfur-vented shores of one of the [[ashwall-islands|Ashwall Islands]]. No record preserves the privateer's name. Missing Dravosi payroll records from the colonial era corroborate the heist.

The cache is supposedly marked by a massive carving of a rat extending its hand, cut into the volcanic rock near the drop site. The carving predates any known colonial settlement of the [[ashwall-islands|Ashwall]] chain.

Toxic sulfur vents make extended land exploration dangerous without alchemical protection. Shifting volcanic rock causes landmarks to change season-to-season. The island on which the cache sits is not identified in any surviving record. Volcanic activity may have buried, eroded, or submerged the rat carving, if it survives. No one has recorded a successful recovery.

No one has raised this legend at the table. It remains speculative and unconfirmed.

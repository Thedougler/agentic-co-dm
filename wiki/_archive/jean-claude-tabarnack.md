## Backstory

Electric-blue skin marked by black patches covers a small, three-foot, scarred frame with the wary eyes of prey. He wears his Ranger gear with a red beret and a false moustache he calls Corto di Velo. He's [[grung|Grung]], blue caste, Botukuri clan *(BOH-too-KOO-ree)*, out of a hermit's background. A permanent Mortis curse leaves him unable to lie or mask his intentions, trading permanent disadvantage on Persuasion and Deception for [[advantage|advantage]] on Perception and Survival, though he still isn't sure whether that's a flaw or simply who he is. What you see is what he is.

He sees in the dark and hides in dim light; daylight, [[blindsight|Blindsight]], or [[tremorsense|Tremorsense]] strips that edge away, and Extra Attack doubles his damage. His [[combat|combat]] style favors walls and water and struggles in tight places, and downed foes trouble him enough at range that allies should warn him before dropping one. He reads a situation before acting, but danger flips that to pure instinct: slavery or abuse breaks his ranks outright, and he attacks instead of holding formation. Birds are his phobia. Mean or fast ones are worst, a genetic terror the table hasn't fully surfaced yet, and Crissdalynn herself is a crow [[aarakocra|Aarakocra]], which makes her a live pressure point or, played gently, a chance to build trust.

He believes touch means a mating ritual, so hugs and handshakes baffle him and he has no idea why. Those who can't fight back get his compassion, unless they're evil. He learned that code alone, not from his clan, and he respects [[umberlee|Umberlee]] for being fair in her own way: "Mean to all alike." Scared, he turns fearsome instead of freezing. He can't hide who he is; Mortis shows it plainly, and no one's sure yet if that's bad luck at talk or something deeper.

[[pell|Pell]], a gnome friend, died at slavers' hands. JC freed her, and the same slaver forces killed her anyway. The screams still haunt him. On what should have been an ordinary day, his plan was to make Pell his slave and then free her; instead he walked home to find his sister, his parents, and Pell all in chains. [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]] had betrayed them all, and the plan died with the household.

After Pell died, JC fled east through the [[midchain|Midchain]], living rough and hidden, staying one island ahead of Simone's hunters at [[sorn|Sorn]].

> [!spoiler]
> Before his conscience woke, Jean-Claude was a slave-raider. As a scout, he identified settlements, acquired slaves, and developed ambush tactics that made the Grung a more effective threat. He pursued glory in this work and excelled at it. No one knows.

For now he wants only to explore and find freedom; later, he means to return and teach the [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]] a better way. He dreams of a Grung society that recognizes every race carries its own gifts, and he lives for both halves of that dream. He trusts no one yet. He hasn't processed the slave pain, Pell's death, or the others who died escaping. He ran, and their screams still haunt him. Freedom is the one thing he'd compromise for, dark deeds included.

Simone, the sister who betrayed them, could mean violence or grief if they ever meet again. He hides her tincture, twenty vials of it stowed in the ship's cargo, and has told no one it's there.

## Arc Notes (DM Only)

His slave-raider years (see Backstory) remain unknown at the table, available as a reveal or pressure point. The twenty vials of [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s tincture he's hiding in the cargo (see Backstory) are a second concealed fact, available the same way.

> [!mechanic]
>
> **Encounter calibration:**
>
> - **Shine:** dark, water, heights, small places, crowds.
> - **Break:** bright light, blindsight, poison, or lost focus.
> - **Struggling:** add dim light or a blinded/restrained foe.
> - **Dominating:** spread foes, add blindsight/tremorsense, use daylight, or break his focus.
> - **Dramatic lever:** slavery, torture, abuse, or caste cruelty snap his calm. Force him to pick: stay in control or stop abuse he sees.
>
> **Umbral Sight/control:** dark blinds his allies too, especially [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]], though [[entangle|Entangle]] breaks on INT/CHA saves or damage. [[flask|Flask]] of Endless Water's Geyser (30-ft line, STR 13, 1d4 + prone) helps control terrain; at level 5, tongue lash for position control and contact poison both matter with Extra Attack.

The search for Simone runs alongside these combat notes.

> [!mechanic] Simone's hunt (source: situations/active/jean-claudes-desertion.md)
> Simone hunts JC as a loose end, not family. She knows he's alive outside the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] but not where. Her toxin trade with [[barnaby-rook|Rook]] burned when Rook went silent and her spy asked wrong questions in [[tidefall|Tidefall]]. [[verity-hollowell|Verity Hollowell]] in the [[central-strait|Central Strait]] could hear about it next.

Sorn anchors the search, holding Simone, Pell, his household, and slavery. The [[calveno|Calveno]] raid closes that gap. [[karath|Karath]] runs six [[vethka|Vethka]] ships with crew and captives. His slave-soldiers help but turn unreliable.

His current exposure risk: the purple handler who spotted JC is dead, so no proof links him to the Le Paludi raid. Simone's command hit but doesn't know JC helped. That stays true until [[felix-aho|Felix Aho]] talks, someone spots JC again, or the Tidefall inquiry links to a defector report.

Party composition creates a tension point: free slaves or keep the [[rattkin|Rattkin]] ally. This clash is inevitable, with both PCs pulling opposite ways.

> [!mechanic] Party tactical note (source: system/party-combat-primer.md)
> His S04 ceiling ambush on a Grung (24 damage instant kill via Dread Strike) was entirely one-sided given surprise + darkness + aerial advantage combined. Enemies that reward stealth approaches too easily make an encounter trivial for him specifically; force a tradeoff between stealth and objectives instead of letting ambush conditions stack freely.

The Calveno handler-cell arc builds on that same stealth advantage.

> [!mechanic] Handler recognition (source: inbox/narrative-islands/Calveno-raid-signs.md)
> Offer one of these per day, quiet and unprompted, when he is in any public space during the Calveno handler-cell raid prep (never more than one per session, and none of them are proof). **Crowd positioning:** a Grung standing in the one position with sightlines to three exits without appearing to watch any of them (not instinctive unless someone taught you to stand there). **Target assessment:** a figure briefly appraising a dockhand's build, tools, and guild mark, then looking away (not a glance, an appraisal). **Exit logic:** a trade-cover Grung walking a specific Le Paludi route twice at pace, marking the time (someone ran that route and handed the timing to the handlers).

Acting on that suspicion resolves into a fight.

> [!mechanic] Handler Cell combat (source: inbox/narrative-islands/Calveno-street-encounters.md)
> Triggers if he acts on the handler-recognition suspicion above, in a Le Paludi canal alley. 2 Blue-Caste Handlers (Scout stats, HP 16, AC 13, poisoned shortbow, Standing Leap); they try to withdraw and break contact, and fight only if cornered. One carries binding rope and a sealed wax message cylinder marked with a Botukuri shipping label he recognises. Capture him alive to close an extraction lane; kill both and Simone never discovers the cell; let them go and Simone learns the crew is in Calveno and his face becomes a report.

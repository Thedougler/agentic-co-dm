# Session 02 - Recap

> [!narration] Recap
> Last time on the Shattered Sea, you ambushed the [[Dravosi Crown]] boarding party in the hold of the [[Saltwright]]. [[Geoffrey Draves]] joined you, and [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] kept quiet about the toxin. You finished the fight on the gangplank of the [[Uncertainty|HCS Surety]]. [[Beaumont Sel]] threw Bisou through a gun port, and she fouled the powder. [[Delmar Fisk]] shot Ensign Wouters through the other. The Surety's guns went quiet. Delmar put [[Barnaby Rook]] into the water a second time.
>
> Beaumont named [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] a Friend of [[Passage|the Passage]]. Quietly, he said [[Nona Black-Jaw]] was hunting her lost grandson, and that she believed someone had already been sent. He passed a [[Truth Stone]] to Jean-Claude, who told the truth: former kinsmen hunted him, he had stowed away, and nobody had sent him. Geoffrey led you below. In the galley, [[Shepherd Grigori]] set his terms. Anyone who wanted off at the next port would walk free. [[Ket]], a starving [[Moucheron]] Rook had kept as a specimen, was fed and freed, and flew toward home in [[Murrat]]. The prize crew was named: Geoffrey, Sem Holst, Alys Kuiper, Old Faas, [[Thunk]], and Noor. Grigori would leave at [[Calven and Calveno|Calveno]]. Perrin told them he was a Black-Jaw. You took [[Letters of Marque]] and the rest of the prize. The Saltwright parted company. The cutter stayed Crown-marked and unnamed, still the HCS Surety, and you set heading for Calveno.
>
> A tropical storm came in from the east. The hull took an impact from below. A second. A third. The Surety lurched hard to port.

## Highlights

You took the [[Uncertainty|HCS Surety]]. [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] was named a Friend of [[Passage|the Passage]]. [[Ket]] flew for [[Murrat]]. A storm from the east, then three impacts from below. The Surety lurched hard to port.

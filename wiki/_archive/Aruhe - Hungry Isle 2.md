# Aruhe - Hungry Isle

Current table location after [[journal/sessions/shattered-sea/10/Session-10-Recap]].

## At a Glance

Between one hundred and one hundred fifty people came off the raid ships. Most are dead. The island is taking the rest. Taking fruit or other island things has gotten people killed. Fishing offshore seemed safer. The Grung are hunted hardest.

## At the table

Keep *[[Uncertainty]]* moving. Do not anchor close. Terraces hold wolfrabbits, fruit marked with blood and skulls, and at least six dead. A crown squid rose when Jean-Claude killed a spore plant, struck Delmar, and stopped at the tree line. Escape by illusion and flight has already worked once. Others remain inland.

## Wiki facts

The party had a brief vision as purple Grung on this island, with no rescue. Captives from [[Calven and Calveno]] may still be moving toward Karath if they survived the hurricane. See [[Story So Far]].

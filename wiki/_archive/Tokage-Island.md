# Totemo Tokage

> **The Island of the Lizard-folk**

Totemo Tokage is one of the large islands in the mid-chain. Most people simply call it **Tokage Island**.

The island is lush with **bamboo forests, damp rice fields, dense woodland, winding rivers, marshlands, and a central mountain range**. It has been home to the lizard-folk for many generations.

Six major clans control and steer the island's reptilian peoples. They are sometimes at war and sometimes friendly rivals, but they unite when an outside threat threatens the island.

# The Six Clans

## 1. Horntail Clan

**Role:** Master archers and samurai-style swordsmen  
**Leader:** **Akibana Besko**  
**Leader Appearance:** Dark red scales; red-headed rock agama-type lizardman  
**Primary Settlement:** **Hōnteiru no Mura**  
**Region:** Western Tokage

### Identity

The Horntail Clan is famous for its archery. Its archers can rival even the best grung archers, and Horntails are known to boast about this when they encounter grung.

They are also respected for their samurai-style swordsmanship.

### Culture

- Fight fiercely and with honor
- Treat the clan leader as a lord
- Hold their leader in great respect
- Constantly seek to improve themselves in combat
- Consider protection of the island from invaders a primary duty

### Settlement: Hōnteiru no Mura

A large Japanese-style village surrounded by fortified walls.

### Relationships

The Horntail Clan shares a type of kinship with the **Akai Hodo**.

When the island is invaded:

- The **Horntails** fight the foreign threat openly.
- The **Akai Hodo** strike from the shadows.

---

## 2. Ebony Scales

**Role:** Yakuza-style gang; drug trade; hired fighters and muscle  
**Leaders:** **Ranga Gex** and **Rango Gex**  
**Leader Appearance:** Green-and-black-scaled, muscular monitor lizardmen  
**Former Leader:** **Don Gex**

### Identity

The Ebony Scales operate more like a Yakuza gang than a traditional clan.

They gained enough status and recognition during the previous generation to become regarded as one of Tokage's clans.

The organization is currently ruled by the twin Gex brothers, **Ranga** and **Rango**, who are trying to live up to their father's name.

### Don Gex

The late **Don Gex**:

- Established the island's main drug trade
- Waged a three-year, island-spanning personal war against slavers
- Targeted both local and foreign slavers
- Killed every known slaver on the island
- Created enough fear that slavery effectively ended on Tokage

### Morality and Reputation

The Ebony Scales have no problem selling drugs, but they will kill a slaver on sight.

Their morality is contradictory, but their position on slavery is absolute.

### Services

For the right price, the Ebony Scales can provide:

- Skilled fighters
- Large, powerful brutes
- Hired muscle

### External Relations

Of the six clans, they conduct the most business with outsiders.

Their external dealings are primarily:

- Drug peddling
- Hired muscle

## 3. Omusubi Family / Ryu no Kage

### Omusubi Family

**Role:** Self-proclaimed royal family  
**Leader:** **Kinryu Omusubi**  
**Primary Settlement:** **Zennitana**  
**Region:** Southwest Tokage

The Omusubi Family claims royal authority over the island.

### Rise to Power

Early in Tokage's development, wealth and influence were concentrated among those who controlled:

- Rice production
- Fishing

The heads of the island's most prominent fishing family and most successful rice-farming family forced a marriage between their children several generations ago.

That marriage led to the rise of the **Omusubi Family**.

---

### Zennitana

**Type:** Capital city  
**Region:** Southwest Tokage

Zennitana is a large, bustling city attached to a huge harbor.

It is:

- The island's capital
- Home of the Omusubi Family
- Home of the Ryu no Kage
- Site of the yearly fishing competition

The surrounding coast is dotted with small fishing villages.

The region is known for:

- Brilliant boat makers
- Masterful swimmers
- Fishing traditions passed down for generations

North and west of the capital are many winding rivers and rice fields tended by generations of farmers and sake makers.

---

### Ryu no Kage

**Role:** Shadow organization loyal to the Omusubi Family  
**Leader:** **Madam Hino**  
**Head General:** **Biggu Baito**

The Ryu no Kage primarily consists of:

- Assassins
- Thieves
- Various kinds of magic users
- Ninjas used as decoys and agents

#### Biggu Baito

A huge, black-scaled crocodile man covered in scars.

He serves as Madam Hino's head general.

#### Madam Hino

Madam Hino's true appearance is a closely guarded secret.

Only the following are stated to know what she looks like:

- **Biggu Baito**
- The current head of the Black Jaw family, **Nona Blackjaw**

Both are sworn to keep her identity secret.

Madam Hino:

- Does not make public appearances
- Commonly sends ninjas disguised as her to meetings
- Rarely meets outsiders in person
- When appearing personally, is covered head to toe in black cloth
- Wears an Oni mask

## 4. Yuki no Kibo

**Role:** Shaolin-style monks  
**Leader:** **Master Miki Trox**  
**Leader Appearance:** Albino-scaled skink-type lizardwoman  
**Primary Settlement:** **Shōrin Hakushin-ji** and its mountain village  
**Region:** Central mountain peak  
**Deity:** **Semuanya**

### Location

At the center of Tokage is a small but extremely tall and daunting mountain range.

At its snowy summit is a temple and village carved into the stone.

Reaching it requires a dangerous mountain climb. Many lizard-folk attempt the ascent, but many do not return.

### Shōrin Hakushin-ji

**Translation:** White Heart / Mind Temple

This is the home of the Yuki no Kibo.

The temple has only a small number of practicing monks.

### The Trials

All are welcome to attempt the path.

Few pass the trials.

Most monks never reach mastery, but both students and masters are considered very strong.

### Albino Scales

Those who live on the mountain for long periods develop albino scales.

This distinguishes recent arrivals from long-term residents.

No one knows exactly why this happens.

The common belief is that the winds of their god have blown across them for so long that the color has been blown from their scales.

### Semuanya

The Yuki no Kibo worship **Semuanya**.

Semuanya does not care about:

- Morality
- Philosophy
- Grand cosmic wars

Semuanya cares that lizard-folk:

- Breed
- Hunt
- Survive

To the mountain clan, surviving:

- Freezing temperatures
- Thin air
- Scarce food

is the ultimate expression of Semuanya's will.

Despite the nature of their deity, the monks and villagers are generally regarded as kind-spoken and friendly.

## 5. Kento Clan

**Role:** Blacksmiths and ore miners  
**Leader:** **Takayama Kongo**  
**Leader Appearance:** Brown-and-orange-scaled armadillo lizardman  
**Primary Settlement:** **Kurogane-mura**  
**Region:** Base of the central mountain

### Kurogane-mura

**Translation:** Iron Village

The village sits at the base of the central mountain.

It is marked by constant smoke rising from its kilns.

### Population

The village contains many armadillo lizard-folk whose spiny scales reflect the heat of the flames.

A small number of native Tortles also live there and work in the mines.

### Industry

The Kento Clan produces:

- Armor
- Weapons
- Mined ore

Their weapons and armor are regularly purchased by the other clans.

---

## 6. Akai Hodo Clan

**Role:** Ninja clan; illusion, camouflage, stealth  
**Leader:** **Mizobasho Red Tail**  
**Leader Appearance:** Dark grey-scaled Eurasian lizardman  
**Primary Region:** Northern marshlands

### Territory

The Akai Hodo live in a northern village surrounded by:

- Marshland
- Thick forest
- Constant red mist / fog

### Composition

The clan is primarily made up of:

- Geckos
- Chameleons

### Specialties

The Akai Hodo are masters of:

- Illusions
- Camouflage
- Stealth

### Patrols

They are known to patrol the:

- Northeast side of the island
- Northwest side of the island

### Relationship with the Horntail Clan

The Akai Hodo share a type of kinship with the Horntail Clan.

During an invasion:

- Akai Hodo eliminate foreign threats from the shadows.
- Horntails engage them openly.

### Signature

Victims of the Akai Hodo are often left with no evidence of how they were killed.

The only trace is usually a **red square cut into the victim's body**, commonly on:

- The hand
- The face

# Economy and Resources

Important sources of wealth and influence include:

- Rice
- Fishing
- Sake
- Boat building
- Ore mining
- Blacksmithing
- Armor production
- Weapon production
- Drug trade
- Hired muscle

Historically, control of rice production and mastery of fishing were major sources of wealth, resources, and political influence.

# Island-Wide Social Dynamics

## Internal Politics

The six clans have existed for many generations.

Their relationships shift between:

- Warfare
- Rivalry
- Cooperation

## Response to Foreign Threats

Despite internal conflict, the clans unite when an outside force threatens Tokage.

Two clans have an explicitly described complementary defense relationship:

- **Horntail Clan:** fights invaders openly
- **Akai Hodo:** eliminates invaders from the shadows

## Slavery

Slavery was eradicated from Tokage following **Don Gex's three-year war against slavers**.

The Ebony Scales maintain a reputation for killing slavers on sight.

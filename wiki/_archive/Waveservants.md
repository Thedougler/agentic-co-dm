# Waveservants
> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Umberlee ingest pending._

## Public function
The Waveservants collect the respect owed to [[Umberlee]] at working harbours. Their salt-bleached grey clothing, tallow-and-seawater shrines, entry basin, wind-defying candles, and little booklets make the ritual ordinary and visible. A blessing is a receipt, not a promise of safe return.

## Doctrine and method
Same rate for merchant, pirate, navy, smuggler, and pilgrim. Coin is easiest; rope, fish bones, buttons, compass needle, carved token, or spoken promise will do. A person with a booklet would rather write a name and debt than preach. Neutrality is not a creed: taking sides would stop collection from half the harbour, so neutrality is how the work stays possible.

Their records turn skipped tribute into a public, actionable default. The clergy can deliver Umberlee's appointments politely before worse arrives. Under pressure they change form or rate, and the [[Chain Council]]'s effort to raise Kalowe's tribute to buy silence only teaches them who else belongs on the ledger.

## Harbour furniture
- [[Kalowe]]: a stone niche in the causeway wall at the reef-gap, under a salvaged timber roof. Every hull passes the basin and one attendant knows ships by what they owe.
- [[Calven and Calveno]] / [[Mercatura]]: a pale stone arch on the canal, shallow enough for boats to read tribute terms posted toward the water, not the door. A basin is filled from the channel; a senior records payment and a junior keeps candles and basin clean.
- [[Calder's Tooth and Port Tidefall]]: a low cut into the cliff at the south end of the Dockfront, roof pressed low. Tallow and salt come first; a junior watches candles while a senior handles the rest.

These are not temples in the land sense. They are harbour furniture, as ordinary and non-negotiable as a mooring post.

## Faces and reach
- [[Umberlee - Branca]] — senior Calveno Waveservant and appointment-keeper.
- Harbour seniors, juniors, basins, ledgers, and port infrastructure form a sea-wide ritual network.
- The hidden Vel Orn shrine is older and quieter, aimed at drowned names and water-debt rather than working-quay traffic.

## Pearl and fissure
The [[Pearl of Souls]] is one of seven Umberlee instruments. The Waveservants guarded it at [[Vel Orn]] until the Chain Council commission stole it. Umberlee's claim stops at elemental water; the Pearl remains below it and signals across the boundary. Tribute may be the procedural system holding the [[Drowned Maw]] fissure shut. The *[[Red Lady - Dead Lady]]* is collection by hull while the Pearl remains out of reach.

## Relations
- [[Umberlee]]: divine owner and source of the daily obligation.
- [[Dravosi Crown]] / [[Tessarine Concordat]] / [[Chain Council]]: political powers that use, evade, or manipulate the ledger.
- [[Sentinels of the Eyrie]]: monastic counter to collection.

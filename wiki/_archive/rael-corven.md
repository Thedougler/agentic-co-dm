# Rael Corven

![[rael-corven-banner.webp]]

Flag Captain of the *[[red-lady|Red Lady]]*, [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]] flagship. Deceased, his soul is inside [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]].

Sea-worn and solid, close-cropped dark hair going iron-grey at the temples. His clothes are plain and properly fitted. A jaw set somewhere between resolve and fatigue defines his face, along with eyes that have stopped performing patience. His hands tell a different story (sorcerer's hands, faintly luminous at the knuckles). Speaks rarely, and everything lands like a ruling.

Rael captained the *Red Lady*, the flagship, and was a sorcerer. He was Fisk's flag captain and the one closest to the Admiral operationally. They met as young crewmen on the [[midchain|Midchain]], where Fisk read people and Rael read conditions, nothing escaped them.

He was the only captain who entered [[umberlee|Umberlee]]'s shrine with Fisk, Finding the Pearl by [[detect-magic|Detect Magic]], and stood at Fisk's side when it ended. He drowned when Umberlee closed the water over all five ships, his soul passing into Delmar. Rael's voice is the loudest of the five captains inside Fisk because he was there, knows what they took, and saw it last before the water closed in.

His father was a merchant sailor, precise and careful. His philosophy was simple: prepare for the worst, work toward the best. The Navy sank his ship on false grounds. They called the cargo pirate salvage. Rael learned what had happened and what they took. He never forgave the Navy. He doesn't separate the officers from the institution.

His father's philosophy survived him. Rael absorbed it young through training rotations and victualling plans, through stocking the magazine against contingencies he hoped never to meet. The *Red Lady* ran the way it ran because Rael treated preparation as a moral position.

**When Active in Delmar:** Rael surfaces most frequently when an ally drops to 0 HP in combat. He manifests with steady, commanding presence that feels both maternal and hard. He stops performing and starts moving people with authority, warmth present but no longer in the lead. His forefront skills are intimidation and history. An environmental trigger (a woman in a dark red dress) causes him to go still and scan the face searching for recognition he won't find. The charm drops. He was loudest of the five because he was inside the shrine with Fisk when the fleet sank.

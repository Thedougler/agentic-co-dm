My name is [[rael-corven|Rael Corven]]. Flag Captain of the Red Lady.

I keep the ledger. I always have. Six barrels of salt beef, six of biscuit, six of black powder, counted twice before every crossing.

I have known Delmar longer than anyone else left standing. We were crewmen together on someone else's ship before either of us commanded anything. He read people. I read conditions. Between the two of us, very little got missed.

Slow ships sailed south, seeking safer straits. Say that clean at the end of a long watch and I'll believe you're not tired yet.

Six sealed barrels, six spare flints, six feet of slow match, coiled and dry. That's the whole magazine.

Everyone has value, whatever their station. That was never a speech. That was just how I ran a ship.

I don't raise my voice. I have never needed to.

*(wry)*

*(earnest)*

I'm not humorless. I decided, long ago, that a joke costs the same breath as a warning, and only one of those keeps a crew alive. Counting the magazine twice was never fear. Refusing to forgive an institution that drowned a good man was never bitterness. It was arithmetic, every single time, and I'd make the same trade again tonight.

*(dry)*

Steady's not a mood. It's a discipline. Somehow that's the part everyone finds funniest of all.

*(quiet)*

That's me. Rael Corven — Flag Captain of the Red Lady, keeper of every tally that never mattered again, five faces behind one man's eyes, and still, after all this, the driest man on any ship I ever sailed.

# Story So Far

Sessions 01 to 11 of [[shattered-sea]]. Season One is closed. Season Two is live on [[aruhe]], mid-watch at a river camp.

> [!narration] Season One
> Rook's men came down into the Saltwright's hold to take your ship and found you waiting between the cargo instead. Perrin put a doorway on a bulkhead that had no door in it, and the first man through brought a crossbow into a space with no room to swing one, so the boarding party lost its best man in the dark and shouted the only name they had for him, Cap'n Gorgeous. Delmar threw Crissdalynn up through the hatch and she opened her wings over the deck, and Perrin and Jean-Claude came up through the planks behind her and took the two men waiting topside. That left one young sailor with a carpenter's hands standing over his own dropped cutlass, and Rook called across that he would be shot for a turncoat, so Delmar asked him which crew he wanted to die for, and Geoffrey Draves picked the cutlass back up and stood with you.
>
> Jean-Claude turned a flask loose along the boards and the water took Rook over the side, so the Crown answered with a canister of gas, and Jean-Claude and Perrin stood in the cloud while Crissdalynn caught it in a gust and put it back into the alchemist's face. Rook came back aboard soaked and put a shot into Beaumont, and the ball hit the bright plate bolted over the patch in Beaumont's shell and whined off across the water, and Beaumont's feet did not move.
>
> Rook came at you alone the next morning and it went fast. Bisou fouled the Surety's powder through a gun port, Delmar shot the navigator through another, Crissdalynn pulled Rook's first shot aside with a gust and caught the second on her bracers, and Delmar knocked him off the rigging with a chair, and something eel-long rose out of the water beside him and he never came up. Beaumont settled his accounts after that. He named himself a Friend of the Passage, passed on Nona Black-Jaw's word that her grandson was missing, and put a Truth Stone in Jean-Claude's hands, and Jean-Claude held it and told you his own family was hunting him and that he had stowed away with nobody's blessing. Ket was down in the brig in a brass cage, three weeks thin. Jean-Claude opened his own arm into a glass for him, Perrin drew steel to finish it, and Crissdalynn put herself in front of the bars and offered Ket her own arm instead, which put Perrin on the floor laughing and gave you the moment to open the cage, and Ket fed and flew for Murrat. Grigori had the galley and a standing offer, that anyone who wanted off at the next port could go. You named a prize crew, and that night a storm came in sideways and something under the hull hit you three times, and the last one put the prize over on her side.
>
> The thing under the hull came back while Perrin had the watch, and for a breath he was inside something sixty feet long with one word in him. Grow. The shark came up where you could reach it, and Crissdalynn held it clear of the water on her talons while the rest of you killed it. Delmar kept the barb, Jean-Claude took three eggs, and the scales went over the hole in your hull. Then it was five days of open water. Crissdalynn found the Drowned Maw written twice in Rook's own charts, both crossings against Imperial law and not one word about what he met out there, and something pale and lit held your course a while without showing a sail or a hull, and in those same days Delmar went into Admiral Fisk and would not come out of it until Crissdalynn reached him.
>
> You took the Crown name off her and she came in as *Uncertainty*, and La Vasca put her in a cradle with Cobb holding the gate. Grigori shook Delmar's hand goodbye with something colder in it than a goodbye, and on that landing the water said Delmar's name out loud, and then it was only water. Branca stopped the two of them in the street with a message about the shrine, and Delmar walked on. Under the Warren Nona counted the loss in money, called off what she had already set running, and asked Perrin for a favor she would not name, and he gave it to her before she finished asking. That afternoon Kyzil dropped out of the sun and had Crissdalynn up in the air and held her, and told her what he had smelled on the wind, and was gone again, and she was left standing in the street on her own.
>
> Jean-Claude sold the eggs that morning and had spent the money by noon, and after Kyzil sparred you on a Ponte Bassa rooftop and told you the Maw had gone wrong since the wreck, Delmar told you on the walk to the harbour what he had been. Five ships, letters of marque, and a shrine robbed of the Pearl of Souls, and the Maw took all five within hours of it. At the harbour shrine Umberlee answered him. She wore Branca's body and gave it back dead and returned, and she named her price. "Bring me the pearl. We will talk then." Delmar tried to tell her who had ordered the theft and she took the name off him before he could, and Crissdalynn put him on his back in the street for going in there alone.
>
> Nona's stone called you back under the Warren, where her men were still dragging out six Grung you had just killed, the first of their kind over the Strait in anyone's memory, and she fed you and sent you down for the rest. You burned a powder boat where it sat, took Felix Aho alive, and Felix talked. Six ships behind the powder, charges stacked in the scaffolds under the streets, the crowds on the second day of the festival, purple garrisons under red-caste leaders. Calveno had two days.
>
> Felix stayed alive behind Nona's door, and the way back down led into the sewers the Grung had turned into powder rooms. Catarina Da'Virelli came along from her canal workshop with her cannon and her owl. Ruma Delacroix gave up his room instead of fighting for it, and two doors further on Bazzoth was standing at his still, and he tipped the last bottle of his own vintage into one of the bombs he had built and lit it, which took the stores and himself and the still into the water. Then you came into Room 8 with the ritual already running. Solange Barret was chanting over a circle, four of the Grung's best stood between her and the door, and one of them was already down.
>
> Above you the festival was on its second day. Kyzil wanted no part of the pranks, Thunk put a thousand gold of cannon on *Uncertainty*'s name at the harbour on nothing but your word, and Nona threw a tomato back at somebody. Below, the fight went on. Catarina lost her cannon, Delmar dropped a guard with one pistol shot, and Perrin called on his patron, got one word back, "Not yet", and kept his feet under him with the sea coming out of his lungs. Crissdalynn took the blind one, Vashu, who felt her way around the room with her tongue, and cracked her throat. Ozzeth went for Delmar's mind and Jean-Claude got there first, and Delmar's head stayed his own until the arm came off and the arrow went in. Ozzeth's last act was a hand on Solange's shoulder and two words, "Do it now." Solange walked out of Crissdalynn's grip and the poison with it, stood beside Catarina, and said one word. Agni. The ceiling came down, and something twice the height of a person stood up where Solange had been kneeling.
>
> The crater broke through into the Mercatura plaza in broad daylight, in the middle of a trading market. Otar the Foul climbed out of it, toad-built, cracks opening in his hide and running with fluid, and every wound you gave him closed while you watched. Five things the colour of him followed, and one of them ate a villager out from under a beam and grew toward a man's shape doing it. Then every door on the plaza opened and the city came out with pots and lamp oil, and Delmar's whistle brought Kyzil down out of the sky onto the brood, and Delmar finished Otar himself with a chair across his throat and a shot through the eye. He fell back into the rubble and rotted, and nobody in the square cheered, and a scrap of paper in the rot has not been read yet. Iacopo Fieschi came down from his house dressed for a signing, folding one letter over and over while he talked, and put your names on Tessarine paper as Calveno's Defenders, since the Crown had sent nobody. Somewhere in that room was Rupert Knighton's order to pack up and run, written days before the ceiling opened, and nobody had warned Fieschi.
>
> Nona took the crater lip for a desk and started writing down names, and the list went past three hundred and fourteen fighting-age men and kept climbing. Jean-Claude called it slaving to her face. She told the crowd the raiders would be hunted down, and sent two Passage ships after a fleet already days ahead of them, and Perrin took a favor out of her instead of coin. The Defender pay went out in the open, a thousand gold in credit, a diamond ring, and a house called Casa Falier, and you spent it the next morning. The Cabinet of Morsani sold you the Snap, and Delmar walked out with a torn scrap carrying the name Osset, a monk who had left the Sentinels when the order came apart, and when Catarina told the shop which corpse that scrap had been taken off she was shown the door and a good part of the street. Crissdalynn's cloak was a Nightmantle, and Lavinia Sordi named the man she had it from Osset as well, which was a name Crissdalynn had never heard at the Eyrie. At Zort's Pits the old goblin already had his new prosthetic on, warned you off the cages, and gave you the name you wanted in the Midchain. The ink on the writ was still wet, the list was still growing, and Osset had been said out loud twice in one day.

> [!narration] Season Two
> *Uncertainty* came out of the cradle for Sparhold, the first port on the raiders' trail, because Nona's spellcaster had already pulled the answer out of a captured purple. The captives were going to Karath, where the Grung keep their power. At sea the water put you inside a purple Grung on Aruhe for a few breaths, with two of another garrison's guards either side of you and the brush behind you filling with wolfrabbits, and you ran and swam and put a spear into the reeds and nobody came after you. Later in that crossing Crissdalynn found three of Calveno's people on a wrecked hull, burnt raw, off a raid ship a hurricane had broken in half.
>
> Two days out the Crown came alongside with ninety-six guns, and Corbin Knighton crossed to your deck first with a demand for your cargo and destination, and behind him on his own ship was Aleksander Malone, the Hound of Tyr, hunting Grigori. So you were merchants. Perrin and Ruma and Jean-Claude went below and stayed there, Delmar talked Knighton on toward Calveno on the story you had built, and Nona heard by stone what was sailing at her harbour. Three rescued captives came with you, and the Hound went on inside the lie.
>
> Grigori came down to you on that crossing in his red and gold coat and thanked you for the warning. He told you a hunter was an hour or two behind you, put there by Talon Vantyrus to take the dreidel he calls the Fate Spinner, and that the whole business belonged to the quarrel over fate that split the Sentinels. He asked you to hurt Corbin Knighton without killing or breaking him and got no answer, and he spoke about the curse on Delmar before he left. The hunter caught up the next day, so you took her speed, put a broadside into her, burned her sails, and sailed on without boarding and left her behind you.
>
> On Aruhe two survivors came off the island, and by their count more than a hundred people had come ashore out of the raid ships with most of them dead already and the island working through the rest. Living things belonged to the island and taking them got people killed, and the Grung had been hunted hardest of anybody. The four of you went up onto the terraces past wolfrabbits, fruit marked with blood and skulls, and six bodies in the open, and Jean-Claude killed a spore plant, and something answered him. A crown squid rose out of the canopy, its mantle lit violet from inside and its arms raking down through the branches, and it hit Delmar and reached for the rest of you, and illusion and wings got you clear of it before it had anyone, and it pulled up short at the trees.
>
> You were still up over the trees, with the squid crashing about below you, when Jean-Claude quit answering and slid off Delmar's back with his face turned up to the sun, and then a charcoal shape as broad as a sail dropped out of it on four wings, red through the feathers, put the terrace in shadow, and broke the trees around the squid with the force of the hit. Delmar pointed you at the trees, Perrin set an illusion off to one side for cover, and you went low over the terrace and inland while the two birds went at each other, with Jean-Claude wrapped in Delmar's coat.
>
> The ship and the shore both stood behind the hawk now, and smoke hung over the river about a day's walk inland, so that was the way you went. At a cold ring of stones with half-eaten fruit left in it, Crissdalynn ate a redheart berry off the ground and her wounds closed, and when she picked the next one off the stem the grass opened in every direction and the wolfrabbits came up with young hawks stooping on them, and you got clear with bird calls and a decoy of Perrin left in the grass. A cat with eight legs and wet leaves across its back came down on Delmar out of the brush, and he shot it exactly where he meant to and Perrin went onto its back, and between the two of them they drove it off.
>
> Where the river slackened again, three otters as long as a longboat had a man in a game. Crissdalynn went into the water and brought him out, and once he stood on the bank they came after him wanting to keep playing, and you wore them down with tricks until they lost interest and swam off. He gave his name as Matteo Scola and he wanted a hull under him, and he told you what you had worked out for yourselves already, that nothing comes off a living plant and anything already on the ground is yours. He also told you about the woman out in the woods who showed as a shape in the dark with orange eyes, who let the people who obeyed her live and killed the ones who did not, and that he had refused her and the otters had been his punishment. Then he ate a ghost plum and left.
>
> You slept where Matteo had been sleeping. Crissdalynn found a black flower at the water's edge with something moving about inside it, and Perrin lifted it clear of the bank on a hand of air, and it answered with a laugh in his head that would not stop until the flower had drifted off downstream. Delmar stood the first watch, Perrin and Matteo the second, Crissdalynn the last. Something out in the dark showed off a garden and asked what you made of it, and the lanterns moved it off a porcupine that had been dead a while, and you ate the meat and kept the quills.
>
> On the last watch a falcon came out of the dark at Crissdalynn. He missed her with the first pass, and on the second he went for the pack the dreidel might be in, and she put everything she had into one blow, and he shook it off and hung in her face. That is where the night stopped. The four of you are camped at the river with Matteo, the falcon is still in Crissdalynn's face and the Fate Spinner is still on her, and Jean-Claude has not spoken since the terrace. *Uncertainty* is offshore, the rest of the survivors are still up the river, and the island is not finished with them.

# Session 12 — Toward the Memorial

> [!abstract] Resolution — the tag line
> The session closes when the party can name what Skarn gained or lost, what Perrin chose when Auralis spoke, and whether the party approaches the memorial as guests, investigators, pursuers, or takers.

## Run the Beat

1. **Show the changed situation.** Return attention to the mass grave, the inland line, the party's packs, and the living route after the Climax's outcome.
2. **Surface the costs.** Name any lost Spinner access, damaged formation, Aruhe claim, delayed route, survivor disagreement, or exposed bond. Do not erase a cost because the party survived.
3. **Echo the choice.** Celia can mark the dead; Oren can point toward the memorial route; Jean-Claude's seal can remain evidence; Perrin's refusal or obedience to Auralis remains his own action.
4. **Hand back the choice.** Ask what the party does next rather than making [[Hinewai]] appear. Hinewai appears only if the party presses toward [[memorial-grove]] and the fiction gives her reason to meet them.

> [!narration] Initial Narration
> Behind you, the mass grave holds its layers of bone, paper, and dull gold metal. Ahead, the grass rises toward a darker line of trees where the wind stops at a clean boundary. Oren's route markers point inland, and Celia has enough names in her hands to prove that the dead were sent there more than once. The way forward is visible. What waits at its end is still a choice.

## Closing Image

Use the branch that matches the actual outcome:

- **Spinner secured:** The party stands with the object still in Crissdalynn's keeping while Skarn's route disappears into the grass. The memorial line remains ahead, unclaimed.
- **Spinner stolen:** The party sees the direction Skarn took and the direction the memorial route continues. Pursuit and respect now point along the same inland line for different reasons.
- **Guava consumed:** The grass is trampled around Perrin and Skarn's larger disturbance, and Aruhe's response has made the living country part of the confrontation.
- **Guava refused:** The guava hangs untouched beside the route, while Perrin's answer to Auralis becomes a private fact with public consequences.

## What Is True Now

| Outcome | Changed world |
| ------- | ------------- |
| **Skarn failed or withdrew** | The party has bought time, not safety. Skarn knows the carrier's protection pattern and can change tactics later. |
| **Skarn took the Spinner** | The party has a live pursuit choice and a clear reason to decide whether the memorial or the theft comes first. |
| **Perrin consumed the guava** | Aruhe has recognized a new living claim involving Perrin, and the party has seen that Aruhe can redirect pressure toward the larger immediate threat. |
| **Perrin refused the guava** | The party has proof that Auralis's voice can be resisted, and the patron's next pressure may depend on that refusal. |
| **The party left the route** | The two graves remain unexplained but no longer abstract. The party can return by a different edge or carry the order evidence away. |

## Consequences

- **Because the party preserved the dead,** [[celia-parel|Celia]] can carry a coherent record of the layered orders instead of a handful of anonymous scraps.
- **Because the party followed or rejected the safe route,** [[oren-vask|Oren]] either remains a guide, returns to camp, or becomes a witness to the party's choice.
- **Because the party faced the two-grave orders,** the Gold-caste objective is no longer a neutral mission statement; it is an inherited cruelty the party can expose, refuse, or investigate.
- **Because Auralis spoke,** Perrin's bond has a new visible pressure without revealing the full patron truth.
- **Because Skarn struck twice,** the next route must account for a rival who now knows the party's protections and who may pursue the Spinner beyond Aruhe.

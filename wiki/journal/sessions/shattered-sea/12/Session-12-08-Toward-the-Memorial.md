# Session 12 — Toward the Memorial

**Follows:** [[Session-12-07-The-Guava-and-the-Spinner]]
**Purpose.** Show what the day cost and kept, let each character answer for their choices, and end with [[Hinewai]] at the edge of her Grove.

**Card.** Ending Cliffhanger in every branch, because the Climax's noise, and any claim or fire, happened at Hinewai's door. Villain Escapes also applies when Skarn got away.
**Outcome.** The party stands at the door of the island's heart with or without the Spinner, and the island's keeper has come to see who they are.
**Budget.** About 15 minutes.

## Outcome branches

| Climax outcome | Card | What the party sees first | Costs visible | Rewards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Spinner kept, Skarn fled** | Villain Escapes + Ending Cliffhanger | Red-orange feathers on the roots and a gap in the east canopy. | Wounds, spent slots, and any vine welts. | The Spinner, Nello alive (if saved), Celia's full record. |
| **Spinner kept, Skarn bound** | Ending Cliffhanger | Skarn roped against the guava's trunk, laughing or silent. | As above. | A captured Countless apprentice who knows why [[talon-vantyrus]] wants the Spinner. |
| **Spinner stolen** | Villain Escapes + Ending Cliffhanger | Crissdalynn's cut strap, and the east canopy where he went to ground. | The Spinner. | Skarn cannot fly until dusk: the party knows where he is and has about two hours. |
| **Spinner traded for a life** | Ending Cliffhanger | The person Skarn held, alive on the roots. | The Spinner, given by choice. | That person, and the knowledge that Skarn keeps his word. |

## Run the Beat

1. **Confirm the outcome.** Say where the Spinner is and where Skarn is.
2. **Show the consequence.** Celia writes. If Nello lived, she writes his name with the living and crosses nothing out. If he died, she writes it with the dead in the same careful hand she used for the Grung.
3. **Pay off the stakes.** Oren tells the party the way home and says he will take the survivors back to [[spoke-ring]] tonight. Nello, if alive, asks to see the garden, just once, just from the flowers.
4. **Let them react.** Use the Character Epilogues prompts.
5. **End on an image.** Hinewai at the flower ring. Stop after her line.

> [!narration] Closing image
> The afternoon light slants into the Hollow and lies across the rings of dead, and behind you Celia's charcoal scratches on paper. Up the path, past the leaning guava, the black flowers at the edge of the clearing stir, though no wind reaches them. A tall, narrow woman stands among them where no one stood a breath ago, pale gray skin, black hair down her back, a ragged dark cloak hanging almost to her boots. Her amber-orange eyes pass over each of you, slow and courteous, and stop on Jean-Claude.

**Her line (pick by what happened).**
- **No claim, no fire:** "You may look. You may not take." Her eyes stay on Jean-Claude. "Not him."
- **Perrin ate the living guava, or anyone made a claim:** "You took from my tree at my door." Her eyes go from the taker to Jean-Claude. "And you brought me a Grung."
- **The Hollow burned:** She looks at the smoke still rising behind the party before she speaks. "You brought fire to my island." Then to Jean-Claude: "And a Grung."

Cut there. Session 13 opens on the answer.

# Session 12 — The Guava and the Spinner

**Card.** Reckoning. Skarn's theft, Auralis's order, the island's law, and the survivors' lives all arrive in the same minute.
**Entry state.** Mid-afternoon at the north rim of the [[turned-hollow]], the Grove edge, or the rim walk, per [[Session-12-06-What-Is-Chosen]]. The party is spent from the fig. [[talon-skarn]] is marked or not, holds 0 to 2 [[ghost-plum]]s (and has eaten one if he has any), and has 0 to 2 Legendary Resistances.
**Party goal.** Keep the [[fate-spinner]] and everyone alive.
**Opposition goal.** Skarn wants the Spinner and a way off the island by dusk. [[Auralis]] wants the Spinner kept from Countless, whatever it costs Perrin.
**Stakes.** Crissdalynn's bonded artifact goes to [[talon-vantyrus]]. Perrin either obeys a voice he never agreed to or refuses it. A claim at the Grove's doorstep tells [[Hinewai]] exactly who the party is.
**Pressure.** Skarn gives this attempt five rounds. After that he breaks for the east canopy and waits for dark.
**End when.** Skarn leaves with or without the Spinner, is captured, or trades for it.
**Memorable element.** The single word in Perrin's head, the guava within reach, and leafless vines uncoiling out of the trees toward something no one can see.
**Budget.** About 50 minutes. **If behind:** skip Phase 3; Skarn breaks off at the end of round 3.

**Next:** [[Session-12-08-Toward-the-Memorial]]

> [!narration] Opening image
> The water running off Perrin's cloak turns from a drip to a stream. Under the leaning guava the rim path is hard and narrow, Crissdalynn in the middle of the party, the heavy fruit hanging at arm's height on its green stem, the black flowers a hundred feet north and the Hollow's pits falling away to the south. The leaves overhead part and fall back with nothing between them. Something steps down onto the roots behind Crissdalynn, and the ground gives under a weight with no body on it. Perrin, a voice arrives inside your head, vast and patient and cold as the bottom of the sea, and it says one word. "CONSUME."

**If Skarn is visible** (he has no plums), replace the unseen footstep with the stoop: a peregrine shape folds out of the open sky over the Hollow and drops straight at Crissdalynn, and Auralis speaks as it falls. Under the guava canopy he cannot dive; he lands at the canopy's edge and walks in.

## Situation

- **What is true now.** Skarn is on the roots within 5 to 10 feet of Crissdalynn, or diving from open sky if he is visible. Perrin's cloak is streaming. The living guava hangs within Perrin's reach if he is on the rim path.
- **If nobody interferes.** Round 1: Skarn cuts the Spinner's container loose. Round 2: Snatch. Round 3: he is gone east into the canopy with it.
- **What made this the climax.** Skarn waited all day for the party to spend itself, and the fig just did that. Every thread the session planted is standing on one rim.
- **What the characters know.** Skarn's kit and priority, whether he is invisible, the island's law, and that a voice has just ordered Perrin to eat.
- **What remains uncertain.** Whether Perrin obeys, whether the island gets involved, and who holds the Spinner at the end.

### Visible levers

- **The living guava.** Perrin eats it as a Bonus Action. Charisma 25 for 1 hour. It is a claim: Perrin is marked until dawn, and at the end of his next turn both vine lashes wake and go for every marked creature within reach, Perrin and Skarn alike.
- **The cloak.** While the stream runs, Perrin knows which way Skarn is within 30 feet. His Whisker Blindsense pinpoints him at 5 feet.
- **The guava canopy.** No straight 30-foot drop under it. Skarn has to come on foot here.

## Pressure

**Tick when.** At the end of each round Skarn is still here without the Spinner.

- [ ] **1. Warning.** The cloak streams, the roots give under an unseen weight, and Auralis speaks.
- [ ] **2. Escalation.** Skarn has the carrier's measure. If the Spinner is still hidden, he uses **Kusarigama Tempest** on everyone within 20 feet of Crissdalynn and Snatches in the chaos.
- [ ] **3. Crisis.** Skarn's desperation move (Phase 3) if he is at 60 HP or less, out of Legendary Resistance, or pinned by the vines.
- [ ] **4. Consequence.** End of round 5: Skarn breaks for the east canopy, with the Spinner or without it.

**When the scene goes static.** Skarn Snatches again. If he cannot reach the carrier, he pulls the nearest defender 10 feet away with the kusarigama to open a lane.

## Opposition

### [[talon-skarn]]

- **Numbers.** **AC 19.** HP: his Hook-end HP, minus 10 if the bloodhawk hit him in Beat 03, plus 30 for his midday rest (maximum 195). Speed 50 feet, fly 90 feet. **Multiattack:** three attacks, one of which can be a **Snatch** (Strength or Dexterity save `DC 19`, holder's choice, or he has the object). **Katana** `+11`, 17 slashing. **Kusarigama** `+11`, reach 20 feet, 15 slashing and a 10-foot pull. **Sai** `+11`, 13 piercing and Disadvantage on the target's next attack. **Stunning Strike (1/Turn):** **Constitution save — `DC 18`** or Stunned. **Peregrine Dive:** after 30 feet straight down, +13 slashing and **Strength save — `DC 18`** or Prone. **Kusarigama Tempest (Recharge 5–6):** **Dexterity save — `DC 19`**, 27 slashing and a 15-foot pull or Prone. **Legendary actions (3):** Chain Snap, Crossing Sai, Wingbeat Step. **Deflect Attack** reduces a hit by 18. **Evasion.** **Step of the Falcon:** Dash or Disengage as a Bonus Action. Legendary Resistance: whatever he has left. Wisdom save `+10`.
- **Wants.** The Spinner, and to be gone before the party can follow.
- **Why now.** The party is at its weakest, and dusk is the only safe time for him to fly off Aruhe.
- **Leverage.** Invisibility if he has a plum, knowledge of where the Spinner rides if Beat 03 gave it to him, and open sky over the Hollow.
- **Opening move.** Invisible: he walks in under the canopy, two attacks on the carrier's straps and a Snatch. Visible: Peregrine Dive on the carrier from open sky wherever there is a 30-foot lane, then a Snatch.
- **Response.** Blocked, he uses Crossing Sai on the blocker and Wingbeat Step around them. Laughed at or Stunned, he burns Legendary Resistance. Grabbed by vines, he cuts at the bundle (AC 12, 8 HP) with his next attack.
- **Desperation.** Phase 3 below.
- **Line he will not cross.** He does not kill anyone who is not fighting him, and he will not harm a hostage. His threat to Nello is a bluff: a **Wisdom (Insight) — `DC 15`** check reads it.
- **Morale / exit.** He leaves at 40 HP or less, when he holds the Spinner, or at the end of round 5. **East canopy:** Dash through the trees; close it by grappling him or pinning him with vines. **Open sky over the Hollow:** a flight over the bowl draws a [[young-bloodhawk]] on a `1d6` roll of 1–3 at the end of that round (it hits for 10, and he must succeed on a **Dexterity save — `DC 15`** or drop whatever he carries into the pits below). Close it by keeping him under the canopy.

### Assets

- **[[vine-lash]] × 2** (responders). AC 12, HP 52, Speed 10 feet, blindsight 30 feet. Two Tendrils `+4`, reach 15 feet, 7 bludgeoning and Grappled (escape `DC 12`), or one Tendril and Constrict (9 bludgeoning) on a held creature. Vulnerable to fire. A grappled creature can use its action to hack the bundle (AC 12, 8 HP) and go free. They attack marked creatures only, and they stop at half HP or after 1 minute.
- **[[Auralis]]'s voice.** He speaks to Perrin once at the opening, and once more if Perrin refuses: "CONSUME. OR LOSE IT." He never compels. Perrin's choice stands.
- **Open sky over the Hollow.** Skarn's stoop lane and his dangerous way out.

## Final Battle

**Win by.** The Spinner is still with the party when Skarn leaves, or Skarn is captured.

**Lose when.** Skarn leaves the rim with the Spinner.

**Battlefield pressure.** Each round Skarn stays, he gets another Snatch. If the vines wake, they drag at every marked body, friend or rival.

### Forces

- **Villain.** [[talon-skarn]], numbers above.
- **Henchmen.** None. Skarn works alone.
- **Other pressure.** The two vine lashes if anyone is marked, the bloodhawk sky over the bowl, and Auralis's voice.

### Tactics

1. **Opening.** Cut gear, then Snatch. He goes after the Spinner's container, not the carrier's life.
2. **Control.** Kusarigama pulls drag defenders 10 feet off the carrier. **Kusarigama Tempest** when three or more are close.
3. **Punish.** A visible Spinner, a carrier who flies into open sky, and a defender who steps away.
4. **Desperation.** Phase 3.

### Ending the fight

- **Objective achieved.** Skarn breaks for the east canopy empty-handed, or lies Laughing and bound.
- **Opposition broken.** At 40 HP or less he leaves, whatever he holds.
- **Objective lost.** Skarn is in the east canopy with the Spinner. He cannot fly off Aruhe until dusk.
- **Escape / pursuit.** East canopy on foot; open sky over the bowl at the bloodhawks' mercy.

**Binding Skarn.** If he is Incapacitated (Laughter, Stunned, or 0 HP), a creature with Oren's rope can take a Utilize action to tie him. He is Restrained until he escapes with a **Dexterity (Acrobatics) — `DC 15`** check as an action.

**Stolen branch (he took the Spinner earlier).** The fight happens at the east canopy, 40 feet along the rim, where Skarn waits for dusk. He wants to survive five rounds and stay under cover. He fights with the Spinner on a cord around his wrist (a Snatch against him uses his Dexterity save `+11`). Everything else on this page applies.

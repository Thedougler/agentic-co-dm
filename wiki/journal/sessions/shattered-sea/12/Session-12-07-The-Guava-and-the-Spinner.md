# Session 12 — The Guava and the Spinner

**Run this**

Call this Climax when the party commits to protecting, abandoning, or exploiting the Spinner near the memorial route and Skarn has a credible close approach. This confrontation harvests the session's threads: imposed Gold orders, chosen survivor rules, Perrin's narrow whisker sense, Auralis's unasked-for bond, and Skarn's pursuit.

## Opening Image

> [!narration] Initial Narration
> The route tightens around a living guava tree and the edge of the memorial's stillness. Crissdalynn stands near the object Skarn has crossed Aruhe to steal, while Perrin is close enough to feel the air move against his whiskers. The grass bends once beside them, then rises. No body stands in the space, but something is already within reach.

## Situation

- **Opposing objective.** [[talon-skarn]] wants the [[fate-spinner]]. He is invisible if he still has a Ghost Plum's effect or another established means of concealment, and he uses close range, reach, and confusion rather than a prolonged duel.
- **Party objective.** The party decides whether to keep the Spinner with Crissdalynn, let Skarn take it to protect someone else, turn Aruhe against him, or withdraw from the memorial approach.
- **Auralis's warning.** When the danger is imminent, Auralis says exactly, **“CONSUME.”** This is an attempted direction, not a compulsion. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] chooses whether to eat the living [[giants-guava]], refuse it, or take another action.
- **Perrin's detection.** Perrin can detect Skarn only within 5 feet through his Whisker Blindsense. If Perrin stays near Crissdalynn, he can call out a close approach even while Skarn remains Invisible. He cannot use the sense through Total Cover or at range.
- **Aruhe's law.** Eating or removing a living guava is a living claim under [[taking-on-aruhe]]. If Perrin takes the guava, Aruhe converges on Perrin and Skarn; the larger visible threat is Skarn, but Perrin is still part of the response.

## Visible Levers

- **Stay close.** Perrin can keep within 5 feet of Crissdalynn and become the party's close warning against Skarn.
- **Break reach.** The party can change the spacing, use cover, force Skarn away from the Spinner, or make the route too costly for a clean grab.
- **Use the fruit.** Perrin can consume the living guava after Auralis's warning and accept Aruhe's convergence as a new field condition.
- **Refuse the fruit.** Perrin can refuse Auralis and protect the party's agency while facing Skarn without the guava's effect.
- **Change the objective.** The party can let Skarn escape, sacrifice position to protect Jean-Claude, or retreat toward the mass grave and leave the memorial line intact.

## Pressure

| Trigger | DM response |
| ------- | ----------- |
| Skarn reaches Crissdalynn's space | Resolve his attempt against the Spinner and let the party interrupt, block, bargain, or change the target. |
| Perrin calls Skarn's location | Skarn spends movement on escape, a feint, or a risky grab; he does not become immune to the party's response. |
| Perrin consumes the living guava | Aruhe converges on Perrin and Skarn. Treat Skarn as the larger immediate target, but apply the established consequences to Perrin as well. |
| The party protects the memorial boundary | Hinewai need not appear yet; the party can use the stillness as a boundary while the pursuit continues. |
| The party abandons the Spinner | Skarn gains the objective only if the fiction gives him access; the party's choice changes the next route rather than granting him a scripted success. |

## Opposition

Use the existing [[talon-skarn]] owner page for Skarn's numbers and abilities. He makes the second attempt to take the Spinner, not to kill a random PC. He treats Crissdalynn as the protected carrier and everyone else as a combatant or obstacle. If he gains the Spinner, he leaves by the first credible route; if the party corners him, he can bargain, flee, or fight for the objective. Do not preserve a planned escape if the party removes every plausible exit.

## Outcome

| If the confrontation ends with… | What becomes true |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| **The Spinner secured** | Skarn is driven off, captured, or forced to abandon the attempt. The party keeps the object but now knows he will attack the carrier rather than wait for a better battlefield. |
| **The Spinner stolen** | Skarn escapes only through an established opening or a player-created gap. The party must choose whether to pursue, protect Jean-Claude, or return toward the memorial. |
| **The guava consumed** | Aruhe converges on Perrin and Skarn. The chaos gives Perrin a larger immediate threat to work around, but it is a consequence of Perrin's chosen action, not a reward that erases the claim. |
| **The guava refused** | Auralis's warning becomes a revealed pressure on the bond. Perrin retains agency, and the party must solve the theft attempt with its own formation and resources. |
| **The party retreats or reframes** | The memorial remains intact, the second attempt changes the party's route, and the next scene records what the party chose to protect rather than forcing a victory state. |

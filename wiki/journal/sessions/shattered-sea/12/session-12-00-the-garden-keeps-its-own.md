## Clues

- [ ] Every seal-bound Grung believed the order was their own conviction; none knew they were compelled.
- [ ] The Gold caste wants the two graves destroyed because the island's curse is anchored under the memorial tree; breaking it reopens Aruhe to [[karath]].
- [ ] [[hinewai]] counts every Calveno who ate fallen fruit as hers. The island's responders never attack them, though ordinary animals still hunt them.
- [ ] Nothing fruits along [[the-burnt-road]]; the island will not feed anyone along the burn.
- [ ] Ghost-plum pollen at the Pantry's treeline shows an invisible creature as a pale shimmer while it is inside the drift.
- [ ] [[terror-bird]]s will not follow prey into eight-foot grass, deep water, or white [[razer-grass]].
- [ ] [[carlo-ferrante]] believes his brother and nephew died in the dark.
- [ ] [[tommaso-brasca]]'s raft is built only from what fell, so the island lets it float. The coast is days away, so the survivors leave on foot with the party.
- [ ] [[matteo-scola]] knew [[renzo-canale]] and Tommaso in the first days after the wreck and split from them when Renzo chose the voice.
- [ ] Skarn saw Matteo vanish at the otter hole in Session 11, which is why he cuts Matteo's sling.

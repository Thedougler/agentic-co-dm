# Session 12 — The Garden Keeps Its Own

## Session Compass

**Opening situation.** Dawn at the [[river-slack-basin]] camp: [[talon-skarn]] is five feet from [[crissdalynn-khinriss]], mid-theft, with one Legendary Resistance spent and the [[fate-spinner]] still on her.

**Immediate pressure.** Keep the Spinner, then follow the inland Calveno survivors before the island or the bloodhawks thin them further.

**Session question.** Can the party bring the Calveno out of Hinewai's garden and keep the Fate Spinner through Skarn's last attempt?

**If the party does nothing.** Skarn tries again at dusk wherever the party camps. The four in the lava tube starve at the bottom of their skylight. The seven at [[the-pantry]] stay [[hinewai]]'s for good.

**Dramatic spine.** Everything that eats from Aruhe belongs to it, and leaving costs something.

**Now:** [[session-12-01-dawn-strike]]

**On deck:** [[session-12-02-the-smoking-skylight]] · [[session-12-03-terror-birds]]

## Floating Clues

- [ ] Every seal-bound Grung believed the order was their own conviction; none knew they were compelled.
- [ ] The Gold caste wants the two graves destroyed because the island's curse is anchored under the memorial tree; breaking it reopens Aruhe to [[karath]].
- [ ] [[hinewai]] counts every Calveno who ate fallen fruit as hers. The island spares them from its responders, though ordinary animals still hunt them.
- [ ] Nothing grows fruit on [[the-burnt-road]]: the island will not feed along the burn.
- [ ] Ghost-plum pollen at the Pantry's treeline shows an invisible mover as a pale shimmer within the drift.
- [ ] [[terror-bird]]s will not follow into eight-foot grass, deep water, or white [[razer-grass]].
- [ ] [[carlo-ferrante]] believes his brother and nephew died in the dark.
- [ ] [[tommaso-brasca]]'s raft is built only from what fell, so the island lets it float. The coast is days away, so the survivors leave on foot with the party.
- [ ] Matteo knew [[renzo-canale]] and Tommaso in the first days after the wreck; he split from them when Renzo chose the voice.
- [ ] Skarn watched Matteo vanish at the otter hole in Session 11, which is why he goes for the sling.

### If play stalls

**Advance pressure.** A shadow crosses high overhead once, too fast to be a bloodhawk. Skarn is still following.

**Surface information.** [[luca-ferrante]] counts aloud the fallen-fruit piles his uncle's group left on leaves, one every few hundred feet north-east.

**Offer a consequence.** [[ettore-ferrante]]'s splint slips, and he goes grey and silent. The column cannot move faster than a walk while he is carried.

**Parachute.** A [[wolfrabbit]] pair crossing the trail ahead, hunting something else, forces the column to freeze or detour for ten minutes.

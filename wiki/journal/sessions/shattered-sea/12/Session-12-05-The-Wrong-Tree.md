# Session 12 — The Wrong Tree

**Card.** Monster, with a Rescuers objective. A rooted predator that the Grung mistook for a memorial tree.
**Tier.** Fright / Henchmen. One CR 8 [[strangler-fig]] that cannot move.
**Thread under test.** What [[Session-12-04-Two-Grave-Orders]] revealed: the fig hunts movement and eats what waits, and the diggers died because they took from it. Can the party take Nello back without becoming the next ring?
**Entry state.** The party is at the south rim or already on the floor of the [[turned-hollow]], with whatever plan they made (decoy, rope, fire, or none) and the minutes left on the trunk clock. [[oren-vask|Oren]] holds the rim; [[celia-parel|Celia]] is at the first ring.
**Trigger.** The first rescuer crosses into the fig's 60-foot blindsight, 60 feet from the trunk.
**PC objective.** Get [[nello-castri|Nello]] out beyond the fig's 20-foot reach, alive.
**Opposition objective.** The fig wants to feed. It lashes at movers and draws one held creature into its trunk when nothing in its curtain moves.
**Stakes.** Nello's life, the party's hit points and spell slots before Skarn's move, and whether the Hollow burns within sight of [[memorial-grove]].
**Ends when.** Nello is beyond the fig's reach and no one is left in the curtain, or Nello dies, or the fig drops to 63 HP and goes still. About four rounds.
**Memorable element.** The deer-stalker's antlers slide out of a gap in the trunk and drop into the pit, and everyone understands at once what the empty trunk is for.

> [!narration] Open on Action
> Every hanging root on the near side of the fig swings toward the first moving thing on the floor, all at once, like a field of grass turning in a gust. The gold plates in the bark clatter. Pits and spoil heaps crowd the ground between you and the tree, and the Grung dead lie thickest in the last few strides. Nello hangs fifteen feet out from the trunk, roots looped around his chest and arms, holding perfectly still. Behind him, a pale branch of antler slides out through a gap in the trunk and drops into the pit with a hollow knock. Then another. The dark inside the trunk is opening up, and Nello's eyes go to it.

### Opposition

- **[[strangler-fig]] × 1.** Huge plant, **AC 15**, **HP 126**, Speed 0. Resistant to bludgeoning and piercing; **vulnerable to fire**. Blindsight 60 feet; blind beyond it. **Multiattack:** two Root Lashes, plus Hollow-Trunk Engulf on one creature it already grapples; or three Root Lashes. **Root Lash:** `+8`, reach 20 feet, 12 bludgeoning, and the target is Grappled (escape `DC 16`); it can hold three creatures. **Trail-Pinch (Recharge 5–6):** a 30-by-10-foot line starting within 60 feet, **Dexterity save — `DC 16`**, 18 bludgeoning and a 10-foot pull toward the fig on a failure, half and no pull on a success; the line becomes Difficult Terrain until its next turn. **Hollow-Trunk Engulf:** one Grappled Large or smaller creature is pulled into the trunk and Restrained, taking `4d6` bludgeoning at the start of each of the fig's turns. One creature at a time.
- **Intent.** Feed. It does not chase and cannot follow.
- **Opening move.** Two Root Lashes on the first creature that moves within 20 feet. Trail-Pinch on the densest line of rescuers inside 60 feet.
- **Adapts.** Movers draw it. While anything moves in the curtain, it lashes at movers instead of engulfing Nello. When nothing moves and the trunk is empty, it engulfs a held creature: Nello, or a Grappled rescuer if one is held closer to the trunk.
- **Breaking point.** At 63 HP or less, it pulls its roots in and goes still for 1 minute, releasing everyone it grapples except anyone already in the trunk.
- **Exit.** None. It is rooted. A party can end the fight by getting everyone beyond 20 feet of the trunk.
- **The root holding Nello.** AC 15, 15 HP, resistant to bludgeoning and piercing, vulnerable to fire. Destroying it frees him and does not damage the fig. Attacking it is fighting back, not a claim.

### Battlefield / Chase / Hazard

- **Space.** The fig stands 100 feet from each rim. Its root curtain reaches 20 feet from the trunk, and Nello hangs 15 feet out on the south side. The floor between the rim and the curtain is Difficult Terrain; crossing 80 feet of it costs 160 feet of movement. The rim walk is firm ground and stays 100 feet from the trunk. Everything within 60 feet of the trunk is inside its blindsight; Invisible creatures are not hidden from it.
- **Hazard.** Pits. A creature pulled, shoved, knocked Prone, or running blind within 5 feet of a pit edge makes a **Dexterity save — `DC 10`** or falls in, takes `1d6` bludgeoning, and lands Prone; climbing out costs 10 feet of movement.
- **Interactive features.**
  - **Decoys.** Anything that moves inside the curtain at the start of the fig's turn takes its attention: a swung pack on a rope, a thrown stone each round, *Mage Hand* waving an object, or an illusion the roots can touch. A decoy object takes one Root Lash and is held. While a decoy moves, Nello is not engulfed.
  - **Oren's rope.** Oren anchors 50 feet of hemp rope at the south rim. A creature tied to it can be hauled 20 feet toward the rim when an ally at the anchor takes a Utilize action, even while Grappled; the haul ends the grapple on a **Strength (Athletics) — `DC 16`** check by the hauler.
  - **Lift the roots.** A rescuer adjacent to Nello can take an action to peel the roots off him with a **Strength (Athletics) — `DC 16`** check. Success frees him. The rescuer is moving in the curtain and draws the next lashes.
  - **Pull from the trunk.** A creature adjacent to the trunk can take an action to drag an engulfed creature out with a **Strength (Athletics)** or **Dexterity (Acrobatics) — `DC 16`** check.
  - **Fire.** Perrin carries 10 flasks of oil and a tinderbox. A thrown flask that hits (AC 15) and is lit deals 10 fire damage to the fig after vulnerability. *Green-Flame Blade* damage is doubled against it. Burning a root that grapples someone is fighting back, not a claim.
  - **Flight.** Crissdalynn can fly over the pits and ignore the Difficult Terrain, keeping under 20 feet up. She can carry Nello out once he is free. Above 30 feet over the open bowl she is in [[bloodhawk]] sky.
- **Change.** If the fig takes 20 or more fire damage in one round, its dry hanging roots catch. The fire burns the curtain for 1 minute (`1d6` fire to anyone who ends a turn inside it) and then dies on the bare dirt. The smoke column rises straight up out of the bowl, in plain sight of [[memorial-grove]].

### The island's law here

- **Not a claim.** Attacking, cutting, or burning a root that grapples anyone in the party or Nello; hitting the fig after it attacked.
- **A claim.** Cutting roots to clear a path before they attack, prying gold plates out of the bark, or digging. The cutter is marked until dawn under [[taking-on-aruhe]], and the fig targets that creature before anyone else for the rest of the fight.
- **Oren's reaction.** He shouts "That's a claim!" at the first cut of any kind. He is wrong about roots that are holding someone and right about roots cut to clear a path. A **Wisdom (Insight) — `DC 10`** check tells a player that Oren is guessing.

## Discoveries

- **Fighting back is not a claim.** No leaning plants and no silence follow a cut to a root that is holding someone. A clearing cut brings both.
- **The fig eats in turns.** The trunk holds one creature at a time, which is how the skeleton and Nello both lasted.
- **Nello's garden.** Once free, Nello says the woman told him her garden lies past the Grung place, and he is this close.

| **Anything else** | The fig cannot move, kills what moves in its curtain, and eats what waits. Skarn has watched all of it from the east rim. | [[Session-12-06-What-Is-Chosen]] |

**Carry forward.**
- **Nello:** alive with the party, or dead.
- **Party resources:** HP, spell slots, Focus, Bardic Inspiration, and oil flasks spent. Skarn chooses his moment by this.
- **Marked PCs:** anyone who made a clearing cut or pried a plate.
- **Fire:** whether the Hollow burned, and whether the smoke rose in sight of the Grove.
- **Fig:** active, still, or dead.
- **Positions:** the party is on the Hollow floor or its rim; the north rim path is 100 feet from the fig.

**Immediate question.** Take Nello back south, or follow his path to the north rim and the tree the Grung never reached.

**If the session ends here.** Across the bowl, under the trees past the north rim, the black flowers stir, though no wind reaches them.

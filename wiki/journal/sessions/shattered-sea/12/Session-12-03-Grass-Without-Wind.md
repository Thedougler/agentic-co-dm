# Session 12 — Grass Without Wind

> [!abstract] At a Glance
> **Stakes.** The party must decide whether the route, the packs, or the invisible movement deserves immediate attention.
> **Contest.** [[talon-skarn]] is shadowing the party after the first theft attempt and is looking for a second opening rather than a fair fight.
> **Change.** By the end, Skarn is driven off, exposed, kept at a distance, or still close enough to threaten the next travel beat.

> [!narration] Initial Narration
> The grass is high enough to hide a hand, but not high enough to hide what moves through it. A pack strap lies open where it was tied. A bitten purple skin rests on the mud, and a line of blades bends toward the party before lifting again one stalk at a time. There is no wind on the route, yet something keeps changing the shape of the grass.

## Open on Action

The party is traveling from the death trail toward the valley mass grave. If Skarn stole a Ghost Plum in the Hook, the bitten skin is from that theft and the missing fruit is confirmed. If he did not steal one, use a disturbed pack, a displaced seal scrap, or a single impossible footprint as the evidence that he remains close. Do not force a second theft here; this contest makes the later attempt legible.

## Run the Beat

1. **Name the pressure.** The party has a visible route forward and a nearby invisible actor who can alter packs, spacing, or evidence.
2. **Let them engage.** They can secure the packs, spread out, move as a protected group, use sound or area control, follow the grass, or abandon the route to flush Skarn into a worse position.
3. **Resolve the contest.** Use the established fiction and ordinary checks only when an outcome is uncertain and costly. Success exposes Skarn's route or forces him to give ground. Failure costs time, a resource, or a better position; it does not make the party lose a required clue.
4. **Hand back the choice.** The party decides whether to press toward the mass grave, stop and investigate, or change its travel formation.

## Opposition

[[talon-skarn]] wants information and leverage more than blood. He tests the edge of the party's formation, touches a pack only when attention turns elsewhere, and abandons the contest if a clean exit is no longer available. If a Ghost Plum is already missing, he has a reason to remain invisible. If it is not, he can still use the pack disturbance as a false trail and save his decisive attempt for the living guava near the next route.

## Pressure

| State | Sign | What changes |
| ----- | ---- | ------------ |
| **Secure** | The party closes packs and keeps a coherent formation. | Skarn loses the easy leverage and must risk a visible reach later. |
| **Split** | Someone follows the grass or carries a pack away from the group. | Skarn can choose the weaker edge and the party reaches the mass grave under tension. |
| **Exposed** | The party corners or catches him. | He gives ground, leaves a false trail, or spends his advantage to keep the Spinner in sight. |

## Resolution

- **If the party secures the route:** Skarn remains a known threat but does not gain a new resource. The party reaches the mass grave with its packs and evidence intact.
- **If Skarn keeps the advantage:** He remains invisible at the edge of the next scene and has learned who guards Crissdalynn, who carries Jean-Claude, and who watches the fruit.
- **If the party drives him off:** He withdraws toward the grass and can return only through a credible route or later opportunity. The party still knows that the first theft was not his last interest.
- **If the party leaves the route:** The death-trail clue travels with them, but the valley mass grave may be reached from a less safe edge.

# Session 12 — Grass Without Wind

**Card.** Pursuit. [[talon-skarn]] stalks the column across [[cutoff-lip]] and [[print-braid]], looking for his second opening.
**Tier.** Scare / Grunts. Two [[wolfrabbit]]s that want Skarn more than the party.
**Thread under test.** Skarn's theft, and the island's law as [[oren-vask|Oren]] explained it in [[Session-12-02-A-Name-for-Matteo]]: does the party's formation hold, and does the law really care who it punishes?
**Entry state.** Mid-morning. The party walks north with [[celia-parel|Celia]] and Oren toward [[spoke-ring]], carrying Jean-Claude or walking with him awake. Matteo is with them only if the party talked him into it.
**Trigger.** Skarn makes a living claim beside the column, and the grass leans toward it with no wind.
**PC objective.** Reach Spoke Ring with the Spinner, the packs, and everyone standing.
**Opposition objective.** Skarn wants to learn where the Spinner rides and who guards it. The wolfrabbits want whoever made the claim.
**Stakes.** Wolfrabbit wounds, a cut pack, and whether Skarn walks away knowing the party's defense.
**Ends when.** The wolfrabbits withdraw or drop and Skarn is gone into the wood fork canopy, about three rounds after the burst.
**Memorable element.** Two wolfrabbits bound straight past the party at empty air, and a young bloodhawk strikes something invisible above the grass in a burst of peregrine feathers.

## Before Open on Action

Ask the players for their marching order on the inland strand: who is next to Crissdalynn, who carries Jean-Claude if he is still catatonic, and whether packs are lashed shut. That answer sets Skarn's read.

| Formation | What Skarn does | Is he marked? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tight.** Perrin within 5 feet of Crissdalynn, packs lashed, and the Spinner out of sight. | He cannot find an opening. He tears a living [[giants-guava]] off the jungle rim and throws it into the middle of the column so the beasts break it apart. | Yes. |
| **Loose.** Anything else. | He walks invisible beside the column, slits the rear pack's strap, and watches where Crissdalynn's hand goes. He learns where the Spinner rides. | Only if he picked living fruit (next row). |
| **Skarn has no plums.** He left the Hook with none. | Before the column arrives he picks a living [[ghost-plum]] in a wood fork. He is marked, and the wolfrabbits come for him first. | Yes. |

When Skarn has no plums, open on the wolfrabbits chasing a visible peregrine out of the wood fork. He eats the plum in the air and vanishes in front of everyone.

> [!narration] Open on Action
> Every stalk of grass along the braid bends the same way at once, toward the jungle fork on your right, and there is no wind. The birds stop mid-call. You stand on the packed inland strand, the grass taller than any of you on both sides, the jungle wall a long stride to the east with pink-gold guavas bowing over it. Oren drops into a crouch with both hands flat on the dirt. Then the grass on the left splits in two long lines, fast and low, closing on the column from thirty feet out.

### Battlefield / Chase / Hazard

- **Space.** The packed inland strand is 5 feet wide and ordinary ground. The grass on both sides is 8 feet tall, Difficult Terrain, and heavily obscures beyond 10 feet. The jungle wall and its wood fork stand 15 feet east of the strand; the claim point is at the fork mouth.
- **Hazard.** Open sky over the grass belongs to the bloodhawks. Anyone who flies higher than 30 feet above the grass draws the [[young-bloodhawk]]'s stoop on round 2 if Skarn is not already its target.
- **Interactive features.**
  - **Jungle wall.** A creature can climb 10 feet into the hanging roots with a **Strength (Athletics) — `DC 10`** check. Wolfrabbits can reach 15 feet high with a jump, so climbing to 20 feet puts a creature out of reach.
  - **Porcupine meat.** The party cooked porcupine in Session 11 and saved the quills. Throwing meat 20 feet or more away draws one wolfrabbit to it for one round. Meat is not a claim.
  - **Stillness.** Oren's rule is to go still because they hunt what moves. A creature that takes no movement on its turn gives wolfrabbits no 20-foot approach, so their Pouncing Bound cannot knock it Prone.
  - **Fallen guava.** Fallen [[giants-guava]] lies in the grass at the jungle rim. Picking one up is receiving, not a claim. See [[print-braid]] for identifying it.
- **Change.** On round 2 the bloodhawk stoop knocks Skarn into the wood fork canopy. The sky strike proves to everyone watching that something invisible was up there.

## Discoveries

- **Skarn is invisible and nearby.** The wolfrabbits run to empty grass, a strap is slit with no hand near it, and feathers burst from bare air. Perrin's Whisker Blindsense confirms it if Skarn comes within 5 feet.
- **The law hunts the taker.** The beasts pass the party to reach the claim, and they turn on the party only when the taker is out of reach. A **Wisdom (Survival) — `DC 15`** check reads it from their tracks; Oren says it outright if asked.
- **Fighting back is not a claim.** Killing or driving off a wolfrabbit that attacked the party draws no new response. Oren does not know this and flinches at every blow.
- **Skarn marked himself.** If he made a claim, he is marked until the next dawn, and Aruhe's responders will find him anywhere within 60 feet today.

**Carry forward.**
- **Skarn marked:** yes or no.
- **Skarn plums:** his count after this beat (he eats one to vanish if he was visible).
- **Skarn knows where the Spinner rides:** yes or no.
- **Spinner:** Held or Stolen.
- **Party:** HP, anyone who was Prone or bitten, and any pack that was cut.
- **Law learned:** whether the party saw that responders hunt the taker and that fighting back is not a claim.

**Immediate question.** Push on through [[spoke-ring]] and [[star-cut]] toward Nello's trail, or stop to deal with a thief who can be anywhere.

**If the session ends here.** A single red-orange feather drifts down out of an empty sky and lands on the packed strand in front of Crissdalynn.

## Travel on to the Turned Hollow

After the contest the column walks the rest of the way without a roll: [[print-braid]] to [[spoke-ring]] (about an hour), then the north spoke to [[star-cut]], then an hour north through the Quiet to the [[turned-hollow]].

- **Spoke Ring.** Four woven mats lie around the cold stone ring. Nello's mat is the one with his boots set side by side at its foot. His bare prints leave by the north spoke.
- **Star Cut.** Fallen [[giants-guava]] lie in the leaf litter under the west wall. Picking up a fallen one is receiving. **Wisdom (Survival) — `DC 15`** finds one ripe enough to work (Primary Ability score 25 for 1 hour); `DC 20` finds a prize fruit that stays good until dusk. A party that carries a working fallen guava to the Hollow gives Perrin a lawful choice in the Climax.
- **Water.** The west spoke at Spoke Ring reaches clear river water in 30 feet. Jean-Claude can soak here; it is not claimed water.

# Session 12 — Two-Grave Orders

**Development — the bump**

**Purpose.** Make the history of obedience concrete without revealing the full Death Bloom.

**Trigger.** The party reaches the valley where the death trail becomes a mass grave.

**Turn.** Bodies, seals, and order scraps prove that Gold knew expeditions were dying and kept sending replacements; the latest fragments move from “go inland and report” toward “find and destroy two graves.”

**Exit.** The party can choose to follow the route toward [[memorial-grove]], return with the survivors, or reject the order's objective while carrying its evidence.

**Target.** About forty-five minutes.

> [!narration] Initial Narration
> The grass drops into a shallow valley where the soil has been turned over many times. Bones lie in layers instead of a single burial, some wrapped in rotted cloth and some still wearing the marks of Grung rank. Broken seals glint between ribs and roots. At the far end, the bodies stop at a clean line of living green that leads inland toward darker forest.

## Run the Beat

1. **Present the situation.** The valley is a mass grave outside the protected memorial. The party can examine bodies and scraps without touching either of the two graves at [[memorial-grove]].
2. **Let them engage.** [[celia-parel|Celia]] sorts the layers. [[oren-vask|Oren]] points out the route that survivors used to avoid living claims. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] can compare authority marks if the party brings the seal close or asks him to read.
3. **Make the turn.** The order trail is now explicit: report, replace, find, destroy. The cruelty is institutional and repeated, not a single Grung's panic.
4. **Hand back the choice.** The party decides whether the two graves are a target to investigate, a place to protect, or a route to approach cautiously.

| [[celia-parel|Celia]] | Preserve names and the change in orders. | Offers the sorted sequence; withholds it from anyone who treats the grave as loot. | The party records a name or refuses a body. |

## Checks & Costs

- **Automatic.** A careful look shows at least three burial layers and more than one generation of authority seals.
- **Intelligence (Investigation) — `DC 13`.** **Success:** Reconstruct report, replacement, and two-grave fragments. **Failure:** Celia or Jean-Claude supplies the missing sequence after the party spends time and gives Skarn a better opening.
- **Intelligence (History) or Wisdom (Insight) — `DC 14`.** **Success:** See that the order system treated dead Grung as replaceable workers. **Failure:** The party still sees the words and must supply its own judgment.

## Player Levers

- **Evidence.** Compare the scraps to [[grung-authority-seal]] and decide whether to carry a fragment forward.
- **People.** Ask Celia what she refuses to forget, or ask Oren why he follows rules he does not trust.
- **Route.** Continue toward the two-grave memorial, turn back toward the survivor camp, or approach from another edge.
- **Refusal.** Reject the final Gold objective without needing to disprove that the graves matter.

**If the Beat Stalls**


Celia places one scrap beside each burial layer and reads only the verbs she can confirm: report, replace, find, destroy. The party does not need a complete translation to choose what it will not do.

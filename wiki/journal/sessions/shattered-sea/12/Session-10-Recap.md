# Session 10 - Recap

Aruhe. The island is still hungry.

> [!narration] Recap
> Two people came to you on that crossing. Delmar had Geoffrey Draves up on deck through the night watch, drilling him, and Shepherd Grigori came below deck with his wine glass and his red and gold coat on. Grigori thanked you for the word you had sent back to Calveno, and then told you that Talon Vantyrus had put a hunter on your wake, running an hour or two back, to take the dreidel he calls the Fate Spinner, and that the whole hunt sat inside the Sentinels' schism over fate. He asked one thing of you, that Corbin Knighton be hurt and neither killed nor broken, and he took no answer when you gave him none. Before he left he spoke about the curse on Delmar.
>
> The ship he had warned you about caught up the next day. You took the speed out of her, put a broadside into her, and set her sails burning. Then you sailed on and left her listing on the water, and none of you went aboard.
>
> Aruhe came after that, and the island had been busy. You pulled two survivors off it. Between a hundred and a hundred and fifty people had come ashore out of the raid ships, and most of them were already dead, and the island was working its way through the rest. Carrying off what belonged to the island had killed people. Fishing off the shore had not. The Grung had been hunted hardest of anybody there.
>
> The four of you went up onto the terraces. Wolfrabbits moved in the grass, fruit hung in the branches with skulls and blood drawn on it, and six bodies at least lay out in the open. Jean-Claude put an end to a spore plant, and something answered it. A crown squid came up between the trees with its mantle hung over the eight arms, its tendrils trailing, and it struck Delmar and reached for the rest of you. You were out from under it on illusion and wings before it could take another one, and it stopped at the tree line and did not come after you.
>
> You kept *Uncertainty* moving rather than anchoring close to that shore. Two survivors were aboard, more of them were still inland, and the island was still hungry.

## Wiki facts

[[shepherd-grigori]] came aboard on the night crossing to name [[talon-vantyrus]] as the sender of the ship hunting the [[fate-spinner]], tied the hunt to the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels]]' fate schism, and asked the party to hurt [[corbin-knighton]] without killing or breaking him; he left with no answer, and he raised [[delmar-fisk]]'s [[umberlee]] curse in the same visit. [[delmar-fisk]] also had [[geoffrey-draves]] drilling on deck that night. The follower ship was slowed, broadsided and set alight, then left listing and never boarded.

On [[aruhe]] the party took two survivors off a landing where between one hundred and one hundred fifty people had come ashore from the raid ships and most had died. Living claims on the island killed people, and the [[grung-clans|Grung]] were hunted hardest. On the terraces the evidence sat in the open, fruit marked with blood and skulls and at least six bodies, and killing a spore plant brought the [[crown-squid|great crown squid]] up out of the canopy until the party broke contact on illusion and flight. *[[Uncertainty]]* stayed off the shore.

# Session 12 — What Is Chosen

**Card.** Personal Stake. The woman who owns this island hates Grung and knows one is coming, and a voice with its own claim on Perrin is about to speak.
**Thread advanced.** Chosen versus imposed obedience: the Gold order, the woman's rule, and the bond Perrin never agreed to. Also Jean-Claude's danger at [[memorial-grove]].
**Entry state.** Mid-afternoon at the north rim of the [[turned-hollow]], after [[Session-12-05-The-Wrong-Tree]]. [[nello-castri|Nello]] is alive with the party or dead in the fig. The party is short on slots and HP. Skarn is watching, marked or not, with 0 to 2 plums.
**Trigger.** The party climbs to the north rim under the leaning guava, where the Grove's black-flower ring shows through the trunks 100 feet on.
**Turn.** Nello told the woman that a Grung came to her island on wings, so Jean-Claude is walking toward a guardian who hates his people and already knows he is here.
**New direction.** Enter the Grove as guests, turn back with the survivors, or hold the rim, and decide what each person here is willing to obey.
**Memorable element.** Seawater dripping from the hem of Perrin's cloak onto dry roots, far inland from any sea it could have come from.
**Target.** About 20 minutes.

> [!narration] Opening
> The path climbs out of the bowl under a guava tree that leans its whole weight over the rim, one heavy pink-gold fruit hanging at arm's height on a green stem and another lying split and brown in the roots below. The air under the leaves is cool and smells sweet, a little rotten. A hundred feet on, through dark trunks, a ring of black flowers circles a single fruit tree standing over two low mounds, and nothing in that clearing moves at all. Behind you the Hollow ticks and creaks. Celia is already looking back at the rings of dead, and Oren is looking at Nello. Then, on the dry roots at Perrin's feet, a drop of water lands. Another. It is running off the hem of his cloak, cold, and it smells like deep ocean.

## Run the Beat

1. **Present the situation.** The graves the Gold order wanted are in plain view. Nobody is in the Grove. The survivors each want something different, and the party has to choose which way to walk.
2. **Let them engage.** Anchors: the view of the Grove, Nello's ribbon and his secret, the guava on its living stem, and Perrin's dripping cloak.
3. **Make the turn.** Nello confesses (see Actors), or if he died, Oren says what the listeners know: "She asks who comes. She always asks." The seawater keeps dripping.
4. **Hand back the choice.** Ask what the party protects first: the Spinner, Jean-Claude, Nello, or its own right to refuse every voice here. Then fire the Climax when they commit or try to rest.

## Checks & Costs

- **Automatic.** Everyone sees the two mounds, the black flowers, and the dripping cloak.
- **Wisdom (Insight) — `DC 10`, on Nello.** **Success:** he is hiding something about the woman and Jean-Claude. **Failure:** he seems only frightened.
- **Intelligence (Nature) — `DC 15`, on the fallen guava.** **Success:** it has turned and does nothing. **Failure:** it looks edible; eating it is a sour mouthful and nothing more.
- **Intelligence (Arcana) or Wisdom (Insight) — `DC 15`, Perrin on his cloak.** **Success:** the water is not rain or river; it is cold deep seawater, and it thickens when he faces east. **Failure:** his cloak is somehow soaked.
- **Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 20`, facing east.** **Success:** leaves on the east rim bend under weight with nothing on them (Skarn, if Invisible), or a peregrine shape sits in the shadow of the canopy (if not). **Failure:** the east rim looks empty.
- **Cost of the good part.** Nello's confession costs gentleness, and forgiving him is the party's choice to make.

## Player Levers

- **Person.** Nello can walk ahead into the Grove and speak for Jean-Claude. Celia can hold the party to its promise. Oren can lead the survivors home.
- **Thing.** A working fallen guava from [[star-cut]], if the party carried one, lets Perrin eat one without a claim. The living guava is a claim ([[taking-on-aruhe]]); the two north-rim [[vine-lash]]es are its responders.
- **Place.** The guava's canopy denies a stoop on the rim path. The Grove's open clearing does not.
- **Promise / problem.** The Gold order is 100 feet from done. Nobody at this rim has to finish it.

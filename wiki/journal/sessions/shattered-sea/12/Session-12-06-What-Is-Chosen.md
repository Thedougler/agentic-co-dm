# Session 12 — What Is Chosen

> [!abstract] Development — the bump
> **Purpose.** Give the party an explicit choice about the memorial route before the second theft forces a closer one.
>
> **Trigger.** The party reaches the inland line of the rule-bound route or refuses the followers' path and pauses to decide its own approach.
>
> **Turn.** Celia and Oren explain that they follow Aruhe's rules by choice because those rules have kept them alive; they do not worship [[Hinewai]] or accept Gold-caste orders as rightful. A living [[giants-guava]] is close enough to become either a temptation or a danger.
>
> **Exit.** The party commits to approaching the memorial, bypassing it, or using the route as a defensive position while Skarn closes.
>
> **Target.** About thirty minutes.

> [!narration] Initial Narration
> The forest thins without becoming open. Ahead, a line of darker trunks stands around a patch of still air, and the grass between you and that line is marked by fallen twigs that Oren placed one at a time. A living guava hangs low enough for a hand to reach, while another fruit lies split and browning beneath it. Beyond the green, something has made a space that the rest of the island does not enter.

## Run the Beat

1. **Present the situation.** The memorial is close, but the party has not entered it and [[Hinewai]] has not appeared. The living guava is visible as an immediate choice, not a required solution.
2. **Let them engage.** The party can ask Celia and Oren about their rules, study the route, leave the fruit, take it, approach the memorial, or turn toward Skarn's signs.
3. **Make the turn.** Auralis senses that the next danger is about to arrive, but the patron's warning belongs to Perrin and does not remove Perrin's choice. Skarn is close enough that staying still is also a decision.
4. **Hand back the choice.** Ask what the party protects first: the Spinner, Jean-Claude, the fruit, the route, or the right to refuse every voice in the scene.

## Situation

- **Where.** The final route edge before [[memorial-grove]], with the protected stillness visible beyond the grass.
- **Present.** [[celia-parel|Celia]] and [[oren-vask|Oren]] can answer questions and disagree about pace. [[talon-skarn]] is an invisible, credible pressure if he survived the earlier contests. [[Auralis]] is present only through the unwitting bond with [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]].
- **Immediate want.** The party must choose an approach that reflects its own judgment before Skarn turns the choice into a target.
- **Friction.** The rule that protects the memorial is also a refusal to take the living guava, and the party may have urgent reasons to want it.
- **Pressure.** Skarn wants the Spinner. Aruhe reacts to living claims. Auralis wants Perrin to use the guava but cannot make him do so.
- **If ignored.** Skarn chooses the next opening, and the party reaches the Climax with less control over formation and distance.

## Revelations

- [ ] **Core.** The followers' obedience is chosen, conditional, and practical; it is not the same thing as Hinewai's hatred or the Gold-caste's command. → **Leads:** [[Session-12-07-The-Guava-and-the-Spinner]].
- [ ] **Support.** The memorial's boundary is visible before Hinewai appears, and the party can approach it without taking living fruit. → **Leads:** [[memorial-grove]].
- [ ] **Optional.** Auralis's voice may direct Perrin toward a choice that helps the patron's unknown objective. → **Leads:** [[Auralis]].

## Actors

| Actor | Wants now | Offers / withholds | Posture changes when |
| ----- | --------- | ----------------- | -------------------- |
| [[celia-parel\|Celia]] | Wants the party to distinguish respect from obedience. | Offers the survivors' reasons; withholds endorsement of the Gold objective. | The party calls the dead expendable or treats the Grove as a prize. |
| [[oren-vask\|Oren]] | Wants one last clear route decision. | Offers the safest approach he knows; withholds a promise that it is safe from Skarn. | The party knowingly makes a living claim. |
| [[Auralis]] | Wants Perrin to use the guava as a hand against the immediate threat. | Offers an early warning and the word “CONSUME.” | Perrin obeys, refuses, or chooses a third action. |

## Player Levers

- **Ask.** Celia and Oren can explain why they follow rules without calling them sacred.
- **Approach.** The party can enter the memorial line as guests, circle it, or stop at the edge.
- **Protect.** Keep Perrin within 5 feet of Crissdalynn, keep Jean-Claude covered, or use a different formation.
- **Refuse.** Leave the living guava untouched and face Skarn without Auralis's proposed tool.

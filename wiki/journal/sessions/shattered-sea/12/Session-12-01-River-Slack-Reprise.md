# Session 12 — River Slack Reprise

**Card.** Play a Cliffhanger — Confrontation. Session 11 stopped mid-fight, so the tension is already on the table.
**Key.** Action. The next beat is a Development: [[Session-12-02-A-Name-for-Matteo]].
**Thread.** [[talon-skarn]]'s theft of the [[fate-spinner]], carried in from Session 11.
**Climax question.** Can the party keep the Fate Spinner out of Skarn's hands, and whose voice will they obey to do it?
**Something happens.** Skarn has already shrugged off [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]'s Stunning Strike with a Legendary Resistance and is 5 feet from her, going for whatever holds the Spinner.
**Why it matters.** The Spinner is Crissdalynn's bonded artifact and a gift from [[master-kyzil]]; [[talon-vantyrus]] wants it, and Skarn is his apprentice.
**Decision.** Protect Crissdalynn and the Spinner, stop Skarn, or protect [[matteo-scola|Matteo]] and his plums. The party cannot cover everything in three rounds.
**Hook lands when.** Skarn breaks contact or drops, and the party has chosen what it does about him at dawn.
**Memorable element.** A CR 13 aarakocra monk who ignores every blow that is not between him and the object, and who steals a sling of invisibility fruit when the object slips away.

> [!narration] Open on
> Grey light is just starting over the eastern ridge. The slack beside you lies flat and turquoise while white water hisses past the rock lip, loud enough to swallow a shout. Crissdalynn, the peregrine you struck is already rolling your blow off his shoulders, five feet from you on the wet sand. A long straight sword hangs in one fist. In the other, a hooked blade swings from a black chain wrapped around his wrist. His amber eyes are not on your face. They are on your satchel strap, your hips, and your hands. Twenty-five feet behind you, the ring of the chain carries to the bedrolls around the coals, where Matteo is up on his knees clawing at his pack and Jean-Claude lies still in Delmar's coat. The chain starts to lift.

## Decision handles

- **Hide the Spinner.** Keeping it out of sight forces Skarn to cut gear. A Snatch needs the Spinner visible or its container cut loose.
- **Hand it off.** Passing it to another carrier makes Skarn switch targets on his next turn. A pass he can see gives him a new target, not a new problem.
- **Deny it.** Throwing it into the slack keeps it from Skarn for now. It costs a claim on the river and gives Skarn the plums instead.
- **Protect the plums.** Guarding Matteo means Skarn leaves with nothing that makes him invisible later today.
- **Laugh him down.** *Tasha's Hideous Laughter* is the one spell that stops him cold. He has two Legendary Resistances to burn against it.

## Action setup

- **Objective.** Skarn wants the Spinner and a clean exit before sunrise. The party wants to keep the Spinner and not lose anyone.
- **Opposition.** [[talon-skarn]] — **AC 19**, **HP 195** (current per Entry state), Speed 50 feet, fly 90 feet. **Multiattack:** three attacks, and he can replace one with a Snatch. **Katana** `+11`, 17 slashing, reach 5 feet. **Kusarigama** `+11`, 15 slashing, reach 20 feet; pulls a Large or smaller target 10 feet toward him. **Sai** `+11`, 13 piercing, and the target has Disadvantage on its next attack. **Stunning Strike (1/Turn):** **Constitution save — `DC 18`** or Stunned until the start of his next turn. **Kusarigama Tempest (Recharge 5–6):** **Dexterity save — `DC 19`** for each chosen creature in 20 feet, 27 slashing and a 15-foot pull or Prone; half on success. **Legendary Resistance:** 2 left. **Legendary actions (3):** Chain Snap, Crossing Sai, Wingbeat Step. **Reaction:** Deflect Attack reduces a hit by 18. **Bonus action:** Step of the Falcon (Dash or Disengage).
- **Snatch (Session 12 ruling).** Replacing one attack, Skarn grabs an object from a creature within reach, using the kusarigama at 20 feet. The holder makes the save in Checks. An unattended object within 20 feet needs no save.
- **Tactics.** Opening: two attacks on Crissdalynn's gear, and two hits in one turn cut the Spinner's container loose. Countered: a Sai on the blocker, a Wingbeat Step around them, then back to Crissdalynn. Break point: he leaves at 97 HP or less, when he holds the Spinner, or at the end of round 3. Exit: Step of the Falcon, then low along the bloody bank into the trees. On the way out he uses a Chain Snap on Matteo's sling if Matteo or the pack is within 20 feet of his path. He steps past anyone who drops to 0 HP.
- **Terrain.** The slack stops him; he will not enter water. The grass wall hides anyone 10 feet into it. The fire gives light for 20 feet; beyond it is dim light.
- **Escalation.** Round 1: he cuts gear. Round 2: Snatch, or a Kusarigama pull that drags the carrier 10 feet away from her defenders. Round 3: **Kusarigama Tempest** if two or more defenders are within 20 feet, then Snatch. End of round 3: sunrise, and he goes.
- **End condition.** Skarn leaves, is Grappled and held until he spends his action escaping, or drops. If he drops to 0 HP he is Unconscious, not dead, and the session recomputes around a captured rival.

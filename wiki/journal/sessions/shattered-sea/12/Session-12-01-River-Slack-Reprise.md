# Session 12 — River Slack Reprise

**At the table — reel in the line**

**Something happens:** [[talon-skarn]] burns through his Stunned opening and makes one last close attempt to take the [[fate-spinner]] from [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]].
**Why it matters:** The party must protect the Spinner, choose whether to pursue, and account for any [[ghost-plum]] Skarn can steal from their visible packs.
**Decision:** Protect Crissdalynn, force Skarn away, pursue him, or let the object and the chase define the next route.
**Hook lands when:** The party commits to a response and the fight's immediate state has changed.

> [!narration] Initial Narration
> The river has gone loud around the stones, but the fight is close enough that every movement is visible. Talon Skarn is still within Crissdalynn's reach, one hand reaching toward the Fate Spinner while his other hand keeps the party from closing cleanly. The chain between his gauntlet and hook is drawn tight over the wet ground, and Jean-Claude's motionless body remains caught in Delmar's coat behind the line of danger. Skarn's eyes flick once toward the party's packs before returning to the Spinner.

## Situation

- **Where.** [[river-slack-basin]], with wet stones, river noise, and the established fight position from Session 11.
- **Who.** [[talon-skarn]] is the active thief. [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] is the protected carrier. The other three PCs are combatants, rescuers, blockers, or pursuers according to their choices.
- **What changed.** Skarn should still be Stunned from the previous exchange, but he spends a Legendary Resistance to end that condition when the party would otherwise capitalize on it. One Legendary Resistance was already spent in Session 11.
- **Pressure.** Skarn stays for only two or three rounds unless the party radically changes the fiction. His first priority is the Spinner; a [[ghost-plum]] is opportunistic leverage if he sees one or both of the party's two plums.
- **Open question.** The party decides whether the object, Crissdalynn, Jean-Claude, or Skarn's escape is the immediate priority.

## Run the Hook

| If the party… | World response |
| ------------- | --------------- |
| **Protects the Spinner** | Skarn cuts gear, hooks a handhold, or changes angle rather than trading attacks with the whole party. Resolve the protection attempt, then let his exit become the next pressure. |
| **Focuses Skarn** | He spends movement on the Spinner whenever possible and uses the river stones and chain to keep one body between himself and a clean surround. If the party drops him, the fight ends on that player-created result. |
| **Pursues** | Skarn gives ground toward the inland grass and leaves a clear enough trail to follow, but he does not wait in a fair duel. Hand to the dead trail once the party commits to the chase. |
| **Rejects the chase** | The party keeps the immediate scene but gives Skarn time to turn stolen fruit, pack access, or the route itself into later leverage. Do not replay this fight. |

## Decision Handles

- **Protect.** Keep Skarn away from Crissdalynn, the Spinner, and the packs, accepting that he may escape.
- **Break the approach.** Cut the chain, force Skarn into the river, or use the basin's stones to deny his reach.
- **Pursue.** Follow the retreat toward the old Grung dead and accept that Aruhe's inland pressure continues.
- **Secure the fruit.** Check the party's two Ghost Plums before Skarn gets another uncontested opening.

## Action Setup

- **Objective.** Skarn wants the Spinner in hand and an exit route. The party wants to keep the Spinner and its carrier together while deciding whether Jean-Claude can be moved safely.
- **Opposition.** [[talon-skarn]]. Use his existing owner page; do not add a second stat block here.
- **Terrain.** Wet stones, close river noise, open handholds, and a grass edge that offers Skarn an escape line.
- **Escalation.** Skarn spends the Stunned opening, shifts to the Spinner, and leaves if the object becomes inaccessible or the party creates a clean surround.
- **End condition.** The Spinner is Held, Stolen, or secured for the moment; Skarn is defeated, driven off, or allowed to escape; and the party has chosen the next route.

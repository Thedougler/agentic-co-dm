# Session 12 — The Raft

**Follows:** [[session-12-05-consume]]
**Purpose.** Show the rescue paid off: the survivors who want out have a way out and know how to live through the trip.

**Card.** Villain Escapes, then Happy Ending. Heroes-lost branch: the Spinner is gone.
**Outcome.** Nine Calveno have a raft, a river road to the coast, and the rules for surviving Aruhe; three stay under the vine because they choose to.
**Budget.** About 15 minutes.

## Run the Beat

1. **Confirm the outcome.** Skarn is gone over the channel, or down.
2. **Show the consequence.** The brothers. [[carlo-ferrante]] says Ettore's name for the first time in nineteen days.
3. **Pay off the stakes.** [[tommaso-brasca]] loads the raft and counts nine seats. [[renzo-canale]], Beppe Sarti, and Marco Lenzi say they are staying, and nothing moves them.
4. **Let them react.** The epilogue prompts below. If Perrin ate, Renzo steps back from him and will not come within arm's reach until dawn.
5. **End on an image.** First light, and the raft pushing off.

> [!narration] Closing image
> The raft goes out at first light, because Piero will not float a river in the dark. Nine men sit on it shoulder to shoulder, with Ettore's splinted leg stretched down the middle and Tommaso at the back with the pole. The channel takes it slowly.
>
> Under the great vine, three men stand and watch it go. One of them lifts a hand. The fruit hangs over their heads, heavy and still, and none of them looks up at it.

**Pay the emotional bill.** Let the brothers have their moment before anyone talks about rivers.

**The rules for living on Aruhe.**

[[tommaso-brasca]] and [[renzo-canale]] teach these to anyone who asks. They are the island's law as the survivors have lived it:

1. Eat only what has already fallen. Never pick.
2. Build only from what has already fallen. Deadwood and shed fibre float; cut wood brings the vines.
3. Never walk at night.
4. Stay out of still water. The otters own the slack pools; pass them midstream, by day, without stopping.
5. Keep off open grass. Walk the tall grass or the treeline edge, where nothing can charge you.

# Session 12 — The Way Out

## At a Glance

The rescue worked: nine Calveno are ready to walk out behind the party at first light, and three are staying under the vine because they choose to.

- **Follows.** [[Session-12-05-consume]]
- **New status quo.** The party leads nine Calveno back the way it came, toward the *[[uncertainty]]* off [[western-landing]]: days of walking with a man on a litter. That walk opens Session 13.
- **Price paid.** Everything spent since last night's rest, any civilian lost in the meadow or the fight, the Spinner if Skarn took it, and Perrin's mark until dawn if he ate.
- **Reward.** Nine lives, the rules for surviving Aruhe, one fallen fruit for each PC, and level 6.

## Consequences

- **[[talon-skarn]].** Driven off, he flies home to [[talon-vantyrus]] empty-handed, with his plums spent, and the [[rule-of-two]] contest sharpens. With the Spinner, he flies it to his master. Captured, he says nothing about Vantyrus that matters, and waits.
- **[[hinewai]].** She has seen a Grung with no gold on his skin, and does not know what to make of him. She counts the nine as hers wherever they walk.
- **[[the-pantry]].** Three stay: [[renzo-canale]], Beppe Sarti, and Marco Lenzi. The raft stays moored.
- **The Ferrantes.** Carlo and Ettore are together. Luca's tally stops at nineteen days.

## Payoffs

- **The Calveno survivors.** Nine walk out behind the party: [[ettore-ferrante]], [[luca-ferrante]], [[piero-sorrentino]], [[gianni-moro]], [[matteo-scola]], Carlo Ferrante, Tommaso Brasca, Sandrino Vale, and Ilario Pozzo. Carlo and Luca lash a litter of fallen poles for Ettore.
- **Luca's promise.** Kept: Ettore goes out on a litter.
- **Perrin's faith.** If he ate, the whale-light glows steady around his cloak until dawn. If he refused, the light does not come when he reaches for it, and [[auralis]] is silent until next session.
- **Matteo.** He walks out with the party toward the ship he has wanted since Session 11.
- **The rules for living on Aruhe.** Renzo and Tommaso teach them to anyone who asks, and the nine carry them home:
  1. Eat only what has already fallen. Never pick.
  2. Build only from what has already fallen. Deadwood and shed fibre float; cut wood brings the vines.
  3. Never walk at night.
  4. Stay out of still water. The otters own the slack pools; pass them midstream, by day, without stopping.
  5. Keep off open grass. Walk the tall grass or the treeline, where nothing can charge you.

## Rewards

- **Fruit.** Renzo gives each PC one fallen [[giants-guava]] or [[stonepear]] of their choice. They are gifts, not claims, and keep until eaten.
- **Skarn's kit.** If he was captured or killed: a katana, two sai, and two kusarigama. No coin.
- **Milestone.** The party reaches level 6 at the end of Session 12.
- **Standing.** Nine Calveno owe the party their lives.

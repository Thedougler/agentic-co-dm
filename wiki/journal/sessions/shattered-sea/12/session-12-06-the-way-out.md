# Session 12 — The Way Out

**Follows:** [[session-12-05-consume]]
**Purpose.** Show the rescue paid off: the survivors who want out have a way out and know how to live through the trip.

**Card.** Villain Escapes, then Happy Ending. Heroes-lost branch: the Spinner is gone.
**Outcome.** Nine Calveno set out behind the party to walk back to the ship, with the rules for surviving Aruhe; three stay under the vine because they choose to.
**Budget.** About 15 minutes.

## Run the Beat

1. **Confirm the outcome.** Skarn is gone over the channel, or down.
2. **Show the consequence.** The brothers come first. [[carlo-ferrante]] kneels by [[ettore-ferrante]] and says his name for the first time in nineteen days.
3. **Pay off the stakes.** Nine Calveno ask the party to lead them to the ship. [[tommaso-brasca]] leaves his raft moored, and Carlo and Luca lash a litter of fallen poles for [[ettore-ferrante]]. [[renzo-canale]], Beppe Sarti, and Marco Lenzi say they are staying, and nothing moves them.
4. **Let them react.** The epilogue prompts below. If Perrin ate, Renzo steps back from him and will not come within arm's reach until dawn.
5. **End on an image.** First light, and the column ready to walk out behind the party.

> [!narration] Closing image
> The fire under the vine burns high all night, and by first light the clearing smells of damp ash and crushed guava. Nine Calveno stand ready with everything they own tied up in bundles of shed fibre, and every one of them is watching you. Carlo and Luca have lashed two fallen poles into a litter for Ettore, and Carlo stands at its head with one hand resting on his brother's shoulder. Down at the channel bank, Tommaso gives the lashings of his deadwood raft a last tug, then leaves it moored and shoulders his bundle. Piero has a coil of cord over each arm, and Matteo keeps at your elbow, asking how far it is to the ship.
>
> The way to the ship runs back the way you came, along the road of charred flowers and across the long meadow. From there it passes the smoking hole in the forest floor and follows the river down to the beach, days of walking with a man on a litter. Luca counts the column off aloud, nine men and all of you, and takes his place beside his father's litter.
>
> Under the great vine, Renzo and the two men who chose to stay stand apart from the column. The old man lifts one big clean hand toward you in farewell, and the fruit hangs heavy above all three of them.

**Pay the emotional bill.** Let the brothers have their moment before anyone talks about the walk.

**The rules for living on Aruhe.**

[[tommaso-brasca]] and [[renzo-canale]] teach these to anyone who asks. They are the island's law as the survivors have lived it:

1. Eat only what has already fallen. Never pick.
2. Build only from what has already fallen. Deadwood and shed fibre float; cut wood brings the vines.
3. Never walk at night.
4. Stay out of still water. The otters own the slack pools; pass them midstream, by day, without stopping.
5. Keep off open grass. Walk the tall grass or the treeline edge, where nothing can charge you.

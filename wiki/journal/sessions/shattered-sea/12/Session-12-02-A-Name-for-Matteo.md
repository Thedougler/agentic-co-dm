# Session 12 — A Name for Matteo

**Card.** Rescuers. A survivor walked toward danger in the night, and the only people who know the way need the party to go after him.
**Thread advanced.** The inland survivors (the crew's Calveno rescue job) and the Gold-caste orders. Celia's first seal scrap shows the dead up north were sent there.
**Entry state.** About two hours after sunrise at the otter camp on [[river-slack-basin]]. The party has had time for one short rest. Skarn is gone into the trees above the bloody bank, with or without Matteo's plums, and the Spinner is Held, in the slack, or Stolen per [[session-12-01-river-slack-reprise]].
**Trigger.** [[oren-vask|Oren]] and [[celia-parel|Celia]] come down the bloody bank from [[cutoff-lip]]. They saw the flyers pass over [[spoke-ring]] yesterday and saw their fire here last night.
**Turn.** [[nello-castri|Nello]] walked north alone last night toward the woman's garden, down the trail where generations of Grung were sent and died, and the party is carrying a Grung the same way.
**New direction.** Go north with Celia and Oren to bring Nello back, or take Matteo toward the ship and leave Nello to the island.
**Memorable element.** Celia kneels on the sand with a charcoal stub to write Matteo's name among the dead, finds him alive, and draws a line through a word instead.
**Target.** About 30 minutes.

> [!narration] Opening
> The sun is up over the ridge now, and steam lifts off the slack in thin sheets while the white water keeps talking past the rock lip. Someone is coming down the bloody bank. A broad man with a sunburned neck comes first, testing each step with the sole of his boot before he trusts it, a bundle of tied twigs bouncing at his belt. Behind him comes a lean woman in a salt-stained shirt, dark hair tied back with red cord, holding a folded cloth flat against her chest. She stops at the bottom of the bank and looks straight at Matteo, and her shoulders drop. She kneels right there on the wet sand, unfolds the cloth over a spread of torn paper and gold-edged scraps, and takes out a stub of charcoal. "Matteo Scola," she says. "I came down to write you with the dead."

## Run the Beat

1. **Present the situation.** Celia and Oren expected to find Matteo drowned. He is alive, and the flyers are real. They want two things at once: Celia wants names kept straight, and Oren wants help getting Nello back.
2. **Let them engage.** The anchor is Celia's cloth: her ledger page of names, and a bundle of spent seal scraps she has picked out of old Grung bones along the grass. Matteo and Oren argue over the woman in the woods. Oren looks hard at Jean-Claude.
3. **Make the turn.** Oren says Nello's mat was empty at first light, his boots were left side by side, and his bare prints went up the north spoke past [[star-cut]]. That trail ends at the place where the Grung dead lie thickest. Oren has never gone down into it and will not go alone.
4. **Hand back the choice.** Oren asks the flyers to come north with them and bring Nello back. Matteo asks them to take him to the ship. Ask what they do.

## Checks & Costs

- **Automatic.** Listening gets the core truth. Oren wants help, and he says so.
- **Wisdom (Insight) — `DC 10`, on Oren.** **Success:** He is afraid of the Grung place, not lying about it, and he will stop at its edge. **Failure:** He seems certain, and the party learns about the edge only when he stops there.
- **Reading Celia's scraps.** Resolve on [[grung-authority-seal]]. A reader who knows Grung authority marks, such as Jean-Claude, reads them without a roll.
- **Charisma (Persuasion) — `DC 15`, to bring Matteo north.** **Success:** He comes as far as Spoke Ring and waits there. **Failure:** He stays at the basin. If Skarn took his plums, the DC is `10`, and on a success he comes all the way.
- **Cost of the good part.** Oren's route and Celia's scraps cost a promise to keep the woman's rules on the way and to leave the dead as they lie. Breaking the promise later costs their help.

### Jean-Claude wakes (if Chad is at the table)

Jean-Claude went catatonic when the adult [[bloodhawk]] stooped on the crown squid; his sheet records that birds are a genetic terror to him. Any one of these wakes him, with no roll, the moment it happens:

| Route | What happens | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Crissdalynn spins the Fate Spinner where he can see it.** | It is what she did for a frightened Grung child in Session 06. The coins of colored light cross his face, and his eyes follow them. | The Spinner is out in the open. If Skarn is watching from the bank, he learns exactly where it rides. |
| **Someone submerges him in moving water for an hour.** | His sheet requires one hour submerged every 24 hours. He surfaces gasping and himself. Use the white water's edge; the slack is claimed water. | One hour. Oren waits but grows impatient, and the party reaches the Hollow an hour later. |
| **Someone presses one of Celia's Gold-marked scraps into his hand.** | His body answers the Gold authority mark before his mind does. He is on his feet at attention and says the Grung reply to an order before he knows where he is. | None, except what it shows everyone about how deep the Gold mark goes. |

**Soak clock.** Canon does not record when Jean-Claude last submerged. If the DM's timeline puts it before the bloodhawk stoop, he gains 1 Exhaustion level at noon today unless he soaks. The water at [[spoke-ring]]'s west spoke is on the route.

**Bird terror (optional, Chad's call).** If Chad wants it, then for the rest of the day, the first time a bird-shaped creature (Skarn, a bloodhawk, or Crissdalynn in flight) swoops within 30 feet of Jean-Claude, he makes a **Wisdom save — `DC 10`** or is Frightened of it until the end of his next turn.

If Chad is absent, Jean-Claude stays catatonic and is carried. Carrying him halves the carrier's Speed.

## Player Levers

- **Person.** Oren can be persuaded to go faster, but not to enter the Grung place. Celia will trade her scraps for the promise. Matteo can be persuaded, or left.
- **Thing.** Celia's seal scraps are evidence, and one of them is a waking route for Jean-Claude.
- **Place.** The otter camp is safe to leave. Matteo can wait here a day.
- **Promise / problem.** Keeping the woman's rules on the road north is Oren's price. Breaking them later is a claim with a cost ([[taking-on-aruhe]]).

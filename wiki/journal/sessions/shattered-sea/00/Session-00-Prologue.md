# Session 00 - Prologue

Read aloud before [[session-01-recap]]. The context a table needs to follow the campaign from its first session.

> [!narration] The Shattered Sea
> The Shattered Sea is about sixty thousand square miles of island, reef, and open water, weeks past the last coast on any chart. A permanent storm belt called the Galewall closes one edge of the chart, the Ashwall Islands stand past it as volcanic spires, and the charted middle holds two long island arcs with the Central Strait running between them. One arc is the Crown Islands, mountainous and fortified. The other is the Midchain, a long run of small tropical islands and reef water, with free ports that keep their own books. Past both arcs the land pinches into the Tail, and then the charts run out at the Drowned Maw, a cold trench where the current turns back on itself and sailors do not say what lies beyond the seal. Every harbour in the sea pays Umberlee through the Waveservants before it does business.
>
> Three powers want the whole sea and none of them holds it. The Dravosi Crown came with hulls and law, and now inspects what floats, keeps the paperwork, and calls unlicensed cargo a crime. The Tessarine Concordat came with paper and courtesy, and holds what it holds by debt. Behind them the Passage moves people the Crown has no business owning along routes no chart admits, and it does not forgive anyone who sells a person. The Grung clans keep their water closed, trade the toxin they harvest, and raid the Midchain for captives.
>
> Passage costs you something either way. Take the official route and you get the strait, the inspections, the records, and a Crown that remembers your name at the next harbour. Take the other route and you owe a favour to people who keep count.
>
> Six days before our story starts, a privateer fleet came away from the shrine at Vel Orn carrying the Pearl of Souls. Over the Drowned Maw the goddess answered. All five ships went down, the water has not been right since, and something came up through the seal with them.
>
> Four strangers washed out of that wreckage onto the same hull. Delmar and Crissdalynn spent two days in open water after their fleet sank under them, until a tortle captain named Beaumont Sel pulled them aboard his brig, the Saltwright, and gave them passage to Calveno. Perrin came ashore alone at Keth Naar and climbed aboard that same brig without asking. Jean-Claude took a berth at the last island, four months clear of the Grung who want him back and one step ahead of the ones they sent after him.
>
> The Saltwright is on her last leg toward Calveno when the Crown cutter HCS Surety closes on her and runs up the signal for inspection. Her captain, Barnaby Rook, reads cargo twice and manifests three times, and he does not take fees for any of it. That is where our story starts.

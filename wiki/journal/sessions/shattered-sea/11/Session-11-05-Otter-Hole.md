# Session 11 — Rescue at the otter hole

![[aruhe-river-slack-basin.jpg|Slack Basin, still claimed pool behind the rock lip]]

````col
## Scene ends when

The beat ends when the party leaves the hole on the bloody-bank prints toward [[Session-11-06-Farthest-Camp]], with or without the man in the grey shirt.

Plan for about thirty minutes.

**If behind:** Put the slack, the man, the prints, and the smoke in front of the party. Resolve the rescue or departure, then hand off.

**If ahead:** End on the bloody-bank detour. Do not start night watch or the deer-stalker.

## At a Glance

- **Stakes:** The man in the grey shirt is caught in a game that has gone too far. If he remains underwater through tick 3, he drops to 0 hit points. Anyone who joins can be dunked as well.
- **Goal / exit:** Get the man onto this bank, then follow the bloody-bank detour toward [[Session-11-06-Farthest-Camp]]. The party can also leave him and follow the prints.
- **Danger:** The twelve-foot otters play with whatever becomes it. Entering the water, throwing a toy, or chasing joins the game. Attacking, fishing, or taking a dead body or pack starts Hunt.
- **Silence:** Present the basin and the man, then wait. The clock advances once at round end while he remains in the hole.
- **Situation magnets:** The man remains a magnet until he reaches this bank. The basin, the water, the dead body, the pack, the party member who is it, and anyone attacking from the bank are also magnets.
```
````

## Now

The party stands on the wet sand of [[river-slack-basin]], facing east and just upvalley of [[line-bank|Line Bank]]. The slack begins 5 feet east. A thin cane stands in the blood smear at the waterline. The man in the grey shirt grips hanging roots 20 feet north, with his hips in the water.

The closed pack lies 15 feet out in clear water. A face-down body lies 20 feet out, and a maroon sleeve hangs 25 feet farther toward the roots. White water breaks over the rock lip 30 feet east across the pool.

Living prints and the blood trail climb 20 feet north into the jungle wall. Gold-green grass begins 10 feet west of the path. A 30-foot move reaches the water, the pack, the man, or the first trees. A 60-foot Dash follows the bloody-bank detour north into the trees.

## Action cards

````col
```col-md
**Two [[river-otter]].** Two otters have a Calveno wreck survivor in this hole. They are playing tag with him, and the game has gone too far.

**Identifying them.** Both bodies are twelve feet long and chocolate-black. Whorl-bib has a tight white whorl on the pale chest. Thin-streak has a thin pale streak at the throat.

**Play.** The otters dunk and chase, but Hunt starts only if someone attacks, fishes this hole, or pulls a dead body or pack.

**Join.** Entering the water, throwing a toy, or chasing joins the game. The otters drop the man and play with the new person or object. A throw from this bank keeps the game in the hole. A party member who enters or chases becomes it.

**Tire.** At tick 4 of play, the otters leave living people for the pack, a body, or rest.

**Boundary.** The otters hold this hole. They do not chase far overland or haul out onto [[razer-grass]]. A body on this sand is off the magnets.

**Creature defaults.** Use `AC 15`, `76 HP`, Speed `20 feet`, Swim `40 feet`, and Hold Breath for 30 minutes.

**Play grab.** In play mode, the otters do not Bite. A grab applies **Grappled** (escape `DC 14`).

**Dunk (Recharge 5–6).** One creature **Grappled** by that otter is pulled up to 20 feet and shoved underwater, or held under if already in this water. That creature is **Restrained** until the grapple ends.

**Tug Toy.** Contest Athletics or Sleight of Hand against a held or worn object within 5 feet. On a success, the otter takes the object and swims 10 feet without dealing damage.

**Hunt Multiattack.** Bite `+6` to hit for `14` piercing damage, with 5-foot reach. A Medium or smaller target is **Grappled** (escape `DC 14`). Tail is `+6` to hit for `11` bludgeoning damage, with 10-foot reach.

**Watery Ambush.** In Hunt mode, an otter has advantage on attack rolls against a creature in the water if the other otter is within 10 feet of that target.

**Easy dial.** Use two play adults.

**[[taking-on-aruhe]].** Fishing this hole starts Hunt and may become a claim on the next roll. Carrying flesh out of the hole is a claim.
```

```col-md
**[[matteo-scola]].** Until the party pulls him onto the bank, call him the man in the grey shirt.

**Look.** His lip is split, one boot is missing, and his grey shirt is salt-stiff. He grips hanging roots 20 feet north with his hips in the slack.

**Want.** He wants this bank and believes the otters are killing him. He went back for the pack after the others climbed out. He does not name the woman.

**Name.** Once he reaches the bank and can speak, he gives his name as Matteo. If he is **Unconscious**, he cannot name himself until he wakes.

**Breath.** A dunked party member holds breath and then begins drowning. Matteo has already spent his breath. If he remains under through tick 3, he drops to 0 hit points and is **Unconscious**. A party member who is it still has unused breath and does not drop to 0 hit points from play on tick 3.

**Stabilize.** Help plus **Wisdom (Medicine) — `DC 10`** stabilizes him. He keeps making death saves until stabilized.
```
````

> [!narration] Initial Narration
> From the wet sand at the edge of a clear turquoise pool, you can reach the water in one step. Twenty feet north, a man in a grey, salt-stiff shirt grips hanging jungle roots with his hips in the slack. One boot is missing, and blood runs from his split lip. A twelve-foot chocolate-black otter circles his legs and cuts him off whenever he reaches for the roots. A thin pale streak marks its throat.
>
> Five feet east, the pool shows pale stone beneath clear water. A second twelve-foot otter stands on the sand with a chewed pole and wet rope, its pale chest marked by a tight white whorl. Amber eyes hold on this bank while water drips from its whiskers.
>
> Fifteen feet out, a closed wooden pack lies on the bottom. Farther into the pool, a face-down body in soaked olive cloth and a maroon sleeve lie near the hanging roots. White water breaks over a rock lip thirty feet east, but the slack holds the pack and bodies in place.
>
> A thin cane stands in a dark smear at the waterline. Living prints climb twenty feet north into the first trees, while gold-green grass begins ten feet west of the path. Wet blood and cool spray reach the bank. The man coughs once, and the streaked otter nudges him a handspan farther from the roots before pausing.
>
> What do you do?

````col
## Procedure

**Play mode.** At the end of each round, advance the clock once if the man remains in the hole or anyone is on a magnet: this basin, fishing, pulling a body, pack, or person, attacking from this bank, or playing tag in the water. A frozen table still advances the clock because the man remains in the hole. Failed checks use only the **Be ready for** ruling.

Whenever someone joins by entering the water, throwing a toy, or chasing, the otters drop the man and play with that person or object. Joining does not start Hunt. A thrown toy from this bank keeps the game in the hole. A party member who enters or chases becomes it. If no one joins in a later round, the otters return to the man if he is still in the hole. At tick 4, they tire or get hungry and stop chasing living people. Do not turn play mode into a fight to the death.

**Combat mode:** If the party commits to killing the [[river-otter]], or Hunt starts from an attack, fishing, or pulling a dead body or pack, stop the clock and run the embedded statblocks.

If a party member takes open valley air, that party member leaves this hole for hawk sky. Point to a [[young-bloodhawk]].
```

```col-md
Get the man onto this sand before tick 3. Joining the game makes the otters drop him while they chase whoever or whatever is it, giving the party a chance to pull him in. If the party leaves him, [[Session-11-06-Farthest-Camp]] has one fewer survivor than the tracks suggest. If he reaches this sand and can speak, he walks the detour, gives his name as Matteo, and says the others left while he went back for the pack. He does not name the woman.
````

## How the Scene Resolves

The party takes the bloody-bank detour toward the smoke on [[cutoff-lip]], with or without the man. Hand to [[Session-11-06-Farthest-Camp]]. The forest-edge walk toward [[spoke-ring]] may take one [[Session-11-00-Random-Tables]] roll if this is still one of the two allowed rolls before beat 6. Do not run that roll during this hole. Carry forward any RAW damage and conditions already applied.

**Grappled / Restrained.** Anyone still in this hole at a break remains in play or Hunt until they leave the water, unless tick 4 of play has already ended the chase.

**Unconscious.** If the man hit 0 hit points and was not stabilized, he remains **Unconscious** at 0 hit points if the party has him, or remains in this hole.

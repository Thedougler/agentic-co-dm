# Session 11 — Torn Crossing hunt

![[attachments/shattered-sea/places/aruhe-torn-crossing.jpg|Torn Crossing, flood-scoured river lip and pale razer-grass stands]]

````col
```col-md
## Scene ends when

End this beat when the hunt clears the print lane and the party can follow the upriver trail, or when the party commits to combat and that fight resolves.

Plan for about thirty minutes.

**If behind:** Run the two crossings, one Pouncing Bound, and the [[spiguar]]'s first choice of kill or easier prey. Then hand off once the party can follow the prints.

**If ahead:** Let the party exploit fire, fruit, water, and the white stands. Do not add another predator.
```

```col-md
flexGrow=2
===
## At a Glance

- **Stakes:** The hunt has crossed the upriver route. Isolation, blood, noise, **Prone**, or tall grass makes a body prey.
- **Goal / exit:** Clear Torn Crossing and keep the prints toward [[line-bank]].
- **Danger:** Two [[Wolfrabbit|wolfrabbits]] are fleeing west while an [[spiguar]] guards a third body in the grass.
- **Pressure:** At the end of each round, one clock tick fires if any character remains a hunt magnet.
- **Routes:** Deep water breaks pursuit. The first bank-woods break the pack's contact. The northbound prints bend around the white stands and the Spiguar's grass.
- **Silence:** Show the crossing, then wait. If the table freezes, advance the clock once after the round.
```
````

## Now

The party starts in the muddy northbound corridor of [[torn-crossing]], facing north. The river lip is 20 feet west and 30 feet south. One [[Wolfrabbit]] crosses west 10 feet north of the party; a second crosses west 20 feet south. Twenty feet east, the [[spiguar]] crouches over a third striped body in the grass. One [[razer-grass]] stand is 15 feet west in the scour; two more are 25 feet east in the grass. Bank-woods begin 40 feet east. Living [[redheart-berry|red berries]] hang at the west wet margin. The upriver prints bend around the white stands and the Spiguar's grass before continuing north. A 30-foot move reaches the south lip and passes the rear runner.

## Action cards

````col
```col-md
===
**Opening.** The [[spiguar]] starts over a wolfrabbit kill. Two flushed [[Wolfrabbit|wolfrabbits]] cross the corridor toward the river, one 10 feet north and one 20 feet south.

The crossings cut across the print lane. The Spiguar keeps its kill until a character becomes easier prey. A rabbit uses Pouncing Bound only against an isolated, bleeding, or exposed target in its escape lane.

Deep water south or west breaks pursuit. The pack breaks contact among the first trunks 40 feet east, but it chases a runner who stays in the grass.
```

```col-md
**Easy pack:** Use only the two crossing [[Wolfrabbit|wolfrabbits]]. Skip Devour the Pack. Name Torn-ear and White-blaze only after the table sees those traits.

**Hard pack:** Use five or six [[Wolfrabbit|wolfrabbits]], with the extras entering from the grass. When one drops, use Devour the Pack and Frenzy Toward the Fallen. The [[spiguar]] treats the nearest isolated character as its next meal.

**Terrain switch:** A rabbit jumps [[razer-grass]] instead of landing in it. The [[spiguar]] does not willingly enter deep water, rush a white stand, or pursue under open sky. A knockdown can send a character into the current or a stand.
```
===
```col-md
**Compact [[Wolfrabbit]].** AC 15. 68 hit points. Speed 50 feet.

- **Pouncing Bound:** Leap up to 30 feet, then make Raking Claws at +7 to hit for `12` slashing. If the leap began at least 20 feet away and hits, the target makes a **Strength save — `DC 15`** or falls **Prone**.
- **Bite:** +7 to hit for `14` piercing.
- **Pack Rend:** Add `5 (1d10)` piercing to a Bite against a **Prone** target when another [[Wolfrabbit]] is within 5 feet.
```

```col-md
**Compact [[spiguar]].** AC 17. Speed 60 feet.

- **Saber Bite:** +10 to hit for `19` piercing and **Grappled** (escape `DC 18`).
- **Claw:** +10 to hit for `17` slashing.
- **Pounce:** Move 20 feet straight, hit with Claw for `17` slashing plus `10 (3d6)`, then require a **Strength save — `DC 18`** or **Prone**. On a failed save, make Saber Bite as a Bonus Action.
- **Drag Through Grass:** As a Bonus Action, drag a **Grappled** creature 30 feet.
- **Bloodied (93 HP):** Drag the current body into the bank-woods and break contact.
```

```col-md
**Compact [[razer-grass]].** The stands are Difficult Terrain and deal `1d4` slashing per 5 feet of movement through intact blades.

- **Careful Passage:** Spend one Action to move 5 feet without movement damage.
- **Shatter:** Affected creatures make a **Dexterity save — `DC 14`**, taking `2d6` slashing.
- **Mineral Dust:** Affected creatures make a **Constitution save — `DC 14`** or become **Glass-Choked**.

![[attachments/shattered-sea/hazards/aruhe-razer-grass.jpg|Razer-Grass]]
```

```col-md
**Wet-margin berries:** Living stems at the west lip are [[redheart-berry]].

- **Fallen fruit:** Eating a fallen berry is a Bonus Action and restores `8d4 + 8` hit points.
- **Living stem:** Plucking one is a [[taking-on-aruhe]] claim and draws an extra [[Wolfrabbit]] from the grass.
```
````

> [!narration] Initial Narration
> Northbound prints enter a muddy corridor between the river on your left and eight-foot gold grass on your right. Flood-scoured stone and black rock break the water, while white stands of glass-edged grass pinch the route ahead. Water pounds the rocks, and the pale blades tick when the wind reaches them.
>
> A tawny, dark-striped runner bounds west across the corridor ten feet north of you, its torn left ear flat against its head. Another follows twenty feet south with a white blaze. Twenty feet east, a spotted cat crouches beneath reeds over a third striped body, pale saber teeth bright beside the carcass.
>
> The prints bend around the white stands and away from the cat's grass. Deep water cuts west and south, and the first dark trunks begin forty feet east beyond the grass. What do you do?

````col
## Procedure

**Escape mode:** After everyone acts, advance the threat clock once if any character remains isolated, bleeding, loud, **Prone**, in the grass, or has struck the [[spiguar]]. A frozen table advances it once at the end of the round. Failed rolls apply only their **Be ready for** ruling and never advance the clock by themselves. A character who reaches deep water or the bank-woods breaks contact; end the hunt when no character remains a magnet.

If a character enters open valley air, remove that character from this hunt and point to a [[young-bloodhawk]].

**Combat mode:** If the party commits to killing the pack or the [[spiguar]], stop the clock and run the embedded statblocks. Killing the Spiguar is lethal for this band.
```

```col-md
## Secondary objective

The northbound prints are the route through the crossing. They curve around the white stands and avoid the Spiguar's grass. Entering tall grass or a white stand creates a hunt magnet. If a character pulls someone from the [[spiguar]], the dragged character remains **Grappled** (escape `DC 18`) and 30 feet closer to the bank-woods. Freeing that character does not end the hunt. Plucking a living stem marks [[taking-on-aruhe]] and draws an extra [[Wolfrabbit]].
```
````

## How the Scene Resolves

The default escape ends when the [[spiguar]] drags its kill into the bank-woods and the pack clears Torn Crossing. The upriver prints remain in the mud. Hand to [[line-bank]] and [[Session-11-04-What-They-Ate]]. Apply any [[taking-on-aruhe]] claim already made.

**Prone:** Anyone knocked down here is **Prone** until they stand.

**Grappled:** Anyone dragged by the [[spiguar]] is **Grappled** (escape `DC 18`) until they break the hold.

> The spotted cat disappears beneath the first dark trunks with its kill, and the last runner clears the corridor. The northbound prints continue through the mud between the white stands.

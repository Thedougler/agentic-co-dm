## At a Glance

- **Stakes:** Exposed or isolated bodies become prey. The party's immediate win is to leave the adult hawk's hunting line, not kill either animal.
- **Goal / exit:** Drop into the eight-foot grass and moving water, reach terrace cover, or force a line back to the beach.
- **Danger:** The adult Bloodhawk is hunting the Crown Squid. The young Bloodhawks drive whatever she flushes. Staying near the squid, attacking either bird, using loud magic, or flying toward the young makes a party member prey.
- **Silence:** Present the dive, ask what the party does, and wait. If they freeze in open air, advance the pressure once at the end of the round.
- **Situation magnets:** Open sky, the squid's flight line, loud magic, attacks, grabbing the squid, and flying toward the young all draw the hunt toward a party member.

> [!narration] Initial Narration
> The old garden terraces fall away beneath you. You are 60 feet above their north edge, with moss-dark walls stepping toward eight-foot gold-green grass and a clear turquoise river. Water rushes through the stems. South, 60 feet of open sky runs toward the short beach and the ship. Nothing at this height offers cover.
>
> The translucent purple squid you escaped among those trees is 30 feet north at your height, pushing through the last tree crowns. Its dark arms brace against separate trunks, and thin hooked lines trail through the leaves. An adult charcoal hawk drops out of the sun toward it, four wings folded tight against its body, crimson feathers flashing under the wings and a pale hooked beak leading the dive. Two smaller charcoal hawks follow her line, their talons oversized for their narrow bodies.
>
> Warm, sugar-sour air rises from split fruit on the terraces. The adult hawk locks her wings, and the gap to the squid closes.
>
> What do you do?

## Situation

- **Where.** The party starts 60 feet above the north lip of [[Aruhe - Old Gardens]]. The [[Aruhe - Crown Squid]] is 30 feet north at the same height, fleeing through the last tree crowns. [[Aruhe - River]] and eight-foot grass lie east and 60 feet below. [[Aruhe - Beach|Western Landing]] and [[Uncertainty]] lie south across 60 feet of open sky.
- **Who.** The adult [[Aruhe - Bloodhawk]] is diving on the squid. Two [[Young Bloodhawk|young Bloodhawks]] follow her line and strike whatever she flushes. [[Talon Skarn]] is the man-sized flyer up-sun; he is not responsible for this hunt.
- **What changed.** The party is still in the squid's air when the adult Bloodhawk enters the same patch of sky.
- **Pressure.** The adult continues after the squid unless the party makes itself the easier target. Each round in open air gives the birds another chance to isolate someone.
- **Open question.** Do the party members drop quietly, stay with the squid, make the birds turn, or cross the exposed sky toward the beach?

## Run the hook

| If the party… | World response |
| --- | --- |
| **Engages** | If they go quiet and drop away from both animals, let them land; the adult Bloodhawk stays on the squid. If they attack the adult, stay close to the squid, use loud magic, grab the squid, or fly toward the young, the adult treats that party member as prey. The young use Flush Dive on whatever the adult flushes. |
| **Hesitates** | Keep the party in open air. The adult continues the dive on the squid, and one young Bloodhawk drives an obvious airborne body toward the terraces. Ask what they do again. |
| **Rejects / leaves** | If they force a line back to the beach, the adult still hunts the squid across that sky and pressures anyone who remains exposed. If they reach the shingle, rebuild from [[Aruhe - Beach]]. |
| **Surprises you** | Improvise from the adult's want, the young birds' flushing behavior, the grass-and-river exit, and the exposed beach route. Do not turn this into a fight to the death. When the party reaches grass, river, terrace woods, or beach, end the hook. Do not run Survival checks or [[Session-11-00-Random-Tables]] here. |

## Decision handles

- **Drop.** A 60-foot Dash from open air reaches the eight-foot grass and moving water; a normal 30-foot move does not. A quiet drop leaves the hunt, and the adult stays on the squid.
- **Stay or strike.** Hitting a bird, using loud magic, grabbing the squid, or flying toward the young makes that party member the easier target. The adult may follow the squid down while making that attack.
- **Force the beach.** The beach is 60 feet south across exposed sky. The hunt crosses that route, so reaching the ship does not make the passage safe.
- **Use the terrain.** Closed canopy and eight-foot grass break the adult's dive. Thin terrace crowns do not. The adult does not pursue the party into either cover.

## Action setup

- **Objective.** The adult Bloodhawk wants the Crown Squid. The party succeeds by leaving the open sky; neither animal needs to die.
- **Opposition.** One adult [[Aruhe - Bloodhawk]] and two [[Young Bloodhawk|young Bloodhawks]]. The [[Aruhe - Crown Squid]] is fleeing rather than helping the party.
- **Terrain.** The party begins above open sky. The last terrace crowns are 30 feet north, the grass and river are 60 feet east and below, and the beach is 60 feet south across open sky.
- **Escalation.** The adult selects the most exposed body that stays in the hunt. A young Bloodhawk drives a body downward after the adult's pass.
- **End condition.** End the action when the party reaches grass, river, terrace woods, or beach. Do not run the Bloodhawk or Crown Squid to 0 hit points as the hook's default endpoint.

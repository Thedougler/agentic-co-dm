# Session 05 - Recap

Played 2026-07-05. Magazines under Calveno.

> [!narration] Recap
> Enzo and Ruk were still hauling the dead out of the Warren behind you when you went back down under Calveno, Felix still alive behind Nona's door. This time the way in led to the powder rooms the Grung operation had built into the city's own sewers. Catarina Da'Virelli came down with you from her canal workshop, cannon and owl included, and she was still telling you her name when the cannon had already taken its place in the line and the owl had gone up into the dark over the barrels.
>
> The first room that mattered gave itself up. Ruma Delacroix handed you Room 6 rather than defend it, and you took his surrender and kept moving.
>
> Two doors on, Bazzoth was waiting at his still. He emptied his last good vintage into one of his own bombs and lit it, and the blast took him and the two laborers working the barrels beside him, and his still, and Magazine Beta's stores down into the water. That was the second cache gone, after the boat you burned in the Warren.
>
> Which left the primary chamber. You came into Room 8 with a ritual already running. Solange Barret was chanting over a circle on the floor, four Grung Elite Warriors stood between her and the door, and one of the four was already down against the wall. The circle is still up, Solange is still chanting, and three of them are still between you and her.

## Wiki facts

[[Enzo]] and [[Ruk]], [[nona-black-jaw]]'s bodyguards, were clearing the Warren of the six Grung the party had killed when the crew went back below for the magazines. [[ruma-delacroix]] surrendered Room 6 of the [[calveno-sewer-magazines|Calveno sewer magazines]]. [[bazzoth-the-steeped|Bazzoth]] died in Room 5 with two Grung laborers, destroying his still and Magazine Beta, the second powder cache after the Warren boat.

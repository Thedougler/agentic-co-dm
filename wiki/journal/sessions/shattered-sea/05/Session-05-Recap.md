# Session 05 - Recap

Played 2026-07-05. Magazines under Calveno.

> [!narration] Recap
> Last time on the Shattered Sea, you brought the prize cutter into Calveno and named her Uncertainty. Delmar Fisk told you he had been an admiral, that his fleet stole the Pearl of Souls, and that the Maw wrecked them for it. At the harbor shrine Umberlee took Branca and named her price: the Pearl. You burned a powder boat in the Warren and took Felix Aho alive. He said they would hit under the festival crowds in two days.
>
> After Felix's interrogation, with Felix still under Nona Black-Jaw, you went back into the Calveno sewer magazines. Catarina Da'Virelli, a Calveno artificer, helped with that push from her local workshop. She came down with her cannon Ragnetto and her owl Strix. She was still giving her name when the cannon and the owl were already in the fight beside you.
>
> Ruma Delacroix gave up Room 6 without a fight. You took the surrender and kept moving. Two rooms over you found Bazzoth at his still. He poured his last good vintage into a reeking bomb and lost. He went into the water with his still and Magazine Beta's barrels, the second powder cache after the boat you burned on the coast.
>
> That left the primary chamber. You reached Room 8 in the middle of a ritual. You heard Solange Barret chanting over the circle. Four Grung Elite Warriors blocked the space between her and you. One of those Elites was already broken against the wall. The ritual is live. Solange is still chanting. The other three are still in the way.

# Session 07 - Recap

Played 2026-07-26. Crater. Defenders.

> [!narration] Recap
> Three nights before any of this, Thunk sat down at a card table and stood up with silver that was not his.
>
> Days later the primary chamber's ceiling came open onto the Mercatura plaza, in daylight, with the market still trading around it. What climbed out was Otar the Foul, twelve feet tall and built like a toad, his hide split into cracks that ran with fluid, standing in a crater the width of a house. Every wound you opened on him closed while you watched.
>
> Five things the colour of his own hide came clawing out of the rubble after him. One found a villager still trapped under a beam and ate, and with every mouthful its body came closer to a person's shape. Then the Warren's alarm went up, doors opened all round the plaza, and the city came out with pots to bang on and lamp oil to throw. Delmar put a whistle up and Kyzil came down out of the sky and took the brood apart with his daggers. Catarina worked the crater's edge. Otar ended with Delmar flying in to swing the chair into his throat and put a shot through his eye.
>
> He came down into the rubble and stayed there, and the rot began on him before you had finished with the plaza. The crowd did not cheer. A scrap of paper lay in the rot, and nobody has read it. Two things came off the corpse with you, the Grung Authority Seal and the Sliver of the Unstable Form.
>
> Iacopo Fieschi came down from his house while the crowd was still standing there, dressed for a signing, folding one piece of correspondence over and over while he talked. *I assume you will want more than simply public acknowledgment for last night's work.* He put your names on Tessarine paper as Calveno's Defenders, because the Crown had not come. Somewhere in that room was Rupert Knighton's order, dated days before the ceiling opened, telling its holder to gather what she could and get out. Fieschi had no warning at all.
>
> Perrin's stone warmed in his hand, and Nona told you to come home. Nobody has counted how many hundreds are missing, and the dust has not settled over the crater.

## Wiki facts

[[otar-the-foul|Otar the Foul]] rose from [[solange-barret]]'s completed ritual into a thirty-foot crater in the [[mercatura]] plaza and died there, healing every wound until [[delmar-fisk]] put the recovered [[fleet-commanders-chair|chair]] and a shot through his eye. [[master-kyzil]] answered the crew's whistle and killed five [[minor-slaad|minor slaad]], the brood that clawed out of the rubble and one of which ate its way toward a human shape. The party carries the [[grung-authority-seal]] and [[a-sliver-of-the-unstable-form]] taken off the corpse. The unread scrap of paper in the rot is still unresolved.

[[iacopo-fieschi]] signed the crew as Calveno's Defenders on [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] paper, because the Crown sent nobody, and he was not warned that [[rupert-knighton]]'s order to gather and evacuate was already days old. The Warren's Rattle put the city into the fight. Hundreds remain missing and the crater dust is still up. Next: [[Session-08-Recap]].

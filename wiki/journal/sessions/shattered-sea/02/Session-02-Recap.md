# Session 02 - Recap

Played 2026-05-18. Prize taken; the sea answers.

> [!narration] Recap
> Rook came at you alone, since the men he had left were either dead or standing with you. Beaumont sent Bisou off his shoulder and through a gun port, and the powder behind it came back too wet to burn, so the Surety's guns never answered. Delmar put a musket ball through the other port into the dark below and killed the navigator working down there, an ensign named Wouters. Crissdalynn threw Rook's first pistol shot wide with a gust of wind and took the second on her bracers. Delmar knocked him off the rigging with a thrown chair, and when Rook hit the dark water something long and eel-like rose alongside him and he did not come up again. Crissdalynn got his hat out of the water on the end of a boat hook, and Beaumont ruled the hat was the captain's now, which settled that.
>
> You went through his cabin and found letters of marque, a pendant engraved For Mira, from the sea, and a crate of flintlocks beside vials of Grung poison. Perrin took Mira's Blade.
>
> Then Beaumont settled his accounts. He told you he was a Friend of the Passage, which opens Passage kitchens and courier doors from the Midchain down to Calveno. He passed along what Nona Black-Jaw had asked him to carry, that her grandson was missing, and that somebody had already been sent looking for him. Then he sent a Truth Stone skidding over the deck to Jean-Claude, and Jean-Claude held it and told you the truth, that the men hunting him were his own kin, that he had come aboard the Saltwright as a stowaway, and that nobody had sent him. Bisou spent that morning carrying fifteen gold out of the Surety's stores one piece at a time, and Beaumont counted it as fair business. The Saltwright stood away for Calveno with Delmar calling across that Admiral Fisk was a friend of his, and Beaumont promising to look for you off Kalowe.
>
> The brig still had its tenant. Ket hung in a brass cage that Rook had nailed up down there, a Moucheron the size of a pigeon, and three weeks in it had left him thin and no gentler. Jean-Claude opened his own arm into a wine glass and pushed it through the bars, which calmed Ket and did not make him safe. Perrin drew his sword to end it, and Crissdalynn got in front of the bars and offered Ket her arm instead. Then Perrin's magic put Ket on the floor laughing, and while he was down you gave him the ensign's body and opened the cage from across the room. Ket fed for a minute, rose, and flew for Murrat, and Grigori watched him go and was glad.
>
> Grigori had the galley and he had terms. "Anyone who wants off at the next port walks free," he said, and then he gave you what he knew about Cap'n Gorgeous, and about Rupert Knighton, and said he would leave you at Calveno. You named your prize crew that day, Geoffrey Draves, Old Faas, Sem Holst, Alys Kuiper, Noor and Thunk, and Perrin told them what he was, a Black-Jaw.
>
> A tropical storm caught you that night, with the rain coming sideways. Then something hit the hull from below. It hit again. The third one put the prize over on her side and left her there.

## Wiki facts

The prize is the cutter later renamed [[Uncertainty]]. [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont]] introduced himself to the crew as a Friend of the Passage, which is the crew's first door into [[Passage]]. [[Ket]] flew for [[Murrat]]. [[barnaby-rook]] went into the water off the Surety and did not surface, presumed dead. The twenty vials of Grung tincture in his crate are the crew's first sight of [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s supply chain, and the narration keeps that connection out of what the players hear, as it keeps [[shepherd-grigori]]'s nature out. Something struck the hull three times from below and is unresolved, and Nona Black-Jaw's hunt for her grandson is live. Next: [[Session-03-Recap]].

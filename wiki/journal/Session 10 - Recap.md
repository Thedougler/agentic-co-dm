# Session 10 - Recap

Aruhe. The island is still hungry.

> [!narration] Recap
> Last time on the Shattered Sea, you took the prize Uncertainty. Delmar Fisk's fleet had stolen the Pearl of Souls, and Umberlee had named that Pearl as her price. Otar the Foul died in the Mercatura crater. Nona Black-Jaw's list climbed past three hundred fourteen fighting-age men. You were bound for Sparhold, while Ordinance sailed for Calveno with your story.
>
> During the night voyage, Delmar trained Geoffrey Draves. Then Shepherd Grigori appeared below deck. He thanked you for warning Calveno and said Talon Vantyrus had sent a ship one or two hours behind you to take the Fate Spinner, the dreidel. He tied the hunt to the Sentinels of the Eyrie schism over fate and asked you to injure Corbin Knighton, not kill him or break him. You gave him no firm answer. Grigori also spoke of Delmar's Umberlee curse.
>
> The follower ship came. You slowed it, hit it with a broadside, and set its sails on fire. You left it listing and fled without boarding.
>
> At Aruhe, the Hungry Isle, you pulled two survivors aboard. Between one hundred and one hundred fifty people had come off the raid ships. Most were dead. The island was taking the rest. Taking fruit or other island things had gotten people killed, while fishing offshore seemed safer. The Grung were hunted hardest.
>
> Delmar, Jean-Claude, Crissdalynn, and Perrin went onto the terraces. They found wolfrabbits, fruit marked with blood and skulls, and at least six dead nearby. When Jean-Claude killed a spore plant, a crown squid rose from below, struck Delmar, and reached for the party. You escaped by illusion and flight. The squid stopped at the tree line and stayed in the water.
>
> You kept Uncertainty moving instead of anchoring close. Two survivors were aboard. Others were still inland. The island was still hungry.

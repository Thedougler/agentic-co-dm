# Murrat's Living Shroud

*A dark mass hangs over an island like weather. The buzzing names it before the ship gets close.*

![[murrats-living-shroud-narration-condition]]

## Actors

- [[moucheron|The Moucheron]] — defend their island against any outsider and kill anyone who lands. Millions circle the island in a permanent living cloud. The first wave targets everyone on deck, crew and party alike, and more follow from every direction.
- [[ket|Ket]] — flies out with the greeting party. Recognizes the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] and warns the crew to leave. Can hold himself back one turn. The others will not wait, and neither will he after that.

## Connections

- [[murrat|Murrat]] owns the island's geography, defenses, culture, and population.
- [[moucheron|Moucheron]] owns the species statblock, feeding clock, and mercenary culture.
- [[ket|Ket]] owns the debt history: freed by the crew in Session 02, flew home to Murrat.
- [[five-blades|Five Blades]] owns the mercenary connection out of [[kalowe|Kalowe]].

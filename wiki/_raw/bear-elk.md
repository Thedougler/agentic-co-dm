## Description

**Elk antlers on a bear's frame, bigger than a draft horse, built lower and wider.** Reddish-dark fur over a chest and shoulders thick enough to turn steel. Full-spread antlers drip with a luminous, viscous sap that glows faintly in the dark. Pale eyes with no visible pupil. Curved fangs and tusks that belong to neither animal. It covers fifty feet at a dead run, the ground shaking under it, faster than anything its weight suggests.

## Ecology

**[[blight|The Blight]]'s corruption fused the two largest animals on [[aruhe|Aruhe]] and didn't stop pushing.** The frame is a bear's, broad through the chest, low to the ground, built for power over distance. The rack is an elk's, full-spread antlers that would snap a normal animal's neck, dripping with a luminous sap that has no source in the animal's own biology. The sap comes from the Blight. It seeps from the antler velvet the way resin seeps from a wounded tree. It glows faintly in the dark, which is how most creatures on Aruhe know to get out of the way.

**The bear-elk doesn't need to eat often.** Its metabolism runs on corruption the same way the island's plants run on sunlight. It patrols because the Blight needs it to patrol, and it has been doing it long enough that every tree along the route carries claw-scored bark at chest height.

**Territory runs the boundary between [[aruhe-the-quiet|the Quiet]] and [[aruhe-the-rot|the Rot]].** The patrol is the same route, the same timing, every day. The challenge is that the route covers every approach between the two zones. A party that wants to pass must time the patrol or find an alternative.

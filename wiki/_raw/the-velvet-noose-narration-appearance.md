> [!narration]
> *The *[[the-velvet-noose|Velvet Noose]]* fills the horizon before the sound arrives. Her hull is black lacquer over dark red underpaint, banded in dull iron from waterline to gunwale. Three mastheads fly long velvet pennants the colour of dried wine.*
>
> *The gunports are closed, all ninety of them. At the prow, a gilded figurehead of a laughing magistrate holds a silk rope in one hand and a broken crown seal in the other. Tar and iron ride the wind well ahead of her bow wash.*
>
> *She does not chase small prizes. She sits in the lane and lets the first hour do the work.*

# Dravosi Crown

![[dravosi-crown-banner]]

*Imperial colonial authority operating through law, paper, and inspection. Order maintained through submission to the document.*

## Members

**The Crown operates under naval charter from Tethyr**, weeks north in [[faerun|Faerûn]], with [[tyr|Tyr]] (god of war and rule) presiding over inspection law, privateer commissions, and courts martial. In 1195 DR, it took the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]], built [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]], and secured the [[shattered-sea|Shattered Sea]] via forts and force. Its logic runs backward from the stamped page: a ship is safe because papers say so, a captain is good because the manifest matches the rating stamp.

**Enforcement instruments** include rating inspection (vessel, crew, cargo checked against registry standards), impound (ships trapped in fee disputes), privateer commissions (legal cover for private captains to inspect non-Crown vessels), species relocation bounty (pays for live-captured [[rattkin|Rattkin]]), and admiralty passes (open forts, piers, archives, sealed roads). On the water, patrol boats carry guns and cannons, privateer captains board with real force, and poison gas canisters supply an unknown Crown-maker network.

**Key individuals:** [[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]] (privateer captain, HCS Surety) fought at the [[saltwright|Saltwright]] in Session 01, still works for the Crown. [[rupert-knighton|Rupert Knighton]] (Commodore, [[knight|Knight]] Squadron) ranks above Rook. [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] (contracted fleet artificer) runs ship work and soldier kits through a binding Crown contract.

**[[aldric-drave|Aldric Drave]], a lich, seeded his bloodline into every Dravosi noble family before colonization.** His genealogical obsession (records, commissions, filings, bloodline certifications) hides him in plain sight. Officials treat deep documentation as proof of legitimacy. [[fort-crestwall|Fort Crestwall]] bears his house name, and the [[drave-vaults|Drave Vaults]] lie beneath it. "The Crown remembers" approaches literal truth.

**The Governor/Admiralty split is currently exploitable.** Governor Voss accepts proper documents, while the Admiralty resists shame but invites overreach. [[aldous-draves|Aldous Draves]], a Crown factor clerk, noticed the Drave family name appeared in Crown genealogical archives predating the colonial administration by more generations than the family's standing explains, and filed the anomalous depth under "old family." Crown custom treats deep documentation as legitimacy.

## Goals & Fronts

**The Crown rules via papers and control:** checks, holds, rights, bounty. It hides a [[grung|Grung]] poison deal with [[simone-tabarnack|Simone Tabarnack]] and keeps this work off any public clock.

**HCS Surety, Crown Search (active).** The Crown seeks the lost [[uncertainty|HCS Surety]] (Registry No. CS-1147) with force, sending courtesy-stop notices (naval grey cutter, brass bow plate, Crown pennant, 4 guns) to [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]]/[[harwick|Harwick]] automatically within ~2 weeks, then to [[kalowe|Kalowe]]/[[calveno|Calveno]] if the Crown presses. Within ~1 month, [[commander-gideon-ault|Commander Gideon Ault]] ([[hcs-warrant|HCS Warrant]]) arrives to detain crew and seize cargo from [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]], whose Surety will tear from him by force.

**Aldric Drave's Phylactery Network (dormant).** Drave uses Crown seals and ledgers to keep his network safe for centuries while searching for living anchors through unwitting Crown staff. Extra forces hold [[fort-crestwall|Fort Crestwall]] under secret orders, creating risk for party members with Crown ties.

Triggers run when the genealogical anomaly, vaults, and garrison connect into one thread; when someone kills or converts a living anchor (forcing Drave to respond openly); or when someone exploits the Governor/Admiralty split to reach Fort Crestwall's sub-basements.

At fill, Drave reviews every anchor, secures the vulnerable ones, then folds secret troops into the garrison. The genealogical anomaly closes, the network deepens its cover, and the [[saltwright|Saltwright]]'s loose thread disappears into shadow.

Party members Delmar Fisk, [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]], and [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] all have Crown ties (commission, heritage, or bounty).

The trail runs through three clues: [[aldous-draves|Aldous Draves]] (Crown clerk) filed the anomalous archive depth as "old family" (reachable via records); the [[drave-vaults|Drave Vaults]] show sealed sub-basements listed as construction errors (reachable via archive); and Fort Crestwall garrison holds excess troops under unissued orders (reachable via conversation).

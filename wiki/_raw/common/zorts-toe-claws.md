# Zort's Toe-Claws

> [!read-aloud]
> A pair of open sandals, the leather worn black at the heel and stiff as board everywhere else.
>
> Each toe sits in its own socket, and filed iron caps every socket in a hooked point.
>
> They weigh more than they look, and one set down on bare dirt does not slide.
>
> Short straps, sized for a foot that does a hand's work.

```meta-bind
VIEW[{reference_image}][image(class(reference-image-view))]
```

*Tool (Footwear), Common.* [[zort|Zort]] has a cobbler in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] cut these to his own pattern and sells the spare pairs to anyone whose work involves a beast digging in its heels. Larger sizes take a week. Weighs 4 pounds.

## Mechanics

**[HB] Set.** While you wear the toe-claws on bare feet and stand on dirt, wood, stone, or deck, you have advantage on ability checks and saving throws made to resist being grappled, shoved, or knocked prone.

**[HB] Hook and Climb.** The iron points take a bite on rough vertical surfaces. You climb at your full speed on wood, rope, netting, and cage bar, without needing a check on a surface a ladder could serve.

**Limitations:** bare feet only, so a wearer keeps boots off while they are on. A wearer standing in a boat's bilge, deep mud, loose sand, or ice gets neither benefit, and nothing here answers a force effect that moves you without touching you.

## Provenance

[[zort|Zort]] has worn a pair every working day since he lost his arms, and sells the overruns off a nail on the desk at [[zorts-pits|Zort's Pits]]. He fits every buyer himself.

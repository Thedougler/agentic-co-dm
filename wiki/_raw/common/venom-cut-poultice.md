# Venom-Cut Poultice

> [!read-aloud]
> A palm of grey paste folded into a sailcloth square, tied off with waxed twine and chalked with a watch tally.
>
> The cloth has gone stiff where the paste dried at the edges, soft in the middle, still giving under a thumb.
>
> It smells of chewed root and slaked lime, sharp enough to sting the back of your throat from a foot away.
>
> The chalk mark is a date, and the date is eleven days old.

*Consumable (Poultice), Common.* Sparhold's watch keeps a crate behind every post, made up in batches whenever the timber crews come back off the Teeth fringe with someone bitten.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **Venom-Cut Poultice [HB].** Single use. As an Action, press it to a wound on yourself or a creature within 5 feet. If that creature is poisoned by a poison or by a creature's venom, the condition ends, and the creature has advantage on saving throws against poison for the next hour.
>
> **Edge cases:** it does nothing for a poisoned condition inflicted by a spell or other magic. A second poultice inside the hour neither stacks the advantage nor extends it.
>
> **Limitations:** restores no hit points, ends no disease, and neutralises nothing still sitting in a vial. Once the twine is cut, the paste dries out inside a minute.

## Provenance

[[sparhold|Sparhold]] has made these since the [[grung-clans|Grung]] raids started climbing out of the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]], and the loft's crate comes out of the same batch the harvest crews carry.

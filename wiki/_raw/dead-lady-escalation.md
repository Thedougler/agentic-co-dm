# Dead Lady — the tribute rampage

[[umberlee|Umberlee]]'s deadline for Delmar passed with no visible movement toward the Maw — nine-odd weeks of him in [[calveno|Calveno]] chasing Otar, buying chairs, teaching Geoffrey card tricks, anything but the Pearl. She doesn't wait out the [[dead-lady|*Dead Lady*]]'s own escalation stages. She starts taking payment herself.

---

Dumping cargo overboard isn't contempt, and it isn't a search coming up empty. It's tribute, her oldest and preferred method. Sailors already throw offerings into the water at shrine basins before a crossing — this is the same transaction with no priest between her and the goods, and no consent asked.

---

Every ship the *Dead Lady* takes loses everyone aboard. No survivors, no negotiation, no pattern to exploit — a shrine tribute is voluntary, this isn't. The only trace left behind is the boat itself: found adrift, holds emptied over the side, crew dead.

---

Whoever finds one of these boats — or whoever's aboard when she comes — sees the drowned crew of the [[red-lady|*Red Lady*]] still working her decks. Spectral, silent, recognizable to anyone who sailed with Fisk's fleet. Bound to her will, still doing the only job death left them.

---

For now it stays fisherman's stories and dock gossip — a red ship that comes out of fog, takes a boat whole, and leaves it drifting empty. Nobody's connected it to Delmar Fisk yet. Nobody's supposed to, not yet.

---

The party first hears it at [[kalowe|Kalowe]] — tavern and dock talk, no single source, nobody owning the story. With the old tithe-ghost hook cleared out, a red ship that takes a boat whole has the free port's haunted-derelict rumor space to itself.

# Fen

A young sailor aboard the Saltwright under [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]]. One of four crew members in [[beaumonts-crew|Beaumont's Crew]], with [[drav-holke|Drav Holke]], [[lenne-vor|Lenne Vor]], and [[wessa|Wessa]].

Seventeen or eighteen. Still growing a beard. He follows orders and does good work.

**Roleplay anchor:** young, earnest, serious about his first sea job.

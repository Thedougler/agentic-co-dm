# Voyvode
> [!narration] Narration
> Voyvode's eagle flies over the opposite Fathomrush pier. Its gate stays open during working hours so strangers can watch a real trial fail.

## Public goal
Prove a working Crown pressure rig and claim the deep shelf honestly.

## Secret goal
Reach the Mid-Works before Sunkline's fraud becomes public, even if honest trials continue to spend crews.

## Methods
Open trials, incomplete but honest notes, Crown money, and company gate access. [[Branimir Karsovine]] will not send a crew under an untested rig.

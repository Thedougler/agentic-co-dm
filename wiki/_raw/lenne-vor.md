# Lenne Vor

Navigator aboard [[saltwright|the Saltwright]]. Works for [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]]. One of four crew members with [[drav-holke|Drav Holke]], [[fen|Fen]], and [[wessa|Wessa]]. All are part of [[beaumonts-crew|Beaumont's Crew]].

Lean and middle-aged, with silver hair cropped close and fingertips stained by twenty years of chart work. She has charted the [[midchain|Midchain]] under three captains. She reads her charts once, then trusts them.

**Roleplay anchor:** methodical skeptic. She has documented everything. She is right more often than not.

## Relationships

- [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]], captain of the Saltwright and her employer.
- [[drav-holke|Drav Holke]], [[fen|Fen]], [[wessa|Wessa]], fellow crew aboard the Saltwright and members of [[beaumonts-crew|Beaumont's Crew]].

# The Knife's Wake

> [!read-aloud]
> A low cutter comes on with patched tan canvas and a tired brown hull, the kind of working boat that should smell of fish and wet rope. Then the gunports open. Black-painted teeth show along the rail, boarding hooks come up from behind the bulwark. The false trade pennant drops before the real flag rises, and what rises is a narrow black streamer, sharp as a knife wound.

*[[pirate|Pirate]] cutter · Tier 1 · unmoored.*

The *Knife's Wake* is a pirate cutter built for quick strikes and fast escape. She looks like a worn trading ship from far away. Up close, the disguise reveals too many clues. Her gun ports are too clean, her rail too bare, and her crew too ready.

She hunts shallow waters, reefs, and harbor mouths. She loves dusk and fog. She is the worst ship to meet in tight waters, but she flees from bigger ships in open sea.

## Purpose

The *Knife's Wake* hunts small valuable prizes. Payroll chests, passenger jewelry, locked dispatch boxes, contraband, spell components, officers worth ransoming, and cargo whose owner cannot safely report the loss all draw her attention.

Her best attack hinges on a short deception. She sails under false colours and closes range, fires one disabling volley to cripple the target's maneuverability, then commits to boarding before the crew can recover and organize a proper defense.

## Layout

**Weather Deck.** Kept clear for rapid boarding. Light cannons sit amidships behind removable false cargo screens (four in standard configuration). Swivel positions at bow and stern. Boarding hooks and weighted lines stowed under the rail.

**Aft Cabin.** One narrow bunk, bolted chart shelf, locked strongbox, rack of false pennants. Real papers in oilcloth below a removable sole plank.

**Below Decks.** Cramped crew hammocks forward, powder and shot amidships, plunder cages aft.

## Operational Notes

**Ambush posture:** prefers dusk, fog, port congestion, shoals, and damaged targets. First volley aims at sails, rudder, and morale.

**Weakness:** cannot win a sustained gun duel. A disciplined patrol cutter that keeps distance and refuses the boarding angle has the advantage.

**Legal risk:** the hull is not registered cleanly, and many fittings likely came from ships whose owners still have friends in port offices.

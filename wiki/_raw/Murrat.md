# Murrat
> [!narration] Narration
> Murrat is a mile-and-a-half limestone reef that looks empty from the water until canopy closes twenty feet inland. Cliff hollows and rope bridges hold kin-villages above the tide. Limestone, wet rope, and leaf mould mark longer eastern reef than a first glance admits.



## At a glance
A mile-and-a-half limestone reef that looks empty until canopy closes twenty feet inland; feels hunted before the first rope bridge.

## Aspects
`cliff villages, blood currency, longer reef`

## Senses
- Empty reef from water, dense canopy, cliff hollows, and rope bridges.
- Tide pulling east and movement above.
- Limestone, wet rope, and leaf mould.

## Who is here
- Dozens of Moucheron kin-villages.
- Outsiders are hunted.

## Connections
- → [[Midchain]] · [[Sparhold Isle]] · [[Verdant Teeth]]

## Hooks
- Land only with a reason that survives local blood custom.
- Find the eastern reef's true length before the tide pulls a boat onto two existing wrecks.

## Secrets (DM)
The kin-villages' internal law and the reason for the two wrecks remain open.

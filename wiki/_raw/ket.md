# Ket

![[ket-banner]]
![[ket-portrait]]

A Moucheron from Murrat, pigeon-sized, sentient, a starving humanoid mosquito, is neither integrated nor mercenary. [[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]] went ashore on Murrat and took him, trapped and caged, brought back as a specimen before the village could respond. Three weeks later the crew of the HCS Surety freed him.

A brass birdcage hung from a ship nail in the Surety's brig, holding him. Feral, he treats every warm-blooded creature as potential prey, and three weeks caged made him hungrier. He speaks Common fluently and talks in ways that seem engaging, but none of it's sincere. Every word is bait. He wants people to forget how close he stands to them.

**Sample lines:**

- *"Come closer. I won't bite."*
- *"You're bleeding a little. From the fight. I can smell it."*
- *"Just your hand for a moment."*

**Session 02, Freed:** [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] offered a wine glass of his own blood, which Ket drank to grow calmer (though not safe) while [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] drew his longsword to execute him. [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] deflected the blade and placed herself between Perrin and the cage, stepping past the safe distance so Ket could feed from her arm before Perrin cast Tasha's [[hideous-laughter|Hideous Laughter]] and dropped him. To free him, they used [[ensign-wouters|Ensign Wouters]]' body as a lure and released the cage from a safe distance with [[mage-hand|Mage Hand]], allowing Ket to feed on the body for about a minute before flying toward Murrat, leaving [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] glad and the crew (who had always hated what Rook did to him) vindicated.

He is free, [[flying|flying]] toward Murrat.

> [!mechanic]
> Owes the crew in the way a freed prisoner owes people. It's complicated and not guaranteed. Open question: why was he this far from Murrat, and what does he know about the [[five-blades|Five Blades]] connection Rook was curious about? If the crew reaches Murrat, [[varet|Varet]] will recognize him as Murrat-born immediately.

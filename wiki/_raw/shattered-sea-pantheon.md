# Shattered Sea Pantheon

*Regional Index of [[campaign-overview|the Shattered Sea]]'s divine landscape, shaped by water, risk, and law.*

Every deity worshipped here belongs to the wider [[faerun|Faerûnian]] pantheon. The colonial charters carried these faiths south with them, and the Scatter kept the ones that suited a life on the water.

## The Fact

[[umberlee|Umberlee]] dominates maritime practice as [[shattered-sea|the Shattered Sea]]'s public sea deity. Sailors pay because the sea kills, not because she is kind.

[[valkur|Valkur]] and [[tyr|Tyr]] act as counterweights to her. Valkur is sailor courage, favorable winds, and private hope, invoked quietly below decks. He has no formal temples in [[verdant-scatter|the Scatter]]. Tyr is Dravosi law, oaths, commissions, courts martial, and civic order. His clergy staff Dravosi military installations and the larger Crown garrison posts.

Neither Valkur nor Tyr has appeared at the table yet. Each carries its own independently-earned `status`, not one inherited from this Index. [[umberlee|Umberlee]] alone is `canon`, earned on play evidence.

A separate sky-pantheon governs [[aarakocra|Aarakocra]] observance. [[remnis|Remnis]] is [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]]'s primary deity. [[syranita|Syranita]] holds secondary observance in her margins, and [[aerdrie-faenya|Aerdrie Faenya]] holds a marginal tertiary place beside it.

Most sailors interact with divinity through tribute, oath, and habit, not theology. Umberlee's public faith is port infrastructure administered by the [[waveservants|Waveservants]], plain text, not a wikilink, since no dedicated doctrine/shrine-custom page exists yet in `vault/`.

Every patron in this pantheon holds hallowed ground the same way: a live claim it has to keep actively backing. [[sin-and-sanctuary|Sin and Sanctuary]] covers how that claim works, why it can go silent, and what happens when a fiend tests it anyway.

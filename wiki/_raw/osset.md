## Relationships

[[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] found Osset's name in Session 08. He spotted the scrap of paper at [[cabinet-of-morsani|the Cabinet of Morsani]]. He pocketed it unseen (Sleight of Hand 25) and read it later (Insight 17). It named Ossanth, or Osset: once a monk of the [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]] and a master among the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]]. He left the order during the schism and went unheard of since.

That same session, [[lavinia-sordi|Lavinia Sordi]] of [[la-cenere|La Cenere]] named him too. She said a falcon-featured man calling himself Osset sold her the cursed [[cloak-of-displacement|Cloak of Displacement]]. She later passed it to [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. Confirmed: [[transcript]].

- [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]]: former member, left during the schism; now trades on his old standing there to run an errand for [[talon-vantyrus|Talon Vantyrus]] of [[the-countless|the Countless]].
- [[lavinia-sordi|Lavinia Sordi]]: sold her the cursed [[cloak-of-displacement|Cloak of Displacement]].
- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]]: the Sentinel he's quietly reading for Vantyrus; she's never heard his name from anyone at the Eyrie.
- [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]]: an old classmate at the High Eyrie who'd recognize his face on sight. Osset avoids any port where they might cross paths.

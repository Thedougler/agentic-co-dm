# The Sunken Crown

![[sunken-crown-banner]]

The Sunken Crown sits in the [[outer-reach|Outer Reach]], northeast of the Drowned Maw crossing. Low islands ring a deep Blue Hole, and the hole takes up most of the water. The islands are the rim of something drowned: black stone, salt grass, wind-bent scrub, and cliffs marked with old waterlines like scars.

Colonial charts thin out before these waters. Most pilots know the place as a rumour, or as a note telling them to steer wide. The people who live here have been here longer than any outside record admits.

Something has unsettled the waters around the Blue Hole lately. Currents reverse between tidal cycles, fish avoid channels they have always run, and weather turns faster than the sky gives notice.

## Locations Within

The Blue Hole takes up most of the Sunken Crown. From high ground it reads as a dark circle inside the reef rim, so deep that a sounding line runs out before the bottom does.

Old volcanic heat still warms the water in patches. Near the edge it runs clear enough to show large shadows moving far below, and the line between turquoise shallow and indigo drop is clean.

## Routes & Access

Requires crossing east of [[the-drowned-maw|the Drowned Maw]] into the [[outer-reach|Outer Reach]]: six hours over the Maw, then open water. Reefs and channels surround these five islands, and they shift unpredictably. Without local knowledge, reef damage becomes likely, and currents can pull you toward the Blue Hole's drop-off. Refer to [[wilderness-travel-and-exploration|travel mechanics]] for maritime journey pacing.

## Hazards

The Blue Hole itself becomes the primary hazard of this region. Soundings fail in its depths. Currents around the rim shift with tides, making navigation treacherous for unprepared crews. Reefs damage unwary ships. The [[sawek|Sawek]] hunts in the depths below. Unstable weather and the failing sea blessing create unpredictable conditions. Dragon turtle wyrmlings nest on the outer beaches and defend their territory fiercely.

## People & Power

The Tabaxi of [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]] organize the region through elders, watch rotations, seasonal knowledge, refuge practice, and shrine obligations.

Without the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]], the shrine still marks debts and remembers the drowned, but its deepest function (stabilizing the Blue Hole and protecting nesting waters) is failing. [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]] stole the Pearl from [[vel-orn|Vel Orn]].

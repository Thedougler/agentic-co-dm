# Widow's Toast

> [!read-aloud]
> A short glass of rum poured near-black, the surface catching lamplight like something about to spill. [[ysolde-sallow|Ysolde]] sets it down flat, same line every time: "Widow's Toast, and no, before you ask, nobody here's ever worn the name." Nobody ever has. You can no longer tell if it's still a joke. It smells of burnt sugar and something darker underneath, sweet at first sip and bitter by the second.

*Consumable (Drink), Mundane.* The house pour at [[the-long-toast|The Long Toast]], dark rum served with a joke about widowhood that stopped landing once nobody on [[otel-karn|Otel Karn]] kept becoming one. One glass counts as one drink, with ordinary intoxication and no other effect.

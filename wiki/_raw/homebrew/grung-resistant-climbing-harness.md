# Grung-Resistant Climbing Harness

> [!read-aloud]
> Treated cordage darker than raw rope wraps this harness, the knots bearing the methodical over-and-back pattern of hands that have done this a thousand times over damp stone. The leather backing where it wraps the ribs carries a faint sheen, oil-treated and scored in cross-hatches fine enough to catch light but deep enough to shed whatever poison-blade venom leaves a trail across skin. The stitching is thick, copper-colored, and every seam wraps with cured hemp so nothing splits under tension or venom-burn.

*Tool, Common.* A climber's harness purpose-built for expeditions into hostile territory where footing fails and air itself carries a sting. Includes secured anchor points, deep pockets sewn at the hip for securing rope coils during ascent, and quick-release buckles.

> [!mechanic]
> **Quick-Release Buckles:** Dexterity DC 8. On a success, jam the mechanism and render the buckles inoperative. On a failure, the buckles remain functional.

Designed to withstand the wear of [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] expeditions, this harness grants advantage on Acrobatics checks made while climbing forest canopy or scaling wet stone where handholds are scarce. The venom-resistant leather reduces poison damage taken from climbing surfaces or rope-work contact with venomous opponents by 2 (to a maximum reduction of 1 damage).

# Tabaxi Culture

*Origin and presence of the Tabaxi people across the Shattered Sea.*

## The Culture

The Cat Lord created the Tabaxi, Medium feline humanoids with a strong territorial streak. [[campaign-overview|This campaign]] occupies [[shattered-sea|the Shattered Sea]], where Tabaxi presence is sparse but old. The [[keth-naar|Keth Naar]] community in the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]] has lived beyond easy colonial contact for generations, and [[elder-anton|Elder Anton]] is the campaign's known Tabaxi from it. See [[tabaxi|Tabaxi]] for the people's mechanical traits.

## Description

**Wrong deer standing upright on two legs.** Digitigrade, nine feet tall, full antler rack, elongated arms ending in claw-like hands matted with dried blood. The eyes sit forward in the skull like a predator's, fixed and unblinking, where a deer's eyes belong on the sides. It stands on a dark forest path in a posture that is neither Human nor animal, and it does not move when you look at it. It stands there. It already stood there.

## Ecology

These were deer. The [[aruhe|Blight]]'s corruption restructured them. The hips rotated. The hooves split into gripping digits. The jaw rebuilt itself around incisors that have no business in an herbivore's skull. Everything about the body says predator in the language of a prey animal's frame, and the dissonance is what makes them wrong to look at.

**They do not herd. They do not graze.** They stand in the tree line and watch, and they remember which way their target walked. Solitary. Two deer-stalkers in the same territory ignore each other entirely, never competing for the same prey. Found from [[aruhe-the-quiet|the Quiet]] into [[aruhe-the-rot|the Rot]], more common in the inner jungle where the canopy cuts the light early.

A deer-stalker picks a target and follows it for hours, motionless at the edge of vision whenever looked at directly. It never approaches during daylight. At night it closes the distance in complete silence, arriving at the campfire's edge without a sound. It prefers isolated targets: a party member on watch alone, someone who wandered from the group. It does not attack groups head-on unless cornered.

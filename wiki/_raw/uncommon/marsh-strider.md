# Marsh Strider

> [!read-aloud]
> A wading bird in a tall pen, grey-legged, its head level with your eyes and turned sideways to keep one on you.
>
> Hard scale runs the length of the legs, knuckled at the knee, thin as a boat hook down at the ankle.
>
> A saddle pad hangs over the rail beside it, sweat-stiff, cut narrow.
>
> It shifts its weight, and the whole pen rail knocks against the post.

*Tool (Trained Animal), Uncommon.* A domesticated fen wader kept across the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] for canal-side hauling and shallow crossings, sold at [[zorts-pits|Zort's Pits]] saddle-broke with pad and reins. Large beast, speed 50 ft., and it carries 480 pounds without slowing. It swims poorly and does not fight.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **[HB] High Water.** A strider crosses mud, flooded street, marsh, and water up to 4 feet deep at full speed and treats it as ordinary ground. Its rider is 8 feet off the surface while mounted.
>
> **[HB] Step Over.** As part of its move, a mounted strider can step over a Medium or smaller creature's space without provoking an opportunity attack from that creature. A creature attempting to grapple the rider from the ground does so at disadvantage.
>
> **[HB] Spooks.** The strider is a beast with no combat training. If it takes any damage, its rider must succeed on a DC 12 [[wisdom|Wisdom]] (Animal Handling) check as a reaction or the strider bolts 50 feet in a random direction, carrying the rider with it and ending the rider's turn.
>
> **Limitations:** one Medium rider and no more, no barding, no deep water, no stairs, and nothing a horse would refuse. Ride it into a fight and the Spooks check comes up inside a round or two.

## Provenance

Raised on the fen edge past [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] and finished in [[zort|Zort]]'s pit, where the goblin hands walk each one through the crowd on the canal walk until it stops caring about people.

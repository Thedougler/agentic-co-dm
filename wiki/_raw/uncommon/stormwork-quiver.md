## Mechanics

**[HB] Lightning Infusion (at-will).** The quiver infuses each drawn arrow with lightning. On a hit, the arrow deals its normal damage plus 1d6 lightning damage. Works at will. Attunement gates the effect.

**Limitations.** Only affects arrows (not bolts, javelins, spell attacks, or melee strikes). Any arrow that leaves the quiver unfired loses its charge immediately. The quiver holds 20 arrows.

## Provenance

Three years ago the Vollask's gnomish crew made the first batch for self-defense during a crossing. Traders kept asking what was in their quiver. The crew started selling the surplus. The gnomes worked the lining in a material no one outside the crew recognizes. Nobody who has asked has gotten a coherent answer. The effect is reliable despite the mystery.

[[cotter-foss|Cotter Foss]] keeps one behind normal shelf stock at [[nimmik-vollask|Nimmik Vollask]]. Other items are available through him.

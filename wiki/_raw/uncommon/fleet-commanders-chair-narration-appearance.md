> [!narration]
> *The [[fleet-commanders-chair|Fleet Commander's Chair]] is a heavy armchair, none the worse for the crater it came out of. Nothing scratches it. Nothing dents it.*
>
> *Privateer wear marks the arms where hands have gripped them through weather. The wood is dark, the joinery tight, the seat a little too upright for comfort. A hollow in the base holds whatever the last occupant left there.*
>
> *Whoever sits in it sits a little straighter without meaning to. The chair does not creak. Even the floor under it gives before the legs do. Dust will not stay on the seat.*

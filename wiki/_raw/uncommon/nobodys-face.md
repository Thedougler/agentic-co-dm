# Nobody's Face

> [!read-aloud]
> The paper is the flat grey of a wet flagstone, unmarked, with no printer's stamp and no wax at either end.
>
> Nothing comes off it. Hold it under your nose and there is less smell there than in the air around it.
>
> It weighs almost nothing, and the leaf gives under your thumb like felt.
>
> Lit, it puts up a thin line of smoke that climbs to chin height and stops, and that smoke has no smell either.

**Rarity:** the Uncommon tier holds this without argument. Hat of Disguise runs all day off an attunement slot and changes what a watcher sees while they are watching. Nobody's Face does nothing in the moment. It spoils the account afterward, once, for an hour, and it cannot save a smoker whose watcher simply keeps them in sight.

**PC connection:** buying off this board is public by design, and Nicco talks at full volume about what his regulars smoke. A crew that wants a blend without the tier remembering which of them carried it away buys this one first and the rest second.

## Mechanics

**[HB]** Lighting Nobody's Face and taking the first breath costs an Action. For 1 hour afterward, every creature that sees the smoker loses the memory of that face as soon as they look away.

An observer who breaks line of sight for a full round loses the smoker's face that instant. Any later attempt to describe them, name them from a description, pick them from a line of suspects, or point them out in a crowd fails. Magic that reads a mind for the memory finds the same blank the observer has.

Edge cases:

- A creature that already knew the smoker well before the hour began recognises them normally.
- Keeping the smoker in unbroken sight defeats it entirely, since the blend spoils the memory and leaves the eye alone.
- It touches nothing the smoker leaves behind, so a written name, a dropped glove, or a voice somebody knows all survive the hour.

**Limitations:** it grants no bonus to any check and covers nobody but the smoker, and a watcher who holds them in sight sees them plainly the whole hour. Witnesses recall what the smoker did in full detail, minus the face that did it.

## Provenance

The mis-bound run came off [[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]]'s benches unsellable, because a grader works by the nose and a blend with no smell defeats one, and no licensed room burns an ungraded run. Nicco Kettley took the batch entire and has never once suggested a use for it to a customer.

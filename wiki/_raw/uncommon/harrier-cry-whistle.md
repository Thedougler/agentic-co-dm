# Harrier-Cry Whistle

> [!read-aloud]
> A finger of hollow bone on a leather cord, sanded down to the grain, one fingerhole burned through the side.
>
> The mouth end is cut on the slant and stained darker than the rest.
>
> Held level it turns slowly on its own cord, and the draught coming through it makes a note too thin to place.
>
> Cover the hole and the note climbs until it stops sounding like breath and starts sounding like a bird.

VIEW[{reference_image}][image(class(reference-image-view))]

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon.* Cut from the wing bone of a crimson-crowned harrier, whose hunting cry breaks like a dropped pane.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **[HB] Cry (action).** The whistle holds 3 charges and regains all of them at dawn. Spend a charge and choose one creature within 30 feet that can hear you. It must succeed on a DC 13 [[constitution|Constitution]] saving throw or lose concentration on a spell it is maintaining, and it takes 1d4 thunder damage whether it saves or not.
>
> **Limitations:** no effect on a deafened creature, on anything immune to thunder damage, or on a creature that cannot hear over its own noise. Every blast carries roughly 300 feet, and everything hunting in that radius hears a wounded bird.

## Provenance

Cut by [[sabine-corvo|Sabine Corvo]] from a harrier that died in transit, and sold at the counter of [[the-live-hold|The Live Hold]] beside the cage of the one that lived.

## Behavior

**Habitat.** Terror-Birds hold the shaded rims, grass cuts, and hard-running lanes around [[Aruhe - Grasslands|the Grasslands]] and [[Aruhe - River|the River]]. Each adult claims about a quarter-mile of edge territory where open ground gives it room for one committed charge.

**Behavior.** A still Terror-Bird reads as a mossed trunk with one yellow eye until it chooses to move. It does not fly; the ragged wings are balance, threat display, and body-language around the charge.

**Diet.** It runs down exposed prey, swallows smaller bodies whole, and leaves flattened digest-circles, bone, and sour bolus where it has fed. The current Aruhe frame is pressure from the Blight gardens, not possession, so its hunger remains animal rather than commanded.

**Social Structure.** Solitary. Each bird holds a claim and avoids other oversized Aruhe predators by instinct; [[Aruhe - Bear-Elk|Bear-Elk]] routes get room, while [[Aruhe - Crown Squid|crown squid]] and [[Aruhe - Bloodhawk|bloodhawks]] only take a Terror-Bird when the terrain gives them a cleaner angle.

## Tactics

**Signs.** A yellow eye inside mossed black feathers, trunk-like stillness at a shaded rim, dust hopping on the path, tremors underfoot, talon prints deeper than a person's hand, and a sour feeding circle pressed flat in the grass.

**Instincts.** It waits as cover, lets the ground-shake announce the danger, and commits to one straight rush when prey breaks into the open. Eight-foot grass, deep water, or a white [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass|razer-grass]] stand ends the hunt.

**Tactics.** It opens with the visible charge rather than subtle stalking: close fast, clamp with Serrated Beak, rake anything that stays near, and Swallow a Medium or smaller grappled target when it can isolate one body from the group.

**Weaknesses.** Its strength is commitment, not turning. It is bad at sharp changes of direction, dense grass, deep water, razer-grass barriers, and prey that refuses the open lane it wants.

**Aftermath.** A Terror-Bird encounter leaves torn moss, claw furrows, churned dust, crushed grass, sour bolus, bone scraps, and a lane through the jungle where smaller creatures went silent after the charge passed.

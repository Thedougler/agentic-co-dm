# Sentinels of the Eyrie

![[sentinels-of-the-eyrie-banner]]

*[[aarakocra|Aarakocra]] monks who keep an unbroken, uninterpreted observation record of The Drowned Maw, and refuse to let anyone turn that record into a weapon.*

## Members

[[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] is the order's current senior master, managing the Maw-crisis pressure day by day. [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]] trained fully before her pilgrimage and remains the crew's closest connection to the order.

Established at [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]] in 895 DR to counter the [[waveservants|Waveservants]], the Sentinels hold that observation stands apart from interpretation. They record what they witness: weather, wrecks, anomalies, ship movements. They record without conclusions or editorializing. Researchers occasionally access the public record at the Eyrie, though few have the context to read it. The order's doctrine says recording comes clean, presence changes what exists, and long memory trumps certainty.

Every student undertakes a pilgrimage. Students work Maw-perimeter circuits, document anomalies, or map reef systems from direct observation. At completion, the master grants an Eldritch Claw Tattoo with an active ability called the Long Reach. The Long Sight is rarer still, reaching toward fate itself. Only the masters grant it, to a handful across the order's whole history. Kyzil gave Crissdalynn early access through her [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]] dreidel, an exception tied to her pilgrimage's unusual danger, though she doesn't yet know what it does.

A [[soul-incarnate|Soul Incarnate]] lies beneath the Eyrie. This body-phylactery lich is neutral and sealed, preserving two centuries of records as the order's true head. Kyzil doesn't know it exists as a conscious entity; he thinks the founding documents are simply "very old." The current Maw escalation exceeds anything in the ledger's history, and it has begun engaging actively instead of passively. Surface observation finds no trace of [[auralis|Auralis]] across two centuries, yet both watch from opposite sides, unaware of each other.

A schism split the order. [[osset|Osset]], a former master, left and vanished. In Session 08, Delmar found a torn scrap naming him at [[cabinet-of-morsani|the Cabinet of Morsani]], and Osset resurfaced the same session in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]], where he sold a cursed cloak. Crissdalynn has never heard his name from anyone at the Eyrie. His reappearance and current whereabouts remain unknown to the order.

## Goals & Fronts

Agenda: maintain [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]] observation record without interpreting it, and refuse roles as an instrument (scouts, witnesses, analysts) for outside powers.

No Front clock governs this faction. The order's core unrevealed tension (gestured at above) remains unmechanized.

### Front: The Soul Incarnate's Watch

**Lifecycle:** dormant

**Aim:** maintain [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]]'s unbroken, uninterpreted observation record indefinitely, including centuries beyond the observer's own death.

**Approach:** the [[soul-incarnate|Soul Incarnate]] watches and records without acting, commanding, or claiming allegiance, facing even severe Maw escalation with heightened attention instead of intervention.

**Off-screen move if unopposed:** it continues its observation while the Maw crisis escalates and the living lose access to the only complete record of what the Maw does across timescales.

**Trigger conditions:**

- Crissdalynn completes her pilgrimage and delivers her full account to [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]], closing the last gap between her and its attention.
- The Fissure Record's escalation crosses a threshold the two-century ledger has never recorded, past what already exceeds every precedent into something it has no pattern for classifying as mere observation.

**Clock:** 5 segments (moderate pace), filled: 0

**Consequence at fill:** it breaks two centuries of pure-observation doctrine and acts directly at The Drowned Maw for the first time since its sealing. The order's founding claim, an unbroken, uninterpreted record, stops being true the moment it happens, visibly enough that no later entry can record it as anything but action.

**Possible outcomes (2-3):** Crissdalynn reaches Kyzil first with a complete account, giving the order a channel to its knowledge that doesn't require it to break doctrine itself. Or the clock fills before she returns: it acts, exposing itself, and Kyzil learns the order's founding documents have a conscious author only after that author has already broken the doctrine he embodies. Or word of it reaches [[the-countless|the Countless]] first, handing [[talon-vantyrus|Talon Vantyrus]] a second route to the transformation technique that doesn't run through Crissdalynn's Long Sight at all.

**PC connection:** [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]]'s incomplete pilgrimage draws her closer to its attention than anyone else alive; her eventual return to Kyzil is the thread that leads here.

**Quest link:** none yet.

### Front: The Schism's Third Name

**Lifecycle:** active

**Aim:** deliver [[talon-vantyrus|Vantyrus]] a complete read of [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]] (how much [[soul-incarnate|Long Sight]] she actually carries, and what [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]] withheld from her about it), so the [[the-countless|Countless]]' move on the transformation technique stops being a gamble.

**Approach:** works the one part no disposable hire can. A master of his own time, he reads a Sentinel from the inside, trading on shared training and old [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]] names to get near the people he needs to judge. Items and favours are how he arrives somewhere, never why.

He will not approach Crissdalynn while Kyzil is close enough to notice. They were masters at the [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]] in the same years and Kyzil would know his face, so Osset waits out any stretch that puts them in the same port and moves only once she is beyond her own master's reach.

**Off-screen move if unopposed:** moves closer to Crissdalynn's circle through grey-market and pilgrim traffic, gathering what the order recorded about her pilgrimage and what it chose not to.

**Trigger conditions:**

- Each time Osset learns something about Crissdalynn's Long Sight or her pilgrimage that Kyzil withheld from her, a segment fills.
- A segment fills if the crew takes him as a source on the order without knowing whose errand he runs.

**Clock:** 5 segments (moderate pace), filled: 0

**Consequence at fill:** Osset hands Vantyrus the finished read, and [[talon-skarn|Skarn]]'s strike stops depending on luck. Whatever Osset knows about why he left during the schism goes back into the Countless' structure with him, reachable only through the pair themselves.

**Possible outcomes (2-3):** the crew reaches Osset before the clock fills and learns firsthand why he left, and whose errand he has been running since. The clock could instead fill unseen if the crew treats a helpful former master as an ordinary source. Or Crissdalynn recognizes the doctrine in how he speaks before he finishes, and the read breaks off half-made.

**PC connection:** pressures Crissdalynn directly. She trained fully under the Sentinels and spent years under Kyzil's instruction without ever hearing Osset's name. Her exile runs on the promise that finishing her pilgrimage earns her the order's complete trust and its complete record. Osset proves the order already kept at least one name from her before she ever left for the Maw, whether by his own omission or a gap in what even reached him.

**Per-PC awareness:** [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] holds the physical evidence (the torn scrap naming Osset) and knows exactly what it said. Crissdalynn knows only that the name means nothing to her own training. Neither yet knows whether the scrap and the cloak point at the same reason.

**Quest link:** none yet.

Clue 2: the questions he asks. Anyone he has spoken to in the [[midchain|Midchain]] remembers a courteous man who wanted to know about pilgrims, patrol routes, and one young Sentinel, and who paid for the conversation without buying anything, reached through Investigation or by asking after him in a port he has already worked, with no combat required.
Clue 3: the Sentinels' own record at [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]], formally requesting or earning access to what the order recorded, or chose not to record, about who left during the schism. Reachable through standing and trust with Kyzil or the archive itself, not combat.

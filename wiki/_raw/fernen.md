# Fernen

![[fernen-banner]]

A sturdy two-masted brigantine, plain-hulled and built for endurance. Dark brown below the waterline, weathered timber above.

*Two-masted brigantine · Unmoored.*

Not revealed to the party: the fleet's ship names and captains are a secret held back at the table (see [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]]).

Fourth ship of [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]]. Mid-sized and without distinguishing characteristics, the Fernen was a working ship fitted for long-range independent operation. Below decks featured crew quarters, a compact galley, a hold fitted for extended provisions, an oversize water cistern, and a navigator's station charting the full eastern Scatter.

Lost with the rest of Fisk's Fleet when [[umberlee|Umberlee]] opened the [[the-drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]] beneath them. All hands lost. See [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]] for the full account of the sinking.

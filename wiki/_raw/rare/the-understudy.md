# The Understudy

> [!read-aloud]
> A square of stiff canvas folded down to the size of a folio, with a face painted on the topmost panel in stage colours, mouth open.
>
> Every fold line has gone soft and furred from use. The paint has cracked along them and been touched up more than once.
>
> It weighs almost nothing and smells of size glue and dust.
>
> The painted mouth is open around a word. Turn the folio over, and the face on the back is wearing yours.

## Mechanics

> [!mechanic]
> **The Understudy [HB].** The folio does this once per day and recovers at dawn. When an attacker the owner can see hits the owner with an attack roll, the owner may use their reaction to open the folio.
>
> The canvas unfolds into a life-size double of the owner in the owner's own space, wearing what the owner wears. The attack strikes the double instead. The double takes the hit, staggers, and collapses to the ground as a heap of canvas at the end of that turn, having absorbed the attack's damage and every rider on it. The owner takes nothing.
>
> Any creature that watched the double fall and has not seen the owner since makes a DC 15 [[wisdom|Wisdom]] (Insight) check to work out it was not the owner who dropped. The canvas can be refolded in 1 minute and is ready again at the next dawn.
>
> **Edge cases:** the double cannot be used against an effect that forces a saving throw rather than an attack roll, nor against an attack the owner cannot see coming. A creature with truesight sees painted canvas and is not fooled for a moment. The double never acts, moves, speaks, occupies a space of its own, or draws an opportunity attack.
>
> **Limitations:** one attack, once a day, and that is the whole of it. It grants no AC, no resistance, no temporary hit points, and no bonus to any roll. It cannot be spent on an ally, cannot be opened as an action to make a decoy in advance, and does nothing whatsoever against area damage, poison, or a failed save.

## Provenance

[[fioravante-selmerin|Fioravante Selmerin]] will say the canvas came out of a scene dock and will not say which, the same answer he gives about the velvet in [[fios-own-curtain|Fio's Own Curtain]].

The fold lines were soft before he owned it. [[cecco-trivani|Cecco Trivani]] counted the touch-ups in the paint one slow afternoon and made it eleven, which means eleven hits somebody else did not take.

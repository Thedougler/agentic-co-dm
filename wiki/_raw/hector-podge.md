# Hector Podge

![[hector-podge-banner]]

|               |                                                              |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Species**   | [[human\|Human]]                                                        |
| **Pronouns**  | he/him                                                       |
| **Location**  | [[hook-and-ledger\|Hook & Ledger]], [[tidefall\|Port Tidefall]] |
| **Role**      | Tavern proprietor                                            |

Heavyset and grey, settled as stone. He stopped moving fast years ago. He runs the [[hook-and-ledger|Hook & Ledger]] with quiet authority. He has outlasted seven garrison commanders and three harbourmasters, and he expects to outlast more.

He thinks about the [[central-strait|Strait]], about forms, about wine. He talks about these things. He calls this hospitality. Crown officers know that asking him directly just makes him answer about something else instead.

Proprietor of the [[hook-and-ledger|Hook & Ledger]], the Shelf tavern where the paper side of Tidefall comes to drink, he knows every regular by their drink, their rank, and the nature of their entanglements. Not a source of information so much as a man shaped like one.

> [!narration]
> *Grey-green water gives way to turquoise, then a blue so clear the reef shadows show through it. Islands crowd the horizon in loose ranks, none more than a half-day's sail from the next. Their coasts are forest, cliff, and pale sand.*
>
> *Smoke threads up from a dozen unmarked coves at once: cook fires, kilns, and something burning that probably should not. Gulls and fishing terns work the shallows in noisy clouds. Salt and green rot ride the wind together.*
>
> *A gap between two islands shows a current fighting the wind. A back channel, or a place better left alone. Only a pilot would know which.*

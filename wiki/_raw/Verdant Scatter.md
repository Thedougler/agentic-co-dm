# Verdant Scatter
> [!narration] Narration
> The Verdant Scatter is two green island arcs between Galewall and the Tail: Crown forts north and Midchain crowded small ground south. Reef, sails, and unofficial routes join them. Surf and inspection calls share the same brine as wet timber and forest cargo.



## At a glance
The inhabited chain between the Galewall/Ashwalls and the Tail/Maw, with Crown water north and Midchain south; feels crowded, green, and only partly charted.

## Aspects
`two arcs, crowded small ground, unofficial routes`

## Senses
- Green island arcs, reef, forts, and sails.
- Surf, pilots, and inspection calls.
- Brine, wet timber, forest, and cargo.

## Who is here
- Crown pilots and officials.
- Midchain councils and free ports.
- Grung clans, Passage, and crews moving between them.

## Connections
- → [[Shattered Sea]] · [[Crown Islands]] · [[Central Strait]] · [[Midchain]] · [[Tail]]

## Hooks
- Choose between the official road and a route someone learned first.
- Find a named island without turning every small place into a new government.

## Secrets (DM)
“Verdant Scatter” is the inhabited chain name in the dump; keep it distinct from the five-island [[Verdant Teeth]] band.
